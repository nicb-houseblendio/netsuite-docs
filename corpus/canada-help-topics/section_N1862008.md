The GST/HST Audit Summary report shows the GST paid on purchases and GST collected on sales, giving you the total GST owed or to be reclaimed.

To view the GST/HST Audit Summary report, go to _Reports > Sales Tax Canada > GST/HST Audit Summary_.

A message on the screen indicates that your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.
