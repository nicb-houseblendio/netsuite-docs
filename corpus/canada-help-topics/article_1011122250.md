After you have installed the Canada Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp, you can start using it to generate and view your report.

#### To generate a tax report using the Canada Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp: {#procedure_1011122428}

1.  Go to Reports > Tax > Country Tax Reports.
    
2.  From the **Subsidiary** list, select your subsidiary.
    
3.  From the **Nexus** list, if you have multiple nexuses in your subsidiary, select your Canada nexus.
    
4.  From the **Report Name** list, select **GST34 Worksheet**.
    
5.  Select the **Start Period** and **End Period** dates to include in the report.
    
6.  (Optional) From the **Accounting Book** list, select an accounting book to use in the report. The **Primary Accounting Book** is selected by default.
    
    Note:
    
    The **Accounting Book** list appears only in accounts with the Multi-book accounting feature enabled.
    
7.  Click **Generate**.
    
8.  On the **Report Execution** subtab, locate the generated report. The latest generated report appears at the top of the list.
    
9.  In the **View | Download** column, wait for the loading bar to complete and change to a **View** and **Download** link.
    
10.  To open the report, click **View**.
