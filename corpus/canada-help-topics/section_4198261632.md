## Overview {#bridgehead_4198261852}

The following help topics provide information about NetSuite features that are specific for companies doing business in Canada. The NetSuite International Edition can be configured with capabilities and reports specific to Canadian business setting and requirements.

To know more about localized editions of NetSuite, as well as setting up NetSuite accounts and subsidiaries, refer to the following help topics:

-   [NetSuite Editions Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N270055.html)
    
-   [Set up NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N268052.html)
    
-   [Creating Subsidiary Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N272471.html)
    
-   [Introduction to NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N266468.html) - for companies using NetSuite across multiple subsidiaries and countries
    

Please note that general NetSuite features are covered in other chapters and topics.

## General Setup {#bridgehead_156932414932}

[Canada Account Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160257422045.html): Provides an overview of NetSuite features to be enabled by the user in Canada and SuiteApps available for installation.

[Setting Up Canada-specific Preferences Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160259228116.html): Set up your NetSuite company and user preferences for Canada, including date, currency, language, time zone, and support for Canadian number, phone, and address format.
