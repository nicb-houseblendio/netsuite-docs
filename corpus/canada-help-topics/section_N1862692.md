The PST on Sales Summary report shows the total sales and other revenue (not including GST or PST), the PST collected or collectible for each sales transaction.

To view the PST on Sales Summary report, go to _Reports > Sales Tax Canada > PST on Sales_.

A message on the screen indicates that your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.
