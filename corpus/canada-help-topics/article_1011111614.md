You can install the Canada Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp from the SuiteApp Marketplace in NetSuite. For more information about installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace, see [Installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539799323.html).

The Canada Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp is a managed SuiteApp. After you install the SuiteApp, your accounts are automatically updated whenever an update to the SuiteApp is available.

For more information about how to generate and view your reports, see [Using the Canada Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1011122250.html).
