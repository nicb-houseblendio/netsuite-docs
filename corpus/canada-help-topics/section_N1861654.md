The GST/HST on Purchases Detail report shows the GST paid, net amount, applicable tax code, and tax amount for each purchase transaction.

To view the GST/HST on Purchases Detail report, go to _Reports > Sales Tax Canada > GST/HST on Purchases > Detail_.

A message on the screen indicates that your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.
