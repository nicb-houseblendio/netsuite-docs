Before you start working with Canadian tax reports, it is important to take the following information into account:

-   It is possible to have some differences between the transactions tax GL impact and tax amounts reflected on tax reports. The difference is due to rounding of tax amounts.
    
-   Tax GL impact is calculated as follows:
    
    | Step | Details |
    | --- | --- |
    | 1 | Tax is computed for each item line. |
    | 2 | Calculated tax amounts are rounded to two decimal places. |
    | 3 | Rounded tax amounts for item lines are summed up. |
    | 4 | Sum of rounded tax amounts is converted from the transaction currency to the subsidiary base currency. |
    
-   Tax amounts on tax reports are calculated as follows:
    
    | Step | Details |
    | --- | --- |
    | 1 | Tax is computed for each item line. |
    | 2 | Calculated tax amounts are converted from the transaction currency to the subsidiary base currency. |
    | 3 | Converted tax amounts are rounded to two decimal places. |
    | 4 | Rounded tax amounts for item lines are summed up. |
