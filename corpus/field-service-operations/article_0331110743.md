Field Service Management (FSM) includes saved search links in the Field Service Center to help you track operational data and exceptions.

Note:

Available categories and links vary by center and role. Some links may be missing if roles don't have permissions to access them.

As an operations manager, you might:

-   Use saved searches to monitor daily operations. For example, you can look up tasks, cases, assets, projects, and consumables.
    
-   Review trends and recurring issues, and share your findings with service leadership.
    

## Example Use Case {#subsect_0331113105}

Use the categories on the Field Service tab and links in your NetSuite Field Service Center to access operational record lists and reports aligned to your role.
