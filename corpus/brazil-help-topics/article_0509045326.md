To access the Brazil Reports documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Reports](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilReports_pt_BR.pdf).

The Brazil Reports SuiteApp enables you to assess the items on your inventory. The assessment generates the inventory ownership and custody report, which lists the inventory items moved in inbound and outbound deliveries, along with their ownership and custody status.

The inventory ownership and custody report is a prerequisite for the EFD ICMS IPI file if you perform inbound and outbound deliveries. For more information, see [Prerequisites for EFD ICMS IPI](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_59134505962.html).

For items to appear correctly on the report, associate a type of inventory ownership and custody with the transactions' location. For more information, see [Adding the Type of Inventory Ownership and Custody to Locations for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0412023139.html).

For more information about inventory assessments, see the following topics:

-   [Generating Inventory Ownership and Custody Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0509045403.html)
    
-   [Adjusting Inventory Assessments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0509045423.html)
