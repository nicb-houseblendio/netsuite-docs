The Brazil Reports SuiteApp enables you to create an inventory assessment and generate an inventory ownership and custody report. Use this report to record the inventory items moved between locations on inbound and outbound delivery transactions on a given period.

#### To generate an inventory ownership and custody report: {#procedure_0509045343}

1.  Go to Reports > Brazil Reports > Inventory Assessment > New.
    
2.  In the **Subsidiary** field, select the subsidiary whose inventory you want to assess.
    
3.  In the **Start Date** field, select the start date of the assessment period.
    
4.  In the **End Date** field, select the end date of the assessment period.
    
    If your subsidiary is a manufacturer or comparable to a manufacturer, make sure the inventory assessment period matches the IPI assessment period.
    
    NetSuite uses the start and end dates to determine the posting period to which the assessment applies. You can't create, edit, or generate a report for the assessment after the posting period closes.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    
    NetSuite saves the assessment record.
    
6.  To generate the inventory ownership and custody report, do the following:
    
    1.  Go to Reports > Brazil Reports > Inventory Assessment.
        
    2.  Click **View** next to the inventory assessment record you want.
        
        The inventory assessment record opens.
        
    3.  Click **Generate Report**.
        
        If the assessment covers a long period, the report generation may take a few minutes to complete. When the report becomes available, NetSuite displays the View link under the Report field and emails you.
        
        The system also updates the inventory assessment record with information about items moved during the period. The update can take longer than generating the report.
        
    4.  In the **Report** field, click **View**.
        
        NetSuite opens the Country Tax Report - Brazil page.
        
    5.  Click the XLS icon ![XLS file icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/XLS_Icon.png) to download the report.
        
7.  (Optional) In the **Status of the Assessment Items Update** field, verify the update status.
    
    -   If the status is **Pending** or **In Progress**, refresh the page until it says **Completed**.
        
    -   If the status is **Completed**, click the **Assessment** subtab to view the inventory item information.
