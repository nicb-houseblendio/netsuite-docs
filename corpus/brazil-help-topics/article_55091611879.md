To add, update, or remove transactions in EFD-Reinf events, you must edit the relevant transaction records in NetSuite, and make the appropriate changes.

After you edit the transactions, open the EFD-Reinf, and refresh the list of transactions included in the events to reflect the changes.

You can refresh the list of transactions either before or after the first EFD-Reinf submission of the month.

#### To refresh the transactions in an EFD-Reinf event:

1.  Go to Reports > Brazil Reports > EFD-Reinf.
    
2.  Next to the EFD-Reinf record you want to update, click **View**.
    
    The EFD-Reinf record opens.
    
3.  Click the subtab of the event that you want to update.
    
    1.  Under the Transaction Data subtab, click **Refresh Transactions**.
        
        The system searches for transactions from the selected subsidiary and period that are eligible for the event.
        
        To determine eligibility, NetSuite considers a set of prerequisites. For more information, see [Prerequisites for EFD-Reinf](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1231094558.html).
        
    2.  Review the transactions included in the event.
        
        Ensure that the event contains all eligible transactions and that you've fixed any issues raised by the tax authority.
        
    3.  Repeat these steps for any other events that you want to update.
        
4.  To send the updated EFD-Reinf to the tax authority, click **Send** **EFD-Reinf**.
    
    If you're rectifying previously sent events, NetSuite automatically reopens the submission.
    
5.  Refresh the page until the **Status** field says **Completed**, **Failed**, or **Failed Partially**.
    
    The **Completed** status indicates that the tax authority received and processed the EFD-Reinf successfully.
    
6.  If the status is **Failed** or **Failed Partially**, do the following:
    
    1.  Click the **Events Logs** subtab.
        
    2.  Verify the **Status of the Event** column to identify the events that failed.
        
    3.  In the **Return Message** column, review the message returned from the tax authority with details about the error.
        
    4.  (Optional) To view additional information about each event, click the corresponding EFD-Reinf event subtab.
