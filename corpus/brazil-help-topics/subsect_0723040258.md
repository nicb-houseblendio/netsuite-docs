To access the Brazil Reports documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Reports](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilReports_pt_BR.pdf).

Brazil Reports provides the following file layouts for this report:

-   **ECF v12 Para Lucro Real** - Includes sections and records that are relevant for companies that adhere to the Lucro Real tax regime.
    
-   **ECF v12 Para Lucro Presumido** - Includes sections and records that are relevant for companies that adhere to the Lucro Presumido tax regime.
    

NetSuite generates the ECF files based on version 12 of the layout.
