To enable NetSuite to identify other sources for PIS and COFINS in your account, set up the e-document categories that qualify as sources for credit or contribution.

When you create a PIS and COFINS assessment, the system searches for vendor bills and non-certified invoice transactions with the e-document categories you set.

Tip:

Check the **Include Other Credits or Contributions** box in PIS and COFINS assessments for the system to search for other sources for credits or contributions. For more information, see [Assessing PIS and COFINS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0513020736.html).

#### To set up e-document categories for other PIS and COFINS sources:

1.  Go to Setup > Brazil Reports > Other PIS and COFINS Sources > New.
    
    The system opens a new other source for PIS and COFINS settings record.
    
2.  In the **E-Document Category for Other Contributions** field, select one or more e-document categories you want to consider as other sources for PIS and COFINS contributions.
    
3.  In the **E-Document Category for Other Credits** field, select one or more e-document categories you want to consider as other sources for PIS and COFINS credits.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    
    You can only have one other source for PIS and COFINS settings record in your account. To change your settings, edit this record.
