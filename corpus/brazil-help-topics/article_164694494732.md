In Brazil, payments can be subject to interest and fine rates. You can set up your terms of payment to include the interest and fine rates NetSuite calculates on late payments.

#### To create a term of payment for Brazil: {#procedure_0610103238}

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Setup Tasks > Accounting Lists > New_.
    
2.  Click **Term**.
    
3.  If you want to create a term of payment with installments, check the **Installment** box.
    
4.  Fill the relevant information.
    
    For more information, see [Creating Terms of Payment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1234186.html) and [Creating Installments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1540928779.html).
    
5.  In the **% Daily Interest for Transactions from Brazil** field, enter the percentage of daily interest to apply if the invoice is paid late.
    
6.  In the **% Fine for Transactions from Brazil** field, enter the percentage of the fine to apply if the invoice is paid late.
    
    NetSuite applies the fine on the invoice's original amount, prior to interests.
    
7.  In the **Payment Indicator for Brazil** field, select the indicator for this term of payment.
    
    For example, if this is an installment term of payment, select the **1 - Pagamento à prazo** option. NetSuite includes this information in the `indPag` element of electronic invoice for goods.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
