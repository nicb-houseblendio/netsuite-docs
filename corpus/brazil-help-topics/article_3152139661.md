To access the Brazil Localization SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Localization](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilLocalization_pt_BR.pdf).

With Brazil Localization, you can choose how NetSuite handles tax amounts withheld on transactions:

-   Add all withheld amounts on the first installment
    
-   Split withheld amounts evenly between all installments
    

Important:

If the withheld tax amount in a transaction is the same as or higher than the first installment's amount,NetSuite ignores this setting. In such cases, the system splits the tax amount between all installments equally.

#### To enable withheld tax amount splitting between installments: {#procedure_1122032426}

1.  Go to _Setup > Tax > Tax Types_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the tax type record you want.
    
3.  To split withheld tax amounts evenly between all installments, check the **Split Tax Amounts Between Installments** box.
    
    If you don't check the box, NetSuite adds the withheld amounts to the first installment.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
