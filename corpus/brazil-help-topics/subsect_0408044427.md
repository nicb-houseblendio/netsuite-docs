Before you generate PIS and COFINS assessments, ensure you meet the following prerequisites:

-   If you withhold PIS and COFINS on services, set up the nature of the withholding at source for customers involved in service transactions.
    
    For more information, see [Setting Up Customers for Brazil Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_69173854800.html).
    
-   If you want NetSuite to search your account for other sources of PIS and COFINS credits or contributions, set up the e-document categories for these sources.
    
    For more information, see [Setting Up E-Document Categories for Other PIS and COFINS Sources](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0711015217.html).
    
-   If your company is entitled to PIS or COFINS credits from the purchase of electricity, natural gas, or water, set up the nature of the calculation basis for credit.
    
    For more information, see [Setting Up the Nature of the Calculation Basis for Credit](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0528093103.html).
    

Ensure that the transactions you enter in NetSuite meet these criteria for inclusion in the PIS and COFINS assessment:

| **Transaction type** | Outgoing transactions
-   Cash sale
-   Invoice
-   Outbound delivery
-   Vendor credit

Incoming transactions

-   Goods return
-   Import bill
-   Inbound delivery
-   Vendor bill

 |
| --- | --- |
| **Calculated taxes** | 

-   PIS
-   COFINS
-   PIS and COFINS as part of the CSRF For more information, see [Separate Withheld COFINS, CSLL, and PIS Taxes from CSRF](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1016816/loc/en_US), SuiteAnswers ID 1016816.

 |
| **Certified e-document number** | Required |
| **Date** | 

-   Outgoing transactions
    -   For invoices involving services with PIS and COFINS withheld an accrual basis, the RPS date
    -   For invoices involving services with PIS and COFINS withheld on a cash basis, the invoice or installment payment date
    -   For transactions involving goods, the certification date
-   Incoming transactions
    -   For vendor bills involving services, the RPS date
    -   For transactions involving goods, the transaction date or certification date
    -   For transactions with the bill of lading or tax, expense, or utility bill e-document categories, the transaction date

Important: NetSuite uses the **Type of Date** parameter type to identify whether the PIS and COFINS were withheld on certification or payment. For more information, see [Parameter Types for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_163664116234.html). |
| **E-document category** | 

-   Electronic bill of lading
-   Electronic invoice for goods
-   Electronic invoice for services
-   SAT cash receipt
-   Tax, expense, or utility bill

 |
| **E-document model** | 

-   Model 57 or 67 required for the electronic bill of lading category
-   Model 55 required for the electronic invoice for goods category
-   Model 59 or 65 required for the SAT cash receipt category
-   Model 06, 66, 21, 22, 28, or 29 required for the tax, expense, or utility bill category

 |
| **E-document status** | E-documents generated from the following transactions require the Certified status:

-   Goods return
-   Import bill
-   Invoice
-   Inbound delivery
-   Outbound delivery
-   Vendor credit

Note: The system has a different criteria for the assessment of PIS and COFINS credits or contributions from other sources. It only considers vendor bills and non-certified invoices when it searches for other sources for PIS and COFINS. |
