If you generate ECD files, you can include additional federal registration numbers in your file's 0007 record. NetSuite supports the inclusion of registration numbers assigned by the following federal institutions:

-   Agência Nacional de Transportes Terrestres (ANTT)
    
-   Banco Central do Brasil (BACEN)
    
-   Comissão de Valores Mobiliários (CVM)
    
-   Superintendência de Seguros Privados (Susep)
    
-   Tribunal Superior Eleitoral (TSE)
    

#### To add other federal registration numbers:

1.  Go to Setup > Brazil Reports > Other Registration for ECD > New.
    
    NetSuite opens a new other federal registration number record.
    
2.  In the **Subsidiary** field, select the subsidiary associated with the registration number.
    
3.  In the **Institution** field, select the federal institution in which the subsidiary is registered.
    
4.  In the **Federal Registration Number** field, enter the federal registration number assigned to the subsidiary by the selected institution.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    
    Repeat these steps to add all federal registration numbers you want to include in ECD files.
