In Brazil, you must record inventory transfers between subsidiaries on e-documents. The Brazil Localization SuiteApp enables you to do that by creating outbound and inbound delivery transactions from intercompany transfer order and item receipt transactions.

Before you transfer inventory items between subsidiaries in Brazil, ensure you meet the setup prerequisites. For more information, see [Prerequisites for Transferring Inventory Between Subsidiaries in Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0601032214.html).

#### To transfer inventory items between subsidiaries in Brazil: {#procedure_0526052911}

1.  Create an intercompany inventory transfer.
    
    For more information, see [Creating Intercompany Inventory Transfer Orders in Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0601115644.html).
    
2.  Fulfill the intercompany inventory transfer.
    
    For more information, see [Fulfilling Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1223349.html).
    
3.  Receive the intercompany inventory transfer.
    
    For more information, see [Receiving Intercompany Inventory Transfer Orders in Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0601014855.html).
