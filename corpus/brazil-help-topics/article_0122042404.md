To connect your bank accounts with NetSuite, associate your bank account details with the respective bank connection.

#### To associate a bank account with a bank connection for Brazil:

1.  Go to Setup > Brazil Banking Integration > Bank Connection.
    
2.  Next to the bank connection for the purpose you want, click **View**.
    
3.  Click the **Bank Accounts** subtab.
    
4.  Click **New Connected Bank Account**.
    
    A new connected bank account record opens.
    
5.  In the **Bank Account Details** field, select the bank account you want to associate with the bank connection.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
