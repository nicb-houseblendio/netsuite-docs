After you create transactions that you intend to pay through banking integration, you can process them for accounts payable automation.

Processing your accounts payable payments with the Brazil Banking Integration SuiteApp enables you to:

-   Communicate to the bank the operation you want to perform.
    
    For example, pay a vendor.
    
-   Record the payment in NetSuite.
    

#### To process accounts payable payments for Brazil:

1.  Go to Transactions > Bank > A/P Automation for Brazil.
    
    The Accounts Payable Automation for Brazil page opens.
    
2.  Define the criteria to search for transactions and installments eligible for accounts payable automation:
    
    1.  In the **Subsidiary** field, select the subsidiary for which you want to process payments.
        
    2.  To search transactions or installments involving a specific vendor or employee, in the **Vendor or Employee** field, enter at least the first three letters of the vendor or employee's name and select it from the list.
        
    3.  In the **Period** field, select the start and end date of the transactions or installments for which you want to process payments.
        
        NetSuite first searches the transactions and installments by their due date. If the transactions don't have due dates, the system looks for the transaction dates.
        
    4.  In the **Number of Results per Page** field, select the maximum number of transactions or installments you want NetSuite to display per page.
        
        By default, NetSuite shows 100 results at a time.
        
3.  Define the payment information.
    
    1.  In the **Bank Account Details** field, select the bank account you want to use to process payments.
        
    2.  In the **Type of Operation** field, select **Payable Creation**.
        
    3.  In the **Payment Method** field, select the payment method you want.
        
        -   **Boleto com Integração Bancária** - For bank slips you want to pay.
            
        -   **DARF com Integração Bancária** - For Documento de Arrecadação de Receitas Federais (DARF) bills you want to pay.
            
        -   **GPS com Integração Bancária** - For Guia da Previdência Social (GPS) bills you want to pay.
            
        -   **GRU com Integração Bancária** - For Guia de Recolhimento da União (GRU) bills you want to pay.
            
        -   **Pix com Integração Bancária** - For instant money transfers you want to make.
            
    4.  To define a specific payment date for all the listed transactions or installments, in the **Scheduled Payment Date** field, select a date.
        
        If you leave this field blank, NetSuite sets the due date of each transaction or installment as the payment date.
        
4.  Click **Search**.
    
    NetSuite lists the eligible transactions and installments.
    
5.  On the table, check the box next to each transaction or installment you want to process.
    
    Tip:
    
    To select all the transactions and installments listed on a page, check the box on the table header.
    
6.  To change the payment dates, do the following:
    
    -   To change the payment date of an individual transaction or installment, in the **Payment Date** column, select a different date.
        
    -   To change the payment date of the selected transactions or installments:
        
        1.  In the **Scheduled Payment Date** field, select a date.
            
        2.  Click **Apply to Selected**.
            
            NetSuite changes the payment date of the selected transactions and installments.
            
7.  To add or edit fine and interest amounts for late payments, do the following:
    
    1.  Find the transaction or installment for which you want to add or edit the fine and interest amounts.
        
    2.  In the **Fine** column, enter a fine rate.
        
    3.  In the **Interest** column, enter an interest rate.
        
    4.  Click **OK**.
        
8.  Click **Process**.
    
    An A/P automation batch record opens. You can check the progress of the integration of the operations for accounts payable on this page.
    
9.  Refresh the page to update the **Status** field.
    
    Continue refreshing the page until the status is one of the following:
    
    -   Pending Payment Approval
        
    -   Integration Completed
        
    -   Failed Integration
        
10.  If the status is **Pending Payment Approval**, wait until all approvers decide on the approval.
     
     For more information, see [Approving Accounts Payable Payments for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0902022859.html).
     
11.  If the status is **Failed Integration**, do the following:
     
     1.  Click the **Operations** subtab.
         
     2.  Review the content of the **Details** column of the operations with the **Failed Integration** status.
