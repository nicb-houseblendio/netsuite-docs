To add, update, or remove withheld taxes automatically included in IRPJ and CSLL assessments, you must:

-   Edit the relevant transaction records in NetSuite.
    
-   Go to the IRPJ and CSLL assessment.
    
-   Refresh the list of transactions with withheld taxes.
    
    To determine eligibility, NetSuite considers a set of prerequisites. For more information, see [Prerequisites for IRPJ and CSLL Assessment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0120102235.html).
    

#### To refresh the transactions in an IRPJ and CSLL assessment:

1.  Go to Reports > Brazil Reports > IRPJ and CSLL Assessment.
    
2.  Next to the assessment you want, click **View**.
    
    The IRPJ and CSLL assessment opens.
    
3.  Click the **Outgoing Withheld Taxes** subtab.
    
4.  In the subtab, click **Refresh Transactions**.
    
    The system searches for eligible transactions from the selected subsidiary and period.
    
5.  Review the withheld IRPJ and CSLL records listed in the subtab. If needed, include or adjust the revenue codes on the records.
    
    For more information, see [Including Revenue Codes for Withheld IRPJ and CSLL in Assessments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_43092011308.html).
