Brazil Localization includes a predefined certification monitoring plug-in implementation to receive information from the LATAM E-Document Certification SuiteApp about the current status of your certification requests.

Associate the predefined certification monitoring plug-in with the tax authority certification service records of the tax authorities with which you certify e-documents.

#### To associate the Brazil Certification Monitoring Plug-in with a tax authority: {#procedure_1116114653}

1.  Go to Setup > E-Document Certification > Tax Authority Certification Service.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the tax authority certification service you want.
    
3.  In the **Certification Monitoring Plug-In Implementation** field, select the **Brazil Certification Monitoring Plug-in**.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    
    Repeat the previous steps for all the tax authorities with which you certify e-documents.
