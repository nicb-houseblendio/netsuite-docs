You must define the type of inventory ownership and custody for your locations from Brazil if you:

-   Perform inbound and outbound delivery transactions
    
-   Report your inventory to local authorities
    

Inbound and outbound deliveries are transactions in which items are moved between locations without being bought or sold. These transactions may affect your inventory levels.

For example, you can receive an inbound delivery with items for repair. The items belong to the vendor who sent you the inbound delivery, but they're in your custody. In this scenario, the location you select on the transaction should identify a third-party inventory in the subsidiary's custody.

#### To add the type of inventory ownership and custody to a location: {#procedure_0412023813}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Classifications > Locations_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the location you want.
    
3.  In the **Inventory Ownership and Custody** field, select the type of inventory the location identifies.
    
4.  If the inventory is owned or held by a third-party, in the **Customer or Vendor** field, select the customer or vendor that owns or holds the items in this location.
    
    If you selected **Subsidiary Inventory**, don't select anything on the Customer or Vendor field.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
