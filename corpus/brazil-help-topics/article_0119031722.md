The Imposto Sobre Serviços de Qualquer Natureza (ISS or ISSQN) tax applies to services provided and received in Brazil.

The ISS assessment calculates the tax paid and the tax due on invoice transactions. The result of the assessment is a summarized report and the total of tax due, withheld, and payable.

The ISS assessment record enables you to define the period of time to which the assessment applies.

To ensure NetSuite accounts for all of your eligible transactions, ensure they meet the prerequisites for ISS assessment. For more information, see [Prerequisites for ISS Assessment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0408041343.html).

#### To assess ISS: {#procedure_0119034153}

1.  Go to Reports > Brazil Reports > ISS Assessment > New.
    
2.  In the **Subsidiary** field, select the subsidiary whose ISS amounts you want to assess.
    
3.  In the **Start Date** field, select the start date of the assessment period.
    
4.  In the **End Date** field, select the end date of the assessment period.
    
    NetSuite uses the start and end dates to determine the posting period to which the assessment applies. You can't create, edit, or generate a report for the assessment after the posting period closes.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    
    NetSuite saves the assessment report.
    
6.  To view the calculated amounts, click the **Assessment** subtab.
    
7.  To generate the assessment report, click **Generate Report**.
    
    If the assessment covers a long period, the report generation may take a few minutes to complete. You can monitor the progress on the Country Tax Reports page. For more information, see [Monitoring the Generation of Tax Assessment Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0727011526.html).
    
    NetSuite emails you when the report becomes available. The system also updates the assessment record with the calculated tax amounts.
    
8.  When you receive the email confirming the report was generated, in the **Report** field, click **View**.
    
    NetSuite opens the Country Tax Report - Brazil page.
    
9.  Choose the format in which you want to download the report:
    
    -   To download the report as a PDF, click the PDF ![Adobe Reader icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/PDF_icon.png) icon.
        
    -   To download the report as a XLS, click the XLS ![XLS file icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/XLS_Icon.png) icon.
        
        Due to a system limitation, you can only download XLS files with sizes up to 10 MB.
