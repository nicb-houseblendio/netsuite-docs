To access the Brazil Banking Integration SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Banking Integration](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilBankingIntegration_pt_BR.pdf).

With Brazil Banking Integration, you can receive electronic bank payments within NetSuite. The SuiteApp enables you to create sales transactions with payments for banking integration and then generate and communicate the receivable payments to Banco do Brasil. The integration eliminates the need to generate, send, and import payment files to record receivable payments.

Brazil Banking Integration supports the following payment methods for the banking integration of accounts receivable:

-   Bank slips
    
-   Pix
