To access the Brazil Localization SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Localization](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilLocalization_pt_BR.pdf).

In Brazil, overdue balances can be subject to fine and interest charges. The Brazil Localization SuiteApp enables you to apply these charges to sales or purchases.

For more information, see the following help topics:

-   [Applying Fine and Interest Charges on Sales for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_164933450907.html)
    
-   [Applying Fine and Interest Charges on Purchases for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_66155257396.html)
