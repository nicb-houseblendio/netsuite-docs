In Brazil, when you receive a purchase, delivery, or return, you receive an electronic invoice.

You may be required to post the information from the electronic invoices you receive. Incoming e-documents include relevant information for bookkeeping and reporting purposes.

In NetSuite, you can post the information from incoming electronic invoices for the following types of transaction:

-   [Adding Electronic Invoicing Information in Purchase Orders for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9085615288.html)
    
-   [Posting Incoming Electronic Invoices in Vendor Bills for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_164684800467.html)
    
-   [Posting Incoming Electronic Invoices in Inbound Deliveries for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0406040529.html)
    
-   [Posting Incoming Electronic Invoices in Goods Returns for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0406044239.html)
    

Tip:

You can use the receiver acknowledgment feature to automatically convert incoming e-documents into vendor bills. For more information, see [Receiver Acknowledgment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0517104030.html).
