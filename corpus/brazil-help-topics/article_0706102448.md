The Guia Nacional de Recolhimento de Tributos Estaduais (GNRE) is an e-document for interstate sales transactions, generated for tax substitution and differential tax purposes.

You can generate GNRE tax collection forms for transactions that have a certified electronic invoice with the following taxes:

-   Substituição Tributária do Imposto sobre Circulação de Mercadorias e Serviços (ICMS ST)
    
-   Diferencial de Alíquota do ICMS (ICMS DIFAL)
    
-   Substituição Tributária do Fundo de Combate à Pobreza (FCP ST)
    

For more information about generating GNRE tax collection forms with the Brazil Localization SuiteApp, see the following topics:

-   [Setup for GNRE for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0429043532.html)
    
-   [Generating GNRE Forms for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0124045135.html)
