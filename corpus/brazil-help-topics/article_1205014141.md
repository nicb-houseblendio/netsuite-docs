To access the Brazil Banking Integration SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Banking Integration](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilBankingIntegration_pt_BR.pdf).

To receive payments through Pix with the Brazil Banking Integration SuiteApp, define the settings to generate these payment instruments with NetSuite.

#### To define Pix settings for Brazil:

1.  Go to Setup > Brazil Banking Integration > Bank Account Details.
    
2.  Next to the bank account you want, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Click the **Pix Settings** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Interest Method for Pix** field, enter the code that identifies the method with which NetSuite should accrue interest on late Pix payments.
    
5.  To send Pix QR codes to your customer's email, do the following:
    
    1.  Check the **Send Pix QR Code Through Email** box.
        
    2.  In the **Pix Email Template** field, enter the internal ID of the template file in your File Cabinet.
        
        For more information about viewing internal IDs, see [Showing Record and Field IDs in Your Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0402065629.html). Brazil Banking Integration provides a standard email template. You can use this template as it is, or you can create your customized template. For more information, see [Customizing Bank Slip and Email Templates for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0703095958.html).
        
        Note:
        
        NetSuite sends the message to the email address set in the customer record. For more information, see [Creating a Customer Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161860867711.html).
        
6.  Click **Save**.
