In Brazil, legal entities must submit accounting and fiscal bookkeeping reports to the federal government. The Sistema Público de Escrituração Digital (SPED) is the system that standardizes, receives, validates, stores, and authenticates these reports.

The Brazil Reports SuiteApp enables you to generate several accounting and fiscal bookkeeping files for SPED. You can also generate and send the EFD-Reinf. For more information about which reports are available, how to generate, or send them, see the following topics:

-   [Available Statutory Reports for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0509052242.html)
    
-   [Prerequisites for Generating Statutory Reports for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0509060252.html)
    
-   [Generating SPED Files](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_164874849258.html)
    
-   [Generating and Sending the EFD-Reinf](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9091344046.html)
