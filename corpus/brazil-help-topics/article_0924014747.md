To access the Brazil Reports documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Reports](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilReports_pt_BR.pdf).

Brazil Reports uses the Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp to generate accounting, financial, fiscal books, and tax assessment reports for Brazil.

You can generate the following reports for Brazil:

| Type | Report | Formats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accounting reports For more information, see [Generating Accounting Reports for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0424102639.html). | General ledger | XLS |
| General journal | XLS |
| Trial balance | XLS |
| Financial statements For more information, see [Generating Financial Statements for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_82104919183.html). | Balance sheet | XLS |
| Income statement | XLS |
| Fiscal books For more information, see [Generating Fiscal Books for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_55171343950.html). | Provided services | XLS |
| Received services | XLS |
| Outbound goods | XLS |
| Inbound goods | XLS |
| Auxiliary reports For more information, see [Generating Auxiliary Reports for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6133908015.html). | Simples Nacional assessment | PDF XLS (Up to 10 MB) |
| Withheld taxes on services | PDF XLS (Up to 10 MB) |

Before you generate a country tax report for Brazil, ensure you meet its prerequisites. For more information, see [Prerequisites for Generating Country Tax Reports for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0919113457.html).

For more information about localized country tax reports in NetSuite, see [Generating Localized Country Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528250223.html).
