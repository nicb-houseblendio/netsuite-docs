Note:

To access the SuiteTax Latam Engine - Brazil Records documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [SuiteTax Latam Engine - Brazil Records](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=SuiteTaxLatamEngineBrazil_pt_BR.pdf).

The IBS Brazil plug-in enables you to calculate the Imposto Sobre Bens e Serviços (CBS) tax on the purchases and sales of goods and services.

The following table summarizes the plug-in requirements and capabilities.

| **Plug-in Implementation** | IBS Brazil |
| --- | --- |
| **Tax Code's Internal Name** | IBS\_2026\_BR |
| **Requires Parent Tax Determination Rules** | No |
| **Requires Parameter Types** | Yes Requires a cClassTrib code. For more information, see [Parameter Types for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_163664116234.html). |
| **Supports Tax Basis and Tax Rate Override** | Yes |
| **Adds Expenses** | Yes Adds shipping and handling costs, as well as expenses included in the Expenses subtab, to the tax calculation basis. |
| **Changes the Tax Calculation Basis** | Yes
-   Subtracts COFINS, FCP, ICMS, and PIS from the IBS tax calculation basis if:
    -   The tax determination rules is part of a parent/child hierarchy.
    -   The parent tax determination rules and the rules at a higher level in the hierarchy have tax determination settings with tax codes with the following standard internal names:
        -   COFINS\_BR
        -   COFINS\_IMPORTACAO\_BENS\_BR
        -   FCP\_BR
        -   ICMS\_BR
        -   ICMS\_DIFAL\_BR
        -   ICMS\_IMPORTACAO\_BENS\_BR
        -   PIS\_BR
        -   PIS\_IMPORTACAO\_BENS\_BR
-   Adds CIDE and II to the IBS tax calculation basis if:
    -   The tax determination rules is part of a parent/child hierarchy.
    -   The parent tax determination rules and the rules at a higher level in the hierarchy have tax determination settings with tax codes with the following standard internal names:
        -   CIDE\_IMPORTACAO\_BR
        -   II\_IMPORTACAO\_BENS\_BR

 |
| **Records Tax Calculation History** | No |
