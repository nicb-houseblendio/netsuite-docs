Note:

To access the Brazil Banking Integration SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Banking Integration](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilBankingIntegration_pt_BR.pdf).

With Brazil Banking Integration, bank account keys are aliases that identify a bank account. In banking systems, these keys stand in for the complete bank account details. In Brazil, the most common type of key is known as Pix key. Each Pix key is unique and can't be associated with multiple bank account details.

You must have a default key. Currently, Brazil Banking Integration is only able to include the default key information in your Pix payments.

If you're testing the connection with Banco do Brasil, use the bank's fictional data. You can find the fictional Pix keys in the Portal Developers BB website.

#### To associate a Pix key with bank account details for Brazil:

1.  Go to Setup > Brazil Banking Integration > Bank Account Details.
    
2.  Next to the bank account you want, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Click the **Bank Account Keys** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Type** field, select one of the following types:
    
    -   CNPJ
        
    -   CPF
        
    -   Email address
        
    -   Mobile phone number
        
    -   Random key
        
5.  In the **Bank Account Key** field, enter the key that identifies the bank account.
    
6.  To use this key in transactions involving the account holder, check the **Default Key** box.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
