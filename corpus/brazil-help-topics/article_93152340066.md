Brazil Reports enables you to classify the items included in EFD Contribuições and EFD ICMS IPI files according to the Sistema Público de Escrituração Digital (SPED) system.

You can associate SPED EFD types with item records subject to the ICMS and IPI taxes. The SPED EFD type field appears for the following types of item records:

-   Assembly item
    
-   Group
    
-   Inventory item
    
-   Item Group
    
-   Kit/package
    
-   Lot Numbered
    
-   Non-inventory item
    
-   Serialized
    
-   Service
    

#### To associate SPED EFD types with items: {#procedure_40153741196}

1.  Go to _Lists > Accounting > Items_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the item you want.
    
3.  In the **SPED EFD Type** field, select the type of the item according to the classification defined by the SPED.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
