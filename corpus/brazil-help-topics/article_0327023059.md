Note:

To access the Brazil Deferred Revenue Recognition SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Deferred Revenue Recognition](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilDeferredRevenueRecognition_pt_BR.pdf).

The Brazil Deferred Revenue Recognition SuiteApp extends the Advanced Revenue Management (Essentials) feature to recognize deferred revenue based on Brazil-specific compliance requirements.

The SuiteApp creates revenue arrangements and elements for deferred revenue from transactions that meet the following criteria:

| Transaction Type | Subsidiary | E-Document Category | Certified E-Document Status | Additional Criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales order | Companies located in Brazil that have deferred revenue settings. | \- | \- | \- |
| Invoice | Companies located in Brazil that have deferred revenue settings. | 
-   Electronic invoice for goods
-   Electronic invoice for services

 | Certified | The invoice wasn't created from a sales order. |
