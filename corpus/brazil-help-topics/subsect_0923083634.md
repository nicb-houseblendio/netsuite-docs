Note:

To access the Brazil Reports documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Reports](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilReports_pt_BR.pdf).

Ensure that the transactions you enter in NetSuite meet these criteria for inclusion in the received services fiscal book:

| **Transaction type** | Vendor bill |
| --- | --- |
| **Certified e-document number** | Required |
| **E-document category** | Electronic invoice for services |
| **Date** | **Date** field required |
