To access the Brazil Banking Integration SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Banking Integration](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilBankingIntegration_pt_BR.pdf).

Based on your subsidiary's approval hierarchy, NetSuite routes for approval the batches of accounts payable payments that surpass the approval-free limit. For each approval level, the system notifies the designated approvers by email, and only they can approve or reject the batch.

For more information about setting up approval, see [Setting Up Accounts Payable Approval for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0902024623.html).

#### To approve accounts payable payments for Brazil:

1.  Go to Lists > Brazil Banking Integration > A/P Automation Batch.
    
2.  Next to the batch you want to approve or reject, click **View**.
    
3.  To approve the batch, do the following:
    
    1.  Click **Approve**.
        
        The A/P Approval record opens.
        
    2.  (Optional) In the **Memo** field, enter a message about your decision.
        
        For example, you can add an instruction for the next level's approvers.
        
    3.  Click **Save**.
        
        NetSuite redirects you to the A/P automation batch record. Your approval is recorded in the **Approval Logs** subtab.
        
4.  To reject the batch, do the following:
    
    1.  Click **Reject**.
        
        The A/P Rejection record opens.
        
    2.  In the **Memo** field, enter a message about your decision.
        
        For example, provide a justification for the rejection.
        
    3.  Click **Save**.
        
        NetSuite redirects you to the A/P automation batch record. Your justification appears in the **Rejection Memo** field.
