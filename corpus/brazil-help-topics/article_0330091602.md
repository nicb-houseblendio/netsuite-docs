Note:

To access the Brazil Localization SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Localization](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilLocalization_pt_BR.pdf).

Brazil Localization enables you to add payment information to sales and purchase transactions without installments. You can then receive or make one-time payments through the Centro Nacional de Automação Bancária (CNAB) and record them on NetSuite.

NetSuite doesn't create installments when you:

-   Can't select terms of payment
    
-   Choose to not select an installment term of payment
    

For more information about adding payment information to transactions without installments, see the following topics:

-   [Adding Payment Information to Sales Transactions Without Installments for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0904113106.html)
    
-   [Adding Payment Information to Purchase Transactions Without Installments for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0904113119.html)
