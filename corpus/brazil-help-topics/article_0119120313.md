To access the Brazil Localization SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Localization](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilLocalization_pt_BR.pdf).

The Brazil Localization SuiteApp adds to transactions several fields that are relevant for electronic invoicing.

When you create a transaction from which you intend to generate and certify an electronic invoice, make sure you enter all the relevant and required information.

For more information, see the following help topics:

-   [Entering Additional Information for Electronic Invoices for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0113054651.html)
