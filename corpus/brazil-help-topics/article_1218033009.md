To access the Brazil Localization SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Localization](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilLocalization_pt_BR.pdf).

The Brazil Localization SuiteApp customizes the address form to meet local business needs.

Note:

If you set your personal preferences to a language other than **English (U.S.)**, **Español (Latinoamérica)**, or **Português (Brasil)**, the **Address from Brazil**, **Number**, and **District** fields are labeled as **Address 1**, **Address 3**, and **Address 2**, respectively.

For more information, see [General Personal Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N475661.html).

#### To enter an address for a customer, vendor, or employee in Brazil:

1.  Go to the customer, vendor, or employee in Brazil.
    
    -   For customers, go to _Setup > Company > Classifications > Subsidiaries_.
        
    -   For vendors, go to _Lists > Relationships > Vendors_.
        
    -   For employees, go to _Lists > Employees > Employees_.
        
2.  Click **Edit** next to the company or individual you want.
    
3.  Click the **Address** subtab.
    
4.  If this isn't the address packages are usually sent to, clear the **Default Shipping** box.
    
5.  If this isn't the address bills are usually sent to, clear the **Default Billing** box.
    
    NetSuite populates the default billing and shipping addresses when you enter a transaction for the customer.
    
6.  Click the pencil icon ![Blue pencil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/Pencil_Icon.png) in the **Edit** column.
    
7.  In the **Country** field, select **Brazil**.
    
8.  In the **Address from Brazil** field, enter the address with the street suffix (for example, avenue, boulevard, road, route, and street) and street name.
    
9.  In the **Number** field, enter the address number.
    
    An address number can contain numbers and letters.
    
10.  In the **Address Complement** field, enter additional information that helps to locate the address.
     
     For example, you can indicate whether the address is a house or an apartment.
     
11.  In the **District** field, enter the name of the district where the address is located.
     
12.  In the **City from Brazil** field, select the city where the address is located.
     
     When you select a city, the **State from Brazil** field automatically displays the acronym of the state associated with the city.
     
13.  In the **ZIP** field, enter the postal code of the address.
     
14.  Click **OK**.
     
15.  Click **Add**.
     
16.  Click **Save**.
