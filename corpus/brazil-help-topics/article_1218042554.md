The Brazil Localization SuiteApp enables you to add tax substitute registrations for your vendors. The tax substitute registration identifies a company that can become liable for paying state taxes instead of the original taxpayer.

#### To add a tax substitute registration to a vendor in Brazil:

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Vendors_.
    
2.  Click **View** next to the record you want.
    
3.  Click **Financial** subtab.
    
4.  Click **Tax Substitute Registration** subtab.
    
5.  Click **New Vendor Tax Substitute Registration**.
    
    The vendor tax substitute registration record opens.
    
6.  In the **State** field, select the state that issued the state tax registration number you want to use for tax substitution.
    
    A vendor can have one tax substitute registration per Brazilian state.
    
7.  In the **Tax Substitute Registration** field, enter the state tax registration number you want to use for tax substitution.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
