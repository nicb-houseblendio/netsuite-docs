The Brazil Localization SuiteApp includes the following predefined expense categories, which you can add to your purchase transactions:

-   **AFRMM** **fee** - The Adicional ao Frete para Renovação da Marinha Mercante (AFRMM) fee applies to imports transported internationally by sea.
    
-   **Freight** - The expense with the freight of goods in and to Brazil.
    
-   **Insurance** - The expense with insurance of goods purchased in Brazil or from abroad.
    
-   **Other costs** - Expenses related to the purchase of goods in Brazil or from abroad to which other expense categories don't apply.
    
-   **SISCOMEX** **fee** - The Sistema Integrado de Comércio Exterior (SISCOMEX) usage fee applies to imports, to cover for maintenance and upgrade costs for the SISCOMEX system.
    

Before you include any of these expenses in your transactions, you must define the expense account and the subsidiaries for each expense category. By default, NetSuite selects the first expense account on your chart of accounts and all the subsidiaries associated with the account. You can select a different account and subsidiaries to fit your business needs.

#### To set up a predefined expense category: {#procedure_1110064746}

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Expense Categories_.
    
2.  Next to the expense category you want, click **Edit**.
    
3.  In the **Expense Account** field, select the GL account to which to post items charged to this category.
    
4.  In the **Subsidiary** field, select the subsidiaries based in Brazil.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
