Note:

To access the Brazil Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Certification Tax Authorities](https://system.netsuite.com/help/helpcenter/shared_resources/PDF/BrazilCertificationTaxAuthorities_pt_BR.pdf).

The Brazil Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp provides predefined e-document validation files in the XSD format.

XSD files validate electronic invoices for goods and services generated using the SuiteApp's predefined e-document templates. XSD validation applies to production, test, and contingency environments. When NetSuite finds issues during the validation, it lists them in the e-document audit trail.

The SuiteApp automatically associates XSD validation files with operations used to send e-documents for the following predefined tax authorities:

-   All state tax authorities
    
-   Specific municipal tax authorities
    
    -   Barueri (SP)
        
    -   Belo Horizonte (MG)
        
    -   Campinas (SP)
        
    -   Cuiabá (MT)
        
    -   Curitiba (PR)
        
    -   Fortaleza (CE)
        
    -   João Pessoa (PB)
        
    -   Maricá (RJ)
        
    -   Natal (RN)
        
    -   Porto Velho (RO)
        
    -   Recife (PE)
        
    -   Rio de Janeiro (RJ)
        
    -   Salvador (BA)
        
    -   São José dos Campos (SP)
        
    -   Sorocaba (SP)
        
    -   Uberlândia (MG)
        
    -   Vitória (ES)
        
-   Specific third-party standards
    
    -   ABACO
        
    -   BETHA
        
    -   DEISS
        
    -   DSF
        
    -   GINFES
        
    -   GISS
        
    -   ISSNET
        
    -   NFe-Cidades
        
    -   NFS-e Nacional
        
    -   Pública
        
    -   SAATRI
        
    -   TIPLAN
        
    -   WEBISS
        

For more information about enabling XSD validation, see [Setting Up Certification Service Operations for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0327111232.html).
