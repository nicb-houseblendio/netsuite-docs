The Imposto de Renda das Pessoas Jurídicas (IRPJ ) and the Contribuição Social sobre o Lucro Líquido (CSLL) taxes apply to services provided and received in Brazil.

The IRPJ and CSLL assessment calculates the taxes paid and the taxes due on transactions in NetSuite.

For subsidiaries that adhere to the Lucro Real tax incidence regime, the assessment can also list tax amounts withheld on outgoing service transactions.

If you withhold the CSLL as part of the Contribuições Sociais Retidas na Fonte (CSRF), NetSuite determines CSLL amounts from the CSRF. Otherwise, the system assesses withheld CSLL amounts directly.

You can define revenue codes for the withheld taxes and NetSuite carries this information forward to the ECF files you generate.

To ensure NetSuite accounts for all of your eligible transactions, ensure they meet the prerequisites for ICMS assessment. For more information, see [Prerequisites for IRPJ and CSLL Assessment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0120102235.html).

The result of the assessment is a summary of the total of IRPJ and CSLL due or the IRPJ and CSLL credits.

Additionally, the IRPJ and CSLL assessment record enables you to:

-   Add information about deductions and subtract their amounts from the payable amounts.
    
-   Add information about ending inventory valuation for subsidiaries that adhere to the Lucro Real tax regime.
    

Tip:

You can leverage the provided services and received services fiscal books to review the assessment. For more information about generating the fiscal books, see [Generating Fiscal Books for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_55171343950.html).

#### To assess IRPJ and CSLL: {#procedure_0517115724}

1.  Go to Reports > Brazil Reports > IRPJ and CSLL Assessment > New.
    
2.  In the **Subsidiary** field, select the subsidiary whose IRPJ and CSLL amounts you want to assess.
    
3.  If the subsidiary adheres to the Lucro Real tax regime, do the following:
    
    1.  In the **Type of Assessment** field, select the type of IRPJ and CSLL tax assessment you want to perform.
        
        For example, you can assess taxes based on estimated amounts or confirmed assessed amounts.
        
    2.  In the **Type of Calculation Basis** field, select the type of calculation basis you want to use to assess IRPJ and CSLL.
        
    3.  If you want to assess withheld IRPJ and CSLL amounts on outgoing service transactions, choose the assessment method for the CSRF or CSLL:
        
        Important:
        
        You must choose the assessment method based on the withholding method set for the **Type of Date** parameter type in your tax determination rules. NetSuite uses this parameter to identify whether the CSRF or CSLL was withheld on certification or payment. For more information, see [Parameter Types for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_163664116234.html).
        
        -   **Include CSRF Withheld on an Accrual Basis** - The system includes in the assessment CSLL amounts withheld separately or as part of the CSRF on outgoing service transactions certified in the period.
            
        -   **Include CSRF Withheld on a Cash Basis** - The system includes in the assessment CSLL amounts withheld separately or as part of the CSRF on outgoing service transactions paid during the period.
            
        
        When you save the IRPJ and CSLL assessment record, NetSuite searches for eligible transactions and lists them in the Outgoing Withheld Taxes subtab.
        
4.  In the **Start Date** field, select the start date of the assessment period.
    
5.  In the **End Date** field, select the end date of the assessment period.
    
    NetSuite uses the start and end dates to determine the posting period to which the assessment applies. You can't create or edit the assessment after the posting period closes.
    
6.  If the subsidiary adheres to the Lucro Real tax regime, you can add an ending inventory valuation to your assessment:
    
    1.  Click the **Ending Inventory Valuation** subtab.
        
    2.  In the **Ending Inventory Valuation Method** field, select the method you use to calculate the value of your inventory at the end of the accounting period.
        
    3.  In the **Cost of Goods Account Code** field, select the code that applies to the cost of goods accounts included in this ending inventory valuation.
        
    4.  In the **Cost of Goods Account** field, select one or more cost of goods sold (COGS) accounts for this ending inventory valuation.
        
    5.  In the **Total Amount** field, enter the total amount of the cost of goods sold (COGS) accounts you selected for this ending inventory valuation.
        
        If you selected one account, enter the balance of the account. If you selected more than one account, enter the sum of their balances.
        
7.  To add the IRPJ calculation basis information, do the following:
    
    1.  Click the **IRPJ Calculation Basis** subtab.
        
    2.  In the **Calculation Basis** field, enter the amount you want to use as basis for calculating the IRPJ due.
        
    3.  If you assess IRPJ and CSLL in the Lucro Presumido tax regime, do the following:
        
        1.  Click the **Gross Revenue** subtab.
            
        2.  In the **Presumption Rate** column, select the tax rate applied on the gross revenue subject to IRPJ.
            
        3.  In the **Amount** column, enter the gross revenue from the assessment period that's subject to IRPJ.
            
        4.  Click **Add**.
            
    4.  To add debit and credit adjustments to the IRPJ calculation basis, do the following:
        
        1.  Click the **Debit and Credit Adjustments** subtab.
            
        2.  In the **Adjustment Code** field, select the code with which you want to associate the adjustment.
            
            The adjustment code determines whether NetSuite adds or subtracts the amount from the tax assessment.
            
        3.  In the **Amount** field, enter the amount to add or subtract from the assessment.
            
        4.  Click **Add**.
            
    5.  To add the tax rate, tax additional, and tax amount paid on previous months, do the following:
        
        1.  Click the **Tax Due** subtab.
            
        2.  In the **Tax Rate** field, enter the tax rate you want to use to calculate the IRPJ due.
            
            The tax rate is a percentage applied on the calculation basis to obtain the IRPJ due before tax additionals.
            
        3.  In the **Tax Additional** field, enter the additional value that applies to monthly IRPJ amounts over 20,000.00 BRL.
            
        4.  If you assess IRPJ as an estimate under the Lucro Real tax regime, in the **Amount Paid on Previous Months** field, enter the amount of IRPJ you paid previously.
            
            Only enter amounts from months in the same year as the assessment.
            
8.  To add the CSLL calculation basis information, do the following:
    
    1.  Click the **CSLL Calculation Basis** subtab.
        
    2.  In the **Calculation Basis** field, enter the amount you want to use as basis for calculating the CSLL due.
        
    3.  If you assess IRPJ and CSLL in the Lucro Presumido tax regime, do the following:
        
        1.  Click the **Gross Revenue** subtab.
            
        2.  In the **Presumption Rate** column, select the tax rate applied on the gross revenue subject to CSLL.
            
        3.  In the **Amount** column, enter the gross revenue from the assessment period that's subject to CSLL.
            
        4.  Click **Add**.
            
    4.  To add debit and credit adjustments to the CSLL calculation basis, do the following:
        
        1.  Click the **Debit and Credit Adjustments** subtab.
            
        2.  In the **Adjustment Code** field, select the code with which you want to associate the adjustment.
            
            The adjustment code determines whether NetSuite adds or subtracts the amount from the tax assessment.
            
        3.  In the **Amount** field, enter the amount to add or subtract from the assessment.
            
        4.  Click **Add**.
            
    5.  To add the tax rate and tax amount paid on previous months, do the following:
        
        1.  Click the **Tax Due** subtab.
            
        2.  In the **Tax Rate** field, enter the tax rate you want to use to calculate the CSLL due.
            
            The tax rate is a percentage applied on the calculation basis to obtain the CSLL due.
            
        3.  If you assess CSLL as an estimate under the Lucro Real tax regime, in the **Amount Paid on Previous Months** field, enter the amount of CSLL you paid previously.
            
            Only enter amounts from months in the same year as the assessment.
            
9.  To add IRPJ deductions to the assessment, do the following:
    
    1.  Click the **IRPJ Deduction** subtab.
        
    2.  In the **Deduction Code** column, select the code for the type of deduction you want to apply.
        
    3.  In the **Description** column, enter a description for the deduction you selected.
        
    4.  In the **Amount** column, enter the amount you want to deduct from the total amount due.
        
    5.  Click **Add**.
        
        Repeat these steps to add more deductions. Be aware that you can't use a deduction code more than one time.
        
10.  To add CSLL deductions to the assessment, do the following:
     
     1.  Click the **CSLL Deduction** subtab.
         
     2.  In the **Deduction Code** column, select the code for the type of deduction you want to apply.
         
     3.  In the **Description** column, enter a description for the deduction you selected.
         
     4.  In the **Amount** column, enter the amount you want to deduct from the total amount due.
         
     5.  Click **Add**.
         
         Repeat these steps to add more deductions. Be aware that you can't use a deduction code more than one time.
         
11.  Click **Save**.
     
     NetSuite saves the record and calculates the IRPJ and CSLL amounts.
     
12.  If you checked one of the boxes for including withheld taxes in the assessment, do the following:
     
     1.  Click the **Outgoing Withheld Taxes** subtab.
         
     2.  Review the withheld IRPJ and CSLL records listed in the subtab. If needed, include or adjust the revenue codes on the records.
         
         For more information, see [Including Revenue Codes for Withheld IRPJ and CSLL in Assessments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_43092011308.html).
         
     3.  (Optional) If you need to make changes to any transactions automatically included in the assessment, refresh the transactions.
         
         For more information, see [Refreshing Transactions in IRPJ and CSLL Assessments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_19135412202.html).
         
13.  To view the calculated amounts, click the **Assessment** subtab.
