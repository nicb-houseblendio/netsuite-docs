The Manaus (AM) tax authority uses its own set of codes to classify provided services. To certify e-documents for Manaus, the tax authority requires you to include a service code for the item in the transaction.

To generate e-documents that comply with this requirement, do the following:

-   Verify which service codes from Manaus apply to your business.
    
-   Verify whether you already have these service codes in NetSuite.
    
    NetSuite provides predefined service codes. To view the predefined codes, go to Lists > Brazil Tax Information > Service and MCN Codes.
    
-   If the service codes that apply to your business aren't available in NetSuite, create them as item code records.
    
    For more information, see [Creating Item Codes in the SuiteTax Latam Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162033274454.html).
    
-   Associate the item codes with your service items for Manaus.
    
    For more information, see [Associating Item Codes With Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162033229352.html).
