Note:

To access the Brazil Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Certification Tax Authorities](https://system.netsuite.com/help/helpcenter/shared_resources/PDF/BrazilCertificationTaxAuthorities_pt_BR.pdf).

Access to the custom records provided by the Brazil Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp requires specific permissions. By default, the Administrator role has full permissions to the custom records. If other roles need to view, access, or edit the custom records, an administrator can grant permission to the roles.

See the following table for more information about the default permissions granted with the Brazil Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp.

| Role | Category | Permission | Access Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Administrator | Custom record | Inbound E-Document History | Full |
| Administrator | Custom record | Inbound E-Document Settings | Full |
| Administrator | Custom record | Incoming E-Document | Full |
| Administrator | Custom record | Incoming E-Document Event | Full |
| Administrator | Custom record | Receiver Acknowledgment Details | Full |
| Administrator | Custom record | Receiver Acknowledgment Request | Full |
| Administrator | Custom record | Receiver Acknowledgment Requests Batch | Full |
| Administrator | Custom record | Web Service Availability Test | Full |
