To access the Brazil Localization SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Localization](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilLocalization_pt_BR.pdf).

After you import the CNAB return file and confirm the bank slips are registered, you can download the slips associated with your transactions.

When a bank slip is confirmed as registered with the bank, NetSuite automatically sends it to the customer in an email.

Brazil Localization leverages the e-document email recipient and the e-document sender settings to send the bank slips. For more information about setting the recipient, see [Defining E-Document Email Recipients](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4592483197.html). For more information about the sender settings, see [Setting Up Electronic Invoicing Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0619104200.html).

#### To download bank slips: {#procedure_0816031007}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Sales > Create Invoices > List_.
    
2.  Click **View** next to the transaction you want.
    
3.  Click the **Billing** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Bank Slip** column, click **Download**.
