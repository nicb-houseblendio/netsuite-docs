The Country Tax Reports page enables you to monitor the generation of the following tax assessments:

-   ICMS assessment
    
-   IPI assessment
    
-   ISS assessment
    
-   PIS and COFINS assessment
    

#### To monitor the generation of a tax assessment report: {#procedure_0727012037}

1.  Go to Reports > Tax > Country Tax Reports.
    
2.  On the **Report Execution Log** subtab, find the tax assessment report you want to monitor.
    
    The latest report appears at the top of the list.
    
3.  In the **View | Download** column, wait for the loading bar to finish and turn into a **View** link.
    
    When you see the View link, your report is ready.
    
4.  Click **View**.
    
5.  Choose the format in which you want to download the report:
    
    -   To download the report as a PDF, click the PDF ![Adobe Reader icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/PDF_icon.png) icon.
        
    -   To download the report as a XLS, click the XLS ![XLS file icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/XLS_Icon.png) icon.
        
        Due to a system limitation, you can only download XLS files with sizes up to 10 MB.
