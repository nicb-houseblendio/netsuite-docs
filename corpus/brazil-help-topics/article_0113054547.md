If you're importing goods on behalf or by order of another company, in your import bills, enter the information about the company that hired you.

#### To enter acquirer or orderer information in an import bill for Brazil:

1.  In the import bill you're creating, under Classification, in the **Acquirer or Orderer Federal Tax Registration** field, enter the Cadastro Nacional de Pessoas Jurídicas (CNPJ) of the company that hired you.
    
    The CNPJ is Brazil's federal tax registration for legal entities. Use one of the following formats for the 14-digit registration: AAAAAAAAAAAAAA or AA.AAA.AAA/AAAA-AA.
    
2.  In the **State of the Acquirer or Orderer** field, enter the acronym of the state where the acquirer or orderer is located.
    
3.  Continue entering other information in the transaction.
    
    For more information, see the following help topics:
