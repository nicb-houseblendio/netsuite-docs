Before you generate EFD Contribuições files, ensure you meet the following prerequisites:

-   If you use cost centers, do the following:
    
    -   Enable the Departments feature.
        
        For more information, see [General Company Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N232470.html).
        
    -   Create department records for your cost centers.
        
        For more information, see [Creating Departments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N262248.html).
        
    -   Add codes to your department records.
        
        For more information, see [Cost Centers in Reports for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0708093202.html).
        
    -   Associate the departments to your transactions.
        
-   If your company is entitled to PIS and COFINS credits from the purchase of electricity, natural gas, or water, set up the nature of the calculation basis for credit.
    
    For more information, see [Setting Up the Nature of the Calculation Basis for Credit](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0528093103.html).
    
-   Create a PIS and COFINS assessment record and generate the PIS and COFINS assessment report.
    
    For more information, see [Assessing PIS and COFINS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0513020736.html).
    
-   Ensure that the transactions you enter in NetSuite meet these criteria for inclusion in the EFD Contribuições file:
    
    | \- | **Sales transactions involving goods** | **Purchase transactions involving goods or utilities** | **Sales and purchase transactions involving services** |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Calculated taxes** | Both PIS and COFINS | Both PIS and COFINS | Both PIS and COFINS |
    | **Certified e-document number** | Required | Required | Required |
    | **Date** | \- | \- | 
    -   **RPS Date** field required for sales transactions
    -   **Date** field required for purchase transactions
    
     |
    | **E-document category** | 
    
    -   Electronic invoice for goods
    -   SAT cash receipt
    
     | 
    
    -   Electronic invoice for goods
    -   Electronic bill of lading
    -   Tax, expense, or utility bill
    
     | Electronic invoice for services |
    | **E-document model** | 
    
    -   Model 55 required for the electronic invoice for goods category
    -   Model 55, 59, or 65 required for the SAT cash receipt category
    
     | 
    
    -   Model 55 required for the electronic invoice for goods category
    -   Model 57 or 67 required for the electronic bill of lading
    -   Model 06, 28, 29, 55, or 66 required for the tax, expense, or utility bill category
    
     | \- |
    | **E-document status** | Certified status required for electronic invoices for goods | Certified status required for electronic invoices generated from import bills | Certified status required for electronic invoices generated from sales transactions |
