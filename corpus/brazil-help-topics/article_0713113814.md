If your account meets the following criteria, create revenue recognition treatment records with the same type of revenue recognition event from your deferred revenue recognition settings for Brazil:

-   The Brazil Deferred Revenue Recognition SuiteApp is installed.
    
-   The Rule-Based Recognition Treatment feature is enabled.
    

If you have multiple deferred revenue recognition settings, with different types of revenue recognition event, create the corresponding revenue recognition treatment records as well.

#### To create a revenue recognition treatment record for Brazil:

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Revenue > Recognition Treatments > New_.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter a unique name for the recognition treatment.
    
3.  In the **Description** field, enter a description to help you identify the recognition treatment.
    
4.  In the **Create Revenue Plans On** field, select the same type of revenue recognition event that you selected in your deferred revenue recognition settings.
    
    For more information, see [Creating Deferred Revenue Settings for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0218054736.html).
    
5.  In the **Revenue Recognition Rule** field, select the revenue recognition rule you want to apply.
    
6.  Fill in other relevant fields.
    
    For more information, see [Creating a Recognition Treatment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1559592154.html).
    
7.  Click **Save**.
