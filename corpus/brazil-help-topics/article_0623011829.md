Note:

To access the Brazil Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Certification Tax Authorities](https://system.netsuite.com/help/helpcenter/shared_resources/PDF/BrazilCertificationTaxAuthorities_pt_BR.pdf).

To enable the validation of inbound e-documents, you must associate the validation plug-in to an e-document package.

If you already have an e-document package associated with your e-document templates for Brazil, set the validation plug-in on your existing e-document package.

If you don't have an e-document package, see [Creating E-Document Packages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4576918342.html).

#### To set the validation plug-in on an e-document package: {#procedure_0623014440}

1.  Go to Setup > E-Documents > E-Document Package.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the e-document package you want.
    
3.  In the **Inbound E-Document Validation Plug-In** field, select the inbound e-document validation plug-in you created.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
