Note:

To access the Brazil Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Certification Tax Authorities](https://system.netsuite.com/help/helpcenter/shared_resources/PDF/BrazilCertificationTaxAuthorities_pt_BR.pdf).

The Brazil Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp provides a predefined inbound e-document validation plug-in implementation. The plug-in ensures that the inbound e-document record complies with formatting and content requirements before converting it to a transaction.

You can use the predefined plug-in implementation to create an inbound e-document validation plug-in record and associate it with an e-document package.

-   [Creating an Inbound E-Document Validation Plug-in for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0623101950.html)
    
-   [Associating the Validation Plug-in with an E-Document Package](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0623011829.html)
