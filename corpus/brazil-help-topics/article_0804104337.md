If you generate EFD ICMS IPI files and your subsidiary manufactures goods or is comparable to a manufacturer, you need to identify the subsidiary as a manufacturer.

#### To set a subsidiary as a manufacturer or comparable to a manufacturer: {#procedure_0804110502}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Classifications > Subsidiaries_.
    
2.  Next to the subsidiary you want to set up, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Check the **Manufacturer or Comparable to Manufacturer** box.
    
4.  In the **Classification of the Manufacturer** field, select the subsidiary classification according with the Sistema Público de Escrituração Digital (SPED).
    
5.  Click **Save**.
