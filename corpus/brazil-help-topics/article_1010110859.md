A tax alias mapping record matches a tax code with a tax alias. A tax alias is a placeholder for the tax amount in e-document templates. The alias indicates to NetSuite in which tag to enter the tax amount from a transaction.

Brazil Localization creates predefined tax alias mappings for the predefined tax codes provided by the SuiteTax Latam Engine - Brazil Records SuiteApp.

If you've created a tax code for a special taxation or accounting scenario, copy the corresponding tax alias mapping record.

#### To copy a tax alias mapping from Brazil: {#procedure_1010095411}

1.  Go to Setup > Brazil Localization > Tax Alias Mapping.
    
2.  Next to the tax alias mapping you want to copy, click **View**.
    
    The tax alias mapping opens in view mode.
    
3.  From the **Actions** dropdown, select **Make Copy**.
    
    A new tax alias mapping record opens, with fields already filled.
    
4.  In the **Tax Code** field, select the new tax code.
    
    For more information, see [Creating Tax Codes for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1019112537.html).
    
5.  Click **Save**.
