Note:

To access the Brazil Localization SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Localization](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilLocalization_pt_BR.pdf).

Brazil Localization adds specific fields to contact records to enable you to store information from accountants and company partners.

If you have the Brazil Reports SuiteApp installed, NetSuite prints the company partner and accountant information in statutory reports. For more information, see [Statutory Reports for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0509053952.html).

Before setting up a contact for Brazil, ensure your role has the required permissions to edit contact records. For more information about contacts, see [Contacts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1075037.html).

#### To set up a contact for Brazil: {#procedure_51120359151}

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Contacts_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the record you want.
    
3.  If the contact is an accountant or company partner, in the **Federal Tax Registration** field, enter the contact's federal tax registration.
    
    -   If the contact is a company, enter the Cadastro Nacional de Pessoas Jurídicas (CNPJ). Use one of the following formats for the 14-digit registration: AAAAAAAAAAAAAA or AA.AAA.AAA/AAAA-AA.
        
    -   If the contact is an individual, enter the Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas (CPF) number. Use one of the following formats for the 11-digit number: 00000000000 or 000.000.000-00.
        
4.  If the contact is an accountant, in the **Accountant License Number** field, enter their Conselho Regional de Contabilidade (CRC) registration number.
    
5.  Click **Save.**
