To add more criteria to your Códigos Fiscais de Operações e Prestações (CFOP) determination rules, you must map the custom fields that should serve as the additional criteria.

You can map the following types of custom field for determining CFOP codes:

-   Custom entity field
    
-   Custom item field
    
-   Custom transaction body field
    

After you map a custom field, add it as a criterion of CFOP determination rules. For more information, see [Adding Criteria to CFOP Determination Rules](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1006114547.html).

#### To map a custom field for CFOP determination:

1.  Go to Setup > Brazil Localization > Field Mapping for CFOP > New.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter a name to identify this custom field as an additional criterion for CFOP determination rules.
    
    For example, enter the label of the field and where in NetSuite it appears: Tax regime from entity records.
    
3.  In the **Custom Field ID** field, enter the ID of the custom field you want to add in CFOP determination rules.
    
    For more information, see [Showing Record and Field IDs in Your Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0402065629.html).
    
4.  Click **Save**.
