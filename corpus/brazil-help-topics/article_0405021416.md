To access the Brazil Localization SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Localization](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilLocalization_pt_BR.pdf).

When you select an installment term of payment in an invoice or vendor bill, NetSuite creates installments for the transaction. With Brazil Localization, you can add payment information to make or receive payments for these installments through the Centro Nacional de Automação Bancária (CNAB) system.

For more information about adding payment information to invoices and vendor bills with installments, see the following topics:

-   [Adding Payment Information in Invoices for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0913030538.html)
    
-   [Adding Payment Information in Vendor Bills for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0913030508.html)
    

For more information about creating installment terms of payment, see [Creating Terms of Payment for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_164694494732.html).
