After you process accounts receivable payments with Brazil Banking Integration, you can monitor the status of their integration and of their payment.

#### To monitor receivable payments for Brazil:

1.  Go to Transactions > Bank > A/R Monitor for Brazil.
    
    The Accounts Receivable Monitor for Brazil page opens.
    
2.  Define filters to search for accounts receivable payments.
    
    1.  To filter payments associated with a specific customer or transaction number, in the **Search** field, enter the name of the customer, or the number of the transaction.
        
    2.  To filter transactions and installments associated with a specific subsidiary, in the **Subsidiary** field, select the subsidiary.
        
    3.  To filter transactions and installments associated with a specific payment method, in the **Payment Method** field, select the method.
        
    4.  To filter transactions and installments with a specific integration status, in the **Status** field, select the status.
        
    5.  In the **Period** field, select a start and end date to search for receivable payments due within the defined period.
        
        If you don't set the optional filters, the page lists all the accounts receivable payments within the date range.
        
    
    After you set the filters, NetSuite automatically searches for the accounts receivable payments that match the criteria.
    
3.  Review the results.
    
4.  To check when the information in the page was last updated, verify the time stamp at the top right corner of the page.
    
5.  To update the information in the page based on the filters you set, click **Refresh.**
    
6.  To open the payment instrument, click the link in the **A/R Payment Instrument** column.
