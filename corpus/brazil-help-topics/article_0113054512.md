In import bills for Brazil, enter information about the transport of imported goods.

#### To enter shipment information in import bills for Brazil:

1.  In the import bill you're creating, under Shipment, in the **Mode of Import** field, select the mode in which the goods were imported based on your company's involvement in the process.
    
2.  In the **Import Type** field, select whether the goods were imported by the company directly or through a trading company.
    
3.  In the **International Transportation Method**, select the method used to transport the goods to the country as stated in the import declaration.
    
4.  In the **Exporter Code** field, enter the code that identifies the exporter in the import declaration.
    
5.  Continue entering other information in the transaction.
