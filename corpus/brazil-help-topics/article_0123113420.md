The Brazil Banking Integration SuiteApp provides several features that enable you to connect your bank accounts in Brazil with NetSuite. To leverage the Brazil Banking Integration features, you need specific permissions.

For more information about the roles and permissions for the SuiteApp, see the following help topics:
