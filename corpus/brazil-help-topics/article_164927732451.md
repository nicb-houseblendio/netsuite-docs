The banking information record stores details about your company's bank account in Brazil and your preferences to use the Centro Nacional de Automação Bancária (CNAB) system.

Create banking information records for the subsidiaries that make and receive electronic bank payments in Brazil.

#### To create a banking information record for Brazil:

1.  Go to Setup > Brazil Localization > Banking Information > New.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter a name to identify the banking information record.
    
3.  In the **Subsidiary** field, select the company you want to associate with the bank account and the CNAB preferences.
    
4.  In the **Bank** field, select the bank in which you maintain your company's bank account.
    
5.  In the **Bank Account Type** field, choose the type of account your company maintains at the selected bank.
    
6.  In the **Branch Number** field, enter the number that identifies the bank branch where your company maintains the account.
    
7.  If the branch number includes a check digit, in the **Branch Check Digit** field, enter the digit.
    
8.  In the **Account Number** field, enter your company's bank account number.
    
9.  In the **Account Check Digit** field, enter your company's bank account check digit.
    
10.  In the **Bank GL Account** field, select the GL account to which to post payment amounts.
     
     NetSuite posts both on time and late payments to the selected account. If you don't select an account, payment amounts will be posted to the Undeposited Funds account.
     
11.  If you generate CNAB 240 delivery files, define your CNAB information.
     
     For more information, see [Defining CNAB 240 Information for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1223042331.html).
     
12.  If you generate CNAB 400 delivery files, define your CNAB information.
     
     For more information, see [Defining CNAB 400 Information for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1223042253.html).
     
13.  If you generate CNAB 750 delivery files, define your CNAB information.
     
     For more information, see [Defining CNAB 750 Information for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1223042402.html).
     
14.  Click **Save**.
