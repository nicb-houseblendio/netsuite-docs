To access the Brazil Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Certification Tax Authorities](https://system.netsuite.com/help/helpcenter/shared_resources/PDF/BrazilCertificationTaxAuthorities_pt_BR.pdf).

The Brazil Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp offers a predefined plug-in to track your receiver acknowledgment requests and update records.

You must associate the monitoring plug-in with the receiver acknowledgment tax authority certification service record.

#### To associate the Receiver Acknowledgment Monitoring Plug-in with a tax authority: {#procedure_65141318447}

1.  Go to Setup > E-Document Certification > Tax Authority Certification Service.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to **Receiver Acknowledgment Tax Authority**.
    
3.  In the **Certification Monitoring Plug-In Implementation** field, select **Receiver Acknowledgment Monitoring**.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
