Create additional information presets to add more details about transactions in the e-documents you generate with NetSuite.

The system populates the preset text in the Additional Information field from transaction records. For more information, see [Creating Invoices for Electronic Invoice Generation for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_164121591419.html).

Brazil Localization also comes with a list of additional information preset records by default.

#### To create an additional information preset: {#procedure_0804022801}

1.  Go to Setup > Brazil Localization > Additional Information Preset > New.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter a name for this additional information preset.
    
3.  In the **Additional Information** field, enter any relevant information you want to have as an option to include on electronic invoices.
    
    For example, you can include information about payment terms, discounts, tax credits, tax benefits, or legal details.
    
4.  In the **SPED Code** field, enter the code associated with this type of additional information in the Sistema Público de Escrituração Digital (SPED) system.
    
    The code can have up to 6 characters, between letters and numbers. Special characters aren't supported.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
