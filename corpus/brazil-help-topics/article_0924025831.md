As part of your business, you may import goods for resale, for your own use, or as raw materials for manufacturing. In Brazil, you must register the purchase of goods from international vendors in electronic invoices.

To record your imports to Brazil in NetSuite with the Brazil Localization SuiteApp, you can:

-   Create a standalone import bill to register the import.
    
    For more information, see [Creating Standalone Import Bills for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_72093304056.html).
    
-   Create a purchase order and then make payments, create the import bill from the order, and more.
    
    For more information, see [Purchasing and Receiving Imported Goods in Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1001094343.html).
    

After you create the import bill, you can generate and certify the electronic invoice for goods. For more information, see [Generating Electronic Invoices for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0114113032.html).
