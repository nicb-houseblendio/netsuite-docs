Note:

To access the Brazil Banking Integration SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Banking Integration](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilBankingIntegration_pt_BR.pdf).

With Brazil Banking Integration, you can make electronic bank payments within NetSuite. The SuiteApp enables you to create purchase transactions and then make payments and communicate them to Banco do Brasil. The integration eliminates the need to generate, send, and import payment files to record payments.

Brazil Banking Integration only supports bank slips for the banking integration of accounts payable.

For more information about making payments through banking integration, see the following help topics:
