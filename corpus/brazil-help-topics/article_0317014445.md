Note:

To access the Brazil Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Certification Tax Authorities](https://system.netsuite.com/help/helpcenter/shared_resources/PDF/BrazilCertificationTaxAuthorities_pt_BR.pdf).

Brazil Certification Tax Authorities provides predefined e-document template files for several receiver acknowledgement (MDe) events.

The SuiteApp also provides an e-document template file for you to convert inbound e-documents into NetSuite transactions.

#### To download e-document template files for receiver acknowledgment events:

1.  Go to _Documents > Files > File Cabinet_.
    
2.  In the sidebar or list, click **SuiteApps**.
    
    The files and folders within this folder appear.
    
3.  Click the **com.netsuite.brazilcertificationtaxauth** folder.
    
4.  Click the **braziltemplates** folder.
    
5.  Next to the **Receiver-Acknowledgment** folder, click **Download**.
    
    NetSuite downloads all the e-document templates in the folder to your device.
    
    For more information about the receiver acknowledgment setup, see [Receiver Acknowledgment Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0623100748.html).
    
6.  If you want to convert inbound e-documents into NetSuite transactions, do the following:
    
    1.  On the sidebar, click the **E-Document Conversion** folder.
        
    2.  Next to the template, click **Download**.
        
        For more information about inbound e-document templates for Brazil, see [Creating Inbound E-Document Templates for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0620032543.html).
