Bank account details records store information about an individual's or a company's bank account.

Create bank account details for:

-   Testing
    
    To test the connection with Banco do Brasil, create the bank details with the bank's fictional data. You can find the fictional bank branch number and account number in the Portal Developers BB website.
    
    Note:
    
    Ensure the branch and account that match the test field value you added to your bank statement requests. For more information, see [Adding the Test Field to Bank Statement Request Headers for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0128010259.html).
    
    After you complete your tests, you can delete the bank account details record with the fictional data.
    
-   Subsidiaries in Brazil
    
-   Vendors in Brazil
    
-   Employees in Brazil
    

Before you create bank account details, ensure you defined your banking integration preferences. For more information, see [Defining Banking Integration Preferences for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0114014706.html).

#### To create bank account details for Brazil:

1.  Go to Setup > Brazil Banking Integration > Bank Account Details > New.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter a name to identify the bank account.
    
3.  In the **Bank** field, select the bank where the company or individual maintains the account.
    
4.  In the **Country** field, select **Brazil**.
    
5.  In the **Type** field, select the type of this bank account.
    
6.  In the **Branch Number** field, enter the number of the bank branch where the account is maintained.
    
7.  In the **Branch Check Digit** field, enter the check digit that identifies the bank branch where the account is maintained.
    
8.  In the **Account Number** field, enter the bank account number.
    
    After you save the bank account details record, NetSuite hides and encrypts the content of this field to protect your account number information.
    
9.  In the **Account Check Digit** field, enter the bank account's check digit.
    
10.  In the **Bank GL Account** field, select the GL account to which to post payments associated with this bank account.
     
11.  Select the owner of the bank account.
     
     -   For subsidiaries, in the **Subsidiary** field, select the company.
         
     -   For employees or vendors, in the **Customer, Employee, or Vendor** field, select the company or individual who maintains the account.
         
12.  Click **Save**.
     
     NetSuite saves the new bank account details.
     
13.  If the bank account belongs to one of your subsidiaries, do the following:
     
     1.  To receive payments through banking integration, do the following:
         
         1.  Define the settings to receive payments with bank slips.
             
             For more information, see [Defining Bank Slip Settings for Banking Integration for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0327111559.html).
             
         2.  Define the settings to receive payments with Pix.
             
             For more information, see [Defining Pix Settings for Banking Integration for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1205014141.html).
             
         3.  Associate Pix keys with the bank account.
             
             For more information, see [Associating Pix Keys with Bank Account Details for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0327023701.html).
             
     2.  To make payments through banking integration, set up accounts payable payments.
         
         For more information, see [Defining Accounts Payable Payment Settings for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0904115452.html).
         
     3.  Set up the payment methods you want to use with the bank account.
         
         Define the applicable fine and interest rates for each payment method you associate with the bank account. For more information, see [Setting Up Payment Methods for Bank Accounts in Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0326054334.html).
         
14.  If the bank account belongs to an employee or vendor, do the following:
     
     1.  To make payments to the employee or vendor through a Pix key, associate their Pix key.
         
         For more information, see [Associating Pix Keys with Bank Account Details for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0327023701.html).
         
         Note:
         
         If you don't associate a Pix key with the bank account and try to make a Pix, NetSuite uses the account information as the key.
         
     2.  To make payments to the employee or vendor with the account information, check the **Set as Default Bank Account Details** box.
