Note:

To access the Brazil Reports documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Reports](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilReports_pt_BR.pdf).

Ensure that the transactions you enter in NetSuite meet these criteria for inclusion in the IRPJ and CSLL assessment:

| **Transaction type** | Invoice |
| --- | --- |
| **Calculated taxes** | 
-   IRRF
-   CSLL separately or as part of the CSRF For more information, see [Separate Withheld COFINS, CSLL, and PIS Taxes from CSRF](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1016816/loc/en_US), SuiteAnswers ID 1016816.

 |
| **Certified e-document number** | Required |
| **Date** | For IRPJ, the RPS date For CSLL withheld on an accrual basis, the RPS date For CSLL withheld on a cash basis, the invoice or installment payment date Important: NetSuite uses the **Type of Date** parameter type to identify whether the CSRF or the CSLL separately was withheld on certification or payment. For more information, see [Parameter Types for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_163664116234.html). |
| **E-document category** | Electronic invoice for services |
| **E-document status** | Certified |
