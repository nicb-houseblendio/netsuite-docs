To access the Brazil Reports documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [Brazil Reports](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=BrazilReports_pt_BR.pdf).

If you generate one of the following reports with the Brazil Reports SuiteApp, you must set up your account records with Brazil-specific information:

-   Brazil balance sheet
    
-   Escrituração Contábil Fiscal (ECF)
    
-   Escrituração Contábil Digital (ECD)
    
-   Brazil income statement
    
-   Brazil trial balance
    

For more information about the account record, see [Creating Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1440518.html) and [Making Changes to Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1442743.html).

Important:

Ensure that you checked the **Use Account Numbers** box on your account's Accounting Preferences page. All accounts you use with your Brazilian subsidiaries must have a number assigned.

For more information, see [Chart of Account Numbering](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1440268.html).

#### To set up an account to appear in reports for Brazil: {#procedure_0816091948}

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Manage G/L > Chart of Accounts_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the account you want to set up.
    
3.  To indicate the account is a subaccount, in the **Parent Account in the Brazilian Chart of Accounts** field, select the parent account.
    
    The parent account must be a summary account. In Brazil, accounts can be subdivided into several levels. The account level affects how NetSuite displays the account in the Brazilian trial balance, balance sheet, and Sistema Público de Escrituração Digital (SPED) files.
    
4.  In the **SPED Referential Chart of Accounts Code** field, enter the code assigned to this type of account in the SPED referential charts of accounts.
    
    This setting maps the account to the referential charts of accounts from the SPED system.
    
5.  In the **Income Statement Section** field, select the section of the income statement with which you want to associate the account.
    
    This setting includes the account's balance in the income statement for Brazil. If you don't choose a section, NetSuite leaves out the account's balance from the income statement.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
