You need specific permissions to access the custom records from the Brazil Reports SuiteApp. By default, the Administrator role is granted full permissions to the custom records. If other roles need to view, access, or edit the custom records, an administrator can give them permission.

-   **Category** - Lists
    
-   **Permission** - Custom Record Entries
    
-   **Access Level** - Full
