The Brazil Banking Integration SuiteApp enables you to connect NetSuite with your bank accounts in Brazil.

With Brazil Banking Integration, you can set up the communication between NetSuite and banking APIs. The SuiteApp leverages custom records and the following types of plug-in to securely access online bank data:

-   Brazil Banking Data Retrieval
    
-   Brazil Banking Integration
    
-   Financial Institution Connectivity
    

The Brazil Banking Integration SuiteApp provides the following features:

-   **Accounts receivable automation** - Custom records, fields, and other settings to receive payments with bank slips and Pix transfers without the need to manually exchange electronic bank files.
    
-   **Accounts payable automation** - Custom records, fields, and other settings to make payments with bank slips without the need to manually exchange electronic bank files.
    
-   **Automatic bank statement import** - Periodical imports that help you with bank reconciliation and reduce the manual effort of importing and matching your bank data.
    

The SuiteApp currently only supports connections with Banco do Brasil.
