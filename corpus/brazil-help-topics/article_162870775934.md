Note:

To access the SuiteTax Latam Engine - Brazil Records documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [SuiteTax Latam Engine - Brazil Records](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=SuiteTaxLatamEngineBrazil_pt_BR.pdf).

The predefined tax determination rules provided by the SuiteTax Latam Engine - Brazil Records SuiteApp include generic information for tax calculation in Brazil.

To use the predefined records for tax calculation in your Brazil transactions, you can add your company-specific information to the records. You can also make copies of the predefined records and set them up to fit your business needs. For example, you can:

-   Select one or multiple subsidiaries to which to apply the rules.
    
    If you don't select at least one subsidiary, the rules don't apply to any transaction.
    
-   Apply the rules to companies operating in a specific line of business.
    
-   Limit the rules to transactions to or from a given location.
    
    -   To limit the rules to transactions to or from a certain country, select a country in the **Country** fields.
        
    -   To limit the rules to transactions to or from a certain state, enter the state's acronym in the **State** fields.
        
        Ensure you entered the correct acronym to enable NetSuite to match the rules with the proper state.
        
        For example, if the rules apply only to transactions from the state of Bahia, under Issuer Information, in the **State** field, enter **BA**.
        
    -   To limit the rules to transactions to or from a certain city, enter the city's name in the **City** fields and the state's acronym in the **State** fields.
        
        Ensure you entered both the correct city name and state acronym so NetSuite can match the rules to the proper city.
        
        For example, if the rules apply only to transactions to the city of Salvador, in the state of Bahia, do the following:
        
        -   Under Receiver Information, in the **City** field, enter **Salvador**.
            
        -   Under Receiver Information, in the **State** field, enter **BA**.
            
        
-   Apply the rules only to transactions with a specific Código Fiscal de Operações e de Prestações (CFOP) code.
    
-   Apply the rules only to transactions involving items identified by a specific service code or Mercosur Common Nomenclature (MCN) code.
    
    For more information, see [Creating Tax Determination Rules for Specific CFOP, MCN, or Service Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_164760698762.html).
    
-   Add a parameter, such as a Código de Situação Tributária (CST) code, to narrow down the applicability of the rules.
    
    For more information about parameter types, see [Parameter Types for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_163664116234.html).
    
-   Apply the rules to items with specific origin codes.
    
    Include the origin code as a supplementary information code in the tax determination settings from the applicable rules. For more information, see [Creating Tax Determination Settings for Specific Origin Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0925050414.html).
    

You must also keep your tax determination rules preferences up to date to stay compliant with any regulatory or business changes. The SuiteTax Latam Engine - Brazil Records SuiteApp doesn't provide updates for the predefined tax determination rules.

For more information about tax determination rules records, see [Creating Tax Determination Rules in the SuiteTax Latam Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162153526964.html).

After you set up your tax determination rules, ensure the following records are associated with the parameters to which the rules apply:

-   Subsidiary
    
-   Vendor
    
-   Customer
    
-   Item
    

For example, verify if the subsidiary and its customers are associated with the proper tax regime and line of business. Also, if the item is associated with the proper item code. For more information, see the following help topics:

-   [Associating Tax Regime and Line of Business With Subsidiaries, Customers, and Vendors](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162033215141.html)
    
-   [Associating Item Codes With Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162033229352.html)
