When including comparative column, assign the **.csr-secondary** class to related value cells.

          `<table>     <!-- ... --> </table>   <div>     <table>         <thead>             <tr>                 <td width="70%" rowspan="2">${VAR.header_label.name}</td>                 <td width="15%">${VAR.header_value_now.name}</td>                 <td width="15%">${VAR.header_value_previous.name}</td>             </tr>         </thead>         <tbody>             <tr>                 <td>${VAR.some_var_1.name}</td>                 <td>${VAR.some_var_1.value}</td>                 <td>${VAR.some_var_1.value2}</td>             </tr>             <tr>                 <td>${VAR.some_var_2.name}</td>                 <td>${VAR.some_var_2.value}</td>                 <td>${VAR.some_var_2.value2}</td>             </tr>         </tbody>     </table> </div>`
