For variables that consist of multiple accounts, you can enable a hierarchical display of the accounts in your report. Instead of a list, the report displays the accounts included in a variable based on the parent-child hierarchy in your Chart of Accounts.

Important:

This display type works only if your report's HTML/XML template contains the macro for handling a hierarchy view. Make sure your List of Accounts macro includes the hierarchy view macro. For more information, see [List of Accounts in Country-Specific Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_159775319391.html).

#### To display the parent-child hierarchy of accounts in your report: {#procedure_1107025918}

1.  Enable the hierarchical display feature on the variable record:
    
    1.  Open a report on Reports > Financial > Country-Specific Reports.
        
    2.  Click **View Structure**.
        
    3.  On the Variables subtab, click **Edit** next to the variable whose accounts you want to be displayed in a hierarchy.
        
    4.  On the variable record, check the **Display Hierarchy** box.
        
    5.  Click **Save**.
        
2.  Go to Reports > Financial > Country-Specific Reports.
    
3.  Select the same report in step 1a.
    
4.  Click **Show Report**.
    
5.  Check the **Show Individual Accounts** box.
