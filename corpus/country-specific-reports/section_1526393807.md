Important:

The Counry-Specific Reports SuiteApp will no longer receive feature enhancements. Support will still be provided to issues but no new features will be added.

Country-Specific Reports SuiteApp enables users to create, edit, and view custom financial reports.

#### To install the Country-Specific Reports SuiteApp:

1.  Go to Customization > SuiteBundler > Search & Install Bundles (Administrator).
    
    Note:
    
    Read the Installation Terms of Service on the use of the NetSuite SuiteBundler. Click **I Agree** if you agree, and **I Disagree** if you don't agree with the terms.
    
2.  In the Keywords field, enter **Country-Specific Reports**, or the Bundle ID **301545**.
    
3.  Click **Search**.
    
4.  In the **Name** column, click **Country-Specific Reports**.
    
5.  On the Bundle Details page, click **Install**.
    

The Country-Specific Reports SuiteApp is currently available as a public SuiteApp. Contact NetSuite Customer Support to request access to the SuiteApp. For information about installing SuiteApps, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).

The Country-Specific Reports is a managed bundle, and is automatically updated whenever improvements or new features are added.
