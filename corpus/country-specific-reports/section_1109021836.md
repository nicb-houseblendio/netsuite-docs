This help topic is about deleting records that you created with the Country-Specific Reports SuiteApp. These records include a report or variable record.

Note:

In most cases, the reports that are provided by Localization SuiteApps are locked. You cannot delete locked reports as well as the variables and filters in these locked reports.

#### To delete a report, filter, or variable: {#procedure_161434323625}

1.  Go to _Customization > List, Records, & Fields > Record Types_.
    
2.  Find the appropriate record that you want to delete:
    
    1.  If you are deleting a report, find CSR - Report.
        
    2.  If you are deleting a variable, find CSR - Variable.
        
3.  Click **List** on the row of the record type where you want to delete a record.
    
4.  Click **Edit** next to the name of the variable or report that you want to delete.
    
5.  Click the **Actions** dropdown arrow and click **Delete**.
    
6.  Click **OK** to confirm.
    

You cannot delete any record that has dependent records. For example, you cannot delete a report that still contains variables or filters. In the same way, you cannot delete a variable that is referenced in a report or in another variable. You need to delete the dependent records first.

You can also consider inactivating a report or variable record. Inactive variables won't be used in the reports. Inactive reports are not visible on the Country-Specific Reports page. For more information, see [Inactivating Country-Specific Report Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1111020839.html).
