To close a work order, you must complete a build that assembles the component items. Complete the steps below to process work orders for assembly builds on your mobile device.

Note:

You must set up a WIP bin location to build assembly items. For more information, see [Creating Bin Locations or Carts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161314312081.html#section_1541433492).

If you use the Lot Auto Numbering SuiteApp, you can activate any of these rules to autogenerate numbers:

-   **Autogenerate Lot Numbers?**
    
-   **Autogenerate Serial Numbers?**
    

When you process an assembly build on the app, you can provide the details for the autogenerate numbers that you specify for the assembly item. To set up system rules, see [System Rules for NetSuite WMS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156406918320.html).

#### To build the assembly item for a work order:

1.  On your mobile device, tap the menu icon at the top-left corner of the screen, and then tap **Main Menu**.
    
2.  On the Main Menu page, tap **Work Orders**.
    
3.  On the Work Orders page, tap **Build Assembly**.
    
4.  On the Select Work Order page, do one of the following:
    
    1.  In the **Scan/Enter Work Order #** field, scan or enter the work order number. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Work Order #**.
        
    2.  To select from the list of work orders, tap the work order number.
        
5.  On the Enter Quantity page, do the following:
    
    1.  In the **Enter Quantity** field, enter the number of items you want to build.
        
    2.  If you use the Inventory Status feature, in the **Inventory Status** field, select the status of the item.
        
    3.  (Optional) If you have set up mobile printing and want to manually print labels, tap **Print**.
        
        Tip:
        
        You can print labels with lot or serial numbers on pages on which you can enter them. See step 6 or step 8, respectively.
        
        For more information about mobile printing, see [Manually Printing Labels from Your Mobile Device](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158681903713.html).
        
    4.  Tap **Enter Quantity**.
        
6.  If you can autogenerate numbers, on the Autogenerate Number page, do the following:
    
    1.  Enter the required and optional details that you want to include in each autogenerated number.
        
    2.  Tap the appropriate **Autogenerate Number** button.
        
        Alternatively, you can tap the more icon, and then tap **Skip** if you don't want to autogenerate numbers for this assembly item..
        
7.  On the Enter Lot page, do the following:
    
    1.  **Scan/Enter Lot** - If you do not autogenerate numbers, scan or enter the lot number.
        
        For autogenerated numbers, the number appears in the **Autogenerated Number** field instead.
        
    2.  **Expiry Date** - (Optional) You can enter the expiry date for the lot.
        
        If you entered the expiry date in step 6 for autogenerated lot numbers, you cannot edit it in this field.
        
    3.  If you want to manually print a label with the lot number, tap **Print**. See [Manually Printing Labels from Your Mobile Device](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158681903713.html).
        
    4.  Tap **Enter Lot**.
        
8.  For warehouse locations that use bins, on the Enter Bin page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the **Scan/Enter Bin** field, scan or enter the bin number. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Bin** to proceed.
        
    -   To select from the list of bins, tap the **Show all bins** toggle, and then tap the bin number.
        
9.  If you want to build a serialized assembly item, on the Enter Serial Number page, in the **Scan/Enter Serial Number** field, scan or enter the serial number. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Serial Number** to proceed.
    
    If you autogenerate serial numbers, you can review them on this page.
    
    Repeat this step to enter more serial numbers.
    
    Tip:
    
    If you want to manually print a label with the serial number, enter the serial number, and then tap **Print**. See [Manually Printing Labels from Your Mobile Device](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158681903713.html).
    
10.  If your warehouse location uses bins and you want to stage inventory items, tap **Go to Staging**, and then on the Enter Staging Bin page, do one of the following:
     
     -   In the **Scan/Enter Staging Bin** field, scan or enter the number of the staging bin. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Staging Bin** to proceed.
         
     -   To select from the list of bins, tap the **Show all bins** toggle, and then tap the bin number.
         
     
     A message appears to confirm that you have staged the items successfully.
     

NetSuite records a completed assembly build on the work order and assigns the work order to Built status. It increases the assembly item's stock level in the storage bin location where you put it away. It also decreases the component items' individual stock levels in the work-in-progress (WIP) bin from which you built the assembly.
