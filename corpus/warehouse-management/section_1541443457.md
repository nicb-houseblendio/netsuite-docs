After you ship items, you can send Advance Shipping Notices (ASNs) to customers using Electronic Data Interchange (EDI). To send ASNs using EDI, you have to configure NetSuite WMS for use with EDI and work with a NetSuite EDI partner.

For more information, see [Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156840265669.html).
