You can assign NetSuite WMS roles that provide access to records, tasks, and capabilities on the mobile app and the NetSuite UI. You can also update existing custom roles with permissions for inbound, inventory, and outbound processing. For more information, see the following topics:

-   [Warehouse Management Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156520478173.html)
    
-   [Warehouse Management Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156520480478.html)
    

Assign roles and permissions to pickers or warehouse operators, warehouse managers, and mobile administrators.

If you want to obtain or assign the Specialized User: WMS role, see [Specialized User: WMS Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0318011053.html).

If you want to provide or limit access to a custom mobile process, see [Assigning Custom Roles to Custom Mobile Processes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161521589524.html).
