If you set up Inbound Staging bin types as carts, you can use them for bin transfers. Before you store items to another bin location, you can temporarily load them onto a cart. You can also transfer items from one cart to another.

On the app, you can perform these tasks through the Bin Transfer Via Cart process. Using the same mobile process, you can transfer the items from the cart onto their new bin location for storage.

Depending on the task you want to complete, see the following procedures:

-   [Loading Items onto Carts](#subsect_10103316155)
    
-   [Transferring Items from Carts into Bins](#subsect_28104210487)
    

## Loading Items onto Carts {#subsect_10103316155}

This procedure shows you how to load items from carts or bins onto a cart:

#### To load items onto carts: {#procedure_0220103346}

1.  Access the mobile process by doing the following:
    
    1.  On the mobile app, tap the menu icon at the left corner of the screen, and then tap **Main Menu**.
        
    2.  On the Main Menu page, tap **Inventory**.
        
    3.  On the Inventory menu page, tap **Bin Transfer Via Cart**.
        
2.  On the Enter Cart page, specify the empty cart by doing one of the following:
    
    Note:
    
    If you enter a cart that already contains items, the **Scan/Enter To Bin or Item** page appears. To transfer the items into a bin, proceed to step 3 of [Transferring Items from Carts into Bins](#subsect_28104210487).
    
    -   In the **Scan/Enter Cart** field, scan or enter the empty cart. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Cart** to proceed.
        
    -   From the Carts list, tap an empty cart.
        
3.  On the Bin Transfer page, in the **Scan/Enter From Bin or Item** field, scan or enter the originating bin or the item. If you enter the data, tap **Enter From Bin/Item** to proceed. Alternatively, you can tap **Find Item** to search for an item by item name, number, or UPC.
    
    If you are redirected to this page to change the bin, you can provide a different originating bin. Alternatively, in case you have remaining items on the cart that you want to transfer into a bin, tap **Putaway**. To continue, see [Transferring Items from Carts into Bins](#subsect_28104210487).
    
4.  Depending on your entry in step 3, do one of the following to provide either the item or originating bin:
    
    -   On the Enter Item page, in the **Scan/Enter Item** field, scan or enter the item. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Item**. Alternatively, you can tap an item from the Items list.
        
        If you want to change the originating bin, tap **Change From Bin**. Return to step 3.
        
    -   On the Enter From Bin page, in the **Scan/Enter From Bin** field, scan or enter the bin. If you enter the data, tap **Enter From Bin**. Alternatively, you can tap the **Show all bins** toggle to view and select rom the bins list.
        
5.  On the Enter Quantity page, do the following:
    
    1.  In the **Enter Quantity** field, enter the number of items that you want to load to the cart.
        
    2.  If the bin contains the item in different inventory statuses, you can select a status.
        
    3.  Tap **Enter Quantity**.
        
6.  On the Enter Item page, you can view the confirmation message about the items loaded onto the cart. Then, you can do one of the following additional tasks:
    
    -   To load another item, you can scan, enter, or select it. Return to step 5.
        
    -   To change the originating bin, tap **Change From Bin.** Repeat from step 3.
        
    -   To transfer items on the cart to a bin, tap **Transfer**. See [Transferring Items from Carts into Bins](#subsect_28104210487).
        

## Transferring Items from Carts into Bins {#subsect_28104210487}

After you load items onto a cart, you can transfer them into the same or different bins. You can load more items onto a cart before you transfer them. You can also transfer all the items on the cart into a single bin at the same time.

#### To transfer items from carts into bins: {#procedure_0220104244}

1.  Access the mobile process by doing the following:
    
    Tip:
    
    If you are continuing from step 6 of [Loading Items onto Carts](#subsect_10103316155), start from step 3 instead.
    
    1.  On the mobile app, tap the menu icon at the left corner of the screen, and then tap **Main Menu**.
        
    2.  On the Main Menu page, tap **Inventory**.
        
    3.  On the Inventory menu page, tap **Bin Transfer Via Cart**.
        
2.  On the Enter Cart page, specify the loaded cart by doing one of the following:
    
    -   In the **Scan/Enter Cart** field, scan or enter the loaded cart. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Cart** to proceed.
        
    -   From the Carts list, tap a loaded cart.
        
3.  On the Cart Details page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the **Scan/Enter To Bin or Item** field, scan or enter the destination bin or the item you want to transfer. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Item/Bin** to proceed.
        
        Tip:
        
        If you want to transfer all the items from the cart, specify the destination bin. Then in step 4, you can choose the **Transfer All** option.
        
    -   From the Items On Cart list, tap the item you want to transfer
        
    -   If you want to load items to the cart, tap **Add Item**. To continue, start from step 3 of [Loading Items onto Carts](#subsect_10103316155).
        
4.  Depending on your entry in step 3, do either of the following:
    
    -   On the Enter To Bin page, in the **Scan/Enter To Bin** field, scan or enter the destination bin. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Bin** to proceed. Alternatively, you can tap a bin in the Recommended Bin field or from the bins list.
        
    -   On the Enter Item page, in the **Scan/Enter Item** field, scan or enter the item you want to transfer. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Item** to proceed. Alternatively, from the Items on Cart list, tap the item.
        
        If you want to transfer all the items from the cart, tap **Transfer All**. You can skip step 5.
        
5.  On the Enter Quantity page, do the following:
    
    1.  In the **Enter Quantity** field, enter the number of items you want to transfer.
        
    2.  (Optional) If the item has multiple inventory statuses, you can assign a new status.
        
6.  Tap **Enter Quantity**.
    

On the Transfer Complete page, you can review the details of the items transferred from the cart to the destination bin. If you want to transfer another item from the same cart, you can tap **Next Transfer**. Repeat from step 3.
