Items might arrive at your warehouse for various reasons, which may include the following:

-   Vendors send ordered items.
    
-   Other warehouse locations transfer items to your warehouse.
    
-   Customers send authorized returns.
    

You receive purchase order items from vendors and suppliers. You receive transfer order items from your company's various warehouse locations. You receive return authorization items as returns from customers.

Before you can receive items into your warehouse, you must enter and approve purchase orders, transfer orders, or return authorizations in NetSuite. These transactions contain details of a purchase, transfer, return, or receipt of items within your warehouse.

NetSuite WMS recognizes and lets you process transactions that meet the following conditions:

-   All orders must be approved.
    
-   The warehouse location for an order must be the same as the warehouse location you select on the app. For transfer orders, it must be the same as the to location or the receiving warehouse.
    
-   Purchase orders and transfer orders must have the Pending Receipt or Pending Billing/Partially Received status. Customer returns orders must have the Pending Receipt or Pending Refund/Partially Received status.
    
-   Outsourced purchase orders are not supported. Within the app, you cannot receive purchase orders created using the **Standard Outsourced Purchase Order** form or related custom forms.
    
-   If you use the Centralized Purchasing and Billing feature, you can receive centralized purchase orders on your mobile device. On purchase orders, make sure you select the target subsidiary and target location in the order lines. Restrictions and limitations that apply to this feature also applies to receiving centralized purchase orders on the app. For more information, see [Centralized Purchasing and Billing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161122386910.html).
    
-   Centralized purchase orders do not support drop shipments and special orders. They don't appear on inbound shipment records.

Depending on the system rules you activate, you can receive items to a staging bin location or put them away directly to their storage locations as you receive them. You can autogenerate lot numbers. For both lot and serialized items, you can receive all quantities of a line entered in a purchase order's inventory details. You can also activate the rule to restock or write-off returned items. Note that only one restock setting can apply to partially received items. When you receive purchase orders, you can activate the rule to capture item weights from your scale.

If you activate the **Manually post item receipts?** rule, you can post receipts for partially and fully received items within the receiving process. Another option is through the separate Post Item Receipt process, after you complete all your orders. To post receipts outside of the receiving process, see [Manually Posting Item Receipts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_155993562169.html).

When you scan item bar codes to receive an item, the following system rules may affect the process flow:

-   **Enable Advanced Barcode Scanning?** - You can set up and scan item bar codes with one of the following advanced bar code types: GS1, composite, or HIBC.
    
-   **Enable Tally Scan?** - Without entering the total received quantity, you can scan each item to automatically increment the received quantity by 1. Includes the option to tally scan across item types.
    
-   **Scan bar codes with bulk serial numbers?** - For PO Receiving only, you can scan a single item bar code of a serialized item to enter up to 300 serial numbers at a time.
    

To receive orders or returns by scanning, see [Receiving Orders by Scanning Through the App](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0416111212.html). This topic provides guidelines and instructions specific to scanning item bar codes.

To activate rules, see [System Rules for NetSuite WMS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156406918320.html).

#### To receive a purchase order, transfer order, or customer return:

1.  Access order receiving by doing the following:
    
    1.  On the WMS app, tap the menu icon at the top-left corner of the screen, and then tap **Main Menu**.
        
    2.  On the Main Menu, tap **Receiving**.
        
    3.  On the Receiving menu, depending on the type of transaction you want to process, tap **Purchase Order**, **Transfer Order**, or **Returns**.
        
        The Receiving page for the specific transaction type appears. The field names and options on this page vary depending on the transaction type.
        
2.  To choose an order, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the **Scan/Enter PO # or Item** field, scan or enter the transaction number or item. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Item** to proceed.
        
    -   To select from the list of transactions, tap **Select PO From List**, and then tap the transaction you want to process.
        
    -   To search for an order, tap one of the following: **Find PO By Vendor**, **Find PO by Item**, or **Find PO by MPN**. If you search by item, you can provide the item name, number, or UPC.
        
        Tip:
        
        If you do not find the vendor from the list, you can search by vendor name by clicking the search icon.
        
3.  On the Enter Item page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the **Scan/Enter Item** field, scan or enter the item name, number, or UPC. Then, tap **Enter Item** to proceed.
        
    -   To select from the list of items, tap the item name or number.
        
        Tip:
        
        If you do not find the item from the list, you can view the list of fully received items per order by tapping **Show Completed**.
        
    -   If you can post receipts manually, you can tap **Post Receipt**. Then, on the Receipt Details page, review the items and quantities included in the receipt before you tap **Post Item Receipt**. You can skip the rest of the steps or receive another item or order.
        
        This option creates an item receipt for all partially and fully received item lines for the order. From the main item table, you can view partially processed items with quantities in the **Received** column. To view fully received items, tap **Show Completed**.
        
4.  If the item has multiple aliases, in the Select Item Alias page, do one of the following:
    
    -   If the item bar code includes the item alias, in the **Scan/Enter Item Alias** field, scan the item.
        
    -   From the list of item aliases, tap the item alias
        
5.  For PO receiving, if you can autogenerate lot numbers, on the Autogenerate Lot Number page, do the following:
    
    1.  In each field, enter the lot item detail.
        
    2.  Tap **Autogenerate Lot Number**.
        
6.  For lot-numbered items, on the **Enter Lot Number** page, do the following:
    
    1.  (Optional) In the **Expiry Date** field, enter the expiry date of the lot item.
        
    2.  In the **Scan/Enter Lot** field, scan or enter the lot number.
        
        Tip:
        
        If you can receive the entire purchase order line, you can review the lot numbers and quantities from the Expected Lots list. Then, tap **Receive All**. To proceed, go to step 9 to enter a bin or return to step 3 to receive another item.
        
    3.  Tap **Enter Lot**.
        
7.  On the Enter Quantity page, do the following:
    
    1.  If you use the Inventory Status feature, in the **Inventory Status** field, select the status of the item.
        
    2.  For customer returns, if you can edit the **Restock?** field, select **YES** or **NO**.
        
        Your selection appears in the same **Restock?** column on item receipts. If you manually post item receipts, you cannot update the Restock? field on the app. It follows your Restock Returned Items preference setting.
        
    3.  To enter the quantity, do one of the following:
        
        Tip:
        
        If you tally scan item bar codes, you can change the Inventory Status for full or partial quantities. You can do the same if you can edit the **Restock?** field on the app.
        
        To partially receive an item, enter or tally scan the partial quantity. Then, tap **Enter Quantity**. You can receive the remaining items at a later time.
        
        -   In the **Enter Quantity** field, enter the number of items you want to receive.
            
        -   If you use regular Tally Scanning, in the **Tally Scan** field, scan the GS1, composite, or HIBC item bar code. After you tally scan all the quantities of an item, repeat from step 3 if you have another item to receive.
            
        -   For purchase orders, if you use Tally Scanning across item types, in the **Tally Scan** field, scan the GS1 or composite item bar code. After you tally scan the different item types, you can proceed to step 5.f if you want to print item labels.
            
    4.  If your item has multiple units, you can change the unit.
        
        Tip:
        
        To view unit conversion rates, tap the unit icon beside the **Quantity Remaining** field. Each time you edit the quantity, make sure that you assign the correct unit.
        
    5.  (Optional) If your warehouse location doesn't use bins and you want to view the list of stock locations associated with the item, tap the **Show Stock Locations** toggle.
        
    6.  (Optional) For PO receiving, if you can capture item weights from your scale, in the **Weight Units** field, select the unit of measure for the weight of each quantity. Then, tap **Capture Weight**.
        
        If you change the weight unit and you also need to update the weight, make sure that you tap **Capture Weight** again.
        
        The **Weight Per Quantity** field displays the weight of each item quantity. You can tap the info icon to view the calculated total weight of the received quantity.
        
    7.  (Optional) For PO receiving, to manually print or save labels for regular or lot items, tap **Print**.
        
        Tip:
        
        For serialized items, if you want to print labels with serial numbers, proceed to step 8.
        
        For more information about mobile printing, see [Manually Printing Labels from Your Mobile Device](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158681903713.html).
        
    8.  Tap **Enter Quantity**.
        
8.  For serialized items, on the Enter Serial Number page, do the following:
    
    Tip:
    
    If you can receive the entire purchase order line, you can review the serial numbers from the Show Expected list. Then, tap **Receive All**. To proceed, go to step 9 to enter a bin or return to step 3 to receive another item.
    
    1.  In the **Scan/Enter Serial Number** field, scan or enter a serial number for the item.
        
        Alternatively, depending on the rules you activate for PO Receiving, you can scan an item bar code that contains the serial numbers. The app doesn't process all the serial numbers if the bar code contains more than the quantity to receive. It also doesn't include those that already exist in your NetSuite account. In case you have remaining serialized items to receive, you can manually enter the serial numbers. You can tap the **Show possible serial numbers** toggle to view numbers from the bar code that have not been entered.
        
    2.  (Optional) For PO receiving, to manually print or save an item label with the serial number, tap **Print**.
        
        For instructions on mobile printing, see [Manually Printing Labels from Your Mobile Device](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158681903713.html).
        
    3.  Tap **Enter Serial Number**.
        
    
    Repeat steps a - c to enter another serial number.
    
9.  For warehouse locations that use bins, on the Enter Bin page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the **Scan/Enter Bin** field, scan or enter the number of the destination bin. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Bin** to proceed.
        
    -   To select from the list of destination bins, tap the bin number.
        

If you have more items to receive from the same order, the Enter Item page appears. Otherwise, a message appears to confirm that you have received all the items.

If you do not post item receipts manually, NetSuite WMS automatically generates item receipts for the received items. It also updates the status and item's received quantity in the associated order transaction. For more information about posting item receipts, see [Item Receipts in NetSuite WMS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541435993.html).
