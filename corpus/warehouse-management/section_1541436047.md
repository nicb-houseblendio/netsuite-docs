After you receive an item, you need to place the item in the warehouse for storage until you ship it out. This process is called putaway. After you put away an item, its inventory count increases in the appropriate bin locations. If you put away a lot numbered or serialized item, the system updates the item's lot number or serial number details in the appropriate bin location. An item is available for allocation to sales orders or transfer orders after you put it away to a storage location. For more information, see [Putaway Strategies](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541436052.html) and [Putting Away Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_155993591245.html).

If you use carts to move and putaway your items, see [Putting Away Items Using Carts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161894286213.html).

Depending on your account configuration, you can put away items from a staging bin location to a storage bin location or directly from the incoming truck to the storage bin location. For more information, see [Enabling Staging at Receiving](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541436089.html).
