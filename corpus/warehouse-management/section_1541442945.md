You may pick, pack, and ship items out of your warehouse for the following reasons

-   To send items customers have ordered - You ship sales order items to a customer to complete a sale.
    
-   To send items you want to transfer to other warehouse locations - You ship transfer order items to your company's various warehouse locations.
    

Fulfillment is based on items that you add to sales order or transfer order transactions. Depending on your business process, when you submit or approve an order, NetSuite fully or partially commits items to that order. Then, you create item fulfillment records when you start picking committed items for an order. On these transactions and records, you can track the sale, transfer, and shipment of items out of your warehouse. For more information about order fulfillment, see [Order Fulfillment Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4737787881.html).

Using NetSuite WMS, you can include the committed items in wave transactions and release them to the warehouse. Released orders in a wave can be processed by pickers to begin fulfillment through the app. After committed quantities of items are fully picked, item fulfillments are created automatically with details submitted by the app. Based on your fulfillment creation preference, NetSuite WMS generates item fulfillments per order, per order line, or per order in a wave. You can track picking and fulfillment generation on waves and pick task records. For partially picked orders that you want to ship, you must manually create the item fulfillment. For more information about fulfillment creation preferences, see [Setting Warehouse Management Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156406848826.html).

See the following topics about item fulfillments for released orders:

-   [Posting Item Fulfillments for Partially Picked Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541443017.html)
    
-   [Viewing the Status of Fulfillment Generation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541442979.html)
    
-   [Viewing and Handling Fulfillment Generation Failures](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0305030933.html)
    

NetSuite WMS recognizes and lets you process only transactions that meet the following conditions:

-   All orders must be approved, whether automatically or manually.
    
-   Orders that appear on the app have a warehouse location that matches the one you select on the app. You can specify the warehouse location on the app to process orders from that location.
    
-   Sales orders and transfer orders must have the Pending Fulfillment or Partially Fulfilled/Pending Billing status.
    

To view additional conditions and limitations for orders that you can release through wave transactions, see [Releasing Orders to the Warehouse](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1548078373.html).
