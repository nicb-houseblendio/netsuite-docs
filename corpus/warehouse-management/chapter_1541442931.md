Outbound processing involves releasing orders that you want to process for shipping out of a warehouse. Fulfillment processing of released orders include picking, packing, and shipping items. You can process orders for both warehouses that use bins and don't use bins.

pDepending on your shipping processes, the tasks you complete to fulfill orders may vary. NetSuite WMS provides capabilities to perform outbound processing tasks on the app and some within the NetSuite UI.

The following topics describe outbound processing through NetSuite WMS:

-   [Fulfilling Released Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541442945.html)
    
-   [Releasing Orders to the Warehouse](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1548078373.html)
    
-   [Picking Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541442950.html)
