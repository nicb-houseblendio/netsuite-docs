You can give access to any of the default NetSuite WMS custom roles or the Specialized User: WMS role to a custom mobile process. After you clone a standard process, you can assign one or more roles to your custom process. For more information, see [Customizing Mobile Processes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163614423814.html).

#### To assign custom roles to custom mobile processes:

1.  Using the Administrator role, go to Setup > Custom > Mobile - Applications.
    
2.  On the Mobile - Applications List page, click **View** next to the mobile app that uses the process.
    
3.  On the Mobile - Process List page, click **Edit** next to the custom mobile process.
    
4.  On the Mobile - Process page, in the **Accessible Roles** field, select the custom roles.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    
    After you assign roles to a process, compile your app updates.
    
6.  On the Mobile - Process List page, at the upper-right corner, click **Mobile Application List**.
    
7.  On the Mobile - Applications List page, click **Update App** next to the mobile app that you have updated.
