Item process groups are groupings of similar products that use a more refined classification than item process families. For example, you can create a pants item process group to more specifically identify items rather than using a clothing item process family. Like item process families, you can use item process groups to define pick and putaway strategies for locations that use bins.

#### To create an item process group: {#procedure_1541433821}

1.  Go to _Lists > Supply Chain > Item Process Group > New_.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter the name of the item process group you are creating.
    
3.  (Optional) In the **Description** field, add details.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

To associate item process groups with items, see [Creating Items for NetSuite WMS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541433512.html).
