After you pick and pack items, you can ship them using either of the following options:

-   [NetSuite WMS Shipping Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541443394.html) - Enables integration with carrier shipping software
    
-   [Manually Shipping Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541443432.html) - Provides the Quick Ship capability on the app
    
-   [Manually Shipping Orders in Pack Cartons or Pallets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163034719923.html) - Provides the Quick Ship feature to users of NetSuite Ship Central packing
    

These shipping options use the WMS Ship Manifest record to associate a tracking number with your orders' cartons and mark the orders as shipped in NetSuite. For more information, see the following topics:

-   [WMS Ship Manifest Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541443388.html)
    
-   [Confirming Customer Shipments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541443457.html)
