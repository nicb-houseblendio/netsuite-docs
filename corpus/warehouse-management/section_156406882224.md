Warehouse management strategies define where received items should be put away or where operators should go to pick items.

NetSuite WMS uses the sequence number of your strategies to determine which strategy should take priority over other applicable strategies. Based on the criteria defined in your warehouse management strategies, it suggests the appropriate bin locations for picking or putting away items. On the Entry Bin pages of mobile processes, bins appear in the Bins list based on your putaway or picking strategies.

You can define the following strategies for warehouse locations that use bins:

-   [Defining Pick Strategies](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1551132634.html)
    
-   [Defining Putaway Strategies](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4436271568.html)
