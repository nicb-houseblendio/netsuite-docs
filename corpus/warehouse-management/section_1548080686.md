Pick tickets provide details of items and pick tasks on wave transactions for processing. The picking type of the wave determines the grouping of items on a pick ticket. For information about picking types, see [Creating Wave Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1548078883.html) or [Picking Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541442950.html).

-   **Single Order Pick Ticket** - Pick ticket for each released order, containing all line items included in the wave.
    
-   **Bulk Picking** or **Multiple Order Pick Ticket** - Pick ticket for each pick task with similar items across the orders included in the wave.
    

For waves in Pending Release status, you can assign the wave form associated with a custom pick ticket that you create, before you release the wave. To create custom pick tickets or wave forms, see [Creating Custom Advanced Templates for Pick Tickets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1560456593.html) or [Creating Custom Wave Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1560457967.html).

When you release orders with multiple shipping routes, single order pick tickets group together the line items that have the same shipping route. Multiple order pick tickets display the shipping address, carrier, and method for each line item. For more information, see [Multiple Shipping Routes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1263041.html).

On each ticket, the pick tasks follow the ascending order of the recommended bins' sequence numbers if you assign them to your bins. Any non-inventory items, having no recommended bin, appear at the bottom of the list.

Note:

To view or print pick tickets, be sure to enable the required Advanced PDF/HTML Templates feature. Before printing pick tickets, make sure that your account doesn't use the **Print Using HTML** personal preference. Pick ticket documents are optimized for PDF, which you can view and print using Adobe Reader. For more information about these prerequisites, see [Enabling the Advanced PDF/HTML Templates Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1496243531.html) or [Personal Preferences for Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N480873.html).

#### To print pick tickets from a wave transaction:

1.  Go to _Transactions > Inventory > Release Orders to Warehouse > List_.
    
2.  From the list of waves, do one of the following: click **View** next to the wave you want to open.
    
    Tip:
    
    For waves in Pending Release status, to use your custom wave form and pick ticket, click **Edit** to open the wave. In the **Custom Form** field, select your wave form. Then, click **Save**.
    
3.  On the Wave page, click the print icon to open the pick ticket.
    
4.  On the toolbar of the pick ticket document, click the print icon.
    
    If you want to save a soft copy of the pick ticket in PDF, click the download icon.
