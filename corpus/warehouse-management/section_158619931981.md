Before you run the WMS Printer Driver and Carrier Integrator application, verify that you have completed the setup and configurations that suit your business requirements. See the following topics in this section:

-   [Activating System Rules for WMS Shipping Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158619718289.html#subsect_0827101020)
    
-   [Setting Up Your Login Credentials](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158619799329.html)
    
-   [Configuring the WMS Printer Driver and Carrier Integrator Application](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158619821210.html)
    
-   [Setting Up and Generating Commodity Shipment Files](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163482505291.html)
    

To start the application, on your computer's desktop, double-click the NetsuiteCarrierIntegrator.exe - Shortcut icon. On the NetSuite WMS Printer Driver and Integrator window, click **Start**. If you want to temporarily pause the application, click **Stop**.
