If you use carts, you can place multiple items for replenishment onto them before you put the items away for storage. During the putaway process, you have the option to transfer all the new items into the same storage bin.

To set up carts, see [Creating Bin Locations or Carts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161314312081.html#section_1541433492).

#### To replenish via carts:

1.  On mobile app, access the replenishment process by doing the following:
    
    1.  Tap the menu icon at the top-left corner of the screen.
        
    2.  Tap **Main Menu**.
        
    3.  On the Main Menu page, tap **Inventory**.
        
    4.  On the Inventory page, tap **Replenishment via Cart**.
        
2.  On the Enter Cart page, in the **Scan/Enter Cart** field, scan or enter the cart name or number. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Cart** to proceed.
    
    Tip:
    
    If you enter a cart that already contains replenishment items, you are redirected to the Cart Details page. See step 8.
    
    Alternatively, from the Carts list, you can tap an empty cart.
    
3.  On the Select Replenishment Tasks page, do either of the following:
    
    -   Tap the item associated with the replenishment task you want to process.
        
    -   If you are redirected to this page after processing one or more tasks, you can tap **Putaway** to proceed. See step 8.
        
        You can tap the Show Completed toggle to display and review the list of tasks you have processed. This list does not include replenishment items that you have already put away.
        
4.  On the Enter From Bin page, in the **Scan/Enter Bin** field, scan or enter the source bin location for the item you want to replenish. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Bin to proceed**.
    
    Alternatively, in the **Recommended Bin** field or from the bins list, you can tap the bin.
    
5.  On the Enter Item page, in the **Scan/Enter Bin** field, scan or enter the item you want to replenish. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Item** to proceed.
    
    Alternatively, in the **Item to Replenish** field, tap the item name or number.
    
6.  On the Enter Quantity page, do the following:
    
    1.  In the **Enter Quantity** field, enter the number of items you want to remove from the source bin. For lot items, you can enter the total quantity for each lot number.
        
        Tip:
        
        If you are redirected to this page during the putaway process, you can enter the number of items you to store in the destination bin.
        
        The **Quantity to Move** field displays the remaining replenishment quantity for the task.
        
    2.  (Optional) If the item uses multiple units, select the unit, other than the default base unit, for the quantity you have entered.
        
    3.  (Optional) If the item has multiple inventory statuses, in the **Inventory Status** field, select a status.
        
    4.  For lot items, in the **Scan/Enter Lot** field, scan or enter the lot number.
        
        Alternatively, from the list, tap the lot number.
        
    5.  Tap **Enter Quantity**.
        
        Based on the value in **Quantity to Move** field, if you have remaining quantity to replenish, you are redirected to the same page. You can repeat from step 6a. Otherwise, if you have no available items in the source bin, tap **No Quantity**. You can proceed to step 8.
        
        If you have no remaining quantity to replenish, you are redirected to the Select Replenishment Tasks page. Return to step 3.
        
7.  For serialized items, on the Enter Serial Number page, in the **Scan/Enter Serial Number** field, scan or enter the serial number. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Serial Number** to proceed.
    
    Tip:
    
    From the Available Serial Numbers list, you can copy a number and paste it into the field. In the **Serial Numbers Entered** field, you can view the quantity of remaining serial numbers that you must enter.
    
8.  To proceed to item putaway, on the Cart Details page, do any of the following:
    
    -   In the **Scan/Enter To Bin or Item** field, scan or enter the destination bin or the item. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Item/Bin** to proceed.
        
        Alternatively, from the Items on Cart list, you can tap the item or destination bin.
        
    -   If you want to add other items to the cart, tap **Add Item**. Return to step 3.
        
9.  Depending on your entry in the previous step, do either of the following procedures to enter the item or storage bin:
    
    -   On the Enter To Bin page, in the **Scan/Enter To Bin** field, scan or enter the storage bin. If you enter the data, tap **Enter Bin**.
        
        Alternatively, in the **Expected Bin** field, tap the storage bin.
        
    -   On the Enter Item page, in the **Scan/Enter Item** field, scan or enter the item from the cart. If you enter the data tap **Enter Item** to proceed.
        
        Alternatively, to put away all the items from the cart to the same bin, tap **Transfer All**. You can skip step 10.
        
10.  After you enter both the item and bin, depending on the item type, do one of the following:
     
     -   For regular inventory items, on the Enter Quantity page, you can enter the number of items to store in the bin. Return to step 6.
         
     -   For lot items, on the Enter Lot page, you can enter or select the lot number. Return to step 6 to enter the quantity.
         
         Alternatively, to put away all the lot numbers from the cart to the same bin, tap **Transfer All**.
         
     -   For serialized items, on the Enter Serial Number page, you can scan or enter each serial number. If you want to put away all the serial numbers from the cart to the same bin, tap **Transfer All**.
         
     
     After you put away the items for a replenishment task, the confirmation page appears. If you want to put away another item, tap **Next Transfer**.
