In many states, the marketplaces collect and remit tax on your behalf. So, the leading practice is to sync orders from the states where the tax is remitted as non-taxable. If you include the tax in NetSuite, you'd be overstating your revenue since you never get that tax money.

#### To automatically omit tax:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the connector and the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Settings > Orders.
    
    The Order Settings page opens.
    
4.  Click **Advanced Options**.
    
5.  Check the **Omit Remitted Tax When Posting Amazon Orders** box.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
