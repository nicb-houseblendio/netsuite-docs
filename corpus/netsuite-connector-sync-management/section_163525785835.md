NetSuite Connector's default way to sync prices during product sync is by pulling them from a designated price level in NetSuite. To set up a custom field on items for the price you want NetSuite Connector to sync, follow these steps:

#### To designate a custom field on items where the price for syncing can be entered:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com/).
    
2.  Select the relevant connector and account.
    
3.  Go to **Mappings > Products**.
    
4.  Click the button of the sync type you are mapping the price for.
    
    Note:
    
    If you're mapping the price for a marketplace like Amazon or Walmart, you'll see a dropdown instead of buttons. Pick the relevant marketplace category from the dropdown.
    
5.  Locate the price field you want to sync, and then click the **Special Mapping - Click to Edit** link of the field.
    
    Note:
    
    If you don't see the price field your storefront uses, click **Add Mapping**.
    
6.  On the Price Mapping page, check the **Draw Price From Custom Field Rather Than Price Level** box.
    
    Checking this box means you're using a custom field instead of a price level.
    
7.  In the **Custom Price Field**, enter the field ID of the custom field that NetSuite Connector should reference the price from.
    
8.  (Optional) Perform arithmetic operations on the mapped value before sending it to the storefront. For more information, see [Configuring Price Calculations Before Sync in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162942956765.html).
    
9.  Click **Save Changes**.
    
10.  (Optional) If the Reload Items is displayed, click **Yes, Reload My Items**. NetSuite Connector will reload your items and pull in the data from the custom field you mapped. The more items you've got in NetSuite Connector, the longer it'll take to reload them from NetSuite.
     
11.  Click **Save**.
