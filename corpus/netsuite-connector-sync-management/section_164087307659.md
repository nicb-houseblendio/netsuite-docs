NetSuite Connector lets you manually push fulfillments from NetSuite to the storefront if you want a fulfillment update for a specific order.

#### To push fulfillments manually from NetSuite in NetSuite Connector:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the connector and then select the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Data Flows > Orders.
    
4.  On the order you want to fulfill, hover over the **Action** menu (pencil icon) and click **Upload Shipment from NetSuite**.
