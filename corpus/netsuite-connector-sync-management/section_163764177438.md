NetSuite Connector supports writing to custom segment fields, but you need to make sure the right permissions are set. If your custom segment only has view permission, the segment field will be blank.

#### To set up writing to Custom Segment fields:

1.  Locate your custom segment in NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to the **Permissions** subtab.
    
3.  Make sure the **Default Record Access Level** and **Default Search/Reporting Access Level** are set to **Edit**.
