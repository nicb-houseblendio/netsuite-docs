If you enable line level taxes, the tax rate might not transfer correctly from the sales order to the invoice or cash sale when you bill the order. To fix this, check the **Match Line-Item Tax Rates Match with Tax Rates Created from Transaction (Beta)** box in NetSuite. You can check this box in the NetSuite Connector Setup page by going to Connector > Configuration > Setup.
