You can preview the data NetSuite Connector sends to the marketplace or cart for a product. There are two places where you can view this data:

1.  From the Products dashboard
    
2.  From the Products Mappings page
    

#### To view data mapping from the Products dashboard:

1.  Go to Data Flows > Products.
    
    The Products dashboard opens and displays the products imported by NetSuite Connector.
    
2.  Search for the product you want to view the data mapping for.
    
    For more information, read [Searching for a Product in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163997029949.html).
    
3.  After you find the product, click the **Action** menu on the right.
    
4.  Select **Show Product Mapping**.
    
    The product mapping test results appear.
    

#### To view data mapping from the Product Mappings page:

1.  Go to Mappings > Products.
    
2.  Click **Test Mappings**.
    
    The Test Product Mappings window opens.
    
3.  In the **Product Internal ID** field, enter the internal ID of product.
    
    Note:
    
    Do not enter the SKU.
    
4.  Click **Test Mappings**.
    
    The product mapping test results appear.
    

## Product Mapping Test Results {#bridgehead_162859953112}

The Test Product Mappings window displays the results of the mappings. In addition to the current mappings results for the test product, NetSuite Connector also shows some values that aren't mapped. These are for reference only. For example, the **FarAppInternalProductID** is both the NetSuite internal ID and the ID NetSuite Connector assigns to the product for internal processes.

![Product Mappings Result](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteConnector/ProductMappingOutput.png)

Note:

When you test mappings for a product that's been sent to the marketplace or cart, the mappings you see are current-not the ones from when the product was originally sent.
