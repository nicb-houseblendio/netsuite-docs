You can view all products imported in NetSuite Connector on the Products dashboard.

#### To search for a product in NetSuite Connector:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the connector and the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Data Flows > Products.
    
    The Products dashboard opens. By default, the **Show Children** filter shows both parent and child items.
    
4.  (Optional) To change the filter, point to **Filter** and pick the one you want from the list.
    
5.  In the **Search Products** field, enter the product SKU.
    
6.  Press **Enter**.
    
    The results are displayed in the list.
