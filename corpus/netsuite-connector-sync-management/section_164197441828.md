NetSuite lets you start batch updates on item records (and other records) based on your criteria. You can update any field on your item records, including flags, tags, descriptions, and more. For more information, read [Defining a Mass Update](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N667342.html)
