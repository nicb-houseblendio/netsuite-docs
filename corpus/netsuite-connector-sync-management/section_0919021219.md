By default, NetSuite Connector doesn't reimport orders from the storefront if the order is modified in the storefront. You can set up your storefront's connector to update synced orders when an order's modified.

#### To enable storefront order corrections: {#procedure_1012023132}

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the connector and the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Settings > Orders.
    
    The Order Settings page opens.
    
4.  Click **Advanced Options**.
    
5.  Check the **Allow Order Corrections** box.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
