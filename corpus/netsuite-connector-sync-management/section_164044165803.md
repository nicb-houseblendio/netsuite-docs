You can reload products in NetSuite Connector using one of these options:

-   [Updating Items in NetSuite](#bridgehead_164044209551)
    
-   [Updating Products in NetSuite Connector in Bulk](#bridgehead_164048882961)
    

## Updating Items in NetSuite {#bridgehead_164044209551}

When you make changes to items in NetSuite, NetSuite Connector automatically marks them for reload during the next product sync.

Note:

Make sure the item's flag field is set to **Add/Update Item** or **Y** if you're using a single character flag.

## Updating a Product in NetSuite Connector {#bridgehead_165124749719}

Here's how to manually reload a product from NetSuite Connector.

#### To update a product in NetSuite Connector:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the connector and then select the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Data Flows > Products.
    
4.  Find the product you want to update from the list.
    
5.  Hover over the **Action** icon for the product and from the list, select **Reload from NetSuite**.
    

## Updating Products in NetSuite Connector in Bulk {#bridgehead_164048882961}

Here's how to update multiple products in bulk from NetSuite Connector.

#### To update products in bulk in NetSuite Connector:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the connector and then select the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Data Flows > Products.
    
4.  (Optional) To change the number of products displayed on a page, select the desired number at the bottom of the page.
    
5.  Perform one of the following operations:
    
    -   To select all items on the page, check the left-side box in the column header.
        
    -   To manually pick items to reload, check the box next to each product you want.
        
6.  From the **Bulk Actions** dropdown list, select **Reload Selected Products from NetSuite**.
    
7.  (Optional) For more than 500 products, click **Next page** and repeat steps **5** and **6**.
