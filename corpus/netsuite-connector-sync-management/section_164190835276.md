Following are some important points for inventory and quantity fields:

-   Inventory syncs to the storefront-not from it. So, you shouldn't expect inventory to sync back to NetSuite, but items on orders from order sync will still affect your inventory.
    
-   Inventory syncs from NetSuite aren't instant. Most connectors support real-time price and quantity sync, but syncing can take a few minutes, depending on other running processes. If you don't use real-time sync, the sync runs every hour by default.
    
-   When syncing inventory from NetSuite locations (not a custom field), NetSuite Connector syncs the available quantity-not the quantity on hand. Quantity on hand covers everything in the warehouse, even what's committed to orders. Available quantity means the amount that's not committed-so that's what you can sell.
    

For further troubleshooting steps, see [Troubleshooting Product Sync Issues](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164361119079.html#subsect_165036292274).
