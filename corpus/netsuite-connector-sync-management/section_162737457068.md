If you need to post large number of orders, such as 500 orders per hour or more, consider upgrading to NetSuite SuiteCloud Plus.

For more information, read [http://www.netsuite.com/portal/assets/pdf/ds-datacenter-premium-tiers.pdf](http://www.netsuite.com/portal/assets/pdf/ds-datacenter-premium-tiers.pdf).

If you need to post more than 1000 orders per hour, contact NetSuite support.
