You can change the records and forms NetSuite Connector syncs to with a mapping, but by default, here's how it works:

-   NetSuite Connector syncs most orders as sales orders, except Point of Sale (POS), Amazon Fulfillment By Amazon (FBA), and Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) sync as cash sales.
    
-   NetSuite Connector doesn't specify which form to use for the record, so NetSuite uses the preferred form.
    

You can check which record or form NetSuite Connector is syncing to in a couple ways:

-   Checking the order mappings by going to Mappings > Orders in your connector account.
    
-   Viewing an order that is successfully synced and checking the record or form to which the order was synced. If a **customForm** field is being sent, then NetSuite Connector syncs to the form whose internal ID shows for that field. If **customForm** field is not sent, then NetSuite uses the preferred form for the record type being synced.
