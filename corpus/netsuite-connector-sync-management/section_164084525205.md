This error indicates NetSuite Connector tried to set the **ccApproved** field, but NetSuite is reporting that the write failed. There are two possible reasons for this failure:

-   **Payment instruments is enabled** - If you've enabled Payment Instruments in NetSuite, the fix is to clear the **ccApproved** box.
    
-   **Field not set to be visible on the form** - The **ccApproved** field isn't visible on the form NetSuite Connector uses for your orders. By default, this is the preferred form for sales orders or cash sales, but you can set NetSuite Connector to use a different form. To fix this error, make sure the **ccApproved** field is visible.
    

## Setting the ccApproved Field to be Visible {#bridgehead_164084633906}

Set the **ccApproved** field to visible on the transaction form you use for syncing.

#### To set the ccApproved field to visible:

1.  Go to Customization > Forms > Transaction Forms.
    
2.  Locate the form NetSuite Connector uses to post orders and on that form row, click **Edit**.
    
    The transaction form page opens.
    
3.  Click the **Sublists** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Sublists** subtab, click the **Billing** subtab.
    
5.  On the **Payment** row, check the **Show** box.
    
6.  Click the **Screen Fields** subtab.
    
7.  In the **Screen Fields** subtab, click the **Billing:Payment** subtab.
    
8.  On the **Credit Card Approved** row, check the **Show** box.
    
9.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

The **ccApproved** field is only for orders paid by credit card. Orders paid with something else, like cash, won't show this field in the payment information.
