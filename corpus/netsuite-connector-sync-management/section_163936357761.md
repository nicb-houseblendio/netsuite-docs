NetSuite Connector posts orders to your preferred form for each record type. For orders, that's usually your preferred sales order form. For Amazon FBA or Shopify POS orders, it might be a cash sale form. To change the form NetSuite Connector uses, either update the preferred form in NetSuite or create a mapping in NetSuite Connector.

For more information about changing the preferred form in NetSuite, read [Defining Preferred Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2873968.html).

## Creating a Form Mapping in NetSuite Connector {#subsect_163938624709}

This process changes the order form NetSuite Connector uses to post orders. You can also use it for the customer form by picking the **Customer** tab in step **4** below.

If you pick a form that doesn't match the sync type, like a cash sale form for sales orders, you'll get an error during sync.

#### To create a form mapping in NetSuite Connector:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the connector and the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Mappings > Orders.
    
4.  Click the **Order** tab.
    
5.  Click **Add Mapping**.
    
    The Add NetSuite Mapping popup window opens.
    
6.  From the **NetSuite Field** list, select **Custom Form**.
    
7.  Click **Add Mapping**.
    
    The mapping is added to the Other Transaction Item Mappings page.
    
8.  Click **Close**.
    
9.  In the new row, in the `<Marketplace or Cart Name>` **Field / Fixed Value** column, if this field in not a dropdown list, click **Reload NetSuite Lists**.
    
    After the reload process completes, the field changes to a dropdown list.
    
10.  Click the list in the **NetSuite Field/Fixed Value** column and select the form that you want to use.
     
11.  Click **Save**.
