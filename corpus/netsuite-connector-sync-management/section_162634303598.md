When NetSuite Connector posts an order to NetSuite, the user shown on the **System Notes** subtab is the one who generated the authentication tokens. This user also has the role for NetSuite Connector Web Services.

To change the user assigned to token authentication, follow step **2** of the procedure in the topic, [Setting Up Token-Based Authentication from NetSuite Connector to NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162606561992.html) and in step **2b**, change the user or employee. After you do this, any future order imports will show the new user in the **Set By** column on the sales order.
