The Pick, Pack, and Ship feature in NetSuite lets you create multiple item fulfillments for a single order. To use this in NetSuite Connector, turn on a setting by following these steps:

#### To enable the pick, pack, and ship feature in NetSuite Connector: {#procedure_164058566457}

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Go to NetSuite > Settings > General.
    
3.  On the NetSuite Settings page, check the **Pick/Pack/Ship Setting is Enabled in Your NetSuite** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
