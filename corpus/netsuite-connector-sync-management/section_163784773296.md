NetSuite Connector can't assign items to tax groups. Set up your taxes in NetSuite, including tax groups and tax codes. NetSuite builds the sales order based on your tax setup, and then NetSuite Connector automatically overwrites that rate with the one from your storefront.

For more information about tax codes, see [Tax Codes Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1805593.html).
