You can create a flag field in any of these ways:

1.  **Using the NetSuite Connector SuiteApp** - When you install the NetSuite Connector SuiteApp, it automatically creates the flag field and the associated list.
    
2.  **Creating a list-based flag field** - For more information, read [Creating a List-Based Storefront Flag Field Manually](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162980729832.html).
