When real-time sync is off, NetSuite Connector updates items based on their last modified timestamp, and the sync runs every 45 minutes.

#### To disable real-time sync from NetSuite:

1.  Go to Connector > Configuration > Setup.
    
2.  Check the **Disable Real-Time Sync** box.
    
3.  Click **Submit**.
    

You can also turn off real-time sync from the Product Sync tile on the Manage Data Syncs page in NetSuite Connector.
