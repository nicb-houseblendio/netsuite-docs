If an order fails to post several times, NetSuite Connector automatically changes its status to **Cancelled**. To get NetSuite Connector to try posting the order again, you'll need to undo the cancellation first. For info on undoing canceled orders, see [Reverting Canceled Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163698597549.html#bridgehead_163705279805).
