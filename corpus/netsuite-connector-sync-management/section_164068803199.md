If you use a repricer in your storefront to adjust prices, NetSuite Connector will override those prices if all the following are true:

-   You are using NetSuite Connector for product sync.
    
-   You have a mapping for the price field.
    

If you don't want NetSuite Connector to override your prices:

-   Don't map the price field. Without a price mapping, product sync won't update the item price and your repricer's price will stay in place.
    
-   If you use full product sync and create items in the storefront with it, the storefront needs a price to create items. In that case, set your price mapping to a custom field and make sure it's blank by default. After the item is created, clear out the custom field so the repricer can control the price.
    
    Note:
    
    If you manually delete the item from NetSuite Connector, it forgets it's already posted the item-so the next posting will include the price.
