If you add a new payment method to NetSuite, NetSuite Connector automatically makes the payment method available for mapping within 24 hours. To map the payment method right away, you should refresh the Payment Mappings page.

#### To refresh the payment methods in the Payment Mappings page:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the connector and the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Mappings > Orders.
    
    The Payment Methods page opens.
    
4.  Click the **Refresh** icon next to any mapping on the page.
    
    Wait for some time for the update to complete.
    
5.  Refresh the page in the browser.
    
    The dropdown should now show the updated list of payment methods from NetSuite.
