The order cutoff date is set by the **Import Orders Created After** setting in your NetSuite Connector account. This sets how far back NetSuite Connector checks for orders in your marketplace or cart when syncing.

#### To set the order cutoff date

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the appropriate connector and account.
    
3.  Go to Settings > Orders > General.
    
    The Order Settings page opens.
    
4.  Click **Advanced Options**.
    
5.  From the **Import Orders Created After** field, select the order cutoff date and time.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
