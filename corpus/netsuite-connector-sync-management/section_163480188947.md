To sync discounts at the order header level, the form NetSuite Connector sends to NetSuite needs to have these field IDs:

-   discountitem
    
-   discountrate
    

These fields usually show up in NetSuite as **Discount Item** and **Discount Rate**.

#### To display the discount item or rate field on the order form:

1.  Go to Customization > Forms > Transaction Forms.
    
2.  Click the **Edit** link of the relevant record and form.
    
    NetSuite Connector usually syncs to the sales order form.
    
3.  Go to the **Promotions** subtab and then click the **Items** subtab.
    
4.  If you don't see **Discount Item** and **Rate** under the **Items** subtab, look under the **Promotions** subtab. Check the **Show** boxes for **Discount Item** and **Rate** (or **Discount Rate**).
    
5.  Click **Save**.
