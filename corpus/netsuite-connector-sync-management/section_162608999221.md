This section discusses order cancellation process from NetSuite to marketplace.

## Order Cancellations from NetSuite to Marketplace or Cart {#bridgehead_162627362200}

When you cancel an order in NetSuite, NetSuite Connector can sync the cancellation back to the marketplace or cart and automatically close the order. This functionality works for the following connectors:

-   Magento2
    
-   Shopify
    
-   Walmart
    
-   WooCommerce
    

#### To enable order cancellations from NetSuite to marketplace or cart:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the appropriate connector and account.
    
3.  Go to Settings > Orders > General.
    
    The Order Settings page opens.
    
4.  Click **Advanced Options**.
    
5.  Check the **If an Order is canceled in NetSuite, Cancel It in \[Marketplace/Cart\]** box.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
