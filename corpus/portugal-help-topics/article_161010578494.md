## Overview {#bridgehead_161011039192}

The following help topics provide information about NetSuite features that are specific to companies doing business in Portugal. The NetSuite International Edition can be configured with capabilities and reports specific to Portugal business settings and requirements.

To know more about localized editions of NetSuite, as well as setting up NetSuite accounts and subsidiaries, refer to the following Help topics:

-   [NetSuite Editions Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N270055.html)
    
-   [Introduction to NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N266468.html) for companies using NetSuite across multiple subsidiaries and countries.
    
-   [Set up NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N268052.html)
    
-   [Creating Subsidiary Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N272471.html) in NetSuite OneWorld.
    

Please note that general NetSuite features are covered in other chapters and topics.

## General Setup {#bridgehead_161011083721}

[Portugal Account Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161011563345.html) - Provides an overview of NetSuite features that Portugal users can enable, and SuiteApps available for installation

[Setting Up Portugal-Specific Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161011601159.html) - Set your NetSuite company and user preferences including date, currency, language, time zone, and support for Portugal number, phone, and address formats.

[Setting Up Portugal Account for PT SAF-T Compliance](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161011605047.html) - Set the correct NetSuite preferences to ensure that the PT SAF-T contains information in the correct formats.
