You must enter the Commercial Registry Office and Commercial Registration Number. This information is required in generating the PT SAF-T file. The PT SAF-T file can't be generated successfully without this information, or if any of the information entered is invalid.

#### To enter the commercial registration information: {#procedure_0529035843}

1.  Go to Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types.
    
2.  Find the record **Compliance - Portugal Record** with ID, customrecord\_portugal, and click **List**.
    
3.  Click **Edit** on the row with **CommercialRegistryOffice** in the Name column.
    
4.  In the Value field, enter the two-letter country code for Portugal, **PT**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    
6.  Click **Edit** on the row with **CommercialRegistrationNumber** in the Name column.
    
7.  In the Value field, enter the registration number.
    
    Note:
    
    Enter only numeric characters in this field.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
