The Portugal Localization SuiteApp supports the following languages:

-   English (U.S.)
    
-   Português (Brazil)
    

For information about selecting your preferred language, see [Choosing a Language for Your NetSuite User Interface](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N479109.html).

Note:

If you set the localization preference to any language other than these supported languages, the pages will be displayed in English (U.S.).
