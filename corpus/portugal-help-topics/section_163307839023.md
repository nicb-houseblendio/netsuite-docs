The Portugal Localization SuiteApp provides an exception in validating the system-generated accounts associated with a Portugal subsidiary. For information about these accounts, see [Feature-Specific, System-Generated Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4078513386.html).

These accounts automatically generated by NetSuite aren't usually used in Portugal transactions, and are therefore not mapped under the **PT-SAFT Account Grouping** mapping category. This means that they haven't been assigned a grouping code: GM, GA, GR, AM, AA, and AR. Normally, unmapped accounts cause a failure in generating the PT SAF-T files, and the SuiteApp sends you a notification to fix the mapping. If a system-generated account is unmapped, has an initial balance of 0.00, and isn't used in any transactions, this account is excluded from the SAF-T files.

If, in some cases, a system-generated account has been assigned a grouping code, it's considered for the SAF-T validation and the SAF-T files, even if they haven't been used in any transaction. Such accounts are treated equally to any other Portugal account with a grouping code.
