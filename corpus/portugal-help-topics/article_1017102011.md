Portugal Localization SuiteApp provides you with updated and compliant templates containing Portugal-specific fields for printing your transaction receipts. You can print your Portugal PDF documents by clicking the **Print** button on your transaction record. It's possible to print one or more copies of your transaction receipts in a single PDF file, and share them with your customers, vendors, and shipping companies. The documents can either be printed in English or Portuguese, depending on the language settings of your NetSuite account.

Note:

If you have Portugal Localization SuiteApp in version 2.3.0 installed in your account, you can only print transactions of Portugal subsidiaries that have been finalized. For more information, see [Limitations of the Transaction Finalization Process](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0606050028.html#subsect_0606053500).

This feature supports the ability to print the following types of Portugal-specific transaction receipts:

-   Cash Sale
    
-   Cash Refund
    
-   Credit Memo
    
-   Delivery
    
-   Invoice
    
-   Payment Receipt
    
-   Sales Order
    

Note:

From the transaction types mentioned above, only **Invoice** and **Credit Memo** allow you to keep track of your printing history. For the other transaction types, this feature belonging to the Advanced Localization Features SuiteApp isn't supported.
