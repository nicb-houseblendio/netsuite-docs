The **System Notes** subtab on the Audits page tracks all changes made to a record. It captures the following important data:

-   Date of change
    
-   Who made the change
    
-   Field modified by the user, including upload and download of attachments and timestamps of the changes to attachments.
    
-   Role of the user who made the change
    
-   Type of change
    
-   Old and new record values
