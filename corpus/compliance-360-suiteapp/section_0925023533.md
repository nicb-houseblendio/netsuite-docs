The Compliance 360 Settings control the data displayed in the Key Performance Indicators portlet on the dashboard.

To go to the Compliance 360 Settings screen, go to Compliance 360 > Settings > View.

The following subtabs are shown in the Compliance 360 Settings screen:

-   [General](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1104084921.html)
    
-   [Email Notifications](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1104085228.html)
