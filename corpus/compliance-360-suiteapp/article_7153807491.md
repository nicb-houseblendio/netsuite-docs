The Compliance 360 SuiteApp gives you a single source of truth for user interactions with customer records.

The SuiteApp provides access to user activity logs for security and compliance monitoring.

Activity logs cannot be modified or deleted by any user, script, or application.

The Compliance 360 SuiteApp enables you to:

-   Track and audit user interactions to customer records within NetSuite
    
-   Create new audits, view and edit existing ones, and track audit progress based on your organization's needs and policies
    
-   Search for user interactions with customer records, including creations, deletions, edits, and more, from the last twelve months
    
-   Access real-time user activity, key performance indicators, and dashboards to monitor system usage
    

The Compliance 360 SuiteApp supports all available languages in the Multi-Language feature of NetSuite. Set your language preference in NetSuite by following the steps in [Choosing a Language for Your NetSuite User Interface](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N479109.html).

## Limitations of Compliance 360 SuiteApp {#subsect_0422101242}

Consider the following limitations for using the Compliance 360:

-   The Compliance 360 SuiteApp logs user activities related to customer records in NetSuite. Activities from other records are not tracked.
    
-   The Compliance 360 SuiteApp stores data for up to twelve months. To keep logs longer than twelve months, download and export them offline. For more information, see [Purging of Activity Logs in Compliance 360](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0422104030.html#subsect_0715035430).
    
-   The Compliance 360 SuiteApp does not log user activity interacting with the following:
    
    -   This includes interactions with the NetSuite Service File Cabinet;
        
    -   NetSuite Mobile Application;
        
    -   Integrations with outside sources;
        
    -   Third Party Applications; and
        
    -   Exported data from the NetSuite Service.
