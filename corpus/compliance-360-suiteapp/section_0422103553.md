The Compliance 360 Dashboard gives you a compliance overview, including what's happening with customer records, logins, and new users.

You can check the compliance metrics of your organization using the following portlets:

-   [Key Performance Indicators Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0925123601.html)
    
-   [Other Compliance 360 Dashboard Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0925033610.html)
    
    -   [Overall Total Activities](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0925033610.html#subsect_0925034055)
        
    -   [Total Activity Trend](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0925033610.html#subsect_0925035915)
        
    -   [Quick Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0925033610.html#subsect_0925040156)
        
    -   [Quick Links](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0925033610.html#subsect_0925040239)
        
    -   [Total NetSuite Logins](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0925033610.html#subsect_0925040334)
        
    -   [Audit Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0925033610.html#subsect_0925040826)
        
    -   [Total User Activity](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0925033610.html#subsect_0925041446)
        
    -   [Total Customer Activity](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0925033610.html#subsect_0925042444)
        
    -   [Total Customer Records Activity](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0925033610.html#subsect_0925042934)
        
    -   [Total Audit Trend](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0925033610.html#subsect_0512061248)
        

To view the Compliance 360 dashboard, go to Compliance 360 > Dashboard > View.
