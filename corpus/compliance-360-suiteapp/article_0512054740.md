You can delete control line item attachments, depending on your permissions.

#### To delete audit control attachments:

1.  Go to Compliance 360 > Audit Documentation > Audits.
    
2.  Edit an existing audit.
    
3.  In the Audit Controls section, locate the control line item where you want to delete attachments.
    
4.  In the **Attachments** column, click the edit attachments (pencil) icon.
    
5.  In the **Attachments** window, select the attachments that you want to delete and click **Delete**.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
