Created audits in the account are displayed in the Audits list page. The list page is filterable and exportable to a CSV file.

Tip:

If you created a new audit and have already applied filters to the Audits list page, you must click **Apply Filters** again to update the list and display your newly created audit.

The screenshot below shows the Audits page:

![A screenshot of the Audits List Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteApps/C360_AuditsListPage.png)

The following icons on the **Actions** column let you do the following to audits:

-   ![An icon on the Actions column to click for editing an audit.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteApps/C360_AuditTemplates_EditIcon.png) - Edit
    
-   ![An icon on the Actions column to click for viewing an audit.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteApps/C360_Audits_ViewIcon.png) - View
    
-   ![An icon on the Actions column to click for printing the existing audit or saving it as a PDF file.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteApps/C360_Audits_PrintIcon.png) - Print
