This section includes the prerequisites and instructions for installing the Compliance 360 SuiteApp.

## Prerequisites {#subsect_0422101307}

Important:

The Compliance 360 SuiteApp is a paid, managed SuiteApp that automatically updates with new features.

Contact your NetSuite account manager for purchasing the Compliance 360.

Before installing, ensure you have met the following requirements:

-   Purchase the Compliance 360 SuiteApp through your NetSuite account manager.
    
-   Verify that the Personal Information Access Log dataset is in your account.
    
    As an Administrator, go to Analytics > Datasets and search for the Personal Information Access Log dataset to confirm it's present.
    

The Compliance 360 SuiteApp is designed for use with NetSuite OneWorld. For more information about NetSuite OneWorld, see [Introduction to NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N266468.html).

## Installation {#subsect_0422101424}

After you've confirmed the dataset is present, you must install Compliance 360 SuiteApp from the SuiteApp Marketplace.

Note:

Before installing, ensure the SuiteApp is provisioned in your account. For more information, contact your account management organization.

For more information about the SuiteApp Marketplace, see [SuiteApp Marketplace in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1539797981.html).

#### To install the Compliance 360 SuiteApp:

1.  Go to the **SuiteApps** tab.
    
2.  In the **Search Apps** field, enter **Compliance 360**.
    
3.  Click the **Compliance 360** tile.
    
4.  On the top right area of the Compliance 360 SuiteApp details page, click **Install**.
    

For more information about installing a SuiteApp from the SuiteApp Marketplace, see [Installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539799323.html).

As a paid and managed SuiteApp, Compliance 360 automatically updates with new features and enhancements.
