The Audit Templates list page displays all the audit templates created in the account.

The following screenshot shows the Audit Templates list page:

![A screenshot that shows a portion of the Compliance 360 Audit Templates list page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteApps/C360_AuditTemplatesListPage.png)

The following icons on the **Actions** column let you do the following:

-   ![An icon on the Actions column to click for editing audit templates.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteApps/C360_AuditTemplates_EditIcon.png) - Edit
    
-   ![An icon on the Actions column to click for deleting audit templates.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteApps/C360_AuditTemplates_DeleteICon.png) - Delete
    
-   ![An icon on the Actions column to click for exporting an audit template as a CSV file.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteApps/C360_AuditTemplates_CSVICon.png) - Export into a CSV file
