The following questions and answers could be helpful if you have a question. If you need further help or want to report an issue, please contact your account administrator, or support team.

### What should I expect from the Account Warmer SuiteApp?

The Account Warmer SuiteApp resolves issues of slow first load interactions in your account. You can now expect first interactions for the most-used record types after a warm-up to be similar to subsequent loads.

Note:

Some record types that don't support preloading (for example, custom records) may be skipped, but this is normal. They will be tried again on future runs.

### What doesn't the Account Warmer SuiteApp do?

The SuiteApp does not speed up the UI rendering itself. It also does not improve the time it takes to save records.

### Why can't I see the Account Warmer Portlet under Personalize > SuiteApps?

It's possible you have a previous version of the SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite Account. You'll need to remove the existing portlet to refresh the list and then you can try adding it again.

### Do I need to add the Portlet for warming to work?

No. Warming runs automatically. The Portlet is only for visibility and manual refreshes.

### Does the Account Warmer SuiteApp modify my data?

No. It does not save records.

### Can I choose which record types are warmed?

No, not currently. The SuiteApp automatically targets your most-used record types.
