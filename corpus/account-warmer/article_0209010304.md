-   Improves first load performance when accessing rarely used non-production accounts
    
-   Specifically addresses slow initial page loading, without changing overall NetSuite performance
    
-   Designed for fast deployment and configuration, allowing teams to seamlessly adapt it to their operational needs
    

Note:

The Account Warmer SuiteApp does not perform write operations, it does not speed up UI rendering, and it does not improve the time it takes to save records.
