The Account Warmer SuiteApp can be installed from the SuiteApp Marketplace. For details on installing SuiteApps, see [SuiteApp Installation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1540236668.html).

After this SuiteApp is installed, log in to your NetSuite account. No further setup is required. The SuiteApp runs automatically in the background. The Account Warmer SuiteApp:

-   Runs seamlessly in the background; no setup or manual configuration is required.
    
-   Operates without saving any records, ensuring data integrity and privacy.
    
-   Automatically targets your most-used record types, providing a smooth experience for accounts that are not accessed frequently.
