The Account Warmer SuiteApp addresses a common issue in NetSuite's non-production accounts, such as Release Preview, Demo, Sandbox, and Next Preview accounts. Infrequently used accounts can experience delays during the first page load, leading to a slower initial experience for users.

To mitigate this, the Account Warmer SuiteApp automatically identifies and preloads frequently used records in these non-production accounts. Note: Core records are included in this process, but custom records are not yet supported. By pre-populating this information, the SuiteApp significantly reduces first load wait times and ensures that the first access to infrequently used accounts is smoother.
