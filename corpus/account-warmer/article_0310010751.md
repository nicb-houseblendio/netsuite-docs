You can create a shortcut on your Home page using the link `[https://<accountid>.app.netsuite.com/app/site/hosting/scriptlet.nl?script=customscript_sw_results_sl&deploy=customdeploy_sw_results_sl](https://\<accountid\>.app.netsuite.com/app/site/hosting/scriptlet.nl?script=customscript_sw_results_sl&deploy=customdeploy_sw_results_sl)`.

Accountid is the number specific to your NetSuite account that can be found in the URL at the top of your browser. For example: `[https://123456.app.netsuite.com/app/site/hosting/scriptlet.nl?script=customscript_sw_results_sl&deploy=customdeploy_sw_results_sl](https://123456.app.netsuite.com/app/site/hosting/scriptlet.nl?script=customscript_sw_results_sl&deploy=customdeploy_sw_results_sl)`.

To create a shortcut on your Home page, complete the following steps:

#### Create a Shortcut on Your Home Page

1.  From the Shortcuts portlet on your Home page, click the three dots beside the title.
    
2.  Create a name for your shortcut, and add the link mentioned above. For example:
    
    https://123456.app.netsuite.com/app/site/hosting/scriptlet.nl?script=customscript\_sw\_results\_sl&deploy=customdeploy\_sw\_results\_sl
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    
4.  From the Shortcuts portlet, click the shortcut.
