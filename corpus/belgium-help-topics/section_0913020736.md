The BE\_TI and BE\_TA tax codes with 100% tax rate are automatically installed by the Belgium Localization SuiteApp. If these tax codes with the same tax rate already exist in your NetSuite account, you can choose to either keep your manually created tax codes, or delete your tax codes and manually install the SuiteApp-provided tax codes.

#### To manually install the VAT 625 codes with 100% tax rate: {#procedure_0913020826}

1.  Go to Setup > Belgium Localization > Setup.
    
2.  Go to the **Tax** subtab.
    
3.  Click **Install Tax Codes**.
