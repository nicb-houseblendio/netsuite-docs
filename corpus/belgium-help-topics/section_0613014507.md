As administrator, you can grant other roles the access to the **Installation of Belgium Chart of Accounts** page.

#### To grant access to the Belgium Chart of Accounts installation page: {#procedure_0613014723}

1.  Go to _Customization > Scripting > Single Page Applications_.
    
2.  Click **Installation of Belgium Chart of Accounts**.
    
3.  On the left pane, click **Configuration**.
    
4.  On the Release Audience field, click **Set Up Audience...**.
    
5.  On the Audience Setup window, check the box next to the role that you want to have access to the Belgium Chart of Accounts installation page.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

Ensure that the role you've granted access to the **Installation of Belgium Chart of Accounts** page has at least create-level permission for Accounts.

#### To set Accounts permissions for a role: {#procedure_0613015458}

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > User Management > Manage Roles_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the role to whom you want to grant the permission for Accounts.
    
3.  On the Permissions subtab, go to the Lists subtab.
    
4.  In the Permission column, enter **Accounts**.
    
5.  In the Level column, select **Create**, **Edit**, or **Full**.
    
6.  Click **Add**.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
