## Overview {#bridgehead_156587325429}

The following Help topics provide information about NetSuite features that are specific to companies doing business in Belgium. The NetSuite International Edition can be configured with capabilities and reports specific to Belgium business settings and requirements.

To know more about localized editions of NetSuite, as well as setting up NetSuite accounts and subsidiaries, refer to the following Help topics:

-   [NetSuite Editions Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N270055.html)
    
-   [Introduction to NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N266468.html) for companies using NetSuite across multiple subsidiaries and countries.
    
-   [Set up NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N268052.html)
    
-   [Creating Subsidiary Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N272471.html) in NetSuite OneWorld.
    

General NetSuite features are covered in other chapters and topics.

## General Setup {#bridgehead_156587326534}

[Belgium Account Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156587331080.html) - Provides an overview of NetSuite features to be enabled by the users in Belgium and SuiteApps available for installation.

[Setting Up Belgium-Specific Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156587333461.html) - Set up your NetSuite company and user preferences for Belgium, including date, currency, language, time zone, and support for Belgium number, phone, and address format.
