Setting up a NetSuite account for a company doing business in Belgium requires enabling several features and installing additional SuiteApps.

## Enabling Features for Belgium Account {#bridgehead_156587332250}

Some features available in your NetSuite account can be useful for users in Belgium. If they're inactive, you should consider enabling them. If you want to get features of a specific product area, see [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_156024393244.html#bridgehead_160189357626) and check the exact requirements.

Note:

If you don't have sufficient permissions to enable features, please contact your account administrator.

#### To enable features:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features (Administrator)_.
    
2.  On the **Company** subtab, under International, check the **Multi-language** and **Multiple Currencies** boxes.
    
3.  On the **Accounting** subtab, under Basic Features, check the **GL Audit Numbering** box.
    
4.  (Optional) On the **Accounting** subtab, under Advanced Features, check the **Period End Journal Entries** and **Foreign Currency Variance Mapping** boxes.
    
5.  On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, enable the following features:
    
    -   SuiteBuilder
        
    -   SuiteScript
        
    -   SuiteGL
        
    -   SuiteFlow
        
    
    For more information about SuiteCloud features, see [SuiteCloud Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N239332.html).
    
6.  If your company is registered for EU One Stop Shop, on the **Tax** subtab, check the **EU One Stop Shop** box.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

## Available SuiteApps {#bridgehead_158461996971}

Some additional features require installation of a specific SuiteApp. SuiteApps are applications that extend NetSuite for specific industry and business needs. For information about the required SuiteApps for specific product areas, see [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_156024393244.html#bridgehead_160189357626).

Belgium-specific features are currently available in the following SuiteApps:

| [Electronic Bank Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1585433.html) | Payments | Enables bank payments of vendor bills, employee expenses, partner and employee commissions, customer refunds, and customer payments within Belgium. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html) | Taxation (NetSuite Accounts Without SuiteTax) | Generates the French VAT reports in English or French, sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports (DEB). If the SuiteTax feature is enabled in your NetSuite account, don't install the International Tax Reports SuiteApp. |
| Supplementary Tax Calculation | Taxation (NetSuite Accounts Without SuiteTax) | Manages EU Reverse charges and supports the calculation of nondeductible input tax. For more information, see [Recording Nondeductible Input Tax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1822590.html) and [Setting Up Tax Codes to Post Notional Tax Amounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4702880164.html). This SuiteApp also supports VAT on prompt payment discounts. For more information, see [Recording Tax on Prompt Payment Discount](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4702990460.html). If your account has the SuiteTax feature enabled, don't install this SuiteApp. |
| [SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1557396965.html) | Taxation (NetSuite Accounts With SuiteTax) | For NetSuite accounts with the SuiteTax feature enabled, the SuiteTax Engine sets default tax codes based on local tax requirements. |
| [Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1528262949.html) | Taxation (NetSuite Accounts With or Without SuiteTax) | The Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp, when installed with the Belgium Localization SuiteApp, enables you to generate the tax reports required in Belgium. |
| [EMEA Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_157122959910.html) | Payments Taxation (NetSuite Accounts With SuiteTax) | Provides custom lists and custom fields that will be used to generate tax reports. and electronic bank payments for countries in the EMEA region. The EMEA Localization SuiteApp is a prerequisite of the Belgium Localization SuiteApp. Note: You can use the EMEA Localization SuiteApp without enabling the SuiteTax feature. But, in this case, you can't use the tax-related features in this SuiteApp. |
| [Belgium Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157501756115.html) | Accounting Taxation (NetSuite Accounts With or Without SuiteTax) Payments | Provides the following features:
-   Belgium Chart of Accounts
-   SuiteTax report templates of Periodic VAT Return 625, Annual Client Listing 725, Intrastat, and EU Sales List 723.
-   Fiche 281.50 tax audit file. This report can be generated with or without SuiteTax.
-   Electronic bank payment templates in the following formats: CIRI-FBF, and SEPA Credit Transfer (Febelfin).

Note: The Belgium Localization SuiteApp can also be used with the International Tax Reports SuiteApp if you aren't using SuiteTax. But, without SuiteTax, you can't use the tax-related features of this SuiteApp. |
| [GL Matching](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1516099228.html) | Accounting | Enables account reconciliation for balance sheet accounts. Account types supported by the GL Matching SuiteApp aren't limited to Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable types. |
| [One Stop Shop](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162918935998.html) | Taxation (NetSuite Accounts With or Without SuiteTax) | Enables the EU VAT One Stop Shop Reports (OSS) generation. OSS is categorized into three specific schemes: Non-Union OSS Scheme, Union OSS Scheme, and Import OSS Scheme. These schemes allow taxable persons to declare and pay VAT due in Member States where the taxable persons aren't established. |
| [European Union Electronic Invoicing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7122551804.html) | Accounting | Allows the processing of [Belgium PEPPOL Inbound and Outbound Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1027041828.html). |

#### To install a SuiteApp:

1.  Go to _Customization > SuiteBundler > Search & Install Bundles_.
    
2.  Search for the SuiteApps that you would like to install using their names or their Bundle IDs.
    
3.  On the Bundle Details page, click **Install**.
    

If you don't have sufficient permissions to enable features, please contact your account administrator.

For information about SuiteApp installation, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).

For more information, see [SuiteApps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_3134602194.html).
