To use the Intrastat Reporting feature, the International Tax Reports SuiteApp must be installed in your NetSuite account. For more information, see [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html).

The International Tax Reports SuiteApp lets you export Intrastat Reports in a format acceptable for upload to the tax agency website.

#### To Export the Belgium Intrastat Report: {#procedure_1553145896}

1.  Generate the Belgium Intrastat Report. See [To Generate the Belgium Intrastat Report:](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1553079891.html#procedure_1553145901).
    
2.  Click the **Export to CSV** or **Export to XML** button to generate CSV or XML files of the report.
    
    Note:
    
    When asked to open or save a CSV file, click **Save File**. Opening the file in Microsoft Excel causes the format to change.
    
    As a best practice, save the file first. Then, use a text editor (for example, Notepad) to open the file.
    

For more details about generating and exporting EU Intrastat Reports, see [Electronic Filing of Intrastat Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2071452.html#bridgehead_4180161062).
