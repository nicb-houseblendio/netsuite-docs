If you received a notification that informed you about field entries that are too long, refer to these help topics for the maximum number of characters allowed in the Fiche 281.50 report:

-   [Fiche Character Limitations in Subsidiary Information](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0417021037.html)
    
-   [Fiche Character Limitations in Employee Information](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0417022239.html)
    
-   [Fiche Character Limitations in Vendor Information](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0417022617.html)
    

Note:

You can still generate the report even though the character limitations were exceeded.
