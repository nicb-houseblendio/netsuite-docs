The **Sales (negate)** filter is applied to specific boxes in the VAT 625 report. With this filter, negative values will be reported as positive in your VAT 625 report. For example, because this filter is applied in Box 48 of the report, the negative VAT due on credit memos will appear in the report as a positive value.

For information about where this filter is applied in the report by default, see [Periodic VAT Return 625](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156775521116.html).
