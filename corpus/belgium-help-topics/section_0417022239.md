The following information from the employee record of the person who generated the report are included in the Fiche 281.50 report and are subject to the Fiche report's character limitations:

Important:

Spaces count as characters. For example, **(123) 456 789 101** has 17 characters.

| Field Name | Maximum Number of Characters | Used in this Tag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Office Phone** (officephone) | 12 | v0018\_telefoonnummer |
| **Name** (firstname) | 34 | v0021\_contactpersoon |
| **Email** (email) | 44 | v0023\_emailadres |

To edit the employee record of the person who generated the report, go to Lists > Employees > Employees. Then, click **Edit** next to the name of the employee record that you want to edit.
