Note:

This topic is for NetSuite accounts that use the Country Tax Reports page to generate the Annual Client Listing report for Belgium. If you're using the Tax Reports (International) page, see [Belgium Annual Client Listing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1843546.html#bridgehead_4259645118).

With Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp, you can generate the Belgium supplementary VAT report, Annual Client Listing Form 725. To generate a report, see [Generating Localized Country Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528250223.html). You can also export this report to PDF and XML. For more information, see [Exporting a Country Tax Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156645000208.html).

The following table describe the content and data source of the generated Belgium Annual Client Listing Form.

| Fields | Description | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VAT No. | Declarant's VAT registration number | Tax registration number from the Company or Subsidiary Information |
| Period | Year of the reporting period | Reporting tax period |
| VAT Number | Customer VAT number | Customer Tax Registration Number from the sales transaction |
| Sales (Exclusive of VAT) | Total net sales of the customers for the reporting period | Total net sales of customers |
| VAT Amount | Total VAT on sales | Total VAT on sales |

To export the Annual Client Listing, see [Exporting a Country Tax Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156645000208.html).

For more information about the reporting features of Tax Reporting Framework, see [Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1528262949.html).
