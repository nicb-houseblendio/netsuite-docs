The Belgium Chart of Accounts page and the Belgium SuiteTax-based reports can be displayed in the following languages:

-   English (U.S.)
    
-   Français (France)
    
-   Nederlands for Dutch
    

Deutsch is also available for any of the Belgium SuiteTax-based reports.

For information about displaying the Belgium Localization SuiteApp pages to your preferred language, see [Setting Language Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543591052.html).

Note:

If you set the localization preference to any language other than these supported languages, the pages will be displayed in English (U.S.).

To display a SuiteTax-based report (generated using the Country Tax Report page) in your preferred language, after clicking **View** next to the generated tax report, select a language from the Language list.
