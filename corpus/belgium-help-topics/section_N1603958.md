Set up the bank account details of each customer to whom you'll send customer refunds. You can set up several bank accounts for each customer.

#### To set up bank details of a customer for refund transactions: {#procedure_N1603970}

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Customers_.
    
    For information about creating a customer record, see [Customers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1076428.html).
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the name of the customer whom you want to send customer refunds through electronic bank payments.
    
3.  On the **Bank Payment Details (Credit)** subtab, check the **EFT Customer Refund Payment** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    
5.  Click **New Bank Details**.
    
6.  In the Payment File Format field, select your preferred payment format.
    
7.  Fill-in the fields depending on the payment format you selected.
    
    For more information, see [Belgium Entity Bank Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160699018095.html).
    
8.  Click **Save**.
    

You can also set up bank records of customers by importing bank details in CSV format into NetSuite using the Import Assistant. For more information, see [Importing Electronic Bank Payments Data](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3739634016.html).
