If you have a Belgium subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the Belgium VAT Return in English, Dutch, or French. The report can be used as a source for filling in the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. For online filing, you can use NetSuite to generate an XML file of your Belgium VAT Return. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.
