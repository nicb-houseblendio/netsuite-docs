The Belgium Localization SuiteApp provides payment templates in the following formats that you can use with the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp to pay vendor bills, employee expenses, customer refunds, and partner commissions:

-   CIRI-FBF specifications of the Belgian Bankers' and Stockbroking Firms' Association
    
-   SEPA Credit Transfer specifications of the Belgian Financial Sector Federation (Febelfin)
    

Note:

The SEPA Credit Transfer format with specifications of ABN AMRO and HSBC, and SEPA Direct Debit (ABN AMRO) are provided by the EMEA Localization SuiteApp. For more information, see [EMEA Localization Payment Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158858392029.html).

If your NetSuite account already has the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp, which provided Belgium payment templates, installing the Belgium Localization SuiteApp will update those templates to the formats compliant to Belgium standards. Without the Belgium Localization SuiteApp, the payment templates installed in your NetSuite account remain outdated.

## Electronic Bank Payments Setup for Belgium {#subsect_159186314935}

The Belgium Localization and EMEA Localization SuiteApps only provides the payment templates. The Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp handles the processing of the payments. To use these templates for bank payments, you must have the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp installed. For more information, see [Electronic Bank Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1585433.html).

You must also set up the relevant bank details and other information that the electronic bank payments system requires. For more information, read the following topics:

-   [Setting Up Company Bank Records in Belgium](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1598529.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Vendors in Belgium](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1603275.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Employees in Belgium](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1603620.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Customers in Belgium](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1603958.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Partners in Belgium](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3851193210.html)
    

For more information about setting up and customizing the payment templates, see [Payment File Formats](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1675118.html).
