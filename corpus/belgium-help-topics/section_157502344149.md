The Belgium Localization SuiteApp provides features for accounting, taxation, and handling payments specific for businesses in Belgium.

## Belgium Chart of Accounts Template {#subsect_161123425351}

For Belgium whose Chart of Accounts is statutory, the SuiteApp provides a template that you can download, adjust, and import. For more information, see [Belgium Chart of Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161123471177.html).

## SuiteTax-Based Reports in the Belgium Localization SuiteApp {#subsect_159179044630}

With Belgium Localization installed, you can generate the following reports in English, Dutch (Nederlands), French (Francais), or Deutsch by using the Country Tax Reports page provided by the Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp:

-   [Periodic VAT Return 625](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156775521116.html)
    
-   [Annual Client Listing 725](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156775647909.html)
    
-   [Belgium Intrastat](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157538424588.html)
    
-   [EU Sales List 723](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159706676046.html)
    

These reports can only be used for SuiteTax-enabled NetSuite accounts. For more information, see [Belgium SuiteTax-Based Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157538136062.html).

## Payment Formats in the Belgium Localization SuiteApp {#subsect_159179059743}

You can pay vendor bills, employee expenses, customer refunds, and partner commissions using the bank payment templates that the Belgium Localization SuiteApp provides in the following formats:

-   SEPA Credit Transfer (Febelfin)
    
-   CIRI-FBF specifications
    

The Belgium Localization SuiteApp only provides the payment templates. The bank processing capabilities are handled by the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp. Setting up bank records for your company, subsidiaries, vendors, employees, customers, and partners in Belgium is necessary. For more information, see [Belgium Payment Formats](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156742828189.html).
