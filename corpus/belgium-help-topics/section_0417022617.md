The following information from the vendor records are included in the Fiche 281.50 report and are subject to the Fiche report's character limitations:

Important:

Spaces count as characters. For example, **(123) 456 789 101** has 17 characters.

| Field Name | Maximum Number of Characters | Used in this Tag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Company Name** (companyname), if the vendor type is Company Last name entry of field **Name**, for vendors of type Individual | 41 | f2013\_naam |
| First name entry of field **Name**, for vendors of type Individual | 15 | f2114\_voornamen |
| **Address 1** (addr1) | 32 | f2015\_adres |
| **Zip** (zip) | 4 | f2016\_postcodebelgisch |
| **City** (city) | 27 | f2017\_gemeente |
| **BCE/KBO/ZDU Number**, for vendors of type Company **National Number**, for vendors of type Individual | 11 | f2011\_nationaalnr |
| **City of Birth** | 27 | f2105\_birthplace |

The vendor information in Fiche 281.50 is obtained from the vendor records where the **Include in Fiche Report** box is checked and whose transactions (with expenses and items that are covered in Fiche 281.50 declaration) equal to or exceed 250 Euros. For more information, see [Including Vendors in the Belgium Fiche Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0417023613.html).

To view a vendor record, go to Lists > Relationships > Vendors.

To edit the **City of Birth**, **National Number**, and **BCE/KBO/ZDU Number** fields, go to the Fiche 281.50 subtab on the vendor record.
