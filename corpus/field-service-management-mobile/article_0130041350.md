The **Quote tab** lets you create a quote and send an email to the person requesting the quote.

Also, if the quote is acceptable to the client, they can sign right on the mobile device and the **Field Service Mobile** will create a sales order from the quote.

This tab has three screens: **Add/Edit/Delete/View Quote Item screen**, **Quote Details screen** and **Quote List screen**.
