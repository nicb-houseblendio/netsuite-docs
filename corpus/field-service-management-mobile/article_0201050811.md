The **Transfer Order tab** lets you order parts from another warehouse and have them delivered to your local (van) warehouse location.

This tab has three screens: the **Transfer Order Details screen**, the **Add/Edit/Delete/View Transfer Order Item screen** and the **Transfer Order List screen**.

![NextService Mobile transfer order tab.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/MobileUserGuide/mobile_transfer_order_tab_icon.png)

For more information, see:

-   [Transfer Order Details Screen in Detail](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_69171500641.html)
    
-   [Add/Edit/Delete/View Transfer Order Item Screen in Detail](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_97172338980.html)
