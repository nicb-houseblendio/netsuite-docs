The **Expenses tab** lets you record expenses for the selected task.

This tab has two screens: **Expense Item List screen**, and the **Add/Edit/Delete/View Expense Item screen**.

After you save data to the cloud, you can still edit and the updated data may be saved over existing data.

![NextService Mobile expenses tab.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/MobileUserGuide/mobile_expenses_tab_icon.png)

For more information, see:

-   [Expense Item List Screen in Detail](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1134345485.html)
    
-   [Add/Edit/Delete/View Expense Item Screen in Detail](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_46135246112.html)
