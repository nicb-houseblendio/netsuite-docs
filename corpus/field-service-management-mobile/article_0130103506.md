The **Details tab** lets you make notes about a task, add your signature, and capture the client's name, email, and signature to record completion of a task.

This tab has one screen known as the **Details screen**.

Even after you save data to the cloud, you can still edit and save updated data over the existing data.

![NextService Mobile details tab.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/MobileUserGuide/mobile_details_tab_icon.png)

For more information, see [Details Screen in Detail](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_52103815722.html).
