The **Contact tab** lets you:

-   View a list of all contacts for a task.
    
-   Select one of those contacts to see more details.
    
-   Launch the mobile device's default phone dialer app to automatically call a mobile or landline phone number.
    
-   Launch the mobile device's default email app to automatically add the customer's email details to a new email message.
    

![NextService Mobile contacts tab.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/MobileUserGuide/mobile_contact_tab_icon.png)
