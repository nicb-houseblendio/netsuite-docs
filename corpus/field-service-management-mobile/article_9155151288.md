Field Service Management (FSM) Mobile is a mobile application that technicians use to manage and record work for their assigned field service jobs. With FSM Mobile, technicians have access to job details and capture work as they go, which helps them stay in sync with office teams and other field staff.

Note:

What technicians see in FSM Mobile can vary by customer and by job type. FSM administrators set up tabs, fields, and requirements that appear in the mobile app.
