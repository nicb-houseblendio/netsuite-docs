This screen lets you record information for a selected task. By default, you must enter information in the **Details tab**. You can't save information in the Details screen until there is data in the **Technician Signature drawing box**. This means that you should sign on this screen and save the data before setting the **Status** of the task to **Complete**.

Optionally, you can also record actions taken, close the entire case of tasks, if necessary, and then add the customer representative's name, email address, and signature.
