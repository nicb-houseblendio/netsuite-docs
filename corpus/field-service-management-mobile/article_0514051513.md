Before you enable this feature, you must have an Oracle OCI account.

#### To enable Enhanced Technician Notes globally:

1.  Go to Field Service > Administration > Configuration.
    
2.  In the **Editor** text area, update the global setting to enable Enhanced Technician Notes for all supported text areas.
    
                    `{   "form": {     "cleanupprompt": "-27",     "textenhance": true   } }` 
                  
    
3.  Click **Save**.
