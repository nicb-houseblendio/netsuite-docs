Complete all procedures in this section to set up DKIM. The following procedures contain instructions for setting up email domains in your NetSuite production account and for creating the necessary DNS records at your domain provider.

-   Only users with an Administrator role or with the Full level of the Set Up Company permission can perform the NetSuite procedure.
    
    Note:
    
    You need the Full level of the Set Up Company permission to set up email domains in NetSuite. For more information, see [NetSuite Permissions Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N325094.html).
    
-   Have someone with DNS experience set up the DNS records at your domain provider.
    

Follow these steps to set up email domains:

-   [Enter Email Domains in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_163604950862.html) on the Email Preferences page.
    
-   [Set Up CNAME Records in DNS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_163604971524.html) with your domain provider.
