As of 2026.1, the NetSuite.com data source was removed. For more information, see [Removal of SuiteAnalytics Connect NetSuite.com Data Source](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0405125116.html). To see how NetSuite.com fields map to NetSuite2.com fields, search for **message** in the NetSuite.com Record ID column in the spreadsheet available in the [NetSuite.com to NetSuite2.com Map](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0822031101.html) help topic.

To query all messages through the analytics data source, you must have the Administrator role, or the Messages for Analytics and REST permission. Without the Administrator role or the Messages for Analytics and REST permission, the query result is limited to messages you have authored or received.

#### To add the Messages for Analytics and REST permission to your roles

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_.
    
2.  Choose the role you want to customize and click **Edit**.
    
3.  Select the **Permissions** subtab.
    
4.  On the **Lists** subtab, choose the **Messages for Analytics and REST** permission.
    
5.  In the **Level** column, set the access level to **View**.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

For more information about permissions, see [Setting Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N288727.html).
