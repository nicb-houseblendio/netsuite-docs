You may see the following messages in the Details column on the Email Domains (DKIM) subtab.

For more information about the statuses that can be displayed, see [Email Domains (DKIM) Status Column](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0124093036.html).

Note:

No messages are displayed in the **Details** column when the **Status** is **Pending Verification**.

| Status | Details Message | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requires Action | **Please ensure that DNS records are correctly configured.** | Indicates that no valid DNS records can be found for this domain. You should add CNAME records to DNS at your domain provider's website. |
| Ready | **This email domain is currently configured with a TXT record in DNS. Do not delete the TXT record, but please create additional CNAME records for this domain with your domain provider.** | Alerts you that you should add CNAME records to DNS at your domain provider's website. Note: You'll also see a Warning banner on the Email Preferences page. |
