Important:

If you don't set up DNS CNAME records after completing the [Enter Email Domains in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_163604950862.html) procedure, the FROM headers of email sent from that domain will continue to be rewritten. For more information, see [FROM Headers in Email Can Be Rewritten](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1536091586.html).

Someone with DNS experience should complete the following procedure.

#### To set up two DNS CNAME records with your domain provider: {#procedure_1544617833}

1.  Open a new browser window, and log in to your domain provider.
    
    Note:
    
    Check your domain provider's documentation for adding a DNS record.
    
2.  Add a DNS record of the type CNAME.
    
3.  Copy the DNS record information from NetSuite and paste it in the record you are adding to DNS.
    
    To view the information generated in NetSuite for the DNS record:
    
    1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Email > Email Preferences_.
        
    2.  Click the **Email Domain (DKIM)** subtab.
        
    3.  Click the **Domain Name** in the row for which you're adding the DNS record.
        
    4.  Click **Show DNS Info**.
        
    5.  Copy the information in the first row of the popup for the first DNS CNAME record you're adding.
        
    6.  Save the record.
        
        Note:
        
        You must add two CNAME records to DNS for each email domain.
        
    7.  Add another record to DNS of the type CNAME.
        
    8.  Copy the information in the second row of the popup in NetSuite for the second DNS CNAME record.
        
    9.  Save the record.
        
        Allow some time for the new DNS records to propagate to all DNS servers, which may take several hours. In some cases, it may take up to 24 hours.
        
4.  In NetSuite, check the **Status** of the email domain. After the DNS records are published, the status changes from **Pending Verification** to **Ready** and the **Details** column will be empty.
    
    For more information, see [Email Domains (DKIM) Status Column](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0124093036.html) and [Email Domains (DKIM) Details Column](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0124095724.html).
