For more information about DKIM, go to:

-   [DKIM.org](http://www.dkim.org/)
    
-   Wikipedia: [Domain Keys Identified Mail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DomainKeys_Identified_Mail)
    
-   See also the DKIM specifications: [RFC 6376 DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM) Signatures](https://tools.ietf.org/html/rfc6376).
    

Tip:

Consider setting up a DMARC policy record for your company's entire email infrastructure. See the subtopic [Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting and Conformance (DMARC)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4820737714.html) in [Email Best Practices](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N993117.html).
