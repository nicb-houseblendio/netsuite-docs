#### To delete strings in a collection:

1.  Go to _Customization > Translations > Manage Translations_.
    
2.  On the **Strings** subtab, click the ellipsis (**...**) next to the string you want to delete.
    
3.  Click **Delete**.
    
4.  You'll be asked to confirm that you want to delete the string and all its translations. If you're sure, click **Yes**. If no, click **No**.
    

Note:

Besides the Manage Translations UI, you can work with translation strings in SDF. In SDF, the "Key" is called "scriptid".
