The lock option for Translation Collections is for users who want to pack translatable fields with their translations and deploy them to another account.

You can't edit, export, or import translations for a locked Translation Collection-you can only view it.

Note:

You need to enable Creating Bundles with SuiteBundler before you can make locked collections. To enable it, go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_, open the **SuiteCloud** subtab, check the **Create Bundles with SuiteBundler** box in the SuiteBundler section, and click Save.

#### To Create and Export Locked Translation Collections:

1.  Create a Translation Collection by following the steps in [Managing Translation Collections](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161581766777.html).
    
2.  BStart creating a bundle by following step 1 and 2 in [Creating a Bundle with the Bundle Builder](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3374254.html).
    
3.  In Step 3 **Select Objects**, pick the Translation Collection you want to lock and deploy to another account. All available Translation Collections are listed under Translation Collections. You can also pick any custom objects with translatable fields to deploy.
    
4.  In Step 4 **Bundle Preferences**, select the **Lock on Install** option next to the Translation Collection you picked. This locks the Collection for deployment.
    
5.  Click **Save**. The bundle will be saved.
    
6.  Go to _Customization>SuiteBundler>Create Bundle>List_.
    
7.  Select **Push** in the Action menu next to the bundle you created in step 5.
    
8.  Select one or more accounts where you want to deploy the bundle with the locked collection, then click **Push** at the top of the list.
    
9.  Switch to the account where you deployed the Translation Collection and go to Manage Translations.
    
    The deployed collection will show with a lock symbol to indicate its status.
