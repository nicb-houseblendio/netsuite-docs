This section of explains the options for mass update of translation strings.

For mass import of translations into the NetSuite application, see [Importing Translations into a Translation Collection](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1544396501.html).

For mass export of translation strings in a Translation Collection, see [Exporting a Translation Collection](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1544396546.html).

For mass export of all standalone and parent strings in the account, see [Exporting and Importing Translation Strings from the Strings Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161226847768.html).
