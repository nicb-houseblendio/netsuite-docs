To use Translation Collections, you'll need:

-   The Multi-Language feature enabled in your NetSuite account. For information, see [Globalization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_8154319431.html).
    
-   Your role's Manage Translation permission. For information about setting permissions for a role, see [Setting Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N288727.html).
    
-   You'll need to add the languages you want to translate to your company's list of supported languages. For more information, see [Enabling the Entry of Translation Strings for a Specific Language](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4164392746.html).
