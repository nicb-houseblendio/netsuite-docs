In the top-right corner of the Manage Translations page, you'll see statistics for all collections, strings, languages, and translations in your account.

![Statistics on the Manage Translations page.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Globalization/ManageTranslationsStatistics.png)

Company Languages shows how many company languages are set up for your company.

Custom STrings is the total number of strings in all of your company's collections. For information about creating strings, see [Adding Strings to Translation Collections](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1544553758.html).

Completed Translations shows the percentage of all strings in all languages in your company's collections. When you click the **See Missing** link, you're take to the **Strings** subtab of the Manage Translations page with the filter set to Missing Translations.
