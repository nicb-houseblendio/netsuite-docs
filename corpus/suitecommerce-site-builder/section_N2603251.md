You can customize your site by selecting a color that fits your business. Select from a multitude of NetSuite colors, or customize your own.

#### To change the colors in your website: {#procedure_N2603285}

1.  Go to _Commerce > Websites > Website List_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to a website.
    
3.  Click the **Appearance** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Web Site Color Theme** field, select a color from the list.
    
    To create a custom color theme, go to _Commerce > Site Builder > Appearance > Color Themes_.
    
5.  Click **Submit**.
    

The changes you make on the Web Site Setup page automatically update in your store or site. To preview your store or site, go to _Commerce > Websites > Preview Website_.
