The Shopping Activity Analysis report shows you how many times your items have been added to a shopping cart from the item list view and item view, how many items total have been placed in a shopping cart from the item list view and item view, and how many orders you've received by item.

Use this report to decide what items your customers are placing in their shopping carts but not purchasing and which area of your site customers use to place items in the cart.

The following abbreviations are used in column headings for this report:

-   **LV** - List View
    
-   **IV** - Item View
    

## To see the Shopping Activity Analysis report: {#bridgehead_N2652739}

Go to _Reports > Web Presence > Shopping Activity Analysis_.

If the report does not display immediately, a message appears indicating that your report is loading. Click Cancel to stop the report from loading.
