The Item Orders report shows the number of items ordered by item, the customer who ordered the item, and the date when the item was last ordered.

## To see the Item Orders Detail report: {#bridgehead_N2649271}

Go to _Reports > Web Presence > Item Orders Detail_.

If the report does not display immediately, a message appears indicating that your report is loading. Click Cancel to stop the report from loading.
