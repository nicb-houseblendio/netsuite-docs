The Specials category is automatically generated by NetSuite in accounts provisioned prior to 2007.

You can use the Specials category to display items you might want to sell at a special price for a limited time. When customers visit your site, they see a link to the Specials category on the Home and Catalog tabs.

## Adding an Item to the Specials Category {#bridgehead_N2597375}

You can add an item to the Specials category by checking a box on the item record, or by selecting an item on the Basic subtab of the Specials category record.

Note:

The On Special option is displayed on the item record only if you have enabled the Specials feature.

#### To put an item in the Specials category: {#procedure_N2597387}

1.  Go to _Lists > Accounting > Items_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the item you want to put on special.
    
3.  Click the **Specials** subtab.
    
4.  Check the **On Special** box.
    
    Note:
    
    The description displayed in the web store comes from the description fields on the Store subtab.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

The item automatically appears in the Specials category of your site.

Alternatively, you can select items on the Specials Category record. Go to _Commerce > Site Builder > Content Management > Categories_. Click Edit next to the Specials category, and then make your selection on the Items sublist on the Basic subtab.

## Using the Specials Category with Multiple Websites {#bridgehead_N2597495}

If you use multiple websites, you can only display the Specials category on one site at a time. Select the site where you want the Specials category to display in the Site field on the Specials category record.

#### To select a website for the Specials category: {#procedure_N2597507}

1.  Go to _Commerce > Site Builder > Content Management > Categories_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the **Specials** category.
    
3.  In the **Site** field, select the website where you want to display the Specials category.
    
4.  In the **Subcategory of** field, select the tab where the Specials category should display.
    

## Removing the Specials Category from your Website {#bridgehead_N2597575}

If you do not want to use the Specials category, go to _Commerce > Site Builder > Content Management > Categories_. Click Edit next to the Specials category, clear the Display in Web Site box, and then click Save.
