When you integrate your web site with the NetSuite shopping cart, your customers can browse products on your site, then head to the shopping cart page to see everything they've added. Cart and checkout customization steps are different depending on whether you use SuiteCommerce Advanced or Site Builder.

To connect your **Site Builder** site with the NetSuite shopping cart, select Full Web Store as the Web Site Scope on the Web Site Setup page. Next, use the Cart tab on the Web Site Setup page to organize how item order details are displayed in the shopping cart. You can also turn on the Extended Cart preference, which lets you shoppers enter coupon codes, gift certificates, and shipping information right on the shopping cart page instead of different parts of the checkout. You can also use Scriptable cart to add SuiteScripts to web order forms.

For more information about shopping cart and checkout customization options for Site Builder, read the following topics:
