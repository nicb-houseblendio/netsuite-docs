This report shows the domain of the website each visitor navigated from to get to your site. This lets you understand which websites lead the most customers to you, helping you to better manage your marketing efforts.

## To see the Referrer Detail report: {#bridgehead_N2650242}

Go to _Reports > Web Presence > Referrer Detail_.

If the report does not display immediately, a message appears indicating that your report is loading. Click Cancel to stop the report from loading.
