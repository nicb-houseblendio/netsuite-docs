The reports listed below provide information about how visitors get to your site. You must use the Advanced Web Reports feature to see these reports.

To turn on Advanced Web Reports, an Administrator can go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_. Click the Web Presence tab, and then check the Advanced Web Reports box.

-   [Keywords Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2649581.html)
    
-   [Keywords Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2649721.html)
    
-   [Referrer Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2650222.html)
    
-   [Referrer Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2650327.html)
