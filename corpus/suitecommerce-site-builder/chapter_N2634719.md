An important aspect of creating an effective website is making sure that shoppers find the products they are looking for. NetSuite Site Builder provides a set of tools that can display relevant search results on your website:

-   Learn how to leverage the global search on your web store by reading [Helping Customers Find Items on Your Site](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2634841.html).
    
-   Learn about [Setting Up Alternate Search Keywords](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2635042.html) to use if a shopper misspells the name of a product when searching on your site.
    
-   Read [Publishing Saved Search Results on Your Website](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2635180.html) for instructions on creating and posting your own searches.
    
-   Learn how to better merchandise items on your website by [Applying Layout Templates to Search Results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2635640.html).
    
-   Allow customers to select their own search criteria by publishing [Search Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2591207.html) on your website.
    
-   Use the NetSuite Sitemap Generator to create an HTML site map for display on your site. For more information, see [Displaying an HTML Site Map on Your Website](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2646187.html)
