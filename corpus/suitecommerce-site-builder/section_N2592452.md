Images are an integral part of making a website look and feel complete and conveying a company style. In NetSuite, any time you upload an image to a record, it is stored in your File Cabinet, found on your **Documents** subtab.

For the purposes of your website, you can upload your item images, company logos, graphic art, and other images to the Images folder of your File Cabinet for access when associating the images with records in your account. You can upload a compressed file of all of your images using the Advanced Add button on the File Cabinet.

Files stored in Images folders using Add File or Advanced Add are automatically available without login. On Advanced Add, the Make All Files Available Without Login box is checked by default. On Add File, available without login is applied automatically in the background.

## Uploading Images {#bridgehead_N2592477}

To use images on your website, upload them to the File Cabinet by going to _Commerce > Site Builder > Content > Images_. Images must be stored in this folder to be accessible for your website. After they are uploaded, you can reference them in your store as many times as you like.

Images can be JPEG, PNG, or GIF files and must not exceed 10 KB per image. Flash animations are SWF files that may not exceed 30 KB and can only be used on the Welcome tab of your site.

After you upload your images, see [Using Images in your Web Store](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2592719.html) for instructions on how to attach images to items, categories, and information items.

#### To upload multiple images:

1.  On your computer, create a ZIP file containing the images you want to upload.
    
2.  In NetSuite, go to _Commerce > Site Builder > Content > Images_.
    
3.  Click **Advanced Add**.
    
4.  From the **Zip Archive to Add** field, select the ZIP file you created and click **Open**.
    
5.  Make sure:
    
    1.  the **Destination Folder** is **Images**.
        
    2.  the **Unzip Files** box is checked. This unzips your files directly into the Images folder.
        
    3.  the **Make All Files Available Without Login** box is checked. This lets all website visitors view the images.
        
        Note:
        
        Images on your website should be marked Available Without Login. Images uploaded to the Images folder are available without login by default. See [Make Images Available Without Login](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162137415332.html#subsect_162137429522) for instructions on performing a mass update for images that are not already available without login.
        
6.  Click **Add**.
    

#### To upload a single image:

1.  Go to _Commerce > Site Builder > Content > Images_.
    
2.  Click **Add File**.
    
3.  Find the image on your computer and click **Open**.
    

After uploading the images, see [Resizing Images](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2594819.html) for instructions on defining image resizing so you can use a single image in multiple places on your website.

You can enter alt text for images on your website to help improve search engine ranking. To enter alt text, go to the File Cabinet and click **Edit** on the desired image. Enter the alt text in the **Alt Image Tag** field and click **Save**.
