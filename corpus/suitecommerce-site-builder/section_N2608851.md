If you use the Advanced Site Customization and Host HTML Files features, you can use a style sheet with your website. You must have both features enabled to upload a CSS file.

#### To use a style sheet with a hosted site: {#procedure_N2608863}

1.  Click the **Documents** tab.
    
2.  Open the **Web Site Hosting Files** folder.
    
3.  Open the **Live Hosting Files** folder, or open the Staging Hosting Files to test your style sheet before publishing.
    
4.  Open the site folder.
    
5.  Click **Add File**, and choose your style sheet.
    

If you host all or part of your site and your hosted files refer to this style sheet, your style sheet is already applied.

#### To use a style sheet with a Site Builder website using Advanced Site Customization: {#procedure_N2608911}

1.  Complete the steps above for hosted site.
    
2.  Go to _Commerce > Site Builder > Appearance > Themes_.
    
3.  Click **Edit** or **Customize** next to the theme you use for your site.
    
4.  On the **General** subtab, in the **Addition to <head>** field, enter a link to your style sheet. Use a relative URL that includes your NetSuite account number, for example **/c.123456/site/sitestyle.css**.
