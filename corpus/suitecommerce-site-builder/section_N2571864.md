Whether you have a hosted HTML site, a NetSuite-generated site, or an External Catalog site, NetSuite automatically displays the shopping cart in your site when you choose Web Store as the Web Site Scope preference on the Web Site Setup form.

To customize the shopping cart, choose the columns you want to show in the cart, you can also modify the width, and the order in which the columns display. All the columns displayed in the shopping cart also display in the order fulfillment email notification sent to the customer. You can also use the extended cart.

Note:

You will not see a Cart subtab on the Set Up Web Site page until after you click Save on the Web Site Setup page for the first time.

#### To customize the shopping cart: {#procedure_N2571909}

1.  Go to _Commerce > Websites > Website List_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to a website.
    
3.  Click the **Cart** subtab.
    
4.  Check the box in the **Show** column next to each column you want to display in your site.
    
5.  Using the handles on the left side of each column name, click and drag the column to the correct position. The top of the list represents the left-most side of the shopping cart.
    
6.  In the **Width** column, enter the percentage of space each column should take, totaling to 100% among shown columns.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
