If you don't want to use Site Builder, disable Site Builder in your account.

#### To disable Site Builder in your account:

1.  Inactivate any Site Builder websites in your account. For more information, see [Making a Website Inactive](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1559227395.html).
    
2.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
3.  On the **Enable Features** page, click the **Web Presence** tab and clear the following boxes:
    
    -   **Web Site** field group:
        
        -   Site Builder (Website)
            
        -   Site Builder (Web Store)
            
    -   **Publishing** field group:
        
        External Catalog Site (WSDK)
        
4.  Click **Save**.
