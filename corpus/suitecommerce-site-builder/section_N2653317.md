The Internal Search Summary and Detail reports show data for the items visitors searched for while viewing your site. You must use the Advanced Web Reports feature to view these reports.

To turn on Advanced Web Reports, an Administrator can go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_. Click the Web Presence tab, and then check the Advanced Web Reports box.

-   [Internal Search Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2653392.html)
