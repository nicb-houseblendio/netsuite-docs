The Item Page Views and Sales Summary report shows you the number of pages views, orders, items sold and revenue by item. Use this report to understand which pages are being visited and which items are being sold.

## To see the Item Page Views and Sales Summary report: {#bridgehead_N2649376}

Go to _Reports > Web Presence > Item Page Views and Sales Summary_.

If the report does not display immediately, a message appears indicating that your report is loading. Click Cancel to stop the report from loading.
