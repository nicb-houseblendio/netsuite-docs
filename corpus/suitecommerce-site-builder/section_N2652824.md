The Shopping Activity Analysis report shows you how many times your items have been viewed, how many have been placed in shopping carts, and how many orders you've received by category. Use this report to decide which items your customers are placing in their shopping carts but not purchasing.

## To see the Shopping Activity Analysis by Category report: {#bridgehead_N2652843}

Go to _Reports > Web Presence > Shopping Activity Analysis by Category._.

If the report does not display immediately, a message appears indicating that your report is loading. Click Cancel to stop the report from loading.
