The email address you use to log in to NetSuite is also used for email notifications and other communications from administrators. You can use the Change Email link in the **Settings** portlet to change your email address.

Important:

Before you change your email address in NetSuite, verify that this change is OK with your account administrator.

#### To change your email address:

1.  Find the **Settings** portlet on the Home page. If you can't locate the **Settings** portlet, see [Finding Your Settings Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1493644302.html).
    
    Tip:
    
    The **Settings** portlet can appear anywhere on a customized Home page (or dashboard). By default, it's located on the left side, and you might need to scroll down to see it.
    
2.  Click **Change Email**.
    
3.  In the **Current Password** field, enter the password you currently use to log in.
    
    You must provide your password when you're changing your email address, to ensure the security of your account.
    
4.  Enter the new email address in the **New Email Address** field, then enter it again in the **Confirm New Email** field.
    
5.  Check the **Just This Account** box to change your login information for only the account to which you're currently logged in. This option only applies if you have access to multiple NetSuite accounts.
    
    If you clear this box, you can log in to all of your NetSuite accounts with the updated email address.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

After you have saved the change to your email address:

-   A notification message is sent to your current email address.
    
-   A validation message is sent to your new email address with a link that you must click to complete the change.
    
-   You must continue to use the current email address to log in to NetSuite until you confirm the change by clicking the validation link.
    
-   The new email address is listed in the **Pending Email Change** field on the Change Email page until you click the validation link or the link expires.
    

The times in the notification and validation messages are based on the time zone preference that you've set at Home > Set Preferences.

Important:

If you receive a notification about an email change you didn't make, alert your account administrator about a possible security issue. If you receive a notification message and you realize that the change is in error, for example you made a typo in the new email address, you can let the validation link expire. Then you can enter the correct email address.
