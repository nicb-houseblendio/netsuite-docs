If you have a 2FA-required role in NetSuite, you may be using a phone to obtain verification codes. If you lost your phone, or purchase a new phone, you must clear the settings (the setup information) of your old phone from NetSuite. After resetting your 2FA settings, you must set up your 2FA preferences in NetSuite again. Even if your phone number remains the same, you may not be able to transfer an authenticator app from one phone to another. You must set up your 2FA preferences in NetSuite again and install and configure an authentication app on your new phone.

Important:

If you lost your phone, you should remove the phone's 2FA settings from NetSuite as soon as possible. You may need to contact an Administrator of your NetSuite account for assistance. An Administrator can use the User Access Reset Tool to help you if you aren't able to log in to NetSuite.

Before you begin, verify that you have a current backup code available. If you can't locate your current backup codes, and you're able to log in to NetSuite, generate a new set of backup codes. See [Backup Codes for Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1535124735.html). If you can't log in to NetSuite, contact your Administrator.

Warning:

Before you start with the reset process, make sure you have either a device that can generate a verification code, or a backup code. If you didn't face the 2FA challenge during login phase of this NetSuite session, you'll be asked to provide a verification code or backup code to finish the reset.

If you can't provide the code, contact your Administrator to help you reset your 2FA.

#### To change your 2FA phone setup in NetSuite:

1.  Click the **Reset 2FA Settings** link in the **Settings** portlet. See [Reset Your 2FA Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1535124947.html). You may be required to enter a backup code to complete the reset. A backup code is required if you're logged in from a device that isn't a trusted device in NetSuite. For more information about trusted devices, see [Managing Your Trusted Devices](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1493329951.html).
    
    Note:
    
    If you can't locate your **Settings** portlet, see [Finding Your Settings Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1493644302.html).
    
2.  Log out of NetSuite.
    
3.  Log in to NetSuite with a 2FA-required role. You're prompted to set up your 2FA preferences before you can access NetSuite. See [Set up Your Preferences for Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1531519158.html). A video about 2FA setup is also available: [2FA Setup for Users](https://videohub.oracle.com/media/2FA+Setup+for+Users/1_45uvcfhf).
    
4.  Follow the on-screen prompts to finish the setup process for obtaining verification codes. See [Complete Your 2FA Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1535136907.html).
