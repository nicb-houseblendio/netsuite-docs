Important:

As of 2026.1, the feature is only available after a user with the Administrator role enables the Multiple Sessions for 2FA-protected Users. More simultaneous sessions are only available to users who have only 2FA-protected roles. If any of your roles in the account aren't 2FA-protected, you can only use one NetSuite session.

You can manage your active sessions using the **Active Sessions** link in the **Settings** portlet when you're logged in to NetSuite.

#### To manage your active sessions:

1.  Find the **Settings** portlet on the Home page. If you can't locate the **Settings** portlet, see [Finding Your Settings Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1493644302.html).
    
    Tip:
    
    The **Settings** portlet can appear anywhere on a customized Home page (or dashboard). By default, it's located on the left side, and you might need to scroll down to see it.
    
2.  Click **Active Sessions**.
    
3.  On the Active Sessions page, you can click the **Invalidate other sessions** button, which logs you out of all active sessions, except the current one you're working with.
    
    You can see how many active sessions you currently have, maximum is three.
    
    Important:
    
    If you're unsure about your active sessions, you should always invalidate them to prevent your account from misuse.
