Requiring a second factor for authentication is a method of improving security. Your administrator can assign you a role that requires 2FA authentication. You'll receive an email the first time you attempt to log in to your 2FA role. The email contains instructions and a verification code for your initial login to that role. The next time you log into a 2FA require role, you'll be prompted to set up your 2A preferences in NetSuite. Use an authenticator app to generate the verification codes necessary during login. While your session remains valid, you won't be asked again for a verification code when you switch between roles, even when you switch between roles in different companies.

If you're assigned a role that requires 2FA authentication, you must set up an authenticator application in NetSuite.

Note:

You must complete the initial 2FA setup in the NetSuite UI on your computer. You can log in using 2FA with the NetSuite Mobile application. However, it isn't possible to perform the initial 2FA setup from the mobile app.

As of NetSuite 2026.2, you can use passkeys instead of an authenticator apps for two-factor authentication (2FA) in the following cases:

-   The passkey you use is FIDO2 compliant.
    
-   Your Administrator doesn't disable the Passkey as 2FA feature, reverting 2FA settings to their previous state.
    
-   You need to generate the 2FA code to log in to NetSuite.
    

Important:

If you use a passkey for 2FA, NetSuite asks you for a code form your authenticator app every three months. This is to ensure that your 2FA setup is working properly and can be used to authenticate during 2FA reset.

Important:

If you lose your phone, buy a new phone, change your authenticator app, you must reset (clear) your 2FA settings in NetSuite. See [Reset Your 2FA Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1535124947.html). After resetting, you'll start the 2FA setup process again.

If you weren't faced with a 2FA challenge last time you logged in NetSuite, you'll need a verification code, backup code, or a help from your Administrator. If you can, reset your 2FA settings **before** you change your authenticator app.

To log in with a role that requires 2FA, you must first enter your email address and password on the NetSuite login page, and click **Log In**. After that step is successful, you're prompted for a verification code. For more information about using 2FA in NetSuite, see:
