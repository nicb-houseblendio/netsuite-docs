The first time you log in to NetSuite, you're prompted to set up three security questions that are used to verify your identity, as described in [Setting Up Security Questions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1548704486.html). You have the option of changing these questions and answers.

You're the only one who can update your security questions. If you can't answer your security questions, see [If You Forget the Answers to Your Security Questions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1548703801.html).

#### To change security questions and answers: {#procedure_N487952}

1.  Find the **Settings** portlet on the Home page. If you can't locate the **Settings** portlet, see [Finding Your Settings Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1493644302.html).
    
    Tip:
    
    The **Settings** portlet can appear anywhere on a customized Home page (or dashboard). By default, it's located on the left side, and you might need to scroll down to see it.
    
2.  Click **Update Security Questions**.
    
3.  Enter your current NetSuite password.
    
4.  In the **Question 1**, **Question 2** and **Question 3** fields, select questions for which you can remember the answers.
    
5.  In the **Answer 1**, **Answer 2**, and **Answer 3** fields, enter the answers to each question you selected. You must answer all three questions.
    
    -   Each answer must be unique.
        
    -   Each answer must be at least three characters long.
        
    -   You can't use your email address or password as an answer.
        
    -   Answers aren't case-sensitive.
        
    -   Answers are masked (hidden) by default. Clear the **Hide Answers** box to see the characters without the mask.
        
6.  Click **Save**.
