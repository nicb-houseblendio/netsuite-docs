You can change your password using the **Change Password** link in the **Settings** portlet when you're logged in to NetSuite. First, you must enter the password you used to log in to your account, and then enter a new password. You must provide your password to ensure the security of your account.

Important:

If you don't see the **Change Password** link in your **Settings** portlet check the role you're currently logged in with. It's possible that the role doesn't have the permission needed to change the password. Try switching to another role or contact your Administrator.

<a id="kaltura_player_30"></a>

You can also reset your password if you're unable to log in because you forgot your password. For more information, see [Getting Access When You Forget Your Password](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N487824.html).

#### To change your password:

1.  Find the **Settings** portlet on the Home page. If you can't locate the **Settings** portlet, see [Finding Your Settings Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1493644302.html).
    
    Tip:
    
    The **Settings** portlet can appear anywhere on a customized Home page (or dashboard). By default, it's located on the left side, and you might need to scroll down to see it.
    
2.  Click **Change Password**.
    
3.  In the **Current Password** field, enter the password you currently use to log in to NetSuite.
    
    If you provide five consecutive wrong passwords, you're locked out of NetSuite for a period of 30 minutes.
    
    Note:
    
    If you can't remember your current password, see [Getting Access When You Forget Your Password](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N487824.html).
    
4.  In the **New Password** field, enter a new password.
    
5.  As you type, the **Password Criteria** fields provide feedback, ensuring that the password you enter meets the required criteria. For more information, see [Creating a Strong Password](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N487704.html).
    
6.  Re-enter the new password in the **Confirm New Password** field.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    
    The next time you log in to NetSuite, you must use the new password.
    
    Note:
    
    You'll receive an email notification when the password associated with your email address is changed. This email notification helps you to be more aware and proactive in cases where there's a fraudulent attempt to change your password.
