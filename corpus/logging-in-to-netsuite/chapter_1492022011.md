Authentication is the process of verifying your identity when you log in to an application. This section explains how your identity can be authenticated when you log in to NetSuite.

The most common way to log in to the NetSuite UI is using your email address and password, which are your user credentials. Depending on the role or roles you're assigned by your account administrator, you might also be required to provide a second factor for authentication, known as two-factor authentication, or 2FA.

See the following sections for more information about how your identity is authenticated when you log in to NetSuite.
