You need to reset (clear) your 2FA settings in NetSuite if you:

-   Change your authenticator app.
    
-   Lose your phone.
    
-   Buy a new phone.
    

Warning:

Before you start with the reset process, make sure you have either a device that can generate a verification code, or a backup code. If you didn't face the 2FA challenge during login phase of this NetSuite session, you'll be asked to provide a verification code or backup code to finish the reset.

If you can't provide the code, contact your Administrator to help you reset your 2FA.

When you reset your 2FA settings, you remove your existing 2FA setup information from NetSuite.

Important:

The Reset 2FA Settings link only clears your existing 2FA settings from NetSuite. You won't be able to set up your 2FA preferences again until the next time you attempt to access NetSuite with a 2FA-required role. In this case, you receive a verification code by email. Enter the code and click **Submit**. You're prompted to set up your 2FA preferences in NetSuite. See [Set up Your Preferences for Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1531519158.html).

You can reset your 2FA settings from the **Settings** portlet on your Home page (Dashboard) by using the **Reset 2FA Settings** link.

#### To reset your 2FA settings:

1.  Find the **Settings** portlet on your Home page. If you can't locate the **Settings** portlet, see [Finding Your Settings Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1493644302.html).
    
2.  Click **Reset 2FA Settings**.
    
    Note:
    
    If you don't see the **Reset 2FA Settings** link on your **Settings** portlet, it indicates that you have already reset (cleared) your 2FA settings from NetSuite. The next time you log in to NetSuite with a 2FA-required role, you'll be prompted to set up your 2FA preferences in NetSuite. See [Set up Your Preferences for Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1531519158.html).
    
3.  On the Reset 2FA Settings page, enter your NetSuite password. You might also need to enter a verification code or backup code.
    
4.  Click **Reset**.
    
    When you click **Reset**, you remove your existing 2FA setup information. A confirmation screen states that your registered 2FA devices were successfully reset. You also receive an email notification from NetSuite that your registered 2FA devices have been reset, and can no longer be used to access your NetSuite account.
    
    The next time you attempt to access NetSuite with a 2FA-required role, you receive a verification code by email. Enter the code and click **Submit**, and you're prompted to set up your 2FA preferences in NetSuite. See [Set up Your Preferences for Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1531519158.html).
    
    Note:
    
    If you can't to log in to NetSuite to reset your 2FA settings, contact your account administrator for assistance. Also contact your administrator if you can't provide your password and a verification code during your 2FA reset attempt.
