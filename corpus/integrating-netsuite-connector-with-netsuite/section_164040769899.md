Change the primary user information from the account settings in NetSuite Connector.

#### To change the primary user information in NetSuite Connector

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  On the top right corner of the dashboard, hover over your username and from the list, select **Account Settings**.
    
    The **Profile** tab opens.
    
3.  Make appropriate changes to your information about the page.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    
5.  Change the password.
    
    1.  Click the **Password and Security** tab.
        
    2.  In the **Current Password** field, enter the current password.
        
    3.  In the **New Password** and **Confirm New Password** fields, enter the new password.
        
    4.  Click **Change Password**.
        

If you don't know the password of the primary user account, make sure you have access to the primary user's email address. Then reset the password from the link [https://app.farapp.com/reset-password](https://app.farapp.com/reset-password).
