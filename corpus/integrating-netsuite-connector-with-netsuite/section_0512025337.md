Variation items are items are displayed in a single listing in your storefront with details for color, size, and other variations. Choosing a specific variation selects a specific SKU for the order.

Variation items in NetSuite are represented as the matrix item type, where you define parent, child, and variation fields, such as color, size, and others. Typically, a parent item contains most of the information about the listing including description, name, and other details. The child item represents each unique variation. Each child item can override parent fields and needs its own unique SKU, as most storefronts require. Using matrix items is the best way to track items by options in NetSuite.

For more information, see [Matrix Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2227654.html).

If the parent-child relationships of your items are already set up on the storefront, you can use virtual variations. For more information, see [Virtual Variations in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0505013121.html).
