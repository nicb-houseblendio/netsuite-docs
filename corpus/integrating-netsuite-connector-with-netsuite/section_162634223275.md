NetSuite Connector setups may need the internal ID of records like orders, forms, customers, items, and more. To find the internal ID of a record, open the record and in the browser's address bar, check id=xxxx, where xxxx is the internal ID, as shown below:

![Example of internal id of a record in the address bar.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteConnector/NC_FindingInternalID.png)

For more information, see [NetSuite Connector Communication with Internal IDs and Field IDs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162611905808.html).

To configure NetSuite to display internal ID values in the user interface, see [Setting the Internal ID Preference](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3423996.html).
