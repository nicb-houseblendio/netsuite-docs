NetSuite links transactions to a specific accounting period. If an order date isn't in an open accounting period in NetSuite, the order won't sync. You can create, edit, or reopen accounting periods whenever you need to sync your transactions.

-   To setup new accounting periods, see [Accounting Period Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1445585.html).
    
-   To edit an accounting period, see [Editing an Accounting Period](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1446881.html).
    
-   To reopen a closed period, see [Reopening a Closed Period](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1457543.html).
