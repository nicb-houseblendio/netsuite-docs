NetSuite Connector requires your fields to be visible in your transaction forms for the connector to sync and send your transactions. If a field in your form is not visible, you will get errors when syncing your transaction data.

When using custom forms with NetSuite Connector, you need to make sure that the following custom fields are visible or editable:

-   NetSuite Connector Storefront
    
-   NetSuite Connector Storefront Order Number
    
-   NetSuite Connector Order Total
    
-   NetSuite Connector Shipping Tax
    
-   NetSuite Connector Line Item Tax
    
-   NetSuite Connector Line Item Amount
    

For more information about customizing your forms and changing visibility of the fields, see [Creating Custom Entry and Transaction Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2853340.html).

For more information about custom forms, see [Custom Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2852749.html).
