If you have quantity-based pricing enabled in NetSuite, you must also enable quantity based price levels in NetSuite Connector. This applies even if you don't set pricing for quantities above the first (0) level.

For more information, read [Mapping Price from a Quantity-Based Price Level](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162935347113.html).

#### To enable the quantity-based pricing feature in NetSuite:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Enable Features.
    
2.  Click the **Transactions** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Quantity Pricing** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
