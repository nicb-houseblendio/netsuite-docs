NetSuite often refers to objects by their internal IDs when sending error messages through API. So, setting up NetSuite to show internal IDs of objects can be helpful when you're troubleshooting. You can show the internal IDs of objects by enabling the **Show Internal IDs** preference.

For more information, read [Setting the Show Internal IDs Preference](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3420345.html).

You can also locate the internal ID of an object by other options mentioned in the topic [Finding the Internal ID of Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162634223275.html).
