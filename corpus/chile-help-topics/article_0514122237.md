Follow these procedures to set up the Latam E-Document Certification SuiteApp for Chile.

## Creating a Tax Authority Certification Service record for Chile {#bridgehead_0514030044}

Follow these steps to create a Tax Authority Certification Service record for Chile.

#### To create a Tax Authority Certification Service for Chile:

After you save the record, several subtabs are displayed within the record.

1.  Go to Setup > E-Document Certification > Tax Authority Certification Service > New.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter the record name.
    
3.  In the **Tax Authority Plug-in Implementation** field, select the tax authority plug-in implementation for the tax authority.
    
4.  In the **Certification Monitoring Plug-in Implementation** field, select one or multiple certification monitoring plug-in implementations you want to associate with this tax authority certification service.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

## Sending E-Document to the Tax Authority for Certification (Chile) {#bridgehead_0514025857}

Follow this procedure to submit the e-document to the tax authority for certification.

#### To send an e-document to the tax authority for certification:

1.  Go to Setup > E-Document Certification > Tax Authority Certification Service
    
2.  Click **View** next to the tax authority certification service you created.
    
3.  Click **New Certification Service Operation**.
    
    A new certification service operation record opens.
    
4.  In the **Name** field, enter Gosocket CL Sending.
    
5.  In the **Tax Authority Certification** field, add CL Gosocket.
    
6.  In the **Operation Type** field, select Send E-Document.
    
7.  In the **E-Document Template** field, select the template that corresponds to the document type:
    
    -   **Electronic Invoice**
        
    -   **Credit and Debit Memos**
        
    -   **Dispatch Guide**
        
    -   **Export Document**
        
    -   **Invoice Settlement**
        
    -   **Electronic Bill**
        
8.  Click **Save**.

## Adding Certification Service URL (Chile) {#bridgehead_0521121927}

Follow these steps to map the Certification Web Service URL and the Certification Service Operation

#### To add certification service URL for Chile

1.  Go to Setup > E-Document Certification > Tax Authority Certification Service.
    
2.  Click **View** next to the tax authority certification service you created.
    
3.  Click the **Web Service URLs** subtab.
    
4.  Click **New Certification Service URL**.
    
5.  A new certification service URL record opens.
    
6.  In the **Tax Authority Certification Service** field, add CL Gosocket
    
7.  In the **Certification Environment** field, select the certification environment from the tax authority this URL connects to.
    
8.  In the **Certification Web Service URL** field, enter the URL for the tax authority's E-Document Certification web service.
    
9.  Check the **Encrypt** box.
    
10.  In the **Certification Service Operation** field, select the type of operation that can be requested from the tax authority through this web service URL.
     
11.  Click **Save**.
     

## Creating API Secret (Chile) {#bridgehead_0521123626}

#### To create an API secret for Chile:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > API Secrets.
    
2.  At the top of the page, click **Create New**.
    
3.  In the **Name** field, enter the record name.
    
4.  In the **Password** field, enter a valid password (between 16 and 32 characters).
    
5.  Under the Restrictions subtab:
    
    1.  Check the **Allow For All Scripts** box.
        
    2.  Check the **Allow For All Domains** box.
        
6.  Click **Save**.
    

## Defining E-Document Certification Settings {#bridgehead_0521022452}

#### To define e-document certification settings:

1.  Go to Setup > E-Document Certification > Tax Authority Certification Service
    
2.  Click **View** next to the tax authority certification service you created.
    
3.  Click the **Certification Settings** subtab.
    
4.  Click **New E-Document Certification Settings**.
    
    A new e-document certification settings record opens.
    
5.  In the **Tax Authority Certification Service** field, select CL Gosocket.
    
6.  In the **Subsidiary** field, select the Chile subsidiary.
    
7.  In the **E-Document Category** field, select Electronic Invoice for Goods.
    
8.  In the **E-Document Folder ID**, enter the internal ID of the file cabinet folder in which to save the certified e-documents.
    
9.  In the **Certification Environment** field, select the environment to which you send the e-documents you generate.
    
10.  In the **API Secret ID** field, enter the ID of the API secret you created in the previous procedure.
     

## Adding Custom Fields for Certification {#bridgehead_0523011015}

#### To add a custom field for certification:

1.  Go to Setup > E-Document Certification > Tax Authority Certification Service.
    
2.  Click **View** next to the tax authority certification service you created.
    
3.  Click the **Certification Settings** subtab.
    
4.  Click **Edit** next to the certification settings record.
    
    The e-document certification settings record opens.
    
5.  Click the **Custom Fields** subtab.
    
6.  Click **Certification Custom Fields** and add the following information:
    
    1.  In the **Custom Field Key** field, enter the key to identify the information the tax authority requires you to provide for the custom field. In this case the value must be **Token**.
        
    2.  In the **Custom Field Value** field, enter the technology provider **Token**.
        
7.  Click **Save**.
    
8.  Open the previous record.
    
9.  On the Custom Fields subtab > Certification Custom Fields, add the following information:
    
    1.  In the **Custom Field Key** field, enter a key to identify the information you need to fill in for the custom field required by the tax authority. In this case, enter **Credentials**.
        
    2.  In the **Custom Field Value** field, enter the technology provider username and password in the format **username:password**, and encode the complete value in Base64.
        
    3.  Check the **Encrypt Custom Field Value** box.
        
10.  Click **Save**.
     
11.  Open the previous record.
     
12.  On the Custom Fields subtab > Certification Custom Fields, add the following information:
     
     1.  In the **Custom Field Key** field, enter a key to identify the information you need to fill in for the custom field required by the tax authority. In this case, enter **Certificate**.
         
     2.  In the **Custom Field Value** field, enter **false**.
         
13.  Click **Save**.
