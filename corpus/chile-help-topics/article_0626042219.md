## Prerequisites {#bridgehead_0626042552}

You must have an invoice or cash sale stamped as:

-   33 Factura Electrónica
    
-   34 Factura Exenta Electrónica
    
-   43 Liquidación Factura Electrónica
    

## Using the CL SII Check Date button {#bridgehead_0626042617}

#### To use the SII Check Date button:

1.  In NetSuite, go to Transactions > Payables > Enter Bill > New
    
2.  Select the **CL Vendor** form
    
3.  Enter the required information for this form.
    
4.  On the Chile Localization subtab enter the following information:
    
    1.  **CL E-invoicing number**: number provided by the SII.
        
    2.  **CL SII Invoice Type**: choose invoice type.
        
5.  Click **Save**.
    
6.  After the record is saved, the CL SII Check Date button appears.
    
7.  Click the **CL SII Check Date** button. After a few seconds the **CL Document Identification TP** and **CL SII Stamped Date** fields show the related information.
    

CL DTE APPROVAL OR CLAIM button

## Using the CL DTE Approval or Claim button {#bridgehead_0626043806}

#### To use the CL DTE approval or claim button

1.  Check the transaction created in the previous step.
    
2.  Click the **CL DTE Approval or Claim** button.
    
3.  Select one of the options:
    
    030: Acuse Commercial
    
    031: Rechazo
    
    032: Acuse de Mercadería
    
    033: Aceptación Comercial
    
    Cancelar
    
4.  The field **CL DTE Approval or Claim Reason** must show the option you selected in the previous step. The **CL SII Acceptance or Claim** field must show the SII response about approval or claim.
