The following table defines the terms used in NetSuite to support Chile-specific business processes:

| BCI | Banco de Crédito e inversiones (Bank of Credit and Investments). |
| --- | --- |
| DTE | Documentos Tributarios Electrónicos Electronic Tax Documents |
| DV | Dígito Verificador. Digit to identify tax id numbers |
| RUT | Rol único tributario. Sole mechanism to identify, locate, and classify individuals and entities who are taxpayers liable to income tax and non taxpayers liable to income and patrimony. In addition, those responsible for the general tax regime. |
| SII | Servicios de Impuestos Internos Administrative unit aimed to guarantee the tax security of the Chilean State. |
