The Chile Localization SuiteApp provides custom fields, records, validations, and other customizations for Chilean businesses to comply with tax in Chile.

NetSuite provides value-added tax (VAT) and withholding tax tracking and reporting for businesses in Chile.

The SuiteTax Latam Process SuiteApp enables you to meet the local tax determination requirements. The companies that do business in different parts of the world must comply with different tax requirements from the government authorities.

The SuiteTax Latam Process helps companies fulfill specific requisites and calculate taxes in their NetSuite transactions. It also provides the deployment of a tax process complement and a structure of custom records for setting up the tax definition in different contexts. The SuiteApp also provides a type of custom plug-in, the SuiteTax Latam Process Plug-in. You can use the custom complement type to create complement deployments to calculate a tax or a group of taxes.
