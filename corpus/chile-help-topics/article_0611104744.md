There are three steps to consider when certifying electronic documents:

#### To certify e-documents for Chile:

If the transaction does not have a certificate, go to the E-Document Audit Trail on the E-Documents subtab to see the reason why it was not certified.

1.  After the transaction has been saved with the required information, the **Generate Electronic Document** button becomes available. Click this button to generate the XML file.
    
2.  After generating the document, the **Certify Electronic Document** button appears. Click this button to certify the XML before the SII. When the document is certified, go to Electronic Documents > Electronic Document Generator subtab to see the XML.
    
3.  Finally, the **Get Documents** button appears. Click this button to download the XML and PDF files that were certified by the SII.
