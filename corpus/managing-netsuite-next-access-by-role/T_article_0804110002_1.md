#### To Manage NetSuite Next Access For One or More Roles:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > AI > NetSuite Next Access_.
    
2.  On the NetSuite Next Access page, select the role or roles you want to update.
    
3.  Select **NetSuite Net Only**, **Optional** or **Blocked** for the selected role or roles.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
