The SAF-T Reporting Framework SuiteApp supports the following languages:

-   English (U.S.)
    
-   Danish
    

For information about selecting your preferred language, see [Setting Language Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543591052.html).

Note:

If you set the localization preference to any language other than these supported languages, the pages will be displayed in English (U.S.).
