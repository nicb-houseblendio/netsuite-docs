The Shipping report lists every order you'll ship during the date range you set in the report's footer. For each transaction, the report includes these fields:

-   **Addressee** - the name of the company or person receiving the item.
    
-   **Street Address** - where the item will be shipped.
    
-   **City** - the city or town where the item will be shipped.
    
-   **State** - the state or province where the item will be shipped.
    
-   **Zip Code** - the zip or area code where the item will be shipped.
    
-   **Weight** - the package weight.
    
-   **Shipping Method** - how the item will be shipped, like by land, sea, or air.
    
-   **Order Type** - how the order started, like a Sales Order.
    
-   **Order #** - the order number for this transaction.
    
-   **Email** - the addressee's email address.
    
-   **Phone Number** - the addressee's telephone number.
    

#### To view the Shipping report:

1.  Go to _Reports > Order Management > Shipping Report_.
    
    NetSuite shows a message that your report is loading, and the status bar tracks its progress.
    
2.  To stop the report from loading, you can click **Cancel Report**.
    
3.  (Optional) To export this report as a CSV file that you can then import into your shipping program, you can click **Export**.
    
    For example, you can export this report as a CSV file and then import the file into UPS WorldShip® so you don't have to enter each customer's information manually.
    

## Adding Tracking Numbers to the Shipping Report {#bridgehead_N1319021}

You can add tracking numbers to the Shipping Report using Advanced Report Customization. Adding tracking numbers is especially important if you use the Shipping Label Integration feature.

#### To add tracking numbers to the Shipping Report: {#procedure_N1319033}

1.  Click the **Customize** button on the Shipping Report.
    
2.  In the **Report Name** field, name your custom report.
    
    This is the name you'll select when you open your new report.
    
3.  In the **Add Fields** section to the left of the page, expand the Item Fulfillment heading.
    
4.  Scroll through the fields, and select **Tracking Number**.
    
    This adds a Tracking Number column to the report. To move the column, use the Move buttons or drag and drop the column.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Now your custom shipping report's available on the Reports tab, under Saved Reports.

You can create a shortcut to this report by clicking **Add to Shortcuts** in the upper right corner while viewing the report. This adds a quick link to your custom report in the Shortcuts portlet on your dashboard or other tabbed pages.

For more information on reporting, see [Reporting Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N698474.html).
