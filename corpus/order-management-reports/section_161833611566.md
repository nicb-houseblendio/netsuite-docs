Before deleting a custom Same-Store Sales calendar that's set as the Primary Calendar for the year, you must first assign another calendar as the Primary Calendar for the year to replace the one you want to delete. Then, on the calendar to be deleted, clear the Default Calendar for the Year box and proceed with deletion.

#### To delete a Same-Store Sales calendar:

1.  Go to Setup > Same-Store Sales > Calendar.
    
2.  On the Calendar List page, click the **Edit** link of the calendar that you want to delete.
    
3.  On the Calendar page, ensure that the **Set as Default Calendar for the Year** box is cleared.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    
5.  Click **Actions** and select **Delete**.
