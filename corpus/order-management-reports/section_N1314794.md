The Open Sales Orders report shows open customer orders that haven't been billed yet. The report includes the date of each sales order, order number, customer, status, and amount.

With Narrative Insights, you can use generative AI to summarize the report, helping you quickly see the total open amount for the period and spotlight the highest value open sales orders.

The generated insights may include:

-   The total open amount for all sales orders in the period
    
-   A prioritized list of sales orders with the highest open amounts, showing the customer name, open amount, and current status for each
    
-   Observations on any potential delays in processing sales orders
    

To generate an insight on the Open Sales Orders report, the **Enable Narrative Insights** preference must be enabled in the **Narrative Insights** tab of the AI Preferences page. This preference is enabled by default. For more information, see [Managing Narrative Insights Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6150024470.html).

For more information about the Narrative Insights, see [Narrative Insights Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_135342304.html).

#### To view the Open Sales Orders report:

1.  Go to _Reports > Order Management > Open Sales Orders_.
    
    NetSuite shows a loading message for your report. The status bar shows the report's loading progress.
    
2.  To stop the report from loading, you can click **Cancel Report**.
    
3.  (Optional) To customize the report, click **Customize**.
    
    The Report Builder opens, letting you add, move, or remove columns, add fields, and add totals.
    

Note:

This report doesn't support reporting by period, even if the Report by Period preference is set to All Reports. Configure the Report by Period preference at _Home > Set Preferences_, on the **Analytics** subtab.

#### To generate an insight on the Open Sales Orders report:

1.  Filter the report to include only the information you want the insight to reflect.
    
2.  Click the **Generate Insight** button.
    
    The Narrative Insights dialog is displayed. You can generate the insight again at any time by clicking the **Generate Insight** button.
    

Important:

Insights may differ each time they're generated, even when you're viewing the same data.
