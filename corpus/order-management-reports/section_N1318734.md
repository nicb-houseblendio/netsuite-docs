The Open Invoices report shows all outstanding invoices that haven't been paid.

By default, the report includes the following columns:

-   **Account** - The customer's unique NetSuite account number.
    
-   **Customer** - The name of the customer.
    
-   **Invoice Date** - The date of invoice creation.
    
-   **Invoice Number** - A unique number that identifies the invoice.
    
-   **Memo** - Notes about the customer, account, or invoice.
    
-   **Date Due** - Payment due date for the invoice or installment.
    
-   **Amount Due** - The total outstanding balance remaining on the invoice or installment.
    

When you enable the Installments feature, the report includes the following additional columns:

-   **Installment Number** - The order in which the installments become due.
    
-   **Installment Amount** - The initial amount of the installment at creation time.
    

Tip:

You can add more fields to your report by selecting them from the **Add Fields** pane.

You can use the Accounts Receivable Register report to find existing transactions and to track receivable transactions. For more information, see [A/R Register Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1533154.html).

#### To view the Open Invoices report:

1.  Go to _Reports > Sales > Open Invoices_.
    
    NetSuite shows a message indicating that your report is loading. The status bar indicates the progress as your report loads.
    
2.  To stop the report from loading, you can click **Cancel Report**.
    

For more information about reporting, see [Reporting Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N698474.html).
