Assign the Override Estimated Cost on Transactions permission to allow a user (or a SuiteScript) to override estimated cost and gross profit values on transactions in the sales cycle. You can only assign the Full level for this permission. Exercise care in assigning this permission.

By default, this permission is assigned to the following two standard roles:

-   Sales Administrator
    
-   Sales Manager
    

You have to explicitly add this permissions to custom roles as needed. See [Changing Custom Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N289485.html).

#### To assign the Override Estimated Costs on Transactions permission: {#procedure_N1757450}

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the name of the role that you want to modify.
    
3.  On the **Transactions** subtab, select **Override Estimated Cost on Transactions** from the **Permission** dropdown, and click **Add**.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
