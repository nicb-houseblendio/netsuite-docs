The Lot and Serial Number Trace SuiteApp helps you to trace components, semi-finished, or finished goods from a raw material supplier through to the customer. The SuiteApp enables you to trace and report the following:

-   Vendors who supplied the lot or serial numbered items
    
-   Customers who bought the lot or serial numbered items
    
-   Procurements, deliveries, internal transfers, and manufacturing inputs and outputs
    
-   Additional details such as expiry date, total quantity, quantity on hand, and so on
    
-   If you have the Quality Management SuiteApp installed, the trace report also displays quality data. For more information about the SuiteApp, read [Quality Management Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1501602139.html).
    

You can trace transactions for the following:

-   Traced item lot - The item lot that is traced.
    
-   Forward assemblies - Items in which the traced item was used as a component.
    

Note:

The SuiteApp does not perform any transactions, it only provides a report of historical transactions.
