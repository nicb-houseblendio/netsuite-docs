By default, any roles can view and generate the Same-Store Sales Report.

Roles that can set up the Same-Store Sales Report Calendar and Preferences are those that use the following centers:

-   Accounting Center
    
-   Classic Center
    
-   Executive Center
    
-   Shipping Center
    

Before these roles can set up the Calendar and Preferences, ensure that they are granted the appropriate permissions by following these steps.

#### To grant a role the permissions to Calendar and Preferences custom records:

1.  Go to Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles.
    
2.  On the Manage Roles page, click the **Edit** link of the role that you want to grant permissions to.
    
3.  On the Role page, go to the **Permissions** subtab and do the following:
    
    1.  Click the **Custom Record** subtab, and do the following:
        
        1.  In the Record list, add **Calendar**, **Calendar Dates**, and **Same-Store Sales Preferences**.
            
        2.  In the Level column, select **Full** for the added custom records.
            
    2.  Click the **Setup** subtab and do the following:
        
        1.  In the Permission list, add **SuiteScript** and **SuiteScript Scheduling**.
            
        2.  In the Level column, select **Full** for both.
            
4.  Click **Save**.
