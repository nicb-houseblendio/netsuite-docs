To view open customer orders that haven't yet been billed, see [Open Sales Orders Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1314794.html).

To view backordered items, see [Sales Back Order Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1315076.html).

To view all orders per customer, see [Sales Order Register Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1315449.html).

To view all open transaction lines for items ready to be fulfilled, see [Sales Orders Pending Fulfillment Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1315755.html).
