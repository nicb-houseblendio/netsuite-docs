The Same-Store Sales Report works with OneWorld NetSuite accounts that have Subsidiary and Location records.

Before installing the SuiteApp, you must enable several features in NetSuite:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features.
    
2.  Click the **Company** subtab and enable the following features:
    
    -   Under Classifications section, check **Locations**.
        
    -   Under Data Management, check **Inline Editing** and **File Cabinet**.
        
3.  Click the **Items & Inventory** subtab, under Inventory, enable and check the following features:
    
    -   **Inventory**
        
    -   (Optional) **Multi-Location Inventory**
        
4.  Click the **SuiteCloud** subtab and enable the following features:
    
    -   Under SuiteBuilder, check **Item Options** and **Custom Records**.
        
    -   Under SuiteScript, check **Client Script** and **Server Script**.
        
    -   (Optional) Check **Advanced PDF/HTML Templates** if you'd like to export and print PDF.
        
5.  On the Enable Features page, click **Save**.
    

Note:

The Same-Store Sales Report SuiteApp works with or without the Multi-Currency feature enabled.
