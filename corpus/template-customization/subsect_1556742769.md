The following synthetic fields are available on the **Cash Sale** record for email sent to web site customers.

| Sublist | Field | Available for Email Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| licenses | 
-   licenseName : Name of the license that the customer has purchased.
-   licenseCode : License code that the customer has purchased.
