You can add borders and alter the orientation of elements of your PDF forms.

#### To add a border to a form: {#procedure_N2870085}

1.  Go to _Customization > Forms > Transaction Form PDF Layouts_.
    
2.  Click **Customize** or **Edit** next to the form you want to change.
    
3.  In the Form Editor, click the element you want to add a border to.
    
4.  Under the **Additional Options** heading, check the **Show Border** box.
    
5.  Click **Preview** to see how this PDF layout will look when printed.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

To change an element's orientation in the PDF layout, click and drag it to where you want it on the form or use the fields under the **Element Position** heading. For more information, see [Customizing Transaction Form PDF Layouts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2869660.html).
