You can create dynamic scriptable templates for email marketing campaigns and system email. Scriptable templates produce personalized output and give you control over the presentation of your email communication.

Scriptable templates, for example, let you create a single template that offers individualized upsell items to each recipient, based on each customer's purchase history.

You can create scriptable templates for any type of email sent from NetSuite, including:

-   marketing campaign email templates
    
-   email and fax templates (including bulk merge templates)
    
-   system email templates
    
-   email and faxes sent from the Communication subtab of records
    
-   email and faxes sent from the Messages subtab of transactions
    

Note:

To work with email templates, you need View level of Email Template permission. For more information about permissions, see [NetSuite Permissions Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N325094.html).
