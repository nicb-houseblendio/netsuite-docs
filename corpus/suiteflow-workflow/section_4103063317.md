A transition moves the record from one state to another in the workflow. A workflow must have at least two states before you can create a transition. For an overview of transitions in SuiteFlow, including transition properties, triggers, and conditions, see [Workflow Transitions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4071954244.html).

You can create and edit workflow transitions in the diagrammer or in the Workflow State window. Each transition has properties that specify the state the workflow transitions to, what triggers the transition, and any conditions needed for it to run. You can also define the transition to run when you click a button, when another state in the workflow finishes, or on a schedule.
