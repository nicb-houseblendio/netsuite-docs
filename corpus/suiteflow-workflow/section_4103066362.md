Delete a workflow transition to keep it from processing on subsequent workflow runs. You can delete a transition using the diagrammer, context panel, or Workflow Transition window.

#### To delete a transition:

1.  Open the existing workflow with the transition you want to delete.
    
    See [Viewing Existing Workflows](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4101532858.html) or [Workflow Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4103089023.html).
    
2.  In the diagrammer, select the transition and press Delete, or click the **Delete** icon on the **Transition** tab in the context panel.
    
3.  (Optional) Double-click the transition in the diagrammer, then in the Workflow Transition window, select **Delete** from the Actions menu.
