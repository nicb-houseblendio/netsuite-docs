You can use conditions to specify a criteria for workflow initiation, and action and transition execution. Conditions can be basic field/value comparisons or formulas. Use these conditions to restrict for whom, when and on which record instances a workflow initiates or actions and transitions execute. You can create conditions by using the **Condition Builder** or a **Formula**.
