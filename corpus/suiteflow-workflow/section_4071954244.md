A transition moves the record in the workflow into another state. A state can contain multiple transitions, with each transition having its own condition. A workflow instance executes the first transition that meets the transition condition and the record in the workflow moves to the specified state.

Each transition has the following elements:

-   Transition properties. Properties common to all transitions. See [Transition Properties](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4079019023.html).
    
-   Trigger. The trigger determines when a transition executes. See [Transition Triggers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4079591327.html).
    
-   Conditions. The condition determine the additional prerequisites that must be met for a transition to execute. All conditions must be satisfied to execute the transition. See [Transition Conditions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4079597309.html).
