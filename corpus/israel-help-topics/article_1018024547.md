To set up your NetSuite account according to Israel-specific preferences, you must adjust your NetSuite company settings.

Note:

Make sure that your NetSuite role has the appropriate permissions to set up company preferences to Israel.

#### To adjust company settings: {#procedure_1018024711}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > States/Provinces/Counties_.
    
2.  Add the relevant districts.
    
3.  Go to _Setup > Company > General Preferences_.
    
    -   In the **Date Format** fields, specify the date format (DD.MM.YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY) and long date format (DD Month YYYY).
        
    -   In the **Number Format** field, select 1,000,000.00.
        
    -   (Optional) On the **Accounting Context** subtab, create an accounting context (for example, you can call it Israel). For more information, see [Accounting Contexts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4640155582.html).
        
    -   On the **Languages** subtab, select the language to use in NetSuite pages. Hebrew and Arabic aren't available.
        
4.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Company Information_.
    
    -   Enter the VAT registration number for the parent subsidiary.
        
    -   In the **Time Zone** field, select (GMT+2:00) Jerusalem.
        
    -   Create a currency record for ILS by clicking the plus sign next to the **Currency** field. After the ILS record has been created, set this as the currency of the company also of the top parent subsidiary. For more information, see [Creating Currency Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1395911.html).
        
5.  Click **Save**.
    

For more information about installing and configuring the Israeli Localization, see [Israel Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162375098341.html).
