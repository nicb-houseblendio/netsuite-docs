## Israeli Check Printing {#bridgehead_162435199933}

You can print Israeli Checks directly from NetSuite.

#### To print Israeli check:

1.  Select the bank account and select **Print** to find relevant checks to print.
    
2.  Go to Transactions > Management > Print Israeli Checks and Forms.
    
    Invoices can also be printed from this page.
