After you set up your NetSuite Account, you should download the Israel Localization SuiteApp before you create any Israeli subsidiaries.

#### To create subsidiaries for Israel:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Subsidiaries_.
    
2.  Click the **New Subsidiary** button.
    
3.  In the **Name** field, enter the name of your subsidiary.
    
4.  From the **Country** list, select **Israel**.
    
5.  From the **Currency** list, select **ILS**. For more information, see [Creating Currency Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1395911.html).
    
6.  Click **Save**. After you save the new subsidiary, the Israel Localization SuiteApp adds the following subtabs to the subsidiary record:
    
    -   **Israeli Subsidiary Details**
        
    -   **Israeli OF Details**
        
    -   **IL Preferences**
        

#### To update the Israeli subsidiary information:

Note:

The IL Preferences subtab defines the check format. Most companies in Israel will use the new check format, unless they have an old stock of check papers. The new regulation for check formats was introduced in 2019.

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Subsidiaries_.
    
2.  Click the **Edit** link next to your Israeli subsidiary.
    
3.  Fill out the relevant details on the **Israeli Subsidiary Details** subtab and the **Israeli OF Details** subtab.
    
4.  On the **IL Preference** subtab, the **IL New Check Tab** is checked automatically.
    
5.  Click the **Israeli Subsidiary Details** tab.
    
6.  Fill out the relevant details on the **Israeli OF** subtab. The open format is used for Audit purposes and is requested by the IRS. It covers all business activities. Information needs to be populated in Hebrew. To see the translation from Hebrew to English, see the table below:
    
    | **Hebrew** | **English** |
    | --- | --- |
    | שם חברה באנגלית | Company Name in English |
    | מספר במס הכנסה | IRS Number |
    | מספר בביטוח לאומי | Social Security Number |
    | מספר תיק ניכויים | Withholding Tax Number |
    | מספר עוסק אילת | Special Business Area Tax Number |
    | מוסד כספי | Financial Institution Check Box |
    | כתובת מייל ל856 | Email Address for 856 |
    | מספר באיחוד עוסקים | Consolidated VAT Number |
    | כתובת חברה באנגלית | Company Address in English |
    | סוג עוסק | Business Type Code |
    | מעמד המשלם | Payer Type Code |
    

Israeli OF requires the Withholding Tax Clearing Account to be set up.

#### To update the Israel Localization subsidiary settings:

1.  Go to _Customization > Lists, Records and Fields > Record Types_
    
2.  Click **IL Subsidiary Settings** in the **Edit** column.
    
3.  Click the **More** button in upper-right corner and select **View Records**.
    
4.  Click the **New IL Subsidiary Settings** button.
    
5.  From the **Subsidiary** list, select the subsidiary you want to update.
    

#### To add Israel-specific accounts to your chart of accounts:

1.  Go to Setup > Accounting > Chart of Accounts. For more information, see [Making Changes to Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1442743.html).
    
2.  Click **New** and add the following accounts specific for Israel:
    

| Name | Type | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MASAV IL | Credit Card | **Optional** - For Israeli EFT Payment (MASAV) Note: Must be used with Credit Card type. |
| VAT on Purchases IL | Other Current Asset | **Required** - for VAT reports |
| Non-Claimable VAT IL Note: As part of the VAT accounts. | Other Current Asset | **Required** - Non-Claimable VAT for Proforma Invoice (Auditor, Lawyer…) and Self-Invoice (Non-dealer) |
| VAT Liability IL | Other Current Liability | **Required** - for VAT reports |
| VAT on Sales IL | Other Current Liability | **Required** - for VAT reports |
| Self-Invoice IL | Other Current Liability | **Required** - Netting Account for Self-Invoice |
| Withholding Tax - Clearing Account IL | Other Expense | **Required** - For Withholding Tax Reports |

Note:

Account numbers for these accounts are not prescribed and you can use any of your choice.

#### To set up the Withholding Tax Clearing Account {#procedure_0920102850}

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Accounting Preferences_.
    
2.  Go to the Items/Transactions subtab.
    
3.  Select **9090 Withholding Tax - Clearing Account IL** from the **Purchase/Discount Account** list.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To set up document printing:

1.  Go to _Customization > Lists, Records and Fields > Record Types_
    
2.  Click **IL Subsidiary Settings** in the **Edit** column.
    
3.  Click the **More** button in upper-right corner and select **View Records**.
    
4.  Click **View** on the IL Settings record.
    
5.  On the **Extended Documents Printing** subtab, ensure the following boxes are checked:
    
    -   **Wire Transfer Request Printing**
        
    -   **WHT Vendor Letter Printing**
        
    
    Proceed with the following steps if you use Sales Orders. In that case, you can also use a commercial or proforma invoice.
    
6.  Click the **Print Options** subtab.
    
7.  If you want to copy text from the PDF transaction, check the **Enable PDF Contents Extract** box. The layout option is solely for the logo definitions.
    
8.  If you want to remove or hide the signature line from the transaction layout, check the **Hide signature** box.
    
9.  If you use the **Delivery** note, select a template from the **Delivery Note Print Return Form** list.
    

#### To resolve printing issues:

1.  1.  Go to IL Settings.
        
    2.  Click **Edit**.
        
    3.  On the **Settings** tab, select the **Reset IL Company Information** box.
        
    4.  Click Save.
        

Important:

Ensure that all required fields in the Company Information are filled out to ensure the **Reset IL Company Information** works properly.

#### To update Israel Localization Settings:

1.  Go to _Customization > Lists, Records and Fields > Record Types_.
    
2.  Click **IL Settings** in the **Edit** column.
    
3.  Click the **More** button in upper-right corner and select **View Records**. IL Settings List page displays the IL Settings record.
    
4.  Click **View** on the IL Settings record.
    
5.  On the IL Settings page, verify that:
    
    -   The **Exchange Rate Service Url** field is populated with [https://illocalizationservice.azurewebsites.net/api/BOIExchangeRates](https://illocalizationservice.azurewebsites.net/api/BOIExchangeRates).
        
    -   The **Show ILS Amounts In Foreign Currency Invoice Print Layout (For ILS Subs)** box is checked.
        
6.  On the **Printing** subtab, verify that the **IL Localization Printing Type** field displays the **Advanced PDF Template** value.
    
7.  Go to the **Allotment Number Rules** subtab.
    
8.  In the **Minimum Amount for Allotment Number** field, enter value **999,999,999.00**.
    
9.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To set up transaction numbering:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Auto-Generated Numbers_.
    
2.  Check the **Use Subsidiary Column** box for all fields.
    
3.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Manage Accouting Periods_ to define or verify accounting periods.
    
4.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Manage Tax Periods_ to define or verify tax periods.
    

#### To define Israeli roles:

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_.
    
2.  Click **Customize** on the **IL Localization** record.
    
3.  In the **Name** field, enter **Custom IL Localization**.
    
4.  In the Subsidiary Restrictions section, go to the **Selected Subsidiaries** list and select the **IL Subsidiary** for the given role.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To assign Israel Localization forms to your Israeli roles:

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** on the **IL Localization Custom** role.
    
3.  Verify that the Israel Localization forms are checked.
    

Important:

You must use the Custom IL Localization role. It's strictly set up to use all IL Entity and IL Transaction forms (for example, IL Vendor, IL Bill Payment, IL Vendor Bill, etc.) included in the SuiteApp. Not using the suggested IL forms may result in issues.

If the suggested IL Entity or IL Transaction forms don't fit your needs, you can customize them to add your requirements. Do not remove any existing setup from the forms.
