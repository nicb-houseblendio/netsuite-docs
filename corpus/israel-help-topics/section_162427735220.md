With the Israel Localization SuiteApp, you can update custom preferences.

#### To update custom preferences:

The Legal name must be in Hebrew to meet the Israeli Tax authorities requirements, as this is reported as part of the tax reports.

The IL WHT results is limited by default to thousand results to simplify the process of submission to the tax authorities.

1.  Go to Setup > Company > General Preferences, and click the **Custom Preferences** subtab.
    
2.  In the section **Israel Localization for One World**, ensure that the **IL Vendor Name** list displays the value **Legal Name**.
