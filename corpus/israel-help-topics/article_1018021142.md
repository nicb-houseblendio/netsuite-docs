Setting up an account for an organization doing business in Israel usually requires enabling several features and installing some additional SuiteApps.

## Enabling Features {#bridgehead_77141248669}

Some features available in your NetSuite account can be important or even essential for users in Israel. If they're inactive, you should consider enabling them. Follow the instructions below to enable the most common features beneficial for users in Israel. However, if you want to get access to some specific product area, go to [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_4121410501.html#bridgehead_57134711584) help topic and check the exact requirements.

If you don't have sufficient permissions to enable features, please contact your account administrator.

#### To enable most common features: {#procedure_1018021627}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **Company** subtab, go to the **International** subtab, and check the **Multi-language** and **Multiple Currencies** boxes.
    
3.  On the **Accounting** subtab, go to the **Basic Features** section and check the **Accounting** box.
    
4.  (Optional) On the **Accounting** subtab, go to the **Basic Features** section and check the **GL Audit Numbering** box.
    
5.  (Optional) On the **Accounting** subtab, go to the **Advanced features** section and check the **Foreign Currency Variance Mapping** box.
    
6.  On the **Tax** subtab, check the **Advanced Taxes** box.
    
    Important:
    
    SuiteTax is not yet compatible with Israel Localization. If your account uses SuiteTax, you will not be able to install the Israel Localization SuiteApp.
    
7.  On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, enable the following features:
    
    -   In the **SuiteBuilder** section, enable the **Custom Records** and **Advanced PDF/HTML Templates** features.
        
    -   In the **SuiteScript** section, enable the **Client SuiteScript** and **Server SuiteScript** features.
        
    -   In the **SuiteGL** section, enable the **Custom GL Lines**, **Custom Transactions**, and **Custom Segments** features.
        
    -   In the **SuiteTalk** section, enable the **SOAP Web Services** feature.
        
    
    For more information about SuiteCloud features, see [SuiteCloud Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N239332.html).
    
8.  Click **Save**.
