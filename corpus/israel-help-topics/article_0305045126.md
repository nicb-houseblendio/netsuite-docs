The Israel Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp helps NetSuite customers in Israel with the certification of electronic documents (e-documents) for invoices and credit memos.

It generates e-documents and sends them for certification. After they are certified by the Israel Tax Authority, it adds the Allocation Number to the e-document and associated transaction.
