With the Israel Localization SuiteApp, you can define or verify VAT Tax Authority for your Israeli subsidiaries.

Important:

SuiteTax is not yet compatible with Israel Localization. If your account uses SuiteTax, you will not be able to install the Israel Localization SuiteApp.

#### To define or verify VAT Tax Authority for the Israeli subsidiary:

Note:

This is automatically defined during the SuiteApp installation but should be verified as the roles are set up.

1.  Go to _List > Relationships > Vendors_.
    
2.  Verify that the details displayed are correct.
    

Tax accounts are added automatically as part of the implementation. It's a good practice to verify that these have been implemented correctly before assigning user roles.

#### To define or verify VAT Accounts:

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Tax Control Accounts_.
    
2.  Verify that the following tax accounts have been created:
    
    -   **VAT on Sales IL**
        
    -   **VAT on Purchases IL**
        
    -   **VAT Liability IL**
        
    -   **Non-Claimable VAT IL**
        
3.  To add additional tax types, go to _Setup > Accounting > Tax Types_.
    
4.  Click **New Tax Type**.
    
5.  Fill in the required fields.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To define VAT Types:

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Tax Types_.
    
2.  Click the **New Tax Type** button.
    
3.  Select **Israel**.
    
4.  Enter the following information:
    
    -   **Name: ILAP**
        
    -   **Description: Israel**
        
    -   **Liability/Sales Tax Account:** blank/empty
        
    -   **Asset/Purchase Account: VAT on Purchase IL**
        
5.  Click **Save**.
    
6.  To create the next tax type, select **Israel** and enter the following information:
    
    -   **Name: ILAR**
        
    -   **Description: Israel**
        
    -   **Liability/Sales Tax Account: VAT on Sales IL**
        
    -   **Asset/Purchase Account:** blank/empty
        
7.  Click **Save**.
    
8.  To create the next tax type, select **Israel** and enter the following information:
    
    -   **Name: ILAPN**
        
    -   **Description: Israel**
        
    -   **Liability/Sales Tax Account:** blank/empty
        
    -   **Asset/Purchase Account: Non-Claimable VAT IL**
        
9.  Click **Save**.
