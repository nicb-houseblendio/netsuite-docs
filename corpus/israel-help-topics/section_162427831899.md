With the Israel Localization SuiteApp, you can set up a customer.

#### To set up customer for Israel Localization:

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Customers > New_.
    
2.  From the **Custom Form** list, select **IL Customer Form**.
    
3.  Enter the **Tax Registration Number**.
    
4.  On the **Address** subtab, enter at least one address located in Israel to ensure the invoicing process is working properly.
    
5.  On the **Preferences** subtab, select the preferences for the customer, including the billing language.
    
6.  On the **Financial** subtab, click the **Tax Registration** subtab:
    
    1.  From the **Country** list, select **Israel**.
        
    2.  Select the Default Tax Code.
        
7.  Click **Save**.
