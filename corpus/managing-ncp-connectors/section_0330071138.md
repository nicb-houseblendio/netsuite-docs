You need to add custom field access permission sets to the profiles of your Salesforce users that need access. This following custom permission sets are required:

-   NetSuite Connector Platform Field Access Permission Set - You need to assign this permission set to your Salesforce users so that they can:
    
    -   View and access NetSuite fields and custom objects added by NetSuite.
        
    -   Perform sync operations.
        
-   NetSuite Connector Platform List Apps Permission Set - You need to assign this permission set to your Salesforce users so that they can view consolidated NetSuite Connector-related flows in a new NetSuite Connector Sales app from App Launcher.
    

#### To add the required permission sets to Salesforce users:

1.  Log in to your Salesforce account.
    
2.  Add the permission set to your Salesforce users.
    
    1.  Go to Setup > Users > Permission Sets and click the Permissions set hyperlink of **NetSuite Connector Platform Field Access Permission Set**.
        
    2.  Click **Manage Assignments**.
        
    3.  Click **Add Assignments**.
        
    4.  Select the list of users who will be involved with the NetSuite Connector fields.
        
    5.  Click Next, then choose **No expiration date**.
        
        Note:
        
        Choose **No expiration date** unless you have a temporary access policy in Salesforce.
        
    6.  Click **Assign**.
        
    7.  Repeat the process for **NetSuite Connector Platform Lists Apps**.
        
3.  Click **Save**.
