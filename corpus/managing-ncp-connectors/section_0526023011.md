By default, Salesforce opportunities that are Closed Won are synced immediately to NetSuite. You can choose from the following Opportunity to Sales Order sync triggers:

-   Sync immediately when the opportunity is created
    
-   Sync depending on criteria defined.
    
    Note:
    
    When you select **Sync depending on criteria defined**, the criteria is set to when a specific opportunity is in Closed Won status by default.
    

If you want to apply the selected sync trigger to record updates, you need to check the **Apply rules for record update scenarios** box.

For more information about configuring sync triggers, see [Salesforce Connector Sync Triggers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1118052320.html).
