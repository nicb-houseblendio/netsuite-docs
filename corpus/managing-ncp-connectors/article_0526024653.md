The Salesforce Connector syncs financial information in NetSuite related to sales orders to Salesforce. The syncs ensure that the sales team has visibility into the status of sales orders.

<a id="svg_1"></a>

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     

There are several synchronizations that send financial data from NetSuite to Salesforce. The syncs documented in this section include:

-   [NetSuite Invoice Sync to Salesforce Financial Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0526024733.html)
    
-   [NetSuite Payment Sync to Salesforce Financial Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0526024903.html)
    
-   [NetSuite Cash Sale to Salesforce Cash Sale Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0526024951.html)
    

Important:

Financial records are created in Salesforce by these syncs, but any edits made to those Financial records in Salesforce are not synced back to NetSuite.

For Financial syncs to succeed, lookups must be defined in NetSuite to ensure that the syncs will be triggered as expected. For more information, see [Manage Lookups](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0602050717.html).
