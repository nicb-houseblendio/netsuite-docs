All prerequisites must be met before you can begin to sync information.

Ensure that the:

-   Salesforce Connector is set up correctly.
    
-   Connections are active.
    
-   Salesforce package is deployed.
    
-   Integrations have been activated.
    
-   Lookups are populated. See [Manage Lookups](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0602050717.html) for more information.
