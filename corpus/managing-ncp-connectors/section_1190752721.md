Important:

The [Spring '26 Release of Salesforce](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=005228017&type=1) disabled the creation of connected apps.

Select the Managed App choice as Managed Connected App in the Salesforce Connection step of the Setup Wizard.

In the Create Connection step of the setup wizard, select **Manage Connected App** and let Salesforce Connector create the connected app for you.
