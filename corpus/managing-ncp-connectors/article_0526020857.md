Use the Product sync to sync Salesforce product records to NetSuite item records.

Sales teams can make changes to Salesforce products, for example, creating different Price Books or changing product attributes. These changes are synced back to NetSuite to update inventory items. For example, changes to Salesforce Price Books are synced to NetSuite and update Price Levels.

Product sync shares the same mappings with Item sync. For more information, see:

-   [Standard Field Mappings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0526020156.html#subsect_0616040855)
    
-   [Custom Field Mappings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0526020156.html#subsect_0616040944)
