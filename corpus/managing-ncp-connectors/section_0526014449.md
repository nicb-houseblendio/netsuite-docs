For an Inventory Item sync, ensure that values are defined in NetSuite for the fields **Default COGS Account** and **Default Assets Account**. In NetSuite, go to _Setup > Accounting > Preferences > Accounting Preferences_ Click the Items/Transactions subtab to select the values for these fields.
