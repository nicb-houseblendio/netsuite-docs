The setup wizard adds the required permission sets to Salesforce but does not assign to users automatically. You need to manually add the required permission sets to the Salesforce users as needed.

The below permission sets are available in your Salesforce only after installation of the Salesforce Connector Managed Package. If you need to add these permissions before completing the setup wizard, you can manually install the managed package. To do so in NetSuite, go to Connector Platform > Overview > Quick Links then click **Install Salesforce Package**.

-   [Adding the Permission Sets to the Salesforce Integration User](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0330070717.html)
    
-   [Adding the Permissions Sets to the Salesforce Users](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0330071138.html)
