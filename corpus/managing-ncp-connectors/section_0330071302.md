When creating a new connection, the steps are Create, Upload, Deploy, and Activate. The steps are similar if you need to update connection: the steps in the UI are Update, Validate, Redeploy, and Reactivate.

#### To update the connection:

1.  Go to Salesforce Connector Platform > Settings > Connector Setup.
    
2.  Click **Edit Connection**.
