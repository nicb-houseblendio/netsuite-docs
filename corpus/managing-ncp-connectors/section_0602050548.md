To set or update preferences, in the NetSuite UI, click the Connector Platform tab, and go to Settings > Preferences.

The configurable settings for preferences are on several tabs on the Preferences page. For more information about Salesforce Connector preferences, see [Configure Salesforce Connector Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0325062748.html).

You can also configure the sync triggers on the Preferences page. For more information about configuring sync triggers, see [Salesforce Connector Sync Triggers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1118052320.html).
