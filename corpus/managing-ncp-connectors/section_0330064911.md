This step activates the Salesforce Connector integrations and can take several minutes to complete. When you start the activation, the screen is updated with an in progress banner message. The setup wizard doesn't automatically update and display a success message when the process completes. You need to periodically click **Refresh** until you get the success message.

When you get the success message, click **Finish** to complete the Salesforce Connector Setup Wizard.
