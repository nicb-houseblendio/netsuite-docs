You can use the Salesforce Flag to Post field to configure what to do with the record during sync. Salesforce Flag to Post can be set to the following values:

-   Blank - when left blank, the record will be skipped when posting to Salesforce.
    
-   Post to Salesforce - when selected, the record will be posted to Salesforce during sync.
    
-   Ignore future postings - when selected, any changes made on the record will be skipped when synced to Salesforce.
