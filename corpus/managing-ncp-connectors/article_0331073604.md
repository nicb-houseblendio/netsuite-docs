You can configure the mappings of the following Salesforce Connector syncs:

-   NetSuite Subsidiary Sync to Salesforce
    
-   NetSuite Customer to Salesforce Account Sync
    
-   Salesforce Account to NetSuite Customer Sync
    
-   NetSuite Contact to Salesforce Contact Sync
    
-   Salesforce Contact to NetSuite Contact Sync
    
-   Salesforce Opportunity to NetSuite Sales Order Sync
    
-   NetSuite Sales Order to Salesforce Order Sync
    
-   NetSuite Fulfillment to Salesforce Fulfillment Sync
    
-   Salesforce Product to NetSuite Item Sync
    
-   NetSuite Item to Salesforce Product Sync
    
-   NetSuite Invoice to Salesforce Financial Record Sync
    
-   NetSuite Payment to Salesforce Financial Record Sync
    
-   NetSuite Cash Sale to Salesforce Cash Sale Sync
