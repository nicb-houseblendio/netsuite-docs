The subsidiary is synced to Salesforce when the following conditions are met:

-   When creating a new subsidiary record in NetSuite.
    
-   When an existing subsidiary record is updated in NetSuite.
    

Important:

Subsidiary records that have been created before installing Salesforce Connector are only synced to Salesforce when they are updated in NetSuite. Edit existing subsidiaries after Salesforce Connector is installed to ensure that all subsidiaries are properly synced to Salesforce.

For successful Subsidiary syncs, you will see **Operation Succeeded** in NetSuite, and the sync status will be **Completed**.

If this sync fails, you will see an appropriate status message in both NetSuite and Salesforce.

By default, NetSuite subsidiaries are synced immediately to Salesforce when created or updated. You can choose from the following Subsidiary sync triggers:

-   Sync immediately when the subsidiary is created or updated (default)
    
-   Sync when the Salesforce Flag to Post field is selected on each subsidiary
    
-   Sync when a specific subsidiary field meets a defined criterion
    

For more information about configuring sync triggers, see [Salesforce Connector Sync Triggers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1118052320.html).
