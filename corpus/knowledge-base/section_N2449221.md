After you add solutions to a topic, you can rearrange their order on the topic record.

#### To order solutions on a topic record: {#procedure_N2449239}

1.  Go to _Cases > Customer Service > Topics_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the topic you want to reorder solutions for.
    
3.  Click the **Solutions** subtab.
    
4.  Click a solution to select it.
    
5.  Use the **Move to Top** and **Move to Bottom** buttons to set the order.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

When you click a topic in a published knowledge base, the solutions show in the order you selected.
