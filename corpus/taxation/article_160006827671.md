Tax Reporting Framework works in conjunction with other SuiteApps and localization SuiteApps to generate localized tax reports. Check the following table to know which SuiteApps you need to install to generate a specific country tax report.

This topic is for Tax Reporting Framework in accounts without SuiteTax. For accounts that have SuiteTax, see [Tax Reporting Framework Localization Requirements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_157303200001.html).

To know more about support for accounts without SuiteTax in Tax Reporting Framework, see [Tax Reporting Framework Support For Accounts Without SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_158676517011.html).

| Nexus | Country Tax Report | Required SuiteApp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Australia New Zealand | [Taxable Payments Annual Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159410857892.html) | [ANZ Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_158677153915.html) |
| France | [Fichier d'Ecritures Comptables (FEC) in France Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539840650.html) | [France Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157294233447.html) |
| Portugal | [Portugal SAF-T for Accounting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161822982183.html) [Portugal SAF-T for Billing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162970531160.html) | [Portugal Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161822794592.html) |
| Germany | [Germany Audit Files: GoBD Data Extract](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539585682.html) | [Installing Germany Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541684686.html) |
| Norway | [Norway Standard Audit File for Tax (SAF-T) Financial](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_43132133850.html) [Norway VAT Return](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164078694374.html) | [Norway Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159005737735.html) |
