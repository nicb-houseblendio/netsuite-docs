If you have a Romania subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your account, you can generate the Romania Value Added Tax Return. You can use the report as a source for filling out the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.

For more information, read the following Romania tax topics:

-   [Romania Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1978614.html)
    
    [Tax Code Table for Romania](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1978614.html#bridgehead_N1978673)
    
-   [Romania VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1979984.html)
    
-   [What goes into each box - Romania VAT report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157493110360.html)
