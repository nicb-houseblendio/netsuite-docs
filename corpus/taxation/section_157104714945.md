Note:

This topic is for NetSuite accounts that use the Country Tax Report page to generate the Intrastat Supplementary Declaration for Dispatches and Arrivals reports. If you are using the EU Intrastat Report page, see [Delivery Terms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2071234.html).

Delivery Terms or International Commercial Terms (Incoterm) is the trade terms defined by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC).

When you choose an EU vendor or customer on a transaction form, the Tax Reporting subtab displays the **Incoterm** field. You must fill in this field for it to be included on the Intrastat report.

The Intrastat Arrivals and Dispatches reports declare the Delivery Term or Incoterm through the following three-letter codes:

| Code | Delivery Term |
| --- | --- |
| CFR | Cost and freight |
| CIF | Cost, insurance and freight |
| CIP | Carriage and insurance paid to agreed destination |
| CPT | Carriage paid to agreed destination |
| DDP | Delivered duty paid |
| DAT | Delivered at terminal |
| DAP | Delivered at place |
| EXW | Ex works |
| FOB | Free on board |
| FAS | Free alongside ship |
| FCA | Free carrier |
| FOB | Free on board |
