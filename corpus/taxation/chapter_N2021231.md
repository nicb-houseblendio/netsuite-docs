The Turkish tax system levies value added tax on the supply and importation of goods and services. The Turkish name for Value Added Tax is Katma Değer Vergisi, abbreviated to KDV. VAT Returns are submitted monthly.

If you have a Türkiye subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the following reports:

-   VAT 1015A Return 1
    
-   Customs Declaration for Exports
    

The reports are in English and can be used as a source for filling in the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also generate sales and purchase reports by tax code.
