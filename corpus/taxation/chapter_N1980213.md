If you have a Serbia subsidiary and nexus and the International Tax Reports SuiteApp is installed in your account, you can generate the Serbia VAT Return Form PPPDV in English or Serbian. You can use the report as a source for filling out the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code.

For more information, refer to the following Serbia tax topics:

-   [Serbia Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1980322.html)
    
    [Tax Code Table for Serbia](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1980322.html#bridgehead_N1980381)
    
-   [Serbia VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1981048.html)
    
    [What goes into each box - Serbia VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4114524038.html)
    

For more information about the International Tax Reports SuiteApp, see [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html).
