If you have a South Korea subsidiary and nexus and have installed the International Tax Reports SuiteApp, you can generate the General Value-Added Tax Return. You can generate this return for South Korea in English or Korean. You can use these tax reports to submit to the National Tax Service of the Republic of Korea (South Korea). You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code.

Fore more information, read the following South Korea tax topics:

-   [South Korea Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1996096.html)
    
    [Tax Code Table for South Korea](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1996096.html#bridgehead_N1996151)
    
-   [South Korea VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1997044.html)
    
    [What goes into each box - South Korea VAT report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1997819.html)
