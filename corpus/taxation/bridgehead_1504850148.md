The International Tax Reports SuiteApp includes nature of transaction codes (NoTC) for Germany and United Kingdom. To set up your nature of transaction codes, see [Setting Up Nature of Transaction Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N2070762.html).

The following help topic includes the updated nature of transaction codes for Germany:

-   [Nature of Transaction Codes for Germany](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164439123518.html)
    

Refer to the following topics to set up the nature of transaction codes for Denmark and Finland:

-   [Nature of Transaction Codes for Denmark](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164379547336.html)
    
-   [Nature of Transaction Codes for Finland](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164379451309.html)
    

To create nature of transaction codes, see [Creating a Nature of Transaction Code](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164379251413.html).
