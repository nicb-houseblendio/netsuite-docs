If you have a Bulgaria subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your account, you can generate the Bulgaria VAT Report, in English or Bulgarian. To generate the report, go to Reports > VAT/GST > Tax Reports (International).

Important:

To generate the tax report for Bulgaria correctly, use the tax code properties provided by the International Tax Reports SuiteApp. See [Bulgaria Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1846495.html).

NetSuite gets the values in the report from your transactions, but you may need to enter some data manually. It's important that you save a PDF file of the report for your own records because NetSuite can't audit manually entered data. For information, see [Generating VAT/GST Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2064551.html).

You should review all the values in the report. You can click the values in the boxes of the VAT report to view drill down details. To understand how NetSuite uses the tax codes to get the values for the Bulgaria VAT report, see [What goes into each box - Bulgaria VAT report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1850889.html).

Important:

The NetSuite VAT/GST tax form is made to look like the official tax form for convenience. It's not intended to be printed and submitted to the tax agency.

Note:

You can also customize saved reports from the International Tax Reports SuiteApp to view both detail and summary reports for purchases or sales, grouped by tax code. For more information, see [Sales and Purchase Reports Grouped by Tax Code](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2067748.html). For EU sales list and Intrastat reports, see [EU Intrastat Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2069213.html).
