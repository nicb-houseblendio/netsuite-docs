The SuiteTax Engine supports assigning access, permissions, and permission levels to roles in NetSuite. Currently, the SuiteTax Engine defines permissions for standard roles. To customize roles, see [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html).

The following topics describe roles and permissions for the SuiteTax Engine in greater detail:

-   [Standard Roles and Default Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557734347.html)
    
-   [SuiteTax Engine Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557734578.html)
