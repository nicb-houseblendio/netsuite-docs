If you have a Viet Nam subsidiary and nexus and have installed the International Tax Reports SuiteApp, you can generate the Viet Nam VAT Return (Form 01 GTGT) in English or Vietnamese. You can also generate supplemental reports for output VAT/sales (Form 01-1) and input VAT/purchases (Form 01-2). You can use these reports as a source for filling in the official tax form to submit to the tax agency.

For more information, refer to the following Viet Nam tax topics:

-   [Viet Nam Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2047326.html)
    
    [Tax Code Table for Viet Nam](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2047326.html#bridgehead_N2047385)
    
-   [Viet Nam VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2048042.html)
    
    [What goes into each box - Viet Nam VAT report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2048375.html)
