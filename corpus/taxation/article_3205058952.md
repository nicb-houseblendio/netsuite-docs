SuiteTax Hub is a centralized page in NetSuite that provides access to key SuiteTax resources. It offers guidance on how SuiteTax works, how to prepare for the transition, and what to expect during the migration process.

## What You'll Find in the SuiteTax Hub {#subsect_0414085605}

-   **Overview** - The landing page for the hub. It includes quick links to commonly visited SuiteTax articles and resources.
    
-   **Differences with Legacy Tax** - A searchable comparison of features between Legacy Tax and SuiteTax to help you understand what will change and what may be required when you move to SuiteTax.
    

## How to Access the SuiteTax Hub {#subsect_0414085630}

Go to Setup > Accounting > Taxes > SuiteTax Hub.

Note:

Accessing the SuiteTax Hub does not convert your account to SuiteTax.

## Who Can Access the SuiteTax Hub {#subsect_0414085719}

The SuiteTax Hub is currently available to all Legacy Tax accounts. Availability for accounts already using SuiteTax will be introduced in upcoming releases later this year.

Enhancements and expanded capabilities will be added to the hub over time. Stay tuned for future updates.
