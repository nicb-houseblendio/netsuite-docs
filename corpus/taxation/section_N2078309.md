To free up space in your NetSuite File Cabinet, you should regularly delete tax audit files that you don't need anymore.

#### To delete a tax audit file: {#procedure_N2078321}

1.  Go to Reports > Tax > Audit Files.
    
2.  Click **Delete** next to the file you want to remove.
