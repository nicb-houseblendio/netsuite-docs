Note:

A customer is considered to be registered for VAT if the 'Customer Tax Reg. Number' field under the Tax Details subtab isn't empty. The Tax Details subtab appears on all taxable transactions. The values in the 'Customer Tax Reg. Number' list are sourced from the customer record.

Note:

A VAT union and an economic union are not the same.

A VAT union includes the countries or regions applying a shared VAT system as a single territory. For example, Switzerland and Liechtenstein form a VAT union. These countries apply the same VAT regime to the transactions between them, as if they were one territory.

An economic union is a group of countries that work together economically. These countries may allow free movement of goods, services, people, and capital, and they sometimes share a currency. If two or more countries form an economic union, they don't necessarily share a common VAT system. Each country in the economic union can still have its own VAT rules, rates, and reporting requirements.
