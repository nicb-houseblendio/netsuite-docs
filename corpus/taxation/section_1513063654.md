When you edit a transaction in SuiteTax, the recalculation of taxes depends on the changes to tax-related fields and on the **Tax Details Override** setting. The Tax Details Override setting isn't available if the nexus determined on the transaction is marked as tax-exempt. If you are editing a migrated transaction from legacy, the **Tax Details Override** setting is available to preserve the details generated by the legacy tax engine.

The following table shows when the tax engine is called to recalculate taxes when editing a transaction. These conditions apply to both migrated transactions and new transactions in SuiteTax.

| Editing a Transaction | Tax Details Override Setting | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No change in tax-related fields | Not checked | Tax engine isn't called. When the transaction is saved, the existing tax information about the **Tax Details** subtab is retained. If you click the **Preview Tax** button before saving the transaction, you will be asked if you want to recalculate taxes for the transaction even if tax-related fields were not changed. Click **OK** on the popup message to call the tax engine and recalculate taxes, or click **Cancel** to keep the existing tax information. |
| With changes in tax-related fields | Not checked | Tax lines on the **Tax Details** subtab are cleared. When you click the **Preview Tax** button or save the transaction, the tax engine is called to calculate the taxes. The previously cleared tax lines are replaced by the new tax details returned by the tax engine. |
| With or without changes in tax-related fields | Checked | Tax lines on the **Tax Details** subtab are retained, even if tax-related fields are changed. When the transaction is saved, the tax engine isn't notified about the changes and all the transaction form data are sent to the tax engine for reporting purposes. You can't preview the tax amounts if the **Tax Details Override** box is checked. |
