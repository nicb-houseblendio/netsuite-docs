The Uruguayan tax authority, DGI (Dirección General Impositiva) requires all legal entities in Uruguay that are subject to IVA (value added tax) tax law to present a monthly declaration of all credits and debits related to this tax. This declaration can be accomplished using the Sigma application, which is made available by DGI in its Web site.

The NetSuite International Tax Reports SuiteApp provides a report similar to the one that can be extracted from the Sigma application (forms 2176 and 2178), so it can be used as a source of information for accountants when filling in the official declaration form. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code.
