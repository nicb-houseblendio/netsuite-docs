You must meet the following prerequisites to use the One Stop Shop feature for SuiteTax:

-   The SuiteTax Engine SuiteApp is installed.
    
-   Your subsidiaries which will use OSS have the SuiteTax Engine.
    
-   These subsidiaries have a valid tax registration and nexus in the Member State of Identification they'll file OSS returns in. The customer can choose a different Member State of Identification per OSS scheme.
    
-   The SuiteTax Engine is installed as the tax engine for your Member State of Identification.
    

EU businesses that sell less than 10,000 EUR annually shouldn't use One Stop Shop.
