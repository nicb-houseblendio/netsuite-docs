With Tax Reporting Framework , you can generate the following supplementary VAT/GST reports:

-   [Annual Client Listing 725](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156775647909.html)
    
-   [Philippines VAT Report: Quarterly Summary Lists of Sales and Purchases](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_25151653746.html)
    

Note:

Tax Reporting Framework must be supplemented with a regional or country-specific localization SuiteApp to generate localized supplementary VAT/GST reports. Check the table on [Tax Reporting Framework Localization Requirements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_157303200001.html) for a list of required SuiteApps that you need to install.

To generate a report, see [Generating Localized Country Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528250223.html).

For information about the reporting features of Country Tax Reports, see [Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1528262949.html).

To install Tax Reporting Framework , see [Installing Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528245293.html).
