The Colombian tax authority, DIAN (Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales) requires all tax legal entities in Colombia that are subject to IVA (value added tax) tax law to present a declaration of all credits and debits related to this tax. This declaration can be accomplished through the official form number 300.

If you have a Colombia subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the VAT report for Colombia. The report is available in English and Spanish, and can be used as a source for filling in the official tax form for submission to the tax authority. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code.
