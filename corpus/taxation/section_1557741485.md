When you select the SuiteTax Engine for a subsidiary with a US nexus, NetSuite creates these tax records to generate tax codes:

-   tax agency
    
-   accounts to post tax on sales and purchase transactions
    
-   tax types
    
    -   State Tax
        
    -   County Tax
        
    -   City Tax
        
    -   District Tax
        
    -   Not Liable
        
    
    For each tax type except Not Liable, you'll need to set separate sales and use taxes.
    
-   Tax codes - all tax codes applicable for the selected nexus (that is all state, county, city and district taxes).
    
-   Tax groups - groups of tax codes for a certain area, including ZIP+4 information. For more information, see [Setting Up Tax Groups in SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1565954398.html)
    

Note:

Don't delete or inactivate system-generated tax accounts, because they'll be created again during monthly updates. You can rename them as needed, or create a new account and assign it to your tax types.

For OneWorld accounts, NetSuite sets up tax records automatically after you assign SuiteTax to the subsidiary's registration. For single instance accounts, you'll need to do it manually.

#### To set up tax records manually for single instance accounts:

1.  Go to Setup > SuiteTax Engine > Tax Engine Data Provisioning.
    
2.  Check the box for the nexus where you want to create tax codes.
    
3.  Click **Run Data Provisioning**.
    

Note:

To see all countries NetSuite supports for monthly tax rates updates, see [Countries Supported for Tax Rate Updates in the SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0723015941.html)

## Customizing Tax Codes View to Filter Based on State {#bridgehead_0823045952}

When you search for a tax code, you can sort by a specific US state by customizing your tax codes view.

#### To customize your tax codes view to filter based on state:

1.  Go to Setup > Tax > Tax Codes.
    
2.  In the top right corner, select **Customize View**.
    
3.  Select the **Available Filters** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Filter** column, select **STE Tax State (Custom)** and click **Add**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    
6.  You'll now see the **STE Tax State** field in the **Filters** menu. To filter by state, enter a two-letter state abbreviation in the field, like "AL" for Alabama.
    
    Note:
    
    To activate the **STE Tax State** field, click outside the field first.
    
7.  Click **Edit View** in the top right to edit your custom view.
    

## Tax Authorities {#subsect_1210112635}

All the tax codes automatically provisioned by the SuiteTax Engine have information regarding the **Imposing Tax Authority** and the **Reporting Tax Authority**. This information is visible under the **Tax Authority** subtab, in the corresponding fields.

You can provide this information for your created tax codes. You can fill this out under the **Tax Authority** subtab, in the corresponding fields.

Note:

Tax authorities aren't the same as tax agencies. They aren't connected in any way.
