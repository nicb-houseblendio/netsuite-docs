If you have a Slovenia subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your account, you can generate the Slovenia VAT Return in English or Slovenian. You can use the report as a source for filling out the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.

For more information, read the following Slovenia tax topics:

-   [Slovenia Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1992450.html)
    
    [Tax Code Table for Slovenia](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1992450.html#bridgehead_N1992508)
    
-   [Slovenia VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1993752.html)
    
    [What goes into each box - Slovenia VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4110092119.html)
    

For more information about the International Tax Reports SuiteApp, see [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html).
