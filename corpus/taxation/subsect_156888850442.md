Note:

This topic is for NetSuite accounts that use the Country Tax Report page to generate the European Community Sales List. If you are using the EU Sales List Report page, see [EU Sales List (ESL) Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2068052.html).

The European Community Sales List (EC Sales List) or also known in NetSuite as EU Sales List (ESL) is a report of your company's supplied goods and services to another EU member state.

When [Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1528262949.html) is supplemented with the [EMEA Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_157122959910.html) SuiteApp, you can generate the EC Sales List or Recapitulative Statement. To generate a report, see [Generating Localized Country Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528250223.html). For more information about the required SuiteApps to generate localized tax reports, see [Tax Reporting Framework Localization Requirements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_157303200001.html).

When you generate the EC Sales List or Recapitulative Statement, the Country Tax Report page shows the following information:

-   Your company's name
    
-   Your company's VAT number
    
-   Reporting period
    
-   List of VAT-registered companies with which you have transferred or sold goods and services
    
-   Page Total
    
-   Grand Total
    

The list of VAT-registered companies with which you have transferred or sold goods and services provides the following information:

| Report Detail | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Country | Customer's or buyer's 2-letter country code |
| Customer's or Buyer's VAT Number | Customer's or buyer's VAT registration number without the 2-letter country code |
| Customer or Buyer | Name of the customer or buyer |
| Value of Supplies | Total value of supplies of goods or services made in the reporting period |
| Indicator or SL/DG | Type of supply:
-   B2B Goods (or Goods)
-   Services (or Other services)
-   Triangular transaction

 |

The generated EC Sales List or Recapitulative Statement can be exported in a file format acceptable for online filing to the tax agency. To export the report, see [Exporting a Country Tax Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156645000208.html).

To check the list of EU countries supported by Tax Reporting Framework, see [EU Cross-Border Reports in Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156870639375.html).
