Tax Reporting Framework supports Multi-Book Accounting. You can choose which accounting book to use when generating your tax reports. This support is extended to both accounts with and without SuiteTax. For more information about multi-book accounting, see [Multi-Book Accounting Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_3831567542.html).

To use Adjustment-Only accounting books, only the book-specific journal entries and transactions posted in the base book and are reported in tax audit files. For more information on Adjustment-Only books, see [Adjustment-Only Books Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1546505267.html)

The following are the prerequisites when using Multi-Book Accounting and Adjustment-Only Book Support in Tax Reporting Framework:

-   Multi-Book Accounting feature is enabled. See [Enabling Multi-Book Accounting Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4842473026.html).
    
-   The country tax report to generate should support multi-book accounting.
    
-   To see other accounting books other than your **Primary Accounting Book**, you must have added other accounting books in your account. For more information, See [Adding Accounting Books](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4842479230.html).
