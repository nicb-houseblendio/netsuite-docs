To use the SuiteTax Engine, you must enable it as a plug-in after installation. This lets you select SuiteTax as your tax engine.

#### To enable the SuiteTax Plug-In

1.  Go to Customization > Plug-Ins > Manage Plug-Ins.
    
2.  On the Manage Plug-In Implementations page, check the **SuiteTax Engine** box.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

Warning:

If you want to uninstall the SuiteTax Engine, you must disable the SuiteTax plug-in first.
