To view tax reports by period, you must set the Report by Period preference to **All Reports** or **Financials Only**. Go to Home > Set Preferences, click the **Analytics** subtab, and then set the Report by Period preference.

If you use the Multiple Calendars feature, the dates for a report range are based on the tax period rollup in the context of the fiscal calendar assigned to the subsidiary.
