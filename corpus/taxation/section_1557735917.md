The SuiteTax Engine creates your tax records when you select SuiteTax as your tax engine. This is because SuiteTax gives you the flexibility to use different tax engines per nexus.

Important:

You must create a nexus before proceeding. In creating a nexus, you can leave the Tax Agency field blank, because the SuiteTax Engine provisions this record as a part of the tax records. For more information, see [Setting Up Nexuses in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4296038572.html).

To begin provisioning your tax records, assign a tax registration to the subsidiary or company in SuiteTax. See [Assigning Tax Registrations to a Subsidiary in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4296038273.html) for details.

To ensure that tax record provisioning for your subsidiary begins, you need to assign another SuiteTax Engine permission to your subsidiary. The permission name is SuiteScript Scheduling. Without this permission, the subsidiary can't be saved and tax record provisioning fails to begin.

Note:

Don't delete or inactivate system-generated tax accounts, as they'll be created again during the monthly update. You'll need to use another existing tax account or create a new account and assign it to your related tax types.

Note:

To view the full list of countries supported by NetSuite for monthly tax rates updates, see [Countries Supported for Tax Rate Updates in the SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0723015941.html)

#### To assign the SuiteScript Scheduling permission:

1.  Go to Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles.
    
2.  Click the **Customize** button next to the relevant role.
    
3.  On the **Permissions** subtab, click the **Setup** button.
    
4.  Select the **SuiteScript Scheduling** permission from the **Permission** list.
    
5.  Ensure that the permission level is set to **Full**.
    
6.  Click **Add**.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

By assigning SuiteTax as your tax engine for your subsidiary, the SuiteTax Engine creates the following tax records:

-   Vendor record for the Tax Agency
    
-   Accounts to post tax on sales and purchases
    
-   Nexus default tax codes
    
-   Tax Types
    
-   Tax Codes (if applicable):
    
    -   Standard
        
    -   Reduced
        
    -   Zero
        
    -   Exempt
        
    -   Reverse charge
        
    -   Reverse charge for sales
        

The records are based on the currently selected nexus.

Note:

Reverse tax codes don't contain any information about the tax rates. This information is stored in and obtained from their related non-reverse charge tax codes. For example, a rate of a standard-rated reverse charge code is obtained from the standard-rated tax code for the related country.

For more information about these records, see [Tax Records in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4797202133.html).

## Running Tax Record Provisioning Manually {#subsect_1559711289}

For OneWorld accounts, the tax records provisioning is executed automatically after you assign SuiteTax to the subsidiary's registration. For single instance accounts, you must run provisioning manually.

#### To run tax record provisioning manually for single instance edition accounts:

1.  Go to Setup > SuiteTax Engine > Tax Engine Data Provisioning.
    
2.  Check the box of the nexus for which you want to create tax codes.
    
3.  Click **Run Data Provisioning**.
    

## Tax Authorities {#subsect_30113253848}

All the tax codes automatically provisioned by the SuiteTax Engine have information regarding the **Imposing Tax Authority** and the **Reporting Tax Authority**. This information is visible under the **Tax Authority** subtab, in the corresponding fields.

You can provide this information for your created tax codes. You can fill this out under the **Tax Authority** subtab, in the corresponding fields.

Note:

Tax authorities aren't the same as tax agencies. They aren't connected in any way.
