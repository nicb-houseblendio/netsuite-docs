To generate an OSS VAT return in accounts with SuiteTax, you first need to set your reporting frequency preferences.

#### To set EU VAT One Stop Shop reporting frequency preferences: {#procedure_26164341631}

1.  Go to Setup > Tax Reporting > Country Tax Report Preferences.
    
2.  On the Country Tax Reports Preferences page, do the following:
    
    1.  From the **Subsidiary** list, select a subsidiary with OSS enabled.
        
    2.  From the **Nexus** list, select your OSS nexus.
        
3.  On the **Period Filters** subtab, from the **Reporting Frequency** list, select the reporting frequency for the applicable report.
    
4.  (Optional) If you want to use tax point date in your report, check the **Use Tax Point Date** box. For more information about tax point dates, see [Tax Point Dates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554806347.html).
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

The Start Period and End Period lists on the Country Tax Report page display values calculated automatically based on the Reporting Frequency preferences on the Country Tax Report Preferences page.
