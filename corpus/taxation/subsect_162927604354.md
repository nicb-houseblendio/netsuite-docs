To validate reported OSS transactions, it is possible to view a detailed report.

In accounts without SuiteTax, this is applicable to boxes 8.1, 10.1, 11.1, 8.2, 10.2, 10.2.1, 11.2 and 26. To view the detailed report, click the value of one of the previously mentioned boxes.

To view the detailed report in accounts with SuiteTax, click the value of **VAT Amount** in your Import OSS report.
