If you have a Hungary subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your account, you can generate the VAT Declaration Form 1165 and its attachments, in English or Magyar. You can use the report as a source for filling out the official tax form for submission to the tax agency in Hungary.

With NetSuite International Tax Reports, you can also generate sales and purchase reports by tax code.
