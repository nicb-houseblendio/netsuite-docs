Here are the key benefits that SuiteTax brings to your organization. You'll be able to experience these benefits regardless of the tax engine you choose to connect with SuiteTax.

| Key Benefit | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Designed with third-party integrations in mind.** | The introduction of the **SuiteTax API** enables NetSuite tax partners, as well as other third-party applications, to seamlessly integrate reliable and auditable processes into NetSuite. This is possible with or without taxes being calculated. |
| **Built using NetSuite's latest technology.** | SuiteTax is engineered to fit NetSuite's current platform and architecture direction, making it scalable and adaptable to future updates and innovations. |
| **Enables more accurate tracking and reporting of taxes.** | 
-   You can now set up multiple tax registrations for each subsidiary, along with its corresponding tax registration numbers, exemption status, and validity dates.
-   Nexus determination is enhanced. SuiteTax considers a broad range of addresses to ensure taxes are always calculated in the proper jurisdiction. Apart from the transaction shipping and billing addresses, the following addresses are also taken into account:
    -   location address
    -   subsidiary main address
    -   subsidiary shipping address

 |
| **Increases tax calculations precision by introducing new date-driven controls.** | 

-   Subsidiary tax registrations include validity dates to support time-bound compliance requirements.
-   **Tax Point Date** on transactions helps apply the correct rate and rules as they change over time.

 |
| **Supports non-standard tax scenarios with override options.** | For special or complex cases, SuiteTax allows controlled override on transactions through the following fields:

-   **Nexus Override**
-   **Tax Details Override**
-   **Tax Point Date Override**

 |
| **Supports multiple tax registrations on entity records.** | You can now assign multiple tax registrations on your customer and vendor records. This enables compliance with local, state, and international tax regulations where a single entity operates in multiple jurisdictions. |
