The **Enhanced Transaction Saving Notifications** feature ensures reporting and auditing compliance when creating or editing a transaction.

When you edit an existing transaction, SuiteTax may determine that a different tax engine is needed. For more information, see [Transactions in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4797202760.html)

For example, when you change the Ship To address in an existing transaction, NetSuite may determine it needs a new nexus. When you click the **Preview Tax** or **Save** buttons, the new tax registration is compared to the original one. If the new tax registration is assigned to a different tax engine, NetSuite checks that the new tax engine complies with reporting and auditing. NetSuite notifies the original tax engine, which either allows the change or blocks it. A blocking error occurs if the transaction has already been reported and can't be edited. You must click **Cancel** and exit the transaction.

If the previous tax engine processed any transactions in the past, NetSuite recommends not changing the tax engine provider. Other potential steps include:

-   Set 'Valid Until' on the current tax registration to the current date or a selected future date.
    
-   Delete any existing future transactions (you can also first export them) to set the 'Valid Until' date.
    
-   Set a new tax engine on the tax registration. don't overlap the 'Effective From' date.
    
-   Edit all future transactions to assign the correct tax engine to them.
