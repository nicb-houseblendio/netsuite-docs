You must enable the plug-in implementation in your account before you can test or use it.

#### To enable the Mexico UUID plug-in implementation:

1.  Go to Customization > Plug-ins > Manage Plug-ins.
    
2.  In the **Active Plug-in** field under MX UUID, select the Mexico UUID plug-in implementation that you created.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
