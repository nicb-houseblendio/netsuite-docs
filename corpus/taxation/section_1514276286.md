All tax-related fields on transactions are migrated. Unless the transactions for a particular period are migrated, the saved search or report may return incorrect values. Therefore, you should not run reports for periods in which not all transactions are migrated yet. To view the status of transaction migration, see [Viewing the SuiteTax Data Migration Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1513582777.html).

SuiteTax provides a standard tax report, which includes tax lines from new and migrated transactions. For more information, see [Generating a Standard Tax Report in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1497941633.html).

Localized reports that are available in legacy are no longer available in SuiteTax. The legacy tax information are exposed through the tax details on transactions. The tax engine reporting can use the tax details for reporting both legacy and SuiteTax transactions. For information about localized report solutions, consult your tax engine provider.
