If you have a Slovakia subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the Slovakia Value Added Tax Return in English or Slovak. You can use the report as a source for filling out the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.
