To create a tax group in SuiteTax, please see [Setting Up Tax Groups in SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1565954398.html).

To create a tax group with both sales/VAT tax codes and withholding tax codes, in the **Tax Code** column, select your Sales/VAT tax code and your withholding tax code.
