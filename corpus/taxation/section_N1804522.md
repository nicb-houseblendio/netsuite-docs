The following steps are for deleting a nexus from the system.

If you want to remove a nexus from a subsidiary, see [Adding or Removing Nexuses from a Subsidiary](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N273510.html).

Note:

Currently, the nexus with internal ID = 1 can't be deleted from the system.

#### To delete a tax nexus: {#procedure_N1804550}

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Taxes > Nexuses_.
    
2.  In the **Description** column, click the nexus that you want to delete.
    
3.  Click **Edit**.
    
4.  Point to Actions and then click **Delete**.
    

Important:

Be sure that there are no transactions associated with a nexus that you want to delete. If any transactions are associated with a nexus, then you must first delete those transactions or modify them.

If a nexus can't be deleted, you can inactivate it at _Setup > Accounting > Taxes > Nexuses_.
