The SuiteTax Engine includes a set of pre-defined formats for many countries; tax registration numbers and uses these to validate your tax registration number. For more information, see [Validating Tax Registrations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557742590.html).
