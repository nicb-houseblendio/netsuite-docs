Use the edit feature to apply changes to your tax codes.

#### To edit a tax code: {#procedure_N1807901}

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Taxes > Tax Codes_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the tax code.
    
3.  On the Tax Code page, make the necessary changes.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

For US sales taxes, you can import state tax tables to update the tax rates. When you upload state sales tax tables, outdated tax rates get replaced with new rates. For information about sales tax tables, see [Importing the State Sales Tax Table](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2041195.html).
