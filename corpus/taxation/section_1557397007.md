To install the SuiteTax Engine, you need to enable the SuiteTax feature in your account.

#### To install the SuiteTax Engine:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Enable Features.
    
2.  On the Tax subtab, check the SuiteTax box, and then click Save. For more information, see [General SuiteTax Topics](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4283850851.html).
    
3.  Additionally, on the SuiteCloud subtab enable the following features:
    
    -   Item Options
        
    -   Custom Records
        
    -   Advanced PDF/HTML Templates
        
    -   SuiteFlow
        
4.  Go to Customization > SuiteBundler > Search & Install Bundles.
    
5.  In the **Keywords** field, enter **SuiteTax Engine**.
    
6.  Click **Search**.
    
7.  Click the SuiteTax Engine bundle with **Bundle ID: 237702**.
    
8.  On the Bundle Details page, click **Install**.
    

The SuiteTax Engine SuiteApp is a managed bundle and is automatically updated whenever updates are available.

For more information about installing SuiteApps, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).

There are setup steps you must complete after installing this SuiteApp. See [Enabling the SuiteTax Plug-In](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557399475.html), [Setting Up the SuiteTax Engine for VAT Countries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557735706.html), and [Setting Up the SuiteTax Engine for the United States](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557741103.html).
