The Non-Union One Stop Shop Scheme applies to all B2C supplies of services taking place in the EU for sellers not established in the EU. Only sellers established outside the EU can use this scheme.

For more information about the Non-Union OSS Scheme, see [OSS](https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/business/vat/vat-e-commerce/oss_en) on the official European Commission website.
