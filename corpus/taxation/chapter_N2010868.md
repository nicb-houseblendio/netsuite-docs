If you have a Taiwan (Province of China) subsidiary and nexus and have installed the International Tax Reports SuiteApp, you can generate the Declaration of Sales and Business Tax by a Business Entity (401). You can generate this report for Taiwan (Province of China) in English or Chinese. You can use these tax reports for submission to the tax authority in Taiwan (Province of China). You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code.
