To apply sales tax to discount items, do the following:

1.  Understand the discount methods that can be applied to the items. For more information, see [Discount Methods (U.S. and Canada)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N1818089.html).
    
2.  Review the [Calculation Logic for Sales Tax on Discount Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N1818089.html#bridgehead_N1819957) to help you understand how the choice of discount methods can affect the tax charged for a sales order or invoice.
    
3.  Choose to apply the discount before or after sales tax has been added to the items. By default, discounts are applied after sales tax. See [Applying a Discount After Sales Tax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N1818089.html#bridgehead_N1821302). To apply a discount before sales tax, see [Applying a Discount Before Sales Tax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N1818089.html#bridgehead_4567397511).
