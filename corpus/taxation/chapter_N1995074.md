If you have a South Africa subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the South Africa VAT Return (VAT 201). The report can be used as a source for filling in the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code.

For more information, read the following South Africa tax topics:

-   [South Africa Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1995172.html)
    
    [Tax Code Table for South Africa](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1995172.html#bridgehead_N1995231)
    
-   [South Africa VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1995782.html)
    
    [What goes into each box - South Africa VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4114602006.html)
    

For more information about the International Tax Reports SuiteApp, see [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html).
