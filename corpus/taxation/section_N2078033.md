#### To download a tax audit file: {#procedure_N2078048}

1.  Generate a tax audit file. For information, see [Generating a Tax Audit File](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2077452.html).
    
2.  When a tax audit file is generating, you can check its progress in the Download column of the Audit Files table. Click **Refresh** to update the Download column. When it's done, you'll see a link to the file.
    
3.  You can find your generated files at Reports > Tax > Audit Files. Click the link in the Download column.
    
    Important:
    
    The maximum file size that you can download is 500 MB.
    
4.  In the download window, click **Save** to download the file to your computer.
    

Note:

The file size displayed by the system may not be the same as the definite file size.

Unless you deleted them, all your generated audit files are available at Reports > Tax > Audit Files. You can click the link in the Download column to download a file.
