If you have a Poland subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the Poland VAT Return Form (VAT-7) in English or Polski. You can use the report as a source for filling out the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.

For more information, read the following Poland tax topics:

-   [Poland Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1967757.html)
    
    [Tax Code Table for Poland](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1967757.html#bridgehead_N1967816)
    
-   [Poland VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1970414.html)
    
    [What goes into each box - Poland VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4037502030.html)
