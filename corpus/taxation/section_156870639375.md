With Tax Reporting Framework , you can generate EC Sales List (ESL) and Intrastat reports for the following nexuses:

-   Belgium
    
-   France
    
-   Germany
    
-   Netherlands
    
-   Sweden
    
-   United Kingdom
    

Note:

Tax Reporting Framework must be supplemented with a regional or country-specific localization SuiteApp to generate localized EU cross-border reports. Check the table on [Tax Reporting Framework Localization Requirements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_157303200001.html) for a list of required SuiteApps that you need to install.

The EC Sales List and Intrastat reports can be generated in English and the country's local language. It can also be exported in a format acceptable for online filing to the tax agency.

To generate an EC Sales List or Intrastat report, see [Generating Localized Country Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528250223.html).

To export the EC Sales List or Intrastat report, see [Exporting a Country Tax Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156645000208.html).

For more information about the contents of the generated EC Sales List and Intrastat reports, read the following topics:

-   [EC Sales List](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156888850442.html)
    
-   [Intrastat Supplementary Declaration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156889008497.html)
    

For information about the reporting features of Country Tax Reports, see [Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1528262949.html).

To install Tax Reporting Framework , see [Installing Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528245293.html).
