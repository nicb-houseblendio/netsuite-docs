In NetSuite, we have three native tax engines that support countries across different regions:

-   **SuiteTax LATAM Engine**\- this is the dedicated engine for Latin America. It covers Brazil, Chile, and Colombia.
    
    For more information, see [SuiteTax Latam Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161833403570.html).
    
-   **India Localization SuiteTax Engine**\- this specialized engine supports India's GST, TDS, and TCS requirements. For more information, see [India Localization SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543359686.html).
    
-   **SuiteTax Engine** - this engine covers major tax types globally, including US Sales & Use Tax, Canadian GST and Provincial Sales Tax (PST), and VAT. For more information, see [SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1557396965.html).
    

SuiteTax is designed for flexibility and choice. In addition to the tax engines we offer natively in NetSuite, users can also choose to connect to any of our SuiteTax tax partner solutions. For more information about partner tax engines, see [Partner Tax Engines](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1557396965.html#subsect_158081644095).
