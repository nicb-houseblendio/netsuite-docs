When you enable the SuiteTax feature, SuiteTax removes most of the tax fields on item records, except for the **Manufacturer Tax ID** field. With the tax engine, the fields that were removed are no longer needed for tax determination in SuiteTax.

| NetSuite Edition/Feature | Legacy Tax | SuiteTax | Migration to SuiteTax |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Field Name | Field ID | Field Name | Field ID |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Advanced Taxes | Tax Schedule | taxschedule | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| EU One Stop Shop (OSS) | OSS Applies | mossapplies | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| International and no Advanced Taxes | Prices Include Tax | pricesincludetax | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| US | Taxable | istaxable | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| OneWorld, US | Apply Before Sales Tax | ispretax | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| No Advanced Taxes | Tax Code | salestaxcode | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| No Advanced Taxes | Purchase Tax Code | purchasetaxcode | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| No Advanced Taxes (available only for some item types) | Tax Code | taxcode | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| Shipping Label Integration feature with International Shipping preference enabled | Manufacturer Tax ID | manufacturertaxid | Manufacturer Tax ID | manufacturertaxid | Retained. |
