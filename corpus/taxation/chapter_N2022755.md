If you have a Ukraine subsidiary and nexus and the International Tax Reports SuiteApp is installed in your account, you can generate the Ukraine VAT form, in English or Ukrainian. You can use the report as a source for filling out the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code.

For more information, read the following Ukraine tax topics:

-   [Ukraine Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2022864.html)
    
    [Tax Code Table for Ukraine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2022864.html#bridgehead_N2022923)
    
-   [Ukraine VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2025824.html)
    

For more information about the International Tax Reports SuiteApp, see [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html).
