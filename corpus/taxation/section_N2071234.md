Delivery terms refer to international commercial terms (Incoterms) defined by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC). These terms are updated regularly, so make sure you've got the latest Incoterms.

The International Tax Reports SuiteApp uses Delivery Terms for Intrastat reporting.

In NetSuite, you'll see the Delivery Terms field on the Tax Reporting subtab of sales and purchase transactions if your nexus is in an European Union (EU) member state. Examples of sales and purchase transactions where you can see this field are:

-   Estimate
    
-   Sales order
    
-   Invoice
    
-   Cash sale
    
-   Purchase order
    
-   Bill
    
-   Vendor return authorization
