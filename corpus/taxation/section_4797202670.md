When you enable the SuiteTax feature, SuiteTax adds the **Default Tax Reg.** field and the **Tax Registrations** subtab, and removes the other tax fields on the Financial subtab of entity records. In SuiteTax, you can record multiple tax registrations for an entity, which you can use on any transaction.

## Customer {#bridgehead_4811842104}

| NetSuite Edition | Legacy Tax | SuiteTax | Migration to SuiteTax |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Field Name | Field ID | Field Name | Field ID |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Financial: Tax Information section** |
| US | Taxable | taxable | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| CA | PST Exempt | taxexempt | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| JP, International | Tax Rounding Precision | taxfractionunit | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| All | Tax Item | taxitem | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| JP, International | Tax Rounding Method | taxrounding | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| US | Resale Number | resalenumber | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| International | Tax Reg. Number | vatregnumber | \- | \- | Removed from form. Field value is transferred to the new **Default Tax Reg.** and **Tax Reg. Number** fields in SuiteTax. |
| All | \- | \- | Default Tax Reg. | defaulttaxreg | Added to the form. Value from the **Tax Reg. Number** legacy field is transferred to this field. |
| **Financial: Tax Registrations (taxregistration) subtab** |
| All | \- | \- | \- | id | Added hidden field for tax registration ID. |
| All | \- | \- | Country | nexuscountry | Added to the form and set to blank. |
| All | \- | \- | Nexus | nexus | Added to the form and set to blank. |
| All | \- | \- | Address | address | Added to the form and set to blank. |
| All | \- | \- | Tax Reg. Number | taxregistrationnumber | Added to the form. Value from the **Tax Reg. Number** legacy field is transferred to this field. |
