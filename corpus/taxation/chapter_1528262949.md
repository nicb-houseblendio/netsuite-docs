**Tax Reporting Framework** is a SuiteApp developed by NetSuite. This SuiteApp is the tax reporting framework of all NetSuite accounts with SuiteTax, accounts without SuiteTax, and localization SuiteApps. It provides the permissions, setup options, and tax reporting features to country tax reports. When supplemented with a regional or country-specific localization SuiteApp, Tax Reporting Framework enables you to generate localized VAT/GST Returns, supplementary VAT/GST reports, EU cross-border reports, and tax audit files. Using this SuiteApp, you can also configure country tax reports for your nexus and submit it through electronic tax filing.

The Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp includes the following tax reporting features:

| Tax Reporting Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [Setting Up Country Tax Reporting Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1557127083.html) | Set up the tax report's start of tax year, reporting frequency, and period filter. |
| [Tax Reporting Framework Support For Accounts Without SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_158676517011.html) | A list of supported features in accounts without SuiteTax when using Tax Reporting Framework. |
| [Tax Reporting Framework Localization Requirements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_157303200001.html) | A list of required SuiteTax localization SuiteApps to generate localized tax reports that work with Tax Reporting Framework. |
| [Tax Reporting Framework Localization Requirements For Accounts Without SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160006827671.html) | A list of required localization SuiteApps to generate localized tax reports that work with Tax Reporting Framework in accounts without SuiteTax. |

Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp supports accounts with or without SuiteTax. To check the list of supported countries in accounts with SuiteTax, see [Country Tax Reports in Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156618598348.html). For supported countries in accounts without SuiteTax, see [Tax Reporting Framework Support For Accounts Without SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_158676517011.html).

To install Tax Reporting Framework, see [Installing Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528245293.html).
