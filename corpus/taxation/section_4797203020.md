When you enable the SuiteTax feature, SuiteTax removes the tax fields on shipping item records. With the tax engine, these fields are no longer needed for tax determination in SuiteTax.

| NetSuite Edition/Feature | Legacy Tax | SuiteTax | Migration to SuiteTax |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Field Name | Field ID | Field Name | Field ID |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Advanced Taxes | Charge tax on this shipping portion of item | taxschedule | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| Advanced Taxes | Charge tax on this handling portion of item | taxschedulehandling | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| US | Charge tax on this shipping portion of item | istaxable | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| US | Charge tax on this handling portion of item | ishandlingtaxable | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| Non-US, no Advanced Taxes | Charge tax on this shipping portion of item | shippingtaxcode | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
| Non-US, no Advanced Taxes | Charge tax on this handling portion of item | handlingtaxcode | \- | \- | Removed from form. |
