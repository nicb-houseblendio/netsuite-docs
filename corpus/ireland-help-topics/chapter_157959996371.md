## Overview {#bridgehead_157960036669}

The following help topics contain information about NetSuite features that are specific to companies doing business in Ireland. The NetSuite International Edition can be configured with capabilities and reports specific to an Irish business setting and requirements.

To know more about localized editions of NetSuite, as well as setting up NetSuite accounts and subsidiaries, refer to the following help topics:

-   [NetSuite Editions Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N270055.html)
    
-   [Set up NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N268052.html)
    
-   [Creating Subsidiary Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N272471.html)
    
-   [Introduction to NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N266468.html) - for companies using NetSuite across multiple subsidiaries and countries
    

Please note that general NetSuite features are covered in other chapters and topics.

## General Setup {#bridgehead_157960113990}

[Ireland Account Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157978380510.html): Provides an overview of NetSuite features to be enabled by the user in Ireland and SuiteApps available for installation.

[Setting Up Ireland-specific Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157978886598.html): Set up your NetSuite company and user preferences for Ireland, including date, currency, language, time zone, and support for Irish number, phone, and address format.
