If you have an Ireland subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the following tax reports for Ireland:

-   [Ireland VAT 3](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1929434.html#bridgehead_N1929589)
    
-   [Annual VAT Return of Trading Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1929434.html#bridgehead_N1929622)
    

The reports can be used as a source for filling in the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. For online filing, you can use NetSuite to generate an XML file of your Ireland VAT 3 Return. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.
