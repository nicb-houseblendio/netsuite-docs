The following table shows how NetSuite uses the tax codes to get the values for the Ireland Return of Trading Details.

Note:

The tax rates for boxes C5, AC5, and BC5 are sourced from the rates specified in the tax codes. Any modifications you make to these tax codes will be reflected in the generated report.
