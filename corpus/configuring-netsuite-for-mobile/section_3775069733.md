The NetSuite for iOS app uses the time zone from your NetSuite account. Calendar dates and schedules show up in that time zone, and the app ignores your phone's time zone settings. You can set your NetSuite account's time zone right from your mobile device.

#### To change your NetSuite account time zone from your mobile device: {#procedure_4120621584}

1.  Tap the Account Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Account Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_account_menu_icon_1.png).
    
2.  Tap **Time Zone**.
    
3.  (Optional) Tap **Notification** to enable or disable notifications.
    
    Note:
    
    When enabled, the app notifies you when your mobile device and your NetSuite account have different time zones.
    
4.  Tap **NetSuite** to display and select a time zone.
    
    Note:
    
    **Local** displays the time zone setting of your mobile device.
    
5.  Tap **Done** to save any changes to your time zone.
    

## Synchronizing Your Time Zone {#bridgehead_4120613282}

If you have notifications enabled, NetSuite for iOS notifies you when your mobile device and your NetSuite account have different time zones. After you log in a pop up window displays.

![Screenshot of the Different Time Zone Detected message](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_different_time_zone_alert.png)

Tap **Settings** to go to Time Zone settings. Adjust your time zone as needed, and then tap **Done**.

No further notifications display until there are new changes to the time zone.
