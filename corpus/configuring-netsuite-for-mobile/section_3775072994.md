NetSuite for iOS enables you to configure the settings for the app in several ways.

#### To change your app settings in NetSuite for iOS:

1.  Tap the Account Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Account Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_account_menu_icon_1.png). The Settings subtab contains the following options:
    
    -   **Remember Me** - Enables you to remove your login details. See [Removing your Login Details from NetSuite for Mobile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157857521373.html).
        
    -   **Time Zone** - Configure the time zone of your app. See [Setting the Time Zone in NetSuite for iOS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3775069733.html).
        
    -   **Phone Call Logging** - Configure phone call logging. See [Configuring Phone Call Logging in NetSuite for iOS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156163479756.html).
        
    -   **Receipt Image Resolution** - Enables you to se a maximum resolution of image attachments. See [Attaching Files To a Record in NetSuite for Mobile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158694201455.html).
        
    -   **Push Notifications** - Enable and configure push notifications.
        
    -   **Submit Feedback** - Share feedback and suggestions directly from the app.
        
    -   **About** - The About screen has info about the app and a link to contact support. It includes:
        
        -   **EULA** - access to read the EULA for the application.
            
        -   **Oracle Privacy Policy** - access to the Oracle Privacy Policy page.
            
        -   **App Version** - displays the version of the app you are using.
            
        -   **Server Version** - displays the version of the server the app is using.
            
        -   **User Login** - displays the username you are currently logged in with.
            
        -   **Account** - displays the account you are currently logged in with.
            
        -   **Role** - displays your current user role.
            
        -   **Documentation** - access to NetSuite for Mobile documentation.
            
        -   **Contact Support** - access to contacting customer support.
            
        -   **Visit SuiteAnswers** - a link to SuiteAnswers.
            
    -   **Log Out** - Tap to log out of the app.
        
    -   **Reset to Defaults** - Tap to perform a factory reset of the app.
        
        Note:
        
        When you reset the app to defaults, all your data including login information, expense details, and time logs are removed.
