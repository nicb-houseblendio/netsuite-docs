NetSuite for Android enables you to configure the settings for the app in several ways.

#### To change your app settings in NetSuite for Android:

1.  Tap the Navigation Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Navigation Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_menu_icon.png), and then tap **Settings**. The Settings screen contains the following options:
    
    -   **Remember Me** - Tap to remove your login details. See [Removing your Login Details from NetSuite for Mobile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157857521373.html).
        
    -   **Receipt Image Resolution** - Enables you to se a maximum resolution of image attachments. See [Attaching Files To a Record in NetSuite for Mobile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158694201455.html).
        
    -   **Favorites** - Configure your favorite record types.
        
    -   **Reset to Defaults** - Tap to perform a factory reset of the app.
        
        Note:
        
        When you reset the app to defaults, all your data-including login info, expense details, and time logs-gets removed.
        
2.  To share feedback and suggestions directly from the app, tap **Submit Feedback**.
    
3.  To open the About screen, tap the Navigation Menu icon, then tap About. The About screen has information about the app and a link to contact support. It includes:
    
    -   **Legal Terms** - access to read the EULA for the application.
        
    -   **Oracle Privacy Policy** - access to the Oracle Privacy Policy page.
        
    -   **App Version** - displays the version of the app you are using.
        
    -   **Server Version** - displays the version of the server the app is using.
        
    -   **User Login** - displays the username you are currently logged in with.
        
    -   **Account** - displays the account you are currently logged in with.
        
    -   **Role** - displays your current user role.
        
    -   **View Documentation** - access to NetSuite for Mobile documentation.
        
    -   **Contact Support** - access to contacting customer support.
        
    -   **Visit SuiteAnswers** - a link to SuiteAnswers.
