Note:

The Phone Call Logging feature is not available with Android devices.

Phone Call Logging lets you track all calls you make from NetSuite for iOS. It can also remind you to log your last call. After a call ends, a popup alert reminds you to log it.

#### To enable or disable phone call logging: {#procedure_4120644955}

1.  Tap the Account Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Account Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_account_menu_icon_1.png).
    
2.  Tap **Phone Call Logging**.
    
3.  Tap **Track Outbound Calls** to turn automatic phone call logging on or off.
    
4.  Tap Prompt to Log Call to enable the popup alert. This alert asks you to log calls after they end.
    
    When switched off, the calls are added to the Phone Calls portlet.
    
    When switched on, a popup reminder displays after a call ends prompting you to log the call. The alert provides two options:
    
    -   Tap **Choose Show Phone Calls** to log the call
        
    -   Tap **Not Now** to mark the call as unlogged in the Recent Calls portlet. See [NetSuite for Mobile Dashboards](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1553858494.html).
        
5.  To return to My Dashboard, tap the Back Arrow icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Back Arrow icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_back_arrow_icon.png), and then tap **Done**.
