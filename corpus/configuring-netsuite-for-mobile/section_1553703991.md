The app uses the same date, time, and number formats as your device. If you're changing time zones (like when you're traveling), make sure your browser's time zone matches your device.

Time zone switching from within the app is only available with iOS devices:

-   **For iOS**, go to Account Menu > Time Zone. See [Setting the Time Zone in NetSuite for iOS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3775069733.html).
    

Note:

**Android** doesn't support time zone switching from within the app
