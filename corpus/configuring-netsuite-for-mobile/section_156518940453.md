The system requirements for iOS and Android devices are as follows:

-   NetSuite for iOS works on any device with iOS 18 or higher, but it's mainly designed for iPhone.
    
-   NetSuite for Android works on any Android device running Android 11 or higher.
    

Note:

What you need to know:

-   The app requires internet connection to access data from NetSuite
    
-   NetSuite for Mobile doesn't work on rooted or jailbroken devices.
    
-   Only the latest versions of NetSuite for iOS and Android are supported. Make sure you've installed the latest version on your device.
