You can download a log of the Copy to Account deployment as a text-formatted LOG file.

#### To view a Copy to Account log:

1.  From the target account, go to _Customization > SuiteCloud Development > Deployment Audit Trail_. You can find the LOG file under the name you entered as the Deployment Audit Trail Name in the Selecting a Target Account step. For more information, see [Selecting a Target Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1548372307.html).
    
2.  Click **Download Log**.
