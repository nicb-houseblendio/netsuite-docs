The SuiteCloud platform lets you both adapt NetSuite for your organization and create customization packages that you can share with others. With the SuiteCloud Platform, you can do the following:

-   Customize the NetSuite UI and business logic with scripts or by using point-and-click tools in the UI.
    
-   Find and analyze information in NetSuite.
    
-   Bring information from other systems into your NetSuite accounts.
    
-   Create custom roles so users access only the tasks and pages they need for their jobs.
    
-   Share your customizations with other accounts.
    

To view videos about creating custom objects using SuiteCloud platform, see the [SuiteCloud](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_65114903717.html) videos page.

For more information about the SuiteCloud platform tools, see [SuiteCloud Platform Customization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1529088392.html) and [SuiteCloud Reference Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1527577831.html).
