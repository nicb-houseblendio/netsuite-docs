## SAFE Guide {#subsect_1568818300}

The SuiteApp Architectural Fundamentals & Examples Guide (**SAFE**) is intended for independent software vendors. These vendors want to use the NetSuite platform and tools to build custom SuiteApp solutions for customer accounts. This guide is available to members of the SuiteCloud Developer Network (SDN) and provides best practices for NetSuite customizations. Some of the guide content is available in SuiteAnswers article 46388, [SuiteApp Architectural Fundamentals & Examples (SAFE)](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1011960).
