Users with roles designated as two-factor authentication roles can use their passkey for the 2FA challenge during the login process to NetSuite.

For more information, see _Passkeys as 2FA_, and [Designate Two-Factor Authentication Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N328126.html)

For more information about 2FA with passkeys available to users, see [Logging In Using Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1493300400.html).
