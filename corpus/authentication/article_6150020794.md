Passkeys provide a passwordless authentication, an alternative to a password. All users in all roles in your account can use a passkey instead of a password during login process to NetSuite.

There are two types of passkeys:

-   **Physical passkeys** - A piece of hardware, for example portable USB tokens.
    
-   **Platform-bound passkeys** - Often provided by biometrics, for example a fingerprint or Face ID that's stored on a device, such as a phone or laptop.
    
-   **Synced passkeys** - Applications like the Google Password Manager, or iCloud Keychain that allow using saved credentials on different devices.
    

Important:

When creating a new passkey, always use a FIDO-certified option. Passkeys that are not certified can provide a risk for your NetSuite account, because they may not be resistant to potential attackers.

Consider choosing passkeys that comply with FIDO2 specification.

When you successfully create a passkey, you can use it the next time you log in to NetSuite. You still need to provide your email address.

All users in your NetSuite account must still remember their password, because passkeys are only available for the login process. Password is still necessary for the self-service 2FA reset.

For more information about passkey for users, see [Passkeys](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0624120652.html).
