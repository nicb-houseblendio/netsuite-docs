There are several ways to remove OpenID Connect access to NetSuite.

-   Either of the following actions removes OpenID Connect access to NetSuite for a user or group of users in your account:
    
    -   Remove the OpenID Connect (OIDC) Single Sign-on permission from the users' roles.
        
    -   Edit the users' employee records in NetSuite to make a user or users inactive.
        
-   Disable the OpenID Connect (OIDC) Single Sign-on feature in NetSuite removes OIDC access to NEtSuite for all users in your account.
