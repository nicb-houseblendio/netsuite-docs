Users with the Administrator role can set up the login notification in their account.

You must first enable the Login Notification feature.

#### To enable the login notification feature:

1.  Go to Company > Enable Features,
    
2.  Click the Company tab,
    
3.  Scroll down to the Access section,
    
4.  Check the **Login Notification** box,
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

After you enable the feature, you can set up the notification.

#### To set up the login notification:

1.  Go to Company > Company Information,
    
2.  Click the Login Notification tab,
    
3.  Enter your message,
    
    Note:
    
    The text field allows simple formatting, like bold, or italics. It doesn't support links, or images. The field doesn't have a maximum number of characters.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Next time anyone logs in to your account, they'll see the login notification on top of the page. Users must click the **Acknowledge** button to get into the account. NetSuite tracks every acknowledgment in the Login Audit Trail.
