The format of the URL is:

`https://<accountID>.restlets.api.netsuite.com/services/rest/auth/oauth2/v1/clients/<ClientID>/certificates/<CertificateID>/revoke`

where <accountID> represents your NetSuite account ID, <ClientID> represents the ID associated with the integration record you want to work with, and <CertificateID> represents the ID of the certificate you want to revoke.
