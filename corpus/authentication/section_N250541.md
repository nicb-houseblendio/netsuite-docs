For NetSuite users who log in with a non-customer center role, password validation combines the following:

-   Account settings that administrators can modify. See [Password Settings That Can Be Modified](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1534873888.html).
    
-   System requirements that cannot be modified. See [System-Defined Password Requirements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1534872487.html).
    
-   PCI DSS requirements that apply to users with either the View Unencrypted Credit Cards permission or the View Unencrypted ACH Account Numbers permission. See [PCI Compliance Password Requirements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3448313.html).
    

Note:

Users are locked out for 30 minutes after five consecutive attempts to log in to NetSuite with an incorrect password. For more information, see [User Access Reset Tool](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156442554498.html).
