As of 2026.1, only users with 2FA-protected roles can keep up to three active NetSuite sessions simultaneously. Also, a user with the Administrator role must first enable the feature. If you don't enable the feature, no user in your account can use more than one NetSuite session.

#### To enable the Multiple Sessions for 2FA-protected Users feature:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Enable Features,
    
2.  Click the Company tab,
    
3.  Scroll down to the Access section,
    
4.  Check the **Multiple Sessions for 2FA-protected Users** box,
    
5.  Click **Save**.
