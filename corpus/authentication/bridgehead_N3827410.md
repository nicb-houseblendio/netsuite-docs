You can add the SAML Single Sign-on permission to custom versions of these center roles: Customer Center, Employee Center, Vendor Center, Partner Center, and Advanced Partner Center. Center roles are different from other NetSuite roles because you can only add a limited set of permissions to them.

Important:

No special permission is required to grant a customer center role SAML access to a website. The SAML permission is enabled for all customer center users, after the SAML setup for the website is completed. For more information about center roles access, see the following: [SAML Single Sign-on Access to Web Store](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3940510119.html).

#### To add the SAML Single Sign-on permission to a customized center role:

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > User Management > Manage Roles_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** for a customized center role or click **Customize** for a standard center role.
    
3.  On the Role page, click the **Permissions** subtab.
    
4.  On the **Setup** subtab, set the **Level** to **Full** for the SAML Single Sign-on permission.
