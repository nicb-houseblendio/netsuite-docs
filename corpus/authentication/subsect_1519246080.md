### When I access my NetSuite production account through SAML SSO, can I switch roles to access my SAML role in a sandbox account? {#question_1541169747}

It depends on how your SAML is set up, and if the account ID is specified. See [SAML SSO in Multiple NetSuite Account Types](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4430963939.html) for more information.

### When I access my NetSuite production account through SAML SSO, can I switch roles to access my non-SAML roles in my production or sandbox accounts? {#question_1541169762}

No. It is not possible to access SAML roles and non-SAML roles in the same session.

### When I log in to my NetSuite production account in a non-SAML role, can I switch roles to other non-SAML roles in my production or sandbox accounts? {#question_1541170128}

Yes.
