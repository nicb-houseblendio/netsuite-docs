HTML attachments of inbound emails render as plain text files to ensure they are secure and don't harm your NetSuite account data. To view the attachment in a readable format, you must download the file first.

#### To view the HTML file attachment: {#procedure_1557239032}

1.  Click the **Communications** subtab on the case record.
    
2.  Click the **Messages** subtab.
    
3.  Click **View** next to the email message, and then click the **Attachments** subtab.
    
4.  Click **Download** next to the HTML file that is rendered as plain text, and save it to your computer.
    
5.  Find the file on your computer. It opens as an HTML document.
