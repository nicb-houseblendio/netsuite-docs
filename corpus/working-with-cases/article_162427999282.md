Existing messages appear in the Messages section of the **Communication** subtab on the case record.

You can forward a message from a case record to another recipient. The text and heading of the original message are added below your message. When you forward the message, it isn't saved in NetSuite and it isn't recorded in the case record. Forwarded messages don't have a reply to address.

#### To reply to existing case messages:

1.  Click **View** for a message to open it in a new window.
    
    In this message you can manage recipients, read receipts and view attachments.
    
2.  At the top of the form, you can **Reply**, **Reply to All**, or **Forward** a message.
    
3.  Click the **Recipients** subtab to add recipients to the email.
    
    You can also add blind carbon copy (BCC) recipients to email sent from a support case record.
    
    1.  Enter an email address in the **Email** column.
        
    2.  Check the box in the **BCC** column.
        
    3.  Click **Add** or **OK**.
        
        Replies from **BCC** recipients are marked **Internal Only**.
        
4.  Click the **Message** subtab to read the existing message.
    
    You can reply to a message on a case record. Your reply is recorded in the message history on the case record.
    
5.  Click the **Attachments** subtab to add attachments to the email.
    
6.  When you're ready to send your email, click **Merge & Send**.
