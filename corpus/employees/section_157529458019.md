With the Performance Management feature, you can create and schedule performance reviews for employees using templates. Performance review templates include questions and an overall rating. Reviews also show the goals worked on during the review period.

Performance reviews show any goals worked on and Kudos received during the review period. Employees and managers can update the employee's area of focus right from the review to help plan for future development.

Learn more in the following topics:

-   [Performance Review Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157590962053.html) - You can set up review questions and rating scales, create templates, and launch reviews for groups or individual employees.
    
-   [Performance Review Process](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157590971891.html) - Employees complete self reviews, managers complete their reviews, HR approves them, and then employees finalize everything. Employees and managers get an email when they need to take action.
    
-   [Performance Review Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161428788168.html) - Default saved searches provide you with information about performance reviews in your organization.
    
-   [Performance Management Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161185085251.html) - You can use performance management datasets and workbooks to analyze goal and performance review information for employees.
