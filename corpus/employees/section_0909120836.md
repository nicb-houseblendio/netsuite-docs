Shift reports within WFM enable you to inform employees and management about important aspects of a week. Different reports are customized for different purposes, such as the " Report" which is a weekly roster including wages and KPI information. This report can be used by management to keep track of important statistics.

It's super simple to create your own custom reports, or edit an existing report!

Ensure you are logged in as a General Manager or Franchisee.

It's important to remember that there is a big difference between weekly reports and daily reports.

## The Different Types of Reports {#subsect_0319114315}

-   [Standard Week Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0909121426.html)
    
-   [Standard Day Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_8134853991.html)
    
-   [Editing Day Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_63135225287.html)
    
-   [Editing Week Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_2134610911.html)
    
-   [Restoring or Deactivating Custom Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_2135623805.html)
    

Several of the Reports cannot be changed, but you are able to create copies that you are then able to edit in a way that simplifies management over the employees.

To access the shift reports go to **Setup > Shift Reports**. The different types of reports or listed on the left, click on the one you want to view.
