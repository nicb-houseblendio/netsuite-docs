Changes made to a template won't take effect on existing performance reviews that use the template.

#### To edit a performance review template:

1.  Go to _Setup > Performance Management > Templates_.
    
2.  Beside a performance review template, click **Edit**.
    
3.  When you finish editing, click **Save**.
