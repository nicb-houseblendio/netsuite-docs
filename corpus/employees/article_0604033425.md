With the Enhanced Timesheets Using Workforce Management Wage Rules feature, employees can enter time in weekly timesheets, preview a breakdown of their hours, and send those hours for approval.

The feature automatically calculates overtime hours based on the employees' entries and the WFM wage rules enabled in the account.

By enabling this feature, organizations can save time, reduce errors, and ensure payroll accuracy with minimal manual effort.

The person in the company who manages WFM and NetSuite, such as a manager or bookkeeper, can configure the time entry options available to employees.

Important:

If your employees mostly use weekly timesheets to enter time, you should enable this feature for quicker overtime calculations.

If your organization uses WFM mobile apps to capture time, your employees shouldn't manually enter time in weekly timesheets to avoid discrepancies.

## Employee Type {#subsect_0812073537}

This feature is applicable for **nonexempt employees** who must be paid overtime work, most commonly employees who need to enter work hours and are paid hourly wages.

## Benefits of Enhanced Timesheets Using WFM {#subsect_0812073547}

When enabled, the Enhanced Timesheets Using Workforce Management Wage Rules feature provides the following benefits:

-   HR Managers or similar roles
    
    -   Use the **Require Employees to Enter Shift Start and End Times on Timesheets** preference to ensure that employees enter complete shift details. For more information, see [Configuring Enhanced Timesheets Using WFM](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0812083043.html).
        
    -   To aid when approving timesheets, view a breakdown of each employee's total regular and overtime hours for the week. See [Approving Hours in Enhanced Timesheets Using WFM](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0812065936.html).
        
-   Employees
    
    -   When entering time, either enter the shift and break times or enter the number of hours worked. See [Entering Time in Enhanced Timesheets Using WFM](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0812065926.html).
        
    -   The feature automatically calculates regular and overtime hours based on the shift and break times you provide. You don't need to create separate entries for overtime hours.
        
    -   View a graphical display of your total weekly hours color-coded between regular and overtime hours.
        
-   Bookkeepers, payroll managers, or similar roles
    
    Timesheet entries and approvals using WFM wage rules are integrated with SuitePeople U.S. Payroll. This process streamlines payroll processing and minimizes the need for manual entry of overtime pay.
    
    Important:
    
    The Enhanced Timesheets Using Workforce Management Wage Rules feature is not available for integration with third-party payroll systems.
