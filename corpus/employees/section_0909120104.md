WFM can send you notifications when an employee's details are changed.

To view your notifications click on the envelope at the top right corner of your screen.

## Setting Up Personal Details Notifications {#subsect_19134145243}

Setting up a notification system to remind you, or any other managers about any change to an employee's details is a simple process. You will need to go to **Setup > Notifications**, and then click **Personal details**.

The first step is to choose from the notification options.

You can then choose which managers will be informed about a change to an employee's details.

Hit "save" to continue.
