You can update your submitted time by retracting timesheets before your supervisor approves them. You can only retract timesheets if they're pending approval. You can't retract time transactions with any other status. The Retract button is not available in edit mode for timesheets or time transactions. Retracting timesheets is not available when you use custom approval routing.

#### To retract weekly timesheets:

1.  Go to _Transactions > Employees > Weekly Timesheet_.
    
2.  Click **View** next to the timesheet you want to retract.
    
    Tip:
    
    You can also [Retracting Individual Time Entries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161529454693.html), instead of the whole weekly timesheet.
    
3.  Click **Retract**.
    
    NetSuite sends an e-mail to notify your supervisor of the retraction. NetSuite also updates the status of all pending time entries to Open, and the supervisor can't see any of the previously pending time entries.
    
4.  Make your updates.
    
5.  Click **Submit**.
    
    NetSuite resubmits the individual time entries.
