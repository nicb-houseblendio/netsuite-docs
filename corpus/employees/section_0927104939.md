In some situations, you may need to view total hours worked throughout a week, including hours across multiple locations.

For example, scheduling employees and making sure they're working less than X hours per week, even across different locations.

You can view those shifts on the **Multi Location** screen.

1.  When you've selected a particular week & location to view, click on **Options**.
    
2.  In the Options dialog select **All shifts for staff** and make sure it says **Yes**
    

If there is a manager that can only view one location, but has an employee that works across multiple, any extra hours or shifts which an employee works will appear here.
