Deploy user event scripts from the installed WFM SuiteApp to your account.

#### To deploy WFM user event scripts: {#procedure_21225548851}

1.  As an Administrator, go to Customization > Scripting > Scripts.
    
2.  Expand the filter options. From the **Type** list, select **User Event**.
    
3.  Click **View** beside **SuitePeople WFM Webhook**.
    
4.  Click the **Deployments** subtab.
    
5.  Under the **Applies To** column, click on the **Employee** record.
    
6.  Click **Edit**.
    
7.  Check the **Deployed** box.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
    
9.  For the **Location** record, repeat steps 5 to 8.
