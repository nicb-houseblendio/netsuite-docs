NetSuite provides you with the tools that you need to manage your employees. You can:

-   Add employee records to NetSuite. For more information, see [Employee Information Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N894090.html).
    
-   Give employees access to NetSuite. For more information, see [Giving an Employee Access to NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N896195.html).
    
-   Log employee expenses and purchase requests. For more information, see [Expense Reports and Purchase Requests](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1494449902.html).
    
-   Track employees' time with the Time Tracking feature. For more information, see [Managing Time Tracking](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N901953.html).
    

NetSuite also offers SuitePeople, a comprehensive and integrated Human Capital Management (HCM) solution. SuitePeople enables Human Resources professionals to manage employees within NetSuite, thus eliminating repetitive and manual processes with one source of where employee data is kept.

For more information, see [SuitePeople Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1495573671.html). For more information about SuitePeople, contact your NetSuite account manager.
