You can delete a performance review only when its status is Launched or Canceled. After employees save or submit their responses, you can't delete the performance review, but you can cancel it. For more information about canceling a review, see [Canceling Performance Reviews](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157799122952.html).

#### To delete a performance review:

1.  Go to _Setup > Performance Management > Performance Reviews_.
    
2.  Next to the performance review, click **Edit**.
    
3.  From the **Actions** list, select **Delete**.
    
4.  When asked to confirm, click **OK**.
