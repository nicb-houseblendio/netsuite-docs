Important:

Starting **August 5, 2025**, SuitePeople Workforce Management apps that are not using the latest version will automatically be updated.

To safely upgrade to the latest version and avoid interruptions later, complete required actions before August 5, 2025.

For more information, see [Action Required: Upcoming SuitePeople Workforce Management Mobile App Version Release and Automatic Update](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1020938).

When you publish your schedule and employees receive their shifts, they need to confirm the shifts they can work if you enable shift confirmation.

To confirm a published shift, the employee clicks the grey arrow next to their shift in the SuitePeople Workforce Management phone app. The grey arrow turns green and a green check mark appears on the scheduled shift in the weekly scheduling view on the manager's end.
