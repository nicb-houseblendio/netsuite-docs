You can view a report containing a history of login sessions in Workforce Management (WFM) across various sources.

To access the page, go to Workforce Management. From the upper right corner of the screen, click the user icon. Click **Login history**.

The page displays the most recent sessions first.

The page contains the following information:

-   Source
    
-   Logged in at
    
-   Logged out at
    
-   IP address
    
-   Session status
