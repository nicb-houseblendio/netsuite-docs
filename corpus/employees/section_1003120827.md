Follow the procedure below.

#### To link a location and job to ADP (US) in WFM:

1.  Go to Workforce Management > Setup > Integrations > ADP (US).
    
2.  Click **Location + Job**.
    
3.  Find the location and job combination you want to link.
    
    -   In the Search field, enter the name of the location or job.
        
    -   From the list beside the Search field, filter to view either:
        
        -   **All** - Shows all location and job combinations.
            
        -   **Unlinked** - Shows only unlinked combinations.
            
        -   **Linked** - Shows only linked combinations.
            
4.  In the **ADP cost code** column of the location, enter the code.
    
    This code may have up to 50 characters.
    
5.  (Optional) To unlink a combination from a cost code, click **Unlink** on the corresponding row.
