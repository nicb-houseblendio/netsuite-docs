You can go to this page by clicking **Shifts > Daily**.

## Getting around the page {#subsect_92165412070}

At the top of the page, you can see a drop-down box labeled "schedule day". This is your typical schedule day, starting and finishing at your open times.

There are more viewing options when you click this box. These are:

-   Schedule day
    
-   Day
    
-   Schedule week
    
-   Week
    
-   Custom
    

You can go throughout the days by using the date picker at the top of the page or the arrows at the bottom left and right-hand side

You can move between schedules by selecting the location name from the next drop-down box.

Stats panel on the right - your projections and targets will appear here, along with your staff wages, and your relevant KPIs.
