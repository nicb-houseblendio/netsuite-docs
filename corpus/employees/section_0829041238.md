WFM has a notification system that is able to inform you when an employee has expiring documents.

A notification will appear on the top right corner of your screen, click the icon to view it.

## Setting Up Expiring Documents Notifications {#subsect_30133211138}

To set up an automatic notification system for an employee's expiring documents you need to go to **Setup > Notifications**. From here, you can go to the "Expiring Document" tab.

From here you have a range of optional settings to set. The first option is whether you would like WFM to send you an automatic reminder, choose this option if you want a reminder for an employee's expiring document **(it is still important to complete the rest of the setup page as this information may still be applicable to managers)**.

You will then be given the option to choose how many days before the employee's documents expire to receive a notification, as well as an option to send a reminder on the day.

The last section focuses on which managers will be informed of the expiring documents; you firstly have the option to make it so that only the managers that have primary control over the employee will receive a notification. If you want other employees to know about this, then you can also include them.

Click **Save** to continue.
