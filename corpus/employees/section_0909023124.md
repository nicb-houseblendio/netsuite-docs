## Default Conditions and Rules {#subsect_0903111752}

By default, WFM provides the following set of conditions and rules in this order. These conditions contain the most common rules found in most classifications and awards in Australia.

| Condition | Rules | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular Hours | Every day | Rates apply when no conditions apply |
| Public Holiday | Every day that is a public holiday | Rates apply when the rules apply |
| Saturday | Every Saturday | Rates apply when the rules apply |
| Sunday | Every Sunday | Rates apply when the rules apply |
| Standard Overtime | 
-   Every day when during the day time worked exceeds 8 hours
-   Every day when during week time worked exceeds 38 hours

 | Rates apply when the rules apply |
| Extended Overtime | 

-   Every day when during the day time worked exceeds 11 hours
-   Every day when during the week time worked exceeds 41 hours
-   Every day when during the day time worked exceeds 8 hours
-   Every day when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours

 | Rates apply when the rules apply |
