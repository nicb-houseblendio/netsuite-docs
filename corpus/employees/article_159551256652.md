SuitePeople HR's Performance Management features help employees stay on track to meet their career goals and objectives. The following three tools are included in the Performance Management feature:

-   [Goal Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159542637440.html)\- Enables employees to create and track goals in collaborations with their managers.
    
-   [Performance Reviews](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157529458019.html) - Enables the set up of performance reviews to help employees and managers assess their performance.
    
-   [Areas of Focus](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162576581483.html) - Enables employees to reflect and plan their career management in collaboration with their managers.
    

The Performance Management tools must be enabled in NetSuite before they can be used. For more information, see [Performance Management Prerequisites](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156380544694.html).

Employees can also recognize the accomplishments, achievements, or milestones of their coworkers by giving [Kudos](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4721459587.html). Kudos must be activated separately from the other Performance Management tools.
