Areas of focus enable employees and managers to work together on a list of focus areas to help grow the employee's career.

If you are an employee, you can do the following:

-   Create and manage your own areas of focus. For more information, see [Managing Your Areas of Focus](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162576654741.html).
    
-   Connect your goals to areas of focus to make sure your goals are contributing to your career development. For more information, see [Creating Goals](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/rc_1495975199.html).
    

If you are a manager, you can provide guidance to your direct reports by creating areas of focus for them. For more information, see [Creating Areas of Focus for Your Direct Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162576655855.html).
