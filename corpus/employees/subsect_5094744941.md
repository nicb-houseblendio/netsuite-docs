The following table shows the options for employees to track breaks after clocking in on SuitePeople Time Clock.

| Break Type | Actions | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid Breaks | Click **Rest**. | The employee is punched into a paid break event. If you have the **Rest Breaks (Paid)** feature and an employee hasn't punched in for a paid rest break during their shift, SuitePeople Time Clock will show a message asking if they've taken a paid rest break. When an employee answers **Yes** or **No**, it'll change the message that appears on their timesheet shift in the Weekly view. If the employee chooses **No**, the message on their timesheet shift will say: 'Employee has indicated they did not take a Rest Break.' |
| Unpaid Breaks | Click **Start Break**. | The employee is punched into an unpaid break event. Employees can take one unpaid break but can take multiple paid rest breaks. After an employee takes one unpaid break, **Start Break** will be unavailable. |
