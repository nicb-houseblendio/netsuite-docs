Weekly timesheets works in conjunction with the existing Time Tracking feature to offer a method of capturing time entries in a weekly format.

To track time with weekly timesheets, an administrator should go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_ and select the Employee subtab. In Time & Expenses check the Time Tracking and Weekly Timesheets boxes, and click Save.

Weekly timesheets contain added features when used with Workforce Management (WFM). For more information, see [Weekly Timesheets with Workforce Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_4163136366.html).

Note:

If your company uses weekly time tracking, see [Weekly Time Tracking](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N904728.html).

Important:

If your company has enabled the Weekly Timesheet Interface, the user interface will differ slightly from the steps described below.
