When an employee leaves your company, you should update the employee's information in NetSuite. NetSuite provides tools to terminate employees, track termination reasons, and inactivate or remove employee records.

For more information, see the following help topics:

-   [Employee Termination](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3753809772.html)
    
-   [Termination Reason Tracking](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1494530951.html)
    
-   [Deleting Employee Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1494515524.html)
    
-   [Inactivating Employee Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1504014571.html)
