Goal management includes the following:

-   [Goal Management for Employees and Managers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/rc_670460565.html)
    
    -   Employees can create their own goals in collaboration with managers. They can record decisions and conversations by adding comments to goals.
        
    -   Employees can manage their current and past goals to identify key trends.
        
    -   Managers and employees can set up notifications for goals at key events.
        
    -   After goals are closed, employees and managers can write reflections on the outcome.
        
-   [Goal Tracking with Performance Metrics](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/rc_1051865006.html)
    
    Access real-time data in NetSuite to automatically update the actual value of a goal. NetSuite provides default performance metrics, and you can create your own.
    
-   [Goal Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161428709201.html)
    
    Use saved searches to find information about goals in your organization.
    
-   [Performance Management Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161185085251.html)
    
    You can use the performance management datasets and workbook templates to analyze goal information for the employees in your organization.
