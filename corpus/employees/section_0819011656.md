There are two ways leave can be entered into WFM, through an application and approval process, or by the manager when they are working on the schedule or approving hours.

Lets look at the approvals process first

## Employee {#subsect_38131724950}

When an employee logs into WFM they can head to the leave tab on the left to request time off. They will see a list of their pending and approved leaves.

Under New Leave Request, employees can fill in the details of the request and click save, sending a request to their "Leave Approvers" to approve the request.

The only other thing an employee needs to do in WFM in regards to the leave request is log back in and check the status of this request.

## Manager {#subsect_60131822163}

After an employee has applied for leave the manager who is the "Leave Approver" will get a notification in WFM. Click on the 'View' button to be taken to the request to approve or deny.

They then head to the employee's leave page, click on the Approve/deny button. From here you will have the option of editing, approving or denying the leave.
