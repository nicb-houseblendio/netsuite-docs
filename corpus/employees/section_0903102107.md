After setting up your wage agreement, classifications, and conditions, you can assign penalties or fixed increases to a shift or period of shifts. You can set up different penalty rates for different employee levels.

#### To set up a penalty:

1.  Open the wage agreement. See [Accessing Wage Agreements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0807100528.html#subsect_0903110736).
    
2.  Click the **Penalties** subtab.
    
3.  Click **\+ New penalty…**
    
4.  On the Create new Penalty window, complete the fields.
    
    -   **Condition** - Select the condition that applies to the penalty.
        
    -   **Increase** - Enter the amount to give to employees when the penalty is applied.
        
    -   **Levels** - Choose an option.
        
        -   **Any** - The penalty applies to any level.
            
        -   **Between level 0 and level 0 (inclusive)** - Enter the range of levels that can receive the penalty. For example, if the penalty is for Level 1 and lower, enter **Level 0 and Level 1**. If it's for Level 2 and above enter **Level 2 and Level 0**.
            
    -   **Jobs** - From the Available Choices panel, select the jobs that can receive this penalty. If the penalty applies to all employees or shifts, do not select jobs. When you assign a job to a penalty, only the shifts with one of those jobs will be eligible for the penalty.
        
5.  Click **Save**.
