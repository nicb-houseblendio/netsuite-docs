Deleting a job can be done in only a few steps, firstly you will have to go to Setup > Jobs, and then select the job that you would like to delete. you have opened up the job information, there will be a **Deactivate** button in the top right corner of the screen.

You will be prompted with a warning before you are able to remove the job. Accept the warning then click **Deactivate (Job name)**.
