If you've enabled the Performance Management feature, there are two permissions that control access to goal and performance review information: Talent Administration and Talent Employee. Roles that have both of these permissions can:

-   Create metrics for tracking employee performance
    
-   View, inactivate, and delete employees' goals
    
-   Create and manage performance review questions, rating scales, and templates
    
-   Create, schedule, and approve performance reviews
    
-   Edit, delete, and cancel performance reviews
    

The Talent Administration and Talent Employee permissions are automatically applied to the Chief People Officer and Human Resources Generalist roles, as well as customized versions of these standard roles. You will need to add both permissions to any other roles that need to have access to Performance Management features. .

Roles with the Talent Employee permission only, such as Employee Center, allow employees to work with their own performance reviews and goals, as well as those of their direct reports.

For a list of standard roles and their permissions, see [Standard Roles Permissions Table](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N295396.html). You can also create custom roles that include this permission. For more information, see [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html).

| Permission | Permissions Subtab | Permission Access Level | Supported Functionality |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Talent Administration | Lists | View | Enables you to view and search for performance metrics, performance review questions, performance review rating scales, and performance review templates. You can't create or edit any of theses. |
| Full | You can create, edit, or delete performance metrics, performance review questions, performance review rating scales, performance review templates, performance reviews, and performance review schedules, with some limitations. |
| Talent Employee | Lists | Full | You can create and edit your own goals or those of your direct reports. You can approve and delete your own goals or those of your direct reports. When combined with the Talent Administration permission at level: View, you can view and search for goal and performance review information. You can view all goals and performance reviews for employees. When combined with Talent Administration at level: Full, you can also approve and cancel performance reviews for all employees. |
