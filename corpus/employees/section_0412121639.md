After you've installed the SuiteApps, you might need to give the WFM role access to more record types in NetSuite.

#### To configure additional permissions in NetSuite: {#procedure_0412121845}

1.  As an Administrator, go to Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles.
    
2.  Next to the **SuitePeople WFM Integration** role, click **Edit**.
    
3.  If needed, add any extra permissions by clicking the **Permissions** subtab, then the **Lists** subtab.
    
    For example, you can add permissions for the following:
    
    -   Workplaces
        
    -   Time-Off Administration
        
4.  Click **OK**. Click **Save**.
    

For more information about setting permissions for a role, see [Setting Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N288727.html).
