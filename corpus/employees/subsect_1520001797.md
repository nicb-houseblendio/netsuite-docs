Important:

This topic applies to you if you use SuitePeople U.S. Payroll in your NetSuite account.

Use the following procedure to set up an accrual based on hours worked.

#### To set up an accrual based on hours worked:

1.  In the time-off rule, under **Accrual Method**, check the **Automatically Accrue** box.
    
2.  From the **Accrues Based On** list, select **Hours Worked (NetSuite U.S. Payroll)**.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

Continue to [Updating the Employee Record for Time-Off Management and Payroll](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519677675.html).
