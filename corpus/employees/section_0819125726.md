The easiest way you can tell which scheduled shifts don't have a Job is by:

-   Heading to the printable PDF reports page (Reports > Shifts > Print Ready)
    
-   Generate the "Multi-Unit Staff Report" for all Locations.
    
-   Wait for the report to generate & then download it.
    
-   Open the report and look for any shifts with no color. These will not have a Job assigned to them unless shift has a Job name.
    
-   You can speak with your managers and direct them to add Jobs onto shifts moving forward by editing those shifts and adding a Job
