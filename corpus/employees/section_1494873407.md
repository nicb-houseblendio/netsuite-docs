You can create ad hoc reports to fit your company's specific needs. For an ad hoc report, select the type of data you want to report on and the report format. Then, decide how you want to subtotal and group the information.

To create a report, go to _Reports > New Report_. Then, choose the data metric to include in your report. For Time-Off Management, you can choose from Time-Off and Time-Off Balance Adjustments.

For more information, see [Ad Hoc Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N708927.html) and [Choices for Ad Hoc Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N709168.html).
