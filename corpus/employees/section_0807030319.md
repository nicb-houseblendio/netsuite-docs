After you prepared the category group requirements for region-based overrides, you can create a custom region-based override. Follow the instructions.

#### To create a region-based override:

1.  Go to Workforce Management.
    
2.  Go to Setup > Wage.
    
3.  On the Region-based wages setup page, scroll to the bottom.
    
4.  Click **\+ New region-based wages override**.
    
5.  In the **Name** field, enter a name for the override. Click **Create**.
    
6.  Enter the start and finish dates for the wage override. Click **Next**.
    
7.  Confirm the settings. Click **Create**.
    

After you create the override, you can create conditions, multipliers, penalties, allowances, groups, locations, and notes.
