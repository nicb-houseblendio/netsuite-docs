To create custom Advanced Employee Permissions, you need the Setup > Manage Custom Permissions permission at access Level Full. The standard role that comes with this permission is the Administrator role. You can also create custom roles that include this permission. For more information, see [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html).
