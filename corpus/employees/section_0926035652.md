After you activate and review the configurations of the Hospitality Industry (General) Award \[MA000009\] preconfigured agreement, it is available to be assigned to employee wages.

The agreement covers employees in the hospitality industry and those who fit within the classifications of the award.

For more information about the full coverage and classification descriptions of the Hospitality Industry Award, go to the Fair Work Ombudsman website.

## Hospitality Industry (General) Award Classifications {#subsect_0927115027}

Refer to the following table for the default classification settings of the preconfigured agreement.

| Classification | Level | Employment Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introductory | Level 0 | Casual |
| Introductory | Level 0 | Full-time and Part-time |
| Introductory PT | Level 0 | Part-time |
| Level 1 | Level 1 | Full-time and Part-time |
| Level 1 | Level 1 | Casual |
| Level 1 PT | Level 1 | Part-time |
| Level 2 | Level 2 | Casual |
| Level 2 | Level 2 | Full-time and Part-time |
| Level 2 PT | Level 2 | Part-time |
| Level 3 | Level 3 | Casual |
| Level 3 | Level 3 | Full-time and Part-time |
| Level 3 PT | Level 3 | Part-time |
| Level 4 | Level 4 | Full-time and Part-time |
| Level 4 | Level 4 | Casual |
| Level 4 PT | Level 4 | Part-time |

## Hospitality Industry (General) Award Conditions {#subsect_0927115201}

Refer to the following table for the default conditions of the preconfigured agreement.

| Condition | Rules | Employment Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular Hours (Regular Rate) | Every day | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Evening (Per-hour Penalty Rate) | Every shift if the shift is on a weekday if any part of the shift is between 7 p.m. and midnight | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Night work (Per-hour Penalty Rate) | Every shift if the shift is on a weekday if any part of the shift is between midnight and 7 a.m. | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Saturday (Penalty Rate) | Every Saturday | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Sunday (Penalty Rate) | Every Sunday | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Public Holiday (Penalty Rate) | Every day that is a public holiday | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Delayed meal break (Overtime Rate) | Every day when during the time worked since last break time worked exceeds 6 hours | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Full Time Overtime (Overtime Rate) | 
-   Every day when during the day time worked exceeds 11.5 hours
-   Every day when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours
-   Every day where there has not been a clear break of 10 hours since the previous day
-   Every day when the spread of time worked on a single day exceeds 12 hours

 | Full-time |
| M-F Overtime after 2 hours (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every weekend when during the day time of type overtime rate worked exceeds 2 hours
-   Every weekday when during the week time worked exceeds 40 hours

 | Full-time |
| Weekend (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every weekend when during the day any time matching Full Time Overtime is worked
-   Every weekend when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours

 | Full-time |
| Part Time Overtime (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every day when during the day time worked exceeds 11.5 hours
-   Every day when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours
-   Every day for the part of the shift that is longer than the scheduled hours
-   Every day when the spread of time worked on a single day exceeds 12 hours
-   Every day where there has not been a clear break of 10 hours since the previous day
-   Every day when more than 5 days are worked this week

 | Part-time |
| M-F Overtime after 2 hours (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every weekend when during the day time of type overtime rate worked exceeds 2 hours
-   Every weekday when during the week time worked exceeds 40 hours

 | Part-time |
| Weekend (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every weekend when during the day any time matching Part Time Overtime is worked
-   Every weekend when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours

 | Part-time |
| Casual Standard Overtime (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every day when during the day time worked exceeds 12 hours
-   Every day when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours

 | Casual |
| M-F Overtime after 2 hours (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every weekend when during the day time of type overtime rate worked exceeds 2 hours
-   Every weekday when during the week time worked exceeds 40 hours

 | Casual |
| Weekend (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every weekend when during the day any time matching Casual Overtime is worked
-   Every weekend when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours

 | Casual |

## Hospitality Industry (General) Award Allowances {#subsect_0927115250}

Refer to the following table for the default allowances of the agreement.

| Allowance | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Broken Shift Allowance 2-3 hours | Every shift where the break during or before the shift is in the range 2.0 hour(s) - 3 hours |
| Broken Shift Allowance Over 3 hours | Every shift where the break during or before the shift is in the range 3.01 hour(s) - 6 hours |
