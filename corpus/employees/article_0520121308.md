You can update your automatch settings for single or multiple locations.

#### To update automatch settings:

1.  Go to Setup > Locations.
    
2.  On the Locations page, choose between the following:
    
    -   Set options for a single location
        
    -   Set options for multiple or bulk locations
        
3.  For a single location, follow the steps.
    
    1.  Select a location.
        
    2.  From the **Approvals** tab, click the **Automatch** subtab.
        
4.  For multiple locations, follow the steps.
    
    1.  Click **Bulk Actions**.
        
    2.  Click **Automatch**.
        
5.  If you are updating for multiple locations, configure location settings.
    
    1.  Set the filters.
        
    2.  From the **Available Choices** panel on the left, select the locations where you want the automatch settings to apply.
        
        If you want to select all locations, click **Choose All**.
        
    3.  To move the chosen locations to the **Chosen Items** panel on the right, click the blue right arrow symbol at the middle of the panels.
        
        If you want to clear your chosen items, click **Clear All**.
        
6.  Set the automatch and rounding settings that you want to apply to all of the selected locations. For descriptions and examples of each setting, see [Automatch and Rounding Settings in WFM](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0314095739.html).
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

After each setting change, you can test them without needing to refresh the page. To test, run automatch from the weekly view on your desired shifts.
