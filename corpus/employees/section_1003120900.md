After linking ADP (US) components to WFM, you can export a report containing leave and work hours.

#### To export ADP (US) reports:

1.  Go to Workforce Management > (Pending).
    
2.  Select the start and end dates of the period you would like to export.
    
3.  On the **Available Choices** panel, select the locations that you want to include in the report. To select multiple locations, press and hold Ctrl as you click each location.
    
    If you want to include all locations, click **Choose all**.
    
4.  To move your selections to the **Chosen Items** panel, click the blue right arrow in the middle of the panels.
    
    If you want to clear your selections, click **Clear All**.
    
5.  To generate a copy of the data, click **Preview**.
    
6.  To download a CSV file of the data, click **Download**.
    

You can also set a **Batch ID, Memo Code** or **Memo Amount** before exporting data. If you need more information about these fields, reach out to an ADP representative.
