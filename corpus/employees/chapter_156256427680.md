HR Dashboard Components is a feature that offers accessible and convenient ways to view KPIs and workforce demographics in your dashboard. With this feature, you can add ready-made content to help you handle HR tasks and plan strategies more easily.

With HR Dashboard Components, you can:

-   Quickly view employee demographics when you open your dashboard
    
-   Minimize the time you need to create visual aids and reports
    
-   Compare human resource metrics changes over time
