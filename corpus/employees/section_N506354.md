When an employee leaves your company, an administrator can delete the events they've scheduled. This is the only way to delete another employee's events that are marked Private or Show as Busy.

Roles other than administrator can't delete events that do not belong to them. During the mass update, administrators may see public events they can't delete.

#### To perform the delete events mass update: {#procedure_N506394}

1.  Go to _Lists > Mass Update > Mass Updates_.
    
2.  Click **Activities**, and then click **Delete Events**.
    
3.  On the **Criteria** subtab in the **Filter** column, select **Calendar**.
    
4.  In the window that appears, select the employee whose calendar you want to delete.
    
5.  Click **Set**.
    
6.  Click **Preview**.
    
7.  On the Mass Update Preview Results page, click **Perform Update**.
