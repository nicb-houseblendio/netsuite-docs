Important:

Starting **August 5, 2025**, SuitePeople Workforce Management apps that are not using the latest version will automatically be updated.

To safely upgrade to the latest version and avoid interruptions later, complete required actions before August 5, 2025.

For more information, see [Action Required: Upcoming SuitePeople Workforce Management Mobile App Version Release and Automatic Update](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1020938).

Employees can see their worked shifts that have been approved by their manager in the SuitePeople Workforce Management phone app. They can use the **Attestation** feature to accept or dispute approved hours.

#### To view approved hours: {#procedure_1019104924}

1.  Open the SuitePeople Workforce Management phone app. The **Scheduled Shifts** screen is displayed by default.
    
2.  If your manager approved your shifts, a red notification badge appears on the **Shifts** icon. You'll also receive a notification to your general notifications folder in your profile. Click on the Shifts tab.
    
3.  Select the **Shifts** tab.
    
4.  Click **Approved Hours** in the upper right-hand corner.
