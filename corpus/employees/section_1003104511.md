Important:

The Xero integration is available only to accounts where it's already provisioned. It's not included with the current SuitePeople WFM solution.

With Workforce Management (WFM), you can export worked hours to Xero.

Linking WFM with Xero lets you:

-   Export employee hours to Xero
    
-   Create an employee in Xero from WFM
    
-   Create an employee in WFM from Xero
    
-   Automatically sync employee details updated in WFM with those in Xero
    
-   Manually update employee details in WFM from the details in Xero
    

Linked data can then be used for payroll and can help you save time and avoid data entry errors.

To manage your Xero integration, see the following procedures in this section.

-   [Setting up a Xero Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1011011911.html)
