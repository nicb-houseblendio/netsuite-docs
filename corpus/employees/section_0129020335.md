You can view your account details in Workforce Management (WFM) to configure various settings.

To open the configuration page, refer to the following:

-   If you are working on the standalone WFM site, from the upper right corner of the screen, click the user icon. Click **Your Details**.
    
-   If you are working on WFM in NetSuite, go to Workforce Management. From the upper right corner of the screen, click the user icon. Click **Your Details**.
    

The Your Details page contains the following settings:

-   **Personal** - Lets you update personal information, such as name and date of birth.
    
-   **Employment** - Shows the status of employment, locations, and notes.
    
-   **Access** - Shows the username for WFM as well as the Time Clock pin code.
    
-   **Wages** - Shows the wage setup that currently applies to you, including the calculation type and rent.
    
-   **Jobs** - Shows your job name, effective date, proficiency, and tools.
    
-   **Availability** - Shows your schedule availability for work.
