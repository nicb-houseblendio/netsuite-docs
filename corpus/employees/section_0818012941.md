#### To deactivate a brand: {#procedure_0818013105}

1.  Go to your brand in Setup > Brands and then select the brand you want to deactivate.
    
2.  On the right side of the page, click **Deactivate**.
    
    A warning appears.
    
3.  Click **Deactivate (Brand Name)**.
    

You can restore a brand after it has been deactivated.

#### To restore a brand: {#procedure_0818013332}

1.  Go to Setup > Brands.
    
2.  Click **Restore Brands**.
    
3.  A list of all of the deactivated brands will show up with an option to restore them.
    
4.  Click **Restore**.
    
    You will be prompted with a warning message explaining that any roster units that were deactivated with the brand will be restored.
    
5.  Click **Restore (Brand name)**.
