You can print mailing or shipping labels for employees without using mail merge or data export. For more information about printing mailing labels, see [Printing Mailing and Shipping Labels](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1099985.html).

To print mailing or shipping labels, go to _Transactions > Management > Print Checks and Forms_. Click the Mailing Labels link.
