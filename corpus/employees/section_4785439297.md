The ACA Reporting SuiteApp displays the Affordable Care Act (ACA) subtab on the employee record. You can assign an offer of coverage plan, add covered individuals who are included in the plan, and apply a safe harbor code.

The following topics detail the ACA setup tasks you can complete on the employee record:

-   [Assigning an ACA Offer of Coverage Plan to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4785444842.html)
    
-   [Adding Covered Individuals to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4785452430.html)
    
-   [Adding Safe Harbor Codes to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4785464694.html)
    
-   [Excluding an Employee from ACA Reporting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4785464986.html)
