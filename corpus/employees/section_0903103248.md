When you create a wage agreement, WFM provides default conditions and rules that you can use. After creating the agreement, you can also create new conditions and rules.

For more information, see [Creating a Condition](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0903103341.html).

The four types of conditions in WFM are:

-   **Regular Rate** - These conditions are used as base hours.
    
-   **Penalty Rate** - These conditions are used to give a different rate to regular hours, rather than a wage increase when particular days or hours are worked. For example, hours worked between 7 pm and 10 pm on a Sunday receive a different rate, rather than an increase per hour worked.
    
-   **Overtime Rate** - These conditions are used to give a different rate to regular hours, rather than a wage increase after a certain number of hours have already been worked. For example, every day where hours worked during the week exceed 38 hours receives a different rate.0
    
-   **Allowance or Penalty** - These conditions are used to give a wage increase rather than a different rate. For example, hours worked between 7 pm and 10 pm receive an extra $1 per hour, rather than a different rate altogether.
