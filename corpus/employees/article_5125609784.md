You can export data from SuitePeople Workforce Management (WFM) by generating any of the available reports under the following categories:

-   [Shifts Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1013041225.html)
    
-   [Employee Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0723104057.html)
    
-   [Location Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0723104651.html)
    

To access the report categories and audit logs, go to Workforce Management > Reports.

To find instructions and details about each report, see [Extracting Data Through WFM Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0625082603.html).
