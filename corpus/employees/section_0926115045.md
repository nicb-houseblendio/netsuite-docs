Workforce Management has a wide variety of different printable shift reports. The different types of reports that can be printed include:

-   Daily by day
    
-   Daily
    
-   Daily Master
    
-   Staff Report
    
-   Master Report
    
-   Multi Unit Staff Report
    

You can print with the shifts grouped by job, wage, category or even WFM access group.

It's also possible to remove and include extra KPIs on an hour-by-hour basis.

You can click on the options button to make the changes.

To obtain a report for printing, go to the **Print** button next to the options.

## Custom Reports {#subsect_7115224493}

A custom report will be available to print after it has been created from **Setup > Shift Reports**. Custom Reports are a way to customize schedule reports. This could include wages or KPI information depending on who it's available for.

## Staff Report {#subsect_13115239841}

The staff report is the typical schedule that employees see. This is something that can be printed out and hung up at work.

It contains shifts, any job roles, and total hours worked.

On the last page of this, any shift notes will be printed.

## Master Report {#subsect_78115255683}

The Master Report is like the staff report, but for management only.

It contains wage and KPI information of previous weeks.

## Daily Report {#subsect_8115314096}

The Daily Report lets you view the week's schedule. It creates a singular report that shows the shifts for one location in a day-by-day format.

This report is useful for managers because of its simple layout.

## Daily Master Report {#subsect_13115339288}

The Daily Master report gives an hour by hour, day by day analysis of the weekly master report.

This report is useful as it gives more in-depth information about important features such as the employee wages and KPI information.

## Daily By Day Report {#subsect_3115402579}

A Daily By Day report is very similar to a daily report, the main difference between the two is that the Daily By Day report print a day per page. This is useful to managers as it leaves extra room for them to add small notes after it has been printed out.

## Multi-Unit Staff Report {#subsect_94115433576}

The Multi Unit Staff report creates a weekly shift report of two or more locations. This report is useful for any management staff overlooking multiple locations.

This type of report is also useful for employees who work at multiple locations.
