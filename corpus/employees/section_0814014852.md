After you create region-based overrides, you should assign locations associated with category groups. If you need to create categories for the overrides, see [Creating Categories for Region-Based Overrides](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0814124420.html).

#### To assign locations to region-based overrides:

1.  Open the region-based override. See [Accessing Region-Based Wages or Overrides](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0909023340.html#subsect_0807020309)
    
2.  On the override page, click the **Groups** subtab.
    
3.  Click **+Add group**.
    
4.  From the list of category groups you created, select the group or groups containing the locations you need to assign.
    
    All locations in the group you select are added to the Locations subtab of the region-based override.
    

After you create region-based overrides and assign locations, ensure that you test new rules with your account. You should create scheduled shifts that aim to trigger the rules as well as other scenarios or business workflows.
