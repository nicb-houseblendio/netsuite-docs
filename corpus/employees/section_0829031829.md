WFM can send you notifications when things happen, to access the notification setup section go to **Setup > Notifications**.

To view your notifications, click on the envelope at the top right corner of your screen.

There are configurable notifications for:

-   Birthday Reminders
    
-   Expiring Documents
    
-   New Staff
    
-   Personal Details
    
-   Financial Details
