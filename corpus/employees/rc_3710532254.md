As an employee, you can delete any of your goals if they have not been approved by their manager. Once a goal is deleted, it is gone forever.

#### To delete a goal:

1.  From the home screen of your Employee Center role, go to Talent > Goals.
    
2.  Select the goal you want to delete.
    
3.  From the **Actions** list, click **Delete**.
    
4.  Click **Ok**.
