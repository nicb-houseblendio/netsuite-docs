Follow the procedure to create a spread of hours rule and condition.

#### To set up a spread of hours condition:

1.  Open the region-based override. See [Accessing Region-Based Wages or Overrides](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0909023340.html#subsect_0807020309)
    
2.  Create the Spread of Hours condition.
    
    1.  On the Region-based override page, click the **Conditions** subtab.
        
    2.  Click **\+ Create new Condition…**.
        
        The New Condition form appears.
        
    3.  c. In the **Name** field, enter **Spread of Hours**.
        
    4.  From the **Type** list, select Allowance or Penalty.
        
    5.  In the **Spread of time on day exceeds** field, enter the number of excess hours that require an allowance to be added. For example, you may enter **10 hours**.
        
3.  Create the Spread of Hours multiplier.
    
    1.  Click the **Multipliers** subtab.
        
    2.  Click **\+ New multiplier…**.
        
    3.  From the **Conditions** list, select **Spread of Hours \[allowance\]**.
        
    4.  On the employment status section, check all the statuses where this multiplier applies.
        
    5.  In the **Rate** field, enter the multiplier rate (%). This multiplier will be applied to the shift base rate.
