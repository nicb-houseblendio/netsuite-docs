Information in NetSuite reports isn't governed by Advanced Employee Permissions. This means that it could be possible to accidentally disclose more information than an employee should have access to through a report. Use caution when giving employees access to reports. For more information, see [Access to Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N698946.html).
