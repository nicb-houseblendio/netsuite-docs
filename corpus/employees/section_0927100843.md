If an employee who wasn't scheduled on a particular day ends up working, you'll need to create an actual shift for them.

1.  Head to the Weekly View and find the employees name on the left-hand side.
    
2.  Select the day on which the employee worked and move your mouse over the "actual" row. By clicking in this area, you'll be able to create an actual shift.
    
3.  Clicking on this will pop up a shift creation dialogue box.
    
4.  When this is filled out and saved, you'll see the actual shift appear in place.
