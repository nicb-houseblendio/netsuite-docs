Follow the procedure.

#### To export shifts for payroll:

1.  Go to Workforce Management.
    
2.  Go to Process Time.
    
3.  On the **From** field, select the start date for the export.
    
4.  On the **to** field, select the end date for the export.
    
5.  Filter your locations.
    
    1.  In the **Locations** field, click the filter icon.
        
        The Filter Locations window opens.
        
    2.  From the list on the left, select a location group.
        
        The Available Choices and Chosen Items panels for groups and locations appear.
        
    3.  From the Available Choices panel at the top, select the group of locations with shifts that you must export.
        
        If you want to select all states, click **Choose All**.
        
        To move your selections to the Chosen Items panel on the right, click the blue right arrow in the middle. All locations in those groups will show up in the Available Choices panel at the bottom.
        
        If you want to clear your selections, click **Clear All**.
        
    4.  From the Available Choices panel at the bottom, select the specific locations with shifts you want to export.
        
    5.  Click **Done**.
        
6.  From the **Employees** list, filter the group of employees with shifts for export.
    
    Choose from the following options:
    
    -   All
        
    -   with shifts to push
        
    -   with shifts
        
    -   with failed pushes
