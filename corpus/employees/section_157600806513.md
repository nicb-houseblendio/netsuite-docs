You can edit a rating scale if it's not used in a performance review, or delete it if it's not used in a template.

When you inactivate a rating scale, it doesn't show up in lists anymore, but it still appears on performance reviews that were launched before you inactivated it.

#### To edit a performance review rating scale:

1.  Go to _Setup > Performance Management > Performance Reviews > Rating Scales_.
    
2.  Beside a rating scale, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Do one of the following:
    
    -   To edit the rating scale, make your changes and click **Save**.
        
    -   To inactivate a performance review rating scale, check the **Inactive** box and click **Save**.
        
        To view a list of inactive rating scales, go to _Setup > Performance Management > Performance Reviews > Rating Scales_ and check the **Show Inactives** box.
        
    -   To delete a performance review rating scale, from the **Actions** list, select **Delete**. When asked to confirm, click **OK**.
