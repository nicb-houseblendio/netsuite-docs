WFM can send you notifications when new employees are added to the system.

To view your notifications, click on the envelope at the top right corner of your screen.

## Setting Up a New Staff Notification {#subsect_21134034949}

Setting up a notification system to remind you, or any other managers about a new employee is a simple process. Go to **Setup > Notifications**, and then click **New employees created**.

The first step that you need to do is choose whether you would like to receive a notification when a new employee is created, you can ignore this section if you have no involvement with employee handling.

You will then need to choose which managers will be informed about a new employee.

Click **Save** to continue.
