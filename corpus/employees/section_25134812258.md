To facilitate ease of viewing, you can switch the Grayscale Mode on in Workforce Management (WFM). This setting changes the display to use high-contrast colors of black, gray, and white.

#### To switch Grayscale Mode on or off:

1.  Go to Workforce Management.
    
2.  From the upper right corner of the page, click the user icon.
    
    ![Menu icons available in WFM with the user icon outlined in red.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/EmployeeManagement/Employees/WFMUserIcon.png)
3.  From the panel, click the **Grayscale Mode** switch to turn it on or off.
