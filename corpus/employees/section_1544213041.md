Important:

If you use SuitePeople U.S. Payroll in your NetSuite account, follow the setup instructions in [Time-Off Management Integration With SuitePeople U.S. Payroll](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519676814.html).

Time-off types can automatically accrue in a plan either by a fixed amount per period or by hours worked.see [Setting up an Accrual Based on a Fixed Amount per Period](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1558460830.html) and [Setting up an Accrual Based on Hours Worked](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1558460869.html).

Note:

If you select a track-only time-off type in the time-off rule, you won't see accrual options.

In a time-off plan, if two or more rules use the same time-off type, they all have to accrue the same way - either by a fixed amount or by hours worked.
