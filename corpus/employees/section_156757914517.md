The following list includes terms that are used in HR Dashboard Components:

-   **Active Employee** - An employee whose records show the following:
    
    -   The hire date is on or before the present date.
        
    -   The termination date is empty, or it is after the present date.
        
    
    Employees whose records show a hire date that is later than the termination date are rehired employees. They are also considered active.
    
    Note:
    
    The SuiteApp runs a script to count the total active employees. For more information about this script, see [HR Dashboard Components Scripts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156756816079.html). The number of active employees is used to calculate various metrics that are available to be added to the KPI Scorecard portlet. For more information about these KPIs, see [Additional Human Resources KPIs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156263780277.html#subsect_156264979611).
    
    You can also run a Total Active Employees Saved Search if you want to see a list of active employees according to subsidiary. For more information, see [HR Dashboard Components Saved Searches for Custom Scorecard KPIs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156263781270.html#subsect_156265038088).
    
-   **New Hire** - An active employee whose hire date is within three months of the present date.
    
-   **Terminated Employee**\- An employee whose employee records show the following:
    
    -   The termination date is not empty.
        
    -   The termination date is later than the hire date.
