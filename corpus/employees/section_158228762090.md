With the Time-Off Analysis workbook, you can check time-off information for your organization. The workbook works with the Time-Off Balance Details and Summary reports by pulling balance totals into two ready-to-use pivot tables. The pivot tables show time-off balances by employee and type, plus time-off activities by employee, year, and type. You can also use the workbook data to make your own charts and pivots to spot trends or issues.

The following Pivot Tables and Charts are predefined in the Time-Off Analysis workbook:

-   **Time-Off Balance** - Shows employees' time-off balances by time-off type.
    
-   **Time-Off Activity** - Summarizes employees' time-off activity by type, breaking totals down into year-end expiry, carryovers, accruals, usage, and manual adjustments.
    

For more information about SuiteAnalytics Workbooks, see [SuiteAnalytics Workbook Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1503949328.html).
