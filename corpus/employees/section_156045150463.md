HR Administrators or other users with appropriate permission can create new employee change requests from the employee record. They can also edit, delete, or inactivate existing employee change requests.

Before you create an employee change request, ensure you've completed [Prerequisites for Employee Change Requests](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156025846348.html).

For more information about managing employee change requests, see the following topics:

-   [Creating an Employee Change Request From the Employee Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156330883780.html)
    
-   [Creating a Base Pay Change Request From the Employee Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162148885271.html)
    
-   [Creating a Promotion Change Request From the Employee Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162269675808.html)
    
-   [Creating a Transfer Change Request From the Employee Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162269735821.html)
    
-   [Editing or Inactivating an Employee Change Request](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156330887492.html)
    
-   [Deleting an Employee Change Request](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156330889462.html)
