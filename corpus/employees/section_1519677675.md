Important:

This topic applies to you if you use SuitePeople U.S. Payroll in your NetSuite account.

After you create time-off rules in your time-off plans, you need to assign a time-off plan to each of your employees. If some of your time-off plans include accruals based on hours worked, you need to set up those accruals on the employee record.

## Assigning a Time-Off Plan to an Employee {#subsect_1519756068}

Use the following procedure to assign a time-off plan to an employee.

#### To assign a time-off plan to an employee:

1.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Employees_.
    
2.  Beside an employee, click **Edit**.
    
3.  In the **Time-Off** subtab, select a plan from the **Time-Off Plan** list.
    
4.  In the **Start Date for Time-Off Calculations** field, enter the date for time-off calculations. The date calculates the number of days that the employee is entitled to time off. It's based on the minimum and maximum tenures set in the time-off plan. If there's no value specified in this field, this employee can't be assigned to a time-off plan.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

## Adding Payroll Items to the Employee Record {#subsect_1525116663}

To make sure payroll items associateed to a time-off type show up on employees' pay statements, you'll need to add those items to each employee's record.

Important:

Payroll items that accrue based on hours worked need additional setup. For more information, see [Setting up Accruals Based on Hours Worked On the Employee Record for Payroll](#subsect_1519756114).

#### To add payroll items to the employee record:

1.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Employees_.
    
2.  Beside an employee, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Click the **Payroll** subtab, and then click the **Accrued Time** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Sick/Vacation Time** list, select a payroll item.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

When a payroll item is associated with a time-off type that accrues based on a fixed amount, the accrual appears on the employee's pay statement. It doesn't appear in the Accrued Hours field on the Accrued Time subtab.

## Setting up Accruals Based on Hours Worked On the Employee Record for Payroll {#subsect_1519756114}

You need to set up accrual rates for payroll items that accrue based on hours worked on each employee's record.

#### To set up accruals based on hours worked for an employee: {#procedure_1527686221}

1.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Employees_.
    
2.  Beside an employee, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Click the **Payroll** subtab, and then click the **Accrued Time** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Sick/Vacation Time** list, select a payroll item that accrues by hours worked. The **Accrues Based On** field is set to **Hours Worked (NetSuite U.S. Payroll)**).
    
    For example, you might have associated a time-off type with a payroll item called **PTO - Vacation**.
    
5.  In the **Accrued Hours** field, enter a beginning balance if the employee has accrued time that's not in the system.
    
    After the beginning balance is set, the system keeps track of this total.
    
6.  In the **Accrue As** list, select **Per Hour Worked**.
    
    Warning:
    
    If you select an option other than Per Hour Worked in the Accrue As list, it could result in inaccurate accrual balances on the employee's pay statement.
    
7.  In the **Accrual Rate** field, enter the number of hours this employee accrues for each hour worked (for example, 0.04).
    
8.  In the **Monetary Rate** field, enter the cost per hour of the accrued time.
    
9.  Depending on whether your time-off policy allows carryover, you can do one of the following:
    
    -   If you **do not** allow employees to carry over unused time off, check the **Reset at Year End** box. At the end of each calendar year, NetSuite sets the total accruals to zero and starts calculations again.
        
    -   If you allow employees to carry over unused time off, **do not** check the **Reset at Year End** box.
        
10.  If your company sets a limit for accrued time, enter the maximum number of hours that can be accrued in the **Maximum Hours** field.
     
11.  Click **Save**.
     

When a payroll item is associated with a time-off type that accrues based on hours worked, the employee's accrual appears in the Accrued Hours field and on the pay statement.
