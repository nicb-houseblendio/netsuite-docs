Before you troubleshoot any issues, ensure SuitePeople Time Clock is using the latest version. For more information, see [Updating SuitePeople Time Clock](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0917011848.html).

To learn more about troubleshooting SuitePeople Time Clock issues, see the following procedures:

-   [To help employees unable to clock in after device activation:](#procedure_1013045458)
    
-   [To help employees unable to clock in with their PIN code:](#procedure_1013050449)
    
-   [To see timesheets in the weekly view:](#procedure_1013050732)
    
-   [To help employees clock in if the device registered them as clocked in already:](#procedure_1013050854)
    
-   [To resolve a blank screen on the Android app:](#procedure_1013051636)

#### To help employees unable to clock in after device activation: {#procedure_1013045458}

1.  Check that the device has an Internet connection.
    
2.  Ensure the device is online and doesn't show any label saying it's offline.
    
3.  Update the device name and wait for the change to go through.
    
    1.  Go to Workforce Management.
        
    2.  Go to Setup > Time Clock.
        
    3.  Click the device name.
        
    4.  On the device settings window, edit the entry in the **Name** field.
        
    5.  Click **Save**.
        
4.  Check the app version.
    
    -   Check that the device is running the latest app version by looking at the version number at the bottom of the app pages. Compare it with the latest version in the app store. For more information, see [Minimum Requirements for SuitePeople Time Clock Mobile Apps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0917112543.html).
        
    -   If the app is outdated, update it. For more information, see [Updating SuitePeople Time Clock](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0917011848.html).
        
5.  Restart the app.
    
6.  Check if the required locations are set to be available on the device.
    
    1.  Open the device settings window. See instructions in Step 3.
        
    2.  On the Locations section, check that the required locations are added on the **Chosen Items** panel. Edit the entry in the **Name** field.
        
        From the **Available Choices** panel on the left, select the locations you want to add.
        
        If you want to select all locations, click **Choose All**.
        
        To move the chosen locations to the **Chosen Items** panel on the right, click the blue right arrow symbol at the middle of the panels.
        
        If you want to clear your chosen items, click **Clear All**.
        
    3.  Click **Save**.
        
7.  Check if the employee can work at the location and has a PIN set.
    
    1.  Go to Workforce Management.
        
    2.  Go to Setup > Employees > List.
        
    3.  Click the employee name.
        
    4.  On the employee profile, from the left-hand panel, click **Location**.
        
    5.  Verify that the employee is assigned to work at the specified location. To add a location, on the **Can work at** section, click **Edit**. From the **Available Choices** panel on the left, move the required location to the **Chosen Items** panel. Click **Save**.
        
    6.  From the left-hand panel, click **Details**. On the AdiClock PIN section, check that a PIN code is assigned. To assign a PIN code, see [Setting up Employee PIN Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1013044154.html#subsect_96164608856).
        

#### To help employees unable to clock in with their PIN code: {#procedure_1013050449}

1.  Check if SuitePeople Time Clock is offline.
    
2.  Check if the employee's PIN code was recently updated. If so, connect the device online to update any PIN codes that were recently changed.
    
3.  Repeat the steps in the earlier procedure. See [To help employees unable to clock in after device activation:](#procedure_1013045458).
    

#### To see timesheets in the weekly view: {#procedure_1013050732}

1.  Check that the device is connected to the Internet.
    
2.  When the device is connected to the Internet, it will upload any timesheets that haven't appeared in the weekly view.
    

#### To help employees clock in if the device registered them as clocked in already: {#procedure_1013050854}

1.  Verify if the employee is clocked in.
    
    1.  On SuitePeople Time Clock, click the **IN** tab on the top left corner.
        
    2.  Check if the employee is on the list of currently clocked-in shifts and note how long they've been clocked in.
        
2.  Verify if the employee clocked out from their previous shift.
    
3.  Verify if the employee accidentally clocked in with another employee's PIN code.
    
4.  Verify if the employee can clock out. If they can't clock out, verify if a manager can clock out the employee for them.
    

#### To resolve a blank screen on the Android app: {#procedure_1013051636}

1.  Fully close the app.
    
    1.  On your Android device, swipe up from the bottom and let go. This automatically closes the app.
        
    2.  Open SuitePeople Time Clock. If the white screen is still present, clear the app data.
        
2.  Clear the app data.
    
    1.  On your device's home screen, press and hold the app.
        
    2.  Select **App info**.
        
    3.  Select **Storage**.
        
    4.  Select **Clear data**.
        

Note:

SuitePeople Time Clock syncs with the server to show the correct time. Even if the system clock changes, it'll always show the correct time.

#### To resolve incorrect time on the app: {#procedure_97194244286}

1.  If SuitePeople Time Clock shows incorrect time but your system time is correct, check your device's time and date settings.
    
    On iOS devices, ensure the **Set automatically** feature is on.
    
2.  Alternatively, ensure the correct time zone is set on the device. If you have set a different time zone in SuitePeople Time Clock but your device's time is different, there'll be a mismatch.
    
    This will also affect the final timesheet.
