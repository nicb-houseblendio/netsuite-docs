To update SuitePeople Time Clock, follow the procedure.

#### To update SuitePeople Time Clock:

1.  Check the current version of SuitePeople Time Clock at the bottom of the app screen.
    
2.  Compare the app version with the latest version of SuitePeople Time Clock.
    
    -   **Android** - Open the Google Play store and search for NetSuite Time Clock. It's available under the **Read More** section in the app description.
        
    -   **iOS or iPadOS** - Open the iOS App Store and search for NetSuite Time Clock. It's available under the **What's New** section in the app's description.
        
3.  On the app, click **Update**.
