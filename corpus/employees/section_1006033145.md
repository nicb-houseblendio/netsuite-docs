If you chose to link WFM locations to ADP departments, follow the procedure.

#### To link locations to ADP (Canada):

1.  Go to Workforce Management > Setup > Integrations.
    
2.  From the **Payroll systems** list, select **ADP (Canada)**.
    
3.  On the ADP (Canada) configurations page, click the configuration you created.
    
4.  On the configuration page, click **Locations**.
    
5.  Locate the locations you want to link.
    
    -   In the Search field, enter the name of the location.
        
    -   From the list beside the Search field, filter to view either:
        
        -   All Locations
            
        -   Unlinked Locations
            
        -   Linked Locations
            
6.  In the **ADP Department number** column of the employee you want to link, enter the number.
    
7.  To link the location, press **Enter**.
    
8.  (Optional) To unlink a location from a code, click **Unlink** on the corresponding row.
