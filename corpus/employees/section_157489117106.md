If you're using SuitePeople U.S. Payroll to pay a bonus, you can delete a bonus only if it's **not** included in a payroll batch.

If you aren't using SuitePeople U.S. Payroll to pay a bonus, you can delete the bonus at any time.

#### To delete a bonus:

1.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Employees_.
    
2.  Beside an employee, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Click the **Compensation Tracking** subtab.
    
4.  In the Bonuses section, click the number beside a bonus.
    
5.  From the Actions menu, click **Delete**.
