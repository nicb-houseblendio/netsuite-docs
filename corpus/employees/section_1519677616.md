#### To set up a time-off rule with payroll:

1.  Go to _Setup > HR Information System > Time-Off Plan_.
    
2.  Next to the plan you want to define rules for, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Click **New Time-Off Rule**.
    
4.  In the **Time-Off Type** field, select the type of time off to apply to the plan. To integrate with Payroll, choose a time-off type that you created in [Associating a Time-Off Type With a Payroll Item](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519677524.html).
    
5.  Specify the minimum tenure for this time-off type and whether it's is in months or years. Typically, minimum tenure is used to increase the amount of time off employees are entitled to over a specific period of time. It can also be used to adjust an accrual or carryover rule at an employment milestone.
    
    For example, you might want employees to start with 10 vacation days, then set an increase after five years of employment to 15 vacation days. To do this, you would add two time-off rules to the plan using the same time-off type. One rule would have a minimum tenure of zero with an entitlement of 10 vacation days. The second rule would have a minimum tenure of five years with an entitlement of 15 vacation days.
    
    Note:
    
    If you set a minimum tenure on a time-off rule, keep in mind that the associated payroll item appears on employee pay statements only after an employee has met the minimum tenure.
    

Next, set up accruals for the time-off rule. Consider if you want time-off types to accrue by a fixed amount or by hours worked:

-   [Setting up an Accrual Based on a Fixed Amount With Payroll](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1520001732.html)
    
-   [Setting up an Accrual Based on Hours Worked With Payroll](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1520001797.html)
    

For more information, see [Accruals Based on Fixed Amounts and Accruals Based on Hours Worked](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519677578.html#subsect_1519682652).
