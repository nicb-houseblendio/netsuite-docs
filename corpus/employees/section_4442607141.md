Important:

This feature requires SuitePeople. For more information, see [SuitePeople Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1495573671.html).

To set up the Health and Safety SuiteApp, see the following topics:

-   [Prerequisites for the Health and Safety SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1494806282.html)
    
-   [Install the Health and Safety SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1494806321.html)
    
-   [Health and Safety SuiteApp Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_159713263059.html)
    
-   [Enabling the Workplace Incident Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4763700189.html)
    
-   [Setting the Default Folder for Attachments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1537334246.html)
    
-   [Configure Your Company Settings for Incident Tracking](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4443155723.html)
    

Note:

Currently, the Health and Safety SuiteApp supports only the US English language.
