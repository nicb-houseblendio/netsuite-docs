Important:

The WageEasy integration is available only to accounts where it's already provisioned. It's not included with the current SuitePeople WFM solution.

With Workforce Management (WFM), you can export worked time to WageEasy.

To set up and export hours to WageEasy, see the following procedures in this section.

1.  [Setting Up Unique WFM IDs](#bridgehead_0624054745)
    
2.  [Setting up WFM Schedules to Export to a WageEasy Department](#bridgehead_0624055135)
    
3.  [Exporting Hours Worked to Import to WageEasy](#bridgehead_0624055445)
    
4.  [Importing Hours Worked to WageEasy](#bridgehead_0624055716)
    

## Setting Up Unique WFM IDs {#bridgehead_0624054745}

All employees in WageEasy need to have their employee numbers and time cards set to the WFM unique ID.

#### To set up a unique WFM ID:

1.  Go to Workforce Management > Setup > Integrations.
    
2.  From the **Payroll systems** list, select **WageEasy**.
    
3.  Select the Roster Locations and pay range.
    
4.  Click **View Staff**.
    
5.  The column titled ID is the unique ID.
    

## Setting up WFM Schedules to Export to a WageEasy Department {#bridgehead_0624055135}

To have the approved hours from WFM appear in WageEasy departments, contact NetSuite Customer Support.

When the setup is ready, you'll see a list of WFM Roster Units with the corresponding department numbers appears in WageEasy. If you try to export before these are set up, the schedules won't export correctly.

If you've created a new roster unit with exportable shifts before the linking is complete, you'll also be unable to export. Send an email message with the correct department ID to the address mentioned.

## Exporting Hours Worked to Import to WageEasy {#bridgehead_0624055445}

Follow the procedure below.

#### To export hours worked from WFM:

1.  Go to Workforce Management > Setup > Integrations.
    
2.  From the **Payroll systems** list, select **WageEasy**.
    
3.  Select the date range for the pay period.
    
4.  Select the rosters you want to export.
    
5.  To show all approved leave for the period, click **Leave Report**
    
6.  Open WageEasy and enter the leave before importing the hours from WFM.
    
7.  Go back to the WageEasy page in WFM.
    
8.  To show the approved hours for the locations selected, click **Preview Shifts**.
    
    Make sure the hours approved are correct.
    
9.  To export the approved hours, click **Export Shifts**.
    
10.  Save the file to a known location to import into WageEasy.
     

## Importing Hours Worked to WageEasy {#bridgehead_0624055716}

After you've saved a file of hours worked from WFM, you can import the hours to WageEasy.

#### To import hours worked to WageEasy:

1.  Open WageEasy.
    
2.  Enter any employee leaves.
    
3.  In WageEasy, go to Wages > Auto.
    
4.  To import the timesheets, follow the prompts.
