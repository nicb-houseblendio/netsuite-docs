Important:

Starting **August 5, 2025**, SuitePeople Workforce Management apps that are not using the latest version will automatically be updated.

To safely upgrade to the latest version and avoid interruptions later, complete required actions before August 5, 2025.

For more information, see [Action Required: Upcoming SuitePeople Workforce Management Mobile App Version Release and Automatic Update](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1020938).

If the SuitePeople Workforce Management phone app loses Internet connection, the **Offline** badge will appear above your profile icon.

The following functions are still available without Internet connection:

-   Viewing your published shifts
    
-   Clocking in and out of your shifts (if this feature is enabled in your account)
    

After you restore Internet connection, a red loading badge appears above the time clock icon. This indicates that the app is retrieving and sending data.
