## Hours {#subsect_0723102709}

To access the **Hours** report, from the Workforce Management tab, go to Reports > Shifts > Hours.

The **Hours** report lets you generate scheduled data and approved shift data.

You can change the shift type on the reporting screen. If you choose **Actual shifts**, it displays shifts approved shifts. If you choose **Schedule shifts**, it displays scheduled shifts.

You can filter the **Hours** report and **Hours and times** report by employment status and staff members' age. These filters are helpful if you want to run a report for only casual or junior-aged staff.

The **Hours** and **Hours and Times** reports are related to employees' scheduled, timesheet, and approved shifts. The Hours reports display the wage breakdown which includes penalty rates and overtime setup in your account.

After setting the Hours report options, you can print employees' shifts by downloading a PDF file of the report.

For a list of all available metrics in each shifts report, see [Shifts Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0625082603.html#subsect_0724122723).

### Blended Overtime {#subsect_0820102724}

Note:

Blended overtime is not available if your setup uses wage agreements, fixed hourly rates, or salary wages.

WFM can calculate a blended or weighted overtime rate for payroll.

The blended overtime rate is calculated using the following formula:

Blended OT Rate = (Total Wage of Regular Hours ÷ Total Number of Regular Hours Worked) × Overtime Rate

The Total Wage of Regular hours covers differing pay rates.

The Overtime Rate is 0.5, or the rate is halved.

#### Calculating for Blended Overtime {#subsect_0820113013}

Refer to the following example:

Abbie Gibson works in an office as a Clerical Administrator for $10 per hour. She answers phones from home at $8 per hour. During the week, she works 44.5 hours of as a Clerical Administrator and 8 hours answering phones. She is paid overtime for any hours worked over 40 during the week.

Because Abbie worked a total of 52.5 hours during the week, she will be paid 12.5 hours of overtime.

Abbie's overtime rate multiplier is 1.5 times the regular rate.

In WFM, you can enter her extra hours for the fixed period type. For example, between 9 am and 11 am, for a total of 2 hours.

#### To calculate for blended overtime:

1.  Calculate the employee's base pay without any overtime.
    
    Abbie's Base Pay = (8 Hours × $8 per Hour) + (44.5 Hours x $10 per Hour)
    
    Abbie's Base Pay = $509
    
2.  Calculate the employee's weighted base rate.
    
    Abbie's Weighted Base Rate = Base Pay ÷ Total Number of Regular Hours Worked
    
    Abbie's Weighted Base Rate = $509 ÷ 52.5 Hours
    
    Abbie's Weighted Base Rate = $9.70 per Hour
    
3.  Calculate the additional loading for overtime.
    
    Additional OT Loading per Hour = Weighted Base Rate × 0.5 OT Multiplier
    
    Additional OT Loading per Hour = $9.70 per Hour × 0.5 OT Multiplier
    
    Additional OT Loading per Hour = $4.85
    
4.  Calculate the cost of the overtime segments within the Clerical Admin shifts.
    
    Cost of Overtime Segments = Clerical Administrator Hourly Pay + Additional OT Loading per Hour
    
    Cost of Overtime Segments = $10 per Hour + $4.85 per Hour
    
    Cost of Overtime Segments = $14.85 per Hour
    

Looking at the totals from the shift hours report, you can see that Abbie will be paid $569.60.

## Variance (Tardy+) {#subsect_93161641037}

To access the **Variance (Tardy+)** report, from the Workforce Management tab, go to Reports > Shifts > Variance (Tardy+).

The Variance (Tardy+) report lets you use presets of company or employee time fraud. You can also create your own report to compare two shift types over a period of time. For example, you can compare timesheet shifts to actual shifts for staff members.

The Variance (Tardy+) report will display the approvers' names for staff members' shifts. If the shift is automatically matched, there won't be an approver's name displayed.

The Variance (Tardy+) report also displays total shift costs. You can compare the variance of hours between the two shifts for a staff member and the cost variance of those shifts.

Note:

You'll only see costs for users in lower access groups.

You can download a CSV file of the data included in the report.

For a list of all available metrics in each shifts report, see [Shifts Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0625082603.html#subsect_0724122723).

## DST Ambiguous Shifts {#subsect_0723010606}

To access the **DST Ambiguous Shifts** report, from the Workforce Management tab, go to Reports > Shifts > DST Ambiguous Shifts.

During a daylight savings time (DST) backward transition, the times between 2:00 am and 3:00 am, or other times depending on your timezone, are repeated. Because of this, any punch in or out events that occur during this two-hour period can be ambiguous. This report shows all actual shifts at every location with events during this period on a selected DST transition day. It won't show any shifts if the selected day isn't a backward DST transition day.

You can download a CSV file of the data included in the report.

For more information, see [Daylight Savings Times (DST)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1019103257.html).

For a list of all available metrics in each shifts report, see [Shifts Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0625082603.html#subsect_0724122723).

For a list of all available metrics in each shifts report, see [Shifts Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0625082603.html#subsect_0724122723).

## Punch Breaks {#section_1013041912}

To access the **Punch Breaks** report, from the Workforce Management tab, go to Reports > Shifts > Punch Breaks.

This report lets you identify paid and unpaid breaks taken by employees. With this report, you can filter by punch breaks by location, break type, shift length, employment status, age range, and number of breaks.

When you run the report, you can filter by:

-   Date range
    
-   Locations
    
-   Employment status
    
-   Age range
    
-   Shift Length (including breaks)
    
-   Minimum unpaid breaks
    
-   Minimum number of paid breaks
    
-   Types of break taken
    
    Note:
    
    If you don't select a type of break, shifts with that break type won't be included. For example, if you don't select unpaid breaks, shifts with unpaid breaks won't show up. The same goes for paid breaks, even if you selected the paid break option.
    

The report lets you download a CSV file with detailed information relevant to punch breaks.

For a list of all available metrics in each shifts report, see [Shifts Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0625082603.html#subsect_0724122723).
