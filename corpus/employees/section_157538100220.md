You can create a rating scale to include on a performance review template. Rating scales are used for questions that need both a text response and a rating, and for the overall rating on a performance review. For example, your rating scale might include the options Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, and Needs Improvement.

For more information, check out the following:

-   [Creating a Performance Review Rating Scale](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158620086799.html)
