Restoring an inactive job is a simple process, the first step is to go to Setup > Jobs and select "Restore Jobs".

A list of all of the inactive jobs will show up, all you have to do is select the job you want to restore.

You will be given a warning before being able to restore your job. Click the "Restore (Job name)" button to complete the process.
