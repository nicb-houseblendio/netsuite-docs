To create custom restrictions for Advanced Employee Permissions, the Setup > Manage Custom Restrictions Permission is required at access Level Full. The standard role that comes with this permission is the Administrator role. You can also create custom roles that include this permission. For more information, see [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html).
