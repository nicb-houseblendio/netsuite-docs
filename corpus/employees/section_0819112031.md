First step that you will need to take is to go on to the job screen, which can be accessed through Setup > Jobs. you are on this screen you will need to click on "New Job" to begin.

Next you will have to enter in a name, code and color for the job. The code can be anything you prefer such as SC for sous-chef. The color will allow you to easily identify the job on the schedule view.

A description of the job is optional, but is highly when being used across numerous locations. you are happy with the job information hit "Save" to finish.
