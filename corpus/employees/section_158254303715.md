This dataset pulls fields from the Employee and Bonus records. It lets you see a summary of employee's current base pay and their bonus history. It's the source data for the [Employee Bonus History and Current Compensation Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157661535012.html).

## Dataset Configuration {#subsect_158254397005}

As you use this workbook template, keep in mind that Advanced Employee Permissions can affect which employee fields you can see. Advanced Employee Permissions don't affect bonus fields, but employee and subsidiary restrictions on your role can limit your access to bonus information.

| Root Record Type | Joined Record Type | Custom Formula Field | Data Grid | Criteria Filters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Employee | Bonus | (none) | The following fields are included in the dataset. Employee:
-   Base Wage
-   Base Wage Type
-   Compensation Currency
-   Entity ID

Bonus:

-   Amount
-   Award Date
-   Bonus Type
-   Currency
