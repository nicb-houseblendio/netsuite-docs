The Time-Off Management feature includes reporting capabilities you can use to retrieve, present, and analyze your time-off data.

The following reports are included with the Time-Off Management feature:

-   [Available Time-Off Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1494612843.html)
    
-   [Time-Off Balance Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1494612906.html)
    
-   [Time-Off Balance Details Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1494612958.html)
    
-   [Time-Off Analysis Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156148807002.html)
    
-   [Time-Off Tracking Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1494947371.html)
    

If no standard report fits your needs, you can create an ad hoc report. For more information, see [Time-Off Management Ad Hoc Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1494873407.html).

You can also customize these standard reports in the Report Builder by clicking **Customize** on the results page. For more information, see [Report Builder Interface](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N736822.html) and [Report Customization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N736328.html).
