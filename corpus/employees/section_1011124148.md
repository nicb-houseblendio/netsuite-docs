To export shifts, you must link wage agreements to MYOB.

#### To link wage agreements to MYOB:

1.  Go to Workforce Management > Setup > Integrations > MYOB.
    
2.  Click **Agreements**.
    
3.  The Link objects to Payrollitems page opens to the **Conditions** subtab.
    
    Determine the payroll items you need to link with each condition, including age group, if required.
    
4.  Link the payroll items. For more information, see [Linking Payroll Items in WFM](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1003104314.html).
