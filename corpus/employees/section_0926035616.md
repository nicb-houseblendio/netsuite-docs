After you activate and review the configurations of the Fast Food Industry Award \[MA000003\] preconfigured agreement, it is available to be assigned to employee wages.

The agreement covers employees in the fast food industry and those who fit within the classifications of the award.

For more information about the full coverage and classification descriptions of the Fast Food Industry Award, go to the Fair Work Ombudsman website.

## Fast Food Industry Award Classifications {#subsect_0927115014}

Refer to the following table for the default classification settings of the preconfigured agreement.

| Classification | Level | Employment Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Level 1 | Full-time and Part-time |
| Level 1 Casual | Level 1 | Casual |
| Level 2 | Level 2 | Full-time and Part-time |
| Level 2 Casual | Level 2 | Casual |
| Level 3a | Level 3 | Full-time and Part-time |
| Level 3a Casual | Level 3 | Casual |
| Level 3b | Level 4 | Full-time and Part-time |
| Level 3b Casual | Level 4 | Casual |

## Fast Food Industry Award Conditions {#subsect_0927115154}

Refer to the following table for the default conditions of the preconfigured agreement.

| Condition | Rules | Employment Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular Hours (Regular Rate) | Every day | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| After 10pm (Penalty Rate) | Every day between 10 p.m. and midnight | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| After Midnight (Penalty Rate) | Every day between midnight and 6 a.m. | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Saturday (Penalty Rate) | Every Saturday | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Sunday (Penalty Rate) | Every Sunday | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Public Holiday (Penalty Rate) | Every day that is a public holiday | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Standard Overtime (Overtime Rate) | 
-   Every day when during the day time worked exceeds 11 hours
-   Every day when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours
-   Every day when more than 5 days are worked this week
-   Every day when more than 4 days are worked this week and more than 5 days were worked last week

 | Part-time and Full-time |
| Overtime more than 2 hours (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every day when during the day time matching Standard Overtime worked exceeds 2 hours
-   Every day when during the week time worked exceeds 40 hours
-   Every Sunday when during the day time worked exceeds 11 hours
-   Every Sunday when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours

 | Part-time and Full-time |
| FT/PT Sunday Overtime (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every Sunday when during the day time worked exceeds 11 hours
-   Every Sunday when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours
-   Every Sunday when more than 5 days are worked this week
-   Every Sunday when more than 4 days are worked this week and more than 5 days were worked last week

 | Part-time and Full-time |
| Public Holiday Overtime (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every day that is a public holiday when during the day time worked exceeds 11 hours
-   Every day that is a public holiday when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours
-   Every day that is a public holiday when more than 5 days are worked this week
-   Every day that is a public holiday when more than 4 days are worked this week and more than 5 days were worked last week

 | Part-time and Full-time |
| Casual Standard Overtime (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every day when during the day time worked exceeds 11 hours
-   Every day when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours

 | Casual |
| Overtime more than 2 hours (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every day when during the day time matching Casual Standard Overtime worked exceeds 2 hours
-   Every day when during the week time worked exceeds 40 hours
-   Every Sunday when during the day time worked exceeds 11 hours
-   Every Sunday when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours

 | Casual |
| Public Holiday Overtime (Overtime Rate) | 

-   Every day that is a public holiday when during the day time worked exceeds 11 hours
-   Every day that is a public holiday when during the week time worked exceeds 38 hours
