To view an employee's time-off balance, go to _Lists >Employees > Employees_. On the Employees list, click **View** next to the employee you want to check, then click the **Time-Off** subtab to see a summary of their time-off balance for the entire year.

The **Available Now** subtab displays how much time off an employee has left, and how much they've used and scheduled for the current year. This subtab includes:

-   **Available This Year** - If the **Days Available Includes Future Accruals** box on the Time-Off Plan page is checked, the time-off plan includes all future accruals and manual increases and decreases for the current year. For more information, see [Time-Off Plans](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1494524174.html).
    
-   **Used This Year** - The amount of time off that's been used as of the current date.
    
-   **Scheduled This Year** - The amount of time off that's been requested and approved, but not yet taken.
    
-   **Available Now** - The total remaining time off for the current year.
    

The **Balances** subtab displays an employee's available balance as of the current date, based on the accruals and carry over rules defined in the time-off plan. This subtab includes:

-   **Carried Over** - The amount of time off that's carried over as of the current date.
    
-   **Accrued** - The amount of time off that's accrued as of the current date.
    
-   **Used** - The amount of used time off.
    
-   **Expired Carryover** - The amount of time off that's expired as of the current date.
    
-   **Balance** - Displays the balance as of the current date based on the amount of time carried over, accrued, used, and any expired carry over.
