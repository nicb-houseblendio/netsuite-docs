SuitePeople Time Clock is the SuitePeople Workforce Management time and attendance app previously referred to as AdiClock.

The app enables organizations to efficiently measure, record, and manage employee work hours digitally. It is a cloud-based app that syncs employees' clock-in and clock-out times with WFM. During approvals, managers or HR employees can compare employee punch data with their scheduled shifts.

## Benefits of SuitePeople Time Clock {#subsect_1126014910}

Following are some benefits of using the app:

-   Employees can clock in and out of shifts directly using an iOS or Android mobile device or a Windows computer.
    
-   Organizations can accurately track employee time. SuitePeople Time Clock automatically records start or end times, breaks, and meal periods. These features reduce manual entry errors and buddy punching.
    
-   Managers and employees experience streamlined calculations for wages and overtime. The app feeds accurate time and attendance data directly into approval processes.
    
-   By accurately tracking work hours and breaks, the app helps organizations comply with labor laws and company attendance policies.
    
-   Managers and HR employees gain real-time visibility into attendance, absences, and labor costs, supporting workforce planning and management.
