When Advanced Employee Permissions is enabled you can view employee templates, but you can't create or edit employee templates. Editing or creating employee templates is supported only with the Lists > Employees permission. For more information about employee templates, see [Creating an Employee Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N899487.html).
