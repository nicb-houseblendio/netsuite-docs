To use Workforce Management, you'll need to:

-   Provide your account ID to your NetSuite representative.
    
-   Enable the Token-Based Authentication feature.
    

## Account ID {#subsect_5232741270}

Give your NetSuite Account ID to your NetSuite rep, and the NetSuite team will make the Workforce Management (WFM) SuiteApp available in your account.

For more information, see [Finding Your NetSuite Account ID](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1498754928.html).

## Enable Token-Based Authentication (TBA) {#subsect_18232753615}

TBA lets WFM use a token to access NetSuite through APIs.

For more information, see [Enable the Token-based Authentication Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4253254429.html).

To continue setting up WFM, see [Installing the SuitePeople Workforce Management SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_24214051946.html).
