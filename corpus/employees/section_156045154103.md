The Employee Center is the primary workspace for processing approvals. You need a custom Employee Center role to access employee change requests in the Employee Center. An administrator can add a custom Employee Center role to your account. For more information, see [Adding an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N894212.html).

To use employee change requests in the Employee Center, see the following topics:

-   [Creating an Employee Change Request from the Employee Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156097562247.html)
    
-   [Approving or Declining an Employee Change Request](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156097609979.html)
    
-   [Viewing an Employee Change Request](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156097755581.html)
