**Where can I view my Form W-2 and Form W-2 C?**

You can view your Form W-2 and Form W-2 C only when you choose Employee Center from the list of assigned roles. To know more about how to switch the roles, see [Roles and Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N295370.html). The latest five records for Form W-2 and Form W-2 C can be viewed under the **Form W-2** subtab of the **Payroll** portlet. Click **View All** in the **Form W-2** subtab to view all the forms that have been uploaded to NetSuite for your organization. See [View your Form W-2 and Form W-2 C](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9150746464.html#subsect_0127043453) for more information.

Note:

The Form W-2 and Form W-2 C will be available for download effective from the financial year of 2024 onward. The forms for 2024 will be available for download from the Employee Center at a later date. If you want to download the forms for 2024 before that date, contact your payroll team.

**What should I do if there is a mistake on my Form W-2? What is Form W-2 C?**

If you notice any inaccuracy in your W-2, you need to inform your payroll team. In such cases, the necessary corrections will be made and a Form W-2 C will be issued. The appended C to a W-2 denotes it is an amendment to the original W-2 issued by the IRS. See [Download Form W-2 and Form W-2 C](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9150746464.html) for more information.

Note:

The Form W-2 and Form W-2 C will be available for download effective from the financial year of 2024 onward. The forms for 2024 will be available for download from the Employee Center at a later date. If you want to download the forms for 2024 before that date, contact your payroll team.

**Who can download and view my Form W-2?**

As an employee, only you can download and view your Form W-2. Your Payroll Administrator can also download and view it. However, neither you nor your Administrator can modify any details in the form. Any modifications or rectifications need to be processed through a support ticket and a Form W-2 C will be issued with the amended information.

**What should I do if I forgot the password I set for my downloaded Form W-2?**

If you forget the password you set for your Form W-2, you'll need to download it again. See [Download Your Form W-2 and Form W-2 C as a PDF Document](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9150746464.html#subsect_0127045100) for more information.

**Where can I view my downloaded Form W-2?**

You should be able to view the downloaded PDF file in the default location where all your downloads are saved on the device.

**How do I print my Form W-2?**

Download the form as a PDF file following the steps in [Download Your Form W-2 and Form W-2 C as a PDF Document](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9150746464.html#subsect_0127045100). After downloading the form, you can print the form following the instructions in the PDF viewer you are using.

**What if I lose my access to NetSuite? Can I still get my Form W-2 and Form W-2 C?**

There are two scenarios where you may lose access to NetSuite.

-   The first scenario is when you are no longer working the organization and your access to NetSuite has been revoked.
    
-   The second scenario is you have left and rejoined the organization. However, your details have been updated in a new Employee record. Thereby, you will not be able to access the old Employee record.
    

In both the scenarios, you need to contact your Payroll Administrator who can download the required forms and share it with you.
