If you remove all widgets from your dashboard you'll see two options.

Click Add Historical Graph to add a graph of a particular KPI for a day in the past

Click Add Live Graph - this will show you a graph of particular KPI you select for the current day

Select your Restaurant and the metric you want to graph.

this has been added you will see that graph as a rectangular widget on screen. You can then add additional tiles by clicking the + in the bottom right

Options to add a historical or live graph then appear. You can then continue adding the graphs you want.

Live graph will ask you for a location, KPI to view, as well as the time period you'd like to appear.

You can hit the + magnifying glass to expand a graph you want to view at a larger resolution.

The following fields are adjustable to your preferences:

-   Date
    
-   Resolution
    

The important one to be able to play with is the date field on the historic graph, so you can have access to any day.
