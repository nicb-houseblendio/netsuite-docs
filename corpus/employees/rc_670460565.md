With the Performance Management feature, you can create, manage, and set targets for your own goals. You can use performance metrics to automatically update the actual value of your goal with data from NetSuite. You can also associate your goals with an area of focus.

If you're a manager, you're responsible for approving your direct reports' planned goals. Your direct reports can't change the status of goals to in progress until they're approved. You can comment on your direct reports' goals to provide guidance, but you can't edit your direct reports' goals or create goals for them.

The best way to manage goals is to use your Employee Center role. You can also manage your goals at _Setup > Performance Management > Goals_.
