If you have the Administrator role, you can enable the feature required to view an enhanced weekly timesheets interface using Workforce Management (WFM). If you don't have the role, reach out to the person in your company who manages NetSuite.

#### To enable the feature:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Enable Features.
    
2.  On the **Employees** subtab, go to **Time & Expenses**.
    
3.  Check the **Enhanced Timesheets Using Workforce Management Wage Rules** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

To read about the complete setup instructions for the feature, see [Setting Up Enhanced Timesheets Using WFM](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0812065915.html).
