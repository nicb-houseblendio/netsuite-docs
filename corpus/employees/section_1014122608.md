Important:

As of February 18, 2025, support ends for SuitePeople Workforce Management Time Clock and SuitePeople Workforce Management mobile applications on Android 11 and earlier versions.

If you're using these versions, update your Android devices to version 12 or later to continue using WFM apps.

For more information, see _SuitePeople Workforce Management on Unsupported Android Devices_.

Read this page to know more about how SuitePeople Time Clock works with timesheets.

## Timesheet Sources {#subsect_28084357022}

There are different icons on a timesheet shift that show how the employee clocked in or clocked out for a shift.

Note:

AdiClock is now called SuitePeople Time Clock.

In the iOS App Store and Google Play Store, SuitePeople Time Clock is called **NetSuite Time Clock**.

In Windows, the app is targeted to be renamed **SuitePeople Time Clock** in June 2024.

-   Phone app - The following screenshot shows an employee's timesheet shift that has a phone icon. This indicates the employee clocked in and out on their phone.
    
-   SuitePeople Time Clock - The following screenshot shows an employee's timesheet shift that has a building icon. This indicates the employee clocked in and out of SuitePeople Time Clock app at their shift's location.
    
-   Mixed or multiple sources - The following screenshot shows an employee's timesheet shift that has a two-arrow icon. This indicates the employee clocked in and out of SuitePeople Time Clock from more than one source. For example, the employee may have clocked in on their phone and clocked out at their shift's location.
    

To display a detailed breakdown of an employee's clock-in and clock-out events during their shift, you can click on the timesheet shift to expand it.

The clock-in and clock-out events have a green pin icon beside them, which means the clock-in and clock-out events were completed on the job site.

## Force Clocking out Employees {#subsect_19085027673}

There are two ways to clock out an employee if they have forgotten to clock out:

-   From the Weekly or Multi-Location view
    
-   Ask a manager to clock out an employee from the device using SuitePeople Time Clock.
    

Note:

SuitePeople Time Clock force clocks out employees after 24 hours.

From the Weekly or Multi-Location view, you can click the **Eject** button on an employee's open timesheet.

After the employee's been clocked out, their timesheet will keep the original start time of the shift and the time they clocked out at.

For more information about clocking out employees, see [Using SuitePeople Time Clock for Windows](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_2105712589.html).

## Auto-closing Timesheets {#subsect_38085349296}

Timesheets will automatically close if they're left open for 24 hours and employees will automatically be clocked out. This occurs on the phone app and SuitePeople Time Clock for Windows, Android, and iOS.
