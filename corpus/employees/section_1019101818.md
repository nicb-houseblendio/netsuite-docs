Important:

Starting **August 5, 2025**, SuitePeople Workforce Management apps that are not using the latest version will automatically be updated.

To safely upgrade to the latest version and avoid interruptions later, complete required actions before August 5, 2025.

For more information, see [Action Required: Upcoming SuitePeople Workforce Management Mobile App Version Release and Automatic Update](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1020938).

If the **Drop Shifts** feature is enabled in your account, employees can start a drop shift request in the app if they can't make their shift.

To drop a shift, the employee selects their scheduled shift in the app and selects **Drop**. They'll be asked to enter a reason and told the shift is still theirs until the manager approves the drop. After entering the reson, they click **Request**.

The manager then receives a trade notification in the app. They can review the reason and either deny the request by clicking **No** or pre-approve the drop and find a fill-in by clicking **Fill**.

Note:

By default, the **Drop Pre-Approval** feature is enabled. If this feature is off, managers don't need to approve the dropped shift. The dropped shift will automatically send a request to all available employees. When someone picks up the shift, the manager will be notified to approve or deny the this change. You can change this by going to **Setup > Adi phone app.**
