When you're logged in, you'll land on a page that shows all today's published shifts and includes:

-   Clicking the icon at the top right displays different options for viewing shifts. For example, you can filter by location, or view only your shifts.
    
-   The dates show each date of the month and week. You can select any days you want to view shifts for and click the **Today** button to return to the current day.
    
-   The navigation bar is located at the bottom of the app. You'll only see Time Clock if your company and locations have it set up. The tabs from left to right are: published shifts, Time Clock (if set up), trades, notifications, and your profile.
    
-   A list of published shifts.

## Viewing Employee Clock-in and Clock-out Events {#subsect_79101631043}

As a manager, you'll get a notification if an employee's shift starts and they haven't clocked in yet.

If employees are late to clock in, the app shows you how late they are with the minutes in red and an exclamation point next to their shift.

When they're clocked in, a clock icon appears next to their shift. The clock-in time appears in black and replaces their scheduled start time, and the scheduled finish time appears in grey.

When the employee clocks out, the clock-out time appears in black and replaces the scheduled finish time. These punch times stay visible for 15 hours, then return to showing the scheduled times.
