With WFM, you can assign preconfigured wage agreements to employees according to the industry or occupation awards that cover their work in Australia.

In preconfigured wage agreements, the following components are set up to apply the specifications and coverage of the each award to employee pay:

-   Conditions and rules designed to calculate pay rates and entitlements
    
-   Employee classifications with assigned pay rates
    
-   Allowances and penalties for entitled employees
    

To see the industry awards covered in WFM as well as their default settings, see [Awards with Pre-configured Wage Agreements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0926035423.html).

You must activate each preconfigured agreement so you can assign them to employees. For more information, see [Using Pre-configured Wage Agreements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0926035940.html).

In preconfigured agreements, you can associate jobs with classifications, allowances, and penalties and view all information. You cannot make any changes to conditions, rules, or rates. For more information, see [Assigning Jobs for Pre-Configured Wage Agreements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1002010513.html).

If you want to ensure that linked payroll items for allowances, penalties, and multipliers are carried over with each new revision, see [Linking New Revisions to Pre-Configured Wage Agreements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0926040000.html).

Important:

Oracle NetSuite provides wage rules as a convenience for customers. Nothing in the above is legal advice. Oracle NetSuite does not warrant the reliability, accuracy, legal compliance, completeness, correctness, or usefulness of its rules or configurations.

You must verify if the wage rules and other preconfigured rules provide the correct pay to your employees in compliance with any applicable local or federal labor laws. Please consult with your own attorney or expert on the appropriate rules to use in your business or industry in all jurisdictions in which you operate.
