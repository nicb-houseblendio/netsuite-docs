Users with Finance Co-ordinator access and above can add Visa details to employees on Visa restrictions.

Entitlements, expiry date, and proof of visa documents can be added. The expiry date will notify selected users within WFM before the visa details expire.

The visa details page includes:

-   Expiry date
    
-   Passport number
    
-   Country of issue
    
-   Nationality
    
-   Visa class
    
-   Work entitlement
    
-   Notes
    
-   Associated documents
    

## Term Breaks in Visa Details {#subsect_0717044041}

Term breaks appear only for employees who have a visa set up in WFM.

For more information about term breaks and how they're used in WFM, see [Term Breaks in WFM for ESS Extended](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0717041303.html).
