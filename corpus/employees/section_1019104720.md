Important:

Starting **August 5, 2025**, SuitePeople Workforce Management apps that are not using the latest version will automatically be updated.

To safely upgrade to the latest version and avoid interruptions later, complete required actions before August 5, 2025.

For more information, see [Action Required: Upcoming SuitePeople Workforce Management Mobile App Version Release and Automatic Update](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1020938).

When employees select the **Time Clock** icon, they are taken to the **Time Clock** page.

An employee can start a paid rest break by selecting **Rest**. The app automatically clocks in the employee to their break. If a employee starts an unpaid break, they need to select **Start Break**.

Employees can only take one unpaid break but they can take multiple paid breaks. After the unpaid break is completed, the **Start Break** option is unavailable.

If you have rest breaks enabled and an employee hasn't clocked in for a paid rest break during their shift, the app asks the employee if they've taken a paid rest break.

Answering either **Yes** or **No** will change the message that appears on their timesheet shift in the weekly view.

If the employee selects **No**, their timesheet shift will display a message that says: "Employee has indicated they did not take a Rest Break".
