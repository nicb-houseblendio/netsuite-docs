Following are the minimum requirements for mobile devices to run **NetSuite Workforce Management** apps.

| Requirement Type | Details | Related Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hardware Requirements | 
-   Global Positioning System (GPS)
-   Front-facing and rear-facing cameras with autofocus

 | \- |
| Software Requirements | 

-   Android version 12.0 or later Important: As of February 18, 2025, support ends for SuitePeople Workforce Management Time Clock and SuitePeople Workforce Management mobile applications on Android 11 and earlier versions. For more information, see _SuitePeople Workforce Management on Unsupported Android Devices_.
-   iPad OS version 13 or later

 | 

-   [NetSuite Workforce Management for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.adiinsights.adi)
-   [NetSuite Workforce Management for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/netsuite-workforce-management/id1516214747)

 |
