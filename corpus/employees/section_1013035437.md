The Employee Payroll Query report lets you view information for staff members' scheduled shifts, timesheet shifts, and approved shifts.

You can run the report for one staff member at a time for any time period.

#### To run the Employee Payroll Query report: {#procedure_1202093001}

1.  From the Workforce Management tab, go to Reports > Shifts > Employee Payroll Query.
    
2.  Choose the staff member whose shift you want to see, and select the start and end dates for the time period you want to view.
    
3.  Choose whether you want to view the staff member's scheduled shifts, timesheet shifts, or approved shifts. You can also select all the options.
    
4.  Select **Fetch**.
    

Note:

You can download this report as a CSV file.

## Scheduled and Timesheet Shifts {#subsect_36103337746}

Scheduled shifts and timesheet shifts can have the following statuses:

-   **Approved -** Approved shifts have been approved by a manager. An approved shift has a thumbs-up icon or a dialog to edit and approve the shift.
    
-   **Denied -** Denied shifts have been denied by a manager. A denied shift has a thumbs-down icon.
    
-   **Unprocessed -** Unprocessed shifts have been neither approved nor denied by a manager.
    

If there is a matching scheduled shift and timesheet shift, one of them will be approved and the other will be unprocessed. New shifts appear on new lines.

## Actual Shifts {#subsect_52103319992}

Actual shifts are scheduled shifts or timesheet shifts which have been approved. Actual shifts may or may not have a matching scheduled shift or timesheet shift.

Actual shifts can have the following statuses:

-   **Processed -** Processed shifts are exported to payroll, or the pay period has been entered in Adi Insights as a locked period.
    
-   **Unprocessed -** Unprocessed shifts are not exported to payroll.
    

If an actual shift hasn't been processed, the "i" icon beside the shift will show which manager approved the shift and when it was approved.
