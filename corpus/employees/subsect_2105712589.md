You'll see different options when employees clock in with or without a scheduled shift.

## Clocking in on a Scheduled Shift {#subsect_0919123328}

Employees who clock in with a scheduled shift can use the Quick Punch feature.

The Quick Punch feature is available if the schedule's been published when employees punch in. If they don't see it, the schedule might not be published or the might not have any scheduled shifts.

#### To clock in with Quick Punch on a scheduled shift:

1.  Open SuitePeople Time Clock on your Windows device.
    
2.  On the keypad screen, enter your PIN code.
    
3.  Click **Enter**.
    
    If the schedule was published, the screen shows a green **Quick Punch** button.
    
4.  (Optional) In the **Notes** field, enter details related to the clock-in event.
    
5.  If you need to clock in to a job or location that's different than what's scheduled, click **Change**.
    
6.  Select the new location and job.
    
7.  Click **Quick Punch**.
    

## Clocking in on an Unscheduled Shift {#subsect_0919022821}

If an employee clocks in for an unscheduled shift, they'll need to select the location and job they are clocking into.

#### To clock in on an unscheduled shift:

1.  Open SuitePeople Time Clock on your Windows device.
    
2.  On the keypad screen, enter your PIN code.
    
3.  Click **Enter**.
    
4.  On the **Locations** section, select the location where you will work your shift.
    
5.  On the **Jobs** section, select the job that you will take for this shift.
    
6.  (Optional) In the **Notes** field, enter details related to the clock-in event.
    
7.  Click **Punch-in**.
    
    The screen shows a green screen that confirms your location, job, and punch-in time.
    
    Note:
    
    When you successfully clock in, you won't be able to clock out for 10 seconds.
    
    The next screen shows the main Time Clock screen, with the employee name listed in the **Currently Working** panel.
    

## Changing Jobs During a Shift {#subsect_0920122959}

If you have multiple jobs set up for the location where you are clocked in, you can change jobs during your shift.

#### To change jobs during a shift:

1.  Open SuitePeople Time Clock on your Windows device.
    
    On the main Time Clock screen, your name is listed in the **Currently Working** panel.
    
2.  On the keypad screen, enter your PIN code.
    
3.  Click **Enter**.
    
4.  On the **Jobs** section, select the job that you must change to for this shift.
    
5.  Click **Job Change**.
    
    The screen shows confirmation of the job change, including the location and current job on the shift.
    

## Clocking Out of a Shift {#subsect_0920103737}

Clock out of shifts on the main screen of SuitePeople Time Clock.

#### To clock out of a shift:

1.  Open SuitePeople Time Clock on your Windows device.
    
    On the main Time Clock screen, your name is listed in the **Currently Working** panel.
    
2.  On the keypad screen, enter your PIN code.
    
3.  Click **Enter**.
    
4.  On the **Jobs** section, select the job that you'll take for this shift.
    
5.  (Optional) In the **Notes** field, enter details related to the job change event.
    
6.  Click **Punch-out**.
    
    The screen shows a screen that confirms that you have punched out.
    
    The next screen shows the main Time Clock screen, with the employee name listed in the **Recently Finished** panel.
    

## Clocking In and Out for Multiple Units {#subsect_1120428653}

On the SuitePeople Time Clock main window, enter your PIN. Select **Enter**.

If multiple locations are active, a dialog appears asking the employee to select where they're working.

Note: The **Please select a unit** dialog appears if the employee clocking in is available at both locations. It can also appear if the **Show all staff** option was selected when the activation code was generated.

In the **Currently active staff** list, you will see the employee you have clocked in.
