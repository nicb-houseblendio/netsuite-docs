When a preconfigured agreement is revised, its conditions are updated and applied to the exported data. You will, however, need to link allowances, penalties, and multipliers to payroll items again.

If you want to automatically link these payroll items to new agreement revisions, switch this WFM setting on.

#### To link new revisions to pre-configured wage agreements:

1.  Go to Setup > NetSuite Integration > Settings.
    
2.  Click the **Configurations** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Auto link new revisions** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
