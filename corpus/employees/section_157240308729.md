Read the answers to the following questions for more information about the SuiteApp.

### How many plans and templates can I create using the SuiteApp?

You can create as many plans and templates as you want.

### Can I assign a task to multiple assignees?

Yes, you can assign a task to more than one person, and there is no limit on the number of assignees.

### Why can't I see the count changing on the Reminders or Onboarding portlet even after I completed a task?

Click the Refresh icon on the Reminders or Onboarding portlet to see the most recent task counts.

### Does the SuiteApp account for business days and holidays when computing due dates?

This feature isn't available.

### What is the time zone used in the SuiteApp?

The SuiteApp uses the time zone set in your company preferences.
