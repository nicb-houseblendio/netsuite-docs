Follow the procedure below.

#### To link locations to ADP (US) in WFM:

1.  Go to Workforce Management > Setup > Integrations > ADP (US).
    
2.  Click **Locations**.
    
3.  Find the locations you want to link.
    
    -   In the Search field, enter the name of the location.
        
    -   From the list beside the Search field, filter to view either:
        
        -   All Locations
            
        -   Unlinked Locations
            
        -   Linked Locations
            
4.  In the **Department number** column of the location, enter the ADP department number.
    
    Department numbers have either 3 or 6 characters, depending on what you have set this to in the initial setup of the integration. To change character count settings, go to Workforce Management > Payroll > ADP (US) > Detail.
    
5.  (Optional) To unlink a location from a department, click **Unlink** on the corresponding row.
