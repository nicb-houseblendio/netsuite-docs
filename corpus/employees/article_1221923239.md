SuitePeople Workforce Management (WFM) gives you one solution to streamline shift scheduling, track time and attendance for your team, and calculate wages.

The visual scheduling tool lets managers see exactly where they need to add or adjust shifts.

Employees can clock in and out on a computer or mobile device with digital time tracking.

The mobile app makes it convenient for managers and employees to view schedules, manage shift swaps, and more.

The built-in wage rules automatically calculates wages and works seamlessly with SuitePeople U.S. Payroll.
