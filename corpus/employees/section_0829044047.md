Follow the procedure.

#### To upload a driver's license for an employee:

1.  Go to Workforce Management > Setup > Employees > List.
    
2.  Click an employee's ID.
    
3.  Under Employment documents, click **Add new**.
    
4.  Fill in the required information.
    
    1.  In the **Label** field, enter **Driver's license**.
        
    2.  From the **Type** field, select **Personal Details**.
        
    3.  Upload the file from your device.
        
    4.  Set the expiry date of the driver's license.
        
    5.  Click **Upload**.
        
    
    The uploaded document will be saved and appear in the list of Employment documents.
    
5.  Make sure document expiry notifications are set up correctly.
    
    1.  In Workforce Management, go to Setup > Notifications.
        
    2.  Go to **Expiring Document**.
        
    3.  Check the reminder boxes and set the notification period that suits you.
        
6.  Make sure document expiry notifications are set up correctly.
    
    You will receive a notification when there is an expiring driver's license. This notification appears in your notification page.
