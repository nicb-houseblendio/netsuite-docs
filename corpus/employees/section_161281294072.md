This workbook uses the Performance Management - Goals dataset and the Performance Management - Reviews dataset. With the Performance Management - Goals and Reviews workbook, you can assess goal and performance review related information for the employees in your organization. The pivot tables and charts included in this workbook have filters to display relevant data.

To access the workbook, click the **Performance Management - Goals and Reviews** link from the Workbook Templates list on the Workbook subtab of the Analytics Home page.

The following pivot tables and charts are predefined in the Performance Management - Goals and Reviews workbook:

-   **Goals by Status** - A chart that displays how many goals are in each status.
    
-   **Overdue Goals** - A pivot table that displays how many days an employee is late to start a goal, close a goal, or if their goal is not overdue.
    
-   **Goal Outliers** - A pivot table that displays how many goals an employee has and how many of their goals haven't been updated in two months. Human resources can use this information to follow up with employees on unusual goal activity.
    
-   **Performance Metrics Alignment** - A chart that displays the number of goals that are tracked with each performance metric.
    
-   **Review by Status** - A chart that displays how many performance reviews tare in each status.
    
-   **Overdue Reviews** - A pivot table that displays the number of days an employee's self review is late, a reviewer's performance review is late, and an employee's acknowledgment is late by more than thirty days. It doesn't display reviews that aren't overdue.
    
-   **Overall Rating Distribution** - A chart that displays the percentage of performance reviews that were given each type of overall rating.
    
-   **Review Outliers** - A pivot table that displays the difference between a reviewer's overall rating and an employee's overall rating on performance reviews, plus how many characters an employee or reviewer wrote per answer on performance reviews. Human resources can use this information to follow up with employees and reviewers on any unusual performance review activity.
