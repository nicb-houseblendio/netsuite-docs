After setting up your wage agreement, classifications, and conditions, you can assign allowances to a shift or period of shifts.

#### To set up an allowance:

1.  Open the wage agreement. See [Accessing Wage Agreements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0807100528.html#subsect_0903110736).
    
2.  Click the **Allowances** subtab.
    
3.  Click **\+ New allowance….**
    
4.  On the Create new Allowance window, complete the fields.
    
    -   **Condition** - Select the condition that applies to the allowance.
        
    -   **Amount** - Enter the amount to give to employees when the allowance is applied.
        
    -   **Employment status** - Check the boxes for all statuses that can receive the allowance.
        
    -   **Jobs** - From the Available Choices panel, select the jobs that can receive this allowance.
        
5.  Click **Save**.
