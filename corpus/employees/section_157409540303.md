The Employee Record Full permission is intended for Human Resources Business Partners, Chief People Officers (CPOs), and Human Resources Directors. Users with this permission have access to all information about the employee record, except for fields and sublists exposed by the Employee Access Tab permission. Roles with this permission can give users access to NetSuite. They can also assign roles to users who fall into the restriction policy defined on the Role page. For more information, see [Employee Access Tab Permission Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541786744.html).
