Important:

Starting **August 5, 2025**, SuitePeople Workforce Management apps that are not using the latest version will automatically be updated.

To safely upgrade to the latest version and avoid interruptions later, complete required actions before August 5, 2025.

For more information, see [Action Required: Upcoming SuitePeople Workforce Management Mobile App Version Release and Automatic Update](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1020938).

Sometimes, the SuitePeople Workforce Management (WFM) phone app gets small tweaks or updates and you need to reload the app instead of doing a full update.

## Guidelines for SuitePeople WFM App Updates {#subsect_0605104642}

Read the guidelines before updating your SuitePeople WFM phone app.

-   Refer to the changed names when updating apps.
    
    -   AdiClock is calledSuitePeople Time Clock.
        
    -   In the iOS App Store and Google Play Store, SuitePeople Time Clock is called **NetSuite Time Clock**.
        
-   Before updating your WFM apps, ensure that any time punches captured when your device was offline are completely synced with the SuitePeople WFM system.
    
    For more information, see [Syncing Time Punches Captured Offline](#subsect_0605101317).
    

## Syncing Time Punches Captured Offline {#subsect_0605101317}

When you're planning to upgrade your WFM apps, first ensure all time punches are synced with the SuitePeople WFM system.

#### To sync time punches captured offline

1.  Before an upcoming upgrade release, turn off automatic updates on your mobile devices.
    
2.  After the upgrade release, complete the following steps.
    
    1.  Connect your mobile device to a cellular or Wi-Fi connection.
        
    2.  Open the SuitePeople Workforce Management or SuitePeople Time Clock mobile app.
        
        The app will automatically sync with WFM servers and ensure all employee time are saved, including any recorded when the device was offline.
        
    3.  Download the latest version of the app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
        
    4.  Turn on automatic updates.
        
    5.  Repeat steps A to D for each app.
        

## Checking for app updates {#subsect_0605101831}

There are two ways to check if your version of the NetSuite Workforce Management phone app is up to date:

-   Check for updates in the SuitePeople Workforce Management phone app
    
-   Check for updates in the Google Play Store and the iOS App Store
    

#### To check for updates in the SuitePeople Workforce Management phone app: {#procedure_1019113356}

1.  Enter your username and password to log in.
    
2.  Click the **Profile** tab.
    

The version number and build is displayed under the device menu section.

#### To check for updates in the Google Play Store: {#procedure_1019113505}

1.  Open the Google Play Store.
    
2.  Search for **NetSuite Workforce Management** and select it from the list.
    
3.  If an update is available, click **Update**. To check the current version of the app, go to the **Read More** section of the app's description.
    

#### To check for updates in the iOS App Store: {#procedure_1019114201}

1.  Open the iOS App Store.
    
2.  Search for **NetSuite Workforce Management** and select it from the list.
    
3.  If an update is available, click **Update**. To check the current version of the app, go to the **What's New** section of the app's description.
