Workforce Management (WFM) lets you to link your wage agreements to your payroll system.

Make sure your payroll system's pay items are linked to WFM. These items may have different names depending on your payroll system. Proper linking ensures that WFM conditions, such as regular hours or public holidays, are recorded in the right place.

Before linking payroll items, make sure your wage agreements are set up on your account. For more information, see [Wage Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_9135444970.html).

WFM lets you link payroll items to the following configurations:

-   **Conditions** - This configuration lets you link a single payroll item to a condition within your agreements. It's useful for payroll systems that don't have individual pay items for each age rate.
    
    For example, if all regular hours can be sent to the same item, you should use this configuration.
    
    Items linked here will be retained for each revision within your agreements, and will be applied to all classifications within the your agreements.
    
-   **Classifications** - This configuration lets you link multiple pay items for each condition in your Payroll system.
    
    For example, if you have different regular hours for different age levels, you should use this configuration.
    
-   **Allowances** - This configuration lets you link payroll items to allowances, like for laundry or food.
    
-   **Penalties** - This configuration lets you link payroll items to penalties or time worked outside contract hours.
    

Employees with the Administrator role can link payroll items to WFM.

#### To link payroll items in WFM:

1.  Go to Workforce Management.
    
2.  Go to Setup > NetSuite Integration > Agreements.
    
    The Link objects to Payrollitems page opens to the **Conditions** subtab.
    
3.  Link items to conditions.
    
    1.  Locate the WFM wage agreement with conditions that you want to link to your payroll items.
        
        The following conditions are available:
        
        -   Regular Hours
            
        -   Public Holiday
            
        -   Saturday
            
        -   Sunday
            
        -   Standard Overtime
            
        -   Extended Overtime
            
    2.  From each condition field, search then select the payroll item you want to link.
        
        Note:
        
        This search will only display up to 15 items at a time, so you may need to increase your search terms if you're not seeing the item you're looking for.
        
        When you select an item, that item is linked to WFM.
        
    3.  If you want to unlink a payroll item from a condition, click **Unlink**.
        
4.  Link items to classifications.
    
    1.  Click the **Classifications** subtab.
        
    2.  From the list on the upper left, select the wage agreement where you want to link payroll items.
        
    3.  From the list on the upper right, select the revision date. Generally, you should select the latest revision date to ensure you're linking payroll items to the current version of the agreement.
        
        All classifications that belong to that agreement and revision appears, divided across multiple subtabs for each age or age range and across multiple condition levels.
        
    4.  Expand the classification level where the payroll item you want to link is mapped.
        
    5.  From the appropriate classification, select the age or age range.
        
    6.  Repeat Steps 3a to 3c.
        
5.  Link items to allowances.
    
    1.  Click the **Allowances** subtab.
        
        All associated allowances for each wage agreement will appear on the page.
        
        Note:
        
        If an allowance or penalty doesn't load from the page, make sure that it's created within payroll.
        
    2.  Repeat Steps 3a to 3c.
        
6.  Link items to penalties.
    
    1.  Click the **Penalties** subtab.
        
        All associated penalties for each wage agreement will appear on the page.
        
    2.  Repeat Steps 3a to 3c.
