If you use MYOB jobs, you can link them to WFM locations for tracking in MYOB.

This is optional to set up. When Workforce Management exports shifts to MYOB, shifts which are worked at each linked location will export for cost-tracking purposes.

#### To link locations to MYOB jobs:

1.  Go to Workforce Management > Setup > Integrations.
    
2.  From the **Payroll systems** list, select **MYOB**.
    
3.  On the MYOB page, click a company file.
    
4.  Click **Locations**.
    
    WFM locations appear on the left, and MYOB jobs appear on the right with corresponding lists.
    
5.  Locate the WFM location you want to link, then select the corresponding MYOB job from the list.
    
6.  Click **Link**.
