Headcount analysis lets you track headcount, hire, and turnover trends in your company. It includes all active and inactive employees from their hire dates onward. Employees who've been terminated aren't included after their termination dates. To access the Headcount Analysis page, go to _Reports > Employees/HR > Headcount Analysis_.
