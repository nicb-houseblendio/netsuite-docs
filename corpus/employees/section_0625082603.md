SuitePeople Workforce Management (WFM) lets you extract different reports for your own purpose and records. To extract data, select from the seven report types and generate the report.

## Maximum Report Duration {#subsect_0625082634}

For most report types, you can generate reports for up to two months at a time.

Depending on the report, you may need to generate multiple reports if you need to export data for more than two months.

Refer to the following table for details and instructions for each report.
