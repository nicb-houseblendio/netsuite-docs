If you used the ACA Reporting SuiteApp to report compliance for the previous year, you can view your filing history on the ACA landing page.

#### To view ACA filing history:

1.  Go to Payroll and HR > Payroll > Affordable Care Act (Payroll Manager).
    
2.  On the ACA landing page, go to the Filing History section and click **View** to view the previous year's filing.
