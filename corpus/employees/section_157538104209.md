After you create performance review questions and rating scales, you need to create performance review templates. Templates let you set up different performance reviews for different groups in your organization or for different time periods. For example, you might have a quarterly performance review template and an annual one.

For more information, check out the following:

-   [Creating a Performance Review Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158619957572.html)
    
-   [Editing a Performance Review Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157592728359.html)
    
-   [Inactivating or Deleting a Performance Review Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157592738275.html)
