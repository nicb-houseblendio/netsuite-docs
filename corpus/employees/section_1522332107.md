When Advanced Employee Permissions is enabled, the **Show Employees as Contacts** field on the General Preferences page isn't available. Any employees saved to a contact record don't appear on the Contacts list page. In addition, any information specific to an employee's contact record is no longer accessible.

If required, move any custom fields from the contact record to the employee record before enabling Advanced Employee Permissions.
