WFM can send you notifications when an employee's details are changed.

To view your notifications click on the envelope at the top right corner of your screen.

## Setting Up Financial Details Notifications {#subsect_56134245085}

Setting up a notification system to remind you, or any other managers about any change made to an employee's financial details is a simple process. Go to **Setup > Notifications**, and then click **Financial details**.

You can choose which managers will be informed about a change of an employee's financial details.

Click **Save** to continue.
