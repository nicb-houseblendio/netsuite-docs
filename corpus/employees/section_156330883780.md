Requesters can create employee change requests from employee records if their role has the Employee Record permission access level at View, Edit or Full. For more information, see [Setting Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N288727.html) and [Advanced Employee Permissions for Employee Change Requests](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156095870335.html).
