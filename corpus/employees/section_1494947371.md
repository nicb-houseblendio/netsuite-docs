The Time-Off Tracking SuiteApp comes with saved searches you can use as reports

Important:

The saved searches aren't public by default. Administrators need to customize them to set who can access each one and where they show up in the Reports menu. For more information, see [Customizing Time-Off Tracking Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1502267109.html).

You can access the following saved searches through their own menu options at Reports > Time-Off:

-   [Approved Time-Off Requests Saved Search](#bridgehead_1488940182)
    
-   [Employees without Time-Off Plans Saved Search](#bridgehead_1488941694)
    
-   [Pending Time-Off Requests Saved Search](#bridgehead_1488941171)
    
-   [Rejected Time-Off Requests](#bridgehead_1488941273)
    
-   [Time-Off Plan Assignment Saved Search](#bridgehead_1488941445)
    
-   [Upcoming Approved Time-Off (1 Month) Saved Search](#bridgehead_1488941351)
    
-   [Work Calendars Saved Search](#bridgehead_1488941941)
    

You can print or export Time-Off Tracking saved search results to share them as reports. Exporting works for Excel, CSV, and PDF. Use the print and export icons at the top of the results list. You can also email saved search results to other users. To do this, see [Saved Search Email](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N680983.html).

For more information about saved searches, see [Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N675442.html).

## Approved Time-Off Requests Saved Search {#bridgehead_1488940182}

The Approved Time-Off Requests saved search lists active employees with approved requests. Results are sorted by employee name, then start date and time-off type.

## Employees without Time-Off Plans Saved Search {#bridgehead_1488941694}

The Employees without Time-Off Plans saved search lists active employees who don't have a time-off plan. Results are sorted by employee name. In the list, click **Edit** for any employee to assign them a plan.

## Pending Time-Off Requests Saved Search {#bridgehead_1488941171}

The Pending Time-Off Requests saved search lists active employees with requests waiting for approval. Results are sorted by employee name, then start date and time-off type.

## Rejected Time-Off Requests {#bridgehead_1488941273}

The Rejected Time-Off Requests saved search lists active employees with rejected requests. Results are sorted by employee name, then start date and time-off type.

## Time-Off Plan Assignment Saved Search {#bridgehead_1488941445}

The Time-Off Plan Assignment saved search lists the plan assigned to each active employee. Results are sorted by plan, job, and employee name. In the list, click **Edit** for any employee to update their plan.

## Upcoming Approved Time-Off (1 Month) Saved Search {#bridgehead_1488941351}

The Upcoming Approved (1 Month) Time-Off saved search lists active employees with approved requests starting in the next month. Results are sorted by employee name, then start date and time-off type.

## Work Calendars Saved Search {#bridgehead_1488941941}

The Work Calendars saved search lists work calendar assignments for active employees. Results are sorted by work calendar and employee name. In the list, click **Edit** for any employee to update their time-off plan.
