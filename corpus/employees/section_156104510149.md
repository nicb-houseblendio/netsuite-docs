When an employee accrues or uses time off, or when time off expires or gets carried over, NetSuite creates a time-off change record. If you need to update an employee's time-off balance, you can create or adjust a time-off change record.

For more information, see:

-   [Viewing Time-Off Changes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1494521594.html)
    
-   [Creating a Time-Off Change](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1494522058.html)
