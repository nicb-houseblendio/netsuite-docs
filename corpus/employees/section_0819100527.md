To deactivate a location, go to Setup > Locations. Select the location you want to deactivate.

On the Location screen, click **Deactivate**. Confirm the deactivation on the warning that appears.

## Reactivating a Location {#subsect_97100928812}

After deactivating a location, you can restore it any time you need.

Go to Setup > Locations.

To see a list of deactivated locations with restore options, check the **Include deactivated units** box. Select **Inactive**.

A warning message appears. Click the **Restore (Location name)**.
