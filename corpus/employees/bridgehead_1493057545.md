A benefit carrier is a third-party company that provides benefits to your employees, for which your company or your employees, or both, pay premiums. For each benefit that you create using the Benefits Tracking SuiteApp, you can specify a carrier to track these benefit providers.

#### To create a benefit carrier:

1.  Go to Setup > Benefits > Benefit Carriers > New.
    
2.  On the Benefits Tracking - Carrier page, in the **Name** field, enter a unique name for the carrier.
    
3.  Optionally, enter the following additional information for the carrier:
    
    -   Address
        
    -   Contact Email
        
    -   Contact Phone
        
    -   Website
        
4.  Click **Save**.
