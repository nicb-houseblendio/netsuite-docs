WFM can send you notifications to remind you of an employee's birthday. This is useful as when an employee gets older they may be entitled to a pay increase.

It also helps with employee morale.

To view your notifications, click on the envelope at the top right corner of your screen.

## Setting Up Birthday Notifications {#subsect_78133110349}

To set up an automatic notification system for employee birthdays, go to **Setup > Notifications**. Click **Birthday reminder**.

From here you have a range of settings to adjust. The first option is whether you would like WFM to send you an automatic reminder, choose this option if you want a reminder for any upcoming birthdays **(it is still important to complete the rest of the setup page as this information may still be applicable to managers)**

You will then be given the option to choose how many days before the employee's birthday you will receive a notification, as well as an option to send a reminder on the day. The last section focuses on which managers will be informed of the birthday; you firstly have the option to make it so that only the managers that have primary control over the employee will receive a notification. If you want for other employees to know about employees' birthdays, then you can also include them.

Click **Save** continue.
