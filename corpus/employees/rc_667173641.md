After your reviewer submits your performance review and HR approves it, you can add final comments and acknowledge your review. After you do this, you can't edit your final comments,

#### To finalize your performance review:

1.  From the home page of your Employee Center, go to Talent > Performance Reviews.
    
2.  Beside your performance review, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Read your reviewer's responses.
    
    Tip:
    
    Take some time to discuss your performance review with your reviewer before you finalize it.
    
4.  Click **Complete My Review**.
    
5.  If you want, enter any final comments in the field.
    
    Tip:
    
    Take some time to read the acknowledgement carefully. It could contain important information.
    
6.  Click **Acknowledge**.
    

If your manager was not your reviewer, your manager can see your performance review after you finalize it.
