The Compensation Tracking feature requires SuitePeople HR. For more information about SuitePeople, see [SuitePeople Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1495573671.html). If your account is set up for SuitePeople HR, you can enable the Compensation Tracking feature.

#### To enable the Compensation Tracking feature:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Employees** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Compensation Tracking** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

When you enable the Employee Change Requests feature, the Compensation Tracking feature is automatically enabled.
