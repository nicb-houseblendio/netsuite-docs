## Generating an Activation Code {#section_1017085615}

Generate an activation code for the device where SuitePeople Time Clock will be installed.

You must have a Manager role or higher level of access to generate an activation code.

#### To generate an activation code: {#procedure_54144037518}

1.  Go to Workforce Management.
    
2.  Go to Setup > Time Clock.
    
3.  Click **New Device**.
    
4.  In the New Device wizard, complete the required fields.
    
    -   **Name and Operating System** - Complete the fields.
        
        **Name** - Enter the name you'd like to assign to the new device.
        
    -   **Operating System** - Select the operating system running on the device. Your options are:
        
        -   Android
            
        -   iOS/iPadOS
            
        -   Windows
            
        
        The available punch-in options depens on the operating system you're setting up.
        
    -   **Locations** - From the **Available Choices** panel on the left, select the locations for employees to clock in and out of this device.
        
        If you want to select all locations, click **Choose All**.
        
        To move the chosen locations to the **Chosen Items** panel on the right, click the blue right arrow symbol at the middle of the panels.
        
        If you want to clear your chosen items, click **Clear All**.
        
        Important:
        
        To follow the Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA), you can't use fingerprint scanning on Windows devices in Illinois. If you're setting up a Windows device there, employees can only punch in with pin codes.
        
    
    -   **Options** - Refer to the table to configure the available punch-in options for each operating system.
        
        | Operating System | Available Options |
        | --- | --- |
        | Android and iOS/iPadOS | **Visual Verification** - By default, Android and iOS devices lets employees punch in with pin codes only. If you turn this option on, the device takes a photo of each employee after every punch. |
        | Windows | **Authenticate by** - Select the authentication method available for employees during punch-in. The options are:
        -   PIN only
        -   Finger scan only
        -   PIN or Finger scan
        -   Two-factor authentication (PIN then Finger scan)
        
        Note: If you are setting up a Windows device in Illinois, your employees can only punch in using a PIN code. **Check for updates every** - Enter how many minutes (at least 5) you'd like the device to wait before syncing updates from WFM. Time Clock automatically syncs with WFM, so timesheets go through without needing to refresh or wait. |
        
    -   **Confirm** - Review the device details.
        
        If you choose to capture biometric information with finger scanning, you'll need to agree to the customer responsibilities for biometric or fingerprint features. Check the **Agree** box.
        
5.  Click **Create**.
    
    On the Time Clock page, view the 8-letter activation code for the new device on the **Activation code** column.
    

You'll use the activation code as the password and your email as the username when you launch SuitePeople Time Clock for the first time.

After you've generated the activation code, install SuitePeople Time Clock on the new device. For more information, see [Installing SuitePeople Time Clock](#subsect_0916034813).

## Installing SuitePeople Time Clock {#subsect_0916034813}

Install SuitePeople Time Clock on a supported device before you activate it. For more information, see [Minimum Requirements for SuitePeople Time Clock Mobile Apps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0917112543.html).

#### To install SuitePeople Time Clock on mobile devices:

1.  Go to the designated app store, either the App Store for iOS or iPadOS or Google Play for Android.
    
2.  Search **NetSuite Time Clock**.
    
3.  Install the app.
    

To install SuitePeople Time Clock on Windows, see [Installing SuitePeople Time Clock Components for Windows](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_95102310959.html).

After installing SuitePeople Time Clock, activate the new device. For more information, see [Activating a New Device](#subsect_24164628247).

## Activating a New Device {#subsect_24164628247}

When you open SuitePeople Time Clock for the first time, you need to activate the device using a previously generated activation code. For more information, see [Generating an Activation Code](#section_1017085615).

#### To activate a new device: {#procedure_1215032630}

1.  Open the SuitePeople Time Clock app on your device.
    
2.  In the **Username** field, enter your email address.
    
3.  In the **Password** field, enter the unique activation code that was generated for the device.
    
    To find the code, go to Setup > Time Clock. From the **Pending Activation Devices** subtab, locate the device code on the corresponding Activation code column.
    
4.  Click **Register**.
    

After you successfully activate the device, the clock-in page appears.

For employees at locations registered with the device to start clocking in and out, you'll need to set up employee PIN codes For more information, see [Setting up Employee PIN Codes](#subsect_96164608856).

## Setting up Employee PIN Codes {#subsect_96164608856}

WFM assigns randomly generated PIN codes to employees. Employees use PIN codes to log in to SuitePeople Time Clock.

If you're an employee with access to a WFM account, you can find your PIN code when you log in. On the Your Details page, click Access. If you need to change your code, reach out to someone with a Manager role or higher.

If your account has a Manager role or higher level of access, you can set up employee PIN codes.

#### To set up employee PIN codes: {#subsect_81102302249}

1.  Go to Workforce Management.
    
2.  Go to Setup > Employees > List.
    
    The employee list appears with corresponding PINS on the **Timesheet PIN** column.
    
3.  Click an employee name.
    
4.  On the employee profile, on the lefthand panel, click **Details**.
    
5.  On the AdiClock PIN section, click **Edit**.
    
6.  Choose how you want to set the PIN code.
    
    -   To assign a PIN, enter a unique 6-digit PIN on the **Timesheet pin** field.
        
    -   To let WFM create a PIN, **Click Generate Random PIN**.
        
7.  Click **Save PIN**.
    

## Preventing Early Punches {#section_1017090325}

If you have a Manager role or higher level of access, you can turn on Early Punch Prevention for certain locations to stop employees from punching in before a set time.

#### To prevent early punches:

1.  Go to Workforce Management.
    
2.  Go to Setup > Time Clock.
    
3.  At the top right corner, click **Early Punch Prevention**.
    
    The Early Punch-in Prevention window appears.
    
4.  From the **Filter by** field, select an option to filter locations.
    
5.  From the **Available Choices** panel on the left, select the locations where you want to prevent early punches.
    
    If you want to select all locations, click **Choose All**.
    
    To move the chosen locations to the **Chosen Items** panel on the right, click the blue right arrow symbol at the middle of the panels.
    
    If you want to clear your chosen items, click **Clear All**.
    
6.  Click the Early Punch-in prevention switch to turn it green.
    
7.  In the **Grace period** field, enter how many minutes before a shift employees aren't allowed to punch in.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
    

## Preventing Short Breaks {#section_1017090351}

If you have a Manager role or higher level of access, you can turn on Short Break Prevention to stop employees from taking breaks that are too short or don't meet the minimum required length.

Note:

This setting is system-wide and applies to all Workforce Management locations and devices employees use to punch in.

This change stops employees from coming back from their break before the minimum time has passed.

If an employee tries to clock in before their break is over, SuitePeople Time Clock will stop them from punching back in and let them know when their break ends.

#### To prevent short breaks:

1.  Go to Workforce Management.
    
2.  Go to Setup > Account Settings > Time & Attendance.
    
3.  On the Time & Attendance page, check the **Short break prevention** box.
    
4.  In the **Unpaid break minimum length** field, enter the minimum number of minutes for unpaid breaks.
    
5.  In the **Paid break minimum length** field, enter the minimum number of minutes for paid breaks
    
6.  Click **Save**.
