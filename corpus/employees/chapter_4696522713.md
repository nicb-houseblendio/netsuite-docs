The Employee Directory SuiteApp lets your employees to quickly and easily search for their colleagues by name, location, department, and job title. If you're using NetSuite OneWorld, employees can also search by subsidiary. This SuiteApp also includes an Org Browser. Employees can quickly browse your organizational chart and see supervisors and direct reports.

If you've set up SuitePeople HR and use the Kudos feature, employees can give Kudos to colleagues directly from the employee directory. For more information, see [Kudos](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4721459587.html).

For more information about this SuiteApp, see the following topics:

-   [Set Up the Employee Directory SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4725012454.html)
    
-   [Using the Employee Directory](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1496279021.html)
