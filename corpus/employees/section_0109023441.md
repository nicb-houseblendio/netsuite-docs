In Setup > Locations, select a location and then go to Scheduling > Deployment you will see an option within the 'tools' menu to generate a report.

This report shows deployment for either the forecast or sales. If you're changing the model and want to know how many hours it generates or how your changes look, this report will help you understand that.

This doesn't take into account any shifts during the period. It only shows the required hours after Team Rule and Extra Hours are applied to sales for that day.
