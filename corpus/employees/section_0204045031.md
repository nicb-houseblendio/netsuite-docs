In Workforce Management (WFM), you can upload locations in bulk using a CSV file.

#### To upload locations in bulk in WFM:

1.  Go to Setup > Locations.
    
2.  On the top right section, click **Bulk Actions**. Click **Uploader**.
    
3.  On the Location uploader page, click the brand associated with the locations.
    
4.  To download a template of the CSV file, click **Template**.
    
5.  Format your locations according to the downloaded template.
    
6.  Drag and drop the formatted CSV file in the upload space at the bottom of the page.
    
    The page updates with the mapping and preview of the bulk upload.
    
7.  On the Header Mappings section, check that the items in the **File Header** column match those in the **WFM header** column as you intended.
    
8.  On the **Mapping Preview** section, review that the data fall under the correct headers.
    
9.  On the **Upload options** section, check the boxes that apply to your upload. Click Upload.
    
    A confirmation page appears.
    
10.  Understand and check all the boxes and click **Upload**.
     
     A summary window appears showing the counts for the successfully or failed data changes.
     
     If there are errors in the upload, click **Errors**. A window appears to help you troubleshoot failed updates.
     
     Important:
     
     To comply with the Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA), devices in Illinois locations may not be used to capture biometric data through fingerprint scanning.
     
     All devices in the Illinois locations must be set to use only pin codes to authorize, otherwise, the location will not be updated.
     
     To view device settings, log in to Workforce Management. Go to Setup > Time Clock. From the **Activated Devices** subtab, click the device name associated with the location. On the Options page of the wizard, from the **Authenticate by** field, select **PIN only** as the authentication method.
     
11.  Click **Done**.
