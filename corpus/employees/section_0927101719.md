With automatch, Workforce Management (WFM) can automatically approve times when they meet a set of criteria.

For assistance on configuring automatch settings, see the following help topics:

-   [Automatch and Rounding Settings in WFM](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0314095739.html)
    
-   [Updating WFM Automatch Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0520121308.html)
    

#### To approve shifts with automatch:

1.  Go to Workforce Management.
    
2.  Open the Shifts page.
    
    -   Go to Shifts > Weekly.
        
    -   Go to Shifts > Multi-Location.
        
3.  Click **Actions**. Click **Automatch**.
    
    WFM automatically approves shifts that match the automatch and rounding settings, then grays them out. You can continue to approve the shifts that require manual approval.
