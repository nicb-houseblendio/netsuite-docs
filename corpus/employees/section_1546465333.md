Kudos are used to recognize coworkers for a job well done. You can give Kudos to someone for making an outstanding contribution, providing a great idea, or for helping out on a project. If required, Kudos can be renamed in your NetSuite account to better match your company culture.

## Prerequisites {#subsect_1546465414}

To rename Kudos, the Kudos feature must be enabled in your account and you must be assigned to a role that includes the Kudos permission.

#### To rename Kudos:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Rename Records/Transactions_.
    
2.  On the Rename Records/Transactions page, the default names for records are autofilled in the fields.
    
3.  In the **Name For Kudos** field, enter the name you want to use.
    
4.  Click **Save.**
