After you activate and review the configurations of the General Retail Industry Award \[MA000004\] preconfigured wage agreements, they are available to be assigned to employee wages.

The following are the two agreements that cover typically used variations of the General Retail Award:

-   **\[MA000004\] General Retail Industry** - Use this agreement to manage employee wages that have the typical span of hours as defined in the Award.
    
-   **\[MA000004\] General Retail Industry Award - Late Trading (After 9pm)** - Use this agreement when the employees you manage have late trading hours that extend beyond:
    
    -   9 p.m. from Monday to Friday
        
    -   6 p.m. on a Saturday or Sunday
        

Workforce Management (WFM) does not provide agreements covering the following sectors:

-   Early trading
    
-   Shift workers
    
-   Early trading news agency
    
-   Late trading video shop
    
-   Apprenticeships
    

For more information about the full coverage and classification descriptions of the General Retail Industry Award, go to the Fair Work Ombudsman website.

### Conditions {#subsect_0128061204}

Refer to the following table for the default conditions of the agreement.

| Condition | Rules | Employment Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular Hours | Every day | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Mon - Fri 6 p.m. - 9 p.m. | Every weekday between 6 p.m. and 9 p.m. | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Saturday | Every Saturday | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Sunday | Every Sunday | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Public Holiday | Every public holiday | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| OT Mon - Sat First 3 hours | 
-   Every day from Monday to Saturday when, during the day, time worked exceeds 9 hours
-   Every day Monday to Saturday when, during the week, time exceeds 38 hours
-   Every weekday between 9 p.m. and midnight
-   Every Saturday between 6 p.m. and midnight
-   Every day from Monday to Saturday between midnight and 7 a.m.

 | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| OT Mon - Sat More than 3 Hours | Every day from Monday to Saturday when, during the day, time of type overtime rate worked exceeds 3 hours | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| OT Sunday | 

-   Every Sunday between midnight and 9 a.m.
-   Every Sunday between 6 p.m. and midnight
-   Every Sunday when, during the shift, time worked exceeds 9 hours
-   Every Sunday when, during the week, time exceeds 38 hours

 | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| Time Between Shifts | Every day where there has not been a clear break of 12 hours since the previous day | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
| OT Public Holiday | 

-   Every day that is a public holiday when, during the day, time worked exceeds 9 hours
-   Every day that is a public holiday when, during the week, time exceeds 38 hours

 | Casual, Part-time, and Full-time |
