This workbook is based on the sales (ordered) dataset and displays information for non-posting transactions like sales orders and return authorizations. In the workbook, transactions are grouped in pivot tables and charts by item, customer, promotion, sales rep, and date. Totals in this workbook match the totals displayed in the sales orders by reports.

To access the workbook, click the Sales (Ordered) Workbook link from the Workbook Templates list on the Workbooks subtab of the Analytics Home page. To view details about the values contained within the underlying dataset, see [Sales (Ordered) Dataset](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158437048986.html).
