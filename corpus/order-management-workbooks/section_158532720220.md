This workbook is based on the SuiteAnalytics dataset and analyzes recurring revenue from subscriptions by customer, subscription, billing account, item, and other options.

Note:

Views aren't in real time.

To access the workbook, click the Monthly Recurring Revenue link from the Workbook Templates list on the Workbooks subtab of the Analytics Home page.

The following components are included in the workbook template:

| Component | Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Table View | Upcoming Renewals | **Upcoming** means occurring in the near future. **Renewals** refers to subscriptions that will expire soon. The customer needs to renew the subscription by extending it or creating a new renewal subscription to maintain revenue. |
| Chart | Recurring Revenue Sources | **Recurring Revenue** refers to subscription revenue coming in at regular intervals. **Sources** refers to subscription items that generate the revenue. |
| Chart | Top 10 Performing Items Last Month | This block chart shows the relative size of revenue sources for the 10 highest revenue-generating items during the most recent month. |
| Chart | Monthly Recurring Revenue Year-Over-Year | Line chart comparing revenue generation year-over-year by month. |
| Pivot table | Current Month Recurring Revenue | This table displays monthly recurring revenue separated according to revenue source:
-   existing recurring revenue
-   upsells
-   downsells
-   churn
-   new revenue

Also included is the new recurring revenue sum at month end. |
| Pivot table | Monthly Recurring Revenue by Class | **Class** refers to segments that you can create to track records such as product lines or region. For example, you own a janitorial service and you want to track income and expenses separately for household and commercial accounts. You set up a class for each account type and track the financial performance of each class over any time period. |
