Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) is the sum of all subscription revenue expressed as a monthly value. MRR is the most important metric for a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) business. You can use this metric to understand how well your business is doing, how it's growing, and how it will evolve in the future.
