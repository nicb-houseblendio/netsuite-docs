To use Single Sign-on (SSO) with your app, first check with your administrator that SAML (Security Assertion Markup Language) has been enabled for your company. See [SAML Single Sign-on](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N3825119.html) for complete setup instructions. Ask your administrator to do the following:

-   Add the **Mobile Device Access** and **SAML Single Sign-on** permissions to a custom role, and assign that custom role to you.
    
-   Forward the Account ID to you. Administrators can find this at _Setup > Company Information > Account ID_.
    
    Note:
    
    Take note of the following:
    
    -   If you are testing SSO in a Release Preview or Sandbox account, be sure to include all numbers and letters in the Account ID, for example, 2000201\_RP.
        
    -   NetSuite for Mobile does not support importing custom certificates that some SSO providers require.
        
    

#### To use SSO to log in to the app: {#procedure_1554105572}

1.  Launch the app and tap **Company SSO**.
    
2.  Enter your NetSuite Account ID.
    
    Note:
    
    With **iOS**, the app remembers your ID automatically. To store your ID with **Android**, tap **Remember Me**.
    
3.  Tap **Continue**.
    
4.  Log in from the third-party authentication screen using your SAML credentials.
    

If you use SAML SSO with Microsoft Entra and you experience issues, see [Error when Logging into NetSuite For Mobile with SAML SSO](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1019509).
