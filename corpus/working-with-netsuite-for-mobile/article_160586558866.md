You can personalize My Dashboard to best suit your needs.

#### To arrange My Dashboard in NetSuite for Android:

1.  From My Dashboard, tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_more_icon_2.png).
    
2.  Tap **Personalize**.
    
3.  Drag a portlet to move it to your preferred spot.
    
4.  To hide a portlet, tap the Remove icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Remove icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_Remove_icon.png).
    
5.  To exit the Personalize screen, tap the Close icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Close icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_cancel_icon.png).
    
    My Dashboard reloads with your changes.
