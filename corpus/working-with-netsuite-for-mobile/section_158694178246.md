Follow this procedure to create a new record in NetSuite for Mobile.

#### To create a record: {#procedure_1554468160}

1.  Go to the records view:
    
    -   **For iOS**, on the Tab bar, tap **Records**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Navigation Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Navigation Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_menu_icon.png), and then tap **Records**.
        
2.  To create a new record, tap the Add icon ![Screenshot of the Android Add icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_add_icon_2.png) next to the record type you want to create.
    
3.  Edit the record details.
    
4.  To save your record, tap **Save** on iOS or the tick icon on Android.
