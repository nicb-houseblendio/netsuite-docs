Follow this procedure to view a record in NetSuite for Mobile.

#### To view a record: {#procedure_1554466937}

1.  To access the list of available record types:
    
    -   **For iOS**, on the Tab bar, tap **Records**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Navigation Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Navigation Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_menu_icon.png), and then tap **Records**.
        
2.  (Optional) To access a list of recently opened records, tap **Recents**.
    
3.  From the list, tap the record type you want to access.
    
4.  (Optional) You can apply filters and set view options for the list.
    
    -   **For iOS**, see the following instructions.
        
    
    1.  From the record list, tap **View** at the top of the list.
        
    2.  Configure the view options and set filters you want to use.
        
    3.  To apply your changes, tap **Done**.
        
    
    -   **For Android**, see the following instructions.
        
    
    1.  Tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_more_icon_2.png), and then tap **Customize List**.
        
    2.  On the Customize List screen, you can set custom settings and filters for your list.
        
        Note:
        
        The available options depend on the record type and your other customizations.
        
    3.  To save your customizations, tap the Back icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Back icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_back_icon.png). Your customizations apply automatically.
        
5.  From the list, tap the record you want to view.
    
6.  The record opens in summary view.
    
7.  A predefined selection of Record sublists are displayed below the summary view. Tap the Record Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Mobile Record Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_record_menu_icon.png) to reveal additional sublists. See [NetSuite for Mobile Sublist Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554718735.html).
