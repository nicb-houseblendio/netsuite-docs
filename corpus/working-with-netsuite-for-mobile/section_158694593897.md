You can approve multiple requests at one time.

#### To approve multiple requests:

1.  From My Dashboard, tap a category (for example Expense Reports) on the My Approvals portlet.
    
2.  Do one of the following:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Select**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Multi-Select icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Multi-Select icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_multi-select_icon.png).
        
3.  Select the requests you want to approve.
    
4.  To approve the selected requests:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Approve**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Tick icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Tick icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_tick_icon.png).
        
5.  (Optional) To approve all requests at one time:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Approve All**, and then tap **Confirm.**
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Select All icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Seleck All icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_select_all_icon.png), and then tap the Tick icon.
