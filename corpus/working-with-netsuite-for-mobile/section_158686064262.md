Follow this procedure to delete a timer log.

#### To delete a timer log: {#procedure_1554458275}

1.  From My Dashboard, on the My Time portlet, tap **To Report**.
    
2.  To delete a timer log:
    
    -   **For iOS**, swipe a timer log entry tap **Delete**, and then tap **Delete** to confirm.
        
    -   **For Android**, touch and hold the timer log entry you want to delete, tap **Delete**, and then tap **Yes** to confirm.
