You can access the complete record from NetSuite and displays it within the app.

#### To access the full record of a request:

1.  From My Dashboard, tap a category (for example Expense Reports) on the My Approvals portlet.
    
2.  To access the full record for a request:
    
    -   **For iOS**, swipe right to left alongside the relevant request, and then tap **Full Record**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_overflow_menu_icon.png), and then tap **Full Record**.
        
3.  You can go to the full record by tapping the Record Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Mobile Record Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_record_menu_icon.png).
    
4.  You can approve or reject the request.
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Approve** or tap the Arrow icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Arrow icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_arrow_icon_2.png), and then tap **Reject**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_more_icon_2.png), and then tap **Approve** or **Reject**.
        

Note:

Administrator and Accountant roles can also edit requests from this view:

-   **For iOS**, tap **Edit**.
    
-   **For Android**, tap the More icon, and then tap **Edit**.
