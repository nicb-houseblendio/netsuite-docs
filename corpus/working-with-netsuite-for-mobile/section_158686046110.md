Follow this procedure to edit a timer log.

#### To edit a timer log: {#procedure_1554456850}

1.  From My Dashboard, on the My Time portlet, tap **To Report**.
    
2.  Tap the timer log you want to edit.
    
3.  You can edit the following fields:
    
    -   Date
        
    -   Duration
        
    -   Memo
        
    
    Note:
    
    Start and End fields are read-only fields.
    
4.  To save your changes:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Timer**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Tick icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Tick icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_tick_icon.png).
