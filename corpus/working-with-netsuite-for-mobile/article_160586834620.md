You can choose between a horizontal or vertical layout of your dashboards to best suit the device you are using.

#### To change dashboard layout in NetSuite for Android:

1.  Go to any dashboard, with the exception of My Dashboard.
    
2.  Tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_more_icon_2.png), and then tap Horizontal Layout or Vertical Layout.
    
    Note:
    
    The available layout depends on the layout you are currently using.
