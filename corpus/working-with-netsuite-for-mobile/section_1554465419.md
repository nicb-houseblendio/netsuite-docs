If you add the Custom Searches portlet to your Home dashboard in a browser session, it also appears on your app's Home dashboard. See [NetSuite for Mobile Dashboards](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1553858494.html).

#### To access a saved search in the app: {#procedure_1554466315}

1.  Do one of the following:
    
    -   **For iOS**, from your Home dashboard in your Custom Searches portlet, tap the Arrow icon ![NetSuite for iOS Arrow icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_View_List_icon.png).
        
    -   **For Android**, from your Home dashboard in your Custom Searches portlet, tap **View List**.
        
    
    Note:
    
    NetSuite for Mobile enables you to configure the Custom Searches portlet for any web dashboard or pin it to My Dashboard. For more information, see [NetSuite for Mobile Dashboards](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1553858494.html).
    
2.  (Optional) If you do not have access to the Custom Searches portlet, do one of the following:
    
    -   **For iOS**, on the Navigation Tab bar, tap **More**, and then tap **Saved Searches**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Navigation Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Navigation Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_menu_icon.png), and then tap **Saved Searches**.
        
3.  Scroll down the list to find the saved search you want to access.
    
    Note:
    
    Take note of the following:
    
    -   You can scroll to the bottom of the list to load additional searches.
        
    -   NetSuite for Android enables you to search the saved searches list by tapping the Search icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Search icon.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_search_icon.png).
        
    
4.  Tap the required saved search.
    
    Note:
    
    Filters for saved searches are not available in the app. You can use a browser session if you require filters for saved searches.
