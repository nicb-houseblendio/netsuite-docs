You can use this procedure to delete items from your calendar.

#### To delete an item:

1.  Tap the Navigation Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Navigation Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_menu_icon.png), and then tap **Calendar**.
    
2.  Tap the required item to access the item detail screen.
    
3.  Tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_more_icon_2.png), and then tap **Edit**.
    
4.  After the edit screen loads, tap the More icon again, and then tap **Delete**.
    
5.  Confirm the action by tapping **Yes**.
    

Note:

Only the event organizer can delete an event.
