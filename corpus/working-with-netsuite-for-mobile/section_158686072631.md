Follow this procedure to convert a timer log into a time entry.

#### To convert a timer log into a time entry: {#procedure_1554459107}

1.  From My Dashboard, on the My Time portlet, tap **To Report**.
    
2.  To convert a timer log into a time entry:
    
    -   **For iOS**, swipe the timer log, and then tap **Use for Time Entry**.
        
    -   **For Android**, touch and hold the timer log, and then tap **Create Time Entry**.
        
3.  Complete the required fields.
    
4.  To save your time entry:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Save**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Tick icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Tick icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_tick_icon.png).
