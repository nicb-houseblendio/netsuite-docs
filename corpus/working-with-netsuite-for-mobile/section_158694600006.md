The detailed view displays a summary of a request. You can approve or reject requests from this view. You can also access attachments or the full record.

#### To view a request detail:

1.  From My Dashboard, tap a category (for example Expense Reports) on the My Approvals portlet.
    
2.  Tap a request you want to view.
    
3.  (Optional) Tap an individual entry under the **Items** subtab to view any attachments associated with that entry.
    
    Note:
    
    Image previews automatically load up to 5 MB on Wi-Fi connections, and 2 MB on cellular connections. You receive a prompt to continue, for files larger than these thresholds.
    
4.  (Optional) You can approve or reject the request.
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Approve** or **Reject**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Tick icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Tick icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_tick_icon.png) to approve or the Cancel icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Cancel icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_cancel_icon.png) to reject the request.
        
5.  (Optional) You can view the full record.
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Full Record**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_more_icon_2.png), and then tap **Full Record**.
