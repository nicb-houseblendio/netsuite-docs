The following table gives an overview of calendar icons. The type of icon is determined by the item type and the current status of that item.

**Event icons**

| Accepted | ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Event Accepted icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/calendar_icon_event_accepted.png) |
| --- | --- |
| Tentative | ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Enent Tentative icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/calendar_icon_event_tentative.png) |
| Awaiting Response | ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Event Awaiting Response icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/calendar_icon_event_awaiting_response.png) |

**Task icons**

| Completed | ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Task Completed icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/calendar_icon_task_completed.png) |
| --- | --- |
| In progress | ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Task in Progress icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/calendar_icon_task_in_progress.png) |
| Not started | ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Task Not Started icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/calendar_icon_task_not_started.png) |

**Phone Call icons**

| Completed | ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Phone Call Completed icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/calendar_icon_phone_call_completed.png) |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduled | ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Phone Call Scheduled icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/calendar_icon_phone_call_scheduled.png) |

Note:

Cancelled events do not appear in the app.
