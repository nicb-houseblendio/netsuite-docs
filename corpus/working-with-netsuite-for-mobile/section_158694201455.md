Follow this procedure to attach files to a record in NetSuite for Mobile.

#### To attach files to a record: {#procedure_1554714268}

1.  Open the required record for editing, or create a new record.
    
2.  Tap the Record Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Mobile Record Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_record_menu_icon.png) to go the record.
    
3.  Go to the required screen.
    
4.  Tap the Add icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Mobile Add icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/add_icon.png) to add a new file to the selected record.
    
5.  Fill in any required fields.
    
    Note:
    
    The fields available depend on your current role and preferred form setup.
    
6.  Tap **Folder** to change the location where the uploaded file will be stored.
    
7.  Tap **Select File**, and then select an image. You can also take a new photo.
    
    Note:
    
    Take note of the following:
    
    -   You can only attach images in JPG or PNG file format.
        
    -   Images are reduced to a maximum resolution of 1024x1024 pixels, maintaining aspect ratio.
        
    
8.  To save your changes:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Save**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Tick icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Tick icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_tick_icon.png).
