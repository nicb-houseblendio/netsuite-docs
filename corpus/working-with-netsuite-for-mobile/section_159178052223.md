Follow this procedure to reject multiple requests.

Note:

Rejecting multiple requests is only available for Timesheets and Time Entries.

#### To reject multiple requests

1.  From My Dashboard, tap a category (for example Timesheets) on the My Approvals portlet.
    
2.  Tap **Select**.
    
3.  (Optional) To reject all requests at one time, tap **Reject All**, and then tap **Confirm**.
    
4.  Select the requests you want to reject.
    
5.  Tap **Reject** to confirm.
    

Note:

A pop-up window may prompt you to specify a reason for rejection.
