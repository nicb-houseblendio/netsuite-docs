The following sections cover information about working with the NetSuite for Mobile Calendar with iOS devices.

To access your calendar with NetSuite for iOS:

-   On your Tab bar, tap the Calendar icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Calendar icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_calendar_icon.png).
    

For your calendar, the app takes the time zone settings from your NetSuite desktop application. See [Setting the Time Zone in NetSuite for iOS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3775069733.html).
