You can edit existing time entries.

Note:

To edit time entries, you need the Track Time permission with Edit or Full level.

#### To edit a time entry: {#procedure_1555072310}

1.  From My Dashboard, on the My Time portlet, tap **Reported This Week**.
    
2.  Tap the time entry you want to edit.
    
3.  Do one of the following:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Edit**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_more_icon_2.png), and then tap **Edit**.
        
4.  Edit the time entry.
    
5.  To save your changes:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Save**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Tick icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Tick icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_tick_icon.png).
