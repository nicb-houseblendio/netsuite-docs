Follow this procedure to view items in a sublist.

#### To view a sublist item: {#procedure_1554719552}

1.  Go to the required record. See [Viewing a Record in NetSuite for Mobile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158694164785.html).
    
2.  Tap the Record Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Mobile Record Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_record_menu_icon.png) to go to the available sublists.
    
3.  Tap the sublist you want to access.
    
4.  Tap the item you want to view.
