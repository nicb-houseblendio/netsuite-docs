You can personalize My Dashboard to best suit your needs.

#### To arrange My Dashboard in NetSuite for iOS:

1.  From My Dashboard, tap **Edit**.
    
2.  Drag a portlet to move it to your preferred spot.
    
3.  Tap the Tick icon to show or hide a portlet. ![NetSuite for iOS Tick icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_Tick_icon.png).
    
4.  To remove a portlet added from another dashboard, tap the Remove icon ![NetSuite for iOS Remove icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_Remove_icon.png).
    
5.  To exit the Personalize screen, tap **Done**.
    
    My Dashboard reloads with your changes.
