My Dashboard is a native mobile dashboard that serves as the center of your mobile workflow. Depending on your role and permissions, My Dashboard can contain up to three mobile-only portlets. You can also add any portlet from other Dashboards by tapping the Star icon on the portlet.

You can rearrange and hide portlets on My Dashboard, see [Arranging My Dashboard in NetSuite for Android](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160586558866.html).

The following table describes three mobile-only portlets available with NetSuite for Android.

Note:

Which portlets appear on My Dashboard depends on your role and permissions.

| Portlet Name | Description | Screenshot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| My Time | Displays the amount of time to report in timer logs, and time entries you have reported this week. For more information, see [NetSuite for Mobile Time Tracking](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554297914.html). Note: This portlet is only available if you enable the Track Time permission, and if you enable either Time Sheet or Time Tracking in your account. | ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android My Time portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_My_Time_Portlet.png) |
| My Expenses | Displays the number of expenses not yet reported, and the total value of outstanding expenses. For more information, see [NetSuite for Mobile Expense Reporting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554107695.html). | ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android My Expenses portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_My_Expenses_Portlet.png) |
| My Approvals | Displays the number of records awaiting your approval. For more information, see [My Approvals in NetSuite for Mobile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554194643.html). | ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android My Approvals portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_My_Approvals_Portlet.png) |
