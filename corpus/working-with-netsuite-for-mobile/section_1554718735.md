When you view a record, a predefined selection of sublists displays below the summary view. You can go to available sublists by tapping the Record Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Mobile Record Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_record_menu_icon.png).

See the following sections for more information about managing sublist.

-   [Viewing a Sublist Item](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158694349792.html)
    
-   [Adding an Item to a Sublist](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158694354734.html)
