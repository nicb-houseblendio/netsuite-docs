Logging in to the app does not affect any NetSuite session you may have open in a browser. Similarly, logging out from a browser session does not affect your access to the mobile app.

If you prefer to use a biometric login method instead of entering a password, enable the **Remember Me** option from the login screen of the app. Your device stores saved passwords securely using standard cryptography like Keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android).
