To access the Timesheet screen, from My Dashboard, on the My Time portlet, tap **Reported This Week**.

On the My Time screen, you see these options:

-   **Add** - You can add a new time entry by tapping the Add icon:
    
    -   ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Add icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_add_icon.png) for **iOS**.
        
    -   ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Add icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_add_icon.png) for **Android**.
        
-   **Today** - View a list of all time entries for today.
    
-   **Go to Date** - Select a specific date to view:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap the current date at the top of the view to display a date picker, choose your desired date, and then tap **Done**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_more_icon_2.png), tap **Go to date** to display a calendar, choose your desired date from the calendar, and then tap **OK**.
        
-   **Next / Previous week** - Use the arrow buttons to view next or previous weeks.
    
-   **Select Day** - Tap the day you want to view.
    
-   **Total** - The bottom of the timesheet shows a summary of total hours entered for the selected day and week. The minimum unit of time displayed is one minute.
    
-   **Open / Edit** - Tap or swipe a record to open and edit entries.
