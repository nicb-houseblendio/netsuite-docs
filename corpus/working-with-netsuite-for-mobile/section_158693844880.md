Follow this procedure to edit a calendar item.

#### To edit a calendar event: {#procedure_3775052075}

1.  On your Tab bar, tap the Calendar icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Calendar icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_calendar_icon.png).
    
2.  Swipe left to right across the item you want to edit to reveal Quick Actions.
    
3.  Tap **Edit**.
    
4.  Tap Record Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Record Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_record_menu_icon.png) to go to the available sublists for the event based on your preferred form setup.
    
5.  Edit the relevant fields.
    
6.  To save your changes, tap **Save**.
