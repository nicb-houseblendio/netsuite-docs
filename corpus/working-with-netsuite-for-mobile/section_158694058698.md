Follow this procedure to respond to an event.

#### To respond to an event:

1.  Tap the Navigation Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Navigation Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_menu_icon.png), and then tap **Calendar**.
    
2.  Tap the required event to access the event detail screen.
    
3.  Tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_more_icon_2.png).
    
4.  Tap one of the following options to choose your response to an event:
    
    -   **Edit** (event organizer only)
        
    -   **Quick Accept** - No further action is needed if you choose this option.
        
    -   **Accept** - Tapping this option takes you to the Response screen.
        
    -   **Decline** - Tapping this option takes you to the Response screen.
        
    -   **Tentative** - Tapping this option takes you to the Response screen.
        
5.  (Optional) Edit and customize your response to the event organizer in the Response screen:
    
    -   Select **Send a response** or **Do not send a response** to either attach a response message or not.
        
    -   You can add and edit your message in the **Message Text** window.
        
    -   To set up a reminder for the event:
        
        -   Tap **Reminder**.
            
        -   From the time picker, tap the time of your reminder.
            
    -   To choose the type of your reminder:
        
        -   Tap **Reminder type**.
            
        -   Select your reminder type.
            
6.  To save and send your response, tap **OK**.
    

The event status is updated, and confirmed by the updated event icon in the calendar view.
