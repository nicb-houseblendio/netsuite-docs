You can remove your login details if you don't want to store them on your device.

To remove your login details:

-   **For iOS**, go to Account Menu > Remember Me, and then tap **Disable**.
    
-   **For Android**, go to Menu > Settings, and then tap **Remember Me** to disable the option.
