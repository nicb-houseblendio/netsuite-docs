NetSuite for Android enables you to take quick actions on records in your record lists.

#### To take quick action on a record:

1.  Tap the Navigation Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Navigation Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_menu_icon.png), and then tap **Records**.
    
2.  Tap the record type you want to access.
    
3.  Touch and hold a record. A message with available quick actions appears.
    
    Note:
    
    The available quick actions depend on the record type and your role.
    
4.  Tap the quick action you want to take.
