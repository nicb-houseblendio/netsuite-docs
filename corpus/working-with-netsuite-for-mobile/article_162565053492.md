NetSuite for Android supports list customizations for standard and custom record types, including records you set as your favorites.

#### To customize a list in NetSuite for Android:

1.  Tap the Navigation Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Navigation Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_menu_icon.png), and then tap **Records**.
    
2.  Tap the record type you want to customize.
    
3.  On the record list screen, tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_more_icon_2.png), and then tap **Customize List**.
    
4.  On the Customize List screen, you can set custom settings and filters for your list.
    
    Note:
    
    The available options depend on the record type and your other customizations.
    
5.  To save your customizations, tap the Back icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Back icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_back_icon.png).
    
    Your customizations apply automatically.
