Follow this procedure to view a list of logged phone calls.

#### To view a list of logged phone calls: {#procedure_156215846370}

1.  On the Tab bar, tap **More**.
    
2.  Tap **Phone Calls**.
    
3.  Control the order, filter, and view options by tapping the icons at the top of the screen.
