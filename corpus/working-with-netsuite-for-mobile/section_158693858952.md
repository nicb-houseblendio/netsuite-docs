Follow this procedure to view an unlisted calendar.

#### To add an unlisted calendar: {#procedure_4392077584}

1.  On your Tab bar, tap the Calendar icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Calendar icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_calendar_icon.png).
    
2.  Tap the Customize icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Customize icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_customize_icon.png).
    
3.  Tap **Add**.
    
4.  From the Calendar Type bar, tap the type of calendar you want to add.
    
5.  Tap the calendar you want to view.
    
    This adds the calendar to your calendar list and sets it as the active calendar.
