Follow this procedure to log out of NetSuite for Mobile.

#### To log out of the app: {#procedure_1554106211}

1.  Do one of the following:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap the Account Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Account Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_account_menu_icon_1.png).
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Navigation Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Navigation Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_menu_icon.png).
        
2.  Tap **Log out**.
    

Note:

Logging out of the app does not affect any open browser sessions.
