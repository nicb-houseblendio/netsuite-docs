Follow this procedure to delete an event from your calendar

#### To delete an event from your calendar:

1.  On your Tab bar, tap the Calendar icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Calendar icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_calendar_icon.png).
    
2.  Go to the event you want to delete.
    
3.  Swipe the event from left to right to reveal quick actions.
    
4.  Tap **Edit** to go to the edit screen.
    
5.  Tap **Delete**.
    
    Note:
    
    Only the event organizer can delete the event.
