Follow this procedure to add items to a sublist.

#### To add an item to a sublist: {#procedure_1554720571}

1.  Go to the required record. See [Viewing a Record in NetSuite for Mobile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158694164785.html).
    
2.  To edit the record:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Edit**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_more_icon_2.png), and then tap **Edit**.
        
3.  Tap the Record Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Mobile Record Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_record_menu_icon.png) to go to available sublists.
    
4.  Tap the sublist you want to add an item to.
    
5.  Tap the Add icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Mobile Add icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/add_icon.png).
    
    Note:
    
    NetSuite for Mobile does not fully support all record types. In such cases, the Add icon is not displayed.
    
6.  Enter the item data.
    
7.  To save your changes:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Save**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Tick icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Tick icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_tick_icon.png).
