Follow this procedure to edit a record.

#### To edit a record: {#procedure_1554468680}

1.  Access the list of available record types:
    
    -   **For iOS**, on the Tab bar, tap **Records**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Navigation Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Navigation Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_menu_icon.png), and then tap **Records**.
        
2.  (Optional) To access a list of recently opened records, tap **Recents**.
    
3.  From the list, tap the record type you want to access.
    
4.  Tap the record you want to edit.
    
5.  To edit the record:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Edit**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the More icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_more_icon_2.png), and then tap **Edit**.
        
6.  Tap the Record Menu icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Mobile Record Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_record_menu_icon.png) to browse the record.
    
7.  Edit the record details.
    
8.  To save your changes:
    
    -   **For iOS**, tap **Save**.
        
    -   **For Android**, tap the Tick icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Tick icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/android_tick_icon.png).
