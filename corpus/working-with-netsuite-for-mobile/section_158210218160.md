You can request a password reset from the login screen. After you request a password reset, you receive a reset link in the inbox of the email address you specified.

#### To reset your forgotten password:

1.  In the login screen, tap **Forgot?** in the Password field.
    
2.  Enter an email address registered with NetSuite, or accept the populated address.
    
3.  Tap **Request Reset Link** on the Reset Password screen with your email address.
    
4.  Tap **Back** for **iOS** or **OK** for **Android**.
    
5.  Follow the instructions sent to your email address.
