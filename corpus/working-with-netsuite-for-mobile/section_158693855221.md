Follow this procedure to respond to edit and send your response to an event.

#### To respond to an event:

1.  On your Tab bar, tap the Calendar icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Calendar icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_calendar_icon.png).
    
2.  Go to the event you want to respond to.
    
3.  Tap the event.
    
4.  Tap **Quick Accept** to accept the event. No further action is needed if you choose this option.
    
5.  To reveal additional response options, tap the Arrow icon ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Arrow icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_arrow_icon_2.png):
    
    -   Tap **Accept** to accept the event.
        
    -   Tap **Decline** to decline the event.
        
    -   Tap **Tentative** to mark your response as tentative.
        
6.  Edit and customize your response in the Response screen:
    
    -   Select **Send a response** or **Do not send a response** to either attach a response message or not.
        
    -   You can add and edit your message in the **Message Text** window.
        
    -   To set up a reminder for the event:
        
        -   Tap **Reminder**.
            
        -   From the time picker, tap the time of your reminder.
            
        -   Tap **Done**.
            
    -   To choose the type of your reminder:
        
        -   Tap **Reminder type**.
            
        -   Select your reminder type.
            
        -   Tap **Done**.
            
7.  To save and send your response, tap **OK**.
    

The event status is updated, and confirmed by the updated event icon in the calendar view screen.
