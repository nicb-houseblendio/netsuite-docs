Follow this procedure to log in to the app using your NetSuite account credentials.

Important:

You need to have access to a role with the Mobile Device Access permission enabled. For more information, see [Mobile Device Access Permission](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158383508120.html).

#### To log in to the app: {#procedure_1554103764}

1.  Enter the email address and password that you use to log in to your NetSuite account.
    
2.  Tap **Log In**.
    
3.  The first time you log in, you must answer one of your three security questions. Fill out your answer and tap **Submit**.
    
    Note:
    
    You can also change the role with which you want to log in by tapping **Change Role**. The app chooses the role you last used in a browser session otherwise.
