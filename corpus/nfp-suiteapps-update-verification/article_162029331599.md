When you update NFP SuiteApps, you might need to update some customizations manually.

The NFP SuiteApps Update Verification SuiteApp helps you check and update these customizations after an update.

The SuiteApp gives you a SuiteApps Update Verification page. Before updating an NFP SuiteApp, check and confirm the right fields on that page.
