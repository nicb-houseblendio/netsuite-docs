You can manually implement approved engineering change orders, or let the system do it automatically with a preference.

#### To enable the engineering change order preference:

1.  Go to Transactions > Management > Supply Chain Management.
    
2.  On the Supply Chain Management page, click the **Preferences** link.
    
3.  On the Supply Chain Preferences page, click **Edit**.
    
4.  On the **Engineering** subtab, check the **Implement Engineering Change Order Upon Final Approval** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

If you don't want to use the preference, refer to the instructions in [Implementing Engineering Change Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1531291499.html).
