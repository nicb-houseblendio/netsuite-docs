Follow these steps to print a work order traveler.

#### To print a work order traveler:

1.  Go to Advanced Manufacturing > Documentation > File Folders.
    
2.  In the **File Cabinet**, click your traveler folder.
    
    Work order travelers are stored in the Advanced Manufacturing SuiteApp folder created by your administrator. For example, the folder name could be AM Travelers.
    
3.  Beside the traveler you want to view, click **Edit** or **View**.
    
4.  Click **Print**.
    
    Click **Download** to save the traveler.
