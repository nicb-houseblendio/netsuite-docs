You can mark items to create work orders when you enter a sales transaction by checking the box in the **Create WO** column. Then, when you save or approve the order, NetSuite creates a work order that is linked to the sale.

#### To create a work order from the sales order: {#procedure_N2330820}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Sales > Enter Sales Orders_.
    
2.  Enter information in the transaction header as needed.
    
    Required fields display a red asterisk (**\***).
    
3.  In the **Item** field, select an assembly item.
    
4.  Check the box in the **Create WO** column.
    
5.  Enter additional information as needed for this line item.
    
6.  Click **Add**.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
