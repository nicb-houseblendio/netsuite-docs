To use the Costed Bill of Materials SuiteApp, enable the features in the following table. For more information, see [Enabling Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N232138.html).

To work with subsidiaries, you need access to a NetSuite One World account and have enabled Subsidiaries. To learn more, see [Working with Subsidiaries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1548783281.html).

Note:

Following are some general information for the Costed Bill of Materials SuiteApp:

-   The currency of the cost displayed in the report is shown based on the default currency of the selected subsidiary.
    
-   The subassemblies should have a master default bill of materials and the default routing for location to get the detailed estimated cost.
    
-   The SuiteApp displays details in the **Materials Costs, Top Level Costs, and Fully Exploded Costs** subtabs irrespective of whether a work center, cost template, or routing is active or inactive.
    
-   The SuiteApp supports up to 900 levels of assembly items and up to 400 sub-assemblies in each level.
