Before you can create and use assembly items, an administrator must enable the Assembly Items and Inventory features.

#### To enable the Assembly Items and Inventory features:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features (Administrator)_.
    
2.  Click the **Items & Inventory** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Assembly Items** box.
    
4.  Check the **Inventory** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
