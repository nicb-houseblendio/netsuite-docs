The Sales Order screen enables you to account for controlled and non-controlled items.

#### To enter sales orders:

1.  Go to Transactions > Sales > Enter Sales Orders.
    
2.  Enter an **Order #** number.
    
3.  Enter an **Item**.
    
4.  In the **From Bin** field, enter the bin number the item is taken from.
    
5.  Enter the **Quantity**.
    
6.  Enter a **Lot Number**.
    
    For non-controlled items, leave the Lot Number box empty.
    
7.  Tap **Save**.
