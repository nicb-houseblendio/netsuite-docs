Employees assigned administrator or Data Scanner roles can log in to the Advanced Manufacturing SuiteApp from a tablet or scanner. However, effective NetSuite 2021.1, the advanced manufacturing scanner is discontinued. Existing installations can continue to use the scanner without any change in functionality.

For more information, see [Tablets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161789181492.html), [Scanners](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161789223254.html), and [Enabling LPN Action](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161789125030.html).
