Assembly Component preferences can help you with component requirement calculations. To use these component preferences, enable the Work Orders feature and adjust your item records settings that are available on the following:

-   **Use Component Yield** - Account for material component yield loss during the ordering and planning process
    
-   **Round Up Quantity as Component** - Round the component quantity up in the units used on work order
