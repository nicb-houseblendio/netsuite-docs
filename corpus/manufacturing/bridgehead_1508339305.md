Complete the Bin Transfer (Lot) window to transfer a lot controlled item from one bin to another.

#### To transfer lot controlled bins:

1.  In the **From Bin** field, enter the bin number the item is taken from.
    
2.  Enter an **Item** name.
    
3.  Enter the item **Lot Number**.
    
4.  In the **To Bin** field, enter the bin the item is going to.
    
5.  Enter the **Quantity** you are transferring.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

To display an item's bin location, enter the item information in the **Scanner Find** field, and then tap **Find**.
