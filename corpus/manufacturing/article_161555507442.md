Some manufacturing customers want to use Advanced BOM, but don't want to copy Legacy BOMs. These customers can enable legacy BOM editing.

#### To enable legacy BOM editing:

1.  Go to _Setup > Manufacturing > Manufacturing Preferences_.
    
2.  In the Product Definition section, check the **Allow Editing of Legacy BOMs** box,
    
3.  Click **I Agree**.
    
    After you click **Agree**, you cannot disable the Advanced BOM feature. It is important to test this feature in your sandbox account before enabling it in your production account.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
