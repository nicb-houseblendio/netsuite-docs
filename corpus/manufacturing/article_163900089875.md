Follow these steps to complete the Shop Calendar subtab.

#### To complete the shop calendar subtab:

1.  Click the **Shop Calendar** subtab.
    
2.  Enter a **Start Time** with the HH:MM am/pm format.
    
    This is when the work day starts.
    
3.  Enter an **End Time** with the HH:MM am/pm format.
    
    This is when the work day ends.
    
4.  Enter the total operating **Hours**.
    
    For example, if a Monday shift starts at 09:00 and ends at 16:30, Monday's operating hours are 7.5.
    
    -   For days that your shop doesn't operate, enter 0.
        
    -   Deduct break times from the total unless the work center continues to run during the break.
        
    -   Ensure to schedule half days, off days, or work stoppages.
        
5.  Repeat steps 2-4 for each day the work center operates.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
