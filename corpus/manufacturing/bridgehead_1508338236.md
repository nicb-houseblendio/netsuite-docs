Infinite capacity scheduling attempts to establish a detailed strategy for scheduling orders and operations. It doesn't account for current work center capacity or resource use. This strategy can result in resource overloads.

NetSuite uses infinite capacity scheduling to schedule work. For more information, see [Mass Creating Work Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2330082.html).
