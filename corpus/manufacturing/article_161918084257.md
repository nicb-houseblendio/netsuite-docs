The Production Data Entry Errors report displays reasons for automated work order transactions (completion and material issuance) failures. For example, incorrect workbench configuration or unavailable material components.

#### To review the Production Data Entry Errors report:

1.  Go to AM > Manufacturing Reports > Production Data Entry Errors.
    
2.  Review the report to see any errors due to production data entry.
