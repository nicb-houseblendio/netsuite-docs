The following procedure describes how to manually change actual production dates.

#### To override a actual production date:

1.  On the work order, below the **Actual Production Start** or **End Date**, click **Enter Manually**.
    
2.  Select a new date from the calendar.
    
    For example, you can change the date from Dec. 2 to Dec. 3.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
