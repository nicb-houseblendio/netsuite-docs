You can update details of an assembly build or unbuild transaction in response to changes in your production process as well as find information about those transactions.

To learn information about an assembly build transactions, go to _Transactions > Manufacturing > Build Assemblies > List_. Click **Edit** next to an assembly build. A **Related Records** subtab displays a list of all related Assembly Unbuilds. If applicable, a new **Unbuilt** field is also displayed, showing the total quantity unbuilt from all Assembly Unbuilds created from this Assembly Build. You can change the Quantity to Build.

Note:

Quantity to Build cannot be below the quantity already unbuilt. For assembly builds related to the same Work Order, the value can be reduced. However, the total Quantity to Build of all assembly builds related to the work order must not be lower than the total Unbuilt quantity. Total **Built** and **Unbuilt** quantities are visible on the parent Work Order and in Manufacturing Transaction Dataset in Analytics.

To learn information about an assembly build transactions, go to _Transactions > Manufacturing > Unbuild Assemblies > List_. Click **Edit** next to an assembly unbuild. A **Created From** field contains a link to the related Assembly Build. You can change the Quantity to Unbuild.

Note:

You cannot unbuild more than the quantity previously built. For standalone assembly builds, the unbuild quantity cannot exceed Built quantity of the assembly build. For assembly builds related to the same Work Order, the Quantity to Unbuild cannot exceed the total Built quantity of all assembly builds associated with the work order.
