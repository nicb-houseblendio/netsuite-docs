Complete the Bin Transfer window to move or transfer non-controlled items from one bin to another.

#### To transfer a non-controlled bin:

1.  In the **From Bin** field, enter the bin number the item is taken from.
    
2.  Enter the **Item** name.
    
3.  In the **To Bin** field, enter the bin the item is going to.
    
4.  Enter the **Quantity** you are transferring.
    
5.  Tap **Save**.
