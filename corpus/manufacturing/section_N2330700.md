When you use the Work Orders feature, you can create work orders for assembly items from sales orders. You can mark items to create work orders in two ways. You can mark the item when you create the sales order, or you can mark the item record. For more information, see [Creating Work Orders From a Sales Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161538656149.html) and [Creating Work Orders From Item Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161538684793.html).
