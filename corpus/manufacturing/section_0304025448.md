On the Manufacturing Scheduler page, you can view the list of work centers or work orders through work views. Based on the selected work view, the left pane shows the operations mapped under that view. By default, the left pane displays the Work Center view where operation tasks appear under their assigned work centers. You can switch to the Work Order view to see all operations that belong to each work order.

In Work Order view, you can also see each work order's assembly item and the assigned work center for every operation. On the chart, the summary bar across each work order represents all operations it includes.

Note:

-   You cannot reassign and reschedule operations by dragging and dropping of the task bars. You must switch back to Work Center view to update the operations directly on the chart.
    
-   You can display a maximum of 20 work centers or work orders per page.
