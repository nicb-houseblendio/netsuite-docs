Follow these steps to log in to the tablet.

#### To log in to the tablet:

1.  Go to Advanced Manufacturing > Tablet Data Entry > Tablet.
    
2.  Enter your NetSuite **email** address and **Password**, and then click **Login**.
    
3.  In the Work Queue, click **List** to select the **Work Center** you want to work in.
    
4.  To choose a **Start Date**, tap **Select** to open the date picker.
    
5.  To choose an **End Date**, tap **Select** to open the date picker.
    
6.  To display specific information in the work queue, enter a search phrase in the **Quick Search** field.
    
    Clear the search field to display all available work queue operations that match the Work Center and Date filters.
    
7.  Tap **OK**.
