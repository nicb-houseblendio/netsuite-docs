The Costed Bill of Materials SuiteApp calculates the total cost of an assembly based on a bill of materials revision, manufacturing routing, and cost templates used. The cost is calculated by adding up the costs of the components.

The SuiteApp also enables you to do the following:

-   View the comprehensive cost breakdown of an assembly item, including bills of materials, operations, and cost types based on selected bill of materials revision.
    
-   View and analyze the costs of each component needed for an assembly, including material and conversion costs for each component.
    
-   Estimate the potential costs of manufacturing assembly items for bill of materials based on costing method, purchase price, and item default price.
    
-   Access cost breakdowns for bills of materials as part of your product engineering and costing processes.
    

You can access the SuiteApp by navigating to Reports > Cost Accounting > Costed Bill of Materials.

Note:

The cost calculations are not based on planned standard cost, cost re-evaluation, or cost roll-up values for any standard cost assembly item.
