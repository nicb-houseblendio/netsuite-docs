In the Work Center view, use the search field to search for a work center that does not appear on the current page.

#### To search for a work center:

1.  On the Manufacturing Scheduler page > Work Center view, in the search field, enter minimum of three characters of a work center name.
    
2.  Click the search icon to start your search. You'll only see the work center found and its assigned work orders on the chart.
    
3.  To return to the initial list of work centers, remove search criteria from the search field, and click the search icon.
    

Use the **Expand All** or **Collapse All** links to show or hide the work orders of all work centers at the same time. Work centers expand or collapse on all pages, and your selection stays the same as you switch pages.

When work centers or work orders are collapsed, the chart displays only summary bars. Use the summary bars to compare work centers and spot overloaded or underloaded resources so you can make adjustments.
