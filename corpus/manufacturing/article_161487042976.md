NetSuite lets you make assembly items available in other product areas.

## Purchase Transactions {#bridgehead_161487123865}

To make assemblies available to purchase transactions, go to _Setup > Accounting > Preferences > Accounting Preferences_. On the Order Management subtab, check the **Allow the Purchase of Assembly Items** box on the Order Management subtab, and then save. For information about preferences, see [Items/Transactions Accounting Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1387022.html).

## Web Sites {#bridgehead_1513089472}

You can offer an assembly in your web site using the **Store** and **Specials** subtabs. Edit the assembly item record to add the item to your web site.

## Work Orders {#bridgehead_N2319400}

The Work Orders feature enables you to track the production of assembly items for stock or to fill orders. Work orders track the quantities of assemblies to be built, and the required quantities of components, or member items. Use **Special Order Work Orders** for a particular sale. Use **Production Work Orders** to increase stock. For more information, see [Assembly Work Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2328390.html).
