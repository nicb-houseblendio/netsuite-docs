You can print all the members of an assembly item, including display names, quantities, descriptions, and rates on an invoice or other transaction form. Alternatively, you can print only the description and amount of the assembly item on the form.

Note:

If you have enabled Advanced Bills of Materials, this feature isn't available.

#### To print raw materials on transactions: {#procedure_N2324357}

1.  Go to _Lists > Accounting > Items_.
    
2.  In the **Type** field, select **Assembly** to filter your item list to show assembly items.
    
3.  Click **Edit** next to the assembly item you want to change.
    
4.  Check the **Display Components on Transaction** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

You must enter a display name on the member item records for an item name to appear.

Your administrator can customize the layout of your printed forms using Advanced PDF/HTML templates.

For more information, see [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4453550706.html).
