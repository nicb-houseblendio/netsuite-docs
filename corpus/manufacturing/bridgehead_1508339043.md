Follow these steps to record scrap using a scanner.

#### To record loss using a scanner:

1.  Enter a **Scrap Reason**.
    
    For example, spoilage, container breakage, or left over material.
    
2.  Enter a scrap **Quantity**.
    
3.  Tap **Save**.
    
    Scrap time is recorded at the time you click save.
