The Shipping Action feature lets you initiate shipping transactions. To pack or ship an item, change the default in the AM Administrator record. Non-controlled and lot-controlled items appear in the same window.

Shipping Action provides access to the bin transfer features. See [Transferring Non-Controlled Bins](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1508339266.html) and [Transferring Lot Controlled Bins](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1508339305.html).

For information about the shipping action option, see [Entering Sales Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1508339413.html).
