An administrator can use the following procedure to enable the Work Orders feature.

#### To enable the Work Orders feature: {#procedure_N2328710}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Items and Inventory** subtab.
    
3.  In the Inventory section, check the **Work Orders** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

After the feature is enabled, you can set the Build Based On Commitment accounting preference. For more information, see [Order Management Accounting Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1388149.html).
