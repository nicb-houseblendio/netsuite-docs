After you create BOM records and revisions, you can link them to individual assemblies. You can also link BOM records to multiple assemblies. For more information, see [Linking a BOM to an Assembly](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1517860901.html) and [Linking Assemblies to BOM Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1518448516.html).
