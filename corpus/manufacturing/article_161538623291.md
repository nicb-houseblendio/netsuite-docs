The following procedure explains how to manually enter planned work orders.

#### To manually enter planned work orders: {#procedure_3891033791}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Manufacturing > Enter Work Orders_.
    
2.  In the **Status** field, select **Planned**.
    
3.  Complete additional fields on the form as necessary.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
