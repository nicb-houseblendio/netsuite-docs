Complete the Receive PO - Lot Controlled window to receive purchase orders with lot controlled items or a mix of non-controlled and lot-controlled items. Lot numbers are not required for non-controlled items, but are required for controlled items.

#### To receive a lot controlled purchase order:

1.  Enter a **Purchase Order** number.
    
2.  Enter an **Item**.
    
3.  In the **To Bin** field, enter the bin the item is going to.
    
4.  Enter the **Quantity**.
    
5.  Enter a **Lot Number**.
    
6.  Enter an optional **Expiration Date** in YYMMDD format.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
