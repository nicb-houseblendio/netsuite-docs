The assembly item inventory detail assembles by calling the following plug-ins:

-   **Assembly Bin Plug-in** - searches for bins associated with the assembly item.
    
    1.  The plug-in reviews the work center to check whether a **Complete To** bin is assigned to it.
        
        If the work center has a bin assigned to it, the plug-in uses that bin.
        
    2.  If a completed to bin isn't assigned, the plug-in checks the assembly item for a preferred bin.
        
        If the preferred assembly has a bin assigned to it, the plug-in uses that bin.
        
    3.  If the assembly doesn't have a bin, the plug-in returns a null, the plug-in returns a null.
        
        The operator then needs to identify a bin during production reporting on the tablet.
        
-   **Assembly Lot/Serial Plug-in** - returns a null to the tablet. The operator must provide lot/serial number(s) to complete the transaction.
