Follow these steps to check for traveler generation errors.

#### To verify traveler generation errors:

1.  Go to Customization > Scripting > Scripts.
    
2.  If necessary, click the + icon to expand the **Filters** pane.
    
3.  In the **Type** list, select **Scheduled**.
    
4.  Beside the traveler record you want to modify, click **Edit**.
    
5.  Click the **Execution Log** subtab.
    
    Errors are displayed in the **Details** column.
