Follow these steps to set the traveler bar code size.

#### To define traveler bar code size:

1.  Go to Customization > Scripting > Scripts.
    
2.  If necessary, click the + icon to expand the **Filters** pane.
    
3.  In the **Type** list, select **Scheduled**.
    
4.  Click **Edit** beside the AM Template or AM Batch Traveler.
    
5.  Click the **Deployments** subtab.
    
6.  Click the traveler title you want to modify.
    
7.  Click **Edit**.
    
8.  Click the **Parameters** subtab.
    
9.  Modify the values in the **Barcode Width (In)** and **Barcode Height (Pix)** fields as necessary.
    
10.  Click **Save**.
