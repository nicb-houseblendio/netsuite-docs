When you use manufacturing routing, define the routing steps associated with the sub-assembly build on the manufacturing routing of the higher-level assembly. For more information , see [Creating a Manufacturing Routing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2345383.html).

An assembly demand plan with a phantom sub-assembly in its bill of materials calculates dependent demand for components of the phantom item. However, it doesn't calculate for the phantom itself. For more information, see [Demand Planning](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2286970.html).
