Before you enable the Engineering Change Order feature, complete the following prerequisites:

| Task | Related Help Topic |
| --- | --- |
| Step 1: Enable the Advanced Bill of Materials (BOM) feature. | [Enabling Advanced BOM](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1497967586.html) |
| Step 2: Install the Supply Chain Management SuiteApp | [Installing the Supply Chain Management SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4258666051.html) |

Important:

Only users with the Administrator role can access the Supply Chain Preferences page.

#### To enable Engineering Change Order:

1.  Go to Transactions > Management > Supply Chain Management.
    
2.  On the Supply Chain Management page, click the **Preferences** link.
    
3.  On the Supply Chain Preferences page, click **Edit**.
    
4.  On the **Features** subtab, check the **Engineering Change Order** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

After you enable the feature, you'll need to set up role records for users of Engineering Change Order. For more information, see [Customizing Roles for Engineering Change Order](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1531289591.html).
