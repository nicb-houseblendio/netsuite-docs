The Engineering Change Order feature is included in the Supply Chain Management SuiteApp starting version 4.0. For more information, see [Installing the Supply Chain Management SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4258666051.html).

The Engineering Change Order feature lets you create engineering change order (ECO) records. These records track changes to your Bills of Materials (BOMs) and allow you to approve those changes.

-   With the ECO record, you can list the items and BOMs to update and say why you're making the change.
    
-   With the SuiteApprovals workflow, you set up approval rules for your ECO records.
    
-   The system evaluates each ECO record and sends it for approval based on the applicable rule.
    
-   After approval,you can apply changes from the ECO record automatically or manually, and the system updates the affected records.
    
-   You can track item and BOM revisions with ECO lists and implementation logs.
    

Make sure you're familiar with the following concepts before you turn on Engineering Change Order:

-   **Advanced Bill of Materials** - List how much raw materials, assemblies, sub-components, and parts you need to make a product at one or more facilities. After your administrator enables the Advanced Bill of Materials feature, the Advanced BOM replaces the Assembly/Bill of Materials record. For more information, see [Advanced Bill of Materials](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1501506444.html).
    
-   **Approval Routing** - When you use approval routing, transactions that are entered are not processed until they are approved.
    
    Approval routing gives people in charge the ability to reject transactions if they don't want them to go through. For more information, see [Approval Routing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2394992.html).
    

## Availability {#bridgehead_1531288918}

The Engineering Change Order feature is available in the Supply Chain Management SuiteApp. You can also contact your NetSuite account manager to get access to this SuiteApp. For information about this SuiteApp, see [Supply Chain Management SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3837065612.html).
