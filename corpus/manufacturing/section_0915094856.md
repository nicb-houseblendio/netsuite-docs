If the Advanced Bill of Materials feature is disabled, the sublevel items of an assembly item are not considered when calculating ATB. In this scenario, the ATB value for an assembly item is calculated from the on-hand quantity. For more information about how to enable or disable Advanced Bill of Materials feature, see the help topic [Enabling Advanced BOM](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1497967586.html).

Note:

The following capabilities are affected when Advanced Bill of Materials feature is disabled:

-   The Item 360 Manufacturing page does not display **Available to Build** portlet.
    
-   You don't need the Advanced Bill of Materials feature to calculate ATB for kit items, the ATB for kits is calculated based on the item master component list.
    
-   On the Sales Order page, you won't see the **Calculate Available to Build** option for assembly items.
