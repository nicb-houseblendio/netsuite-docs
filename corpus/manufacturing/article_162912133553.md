Use the following procedure to copy an engineering change order record.

#### To copy an engineering change order record:

1.  Go to Lists > Engineering > Engineering Change Order.
    
    Important:
    
    The Make Copy function for ECO records doesn't copy Product Changes entries.
    
2.  On the Engineering Change Order List page, find the ECO record you want to copy.
    
3.  Click the **View** link for the ECO record.
    
4.  On the ECO record, from the **Actions** list, select **Make Copy**.
    
5.  Make changes in the Primary Information fields, as needed.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
