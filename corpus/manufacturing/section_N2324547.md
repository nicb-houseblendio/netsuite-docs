The following procedure describes how to print labels for assembly member items and the assembly item.

#### To print labels for each member item and the assembly item:

1.  Go to _Transactions > Management > Print Checks and Forms_.
    
2.  Click **Item Labels**.
    
3.  On the **Print Item Labels** page, click **Customize**.
    
4.  On the **Customize Sublist** page, click the **Additional Filters** sublist.
    
5.  Check the **Component Of** box.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
7.  On the **Print Item Labels** page, select the assembly from the **Component Of** list.
    
8.  Click **Mark All**.
    
9.  Click **Print**.
