Production planning enables companies to optimize their manufacturing processes through the efficient use of available resources. The Advanced Manufacturing SuiteApp lets planners create capacity plans that capture and analyze an organization's production capacity. Effective production plans set the route for each item, estimates item start and end dates, and evaluates the available capacity for completing the work.

Advanced Manufacturing defines capacity as the maximum amount of work your work centers and equipment can complete over a given period.
