The Advanced Manufacturing SuiteApp offers a variety of reports that you can use to present and analyze real-time manufacturing results. Each report appears in a separate window where you can preview results and refine your criteria before saving or printing them.

To save any report, in the report viewer tool bar, click **Export**, and then click **Save** or **Print**. The available formats are XML, CSV, PDF, HTML, Excel, TIFF, or Word.

Reports are grouped based on usage:

-   [Administrative Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1508342105.html)
    
-   [Production Planning Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1508342615.html)
    
-   [Shop Floor Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1508342965.html)
    
-   [Operating Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1508343204.html)
