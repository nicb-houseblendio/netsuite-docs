Downtime refers to a period of time when a system fails to perform its primary function. Downtime is usually a result of system failure due to an unplanned event. For example, human error, equipment failure, or resource shortage.

Planned events can also be recorded as downtime. For example, routine maintenance.

#### To record downtime:

1.  Go to Advanced Manufacturing > Data Collection > Enter Results/Loss/Downtime/Labor.
    
2.  Click the **Downtime** subtab.
    
3.  Enter the downtime **Start Time**.
    
    Use the 7/11/2020 7:25:04 am format.
    
4.  Enter the total time in minutes the asset was not available in the **Duration (Min)** field.
    
5.  Select a **Downtime Reason**.
    
    For example, machine breakdown, power outage, or material shortage.
    
6.  (Optional) Select the asset affected from the **Asset Name** list.
    
    If you don't select an asset, NetSuite uses the asset the work order was scheduled for.
    
7.  (Optional) Select the name of the **Employee** who entered the data.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
