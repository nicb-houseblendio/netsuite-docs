## A {#subsect_0916043428}

**All Levels**

The All levels approach considers the entire BOM hierarchy including sub-assemblies and their respective components. The ATB value is calculated at all levels by considering both immediate components and multilevel components, including sub-assemblies and sub-kit items.

**Assembly Item**

An assembly item is an inventory item made of several components (defined by a Bill of Materials) but is identified as a single item. The available to build is calculated for assembly items.

**Available**

This term represents the available quantity in the inventory search after the commitment of stock in the units of measure (UOM).

**Available to Build**

The ATB value is a metric derived from the Available to Build SuiteApp. The resulted value is a comprehensive calculation of either assembly items or kit item types at top level or all levels.

## K {#subsect_0916053417}

**Kit Item**

A kit item consists of a group of components, parts, or sub-assemblies. These items are grouped together based on a predefined configuration to form a complete unit.

## O {#subsect_0916053444}

**On-hand**

This term represents the available quantity of an item as base multiple units of measure (UOM) in the inventory for a selected location.

## T {#subsect_0916053510}

**Top Level**

The top level refers to the highest level in a BOM hierarchy. It represents the end product that is assembled from various components and sub-assemblies. The ATB value is calculated at the top level based on the on-hand quantity of immediate components.

## U {#subsect_0916053535}

**Units**

The resulted ATB value is in the format of base unit of an item record.

## W {#subsect_0916053540}

**Work Order Quantity**

In a work order transaction, the sum of outstanding work order quantities of work orders with the statuses **Released** or **In Progress**.
