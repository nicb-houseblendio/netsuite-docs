When **Multiple Currencies** feature is enabled in the **Enable Features** page after the SuiteApp is installed, you must perform the following steps.

## To enable Multiple Currency feature: {#bridgehead_60153136176}

1.  Go to Setup > User/Roles > Manage Roles.
    
2.  On the **Costed\_BOM\_Role** role row, click **Customize**. The Role page opens.
    
3.  Create a custom role.
    
    1.  (Optional) To rename the custom role, enter the role name in the **Name** field.
        
    2.  In the **Permissions** subtab, click the **Lists** subtab.
        
    3.  In a new row, select **Currency**.
        
    4.  From the level list, select **View**.
        
    5.  Click **Add**.
        
    6.  Click **Save**.
        
4.  Add the custom role to the scripts:
    
    1.  Go to Customization > Scripting > Scripts.
        
    2.  On the **Costed Bill of Materials** script row, click **Deployments**.
        
    3.  On the Script Deployments page, click **Edit**.
        
    4.  In the **Execute As Role** field, select the custom role that you created in step **3**.
        
    5.  Click **Save**.
        
    6.  Repeat steps **4.1** to **4.5** for **Costed BOM DataService** script.
