You can also enter a close for a single work order.

#### To enter an individual work order completion: {#procedure_N2340492}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Manufacturing > Enter Work Orders > List_.
    
2.  Beside the work order you want to close, click **View**.
    
3.  Click the **Close** button.
    
4.  Complete additional fields on the Work order Close page as necessary.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
