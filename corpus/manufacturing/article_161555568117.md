The following procedure describes how to create a BOM from an assembly.

#### To create a BOM from an assembly: {#procedure_161287803878}

1.  To create a new assembly or use an existing assembly, go to _Lists > Accounting > Items_.
    
2.  Beside the assembly you want to create a BOM for, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Click the **Manufacturing** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Bill of Materials** list, click **New**.
    
5.  Complete the **Bill of Materials** window.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

Assemblies with six or more BOM levels may interfere with the Material Requirements Planning process performance.
