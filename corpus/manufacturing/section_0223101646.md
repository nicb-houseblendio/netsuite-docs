The Manufacturing Scheduler SuiteApp requires a NetSuite OneWorld account.

Only users with the Administrator role can install the SuiteApp. For more information about installing a SuiteApp, see [Installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539799323.html) and [SuiteApp Marketplace in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1539797981.html).
