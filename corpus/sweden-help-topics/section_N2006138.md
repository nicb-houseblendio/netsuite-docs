If you have a Sweden subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the Sweden VAT Report, in English or Swedish, from Reports > VAT/GST > Tax Reports (International).

Important:

To generate the tax report for Sweden, use the tax code properties provided by the International Tax Reports SuiteApp. See [Sweden Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2004717.html).

NetSuite gets the values in the report from your transactions, but some boxes in the form may require you to enter data manually. It is important that you save a PDF file of the report for your own records because NetSuite cannot audit manually entered data. For information, see [Generating VAT/GST Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2064551.html).

You should review all the values in the report. You can click the values in the boxes of the VAT report to view drilldown details. To understand how NetSuite uses the tax codes to get the values for the VAT declaration for Sweden, see [What goes into each box - Sweden VAT report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2006403.html).

Important:

The NetSuite VAT/GST tax form is designed to look like the official tax form for ease of use when completing the official return form. It isn't intended for submission to the tax agency.

![Screenshot of Sweden VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/SwedenVATReport.png)

Note:

You can also use customizable saved reports provided by the International Tax Reports SuiteApp to view both detail and summary reports for purchases or sales, grouped by tax code. For more information, see [Sales and Purchase Reports Grouped by Tax Code](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2067748.html). For EU sales list and Intrastat reports, see [EU Sales List for Sweden](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2009636.html) and [EU Intrastat Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2069213.html).
