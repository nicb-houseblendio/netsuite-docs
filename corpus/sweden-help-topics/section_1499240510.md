The Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp lets businesses in Sweden to pay vendor bills and employee expenses by generating payment files formatted using the following formats:

-   Bankgiro (SEB)
    
-   Plusgiro (SEB)
    
-   SEPA Credit Transfer (SEB)
    

To set up the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp for your company, subsidiaries, vendors, employees, customers, and partners in Sweden, read the following topics:

-   [Setting Up Company Bank Records in Sweden](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1499240872.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Vendors in Sweden](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1499244850.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Employees in Sweden](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1499247519.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Customers in Sweden](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1499248325.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Partners in Sweden](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1499248946.html)
