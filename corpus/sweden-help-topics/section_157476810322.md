If you have a Sweden subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the VAT declaration for Sweden, in English or Swedish. The report can be used as a source for filling in the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.

For more information, read the following Sweden tax topics:

-   [Sweden Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2004717.html)
    
    -   _Sweden Tax Code Changes_
        
    -   [Tax Code Table for Sweden](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2004717.html#bridgehead_N2004861)
        
-   [Sweden VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2006138.html)
