You should also setup accounting periods for a full year.

#### To setup an accounting period: {#procedure_157598325997}

1.  Go to Setup > Accounting > Manage G/L > Manage Accounting Periods.
    
2.  Click the **Set Up Full Year** button.
    
3.  In the First Fiscal Month dropdown list, select the month that marks the beginning of a fiscal year.
    
4.  In the Fiscal Year End field, enter or accept the four-digit fiscal year for which you want to generate accounting periods.
    
5.  Select the format you want to use for the accounting periods in the year from the Period Format dropdown list.
    
6.  In the Year in Period Name dropdown list, select either starting year or ending year as the name of the fiscal period.
    
7.  Click Save.
    

For more information, see [Setting Up Accounting Periods for a Year](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1446183.html).

To complete the account setup, you should also enable the SuiteTax Plug-In and assign a tax registration to a subsidiary. Enabling the SuiteTax Plug-In permits you to select SuiteTax as your tax engine. You can enable the plug-in at _Customization > Plug-Ins > Manage Plug-Ins_. For more information, see [Enabling the SuiteTax Plug-In](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557399475.html).

To add a tax registration for a subsidiary, go to _Setup > Company > Classifications > Subsidiaries_. Click Edit next to the subsidiary. On the Tax Registrations subtab, select a country, nexus, and tax engine for this nexus. Enter a tax registration number, and click Save. For more information, see [Assigning Tax Registrations to a Subsidiary in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4296038273.html).
