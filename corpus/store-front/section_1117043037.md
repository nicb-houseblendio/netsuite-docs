Note:

For information about migrating from Google Universal Analytics (GUA) to Google Analytics 4 (GA4) without Google Tag Manager (GTM), see [Migrating from Google Universal Analytics to Google Analytics 4](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1116015217.html).

Google will end data collection for the GUA service on July 1, 2023. You will no longer be able to use GUA to analyze new data, such as new visitor data to your NetSuite Commerce web store. Migrate to GA4 to continue using Google's analytics services to collect new data on your Commerce website.

Note:

You will still be able to analyze existing data you gathered through GUA for an additional six months from July 1, 2023.

#### To migrate from GUA to GA4 (SuiteCommerce, SuiteCommerce My Account, SuiteCommerce Advanced 2018.2 and later): {#procedure_1117043640}

1.  Log in to your Google Analytics account and set up GA4. See [Migrating from GUA to GA4 in Your Google Analytics Account](#subsect_8164112754).
    
2.  Ensure you have installed the bundle for the latest SuiteCommerce GTM Editor extension. See [Commerce Extensions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1530632028.html) for the latest bundle ID and [Installing Theme and Extension SuiteApps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510953648.html) for information about installing Commerce Extension SuiteApps.
    
3.  Implement GA4 using GTM by adding the GA4 service to the GTM Editor. See [Modify Google Tag Manager Services](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1535475532.html) for information about implementing GA4 using GTM and the SuiteCommerce Google Tag Manager Editor extension.
    
    Note:
    
    For implementations of SuiteCommerce Advanced (SCA) 2018.2 - 2022.2 using Google Analytics 4 with the Google Tag Manager Commerce Extension, review the data-layer structure of SCA version 2023.1 or later and compare it with your current version. With the release of SCA 2023.1, modifications were made in the data-layer code to comply with Google and correctly track information with Google Analytics events. For information about the suggested data-layer structure you should use, see [Google Tag Manager Events Not Displaying in Data Layer Upon Checkout](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1151545068.html).
    

#### To migrate from GUA to GA4 (SuiteCommerce Advanced Mont Blanc, Vinson, Elbrus, Kilimanjaro, and Aconcagua): {#procedure_0110023723}

1.  Log in to your Google Analytics account and set up GA4. See [Migrating from GUA to GA4 in Your Google Analytics Account](#subsect_8164112754).
    
2.  Confirm you have correctly configured GTM for your SCA implementation. See [Set Up Google Tag Manager (SuiteCommerce Advanced Aconcagua and Earlier)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1532009142.html) for information about implementing GA4 using GTM and the SuiteCommerce Google Tag Manager container generator.
    

#### To migrate from GUA to GA4 (SuiteCommerce Advanced Denali, ShopFlow, and Site Builder): {#procedure_0110024001}

1.  Log in to your Google Analytics account and set up GA4. See [Migrating from GUA to GA4 in Your Google Analytics Account](#subsect_8164112754).
    
2.  GTM isn't available for sites built on SCA Denali and earlier, ShopFlow, or Site Builder. To implement GA4 on these sites, your development team will need to customize your site's code.
    
    For SCA Denali and earlier, your development team can add Google Analytics events in your data layer. See [Enable Google Analytics 4 (Denali - Mont Blanc)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1129124501.html).
    
    See [Google Analytics with ShopFlow and Site Builder](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0110020411.html) for information about implementing Google Analytics on a Site Builder website.
    

## Migrating from GUA to GA4 in Your Google Analytics Account {#subsect_8164112754}

If you're already using GTM for your Commerce website, most of the steps you must take will need top be performed in your Google Analytics account. To install GTM, see [Set Up Google Tag Manager](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1525975256.html).

See the following Google support topics for information about migrating from GUA to GA4:

-   [Migrate from Universal Analytics to Google Analytics 4](https://support.google.com/analytics/?hl=en#topic=10737980) - Table of contents.
    
-   [Make the switch to Google Analytics 4](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10759417?hl=en) - Instructions about getting started and finishing the migration to GA4.
    
-   [Comparing metrics: Google Analytics 4 vs Universal Analytics](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11986666?hl=en&ref_topic=10737980) - Information about comparing metrics between GA4 and GUA.
    
-   [Migration reference](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10607999?hl=en&ref_topic=10737980) - Instructions about identifying the GA4 functionality you need to migrate.
    
    Note:
    
    You will not be able to import data collected in GUA into GA4.
    
-   [Universal Analytics view-related features in Google Analytics 4 properties](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10845666?hl=en&ref_topic=10737980) - A side-by-side list of configurations and features associated with reporting views in GUA and GA4.
    
-   [Data collection and tagging](https://support.google.com/analytics/topic/11192706?hl=en&ref_topic=10737980) - Information about data collection and tagging.
    
-   [Google Ads links migration tool](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10646596?hl=en&ref_topic=10737980) - Instructions about migrating your GUA property's Google Ads links to your GA4 property.
    
-   [Goals migration tool](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10762548?hl=en&ref_topic=10737980) - Information about the goals migration tool which lets you recreate eligible goals from your connected GUA property as conversion events in your GA4 property.
    
-   [User migration tool](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11894572?hl=en&ref_topic=10737980) - Information about the user migration tool which lets you migrate your GUA users to your GA4 property.
    
-   [Event migration guide](https://support.google.com/analytics/topic/11091421?hl=en&ref_topic=10737980) - Information about migrating your existing events from a GUA property to a GA4 property.
    
-   [Goal and conversion migration guide](https://support.google.com/analytics/topic/11053232?hl=en&ref_topic=10737980) - Information about replicating your GUA goals and conversions in GA4.
    
-   [Audiences migration guide](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11184423?hl=en&ref_topic=10737980) - Information about migrating audiences from your GUA property to your GA4 property.
    
-   [How to check property type](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11583832?hl=en&ref_topic=10737980) - Instructions about how to identify if you have a GAU property or a GA4 property.
