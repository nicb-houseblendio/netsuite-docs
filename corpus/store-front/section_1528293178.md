The Columns extension lets you add, set up, or delete up to three columns of text, images, and call-to-action buttons with Site Management Tools (SMT). You can choose to add one, two, or three columns. The column layout automatically adjusts to fit your page and screen size. With Columns, you can organize your web content into manageable sections.

Important:

Commerce extensions are only available if provisioned, installed, and activated for a selected domain in your NetSuite account. For more information, see [Commerce Extensions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1530632028.html).

Note:

Extensions require SuiteCommerce or the Aconcagua release of SuiteCommerce Advanced or later.
