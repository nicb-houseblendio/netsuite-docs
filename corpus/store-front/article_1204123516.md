Removing a blog post makes it unavailable for visitors to your site.

#### To remove a blog post:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Click the Overview Mode icon ![Overview Mode Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/OverviewModeIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
3.  Select the **Blog** subtab.
    
4.  Find the blog post you want to delete and click the ellipsis.
    
5.  Click **Remove**.
    
6.  When prompted, click **OK** to remove the blog post.
    
7.  Publish the change. For details, see [Review and Publish Content in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4419210472.html).
