The Web Store Transactions chapter ties together the checkout, payment, and shipping options for your site. We'll walk you through all the choices, like setting up your checkout steps, Paypal accounts, taxes for different countries, and shipping options.

Explore the following topics to learn more about the transaction features available for your Commerce site:

-   [Checkout](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3753900404.html) - This section describes the different checkout flows and options available for your web store checkout experience.
    
-   [Payment Options for Commerce Web Stores](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2469683.html) - These topics guide you step-by-step to set up payment options for your web store, like credit cards, Paypal, and other methods.
    
-   [Shipping Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2462067.html) - This section lets you set up how shipping works on your site and what options your customers have.
    
-   [Device Fingerprinting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4652727157.html) - This option lets your site collect information about the device placing the order to see if it's trustworthy.
    
-   [Commerce Web Store Taxes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1501094122.html) - SuiteCommerce Advanced enables you to display tax amounts for different International editions of NetSuite.
