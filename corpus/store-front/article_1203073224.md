You can edit text content when you need to make changes.

#### To edit text content:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Go to the page where the text content is located.
    
3.  Click the Edit Mode icon ![Edit Mode Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/EditModeIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
4.  Hover over the text content to display the content controls.
    
5.  Click **Edit** to open the Text Settings in the side panel.
    
6.  Make any required changes and click **Save**.
