If you haven't published your text content yet and want to get rid of it, you can discard it. If it's already published, you can remove it directly from the page. To learn the difference between discarding and removing content, see [Discard or Expire Content in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1513888480.html).

#### To discard text content (unpublished content):

1.  Log into Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Click the Review & Publish Changes icon ![Review and Publish Changes Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/ReviewAndPublishChangesIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
3.  Select the text content from the Unpublished Changes list.
    
4.  Click **Discard**.
    
5.  In the pop up window that appears, click **Yes**.
    

#### To remove text content (published content):

1.  Log into Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Go to the page where the text content is located.
    
3.  Click the Edit Mode icon ![Edit Mode Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/EditModeIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
4.  Hover over the text content to display the content controls.
    
5.  Click **X** to remove the text content from the page.
    
6.  In the pop up window that appears, click **Yes**.
