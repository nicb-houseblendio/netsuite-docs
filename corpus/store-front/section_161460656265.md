Subscriptions for Commerce Websites provides two pricing modifiers:

-   [Discount Pricing Modifiers](#subsect_158102201842)
    
-   [Usage Pricing Modifiers](#subsect_158102208603)
    

You can combine these modifiers with different pricing models, like Tiered or Volume, to give your customers extra savings or volume discounts.

Subscriptions for Commerce websites supports the pricing model and modifier combinations shown below.

|  | **Tiered Pricing Model** | **Volume Pricing Model** | **No Pricing Model** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Discount Pricing Modifier** | X | X | X |
| **Usage Pricing Modifier** |  |  | X |

## Discount Pricing Modifiers {#subsect_158102201842}

Discount pricing modifiers take a percentage off the total purchase amount, based on the total net amount.

**Example:**

-   Total Amount: $95.00
    
-   Discount: 10%
    
-   Calculated value of the discount ($95.00 \* 10%) = $9.50
    
-   Total amount after discounts ($95.00 - $9.50) = $85.50
    

## Usage Pricing Modifiers {#subsect_158102208603}

Usage Pricing Modifiers don't change the total price directly, but they affect how the price shows up in Commerce websites. Since the total amount is based on how much a service is used, the total is not known prior to billing. For example, if you charge $5.00 per megabyte used per month, the total isn't known until the end of the month.
