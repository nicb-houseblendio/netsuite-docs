Note:

If you're a SuiteCommerce Advanced customer, you need to be on the Kilimanjaro release or later to use this feature.

SuiteCommerce supports the display of VAT, GST, and PST tax amounts for Canadian, UK, Australian, and New Zealand editions of NetSuite.

Before you enable VAT/GST/PST display in your Commerce web store, make sure your NetSuite account has the right tax types and codes set up. For detailed information about setting up taxes for your NetSuite account, see [Taxation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/set_N1791082.html).

Note:

You can only show VAT/GST/PST tax breakdowns in single Australian, New Zealand, UK, or Canadian editions, or in OneWorld editions with only one subsidiary set up.

#### To enable the display of VAT/GST/HST in your Commerce web store:

1.  Go to Commerce > Websites > Configuration and pick the website and domain you want to set up.
    
2.  On the **Shopping Catalog** subtab, enable the option **If the Information is Available to your Country, shows detailed taxes in the cart**.
    
3.  Make sure the right tax codes are entered on the **Accounting** subtab of your Item records.
    
4.  For Australian and New Zealand accounts, set the **Prices Include Tax** option on the **Sales/Pricing** subtab of the Item record. Here's what happens depending on your selection:
    
    -   If **Prices Include Tax** is enabled, the product page shows the total cost including tax. At checkout, you'll see the item cost and tax broken out separately.
        
    -   If **Prices Include Tax** isn't enabled, the product page shows the cost without tax. At checkout, you'll still see the item cost and tax broken out separately.
        

After you've set up VAT/GST/HST display, users see the breakdown of VAT/GST/PST tax amounts during checkout.

Note:

For Canadian accounts, a user must be logged in and have a default address selected. If not, a tax code error is returned.
