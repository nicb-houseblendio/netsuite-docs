If you've enabled the Advanced Employee Permissions feature, your customers might not be able to view their transaction information in the My Account area of your Commerce website.

Note:

To confirm if Advanced Employee Permissions have been enabled, go to Setup > Company > Enable Features. Go to the Employees tab and the Permissions area. If enabled, the **Advanced Employee Permissions** box will be checked.

Note:

When the Advanced Employee Permissions feature is enabled, your customer must be assigned a custom Customer Center Role. See the Account Administration topic [Customizing the Customer Center Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N291685.html).

Follow the steps in the Employee Management help topic, [Setting Employee Access for Advanced Employee Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1494598267.html), to set access for advanced employee permissions. However, to let Commerce website customers view their My Account transactions, you must perform these extra steps:

-   Select **Employee Self** from the **Permission** list.
    
-   Select **View** from the **Level** list.
    
-   Select **Active and Non-Terminated** from the **Restrictions** list.
