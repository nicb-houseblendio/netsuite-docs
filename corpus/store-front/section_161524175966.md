You can enable JSON-LD for your blog. JSON-LD adds extra data to your blog pages to improve search engine optimization (SEO). NetSuite uses JSON-LD according to the BlogPosting schema found at [Schema.org](https://schema.org/).

Note:

This feature requires SuiteCommerce or the 2020.1 release of SCA or later.

In addition to the Blog Post fields, JSON-LD uses the Display Name field from the Website Setup record and the Logo URL field located on the SuiteCommerce Configuration record (Layout > Header subtab). The Display Name field is required for your site. The Logo URL field determines your site's header logo and isn't required, but it's a good idea to set it to improve the information included in JSON-LD and SEO.

#### To enable JSON-LD:

1.  In NetSuite, go to Commerce > Websites > Configuration.
    
2.  Select the website and domain where the Blog extension is activated and click **Configure**.
    
3.  Go to the **Shopping Catalog** subtab, then the **Structured Data Markup** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Markup Type** field, select **JSON-LD**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
