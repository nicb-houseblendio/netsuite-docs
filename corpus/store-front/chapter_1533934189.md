Text on your site can originate from many places. For example, the item descriptions text comes from the item record in NetSuite. The text for your category labels comes from the category record. Facet navigation labels originate from the SuiteCommerce configuration record.

You can also add other types of text content to your site that don't depend on item or configuration records. This is text-based content you add directly in your site. For example, maybe you want to add marketing copy for an upcoming sale, or share your company's history on your About Us page.

See the following help topics for information about the available types of text content:

-   [Site Management Tools Text Content](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4123521463.html)
