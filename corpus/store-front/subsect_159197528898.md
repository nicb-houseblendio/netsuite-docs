NetSuite lets you set customer price levels. After you set customer price levels, customers can view this price in your web store after they log in.

#### To set the price level for a customer:

1.  Go to Lists > Relationships > Customers.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the customer you want to set a price level for.
    
3.  Click the **Financial** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Price Level** field, select the price level you want to offer.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

For more information, see [Setting Up Price Levels for Customers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2182485.html).
