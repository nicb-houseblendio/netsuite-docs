You can set up the following properties for the Quotes feature:

-   Quotes text displayed on your website. See [Customize Request a Quote Properties](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156933397554.html).
    
-   Status that triggers functionality to let customers make purchases from a quote. See [Set the Customer Status Internal ID](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4527214444.html).
    
-   The invoice form used to create a purchase from a quote. See [Define an Invoice Form](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4527219186.html).
    
-   Time (in days) within which the Quote Expiration Notification appears on your website and the quote expires. See [Set Quote Expiration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4706652617.html).
    
-   Sales representative information displayed on your website's Quote Details page. See [Customize Sales Representative Information](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4533229975.html).
    
-   Functionality to let shoppers view quotes and make purchases without creating quotes directly from your website. See [Disable Quotes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4719036553.html).
    

See [Quotes Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4678712900.html) for information about configurable properties.
