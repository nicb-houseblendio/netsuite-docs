Users can access specific features and information by accessing their account on your web store. We call this area of the site My Account.

To give your existing customers access to My Account, see [Give Customers Access to My Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7150741863.html).

Note:

This section also introduces you to SuiteCommerce MyAccount, a type of Commerce website, not to be confused with the My Account area found on all Commerce sites. To learn more about SuiteCommerce MyAccount (the product) and how it's different from My Account (the account area), see [SuiteCommerce MyAccount Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_159786656208.html).

Explore the following topics to learn more about the features of the My Account area of your site:

-   [Product Lists](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3952550164.html)
    
    \- Let users save items to a Product List (Wish List) or a private list for later. They can add the whole list to their cart with one click.
    
-   [Support Cases for Commerce](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_159292925828.html) - Set up support cases for your SuiteCommerce, SuiteCommerce MyAccount, or SuiteCommerce Advanced site.
    
-   [Transaction Lists](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1548081625.html) - Choose how My Account transactions display on your site and which fields to include, like Order History, Invoices, Quotes, and Return Authorizations.
    
-   [Save for Later](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4024260541.html) - Let users move items from their cart to a private list to save for later.
    
-   [Quotes for Commerce Websites](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4464799196.html) - Let users request a quote directly from your web store. They can add the items they want and enter shipping information for the quote.
    
-   [Reorder Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_161286223878.html) - Let SuiteCommerce MyAccount (SCMA) users reorder items they've bought before.
    
-   [Subscriptions for Commerce Websites](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_156079542004.html) - Let users view and manage their SuiteBilling subscriptions. Users can perform many of the subscription management tasks previously handled by company employees.
