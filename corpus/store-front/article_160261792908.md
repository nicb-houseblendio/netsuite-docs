SuiteCommerce Analytics Data uses related records to organize data captured during web store visits. The Session Record represents a shopper visit and forms the basis for the other analytics data records.

The related records provide details about shoppers, devices, locations, acquisition sources, pages viewed, searches performed, and actions taken during a visit.

Start with the **Session Record** to understand a shopper visit. Use the **User Interaction Record** to review the shopper actions and page activity captured during that visit.
