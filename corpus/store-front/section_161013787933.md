This information refers to SuiteCommerce and the 2021.1 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced or later.

With Site Management Tools (SMT), you can export bulk content that needs translation from your web store and import the translated content back into SMT from the Translations tab in Overview Mode. Content includes CMS Pages and CMS Content Types. All formatting, like HTML content and special characters, is preserved. Content is exported as individual XLIFF 1.2 files, compressed into a ZIP, and then sent to a third-party translator. After you get the translated content, you can import it back into SMT, review it, and publish it to the language-specific domain.

## Prerequisites for Exporting and Importing Bulk Translation Content in SMT {#subsect_160564630170}

To export and import bulk translation content, your account must meet the following conditions:

-   Your account must be using SMT version 3. To check your version of SMT, see [Site Management Tools Versions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4806124052.html).
    
-   Your account must be using SuiteCommerce and SuiteCommerce Advanced 20.2 or later.
    
-   Multi-Languages must be set up for your site. For details, see [Prerequisites for Using Multi-Language](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160063618979.html).
    
-   You need to configure multiple languages on the website record. For details, see [Web Site Language Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N2567348.html).
    
-   All content for your site must be added to the CMS records of the default language. For more information, see [CMS Records for Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1497970419.html).
