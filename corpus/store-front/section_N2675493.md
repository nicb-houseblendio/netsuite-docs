After installing your application, you're ready to set preferences for your site. To do this, access the Web Site Setup Record in NetSuite and follow these steps. After that, you can view your site on a development domain.

Explore the following topics to learn how you can edit the Web Site Setup record and prepare your site:

-   [Set Minimum Site Record Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1520292597.html)
    
-   [Set Up Initial Field Sets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3716578295.html)
    
-   [Set Up a Initial Search Index](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1522121409.html)
    

You can also view the [Comprehensive Web Site Setup Reference](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159499875581.html) topic for a list of all options located on the Web Site Setup record.
