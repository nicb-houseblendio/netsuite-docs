The Gift Certificate Management extension lets you do the following:

-   [Group Gift Certificates in Your Web Store](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156295511195.html) - group existing Gift Certificates to display them as item options in your web store.
    
-   [Add Gift Certificate Value Check](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156295197141.html) - allow users to check the remaining balance of gift certificates on a new landing page.
    
-   [Display Gift Certificates in My Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156295198709.html) - let users check the remaining balance of gift certificates, review gift certificate details, and store the gift certificates in their My Account.
    

Important:

Commerce extensions are only available if provisioned, installed, and activated for a selected domain in your account. For more information, see [Commerce Extensions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1530632028.html).

Note:

Extensions require SuiteCommerce or the Aconcagua release of SCA or later.

## Prerequisities {#bridgehead_156330345394}

To take advantage of the Gift Certificate Management extension, you must have previously set up Gift Certificates for your site. For more information, see [Setting Preferences for Gift Certificate Authentication Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2245256.html).
