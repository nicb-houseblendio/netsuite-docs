You delete a contact form using Site Management Tools (SMT) in one of two ways, depending on whether you have published the content to your website:

-   **Discard** the contact form if you have not published the content.
    
-   **Remove** the contact form if you have already published the content.
    

#### To discard a contact form (unpublished content):

1.  Log into Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Select the Review & Publish Changes icon ![Review and Publish Changes Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/ReviewAndPublishChangesIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
3.  Select the Content Form content from the Unpublished Changes list.
    
4.  Click **Discard**.
    
5.  In the pop up window that appears, click **Yes**.
    

#### To remove the contact form (published content):

1.  Log into Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Go to the page that contains the contact form you want to remove.
    
3.  Select the Edit Mode icon ![Edit Mode Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/EditModeIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
4.  Hover over the column content box to display the content controls.
    
5.  Click **X** to remove the column from the page.
    
6.  In the pop up that appears, click **Yes**.
