Use the SuiteCommerce Configuration record to determine how intelligent item recommendations appear in the Recommended Items SMT content area on your website.

#### To configure intelligent item recommendations: {#procedure_0725053418}

1.  In NetSuite, go to Commerce > Websites > Configuration.
    
2.  Select the website and domain where the Intelligent Item Recommendations extension is activated and click **Configure**.
    
3.  Go to the **Extensions** tab and the **Intelligent Recommendations** subtab.
    
4.  Configure the following fields as required:
    
    | Field | Description |
    | --- | --- |
    | Minimum Number of Items in Content Area | The minimum number of items that should be shown in each Recommended Items content area on your website. |
    | Maximum Number of Items in Content Area | The maximum number of items shown in the content area at a time. For example, if the total number of recommended items is 20, and the maximum is set to 5, the shopper can see a maximum of 5 items in the content area at a time. They can scroll to see the remaining 15 items. The default maximum number is 10 and it can't exceed 20. The number of items visible to a shopper at a time also depends on the display size of their device. Note: You can adjust the total number of recommended items shown in the recommended items content area using the **Number of Items Displayed** field. For more information, see [Add and Remove Intelligent Item Recommendations from your Website](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_165027326313.html). |
    | Width of Item Image | The width, in pixels, of the item images to be shown in the Recommended Items content areas on your website. |
    | Height of Item Image | The height, in pixels, of the item images to be shown in the Recommended Items content areas on your website. |
    | Enable Quick View for Items | Add a **Quick View** button to each recommended item displayed on your website. Clicking the **Quick View** button lets shoppers see details of the recommended item without leaving the product page of the item they are already on. |
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

After configuring the Intelligent Item Recommendations extension, you can add intelligent item recommendations to your website. See [Add and Remove Intelligent Item Recommendations from your Website](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_165027326313.html).
