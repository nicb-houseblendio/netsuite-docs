The Newsletter Sign Up extension lets you add a newsletter sign up form to your website using Site Management Tools (SMT). After activating this extension, you can:

-   Determine how the lead record is created in NetSuite.
    
-   Decide which fields to display on the sign up form.
    
-   Choose the layout and style of the sign up form.
    
-   Customize the error and confirmation messages displayed to users.
    

The SuiteCommerce Newsletter Sign Up extension is available for SuiteCommerce and for the Aconcagua Release of SuiteCommerce Advanced or later. If you're implementing the Aconcagua Release of SuiteCommerce Advanced, you must disable the Newsletter feature implemented in the core source code. See [Disable the Newsletter Form](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4653133028.html#subsect_1543419897).

Important:

SuiteCommerce extensions are only available if provisioned, installed, and activated for a selected domain in your account. For more information, see [Commerce Extensions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1530632028.html).

Before you can implement the Newsletter Sign Up extension, you must first:

1.  Set up your SuiteCommerce site for the Newsletter subscription process. See [Set Up Newsletter](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4642098755.html).
    
2.  Install and activate the SuiteCommerce Newsletter Sign Up extension. See [Themes & Extensions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_1518478184.html).
    

This topic explains how to:

-   [Add a Newsletter Sign Up Form](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156831077298.html)
    
-   [Configure a Newsletter Sign Up Form](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4654839571.html)
    
-   [Remove a Newsletter Sign Up Form](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156831061698.html)
    

Use SMT to add, configure, or delete a newsletter sign up form on multiple areas of your website. Adding this form to enhanced or custom landing pages allows you to promote your newsletter in the place and style you want. For more information about Site Management Tools, see [Site Management Tools Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4165123763.html).
