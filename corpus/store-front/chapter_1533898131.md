The SuiteCommerce Map & Contact extension lets you:

-   Embed a third-party map
    
-   Display contact details and store information, such as a phone number and operating hours
    
-   Create a contact form for customers to get in touch
    
-   Configure various Layout Settings, such as map position and section height
    
-   Add a CAPTCHA challenge to the contact form for extra security
    

Important:

Commerce extensions are only available if provisioned, installed, and activated for a selected domain in your account. For more information, see [Commerce Extensions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1530632028.html).

Note:

Extensions require SuiteCommerce or the Aconcagua release of SCA or later.

Use Site Management Tools (SMT) to add these content types to your website:

-   [Map and Contact Content Type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1548712615.html)
    
-   [Contact Form Content Type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1548712640.html)
    

Adding these content types to enhanced or custom landing pages allows you to include a map, contact information, and a contact form to your web store. For more information about SMT, see [Site Management Tools Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4165123763.html).
