The SCMA cart component doesn't have all the shopping cart features of SuiteCommerce or SCA. Limitations to the SCMA cart include the following:

-   The **Continue Shopping** button on the Thank You page takes the user to the SCMA Home page and not to the shopping catalog as it does for SuiteCommerce and SCA.
    
-   Users can only view items they ordered in the last 180 days.
    
-   Users can view a product image, but not the product details page.
    
-   Items remain in the cart. Users can't move them between the cart and the Save for Later list.
    
-   Users can only increase the quantity of an item. All other item details remain the same.
