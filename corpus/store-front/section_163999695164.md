After you enable Quotes for Commerce websites, your customers, logged in or not, will see a **Request a Quote** link in your site's Shopping header.

See the following sample Commerce website with the **Request a Quote** link displayed in the Shopping header:

![Request a Quote Displayed in Shopping Header](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/MyAccount/Request-a-Quote-in-Shopping-Header.png)

If customers aren't logged in, they'll be asked to log in after they click **Request a Quote**.

Note:

Customers need permission to request a quote. See [Give Customers Permission to Request and View Quotes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4520209178.html).

The **Request a Quote** link directs logged-in customers to a page where they can:

-   Search for items and add them to a quote
    
-   Enter, edit, and remove shipping information
    
-   Add optional comments
    
-   Submit the quote
    

See the following sample Request a Quote page:

![Request a Quote page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/MyAccount/Request-a-Quote-Page.png)

After a customer submits a quote request, the following updates take place:

-   NetSuite creates an Estimate/Quote record
    
-   An **Add to Quote** button appears on the Product Details Page (PDP)
    

See the following sample Product Details Page with the **Add to Quote** button displayed:

![Product Details Page with Add to Quote button](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/MyAccount/PDP_AddToQuote.png)

Customers can view their quotes in the Purchases area of My Account.

See the following sample Quotes page:

![Quotes Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/MyAccount/Quote-Records.png)

After a customer clicks on a quote, they can:

-   Download the quote as a PDF
    
-   Review the quote and place an order
