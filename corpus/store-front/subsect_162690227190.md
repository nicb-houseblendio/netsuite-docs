The first task is to enable the feature in NetSuite. You must log in to NetSuite with an administrator role.

Important:

You see the box to enable this feature only if you've ordered the service and agreed to the terms of service.

#### To enable the SuiteCommerce Analytics Data feature:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Enable Features > Web Presence > Web Sites​.
    
2.  Check the **SuiteCommerce Analytics Data** box.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    
    Note:
    
    You get an alert if your current SuiteCommerce release doesn't meet the prerequisite. See [Prerequisites](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160339216711.html#subsect_162696485572).
