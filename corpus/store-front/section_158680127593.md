Important:

To configure a theme, you must also have the companion theme extension activated. For details, see [Activating Themes and Extensions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1549034518.html).

Note:

Themes require SuiteCommerce or the Aconcagua release of SuiteCommerce Advanced or later.

To determine how theme properties show up on your website, you need to configure the properties on the SuiteCommerce Configuration record. This section covers how to configure global theme settings, as well as settings specific to each theme.

To learn more about how to configure themes, explore these topics:

-   [Configuring Settings Specific to Individual Themes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159863897414.html)
    
-   [Configuring Global Theme Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159863912269.html)
