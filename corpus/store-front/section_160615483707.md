Though removing a merchandising zone makes it unavailable for visitors to your site, it does not permanently delete it. However, if you no longer need a merchandising zone you are able to permanently delete it.

## Removing a Merchandising Zone {#subsect_160622691361}

When you remove a merchandising zone the visibility end date and time is set to 'now.' For details, see [View Site as of a Specific Date in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1513783026.html). If you have unpublished content and you decide to remove it rather than publish it, you can choose to discard or expire the content. For more information, see [Discard or Expire Content in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1513888480.html).

#### To remove a merchandising zone:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Go to the page that contains the merchandising zone.
    
3.  Click the Edit Mode icon ![Edit Mode Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/EditModeIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
4.  Hover over the merchandising zone to display the content controls.
    
5.  Click **Remove**.
    
6.  When prompted, click **Yes**.
    
7.  When you're ready, publish your changes. For details, see [Review and Publish Content in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4419210472.html).
    

## Deleting a Merchandising Zone {#subsect_160622696146}

You can permanently delete a merchandising zone if it's no longer needed. If it hasn't already expired, you must remove it before it can be deleted. See [Removing a Merchandising Zone](#subsect_160622691361).

Important:

You cannot recover a deleted merchandising zone.

#### To delete a merchandising zone:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Select the Overview Mode icon ![Overview Mode Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/OverviewModeIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
3.  Click the **Expired Content** subtab.
    
4.  From the list, select the merchandising zone that you want to delete.
    
5.  Click **Delete**.
    
6.  When prompted, click **Yes**.
