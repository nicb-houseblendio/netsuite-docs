This topic covers common SuiteCommerce Analytics use cases a web store might use to measure performance and figure out next steps for success.

-   [Shoppers Who Purchased Item X and Also Viewed Item Y](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162768073800.html)
    
-   [Shoppers Who Have Not Visited My Web Store Within the Last X Days](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162768075514.html)
    
-   [Top Items Viewed by Shoppers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162768077691.html)
    
-   [Top Keywords Searched by a Specific Shopper](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162768078440.html)
    
-   [Shoppers Who Added Items to the Cart, But Did Not Make a Purchase](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162768079175.html)
    
-   [Items a Shopper Viewed Within a Time Frame](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162768079838.html)
    

The included examples show how to query the data from a Saved Search. Some examples include criteria for an Analytic Workbook setup. For more information, see:

-   [Creating a Saved Search for SuiteCommerce Analytics Data](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160260503890.html)
    
-   [Creating a Workbook for SuiteCommerce Analytics Data](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160260516768.html)
    

Tip:

When creating a Saved Search or Analytics Workbook, you'll use the session record as the starting point for most filters.

Note:

The Saved Search > Results setups shown here are examples. You can pick from the available fields to tailor results for your business model.
