If you haven't published your slideshow and you decide to remove it, you can discard it. If it's already published, you can remove it right from the page. For more information, see [Discard or Expire Content in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1513888480.html).

#### To discard a slideshow (unpublished content): {#procedure_1537878329}

1.  Log into Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Select the Review & Publish Changes icon ![Review and Publish Changes Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/ReviewAndPublishChangesIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
3.  Select the slideshow from the Unpublished Changes list.
    
4.  Click **Discard**.
    
5.  In the pop up window that appears, click **Yes**.
    

#### To remove a slideshow (published content): {#procedure_1531320711}

1.  Log into Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Go to the page that contains the slideshow you want to remove.
    
3.  Select the Edit Mode icon ![Edit Mode Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/EditModeIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
4.  Hover over the slideshow content box to display the content controls.
    
5.  Click **X** to remove the slideshow from the page.
    
6.  In the pop up that appears, click **Yes**.
