After you complete the steps outlined in the [Getting Started](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2655840.html) section, you can view your site on your development domain. At this point, this is a basic site to get you started. Your next steps depend on the type of site you are creating:

-   [SuiteCommerce MyAccount Sites](#subsect_158766702813)
    
-   [SuiteCommerce and SuiteCommerce Advanced Sites](#subsect_158766627890)
    

## SuiteCommerce MyAccount Sites {#subsect_158766702813}

SuiteCommerce MyAccount sites don't incorporate shopping or checkout-related features. Browse through the following setup options to see what is best for your site. But keep these main topics in mind as your most important next steps:

-   [Item Record Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1489003607.html) - Set up your item records to allow product images to appear on a customer's purchase history, returns, invoices, and quotes.
    
-   [Themes & Extensions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_1518478184.html) - Install and activate themes to change the look and feel of your site or extensions to add functionality.
    
-   [My Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_158100703898.html) - Set up your site's My Account area so your customers can access subscriptions, review orders, etc.
    

## SuiteCommerce and SuiteCommerce Advanced Sites {#subsect_158766627890}

The SuiteCommerce Storefront topics offer many options for your site, from item search preferences to marketing features and site analytics. Browse through the various set up options to see what is best for your site. But keep these main topics in mind as your most important next steps:

-   [Web Store Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_1518477920.html) - Make items that are set up in NetSuite available to site visitors for viewing and purchasing on your web store.
    
-   [Web Store Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_1518478322.html) - Set up payments, shipping, and checkout.
    
-   [Themes & Extensions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_1518478184.html) - Install and activate themes to change the look and feel of your site or extensions to add functionality.
    
-   [Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_1518478010.html) - Set up item search to help customers quickly find items they are looking for.
    
-   [Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_1518477823.html) - Make online ordering quick and simple for your customers.
    
-   [My Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_158100703898.html) - Set up your site's My Account area so your customers can access subscriptions, wishlists, review orders, etc.
    
-   [SEO & Analytics](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_1518477612.html) - Optimize your site for search engines and set up site analytics.
    
-   [Marketing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_1518006459.html) - Enable features to help you reach out and attract customers.
    

## Site Management Tools {#subsect_1520310166}

The Site Management Tools user interface lets you manage and preview content in real-time using an unpublished version of your site. You drag and drop new content, edit or remove existing content, and rearrange content by dragging it from one location to another. See [Site Management Tools Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4165123763.html) to learn more about SMT, including the types of content you can manage, how to set it up, and how to use it.

Site Management Tools help you perform several important website management related tasks, including:

-   Manage content (HTML, text, images, and merchandising zones)
    
-   Add landing pages and enhance existing pages
    
-   Customize and enable skins for themes
    
-   Change page layouts
    
-   Manage commerce categories and subcategories
    
-   Assign items to categories
