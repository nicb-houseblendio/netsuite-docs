If you don't want to associate an entire group of items with an item, you can select individual related items on an item record.

#### To associate related items to an item record: {#procedure_N2588789}

1.  Go to _Lists > Accounting > Items_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the item you want to add a related item to.
    
3.  Go to the **Web Store** tab and the **Related Items** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Related Item** column, choose an item to associate with the item you're selling.
    
5.  Click **Add**.
    
    Click **Add Multiple** to add several items quickly.
    
6.  You can move items up and down to change the order they appear in your web store.
    
7.  When you have finished, click **Save**.
    

Now your related items appear beneath the item you're selling in your web store.
