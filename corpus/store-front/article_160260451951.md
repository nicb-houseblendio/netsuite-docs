## PII and Data Minimization {#subsect_163484239439}

Personally identifiable information (PII) is data that can be used to identify someone. PII includes basic details like name, location, or phone number, and can also cover browsing or buying habits. PII rules vary by country, but they usually have strict guidelines for collecting, storing, and deleting personal information. There are also rules about who can access PII and how long it is kept.

A common theme is collecting the least amount of data you need to do the job. This theme is often called **data minimization**.

Keep reading to learn how NetSuite works to follow these PII guidelines.

### Data Retention {#subsect_163484247219}

NetSuite applies guidelines to ensure aging analytics data is deleted on a timely basis. The way the data is deleted keeps it from being recreated, accessed or read.

-   **Time-based Deletion** - Shopper analytics data is deleted six months after it is collected.
    
-   **Related Record Deletion** - If a shopper's customer record is deleted, that shopper's analytics data is deleted six months after the record deletion.
    
-   **Deletion by SuiteCommerce Customer** - Customers can delete their shopper's customer record in NetSuite.
    
    Customers cannot delete the End User Identifiable Information (EUII) data saved in temporary data stores. Temporary EUII data is automatically removed from NetSuite after 30 days plus an offline index retention period.
    
-   **Deletion by NetSuite** - Authorized NetSuite personnel can delete shopper data upon receiving a request from the shopper or the SuiteCommerce customer.
    

Note:

Customers and shoppers cannot delete the EUII data saved in temporary data stores. However, this data is automatically removed from after 30 days.

### Data Security {#subsect_160461316382}

Only authorized NetSuite staff can access shopper data, and safeguards limit how much and what kind of data they can see. Access to data that is specific to a SuiteCommerce web store is determined by the web store owner's administrator and by NetSuite.

### Data Portability {#subsect_160571717761}

SuiteCommerce Analytics Data gives customers different ways to get structured, machine-readable data they've permitted to be uploaded and stored in NetSuite.

-   Analytics data is saved as NetSuite records, and the platform supports several ways to export this data.
    
-   Customers can use NetSuite's Saved Search and Workbook Analytics features to export analytics data in CSV format.
    

To keep a customer-managed copy of analytics data, create a Saved Search that includes the required fields. You can export the results to a CSV file or schedule an email that includes the results as a CSV attachment.

Note:

Exports can include shopper analytics data, including personal or behavioral details. Restrict internal access to saved searches and records. This ensures that people who require the data can view or export it, or schedule emails containing it. When scheduling emails, select recipients carefully. Recipients can receive record details that they can't view in NetSuite.

### Data Availability {#subsect_160572668308}

To ensure availability of shopper analytics data stored in NetSuite, information saved to customer or contact records is stored in the Oracle database with all other NetSuite customer data.

Oracle NetSuite backs up production account data multiple times daily to support disaster recovery (DR). When Oracle NetSuite enacts its disaster recovery plan, it can restore production accounts in the DR data center. For more information, see [Disaster Recovery](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_31104740147.html).
