You can edit or delete services using the Google Tag Manager (GTM) Editor.

You should use GTM Editor rather than GTM to modify GTM services.

-   To change service settings, see [Edit a Service](#subsect_1534173739).
    
-   To delete a service, see [Remove a Service](#subsect_1534523637).
    
-   To restore a service, see [Restore a Supported Service](#subsect_1535056949).
    

## Edit a Service {#subsect_1534173739}

After adding a service, you can change the settings using the GTM Editor. If you entered an incorrect service ID or other parameter, follow these steps to correct any of the setup parameters.

#### To edit a service using Google Tag Manager Editor:

1.  In NetSuite, go to Setup > SuiteCommerce Advanced > Google Tag Manager Editor.
    
2.  Click **Sign In With Google** and choose your account.
    
3.  Authorize NetSuite to access your Google account.
    
4.  Click the name of your container record.
    
5.  Go to the **Configured Services** tab and click **Edit** for the service you want to change.
    
6.  Make your changes.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

## Remove a Service {#subsect_1534523637}

You can delete a service from your GTM container using the GTM Editor.

#### To remove a service using Google Tag Manager Editor:

1.  In NetSuite, go to Setup > SuiteCommerce Advanced > Google Tag Manager Editor.
    
2.  Click **Sign In With Google** and choose your account.
    
3.  Authorize NetSuite to access your Google account.
    
4.  Click the name of your container record.
    
5.  Go to the **Configured Services** tab and click **Remove** for the service you want to remove.
    
6.  Click **Remove** to confirm removal of the service.
    

## Restore a Supported Service {#subsect_1535056949}

To restore services, you can use two methods:

-   [Restore Services Using the Restore Defaults Feature](#subsect_160442265701)
    
-   [Restore Services by Removing and Adding a Service](#subsect_160442276071)
    

### Restore Services Using the Restore Defaults Feature {#subsect_160442265701}

You can use the **Restore Defaults** feature to do the following:

-   Update default variable values.
    
-   Add missing default variables.
    
-   Add missing default triggers.
    
-   Update valid service tag values.
    

#### To restore defaults:

1.  In NetSuite, go to Setup > SuiteCommerce Advanced > Google Tag Manager Editor.
    
2.  Click **Sign In With Google** and choose your account.
    
3.  Authorize NetSuite to access your Google account.
    
4.  Click the name of your container record.
    
5.  Click **Restore Defaults**.
    

### Restore Services by Removing and Adding a Service {#subsect_160442276071}

If you edited a service in GTM, instead of using GTM Editor, the service might be moved to the **Other Services** tab. This tab also lists services not currently supported by GTM Editor.

#### To restore a supported service: {#procedure_1535057192}

1.  In NetSuite, go to Setup > SuiteCommerce Advanced > Google Tag Manager Editor.
    
2.  Click **Sign In With Google** and choose your account.
    
3.  Authorize NetSuite to access your Google account.
    
4.  Click the name of your container record.
    
5.  Go to the **Other Services** tab and click **Remove** for the service you want to restore.
    
    For more information, see [Remove a Service](#subsect_1534523637).
    
6.  Add the service back to your container.
    
    For more information, see [Add Services to Your Google Tag Manager Container](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1525975256.html#subsect_1532366178).
