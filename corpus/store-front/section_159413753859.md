Removing a landing page sets the visibility end date and time to 'now'. For details, see [View Site as of a Specific Date in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1513783026.html). Removing a landing page makes it unavailable for visitors to your site, it does not permanently delete the page record. You can permanently delete a landing page if it is no longer needed. If the page is not already expired, you must remove the landing page before it can be deleted.

Important:

You cannot recover deleted landing pages.

#### To remove a landing page:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Click the Overview Mode icon ![Overview Mode](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/OverviewModeIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
3.  Click the **Pages** tab.
    
4.  Find the page you want to delete and click the **Ellipsis**.
    
5.  Click **Remove**.
    
6.  When prompted, click **OK** to remove the page.
    

#### To delete a landing page:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Click the Overview Mode icon ![Overview Mode](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/OverviewModeIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
3.  Click the **Expired Content** subtab.
    
4.  From the list, select the landing page you want to delete.
    
5.  Click **Delete**.
    
6.  When prompted, click **Yes** to delete the page.
