The Items by Customer workbook lets you select specific customers and view the items they can see, and whether they can see the item prices and buy them. The workbook shows you:

-   which customer segments the selected customers are in
    
-   which item segments are mapped to those customer segments
    
-   which items are included in the item segments
    
-   the website visibility level of the item segment to the customer segment
    

#### To view which items are viewable or purchasable by specific customers:

1.  Go to _Commerce > Marketing > Personalization > Customer Segments Manager_.
    
2.  Click **Save & View Report** and select the Items by Customer workbook.
    
    Alternatively, access the workbook by going to the Workbook Listing page and clicking the **Items by Customer** link in the Standard Workbooks list. For more information, see [Accessing and Sharing Workbooks and Datasets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1531766899.html).
    
    Note:
    
    If the **Save & View Report** button is not visible, check your permissions - you may not have access to workbooks. See [Personalized Catalog Views Roles and Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1560331283.html) for more information.
    
3.  In the Items by Customer pivot table, click the Field Menu icon ![field menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/Products/FieldMenuIcon18_1.png) next to Customers, then click **Filter Customers**.
    
4.  Select the desired customers in the left pane and click **\>** to add them to the right pane of the filter window.
    
5.  Click **Apply**.
    
6.  Click the Field Menu icon ![field menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/Products/FieldMenuIcon18_1.png) next to Website Visibility Level, then click **Filter Website Visibility Level**.
    
    To see the items that each selected customer:
    
    -   can view and buy, select **Display Fully** in the left pane of the filter window
        
    -   can view with prices but not buy, select **Disable Purchase** in the left pane of the filter window
        
    -   can view without prices and not buy, select **Disable Purchase and Hide Price**
        
    -   cannot see on the web store, select **Hide Fully** in the left pane of the filter window
        
7.  Click **\>** to add the selected Website Visibility Level to the right pane.
    
8.  Click **Apply**.
    
    The pivot table updates to show the items each selected customer can view and buy.
