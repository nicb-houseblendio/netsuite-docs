Edit a landing page when you must make changes to the properties of the landing page, such as the page title, description, or heading.

#### To edit a landing page:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Click the Overview Mode icon ![Overview Mode](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/WebsiteContent/OverviewModeIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
3.  Click the **Pages** subtab.
    
4.  Find the landing page you want to edit and click the **Ellipsis**.
    
5.  Click **Edit** to display the Landing Page Settings on the side panel.
    
6.  Make any required changes.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    
8.  Publish the changes. For details, see [Review and Publish Content in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4419210472.html).
