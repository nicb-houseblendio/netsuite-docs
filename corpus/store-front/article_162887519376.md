The SuiteCommerce Analytics Data search record gives details about the search terms a shopper entered during their visit, including the date and time of the search.

| Analytics Workbooks | Saved Search | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Label | Type | Label | Type | Provides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Time** | DateTime | **Search: Time** | DateTime | Date and time a shopper entered a search term |
| **Search Term** | Text Select | **Search: Search Term** | Text Select | Search term entered by a shopper |
