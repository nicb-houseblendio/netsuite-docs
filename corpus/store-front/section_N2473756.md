PayPal integration without Express Checkout is supported only on Standard Site Builder sites.

For implementations of SuiteCommerce Advanced, SuiteCommerce, and SiteBuilder Extensions Denali to Aconcagua, you must enable Express Checkout to use PayPal as a payment method. If Express Checkout is disabled for these implementations, and you use PayPal, checkout will fail.

If you have a standard implementation of Site Builder and you enable PayPal Integration but don't check the Use Express Checkout box, customers can still choose PayPal as a payment option at checkout. When customers choose PayPal, they click the Pay With PayPal button at the end of checkout and payments are deposited in your PayPal merchant account.

If Express Checkout is not enabled for your Site Builder implementation, then transactions processed using the PayPal payment option can't be refunded in NetSuite. You'll need to log in to the PayPal website to issue refunds.
