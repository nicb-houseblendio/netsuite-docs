The SuiteCommerce Analytics Data customer record gives details about a shopper's visit, like the date and time, how they found the site, their device type, and a link to their session details.

| Analytics Workbooks | Saved Search | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Label | Type | Label | Type | Provides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Last Session Date** | Date | **Customer: Last Session Date** | Date | Date and time of shopper's last visit to web store |
| **Acquisition Source** | Text Select | **Customer: Acquisition Source** | Text | Source that prompted shopper to visit. For example, an email campaign |
| **Most Used Device Category** | Text Select | **Customer: Most Used Device Category** | Text | Device used by the shopper to browse web store
-   Desktop
-   Phone
-   Tablet
