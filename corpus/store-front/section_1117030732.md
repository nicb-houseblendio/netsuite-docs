Note:

By default, Strike-Through Pricing is enabled on the Shopping Catalog subtab of the SuiteCommerce Configuration record, and the default price level is set to `pricelevel1`. For more information, see [Enable Strike-Through Behavior](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4667037093.html#subsect_159182443844) and [Default Price Level](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4667037093.html#subsect_159845169195).

#### To verify Strike-Through Pricing is enabled:

1.  Go to Commerce > Websites > Configuration.
    
2.  Select your website and domain and click **Configure**.
    
3.  Click the **Shopping Catalog** subtab.
    
4.  Confirm the **Enable Strike-Through Behavior** box is checked.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To verify the default price level is set:

1.  Go to Commerce > Websites > Configuration.
    
2.  Select your website and domain and click **Configure**.
    
3.  Click the **Shopping Catalog** subtab.
    
4.  Confirm the **Default Price Level** field is set to pricelevel1.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
