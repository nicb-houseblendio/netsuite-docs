You can create payment instruments in two ways:

-   You create a payment instrument using the Order Management feature in NetSuite.
    
-   Your customer creates a payment instrument on your Commerce website. See [Web Store User Experience for ACH Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162737365006.html).
    

Note:

To create a payment instrument, you'll first need to enable the Payment Instruments feature (Setup > Company > Enable Features > Transactions > Payment Processing).

Follow the steps in the Order Management help topic, [Payment Instruments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1538492538.html), to set up a payment instrument in NetSuite.

#### To create an ACH account for a customer:

1.  Go to Lists > Relationships > Customers, and select the customer you want to create an ACH account for.
    
2.  Go to the **Financial** subtab and the **Payment Instruments** subtab.
    
3.  Click **New ACH**.
    
4.  Fill out the following fields on the ACH payment instrument page:
    
    -   **Type** - Select **ACH**.
        
    -   **Payment Method** - Select your ACH payment method.
        
    -   **Limit** - Enter the limit per transaction for this account.
        
    -   **Bank Account Number** - Enter the bank account number, up to 20 digits.
        
    -   **Account Type** - Select Checking, Corporate Checking, or Saving.
        
    -   **Routing Number** - Enter the nine-digit routing number.
        
    -   **Bank Name** - Enter the name of the bank. **Bank Name** is not displayed as a required field in NetSuite but is required for your Commerce website.
        
    -   **Account Owner Name** - Enter the name of the account owner.
        
    -   **Customer Consent** - Enter a customer consent message to be included with all ACH transmissions using this account.
        
5.  Click **Save**.
