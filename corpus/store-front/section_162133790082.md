Customers can submit reviews using a review form. The form allows customers to rate the product and add a brief review. The following image shows a sample Review Form page.

![Sample Review Form](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/Products/review_form.png)

After a review is submitted and approved by a moderator, it's published on your site. Customers can view item ratings and read reviews. Customers can also vote whether a review was helpful. The following image shows a sample review.

![Sample Review](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/StoreFront/Products/sample_review.png)

The appearance of product reviews varies depending on your chosen theme. For more information about themes, see [Commerce Themes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_158386920036.html).
