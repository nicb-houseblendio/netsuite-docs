Marketing strategy and planning is about getting your products to consumers at the right place, at the right time, and at the right price.

Commerce web stores let you:

-   Guide consumers in their decision-making by suggesting complementary products
    
-   Create up-sell opportunities by personalizing the static items to be displayed together or by automating the selection based on what your customers buy
    
-   Create up-sell opportunities by personalizing recommended items for shoppers, either manually or automatically, including with machine learning that suggests products based on what customers buy
    
-   Let shoppers sort products by best sellers so your top items stand out
    
-   Feature entire product categories and individual items to attract consumers' attention
    
-   Encourage business by offering customers gift wrap options and prepaid stored-value money cards for future purchases
    
-   Promote collections featuring various curated looks by displaying a book of must-have styles
