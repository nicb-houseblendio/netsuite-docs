An important part of managing your website is controlling the content you place on the site. The type of content can be vast and varied, from a banner image on the home page to marketing copy promoting a sale. See the following help topics to learn more about the types of content you can add to your website, and how to add them.

-   [Content Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4123521177.html) - See the following for an overview of Content Types, moving content, and secure and unsecure content.
    
-   [Pages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4314594735.html) - Learn how you can add pages to your website and how to use the Site Management Tools Page Layout Selector.
    
-   [Text Content](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1533934189.html) - Learn how to add text to pages on your website.
    
-   [Image Content](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1536078006.html) - Images are an important aspect of the information you present on your website. Learn how you can add images to your website and how to use the Site Management Tools Image Manager.
    
-   [HTML Content](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4314426534.html) - Are you comfortable working with HTML and JavaScript? Learn how you can add HTML, JavaScript, or HTML-based content with Site Management Tools.
    
-   [Multi-Language Sites](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_159847476054.html) - Learn how to set up language-specific domains for your website, add translations, and export and import bulk translations within Site Management Tools.
    
-   [Merchandising Zone Content](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_159561426850.html) - Find out how to display complementary, cross-sell or up-sell products on your website.
    
-   [Commerce Custom Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4837340253.html) - Learn how to add custom fields to your website.
