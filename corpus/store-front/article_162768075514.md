This use case shows shoppers by company name and email who haven't visited your web store within a specific number of days from today. You can adjust the period for days, weeks, and so on.

## Saved Search {#subsect_161255173873}

**Search type:** Customer

**Select filter:** Session Record

| **Criteria Tab** | **Results Tab** |
| --- | --- |
| **Subtab:** Standard
-   **Filter:** Session Record: Customer: Last Session Date **Description:** is not after ten days ago
-   **Filter:** Session Record: Customer: Last Session Date **Description:** is not empty

 | **Sort By:** Company Name (ascending) **Subtab:** Columns **Fields:**

-   ID
-   Company Name
-   Email
-   Session Record: Customer: Last Session Date

 |

## Analytic Workbook {#subsect_161255388730}

**Create dataset from:** Customer Record or Session Record

| **Root Record Type** | **Joined Record Type** | **Custom Formula Field** | **Data Grid** | **Criteria Filters** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer | (none) | (none) | 
-   Session: Customer
-   Email
-   Last Session Date

 | Last Session Date: before 10 Days ago |
