The ACH Payments feature offers Automated Clearing House (ACH) payments for the My Account and Checkout flows in SuiteCommerce, SuiteCommerce MyAccount, and the 2023.1 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced (SCA) and later versions.

By enabling ACH Payments for Checkout and My Account, you'll lower the risk of lost or stolen checks, reduce administrative work, and boost customer security.

Important:

This topic discusses the benefits and the process of enabling ACH Payments on your website for your customers and is unrelated to ACH Processing. To learn about ACH Processing, see [ACH Processing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1558443.html).

Important:

The Payment Instrument feature must be enabled to create ACH payment methods and for ACH payments to work on your Commerce website. See the Order Management topic [Enabling the Payment Instruments Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1538492538.html#subsect_160692583086).

To set up ACH Payments for your Commerce website, complete payment setup tasks in NetSuite. See [Prerequisites for ACH Payments on Web Stores](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162737361569.html) for more information.

The ACH Payments feature lets users on your Commerce website do the following:

-   Pay an invoice using an ACH transaction
    
-   Pay a sales order during the checkout flow
    
-   Add, edit, or remove an ACH payment instrument
