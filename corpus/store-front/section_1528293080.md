With the Image Text Overlay extension, you can create eye-catching images for your home and landing pages to showcase your brands. Enhance images with light or dark overlays, choose your text color, and choose where your content goes. You can also add a call-to-action button and select a specific button style.

Important:

Commerce extensions are only available if provisioned, installed, and activated for a selected domain in your account. For more information, see [Commerce Extensions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1530632028.html).

Note:

Extensions require SuiteCommerce or the Aconcagua release of SCA or later.
