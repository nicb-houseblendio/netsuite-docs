Product review attributes let you define individual review components for an item. If you don't set any attributes for an item, only the overall rating will show on your site.

Note:

Product review attributes can't be set for Assembly/Bill of Materials items.

#### To define product review attributes for an item:

1.  Go to _Lists > Accounting > Items_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the item you want to add attributes to.
    
3.  Click the **SuiteCommerce Product Reviews** tab.
    
4.  In the top panel, under the **Product Reviews Attributes** field, select the attributes you want to display on your site.
    
    By default, the attributes Quality, Price, and Durability are available.
    
5.  If needed, create a custom product review attribute.
    
    1.  Click **New** next to the **Product Reviews Attributes** field.
        
    2.  Enter the attribute name and click **Save**.
        
    
    Note:
    
    You can also define additional product review attributes on the Product Reviews Attributes custom record at _Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Lists_.
    
6.  Select the attributes for the item.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

Important:

Select all the attributes you want to display before saving the Item record. After saving, the Item record is re-indexed. It may take a few minutes for the attributes to appear on your site.
