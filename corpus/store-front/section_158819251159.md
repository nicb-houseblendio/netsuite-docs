Note:

Themes require SuiteCommerce or the Aconcagua release of SuiteCommerce Advanced or later.

Important:

Commerce themes are only available if provisioned, installed, and activated for a selected domain in your account. For details, see [Installing Theme and Extension SuiteApps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510953648.html).

Important:

To configure a theme, you must also have the companion theme extension activated. For details, see [Available Commerce Themes and Supported Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158695491035.html) and [Activating Themes and Extensions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1549034518.html).

You can configure properties to customize a Commerce theme's header. Depending on which theme's active on your site, you can choose whether your header stays fixed, and enter the text to display in the welcome area or banner.

#### To configure the header:

1.  Go to Commerce > Websites > Configuration.
    
2.  Select the website and domain where the theme is activated and click **Configure**.
    
3.  Go to the theme name subtab.
    
4.  Go to the **Header** subtab.
    
5.  Use the following table to fill out the fields.
    
    | Field | Description | Applies To |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Fixed Header | Check this box if you want the header to stay visible at the top of the page as you scroll down the page. | 
    -   Bridge Theme
    -   Horizon Theme
    -   Summit Theme
    
     |
    | Banner Text | Enter the text you want to show in the banner. It appears in the top-left corner. For example, Two Days Only - Free Shipping on Orders Over $50. | 
    
    -   Manor Theme
    -   Posh Theme
    -   Threads Theme
    
     |
    | Welcome Text | Enter the text for the welcome area. It appears in the top-right corner. | Posh Theme |
    
6.  When you're finished making changes, click **Save**.
