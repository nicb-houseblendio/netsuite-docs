The SuiteCommerce Analytics Data journey record shows what transactions a shopper made when visiting a web store, like purchases and returns.

This record is available through the Analytics Workbooks feature only.

| Analytics Workbooks | Saved Search | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Label | Type | Label | Type | Provides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Transaction** | Select | **Journey: Transaction** | N/A | Transaction information |
