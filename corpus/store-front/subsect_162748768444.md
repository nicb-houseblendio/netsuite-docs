To meet SuiteCommerce Analytics Data consent requirements, your web store landing page, or any other selected pages you choose, must have the following footer links:

-   **Cookie Policy**: This link directs customers to your cookie or privacy policy page. You can add the link with Site Management Tools (SMT) by placing it as a text or link content item in the footer area. See [Site Management Tools Text Content](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4123521463.html).
    
    You can also add a link using your theme's footer link settings. See [Configuring the Footer](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158819258356.html).
    
-   **Cookie Settings**: This link opens a cookie preferences dialog, where customers can view or change their consent settings. The SuiteCommerce Cookie Consent extension includes a custom content type (CCT), Cookie Modal Link, that you should add to your site using SMT.
    

#### To add the Cookie Modal Link:

1.  Confirm the Cookie Modal Link content type is enabled in SMT. See [Enabling or Disabling Content Types in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4815773002.html).
    
2.  Log in to SMT. See [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
3.  Go to the page where you want to add the cookie modal link.
    
4.  Click the **Edit Mode** icon from the Site Management toolbar.
    
5.  Click the **Add** icon from the Site Management toolbar.
    
6.  Select the **Cookie Modal Link** from the available content types that display.
    
    ![Example content types to add](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SuiteCommerceAnalyticsData/ExampleSelectContentTypesDisplay.png)
7.  Drag the **Cookie Modal Link** in your site's footer area.
    
    The cookie consent text and options you set up for the SC Cookie Consent appear in the cookie preferences dialog. See [Configure the SC Cookie Consent Extension](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162429920925.html).
    
8.  Publish your changes to make them live on your site. See [Review and Publish Content in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4419210472.html).
