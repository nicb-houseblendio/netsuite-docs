Edit the Customer Center role in NetSuite so your customers can request a quote and view their submitted quotes.

#### To edit the Customer Center role permissions for Quotes:

1.  Go to Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles.
    
2.  Click **Customize** next to the Customer Center role.
    
3.  Go to the **Permissions** tab and the **Transactions** subtab.
    
4.  Change the level of access for the **Estimate/Quote** permission to **Create**.
    
5.  Click **OK** to accept the change.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
7.  Give your customers access to the Customer Center role so they can view quotes. See the Account Setup help topic [Giving Customers Access](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N322750.html) for instructions.
    
    See the Account Setup help topic [Setting Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N288727.html) for detailed information about setting the level of access for a permission.
    

After a customer submits a quote request, NetSuite creates an Estimate/Quote record.
