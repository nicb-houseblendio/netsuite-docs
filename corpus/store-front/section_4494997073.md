Perform the following setup tasks in NetSuite to enable Quotes on your Commerce website:

-   [Give Customers Permission to Request and View Quotes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4520209178.html)
    
-   [Confirm Item Searcher Fields are Defined](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4520247759.html)
    
-   [Set Up Search Fields for Quotes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4520245527.html)
    
-   [Customize the Standard Quote Form to Display the Shipping Address](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4527222910.html)
    
-   [Enable Online Ordering and Customer Access Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1499798186.html)
    

Note:

You should install the latest Reference Product List Records bundle. This bundle includes the custom records used to store and manage quote items for each logged-in customer. See [Install Your Commerce Website Applications](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2675216.html) for details on installing bundles.

Quotes for Commerce websites works without the Reference Product List Records bundle installed. However, installing this bundle ensures that product list items are persistent and won't be lost if the customer leaves the site before submitting a quote. Shipping information and comments aren't persistent, regardless of bundle installation.
