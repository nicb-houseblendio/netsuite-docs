Configure Store Locator by going to the **Store Locator** subtab in the SuiteCommerce Configuration record. This subtab includes properties for Google Maps and general Store Locator settings. To set up location records for your stores, including address and business hours, see [Creating Locations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N263263.html).

Learn more about configurable Store Locator properties in the following topics:

-   [Store Locator Google Maps Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4695842990.html)
    
-   [Store Locator Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4700825017.html)
    

#### To configure Store Locator properties:

1.  Go to Commerce > Websites > Configuration.
    
2.  Select your website and domain, then click **Configure**.
    
3.  Click the **Store Locator** subtab.
    
4.  Set feature properties in the **Store Locator Google Maps** and **Store Locator** subtabs as needed.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
