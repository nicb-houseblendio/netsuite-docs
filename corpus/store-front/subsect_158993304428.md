To use these themes, your account must meet the following conditions:

-   Your account must be provisioned with a Commerce theme bundle. For details, see [Available Commerce Themes and Supported Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158695491035.html).
    
-   Your account administrator must install the bundle. For details, see [Installing Theme and Extension SuiteApps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510953648.html).
    
-   Your account administrator must activate the theme and the companion theme extension for your website domain. For details, see [Activating Themes and Extensions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1549034518.html).
    
-   Your account must be using SuiteCommerce or SuiteCommerce Advanced (Aconcagua release or later).
    
-   Your account must have the SuiteCommerce Extension Management SuiteApp installed.
