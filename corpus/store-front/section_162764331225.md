To set up ACH payments on your Commerce website, complete the following tasks:

-   [Create a Payment Instrument for ACH Payments on Web Stores](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162763385029.html)
    
-   [Enable ACH Payments for Web Stores](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162763391301.html)
    

Note:

If you want to use ACH Payments during checkout, you'll also need to enable the **Allow non-credit card payment methods during checkout** feature (Commerce > Websites > Website List > \[your website\] > Shopping tab > Payments Page).
