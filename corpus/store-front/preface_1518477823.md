When customers visit your Commerce web store, you want to make online ordering and order tracking seamless and intuitive.

With SuiteCommerce, you can:

-   Offer quantity discounts to your web store customers
    
-   Speed up the ordering process with SKU/reference ordering
    
-   Provide customers with the option to pick up in store
    
-   Help customers find your physical stores
    

See the following topics for more information about online ordering and order tracking:

-   [Payment Link](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_162516600357.html) - Let your customers conveniently settle their invoices online, using a computer or mobile device. They can receive an email with the invoice and a link to the payment page, which allows them to choose their preferred payment method.
    
-   [Direct Invoice Payment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9160903634.html) - Let your customers access and pay their invoices securely within their My Account area, using a computer or mobile device. Your system sends them an email notification with a link to the invoice, and after logging in, they can pay the invoice using a stored payment method.
    
-   [Order Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1546625199.html) - Show customers their billing and shipping details on your website. Both guests and registered customers can view order fulfillment progress, tracking information, and payment details.
    
-   [Quantity Pricing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4618800801.html) - Offer quantity-based discounts to encourage upselling.
    
-   [Strike-Through Pricing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159190992483.html) - Show discounts on specific items with strike-through pricing.
    
-   [Quick Order](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4618800500.html)
    
    \- Let customers quickly add multiple items to their cart. Speed up ordering for your customers who know your catalog and prefer to shop by SKU or reference number.
    
-   [Store Pickup for SuiteCommerce Websites](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4793321418.html) - Let customers pick up orders at physical stores. Set up store locations for pickup and limit pickup hours.
    
-   [Store Locator](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4594388350.html) - Help customers find nearby stores or view a store address on a map. Use with the Pickup In Store feature to let customers pick up purchases instead of shipping them.
    
-   [Web Store Orders Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_156759301108.html) - Track and retrieve data on items ordered through your web store. Workbook users can also analyze which items have been purchased in which site.
