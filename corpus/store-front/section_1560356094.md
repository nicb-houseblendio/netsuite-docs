Personalized Catalog Views can be tricky to set up, especially if you have items in several item segments and customers in multiple customer segments. It's important to think about what each customer segment can and can't see or buy before you set up Personalized Catalog Views in NetSuite.

The following examples show how you can work through the layout and setup process:

-   **[Exclusive Items for VIP Customers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1560356141.html)** - This example covers a scenario where some inventory items are visible to everyone, but only a limited group can buy them. All other items are visible to all users, but only logged-in users can buy them.
    
-   **[Different Catalogs based on Customer Location](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1560356161.html)** - This example limits which items are visible based on customer location. Some items are visible and can be bought by customers from more than one location.
    
-   **[Items Available only to Certified Customers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1560356190.html)** - This example lets only customers who've earned the right certification buy products from certain product lines. Some customers might be certified for more than one product line.
    
-   **[Different Product Lines Available to Different Customers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161133053638.html)** - This example limits certain product lines to specific customers, while letting other customers access more than one product line.
