To let your customers use support cases, make sure the Customer Support and Service feature is enabled for your account.

#### To enable the Customer Support and Service feature:

1.  In NetSuite, go to _Settings > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Go to the **CRM** stab.
    
3.  In the Basic Features area, check the **Customer Support and Service** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

After enabling the Customer Support and Service feature, you need to configure support case values. See [Configure Commerce Support Cases Properties](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_159346247857.html).
