Before you can assign credit card payment processing profiles to your website, you'll need to create one or more payment methods. Create a payment method for each credit card and payment type that customers can use on your website. For more information, see the Order Management help topic, [Creating a Payment Method](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1292421.html).
