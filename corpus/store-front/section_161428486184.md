Note:

This only applies if your account is using SuiteCommerce and the 2021.1 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced or later, and SMT version 3. To check your version of SMT, see [Site Management Tools Versions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4806124052.html).

If you use Commerce themes, the enhanced merchandising zone (CMS MerchZone2) inherits all styles from the theme. However, if required, you can make changes using the Theme Skin Manager. For more information, see [Theme Skin Manager](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1512491665.html).

#### To change merchandising zone elements:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Make sure you have added an enhanced merchandising zone (CMS MerchZone2). For details, see [Adding a Merchandising Zone](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159706874340.html).
    
3.  Click the gear icon on the site management toolbar and select **Themes**.
    
4.  From the **Customized Theme Skins** list, click the ellipsis beside the active skin.
    
    Tip:
    
    The currently active skin has a blue check mark and the words **Active on site**.
    
5.  Click **Edit** to open the Theme Settings panel.
    
6.  Scroll down to the **CMS MerchZone2** section.
    
7.  Select **Section Heading** and make any required changes.
    
8.  Select **Images** and make any required changes.
    
9.  Select **Buttons** and make any required changes.
    
10.  Click **Apply Changes** to save your changes and make your edits live.
