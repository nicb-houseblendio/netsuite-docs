To display intelligent item recommendations, your Commerce website must use both:

-   SuiteCommerce or SuiteCommerce Advanced 18.2 or later.
    
-   Site Management Tools (SMT) version 3 or later.
    

Whether a particular type of recommendation is available on a specific website depends on the following:

| Type of Recommendation | Subsidiary/Site Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| 
-   Order or Cart Items
-   Customers Who Bought This Also Bought
-   Recently Viewed Items
-   Customer Purchase History

 | 

-   At least 50 items in the website catalog
-   A minimum number of transactions in the past 12 months

 |
| 

-   Buy Again

 | 

-   A minimum number of repeat purchases in the past 12 months

 |
| 

-   Alternative Items

 | 

-   At least 50 items in the website catalog
-   A system-supported language, excluding Vietnamese

 |

To check which types of recommendations are available for a website, go to _Commerce > Marketing > Recommendation Availability_.
