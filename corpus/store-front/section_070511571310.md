Online payment card processing requirements have changed in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK. Now, these regions enforce Strong Customer Authentication requirements under the [Payment Services Directive (PSD2)](https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/payment-services-psd-2-directive-eu-2015-2366_en).

To meet Strong Customer Authentication requirements, first-generation 3D Secure (3D Secure 1 or 3DS 1.x) is being replaced by newer 3D Secure 2 (also called 3DS 2.x, EMV 3-D Secure, or EMV 3DS).

Support for 3D Secure 1 ended on October 15, 2022. If you keep using 3D Secure 1 for your Site Builder or SuiteCommerce web store, your card payments may fail or get declined. To avoid this, switch to 3D Secure 2. See [Migrating from 3D Secure 1 to 3D Secure 2](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_070512231910.html).

To reduce declined transactions and comply with Strong Customer Authentication rules, make sure:

-   Your web store's checkout or payment forms support 3D Secure 2 challenges.
    
-   Your payment gateways and NetSuite integrations handle the 3D Secure 2 cardholder challenge for you.
    
    If your payment gateway partner doesn't support 3D Secure 2, select a new partner. For a list of partners that do, see [Payment Gateway Support for 3D Secure 2](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_070511583110.html).
    
    To set up a new payment processing profile for your new payment gateway, see the Order Management topic [Creating Payment Processing Profiles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161891823865.html).
    
-   The issuer of the payment card and the payment gateway both support 3D Secure 2.
