Communications are a key part of your marketing strategy and aim to optimize customer engagement. Commerce web stores let you personalize your messages to make them more effective and build brand awareness.

You can:

-   Create custom templates to be used for email communications from your website
    
-   Choose the default template to be used for different communication types, such as order status emails, password recovery emails, and checkout error alerts
    
-   Set the sender address for your emails.
    
-   Manage users' newsletter subscriptions.
    
-   Add social sharing capabilities to your website
