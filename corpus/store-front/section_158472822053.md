The Subscriptions website interface displays several dates users need to know to effectively manage their subscriptions:

-   **Activation Date:** When the subscription first becomes available to the customer.
    
-   **Last Billing Date:** When the customer was last billed for the subscription.
    
-   **Next Billing Date:** When the customer will get their next bill for the subscription.
    
-   **Renewal:** When the subscription was last renewed.
    
-   **Added:** When the add-on was added to the subscription.
