Merchandising zone content lets you display complementary, cross-sell or up-sell products. You can choose between two merchandising zones: the legacy merchandising zone and the enhanced merchandising zone. Both merchandising zones are similar, but the enhanced merchandising zone offers a configurable display, supports multiple languages, and works with SuiteCommerce themes. The enhanced merchandising zone lets you present products consistently, but you can also display them differently across your website to match your brand.

Important:

To benefit from and use merchandising zones, your account must have the SMT Core Content Types bundle (ID: 190323) installed. For more information, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).

To use the enhanced merchandising zone, your account needs SuiteCommerce and SuiteCommerce Advanced 2021.1 or later. Your account also needs SMT version 3. To check your version of SMT, see [Site Management Tools Versions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4806124052.html).

With the enhanced merchandising zone, you can:

-   Display products in a grid, a horizontal slider, or a vertical slider
    
-   Include a heading
    
-   Choose the number of products to display
