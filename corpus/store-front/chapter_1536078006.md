Images play an important role in creating an engaging and effective website. They can showcase products, highlight promotions, or add visual interest to your pages. Managing images efficiently helps you maintain a professional and dynamic web presence.

To learn more, explore the following help topics:

-   [Image Manager](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1530202893.html) - The Image Manager is your main tool for uploading and organizing website images. With it, you can:
    
    -   Upload multiple image files by browsing your device or by dragging and dropping files.
        
    -   Organize images into folders and subfolders for easier management.
        
    -   Preview images in thumbnail or list views.
        
-   [Site Management Tools Image Content Type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4123521956.html) - Single images, such as banners or featured products can be placed on your web pages using the SMT image content type. You simply drag the image onto your page and adjust basic settings.
    
-   [Image Text Overlay](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528293080.html) - Use this feature to add text, a call-to-action button, and overlays (dark or light) on top of an image. You can control text and button styles, alignment, and link settings. This feature is ideal for creating banners and promotional images with clear messages.
    
-   [Photo Gallery](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528293246.html) - The Photo Gallery extension lets you arrange up to eight images in an attractive display, either as a grid or a carousel. Each image can have its own heading, description, link, and overlay options. This is useful for showcasing product collections or event photos.
    
-   [Slideshow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528293224.html) - With the Slideshow extension, you can add rotating images to your site. Set image order, transition timing, overlays, and include text and buttons on each slide. Each image in the slideshow can have its own link, allowing for interactive and visually appealing banners or featured sections
