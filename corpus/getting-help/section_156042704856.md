NetSuite offers features tailored to business needs that are specific to some countries. These features help you adapt to local business practices and comply with regulations or requirements in the countries that you operate in.

| **Country-Specific Topics** | NetSuite provides solutions for a growing number of countries as part of its global offerings. For many of them, you can find help under the [Overview of Country-Specific Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4169633414.html). This section covers features, SuiteApps for individual countries, best practices for account setups, and country-specific information related to taxes and payment formats. For countries without a separate country-specific help topic but with their own specific features supported by NetSuite, see [General International Tax Topics](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1813074.html) or [Payment File Formats](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1675118.html). |
| --- | --- |
| **Country-Specific Tax Topics** | Taxation in global business involves both international and country-specific topics. For some countries, you can find country-specific tax help topics in [Overview of Country-Specific Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4169633414.html). For countries that don't have separate tax help topics, see [General International Tax Topics](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1813074.html). This section introduces general tax topics and links to country-specific topics. |
| **Country-Specific Payment File Formats** | The Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp offers payment file formats for payments, receivables, and Positive Pay transactions. For some countries, country-specific payment formats help topics are available in [Overview of Country-Specific Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4169633414.html). For countries without a separate country-specific help topic, see [Payment File Formats](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1675118.html). |
