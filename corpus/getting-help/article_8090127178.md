NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers is designed to make your searches more powerful. Sometimes, keyword searches return too many results to be useful. With NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers, you can ask questions in natural language and get more focused answers.

Note:

NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers available to all NetSuite accounts, but only for English-language content.

NetSuite Expert has access to all the knowledge that is in SuiteAnswers, including NetSuite Help Center topics and SuiteAnswers articles. NetSuite Expert uses retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG, to respond with a summary of the information you seek. The summary also shows which sources the information comes from and includes links to specific articles so that you can explore further.
