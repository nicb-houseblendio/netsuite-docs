NetSuite OneWorld helps you manage multiple areas of your multinational, multicompany business including financial statements, customers, e-commerce, services resource planning (SRP), business intelligence, and tax and compliance management. The solution crosses product areas, and the documentation for OneWorld is integrated with the various functional areas.

| **Getting Started with OneWorld** | This section of the help introduces OneWorld and its features across all NetSuite product areas. Begin here and follow the links to learn how OneWorld affects accounting and ERP, CRM, SRP, and analytics. See [Introduction to NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N266468.html). |
