The Support team categorizes issues as defects, enhancements, or minor enhancements.

-   A **defect** is when a feature doesn't work as intended.
    
-   An **enhancement** is a request for new or improved functionality.
    
-   A **minor enhancement** is a small change to existing functionality.
    

## Defect Resolution Process {#bridgehead_1531920493}

The Oracle NetSuite process for handling customer-reported defects has the following goals:

-   Resolution of customer-reported defects within a targeted time frame that's appropriate to each defect's urgency.
    
-   Consistent and clear communication to customers throughout the defect resolution process.
    

As part of this process, an urgency level to each defect, based on the following key inputs:

-   Business Impact - business categorization that measures how much the defect affects operations. Defined levels are B1-B4, with B1 representing the biggest impact.
    
-   Severity - technical categorization that reflects the defect's effect on functionality or performance. Defined levels are S1-S4, with S1 representing the greatest severity.
    
-   Other factors - such as the number of impacted customers, whether the problem is a recently introduced defect, or a sudden change to operational behavior has occurred.
    

The urgency level of a defect sets the target timeframe for resolution.

See [NetSuite Issue Resolution Process](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/74573) for an up to date timeframe.

During the resolution process, the Support team communicates the following milestones to impacted customers:

-   Assignment of the defect to an engineer
    
-   Assignment of a target date for the release of a fix
    
-   Completion of code review, testing, and authorization to release the fix
    
-   Release of the fix to the NetSuite production environment
    

The Support team can also provide further assistance as needed. If you want to request additional information or discuss an issue's urgency level, please update your support case for assistance.

For the minor release schedule, see [NetSuite minor release Release Schedule](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/103478).

## Viewing Defects and Enhancements {#bridgehead_N338799}

If you have a role with permission to access NetSuite 360, you can view defects and enhancements associated with your support cases. For more information, see [Accessing NetSuite 360](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N337869.html).

On the NetSuite 360 page, click **See Support Issues** in the Customer Center - Home Links portlet to view a list of issues entered for your company. Use the Quick Sort dropdown list to select a subset of issues to display: Recently Created, Recently Modified, or Recently Viewed.
