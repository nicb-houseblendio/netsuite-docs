Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and Sales Force Automation (SFA) with NetSuite includes sales, marketing, partners, and support management. The following help is available for CRM and SFA in NetSuite:

| **Customer Service & Support** | For information about setting up case management including case types, origins, and more, see [Setting Up Case Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2421072.html). For information about working with cases including creating, escalating, tracking time, and more, see [Working With Cases](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2434254.html). For information about issue management including statuses, severities, reproducibility, and more, see [Issue Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2438260.html). For information about the knowledge base including creating topics, solutions, and more, see [Knowledge Base](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2445950.html). For information about monitoring support performance including case alerts on support cases, performance metrics, and more, see [Monitoring Support Performance](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1483444833.html). For access to all of the NetSuite user guides, see [User Guides](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_3710621755.html). |
| --- | --- |
| **Sales and Marketing** | For information about marketing automation including capturing leads, marketing campaigns, and more, see [Marketing Automation Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N973384.html). For information about sales force automation including set up, sales forecasting, commission, and more, see [Sales Force Automation Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1035383.html). For information about managing partners including creating partner records and more, see [Managing Partners](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1166465.html). For information about partner commissions and royalties including schedules, plans, and more, see [Partner Commissions & Royalties](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1169123.html). For access to a variety of partner reports, see [Partner Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1176274.html). For access to all of the NetSuite user guides, see [User Guides](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_3710621755.html). |
