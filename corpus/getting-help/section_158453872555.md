The version of NetSuite that you're currently using is displayed on your Home page.

#### To find the version of NetSuite your company uses:

1.  Log in to NetSuite.
    
2.  On the Home page, scroll down to the bottom of the page.
    
3.  Find the NetSuite version number in the page footer. The version number should look like this: NetSuite (Edition: United States) Release 2020.1 Copyright © NetSuite Inc. 1999-2020.
