This section may help answer questions you have about using NetSuite Expert.

Note:

NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers available to all NetSuite accounts, but only for English-language content.

If you have other questions about how to use NetSuite Expert, see [Using NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0910111102.html).

### What kind of things can I ask?

If your question is about NetSuite, ask anything that you would like to ask. NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers only summarizes answers about things related to NetSuite.

See the following table for examples of searches that NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers can help with.

Note:

Some examples in the following table use quotation marks, but you don't have to use them in your queries. Sometimes quotation marks help you get more focused results, especially for multi-word concepts, like "saved search" or "bank feeds".

| Instead of keywords like this: | Add context like this: |
| --- | --- |
| suitetax | What is SuiteTax? enable suite tax |
| record module | What actions are supported by record module? suitescript "record module" |
| bank feeds | How do I configure my bank feed? supported "bank feeds" |
| saved search | How do I create a saved search? "saved search" examples |
| suiteQL | How do I use SuiteQL? show me SuiteQL examples |
| bill capture | how do I capture vendor bills? "bill capture" preferences |

### Do I have to ask a question?

Asking a question helps, because it gives NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers more context for your search. However, asking a question is not required. What is needed to activate NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers is that you submit at least three (3) words.

The more context you give, the better your results will be. Using a phrase that is between three and seven words usually works well.

Note:

If you are not asking a question, you may want to enclose your phrase or keywords inside quotation marks.

### Is there a limit on how many queries can I submit?

Each user can submit up to 20,000 queries (requests) within 30 days. If a user goes over this limit, NetSuite Expert won't respond with a summary.

### Is there a limit to how long my query can be?

The query limit is 255 characters. However, that's a lot of characters, about 35 words in a three-sentence paragraph. You'll probably get better results with a shorter, more focused query.

### The summary answer doesn't seem right, why not?

You may see Generative AI mentioned everywhere in the news these days. NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers uses Oracle's Generative AI (GenAI) service. Be aware that GenAI is still new and experimental. Sometimes the answer isn't as accurate as you would like. You should always verify the information that is presented in the summary. We are always improving how NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers responds. You can help make the answers better by sending us your feedback!

### I have the NetSuite app, and SuiteCommerce Advanced, and a few SuiteApps. How do I make sure I get an answer about the right product?

You may have access to more than one NetSuite product or SuiteApp, all of which have release notes and accompanying documentation. To help in your search, use the name of the product or SuiteApp in your query. For example, instead of submitting a generic request, try something like one of the following phrases:

-   show me the NetSuite 2026.1 release notes
    
-   where are the release notes for SuiteCommerce Advanced?
    
-   How do I use the Bank Feeds SuiteApp?
    
-   I can't find the Field Service Management documentation
    

### How can I submit feedback on the summary?

Click the thumbs-up icon if you liked the summary. If you want to send feedback, click the thumbs-down icon, enter your comments, and click **Submit**.

### I have good luck with my keyword searches. Can I enter keywords in the search box?

Yes, you can still search with keywords in SuiteAnswers. NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers won't activate if you only enter one or two words. To get a summary, enter three or more words, as keywords (perhaps inside quotation marks) or ask in the form of a question.

### Will NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers understand my question if I make some spelling or grammar mistakes?

NetSuite Expert in SuiteAnswers can handle a few mistakes in your query. You can use contractions and common industry terms. If you don't get a good answer, try rephrasing or fixing any spelling errors.

### Can I ask questions in a language other than English?

For the best results, you should ask your question or enter your phrase in English only.

### Can I ask follow-up questions?

You can ask the same question multiple times, and the AI Summary may vary slightly with each response. However, you can't really ask a follow-up question. NetSuite Expert does not retain any memory of your previous query or queries.
