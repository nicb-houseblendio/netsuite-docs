Advanced Customer Support (ACS) Playbooks guide organizations along their growth journey with expert direction and leading practices based on over 20 years of experience. ACS Playbooks offer a set of prescriptive services to help overcome business challenges and support key growth milestones with NetSuite.

The following ACS Playbooks are exclusively available to ACS Optimize and Architect customers:

## Financial Management {#bridgehead_164198512895}

Improves operational efficiencies by optimizing key financial methodologies such as:

-   Planning and budgeting
    
-   Forecasting
    
-   Accounts payable and accounts receivables automation
    
-   Revenue recognition
    
-   Month-end closing
    

## Human Capital {#bridgehead_164198515181}

Simplifies key processes with HR, such as:

-   Payroll
    
-   Workforce management
    
-   Compensation
    
-   Performance management
    
-   Onboarding new employees
    

## Supply Chain Operations {#bridgehead_164198516411}

Increase efficiencies across the supply chain in areas such as:

-   Inventory and warehouse management
    
-   Demand and supply planning
    
-   Outsourcing
    
-   Procurement
    
-   Production management
    

## Customer 360 {#bridgehead_164198517547}

Improves customer engagement tactics around deploying:

-   Unified ecommerce experience
    
-   Mobile and point of sale revenue streams
    
-   Customer relationship management
    
-   Order management
    
-   Customer analytics reporting
    

## Project Profitability {#bridgehead_164198518617}

Simplifies key project-related processes, such as:

-   Project accounting and billing
    
-   Project and resource management
    
-   Timesheet and expense management
    

## International Expansion {#bridgehead_164198519666}

Handles the financial complexities of entering international markets with guidance on:

-   Operating in multiple countries
    
-   Managing multiple currencies and languages
    
-   Tax compliance
    
-   Bank automation
    
-   Localization
    

## Performance and Risk Management {#bridgehead_164198520681}

Ensures NetSuite maintains peak performance and security as the business scales, providing guidance on:

-   System architecture
    
-   Capacity planning
    
-   Integrations
    
-   Access controls
    
-   Environment management
    
-   Script reviews
    

## Capital Markets {#bridgehead_164198521835}

Prepares for a public listing or merger/acquisition by establishing and optimizing required governance and internal controls or developing the financial reports needed to comply with audit requirements and tax regulations.

## Cross-Functional {#bridgehead_164198522850}

Leverages the power of NetSuite ensuring cross-functional processes, like quote to cash and procure to pay remain optimized.

## Industry {#bridgehead_164198524100}

Optimizes business functions through NetSuite's more than 20 years of industry-specific KPI's and insights across industries - retail, software, services, manufacturing, wholesale distribution, and more.
