When you want a feature or functionality added to NetSuite, visit SuiteIdeas to submit a new enhancement request or vote for an existing idea. You must have the NetSuite Support Center role or the NetSuite Support Center (Basic) role to access SuiteIdeas.

From the Support tab, use the SuiteIdeas portlet to visit SuiteIdeas and search through all existing NetSuite enhancement requests. Vote for the ideas you like, or enter your own ideas to make NetSuite better.

#### To search and vote for enhancements: {#procedure_N339295}

1.  Go to the **Support** tab, and click **Go to SuiteIdeas** in the SuiteIdeas portlet.
    
    Note:
    
    If you don't see the **Go to SuiteIdeas** button in the SuiteIdeas portlet, ask your account administrator if you can be given access. See [Accessing NetSuite 360](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N337869.html).
    
    The SuiteIdeas site opens in a new tab or window, depending on your browser preferences.
    
2.  Click one of the following links within the SuiteIdeas site:
    
    -   **Search** to see if your idea has been requested by others.
        
    -   **Browse** for ideas by categories.
        
    -   View new ideas, the most recently updated ideas, and ideas that have received the most votes.
        
    -   View the ideas you have voted for.
        
        Click an idea to open it in a new window.
        
3.  If you find the idea you're looking for, click **Vote for this Idea**.
    
4.  Rate the idea, and click **Submit Vote**.
    

If you don't find the idea you're searching for, you can enter your own.

#### To enter a new idea:

1.  Click **File a New Idea** on the SuiteIdeas home page.
    
2.  Enter a description of your idea, and click **Search Idea**.
    
3.  Review the list of search results to see if your idea has already been entered.
    
4.  If your idea isn't in the list or results, click **File a New SuiteIdea** at the bottom of the page.
    
5.  Enter a title for your idea.
    
6.  Select the product area and feature your idea is related to.
    
7.  Enter a detailed description of your idea.
    
8.  Click **Submit Idea**.
    

Each SuiteIdea is reviewed, and after it's approved, it's listed and can be viewed and voted on by other NetSuite users.

Important:

When you're finished with SuiteIdeas, return to your original NetSuite tab or window. The Support tab changes to NetSuite 360 when you go to SuiteIdeas. To exit NetSuite 360 and return to your previous NetSuite center, click **Return to Previous Role** in the upper right corner.

Note:

Your idea gets Enhancement Request number after it has been submitted and reviewed. You can find the number on the page of the Idea you created.
