Advanced Customer Support (ACS) offers four levels of support: Advise, Monitor, Optimize, and Architect. As you move up subscription levels, the amount of engagement, proactivity, and resource expertise increases.

## ACS Advise {#bridgehead_164129290296}

As the entry-level ACS offering, advise is available for customers who are getting started with NetSuite. Advise has access to NetSuite specialists to provide product guidance and system troubleshooting.

## ACS Monitor {#bridgehead_164129293342}

Monitor level is designed for established businesses that need hands-on technical and functional support. A designated functional consultant assists with project activities to ensure key milestones are met in a timely fashion.

## ACS Optimize {#bridgehead_164129294754}

Optimize provides expert solution management for a deeper and more proactive engagement. You partner with a designated Customer Success Manager (CSM) to ensure strategic milestones are accomplished and work with a functional consultant to optimize your solution.

## ACS Architect {#bridgehead_164129296730}

For enterprise businesses with complex international or subsidiary configurations, Architect delivers a best-in-class solution designed for optimal scalability and performance. Architect also provides comprehensive support with a senior CSM and a named team of expert technical, functional, and support resources.
