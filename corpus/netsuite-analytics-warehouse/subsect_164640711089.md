The notification recipient gets emails about your Analytics Warehouse instance, such as service availability. You can manage the notification recipient email address from the Configuration page.

#### To change the notification recipient email:

1.  Go to the Configuration page. For more information, see [Accessing the NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Configuration Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159472942033.html#subsect_0129115424).
    
2.  Click the **Update Notification Email** tile. The Update Notification Email window opens.
    
3.  Choose a user from the dropdown list and click **Update NSAW notification recipient email**.
    

You can use email addresses that aren't linked to NetSuite users. To do this, click the NSAW notification recipient email field, and then the clear icon ![images/clear.png](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/NetSuiteAnalyticsWarehouse/clear.png). After clearing the field, type the email address you want. You can also add multiple emails as notification recipients by entering an email distribution list.
