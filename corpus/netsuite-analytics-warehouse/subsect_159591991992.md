NetSuite groups your data into functional areas in the Analytics Warehouse. To add a functional area to your data transfer, see [Setting Up the Data Transfer](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_159497492876.html).

To see which record types and fields are available in the NetSuite2.com data source, see [Record Types and Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_164312261929.html).

The following functional areas are currently supported in NetSuite Analytics Warehouse:

-   Bank
    
-   Employee Expense
    
-   Financials
    
-   Inventory
    
-   Manufacturing
    
-   Payroll
    
-   Projects and Support Management
    
-   Purchases
    
-   Sales
    
-   Sales Snapshot

## Salesforce Functional Areas {#subsect_0625012011}

Before NetSuite 2026.2, the NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Data Transfer page supported only NetSuite as a data source. All functional areas like Customers, Invoices, and Orders were treated as NetSuite data by default.

As of NetSuite 2026.2, if you're a Premium or Enterprise user, you can select Salesforce as data source. Salesforce data is grouped into its own set of functional areas, similar to the way to NetSuite data is organized.

For more information about transferring and reloading your Salesforce functional areas to your Analytics Warehouse instance, see [Transferring Salesforce Data to NetSuite Analytics Warehouse](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0703033717.html).
