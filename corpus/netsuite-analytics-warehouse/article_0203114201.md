## Access NetSuite Analytics Warehouse by Single Sign-On {#subsect_0203041744}

If you have an account with SAML SSO or OIDC SSO set up, you can use it to access your NetSuite Analytics Warehouse instance. For more information about how to setup this feature, see [Using Single Sign-On with NetSuite Analytics Warehouse](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9113941774.html).

## Merge Your NetSuite Accounts Data into a Single NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Instance {#subsect_0203114222}

If you own more than one NetSuite production account, you can blend all your NetSuite data into your NetSuite Analytics Warehouse instance. This enables you to consolidate financial and operational data and saves you time and effort in building a business intelligence solution. For more information, see [Merging Your NetSuite Accounts Data Into Your NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Instance](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_4155021337.html).
