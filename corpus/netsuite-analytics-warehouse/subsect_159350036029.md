When you enable the Analytics Warehouse feature in your account, you're automatically assigned the Data Warehouse Integrator role. You can also assign this role to any user. After you assign the role, the user's name appears in the **User Account for Data Transfer Credentials** dropdown list on the Configuration page.

The Data Warehouse Integrator role can view all standard and custom production data. This role is for machine-to-machine integration, you can't sign in with it.

Note:

The Data Warehouse Integrator role doesn't count against the Full Licensed Users count.

#### To assign the Data Warehouse Integrator Role to users

1.  Log in to NetSuite with an administrator role.
    
2.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Employees_.
    
3.  Click **Edit** next to the employee you want to assign the Data Warehouse Integrator role to.
    
4.  Click the **Access** subtab.
    
5.  On the Roles subtab, select the Data Warehouse Integrator role from the list.
    
6.  Click **Add**.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

Important:

There needs to be at least one user with the Data Warehouse Integrator role assigned. If there are no users with this role, the data pipeline doesn't work.

For more information, see [Assigning Roles to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N897798.html).
