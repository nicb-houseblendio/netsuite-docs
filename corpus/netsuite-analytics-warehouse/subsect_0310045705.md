To keep your data private and secure, it's important to remove personal information (PI) from both NetSuite and the Analytics Warehouse when required. To do so, remove the PI from your NetSuite fields, then reload the data in the corresponding functional areas and custom fields. This action ensures that the PI is fully removed from both systems.

For more information about removing PI from your NetSuite fields, see [Personal Information (PI) Removal](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_156596387080.html). To learn more about reloading your data, see [Reloading Your Data to NetSuite Analytics Warehouse](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0310044705.html).
