## NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Data Transfer Audit Log Page {#subsect_1222020718}

You can view an audit log that details all of the data transfer operations, their statuses, and the time they occurred. You can access the audit log from the NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Data Transfer Setup page. For more information, see [Viewing the Data Transfer Audit Log](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_159497492876.html#subsect_0429042836).

## Functional Area Reload Configuration Page {#subsect_1222020734}

A page is available that you can use to reload and update your data by functional area in your NetSuite Analytics Warehouse instance. This makes your data transfer and reload experience accurate to your business needs by letting you choose the functional areas that you want to reload your data from. You can access this page from the NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Data Transfer Setup page. For more information, see [Reloading Your Data to NetSuite Analytics Warehouse](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0310044705.html).

## Custom Fields Data Transfer to NetSuite Analytics Warehouse {#subsect_1222020554}

You can transfer the data from your custom and supplemental standard fields to NetSuite Analytics Warehouse from the data transfer setup page in NetSuite. This simplifies the data augmentation process and saves you time by not requiring you to go to the NetSuite Analytics Warehouse interface to complete this task. For more information, see [Transferring Custom and Supplemental Fields to NetSuite Analytics Warehouse](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0304031642.html).

## NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Data Reload {#subsect_1222020633}

You can easily reload and update the data in all of your standard and custom fields in your NetSuite Analytics Warehouse instance. You can reload your data from the data transfer setup page in NetSuite. With this feature you ensure data consistency and synchronization with your NetSuite account after you modify your fields in NetSuite. For more information, see [Reloading Your Data to NetSuite Analytics Warehouse](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0310044705.html).
