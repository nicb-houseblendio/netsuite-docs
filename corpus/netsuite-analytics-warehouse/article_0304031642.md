In NetSuite, you can create custom fields to enhance standard records and transactions, and record information specific to your business needs. When you transfer your NetSuite data to the Analytics Warehouse, only some of the standard NetSuite fields are included. To transfer your custom fields and the supplemental standard fields, you need to do it manually on the Data Transfer Setup page. This procedure makes sure you can work with all your NetSuite data in the Analytics Warehouse and build accurate data visualizations for your business.

#### To transfer custom and supplemental fields data to NetSuite Analytics Warehouse:

1.  Go to the Data Transfer Setup page. For more information, see [Setting Up the Data Transfer](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_159497492876.html).
    
2.  On the Data Transfer Setup page, click the Edit icon ![images/edit.png](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/NetSuiteAnalyticsWarehouse/edit.png) next to the functional area from which you want to transfer custom or supplemental fields.
    
    Note:
    
    For functional areas that are not active, click the **Select data for transfer** button on the Data Transfer Setup page. Then, click the switch button next to them, and click **Next**.
    
3.  In the **Custom Fields** tab, select the custom fields that you want to transfer by checking the box next to the field.
    
4.  In the **Supplemental Fields** tab, select the supplemental standard fields that you want to transfer by checking the box next to the field.
    
    Note:
    
    Use the search bar and filters to make selecting fields easier, especially if you have a long list. Transferring both custom and supplemental fields is optional, choose only the data that you need.
    
    Important:
    
    You can transfer custom and supplemental fields from up to 10 different source tables at a time. If you want to transfer fields from more than 10 source tables, select up to 10 tables, complete the transfer, then repeat the process with the remaining fields.
    
5.  Click **Next**.
    
6.  Review the fields you've selected to ensure your data transfer is accurate.
    
7.  Click **Transfer Data**.
    
    Note:
    
    The data transfer starts 20 minutes after you click **Transfer Data**.
