## Updated NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Configuration Page {#subsect_0707035720}

The NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Configuration page has been redesigned. You can access it through _Setup > Integration > Configuration_ or through Analytics > NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Configuration. The new layout improves the navigation experience and includes additional resources such as direct links to the newest release notes, training courses, and product documentation. For more information, see [Accessing the NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Configuration Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159472942033.html#subsect_0129115424).

## Transfer Multi-Select Custom Fields to NetSuite Analytics Warehouse {#subsect_0707035744}

You can now transfer multi-select custom fields to your Analytics Warehouse instance from the Data Transfer Setup page. For more information about multi-select fields, see [Create Segments as Multi-Select Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4325673845.html). For more information about transferring custom fields to your Analytics Warehouse instance, see [Transferring Custom and Supplemental Fields to NetSuite Analytics Warehouse](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0304031642.html).
