You can add your NetSuite Analytics Warehouse workbooks to your dashboard to have at-a-glance information about your business without having to log in to a different interface. To do so, you need to:

1.  Enable the feature in the NetSuite Analytics Warehouse console.
    
    Note:
    
    You need to have the NSAW Administrator role assigned to complete this step.
    
2.  Add a NSAW portlet and set it up with your NetSuite Analytics Warehouse workbook.
    

Note:

You need to set up SAML SSO for NetSuite Analytics Warehouse before adding the workbooks to your dashboard. For more information, see [Setting up SAML SSO for NetSuite Analytics Warehouse](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1211045628.html).

After personalizing a dashboard, you can share it with other users in your center by publishing it. For more information, see [Publishing a Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N633149.html#bridgehead_4640072108).

#### To enable the feature in the NetSuite Analytics Warehouse console:

1.  Log in to your Analytics Warehouse instance.
    
2.  Click the Navigator menu icon ![Navigator icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/NetSuiteAnalyticsWarehouse/AnalyticsWarehouseNavigator_icon.png). Then, select **Console**.
    
3.  Click **Safe Domains**_._
    
4.  Click **Add domain** and paste your NetSuite account URL (https://_<accountID>_.app.netsuite.com/), where _<accountID>_ is your NetSuite account ID.
    
5.  Check all the boxes for the URL you added.
    

#### To add a portlet and set it up:

1.  Open NetSuite Analytics Warehouse. Go to your workbooks, click the actions icon ![Three dot menu](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/NetSuiteAnalyticsWarehouse/threedotmenu.png) of the workbook you want, and select **Export**. Then, click **Copy Link**.
    
2.  Open your NetSuite account in another tab. Go to your dashboard and click **Personalize**.
    
3.  In the **Standard Content** tab of the **Personalize Dashboard** palette, click the NSAW portlet icon or drag it onto your dashboard.
    
4.  In the NSAW portlet, click ![Three dot menu](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/NetSuiteAnalyticsWarehouse/threedotmenu.png) and select **Set Up** from the menu.
    
5.  Paste the workbook URL into the **Visualization URL** field, then click **Save**.
    

Note:

The portlet shows data from the last incremental data transfer.

You can also use a SuiteApp portlet to add NetSuite Analytics Warehouse workbooks to a dashboard. For more information, see [Adding NetSuite Analytics Warehouse Workbooks Using a SuiteApp Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1006014730.html).
