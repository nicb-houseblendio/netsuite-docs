NetSuite may deliver selected enhancements in monthly minor releases after the NetSuite Version 2026.1 scheduled version upgrade and before the next scheduled version upgrade.

To view descriptions of the enhancements and changes included in these monthly minor releases, see [Monthly Minor Release Notes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_8174941789.html).

For more information about timing and availability, see [NetSuite Minor Release Schedules](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/103478/loc/en_us).
