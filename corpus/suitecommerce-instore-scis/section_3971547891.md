When performing a sales transaction in SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS), you have the option to assign a sales representative to the transaction.

Employees defined as Sales Reps can participate in commission plans. Transactions attributed to sales representatives can be used to determine commissions or calculate sales metrics. When you assign a sales representative to a transaction, this information is stored as part of the transaction record.

Note:

Depending on how your system is configured, selecting a sales representative may be required before you can perform a transaction.

#### To select a sales representative:

1.  Log in to SuiteCommerce InStore.
    
2.  Tap **Select Sales Rep** in the transaction summary.
    
    ![Select Sales Rep Button](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SCIS/User_SelectSalesRep2018.1.png)
3.  Tap the name of the sales representative you want to select.
    
    ![Names of Sales Reps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SCIS/User_SelectSalesRep2018.1_Choose.png)

The name of the sales representative appears at the bottom of the transaction summary.

![Name of Sales Rep at bottom of Transaction Summary](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SCIS/User_SelectSalesRep2018.1_Name.png)

#### To change the current sales representative:

1.  Tap the name of the current sales representative in the transaction summary.
    
2.  Tap the name of the new sales representative you want to select.
    
3.  Select **None** if you do not want to associate a sales representative with the transaction.
