You can add a new customer or edit a customer profile in SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS). The Customer Profile in SCIS lets you to view customer information along with their purchase history. It has basic contact information, such as name, street address, and email address.

Note:

When operating in Fallback, you can't create, edit, or view customer information. However, you can still assign a customer to an order. For more information, read [Assigning a Customer to an Order in Fallback](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1518651979.html#subsect_159102703116).
