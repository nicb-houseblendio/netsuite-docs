Administrators can use the SCIS Customer Note Types custom record to define notes for types of information associated with a customer. This custom record is installed with SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS).

After an administrator defines customer notes, sales associates can add them to the customer profile in SCIS. For example, you may want to track the customer's item preferences or frequent purchases.

#### To define SCIS Customer Note Types:

1.  Go to Commerce > InStore > Customer Notes.
    
    The note types include a **Custom Note** by default. Sales Associates can enter any text into the note in SCIS.
    
2.  To create a new note type, click **New SCIS Customer Note Types**.
    
3.  Enter a **Name** for the customer note type. The name is displayed in SCIS, so sales associates can select it and enter additional information.
    
4.  You can choose particular **Locations**, **Websites**, and **Subsidiaries**, or make the note available to all of them.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

In SCIS, sales associates can add notes to customer profiles and see existing notes. The notes are also saved in the system on the customer record on the **Communication** subtab, under **User Notes**.
