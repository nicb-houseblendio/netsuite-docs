Administrators must first complete the following general Cash Drawer Management setup steps whether they plan to use Advanced Cash Drawer Management in SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS).

For more information about setting up Advanced Cash Drawer Management, see [Advanced Cash Drawer Management Setup for SCIS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1542737728.html).

For reference information about the custom fields and records used in Cash Drawer Management, see the section on [Cash Drawer Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1503104899.html) custom fields and records.

#### To set up Cash Drawer Management in SCIS:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Locations_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to each location using SCIS, and then click **Save**.
    
    This action triggers the automatic creation of an **SCIS Store Safe Account** and **SCIS Cash Drawer Difference** account for each Location. The accounts are also saved on Location records automatically.
    
3.  Ensure the **Use Cash Drawer Account** box is checked on the SCIS Payment record for cash, and for any other payment method for which you want the cash drawer to open.
    
    1.  Go to Commerce > InStore > Payment Methods.
        
    2.  View or edit the SCIS Payment record used for cash. Ensure the **Use Cash Drawer Account** box is checked. This box must be checked on all payment methods for which you want the cash drawer to open.
