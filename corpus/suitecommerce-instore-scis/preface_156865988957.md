SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS) is a web-based point-of-sale (POS) application. SCIS provides a touch-screen interface designed for tablets. It works with common POS devices, such as card readers (PIN pads or other payment devices), printers, barcode scanners, and cash drawers.

SCIS is a full-featured solution that can scale to meet demands as retailers increase sales volume, add new items and pricing structures, and expand to new locations.

## SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS) Administrator Guide {#bridgehead_157237716348}

The administrator guide covers setting up SCIS on tablets and in NetSuite, ensuring that everything runs smoothly at each integration point, setting up fallback mode, and making other changes to fit your business processes. It also covers employee roles and permissions, custom fields, lists and records, and how to use saved searches for your reporting needs.

[SCIS Administrator Guide](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/part_4260908985.html)

## SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS) User Guide {#bridgehead_157237720244}

The user guide helps sales associates and managers use SCIS to handle sales, returns, and other transactions. It covers the user interface, working in fallback mode, handling beginning- and end-of-day tasks, and managing customer records.

[SCIS User Guide](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/part_4260909468.html)

## SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS) 2.0 Release Guides {#bridgehead_10163249783}

SCIS 2.0 uses SuiteScript 2.X to boost performance with updated payment processes. See these guides to learn about and enable SCIS 2.0 in your account.

[SCIS User Guide](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/part_4260909468.html)

The Commerce web stores line includes another point of sale solution - NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS). NSPOS runs primarily on desktop cash register setups.
