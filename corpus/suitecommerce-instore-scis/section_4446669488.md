The following custom records are included to store printer settings for SCIS. Note that SCIS only supports specific paper sizes. Don't enter additional paper sizes. For more information, see [Setting Up Receipt Printers for SCIS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4298266164.html).

## SCIS Available Printers {#bridgehead_4399402044}

-   **ID**: customrecord\_ns\_pos\_printers
    
-   **Description**: List of known devices for printing receipts. You must configure a custom record for each printer associated with a point-of-sale device configured for SCIS.
    

## SCIS Paper Size {#bridgehead_1490306781}

-   **ID**: customrecord\_ns\_pos\_paper
    
-   **Description**: SCIS only supports the following paper sizes: A4 or Letter for Air print, and 2 inches or 3 inches for Star printers.
    

## SCIS Printing Settings (AirPrint) {#bridgehead_1490307352}

-   **ID**: customrecord\_ns\_pos\_printing\_airprint
    
-   **Description**: Stores preferences for the AirPrint receipt printer, such as the printer name, paper size, and SCIS settings record.
    

## SCIS Printing Settings (Star) {#bridgehead_1490307397}

-   **ID**: customrecord\_ns\_pos\_printing\_star
    
-   **Description**: Stores preferences for the Star receipt printer, such as the printer name, paper size, and SCIS settings record.
