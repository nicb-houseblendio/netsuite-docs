SCIS Location Distance is a custom record associated with SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS). This custom record defines the distance between store locations. Information from this custom record shows on the **Nearby Stores** tab on the product display page in SCIS.

#### To configure Location Distance:

1.  Go to Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types.
    
2.  Find **Location Distance** in the list of custom records, and then click **List** in that row.
    
3.  Select store locations in the **From** and **To** fields on the custom record.
    
4.  Enter the **Distance**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
