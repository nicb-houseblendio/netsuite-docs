In Fallback, sales associates work as they do in SCIS online. However, SCIS Permissions which you may have applied to restrict certain operations in SCIS online aren't supported in Fallback. For example, SCIS Permission restrictions on custom discounts aren't supported. Sales associates can apply a discount of any value without manager approval.

In general, SCIS Permissions are **not** supported in Fallback. Note that SCIS permissions aren't enforced for the following operations:

-   Deleting a transaction
    
-   Voiding a line
    
-   Payment Methods
    
-   100% discount
    
    Note:
    
    Only custom discounts are supported in Fallback.
