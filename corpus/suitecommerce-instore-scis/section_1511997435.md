Fallback enables sales associates to perform basic point-of-sale operations when SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS) can't connect to NetSuite. Fallback is included in the SCIS SuiteApp and runs locally on the mobile device you use for point-of-sale transactions. To start using Fallback when NetSuite isn't available, an administrator must enable it and perform some configuration tasks in NetSuite.

While SCIS relies on backend capabilities provided by NetSuite, such as payment processing, tax calculations, and storing customer and product data, Fallback runs independently. The SCIS mobile app stores assets such as images (logos and background), printing settings, and specific Fallback settings. After Fallback has been configured in NetSuite, logging in to SCIS on the mobile device triggers the download of the product catalog. The product download is transparent to the sales associate who's logged in. It occurs automatically upon login and doesn't disrupt the typical workflow.

## Best Practice for Using Fallback {#subsect_1518650437}

If the connection to NetSuite is interrupted, the Fallback login screen displays. After logging in to Fallback with a Fallback PIN, the sales associate can continue creating orders and submitting sales transactions.

Fallback can be used during the following events when NetSuite isn't available:

-   NetSuite twice yearly major updates
    
-   NetSuite scheduled maintenance
    
-   Temporary internet outage
    

## Operating in Fallback {#subsect_1518650636}

Note the following considerations for sales associates entering orders in Fallback, and for administrators and store managers setting up Fallback and working in NetSuite.

### Sales Associates {#subsect_1518650816}

After logging in to Fallback, sales associates can enter orders when SCIS isn't available, but with limited capability. For example, orders aren't submitted to the system in real time. Also, some SCIS operations such as shipping and in-store pickup aren't available in Fallback.

Note:

In Fallback, sales associates can use custom discounts regardless of authorization requirements.

### Administrators and Store Managers {#subsect_1518650808}

Orders submitted in Fallback are posted to NetSuite through a consolidation process that occurs asynchronously. An administrator or manager may need to reconcile a Fallback transaction if it fails to create a transaction in NetSuite. General ledger accounts and inventory will be out of date until transactions are posted in NetSuite.
