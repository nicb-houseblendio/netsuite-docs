SuiteCommerce InStore helps sales associates view information about a customer to better understand a customer's needs and enhance up sell possibilities.

Note:

Customer statistics only appear when a customer has previous purchases.

The customer statistics area displays information about a customer's previous purchases in graphical form. The following screenshot shows an example of how this information appears in the customer profile.

![Customer Statistics](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SCIS/User_CustomerProfile_Charts.png)

This information is divided into the following graphs and charts:

-   Purchase and Returns
    
-   Visit Count
    
-   Purchases Breakdown
