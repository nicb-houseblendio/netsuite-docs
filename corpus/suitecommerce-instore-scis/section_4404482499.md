The following saved searches affect the data included in the sales report available to sales associates who are logged in to SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS). You can modify these saved searches to create sales reports you want to see in NetSuite. For more information, see [Custom SCIS Sales Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4409437631.html).

## SCIS Retail Tenders {#bridgehead_4395027303}

-   **ID**: customsearch\_ns\_pos\_retail\_tenders
    
-   **Description**: Returns the information that's used in Sales Reports, on the Retail Tenders Chart.
    

## SCIS Sales by Department {#bridgehead_4395029020}

-   **ID**: customsearch\_ns\_pos\_sales\_by\_department
    
-   **Description**: Returns the information that's used in the Sales Report, on the Sales by Class Chart.
    

## SCIS Daily Sales {#bridgehead_4395029059}

-   **ID**: customsearch\_ns\_pos\_daily\_sales
    
-   **Description**: Returns the Net Sales information that's displayed on the Sales Report in the SuiteCommerce InStore User Menu.
