Sales associates use a Personal Identification Number (PIN) to log in to Fallback. Administrators or Store Managers need to share the PIN with employees. All employees at a location use the same PIN.

#### To log in to Fallback:

1.  To log in to Fallback, you can use:
    
    -   The login window
        
    -   The Fallback option in the **User menu**
        
2.  Enter the PIN.
    
3.  Tap **Enter Fallback**.
    
4.  The Fallback shopping cart displays, and you can start entering orders in Fallback.
    

Tip:

Although you can use Fallback at any time by selecting the option in the **User menu**, you should use Fallback only when SCIS isn't available.
