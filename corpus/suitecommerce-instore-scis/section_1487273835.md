In SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS), store managers and sales associates can override an order subtotal. The price override capability lets you increase the amount of the order. Administrators don't need to do any additional configuration to support the price override capability.

To apply a price override or markup in SCIS, the sales associate clicks the amount in the line item in the cart or the order subtotal. They can then use the custom discount dialog to increase the amount. For more information, see [Applying a Markup or Price Override](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3971553854.html#bridgehead_4708548123).
