If you have multiple devices using SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS), consider creating a shortcut for quick access to the SCIS Mobile Device list in your NetSuite account.

#### To create a shortcut to the SCIS Mobile Device list:

1.  Go to Commerce > InStore > Mobile Devices.
    
2.  Click the **Add To Shortcuts** link in the Shortcut menu.
    

The Shortcut portlet on your dashboard shows a link to the list of mobile devices associated with your account.
