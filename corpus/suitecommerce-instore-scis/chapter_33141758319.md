SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS) 2023.2 and later include the components to activate SCIS 2.0. You can enable mobile devices one at a time for testing to ensure SCIS 2.0 meets your needs.

SCIS 2.0 activates by enabling SuiteScript 2.0 in your account and on each device:

1.  Enable SuiteScript 2.0 in your NetSuite ERP account (one-time setup)
    
2.  Enable SuiteScript 2.0 on a mobile device
    

#### To enable SuiteScript 2.0 in your NetSuite ERP account: {#procedure_0720022913}

1.  In NetSuite ERP, go to Commerce > InStore > (Global Settings) Settings.
    
    ![Commerce tab InStore choice for SCIS settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SCIS/SCIS_Commerce_InStore_Settings_tab.png)
2.  Click **Edit** for the Settings record you use.
    
    If you have more than one Settings record, you can ask Customer Support which record to edit.
    
3.  Open the **Order** subtab.
    
4.  Check the **Use SuiteScript 2.0** box.
    
    Tip:
    
    Checking the SuiteScript 2.0 box doesn't activate SCIS 2.0, it only enables it.
    
    This one-time setup stays active for all mobile devices that you enable for SCIS 2.0 later.
    
5.  Click **Save**. Now you can activate any device associated with your NetSuite SCIS account to use SCIS 2.0.
    
    Note:
    
    **Optimize EMV** is hidden after checking the SuiteScript 2.X box. The optimize option isn't required under SCIS 2.0. You don't have to wait for payment to be processed in NetSuite before printing a receipt. Instead, the entire process is 'optimized.'
    

#### To enable SuiteScript 2.0 on a mobile device: {#procedure_0907115609}

1.  Open SICS on the mobile device.
    
2.  Log in as an **Administrator**.
    
3.  Tap the SCIS menu and choose **About**.
    
    ![SCIS Menu](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SCIS/SCIS_Mobile_Device_Menu.png)
4.  Open the **Current Session Info** tab.
    
    Tap the **Use SuiteScript 2.0** option near the bottom.
    
    ![Session information tab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SCIS/SCIS_Mobile_Device_Session_Info.png)
    
    The device is now running SCIS 2.0. You can close the device menu page.
    
    Note:
    
    After enabling SuiteScript 2.0, the option will change to **Use SuiteScript 1.0**.
