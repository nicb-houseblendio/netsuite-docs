In SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS), you can add an existing customer to an order or add a new customer while creating an order. To do this, tap the Add Customer link in the cart, then start typing the customer's name. If there's a match in the system, the customer's name is displayed. You can select the customer to add them to the order. If there is no match, or you don't see the customer you want, tap the link to add new customer information.

#### Adding an existing customer to a transaction:

1.  In the SCIS shopping cart, tap **Add Customer**.
    
2.  Enter the name of an existing customer. If there is a match in the system, you can select the customer you want to add to the order.
    
    To remove the customer on the order, touch or tap the **X**.
    
3.  (Optional) Tap **New Customer** to enter information to create a new customer in the system. The **Add New Customer** dialog window appears.
    
    1.  Decide whether the customer should be recorded as an **Individual** or as a **Company**.
        
    2.  Tap the **Required**, then enter the customer information as necessary.
        
    3.  (Optional) Tap the **Optional**, then enter information as necessary.
        
4.  Tap **Create and Add** to save the new customer information and add the customer to the transaction in the cart.
