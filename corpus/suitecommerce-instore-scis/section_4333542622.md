Sales transactions are the core of SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS). The general workflow for entering transactions in SCIS is:

1.  Choose or add a customer (optional).
    
2.  Add items to the order.
    
3.  Add a discount (if applicable).
    
4.  Accept payment.
