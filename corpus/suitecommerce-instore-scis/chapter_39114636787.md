Use this checklist to plan and upgrade your devices to SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS) 2.0.

1.  Review the SCIS 2.0 release documentation:
    
    -   [SCIS 2.0 Reliability, Performance, and Refactored Processing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_162204598144.html)
        
    -   [SCIS 2.0 Payment Processing Comparison With SCIS 1.0](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_162204605085.html)
        
    -   [SCIS 2.0 Frequently Asked Questions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_44130453170.html)
        
    -   [SCIS 1.0 to 2.0 Upgrade Checklist](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_39114636787.html)
        
    -   [SCIS 2.0 Enabling a Mobile Device](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_33141758319.html)
        
    
    These documents are available in the Help Center and as PDFs. We're also updating the administrator and user guides for SCIS 2.0.
    
2.  Review your scripts, customizations, and saved searches, and make changes if needed.
    
    NetSuite Support (CS), Advanced Customer Support (ACS), or Professional Services (PS) can help determine what, if any, changes to your environment might be needed.
    
3.  Contact all partners integrated with your NetSuite/SCIS environment to ensure their services are compatible with your SCIS 2.0 workflows.
    
4.  Enable SCIS 2.0 in a test environment and test your most common use cases.
    
    NetSuite Support (CS), Advanced Customer Support (ACS), or Professional Services (PS) can help you with your test plans.
    
5.  To minimize risk, start your update by enabling SCIS 2.0 for only a few devices.
    
6.  Add more devices when you are confident that SCIS 2.0 is performing in your environment as expected.
