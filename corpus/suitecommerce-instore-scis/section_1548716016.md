You may need to create a new entry in the list of SCIS Mobile Certificates records for SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS). Creating a new entry may be required to store authorizing certificates for a new domain associated with SCIS. Follow these instructions.

#### To create a new SCIS Mobile Certificate entry:

1.  Go to _Customization > Lists, Records & Fields > Record Types_
    
2.  Scroll to **SCIS Mobile Certificates** and click **New Record**.
    
3.  Enter the domain in the **Name** field.
    
4.  Copy and paste the fingerprint hash in the **Certificate Fingerprint** field.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
