This role is only used with Advanced Cash Drawer Management. Employees with the SCIS Store Manager role can view shifts for all sales associates at the store. The Store manager can close shifts for other employees. This capability is useful if a sales associate is called away unexpectedly and is unable to close a shift on their own.

Important:

Before closing a sales associate's shift, the SCIS Store Manager must ensure that the sales associate has stopped entering orders. If there is an order in progress when the store manager closes a shift, one order could be split across two different shifts. This could impact the closing drawer transaction.

#### To allow the SCIS Store Manager role to view and close shifts:

1.  Go to Commerce > InStore > Shifts.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to **Store\_Manager**.
    
3.  In the **Authorized Roles** list, select **SCIS Store Manager**.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
