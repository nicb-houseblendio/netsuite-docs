## SCIS Store Safe Account {#bridgehead_1503105061}

-   **ID**: custrecord\_ns\_pos\_location\_safeaccount
    
-   **Description**: Account used for tracking the amount in the store safe. You must define a store safe account for each point-of-sale location. Set this field on the Location record.
    

## SCIS Cash Drawer Difference {#bridgehead_1503105145}

-   **ID**: custrecord\_ns\_pos\_location\_diffaccount
    
-   **Description**: A custom field on location records. For each location that uses SCIS, you must select the account to use when there's a discrepancy between the amount in the cash drawer and the amount entered in SCIS for the Closing Drawer transaction.
    

## SCIS Register {#bridgehead_1503105754}

-   **ID**: customrecord\_ns\_pos\_registers
    
-   **Description**: (Required for Advanced Cash Management) A custom record that must be configured for each cash drawer and printer combination used at the retail store.
    

## SCIS Shift {#bridgehead_1503105800}

-   **ID**: customrecord\_ns\_shifts
    
-   **Description**: (Advanced Cash Management) A custom record that's created automatically each time a sales associate opens a shift in SCIS.
