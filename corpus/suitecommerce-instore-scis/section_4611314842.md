You can update a customer record in SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS) by editing the Customer Profile. Note that any changes you make in SCIS are also recorded in NetSuite.

#### To update customer information:

1.  Search for the customer whose data you want to view.
    
    1.  In the search bar, type the first few letters of the customer's name.
        
    2.  Tap the **Customers** list to view the customer search results.
        
2.  Tap the customer name. The customer profile displays.
    
3.  Tap **Edit Profile**.
    
4.  Edit the customer information as required.
    
5.  Tap **Save** to save the customer profile. SuiteCommerce InStore displays the updated customer profile and updates the customer record in NetSuite.
