Employees entering transactions in SCIS, must use the SCIS Clerk role, or a role configured using the SCIS Clerk role as a template. Removing any permissions that are already configured on an SCIS role can prevent employees from performing basic tasks.

## Role-Based Permissions for SCIS Clerk {#subsect_1516912191}

The following table includes a list of permissions required on the SCIS Clerk role. If you use a custom role, go to Setup > Manage Roles, and verify that the role you use for SCIS includes all of the following permissions.
