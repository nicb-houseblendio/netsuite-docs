In NetSuite, you can view sales reports by using the saved searches included in SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS). The following sales reports are installed by default:

-   SCIS Daily Sales
    
-   SCIS Sales by Department
    
-   SCIS Daily Tenders
    

To modify the data displayed in NetSuite, you must first make a copy of an SCIS saved search. Then, create your own custom search to meet your business needs. For example, you may want to create a saved search that shows SCIS sales for each month. To do this, copy one of the saved transaction searches installed by SCIS. Next, set the date filter, and then save a report for each month.

#### To create a sales report for each month:

1.  Follow the instructions for [Modifying Included Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4401961202.html).
    
2.  On the criteria subtab, select **Date Created**, and then select the first and last day of the month.
    
3.  Click **Save As**.
