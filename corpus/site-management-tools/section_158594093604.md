You can copy any of the default skins and customized skins from the Theme Skin Manager.

Note:

To make edits to a default skin, you first need to make a copy of it and then you can make any desired changes. For details, see [Editing a Theme Skin](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158497653583.html).

#### To copy a theme skin:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Click **Settings** and then **Themes**.
    
3.  Select the **Ellipsis** beside the skin you want to copy and click **Copy**.
    
4.  The skin now appears in the **Customized Theme Skins** list.
    

After copying a skin, you can edit it and activate it when you are ready. For details, see [Editing a Theme Skin](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158497653583.html) and [Activating a Theme Skin](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158764877898.html).
