If you have unpublished content and decide to remove it instead of publishing it, you have two options:

-   Discard the Content
    
-   Expire the Content
    

Discarding content is the best way to remove unpublished content you don't want.

#### To discard content in Site Management Tools:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Click the Review & Publish Changes icon ![Review and Publish Changes Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SiteManagement/ReviewAndPublishChangesIcon.png) on the site management toolbar.
    
3.  From the Unpublished Changes list, select the content you want to discard.
    
4.  Click **Discard**.
    

#### To expire content in Site Management Tools:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Go to the page with the content you want to expire.
    
3.  Hover over the content and click **X**.
    

When you do this for unpublished content, its visibility end date is set to now. It won't show on the site, but stays in the **Unpublished Changes** subtab. To remove it completely, either publish it or select and discard it.
