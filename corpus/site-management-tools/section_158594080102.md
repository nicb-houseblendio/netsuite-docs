To use the Theme Skin Manager in Site Management Tools (SMT), your domain and theme must meet the following requirements:

-   Your account must be using SMT version 3. To check your version of SMT, see [Site Management Tools Versions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4806124052.html).
    
-   Your web store must use SuiteCommerce and the 2019.2 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced or later.
    
-   The theme must have skins.
