The Theme Skin Manager lets you preview and manage the skins associated with the active theme on your domain. A skin is a set of complimentary elements in a theme. You can preview any of the skins included with the theme, copy any of the default skins, and customize the copies however you like. This lets you keep the overall look and feel of the theme, and apply skins when needed.
