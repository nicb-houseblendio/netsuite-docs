With Site Management Tools (SMT), you can assign items to categories and subcategories and manage them easily. This includes:

-   Adding new items to the category
    
-   Changing the order of items
    
-   Removing the items from a category
    

Any changes you make to item assignments in SMT show up on the **Items** subtab in the NetSuite commerce category record. Likewise, any item management you perform in NetSuite is reflected in SMT.

To learn more, explore the following help topics:

-   [Assigning Items to a Category or Subcategory](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4677264841.html)
