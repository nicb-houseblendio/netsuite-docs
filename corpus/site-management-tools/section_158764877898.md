An active skin displays a blue checkmark with the words **Active on site** next to the **Ellipsis**. When you activate a skin, it goes live immediately. You can quickly change the appearance of your domain simply by activating a different skin.

You can't activate any of the default skins included with the theme. Instead, make a copy and then activate it from the Customized Theme Skin list. For details, see [Copying a Theme Skin](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158594093604.html).

#### To activate a theme skin:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Click **Settings** and then **Themes**.
    
3.  From the **Customized Theme Skins** list, click the **Ellipsis** beside the skin you want to make active.
    
4.  Select **Set to Active**.
    
5.  In the confirmation popup, click **Yes**.
