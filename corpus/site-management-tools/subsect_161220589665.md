The Theme Skin Manager shows the default skins that are included with the active theme on your domain. Preview the skins to view the style elements, such as fonts and colors.

#### To preview a theme skin:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Click **Settings** and then **Themes**.
    
3.  Select the **Ellipsis** beside the skin you want to copy and click **Preview**.
