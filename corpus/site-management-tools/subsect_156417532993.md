Click the Preview Mode icon ![Preview Mode Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SiteManagement/PreviewModeIcon.png) on the site management toolbar to see how your site looks. If you use SuiteCommerce or the 2019.2 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced or later, preview mode lets you view your website as individual customer groups view it. Select **Advanced Preview** and then select the customer segment from the side panel. For details, see [Personalized Catalog Views](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557154874.html). The following screenshot shows the advanced preview settings:

![Site Management Tools Preview Mode](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SiteManagement/SiteManagementToolsPreviewMode.png)
