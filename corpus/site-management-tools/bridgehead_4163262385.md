Note:

Site Management Tools (SMT) doesn't currently support SAML SSO for SMT users.

Use one of the two following options to log in to SMT:

## Log into NetSuite to Access Site Management Tools {#bridgehead_4176184362}

Log in to your NetSuite account using a role with the Site Management Tools permission. Go to your website and press the Esc key, if that feature is enabled, or use the SMT administrator URL. For more information about the Esc to Login feature and the SMT administrator URL, see [Configuring Access to Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4787945118.html).

## Log into Site Management Tools From Your Website {#bridgehead_4176215752}

Log in to SMT from your website with your NetSuite user name and password. SMT checks which roles you're assigned. If you only have one role with SMT permissions, you're automatically signed into that role. If you have more than one role with SMT permissions, a list appears where you can choose the role you want to use. If you don't have a role with SMT permissions, you'll see a message letting you know you don't have a role with the proper permissions to manage content.

Note:

To sign out of SMT, select **Sign Out** from the dropdown menu. When you sign out of SMT you're also signed out of your NetSuite account.
