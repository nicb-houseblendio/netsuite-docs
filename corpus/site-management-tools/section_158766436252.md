You can only delete inactive custom skins.

#### To delete a theme skin:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Click **Settings** and then **Themes**.
    
3.  From the **Customized Theme Skins** list, click the **Ellipsis** beside the skin you want to delete.
    
4.  Click **Delete**.
    
5.  In the confirmation popup, click **Yes**.
