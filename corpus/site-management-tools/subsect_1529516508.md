This information refers to SuiteCommerce or the 2018.2 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced or later.

You can decide exactly when a category and the items assigned to it show up on your site.

**Example:**

Say you're running a spring sale in March. You want the 'Spring Sale' category and its items to show up only for that month. Set the visibility to start March 1 at 12:00 am and end March 31 at 11:59 pm.

After creating the category, add your sale items. When you're done, publish the category, and everything will show up only during the dates you set. For details, see [Adding a Commerce Category](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4672057527.html) and [Create and Publish Content in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1513888447.html).

Other factors can impact how items display outside of this category:

-   If an item is in a category with a future start date, it won't show up until that date
    
-   If an item is assigned to a category that has passed its end date, the item won't display.
    
-   If an item is in a category that has a future start date and a past end date, but is also assigned to a different category that is visible, the item is accessible through that category and also through search.
    

For more information, see [Item Availability](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2656387.html).
