To enable Site Management Tools, your account needs to be using SuiteCommerce or SuiteCommerce Advanced. For details, see [SuiteCommerce Solutions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4411478210.html).

#### To enable SMT:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Web Presence** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Site Management Tools** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
