Copying a subcategory lets you associate the subcategory with other parent categories. Copying a category doesn't create a new commerce category record, instead it creates a new association between the subcategory and another parent category.

#### To copy a category or subcategory:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools (SMT). For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Click the Overview Mode icon ![Overview Mode Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SiteManagement/OverviewModeIcon.png) on the site management toolbar.
    
3.  In the category list, go to the category or subcategory you want to copy. Click the ellipsis to display the **More Actions** menu.
    
4.  Click **Copy**.
    
5.  Expand the parent level category where you want to copy the category or subcategory and click the location where you want to place the copy.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
