Your content stops displaying at its specified end date and time. Content with a past end date and time is referred to as expired content. To expire content without an end date, set its end date and time to now. Or, you can go to the page with the content and click **X** to expire it right away.

If content has a future start date, expiring it sets its end date and time to the current date and time. To remove content from your site, click the Review & Publish Changes icon ![Review and Publish Changes Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SiteManagement/ReviewAndPublishChangesIcon.png) on the site management toolbar. From the Unpublished Changes list, select the content and publish the change to remove it.
