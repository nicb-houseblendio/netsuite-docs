When content reaches its end date or you remove it, it becomes expired content and appears in the Expired Content subtab in overview mode. You can edit or restore expired content to your website.

#### To reuse expired (discarded) or deleted content:

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Click the Overview Mode icon ![Overview Mode Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SiteManagement/OverviewModeIcon.png) on the site management toolbar.
    
3.  Select the **Expired Content** subtab.
    
4.  Select the content you want to reuse. You can restore one or multiple pieces of content. Select the URL row to select all content on that URL fragment.
    
5.  Click **Edit Visibility**.
    
6.  Edit the start and end dates, or remove visibility dates by selecting **Always Visible**. To reuse content, set the end date in the future or remove it. You can also change the start date if you want the content visible at a future date.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    
    The content is now unpublished and you can view it on the **Unpublished Changes** subtab. For details, see [Review Unpublished Changes in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1513888436.html).
