Note:

This information applies to SuiteCommerce or the 2018.2 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced and later.

Because some content might be visible only during a specific time frame, you can preview your site as it would appear to a visitor on a selected date and time. Use the **View Site As Of** date picker on the site management toolbar to choose a date and time to see how your site displays. For details, see [Setting Content Visibility in Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162153815856.html). The following screenshot shows the View Site As Of option on the toolbar:

![View Site As Of Date Picker](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SiteManagement/ViewSiteAsOf.png)

#### To view your site as of a specific date in Site Management Tools:

Note:

To see or edit upcoming published changes, click the Review & Publish Changes icon ![Review and Publish Changes Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SiteManagement/ReviewAndPublishChangesIcon.png) on the site management toolbar and select **Visibility Timeline**. For details, see [View Upcoming Content Using Visibility Timeline](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1515603492.html).

1.  Log in to Site Management Tools. For details, see [Logging into Site Management Tools](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4163262385.html).
    
2.  Select the Preview Mode icon ![Preview Mode Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SiteManagement/PreviewModeIcon.png) or the Edit Mode icon ![Edit Mode Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/SiteManagement/EditModeIcon.png) from the site management toolbar.
    
3.  Click **View Site As Of**.
    
4.  In the date picker, choose the date and time you want to view your webstore, and click **Set**.
