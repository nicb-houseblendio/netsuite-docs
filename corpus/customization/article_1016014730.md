**Issue:**

Dropdown lists are missing expected options, especially in new or complex setups.

**What to Check:**

-   Do test records exist for every expected combination of controlling field values?
    
-   Are all data records active and correctly linked?
    

**How to Fix:**

-   Create test records that cover all combinations of controlling and dependent fields.
    
-   Use this data for thorough end-to-end testing.
    

For more troubleshooting tips, see [Troubleshooting Dependent Dropdown List Issues](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1016111656.html).
