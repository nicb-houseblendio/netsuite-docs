Here are some general troubleshooting steps you can follow if you're having issues with dependent dropdown lists:

-   Clearly name your fields and lists to avoid confusion, for example Vehicle Make (List) vs. Vehicle Make (Record).
    
-   Walk through field type, source, order, filtering setup, and test with verified data.
    
-   Temporarily disable client scripts or workflows during troubleshooting.
    
-   If stuck, resave all fields and the record type, then refresh your browser.
    
-   Before updating production, try the setup in a sandbox first, especially if there are lots of dependencies.
    

For more troubleshooting tips, see [Troubleshooting Dependent Dropdown List Issues](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1016111656.html).
