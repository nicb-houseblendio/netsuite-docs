**Issue:**

After changes to field setup, dropdown filtering doesn't reflect recent updates or old options continue to display.

**What to Check:**

-   Have you cleared your browser cache?
    
-   Have you tried accessing the form in a private/incognito window or after a full logout/login?
    

**How to Fix:**

-   Clear your browser cache and cookies.
    
-   Try the form in a private/incognito window.
    
-   If changes still aren't showing, log out of NetSuite and back in.
    
-   If needed, resave the related fields and record types.
    

For more troubleshooting tips, see [Troubleshooting Dependent Dropdown List Issues](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1016111656.html).
