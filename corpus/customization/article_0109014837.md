For custom objects such as custom record types, custom transaction types, and custom fields, provide detailed information about them in the AI Description field. The AI Description helps AI features understand the purpose, meaning, and business context of your custom objects. These details help them provide more accurate and relevant answers about what the objects are, what they're used for, and how they're used in your business.

Descriptions added in the AI Description field are used by NetSuite AI features.
