This topic describes how to view a custom record entries list. For general information about using custom record entries, see [Using Custom Record Entries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2888872.html).

You can view a custom record entries list for a custom record type.

#### To view a custom record entries list: {#procedure_N2889441}

1.  Go to _Customization > Lists, Records & Fields > Record Types_.
    
    You can also reach the custom record entries list by placing a link directly to the list in your center. For more information, see [Creating Links to Custom Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2880942.html).
    
2.  Click **List** for the custom record entries list you want to see.
    
    Your list of custom records appears.
    
    You can also reach the custom record entries list by placing a link directly to the list in your center. For more information, see [Creating Links to Custom Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2880942.html).
