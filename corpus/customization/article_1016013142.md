**Issue:**

Some users see different dropdown options or fields compared to others.

**What to Check:**

-   Are custom roles or permissions restricting access to certain fields, lists, or record types?
    
-   Are field or record restrictions set at the role level?
    

**Issue:**

-   Test the form as users with different roles.
    
-   Adjust permissions to ensure all required users can access needed options.
    

For more troubleshooting tips, see [Troubleshooting Dependent Dropdown List Issues](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1016111656.html).
