**Issue:**

Filtering works in setup but not when entering data on the form.

**What to Check:**

-   Are the controlling fields placed before the dependent fields on the form?
    
-   Are you testing with the correct form layout?
    
-   Has the form been customized or changed recently?
    

**How to Fix:**

-   Place controlling fields before dependent fields.
    
-   Test by creating a new record and selecting controlling values first.
    
-   If fields were reordered, verify that filtering logic still functions.
    

For more troubleshooting tips, see [Troubleshooting Dependent Dropdown List Issues](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1016111656.html).
