**Issue:**

Dependent dropdown filtering doesn't work as expected, even though configuration seems correct.

**What to Check:**

Are there any client scripts (SuiteScript) or workflows applied to the form or record type that reference the controlling or dependent fields?

**How to Fix:**

-   Temporarily disable or bypass any associated scripts/workflows and test the dropdown filtering again.
    
-   If disabling scripts resolves the issue, review their logic for interference with field values or filtering behavior.
    

For more troubleshooting tips, see [Troubleshooting Dependent Dropdown List Issues](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1016111656.html).
