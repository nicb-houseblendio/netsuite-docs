When you remove filtering from a segment, all of its values become available for selection. Use the following procedure to remove filtering from a segment.

#### To remove filtering from a segment: {#procedure_4383367912}

1.  Edit the custom segment that no longer needs filtering.
    
2.  To remove filtering, clear the **Filtered by** field selections. To clear a selection, hold down the Ctrl key and click a selected field. Note that you may have to use the scroll bars to see all classifications that have been selected.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
