The following record types don't support Class, Department, Location, and Subsidiary filtering:

-   Custom records
    
-   Custom segments
    
-   CRM record types
    
-   Other record types not listed in [Record Types that Support Filtering by Class, Department, Location, and Subsidiary](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_40105008662.html).
    

For general information about filtering segments by Class, Department, Location, or Subsidiary, see [Filtering Segments by Class, Department, Location, or Subsidiary](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_43104707091.html).
