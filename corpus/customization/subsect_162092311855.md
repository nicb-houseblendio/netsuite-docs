This topics in this section provide a high-level overview of the process for creating a custom record type. For general information about custom record types, see [Record Types](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N2824771.html).

In this scenario, we'll assume your company needs to track information about computer and electronics equipment.

Complete the following steps:

1.  [Creating the Equipment Custom Record Type for the Equipment Service Example](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162092326625.html)
    
2.  [Creating the Parent-Child Relationship for the Equipment Service Example](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162092342312.html)
    
3.  [Creating a New Service Record Instance for the Equipment Service Example](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162092352234.html)
    
4.  [Customizing the Form Used to Enter Custom Records for the Equipment Service Example](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162092384856.html)
