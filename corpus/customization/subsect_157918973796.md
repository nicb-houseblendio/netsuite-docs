On the Expenses and Items subtab of a new purchase custom transaction instance, you can purchase items, add expenses, apply Amortization and Landed Costs and, and more. For more information, see [Sales and Purchase Functionality Available in Custom Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156589081289.html).

For more information about creating a new purchase custom transaction instance, see [Creating Sales and Purchase Custom Transaction Instances](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157911898130.html).

#### Complete the following steps as needed:

1.  Click the **Expenses and Items** subtab.
    
2.  Click the **Items** subtab.
    
3.  Add an item to the purchase transaction.
    

See the following topics:

-   [Landed Costs for Expenses and Items on a Purchase Custom Transaction](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_157919042626.html)
    
-   [Amortization for Expenses and Items on a Purchase Custom Transaction](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_157919006834.html)
    

For information about other subtabs available for purchase custom transactions, see [Creating a Purchase Custom Transaction Instance](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157911967123.html).
