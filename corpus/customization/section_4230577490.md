Before you can create custom transaction types, you must enable the feature. To enable the feature, go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_. On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, check the **Custom Transactions** box, and click **Save**.

After you enable the feature, you can begin creating custom transaction types, as described in [Creating a Custom Transaction Type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4172789910.html).
