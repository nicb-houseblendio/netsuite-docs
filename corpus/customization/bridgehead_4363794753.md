After assigning a custom center to a custom role, you can test your custom center. Do this by assigning the new role to yourself and switching to it. For more information about assigning a custom center to a role, see [Assigning a Custom Center to a Custom Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4363781223.html).

#### To test a new custom center:

1.  Go to _List > Employees > Employees_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to your name.
    
3.  Click the **Access** subtab.
    
4.  From the **Role** list, select the new role that you created.
    
5.  Click **Add**.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
7.  Switch to your new role. For information, see [Roles and Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N295370.html). The dashboard for the role appears with your custom center.
    
8.  Test the links in your new custom center.
