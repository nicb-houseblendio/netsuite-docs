You can add a footer to the bottom of your standard transaction forms. Footers can be used to add text and space for a variety of reasons such as:

-   Adding a salutation or company slogan
    
-   Familiarizing customers with disclaimers, company policies, or procedures
    
-   Providing space for an approval signature
    

#### To add a disclaimer to standard transaction forms: {#procedure_N2874508}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Preferences > Printing & Fax_.
    
2.  Click the **Printing** subtab.
    
3.  Enter a disclaimer or other message in one or more of the disclaimer fields. Disclaimers can have up to 4000 characters and appear at the bottom of the form indicated.
    
    -   **Sales Form Disclaimer** - Standard sales forms.
        
    -   **Purchase Form Disclaimer** - Standard purchase forms.
        
    -   **Statement Disclaimer** - Standard statements.
        
    -   **Payment Receipt Disclaimer** - Standard payment receipts.
        
    -   **Packing Slip Message** - Standard packing slips.
        
    -   **Return Policy** - Return forms.
        
    -   **Remittance Slip Message** - Remittance slips.
        
4.  When you've finished, click **Save**.
    

For the disclaimers to appear, you must print your forms using HTML. To set this preference, go to _Home > Set Preferences_. On the Transactions subtab, check the **Print Using HTML** box and click **Save**.

Disclaimers defined aren't used in printed forms based on advanced PDF/HTML templates. To include disclaimers on this kind of printed form, you can add disclaimer text to the template on which the printed from is based.

You can also add disclaimers to specific forms using transaction form customization. For more information about customizing transaction forms, see [Custom Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2852749.html).
