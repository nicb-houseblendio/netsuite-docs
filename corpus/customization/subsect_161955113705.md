The following procedure describes how to apply role-based access restrictions to a custom record if the field already exists.

For more details about setting role-based restrictions, see [Applying Role-Based Restrictions to Custom Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2880594.html).

Note:

Applying center-based restrictions to custom records is similar to applying role-based restrictions to custom records. For more information about how you can prevent sensitive data from being displayed to unauthorized contacts, see [Limiting Search Access to Custom Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2880332.html).

#### To apply role-based access restrictions to a custom record if the field already exists: {#procedure_160693155876}

1.  On the **Fields** subtab of a custom record definition page, click the field name.
    
2.  On the field definition page, check the **Apply Role Restrictions** box and click **Save**.
