On the Billing subtab of a new sales custom transaction instance, you can apply gift certificates and installments, as well as perform other billing-related tasks like setting terms, adding billing addresses, and so on.

See the following topics:

-   [Gift Certificates for Billing on a Sales Custom Transaction](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_157918844485.html)
    
-   [Installments for Billing on a Sales Custom Transaction](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_157918901684.html)
    

For more information about creating a new sales custom transaction instance, see [Creating Sales and Purchase Custom Transaction Instances](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157911898130.html).

For information about other subtabs available for sales custom transactions, see [Creating a Sales Custom Transaction Instance](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157911961960.html).
