**Issue:**

Custom fields (for example, Vehicle Make, Vehicle Model, Vehicle Selection) don't show up on the intended form.

**What to Check:**

-   Did you add the fields to the form layout (Fields subtab for custom records, Screen Fields/Columns for transactions)?
    
-   Are the fields set to Show on the form?
    
-   Are you editing and using the right form (if multiple forms exist)?
    
-   Are fields hidden by role-level or conditional display logic?
    

**How to Fix:**

-   Edit the form layout and check the Show box for each field.Ensure fields are present on all required or relevant forms.
    
-   Move fields to correct section or subtab as needed.
    
-   Adjust field display logic and role-based visibility as required.
    

For more troubleshooting tips, see [Troubleshooting Dependent Dropdown List Issues](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1016111656.html).
