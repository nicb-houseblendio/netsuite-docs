This topic describes how to assign custom segment permissions. For an overview of granting roles permission to manage custom segments, see [Granting a Role Permission to Manage Custom Segments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4313465560.html).

Use the following procedure to assign the Custom Segments permission to a role.

#### To assign the Custom Segments permission: {#procedure_4375568459}

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_.
    
2.  Find the role you want to change and click **Edit** or **Customize**.
    
3.  On the **Permissions** subtab, click **Setup**.
    
4.  Do one of the following:
    
    -   To grant the role access, add a line to the sublist: In the **Permission** column, set the list to **Custom Segments**. In the **Level** column, select the appropriate access level. Then click **Add**.
        
    -   To remove a role's existing access, find the Custom Segments permission in the sublist. Click it to enable a series of buttons. Then click **Remove**.
        
    -   To change the role's existing access, find the Custom Segments permission in the sublist. Edit the corresponding value in the **Level** column. Then click **OK**.
        
5.  Click **Save**.
