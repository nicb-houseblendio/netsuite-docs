**Issue:**

Dropdown lists or filter options didn't update after changing the field's source.

**What to Check:**

-   Have you resaved all related fields and the record type after making changes?
    
-   Have you cleared your browser cache or tried using a private/incognito window?
    

**How to Fix:**

-   After changing a field source, resave the field, any dependent fields, and the record type or form.
    
-   Refresh your browser or log out and back in if changes don't appear immediately.
    
-   Clear cache or try a different browser window to rule out UI caching delays.
    

For more troubleshooting tips, see [Troubleshooting Dependent Dropdown List Issues](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1016111656.html).
