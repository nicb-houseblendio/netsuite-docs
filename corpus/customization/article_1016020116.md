**Issue:**

Dropdown-driven fields don't filter correctly or show unexpected results in SuiteAnalytics or saved searches.

**What to Check:**

-   Are the related fields included in the dataset or saved search criteria?
    
-   Are permissions for those fields correct in reporting tools?
    

**How to Fix:**

-   Ensure all controlling and dependent fields are included in reports or searches.
    
-   Review SuiteAnalytics dataset settings and permissions.
    

For more troubleshooting tips, see [Troubleshooting Dependent Dropdown List Issues](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1016111656.html).
