For each custom transaction type you create, you must choose a transaction style. The style determines how instances of the transaction type will appear and behave, how users will interact with transaction instances, and how they will affect your general ledger. After a transaction type has been saved, you can't change its style. For this reason, you may find it useful to review this section prior to creating your custom transaction types.

The available transaction styles include the following:

-   [Basic](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4210694826.html)
    
-   [Journal](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4210694937.html)
    
-   [Header Only](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4210695023.html)
    
-   [Sales](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1546599788.html)
    
-   [Purchase](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1546599828.html)
