This topic describes how to define the subtab on the parent record where the child record appears. For a general overview of establishing parent-child relationships, see [Establishing a Parent-Child Relationship](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N2885814.html).

On the child field definition page, click the Display subtab. Then on the Parent Subtab field, enter of the subtab where the child record appears.
