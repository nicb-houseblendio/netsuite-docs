The Online Forms subtab lets you create online entry forms to capture information and create new records from outside of your NetSuite account.

Important:

This subtab isn't available until the record type has been saved.

## To add custom online forms: {#procedure_N2879179}

On the **Online Forms** subtab, do one of the following:

-   To create an online form for this record type, click **New Online Form**.
    
-   To create an online form based on an HTML template, click **New Online HTML Form**.
    
-   To edit an existing online form, click the name of the form.
