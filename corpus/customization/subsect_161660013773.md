This topic describes how to enter translation labels for a custom center. For general information about creating and editing custom centers, see [Creating and Editing Custom Centers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2890334.html).

After you create and save a custom center, you can edit the center to define translated labels.

#### To translate labels in a custom center:

1.  On the **Centers** list page, click **Edit** next to the center you want to define translations for.
    
2.  On the **Translation** subtab, enter the translated labels.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

For more information about translating languages, see [Configuring Multiple Languages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N247147.html).

After you've created a custom center, you can add tabs and links for the center. You can create a custom tab from the following locations:

-   [Creating Center Tabs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2890512.html)
    
-   [Creating Center Categories](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2890916.html)
