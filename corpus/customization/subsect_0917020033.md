After creating the custom record type described in [Creating the Custom Record Type for Steps in Creating Hierarchical Custom Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0917015524.html), you can create the required record instances.

For this example, assume your company has multiple locomotives, each with associated wagons. The following steps create record instances for one locomotive and its wagons.

#### To create the record instances:

1.  From the **Record Types** page, locate **Train Carriage** and click **New Record**.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter **Locomotive 1** (the parent record).
    
3.  Leave the **Parent** field blank.
    
4.  Click **Save & New**.
    
5.  In the **Name** field, enter **Wagon 2** (the first child record instance).
    
6.  In the **Parent field**, select **Locomotive 1**.
    
7.  Click **Save & New**.
    
8.  In the **Name** field, enter **Wagon 3** (the second child record instance).
    
9.  In the **Parent** field, select **Locomotive 1 : Wagon 2**.
    
10.  Click **Save & New**.
     
11.  In the **Name** field, enter **Wagon Last** (the final child record).
     
12.  In the **Parent** field, select **Locomotive 1 : Wagon 2 : Wagon 3**.
     
13.  Click **Save**.
     

The final list of record instances looks like this:

![Train Carriage List table with four hierarchical locomotive and wagon entries.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteCloudCustomizationScriptingWebServices/SuiteBuilderCustomization/LocomotiveExampleList.png)

Next, complete the steps described in [Creating the Custom Field for Steps in Creating Hierarchical Custom Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0917021613.html).
