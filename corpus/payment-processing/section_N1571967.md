To view customer credit card transactions, go to _Transactions > Customers > View Credit Card Transactions (Administrator)_.

For each transaction, the list shows the following information:

-   Date of the transaction
    
-   Transaction type and transaction number
    
-   Name on the credit card
    
-   Type of credit card
    
-   Credit card number
    
-   Status of the charge
    
-   Authorization code of the charge
    
-   Currency amount of the charge
    

Click an item in the Transaction column to open the transaction.

Note:

To see unmasked credit card numbers on records and transactions, such as cash sales and sales orders, you **must** be logged in with a role that has the View Unencrypted Credit Cards permission. An account administrator can grant roles this permission only after contacting NetSuite Customer Support and providing a signed agreement. The View Unencrypted Credit Cards permission may only be granted for project work for a period of 90 days. For more information, see [Payment Card Number Security and Compliance](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1567137.html). Also, you must be in **Edit** mode to see unmasked credit card numbers. Even if you are logged in with a role that has the permission, you won't see full numbers if you are in View mode.
