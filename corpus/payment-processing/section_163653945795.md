Before creating a rebate agreement, you need to create a rebate preferences record to associate to the agreement. Read [Setting Rebate Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163653866086.html).

Refer to the topic [Rebate Agreement Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1231073906.html) to know valid inputs for each field in completing step 4 of the following procedure.

#### To create a rebate agreement:

1.  Go to Rebate Management > Rebate Transaction > Rebate Landing Page.
    
2.  Click the **Receiving** tab if you are receiving rebates or the **Paying** tab if you are paying rebates.
    
3.  Under the **Sale** or **Purchase** subtab, click **Rebate Agreement**.
    
    Aside from the Rebate Landing page, you can also create a rebate agreement by going to Rebate Management > Rebate Agreements > Rebate Agreements > New.
    
4.  Select or enter required information in Rebate Agreement fields: See [Rebate Agreement Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1231073906.html).
    
5.  Click **Save** then **Edit** to add rebate agreement details. See [Adding Agreement Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163654409520.html).
    
    To view the list of all rebate agreements, go to Rebate Management > Rebate Agreements > Rebate Agreements.
    
    The created rebate agreement record contains subtabs described in the topic [Rebate Agreement Subtabs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_163966653922.html).
    

Alternatively, you can import rebate agreements into NetSuite using CSV import. See [Importing Rebate Agreements Using a CSV File](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0222030923.html).
