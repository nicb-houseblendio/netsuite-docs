You can override the system-suggested best deal to select a different rebate deal.

#### To select a rebate manually:

1.  From the transaction line, scroll to the right side of the page to locate the **View Applicable Rebates** column.
    
2.  Under the **View Applicable Rebates** column, click the box to open the Applicable Rebates popup window.
    
3.  (Optional) Filter the applicable rebates by payment type. The options include **All**, **Paying**, and **Receiving**. **All** is selected by default.
    
4.  Review and compare the details in the **Stackable**, **Amount** and **Price Pass-through Value** columns.
    
5.  Review the values in the rebate information section:
    
    -   **Actual Gross Amount** - This field shows the actual gross amount indicated on the line item.
        
    -   **Price Pass-through Value** - This field shows the price pass-through amount based on the selected rebate agreement.
        
    -   **Adjusted Sale Amount** - This field shows the amount after deducting the price pass-through value from the actual gross amount.
        
    -   **Item** - This field shows the line item the selected rebate applies to.
        
6.  Check the box under the **Apply** column to select the rebate deal.
    
7.  Click **Submit**.
