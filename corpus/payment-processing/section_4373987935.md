When a customer payment has a payment hold or is waiting to be validated outside of NetSuite, the payment is saved with the Unapproved Payment status.

You can find a list of unapproved customer payments on the Unapproved Customer Payments Register page at _Setup > Accounting > Manage G/L > Chart of Accounts_.

For more information on approving customer payments, see [Approving Customer Payments that Have a Payment Hold](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4373993906.html) and [Approving Online Customer Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1294151.html).
