In Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp the following roles are segregated with different permission levels to avoid frauds and misuse of the SuiteApp by different users. These roles have their own abilities and permission restrictions:

-   **EP Configurator** - This role is used to setup and configure electronic payments preferences and settings
    
-   **EP Processor** - This role is used only to submit payments for processing.
    
-   **EP Approver** - This role is used only to approve batch payments when required.
