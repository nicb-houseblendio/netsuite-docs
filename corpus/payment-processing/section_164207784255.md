Create non-inventory items or other charge items so you can associate them to a settlement method. Ensure that settlements do not impact your inventory.

The accounting items you create will display in the dropdown list of the **Accounting Item** field on the rebate agreement.

You need to create the following item records:

-   Other Charge Resale Item
    
-   Other Charge Sale Item
    
-   Other Charge Purchase Item
    
-   Non-inventory Resale Item
    
-   Non-inventory Sale Item
    
-   Non-inventory Purchase Item
    

To create an item record, see [Creating Item Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2166469.html).
