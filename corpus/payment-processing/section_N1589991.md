You can configure the Payments tab to be accessible only to specific roles or users.

#### To set up permissions for the Payments tab: {#procedure_N1590003}

1.  Go to _Customization > Centers and Tabs > Center Tabs_.
    
2.  From the Customs Center Tabs list, click the Edit link next to Payments.
    
3.  Click the **Audience** subtab.
    
4.  Select the users you want to give access to the **Payments** tab.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

For information about NetSuite centers, see [NetSuite Standard Centers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N132466.html).
