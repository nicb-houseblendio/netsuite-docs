Manual settlement covers both manual claims and manual disbursements.

You can manually generate a settlement for any rebate agreement, whether the **Auto Claim** or **Auto Disburse** box is checked. Before you generate a claim or disbursement, ensure you specify the settlement method on the rebate agreement.

To process a manual settlement, read [Generating Settlements Manually](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_163659120846.html).

## Partial Rebate Settlements {#subsect_164027155156}

You can settle a partial amount even for rebate agreements with auto settlement. The **Auto Settlement** script only processes the remaining unsettled amount after it runs.

For rebate agreements with an outstanding pro forma and a remaining unsettled amount, you can use these settlement processes:

-   Partial settlement
    
-   Pro forma with a partial unsettled amount
    
-   Full settlement
