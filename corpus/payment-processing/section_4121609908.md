The Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp enables businesses in Hong Kong to pay vendor bills, employee expenses, customer refunds, and partner commissions by generating payment files formatted using the ISO 20022 payment file specifications of HSBC.

To set up the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp for your company, subsidiaries, vendors, employees, customers, and partners in Hong Kong, read the following topics:

-   [Setting Up Company Bank Records in Hong Kong](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4121615137.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Vendors in Hong Kong](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4122155750.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Employees in Hong Kong](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4122276771.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Customers in Hong Kong](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4122319412.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Partners in Hong Kong](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4122334982.html)
