The Rebates and Trade Promotions SuiteApp offers complete flexibility in managing which rebate deal gets applied to an item on a sales or purchase transaction.

NetSuite lets you apply rebates in two ways:

-   [Best Deal on Rebates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163654992232.html) - By default, the system selects automatically the rebate agreement with the highest rebate amount and applies it to the line items.
    
-   [Manually Selecting a Rebate](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163655007653.html) - You can override the system-suggested best deal and manually select which rebate to apply using the Applicable Rebates popup window from the transaction record.
    

Additionally, you can choose to use rebates from the latest version of an agreement or from the version that is applicable for the transaction date.

The system selects a new best deal if the previous best deal is no longer the highest or lowest applicable rebate amount for an item. This action follows your setting in the Apply Best Deal field of the Rebate Preferences record.

For more information, see [Rebates on Edited Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163658963343.html).
