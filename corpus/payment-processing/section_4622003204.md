You can assign a user who is responsible for managing the chargeback.

#### To add an assignee to a chargeback:

1.  Go to Transactions > Sales > Chargebacks > Lists.
    
2.  From the Chargeback list, click the **Edit** link next to the chargeback that you want to write off.
    
3.  In the **Assignee** field, select a user that will be responsible for the chargeback.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

To assign yourself to a chargeback click the **Sign Me Up** button.
