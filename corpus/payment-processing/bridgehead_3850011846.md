Electronic Bank Payments supports the following positive payment formats:

Note:

Positive Pay templates BoA/ML, RBC, and SVB - CDA are available for all countries, even without the Electronic Bank Payments license. You can also customize or create new Positive Pay file formats without a license. For information about creating Positive Pay formats, see [Creating a New Custom Payment File Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4002284424.html).

-   BoA/ML (Bank of America Merrill Lynch)
    
-   RBC (Royal Bank of Canada)
    
-   SVB - CDA (Silicon Valley Bank - Controlled Disbursement Accounts)
