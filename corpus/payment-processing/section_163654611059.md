To receive or pay rebates, you can do any of the following to create or enter transactions with rebates:

-   To manually enter transactions, see [Creating a Sales or Purchase Transaction with Rebates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163654836050.html).
    
-   To import CSV data, see [Adding Transactions Using CSV Import](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0912042122.html).
    

Additionally, you can also apply rebates on sales orders created through SOAP integration. See, [Integration Record Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4389727047.html).
