When editing the preferences, follow the maximum allowed limit for both the inclusions and exclusions lists.

Note:

Apply best deal, transaction type, and payment type selections cannot be modified when editing a rebate preferences record.

#### To edit a rebate preferences record:

1.  Go to Rebate Management > Preferences > Preferences.
    
2.  On the Rebate Preferences List, click **Edit** beside the record that you want to edit.
    
3.  On the Rebate Preferences record, you can update the following information:
    
    -   Add or remove customer or vendor attributes
        
    -   Add or remove item attributes
        
    -   Update accounting information
        
4.  Click **Save**.
    
    All changes to the rebate preferences record apply to future transactions, new rebate agreements, and existing rebate agreements with no agreement details.
