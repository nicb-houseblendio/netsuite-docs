The SuiteApp lets you calculate rebates in real time as you enter transactions line by line or after you complete and save an order entry.

Note:

Calculate Rebates at Real-time box does not apply to purchase-related transactions and linked returns.

To calculate rebates in real time, go to Setup > Company > General Preferences. On the **Custom Preferences** subtab, go to **General** field group. Then, check the **Calculate Rebates at Real-time** box. This preference is checked by default

The **Calculate Rebates at Real-time** box is also available on the sales or purchase transaction page. If there are existing line items on a transaction page before you check the box, the rebates are shown only for the newly added line. You need to edit the previous line items to display their calculated rebates.

If you clear the box only on the sales or purchase transaction page, the box remains checked on the General Preferences page. Since this preference inherits the setting from General Preference, the box remains checked in View Mode of a sales or purchase transaction.

This preference does not apply to rebate calculations that use custom fields for calculation basis.
