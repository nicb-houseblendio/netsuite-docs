The following tables contain examples of rebate calculations using different calculation methods, calculation basis, and price pass-through:

-   [Flat Amount](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_164024227091.html)
    
-   [Percent (%) Calculation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_164024234358.html)
    
-   [Fixed Cost](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_164024230508.html)
    
-   [Guaranteed Profit %](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_164024249122.html)
    

Note:

The calculation examples shown below, except for Guaranteed Profit %, are applicable to the item types the SuiteApp supports for both sale and purchase agreements. However, the following columns do not apply to purchase agreements:

-   Gross Profit Amount after Rebate
    
-   Gross Profit % after Rebate
    
-   Actual Gross Amount
    
-   Adjusted Sale Amount
    
-   Price Pass-through
