When you create multiple transactions and use different currencies for each transaction, the rebate value or calculation amount remains the same.

The SuiteApp does not support multi-currency conversion for the calculation amount set on the agreement details. To set a rebate amount relative to the currency and customer on your transaction, you need to create a new agreement details record.
