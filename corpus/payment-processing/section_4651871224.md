Payer authentication using eWay Rapid's 3D Secure service is available for Visa and MasterCard credit cards used by shoppers on your web store. During checkout, shoppers who submit their orders are redirected to eWAY's payer authentication site where they can choose a payment option and enter payment information. After shoppers have finished submitting their payment, they are returned to the Review and Submit page of your web store.

Review the following guidelines when setting up payer authentication for eWay Rapid:

-   Payer authentication through 3D Secure is available as an add-on service. Please contact eWay for activation before setting up your payment processing profile for eWay Rapid Payer Authentication.
    
-   Before setting up payer authentication, see the prerequisites in [Setup Requirements for eWay Rapid Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4651858847.html).
    
-   External checkout of eWay Rapid comes with a built-in integration with PayPal, MasterPass, and Visa Checkout. Please contact eWay for activation of these services. No additional setup process in NetSuite is required.
    

Note:

Payment gateway plug-in supports 3D Secure 2.0 as an add on service by eWay Rapid.

Follow these procedures to set up eWay Rapid payer authentication in your NetSuite account:

1.  [Creating Payment Methods for eWay Rapid](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4651872567.html)
    
2.  [Setting Up Payment Processing Profiles for eWay Rapid](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4651872761.html)
