Builds the customized Bill Payment Processing page. This method must be called after all changes are made. For an example, see [Example Custom Bill Payment Processing Suitelet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0724085756.html).

## Returns {#bridgehead_4000009649}

-   void
    

## Throws {#bridgehead_4448299224}

-   `IPM_NOT_LICENSED` - Thrown when the installed Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp doesn't have a OneWorld license. Logs 'This edition does not support addition and removal of Filters or Columns.'
