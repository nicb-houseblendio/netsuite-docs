Removes an existing (default) field from the Payment Information group of the Invoice Payment Processing page.

## Parameters {#bridgehead_14082718895}

-   `removeField(id)` {string} \[required\] - The field ID of the existing field that you want to remove.
    

## Throws {#bridgehead_41082836535}

-   `EP_API_REMOVEFIELD` - Thrown when a required field is removed.
    
-   `EP_API_REMOVEFIELD_INVALID_ID` - Thrown when id is not provided.
    

## Example {#bridgehead_6084947123}

          `epPaymentSelectionForm.RemoveField('custpage_2663_vendor');`
