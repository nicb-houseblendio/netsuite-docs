Any volume rebates on returns reflect the tier achieved when the transaction took place.

The example below uses Marginal, Volume Quantity, and Rate per Unit configuration on a sales transaction. Based on the **Rebate Value** column, the total item quantity of 6 has a total rebate amount of $60 before recalculation.

| Minimum Tier | Maximum Tier | Rebate Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 | $10 |
| 3 | 4 | $20 |
| 5 | 8 | $30 |

| Transaction | Item Quantity | Rebate Amount (Before Reconciliation) | Rebate Amount (After Reconciliation) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cash Sale #1 | 2 | $20 | $20 |
| Cash Sale #2 | 2 | $20 | $40 |
| Cash Sale #3 | 2 | $20 | $60 |

A return authorization for Cash Sale #1 uses the rebate amount for the highest tier the transaction qualified for. So, a credit memo for this return has $60.

If you do a partial return for 3 items after recalculation, the rebate in credit memo would show $80. Two items multiplied by tier 3 rebate of $30 plus one item multiplied by tier 2 rebate of $20.
