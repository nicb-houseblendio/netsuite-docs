You need to complete the following actions before setting rebate preferences or creating rebate agreement records:

| **Step** | **Related Help Topic** |
| --- | --- |
| 1\. Log in as a NetSuite Administrator. | [Roles and Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N295370.html) |
| 2\. Enable the required features. | [Prerequisites for Rebates and Trade Promotions SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163653688363.html) |
| 3\. Install the SuiteApp. | [Installing the Rebates and Trade Promotions SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163653731704.html) |
| 4\. Configure the roles and permissions. | [Rebates and Trade Promotions Roles and Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163653769849.html) |
| 5\. Create vendor and customer records. | [Creating Vendor and Customer Records for Rebates and Trade Promotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163647652806.html) |
| 5\. Create accounting item records. | [Creating Accounting Item Records for Rebates and Trade Promotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164207784255.html) |
