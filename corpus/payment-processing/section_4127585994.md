The Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp enables your banks to verify check issuances by generating a file format that contains a list of checks issued by your company which the bank can use to deter check fraud-a service known as Positive Pay. The following Positive Pay templates are available:

-   **BoA/ML** - Bank of America Merrill Lynch
    
-   **RBC** - Royal Bank of Canada
    
-   **SVB-CDA** - Silicon Valley Bank - Controlled Disbursement Accounts
    

For more information, see [Verifying Issued Checks with Positive Pay](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1673097.html).

Note:

Positive Pay templates BoA/ML, RBC, and SVB - CDA are available for all countries even without an active Electronic Bank Payments license.
