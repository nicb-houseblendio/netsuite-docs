You can make changes to payments previously applied to invoices on the Customer Payment form.

If you need to change payments applied during a closed accounting period, you need to reopen the closed period. For more information, read [Reopening a Closed Period](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1457543.html).

#### To edit customer payment transactions: {#procedure_N1292903}

1.  Go to _Customers > Accounts Receivable > Accept Customer Payments_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the transaction you want to change.
    
3.  Make the necessary corrections to the transaction.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
