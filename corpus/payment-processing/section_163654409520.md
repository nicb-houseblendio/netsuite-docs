The agreement details record lets you specify the values for each customer, vendor, and item attribute you select on the Rebate Preferences record. One rebate agreement can hold multiple agreement details and displays ten records at a time.

#### To add agreement details:

1.  Go to Rebate Management > Rebate Agreements > Rebate Agreements.
    
2.  On the Rebate Agreements List, click **Edit** beside the rebate agreement that you want to edit.
    
    If you are adding agreement details on a new rebate agreement, click **Edit** after you save the record.
    
3.  Go to the **Agreement Details** subtab and then click **Add Agreement Details**.
    
4.  On the Agreement Details popup window, select or enter the required information in Agreement Details fields. See [Agreement Details Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1231080218.html).
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    
    A corresponding agreement detail ID is populated in the **Agreement Details** subtab to indicate the sequence in which agreement details are created.
    
    If you change the agreement details, those changes apply to future transactions only. You can also remove unused agreement details from the rebate agreement. See [Editing Rebate Agreements and Agreement Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163654496206.html).
    

You can also import multiple agreements details into NetSuite using CSV import. See [Importing Agreement Details Using a CSV File](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0222030950.html).
