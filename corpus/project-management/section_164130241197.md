Before using the Invoice Presentation Template SuiteApp, be aware of the following limitations:

-   The **Invoice Presentation Template** field on an entity record can list upto 160 IPTs that are not assigned to any subsidiary.
    
-   The total file size for all expense receipt files must be 10 MB.
    
-   The expense receipt files must be of type: PNG, JPEG, GIF, or TIFF.
    
-   The IPT Preference Center Link is not translatable.
