You can make a copy from an existing invoice presentation template when you view or edit a template.

#### To make a copy of an invoice presentation template:

1.  Go to Transactions > Sales > Invoice Presentation Templates (Administrator).
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the template that you want to copy.
    
3.  Enter a unique name and other details as necessary.
    
4.  Click **Make Copy**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

You can view the copied template on the Invoice Presentation Template list.
