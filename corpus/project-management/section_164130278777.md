The Invoice Presentation Template List page lists all the invoices presentation templates (IPTs). To view an IPT, you can click **View** next to the template.

To edit an invoice presentation template, perform the following.

#### To edit an invoice presentation template:

1.  Go to Transactions > Sales > Invoice Presentation Templates (Administrator).
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the template that you want to edit.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

If an invoice presentation template has dependent records, you cannot edit the **Subsidiary** field in the template.

Note:

If you have set an IPT as default for a subsidiary and then inactivated the subsidiary, you cannot edit **Subsidiary** field on the IPT. When you click **View Dependent Records**, the IPT preference page will not list the inactive subsidiary.
