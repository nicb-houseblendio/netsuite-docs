#### To view the total invoiced amount from billable items and expenses for a project:

1.  Go to Lists > Relationships > Projects.
    
2.  Click **View** next to the project with the details you want to view.
    
3.  On the project record, click the **Procurement** subtab.
    
    You can view the total invoiced amount from billable items and expenses for the project on the left corner of the subtab. The amount is in the project's primary currency and does not include taxes.
