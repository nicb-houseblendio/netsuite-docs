The IPT SuiteApp enables you to print or email expense receipts along with an invoice. To include receipts to an invoice, check the **Include Receipts** box in an invoice presentation template.

Ensure the following when you include receipts:

-   Expenses are attached to expense reports.
    
-   The IPT **Include Receipts** box is checked.
    
-   Expense receipt files must be in PNG, JPEG, GIF, or TIFF format only.
    
-   The total file size of all expense receipts must not exceed 10 MB.
    
-   The charge or summary type selected on invoice presentation template is **Expense-Based**.
    
-   Invoice should include expense based charges.
    
-   The **Company-Wide Usage** box is checked for each receipt attached to an invoice to print or email.
