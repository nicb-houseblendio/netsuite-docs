-   [Customizing Invoice Forms](#subsect_0723083129)
    
-   [Update Billing Information](#subsect_0723083157)
    
-   [Enabling Features](#subsect_0723085024)
    

## Customizing Invoice Forms {#subsect_0723083129}

To use invoice presentation template features on your invoices, you must customize your preferred invoice form.

#### To customize your preferred invoice form:

1.  Go to Customization > Forms > Transaction Form.
    
2.  Click **Customize** next to your preferred invoice form.
    
3.  Set the following fields:
    
    1.  In the **Printing Type** field, choose **Advanced**.
        
    2.  In the **Print Template** field, select **Invoice Presentation Print Template**.
        
    3.  In the **Email Template** field, select **Invoice Presentation Email Template**.
        
        Warning:
        
        Don't edit the contents of the Invoice Presentation Print and Email templates. Changing them could affect how the IPT SuiteApp works.
        
4.  Click **Save**.
    

## Update Billing Information {#subsect_0723083157}

To use invoice presentation template features on your invoices, you must enable the following in the billing information.

#### To update the biling information:

1.  Go to Set Up > Company > NetSuite Account Information > View Billing Information.
    
2.  Click the **Add-On Modules** subtab.
    
3.  Enable the following:
    
    -   Charge Based Billing
        
    -   Project Accounting and Management
        

## Enabling Features {#subsect_0723085024}

To use invoice presentation template features on your invoices, you must enable the following features.

#### To enable the required features:

1.  Go to Set up > Company > Enable Features.
    
2.  Click the **Company** subtab and check the following boxes:
    
    -   Projects
        
    -   Project Management
        
    -   Multiple Units of Measure
        
3.  Click the **Accounting** subtab and check the following boxes:
    
    -   Accounting
        
    -   A/R
        
    -   Accounting Periods
        
4.  Click the **Transactions** subtab and check the following boxes:
    
    -   Charge Based Billing
        
    -   Billing Operations
        
5.  Click the **Employees** subtab and check the following boxes:
    
    -   Expense Reports
        
    -   Per-Employee Billing Rates
        
    -   Purchase Requests
        
    -   Time Tracking
        
6.  Click the **CRM** subtab and check the following boxes:
    
    -   Customer Relationship Management
        
    -   Mail Merge
        
7.  Click the **SuiteCloud** subtab and check the following boxes:
    
    -   Custom Records
        
    -   Advanced PDF/HTML Templates
        
    -   Client SuiteScript
        
    -   Server SuiteScript
        
    -   SuiteCloud Development Framework
        
8.  On a project record, set the **Billing Type** to **Charge-Based**.
