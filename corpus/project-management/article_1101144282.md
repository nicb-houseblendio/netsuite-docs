The Project Cost-to-Cost Percent Complete SuiteApp is a cost-based percent complete approach to recognize revenue on projects. It provides you the flexibility to define the basis for budget and cost on your projects. It also lets you calculate your project's completion percentage based on the budget and cost.

Watch the following video for information about the Project Cost-to-Cost Percent Complete SuiteApp.

<a id="kaltura_player_102"></a>

## Features {#bridgehead_97102419452}

The Project Cost-to-Cost Percent Complete SuiteApp enables you to:

-   Use a cost-based percent complete approach to recognize revenue on project contracts.
    
-   Define the basis of the cost budget and real cost for the project cost percent complete rule.
    
-   Preview project details and calculations before start.
    
-   Change the percent complete calculation based on your business requirements.
    
-   Provide detailed audit trails for every step.
    
-   Skip extracting and calculating percentages manually.
    
-   Get accurate and up-to-date revenue numbers.
