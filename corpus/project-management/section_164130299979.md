You can delete invoice presentation templates (IPTs) from the Invoice Presentation Template List page. However, the IPTs with dependent records cannot be deleted.

#### To delete an invoice presentation template:

1.  Go to Transactions > Sales > Invoice Presentation Templates (Administrator).
    
2.  Hover over the template that you want to delete.
    
3.  On the new icon, click **Delete Record**.
    
4.  Click **OK** to confirm deletion of the template.
