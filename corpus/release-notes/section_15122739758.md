## 2025.1.30 Minor Release of SuiteCommerce, SuiteCommerce MyAccount, and SuiteCommerce Advanced {#subsect_0718011931}

### Fixes {#subsect_0718012010}

-   Fixed an issue that prevented the Open Invoices list on the My Account area of Commerce websites from updating when a filter option was selected.
    
    This issue has been fixed for SuiteCommerce and the 2025.1 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced.
    
-   Fixed an issue with creating then deleting new product lists (wishlists) in the My Account area of SuiteCommerce and SuiteCommerce Advanced websites.
    
    This issue caused the spinner to get stuck and an error message to appear instead of a confirmation.
    

## 2025.1.20 Minor Release of SuiteCommerce, SuiteCommerce MyAccount, and SuiteCommerce Advanced {#subsect_13123540932}

### Fixes {#subsect_40123553227}

-   The correct facet value is now displayed for the canonical URL.
    
-   The icon next to **Status: Shipped** on the sales order now toggles the package items correctly when clicked.
