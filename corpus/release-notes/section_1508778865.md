Following are the 2026.2 inventory management enhancements to SuiteApps:

## Quality Management Enhancements {#subsect_16125725954}

The Quality Management SuiteApp version 2026.2 includes the following enhancements:

-   [Non-Conformance Register (NCR) and Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA)](#subsect_0601111315)
    
-   [Use Quality Management Tablet with Custom Roles](#bridgehead_0806035816)
    

### Non-Conformance Register (NCR) and Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA) {#subsect_0601111315}

The Quality Management SuiteApp now supports Non-Conformance Register (NCR) and Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) processes. These capabilities help you track quality issues, improve resolution times, and maintain audit-ready records.

This enhancement provides the following capabilities:

-   Create and manage NCR records for failed inspections.
    
-   Track NCR activity by item, lot or serial number, defect type, status, and aging.
    
-   Manage CAPA activities from root cause analysis through verification and closure.
    
-   Record inventory disposition decisions and maintain a complete audit trail.
    

#### Automatic Creation of NCR Record for Failed Inspections {#bridgehead_0806033532}

The Quality Specification Context page now includes the **Required Non-Conformance Register** box. When you check this box, the system automatically creates an NCR record for failed inspection results, helping you to capture quality issues sooner.

#### Submitting NCR Record Creation Requests for Failed QIRs {#bridgehead_0806033614}

You can initiate NCR record creation manually for failed Quality Inspection Reports (QIRs). Use the Create Non-Conformance Register (NCR) page to submit an NCR creation request for failed QIRs that require investigation.

#### NCR and CAPA Processing Workflow {#bridgehead_0806033755}

NCR records support a controlled lifecycle from initiation through verification and closure.

The lifecycle includes the following stages:

-   **Initiated**\- Captures basic non-conformance details
    
-   **Initial Review Completed**\-You can add the NCR title, summary, affected quantity, and inspection level findings.
    
-   **Root Cause Analysis (RCA) Completed**\- Identify the root cause to help prevent recurring issues.
    
-   **Corrective Action Implemented**\- Record corrective and preventive actions, disposition, owner, priority, and due date.
    
-   **Verified & Closed**\- Confirm that all required actions have been completed.
    

#### Use Quality Management Tablet with Custom Roles {#bridgehead_0806035816}

The Quality Management SuiteApp now supports custom roles for the Quality Management Tablet.

To use the tablet with a custom role, map the role on the Quality Management Tablet Role Access List page and assign the required Quality Management permissions.

For more information, see the help topic [Quality Management User Guide](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1503070323.html).
