These PDFs summarize the changes to NetSuite between 2026.2 and the previous release, at the time they were translated. To view the most current release notes in U.S. English, see [NetSuite Version 2026.2](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N3944673.html).
