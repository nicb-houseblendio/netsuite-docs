The following information lists the features and updates associated with SuiteCommerce, SuiteCommerce MyAccount, and SuiteCommerce Advanced. To learn more about how SuiteCommerce releases work, see [Commerce Patches and Upgrades](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4043708822.html).

Important:

All features described in the Commerce Release Notes may not be available in your NetSuite account. Your access to these features is subject to the terms of service in your NetSuite contract. Some features may require extra purchase.
