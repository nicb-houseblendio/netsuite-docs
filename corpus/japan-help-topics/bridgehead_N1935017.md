Consumption tax is tracked by these accounts by default.

| Name | Description | English Description | Tax Account Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 仮受消費税 | 仮受消費税 | Suspense received consumption tax | 消費税 Sale |
| 仮払消費税 | 仮払消費税 | Suspense paid consumption tax | 消費税 Purchase |
| 未払消費税 | 未払消費税 | Consumption tax payable | 消費税 Sale |
