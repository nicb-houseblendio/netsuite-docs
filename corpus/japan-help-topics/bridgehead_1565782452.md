The Purchase Tax by Tax Category report indicates tax amounts for each tax category used on your purchases. The report can be accessed by standard NetSuite Accountant and Accountant (Reviewer) roles that use the Accounting Center.

#### To generate the Purchase Tax by Tax Category report:

1.  Go to Reports > Financial > Purchase Tax by Tax Category.
    
2.  At the bottom of the page, select a date range.
    
3.  (Optional) You can use the **Department** and **Tax Code** fields as optional filters for the report.
    
4.  Click **Refresh** to generate the report.
    

The report includes these details:

-   Tax Category: Main Category
    
-   Document Number
    
-   Net Amount (excluding Tax)
    
-   Tax Code
    
-   Tax Rate
    
-   Amount (Tax)
    
-   Gross Amount (including Tax)
    
-   Tax Category: Name
    
-   Transaction Type
