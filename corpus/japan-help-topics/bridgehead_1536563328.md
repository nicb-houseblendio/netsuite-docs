Japan financial statements have corresponding templates that are included with the Japan Financial Statements SuiteApp. You can access the templates from Reports > Saved Reports and are also available for selection from corresponding fields on the financial statement page. Each template has an English and Japanese version.

The table lists the financial statement templates included in the SuiteApp.

| Financial Statement | Language | Report Name | Layout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Balance Sheet | English | Sample Balance Sheet JP | Sample BS Layout(JP) |
| Japanese | サンプル貸借対照表 | サンプルBSレイアウト（日本) |
| Comparative Balance Sheet | English | Sample Comparative Balance Sheet JP | Sample BS Layout(JP) |
| Japanese | サンプル比較貸借対照表 | サンプルBSレイアウト（日本) |
| Income Statement and Comparative Income Statement | English | Sample Income Statement JP | Sample IS Layout(JP) |
| Japanese | サンプル損益計算書 | サンプルISレイアウト（日本) |
| English | Sample Income Statement JP New | Custom Income Statement Layout(JP) |
| Japanese | サンプル損益計算書詳細 | 損益計算書レイアウト詳細 |
| Cash Flow Statement | English | Sample Cash Flow Statement JP | Sample CF Layout(JP) |
| Japanese | サンプルキャッシュフロー計算書 | サンプルCFレイアウト（日本) |

You can use the Financial Report Builder in NetSuite to customize the financial statement templates. For more information about using the Financial Report Builder to create or customize financial reports, see [Financial Report Builder](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2105415.html).

Note:

If the Japan Financial Statements SuiteApp is upgraded and it includes new financial statement templates, the name and layout of the old templates from the SuiteApp will be overwritten with the new ones. If you customized any of the old templates but retained their original template names, these will also be overwritten. Therefore, you should rename your customized templates to have different names from the old templates before the SuiteApp is upgraded, to prevent them from being overwritten.
