The NetSuite Japan (JP) edition user interface is in Japanese and includes accounting features specific to Japanese business needs. For more information about NetSuite localized editions, including Japan, and setting up accounts and subsidiaries, see these Help topics:

-   [NetSuite Editions Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N270055.html) - Provides information about NetSuite Japan edition.
    
-   [Set up NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N268052.html)
    
    [Creating Subsidiary Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N272471.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Japan-specific Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1522920167.html)
    

When you create a Japanese subsidiary in NetSuite OneWorld, NetSuite adds default Japanese accounts to your chart of accounts. These accounts are based on the Japanese Industrial Standard (JIS) X 0406:1984, and included in NetSuite Japan edition.

For more information, see [Chart of Accounts Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1439850.html).

Note:

The NetSuite for Outlook SuiteApp is **not** supported by NetSuite Japan edition.
