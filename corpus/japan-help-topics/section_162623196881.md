By default, the Administrator, Accountant, and DATR Custom Role can use the SuiteApp and access these records:

-   FAM Assets
    
-   Reporting Information
    
-   Municipal Master
    
-   Municipal Information
    
-   Depreciable Assets
    
-   Municipal Transfers
    
-   Generated report records - Depreciable Asset Report and Depreciable Asset Tax Report
    

The roles can also generate the Depreciable Asset Report and Depreciable Asset Tax Report.

The Administrator and DATR Custom Role can create depreciable asset records from FAM asset records. For more information, see [Creating Depreciable Asset Records from FAM Asset Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162624250665.html).

You can edit the DATR Custom Role to rename it or grant it other permissions. For more information, see [Changing Custom Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N289485.html) and [Setting Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N288727.html).

However, other roles can't be granted access to or usage of the Japan Fixed Assets Reports SuiteApp.
