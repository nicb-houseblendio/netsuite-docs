The Japan Localization SuiteApp includes many features that support different aspects of doing business in Japan. The two main features of the SuiteApp are for Japan tax and invoicing, with these details.

**Tax Features:**

-   Setup for consumption tax, rounding, and preferences
    
-   Consumption tax form completion and generation
    
-   Deductible purchase tax
    
-   Tax reports
    

**Invoicing Features:**

-   Setup for billing cycle and payment terms
    
-   Setup for custom fields, transaction forms, and PDF templates
    
-   Support for Japan Qualified Invoicing System
    
-   Setup for invoice summary record form, template, file naming, storage
    
-   Inclusion of customers, transactions, tax and payment adjustments, discount items
    
-   Invoice summary generation, regeneration, and status tracking
