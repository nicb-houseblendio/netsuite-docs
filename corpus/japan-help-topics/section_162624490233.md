A list of all generated Depreciable Asset Tax Reports can be viewed from Depreciable Assets > Reports > Depreciable Asset Tax Report. The Report Request List page contains all generated reports, with details on the Reporting Year, Subsidiary, Date Created, and Status. Clicking the View link of a report opens generation or compilation record.
