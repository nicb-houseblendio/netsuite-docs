If a depreciable asset is moved to another Municipal, the transfer should be recorded and adjustment in corresponding tax reporting can be made.

## Validations Before Specifying Municipal Transfer of an Asset {#bridgehead_162624199133}

Before specifying municipal transfer of an asset, you should consider these validations:

-   Before creating a Municipal Transfer record, there must be at least one other Municipal Information instance from the same subsidiary as the current Municipal Information.
    
-   Municipal (After Transfer) mustn't be the same as Municipal (Before Transfer).
    
-   Municipal (After Transfer) must be in the same subsidiary as Municipal (Before Transfer).
    
-   Date Transferred must be at a later date than all existing transfer dates of the depreciable asset.
    
-   Municipal Transfer record can't be edited, but can be deleted.
    
-   When saving or deleting a Municipal Transfer record, the Municipal in the Depreciable Asset record must be updated to the Municipal (After Transfer) of the Municipal Transfer record with the latest Date Transferred.
    

#### To specify Municipal Transfer of an Asset:

1.  Go to Depreciable Assets > Lists > Depreciable Assets.
    
2.  On the Depreciable Asset List page, click the **View** link of the asset that was transferred to another Municipal.
    
3.  On the Depreciable Asset page, open the **Municipal Transfer History** subtab and then click **New DATR Municipal Transfer**.
    
4.  On the Municipal Transfer page, specify information in these fields:
    
    -   **Municipal (After Transfer)** - Select the Municipal where the asset was transferred to.
        
    -   **Date Transferred** - Specify the date when the transfer was made.
        
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

If you go to Depreciable Assets > Lists > Municipal Transfers > New, a page is displayed with an Invalid Data banner. Follow the instructions on the page banner.
