Before installing the SuiteApp, these features must be enabled from Setup > Company > Enable Features:

-   On the **Company** subtab, check **Multi-Language** under International.
    
-   On the **Accounting** subtab, check **Accounting Periods**.
    
-   On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, check **Advanced PDF/HTML Templates**.
    

For more information, see [Enabling Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N232138.html) and [SuiteCloud Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N239332.html).

In addition, SuiteScript 2.1 must be enabled to access or view the comparative balance sheet, comparative income statement, and the new trial balance. To do this, go to Setup > Company > General Preferences and select **2.1** in the **Execute SuiteScript 2.X Server Scripts As** field.
