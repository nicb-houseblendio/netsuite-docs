The Japan Localization SuiteApp supports the NetSuite standard customer, vendor and employee records, as well as the transaction forms for invoice, credit memo, bill and expense report.

You can also use your own custom forms for entities and transactions. However, you must add custom fields provided by the Japan Localization SuiteApp to your custom forms.

Add these fields to custom customer forms:

-   Use Invoice Summary
    
-   Due Date Adjustment
    

Add these fields to custom invoice forms:

-   Include in Invoice Summary
    
-   Closing Date
    
-   Invoice Summary Transaction
    

Add these fields to credit memos:

-   Include in Invoice Summary
    
-   Invoice Summary Date
    
    Note:
    
    The Invoice Summary Date field pertains to the Closing Date.
    
-   Invoice Summary Due Date
    
-   Invoice Summary Transaction
    

Add the Due Date Adjustment field to custom vendor forms.

Add the Closing Date field to vendor bills.

Add the Due Date Adjustment field to custom employee forms.

Add the Closing Date field to expense reports.

To add Japan Localization custom fields to your custom forms, refer to these topics:
