The Japanese address form provided by the Japan Localization SuiteApp uses the NetSuite custom address form format.

#### To enable the system to display the Japanese address form on Japanese records:

1.  Go to Customization > Forms > Address Forms.
    
2.  Ddit the custom address form named **NS Japan Address** to assign it to Japan.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

Whenever the Japan Localization SuiteApp gets updated in your account, you will need to assign the form to Japan. The current system behavior is to revert to the default address template each time the SuiteApp is updated in your account.
