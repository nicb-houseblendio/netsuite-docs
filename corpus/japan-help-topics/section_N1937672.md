The Japan Consumption Tax Form is the required tax form starting October 1, 2019. It's available from Reports > Financial > Japan Consumption Tax Form.

Note:

Only the transactions of subsidiaries you've access to will be included or applied when you work on the Japan Consumption Tax Form.

Features of the Japan Consumption Tax Form include:

-   Accessed from Reports > Financial > Japan Consumption Tax Form
    
-   The form or page has 8 subtabs:
    
    -   2 subtabs for main tables
        
    -   4 subtabs for appendices on deductible purchase tax and taxable sales ratio calculation for old tax rate
        
    -   2 subtabs for appendices on deductible purchase tax and taxable sales ratio calculation for current tax rate
        
-   Automatic calculation of deductible purchase tax and taxable sales ratio is integrated in the appendices. Automatic calculation is based on the selected Consumption Tax Form Print Option, on the subsidiary
    
-   Decimals aren't supported
    
-   Compatible with NetSuite-supported browsers
    
-   Uses tax classification for sales transactions
    
-   Uses tax classification and main tax category for purchase transactions
    
-   Uses these saved reports:
    
    -   Purchase by Main Tax Category Report
        
    -   Sales by Tax Classification Report
        

The Japan Consumption Tax Form has 8 subtabs, each containing a table with tax amounts or values that are automatically calculated based on the selected Consumption Tax Form Print Option, on the subsidiary.

Tax Tables 1-3 and 2-3 are required if selected the print option on the subsidiary is **Print Current Standard Rate and Reduced Rate Only**.

If the selected print option is **Print Including Previous Rate**, tax Tables 1-1, 1-2, 2-1 and 2-2 are required.

#### To accomplish and generate the Japan Consumption Tax Form:

1.  Go to Reports > Financial > Japan Consumption Tax Form.
    
2.  If you're using a OneWorld account, select a subsidiary in the **Subsidiary** field.
    
    Note:
    
    The Subsidiary field displays a list of only those subsidiaries that you've access to.
    
3.  In the **Calculation Method** field, select either Itemized or Proportional.
    
4.  In the **Tax Period** fields, select start and end dates.
