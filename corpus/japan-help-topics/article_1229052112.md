On the generated trial balance, you can click **Expand All** button, beside **Financial Row**, to expand all the header accounts and display the account rows under them.

![Trial Balance New Expand All button](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/JFS_TrialBalanceNew_ExpandAll_button.png)

Subtotals are indicated on formula rows.

You can adjust the length of all columns by dragging their line borders.

You can also click the name of an account row to display the **Trial Balance Detail** page on a separate browser tab. This shows a more detailed report about that account.

![Trial Balance Detail Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/JFS_TrialBalanceNew_TrialBalanceDetail_page.png)
