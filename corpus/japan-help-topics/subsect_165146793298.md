You can display the tax rates in the Tax Details section in descending order, in both consolidated\_default\_multi\_currency\_template.xml and consolidated\_default\_template.xml. To do this, replace this line of code:

          `<#list taxdetails.details as taxdetail>` 
        

, with:

          `<#list taxdetails.details?sort_by("rate") as taxdetail>` 
        

The next time you generate an invoice summary, the tax rates in the Tax Details section will be in descending order.
