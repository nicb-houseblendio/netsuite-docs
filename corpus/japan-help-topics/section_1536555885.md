The Japan Financial Statements SuiteApp (Bundle ID 519372) includes these reports:

-   [Japan Comparative Balance Sheet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0306041948.html)
    
-   [Japan Comparative Income Statement](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0306042055.html)
    
-   [Japan Trial Balance New](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0306042140.html)
    
-   [Japanese Balance Sheet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1536564085.html)
    
-   [Japanese Income Statement](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1536564630.html)
    
-   [Japanese Cash Flow Statement](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1536565736.html)
    
-   [Japanese Trial Balance](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157734525306.html)
    

Using the SuiteApp, you can define and generate these Japanese financial statements. The SuiteApp provides two templates for the financial statements, one template in English and another in Japanese.

On each financial statement page, you can specify the saved report or template to use, as well as define the period and subsidiary. For the balance sheet, you can choose to include individual accounts in rows under major sections that you defined in Financial Report Builder. After defining the scope and content, you can then generate the financial statement according to the values you specified. The financial statements can be exported to PDF or Excel.

Note:

You can install Japan Financial Statements SuiteApp to accounts with or without the SuiteTax feature enabled.

Refer to these topics to understand the prerequisites and steps for installing the Japan Financial Statements SuiteApp:

-   [Prerequisites for Installing the Japan Financial Statements SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0322041452.html)
    
-   [Installing the Japan Financial Statements SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1536556188.html)
    

## Limitations of the Japan Financial Statements SuiteApp {#bridgehead_1536560571}

The Japan Financial Statements SuiteApp has these limitations:

-   The SuiteApp is only available in English and Japanese.
    
-   The SuiteApp doesn't support multibook accounting.
