The Municipal record contains information such as the Municipal Name, Code, and Tax Agency.

#### To create a Municipal record:

Repeat the steps to add more Municipal records.

1.  Go to Depreciable Assets > Setup > Municipal > New.
    
2.  On the Municipal page, enter values for these fields:
    
    -   **Name** - Enter the name of the Municipal.
        
    -   **Municipal Code** - Enter the Municipal Code.
        
    -   **Municipal Tax Agency** - Enter the Municipal Tax Agency.
        
3.  Click **Save**.
