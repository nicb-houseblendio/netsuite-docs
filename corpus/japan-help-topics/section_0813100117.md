The Comparative Income Statement and non-comparative income statement can use either of two templates: **Sample Income Statement JP New**, and **Sample Income Statement JP**, either the English or Japanese version.

The Sample Income Statement JP New template includes rows for **Income Tax Expenses**, **Extraordinary Income** and **Extraordinary Expenses**. If you use this template, these rows will be displayed on the Comparative Income Statement, non-comparative income statement, and New Trial Balance. The extraordinary accounts are non-recurring, unusual or seldom incurred gains or losses that aren't part of ordinary, day-to-day operations, but has impact on your company.

If you customize any of the income statement templates, ensure these:

-   the order of account rows in your custom template is the same as that in the sample template included in the SuiteApp;
    
-   the account sections and formula rows must have unique filters; and
    
-   you must provide values to the filters for the account section and formula row.
