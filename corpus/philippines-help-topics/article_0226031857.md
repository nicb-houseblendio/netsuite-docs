Enter the VAT on receivables written off as uncollected during the quarter. This refers to the output VAT previously declared on sales that remain unpaid and are now being claimed as a deduction. Enter the amount shown in the **Amount (Transaction Tax Total)** field of the corresponding transaction in Box 35. For more information, see [How Output VAT is Displayed in Search Results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0226031740.html).

Customize the **Philippines Recovered Uncollected Receivables** saved search to display transactions marked as **Bad Debt** or **Recovered Bad Debt**. For more information, see [Updating Transactions to Write-off or Recover Bad Debt](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0226032242.html) and [Defining a Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N676039.html).
