1.  In NetSuite, go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **Company** subtab, under the International section, ensure the **Multi-language** box (if you're using multiple languages) and **Multiple Currencies** box (if you're using multiple currencies) are checked.
    
3.  On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, ensure all boxes under SuiteBuilder, SuiteScript, and SuiteGL are checked.
