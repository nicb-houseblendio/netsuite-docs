You can include or exclude VAT details in the transaction amount on printed Philippines documents.

#### To include VAT amount details on printed Philippines documents: {#procedure_0418015423}

1.  Edit a Philippines subsidiary record.
    
2.  Click the **Philippines Invoicing** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Print Inclusive of VAT** box to include VAT details in the transaction summary on printed Philippines documents.
    
    Note:
    
    When checked, the Total Sales (VAT Inclusive), Less: VAT, Amount: Net of VAT, Add: VAT, and Total Amount Due are listed. When you clear the box, a separate Tax Amount column for line items is added to the document. Also, the Subtotal, Tax Total, and Total amounts are listed.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

When checked, printed Philippines documents include the VAT breakdown for applicable mixed transactions.
