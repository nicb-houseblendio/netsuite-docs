To use the Philippines Transaction Form Mapping page, customize the role you want to use to configure transaction forms as an Administrator. For the required permission, refer to the following table and add it to your role.

| Philippines Invoicing Feature | Subtab | Permission | Access Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Transaction Form Mapping | Lists | Custom Record Entries | Edit |

For more information about updating your permissions, see the help topics [Setting Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N288727.html) and [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html).
