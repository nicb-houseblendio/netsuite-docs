You can include the Default Tax Registration Number of customers when you print receipts and invoices. In addition, you can also specify the business style of customers in the **Customer Business Style** field, which is only available and applicable to Philippines subsidiaries. For more information, see the following topics:

-   [Specifying Customer's Default Tax Registration Number](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1219040848.html)
    
-   [Specifying Customer's Business Style](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1219040914.html)
