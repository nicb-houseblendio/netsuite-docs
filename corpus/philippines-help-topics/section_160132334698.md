You can customize the Philippines PDF/HTML invoicing templates from Customization > Forms > Advanced PDF/HTML Templates to suit your business needs or instill corporate identity. After customizing a template, you should save or create a new copy of the template with Save As.

#### To customize a Philippines PDF/HTML template:

1.  Go to Customization > Forms > Advanced PDF/HTML Templates.
    
2.  Click the **Edit** link of the template you want to customize.
    
3.  Click **Template Setup** and change the title.
    
4.  Make the changes to the template body as necessary.
    
    Note:
    
    When switching from Source Code to WYSIWYG mode, in previewing, or saving an advanced template, an error may be encountered. You can ignore the error and proceed with saving the template, which will still work well. For more information, see **Template Errors When Saving** under [Saving an Advanced Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156580675374.html).
    
5.  Click the dropdown arrow beside Save, and then click **Save As**.
    

For more information, see [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4453550706.html) and [Advanced Templates Customization in the Template Editor](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2863632.html).
