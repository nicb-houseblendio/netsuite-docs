Accounts with the Advanced PDF/HTML Templates permission can customize printed and emailed transactions. To use advanced templates in your account, see [Enabling the Advanced PDF/HTML Templates Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2862977.html). When the feature is enabled, you can associate advanced templates with custom transaction forms so that these templates are used to format printed and email versions of transactions.

For more information, see [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4453550706.html) and [Advanced Templates Customization in the Template Editor](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2863632.html).
