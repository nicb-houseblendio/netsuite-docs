Perform any of the following actions to send a Philippines document by email:

-   Select **Save & Email** from the **Save** menu as you complete or edit a transaction.
    
    **Save & Email** verifies that the **To Be E-Mailed** box is checked and the field contains an email address. The box and field are located on the **Communication > Messages** subtab of the transaction record.
    
-   Go to the **Communication > Messages** subtab, check **To Be E-Mailed** to send a transaction to one or more specific email addresses.
    
-   Select **Email** from the **Actions** list to send a saved transaction by email in view mode.
    

To learn more, see [Emailing Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N513303.html).
