The system generates Philippines invoicing documents in PDF or HTML format. Depending on your preference, you can create transactions as a printout for immediate printing or send them to your email as an attachment. The following section lists preferences that affect how you create Philippines documents:

-   [Transaction Form Printing Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4453516555.html)
    
-   [Personal Preferences for Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N480873.html)
    
-   [Setting Printing and Fax Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N253916.html)
    
-   [Transaction System Information and Communication Subtabs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N554714.html)
    

You can override set preferences on a transaction record by clicking actions such as **Save and Print** or **Save and Email**.
