The following table lists the standard roles and their capability to print Philippines transactions or send them by email.

| **Standard Role** | **Print or Email Sales Invoice** | **Print or Email Billing Statement** | **Print or Email Credit Memo** | **Print or Email Official Receipt** | **Print or Email Collection Receipt** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OneWorld | Without OneWorld | OneWorld | Without OneWorld | OneWorld | Without OneWorld | OneWorld | Without OneWorld | OneWorld | Without OneWorld |
| A/R Clerk | Yes | No | Yes | No | No | No | No | No | No | No |
| Accountant | Yes | No | Yes | No | Yes | No | No | No | No | No |
| Bookkeeper | Yes | No | Yes | No | Yes | No | No | No | No | No |
| CEO | Yes | No | Yes | No | Yes | No | No | No | No | No |
| CFO | Yes | No | Yes | No | Yes | No | No | No | No | No |
