Printing sales invoices, billing statements, credit memos, and other supported Philippines document types is a native feature of NetSuite. To learn more, see the following help topics:

-   [Printing an Invoice](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1242104.html)
    
-   [Printing a Customer Credit Memo](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1313142.html)
