You can't lock the Philippines PDF/HTML templates and their folder, which you can access from Customization > Forms > Advanced PDF/HTML Templates. When you edit templates directly, the changes will be overridden during the next SuiteApp upgrade. To save your changes, save the edited template as a new copy by clicking Save As.
