NetSuite can also be localized based on the specific country requirements and preferences. For the Philippines, you're setting up the following:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Company Information_ and do the following:
    
    1.  Set **Currency Locale** for the Philippines, with Philippine Peso (PHP) as **Currency Symbol**.
        
    2.  In the **VAT Registration No.** field, specify the Tax Identification Number.
        
2.  Go to _Setup > Company > Preferences > General Preferences_.
    
    1.  Specify the Date and Long Date Format, typically MM/DD/YYYY and Month DD, YYYY.
        
    2.  Specify the time format, typically hh:mm AM/PM.
        
    3.  Specify the number format: 1,000,000.00
        
        For more information about localizing general account preferences in NetSuite, see [Set Company Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N242860.html).
