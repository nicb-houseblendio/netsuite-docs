Enter the VAT on receivables recovered during the quarter that were previously written off as uncollected and for which Output VAT was claimed as a deduction. Enter the amount shown in the **Amount (Transaction Tax Total)** field of the corresponding transaction in Box 36. For more information, see [How Output VAT is Displayed in Search Results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0226031740.html).

Customize the **Philippines Recovered Uncollected Receivables** saved search to display transactions marked as **Bad Debt** or **Recovered Bad Debt**. For more information, see [Updating Transactions to Write-off or Recover Bad Debt](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0226032242.html) and [Defining a Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N676039.html).
