You can include the Default Tax Registration Number of customers when you print receipts and invoices. The number will be indicated in the TIN under the Received From section on receipts and Bill To section on invoices. This is required by the BIR.

#### To specify the Default Tax Registration Number of a customer:

1.  Go to Lists > Relationships > Customers.
    
2.  Among Philippines customers, click the **Edit** link of the record that you want to edit.
    
3.  On the customer record, click the **Financial** subtab.
    
4.  Click the **Tax Registration** subtab, and do the following:
    
    1.  In the **Country** column, select Philippines.
        
    2.  In the **Tax Reg. Number** column, enter the TIN.
        
    3.  Enter other applicable details.
        
5.  Click **Save**.
    
6.  On the **Default Tax Reg** field, select the Philippines Tax Registration Number you want to set as the default on your transactions.
    

For more information, see [Setting Up Entity Default Tax Codes and Tax Groups](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557742165.html).
