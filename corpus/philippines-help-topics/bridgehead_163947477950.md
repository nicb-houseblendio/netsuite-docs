The SuiteApp also keeps track of original and reprints of Philippines transactions. For more information, see [Printing Philippines Invoicing Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161530891415.html).

The Philippines Localization SuiteApp shows the following details in invoicing documents to comply with the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR):

-   [Seller and Buyer Information](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0805090033.html)
    
-   [Transaction Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0805090130.html)
    
-   [Footer Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0805090206.html)
