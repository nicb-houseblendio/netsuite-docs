The Philippines Tax Reports SuiteApp enables accounts with SuiteTax to generate Philippines tax audit files directly from NetSuite. The SuiteApp generates files in the format specified by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) Revenue Regulation 09-2009 and automatically includes required company and period information.
