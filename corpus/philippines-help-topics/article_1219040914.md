You can specify the business style of customers in the **Customer Business Style** field, which is only available and applicable to Philippines subsidiaries.

#### To specify the Customer Business Style:

1.  Edit the customer record that you want to indicate the business style on.
    
2.  Under Classification, in the **Customer Business Style** field, enter the business name registered by the taxpayer.
    
    The business name is different from the registered name and company name.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
