When the Location feature is enabled in your account with a Philippines nexus, the **Print Address on Form** field is added to your location record. This field allows you specify the logo and address that appear on printed Philippines documents.

#### To set the address on printed forms: {#procedure_1103054619}

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Classifications > Locations, then click the **Edit** link of the Philippines location you want to update.
    
2.  On the Locations record, click the **Philippines Invoicing** subtab.
    
3.  On the Philippines Invoicing subtab, click the **Document Setup** subtab.
    
4.  Click **Print Address on Form**, then select the logo and address you want to appear in printed documents. Choose from the following preferences:
    
    -   **Use Company Preferences** - Use details in your company-wide preferences. See [General Printing Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163594333567.html) for more information.
        
    -   **Use Location** - Use details in the location record indicated in the transaction.
        
    -   **Use Subsidiary** - Use details in the subsidiary record indicated in the transaction. For accounts without OneWorld, the **Use Subsidiary** option is replaced with **Use Company Information**. Select this option to use details in the company information record indicated in the transaction.
        
        Note:
        
        **Use Company Preferences** is selected by default. To check existing company preferences, go to Setup > Company > Preferences > Printing & Fax Preferences.
        
5.  Click **Save**.
    

When you print transactions that use Philippines invoicing templates, the selected logo and address will appear.

Note:

You can't change the seller, buyer, and footer details of approved and printed transactions. To ensure BIR-required details such as address details display correctly, update the **Print Address on Form** setup of your subsidiary or location before creating a transaction.
