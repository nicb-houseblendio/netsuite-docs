With the standard Administrator role, follow these steps to install the Philippines Tax Reports SuiteApp from the SuiteApp Marketplace.

#### To install the Philippines Tax Reports SuiteApp from the SuiteApp Marketplace:

1.  Go to SuiteApps.
    
2.  In the **Search** field, enter **Philippines Tax Reports**.
    
3.  Click the Philippines Tax Reports SuiteApp tile.
    
4.  On the SuiteApp details page, click **Install**, then click **OK**.
    
    The installation will start.
    

For more information about installing SuiteApps from the SuiteApp Marketplace, see [Installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539799323.html).
