You can use the Job Details portlet on the SuiteCloud Processors Job Details dashboard to see details for specific deployments. It has a table of performance stats and a timeline.

The Job Details portlet only logs jobs that ran long enough to help you troubleshoot issues. SuiteCloud Processors Job Details doesn't track every job your account runs.

Note:

Be aware of the following:

-   Deployments handled by SuiteCloud Processors are marked as **\- None -** in the Queue column.
    
-   You can't sort script jobs by the **Execution Time** column since it's a summary formula column.
    

The timeline shows how long your scheduled and map/reduce script jobs waited and ran. Hover over any value on the timeline chart to see the stats.
