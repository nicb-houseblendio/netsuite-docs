You can use the Elevated Priority portlet to compare jobs with elevated priority levels to those that kept their original priorities. It shows a simple pie chart with the total job counts.

Note:

Keep an eye on this portlet and the Wait Time by Priority portlet whenever you adjust your processor settings. That way, you can see how changing priority elevation affects wait times and find ways to reduce them.
