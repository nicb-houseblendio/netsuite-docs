You can use the Processor Concurrency portlet to spot peaks and gaps in your processing bandwidth. This portlet shows your concurrency count over time.

The portlet's graph changes based on the time range you pick. It shows a line graph if you choose less than seven days, or a heat map if you pick seven days or more.

![Processor Concurrency portlet.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/AccountAdministration/Performance/APM_SCPM_ProcessorConcurrency.jpg)

Click any point on the heat map to see a detailed line graph for that time.
