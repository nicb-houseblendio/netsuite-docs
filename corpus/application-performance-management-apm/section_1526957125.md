Here's what you'll see in the Overview portlet on the Concurrency Monitor dashboard:

-   **Concurrency Limit** - The maximum number of concurrent web services and RESTlet requests you can run. This value is based on your service level and SuiteCloud Plus licenses for your production, sandbox, and release preview accounts. Web services and RESTlet integrations are rejected if you go over your limit.
    
-   **Peak Concurrency** - The estimated highest number of concurrent requests during your selected date range. It also shows the date and time of that peak.
    
    Note:
    
    The peak concurrency here is an estimate and might differ from the exact value on the Concurrency Details dashboard.
    
-   **Close to the Limit Rate** - How often your requests got close to the concurrency limit. It also shows the range used to define this rate.
    
-   **Over the Limit Rate** - How often your requests went over the concurrency limit.
    
-   **Error Rate** - How often your concurrency requests ran into errors.
