The SOAP Web Services Status Breakdown portlet shows the status of web services operations and record processing with pie charts. You can use this portlet to keep an eye on your web services health and quickly respond to failures or errors. Point to any section of the pie chart to see the percentage and number of requests or records.

![SOAP Web Services Status Breakdown portlet.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/AccountAdministration/Performance/APM_SOAP_WSA_StatusBreakdown.jpg)

You can also click any status in the legend to hide it from the chart.

![SOAP Web Services Status Breakdown portlet without Rejected status.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/AccountAdministration/Performance/APM_SOAP_WSA_StatusBreakdown_Exclusions.jpg)
