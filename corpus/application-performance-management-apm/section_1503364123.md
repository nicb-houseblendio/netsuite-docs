To filter data for all portlets, click **Date Range** at the top right of the page.

![Date Range dropdown list.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/AccountAdministration/Performance/APM_SOAP_WSA_Filters.jpg)

Set the data according to the following filters:

-   **Date Range** - Lets you pick a preset or custom range. Each range has its own resolution, which sets the plot point intervals on the x-axis.
    
    | Date Range | Resolution |
    | --- | --- |
    | 1 hour | 3 minutes |
    | 3 hours | 10 minutes |
    | 6 hours | 15 minutes |
    | 12 hours | 30 minutes |
    | 24 hours (default) | 1 hour |
    | 3 days | 3 hours |
    | 7 days | 8 hours |
    | 14 days | 12 hours |
    | 30 days | 1 day |
    
-   **Integration** - Lets you filter data for a specific web service integration.
    

Click **Apply** to set your filters for all portlets.

Click **Refresh** at the top left to update the data with your selected filters.
