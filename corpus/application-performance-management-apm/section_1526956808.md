You can use the Job Status portlet to compare the number of jobs by status. It shows a simple pie chart of job statuses.

Note:

Keep an eye on this portlet and the Processor Utilization portlet when you tweak your processor settings. That way, you'll see which settings make your processors run efficiently and help jobs finish successfully.
