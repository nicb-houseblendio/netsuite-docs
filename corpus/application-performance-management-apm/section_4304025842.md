By default, Record Pages Monitor shows data for the last 24 hours. You can change the date range by choosing a preset filter or creating a custom date range. To find out when an issue started, set the date range to a longer period.

The resolution value sets the plot point intervals on the x-axis of the data visualizations.

If you want to review performance for different time periods, create custom filters.

#### To add or remove a custom date and time range filter: {#procedure_1488872054}

1.  Go to Customization > Performance > Record Pages Monitor.
    
2.  On the Record Pages portlet, point to the upper right corner.
    
    Icons appear.
    
3.  Click the More icon.
    
4.  Select **Set up**.
    
5.  On the Set up Record Pages window, click **Custom Date and Time**.
    
    -   To add a custom filter, click **Add Date and Time**, pick the start and end dates and times, then click **Add**.
        
        Note:
        
        A custom date and time range filter cannot span more than 30 days.
        
    -   To remove a custom filter you added, click the X icon next to the filter.
        
    -   To remove all custom filters you added, click **Remove All**.
        
6.  Click **Save**. The changes show up in the date filter dropdown, and new custom filters appear at the bottom of the list.
