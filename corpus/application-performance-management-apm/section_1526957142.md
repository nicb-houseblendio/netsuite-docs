Use the Estimated Concurrency Rates chart in the Concurrency Monitor dashboard to see peak concurrency counts and error rates for each hour. Each cell in the chart stands for one hour in your selected date range.

The total number of concurrency counts in this table might be different from what you see on the Concurrency Details dashboard. To give you a fuller picture, Concurrency Monitor might fill in generic values when the real limit isn't available. For accurate estimates at a specific time, click a cell to see the full details on the Concurrency Details page.

Click any data in the chart to go to the Concurrency Details dashboard-it shows concurrency data by the minute. To learn more, see [Using the Concurrency Details Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1526957070.html).
