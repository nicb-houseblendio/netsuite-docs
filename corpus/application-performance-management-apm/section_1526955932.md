You can use the SuiteCloud Processors Monitor to view and analyze how your map/reduce and scheduled script jobs are running. With this tool, you'll spot changes in processor usage and quickly find out what's causing slowdowns. Monitor your performance to get the most out of your processors and see if you'll need more SuiteCloud Plus licenses.

SuiteCloud Processors Monitor replaces Script Queue Monitor, so you can still check how queue-based deployments are doing.

Note:

Your performance logs don't move with your NetSuite account to the new OCI data center. For the first 29 days, values use only data collected since the move, not the last 30 days.

This tool consists of the following dashboards:

-   **SuiteCloud Processors Monitor** - Shows important processor settings and stats in different portlets, so you can compare data and boost your performance.
    
-   **SuiteCloud Processors Job Details** - Gives you detailed info about specific deployments based on the filters you pick.
    

See the following help topics:
