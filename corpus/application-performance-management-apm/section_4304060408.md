The Application Performance Management (APM) SuiteApp is organized to provide different levels of detail, depending on what you need. You can use data aggregates, summaries, and visualizations to help you find the individual logs and instances that contribute to poor response times.

The APM SuiteApp displays data for scripts and plug-ins that ran long enough to help you diagnose issues in your account. The APM tools are not meant to trace every script and plug-in processed in your account.

To view a related help topic, click any information icon in the APM tool. ![Information icon on Application Performance Management.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/AccountAdministration/Performance/APM_InfoIcon.jpg) available on the page.

Note:

Your performance logs aren't included when your NetSuite account moves to the new data center on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI). For the first 29 days after the move, the displayed values will be based on data collected since the move, not the last 30 days.
