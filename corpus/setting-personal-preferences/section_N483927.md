On the Alerts subtab of the Set Preferences page, you can choose to receive two different types of email alerts:

-   You can choose to receive a Daily Facts email alert up to three times a day. This standardized message summarizes the latest information in the Events, Reminders, Key Performance Indicators, and standard Report Snapshots portlets on your Home page. These portlets must be visible and not minimized on your dashboard. Note that these messages don't include data from custom and sales management report snapshots.
    
    Important:
    
    The Daily Facts email alert doesn't include contents from portlets that are minimized on your dashboard or not showing on your dashboard.
    
    You can also email **alerts@alerts.netsuite.com** from the email address you use to log in and automatically receive an email alert.
    
-   You can choose to receive email updates when information in one or more of your most important saved searches changes.
    

#### To set up email alerts: {#procedure_N483970}

1.  Go to _Home > Set Preferences_.
    
2.  Click the **Alerts** subtab.
    
3.  Choose the times of day you want to receive your three email alerts.
    
    If you don't want to receive any of these email alerts, change the value for the **First Selection** dropdown list to be blank.
    
4.  Check the **Include Links in HTML Alerts** box to enable you to click links in email alerts and open your account to the relevant information.
    
5.  Check the **Send an On-Demand Alert** from the **Role** box to enable you to send email to **alerts@alerts.netsuite.com** and automatically receive your email alerts.
    
    You must send email to this address from the email account you use to log in to NetSuite.
    
6.  To receive email alerts when saved search data changes, select the record type that search is based on in the **Record Type** field.
    
7.  In the **Search** field, select the name of the saved search.
    
    A search must have the **Available as Alert** box checked to appear in this list. Only Administrators can make searches available as alerts.
    
    For information about how to make saved searches available for alerts, see [Enabling Saved Search Email Alerts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N681962.html).
    
8.  Check the **Send on Update** box if you want to receive alerts both when new records are added and when current records are updated.
    
9.  Check the **Show Recent Changes** box if you have enabled the Send on Update option and you want these alerts to include information about changes.
    
10.  Click **Add/Edit**.
     
11.  Repeat steps 6 - 9 to add up to five saved searches.
     
12.  Click **Save**.
     

You will now receive email alerts at the times you set.
