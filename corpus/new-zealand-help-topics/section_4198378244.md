## Overview {#bridgehead_4256377581}

The Help topics below cover NetSuite features for companies in New Zealand. You can set up NetSuite with tools and reports for New Zealand business needs.

To learn about localized editions of NetSuite, as well as setting up NetSuite accounts and subsidiaries, refer to the following Help topics:

-   [NetSuite Editions Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N270055.html)
    
-   [Introduction to NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N266468.html) for companies using NetSuite across multiple subsidiaries and countries.
    
-   [Set up NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N268052.html)
    
-   [Creating Subsidiary Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N272471.html) on NetSuite OneWorld.
    

Please note that general NetSuite features are covered in other chapters and topics.

## General Setup {#bridgehead_1541572496}

-   [New Zealand Account Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541645561.html) - Gives you steps to set up your account for New Zealand, including extra features and relevant SuiteApps.
    
-   [Setting Up New Zealand-specific Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1541646578.html) - Gives you steps to set your NetSuite company and user preferences for New Zealand, like currency format, language, date, and time zone.
