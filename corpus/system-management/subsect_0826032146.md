The following best practices can help you set up and manage SuiteCommerce Email Verification on your website while keeping the customer experience positive:

-   Set up DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM), which digitally signs your email messages to demonstrate authenticity and protect your customers from spam. See [DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N999544.html).
    
-   Advise your customers to check their spam or junk folder if they don't receive the verification email.
    
-   Encourage customers to enter a valid, personal email address to ensure successful verification.
    
-   Set the code validity period so customers have enough time to check their email and enter the code, but keep it short enough to minimize misuse.
    
-   Set the resend interval to prevent repeated requests, but don't make customers wait too long.
