SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist lets you prevent customers from performing key actions in your web store if they use an email from a specific, untrusted domain. Blocking disposable and suspicious email domains helps improve data quality, reduce fraud, and make your store safer for everyone.

SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist works with SuiteCommerce, SuiteCommerce Advanced 2025.2 and later, and SuiteCommerce MyAccount.

## Key Benefits of SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist {#subsect_0901023139}

-   Blocks account creation or creation of a guest shopper account when using email addresses from blocked domains
    
-   Helps prevent bots, spam accounts, and fraudulent registrations
    
-   Supports both predefined and custom domain lists
    
-   Enhances customer and business security by reducing exposure to malicious activities
