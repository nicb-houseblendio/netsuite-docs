After you enable SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist for your web store, here's how it works:

1.  A customer enters their email address in a form as part of one of the following flows on your web store
    
    -   Registration
        
    -   Guest checkout
        
    -   Skip checkout login
        
2.  After the customer submits the form, NetSuite checks the domain part of their email to see if it's blocked. For example, if the customer's email is `customer@example.com`, NetSuite checks `example.com`.
    
3.  The blocklist automatically combines a predefined list from NetSuite with your custom list of domains, removing any duplicates.
    
4.  If the domain is on the blocklist, the customer sees a message telling them that they can't register with that email and need to use a different one.
    
5.  If the domain isn't on the blocklist, the customer proceeds to registration or checkout.
    

## SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist Configuration Options {#subsect_0901031526}

You can set up SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist with the SuiteCommerce Configuration record in NetSuite. Options include:

-   Enabling or disabling SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist
    
-   Adding or removing domains from your custom blocklist
    

For more information, see [How to Enable and Configure SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0901031720.html).
