Warning:

NetSuite provides CDN and caching services that must be enabled in NetSuite. Third-party CDN and caching services aren't supported and must not be used as they can cause operational problems.

Important:

Starting September 2025, CDN caching will be required for all websites associated with NetSuite production accounts. You should turn on CDN caching before the deadline to avoid performance issues with your site.

You can enable CDN caching for your website domains. For more information, see [Set Up Domains for Web Stores](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2484898.html).

#### To enable the CDN cache:

1.  Go to _Commerce > Hosting > Domains_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the domain name that points to your main website.
    
    Note:
    
    Another way to access the Domain record is to go to Commerce > Websites > Website List (Administrator). Click **Edit** next to your domain and go to the **Domains** tab. Then, click **Edit** next to your domain.
    
3.  On the Domain record, in the Advanced section, check the **Use CDN Cache** box.
    
4.  Click **Save** to apply the change.
    

Read the following topics about how to use CDN caching capabilities to help in website development:

-   After enabling CDN caching, data and site assets that are reused across your website are cached for a set amount of time, which you can adjust. For step-by-step instructions on configuring CDN caching, read [Configure CDN Caching](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3937695730.html).
    
-   For instructions on setting up a subdomain to bypass the CDN cache while testing your site, see [Bypass CDN Cache](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3937695894.html).
