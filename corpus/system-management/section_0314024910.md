The property on this subtab lets you control how the out-of-stock error message works.

## Show Quantity Available on the Out-of-Stock Error Message {#subsect_0314024959}

The **Show Quantity Available on the Out-of-Stock Error Message** setting works with SuiteCommerce MyAccount, SuiteCommerce, and the 2023.2.4 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced and later.

An out-of-stock error message displays on the review page of your Commerce website's checkout flow if your customer orders a larger quantity of an item than you have in stock.

This boolean lets you choose whether to show the number of items in stock on the out-of-stock error message. Check the **Show Quantity Available on the Out-of-Stock Error Message** box to display how many items are in stock on the error message. By default, this property isn't checked.

| **ID** | checkoutApp.showItemRemainingQuantity |
| --- | --- |
| **UI location** | Checkout > Quantity Stock Message |
| **JSON file** | CheckoutApplication.json |

For more information, see [Out-of-Stock Messaging During Checkout](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0304044915.html).
