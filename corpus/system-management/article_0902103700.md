You must log in to NetSuite with an Administrator role to configure and manage SuiteCommerce CAPTCHA.

#### To enable and configure SuiteCommerce CAPTCHA:

1.  In NetSuite, go to Commerce > Websites > Configuration.
    
2.  Select your website and domain, then click **Configure**.
    
3.  Go to the **Integrations** tab and click the **CAPTCHA** subtab.
    
4.  Choose where CAPTCHA challenges appear on your web store. The options are:
    
    -   **Enable for Registration:** Check this box to require customers to complete a CAPTCHA challenge when creating a new account.
        
    -   **Enable for Login:** Check this box to require customers to complete a CAPTCHA challenge during login.
        
    -   **Enable for Guest Checkout:** Check this box to require customers to complete a CAPTCHA challenge when checking out as a guest.
        
    -   **Enable for Place Order:**: Check this box to require customers to complete a CAPTCHA challenge before completing an order at checkout.
        
5.  From **CAPTCHA Provider**, choose your CAPTCHA provider (Google reCAPTCHA v2 or hCaptcha).
    
6.  In **Site Key**, enter the public key from your CAPTCHA provider.
    
7.  In **Secret Key ID**, enter your private key's ID. To enter a secret key or view the ID, see [Creating Secrets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160216498405.html).
    
8.  Click **Save**.
    

See [Captcha Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0902110432.html) for more information about the SuiteCommerce CAPTCHA configuration properties.
