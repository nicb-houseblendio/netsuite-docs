If you don't need a domain anymore, you might want to delete it permanently. Deleting a domain removes it permanently and irrecoverably. No one can access it, and there's no history or audit trail kept.

#### To delete a domain:

1.  Go to _Commerce > Hosting > Domains_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the domain you want to delete.
    
3.  In the **Actions** menu, click **Delete**.
    
4.  Review the confirmation messages, then click **Yes** to delete the domain.
    
    Deleting a domain can take some time. When it's gone from your list of domains, it's been deleted.
    

Note:

Deleting a secure domain doesn't remove the SSL certificate. NetSuite keeps it until it expires, but there's no security risk. However, if you've got a manual certificate and want to revoke it, contact your certificate authority (CA).
