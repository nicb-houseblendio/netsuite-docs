## Sales Order {#bridgehead_1486488592}

This setting lets you choose which custom transaction body fields (by Field ID) are displayed in your web store.

More Information: [Commerce Custom Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4837340253.html)

| **ID** | customFields.salesorder |
| --- | --- |
| **UI location** | Advanced > Custom Fields |
