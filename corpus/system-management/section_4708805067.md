The settings on the My Account tab let you configure properties related to the My Account application on your Commerce web store. This tab includes the following subtabs:

-   [Subscriptions Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156503582879.html)
    
-   [Return Authorization Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4678712685.html)
    
-   [Preferences Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161278879329.html)
    
-   [Overview Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4678713056.html)
    
-   [Transaction List Columns Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1544554335.html)
    
-   [SCIS Integration Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4678713532.html)
    
-   [Cases Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4678713315.html)
    
-   [Quotes Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4678712900.html)
    
-   [List Header Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4678713154.html)
    
-   [Addresses Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1495046472.html)
