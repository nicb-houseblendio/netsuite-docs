The following best practices can help you set up and manage SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist on your website while keeping the customer experience positive:

-   Encourage customers to use a valid, personal email address to ensure successful account registration and order placement.
    
-   Tell customers not to use temporary email providers, since those might be blocklisted.
    
-   Review and update your domains blocklist regularly to keep up with new threats.
    
-   Consider enabling logging for attempts to register with blocklisted domains for additional security monitoring.
    
-   If customers report issues, provide clear instructions and support options, and ensure your team is aware of the domains on your blocklist.
