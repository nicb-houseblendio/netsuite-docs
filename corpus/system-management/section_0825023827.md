SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist works with SuiteCommerce MyAccount, SuiteCommerce and SuiteCommerce Advanced 2025.2 and later. See [SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5142959157.html) for more information about this feature.

These settings let you control how SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist works in your Commerce web store.

## Enable Email Domains Blocklist {#subsect_0825025624}

This boolean setting lets you enable the SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist feature for your web store. When enabled, email addresses your customers enter in forms on your web store are checked to verify that the email domain isn't on a blocklist. This setting is disabled by default.

| **ID** | blockListEmailDomains.enableEmailBlockList |
| --- | --- |
| **UI location** | Advanced > Blocklist Email Domains |
| **JSON file** | BlockListEmail.Domains.Options.json |

## Blocklisted Email Domains {#subsect_0825025856}

This array lets you add email domains to your custom list of blocked domains.

| **ID** | blockListEmailDomains.domainList |
| --- | --- |
| **UI location** | Advanced > Blocklist Email Domains |
| **JSON file** | BlockListEmail.Domains.Options.json |
