The settings on this tab let you set up properties related to different integration options available for your site, such as Google, Twitter, and Facebook. This tab includes the following subtabs:

-   [Twitter Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4667036013.html)
    
-   [Pinterest Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4667036133.html)
    
-   [Facebook Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4667036337.html)
    
-   [Google Analytics 4 Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0930101732.html)
    
-   [Google Universal Analytics Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4667032443.html)
    
-   [Google Tag Manager Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4667032233.html)
    
-   [Google AdWords Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4667032647.html)
    
-   [Captcha Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0902110432.html)
    
-   [Site Management Tools Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4667032981.html)
    
-   [Categories Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4701522812.html)
