These settings let you set up your invoice options.

## Invoice Terms and Conditions {#bridgehead_4700784181}

This string lets you enter your invoice terms and conditions in HTML format.

| **ID** | checkoutApp.invoiceTermsAndConditions |
| --- | --- |
| **UI location** | Advanced > Invoice |
| **JSON file** | Invoice.json |
| **Configuration file (pre-Vinson)** | SC.Checkout.Configuration.js |

## Maximum Number of Invoices That Can Be Paid {#subsect_62160740118}

This number lets you define the maximum number of invoices a customer can pay at the same time in the My Account area of your Commerce site. By default, the maximum is 20 invoices. If you change this, enter a value of less than 1000.

| **ID** | checkoutApp.invoiceMaxCountPayment |
| --- | --- |
| **UI location** | Advanced > Invoice |
| **JSON file** | Invoice.json |
