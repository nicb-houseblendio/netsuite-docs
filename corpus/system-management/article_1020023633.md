SuiteCommerce Email Verification supports the following flows:

-   **Register a New Customer:** A customer enters their email address and submits the form. NetSuite sends a verification code to their email, and their account is created after they enter and validate the code.
    
-   **Guest Checkout:** After entering an email address during guest checkout, a verification code is sent. The order process continues after the code is entered and validated.
    
-   **Skip Checkout Login:** An anonymous shopper skips the login or register page at checkout and goes straight to the first checkout step. After the shopper enters an email address during checkout, they get a verification code. The order process continues after the code is validated.
