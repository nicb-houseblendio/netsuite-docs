Commerce and Site Builder websites use the Customer Center permissions to enable some features and generate menu items for the My Account application. You configure these permissions in the NetSuite account that contains the website record for your site. For more information, see [Custom Centers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2890160.html).
