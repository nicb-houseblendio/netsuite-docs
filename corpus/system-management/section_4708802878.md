The settings on this tab let you set up properties related to your site layout. This tab has the following subtabs:

-   [Header Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4393268747.html)
    
-   [Cookies Warning Banner Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4393102020.html)
    
-   [Images Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4666175954.html)
    
-   [Navigation Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4393094608.html)
    
-   [Carousel Images Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4666978268.html)
    
-   [Bottom Banner Images Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4393296407.html)
    
-   [Color Palettes Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4667037557.html)
    
-   [Light Colors Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4701517042.html)
