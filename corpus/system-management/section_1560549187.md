Note:

This topic applies to SuiteCommerce and the 2020.1.1 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced and later.

Administrators need to grant employees permission to view performance data in SuiteCommerce (SC) APM and SuiteCommerce InStore (SCIS) APM.

See the following help topics:

-   [Granting Access to SuiteCommerce APM](#subsect_159901815337)
    
-   [Granting Access to SuiteCommerce InStore APM](#subsect_159901808275)
    

## Granting Access to SuiteCommerce APM {#subsect_159901815337}

Use the **SuiteCommerce Performance Setup** page to give roles and employees access to SC APM.

#### To grant permission to view SuiteCommerce APM:

1.  Go to Customization > SuiteCommerce Performance > SuiteCommerce Performance Setup.
    
2.  To let someone view SC APM data, do one of the following:
    
    -   To give access to a role:
        
        1.  Click the **Roles** subtab.
            
        2.  From the **Role** field, select a role.
            
        3.  Click **Add**.
            
        4.  Repeat these steps for each role you want to add.
            
    -   To give access to an employee:
        
        1.  Click the **Employees** subtab.
            
        2.  From the **Employee** field, select an employee.
            
        3.  Click **Add**.
            
        4.  Repeat these steps for each employee you want to add.
            
3.  Click **Save**.
    

## Granting Access to SuiteCommerce InStore APM {#subsect_159901808275}

Use the **SuiteCommerce InStore Performance Setup** page to give roles and employees access to SCIS APM.

#### To grant permission to view SuiteCommerce InStore APM:

1.  Go to Customization > SCIS Performance > SCIS Performance Setup.
    
2.  To let someone view SCIS APM data, do one of the following:
    
    -   To give access to a role:
        
        1.  Click the **Roles** subtab.
            
        2.  From the **Role** field, select a role.
            
        3.  Click **Add**.
            
        4.  Repeat these steps for each role you want to add.
            
    -   To give access to an employee:
        
        1.  Click the **Employees** subtab.
            
        2.  From the **Employee** field, select an employee.
            
        3.  Click **Add**.
            
        4.  Repeat these steps for each employee you want to add.
            
3.  Click **Save**.
