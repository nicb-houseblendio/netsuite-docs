Integrating Commerce web stores with other NetSuite applications lets you take advantage of extra features they offer, such as:

-   NetSuite web store account access for shoppers directly from your existing website, no extra login needed
    
-   Automated marketing features, like post-purchase campaigns, on your web store
    
-   Access to NetSuite features for brick and mortar stores
    
-   Creation of content pages and content areas on your website
    

See the following topics for more information about integration:

-   [Single Sign-on Integration with External Websites](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1518799801.html) - Single Sign-on lets users access your Commerce web store from an external website, or conversely, access an external website from your Commerce web store, without having to log in to each site separately.
    
-   [SuiteCommerce InStore Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4515028501.html) - SCIS is a web-based point-of-sale application available for the U.S. market. It's built on the same platform as Commerce web stores and gives you access to NetSuite records through an intuitive interface made for touch tablet screens.
    
-   [Content Delivery Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2688958.html) - The Content Delivery SuiteApp brings together customer forms, service files, and customer records so you can easily create and manage content pages and enhanced content areas for your website. You don't need any website scripting experience-marketing managers, site administrators, and others can easily add graphics, multimedia, and marketing content without writing any code.
