SuiteCommerce CAPTCHA adds an extra layer of security to your SuiteCommerce web store by integrating the third-party CAPTCHA providers: hCaptcha and Google reCAPTCHA v2. This helps prevent automated abuse and fraudulent activity. CAPTCHA challenges prompt customers to complete simple tasks, confirming that they're human before they can perform certain actions on your website. This helps block bots, prevent fake account creation, and keep your store secure without negatively affecting the shopping experience for genuine customers.

SuiteCommerce CAPTCHA works with SuiteCommerce, SuiteCommerce Advanced 2025.2 and later, and SuiteCommerce MyAccount.

## Key Benefits of SuiteCommerce CAPTCHA {#subsect_0902103504}

-   Prevents bots from creating fake accounts, placing fraudulent orders, or abusing your web forms.
    
-   Protects sensitive actions such as registration, login, and checkout.
    
-   Works seamlessly across devices.
    
-   Protects your store's reputation without disrupting genuine customers.
    
-   Lets you choose where CAPTCHA challenges appear in your web store.
    

Note:

SuiteCommerce CAPTCHA doesn't support translations. If you want to add translations for CAPTCHA challenges on your website, you must add them manually on the SuiteCommerce Configuration record. See [Translations Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4688691652.html).
