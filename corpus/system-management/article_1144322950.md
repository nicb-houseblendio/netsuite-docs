SuiteCommerce Email Verification lets you require customers to verify their email addresses before completing key actions in your web store. Email verification helps prevent fraud, ensures reliable communication, and improves data quality.

SuiteCommerce Email Verification works with SuiteCommerce, SuiteCommerce Advanced 2025.2 and later, and SuiteCommerce MyAccount.

## Key Benefits of SuiteCommerce Email Verification {#subsect_0826024809}

-   Confirms customer email addresses before account creation or creating a guest shopper account
    
-   Prevents bots and fraudulent registrations or orders
    
-   Supports configurable code expiration and resend intervals
    
-   Improves communication reliability
