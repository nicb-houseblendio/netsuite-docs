SuiteCommerce CAPTCHA can help safeguard the following flows in your web store:

-   **Customer Registration:** When a customer submits the registration form, a CAPTCHA challenge appears to ensure that only genuine users can create new accounts.
    
-   **Login:** A CAPTCHA challenge appears during login to prevent automated login attempts and protect customer accounts.
    
-   **Place Order:** Before completing an order at checkout, customers are prompted with a CAPTCHA challenge to block bots and reduce fraudulent orders.
    
-   **Guest Checkout:** Before completing an order at checkout, guest shoppers are prompted with a CAPTCHA challenge to block bots and reduce fraudulent orders.
    

SuiteCommerce CAPTCHA isn't currently available for the following flows:

-   Quotes to Sales Order
    
-   Invoice Payment
    
-   Skip Checkout Login
    
-   Support Cases
