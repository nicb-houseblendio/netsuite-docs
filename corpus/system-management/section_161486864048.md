The following table shows the template files and data-view placeholders for the shopping area of your SuiteCommerce theme.

| Area | Template | Placeholder | Location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Extended Cart Summary | cart\_summary.tpl | CartSummary.AfterTitle | After .cart-summary-title |
| CartSummary.AfterSubtotal | After .cart-summary-subtotal |
| CartSummary.AfterTotal | After .cart-summary-total |
| Mini Cart | header\_mini\_cart\_item\_cell.tpl | ItemThumbnail | .header-mini-cart-item-cell-image |
| header\_mini\_cart.tpl | MiniCart.Subtotal | .header-mini-cart-subtotal container |
| cart\_lines.tpl | MiniCart.Actions | .header-mini-cart-buttons container |
| Product Details Page | product\_details\_full.tpl | ItemDetails.Header | Product details header, after the Global.StarRating placeholder |
| ItemActions | .product-details-full-actions container |
| Product Listing Page | item\_relations\_related\_item.tpl | ItemThumbnail | Product thumbnail wrapper |
