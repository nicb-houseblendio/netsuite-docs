Site security features let you monitor changes made to your Commerce website and protect it from unauthorized access. You're able to:

-   Restrict customer access to specific website features, such as Order History
    
-   Restrict access to your entire website
    
-   Restrict access to price information
    
-   Track changes that have been made to your website records
    
-   Prevent customers from performing key actions on your website if they use unverified email or email from untrusted domains
    
-   Use CAPTCHA challenges to confirm visitors to your website are human before they can perform certain actions
    

NetSuite's SuiteCommerce platform uses a state-of-the-art content delivery network (CDN) to protect our customers and users from internet threats such as DDoS attacks, aggressive bots, and other fraudulent activities.

SuiteCommerce applies several protective measures, such as:

-   Rate limiting
    
-   Bot management
    
-   Web application firewall
    
-   IP reputation management
    
-   Geo-blocking
    

All security controls are configured and fine-tuned to comply with Oracle policy. A dedicated team of network and security engineers constantly monitors and updates these measures.

See the following topics for more information about site security:

-   [Feature Access Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4443924472.html) - Restrict access to specific features by modifying the Customer Center role permissions for each user.
    
-   [Website Restriction](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4513277250.html) - Restrict access to either your entire website or to pricing information.
    
-   [McAfee Secure](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1536048271.html) - Certify your website is secure with the McAfee SECURE service.
    
-   [Website System Notes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4762462531.html) - Use system notes to track changes that have been made to your website records.
    
-   [SuiteCommerce Email Verification](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1144322950.html) - Require customers to verify their email addresses before completing key actions on your website.
    
-   [SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5142959157.html) - Prevent customers from performing key actions on your website if they use an email from untrusted domains.
    
-   [SuiteCommerce CAPTCHA](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9103248353.html) - Require customers to complete simple tasks, confirming that they're human before they can perform certain actions on your website.
