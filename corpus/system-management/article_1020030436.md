SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist applies to the following flows:

-   **Register a New Customer:** After a customer enters their email address on your site's account registration form, the domain is checked against the blocklist. If the domain is blocked, registration is prevented and the customer sees an error message.
    
-   **Guest Checkout:** After entering an email address during guest checkout, the domain is checked against the blocklist. If the domain is blocked, checkout is prevented and the customer sees an error message.
    
-   **Skip Checkout Login:** An anonymous shopper skips the login or register page at checkout and proceeds directly to the first checkout step. After entering an email address during checkout, the domain is checked against the blocklist. If the domain is blocked, checkout is prevented and the customer sees an error message.
