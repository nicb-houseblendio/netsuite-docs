You must log in to NetSuite as an Administrator to set up and manage your email domains blocklist.

#### To enable and configure SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist:

1.  In NetSuite, go to Commerce > Websites > Configuration.
    
2.  Select your website and domain, then click **Configure**.
    
3.  Go to the **Advanced** tab and the **Blocklist Email Domains** subtab.
    
4.  Check the **Enable Email Domains Blocklist** box to enable SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist for your website.
    
5.  In the **Domain** field, enter an email domain you want to block. For example, example.net.
    
6.  Click **Add** to add the domain to your blocklist.
    
7.  Repeat steps 5 and 6 for each additional domain you want to block.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
    

See [Blocklist Email Domains Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0825023827.html) for more information about the SuiteCommerce Email Domains Blocklist configuration properties.

## What Happens When a Blocked Domain is Used? {#subsect_0901032851}

When a customer uses a blocked email domain, the following occurs:

-   NetSuite checks the domain against a combined list taken from the following:
    
    -   Your custom blocklist
        
    -   NetSuite's predefined blocklist
        
-   An on-screen message informs the customer that they can't proceed using that email and that they should try again with one from a different provider
