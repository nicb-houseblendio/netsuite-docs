Important:

The Content Delivery Service is supported until the Denali R2 (revision 2) release of SuiteCommerce Advanced only. Customers should use Site Management Tools instead of Content Delivery for SuiteCommerce and for the Mont Blanc release of SuiteCommerce Advanced and later.

There are two types of Content Delivery:

-   **Landing Pages** : Let you create full custom pages with custom URLs. You can add as many different content areas as you want and swap out content based on a set of predefined custom rules.
    
-   **Enhanced Pages** : Let you add custom content to existing pages. For example, on a page that shows item records based on search criteria from faceted navigation links, you could add a descriptive content area at the top. You can also customize this area to show different content based on attributes assigned to the enhanced page content area.ther customized to display different content based off of attributes assigned to the enhanced page content area.
    

Content administrators create pages with containers to display content and define custom content tags to characterize page attributes such as language, delivery method, geography, or domain. After pages are designed, you can create new content records with rules that let users decide how and when their content shows up on the website. Users can set up rules with different attributes to support marketing efforts or scheduled website changes.

Content is generated as JSON data and can be presented on your NetSuite-powered website, mobile applications, or third-party services, such as an externally hosted website or content marketing services.

Note:

Anchor Tags are not supported by Content Delivery.

Important:

Before you set up Content Delivery pages, work with your developer to figure out the initial page layout, including content layout, content tag definitions, and CSS selectors used to call the content pages. Good planning up front will make development and maintenance easier and let non-developers add custom content when needed.
