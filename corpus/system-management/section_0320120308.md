The **Payment Instrument ACH** settings let you set up ACH payments on your site.

For more information, see [ACH Payments on Commerce Web Stores](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162737287771.html).

## Enable ACH Payments from Companies Using Checkout {#subsect_0320120557}

The **Enable ACH Payments from Companies Using Checkout** setting works with SuiteCommerce MyAccount, SuiteCommerce, and the 2023.1 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced and later.

This boolean lets you choose whether companies can make ACH payments during checkout on your site.

| **IDs** | checkoutApp.isAchEnabledInCheckout |
| --- | --- |
| **UI location** | Payment Instruments & Methods > Payment Methods |
| **JSON file** | CheckoutApplication.json |

## Enable ACH Payments from Individuals Using Checkout {#subsect_0320120853}

The **Enable ACH Payments from Individuals Using Checkout** setting works with SuiteCommerce MyAccount, SuiteCommerce, and the 2023.1 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced and later.

This boolean value lets you choose whether individual customers can make ACH payments during checkout on your site. Companies can also make ACH payments if you enable this option.

| **IDs** | checkoutApp.isAchEnabledForAllCustomers |
| --- | --- |
| **UI location** | Payment Instruments & Methods > Payment Methods |
| **JSON file** | CheckoutApplication.json |

## Enable ACH Payments for Make a Payment {#subsect_0320121034}

The **Enable ACH Payments for Make a Payment** setting works with to SuiteCommerce MyAccount, SuiteCommerce, and the 2021.2 release of SuiteCommerce Advanced and later.

This boolean lets you choose whether shoppers can pay invoices using ACH payments.

| **IDs** | paymentInstrumentACHEnabled |
| --- | --- |
| **UI location** | Payment Instruments & Methods > Payment Methods |
| **JSON file** | PaymentInstrumentACH.json |
