Important:

The Yodlee format profile isn't available to new customers as a preinstalled option. If the preinstalled format profiles don't support your financial institution, you may set up Yodlee manually.

For more information, see [Creating a Custom Format Profile to Connect to a U.S. or Canada (Yodlee) Financial Institution](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1024175).

The Yodlee format profile remains available in Bank Feeds SuiteApp versions installed before February 4, 2026.

The following procedure describes how to connect one or more compatible financial institutions in the United States or Canada to NetSuite through Yodlee.

## Guidelines for U.S. and Canada (Yodlee) Connections {#subsect_0723122401}

Before you connect a U.S. or Canada financial institution to NetSuite through Yodlee, review the following guidelines:

-   Not every financial institution connection experience is the same and not all financial institutions can be accessed, or accessed reliably, using the Bank Feeds SuiteApp. Several factors may affect whether the Bank Feeds SuiteApp can connect to your financial institutions, including factors that Oracle can't control.
    
    Also, the Bank Feeds SuiteApp only supports certain bank or payment accounts. Selecting an unsupported account type such as a loan, mortgage, or investment may not work with your bank feeds setup.
    
-   You'll need Administrator access or a custom role with required permissions to set up bank feeds. For more information, see [Bank Feeds Roles and Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157734104117.html).
    
-   You must enable popups in your browser so the configuration window can open during bank feeds setup.
    
-   When you're setting up your connection, the Format Profile page session generally expires after 15 minutes. To keep editing your format profile, refresh the page to see the content of your Connectivity Configuration subtab.
    
-   Some financial institutions may require authorization, a PIN, or additional setup before you can connect to NetSuite. Contact your financial institution for assistance.
    

#### To connect NetSuite to a financial institution operating in the United States or Canada through Yodlee:

1.  Go to Setup > Accounting > Financial Statements > Financial Institution > List.
    
2.  On the Financial Institution page, click **View** next to the **Bank Feeds** financial institution record.
    
3.  On the **Format Profile: Configuration** subtab, click **Edit** next to **U.S. and Canada Accounts (Yodlee)**.
    
4.  On the U.S. and Canada Accounts (Yodlee) Format Profile page, click the **Connectivity Configuration** subtab.
    
5.  If you're connecting to a financial institution for the first time, you'll see the following notices before you can start the configuration:
    
    1.  **Disclaimer of Warranties and Terms of Use of Oracle** - Read and acknowledge by clicking the **Accept** button at the bottom of the page.
        
    2.  **Authorized Information Service Provider (AISP) User Agreement of NetSuite** - Read and acknowledge by clicking the **Accept** button at the bottom of the page.
        
6.  Click **Connect New Institution**.
    
    The connection configuration setup popup window appears.
    
7.  In the configuration setup window, select your financial institution from the list. You can also use the search box at the top of the financial institution list to enter a name or a keyword.
    
    The Connectivity Configuration subtab is masked, and you can't make any changes until you have completed the configuration setup. If you want to go back to the Connectivity Configuration subtab, close the window by clicking X in the upper-right corner of the browser window.
    
8.  If a consent information appears, read the notice on data sharing and then acknowledge by clicking **Continue**.
    
9.  Depending on your selected financial institution, perform the following actions:
    
    -   If you're asked to enter your login credentials on the configuration setup page, do the following:
        
        1.  Enter your financial institution account's login credentials.
            
        2.  Click **Continue**.
            
        3.  If you're setting up a multifactor authentication (MFA) account which requires additional validation, enter the required confirmation.
            
        4.  Click **Continue**.
            
            If your credentials are valid, the system retrieves all bank and credit card accounts from your financial institution and automatically connects them to your NetSuite account.
            
    -   If the system redirects you to your financial institution's website to authenticate, do the following:
        
        1.  Enter your financial institution account's login credentials.
            
        2.  If you're setting up a multifactor authentication (MFA) account which requires additional validation, enter the required confirmation.
            
        3.  On your financial institution's authorization page, review and select the bank and credit card accounts you want to connect to NetSuite.
            
        4.  Grant NetSuite access to your financial institution account. You'll need to do this before the system can start establishing a secure connection.
            
            The system automatically redirects you back to the bank feeds configuration setup page in NetSuite.
            
10.  Review the list of bank and credit card accounts associated with your financial institution connection.
     
     To exclude an account from your connection, clear the box next to the account name.
     
     Note:
     
     The box next to the financial institution account name only becomes available if you didn't select a bank or credit card account from the financial institution's website.
     
     If you don't see the box on the summary page, you can add or exclude an account from your financial institution connection after you finish the setup. Perform any of the following actions:
     
     -   Rebuild your connection. Read [Managing U.S. and Canada (Yodlee) Financial Institution Connections](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160438353354.html) to know how to rebuild your financial institution connection.
         
     -   Exclude an account from the Connectivity Configuration subtab on your format profile page. For more information, see [Managing U.S. and Canada (Yodlee) Bank and Payment Accounts for Bank Feeds](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160438756133.html).
         
     
11.  To complete the configuration setup and go back to the format profile page, click **Save and Finish**.
     
     To add another financial institution account in NetSuite, click **Save and Connect More Accounts**. You can connect to a different financial institution or the same financial institution with a different login credential.
     
     The Connectivity Configuration subtab refreshes with your new connection. All active financial institution connections are listed under the Connections subtab, and all connected bank and credit card accounts are listed under the Accounts subtab.
     
12.  Go to the **Account Linking** subtab. From the **Linked GL Account** dropdown list, select a bank or credit card account in NetSuite you want to link to the adjacent accounts. For more information, see [Linking Accounts for Financial Institution Connectivity](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157894482115.html).
     
13.  Click **Save** on the format profile record.
