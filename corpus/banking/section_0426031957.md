**What should I do if I want to request a copy of an issued or paid check to provide proof of payment to the beneficiary who is claiming non-receipt?**

Contact NetSuite Customer Support to request a copy of a check from HSBC that has been issued or paid.

To process your request, you will have to provide the following information:

-   Account number
    
-   Check number
    
-   Date when the check has been posted
    

HSBC will send you an email message with a copy of the check image requested.
