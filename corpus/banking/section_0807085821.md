**What should I do if I lose virtual card information?**

If you lose the Virtual Card information provided in the remittance email, view the Remittance Voucher to see the Virtual Card details. For more information, see [Reviewing Remittance Email](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1006030520.html).

You can contact the Virtual Card Customer Support available 24/7 at 1-800-411-9264 to obtain the account information for that specific invoice.
