Important:

Starting **December 31, 2026**, support is targeted to end for the Payment Automation SuiteApp. This change is in line with the upcoming end of partnership with HSBC.

You can no longer apply for HSBC online accounts in NetSuite. Only customers who applied by **March 4, 2026** can continue using the SuiteApp until support ends in December. New or pending applications for HSBC online accounts will not be processed.

To continue enrolling accounts or subsidiaries for online payments, switch to the Intelligent Payment Automation SuiteApp. For more information, see [Intelligent Payment Automation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_8155141819.html).

For more information about off-boarding, see [Customer Off-boarding](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1165104240.html).

Use daily and monthly statements for HSBC Online Account and HSBC Virtual Card reconciliation. Bank account monthly statements, virtual card statements, and invoices become automatically available for download as PDF files on the second day of every month.

To access the statements, go to SuiteBanking > Payment Automation > Dashboard, and click **Account Details** in the HSBC Online Account portlet. You can also access the statements by clicking **Card Details** in the HSBC Virtual Card portlet. Click **Refresh** in the Statements portlet to get available new invoices or statements.

Note:

Invoices are generally available by the 15th of a month at 5: 30 p.m. Eastern Time (UTC - 5). If no new invoices are available by the next working day at 5: 30 p.m. Eastern Time (UTC - 5), click **Refresh**. All available invoices will be updated in the Invoices tab of the Statements portlet.

For an overview of dates relevant to bank and virtual card data reconciliation, see [Statement and Auto-Debit Payment Schedules](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1013073503.html).

A format profile is created automatically for your account. For information about format profiles, see [Format Profile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557261357.html).

Journal entries for transaction fees are automatically created based on the monthly statement on the 15th day of every month. The transfer status of the journal entry is "Sent to the Bank" and the Memo is "Monthly Invoice Fee". The journal entry is created to record the bank transfer from the source bank to HSBC to pay the fees.

On the second day of every month, statement files of posted transactions are automatically retrieved from HSBC. NetSuite existing auto matching rules match the transactions from the files with bill payments in NetSuite. For more information, see [Matching Bank Data](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_32131451402.html).

The HSBC ACH Reconciliation format profile lets you see individually all the ACH transactions that are posted in each batch.

Billing fees and virtual card payments are automatically debited from HSBC when due:

-   Billing fees - debited five days after the statement is generated, which is typically every 20th day of the month.
    
-   Virtual card payments - due every 25th day of the month. Virtual card payments are included in the ACH CSV report. The CSV reconciliation report does not include any Memo information. However, the report includes the payee.
    

Billing fees and virtual card payments are posted in the HSBC Online Account and are included in the daily reconciliation report.

The daily reconciliation file of the HSBC Virtual Card will include the statement credit as well as any other charges like late fees and penalties imposed by HSBC Bank. These transactions will appear on the Match Bank Data page.

#### To download a bank account monthly statement or an invoice: {#procedure_1011054707}

1.  On the left navigation pane, click **Bank Accounts.**
    
2.  In the **Statements** portlet, click the **Statements** subtab or the **Invoices** subtab.
    
3.  Click **Refresh** to get all available statements or invoices.
    
4.  Locate the statement or invoice that you want to download.
    
5.  Click **Download.**
    

#### To download a virtual card statement: {#procedure_1011054840}

1.  On the left navigation pane, click **Cards.**
    
2.  In the **Statements** portlet, click **Refresh** to get all available statements.
    
3.  Locate the statement you want to download.
    
4.  Click **Download.**
    

## Manually Refresh Monthly Statements or Invoices {#subsect_0423121458}

#### To manually refresh monthly statements or invoices:

1.  Go to SuiteBanking > Payment Automation > Dashboard.
    
2.  Click **Account Details** in the HSBC Bank Account portlet. Click **Card Details** in the HSBC Virtual Card portlet.
    
3.  To switch between viewing the statements or invoices, click the Statements or Invoices tab in the Statements portal of your bank account.
    
4.  Click **Refresh** to get the statements or invoices.
    
5.  If you still do not see statements or invoices, contact NetSuite Customer Support.
