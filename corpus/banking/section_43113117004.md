To ensure bill payments are processed correctly, the system validates payments against weekends and federal holidays. NetSuite doesn't allow scheduling bill payments for these dates. To view a list of federal holidays, see [Federal Reserve Board Holidays Observed](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/k8.htm)

The following lists example scenarios where payment date restrictions apply:

-   **PSV Bill Payments** - If the **Date** indicated in a Pay Single Vendor (PSV) bill payment is a weekend or federal holiday, you won't be able to create the bill payment.
    
-   **Payment Runs** - If the **Payment Creation Date** selected for a payment run falls on a weekend or federal holiday, you won't be able to create the payment run.
    
-   **Payment Submission Cut-off Times** - Submit payments to BILL before 6 p.m. Pacific Time (UTC - 8) to ensure processing on the same business day.
    
    If you submit payments after 6 p.m. Pacific Time (UTC - 8), BILL will process them on the next available business day. A business day is any day except Saturday, Sunday, or a US federal holiday observed by Federal Reserve Banks.
