**What email address will the vendors receive the remittance notifications from?**

For ACH and check payments, your vendors will receive remittance emails from the email address of the user who initiated the payment.

-   In the case of bulk bill payment processing, if batch approval routing is enabled, the email is sent from the approver's email address.
    
-   If an email address is specified in Home > Set preferences > General > User profile > From Email Address, then the email is sent from this address.
    

For virtual card payments, the remittance email is sent from noreply@payment-automation.netsuite.com.
