**Is it possible to change the setup of my auto-debit account? I have another bank account that is used to manage other payments on behalf of my business, and I would like all debits to be sent from one account.**

As part of the SuiteBanking proposition, you have been provided with an HSBC Online Account. This account can't be changed.
