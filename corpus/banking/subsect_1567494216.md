In MT940 files, the transaction code starts with F,S, or N, followed by a 3-character SWIFT or bank code. For example:

![A section of a MT940-formatted file](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Accounting/Banking/BSP_BTCMT940.png)

The following table lists the supported transaction codes for the MT940 parser:

| BNK | CMP | DIV | MSC | RTI | TCM |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BOE | CMS | EQA | NWI | SAL | TRA |
| BRF | CMT | EXT | ODC | SEC | TRF |
| CAR | CMZ | FEX | OPT | SLE | TRN |
| CAS | COL | INT | PCH | STO | UWC |
| CHG | COM | LBX | POP | STP | VDA |
| CHK | CPN | LDP | PRN | SUB | WAR |
| CLR | DCR | MAR | REC | SWP |  |
| CMI | DDT | MAT | RED | TAX |  |
| CMN | DIS | MGT | RIG | TCK |  |
