Outstanding checks help you monitor checks that are yet to clear your bank.

#### To review outstanding checks: {#procedure_68130119696}

1.  Go to SuiteBanking > Payment Automation > Dashboard.
    
2.  On the left pane, click **Payments.**
    
3.  Click the **Outstanding Checks** subtab.
    
4.  Click the Reference No. link of the transaction you want to investigate.
    

Click **Export to CSV** to export the outstanding check payments in CSV format from the Dashboard. Use the quick search and date filters to display specific transactions. Select the date range from the date fields for the export.
