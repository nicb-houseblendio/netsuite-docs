Intelligent Payment Automation enables you to create bill payments for single or multiple vendors.

-   To pay a single vendor, see [Paying a Single Vendor Through BILL](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606092816.html).
    
-   To pay for multiple vendors at the same time, see [Creating a Payment Run](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606092833.html).
    

For locked or closed accounting periods, you can set up roles to enable bill payment creation and submission. See [Setting Up Intelligent Payment Automation for Closed Accounting Periods](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1224080154.html).

After you create a bill payment, it can be approved and submitted to BILL for processing. For instructions, see the following topics:

-   [Approving Bill Payments and Payment Runs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606092845.html)
    
-   [Payments to Submit](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0609053659.html)
    
-   [Viewing Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606093101.html)
    

Whether you pay for single or multiple vendors, you can view the following payment methods and statuses for reference:

-   [Canceling and Voiding Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0805062605.html)
    
-   [Bill Payment Statuses](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0807052012.html)
    
-   [Payment Remittance](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0804094536.html)
