**My license expired, can I process payments with HSBC Bank?**

No, you need a valid AP Automation license to process payments with HSBC Account. Please contact your account manager.

**My license is renewed, still I am facing license expired error when trying to process payments from HSBC Bank Account.**

Go to Setup > License Client > License Dashboard and check the **Status** of the **AP Automation** license in the License Dashboard.

If the status is **Expired** or **Stale**, manually fetch the license from License server. Go to Setup > License Client > Manual License Request to fetch the license. After the manual license request, renewed license details are shown in NetSuite SuiteApp Licenses. If the **AP Automation license** is active, the License Dashboard is updated with the **Active** status. Now you shall be able to process the payments. Please contact Customer Support if your license status is not updated.
