In BAI2 files, you'll find the transaction code in the second field of Record 16. For example:

![A section of a BAI2-formatted file.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Accounting/Banking/BSP_BTCBAI2.png)
