Transactions posted in HSBC bank accounts are imported daily from 4 AM to 7 AM PST.

#### To review automatically matched transactions: {#procedure_1006032037}

1.  Go to SuiteBanking > Payment Automation > Dashboard.
    
2.  On the left pane, click **Bank Account.**
    
3.  Click **Match Bank Data.**
    
4.  In the **Account** list, select HSBC Bank Account or HSBC Virtual Card.
    
5.  Click the **Review** subtab.
    

For more information, see [Bank Data Matching and Reconciliation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4842302228.html).
