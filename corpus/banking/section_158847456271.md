Go to the Banking Import History page (formerly Imported Statement List) to see the status and details of your imported bank transactions. For more information, see [Banking Import History](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159622181091.html).

The Match Bank Data page (Transactions > Bank > Match Bank Data) also gets updated with your account's latest bank data after each successful import. You can see your updated account balance and match or confirm your bank transaction data with your NetSuite transactions. For more information, see [Reconciling Bank and Credit Card Statements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_61161354663.html).

For more information about automatic bank data import through Financial Connectivity Plug-in, see [Automatic Bank Data Import](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157895109986.html).
