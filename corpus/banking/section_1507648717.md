Instead of deleting transaction reconciliation rules, you can temporarily make them inactive.

You cannot delete system rules, but you can inactivate them.

#### To inactivate reconciliation rules:

1.  Go to _Transactions > Bank > Reconciliation Rules_.
    
2.  Under the **Active** column, in the row for the matching or auto-create rule you want to inactivate, clear the box.
    
    NetSuite stops running the rule for subsequent imports, but the rule is still in the list.
    
3.  To activate the rule again, check the box.
