Delete a funds transfer if it was made in error.

Note:

You need the Deleted Records permission with full-level access to do this procedure.

#### To delete a funds transfer:

1.  Go to _Transactions > Bank > Transfer Funds > List_.
    
2.  From the Transfers list, click **Edit** next to the funds transfer you want to delete.
    
3.  On the Transfer page, under **Actions**, click **Delete**.
    
4.  At the prompt, click **OK** to confirm.
