When you use the Auto Bank Statement Import SuiteApp, keep these limitations in mind:

-   The SuiteApp doesn't support:
    
    -   IP address allowlisting
        
    -   Importing of bank statement files in ZIP format
        
    -   Importing of PGP (Pretty Good Privacy) encrypted files
        
-   The time zone you set for your SFTP connectivity might be different from the time zone used for automatic bank data imports.
    
    For more information, see [Automatic Bank Statement Files Import Schedule](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/93742)
    
-   To avoid performance issues, don't set up a bank data import with more than 10,000 transactions or 500 linked accounts.
