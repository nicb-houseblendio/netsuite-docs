You can link multiple bank accounts to a BILL account. You can choose to connect your bank account instantly or do it manually. If you choose to link your bank account manually, you need to complete the deposit verification process before you can use it for processing payments.

#### To add bank accounts

1.  Go to Payment Automation > Payment Dashboard > View.
    
2.  If you use a NetSuite OneWorld account, on the Subsidiary Dashboard, click **Enabled**. In the **Actions** column, click **View** to open the Payment Dashboard for a subsidiary.
    
3.  Click **Manage Bank Accounts** in the Bank Accounts portlet of the dashboard to view the Bank Accounts page.
    
    Alternatively, in the left panel, click **Settings** > **Bank Accounts** to view the Bank Accounts page.
    
4.  Click **Add**.
    
5.  Follow the instructions displayed on the screen and complete the MFA.
    
6.  If you choose to instantly link your bank account, follow the procedure described in [Linking Your Bank Account Instantly](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035348.html#subsect_0530040102).
    
7.  If you choose to manually link your bank account, follow the procedure described in [Linking Your Bank Account Manually](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035348.html#subsect_0530040114).
    
8.  If you manually link your bank account, follow the procedure described in [Deposit Verification](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035348.html#subsect_0530040142) to verify the bank account added.
    
9.  The bank accounts you add show in the list of linked bank accounts in BILL and NetSuite.
