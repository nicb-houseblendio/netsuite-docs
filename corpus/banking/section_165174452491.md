-   You can only add a maximum of 1000 chart of accounts for a single category. For more information, see [Adding Cash 360 Account Categories](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164871706068.html).
    
-   The SuiteApp supports up to one million transactions. If your NetSuite account exceeds this limit, forecasts may not display properly in the Cash 360 dashboard.
    
-   Additional values that are included in the cash forecast are shared across users within the same subsidiary. For more information about additional values, see [Setting Cash 360 Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164974435218.html).
    
-   In the inclusion of sales orders in cash forecasts, those with installments or subscription lines are treated as regular sales orders and only the total sales order amount is considered in forecast computations. Also, only standard payment terms are included; date-driven and installment payment terms are excluded.
    
-   In the inclusion of purchase orders in the cash forecasts, only purchase orders without installments or subscriptions are considered. Also, only standard payment terms are included; date-driven and installment payment terms are excluded.
    
-   The Opening Balance, Undeposited/Future-Period Funds, Accounts Receivable, Sales Orders, Sales Orders with Billing Schedules, Accounts Payable, and Purchase Orders windows can only display the 100 most recent transactions. To view more than 100 transactions, you can use the Export to CSV function.
    
-   When exporting records through CSV for the Opening Balance, Undeposited/Future-Period Funds, Accounts Receivable, Sales Orders, Sales Orders with Billing Schedules, Accounts Payable, Purchase Orders windows, there is a limit of 40,000 rows and a maximum file size of 10MB. It may take some time to export transactions that reach the transaction number limit or file size limit.
    
-   For sales orders with billing schedules, there may be a discrepancy of 0.01 in the open amount forecasts if the billing amounts per schedule are billed partially. For example, instead of 100, only 99.99 is included in the forecasts.
    
-   The SuiteApp supports line-level billing schedules. However, it does not support sales orders that include items with both billing schedules and items without billing schedules on the same sales order.
    
-   Forecast amounts may not be accurate for sales orders with billing schedules that have both Legacy Tax and discounts applied.
    
-   The navigation menus for the Cash 360 dashboard and Account Categories are only available in English.
    
-   You can access the Cash 360 SuiteApp only if your subsidiary is active.
