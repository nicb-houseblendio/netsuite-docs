Contact NetSuite Customer Support with any inquiries regarding Payment Automation, with the exception of virtual cards.

If needed, NetSuite Customer Support will pass the inquiry to HSBC for resolution of banking issues.

Contact Virtual Card Customer Support available 24/7 at 1-800-411-9264 with any queries regarding Virtual Card payments.

HSBC offices and HSBC help lines **cannot** provide assistance with Payment Automation issues.
