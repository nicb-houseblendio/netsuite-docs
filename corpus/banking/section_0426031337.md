**How can I update the names provided on Certificate of Banking Authority?**

Request to change the Certificate of Banking Authority must be submitted by an Authorized Person only. Please submit the request by contacting your NetSuite Support Team.

The request needs to originate from those authorized in the manner stated in the Certificate of Banking Authority. Please consult your internal company records for this information.
