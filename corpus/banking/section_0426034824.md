**What should I do if I disagree the monthly billing statement that I received for my Virtual Card account because of additional fees?**

Late fees are calculated at the rate of 3% of the outstanding balance. You can view your Virtual Card invoice and late fee details through the Payment Automation dashboard. For further assistance, call 1-800-411-9264 to reach a Virtual Card support specialist.
