**What should I do if I have been notified that there were insufficient funds in my account when the recipient tried to deposit the check?**

You will receive an email notification from HSBC that will include the check payment details being held because of insufficient funds in your HSBC Online Account, including a deadline of when the account must be funded to support settlement.

For instructions about how to make a one-time payment to fund your HSBC Online Account, see [Making Money Transfers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1123092904.html).

For rejected payments, you'll need to manually void the payment on your Payment Automation dashboard and submit a new payment. To void a payment, click the Payments tab on the left navigation pane, select a payment, and click **Void.**
