**What should I do if I want to request a change to the allocation of credit between my ACH and Virtual Card products because I expect to initiate more payments through ACH or Virtual Cards?**

Credit allocation between ACH and Virtual Card products can't be interchanged. You may request to decrease either ACH or Virtual Card credit limit by contacting NetSuite Customer Support.
