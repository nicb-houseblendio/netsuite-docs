**What should I do if I want to initiate a Virtual Card non-fraud dispute because I have a disagreement on the goods, services, or both, received with the recipient of the payment?**

Contact your supplier to review the items in dispute. If you are unable to resolve the issue, call 1-800-411-9264 to reach a Virtual Card support specialist who can conduct a formal investigation on your behalf.
