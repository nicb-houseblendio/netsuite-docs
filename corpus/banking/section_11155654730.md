**What are the functionalities of remittance notifications using Advanced PDF/HTML templates?**

Starting version 5.0, the Payment Automation SuiteApp will send email remittance notifications to vendors for ACH and check payments. You can resend notifications, track sent notifications, and include accurate payment details with vendor credits applied to bills.
