After [Submitting Details for the KYB or KYC Verification](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035100.html#subsect_0530035314), you need to link the bank accounts used for funding your bill payments.

Note:

The process of linking the bank account for funding payments with the BILL account is processed using the BILL widget. When you connect to the BILL widget, you may be asked to complete multi-factor authentication (MFA). Follow the instructions displayed on the screen to complete MFA. See [Generating MFA](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0813115942.html).

BILL uses [Plaid](https://plaid.com/what-is-plaid/), a third-party application for connecting with bank accounts, to [Linking Your Bank Account Instantly](#subsect_0530040102) through online banking and complete online verification. If your bank isn't listed for account linking using online banking or if you want to link your bank account manually, see [Linking Your Bank Account Manually](#subsect_0530040114). If you choose to link your bank account manually, you need to get it verified through the [Deposit Verification](#subsect_0530040142) process before you can use it for processing payments.

The bank account details that are first verified are considered as the default bank account in BILL. The default bank account is the account that is selected primarily for processing payments. You can change the account to be used when processing each payment. To know more about processing payments, see [Payment Processing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530033208.html).

You need to map a GL Bank Account in NetSuite for every bank account that you have linked in BILL. See [Mapping GL Bank Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035513.html) for more information.

After you link your bank accounts during the three-step sign up process, you can manage bank accounts using the BILL widget. See [Managing Bank Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606094105.html) for more information.

## Provide Your Bank with ACH ID Numbers {#subsect_0917014625}

Before linking your bank accounts for payments, give your bank the required ACH ID numbers. To process BILL ACH debits successfully, provide all of BILL's ACH ID numbers to your bank.

Without the required ACH ID numbers, a bank account with a debit block or filter can cause BILL transactions to fail. Share the ACH ID numbers with your bank to keep payments running smoothly.

## Linking Your Bank Account Instantly {#subsect_0530040102}

You can automatically link your bank account through online banking and complete online verification using Plaid.

#### To instantly link your bank account to BILL,

1.  Select **Instantly connect account** and click **Next**.
    
    1.  Click **Continue** to accept Plaid's privacy policy.
        
    2.  Enter your phone number and click **Continue** to sign up with Plaid or log in to your existing Plaid account. Click **Continue as guest** to skip signing up with Plaid or logging in to your existing Plaid account.
        
    3.  Select your bank from the options displayed on the screen. You can also search for your bank using the **Search** option. If your bank is not listed in the options displayed, you need to [Linking Your Bank Account Manually](#subsect_0530040114).
        
    4.  Click **Continue to login** in the screen that appears. You are redirected to your bank's login page in a new window.
        
    5.  Enter your credentials and log in to your bank.
        
    6.  Select the type of account (checking or savings) and click **Submit**.
        
        Note:
        
        Don't select **Cards** as the account type. Intelligent Payment Automation SuiteApp doesn't support card payments.
        
    7.  Select the accounts you want to link and click **Continue**. You can select more than one account at a time.
        
    8.  If you clicked **Continue as Guest** to link your bank account:
        
        -   Enter your phone number and click **Save** to create an account with Plaid.
            
        -   If you don't want to create an account with Plaid, click **Finish without saving**.
            
2.  Click **Continue** to be redirected to the Link Your Bank Account page.
    
3.  Enter the account holder's name for each added account and click **Add Account**.
    
4.  The selected bank accounts are now linked to your BILL account. You need to map the linked bank accounts to GL Bank Accounts. See [Mapping GL Bank Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035513.html) to know how you can map your linked bank accounts to GL Bank Accounts.
    

## Linking Your Bank Account Manually {#subsect_0530040114}

If your bank isn't listed for account linking using online banking or if you want to link your bank account manually, use this method. If you choose to link the account manually, you need to complete the deposit verification within 10 days. See [Deposit Verification](#subsect_0530040142) for more information.

If you have linked your bank account manually, you get a reminder complete the deposit verification in the **Reminders** portlet on the dashboard.

Important:

Only the person who manually linked a bank account can complete deposit verification for that account. No one else can complete the deposit verification for it.

#### To manually link your bank account to BILL,

1.  Select **Manually connect account** and click **Next**.
    
2.  On the Enter bank Details page, enter information in the following fields:
    
    -   Account Holder's Name
        
    -   Account Type
        
    -   Routing Number
        
    -   Bank Name
        
    -   Account Number
        
    -   Re-enter Account Number
        
3.  Click **Add Account**.
    
4.  The selected bank accounts are now linked to your BILL account. You need to map the linked bank accounts to GL Bank Accounts. See [Mapping GL Bank Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035513.html) to know how you can map your linked bank accounts to GL Bank Accounts.
    
5.  BILL deposits and withdraws a small amount less than one dollar to your bank account. You need to enter this amount to complete [Deposit Verification](#subsect_0530040142) and start making payments from the linked bank account.
    

### Deposit Verification {#subsect_0530040142}

After you submit the account details for manual linking, a small deposit is made by BILL to the account within one to two hours.

Important:

To allow BILL ACH debits to process successfully, provide your bank with all of BILL's ACH ID numbers. For an up to date list of BILL's ACH ID numbers, see [Provide Your Bank with ACH ID Numbers](#subsect_0917014625).

You have 10 days from the time you link the bank account manually to complete the deposit verification. If deposit verification isn't completed within 10 days, the bank account becomes inactive and you can't use it to make payments.

You need to enter the exact amount deposited to your bank account when you verify the deposit.

If your bank account becomes inactive because the verification window expired or you made three incorrect attempts, it cannot be used for payments.

If this was the only bank account you entered during the three-step sign-up and you want to use it, re-enter and verify it before making payments.

When you are adding another bank account, you can choose to [Linking Your Bank Account Instantly](#subsect_0530040102) or [Linking Your Bank Account Manually](#subsect_0530040114). If you enter new bank account details manually, you must complete deposit verification before you can use it to make payments.

#### To verify the deposit for the bank account added manually,

1.  Go to Payment Automation > Payment Dashboard > View.
    
2.  If you use OneWorld:
    
    1.  The **Subsidiary Dashboard** opens. Click **Enabled**.
        
    2.  In the Actions column, click **View** to open the Payment Dashboard for the selected su bsidiary.
        
    
    If you do not use OneWorld, the **Payment Dashboard** opens directly.
    
3.  Click **Verify Deposit** for the bank account you added in the **Bank Accounts** portlet.
    
4.  In the **Verify Deposit** window, enter the exact amount deposited by BILL in the **Amount** field and click **Verify**.
    
    Important:
    
    If you enter the amount deposited by BILL incorrectly multiple times, the bank account becomes inactive and you can't use it for payments. See [Viewing Inactive Bank Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0617045744.html).
    
5.  The bank account is now verified and you can make payments from this bank account. See [Paying a Single Vendor Through BILL](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606092816.html), [Creating a Payment Run](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606092833.html) for more information.
    
6.  If you enter the deposit amount from BILL incorrectly three times, click **Cancel** to exit the window.
