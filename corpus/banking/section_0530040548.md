-   Intelligent Payment Automation can only be installed and used in a NetSuite production account. You can't use Intelligent Payment Automation features in NetSuite sandbox, development, or Release Preview accounts.
    
-   Only companies with U.S. business subsidiaries can use this SuiteApp to pay vendors in the United States. The SuiteApp is not supported in Canada, China, or Japan.
    
-   You can only make bill payments in U.S. Dollars (USD).
    
-   Intelligent Payment Automation with BILL can't be processed using the standard Pay Bills page, which you can access through Transactions > Payables > Pay Bills.
    
-   Bill payments can only be made by checking the **Pay using Intelligent Payment Automation** box on each record. Other methods, such as importing payments using CSV into NetSuite, are not supported.
    
-   A maximum of 35 transactions, including bills and installments, can be selected per vendor payment.
    
-   A maximum of 10,000 transactions, including bills and installments, can be processed per payment run for all vendors, combining selected bank and AP accounts.
    
-   You can submit up to 600 payments to BILL at a time. If there are more than 600 payments, submit them in multiple batches.
    
-   Any discounts applied to your payments are only captured in NetSuite and don't appear in the payment remittance sent to vendors.
    
-   If the vendor credit is fully applied to a payment and the total payment amount is zero, the payment is only recorded in NetSuite. The vendor credit transaction and the bills where this credit is applied don't appear in the payment remittance sent to vendors.
    
-   Reference Numbers that exceed 50 letters and numbers are truncated to 50 characters.
    
-   Bill or Credit Memos that exceed 4,000 letters and numbers are truncated to 4,000 characters.
    
-   Bill Payment Memos that exceed 70 letters and numbers are truncated to 70 characters.
    
-   Remittance emails can't be customized.
