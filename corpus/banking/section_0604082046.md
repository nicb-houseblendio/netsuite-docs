Within Intelligent Payment Automation (IPA), you can automate bill payments for qualified U.S. vendors that you sync with BILL. If you have multiple U.S. subsidiaries, note that bills for synced vendors may be associated with subsidiaries that have and do not have BILL accounts. For subsidiaries with no BILL accounts, you cannot use Intelligent Payment Automation to pay bills even for synced vendors.

At any time, you can create or update U.S. vendors, including their bank details, through the following ways:

-   [Creating Vendors for Intelligent Payment Automation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0729012011.html)
    
-   [Importing Vendors and Vendor Bank Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0729011708.html)
    

For migrated vendor bank accounts, see [Switching and Migrating to Intelligent Payment Automation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1003021504.html).

## Vendor Syncing with BILL {#subsect_0905023643}

For subsidiaries with approved and active BILL accounts, automatic syncing for associated vendors occurs every day at 5 p.m. Pacific Time (UTC - 8). For each U.S. vendor, syncing includes new and updated addresses and any available bank details. Vendor records are initially submitted for syncing through one of the following ways:

-   For newly set up U.S. subsidiaries, when you have existing non-synced vendors with an open payable balance
    
-   When you create vendor records
    
-   When you import vendors
    

Note:

You won't be able to update vendor details when the payment status is one of the following:

-   For Submission
    
-   Scheduled
    
-   Processing
    

You can make updates after the payment has been sent.

On a vendor record, NetSuite adds a Vendor Profile Information subrecord after its initial syncing, regardless of the result or status. On this record, the **Pay with Intelligent Payment Automation** box is automatically checked to enable payment processing with BILL. For more information about this record, see [Creating Vendors for Intelligent Payment Automation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0729012011.html).

To prepare vendors for initial syncing or resyncing, be sure to update the vendor details according to the qualification criteria. See [Vendor Qualification Criteria](#subsect_0905024201).

Without waiting for the next syncing schedule, you can manually submit vendors for syncing. For instructions and requirements, see [Manually Syncing Vendors with BILL](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0905031506.html).

From the Payments and Vendors section of the dashboard, you can view the syncing status for each vendor. See [Tracking Vendor Syncing and Network Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0729011519.html).

## Excluding Vendors from Intelligent Payment Automation {#subsect_0730110127}

You can exclude a vendor from Intelligent Payment Automation when you don't want the vendor's payables to be included in automated payment processing.

#### To exclude a vendor from Intelligent Payment Automation:

1.  Go to Lists > Relationships > Vendors.
    
2.  Open the vendor record.
    
3.  Click the **Intelligent Payment Automation** subtab.
    
4.  Check the **Exclude Vendor from Intelligent Payment Automation** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

In OneWorld accounts, this setting applies across all subsidiaries associated with the vendor.

Important:

You cannot exclude a vendor that has a payment or payment run with a Pending Approval status. Reject or complete the payment or payment run before you exclude the vendor.

When you check the **Exclude Vendor from Intelligent Payment Automation** box, IPA:

-   Removes the vendor and the vendor's open bills from the Vendors and Payables pages.
    
-   Removes the vendor's outstanding balance and bill count from IPA portlets.
    
-   Displays the vendor on the Excluded Vendors subtab of the Vendors page.
    
-   Archives an existing vendor profile in BILL.
    

Excludes the vendor from scheduled synchronization jobs.

## Vendor Qualification Criteria {#subsect_0905024201}

Only vendors that meet the following qualification criteria can be automatically or manually synced with BILL:

-   The **Exclude Vendor from Intelligent Payment Automation** box must be clear on the vendor record.
    
-   Subsidiaries must have an active BILL account and doesn't have a rejected BILL application.
    
    To view your subsidiaries, see the help topic [Subsidiary Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530040413.html).
    
-   Vendors must be within the United States and have a valid U.S. address. Be sure to enter valid entries for the following:
    
    -   Street address (enter this information in the **Address 1** field)
        
    -   U.S. city
        
    -   U.S. state
        
    -   U.S. zip code with length of five to ten digits
        
        For more information about creating vendor records, see [Creating Vendors for Intelligent Payment Automation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0729012011.html).
        
-   Vendor details must be updated correctly in NetSuite, including the postal address and contact information.
    
    If you add bank details to the record, they must be complete and accurate too. To validate bank accounts, BILL sends a deposit of 0.01 USD and debits the same amount. It blocks the bank account if either of these transactions fails.
    
-   Vendors must have an opening balance greater than zero.
    
    To verify the account, a deposit of 0.01 USD is made. If a debit block prevents BILL from withdrawing the 0.01 USD amount, the vendor keeps it. This process helps identify accounts with default debit blocks and ensures smooth future transactions.
    

If vendors fail to qualify, be sure to check and update any missing details before you try to resync. To update vendor details and view balances, see [Creating Vendors for Intelligent Payment Automation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0729012011.html). For more information about syncing, see [Vendor Syncing with BILL](#subsect_0905023643).

## BILL Accounts and BILL Network for Vendors {#subsect_0905024857}

The BILL network is a collection of customers and vendors that use the BILL platform to transact with one another. Vendors connected to the BILL network can choose their preferred mode of payment, update their own contact and payment information, and get automatic status updates from BILL. See [BILL network frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://help.bill.com/direct/s/article/4413952049293) for more information about the BILL network and how it works.

A vendor record in BILL is created for each synced vendor. After a record is created, further verification is done to determine whether a vendor has a BILL account and is part of the BILL network. Email requests may be sent a vendor based on the following scenarios.

Note:

BILL uses the vendor company name and other vendor record details to search for matches with BILL accounts, BILL network, and Verified National Vendor directory. Be sure to keep these vendor details up to date.

Also, statuses in BILL get updated on a weekly basis. Status changes that occur after an update reflect in the Vendor Profile Information record after the next update.

For more information about vendor details and statuses, see [Tracking Vendor Syncing and Network Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0729011519.html).

-   If a vendor doesn't have a BILL account, an email is sent requesting them to create a BILL account and join the BILL network.
    
    Your updates to vendors with no BILL accounts automatically reflect on their BILL vendor record.
    
-   If a vendor has a BILL account but is not part of the BILL network, an email is sent requesting them to join the BILL network.
    
    Your updates to vendors with BILL accounts but not connected to the BILL network automatically reflect on their BILL vendor record.
    
    If a vendor chooses to be part of the BILL network, their details are managed by BILL. Your updates do not reflect on their BILL vendor record.
    

If vendors are part of the BILL network, they may also be identified as a Verified National Vendor. In this case, the verified national vendor information for each vendor is updated in their NetSuite vendor record. Verified national vendors can include water, power, cable, phone, electric, and other well-known companies. These businesses are often part of a verified national vendor directory. Matching to a vendor in this directory connects you immediately, allowing you to pay electronically. See [Verified national vendor ePayment setup](https://help.bill.com/direct/s/article/115005307563) for more information about Verified National Vendors and how they are managed by BILL.

To view payment options available to vendors within the BILL network or identified as a Verified National Vendor, see [Bill Payment Options](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606092711.html).

### Manage Vendor Auto-Connect to BILL {#subsect_1028091010}

Auto-connect allows BILL to automatically link vendors to the BILL network. If your organization needs to limit automatic vendor connections, you can request an exception to disable auto-connect. Contact NetSuite customer support to request this exception.

Even with auto-connect enabled, you can review vendor sync status in BILL to align with your organization's requirements and records. You can also contact NetSuite customer support to request disconnecting a vendor from the BILL network.
