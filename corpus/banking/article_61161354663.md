Note:

This section pertains to the redesigned reconciliation feature. The original reconciliation feature isn't supported as of NetSuite 2021.1. If you're still using it and don't want to switch yet, see [Bank Account Reconciliation and Reporting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1552053.html). To use the redesigned reconciliation feature, see [Editing Accounts to Use the Match Bank Data and Reconcile Account Statement Pages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_20260810001.html).

To reconcile your bank or credit card statement, from beginning to end:

-   If necessary, import your bank or credit card data. See [Bank Data Import](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1550803.html).
    
    Note:
    
    You can clear general ledger transactions without matching imported bank transactions, so this import step is not required.
    
-   For an imported bank transaction that is a full payment for one open invoice or one open payable, use a payment application suggestion. See [Creating, Applying, and Matching Payments for Open Invoices Using Payment Application Suggestions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_202608110103.html) or [Creating, Applying, and Matching Payments for Open Payables Using Payment Application Suggestions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_202608110104.html).
    
-   If an imported bank transaction does not have a suitable payment application suggestion, you can manually create a customer payment or bill payment from the **Transactions to Match** subtab. See [Creating Customer Payments from Transactions to Match](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_202608110105.html) or [Creating Bill Payments from Transactions to Match](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_202608110106.html).
    
-   Use Automated Cash Application for accounts receivable workflows that require partial application, multiple invoices, invoice IDs, or customer-mapping flexibility. The feature generates customer payments, applies them to open invoices, and automatically matches and clears them. See [Generating Customer Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_38152706945.html).
    
-   Review match suggestions for imported bank transactions. If an imported bank transaction does not have a suitable suggestion, use the **Transactions to Match** subtab to manually match it or to clear a general ledger transaction without a matching imported bank transaction. See [Matching Bank Data](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_32131451402.html). NetSuite automatically submits matched or cleared transactions for reconciliation.
    
-   Reconcile the transactions. See [Reconciling Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_20260810009.html).
