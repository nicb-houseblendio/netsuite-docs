**My vendor is claiming a balance that is overdue. When will the payment be sent?**

Your vendor should contact your accounts payable department to discuss the status of the payment. For more information, see [Virtual Card Statuses](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1006030901.html#bridgehead_41125409261).

Virtual cards are usually sent to vendors within 15 minutes after the payment has been sent for processing. Vendors should check their spam or junk email folders to ensure they didn't receive the remittance emails.

Remittance emails may arrive from this email address:

-   no-reply@payment-automation.netsuite.com
