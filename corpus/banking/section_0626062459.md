Sign up for a BILL account by completing the following three-step process.

Note:

You can't use or sign up with existing BILL accounts that are not created within NetSuite. To use Intelligent Payment Automation, be sure to sign up and create a BILL account within NetSuite.

If you use NetSuite OneWorld, the sign up process must be completed for each subsidiary that uses Intelligent Payment Automation for making vendor payments. You can only complete the three-step sign up process for active subsidiaries in NetSuite. See [Multi-Subsidiary Support](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530040533.html).

Go to Payment Automation > Payment Dashboard > View to begin the sign up process.

If you use NetSuite OneWorld, the first time you access Payment Dashboard, the Sign Up page opens. Click Start Setup to open the Subsidiary Dashboard and begin setup. On subsequent access, the Subsidiary Dashboard opens directly.

If you do not use NetSuite OneWorld, the **Sign Up** page opens. Click **Start Setup** to begin the three-step sign up process.

Complete the following steps:

1.  [Step 1 Create a BILL Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035100.html)
    
2.  [Step 2 Link Your Bank Account for Funding Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035348.html) and [Mapping GL Bank Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035513.html)
    
3.  (Optional) [Step 3 Set Up Payment Run Approval Routing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035733.html)
    

After you complete the three-step sign up process, qualified vendors with open balances are automatically synced with BILL for making payments. You can also sync your vendors manually through the **Sync Vendors with BILL** option. See [Vendor Setup and Management for Intelligent Payment Automation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0604082046.html).
