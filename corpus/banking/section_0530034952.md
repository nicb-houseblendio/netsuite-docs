NetSuite requires enrollment to use Intelligent Payment Automation. Authorized signatories must sign the Estimate/Order Form electronically. This action must be completed only for your first time signing up. If you have a NetSuite OneWorld account, you don't need to complete this action for each subsidiary.

Note:

The primary contact does not need to sign the Estimate/Order Form again if the customer meets one of the following conditions:

-   The customer has an active Intelligent Payment Automation SuiteApp license.
    
-   The customer has a sales order with a future contract end date.
    

For Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) customers, the parent account's primary contact must sign the Estimate/Order Form.

If you have a NetSuite OneWorld account, you don't need to complete this action for each subsidiary.

Employees with the Administrator role, CFO role, or a custom role with the required permissions can review the Estimate/Order Form. After reviewing the form, click **Share with Primary Contact** to send the form to the primary contact for signature.

The following procedures apply when the Estimate/Order Form requires a signature.

#### To review the documents:

1.  Go to Payment Automation > Payment Dashboard > View.
    
2.  If you use OneWorld:
    
    1.  The **Subsidiary Dashboard** opens. Click **Enabled**.
        
    2.  In the Actions column, click **View** to open the Payment Dashboard for the selected subsidiary.
        
    
    If you do not use OneWorld, the **Payment Dashboard** opens directly.
    
3.  Click the **Estimate/Order Form** link to review the unsigned document.
    
4.  After you have reviewed the document, click **Share with Primary Contact**.
    
5.  On the popup window, click **Send** to send it to the primary contact's email address.
    
    Optionally, you can customize the Subject and Message of the email.
    
    Alternatively, click **Copy a Link** to share it with the primary contact through other means of communication.
    

Only the primary contact for your NetSuite account, as authorized signatory, can sign the Estimate/Order Form.

Note:

To update primary contact details, contact your account manager. After an update, it may take up to an hour for changes to appear. The system refreshes primary contact details every hour until the **Estimate/Order Form** is signed. Electronic signing is integrated with DocuSign.

#### To sign and accept the documents:

1.  As the primary contact, you must log in to NetSuite with the Administrator role or custom role with required permissions.
    
    To review the permissions, see [Sign the Estimate/Order Form Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0802124513.html#subsect_0802012820).
    
2.  Go to Payment Automation > Payment Dashboard > View.
    
3.  Check the **By checking this box, I confirm that I am authorized to enter into contracts on behalf of.** box to open the document using DocuSign.
    
    1.  Review the document in DocuSign, agree to the usage of electronic records and signature, and follow the prompts on the screen to sign it.
        
    2.  Click **Finish**. An email is sent with details of how you can view and download the signed Estimate/Order Form.
        
4.  After you electronically sign the document, do one of the following:
    
    -   Click **Start Setup** to proceed to [Step 1 Create a BILL Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035100.html).
        
    -   Click **Invite Teammate to Setup** to send an invite to someone else to start the application for a BILL account. On the popup window, click **Send** to send it to the employee's email address. Optionally, you can customize the **Subject** and **Message** of the email.
        
        Alternatively, click **Copy a Link** to share it through other means of communication.
        

Employees who receive the invite must to log in to NetSuite and click **Start Setup** to proceed to [Step 1 Create a BILL Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035100.html).

#### To view the signed and accepted documents:

1.  Go to Payment Automation > Payment Dashboard > View.
    
2.  If you use OneWorld:
    
    1.  The **Subsidiary Dashboard** opens. Click **Enabled**.
        
    2.  In the Actions column, click **View** to open the Payment Dashboard for the selected subsidiary.
        
    
    If you do not use OneWorld, the **Payment Dashboard** opens directly.
    
3.  In the left panel, click **Settings** , and then click **Documents and Statements**.
    
4.  Click the **Estimate/Order Form** link to open the document on a new tab.
