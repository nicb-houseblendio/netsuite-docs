The Bank Feeds SuiteApp's automated bank data import feature helps you manage your bank and credit card transactions in NetSuite more efficiently.

Track your daily import status on the Banking Import History page. The SuiteApp also lets you update your imported bank data manually on the Match Bank Data page.
