Currently, the AMEX Corporate Card Integration SuiteApp to automate AMEX corporate card charge imports and a custom connectivity plug-in to configure a direct connection to another provider are supported. See [American Express Integration for Expense Reporting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159122395131.html) and [Importing Corporate Card Data](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162885823036.html). If you want to import corporate card charges from another provider, you can use the Financial Institution Parser Plug-in. For more information, see [Importing Data Using a Financial Institution Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1556568135.html).
