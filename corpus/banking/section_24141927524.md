**What to do in case of an urgent payment and the daily limit is exceeded?**

If no payments are pending approval and the daily limit is exceeded, then no payments can be processed for that day. You can pay the vendor using a virtual card, provided the vendor accepts payments in virtual cards. Otherwise, create the payment for the next business day.

**How to approve a payment when it is pending approval and the daily limit is exceeded?**

Edit the payment and change the payment date to the next business day.

**What to do in case the outgoing limit is exceeded and a money request withdrawal request cannot be initiated?**

Wait until the next business day to initiate the withdrawal request.

**What to do in case you are unable to approve the payment batch created from bulk bills with limit exceeded error?**

Edit the payment batch and change the payment date to a future date, or remove some bills, or reject the batch.
