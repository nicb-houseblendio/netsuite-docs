For information about ACH processing fees and check processing fees, check the 'HSBC Terms and Charges' section within the Oracle NetSuite Platform HSBC Terms PDF file provided by HSBC [Embedded Banking Useful Documents and Links](https://www.business.us.hsbc.com/en/embedded-banking-download-center).

No fee is associated with virtual card payments.

For information about what happens in case of insufficient funds in the HSBC Online Account and how funds move after a payment is submitted, see [Auto-Funding](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1006104800.html).

For billing schedules, see [Statement and Auto-Debit Payment Schedules](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1013073503.html).
