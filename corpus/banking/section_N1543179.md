Print slips for cash and check deposits. Print a deposit slip for an individual deposit, or queue multiple slips and print them together.

Deposit slips print on printer paper and don't require special forms. They're printed as PDFs from your local or network printer. Download the latest Adobe Reader for free from the [Adobe website](http://www.adobe.com/products/acrobat.html).

To customize the fields on your deposit slips, go to _Customization > Forms > Transaction Forms_. Click the deposit slip name you want to customize and complete the fields as needed. For more information, see [Creating Custom Entry and Transaction Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2853340.html).

You can also order deposit slips from [http://www.netsuiteforms.com](http://www.netsuiteforms.com). There are two layouts, both 378L in correct alignment.

Note:

You can't customize the printed deposit slip layout.

## Printing a Single Deposit Slip {#bridgehead_N1543206}

Print a single deposit slip when you enter a deposit at _Transactions > Bank > Make Deposits_. After entering all deposit information, click **Save & Print**, and then click **Print** in the Adobe application.

Print deposit slips you entered previously by going to _Transactions > Bank > Make Deposits > List_. Click **Print** next to the deposit slip you want to print.

## Printing Multiple Deposit Slips {#bridgehead_N1543222}

If you enter multiple deposits before printing, you can print all slips at one time by checking the **To Be Printed** box for each deposit.

#### To print multiple deposit slips: {#procedure_N1543235}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Management > Print Checks and Forms_.
    
2.  Click **Deposits**.
    
3.  On the Deposits page, select the **Account** to print deposit slips for.
    
4.  Check the box next to each deposit you want to print, or click **Mark All** to print all listed deposits.
    
5.  Click **Print**.
    
    -   If you din't enable the Download PDF Files preference, a preview of your deposits opens in Adobe Reader.
        
    -   If you enabled the Download PDF Files preference, save your deposits first, and then open them with Adobe Reader.
        
6.  Click **Print** in Adobe Reader.
    
7.  To print multiple copies, enter the number in the print message.
    
8.  Click **OK**.
