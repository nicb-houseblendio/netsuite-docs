**If I close my virtual card before my anniversary date, will I still get credit for the annual statement credit?**

You won't be eligible to receive credits for the annual statement credit if you decide to close your HSBC Virtual Card Account before the card's anniversary date. To ensure you understand the conditions for receiving your annual statement credit, refer to section 3 of the Pricing Addendum for HSBC Virtual Cards offered through Oracle NetSuite AP Automation, which is a part of your HSBC Terms.
