The BAI2 parser plug-in recognizes default and standard BAI2 bank transaction codes and supports account total and record count import validation. The SuiteApp also lets you set the decimal precision for the BAI2 parser plug-in.

The following topics show how you can set up your BAI2 bank transaction parser:
