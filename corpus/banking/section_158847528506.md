The Bank Feeds SuiteApp uses the Financial Institution Connectivity Plug-in to automatically retrieve bank data and import it into your NetSuite account. When a format profile is saved for the first time, take note of the following:

-   NetSuite downloads the last 60 calendar days of available bank data from your financial institution. You can change the earliest transaction date to import in the Account Linking subtab in your format profile. For more information, see [Linking Accounts for Financial Institution Connectivity](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157894482115.html)).
    
-   NetSuite creates a schedule to automatically import your data. Every day, NetSuite imports any new data from your financial institution to your accounts on the Match Bank Data page. If the data import fails, NetSuite requests for data starting from the last successful update plus seven calendar days back.
    
    For more information how to match your imported bank lines with account transactions in NetSuite, see [Matching Bank Data](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_32131451402.html).
    

You can check your import status on the Banking Import History page. To get to the Banking Import History page, go to Transactions > Bank > Banking Import History.

For more information, see [Automatic Bank Data Import](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157895109986.html).
