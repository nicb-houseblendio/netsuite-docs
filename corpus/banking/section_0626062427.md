The Payment Dashboard shows payment activity and provides access to key areas for processing and tracking payments.

From the Payment Dashboard, you can:

-   Access the **Payment Processing** section.
    
-   Access the **Payments and Vendors** section.
    
-   Access **Billing Statements**.
    
-   For OneWorld accounts, view dashboard details for a specific subsidiary.
    
-   Save supported Intelligent Payment Automation pages as browser bookmarks.
    

Important:

Rejected applications or subsidiaries deactivated by BILL cannot access the dashboard. For more information, see [Viewing Rejected Subsidiaries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530040413.html#subsect_0423032104).

If you use multiple subsidiaries, complete the following tasks for each subsidiary to view the Payment Dashboard:

1.  Set up subsidiaries for Intelligent Payment Automation. See [Multi-Subsidiary Support](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530040533.html).
    
2.  Assign employees to subsidiaries. See [Assigning a Subsidiary to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N276604.html).
    

## Bookmarking Payment Dashboard Pages {#subsect_0730115017}

You can save the following Intelligent Payment Automation pages as browser bookmarks and return directly to the saved page:

-   **Payment Dashboard**
    
-   **Payment Processing**
    
    -   Payables
        
    -   Payment Runs
        
    -   Payments to Submit
        
-   **Payments and Vendors pages**
    
-   **Settings**
    
    -   Preferences
        
    -   Bank Accounts
        
    -   Payment Run Approval Routing
        
-   **Documents and Statements**
    

If you open a bookmark without an active NetSuite session, log in when prompted. After successful authentication, NetSuite opens the saved page.

For OneWorld accounts, a bookmark includes the subsidiary context, so the page opens for the correct subsidiary. You can save a separate bookmark for each subsidiary and page you use.

Note:

Your existing role, page permissions, and subsidiary access still apply to bookmarked pages. If you don't have access, or you don't have the required permission for a subsidiary, NetSuite displays the an error message.

## Viewing the Payment Dashboard {#subsect_0730115429}

From the Payment Dashboard, you can open the Payments or Vendors page for any subsidiary that you have access to.

Note:

By default, the Administrator, Payment Manager, and Payment Clerk roles have preconfigured permissions to access the dashboard. For standard or custom roles and permission requirements, see [Setting Up Intelligent Payment Automation Roles and Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0531124014.html).

#### To view the Payment Dashboard:

1.  Go to Payment Automation > Payment Dashboard > View.
    
2.  Do one of the following:
    
    -   If you use Oneworld:
        
        1.  The first time you access Intelligent Payment Automation, the Sign Up page opens. Click **Start** to begin the setup.
            
        2.  On subsequent access, the Subsidiary Dashboard opens directly.
            
        3.  Click **Enabled**.
            
        4.  In the **Actions** column, click **View** for the subsidiary.
            
        
        The Payment Dashboard opens for the selected subsidiary.
        
    -   If you do not use OneWorld:
        
        If setup is complete, the Payment Dashboard opens directly. If setup is not complete, the Sign Up page opens. Click **Start Setup** to begin setup.
        
3.  If you use OneWorld, you can switch subsidiaries from the Payment Dashboard. At the top of the left panel, click the current subsidiary name. Then, select another subsidiary that you can access from the list.
    

## Dashboard Portlets {#subsect_0423023020}

The Payment Dashboard includes the following portlets:

-   [Recommendations Portlet](#subsect_0707033608) - Shows pending actions for payments, bank account, and preferences setup.
    
-   [Payables Portlet](#subsect_0707033716) - Shows outstanding balances and links to bills.
    
-   [Reminders Portlet](#subsect_0707033634) - Shows tasks that require attention.
    
-   [Bank Accounts Portlet](#subsect_0707033832) - Shows bank account details and status.
    
-   [Account Register Portlet](#subsect_0707033916) - Shows GL balances for the selected bank account and recent transactions.
    

## Recommendations Portlet {#subsect_0707033608}

The Recommendations portlet appears when actions are required. Depending on the status of your setup, you can do any of the following:

-   **Link Bank Accounts**
    
    -   Administrators and Payment Managers can click **Add Bank Account**. See [Step 2 Link Your Bank Account for Funding Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035348.html) for more information.
        
    -   Payment Clerks can view notifications only.
        
-   **Verify Deposit**
    
    -   Administrators or Payment Managers can click **Verify Deposit**
        
    -   Note:
        
        Only the user who added a bank account manually can verify the deposit.
        
    -   Payment Clerks can only view notifications when a bank account needs to be verified.
        
    
    For more information, see [Deposit Verification](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035348.html#subsect_0530040142).
    
-   **Map GL Account**
    
    Click **Map GL Bank Account** to link the account to a GL account.
    
-   **Make Your First Payment**
    
    -   Administrators or Payment Managers can click **Notify AP Clerk** to send a reminder make the first payment.
        
    -   Payment Clerks can click **Pay Bills** to initiate the first payment.
        
        For more information, see [Paying a Single Vendor Through BILL](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606092816.html).
        
-   **Set Up Preferences**
    
    Payment Clerks and Payment Managers can configure automatic recording of BILL transaction fees. This recommendation appears when automatic transaction fee recording is not configured.
    

## Payables Portlet {#subsect_0707033716}

The Payables portlet shows outstanding bills:

-   Due in 7 days
    
-   Due in more than 7 days
    
-   Overdue
    

It also shows the Total Bill balance of the outstanding bills.

Click a status to view bills and initiate payments. Click **Pay Bills** to view all bills.

## Reminders Portlet {#subsect_0707033634}

The Reminders portlet shows payment-related tasks. Depending on your role and approval level, you can view:

-   Payments Pending Approval
    
-   Payment Runs Pending for Approval
    
-   Outstanding Checks
    
-   Payments to Submit
    

Click a link to open the related records or transactions.

## Bank Accounts Portlet {#subsect_0707033832}

The Bank Accounts portlet shows all the bank accounts linked to BILL, including unverified bank accounts.

If you are an Administrators or Payment Manager, you can:

-   **Manage Bank Accounts** - Open the Bank Accounts page to add, edit, or delete accounts. For instructions, see [Managing Bank Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606094105.html).
    
-   **Verify Deposit** - Verify deposits for manually linked accounts. For instructions, see [Deposit Verification](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035348.html#subsect_0530040142).
    
-   **Map GL Account** - Map a bank account to a NetSuite GL account. For instructions, see [Mapping GL Bank Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530035513.html).
    

## Account Register Portlet {#subsect_0707033916}

The Account Register portlet shows the ledger balance for the GL account mapped to a bank account.

To view or filter the details:

-   In the **Bank Account** filter, select a bank account.
    
    The system shows the balance and transactions for the past month.
    
-   (Optional) In the **Date** filter, enter a start date and end date.
    

To view details:

-   In the **Payee** column, click a vendor name to open the vendor record.
    
-   In the **Number** column, click a document number to open the Bill Payment page.
