After a payment is processed, BILL sends an email to the vendor confirming that the payment has been processed.

If the vendor is part of the BILL network, the email is sent to the vendor remittance email updated in BILL.

If the vendor isn't part of the BILL network, the email is sent to the vendor remittance email updated in NetSuite. See [Creating Vendors for Intelligent Payment Automation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0729012011.html) to know how to update the vendor remittance email for vendors that aren't part of the BILL network.

Note that the email shows the official email address of the sender: **OracleNetSuite-PaymentAutomation@bill.com**.

![BILL email](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Accounting/Banking/Bill_email_mockup.png)

![Sample check payment with remittance details for a vendor.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Accounting/Banking/IPA_check_image.png)

The following image shows a sample envelope for a check payment:

![Sample payment envelope for a vendor check payment.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Accounting/Banking/IPA_check_envelope_image.png)

Note:

Any discounts applied to your payments are only captured in NetSuite and won't appear in the payment remittance sent to vendors.
