**What should I do if I want to make a one-time payment to fund my HSBC Online Account?**

If you have a pending outgoing payment that is unable to be processed due to funding that has been canceled or returned after the payment was executed on your HSBC Online account, you should transfer funds to the account. For instructions, see [Making Money Transfers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1123092904.html).
