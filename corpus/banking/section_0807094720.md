**How will I be notified when a Virtual Card is assigned to an invoice?**

If your server is secure, each email remittance will include all the information needed to process the virtual card payment: the expiration date, card verification code (CVC), invoice details, and the 16-digit virtual card number.

If your server is NOT secure because the transport layer security (TLS) protocol is not used, the virtual card number will be masked in the email remittance.

You will be asked to register at [https://www.csipaysystems.com/payee/auth](https://www.csipaysystems.com/payee/auth). You must register using the email address that the remittance was sent to. This is a one-time registration process, and you will use your login credentials to receive all future payments.

After you log in to the website, you will have access the information needed to process your virtual card payment, including invoice details, the 16-digit virtual card number, expiration date, and card verification code (CVC).
