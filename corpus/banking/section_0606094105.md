From the dashboard, the Bank Accounts page enables you to link additional bank accounts, and manage and delete bank accounts already linked with BILL.

Important:

After you add bank accounts, click **Verify your identity** in the Payment methods widget of the Bank Accounts page to start processing payments.

#### To view the Bank Accounts page:

1.  Go to Payment Automation > Payment Dashboard > View.
    
2.  If you use a NetSuite OneWorld account, on the Subsidiary Dashboard, click **Enabled**. In the **Actions** column, click **View** to open the Payment Dashboard for a subsidiary.
    
3.  Click **Manage Bank Accounts** in the Bank Accounts portlet of the dashboard to view the Bank Accounts page.
    
    Alternatively, in the left panel, click **Settings** > **Bank Accounts** to view the Bank Accounts page.
    
4.  When you perform any action in the Bank Accounts page, you need to complete multi-factor authentication (MFA). Follow the instructions displayed on the screen to complete MFA. You can perform the following tasks from the Bank Accounts page:
    
    -   [Adding Bank Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606094141.html)
        
    -   [Updating Bank Account Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0617073518.html)
        
    -   [Deleting Linked Bank Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606094201.html)
        
    -   [Changing the Default Bank Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606094221.html)
        
    -   [Viewing Inactive Bank Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0617045744.html)
        
    
    Note:
    
    All changes made in the BILL widget is automatically updated in NetSuite. This includes adding, deleting, or updating bank accounts and changing default bank account.
