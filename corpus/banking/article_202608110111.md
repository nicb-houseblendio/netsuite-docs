When the **Automatically Accept Exact Matches** preference is enabled, NetSuite automatically accepts eligible bank and credit card match suggestions. The preference is enabled by default.

Eligible suggestions include transaction-match and match-group suggestions generated by system and user reconciliation rules. NetSuite can generate these suggestions during bank data import or when you manually run reconciliation rules.

The **Automatically Accept Exact Matches** preference applies to all supported bank and credit card accounts in your account. Administrators can turn off the preference on the Accounting Preferences page.

When you turn off the preference, eligible suggestions remain available for manual review and submission on the **Match Suggestions** subtab. The same occurs if you do not have the required **Reconcile** permission when you import bank data or run reconciliation rules.

Suggestions that require review or input remain available for manual review and submission on the **Match Suggestions** subtab. These include:

-   Entity-enriched suggestions
    
-   Suggestions with multiple match options
    
-   Transaction Matching Assistant suggestions. These suggestions are available only to accounts eligible for NetSuite Next. NetSuite Next is being made available to customers in phases and may not yet be available in your account. You receive in-app notifications when your account is eligible for NetSuite Next.
    
-   Accounts payable or accounts receivable payment-application suggestions
    
-   Auto-create suggestions
