The MT940 parser plug-in recognizes standard SWIFT transaction bank codes and supports using a period (.) or comma (,) as a decimal separator. The plug-in also decodes remittance information from the bank and display it in a more readable format.

The following topics show how you can set up your MT940 bank transaction parser:

-   [Configuring MT940 Parser](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1107041103.html)
