User Verification is a one-time process where you need to verify your identity and confirm that you're authorized to make payments from the listed bank accounts. If you add more bank accounts or verify a bank account that was pending deposit verification, you may need to complete this process again.

#### To complete user verification:

1.  Go to Payment Automation > Payment Dashboard > View.
    
2.  If you use OneWorld:
    
    1.  The **Subsidiary Dashboard** opens. Click **Enabled**.
        
    2.  In the Actions column, click **View** to open the Payment Dashboard for the selected subsidiary.
        
    
    If you do not use OneWorld, the **Payment Dashboard** opens directly.
    
3.  In the left panel, under Settings, click **User Verification**.
    
4.  On the User Verification page, click **Verify Identity** to verify your identity for all the listed bank accounts.
    
5.  Follow the instructions on the screen and provide information for the questions to complete the user verification.
