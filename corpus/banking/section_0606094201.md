You can delete bank accounts linked with BILL in the Payment methods widget of the Bank Accounts page.

Examples of when you may need to delete a bank account:

-   You entered the wrong account details and cannot verify the bank account.
    
-   You no longer use the bank account that is linked with BILL.
    

When you delete a linked bank account,

-   Any payments which are pending approval and not yet submitted to BILL for processing fail and no amount is debited. The **Pay with Intelligent Payment Automation** box is cleared for these payments.
    
-   Any payment runs which are pending approval and not yet submitted to BILL for processing fail and no amount is debited.
    
-   Any ACH payments which are sent to BILL but not paid fail and the funding amount is credited back to your bank account.
    
-   Any check payments which are sent to BILL but not credited to vendor's bank are voided and the funding amount is credited back to the bank account.
    

Important:

If any payments fail because the linked bank account is deleted, you need to initiate new payments for the bills processed as part of the failed payments.

If the deleted bank account is the default bank account, you need to add a new default bank account.

If this is the only bank account linked to your BILL account, you can't delete it.

When a bank account is deleted, the corresponding mapping to the GL Bank Account is removed. The GL Bank Account can be mapped to any new bank account that may be added in place of the deleted bank account.

#### To delete a linked bank account:

1.  Go to Payment Automation > Payment Dashboard > View.
    
2.  If you use a NetSuite OneWorld account, on the Subsidiary Dashboard, click **Enabled**. In the **Actions** column, click **View** to open the Payment Dashboard for a subsidiary.
    
3.  Click **Manage Bank Accounts** in the Bank Accounts portlet of the dashboard to view the Bank Accounts page.
    
    Alternatively, in the left panel, click **Settings** > **Bank Accounts** to view the Bank Accounts page.
    
4.  Select the bank account that you want to delete from the list of linked bank accounts in the BILL widget.
    
5.  Click **Delete** next to the bank details.
    
6.  Click **Delete bank account** in the confirmation page that appears.
    
7.  The bank account is deleted from the list of linked bank accounts in BILL and NetSuite.
