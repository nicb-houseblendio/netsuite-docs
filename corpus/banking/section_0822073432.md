**What should my vendor do if they lose the card information provided in the remittance?**

You can email a copy of the remittance email to the vendor. You can also cancel the virtual card and issue a new one. See [Canceling Virtual Cards](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1006030129.html) for more information.

In addition, your vendor can contact your accounts payable department if they've lost the virtual card's information. For more information, see [Reviewing Remittance Email](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1006030520.html).
