**What should I do if I want to access an older Virtual Card billing statement that is not available on the Payment Automation dashboard?**

You can view, download, and print your Virtual Card statements from your Payment Automation dashboard for the last six months. The Program Administrator can request a paper copy of the statement older than six months to be mailed to the company's billing address. To raise the request for a paper copy of the statement, call 1-800-411-9264 to reach a Virtual Card support specialist.
