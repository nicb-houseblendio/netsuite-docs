In NetSuite, use the Write Checks transaction to issue a check to pay an expense or record a non-check transaction. For example, you can:

-   reimburse employees for expenses if you don't use Direct Deposit or Accounts Payable
    
-   pay vendors if you don't use Accounts Payable
    
-   record cash transactions
    
-   enter other non-check debits, such as debit card transactions
    

If you use Accounts Payable, pay vendors at _Transactions > Payables > Pay Bills_.
