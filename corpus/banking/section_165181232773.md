Automate forecasts based on your general ledger history. Define account categories based on one or several GL accounts to forecast at your preferred level of detail. Account categories in the Cash 360 SuiteApp let you classify chart of accounts, set them as inflow or outflow accounts, and include them in the cash forecast.

Note:

An administrator must set permissions so users can access the Cash 360 account category pages. For more information, see [Setting Permissions and Subsidiary Restrictions for a Cash 360 Custom Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_165164965370.html#subsect_165182268972).
