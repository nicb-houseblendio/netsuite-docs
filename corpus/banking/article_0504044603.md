Before you install the Intelligent Payment Automation SuiteApp, enable the following required features for the SuiteApp.

Enable the following prerequisite features as an Administrator:

-   Company features: **File Cabinet**
    
-   Accounting features: **A/P** and **Accounting Periods**
    
-   Transactions features: **Vendor Payment Instruments**
    
-   CRM features: **Online Forms** and **Customer Relationship Management**
    
-   SuiteCloud features: **Custom Records**, **Advanced PDF/HTML Templates**, **Client SuiteScript**, **Server SuiteScript**, and **SuiteCloud Development Framework**
    

To enable features for your NetSuite account, see [Enabling Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N232138.html).

To continue your setup, see [Installing the Intelligent Payment Automation SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530033844.html).
