**What should I do if I submitted a mid-cycle payment that I do not see on my HSBC Virtual Card account, and I would like to understand if this payment has been credited to my account?**

Payments can take up to five business days to post to your account. If the payment is not reflected in your account after five business days, call 1-800-411-9264 to reach a Virtual Card support specialist for assistance.
