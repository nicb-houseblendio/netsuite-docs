**What should I do if I have made an ACH payment, but I do not see this payment deducted from my account and would like to confirm that the payment was successful?**

HSBC will assist you with investigating payments made from your HSBC Online Account. Contact NetSuite Customer Support, and provide the following information:

-   The date of the payment
    
-   Transaction amount
    
-   Customer account number
    
-   Any associated message on the payment
    

HSBC will investigate and contact you with a response to your query.
