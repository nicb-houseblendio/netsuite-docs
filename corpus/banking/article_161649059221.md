The following procedure describes how to connect one or more non-PSD2 regulated financial institutions in Europe to NetSuite.

## Guidelines for Nonregulated Europe (Salt Edge) Connections {#subsect_0728125515}

Before you connect a non-PSD2 regulated financial institution in Europe to NetSuite, review the following guidelines:

-   Not every financial institution connection experience is the same and not all financial institutions can be accessed, or accessed reliably, using the Bank Feeds SuiteApp. Several factors may affect whether the Bank Feeds SuiteApp can connect to your financial institutions, including factors that Oracle can't control.
    
    Also, the Bank Feeds SuiteApp only supports certain bank or payment accounts. Selecting an unsupported account type such as a loan, mortgage, or investment may not work with your bank feeds setup.
    
-   You'll need Administrator access or a custom role with required permissions to set up bank feeds. For more information, see [Bank Feeds Roles and Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157734104117.html).
    
-   You must enable popups in your browser so the configuration window can open during bank feeds setup.
    

#### To connect NetSuite to a non-PSD2 regulated financial institution operating in Europe:

1.  Go to Setup > Accounting > Financial Statements > Financial Institution > List.
    
2.  On the Financial Institution page, click **View** next to the **Bank Feeds** financial institution record.
    
3.  On the **Format Profile: Configuration** subtab, click **Edit** next to **Nonregulated Europe Accounts (Salt Edge)**.
    
4.  On the Nonregulated Europe Accounts (Salt Edge) Format Profile page, click the **Connectivity Configuration** subtab.
    
5.  Click **Connect New Institution**.
    
6.  In the popup window that appears, do the following actions:
    
    1.  Search your financial institution by entering a name or a keyword.
        
    2.  Select your preferred financial institution from the list of results.
        
    
    The Connectivity Configuration subtab is masked, and you can't make any changes until you have completed the configuration setup. To close the popup window, click **Cancel**, or close the window to go back to the Connectivity Configuration subtab.
    
7.  Depending on your selected financial institution, enter your log in credentials or grant NetSuite access to your account.
    
    Click the Show Instructions expandable content for more information.
    
8.  Click **Next**.
    
9.  Read the data sharing notice and then acknowledge by clicking **Confirm** to grant consent to Salt Edge Limited.
    
    The system will then validate your login credentials and start setting up a secure connection to your financial institution.
    
    You may also be redirected to your financial institution's website to give NetSuite access to your account before the system can set up a secure connection.
    
10.  A progress of your configuration process appears.
     
     If you're setting up a multifactor authentication (MFA) account which requires additional validation, enter the required confirmation and then click **Next**.
     
11.  The setup retrieves all bank and credit card accounts from your financial institution. In some situations, your financial institution will require you to select accounts you want to include for bank feeds integration.
     
     To proceed, check the box next to each bank or payment account you want to include.
     
     Note:
     
     Select only bank or payment accounts supported by the Bank Feeds SuiteApp. Selecting an unsupported account type such as a loan, mortgage, and investment may not apply to your bank feeds setup.
     
     When the configuration process is done, the configuration window closes automatically and you'll be redirected back to the Connectivity Configuration subtab.
     
     The Connectivity Configuration subtab refreshes with your new connection. All active financial institution connections are listed under the Connections subtab, and all connected bank and credit card accounts are listed under the Accounts subtab.
     
12.  To add another financial institution account in NetSuite, click **Connect New Institution**. You can connect to a different financial institution or the same financial institution with a different login credential.
     
13.  After entering the required configuration data, go to the **Account Linking** subtab. From the **Linked GL Account** list, select a bank or credit card account in NetSuite you want to link to the adjacent accounts.
     
     For more information, see [Linking Accounts for Financial Institution Connectivity](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157894482115.html).
     
14.  Click **Save** on the format profile record.
