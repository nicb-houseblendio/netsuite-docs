## Overview and Setup Questions {#bridgehead_0911110620}

### How can I change the phone number?

To change the phone number, contact NetSuite support and provide the phone number to be updated. Oracle NetSuite Support requests BILL to update your phone number. After that, BILL contacts you to provide the required documentation for the update.

### What actions require user verification of MFA? {#question_79154950176}

You need multi-factor authentication (MFA) for account creation, bank linking, submitting payments, and more. User verification is a one-time process to confirm your identity and authorization to process payments. You may need to do it again if you add or verify bank accounts.

### What do I do if my company's EIN or legal name changes?

If you need to change your company's EIN (Employer Identification Number) or legal name for an existing BILL account, contact Oracle NetSuite Support to submit a request. A new account may be required by BILL, especially for EIN or legal name changes.

### What is the Intelligent Payment Automation SuiteApp? {#question_20154847949}

The [Intelligent Payment Automation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_8155141819.html) SuiteApp lets U.S.-based companies automate vendor payments directly within NetSuite. With this SuiteApp, you can pay vendors by check, Automated Clearing House (ACH), virtual card, wallet payment, or Mastercard Remote Payment and Presentment Service (RPPS).

To setup, follow the sequence of procedures in this topic, [Setting Up Intelligent Payment Automation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530033102.html).

### Who can use the Intelligent Payment Automation SuiteApp? {#question_92154855394}

Organizations in the U.S. that have a valid U.S. address can use this SuiteApp to pay vendors in the U.S.

## Payment and Transaction Related Questions {#bridgehead_0911035023}

### Are there fees associated with using the Intelligent Payment Automation SuiteApp? {#question_21155120465}

Yes, BILL charges transaction and service fees for payments and some related processes. For the latest charges, see [NetSuite Intelligent Payment Automation powered by BILL](https://connectonline.bill.com/Partner-Pricing.html).

### Can a vendor change how they want to get paid if they are connected?

Yes. A connected vendor can log in to their BILL account and update their payment method preferences. Options may include ACH, check, or virtual card (vCard).

### Can I manage payment automation for multiple subsidiaries? {#question_70160453026}

Yes, the SuiteApp supports multiple subsidiaries. Each subsidiary must have a valid U.S. address, and you need to complete sign-up for each separately.

### Can I process payments in currencies other than USD? {#question_12155115235}

No, payments can only be made in US Dollars (USD).

### Can Intelligent Payment Automation automatically record BILL transaction fees?

Yes. You can configure IPA to create journal entries automatically for monthly BILL transaction fees. For more information, see [Setting Up Automatic Recording of BILL Transaction Fees](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0512024421.html)

### Do I need to set up payment approvals? {#question_74160643776}

This step is optional. Payment approval workflows let you set approval limits and levels. If they're enabled, any payments over the limit need to be approved before processing.

### How do I send a Vendor Virtual Card? {#question_14142246267}

You do not need to take any action. BILL automatically checks if your vendor accepts virtual cards and sends one if the vendor is eligible.

### Is there a transaction limit? {#question_98162302071}

Yes, you can select up to 35 transactions per vendor per payment. Across all vendors, you can create up to 10,000 transactions in a single payment run.

### What are the different payment statuses and what do they mean? {#question_39160640324}

Payment statuses show what stage a payment is in. Examples are For Submission, Scheduled, Processing, Payment Sent, and Paid. For a full list and details, see [Bill Payment Statuses](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0807052012.html).

### Can customers access BILL.com directly to view payment transactions? {#question_56150535971}

No. BILL.com is the payment processing platform used by Intelligent Payment Automation. You can't access Bill.com directly. View and manage payment and transaction details in the NetSuite interface.

### What payment methods are available for connected vendors?

Connected vendors can choose from ACH, check, or virtual card (vCard) payments. The vendor selects their preferred option in their BILL account.

### What payment methods are supported? {#question_54154958299}

Supported payment methods are checks and ACH. For vendors on the BILL network, you can also use virtual cards, wallet payments, and for verified national vendors, Mastercard RPPS.

### What should I do if a vendor needs to change their payment method?

If a vendor asks to change their payment method, remind them to update their preferences in BILL. You can void a payment and resend it after confirmation.

### What should I do if a payment fails? {#question_72160937678}

If a payment fails, start a new payment for the affected bills.

### How do I increase my Accelerated Payment Timing per payment amount limits? {#question_97141510214}

To request higher Accelerated Payment Timing (APT) per payment amount limits, submit a Limit Increase Request to NetSuite Customer Support.

## Vendor and Bank Account Management Questions {#subsect_0911040947}

### Can I change the default bank account? {#question_41161327856}

Yes, if you have more than one bank account linked, you can change the default one anytime from the Bank Accounts page. You can only have one default account at a time. See [Managing Bank Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606094105.html) for more information.

### How can I manage my bank accounts in the Intelligent Payment Automation SuiteApp? {#question_17161226033}

To manage your bank accounts, go to Payment Automation > Payment Dashboard > View.

-   If you do not use a OneWorld account, the Payment Dashboard opens.
    
-   If you use a OneWorld account, on the Subsidiary Dashboard, click **Enabled**, and then click **View** in the **Actions** column to open the Payment Dashboard.
    

Click **Manage Bank Accounts**, or go to Settings > Bank Accounts.

You can add, update, delete, or set a default bank account. Most actions require multi-factor authentication (MFA). Updates appear in both BILL and NetSuite.

### How does vendor management and syncing work? {#question_68161130290}

NetSuite syncs vendor information to BILL at regular intervals. Adding, updating, or importing vendors can trigger syncs. Only vendors with a valid U.S. address and open balance are included.

### What happens if a bank account cannot be verified? {#question_72161300169}

If a manually linked bank account isn't verified in time, or fails verification, it becomes inactive and can't be used for payments. Try adding and verifying it again, or add a new bank account.

### What information is required to set up a BILL account? {#question_88161048008}

To create a BILL account, you need your business or subsidiary details, a valid U.S. address, your EIN or SSN/ITIN and bank account information. See [Setting Up Intelligent Payment Automation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530033102.html) for more details.

## Troubleshooting Questions {#subsect_0911041503}

### How can I disable the automatic connection of vendors (Auto-Connect) in BILL for my organization?

Auto-connect links vendors to the BILL network automatically. If you want to restrict this feature, you can request to disable auto-connect. Contact NetSuite customer support to start this request.

### How do I disconnect a vendor from the BILL network if auto-connect is enabled? {#question_68142514935}

Auto-connect links vendors to the BILL network automatically. If you want to restrict this feature, you can request to disable auto-connect. Contact NetSuite customer support to start this request.

### My user verification status is In\_review. How long should I wait before submitting payments to BILL? Is user verification instant? {#question_18161550859}

User verification isn't always instant. If your status is **In\_review**, wait up to 48 hours for BILL to finish the process. You can submit payments to BILL only after your user verification is approved. If your status doesn't update after 48 hours, contact support for assistance.

### What happens if BILL unenrolls my account? {#question_45172300925}

BILL performs necessary Know Your Customer or Business (KYC or KYB) and fraud checks on all accounts prior to money movement. In some instances, BILL will request additional documentation to verify the organization prior to moving money. In rare cases, BILL will close an account if there are risk concerns. If BILL needs more documentation, the customer will be contacted with details on the requested information for review. If the customer does not respond to the request, BILL will close the account. BILL will then contact the customer with instructions on how to appeal or re-enroll. The same is true if an account is automatically closed. If any payments had been submitted prior to the closure, those payments will be voided or canceled and returned to the customer.

### What if my application for a BILL account is rejected? {#question_53162329114}

If BILL rejects your application, you can't use Intelligent Payment Automation to pay vendors for that subsidiary. BILL will inform you of the reason it was rejected. Payments for rejected subsidiaries need to be processed another way.

### What should I do if I need to void or cancel a payment? {#question_35162333012}

You can void or cancel payments with statuses such as For Submission, Scheduled, or Processing, in the Payment Processing section of the dashboard. Resubmit any voided payments as part of a new payment run. For more information, see [Canceling and Voiding Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0805062605.html).

### Why does my bank connection through Plaid get stuck at the login page or keep asking me to log in? {#question_52114329790}

Some banks require you to enable third-party sharing or similar permissions before you can share banking data with Plaid or other external apps. For example, First Carolina Bank may require this setting. Check your online banking privacy and security settings and enable third-party data sharing. After updating your settings, try connecting again.
