The Intelligent Payment Automation SuiteApp lets you request and download images of cleared checks processed through BILL. With this feature, you can access check records for reconciliation and audit purposes.

In the payment record, you can view the **Sent Date** and **Check Number** of cleared checks processed through BILL. You can also view and download images of cleared checks with a **Paid** status.

#### To view or download a cleared check:

1.  Go to Intelligent Payment Automation > Payment Dashboard > View.
    
2.  If you use OneWorld:
    
    1.  The **Subsidiary Dashboard** opens. Click **Enabled**.
        
    2.  In the Actions column, click **View** to open the Payment Dashboard for the selected subsidiary.
        
    
    If you do not use OneWorld, the **Payment Dashboard** opens directly.
    
3.  On the left pane of the navigation drawer, select **Payments and Vendors**, and then, select **Payments**.
    
4.  On the Payments page, in the **Reference No.** column, click the reference number of the check you want to download.
    
5.  To view the check, on the Bill Payment page, click **View Check**.
    
    The image displays in another window, so make sure you turn off the popup blocker in your browser.
    
6.  To download the check you are viewing, click the download or the save icon.
    

For more information about payment processing with BILL, see [Payment Processing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0530033208.html) and [Viewing Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0606093101.html).
