**What should I do if I want to submit a mid-cycle payment to free up part or all of my Virtual Card credit line?**

A Program Administrator can call 1-800-411-9264 to reach a Virtual Card support specialist and request to make a one-time payment from a preferred bank account. Support will request details of the debiting account.
