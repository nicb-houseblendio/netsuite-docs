**How can I request a bank reference letter that would confirm that our company holds an account with HSBC Bank USA, N.A., as requested by a supplier we will be paying from this account?**

You can submit a request for a bank reference letter by contacting NetSuite Customer Support. You will be asked to provide the following information:

-   Account name
    
-   Account number
    
-   Account address
    

Your request will be reviewed by HSBC and a bank reference letter will be sent to the email address HSBC holds on your account.
