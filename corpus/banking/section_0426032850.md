**What should I do if I want to request funds be moved from my HSBC Online Account into another account because the funds are no longer required?**

For information about topping up the HSBC Online Account or moving funds to another account, see [Making Money Transfers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1123092904.html).

**Why can't I withdraw funds from my HSBC Online Account?**

The Payment Automation SuiteApp prevents excess withdrawal request submission if the total number of withdrawal requests for the day exceeds the current HSBC balance or if there are any pending withdrawals that have yet to be processed at HSBC.
