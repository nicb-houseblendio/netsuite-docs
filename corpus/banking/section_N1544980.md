You can add shortcuts on your dashboard to access the register for each bank account.

To use shortcuts, first enable the Shortcuts portlet on your dashboard at Home > Dashboard. Click **Personalize** to open the Personalize Dashboard panel. Under Standard Content, click or drag **Shortcuts**.

#### To create a shortcut to a bank account register: {#procedure_N1544994}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Bank > Write Checks_.
    
2.  In the **Account** field, select a bank account.
    
3.  Under **Actions**, select **Go to Register**.
    
4.  When the register opens, click **Add To Shortcuts**, available in the Shortcuts menu.
    
5.  On the Add Shortcut page, accept the account name from your chart of accounts or change it to the name you want to appear on your NetSuite Home page. Check **Open in New Window** to open the register in a new window.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

Repeat these steps for each bank account. You can also add a shortcut to any ledger account.

To access a bank account register, click its link in your Shortcuts portlet.
