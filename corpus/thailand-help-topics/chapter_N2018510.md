If you have a Thailand subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the Value Added Tax Return under the Revenue Code for Internet Filing (P.P.30) for Thailand, in English or Thai. The tax report can be used as a guide for preparing the tax returns to be submitted to the Thai Revenue Department. You can also view supplemental sales and purchase reports already formatted to match the local formats used for Thai businesses.
