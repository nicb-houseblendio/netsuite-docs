## Overview {#bridgehead_1523946809}

The following Help topics provide information about NetSuite features that are specific to Thailand business setting and requirements only. General NetSuite features are covered in other chapters and topics.

The NetSuite International edition can be configured to enable the following features and reports for companies doing business in Thailand.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Required Setup | [Setting up Thailand-specific Features and Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157733957918.html) | Enable features and preferences specific to Thailand business setting. |
| [Choosing a Language for Your NetSuite User Interface](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N479109.html) | You can enable multiple languages in NetSuite. Set up English (International) for the user interface and Help Center. Or you can enable Simplified and Traditional Chinese in the user interface. Trading documents can be configured to comply with local requirements and issued in partner's language. |
| [Configuring Company Information](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N240902.html) [Setting Personal Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N475297.html) | Support for your preferred number, date, address, phone, and currency formats as well as time zone |
