The Thailand tax invoice and credit memo templates must be applied to custom invoice and credit memo forms.

#### To apply the Thailand tax invoice and credit memo templates to custom transaction forms:

1.  Go to _Customization > Forms > Transaction Forms_.
    
2.  Click the Customise or Edit link of the custom invoice or credit memo form that you want to apply the templates to.
    
3.  For **Printing Type**, choose Advanced.
    
4.  In the **Print Template** field, select Thailand Tax Invoice PDF/HTML Template for an invoice, or Thailand Credit Memo PDF/HTML Template for a credit memo.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
