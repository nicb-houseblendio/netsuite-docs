If the nexus country in a transaction is Thailand, the **Transaction Amount in Words** custom field is added to sale transaction records. You can enter text in this field to indicate the word representation of the total amount of the invoice. The Thailand tax invoice and credit memo templates display in the printed PDF the amount in words entered in an invoice and credit memo record, respectively.
