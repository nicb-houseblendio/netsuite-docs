Open any invoice record of the type that you applied the Thailand Credit Memo PDF/HTML Template to. Click the print icon ![Print invoice](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/Singapore_print_icon.png).

The PDF file of the credit memo is generated and displayed in a separate browser.
