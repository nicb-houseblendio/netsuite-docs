The Netherlands SuiteTax-based reports, and the Work-Related Costs Report and Configuration pages can be displayed in the following languages:

-   English (U.S.)
    
-   Nederlands
    

To display a report (generated using the Country Tax Report page) in your preferred language, after clicking **View** next to the generated tax report, select a language from the Language list.

To display the Work-Related Costs Report and Work-Related Costs Configuration pages in English or Nederlands, you must add the language in the **Languages** list on your company preferences. To add a language, go to Setup > Company > Preferences > General Preferences. Then, on the Languages subtab, add English or Nederlands.
