Generating the XAF report may fail if the Full Multi-Book Accounting feature is enabled only after the Netherlands Localization SuiteApp has been installed. To resolve this issue, you must reinstall some saved searches.

#### To resolve the failure to generate the XAF report: {#procedure_1021102744}

1.  Go to Setup > Tax Reporting > Features/Components Register.
    
2.  Go to the Netherlands Localization SuiteApp subtab.
    
3.  In the **Saved Searches** row, click **Reinstall**.
    
4.  When the status is Complete, go to Reports > Tax > Country Tax Reports and try generating the XAF report again.
