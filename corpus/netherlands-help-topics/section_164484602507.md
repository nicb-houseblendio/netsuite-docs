The following constitutes the total fiscal salary amount in the Work-Related Costs (WKR) report:

-   All transactions assigned with a Fiscal Salary category.
    
-   Transactions that post to an account that has been assigned a Fiscal Salary category.
    
-   Transactions with items that have been assigned a Fiscal Salary category.
    
-   Expenses under an expense category that has been assigned a Fiscal Salary category.
    

Note:

Transactions assigned with the category **Exclude from WKR** won't be included in the computation of the fiscal salary amount, even though they post to a Fiscal Salary-categorized account.

It's important to assign accounts, items, and expense categories with the appropriate WKR categories. For more information, see [Assigning Accounts, Items, and Expense Categories to WKR](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164458361603.html).

The first row in the WKR report displays the fiscal salary of each month. However, to compute the WKR budget, the cumulative fiscal salary amounts are used.

## Sample Fiscal Salary Computation

For example, the total fiscal salary amount (derived from the transactions with a Fiscal Salary category) in a given fiscal year is 300,000 EUR each month. The report displays 300,000 EUR on each month within a fiscal year. However, to compute the WKR budget, the following fiscal salary amounts are used: 300,000 EUR for the first month of the fiscal year, 600,000 EUR for the second month as the cumulative amount from the first month.

For more information, see [WKR Budget Calculation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164482997453.html).
