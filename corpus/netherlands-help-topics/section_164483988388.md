The **Budget Left After Costs** _(B)_ component of the Work-Related Costs (WKR) report is derived from the difference of the **Accumulated Budget for the Month** _(A)_, which shows the cumulative WKR budget, and the cumulative amount of expenses (C) charged to the WKR budget, thus:

B = A - C

## Sample Residual Budget Calculation

| Month | **January** | **February** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Accumulated Budget for the Month (WKR Budget)** _(A)_ | 9,000 (This amount is derived based on a 300,000 fiscal salary multiplied by 3.0%). | 14,360 (This amount is derived based on a cumulative fiscal salary of 600,000. For information about its calculation, see [WKR Budget Calculation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164482997453.html). |
| **Other Work-Related Costs** _(O)_ | 500 | 1000 |
| Cumulative Costs _(C)_ Note: This component isn't displayed in the report. | 500 | 1,500 This amount is a result of O + C. |
| **Budget Left After Costs** _(B)_ | 8,500 | 12,860 |

The amount in **Residual WKR Budget** is, thus, equal to the amount in **Budget Left After Costs** row of the latest month.
