If you have a Netherlands subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the Netherlands Value Added Tax Return, in English or Dutch. The report can be used as a source for filling in the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.

For more information, read the following Netherlands tax topics:

-   [Netherlands Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1942969.html)
