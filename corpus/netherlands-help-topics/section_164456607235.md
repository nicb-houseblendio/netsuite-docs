On the Work-Related Costs Configuration page, you can:

-   Set the terms for calculating your Work-Related Costs (WKR) budget in a given Fiscal Year.
    
-   Define the WKR categories.
    
-   Set the permissions of your employees who perform WKR reporting tasks
    

Important:

The SuiteApp's features for WKR reporting are only compatible with NetSuite OneWorld accounts.

#### To configure the Work-Related Costs (WKR) report:

1.  Go to Setup > Netherlands Localization > Work-Related Costs (WKR).
    
2.  On the Calculation Terms subtab, enter the percentages used to calculate your WKR budget and the relevant taxes.
    
    These percentages are based on the Netherlands legislation. For more information, see [WKR Calculation Terms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164456697988.html).
    
3.  On the WKR Categories subtab, add your employee benefits, allowances, and provisions under the appropriate categories.
    
    For example, you can add office refreshments under the Zero Valuation category. The expenses for these refreshments will therefore not be charged to your WKR budget. For more information, see [WKR Categories](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164456733169.html).
    
    Note:
    
    You can click the translate icon ![translate icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/WKRReport_TranslateIcon.png) to see the Nederlands translation of the WKR categories. Make sure that you have the Nederlands language added to the Languages list in your company preferences. To verify, go to Setup > Company > Preferences > General Preferences. On the Languages subtab, add Nederlands into the list, if the language hasn't been added yet.
    
4.  On the WKR Availability subtab, select the subsidiaries in which WKR reporting won't be available.
    
    Transactions under the selected subsidiaries won't be included in the WKR report.
    
5.  On the Permissions subtab, select the roles that will be permitted to access the Work-Related Costs Report and Work-Related Costs Configuration pages, and to set the WKR categories on transactions.
    
    For more information, see [WKR Reporting Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164456834245.html).
    
6.  Click **Save**.
