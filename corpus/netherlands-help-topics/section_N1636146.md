The Netherlands Localization SuiteApp provides payment templates in the following formats that you use with the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp to pay vendor bills, employee expenses, customer refunds, and partner commissions:

-   Equens - ClieOp
    
-   Equens - ClieOp (ING Bank)
    
-   SEPA Credit Transfer (Netherlands)
    

Note:

The formats SEPA Direct Debit (ABN AMRO), SEPA Credit Transfer (ABN AMRO), SEPA Credit Transfer (HSBC), which can also be used in the Netherlands, are provided by the EMEA Localization SuiteApp. For more information, see [EMEA Localization Payment Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158858392029.html).

If your account already contains the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp with templates compatible with the Netherlands, installing the Netherlands Localization SuiteApp will update those templates to comply with the current standards in the Netherlands.

## Electronic Bank Payments Setup for the Netherlands {#subsect_159912670853}

The Netherlands Localization and EMEA Localization SuiteApps only provide the payment templates. The Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp handles the processing of the payments. To use these templates for bank payments, you must have the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp installed. For more information, see [Electronic Bank Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1585433.html).

You must also set up the relevant bank details and other information that the electronic bank payments system requires. For more information, read the following topics:

-   [Setting Up Company Bank Records in the Netherlands](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1636511.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Vendors in the Netherlands](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1638884.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Employees in the Netherlands](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1639339.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Customers in the Netherlands](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1639791.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Bank Records of Partners in the Netherlands](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3851230281.html)
    

You can also set up bank records of your company, vendors, employees, customers, and partners by importing bank details in CSV format using the Import Assistant. For more information, see [Importing Electronic Bank Payments Data](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3739634016.html). For more information about the required fields for import for every payment format, see [Netherlands Entity Bank Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159921152297.html).
