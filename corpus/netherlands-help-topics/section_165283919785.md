To use the Intrastat Reporting feature, the International Tax Reports SuiteApp must be installed in your NetSuite account. For more information, see [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html).

The International Tax Reports SuiteApp lets you export Intrastat reports in a format acceptable for upload to the tax agency website. Starting January 1, 2022, the Netherlands government no longer accepts submission of file reports in ASCII format. However, the **Export to ASCII** button is still available when viewing older reports.

#### To export the Netherlands Intrastat report:

1.  Generate the Netherlands Intrastat report. See [Generating the Netherlands Intrastat Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_165283883303.html).
    
2.  Click the **Export to Excel** button to generate an .xls file of the report.
    
    Note:
    
    Submitting through UPLOAD/SRL will no longer be available beginning January 1, 2022. For more information about uploading your report see [Importing data in IDEP+ 2022](https://www.cbs.nl/en-gb/participants-survey/businesses/overview/international-trade-in-goods/importing-data-in-idep-2022).
    

For more details about generating and exporting EU Intrastat Reports, see [Electronic Filing of Intrastat Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2071452.html#bridgehead_4180161062).
