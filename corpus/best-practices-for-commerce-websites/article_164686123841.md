You can analyze and report on your Commerce site traffic using several different features and services. Reports show data like the number of visitors, which pages they visited, conversion rates, and revenue.

Reports also help you better understand shopper behavior. For example, SuiteCommerce Analytics Data shows the top items shoppers view, their top keyword searches, and more.

## SuiteCommerce Analytics Data {#subsect_164686144087}

The SuiteCommerce Analytics Data feature uses cookies to track detailed shopper behavior on your Commerce website. You'll need to enabled this feature in your NetSuite account. It comes with a companion extension that lets you set up a banner and menu to get each shopper's consent to collect their data.

You can use the collected data to report on use cases like these:

-   Shoppers who have not visited your website within a specified timeframe.
    
-   Other items viewed by shoppers who purchased a particular item.
    
-   Items a particular shopper viewed within a specified timeframe.
    
-   Shoppers who added items to their cart but did not make a purchase.
    

The SuiteCommerce Analytics Data feature requires the following application versions:

-   NetSuite 2021.2 or later
    
-   SuiteCommerce 2021.2 or later
    
-   SuiteCommerce Advanced (SCA) 2021.2 or later
    

If you have the required versions installed, see the following topics to learn how to get started with SuiteCommerce Analytics Data:

-   [SuiteCommerce Analytics Data Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160260490967.html)
    
-   [Setting Up the SuiteCommerce Analytics Data Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160339216711.html)
    

## Google Analytics 4 (with Google Tag Manager Commerce Extension) {#subsect_164686146557}

Important:

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) has replaced Google Universal Analytics (GAU) as Google's cross-platform Analytics solution. All standard GUA properties stopped processing new hits on July 1, 2023. 360 Universal Analytics properties will stop processing new hits on July 1, 2024. See [Migrating from Google Universal Analytics to Google Analytics 4 with GTM](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1117043037.html) for information about migrating from GUA to GA4.

Google Tag Manager (GTM) is a free application from Google that lets you manage all your tags in one place. Tags can perform functions such as:

-   Measure site traffic.
    
-   Measure visitor behavior.
    
-   Provide information about the impact of online advertising and marketing campaigns.
    

Note:

SuiteCommerce Advanced (SCA) customers must implement the Mont Blanc version or later to use this feature.

For SuiteCommerce and SCA 2018.2 and later, you need to install the SuiteCommerce GTM as part of your GTM set up.

## Google Analytics 4 (without Google Tag Manager Commerce Extension) {#subsect_89112342300}

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is a third-party analytics solution that helps you evaluate traffic on your website using data based on visitor tracking information.

Note:

Do not implement GA4 if you use GA4 with Google Tag Manager (GTM). Doing so causes duplicate tracking results. See [Google Tag Manager](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4546835898.html) for information about GTM.

## Site Builder Reports {#subsect_164686153724}

Site Builder is NetSuite's legacy Commerce solution. Site Builder offers these reports, and some are also available for SuiteCommerce and SuiteCommerce Advanced (SCA) sites.

Note:

Some Site Builder reports are also available for SuiteCommerce and SCA sites, but newer versions offer more robust reporting options. You should use reporting tools, like SuiteCommerce Analytics Data, to analyze traffic for your SuiteCommerce or SCA site.

| Reports | Description | Available With | SuiteCommerce/SCA Reporting Options |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Page Hit Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2646919.html) | Show the number of page views you receive on your site | Site Builder sites | \- |
| [Web Sales Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2648945.html) | Provide data about the sales you receive from your site | Site Builder, SuiteCommerce, and SCA sites | [SuiteCommerce Analytics Data](#subsect_164686144087), [Google Analytics 4 (with Google Tag Manager Commerce Extension)](#subsect_164686146557) |
| [Keyword and Referrer Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2649461.html) | Provide information about how visitors get to your site | Site Builder sites | \- |
| [Web Activity Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2650777.html) | Provide data about the actions performed by individual visitors | Site Builder, SuiteCommerce, and SCA sites | [SuiteCommerce Analytics Data](#subsect_164686144087), [Google Analytics 4 (with Google Tag Manager Commerce Extension)](#subsect_164686146557) |
| [Internal Search Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2653317.html) | Provide data about the items visitors searched for while viewing your site | Site Builder sites | \- |
