## Required Permissions {#subsect_1145327662}

Payment run users must have the Pay Bills permission. They are affected by the user's permission level and available subsidiary, location, employee, and record restrictions.

The Payment Run sublist and Payment Run Status pages provide information to review errors. This page does not require Reports or Saved Search permissions for users to review it.
