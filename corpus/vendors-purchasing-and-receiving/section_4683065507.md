Before you can use estimated landed cost, complete the following setup tasks:

| Requirement | Purpose | Related Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enable the Landed Cost feature | Provides access to Estimated Landed Cost and landed cost templates. | Complete step 1 in [Tracking Landed Cost for Items and Expenses](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162642325864.html) |
| Install the Supply Chain Management SuiteApp | Provides the Estimated Landed Cost functionality. | [Installing the Supply Chain Management SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4258666051.html) |
| Configure roles and permissions | Enables custom roles to access landed cost templates. | [Configuring Roles and Permissions for Estimated Landed Cost](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4576794756.html) |
