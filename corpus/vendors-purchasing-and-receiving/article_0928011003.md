On the SuiteProcurement Preferences page, you can sync trading partners registered on OBN for a subsidiary.

The SuiteProcurement SuiteApp supports both curated (Amazon US, Amazon Canada, and Staples) and non-curated trading partners.

After you sync trading partners, the **Account Mapping** subtab lists the trading partners associated with the selected subsidiary.

#### To sync trading partners for a subsidiary:

1.  Go to Setup > SuiteProcurement > Preferences.
    
2.  In the Set Up Trading Partners section, select a subsidiary. The field lists all subsidiaries registered on OBN.
    
3.  Click **Sync Trading Partners**.
    

After syncing, proceed to connecting trading partners with NetSuite vendors. For more information, see [Mapping Trading Partners with NetSuite Vendors](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0928011028.html).
