To procure items from a non-curated trading partner, you must install the required SuiteApps, activate the required roles, and enable the required features. Here's a summary of the steps that you must perform. For detailed instructions on the setup requirements, see [Prerequisites for the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426114231.html).

After completing the prerequisites, you can proceed to [Installing the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426121433.html), [Setting Up SuiteProcurement Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426121447.html), and [Working with SuiteProcurement](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426121512.html)

## Registration and Setup {#subsect_0407043206}

You must ensure you have a NetSuite account and access to the OBN. You must also complete the prerequisites before you start using the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp for procurement.

To do so, see the following help topics:

-   [Connecting NetSuite with Oracle Business Network](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426114231.html#subsect_0426114706)
    
-   [Registering NetSuite Parent Company and Subsidiaries with Oracle Business Network](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426114231.html#subsect_0928120358)
    

## Adding Trading Partners {#subsect_0407043340}

Request punch-out access from your trading partners and add them to your OBN account. For more information, see [Adding Trading Partner Punch-outs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426114231.html#subsect_0426114751).

## Activating Transactions {#subsect_0407043547}

Activate the transactions (documents) you want to exchange with trading partners. For more information, see [Activating Transactions for SuiteProcurement Purchases](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426114231.html#subsect_0928124016).

## Installing the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp and setting up preferences {#subsect_0413022722}

Install the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp and set up the preferences. For more information, see [Installing the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426121433.html) and [Setting Up SuiteProcurement Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426121447.html).

## Punching Out {#subsect_0407043711}

Browse the trading partner's online catalog, select items for purchase, and add them to a purchase requisition. For more information, see [Purchasing Items from Trading Partner Stores](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0930111901.html).

## Submitting Purchase Requests {#subsect_0407043756}

Submit the purchase requisition for approval and ensure the purchase request is sent to the trading partner after approval.

For more information, see the following help topics:

-   [Reviewing and Saving SuiteProcurement Purchase Requests](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0930111904.html)
    
-   [Submitting Purchase Requests to Trading Partners](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1001122927.html)
    

## Receiving and Verifying Documents {#subsect_0407043926}

Receive documents (PO Acknowledgment, Ship Notice, Invoice) from the trading partner through the OBN. For more information, see [Receiving Documents from Trading Partners](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0930111909.html).
