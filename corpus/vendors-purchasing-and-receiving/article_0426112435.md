The SuiteProcurement SuiteApp lets you streamline and automate indirect procurement processes, from procuring items to creating purchase requests, managing approvals, and processing bills. Indirect procurement involves purchase of goods and services required to operate a business.

Using the SuiteApp, you can select and purchase items from trading partner stores, view purchase order acknowledgments and shipping details, and receive vendor bills.

Note:

The SuiteProcurement features are compatible with accounts without SuiteTax.

The SuiteProcurement SuiteApp lets you:

-   Set procurement preferences at the subsidiary level.
    
-   Sync trading partner data with NetSuite vendors.
    
-   Browse and shop items from trading partner stores.
    
-   Automatically create purchase requests for items being purchased.
    
-   Send approved purchase requests to trading partners.
    
-   Receive purchase request confirmation details, shipment details, and bills from trading partners.
    
-   Generate an item receipt after receiving all items in a purchase request.
    

The SuiteProcurement SuiteApp works together with Oracle Business Network (OBN) and NetSuite Electronic Business (NSEB) SuiteApp to provide the indirect procurement features.

The SuiteApp supports both curated and non-curated vendors. The curated vendors include the following:

-   Amazon United States
    
-   Amazon Canada
    
-   Staples Contract & Commercial, Inc.
    

If you want to procure items from a non-curated trading partner, ensure that your trading partners have completed the setup steps mentioned in [SuiteProcurement Support for Non-curated Trading Partners](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0407041433.html). You also must have Advanced Procurement provisioned in your account.

If you want to procure items from Amazon United States, see [Procuring Items from Amazon Using the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1020119/kw/1020119).

If you want to procure items from Amazon Canada, see [Procuring items from Amazon Canada using SuiteProcurement SuiteApp](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1020875/kw/1020875)

For all other trading partners, see [Prerequisites for the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426114231.html).
