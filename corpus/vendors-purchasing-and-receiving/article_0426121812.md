If you want to uninstall the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp, perform the following steps.

#### To uninstall the SuiteApp:

1.  Go to Customization > SuiteCloud Development > Installed SuiteApp List.
    
2.  Hover over the Actions menu next to the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp and click **Uninstall**. The uninstallation process begins.
    
    Uninstallation of a SuiteApp might take a while. When it's done, the SuiteApp is removed from the Installed SuiteApps list.
