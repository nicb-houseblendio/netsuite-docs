After submitting your order on the trading partner portal, the SuiteApp automatically generates a requisition or purchase request based on your preferences. It then sends the request for approval or automatically approves it, depending on the approval routing settings.

If you want to configure the approval routing settings to keep requests in a **Pending Approval** state, see [Setting Approval Preferences for Purchase Requests](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0403043357.html).

Important:

Currency conversion is not currently supported. The purchase request retains the requisition's currency.

## Requisitions {#subsect_0413014907}

If your requisition is approved and still in **Pending Order** status, you can convert the requisition into a purchase request.

Note:

To use the Requisitions feature, ensure that Advanced Procurement is provisioned in your account.

If you want to want to add an item to an existing purchase requisition, see [Adding Items to an Existing SuiteProcurement Purchase Request or Requisition](#subsect_1001120224)

When working with a requisition, note the following points:

-   **Partial/Bulk Orders**\- These are permitted only if your vendor supports them.
    
-   **Vendor consistency**\- Ensure the vendor on the requisition matches the one in your shopping cart.
    
-   **Pricing and contracts**\- Purchase contracts don't apply to requisitions. Cart prices may override contract prices.
    

#### To convert a requisition with a single order:

1.  Go to the requisition you want to convert.
    
2.  To view a requisition, click **View**.
    
3.  On the Requisitions page, click **Create Purchase Order**.
    
4.  On the Order Requisitions page, select all the items.
    
5.  Click **Submit**.
    

To convert requisitions with bulk orders, see [Ordering Requisitions in Bulk](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3873973062.html).

## Purchase Requests {#subsect_0413015447}

Ensure that you review order details before saving your purchase request. If you want to add an item to an existing purchase request, see [Adding Items to an Existing SuiteProcurement Purchase Request or Requisition](#subsect_1001120224).

Note:

You should not customize the purchase order form and use the custom form included with the SuiteApp. Any customizations made will be ineffective, and you won't be able to use the form.

A SuiteProcurement purchase request contains the following information:

-   **Items**\- View the purchased items.
    
-   **Expenses**\- Review additional costs such as discounts and handling charges.
    
-   **Billing**\- Access vendor details such as address and email. By default, primary billing address is selected.
    
-   **SuiteProcurement Transactions**\- Provides transaction, shipping, and purchase order acknowledgment details.
    
    **Shipping**\- Displays shipping address (employee's primary address) and receiver name. You can edit shipping details.
    
-   **SuiteProcurement Transaction**\- When this box is checked, indicates generation of a purchase request by the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp.
    
-   **Submitted to Trading Partner**\- When this box is checked, confirms successful submission of your purchase request to the trading partner.
    

## Adding Items to an Existing SuiteProcurement Purchase Request or Requisition {#subsect_1001120224}

If a purchase request is pending approval and your trading partner supports these following actions, you can:

-   Add new items from the same store or other stores.
    
-   Modify item quantities.
    
-   Copy the purchase request or requisition.
    

Note:

If you are procuring items from Amazon, you shouldn't re-punchout or modify a purchase order.

You can't edit a purchase request that's already approved and sent to the trading partner for order fulfillment. For more information, see [Submitting Purchase Requests to Trading Partners](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1001122927.html).

#### To add items to a SuiteProcurement purchase request or requisition:

1.  Go to the requisition or purchase request.
    
2.  Click **Edit** or **View**.
    
3.  Click the **Items** subtab.
    
4.  Click **Go to Trading Partner Website**. This button is available only if purchase requests or requisitions are pending approval.
    
5.  In the Stores window, click the store where you want to add the items for purchase.
    
    The online store opens in a new tab.
    
6.  Select and add items to the cart.
    
7.  Submit the order.
