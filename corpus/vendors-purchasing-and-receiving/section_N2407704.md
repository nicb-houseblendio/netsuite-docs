To print purchase orders, you need Adobe® Reader®. Visit the [Adobe Web site](http://www.adobe.com/products/acrobat.html) to download the latest version at no charge.

Your administrator can customize the layout of your printed forms using Advanced PDF/HTML templates. For more information, see [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4453550706.html).

See the following procedures:

-   [Printing a Single Purchase Order](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162013355635.html)
    
-   [Printing Purchase Orders in Bulk](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162013361228.html)
    
-   [Printing Labels for Purchase Order Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162013368875.html)
