You can choose to have a supervisor or an approver assess requisitions that are entered to decide if they are approved for processing. To require approval for requisitions, you must use a SuiteFlow workflow.

-   [Setting up Suiteflow Workflow-Based Approvals for Requisitions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3909302265.html)
    
-   [Approving a Requisition Using SuiteFlow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3965424923.html)
    

For an approval process with additional customization options, you can set up the Requisition Approval Workflow. For more information, see [Requisition Approval Workflow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3960249592.html).
