After you click Submit on the Order Items page, the Order Items Status page appears and gives details about the orders being processed.

If this page doesn't show orders as expected, refresh the page to display the most current information.

To go to the Order Items Process Status page, go to _Transactions > Inventory > Order Items > Status_ > Status.
