Enabling the Vendor Access feature lets you give vendors access to NetSuite and assign roles to them. You can assign multiple roles to customize the level of access needed by a specific vendor. For example, the Vendor Center role enables a vendor to view prior transactions and open purchase orders. For information about how to assign roles to vendors, see [Assigning a Role to a Vendor](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2363606.html).

#### To enable the Vendor Access feature: {#procedure_N2363698}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Web Presence** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Vendor Access** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
