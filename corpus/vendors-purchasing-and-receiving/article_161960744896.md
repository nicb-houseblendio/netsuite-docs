Follow this procedure to set an individual preference for receiving of vendor credit limit warnings.

Note:

To set individual preferences for vendor credit limit warnings, the **Allow Override** preference for vendor credit limit warnings must be set in company preferences. For more information, see [Setting Company Preferences to Receive Vendor Credit Limit Warnings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161960744012.html).

#### To set an individual preference:

1.  Go to Home > Set Preferences > General.
    
2.  Check or clear the **Vendor Credit Limit Warnings** box.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
