The SuiteProcurement SuiteApp provides the following saved searches:

-   **SuiteProcurement Purchase Transactions** - You can add this saved search as a portlet to your dashboard. This portlet lets you view all SuiteProcurement purchase requests that you've created.
    
-   **SuiteProcurement Purchase Orders for Approval**\- You can select this saved search to filter out SuiteProcurement purchase requests for approval. To select the saved search, go to the Approve Purchase Request page, and click the **View** field.
    
-   **SuiteProcurement Requisitions for Approval** - You can select this saved search to filter out SuiteProcurement requisitions for approval. To select the saved search, go to the Approve Purchase Request page, and click the **View** field.
    

#### To add the SuiteProcurement Purchase Transactions portlet:

1.  Log in to NetSuite using the employee center role.
    
2.  Click **Personalize**.
    
3.  Click **Standard Content**.
    
4.  Click **Custom Search**.
    
5.  Click **Set Up**.
    
6.  In the **Search** field, select **SuiteProcurement Purchase Transactions**.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
