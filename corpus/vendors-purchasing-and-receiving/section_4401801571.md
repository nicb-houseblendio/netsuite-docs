Follow this procedure to check the status of a bill payment.

#### To check the status of a bill payment:

1.  Go to _Transactions > Payables > Pay Bills_.
    
    A list of bill payments appears.
    
2.  To expand the available filters, click **Filters**. By default, the filter is set to today's date.
    
3.  Click the **Date Created** field, and select a time frame.
    
4.  Optionally, in the **From** and **To** fields, select your own date range.
    
5.  Click **Refresh** to update the list.
