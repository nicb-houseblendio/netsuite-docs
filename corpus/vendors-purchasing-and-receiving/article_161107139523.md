After you enable the Advanced PDF/HTML Templates feature, you can print vendor credits. See [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4453550706.html).

You can print bills in HTML or PDF format. Printing to PDF format requires Adobe® Reader®. Visit the [Adobe Web site](http://www.adobe.com/products/acrobat.html) to download the latest version at no charge. You can print individual bills, you can also print multiple bills in bulk.

See the following:

-   [Printing a Single Vendor Credit](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162004649120.html)
    
-   [Printing Vendor Credits in Bulk](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162004656575.html)
    

You can use custom printing templates by creating a custom bill credit form with a custom template assigned in the Print Template field. See [Advanced Templates Customization in the Template Editor](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2863632.html).
