An administrator can create or customize roles for the Transaction Email Capture SuiteApp. For more information, see [Assigning Roles to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N897798.html) and [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html).

By default, the following roles can access the Transaction Email Capture subtab in the vendor record:

-   Administrator
    
-   A/P Clerk
    
-   CEO
    
-   CFO
    

Other roles need the Vendor Bill Sender permission to access the subtab.

#### To grant the vendor bill sender permission to custom roles: {#procedure_0609063930}

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the role you want to give access to the Transaction Email Capture subtab.
    
3.  On the **Permissions** subtab, go to the **Custom Record** subtab.
    
4.  From the Records list, select **Vendor Bill Sender**.
    
5.  In the Level column, select **Full**.
    
6.  To save the custom role record, click **Save**.
