To disable the Vendor Prepayments feature, you must first delete all vendor prepayment transactions. For more information, see [Deleting a Vendor Prepayment Transaction](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156380292896.html#subsect_156389459594).

#### To disable the Vendor Prepayments feature: {#procedure_156396897711}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **Accounting** subtab, clear the **Vendor Prepayments** box under the Advanced Features section.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
