If you're a noncurated trading partner, complete the following steps.

## Registration and Setup {#subsect_0407044047}

The Oracle Business Network has a self registration process for trading partners to register their company and begin sending and receiving transactions. To perform business transactions with a NetSuite customer using the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp, you must be a registered trading partner on the OBN.

-   If your company is already registered, and has an OBN account, then follow the next steps.
    
-   If you don't have an account on OBN, register your company to create one. To register as a trading partner on OBN, follow the steps in Trading Partner User Guide available in [OBN](https://businessnetwork.oracle.com/obn/vp/shell/help).
    
-   If you are not sure whether your company has an OBN account, contact your company administrator.
    

After registering your company with OBN, define the primary identifier.

### Defining Your Primary Identifier {#subsect_0407044313}

Each trading partner registered on OBN is required to select a unique identifier. The sending and receiving trading partners use this identifier for any transaction (such as business documents, punch-out). You (trading partners) can use multiple identifiers for your account, but you must designate one of them as the primary identifier.

#### Defining Identifier for your Company

1.  Log in to your OBN account.
    
2.  At the bottom of the page, click the **Messaging** tab.
    
    ![A screenshot of list of identifiers on the Messaging tab on OBN.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/VendorsPurchasingReceiving/PurchasingReceiving/Identifier.png)
3.  Click **Add** to open the Add Identifier window.
    
4.  In the **Identifier Type** field, select an identifier type.
    
5.  Enter an identifier value.
    
6.  Click **Add**.
    

#### To set an identifier as primary:

1.  Click **Edit** next to the identifier that you want to set as primary.
    
2.  Check the **Mark as Primary?** box.
    
3.  Click **Update**.
    

## Setting Up Delivery Methods {#subsect_0407044052}

For each trading partner, OBN requires the details of the destination server; the trading partner uses the destination server to receive business transactions from OBN (or other services).

This destination server needs to be configured for trading partners accounts. A trading partner can provide a destination server address for each delivery method. This is used for transmission of different transaction types. Each delivery method allows for separate URL addresses to be used for the test and production modes supported on OBN.

#### To set up a delivery method:

1.  Log in to your OBN account.
    
2.  At the bottom of the page, click the **Messaging** tab.
    
3.  At the top of the page, click the **Delivery Methods** tab.
    
4.  Under **Receive from OBN** tab, click **Add**.
    
5.  In the Add Business Document window, complete the following fields:
    
    1.  In the **Delivery Method Type** field, select a delivery method.
        
    2.  In the **Delivery Method Name** field, enter a name.
        
6.  Configure the settings for both **Production** and **Test** modes.
    
7.  Click **Add**.
    

## Activating Transactions {#subsect_0407045333}

The OBN lets you manage the document types to be exchanged between buyers and suppliers. You can choose the document types that you want to support, specify the delivery method for each type of document, and specify whether you will send or receive the document.

The SuiteProcurement SuiteApp supports the following transaction types:

-   Advance Shipment Notice
    
-   Invoice
    
-   Purchase Order
    
-   Purchase Order (PO) Acknowledgment
    

#### To activate the transactions:

1.  Log in to your OBN account.
    
2.  At the bottom of the page, click the **Messaging** tab.
    
3.  At the top of the page, click the **Transaction Setup** tab.
    
4.  Under the **Send: Setup documents you want to send from your ERP system through OBN to your customers** section, edit the following business documents:
    
    -   Advance Ship Notice
        
    -   Invoice
        
    -   Purchase Order Acknowledgement
        
    
    ![A screenshot of the documents that need to be set up.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/VendorsPurchasingReceiving/PurchasingReceiving/OBN_SendDocument.png)
5.  In the Add Business Document popup window, complete the following:
    
    1.  In the **Status** field, select **Active**.
        
    2.  Under Document Format, in the **Production Mode** and **Test Mode** fields, select **cXML 1.2x**.
        
    3.  Click **Add**.
        
6.  To transmit documents from your server to OBN, under the **Receive: Setup documents you want to receive in your ERP system through OBN from your customers** section, edit **Purchase Order**.
    
    ![A screenshot of the document that need to set up under Receive section.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/VendorsPurchasingReceiving/PurchasingReceiving/OBN_ReceiveDocument.png)
7.  In the Add Business Document popup window, complete the following:
    
    1.  In the **Status** field, select **Active**.
        
8.  Under Production mode, complete the following fields:
    
    1.  In the **Document Format** field, select **cXML 1.2x**.
        
    2.  In the **Delivery Method Name** field, select a value.
        
9.  Under Test Mode, complete the following fields:
    
    1.  In the **Document Format** field, select **cXML 1.2x**.
        
    2.  In the **Delivery Method Name** field, select a value.
        
10.  Click **Update**.
     

## Configuring a Punchout Catalog {#subsect_0407050613}

Punchout is the process using which a buyer can browse a trading partner's online catalog of goods or services, select them for purchase, and add them to a purchase requisition. After submission and approval of the purchase requisition, a purchase request is generated. The purchase request is then sent to the trading partner for the delivery of the goods and services.

You must set up at least one catalog to enable NetSuite customers to punch out to the online catalog and select items for purchase.

![A screenshot of the location where a punchout catalog is added.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/VendorsPurchasingReceiving/PurchasingReceiving/AddPunchoutCatalog.png)

## Approving Punch-out Request from NetSuite Customers (Buyer) {#subsect_0407050724}

Before you can start exchanging documents and conducting transactions with your NetSuite customers, you must approve the punch-out request sent by them. When a NetSuite customer selects your company, you receive a notification request to review.

Trading partners that initiated the request appears with the status **Pending Acknowledgment** in the following locations on OBN. You can choose any of the locations to approve the request.

-   On the **Partners** tab, in the Pending Requests table.
    
-   On the **Punchout** tab, in the Pending Customer Requests table.
    

The request contains the company contact, email address, OBN ID, customer identifier, and others.

#### To accept the request:

1.  Select the customer from the **Partners** or **Punch-out** tab.
    
2.  Under the **Action** column, click the **Review** icon.
    
3.  In the Review Punch-out Request window, do the following
    
    1.  In the **Assign Punch-out Catalog** field, select a punch-out catalog. This is the catalog that your NetSuite customers will access when procuring goods or services from you.
        
    2.  (Optional) In the **Note from Customer** field, enter a comment.
        
4.  Click **Acknowledge**. This action leads to the following steps.
    

After you acknowledge a request from your buyer, the following actions takes place.

-   The NetSuite customer is accepted as a trading partner but the punch-out connection is still pending.
    
-   A notification is sent to your NetSuite customer informing them about the acknowledgment of the request.
    
-   The request status changes to Pending Activation.
    
-   The customer is added to the Your Trading Partners table on the Partner tab.
    

#### To activate the punch-out connection:

1.  Click the **Punchout** tab.
    
2.  Locate the NetSuite customer and under the **Action** column, click the **Review** icon.
    
3.  In the Activate Punchout window, do the following:
    
    1.  (Optional) In the **Note from Customer** field, enter a comment.
        
    2.  In the **Assign Punchout Catalog** field, select a punchout catalog.
        
    3.  In the **Identifier Type & Value** field, select the identifier received from the NetSuite customer.
        
    4.  Click **Activate**. This action enables your NetSuite customers to access your catalog for procurement.
        

Note:

The identifier type and value received from your NetSuite customer is the same in both the **Partners** and **Punchout** tab. If the value differs, ensure to update the identifier as per the identifier type and value received from your NetSuite customer.
