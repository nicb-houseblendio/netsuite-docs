Follow this procedure to set a company preference to receive vendor credit limit warning.

#### To set a company preference to receive vendor warnings:

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Preferences > Accounting Preferences_.
    
2.  On the **General** subtab, check the **Vendor Credit Limit Warnings** box
    
3.  (Optional) To allow individual override of the company preference, check the **Allow Override** box. For more information, see [Setting Individual Preferences for Vendor Credit Limit Warnings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161960744896.html).
    
4.  Click **Save**.
