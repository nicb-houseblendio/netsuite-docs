The SuiteProcurement SuiteApp is a paid and managed SuiteApp. To purchase it, contact NetSuite Account Management Organization (AMO) or your Customer Support representative.

If you have the Administrator role, you can install the SuiteApp from the SuiteApp Marketplace. For information about installing the SuiteApp, see [Installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539799323.html).

After installing the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp, proceed to [Setting Up SuiteProcurement Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426121447.html).

If you want to uninstall the SuiteApp, see [Uninstalling the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426121812.html).

Following table lists some of the errors encountered during installing the SuiteApp:

| Errors | Solution |
| --- | --- |
| You must specify expense and income accounts for this item. | Create a non inventory purchase item manually with the name **Default SuiteProcurement Category** and try installing again. |
| You may not use duplicate accounts on an item. |
| The subsidiary restrictions on this record are incompatible with those defined for account: <ExpenseAccount>. Subsidiary access on this record must be a subset of those permitted by the account. |
