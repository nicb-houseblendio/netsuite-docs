The Transaction Email Capture SuiteApp is a paid SuiteApp. It's a managed SuiteApp that updates automatically when new features or enhancements are added.

Before you install the Transaction Email Capture SuiteApp, reach out to your NetSuite account manager to get access.

#### To install the Transaction Email Capture SuiteApp: {#procedure_1114013333}

1.  Go to _Customization > SuiteBundler > Search & Install Bundles_
    
2.  In the **Keywords** field, use the following information to search for the SuiteApp:
    
    -   Bundle Name: Transaction Email Capture
        
    -   Bundle ID: 459771
        
3.  On the Bundle Details page, click **Install**.
    

For more information about installing SuiteApps, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).
