When you enable the Advanced Receiving feature, you can create bills directly from item receipts. If the quantities on the item receipt and originating purchase order differ, NetSuite deducts the quantity on the item receipt from the purchase order. This enables you to manage the creation of billing information if your purchase order is split into multiple receipts.

#### To create a bill from item receipt

1.  Go to _Transactions > Purchases > Receive Orders > List_.
    
2.  Click **View** next to an item receipt.
    
3.  Click **Bill**.
    
    NetSuite generates a bill based on the information from the item receipt.
    
4.  To save the bill, click **Save**.
