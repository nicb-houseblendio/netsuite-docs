To use in-transit payments in your account, you must enable the feature.

Note:

After the feature is enabled, it can"t be disabled.

NetSuite automatically creates the Clearing Payment account when you enable this feature.

The In-Transit Payments feature is not compatible with the Automated Clearing House (ACH) processing.

#### To enable the In-Transit Payments feature:

To use the feature, you must create a Cash-In-Transit clearing account. For more information, see [Creating a Cash In-Transit (CIT) Clearing Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1535485844.html).

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Accounting** subtab.
    
3.  Under the Advanced Features heading, check the **In-Transit Payments** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
