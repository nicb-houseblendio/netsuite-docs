Before you can enter purchase requests, you need to enable the Purchase Requests feature in your account.

#### To enable the Purchase Requests feature:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **Employees** subtab, click the **Purchase Requests** box.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

After you enable the feature, employees with the Employee Center role can enter purchase requests. For more information, see [Giving an Employee Access to Purchase Requests](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N914882.html).
