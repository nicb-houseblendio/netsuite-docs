SuiteProcurement currently supports up to 1000 item category mappings per trading partner. If you need to upload 1000 item category mappings, you need to ensure that there are no existing category mappings for the trading partner. Since only up to 1000 item category mappings are supported, some records may fail to save if the limit has been reached.
