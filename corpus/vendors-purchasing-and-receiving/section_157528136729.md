NetSuite provides portlets that employees can add to the Employee Center dashboard. You can configure a Reminders portlet on the Employee Center to show users the bills that are waiting for their approval. For more information about dashboard portlets, see [Dashboard Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N576951.html).

By default, the Reminders portlet is available in the user's Dashboard. If the Reminders portlet is not available, add the portlet following the instructions in the [Adding a Portlet to a Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N580906.html) section.

#### To configure a reminder for bills pending approval in the Employee Center dashboard: {#procedure_157528140438}

1.  Log in to NetSuite with the user account with the custom Employee Center role.
    
2.  In the Reminders portlet, click the menu icon and then click **Setup**.
    
3.  In the **Select reminders** menu, click **Bills to Approve**.
    
    Note:
    
    The Bills to Approve reminder is only available after configuring the required permissions in the Employee Center role. For more information, see [Configuring Vendor Bill Approvals in the Employee Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157528129125.html).
    
4.  Click **Save**.
