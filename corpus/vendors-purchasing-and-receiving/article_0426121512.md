The SuiteProcurement SuiteApp is an indirect procurement solution designed to procure items online from trading partners. For more information about the SuiteApp, see [SuiteProcurement Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426112435.html).

Before using the SuiteApp, you must [Prerequisites for the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426114231.html), [Installing the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426121433.html), and [Setting Up SuiteProcurement Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0426121447.html).

To work with the SuiteProcurement SuiteApp, read the following topics:

-   [Purchasing Items from Trading Partner Stores](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0930111901.html)
    
-   [Reviewing and Saving SuiteProcurement Purchase Requests](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0930111904.html)
    
-   [Receiving Documents from Trading Partners](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0930111909.html)
    
-   [Receiving Items and Generating Item Receipts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0930111913.html)
