You can print mailing or shipping labels for vendors without using mail merge or data export. For more information about printing mailing labels, see [Printing Mailing and Shipping Labels](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1099985.html).

#### To print mailing or shipping labels:

1.  Go to _Transactions > Management > Print Checks & Forms._.
    
2.  Click Mailing Labels.
