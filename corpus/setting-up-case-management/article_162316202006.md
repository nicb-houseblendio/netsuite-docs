You can include images in your online HTML forms if you've uploaded them to the File Cabinet.

#### To include images in your form: {#procedure_N2430576}

1.  Go to _Documents > Files > Images_ and access the **Images** folder.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the image you want to include in the form.
    
3.  Make sure the **Available Without Login** box is checked.
    
4.  Copy the URL information from the **URL** field.
    
5.  When you add the image URL to your template, enter **https://system.netsuite.com** followed by the information from the **URL** field.
    
    The image URL should match the following format:
    

          `<p><IMG SRC="https://system.netsuite.com/core/media/ media.nl?id=20&c=ACCT83022&h=9087c4a3c3d4b23aba42"></p>` 
        

After you've prepared your template file, upload it to the File Cabinet.
