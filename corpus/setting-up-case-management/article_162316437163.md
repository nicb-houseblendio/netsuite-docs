You can create email templates outside of NetSuite in a word processing application, or enter them directly in the Template field of email template records. If you create an email template outside of NetSuite, save it as a plain text (.txt) file.

#### To create an email template: {#procedure_0616094700}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Email > System Email Templates_.
    
2.  Click **New** and create an email template in the text editor.
    
3.  You can also customize the default email templates by going to _Setup > Company > Email > System Email Templates_ and clicking **Customize** or **Edit** on the template you want to edit.
    

You can personalize email templates as scriptable templates that include information from NetSuite records. For more information, see [Scriptable Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3775087812.html).

To upload a template you created outside of NetSuite, see [Uploading Your Email Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162316440035.html).
