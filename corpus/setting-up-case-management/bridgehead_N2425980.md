The following email case capture preferences are available on the External subtab of the Support Preferences page.

| Preference | Description |
| --- | --- |
| External Case Form Redirect URL | Enter the web page you want customers to land on after they submit a case. This applies to cases sent from the Customer Center or the My Account Tab of your NetSuite website. |
| Default Customer Center Case Form | Select the online case form you want to link to in your Customer Center. Your customers access this form when they click Contact Support in the Customer Center. |
| Create New Lead on No Match | Check this box to create a lead record if the case information from the external case form doesn't match any existing records. |
| Site Color Theme | Select the color theme you want to display in the Customer Center. You can create custom color themes at _Commerce > Site Builder > Appearance > Color Themes > New_. |
