By default, the Subsidiary field appears on the Standard Case Form and the Standard Help Desk Form. You must add the Subsidiary field to the Standard External Case Form and any custom case forms where you want it to appear.

#### To add the Subsidiary field to a form: {#procedure_N2433842}

1.  Go to _Setup > Customization > Forms > Entry Forms_.
    
2.  Click **Customize** next to the form you want to add the field to.
    
3.  Make sure the **Form is Preferred** box is checked.
    
4.  On the custom form page, click the **Fields** subtab.
    
5.  On the **Main** subtab, check the box in the **Show** column next to Subsidiary.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

When a case saves through the Customer Center or the My Account page of your website, the customer's subsidiary appears on the case.
