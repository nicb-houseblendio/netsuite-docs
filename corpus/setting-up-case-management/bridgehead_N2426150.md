The following fields are found on the Inbound Email subtab of the Support Preferences page.

If you use NetSuite OneWorld, you can use a separate inbound email address and anonymous customer placeholder for each subsidiary. For more information, see [Setting Subsidiary-Level Support Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N2433676.html).

| Preference | Description |
| --- | --- |
| NetSuite Address | Use this email address to forward email from your support email address to NetSuite. When email arrives at this address, it is converted into a case record in NetSuite. You need to configure your email server to activate this feature. For more information, see [Using Email Case Capture](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2427546.html) Warning: Only use this email address for capturing emails as case records. All messages sent to this address are converted to cases in your NetSuite account. |
