#### To set a default discount on a customer record:

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Customers > New_.
    
2.  Specify the details for the customer record. See the help topic [Customers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1076428.html).
    
3.  In the Classification field group, select the applicable discount in the **Default Discount** field.
    
    To create a new discount, see the help topic [Configuring Discounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4231800589.html).
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Important:

Read the [Default Discounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1515573637.html) topic. Certain scenarios may result to different default discounts being applied to customers on transactions.
