Before using Contract Renewals for the first time, you should set your preferences according to [Setting Initial Renewals Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N1321151.html).

To set your Software Vertical Contract Renewals SuiteApp preferences, go to Contract Renewals > Setup > Contract Renewals Preferences, and click **Edit**. When you're finished making changes, click **Save**.

Note:

Don't undeploy the script for the Suitelet - CR Translation Bridge, because it displays the text of the labels for the title, buttons, and subtabs on the Contract Renewals Preferences page. If the Suitelet script is undeployed, the text of the labels won't be displayed; instead, the IDs of the user interface components will be displayed.
