Install this bundle for the Contract Renewals SuiteApp:

-   Bundle Name: SWV Contract Renewals Module
    
-   Bundle ID: 187488
    
-   Availability: Shared
    

For information about purchasing the Software Vertical Contract Renewals SuiteApp, contact your account representative.

For information about installing SuiteApps, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).

The Software Vertical Contract Renewals SuiteApp is a managed SuiteApp and is automatically updated whenever there are updates. These issue fixes and enhancements are available after the SuiteApp is updated in your account.
