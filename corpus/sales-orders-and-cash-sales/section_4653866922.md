The following diagram shows the different states of a contract.

![Workflow diagram showing the different states through which a contract (sales order) may pass.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/OrderManagement/SalesOrdersCashSales/ContractStatus.png)

The contract's status is shown in the **Status** field on the contract record. For information about contract fields, see [Contract Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4460975456.html).

Non-perpetual items have the same status as the contract where they belong, while perpetual items always have a status of Active. The contract item's status is shown in the **Contract Item Status** field on the contract item record.

The contract lifecycle can be tracked by understanding the contract item statuses, which you can learn from [Contract Item Statuses](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1230013212.html).
