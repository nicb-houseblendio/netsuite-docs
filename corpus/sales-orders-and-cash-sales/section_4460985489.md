The following table shows which actions are applicable to a contract based on its current status. For information about contract states, see [Contract Workflow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4653866922.html).

| Contract Status | Available Actions (Contract Record Buttons) |
| --- | --- |
| Edit | Upsell1 (Edit Mode) | Downsell1 (Edit Mode) | Renew Now (View Mode) | Cancel Contract (View/Edit Mode) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| **Active** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Renewal Generated** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| **Renewal Processed** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| **Renewal Rejected** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes2 | Yes |
| **Canceled** | No | No | No | No | No |
| **Closed** | No | No | No | No | No |
| 1 Contract is also available for selection in the Contract field on a standalone upsell sales order or downsell RMA. 2 Contract is renewable only through the Renew Now button. Renewal rejected contracts won't be renewed by the contract renewal script (R05) or by Renew Now: Batch Process. |
