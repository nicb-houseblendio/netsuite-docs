When a sales order is only partially fulfilled, the remaining quantity becomes a back order. But in many cases, these back order quantities aren't fulfilled, and the sales order is closed instead. The Auto Close Back Orders SuiteApp lets you automatically close these back orders after billing. You can also use the SuiteApp to keep a list of items to exclude or include when automatically closing back orders.
