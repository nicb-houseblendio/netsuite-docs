If you accidentally close a sales order, you can reopen it if it hasn't been billed or fulfilled yet.

There are two ways to reopen the sales order:

-   add a new line
    
-   reopen a closed line
    

#### To reopen a closed line:

1.  On the sales order record, click **Edit**.
    
2.  On the closed line, scroll right until you can see the **Closed** column.
    
3.  Click on the word **Yes**.
    
    A checked box appears.
    
4.  Click on the box to clear it.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    
    The status of the sales order changes to **Pending Fulfillment** or **Partially Fulfilled**.
