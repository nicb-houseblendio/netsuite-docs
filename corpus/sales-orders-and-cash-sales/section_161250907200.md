Control how current prices appear in the Order Guide Entry popup for the Customer Center role.

#### To set up customer center price preferences:

1.  Go to Order Guide > Customer Center Price Settings > Customer Center Price Settings.
    
2.  On the Customer Center Price Settings List page, click **Edit**.
    
    NetSuite displays the Customer Center Price Settings page.
    
3.  In the Customer Price Setting sublist:
    
    1.  From the **Customer** list, select the customer
        
    2.  To show the current price for the customer, check the box in the **Display Price** column.
        
    3.  Click **Add**.
        
4.  Click **Save**.
    

There's a **Display Price** box above the **Customer Price Setting** sublist. If you check this box, customers who don't have the display price checked in the sublist will show current price in the Customer Center role. When unchecked, current price does not appear for customers who don't have the display price checked in the sublist.
