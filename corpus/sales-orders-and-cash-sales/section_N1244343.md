Note:

For information specific to Not-for-Profit (NFP), see [Creating Cash Donations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519955925.html) and [Creating Merchandise Sales](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519956611.html).

A cash sale is a transaction that records the sale of goods or services for which you receive immediate payment. Enter a cash sale when you receive payment for goods or services at the time of delivery.

Add each item you've sold from your Items list to record a cash sale. Cash sale line items show the goods and services sold, along with their sales amounts. The total sale amount is the sum of all sales amounts plus any applicable tax.
