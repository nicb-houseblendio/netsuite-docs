Refer to these topics to view tables listing custom records of contracts and contract items with their corresponding fields and details. You can refer to them for scripting.

-   [Contract Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N1329161.html)
    
-   [Contract Item Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N1331821.html)
    

Note:

These custom records are locked and aren't for customization.

You can create new records based on some of these custom records. For example, you can create new contract records and subsequently edit them. For more information, see the help topics [Creating a New Contract](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1325805.html) and [Editing a Contract](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4448152522.html).
