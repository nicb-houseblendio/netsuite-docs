You can process an upsell from contract records by following these steps.

#### To process an upsell from the contract record: {#procedure_N1326356}

1.  Open the contract record. For information about viewing a contract record, see [Viewing a Contract](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4461755042.html).
    
2.  On the contract record, click **Edit**.
    
3.  On the **Contract Items** subtab, check the box in the **Process** column and click **OK** for each item that you want to upsell.
    
    To check the **Process** box for all contract items, click **Mark All**. To clear the **Process** box for all contract items, click **Unmark All**.
    
4.  Click the **Create Up-sell Order** button.
    
5.  On the transaction page, verify that the contract items that you're upselling are properly set on the **Items** subtab.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
