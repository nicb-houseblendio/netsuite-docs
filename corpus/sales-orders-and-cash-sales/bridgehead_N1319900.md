There are three types of discounts available in the Software Vertical Contract Renewals SuiteApp:

-   Transaction body discounts affect all transaction lines and can't be renewed.
    
-   Transaction line discounts affect the line above the discount in a transaction and also can't be renewed.
    
-   In-line discounts affect an individual transaction line and are stored in the corresponding contract item. These discounts can be renewed.
    

Note:

When a contract is renewed, if there is a customer discount set on the customer record, this customer discount is applied to the renewal contract items in place of any in-line discounts set on the original contract.
