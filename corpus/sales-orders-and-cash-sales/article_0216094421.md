Before installing the SuiteApp, you must enable these features from Setup > Company > Enable Features:

-   On the **Transactions** subtab:
    
    -   under Basic Features section, check **Sales Orders**
        
    -   under Billing section, check **Advanced Billing**
        
-   Under the **SuiteCloud** subtab:
    
    -   check **Client SuiteScript**
        
    -   check **Server SuiteScript**
