You can add these items to the sales orders that you want to renew:

-   Inventory
    
-   Non-inventory
    
-   Kit/Package
    
-   Assembly Item/Bill of Materials
    
-   Services
    
-   Discount
    
-   Item Group (End)
    
-   Item Group (Header)
    
-   Subtotal
    
-   Description
    
-   Other
    
-   Markup
    

If you selected **Current price at the time of renewal** in the **Renewal Price** field, the price level and rate of the following item types in your sales order will change accordingly:

-   Inventory
    
-   Non-inventory
    
-   Kit/Package
    
-   Assembly Item/Bill of Materials
    
-   Services
    
-   Discount
    
-   Other
    
-   Markup
    

If the line items in your sales order are not in this list, their price level and rate will not change even if Current price is specified in Renewal Price at the time of renewal.

If you select Same as original sales order in Renewal Price, the price level and rate of line items will not change.

The rate of the following item types will increase based on the value you entered in the **Uplift Percentage** field:

-   Inventory
    
-   Non-inventory
    
-   Kit/Package
    
-   Assembly Item/Bill of Materials
    
-   Services
    
-   Other
    

If the line items in your sales order are not in this list, their rates will not change even if Uplift Percentage value is specified.
