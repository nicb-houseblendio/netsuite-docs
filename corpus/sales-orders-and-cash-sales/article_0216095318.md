These roles have default access to sales order records will be able to view and configure the Sales Order Renewal custom form:

-   A/R Clerk
    
-   Accountant
    
-   Accountant (Reviewer)
    
-   Bookkeeper
    
-   CEO
    
-   CEO (Hands off)
    
-   CFO
    
-   Customer Center
    
-   Retail Clerk
    
-   Retail Clerk (Web Services Only)
    
-   Revenue Accountant
    
-   Revenue Manager
    
-   Sales Administrator
    
-   Sales Manager
    
-   Sales Person
    
-   Sales Vice President
    
-   Specialized User: CRM
    
-   Specialized User: WMS
    
-   Store Manager
