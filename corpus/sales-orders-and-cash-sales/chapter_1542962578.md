## Overview {#subsect_156764759740}

Customer Lifetime Value SuiteApp identifies premier customers based on the value of transactions. It provides valuable information when analyzing customer base in terms of dollars spent. You can use this SuiteApp for targeted marketing efforts and customer satisfaction strategies, such as promotional campaigns and loyalty programs.

After you install Customer Lifetime Value, it creates a field in the customer record that displays the total revenue for a customer. The Customer Lifetime Value field amount is calculated as total cash sales plus invoices minus credit memos and cash refunds. The calculation of the amount depends on the script's default parameters, which you can change in the saved search criteria.

This SuiteApp is available in all languages that NetSuite supports. For more information, read [Configuring Multiple Languages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N247147.html) and [Choosing a Language for Your NetSuite User Interface](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N479109.html).
