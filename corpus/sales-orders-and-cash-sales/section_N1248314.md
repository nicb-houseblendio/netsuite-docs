When you receive cash or check payments, your bank account won't be credited until you deposit them.

#### To enter a cash sale and place it in Undeposited Funds: {#procedure_N1248326}

1.  Go to _Opportunities > Transactions > Cash Sales_.
    
2.  On the Cash Sale page, select a customer and enter the transaction information.
    
3.  Select **Undep. Funds**.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Later, when you go to _Transactions > Bank > Make Deposits_ (Administrator), you'll see this sale on the Payments subtab, ready to be deposited.

If you're paid by credit card and your bank account is automatically credited, you'll place the funds into a bank account.

#### To enter a cash sale and place it in a bank account: {#procedure_N1248414}

1.  Go to _Opportunities > Transactions > Cash Sales_.
    
2.  On the Cash Sale page, select a customer and enter the transaction information.
    
3.  In the **Account** field, select a bank account from the list.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

When you view previous cash sales, you'll see if they're marked **Deposited** or **Not Deposited** at the top of the page. To view a cash sale, go to _Opportunities > Transactions > Cash Sales_ and click **View** next to the one you want to see.
