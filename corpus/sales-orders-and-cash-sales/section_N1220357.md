You can manually close line items on sales orders when you don't plan to fulfill them.

For example, let's say you sell ten widgets to a customer and have already fulfilled five of them. Then, you discontinue the item and can't fulfill the remaining five. If you close the line manually, you'll keep a record of the original order quantity.

#### To manually close a line item on a sales order:

1.  Go to _Opportunities > Transactions > Sales Orders_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the sales order with the line you want to close.
    
3.  On the **Items** subtab of the sales order, click the line you want to close.
    
4.  Check the box in the **Closed** column, and click **Done**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

If you're closing a partially fulfilled line item, bill the sales order first. When all line items are fulfilled or closed, the sales order is removed from the billing and receiving queues.
