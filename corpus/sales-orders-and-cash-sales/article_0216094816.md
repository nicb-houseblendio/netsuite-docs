You can install the Sales Order Renewals SuiteApp from the SuiteApp Marketplace in NetSuite. For more information about installing the SuiteApp, see [Installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539799323.html).

The Sales Order Renewals SuiteApp is a managed SuiteApp. After you install the SuiteApp, your accounts automatically update whenever an update to the SuiteApp is available.
