Ensure that these features are enabled in your account before installing Contract Renewals:

-   Custom Records
    
-   Client SuiteScript
    
-   Server SuiteScript
    

For information about enabling these features, see [Enabling Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N232138.html).
