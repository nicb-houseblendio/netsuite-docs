Be aware of the following limitations with scheduled search emails:

-   The Show Internal IDs preference doesn't work for scheduled saved search emails. Add Internal ID as a results column to include them.
    
-   If a saved search includes criteria that have non-specific values, such as **\-Mine-** or **\-My Team-**, scheduled emails may not be sent. Avoid using these values in your criteria filters.
    
-   These situations may cause saved search emails to not be sent:
    
    -   The search owner has been changed to a user who hasn't scheduled a saved search before. In this case, the new owner must log in with the appropriate role and resave the search.
        
    -   The search owner can't log in anymore (for example, they left the company). In this case, a user with the administrator role must assign a new owner, who must then log in and resave the search.
