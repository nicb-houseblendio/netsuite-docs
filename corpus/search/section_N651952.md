Some record types let you set filters and show result columns based on join field values in related records when you use advanced and saved searches. This feature makes your searches more powerful. For example, you can use this capability to search for customers according to the dates of their order transactions, or to search for deposit transactions based on their related account numbers. The table below shows which related record types have join fields you can use for advanced search filtering, organized by record type. You can also find this information in the **Filter** dropdown list on the **Criteria** subtab of any advanced search page. In most cases, you can also see these join fields as results columns, listed in field dropdown lists on the **Results** subtab.

Note:

You may not have access to all of the record type searches or related record types listed in the table. The record types you see depend on your role, permissions, and search access. See [Permissions for Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N664557.html).
