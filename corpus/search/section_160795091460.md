The **Drill Down Fields** sublist is available on the **Results** subtab if you have the Presentation Categories permission, a Lists type permission. You don't have to be an administrator.

#### To define fields displayed when users drill down on search records (saved searches only): {#procedure_N651786}

1.  Click the **Drill Down Fields** sublist.
    
2.  Select a field from the dropdown list, enter a custom label if needed, and click **Add**.
    
3.  Repeat step 2 as needed.
    
4.  Use **Move Up**, **Move Down**, **Move To Top**, or **Move To Bottom** as needed.
