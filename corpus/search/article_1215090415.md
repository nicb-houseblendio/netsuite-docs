#### To add translations for saved search highlighting: {#procedure_N689063}

1.  From the Saved Search definition page, click the **Highlighting** subtab.
    
2.  Click a highlighting condition.
    
3.  Click the Translation column.
    
4.  Enter the translated text for each language.
    
5.  Click **Done**.
    
6.  Repeat steps 2-5 for every highlighting condition that requires translating.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

![Translations on the Highlighting subtab.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Search/highlighting1.png)

Translations for Saved Search highlighting of summary rows can be added in the same way.

1.  From the Saved Search definition page, click on the Highlighting subtab.
    
2.  Click on the Highlighting if..(Summary) subtab.
    
3.  Repeat steps 2-5 (above) for every summary row that requires translating.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
