#### To add translations for saved search titles: {#procedure_N688896}

1.  From the Saved Search definition page, click the **Search Title Translation** subtab.
    
2.  For each language listed, enter the translated text into the **Label** column.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

If the Multi-Language feature is enabled, the Saved Search definition page will display an additional subtab **Search Title Translation**.
