You may want to quickly find a specific record to review or update its details. NetSuite has quick search tools where you enter keywords to find matching records. Here are some quick ways to find records:

-   Many NetSuite pages, including transaction records and some lists of records, have a Quick Find icon at the top. Click it to search for other records of the same type. See [Quick Find](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N643277.html).
    
-   You can add a Quick Search portlet to your home page dashboard or other pages to enter keywords and search for records of a specific type. See [Quick Search Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N643459.html).
