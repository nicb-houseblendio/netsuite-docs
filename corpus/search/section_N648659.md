By default, a search results column header is the field name or formula type. On the **Results** subtab, you can add more descriptive labels for columns.

#### To enter a custom label for a field: {#procedure_N648671}

1.  Click the **Custom Label** column next to a field and enter a name to be shown for it on the results page.
    
2.  Click **OK**.
    

#### To add translations for a custom field:

1.  Click the **Custom Label Translation** column next to the custom label and add translations in any languages you need.
    
2.  Click **Done**.
