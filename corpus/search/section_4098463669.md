You can mark any saved search you own as inactive. The creator is the owner by default.

#### To mark a search inactive:

1.  Go to the list of your saved searches.
    
2.  Be sure that the **Show Inactives** option is selected.
    
3.  Check the **Inactive** box next to the search you want to make inactive.
    
    ![Inactive box](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Search/inactive.png)
4.  Click **Submit**.
