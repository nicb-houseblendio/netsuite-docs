#### To add translations for saved search filters: {#procedure_N689183}

1.  From the Saved Search definition page, click the **Available Filters** subtab.
    
2.  Click on a filter.
    
3.  Click the **Translation** column.
    
4.  Enter the translated text for each language.
    
5.  Click **Done**.
    
6.  Repeat steps 2-5 for every filter that requires translating.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
