NetSuite lets you quickly find other records of the same type from many pages. Quick Find is available when you see a magnifying glass icon ![Quick find icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Search/QuickFindIcon.png) in the page header.

## Transactions Quick Find {#bridgehead_N643303}

Click the Quick Find icon on a transaction page to find a specific transaction, or set of transactions of the same type, by date, number, or associated entity name.

When you click the icon, a Quick Find popup opens. Enter a name, date, transaction number, or a combination, and click Submit.

![Quick Fnd popup window.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Search/QuickFindPopup.png)

-   If only one transaction matches, as in cases where you enter a number, that transaction record opens.
    
-   If more than one transaction matches, as in cases where you enter a name or a date, you see a list of matching transactions. Click their corresponding links to open its transaction record.
    

## Records Quick Find {#bridgehead_N643355}

Click the Quick Find icon on a record page to quickly search records of the same type.

When you click the icon, a popup box opens. Type your keywords and press Enter or click **Go**.

![Quick Find keyword popup window.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Search/QuickFindKeywords.png)

-   If only one record matches your keywords, the record opens.
    
-   If more than one record matches, you see a Search Results page with a list of matching records. Each record has links you can click to view or edit it, and you can also email or export the results.
    

For example, when you're viewing a customer record, click the Quick Find icon in the header, enter your keywords, and press Enter to find all matching customer records.
