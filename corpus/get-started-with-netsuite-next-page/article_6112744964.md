Note:

This topic describes functionality available to users eligible for **NetSuite Next**. NetSuite Next is being made available to customers in phases, so these features may not yet be available in your account. You'll receive in-app notifications when your account is eligible for NetSuite Next.

Learn about NetSuite Next options available for your account on the Get Started with NetSuite Next page.

As an administrator, you can use this page to:

-   Request preview access to NetSuite Next.
    
-   Set up Ask Oracle in your current NetSuite account.
    
-   Review frequently asked questions about NetSuite Next.
    

A NetSuite Next preview account lets you explore the new NetSuite Next interface with AI at its core. Preview access uses a copy of your NetSuite data, so you can try NetSuite Next without affecting the data in your main NetSuite account. You can use natural language to work with an AI assistant, create canvases to discover business insights, and use AI-powered features to help you complete work more efficiently.

When the **Get Started with NetSuite Next** page is available for an account, administrators will see a **Start exploring NetSuite Next** banner after logging in to NetSuite. To open the **Get Started with NetSuite Next** page, click the **Explore options** button. Note that the text in the banner, including title and call to action, are subject to change.
