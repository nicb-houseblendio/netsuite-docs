#### To Set Ask Oracle Access for Roles:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > AI > Ask Oracle Access_.
    
2.  To find a role, enter all or part of the role name in the **Search** field.
    
3.  Do one of the following:
    
    -   To give role access, click **Has access**.
        
    -   To remove the role's access, click **No access**.
        
4.  Click **Save**.
