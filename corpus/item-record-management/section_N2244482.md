To special order items, an administrator must enable the Drop Shipments and Special Orders feature.

#### To enable Drop Shipments and Special Orders: {#procedure_N2244506}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Items & Inventory** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Drop Shipments and Special Orders** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
