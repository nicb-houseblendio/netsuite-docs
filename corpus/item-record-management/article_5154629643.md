There's one preference for Advanced Pricing: **Price To Use if Multiple Prices Found For an Item**.

This preference lets you decide what happens if NetSuite finds more than one applicable price for the same item. By default, NetSuite uses the lowest price.

#### To switch to the Highest Price setting:

1.  Go to Setup > Accounting > Accounting Preferences.
    
2.  On the Items/Transactions subtab, scroll down to the Sales & Pricing section.
    
3.  From the **Price To Use If Multiple Prices Found For an Item** list, select **Highest Price**.
