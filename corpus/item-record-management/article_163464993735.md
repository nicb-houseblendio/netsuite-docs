Use the following procedure to create a markup item.

#### To create a markup item:

1.  Go to _Lists > Accounting > Items > New_.
    
2.  On the New Item page, click **Markup**.
    
3.  On the Markup page, enter an identifier for the item in the **Item Name/Number** field.
    
4.  In the **Rate** field, enter the rate for this markup as either a percentage or a dollar amount. To indicate a percentage, type a % sign after the number. To enter a dollar amount, enter a positive number.
    
5.  On the **Accounting** subtab, select an **Account**.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
