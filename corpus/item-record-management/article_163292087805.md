An administrator can use the following procedure to enable the Bar Coding and Item Labels feature.

#### To enable the Bar Coding and Item Labels feature:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Items & Inventory** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Bar Coding and Item Labels** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

Although some use the term **barcode**, NetSuite uses the term **bar code**.
