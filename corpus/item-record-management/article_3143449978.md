Price rules can use item collections and customer groups to decide which pricing applies. If those collections or groups are dynamic (based on a saved search), NetSuite refreshes their membership automatically on a schedule.

When you use a saved-search-based (dynamic) item collection or customer group in a price rule:

-   NetSuite creates and stores a snapshot of the items or customers that belong to the collection or group.
    
-   Price rules use this snapshot during price evaluation.
    

Use of this snapshot improves performance because NetSuite doesn't need to run the saved search every time it evaluates pricing.

## How the Refresh Schedule Works {#subsect_2152156012}

NetSuite refreshes dynamic membership using a periodic materialization process. This is how it works:

-   The system checks hourly for dynamic item collections and customer groups that are used by price rules.
    
-   During that hourly check, NetSuite refreshes any eligible collection or group whose snapshot is older than 12 hours.
    

In Advanced Pricing, changes to a saved search don't always take effect immediately. In most cases, the snapshot updates within a 12-hour freshness window.

## Forcing an Immediate Manual Refresh {#subsect_29152139009}

If you need changes to take effect right away, you can trigger an immediate refresh.

#### To force a refresh:

1.  Open the item collection or customer group record.
    
2.  Make a small edit (if needed).
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    
    Saving the record triggers immediate materialization, which updates the stored snapshot.
    

## When Pricing Might Look Out of Date {#subsect_13152144993}

You might see a short delay between changing a saved search and seeing updated pricing results.

This delay can happen when:

-   You update the underlying saved search used by a dynamic item collection or customer group.
    
-   The snapshot remains unchanged until either:
    
    -   the next scheduled refresh, or
        
    -   you manually refresh by editing and saving the record
        

Tip:

If pricing needs to reflect the change quickly, manually refresh the item collection or customer group after you update the saved search. For details, see [Forcing an Immediate Manual Refresh](#subsect_29152139009).

## Managing Visibility Differences for Saved Search Snapshots {#T_change_me_2}

Materialization runs the saved search as the System user. Running the saved search as the System user affects what gets included in the snapshot. Here's what you might notice:

-   You may see different results depending on your role and access restrictions.
    
-   Pricing might still apply to items or customers that you can't see, because the snapshot was built using System user access.
    

If you're a system administrator:

-   You don't need to schedule anything. The refresh happens automatically.
    
-   Manual refresh is the quickest way to align pricing with recent saved search changes.
    

#### To ensure accurate saved search snapshots:

1.  Review saved search criteria carefully, especially if you rely on role-based visibility.
    
2.  If the saved search depends on fields or filters that vary by user access, validate results with an administrator role.
