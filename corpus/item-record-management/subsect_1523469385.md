This topic explains how to create a Price Detail Update for the Customer Group type in the Effective Date Pricing SuiteApp.

#### To create the price detail for a customer group:

1.  From the price update record, on the **Prices** subtab click **New Price Detail Update**.
    
    The Price Update field group displays the information about the price update record.
    
    Note:
    
    The price update ID is a system-generated identifier that is unique to the price update record that you created.
    
2.  In the **Currency** field, select the currency to be used in the price.
    
3.  In the **Modified Price** field, specify the new price for the selected item.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    
    The new price for the Customer Group Type is applied to the customer record when the start date occurs.
