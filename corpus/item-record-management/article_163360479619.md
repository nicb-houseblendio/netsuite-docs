An administrator can use the following procedure to enable the Lot Tracking feature.

#### To enable Lot Tracking feature:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Items & Inventory** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Lot Tracking** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

After the Lot Tracking feature is enabled, you can set up lot item records to track information about each lot.
