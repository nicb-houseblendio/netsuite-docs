If you can't view the Allergen subtab on the Items page for some custom forms, perform the following steps:

#### To enable Allergen subtab in custom forms:

1.  Go to Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Item Fields.
    
    The Custom Item Fields page opens.
    
2.  Click an allergen field link. For example, **Celery**.
    
3.  On the Custom Item Field page, click the **Apply to Forms** button.
    
4.  On the Apply Custom Fields to Forms page, for the custom forms, check the box in the **Show** column.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    
6.  Repeat steps 2 to 5 for all allergen field links.
