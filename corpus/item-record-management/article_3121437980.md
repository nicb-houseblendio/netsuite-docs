This pivot table gives users a clear view of how your Advanced Pricing strategy is performing over time. The table aggregates the total value of completed sales transactions and breaks them down by each active price level within a selected date range.

With this view, you can:

-   Quickly compare which price levels are driving the most revenue
    
-   Identify trends across different pricing tiers
    
-   Make more informed decisions about pricing optimization and sales strategy
    

This pivot table includes the following rows and measures:

<a id="table_0401123201"></a>

| Label | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Price Level | Price level name. You can sort and filter by price level. |
| Subsidiary | Subsidiary associated with the transaction. Impacts permissions and currency context. You can sort and filter report data by subsidiary. |

<a id="table_0401123307"></a>

| Label | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Number of Transactions | This is how many transactions have been created at this price level. You can sort by number of transactions and filter by price level or subsidiary. |
| Net Amount (Sum) | Sort by net amount for the transaction line (Net Amount = Amount - Posting Discount) |
