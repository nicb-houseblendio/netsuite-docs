This workbook is based on the sales pricing overview dataset and lets you compare transactions through the sales pricing overview customizable view. In the workbook, sales transactions are grouped in pivot tables and charts by price level, subsidiary, amount, and date. Totals in this workbook match the totals displayed in the sales pricing reports.

Note:

The view is not in real time.

To access the workbook, click the Sales Pricing Overview card on the Analytics Home page. To view details contained within the underlying dataset, see the help topic [Sales Pricing Overview Dataset Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_2122854702.html).

The following component is included in the workbook template:

| Component | Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Table View | Price Level List | The customizable table view lets you visually compare transactions in different price levels for the specified date range. |
