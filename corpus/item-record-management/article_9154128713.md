To use Advanced Pricing, you must enable the feature, which is off by default.

#### To enable Advanced Pricing:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Enable Features.
    
2.  On the Transactions tab, in the Sales section, check the **Advanced Pricing** box.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

Enabling Advanced Pricing significantly changes the Item record. For more information, see Advanced Pricing and the Item Record.
