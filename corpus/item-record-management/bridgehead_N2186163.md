NetSuite lets you customize your price list pages and forms.

-   When you customize a page, NetSuite changes the fields that show up on the price list page used to generate lists. For more information, see [Generating Bulk Price Lists and Customizing Lists](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2187796.html) or [Generating an Individual Price List](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2188411.html).
    
    To make price lists with fields you can't add through customization, you'll need to create a new Search. For more information, see [Working With Pricing Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2189322.html).
    
-   When you customize a form, NetSuite changes what gets printed on the price list by adding your logo or other custom fields. For more information, see [Customizing Price List Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2188838.html).
