You can search for a serialized inventory item, find serial numbers associated with a serialized inventory item, or search for a specific serial number. On any serial number record, click the Search Transactions button to find all transactions associated with the serial number. For more information, see [Simple and Advanced Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N643948.html).

To export your search results as files, see [Exporting Search Results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N663983.html).

#### To search for a serialized inventory item: {#procedure_1508171670}

1.  Go to Lists > Search > Saved Searches > New.
    
2.  Click **Item**.
    
3.  Complete the **Saved Item Search** page.
    
    To learn more, see [Publishing Saved Search Results on Your Website](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2635180.html).
    
4.  Click **Save**, **Save & Run**, or **Save & Email**.
    

To find serial numbers associated with the serialized inventory item, on the **Inventory Detail** subtab, click **Inventory Numbers**.

#### To find serial numbers associated with a serialized inventory item: {#procedure_1508184455}

1.  Go to Lists > Search > Saved Searches > New.
    
2.  Complete the **Saved Item Search** page.
    
    To learn more, see [Publishing Saved Search Results on Your Website](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2635180.html).
    
3.  Click **Save**, **Save & Run**, or **Save & Email**.
    

#### To search for a serial number: {#procedure_1508184058}

1.  Go to Lists > Search > Saved Searches > New.
    
2.  Complete the **Saved Item Search** page.
    
    To learn more, see [Publishing Saved Search Results on Your Website](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2635180.html).
    
3.  Click **Save**, **Save & Run**, or **Save & Email**.
    

To find all transactions related to the serial number, click **Search Transactions**.
