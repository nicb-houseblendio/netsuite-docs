You can edit a price level and update your existing prices.

For example, Christy's Catering wants to raise Price Level One prices by 5%. Christy edits the Price Level One record by adding a 5% markup and updating existing prices.

#### To edit a price level: {#procedure_N2182820}

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Setup Tasks > Accounting Lists_.
    
2.  Click the price level you want to edit.
    
3.  Click **Edit**.
    
4.  In the **Price Level** field, you can change the name of this price level.
    
5.  In the **Markup/Discount %** field, you can enter a + or - percentage to mark up or discount prices for this price level.
    
    Prices for this level are calculated by applying the + or - percentage to the Base Price level.
    
    Note:
    
    Any value you enter gets rounded to the nearest 0.01%
    
6.  If you want to update all existing prices in this price level, check the **Update Existing Prices** box.
    
    After you click **Save**, your prices update automatically.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
