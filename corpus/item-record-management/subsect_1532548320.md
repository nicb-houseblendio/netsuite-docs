The Effective Date Pricing SuiteApp supports the following item types:

-   Inventory items
    
    -   Matrix (Lot Numbered, Serialized)
        
    -   Non-Matrix (Lot Numbered, Serialized)
        
-   Non-Inventory items
    
    -   Matrix (Sale, Resale)
        
    -   Non-Matrix (Sale, Resale)
        
-   Assembly items
    
    -   Matrix (Lot Numbered, Serialized)
        
    -   Non-Matrix (Lot Numbered, Serialized)
