When you map a serial format and fill in the additional serial fields for an item with serial autonumbering enabled, the system automatically creates a serial number. The generated serial number is then displayed in the **Serial Number** column of the Inventory Detail popup window.

#### To generate serial numbers:

1.  Open a purchase order.
    
2.  In the **Items** sublist, select an item that has **Enable Lot/Serial Numbering** checked. You can check this box in the **Lot/Serial Numbering** subtab of an item.
    
3.  Click the **Inventory Detail** icon to open the Inventory Detail popup window.
    
    Note:
    
    Let the popup window load completely to view the Lot/Serial Inventory Detail section.
    
4.  In the **Primary Information** section, enter the required serial information, such as starting sequence number, country of origin, and manufactured date.
    
5.  (Optional) If the Bin Management and Inventory Status options are enabled, you'll see those fields in the **Primary Information** section. Select the **Bin** and **Status** for the item.
    
6.  Click **Generate**.
    
    The serial number automatically generates and displays in the **Serial Number** column of the **Auto-Generated Serial Number** sublist.
    
    With the **Generate** option, you can save the autogenerated serial numbers data for the current instance.
    

Note:

Note the points below:

-   The SuiteApp can generate up to 700 serial numbers for an item in an instance.
    
-   The **Auto-Generated Serial Numbers** subtab shows up to 10 serial numbers at a time. If your list of serial numbers exceeds this limit, you can use the pagination feature to go through additional rows.
    

When adding multiple rows for the same serial number, values in all other fields must be the same, except for the following:

-   **Quantity**
    
-   **Bin** (applies to item receipts, work orders, and assembly builds)
    
-   **Status** (applies to item receipts, work orders, and assembly builds)
    

The automatically generated serial number replaces a space character in a serial number format element with a hyphen.

Note:

When checking the **Enable Lot/Serial Numbering** box in the **Lot/Serial Numbering** subtab for an item, you must select **Number Format**. If you fail to do so, the SuiteApp displays an error.

In the **Lot/Serial Numbering** subtab of an item, if the **Enable Lot/Serial Numbering** box is unchecked, the core Inventory Detail popup window is displayed.

The **Date** and **Time** field formats in this window are derived from the Set Preferences page. The **Time of Day** field follows the date and time format selected on the General Preferences page.
