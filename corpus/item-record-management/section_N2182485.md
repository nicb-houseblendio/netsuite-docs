If you want to customize a price for a customer, select Custom in the Price Levels field on transactions. Then, enter the amount you want to charge in the Rate field.

NetSuite also lets you set up custom price levels for customers. If you have a web store and you've set up custom price levels, customers see this price in your store after they log in.

#### To set up custom price levels for customers: {#procedure_N2182501}

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Customers > New_.
    
2.  Enter the customer's name.
    
3.  Click the **Financial** subtab.
    
4.  Under Account Information in the **Price Level** field, choose this customer's price level.
    
5.  Fill in other information as appropriate.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

To edit a customer record for custom price levels, go to _Lists > Relationships > Customers_ and click **Edit** next to the customer you want to update.

After you set up custom price levels, you can still enter a custom amount or choose a different price level on transactions. You can set up to 1,000 price levels.

You can also set price levels for specific items for customers.

#### To set item price levels for a customer: {#procedure_N2182616}

1.  Open the customer record you want to assign an item price level to.
    
2.  Click the **Financial** subtab.
    
3.  Click the **Group Pricing** subtab.
    
4.  Select a pricing group and the price level for this group.
    
5.  Click the Item **Pricing** subtab.
    
6.  Select an item and a price level for that item.
    
7.  Click **Add**.
    
8.  Repeat these steps for each item you want to assign a price level to for this customer.
    

Item price levels override the selection in the Price Level field on the customer record and the price levels for pricing groups.
