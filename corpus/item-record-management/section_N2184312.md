You can quickly update price changes to your items.

You can update up to 10,000 items in a single mass update. For more information, see [Defining a Mass Update](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N667342.html).

Warning:

**You can't undo this action.**

Note:

During a mass update with lots of data, old prices might still be visible.

#### To update prices: {#procedure_N2184346}

1.  Go to _Lists > Mass Update > Update Prices_.
    
2.  On the Update Prices page, in the **Amount** field, enter a negative or positive amount for the price change.
    
    You can enter a currency amount or a percent in this field.
    
3.  In the **Based on** field, choose what you want to base this price change on:
    
    -   **Existing Price** - Updates the existing price for each price level.
        
        Important:
        
        To update all price levels, set the **Based on** field to **Existing Price**.
        
    -   **Average Cost** - Adjusts the price based on the average cost of the items you select.
        
    -   **Most Recent Cost** - Adjusts the price based on the most recent cost of the items you select.
        
    -   **Entered Cost** - Adjusts the price based on the purchase price entered on the item record.
        
4.  In the **Price Levels** field, select the price levels you want to change.
    
    When this page first appears, not all price levels are selected.
    
5.  In the **Rounding** field, select rounding options:
    
    -   **None** - doesn't round your price update
        
    -   **To nearest** - rounds your prices to the nearest dollar
        
    -   **Round up** - rounds your prices up
        
6.  In the **Round To** field, select the way in which you want to round.
    
    For example, if you're increasing your inventory items by $2 and want them to round to even dollar prices, select **Even Dollar** from the list.
    
7.  Check the **Public** box if you want to make this mass update available to everyone else with access to your account.
    
8.  On the **Criteria** subtab, set search criteria for the items you want to update prices for.
    
9.  On the **Results** subtab, choose how you want the search results to show.
    
10.  Click **Preview** if you want to look at the changes before submitting them.
     
11.  Click **Save**.
