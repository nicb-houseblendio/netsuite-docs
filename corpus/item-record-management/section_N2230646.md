An administrator can use the following procedure to enable the Serialized Inventory feature.

#### To enable the Serialized Inventory feature: {#procedure_N2230655}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Items & Inventory** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Serialized Inventory** box and the **Inventory** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

You must create new inventory items to track serialized inventory because you cannot convert existing inventory items into serialized items. For more information, see [Creating Item Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2166469.html).
