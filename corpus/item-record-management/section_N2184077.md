Price groups let you set customer-specific price levels for groups of items. For example, you can create a pricing group called Laptops and link it to all your laptop items.

Tip:

The maximum number of pricing groups depends on things like the total number of price levels and customer records. In general, you shouldn't create more than 1,000 pricing groups to keep NetSuite running smoothly.

#### To create a pricing group:

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Setup Tasks > Accounting Lists > New._.
    
2.  In the Add to Accounting Lists window, click **Pricing Group**.
    
3.  Enter a **Pricing Group** name.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

The price level for a pricing group overrides the customer's price level, but not item pricing.
