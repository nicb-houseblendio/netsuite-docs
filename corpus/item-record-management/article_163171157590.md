If you've set an absolute price for an item in the Unit Price field on a customer's record, price lists will always show that absolute price for the customer. For more information, see [Absolute Pricing for Customers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1083860.html).
