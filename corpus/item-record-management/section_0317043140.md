If you are unable to view the lot or serial inventory details or get an error when opening the Inventory Detail window, try the following steps. These steps will reattach the **ALN\_InventoryDetails\_CC\_main.js** script to the **Custom Inventory Detail Form** custom entry form.

#### To reattach a script in Custom Inventory Detail form: {#procedure_1019040629}

1.  Go to Customization > Forms > Entry Forms.
    
2.  In the **Custom Inventory Detail Form**, click **Edit**.
    
3.  On the **Custom Code** subtab, in the **Script File** list, select an empty value.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    
5.  In the **Custom Inventory Detail Form**, click **Edit** again.
    
6.  On the **Custom Code** subtab, in the **Script File** list, select **ALN\_InventoryDetails\_CC\_main.js**.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
