Advanced Pricing gives you additional options for importing CSV files using the Import Assistant. With Advanced Pricing enabled, the following record types are available from the Record Type list:

-   Sales Price Rule
    
-   Sales Price Rule Entry
    

To access the CSV Import Assistant, go to Setup > Import/Export > Import CSV Records.
