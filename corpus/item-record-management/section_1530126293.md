You can view a list of all price updates in the Effective Date Pricing SuiteApp.

To view all price updates:

1.  Go to Lists > Effective Date Pricing > Price Update.
    
2.  On the Price Update list, locate the item you want to view and click **View** to display the price update record.
    
    -   You can click **Edit** on the record to make changes to the item, or click **Edit** instead of View on the Price Update page.
        
    -   You can also create a new price detail update by clicking **New Price Detail Update** on the Price Update page.
