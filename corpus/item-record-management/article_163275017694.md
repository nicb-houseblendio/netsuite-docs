Use the following procedure to delete a revaluation.

#### To delete a revaluation:

1.  Go to _Transactions> Inventory> Revalue Inventory Cost > List_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the revaluation you want to delete.
    
3.  On the page, in the **Actions** list, select **Delete**.
    
    To maintain an audit trail, don't delete the revaluation. Instead, run the inventory cost revaluation again using the same parameters as the initial one.
