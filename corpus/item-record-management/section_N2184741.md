NetSuite lets you update the purchase price on selected items to match the price from the most recent purchase of that item. This price reflects the most recent transaction where the item was added to inventory, like a purchase receipt, inventory transfer, or inventory adjustment. It doesn't include item returns or assembly unbuilds.

You can update up to 10,000 items in a single mass update. For more information, see [Defining a Mass Update](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N667342.html).

Warning:

**You can't undo this action.**

#### To update purchase prices: {#procedure_N2184768}

1.  Go to Lists > Mass Update > Mass Updates.
    
2.  To open the list, click **Items**.
    
3.  Click **Update Purchase Price from Most Recent Purchase**.
    
4.  Verify or enter a **Title of Action** for this mass update.
    
    The **Type** field shows the type of record you're updating.
    
5.  In the **Criteria** subtab:
    
    1.  Check the **Use Expressions** box.
        
    2.  Select a **Filter** method.
        
    3.  In the **Mass Update** box, in the **Account** field, select **any of** or **none of**.
        
    4.  Select a value.
        
    5.  Click **Set**.
        
6.  To choose how to display search results, click the **Results** subtab.
    
7.  To review your changes before submitting them, click **Preview**.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
