Use the following procedure to remove a subitem from your item matrix.

#### To remove a subitem from your item matrix:

1.  Inactivate or delete the subitem.
    
    Note:
    
    You can't delete an item if it has been referenced on another record or transaction.
    
2.  To inactivate a subitem:
    
    1.  Go to _Lists > Accounting > Items > New. Check the Show Inactives box_.
        
    2.  Check the **Show Inactives** box.
        
3.  To delete a subitem:
    
    1.  Go to _Lists > Accounting > Items > New_.
        
    2.  Click **Edit** next to the subitem you want to delete.
        
    3.  Click **Delete**.
        
    
    -   If you use the Editing feature, click the field in the **Inactive** column and check the box for the subitem you want to inactivate. Click away from the field to save your changes.
        
    -   If you don't use the Editing feature, check the box in the **Inactive** column for each subitem you want to inactivate. Click **Submit** to save your changes.
