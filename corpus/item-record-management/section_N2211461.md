You can use the following reports to analyze cost versions and variances.

To customize a report, you can open the standard report and then click **Customize**.

## Production Variances by Item Report {#bridgehead_N2211549}

This report lets you view variances by cost categories and cost components. This information lets you quickly identify areas where too much scrapping can occur due to significant quantity variances.

To view this report, go to _Reports > Cost Accounting > Production Variances by Item_.

To customize this report, go to _Reports > Cost Accounting > Production Variances by Item > Customize_.

## Purchase Price Variances by Item Report {#bridgehead_N2211603}

This report shows price variances generated by purchases on an item-by-item basis. It lets you identify opportunities to manage inventory costs and negotiate with suppliers.

To view this report, go to _Reports > Cost Accounting > Purchase Price Variances by Item_.

To customize this report, go to _Reports > Cost Accounting > Purchase Price Variances by Item > Customize_.

## Planned Standard Costs By Cost Version Report {#bridgehead_N2211657}

This report provides a side-by-side comparison of different standard costs for items and assemblies based on each cost version.

To view this report, go to _Reports > Cost Accounting > Planned Standard Costs by Cost Version_.

To customize this report, go to _Reports > Cost Accounting > Planned Standard Costs by Cost Version > Customize_.
