This workbook is based on the item pricing performance metrics dataset and lets you compare items through the item-level pricing customizable view. In the workbook, sales transactions are grouped in pivot tables and charts by margin and revenue. Totals in this workbook match the totals displayed in the sales pricing reports.

Note:

The view is not in real time.

To access the workbook, on the Analytics Home page, click the Item Pricing Performance card or use the search field. To view details contained within the underlying datasets, see the help topic Item Pricing Performance Metrics Dataset Details.

The following component is included in the workbook template:

| Component | Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Table View | Item Pricing Performance | The customizable table view lets you visually compare cost, margin, rate, sales, and revenue for individual items. |
