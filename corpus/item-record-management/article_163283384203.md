An administrator can enable the Multiple Units of Measure feature.

#### To enable the Multiple Units of Measure feature: {#procedure_N2211996}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Accounting** subtab.
    
3.  Verify that the **Accounting** box is checked.
    
4.  Click the **Company** subtab.
    
5.  Check the **Multiple Units of Measure** box.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
