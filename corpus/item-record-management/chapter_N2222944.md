The Inventory feature lets you track and manage the items and services your company buys and sells. Item records track a wide range of information in your account, including physical inventory, and more.

Item records include the following:

-   Records to track physical items that you manage, including:
    
    -   [Inventory Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2247990.html)
        
    -   [Serial Numbered Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2230290.html)
        
    -   [Lot Numbered Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2235684.html)
        
    -   [Drop Ship Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2239232.html)
        
    -   [Special Order Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2242662.html)
        
    -   [Non-Inventory Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2249092.html)
        
-   Records include items you purchase and sell, but do not manage a physical inventory for, including:
    
    -   [Service Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2248153.html)
        
    -   [Download Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2248348.html)
        
    -   [Gift Certificates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2244991.html)
        
-   Clustering item records are used to assemble or package several items into one unit, including:
    
    -   [Item Groups](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2224824.html)
        
    -   [Kit/Package Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2225190.html)
        
    -   [Assembly Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2318089.html#bridgehead_N2318216)
        
-   Item records can help you with transaction management for selling and purchasing items, including:
    
    -   [Subtotal Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2248793.html)
        
    -   [Markup Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2248975.html)
        
    -   [Payment Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2249363.html)
        
-   Use [Other Charge Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2249208.html) to track purchase or sale charges that don't fit into any of the other categories. For example, for gift wrapping charges or vendor rush shipment charges.
    
-   Use [Description Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2248894.html) to add text to purchase and sale transactions, such as special shipping instructions or disclaimers.
    
-   Use [Discount Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2248474.html) on sales transactions to apply discounts to the items being sold.
    
-   Use [Expense Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4042372470.html) to charge tracked project expenses back to customers using the Charged-Based Billing feature.
    
-   Use [Matrix Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2227654.html) to create a matrix of records. An item matrix uses parent item and subitems to track items by options such as size and color. You can create a matrix for inventory, non-inventory, other charge or service items.
    

Note:

To convert Non-Inventory and Other Charge Items to an Inventory Item, enable Serialized and Lot items first. Then choose to convert to Simple Inventory Item, Lot Inventory Item, or Serialized Inventory Item.
