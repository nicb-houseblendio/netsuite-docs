The Mass Update feature is available for Advanced Pricing. You can define a mass update if you have the Mass Updates permission.

For more information, see [Mass Updates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1103335211.html).

#### To update prices using a mass update:

1.  Go to Lists > Mass Update > Update Prices.
    
2.  In the **Title of Action** field, enter a title for this update.
    
3.  In the **Price Levels** list, select all price levels to which you want to apply this update. Use **Shift** and **Ctrl** to select multiple price levels if desired.
    
4.  In the **Amount** field, enter the amount by which you want to update all prices.
    
    If this is a price reduction, include the negative sign.
    
5.  From the list in the **Based On** field, select your desired option.
    
6.  Select your desired rounding options.
    
7.  On the **Criteria** subtab, from the Filter list, select all desired options.
    
8.  (Optional) On the **Results** subtab, specify how you want the output to appear.
    
9.  (Optional) On the **Audience** subtab, select the desired roles.
    
10.  (Optional) On the **Schedule** subtab, specify when you want the mass update to start.
     
11.  Click **Save**.
