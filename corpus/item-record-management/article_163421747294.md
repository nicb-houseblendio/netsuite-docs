Before you can use the gift certificate item type, an administrator must enable the Gift Certificates feature.

#### To enable the Gift Certificates feature:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **Items & Inventory** subtab, check the **Gift Certificates** box.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
