You can delete a price rule if it's no longer needed and is not currently in use.

Note:

You can only create, edit, or delete price rules if you have Administrator permissions. This restriction ensures that pricing data remains secure and accurate.

#### To delete a price rule:

1.  Make sure Advanced Pricing is enabled (see [Enabling Advanced Pricing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9154128713.html)).
    
2.  Go to Lists > Pricing > Price Rules.
    
3.  From the list on the Price Rules page, find the price rule you want to delete and click the name to open it.
    
4.  Click **Edit**.
    
5.  From the **Actions** list, select **Delete**.
    
6.  Click **Delete Price Rule**.
