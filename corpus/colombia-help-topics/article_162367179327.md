NetSuite provides value-added tax (VAT) and withholding tax tracking and reporting for businesses in Colombia.

The SuiteTax Latam Engine SuiteApp helps you meet local tax determination requirements. Companies that do business around the world must comply with different tax rules from local authorities. The SuiteTax Latam Engine helps companies fulfill specific requisites and calculate taxes in their NetSuite transactions. The SuiteTax Latam Engine SuiteApp provides the deployment of a tax engine complement and a structure of custom records for setting up the tax definition in different contexts. The SuiteApp also provides a type of custom plug-in, the SuiteTax Latam Engine Plug-in. You can use the custom complement type to create complement deployments to calculate a tax or a group of taxes. With SuiteTax Latam Engine, you can set up tax calculations such as the income tax, the value-added tax (VAT), tax withholding, and others. The SuiteApp doesn't provide a predefined plug-in or record to calculate the taxes of any country or region. You'll need to create your own complements and records or download another SuiteApp that can provide the records. For example, NetSuite offers the SuiteTax Latam Engine - Colombia Records SuiteApp, which provides a group of plug-ins and records required to calculate taxes for companies doing business in Colombia. For more information, see [SuiteTax Latam Engine - Colombia Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161970452622.html).
