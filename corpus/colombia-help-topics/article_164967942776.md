In general, taxes aren't applied to all the transactions in the same way. Some only apply to services and others only apply to goods. Local governments can restrict some taxes to specific company types or establish special tax regimes. To help NetSuite identify the taxes that should be applied to a transaction, the SuiteTax Latam Engine uses the tax definition rules record. The records collect information related to the tax definition process, such as what affiliates and items are subject to tax codes, rates, and effectiveness periods. For more information, see [SuiteTax Latam Engine - Colombia Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161970452622.html).
