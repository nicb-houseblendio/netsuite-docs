Before you use the Colombia Localization SuiteApp, be aware of the current limitations and best practices.

## Best Practices When Installing the Colombia Localization SuiteApp {#bridgehead_164259971117}

When you're installing the Colombia Localization SuiteApp, keep these best practices in mind:

-   Enable the SuiteCloud features listed in the prerequisites section before installing the required SuiteApps for Colombia.
    
-   Install the required SuiteApps for Colombia before installing the Colombia Localization SuiteApp.
    
-   The permission with the highest CO Invoice Resolution level should be assigned to the specific role for invoice numbering setup.
    
-   Make sure the **Third Party Entity** field is enabled for all transaction forms at both the line and header level.
    
-   If you use group items in sales transactions, check the **Override Third Party** field so that the script fills in the **Third Party Entity** field at the line level. Otherwise, this field won't be filled in, which affects third-party and Magnetic Media reports.
    
-   When setting up the invoice numbering, the **Current Number** field must indicate the starting invoice number.
    
-   For the massive invoice process, the role must have the permission with the highest CO Massive invoicing process level.
    
-   To identify documents with errors or pending certification, add the Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp to your control panel.
    
-   The delivery of electronic documents to customers is directly carried out by a technological service provider.
    
-   It takes approximately 15 minutes for the XML to be assigned to the certified transaction.
    
-   When certifying a Credit Memo, all related invoices or documents should be previously electronically certified before the DIAN.
    
-   To obtain the Support Document, make sure that the following fields in the Vendor Bill form on the CUFE subtab are visible for filling:
    
    -   **DIAN Resolution Number**
        
    -   **DIAN Support Document**
        
    -   **DIAN Transaction Type**
        
    -   **DIAN Resolution**
        

For more information about the installation prerequisites for Colombia Localization, see [Installation Prerequisites for Colombia Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162367074300.html#bridgehead_164251801926)

## Limitations of the Colombia Localization SuiteApp with the SuiteTax Feature Enabled {#bridgehead_164259991981}

The Colombia Localization SuiteApp is compatible with the SuiteTax feature.

For more information about the current limitations of using the SuiteTax feature, see [Known Limitations of SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_161365561211.html).

## Other Known Limitations {#bridgehead_164259998510}

-   The global discount applies to the whole document, not to each product or service individually. Since the discounts are applied by product line, this DIAN concept doesn't apply to NetSuite. Therefore, all the discounts assigned to the transaction are always by product line.
    
-   If the entity requires the use of multibook accounting, the primary book must be expressed in Colombian pesos (COP). This is needed for the Colombia Localization reports and to ensure that third parties are included in all transactions. If a specific ledger entry was required, and an account receivable or account payable was assigned, the entity field wouldn't be available. Therefore, the transaction would not have a related third party.
    
-   Billable items, Billable Expenses, Billable Time aren't supported on the Electronic Invoicing inbound/outbound e-Document process for Colombia.
