The IVA Colombia plug-in enables you to calculate the Impuesto al Valor Agregado (IVA) tax on goods and services.

The following table outlines the plug-in requirements and capabilities.

| **Plug-in Implementation** | IVA Colombia |
| --- | --- |
| **Tax Code's Internal Name** | IVA\_CO |
| **Requires Parent Tax Determination Rules** | No |
| **Requires Parameter Types** | No |
| **Supports Tax Basis and Tax Rate Override** | Yes |
| **Adds Expenses** | No |
| **Changes the Tax Calculation Basis** | No |
| **Records Tax Calculation History** | No |
