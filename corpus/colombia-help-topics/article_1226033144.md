The RETEIVA Colombia plug-in enables you to calculate the Retención en la Fuente por IVA (RETEIVA) tax on the sales of goods and services.

The following table outlines the plug-in requirements and capabilities.

| **Plug-in Implementation** | RETEIVA Colombia |
| --- | --- |
| **Tax Code's Internal Name** | RETEIVA\_CO |
| **Requires Parent Tax Determination Rules** | No |
| **Requires Parameter Types** | No |
| **Supports Tax Basis and Tax Rate Override** | Yes |
| **Adds Expenses** | No |
| **Changes the Tax Calculation Basis** | Yes Considers the IVA tax amount as the tax calculation basis if:
-   The tax determination rules has settings with the IVA as Tax Basis parameter.
-   The tax determination rules record has a parent.
-   The parent tax determination rules have tax determination settings with an IVA tax code with the standard internal name.

 |
| **Records Tax Calculation History** | No |
