The Grant Management SuiteApp enables you to streamline grant management processes for organizations, particularly those that rely on grants for revenue and growth.

The SuiteApp helps businesses to improve their grant management processes within the NetSuite ecosystem. The SuiteApp is suitable for organizations that are not currently using a NetSuite Social Impact edition but need specialized grant management features.

The key benefits of using the Grant Management SuiteApp include:

-   **Single Source of Truth** - The SuiteApp establishes a centralized platform for managing grants, ensuring updated, accurate, and accessible grant-related information.
    
-   **One-page Dashboard Overview** - The SuiteApp provides a dashboard that displays key performance indicators and portlets that help manage and track grant-related data.
    
-   **Financial Insights** - The SuiteApp's reporting features provide valuable financial insights, helping organizations understand grant expenses, net assets, and the overall financial health of grant-funded programs.
    
-   **Flexibility and Customization** - Custom segments, records, and roles allow organizations to tailor the SuiteApp to their specific grant management needs.
    

The Grant Management SuiteApp supports all available languages in the Multi-Language feature of NetSuite. To set the language preference in NetSuite, see [Choosing a Language for Your NetSuite User Interface](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N479109.html).

## Limitation of Grant Management SuiteApp {#bridgehead_0422045826}

The Grant Management SuiteApp cannot be installed with accounts that already have the NFP Financials SuiteApp or bundle installed from the SuiteApp Marketplace or Control Center.
