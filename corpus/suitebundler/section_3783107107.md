NetSuite provides the following pages where bundle developers can review status and take action on bundles in target accounts:

-   The **Bundle Install Base** page lists details about accounts that have a bundle installed. See [Viewing Your Bundle Install Base](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3392340.html).
    
-   The **Push Bundle to Accounts** page lists the accounts where you can push a bundle to be installed (accounts where you have administrator access). See [Pushing a Bundle to Other Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3391992.html).
    
-   The **Copy Bundle to Accounts** page lists the accounts where you can copy a bundle. See [Copying a Bundle to Other Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3391248.html).
    
-   The **Copy Bundle Status** page lists details about bundles you have copied. See [Reviewing Copy Bundle Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3391248.html#bridgehead_N3391649).
