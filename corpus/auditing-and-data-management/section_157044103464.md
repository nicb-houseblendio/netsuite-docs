Before you can remove personal information, you must enable the feature.

#### To enable the Remove Personal Information feature:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, check the **Remove Personal Information** box, and review the SuiteCloud Terms of Service, if necessary.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

After you enable the feature, you can review and submit PI removal requests. For information, see [Reviewing PI Removal Requests](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156597280900.html) and [Creating and Approving a New PI Removal Request](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156597435833.html).

If the NetSuite Analytics Warehouse feature is enabled in your account, when you remove personal information from your NetSuite fields and records, you must reload the data in the corresponding functional areas and custom fields in the Analytics Warehouse. This action ensures that the personal information is removed from both systems. For information about how to achieve this, see [Reloading Your Data to NetSuite Analytics Warehouse](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0310044705.html).
