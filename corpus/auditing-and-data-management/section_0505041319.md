You can't use the Personal Information Removal feature to remove details on a sublist field. You must remove the sublist field details manually.

#### To remove sublist field details manually: {#procedure_0505041438}

1.  Clear the details from the sublist field on the record manually.
    
2.  Use the Personal Information Removal feature to remove the system note details for the sublist field.
