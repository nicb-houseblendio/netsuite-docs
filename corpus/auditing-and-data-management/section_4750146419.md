The following internal controls don't require any setup after your NetSuite account is operational.

-   Accounts Receivable
    
    Outstanding invoices are aged and added to an A/R aging report in real time.
    
-   Financial Close
    
    -   General ledger accounts automatically roll up into financial statement line items.
        
    -   NetSuite performs financial statement consolidation in real-time.
        
    -   The general ledger is automatically configured to include all accounts with balances.
        
    -   All transactions automatically roll up into the general ledger in real-time.
        
    -   Transactions can't be posted to closed periods in NetSuite.
        
    -   Out of balance transactions are automatically rejected.
        
    -   Transactions that reference a closed period are automatically rejected.
        
    -   Transactions that contain an invalid or inactive GL segment, for example, account or cost center, are automatically rejected.
        
    -   CTA (cumulative translation adjustments) are automatically calculated in NetSuite.
        
    -   FX (currency translation) related adjustments are automatically calculated in NetSuite.
        
    -   Only selected users have access to open and close the GL accounting period in NetSuite. After a GL period is closed, NetSuite subsequently doesn't allow posting of any GL-impacting entries to a closed period.
        
    -   Allocation weight is dynamically calculated when an allocation journal is generated, based on the current statistical account balance.
        
    -   Elimination journal entries are automatically generated based on intercompany transactions.
        
    -   Password strength for access to NetSuite accounts is required to comply with password policy.
        
    -   Calculation is automatic for a reversing journal entry that voids checks and transactions on days or periods different from the original transaction date.
        
    -   Journal entries require approval in line with Journal Entry Approval Policy prior to posting to the general ledger.
        
    -   Transactions posted outside of a posting period can be prevented or can initiate a warning.
        
    -   Limitations can be set on the individuals who can impact the account listed on an item record.
        
    -   A gapless numbering sequence is applied to all GL-posting transactions.
        
-   Financial Reporting
    
    -   Financial statements are automatically generated for each separate entity in NetSuite.
        
    -   Financial reports for each entity are automatically prepared in their local currency and are converted in real-time into headquarters currency for consolidation purposes.
        
-   Fixed Assets
    
    -   NetSuite automatically calculates depreciation expense.
        
    -   The Fixed Asset Module automatically calculates and posts the depreciation expense to the asset record and to the related journal entries to the General Ledger.
        
-   Inventory
    
    -   NetSuite values inventory at standard cost.
        
    -   Inventory transactions created and edited in closed periods can be disallowed.
        
-   IT General Controls
    
    -   You must have a minimum password length to comply with the password policy.
        
    -   You must update your passwords regularly.
        
    -   NetSuite automatically captures system notes when records are created, and they can't be edited by any user.
        
    -   When records are edited, NetSuite captures system notes, which can't be edited by any user.
        
    -   NetSuite automatically captures system notes when custom fields are imported or exported.
        
-   Order to Cash
    
    -   Items purchased in a sales order for a customer are automatically reflected in the invoice and are automatically sent to the customer by email when you click the **Save & Email** button on the Next Bill subtab. The order status of the sales order is automatically changed to Partially Fulfilled (several invoices or installment) or Billed (single invoice).
        
    -   For orders on an installment basis, the billing schedule in the body of a sales order for a customer is automatically populated in NetSuite, which you can view on the History subtab of the customer record. This subtab indicates scheduled billing dates and amounts for each date.
        
    -   NetSuite creates an entry automatically when you invoice a customer.
        
    -   You can't create a customer in NetSuite without a credit limit.
        
    -   NetSuite creates invoices only after an order has been shipped.
        
    -   NetSuite restricts changes made to item quantity, pricing, and shipping income per sales order during the invoicing process.
        
    -   NetSuite books an entry to debit Accounts Receivable and to credit Revenue upon invoice creation.
        
    -   If a customer exceeds their credit limit, NetSuite puts the order on hold or displays a warning message. An increase in the credit limit is generated and approved by the CFO.
        
    -   Orders that are entered into NetSuite, but not yet billed, can be included in customer credit limit calculations.
        
    -   You can set up standard grace periods for overdue invoices prior to placing a customer on hold according to their customer credit policy.
        
    -   You can't edit a sales order after it's been approved.
        
    -   After the A/R period is closed, you can't change the revenue recognition schedule for a transaction in NetSuite.
        
-   Procure to Pay
    
    -   You can limit records and transactions by department of the person entering or editing them.
        
    -   You can also limit records and transactions by class of the person entering or editing them.
        
    -   You can limit records and transactions by location of the person entering or editing them.
        
-   Revenue
    
    NetSuite doesn't allow revenue recognition greater than 100% of the total amount of the project.
    
-   Time Entry
    
    -   Time entry requires supervisor approval.
        
    -   Employees can't enter more than a certain number of hours per week, as per the HCM policy.
        
    -   Employees can't enter fewer or more than a certain number of hours per day, as per the HCM policy.
