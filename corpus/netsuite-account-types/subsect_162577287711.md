The Account Notifications section contains notifications related to each of your accounts. Notifications will alert you to required action items or status updates for each account, along with any instructions you need.

For more information, see [Sandbox Refresh Notifications](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162577320168.html).
