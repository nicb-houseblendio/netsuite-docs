The production (or live) account is where you do the daily work necessary to run your business. Many users spend most of their time working in the production account.

You access your production account by logging in to `system.netsuite.com` with your email and password. For more information, see [Understanding NetSuite URLs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4295564687.html).
