In addition to the live production account, NetSuite provides customers with various types of accounts to use to run their business. An account is a place to work, where you'll do your daily tasks.

Types of NetSuite accounts include:

-   [The Production Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1121105140.html)
    
-   [The Release Preview Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1121105228.html)
    
-   [The Sandbox Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1121105332.html)
    
-   [The Development Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1121105419.html)
