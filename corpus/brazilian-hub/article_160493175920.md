The Brazilian Hub SuiteApp enables canceling electronic invoices for goods, known in Brazil as notas fiscais de produto eletrônicas (NF-e), and for services, known as notas fiscais de serviço eletrônicas (NFS-e).

You can cancel an electronic invoice in the following cases:

-   If you identify errors on the information provided in the original transaction
    
-   If you identity errors on the tax calculation
    
-   If the customer gives up on a purchase
