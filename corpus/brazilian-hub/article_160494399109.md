The Instituto Nacional do Seguro Social Patronal (INSS Patronal) tax applies to the calculation of the Recibo de Pagamento Autônomo (RPA).

#### To set up INSS Patronal calculation:

1.  Go to BRHub > Fiscal > Parametrização de Impostos de Serviço.
    
2.  Click **Edit**.
    
3.  Click the **INSS** subtab.
    
4.  Click the **INSS Patronal** sublist.
    
5.  In the **Alíquota** column, enter the INSS rate on the calculation basis.
    
6.  In the **Data Inicial** column, enter or select the start date for the effectiveness of the INSS parameter.
    
    The start date is the date on which the parameters start to be effective for transactions subject to INSS. Brazilian legislation defines the date.
    
7.  In the **Data Final** column, enter or select the end date for the effectiveness of the INSS parameter.
    
    The end date is the date on which the parameter expires, losing its effect. Brazilian legislation defines the date.
    
8.  Click **Add**.
    
9.  Repeat the previous steps to add as many calculation basis ranges as required.
    
10.  Click **Save**.
