Brazilian Hub leverages Brazil Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp's predefined templates, records, and tax authority plug-ins required for e-document certification. You should use these templates, plug-ins, and records as a reference to create your own records and customize the settings according to your business needs.

Brazil Certification Tax Authorities adds predefined records for the SEFAZ tax authorities to certify the following e-documents for all 26 Brazilian States and the Federal District:

-   EFD-Reinf reports
    
-   Electronic invoices for goods
    
-   GNRE collection forms
    
-   Receiver acknowledgment events
    

To certify electronic invoices for services, the SuiteApp supplies predefined tax authority records for the following tax authorities:

-   **Bahia** - Salvador
    
-   **Minas Gerais** - Belo Horizonte, Uberlândia
    
-   **Paraná** - Curitiba
    
-   **Pernambuco** - Recife
    
-   **Santa Catarina** - Joinville, Florianópolis
    
-   **São Paulo** - Barueri, Campinas, Jundiaí, Santos, São Carlos, São José dos Campos, São Paulo, Sorocaba
    
-   **Rio de Janeiro** - Rio de Janeiro
    
-   **Rio Grande do Sul** - Porto Alegre
    

Cities may also adopt a third-party standard or the **Nota Fiscal de Serviços (NFS-e) Nacional**. For Brazilian Hub, the following third-party standards are available:

-   ABRASF v2.03 and v2.04
    
-   GIAP
    

Note:

Brazilian Hub includes information related to Brazil's tax reform on consumption in NFS-e generated for:

-   Cities that adopt the NFS-e Nacional, such as Porto Alegre (RS) and Florianópolis (SC)
    
-   Barueri (SP)
    
-   Jundiaí (SP)
    
-   São Paulo (SP)
    

Contact the tax authority directly to verify which standard they use.

Tip:

To generate PDF files from electronic invoices that adopt the NFS-e Nacional standard, set up a PDF template. For more information, see [Predefined PDF Templates for Brazil](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0724013953.html).

The predefined files are available in your account's File Cabinet. For more information about the File Cabinet, see [Searching for and Viewing Files in the File Cabinet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4799226178.html).

For more information about downloading the predefined files, see:

-   **Electronic invoices for goods** - To download e-document template files to generate electronic invoices for goods and receiver acknowledgment events, see [Finding the Predefined E-Document Template Files for Electronic Invoices for Goods (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0512012758.html).
    
-   **Electronic invoices for services** - To download e-document template files for electronic invoices for services, see [Finding the Predefined E-Document Template Files for Electronic Invoices for Services (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_15114615576.html).
    
-   **GNRE and EFD-Reinf reports** - To download e-document template files to generate GNRE tax collection forms and EFD-Reinf reports, see [Finding the Predefined E-Document Template Files for GNRE and EFD-Reinf Reports (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_35133448415.html).
    

After you download and possibly customize the templates, you can use them to create e-document template records in NetSuite. For more information, see [Creating E-Document Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4536871429.html).
