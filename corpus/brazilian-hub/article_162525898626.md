The Brazilian Hub (BRHub) SuiteApp enables you to receive instant electronic payments with Pix in NetSuite.

Pix is a solution created and maintained by the Brazilian Central Bank, which offers instant fund transfers and payments. Pix transactions can be performed at any day or time, in a few seconds.
