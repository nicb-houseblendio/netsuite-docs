NetSuite uses the electronic invoice for services series to generate this type of electronic invoice. The series determines the electronic invoice for services number assigned to each e-document. With the série NFS-e (electronic invoice for services series) record, NetSuite automatically assigns the numbers to each invoice, following a numbering sequence.

Cities may have specific requirements for the series name. Before you create the series in NetSuite, refer to the manual of the city you issue electronic invoices for, and review the requirements.

#### To create an electronic invoice for services series:

1.  Go to BRHub > Fiscal > Série NF-e > New.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter a name for the series.
    
    You can use alphanumeric characters to name the series.
    
3.  In the **Subsidiária** field, select the subsidiary that uses this series to generate its electronic invoices for services
    
4.  In the **Número Atual RPS** field, enter the number of the last transitory receipt for services (RPS) number assigned to a service invoice.
    
    NetSuite continues the numbering sequence from the number you enter in this field. This number is automatically updated whenever you generate a new electronic invoice.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

If you're an account administrator and you use multiple SuiteCloud Processors to send your e-documents for certification, review the Script Invoice (Script ID `customdeploy _sit_invoice_ue`) script deployment record.

For more information, see the script deployment review guide. The guide is available in the **Admin Documentation** field of Brazilian Hub's Bundle Details page.
