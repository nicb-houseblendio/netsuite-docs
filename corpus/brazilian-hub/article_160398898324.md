If your company's economic activities involve the movement of goods in interstate transactions, you can set up information related to the generation of the Guia Nacional de Recolhimento de Tributos Estaduais (GNRE) tax collection form.

Interstate sales that are subject to tax substitution, and other taxes owed to the state and collected in a different federative unit, may require a GNRE tax collection form.

Before you enable the GNRE generation, create the required e-document templates. For more information, see [GNRE Tax Collection Form Setup (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162188036978.html).

#### To enable GNRE generation for a Brazilian subsidiary:

1.  Go to BRHub > Informações Complementares > Subsidiária.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the subsidiary complementary record you want to edit.
    
3.  Click the **GNRE** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Ambiente GNRE** field, select the environment for issuing the GNRE form.
    
    If you want to send the file to the SEFAZ, select **Produção**.
    
5.  To enable the generation of GNRE tax collection forms by the subsidiary, check the **Emite GNRE** box.
    
6.  In the **Modelo E-Doc GNRE** field, select **GNRE**.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
