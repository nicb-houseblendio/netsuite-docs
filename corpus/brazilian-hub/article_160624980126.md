You can record a customer payment directly from the invoice.

#### To apply a payment on an invoice in Brazil:

1.  Go to _Transactions > Sales > Create Invoices > List_.
    
2.  Click **View** next to the transaction to which you want to apply the payment.
    
    If you use the approval workflow, you can only apply payments to approved invoices.
    
3.  Click **Accept Payment**.
    
    A new payment transaction opens.
    
4.  If you use the Classification feature, select a department, class, or location to associate this transaction with a classification.
    
5.  Enter other information relevant to the payment.
    
    For more information, see [Applying a Payment on the Customer Payment Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1288824.html).
    
6.  Click the **Apply** subtab.
    
7.  Click the **Invoices** subtab.
    
8.  Find the invoice transaction to which you want to apply the payment.
    
9.  In the **Apply** column, double-click the box next to the invoice you want.
    
    The Parcelamento window opens.
    
10.  On the Parcelamento window, click the **Parcelamento** subtab.
     
11.  In the **Quitar** column, click **Selecionar** to select the installment to be paid.
     
12.  (Optional) If you're applying a partial payment, in the **Valor Pagamento** field, enter the payment amount.
     
     By default, this field displays the installment's full amount.
     
13.  (Optional) If a fine applies to the payment, in the **Valor Multa** field, enter the fine amount.
     
14.  (Optional) If interest applies to the payment, in the **Valor Juros** field, enter the interest amount.
     
15.  (Optional) If a discount applies to the payment, in the **Valor Desconto** field, enter the discount amount.
     
16.  (Optional) If the payment date isn't the current date, in the **Data Quitação** field, enter or select the payment date.
     
     By default, this field displays the current date.
     
17.  Click **Quitar**.
     
     Repeat the previous steps to apply the payment to as many installments as required.
     
18.  Click **Salvar Quitação**.
     
     The Parcelamento window closes.
     
19.  On the Customer Payment page, click **Save**.
     
     The customer payment is saved. The payment transaction status is now **Deposited**.
     
     If the payment equals the total amount of the invoice, the invoice status becomes **Paid in full**. If you applied a partial payment, the invoice status remains **Open**.
     

For more information about receiving payments on invoices, see [Applying a Payment on an Invoice](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1289458.html).
