Brazilian Hub provides a predefined Certification Monitoring plug-in for you to receive from tax authorities the details about the current status of your certification requests.

To keep the certification information of your transactions and records updated, create a Certification Monitoring plug-in implementation.

Important:

After you create the plug-in implementation, you must associate it to the tax authority certification service records you use to certify e-documents. For more information, see [Creating a Tax Authority Certification Service Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161280503180.html).

#### To create a certification monitoring plug-in implementation: {#procedure_68140454137}

1.  Go to Customization > Plug-ins > Plug-in Implementations > New.
    
2.  In the **Script File** field, select **\_brhub\_pl\_ecs\_cert\_monitor.js**.
    
3.  Click **Create Plug-in Implementation**.
    
    NetSuite opens the Select 2.0 Plug-in Type page.
    
4.  In the **Type** field, click **Certification Monitoring Plug-In Type**.
    
    NetSuite opens the Plug-In Implementation record.
    
5.  In the **Name** field, enter **BRHub Certification Monitoring**.
    
6.  In the **ID** field, enter **\_brhub\_pl\_ecs\_cert\_monit**.
    
7.  In the **Status** field, select **Released**.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
