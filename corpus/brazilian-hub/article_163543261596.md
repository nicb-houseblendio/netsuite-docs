You can issue a return authorization and generate an electronic invoice for goods (NF-e) from it.

#### To enter a return authorization with NF-e generation:

1.  Create a return authorization transaction for a customer or vendor:
    
    -   To create a customer return authorization, go to _Transactions > Customer > Issue Return Authorizations_.
        
    -   To create a vendor return authorization, go to _Transactions > Purchases > Enter Vendor Return Authorizations_.
        
2.  Under Primary Information, select the entity of the transaction.
    
    -   If you created a customer return authorization, in the **Customer** field, select the customer.
        
    -   If you created a vendor return authorization, in the **Vendor** field, select the vendor.
        
3.  In the **Status** field, select the transaction approval status.
    
    You can only generate electronic invoices in transactions with the **Approved** status.Under Classification,
    
4.  the **Subsidiary field automatically displays the primary subsidiary associated with the ent** ity.
    
    If the transaction relates to a different subsidiary, select the appropriate subsidiary.
    
5.  In the **Location** field, select the city where the subsidiary is located.
    
6.  Under Informações Fiscais, in the **Tipo Documento** field, select **NF-e**.
    
7.  In the **Tipo Emitente** field, select **0 - Próprio**.
    
8.  In the **Natureza da Operação** field, select the transaction nature of the electronic invoice.
    
9.  In the **Modelo** field, select the electronic invoice template number.
    
    For NF-e generation, select **55**.
    
10.  In the **Série** field, select the electronic invoice for goods series.
     
11.  To add the returned items, click the corresponding subtab:
     
     -   If you created a return authorization from a customer, click the **Items** subtab.
         
     -   If you created a return authorization from a vendor, click the **Expenses & Items** subtab, and then click the **Items** subtab.
         
     
     1.  In the **Item** column, select the item being returned.
         
     2.  In the **Quantity** column, enter the quantity for this item.
         
     3.  In the **Unit Price** column, enter a price for single unit of this item.
         
     4.  In the **CFOP** column, enter the Código Fiscal de Operações e de Prestações (CFOP) code of this item.
         
     5.  Click **Add**.
         
         You can add more items to the transaction by repeating the preceding steps.
         
12.  Click the **E-Document** subtab.
     
13.  In the **E-Document Template** field, select the electronic invoice for goods certification template.
     
     The certification template provided by the Brazilian Hub (BRHub) SuiteApp is usually named **NF-e - Autorização**.
     
14.  In the **E-Document Sending Method** field, select the method by which to send the e-document to the tax authority.
     
     The sending method provided by BRHub is usually named **BRHub - Envio - Produto**.
     
     Tip:
     
     You can monitor the generation and sending statuses or processing errors on the **E-Document Audit Trail** subtab.
     
15.  Click the **NF-e** subtab.
     
     The information you need to provide on the **NF-e** subtab depends on your company's business and the transaction's purpose.
     
     1.  If you're generating the electronic invoice in contingency mode, under NF-e, fill in the fields.
         
         1.  In the **Data de Saída/Entrada** field, select the items exit or entry date.
             
         2.  In the **Hora de Saída/Entrada** field, select the items exit or entry time.
             
     2.  Under NF-e Referenciada, fill in the information related to the electronic invoice referenced on the transaction.
         
         1.  In the **Número da Nota Fiscal Referenciada** field, enter the number of the electronic invoice you want to reference
             
         2.  In the **Série da Nota Fiscal Referenciada** field, enter the series of the electronic invoice you want to reference.
             
         3.  In the **Chave da Nota Fiscal Referenciada** field, enter the access key of the electronic invoice you want to reference.
             
             Tip:
             
             Press Tab after entering the access key to automatically fill the remaining fields in this group.
             
         4.  In the **Modelo da Nota Fiscal Referenciada** field, enter the template number of the electronic invoice you want to reference.
             
         5.  In the **Ano/Mês de Emissão da Nota Fiscal Referenciada** field, enter the year and month in which the electronic invoice you want to reference was issued.
             
     3.  Under Frete, fill in the information about the items' shipping.
         
         1.  In the **Frete por Conta** field, select the type of shipping used.
             
             By default, the **9 - Sem ocorrência de transporte** (No shipping) option is populated.
             
         2.  In the **Código ANTT** field, enter the shipping company's Agência Nacional de Transportes Terrestres (ANTT) code.
             
         3.  In the **Placa do Veículo** field, enter the license plate number of the vehicle assigned to ship the items.
             
         4.  In the **UF Placa** field, select the federative unit of the vehicle's license plate.
             
         5.  If you need a second vehicle to ship the items, in the **Placa do Veículo 2** field, enter the license plate number of the second vehicle.
             
         6.  In the **UF Placa 2**, select the federative unit of the second vehicle's license plate.
             
         7.  If you need a third vehicle to ship the items, in the **Placa do Veículo 3** field, enter the license plate number of the third vehicle.
             
         8.  In the **UF Placa 3**, select the federative unit of the third vehicle's license plate.
             
     4.  Under Carga, fill in the information about the shipment.
         
         1.  In the **Quantidade** field, enter the amount of items shipped.
             
         2.  In the **Espécie** field, enter the type of unit measure, such as boxes or units.
             
         3.  In the **Marca** field, enter the brand of the items.
             
         4.  In the **Numeração** field, enter the serial number of the shipment.
             
         5.  In the **Peso Bruto** field, enter the gross weight of the shipment.
             
         6.  In the **Peso Líquido**, enter the net weight of the shipment.
             
     5.  If additional expenses apply to the transaction, under Outros Valores, fill in the information.
         
         1.  In the **Valor do Seguro** field, enter the amount covered by the goods's insurance.
             
         2.  In the **Outras Despesas** field, enter the amount of other expenses related to the goods.
             
         3.  If you're selling fuel, in the **Base de Cálculo ICMS Comb.** field, enter the base value for the calculation of the Imposto sobre Circulação de Mercadorias e Serviços (ICMS) tax.
             
         4.  If you're selling fuel, in the **Valor ICMS Comb** field, enter the amount of ICMS tax due.
             
     6.  Under Informações, review the information about the subsidiary and the transaction relevant to the Secretaria da Fazenda (SEFAZ).
         
         -   The **Informações adicionais** field displays the content of the **Informações Legais** (Legal Information) field as set in the informação complementar da subsidiária record.
             
             You can edit the field with additional content relevant to the SEFAZ, if required. For more information, see [Setting Up Tax Calculation for a Brazilian Subsidiary (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160398917925.html).
             
         -   The **Chave Acesso Referenciada** field displays the access key of the referenced electronic invoice.
             
     7.  If you're exporting the items, under Exportação, fill in the shipment information.
         
         1.  In the **UF de Embarque** field, select the federative unit from which the shipment was shipped or sent across the border.
             
         2.  In the **Local de Embarque** field, enter a description for the place of shipping.
             
         3.  In the **Local de Despacho** field, enter a description for the place of dispatch.
             
16.  Click **Save**.
     
     The system saves the transaction and the **Generate E-Document** button appears at the top.
     
17.  Click **Generate E-Document**.
     
     If the e-document is successfully generated, the **Send E-Document** button appears at the top.
     
18.  Click **Send E-Document**.
     
     If the e-document is successfully sent, a confirmation message appears at the top of the page.
     
19.  (Optional) To view the electronic invoice generated, click the **NF-e** subtab.
     
     1.  Under NF-e, review the electronic invoice information.
         
         1.  In the **Chave Acesso** field, review the the electronic invoice access key.
             
         2.  In the **Protocolo de Autorização** field, review the electronic invoice certification protocol number.
             
         3.  In the **Data Envio** field, review the electronic invoice sending date.
             
         4.  In the **Status NFE** field, review the return status.
             
         5.  In the **Mensagem Retorno** field, review the electronic invoice return message details.
             
     2.  To view a mirror image of the electronic invoice, under Links, click **Visualizar NF-e**.
         
     3.  To download the NF-e XML file to your computer, click **Visualizar XML NF-e**.
