The GNRE is an e-document used in interstate sales transactions subject to tax substitution and other taxes due to the state and collected in another federative unit.

To generate the Guia Nacional de Recolhimento de Tributos Estaduais (GNRE) tax collection form, set up your account as described in the following topics:

-   [Creating the GNRE Template (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162188134099.html)
    
-   [Creating the GNRE Status Request Template (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162188189422.html)
    
-   [Creating the DUA Download Template (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162188249197.html)
