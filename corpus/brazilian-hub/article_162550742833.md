To set contacts as recipients of emails containing Pix payment information, associate the contacts with customer records and assign them the Pix contact role.

#### To set a contact as a Pix email recipient:

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Customers_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the customer you want.
    
3.  Click the **Relationships** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Contacts** subtab, in the **Contact** field, select a contact from the list.
    
5.  In the **Role** field, select **Pix**.
    
    For more information about the Pix contact role, see [Creating the Pix Contact Role (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162525892597.html).
    
6.  Click **Attach**.
    
    For more information about associating contacts with other records, see [Associating a Contact with a Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1075740.html).
    
7.  Click **Save**.
