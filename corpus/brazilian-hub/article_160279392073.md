The Brazilian holiday chart enables you to create records for the local holidays or non-operational days that apply to your subsidiary. When you create a tabela de feriado (holiday chart) record, NetSuite skips its date when it calculates installments' due dates.

Note:

To skip holidays in due dates, you must set the **Contagem dos Prazos em Dias Úteis?** preference in the condição de pagamento (term of payment) record.

#### To set up Brazilian holidays:

1.  Go to BRHub > Financeiro > Tabela de Feriados > New.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter a name to identify the holiday.
    
3.  In the **Subsidiária** field, select the subsidiaries to which the holiday applies.
    
4.  In the **Data do Feriado** field, enter or select the date of the holiday.
    
5.  (Optional) If the holiday occurs on different dates each year, check the **Data Varia Conforme o Ano** box.
    
6.  In the **Tipo de Feriado** field, select the type of holiday, indicating whether it's a city, state, or national holiday.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
