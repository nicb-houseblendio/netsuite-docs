The Brazilian Hub (BRHub) SuiteApp enables you to calculate taxes levied on different purchase and sales operations in Brazil. The BRHub tax calculation features are compatible with accounts with or without SuiteTax.

To use BRHub's tax calculation features, you must set up your account as described in the following topics.

-   [Brazilian Custom GL Lines Plug-In Deployment (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160337504697.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Accounts Without SuiteTax for Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160743687629.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Accounts With SuiteTax for Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160337507258.html)
    
-   [Tax Parameters for Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160405854730.html)
