To use the electronic invoice for services templates on your transactions, you must associate the templates with your Brazilian subsidiaries on the informação complementar subsidiária (subsidiary's complementary information) record.

You must also enter additional preferences for generating and sending electronic invoices for services in the informação complementar subsidiária record. For more information, see [Defining Electronic Invoicing for Services Preferences for a Brazilian Subsidiary (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160398911925.html).
