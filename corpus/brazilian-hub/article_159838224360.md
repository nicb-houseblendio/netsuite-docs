You can use item records to define and track a product or service your company bought or sold. In Brazil, different taxes and rates apply to items depending on their origin, where they're bought or sold, and if they're services or products.

The Brazilian Hub (BRHub) SuiteApp localizes item records to meet Brazilian business needs by building on the localization fields provided by the Former Brazil Localization SuiteApp.

For more information, see [Setting Up Items for Brazil (Former Brazil Localization SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4658923476.html).

The following item types are customized by Brazilian Hub:

-   Non-inventory item
    
-   Inventory item
    
-   Lot numbered item
    
-   Serialized item
    
-   Service for purchase
    
-   Service for sale
    
-   Service for resale
    

For more information about item types in NetSuite, see [Item Types](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2222944.html).

For more information about how to create and set up items for Brazilian Hub, see the following topics.

-   [Setting Up Service Items for Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159838228962.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Product Items for Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159838230568.html)
    

For more information about creating and using item records in NetSuite, see [Using Item Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2164525.html).
