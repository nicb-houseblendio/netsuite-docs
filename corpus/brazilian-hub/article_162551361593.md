You can view the static QR codes that NetSuite generated for each installment of an invoice.

#### To view a static QR code in an installment:

1.  Go to BRHub > Financeiro > Parcelas a Receber.
    
2.  Click **View** next to the installment you want.
    
3.  Click the **Pix** subtab.
    
    -   The **QR Code Estático** field displays the static QR code which NetSuite generated.
        
    -   The **Caracteres do QR Code** field displays a string of characters contained in the static QR code.
