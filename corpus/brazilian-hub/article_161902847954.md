To use the electronic invoice for goods templates on your transactions, you must associate the templates with your Brazilian subsidiaries. You can establish this link between the templates and the subsidiaries on the informação complementar subsidiária (subsidiary's complementary information) record.

You must also enter other preferences for generating and sending electronic invoices for goods on the informação complementar subsidiária record. For more information, see [Defining Electronic Invoicing for Goods Preferences for a Brazilian Subsidiary (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160398915572.html).
