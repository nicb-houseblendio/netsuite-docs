The Brazilian Hub (BRHub) SuiteApp localizes the payment process in NetSuite for Brazilian business needs. The SuiteApp adds custom records to your account, such as the condição de pagamento (Brazilian term of payment) record, and custom fields on purchase, sales, and payment transactions. BRHub also enables you to generate and process payment files to integrate your bank payments with NetSuite.
