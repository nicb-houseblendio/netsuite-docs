If your company provides or hires services in Brazil, you must define parameters for calculating taxes on services in NetSuite.

The Brazilian Hub SuiteApp provides custom records for you to set information such as the following:

-   Type of service you provided or hired
    
-   Tax rate on the service
    
-   All the other relevant legal requirements for taxation in Brazil
    

You should define tax parameters for all the taxes that meet your business needs.
