NetSuite doesn't support the cancellation of electronic invoices certified by the Salvador (BA) tax authority because the city doesn't provide a web service for this operation.

To cancel an electronic invoice for Salvador, request the cancellation with the tax authority. To register the cancellation in NetSuite, click Credit on the transaction. For more information about the credit memo, see [Applying a Customer Credit Memo](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1312521.html).
