To generate outbound electronic invoices and post inbound electronic invoices using the Brazilian Hub SuiteApp, you must create modelos de documento fiscal (electronic invoice template numbers) records. The electronic invoice template number identifies the electronic invoice. It's mandatory on electronic invoices for goods.

#### To create electronic invoice template numbers:

1.  Go to BRHub > Fiscal > Modelos Documento Fiscal > New.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter the number that identifies the electronic invoice template number.
    
    For instance, the standard electronic invoice for goods template number is **55**.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
