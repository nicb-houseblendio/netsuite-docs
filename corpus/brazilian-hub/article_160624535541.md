In Brazil, the taxes collected depend on the type of expense. For NetSuite to calculate the appropriate taxes on your expenses, you must set up tax calculation.

The following taxes can apply to shipping, electricity, and telecommunication services:

-   Programa de Integração Social (PIS)
    
-   Contribuição para o Financiamento da Seguridade Social (COFINS)
    
-   Imposto sobre Circulação de Mercadorias e Serviços (ICMS)
    
-   Instituto Nacional do Seguro Social (INSS)
    

To learn how to set up tax calculation on shipping, electricity, and telecommunication expenses, see the following topics:

-   [Setup for Tax Calculation on Shipping Expenses (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162127314022.html)
    
-   [Setup for Tax Calculation on Electricity and Telecommunication Expenses (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162127312568.html)
