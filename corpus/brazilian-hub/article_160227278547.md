Billing a purchase order means you've received an electronic invoice from your vendor confirming the purchase. In Brazil, when you bill the order, you should also post the inbound electronic document associated with the transaction.

Each type of fiscal document requires different information on its posting. See the following topics to learn more about billing purchase orders and posting electronic invoices in Brazil.

-   [Billing a Purchase Order and Posting an Electronic Invoice for Goods (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162127262957.html)
    
-   [Billing a Purchase Order and Posting an Electronic Invoice for Services (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162127260993.html)
    
-   [Billing a Purchase Order and Posting an Invoice Document (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162127258060.html)
    

For more information about billing purchase orders in NetSuite, see [Billing a Purchase Order With Advanced Receiving](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2403862.html).

After you bill an order, you can enter its payment. To learn more about payments in Brazil, see [Payments for Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160268939174.html).
