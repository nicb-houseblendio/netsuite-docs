You must enable the Brazilian Hub (BRHub) Tax Engine Plug-in to use the Brazilian tax calculation feature with SuiteTax.

#### To enable the Brazilian tax engine plug-in:

1.  Go to _Customization > Plug-ins > Manage Plug-ins_.
    
2.  Check the **BRHub - Engine Tax** box.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
