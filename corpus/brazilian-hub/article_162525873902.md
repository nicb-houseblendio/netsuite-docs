When you create an invoice using a Pix term of payment, you can send the payment information to the customer via e-mail.

NetSuite sends Pix emails to the contacts that meet the following requirements:

-   Attached to the customer record
    
-   Assigned to the Pix contact role
    

For more information about creating contact records, see [Creating a Contact](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161792162006.html).
