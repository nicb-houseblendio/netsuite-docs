You can enter information about the subsidiary's profits or losses on the Lucros/Prejuízos subtab. They impact the assessment of the Imposto de Renda das Pessoas Jurídicas (IRPJ) and the Contribuição Social sobre Lucro Líquido (CSLL).

#### To enter a subsidiary's profits or losses records:

1.  Go to BRHub > Informações Complementares > Subsidiária.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the subsidiary complementary record you want to edit.
    
3.  Click the **Lucros/Prejuízos** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Ano** field, enter the year the profits or losses were recorded.
    
5.  In the **Lucro/Prejuízo** field, select profit or loss.
    
6.  In the **Saldo** field, enter the amount of profits or losses for the year.
    
7.  Click **Add**.
    
    To add more profits or losses records, repeat these steps.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
