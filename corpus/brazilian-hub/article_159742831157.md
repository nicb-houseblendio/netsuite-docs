The Brazilian custom address form enables you to enter addresses for Brazilian subsidiaries, customers, vendors, and employees.

#### To set up the Brazilian custom address form:

1.  Go to _Customization > Forms > Address Forms_.
    
2.  Next to the **Custom Address Form - BRHub**, click **Edit**.
    
3.  On the form record, go to the **Country** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Country** field, select **Brazil**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
