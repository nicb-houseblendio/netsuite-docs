Tax groups enable you to restrict tax parameters to a specific group of customers or vendors. When you assign an entity to a tax group, only the group's tax parameters apply to the transactions you create with the entity.

The Brazilian Hub SuiteApp provides the grupo de tributação (tax group) record. The grupo de tributação record enables you to restrict the relevant tax parameters to the entities you do business with in Brazil. Tax groups only apply to Brazilian taxes on goods.

#### To create a grupo de tributação record:

1.  Go to BRHub > Fiscal > Grupo de Tributação > New.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter a name for the tax group.
    
3.  (Optional) In the **Código** field, enter a code to identify the tax group.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

For more information about assigning a tax group to a customer or a vendor, see [Setting Up a Customer for Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159898071003.html) and [Setting Up a Vendor for Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160008700666.html).

For more information about assigning a tax group to a tax parameter, see [Tax Calculation for Taxes on Goods in Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160581800042.html).
