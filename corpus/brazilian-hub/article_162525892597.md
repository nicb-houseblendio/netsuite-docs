Create a contact role to assign to contacts to which you want to send Pix payment information via email.

#### To create the Pix contact role:

1.  Go to _Setup > Sales > Setup Tasks > CRM Lists > New_.
    
2.  In the Add to CRM Lists page, click **Contact Role**.
    
3.  In the **Role** field, enter **Pix**.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
