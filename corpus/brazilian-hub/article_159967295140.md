The CNAB 400 file format enables you to generate payment slips. The payment slip, also known as boleto bancário, is an offline payment method that's exclusive to Brazil.

With the Brazilian Hub SuiteApp, you can perform the following operations with payment slips:

-   [Generating Payment Slips in Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159983674534.html)
    
-   [Deleting or Canceling Payment Slips in Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160018978801.html)
    
-   [Updating Payment Slips in Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160019154369.html)
    
-   [Generating a CNAB 400 Delivery File (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160019244031.html)
    
-   [Processing a CNAB 400 Return File (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160026450276.html)
    

Before you perform these tasks, ensure that you've at least one condição de pagamento (term of payment) record that uses payment slips as a payment method. For more information about setting up a term of payment for this purpose, see [Setting Up a Payment Slip Condição de Pagamento Record (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159983672116.html).
