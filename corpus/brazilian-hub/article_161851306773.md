NetSuite uses the term nexus to describe a tax jurisdiction or geographic area where you do business. A nexus has its own tax regulations.

Nexuses are part of the NetSuite Advanced Taxes feature, required for NetSuite OneWorld. You must associate each subsidiary with at least one nexus. To ensure that NetSuite calculates the Brazilian taxes for your Brazilian subsidiaries, you must have a Brazilian nexus record on your account.

For more information about the nexuses in SuiteTax, see [Understanding Nexuses in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4283851663.html). For more information about setting up the nexus record, see [Setting Up Nexuses in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4296038572.html).

## Setting Up a Brazilian Nexus in Accounts With SuiteTax {#subsect_161851317275}

Set up the Brazilian nexus record as described in this topic.

#### To set up a Brazilian nexus in an account with SuiteTax:

1.  Go to _Setup > Tax > Nexuses_.
    
2.  Review the records list and verify if a Brazilian nexus record is listed.
    
    -   If a Brazilian nexus is listed, do the following:
        
        -   Next to the Brazilian nexus, click **Edit**.
            
        -   In the **Description** field, replace the existing description with **BRHub - Cálculo Impostos Brasil**.
            
        -   Click **Save**.
            
    -   If no Brazilian nexus is listed, do the following:
        
        -   Go to _Customization > Scripting > Script Deployments_.
            
        -   Next to the **Carga Nexus** (Script ID `customscripto2s_carga_nexus`) script deployment record, click **Edit**.
            
            Important:
            
            Don't edit any information on the script deployment record.
            
        -   From the **Save** dropdown list, click **Save and execute**.
            
3.  Assign the tax registration to a Brazilian subsidiary.
    
    -   Go to _Setup > Company > Classifications > Subsidiaries_.
        
    -   Next to the Brazilian subsidiary record, click **Edit**.
        
    -   Click the **Tax Registrations** subtab.
        
    -   In the **Country** column, select **Brazil**.
        
        When you select the country, the **Nexus** column automatically displays the **BRHub - Cálculo Impostos Brasil** option. If a tax agency is associated with the nexus record, the **Tax Agency** column is also populated with the tax agency.
        
    -   If no tax agency appears in the **Tax Agency** column, review the nexus record and verify if there's a tax agency set.
        
        For more information about nexuses and tax agencies, see [Setting Up Nexuses in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4296038572.html) and [Setting Up Tax Agencies as Vendors](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1796409.html).
        
    -   In the **Tax Engine** column, select **BRHub - Engine Tax**.
        
    -   In the **Effective From** column, select a date from which the nexus is in effect for the subsidiary.
        
    -   Click **Add**.
        
    -   Click **Save**.
        
    -   Repeat the previous steps for all the Brazilian subsidiary records on your account.
        
        For more information about assigning tax registrations to subsidiaries, see [Assigning Tax Registrations to a Subsidiary in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4296038273.html).
