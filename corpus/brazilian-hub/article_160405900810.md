The parâmetros do IBPT (IBPT parameters) record stores information about the indicators established by the Instituto Brasileiro de Planejamento e Tributação (IBPT) chart.

The IBPT chart shows the average tax load levied on goods and services, which impacts the sales price of the items.

You must create parâmetros do IBPT records for the goods and services your company sells and that the IBPT chart includes. The information entered on the parâmetros do IBPT record must be the same as the information provided on the IBPT chart.

#### To create a parâmetros do IBPT record:

1.  Go to BRHub > Fiscal > Parâmetros - IBPT > New.
    
2.  In the **Subsidiária** field, select the subsidiary that sells the item.
    
3.  In the **NCM** field, select the Mercosur Common Nomenclature (MCN) code for the item.
    
4.  (Optional) If there's an exception to the MCN code you selected, in the **EX** field, enter the exception code.
    
5.  In the **Tipo** field, enter the code for the type of the item.
    
6.  In the **Descrição** field, enter the description of the item.
    
7.  Enter the tax rates for the federal taxes levied on the item.
    
    -   If the item's origin is domestic, in the **Tributação Federal para Nacionais** field, enter the tax rate for the federal taxes levied on the item.
        
    -   If the item's origin is foreign, in the **Tributação Federal para Importados** field, enter the tax rate for the federal taxes levied on the item.
        
8.  In the **Tributação Estadual** field, enter the tax rate for the state taxes levied on the item.
    
9.  In the **Tributação Municipal** field, enter the tax rate for the city taxes levied on the item.
    
10.  In the **Data Inicial Vigência** field, enter or select the start date for the enforcement of the IBPT parameter.
     
     The start date indicates when the parameter takes effect.
     
11.  In the **Data Final Vigência** field, enter or select the end date for the enforcement of the IBPT parameter.
     
     The end date indicates when the parameter expires.
     
12.  In the **Chave** field, enter the key number.
     
13.  In the **Versão** field, enter the version number of the IBPT chart you used as a reference to create the record.
     
14.  In the **Fonte** field, enter the source of the information you entered in the record.
     
15.  Click **Save**.
