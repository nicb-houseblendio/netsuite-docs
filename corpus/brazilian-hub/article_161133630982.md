To send e-documents generated in NetSuite to tax authorities in a secure manner, you must upload the company's digital certificates to your account.

The Brazilian Hub SuiteApp supports digital certificates of the A1 type. Your company's digital certificates must include the private key and all the certificates in the certification path. Remember you need different certificates for generating electronic invoices for goods and for services.

For more information, see [Uploading Digital Certificates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1542656620.html).

If you aren't using the Administrator role, review the permissions required to access and upload digital certificates in NetSuite. For more information, see [Access to Digital Certificates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1547501301.html).
