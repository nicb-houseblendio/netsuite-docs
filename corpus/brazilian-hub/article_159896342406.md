Access to Brazilian Hub (BRHub) features requires several specific permissions. The roles assigned to users include these required permissions.

For more information about roles, see [NetSuite Users & Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N284861.html).

When you install the Brazilian Hub SuiteApp, the following standard NetSuite roles automatically include the required permissions to access BRHub features.

-   A/P Clerk
    
-   A/R Clerk
    
-   Accountant
    
-   Accountant (Reviewer)
    
-   Administrator
    
-   Bookkeeper
    
-   Buyer
    
-   CEO
    
-   CEO (Hands Off)
    
-   CFO
    
-   Chief People Officer (CPO)
    
-   Customer Center
    
-   Employee Center
    
-   Full Access
    
-   Human Resources Generalist
    
-   Intranet Manager
    
-   NetSuite Support Center (Basic)
    
-   Online Form User
    
-   Partner Center
    
-   Retail Clerk
    
-   Retail Clerk (Web Services Only)
    
-   Sales Administrator
    
-   Sales Manager
    
-   Sales Person
    
-   Sales Vice President
    
-   Shopper
    
-   Store Manager
    
-   System Administrator
    
-   Vendor Center
    

The following Brazilian Hub SuiteApp custom roles are also granted permissions to access Brazilian Hub features:

-   BRHub - Admin
    
-   BRHub - Contador
    

Note:

BRHub custom roles use the Accounting Center. You can also use the Classic Center. Other centers aren't supported by the Brazilian Hub SuiteApp.

You must use these standard and custom roles as references to create your own roles, in accordance with your company's security policies.

The roles created from the aforementioned standard roles and from the SuiteApp's custom roles inherit the permissions assigned to the original roles.

If you create any roles from standard or custom roles not listed in this section, the required permissions aren't inherited.

For more information about the creation and customization of roles, see [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html).

If you need to review or customize roles in your account, consider the required permissions to access Brazilian Hub features.

-   [Permissions Required for Brazilian Hub](#subsect_159896457100)
    
-   [Additional Permissions for the Employee Center Role for Brazilian Hub](#subsect_162197471268)

## Additional Permissions for the Employee Center Role for Brazilian Hub {#subsect_162197471268}

The Employee Center role requires additional permission and access settings to use Brazilian Hub.

-   In the Employee Center role record, you must assign at least the minimum access level to the following custom records permissions:
    
    -   Benefícios Fiscais
        
    -   Condição de Pagamento
        
    -   Condição de Pagamento Prazos
        
-   In the Employee Center role record, you must restrict the role to the subsidiaries that the users with the Employee Center role should access.
    
    For more information, see [Restricting Role Access to Subsidiaries (OneWorld Only)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N286421.html).
    
-   In the employee record of each employee that has the Employee Center role, you must assign them the following global permissions:
    
    -   Custom Record Entries
        
    -   Customers
        
    -   Employees
        
    -   Items
        
    -   Record Custom Field
        
    -   Subsidiaries
        
    -   Tax Records
        
    -   Vendors
        
    
    For more information, see [Using the Global Permissions Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N326630.html).
