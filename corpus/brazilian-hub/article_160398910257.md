If your company receives tax benefits, you should enter the information that affects the Imposto de Renda das Pessoas Jurídicas (IRPJ) and Contribuição Social sobre Lucro Líquido (CSLL) calculations.

#### To set up tax benefits calculations for a subsidiary:

1.  Go to BRHub > Informações Complementares > Subsidiária.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the subsidiary complementary record you want to edit.
    
3.  Click the **Incentivos** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Texto Legal** field, enter the legal text that regulates the concession of the tax benefit.
    
5.  In the **Alíquota** field, enter the tax rate related to the benefit.
    
6.  Click **Add**.
    
    To add more benefits, repeat these steps.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
