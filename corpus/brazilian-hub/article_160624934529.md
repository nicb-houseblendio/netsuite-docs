To record a payment you receive from a customer, you can apply the payment to the appropriate transaction in NetSuite.

In Brazil, due to the Brazilian Hub SuiteApp terms of payment feature, you must apply customer payments to transactions as described in the following topics. You can receive payments using:

-   Customer payment transactions
    
-   Invoice transactions
    
-   Bulk payment feature
    

For more information about receiving payments in Brazil, see the following topics:

-   [Receiving a Payment on the Customer Payment Page for Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160624937675.html)
    
-   [Receiving a Payment on an Invoice in Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160624980126.html)
    
-   [Receiving Payments in Bulk in Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160631655561.html)
