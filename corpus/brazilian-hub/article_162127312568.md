The taxes levied on the purchase of electricity or telecommunication services are ICMS, PIS, and COFINS.

-   **PIS and COFINS** - To set up PIS and COFINS calculation on electricity and telecommunication expenses, see [PIS and COFINS Calculation on Goods (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160581802662.html).
    
-   **ICMS** - To set up ICMS calculation on electricity and telecommunication expenses, see [ICMS Calculation (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160581807419.html).
