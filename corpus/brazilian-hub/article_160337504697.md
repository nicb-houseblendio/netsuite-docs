The Brazilian Hub SuiteApp provides a Custom GL Lines Plug-in to enable posting amounts according to Brazilian business needs. You must deploy the Custom GL Lines Plug-in to your account to ensure the proper posting of your amounts to the balance sheet. The plug-in is compatible with accounts with or without SuiteTax.

-   [Enabling the Brazilian Custom GL Lines Plug-In](#subsect_160338971871)
    
-   [Setting Up the Brazilian Custom GL Lines Plug-In](#subsect_160338973060)
    

## Enabling the Brazilian Custom GL Lines Plug-In {#subsect_160338971871}

To deploy the Brazilian Custom GL Lines Plug-in, you must first enable the plug-in on your account.

#### To enable the Brazilian custom GL lines plug-in:

1.  Go to _Customization > Plug-ins > Manage Plug-ins_.
    
2.  Check the **BRHub - GL Impact** box.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

## Setting Up the Brazilian Custom GL Lines Plug-In {#subsect_160338973060}

After enabling the plug-in, you must set up its deployment record.

#### To set up the Brazilian custom GL lines plug-in:

1.  Go to _Customization > Plug-ins > Plug-in Implementations_.
    
2.  Next to the **BRHub - GL Impact** (ID: `customscript_sit_gl_impact_parc`) plug-in implementation record, click **View**.
    
3.  Click **Configure**.
    
    The custom GL lines: configuration record opens.
    
4.  Click the **Execution Rules** subtab.
    
5.  In the **Transaction Type** field, select all transaction types, except Item Fulfillment.
    
6.  In the **Subsidiaries** field, check the **All** box.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
