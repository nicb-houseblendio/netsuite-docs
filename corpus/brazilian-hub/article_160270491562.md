To register your company's movement of goods, you must issue electronic invoices for goods, known in Brazil as notas fiscais eletrônicas de produto (NF-e). The Brazilian Hub SuiteApp enables the generation of this type of e-document in different scenarios. See the following topics for more information about NF-e generation on NetSuite.
