The Instituto Nacional do Seguro Social de Pessoa Física (INSS PF) tax applies to services provided by natural persons.

You must create the INSS PF parameters according to the tabela de contribuição mensal do INSS (INSS monthly contribution chart).

#### To set up INSS PF calculation:

1.  Go to BRHub > Fiscal > Parametrização de Impostos de Serviço.
    
2.  Click **Edit**.
    
3.  Click the **INSS** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Valor Teto de INSS - PF** field, enter the maximum INSS PF taxable amount from the current year.
    
    The maximum taxable amount is the final value of the highest range on the INSS PF tax brackets.
    
5.  Click the **INSS PF** sublist.
    
6.  In the **Data Inicial** column, enter or select the start date for the effectiveness of the INSS parameter.
    
    The start date is the date on which the parameters start to be effective for transactions subject to INSS. Brazilian legislation defines the date.
    
7.  In the **Data Final** column, enter or select the end date for the effectiveness of the INSS parameter.
    
    The end date is the date on which the parameter expires, losing its effect. You can edit this field later if the law changes and affects the effectiveness of the parameter.
    
8.  In the **Valor Inicial** column, enter the starting amount for the INSS calculation basis.
    
9.  In the **Valor Final** column, enter the ending amount for the INSS calculation basis.
    
10.  In the **Alíquota** column, enter the INSS rate levied on the calculation basis.
     
11.  Click **Add**.
     
12.  Repeat the previous steps to add as many calculation basis ranges as required.
     
13.  Click **Save**.
