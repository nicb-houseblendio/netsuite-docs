To enable the purchasing and receiving of items in Brazil, the Brazilian Hub (BRHub) SuiteApp customizes purchase transactions in NetSuite by adding localization fields to the transaction records. The SuiteApp also lets you record inbound electronic invoice details for your purchases, which is useful for bookkeeping.

To enter purchases on NetSuite, you need to create vendor records. For more information about creating vendor records for Brazil, see [Setting Up a Vendor for Brazil (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160008700666.html).
