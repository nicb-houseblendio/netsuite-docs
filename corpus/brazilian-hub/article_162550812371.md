You can set the static QR code as the payment format of invoice transactions. The static QR codes contain the installment's amount and the Pix key you select in the transaction.

#### To generate static QR codes as the payment format in an invoice:

1.  Go to _Transactions > Sales > Create Invoices_.
    
2.  Enter the information relevant to the transaction.
    
3.  Click the **Parcelas** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Condição de Pagamento** field, select a Pix term of payment.
    
5.  In the **Banco Previsão** field, select the account to which to post the payment received.
    
    Note:
    
    This account must be the same account that you selected in the **Conta Contábil** field in the banco contas record associated with the Pix key.
    
6.  In the **Formato de Pagamento do Pix** field, select **QR Code Estático**.
    
7.  In the **Chave Pix** field, select the Pix key you want to use to receive the payment.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
    
    NetSuite generates a static QR code for each installment in the transaction.
    
9.  Click the **Parcelas** subtab.
    
10.  Click **Visualizar Parcelamento**.
     
     The Parcelamento window opens. The window displays the installments NetSuite created according to the term of payment.
     
11.  (Optional) To send the payment information to the customer, at the top of the transaction, click **Enviar Email do Pix**.
     
     For more information, see [Sending Emails with Pix Payment Information (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162551252965.html).
     
12.  (Optional) View the generated static QR codes in the parcela (installment) record.
     
     For more information, see [Viewing Static QR Codes in Installments (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162551361593.html).
