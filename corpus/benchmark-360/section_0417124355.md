The Benchmark 360 SuiteApp requires a OneWorld account. If you do not have a NetSuite OneWorld account, contact your NetSuite account manager.

There are no other features required to install the Benchmark 360 SuiteApp. To continue with the installation process, see [Installing the Benchmark 360 SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0417125003.html).
