You need to add a Data Exchange or Planning and Budgeting job in NetSuite, and then select a plug-in to process data for the job.

#### To add a plug-in to a job: {#procedure_0112013850}

1.  In NetSuite, go to Planning & Budgeting > Jobs > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  On the **Planning & Budgeting Jobs** page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to a job of your choice.
        
    -   If you don't see the job in the list, and you have the Administrator role, click the **Add Job** button, and follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_160527233569.html).
        
3.  Click the **Options** subtab. Take one or more of the following actions, depending on what is applicable to the job type you've selected:
    
    -   From the **Post-processing Plug-in** dropdown list, select the plug-in you want to use to process data.
        
    -   If you want to store the log file of the executed job in NetSuite, check the **Save Execution Logs in NetSuite** box.
        
    -   If you don't want to run the import job, and only want to upload the CSV file to Data Exchange, check the **Upload Only** box.
        
4.  Click **Save**.
