The Income Statement form displays Net Income (all income and expenses) for closed Actual periods and open Forecast periods, by month. You can analyze the Income Statement form by Version, Subsidiary, Department and Class, in either an input or reporting currency. You can also view Accounts at a detailed level in the form.

#### To review the income statement: {#procedure_0708022815}

1.  From the Home page, go to Financials > Income Statement.
    
2.  Click **Income Statement**.
    
3.  In the Point of View, choose a Version, Subsidiary, Currency, Department, and Class.
    
4.  To refresh the form with the latest values from the Revenue and OpEx Forecast forms, right-click the data grid and select **IS Forecast Data Refresh**.
    
5.  Review the Actuals and Forecasted amounts by Account.
    
6.  (Optional) To see Account-level details for an Account, right-click the Account and select **Drill to Income Statement Details**.
    
    The Income Statement - Account Details form opens in a new tab. The form shows the Actual and Forecasted Account details for the selected Account by Relationship, Department, Class, Item, and Location.
    
    From the Account Details form, you can drill back to your NetSuite general ledger entry. For details, see [Drilling Through to NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7004007906.html#subsect_55011241130).
    
7.  (Optional) To aggregate the application:
    
    1.  Go to the Home page and click the **Rules** card.
        
    2.  Beside the ruleset **NFS\_All Accts Forecast Data Refresh**, click the Launch arrow.
