After you rename dimensions, update the data load rules for the metadata of those dimensions. This task makes sure the metadata loads correctly to the application.

#### To update the dimension names in the data load rules: {#procedure_1111123602}

1.  Click the Navigator icon ![The Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/IntegrationGuide/NSPB-SP_MenuIcon.png), and then under Integration, click **Data Management**.
    
    The Data Management window opens.
    
2.  Click the **Workflow** tab, and then under Data Load, click **Data Load Rule**.
    
3.  In the Point of View bar at the bottom of the page, click the link next to **Location**.
    
4.  In the Select Point of View window, set **Location** to the metadata for the dimension you renamed, and then click **OK**.
    
    For example, if you renamed the Department dimension to **MD\_Dept**, set **Location** to **MD\_Dept**.
    
5.  Click the **Target Options** subtab.
    
6.  In **Dimension Name**, enter the new name that you gave the dimension.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    
8.  If you renamed multiple dimensions, repeat steps 3 - 7 for each one.
