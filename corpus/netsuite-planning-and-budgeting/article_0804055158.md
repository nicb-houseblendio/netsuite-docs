When you log in for the first time, the application asks you to change your password and set your security questions.

#### To change your password for the first time and set your security questions: {#procedure_0804055449}

1.  In the Password Management screen, enter your temporary password in the **Old Password** field.
    
2.  In **New Password**, enter a new compliant password and then re-enter it in **Confirm New Password**.
    
3.  In **Register challenge questions for your account**, choose your challenge questions, and enter the answers.
    
4.  Click **Submit**.
    
5.  On the Set Preferences page, in the **Languages** field, select your preferred language.
    
6.  In the **Timezone** field, select your time zone. If needed, choose your specific time reference.
    
7.  Click **OK**.
