You can create and format your custom plug-ins as described in the help topic [Creating Custom Plug-ins](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1201050328.html), and then use these plug-ins with Planning and Budgeting Sync.

#### To use a custom plug-in with Planning and Budgeting Sync: {#procedure_84174408603}

1.  In NetSuite, go to _Customization > Plug-ins > Plug-in Implementations_.
    
2.  Click **New Plug-in Implementation**.
    
3.  Click the plus (+) sign to upload your custom script, and then click **Save**.
    
4.  Click **Create Plug-in Implementation.**
    
5.  From the list of plug-in types, click **NSPB | PI Job Post Process**.
    
6.  In the **Name** field, enter a name for your custom plug-in.
    
7.  Set the Status field to **Released**.
    
8.  Set the Log Level field to **Debug**.
    
9.  Click **Save**.
    
10.  Add the plug-in to a job, as described in [Selecting a Plug-in for Data Processing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1201052013.html).
