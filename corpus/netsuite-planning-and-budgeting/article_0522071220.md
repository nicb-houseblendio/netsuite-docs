The YTD Act vs Fcst Var and Comment form shows net income variances between the year-to-date (YTD) Actual and YTD Forecast by Subsidiary and Department. You can use this form to review and compare YTD Actual and Forecast values. Each variance shows as a value and a percentage. The form also lets you add commentary at the Department level.

Note:

Substitution variables set up Years, Current and Previous Periods, and Version members.

The form fills in values based on these calculations:

-   The **Act vs Fcst $** column shows the variance between the YTD Actual and Forecast values.
    
    Act vs Fcst $ = YTD Actual - YTD Forecast
    
-   The **Act vs Fcst %** column shows the variance between the YTD Actual and Forecast values, expressed as a percentage.
    
    Act vs Fcst % = ((YTD Actual - YTD Forecast) ÷ YTD Forecast) × 100
    

#### To review YTD Actual and YTD Forecast variances: {#procedure_0522071446}

1.  From the Home page, click the **Reporting and Analytics** card.
    
2.  To open Forecast Variance Reports, click the **1** tab on the left.
    
3.  Click **YTD Act vs Fcst Var and Comment**.
    
4.  In the Point of View, choose a Version, Subsidiary, Currency, and Department.
    
    The YTD Actuals show in the **Actual** column. The YTD Forecast values show in the **Forecast** column for comparison.
    
5.  If needed, enter any comments in the open (white) cells in the last column.
    
    Note:
    
    You can't enter commentary at Total Department level.
    
    Tip:
    
    To see all the text on a row, click the cell to open the text box, then drag the box's handle to resize it.
    
6.  (Optional) To see a detailed analysis of a specific Account member, right-click the Account and select **Drill to Income Statement Details**.
    
    The Income Statement - Account Details form opens in a new tab. The form shows Actual and Forecast data at a detailed level by Relationship, Department, Class, Item, and Location.
    
7.  To go back to the initial form, click **YTD Act vs Fcst Var and Comment** at the bottom of the page.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
    
9.  To update the data area with the latest updates, click **Refresh**.
