Use the operating expense (OpEx) adjustment forms in the Budget card to enter, validate, and adjust your operating expense Budget. To access these forms, from the Budget card, open the **3** tab on the left.

The Budget process has these OpEx adjustment forms:

-   OpEx By Dept Budget
    
    For more information, see [Entering Operating Expense Budget Values by Department](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206084251.html).
    
-   OpEx Detail Budget
    
    For more information, see [Entering Departmental Operating Expense Budget Values by Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206102051.html).
    
-   OpEx Top Level Adj Budget
    
    For more information, see [Making Top-Level Adjustments to the Operating Expense Budget](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206103637.html).
