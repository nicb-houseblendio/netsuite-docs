If you want to use NetSuite to schedule and run a batch job configured in Data Exchange or your Planning and Budgeting, you need to add the batch job in NetSuite. Then, you can set up the batch job as needed.

Note:

NSPB Sync only supports the Serial batch process method. See [Working with Metadata Batch Definitions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/enterprise-performance-management-common/erpia/metadata_batch_definition_100x2df58430.html).

#### To add and set up a batch job:

1.  Log in to NetSuite, and go to Planning & Budgeting > Jobs > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  On the **Planning & Budgeting Jobs** page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to a batch job of your choice.
        
    -   If you don't see the batch job in the list, and you have the Administrator role, click the **Add Job** button, and follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_160527233569.html).
        
3.  If you want to schedule the batch job, click the **Schedule** subtab, and follow step 3 in [Scheduling a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_160527228086.html).
    
4.  For optional setup of the data load rule, click the **Options** subtab, and do the following:
    
    1.  From the **Post-processing Plug-in** dropdown list, select a plug-in of your choice. The field is set to **None** by default.
        
        For information about how you can use post-processing plug-ins for the integration purposes, see [Using Post-Processing Plug-ins with NSPB Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_160527537382.html).
        
    2.  If you want to store the log file of the executed job in NetSuite, check the **Save Execution Logs in NetSuite** box.
        
5.  Click **Save**.
