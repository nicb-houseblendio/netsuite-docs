Use the OpEx by Account Dashboard to analyze the OpEx by Account Trend and OpEx by Account year-over-year (YoY) variance graph.

This section includes general instructions for using these interactive subforms OpEx by Account Dashboard:

-   **OpEx by Account Trend** - Shows the trend by Account for the selected Department. You can see values as an amount or percentage of total expenses. The subform lets you drill down into specific Accounts.
    
-   **OpEx by Account: YoY Variance** - Shows the percentage variances for the selected Department between the current and previous year by month, year-to-date, full year, and next year variances. The subform lets you drill down into specific Accounts.
    

#### To analyze the operating expense trends: {#procedure_0722013833}

Note:

Maximize or refresh each trend graph by clicking the Maximize icon ![Screenshot of the Maximinze icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/UserGuide/NSPBMaximizeIcon.png) and Refresh icon in each subform ![Screenshot of the Refresh icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/UserGuide/NSPBRefreshIcon.png).

1.  From the Home page, click the **OpEx** card.
    
2.  To open OpEx Dashboards, click the **2** tab on the left.
    
3.  Click **OpEx by Account Trend**.
    
4.  In the Point of View (POV), choose a Version, Subsidiary, Currency, and Department.
    
5.  To review the YoY variance percentage by Account:
    
    1.  From the dropdown list in the OpEx by Account Trend subform, choose one of these options:
        
        -   To see the graph by value, select **Amount**.
            
        -   To see the graph by percentage of the total revenue, select **% of Total Expenses**.
            
    2.  (Optional) Click on a line in the graph or an Account name in the legend to drill into the Account's operating expense.
        
6.  To review the YoY variance percentage by Account:
    
    1.  Hover over a series bar in the graph to see the series name and variance percentage.
        
    2.  (Optional) Click a bar in the graph to drill into an Account's operating expense for the selected Department. Or, click the Department name in the legend.
