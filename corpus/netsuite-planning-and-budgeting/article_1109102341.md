Using the Administrator role, you can assign the NSEPM Implementer role to users who should set up and configure Planning and Budgeting Sync on NetSuite accounts.

#### To assign the NSEPM Implementer role to a user: {#procedure_67110925131}

1.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Employees_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the employee to whom you want to assign the NSEPM Implementer role.
    
3.  Click the **Access** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Role** column, select **NSEPM Implementer**, and then click **Add**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
