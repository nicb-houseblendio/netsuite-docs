When you import Subsidiary metadata to your Planning and Budgeting, the Subsidiary metadata hierarchy is built with the following logic:

-   If a subsidiary from NetSuite has a parent, the parent of the subsidiary is used in your Planning and Budgeting as the parent.
    
-   If a subsidiary from NetSuite doesn't have a parent, the top-level subsidiary is used in your Planning and Budgeting as the parent.
    
    Note:
    
    You can customize the top-level hierarchy at NetSuite EPM > Planning and Budgeting > Manage Saved Searches. See [Customization of Executed Saved Searches in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114111339.html).
