After you install and set up the Planning and Budgeting Sync SuiteApp, you need to create saved searches to load data and metadata from NetSuite to your Planning and Budgeting. You can customize the created Planning and Budgeting Sync saved searches, or clone and customize any other saved searches you want to use with Planning and Budgeting Sync.

For information, see the following help topics:

-   For general information about the saved searches included in the Planning and Budgeting Sync SuiteApp, see [Saved Searches for Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_4153122253.html).
    
-   To create and customize the Planning and Budgeting Sync saved searches, see the following help topics:
    
    -   [Creating a Planning and Budgeting Sync Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_42135416383.html)
        
    -   [Recreating a Planning and Budgeting Sync Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1208015722.html)
        
    -   [Customizing a Planning and Budgeting Sync Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_98174727806.html)
        
-   To create custom saved searches to use with your Planning and Budgeting Sync, see the following help topics:
    
    -   [Creating Custom Saved Searches for Planning and Budgeting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1027033315.html)
        
    -   [Creating Custom Saved Searches from Planning and Budgeting Sync Record Types](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1027033539.html)
        
-   To view all the saved searches you have in NetSuite for your Planning and Budgeting, change search settings, and review search results, see [Saved Search Settings in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_8111232373.html).
