If a dataset is in the **Created** status, you can no longer select it for submission. If you want to use the latest release version of the dataset, you need to delete the dataset and recreate it in NetSuite.

#### To recreate a Planning and Budgeting Sync dataset: {#procedure_0711120926}

1.  In NetSuite, click the **Analytics** tab.
    
2.  On the Analytics Home page, click the **Datasets** subtab.
    
    NetSuite displays a list of datasets.
    
3.  For the Planning and Budgeting Sync dataset you want to recreate, under **Actions**, click **Delete**.
    
4.  At the prompt to confirm the action, click **Delete**.
    
5.  Go to NetSuite EPM > Planning and Budgeting > Manage Datasets.
    
6.  In the upper-right corner of the page, click **Create Default Datasets**.
    
    In the list of datasets, the status of the dataset changes to **Not Created**.
    
7.  Check the **Select** box next to the dataset.
    
8.  Click **Submit**.
    
    In the list of datasets, the status of the dataset changes to **Created**.
    

You can click the created dataset to review or customize it. To customize the dataset, see [Customizing a Created Planning and Budgeting Sync Dataset](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_36163231143.html) for information. To export, review, or customize the hierarchy of metadata in the dataset, see [Dataset Settings in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_94163304747.html).
