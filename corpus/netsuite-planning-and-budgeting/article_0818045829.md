After you run a job, you should go to the Jobs page and check the job logs to see the status of the rules that ran. On the Jobs page, you can see if a database refresh was successful. If a job runs successfully, its status is **Completed**. If the status isn't **Completed**, check the error log to see the specific errors what you may need to fix.

#### To check if a business rule ran: {#procedure_0818050704}

1.  Click the Navigator icon ![Screenshot of the Navigator icon in NSPB](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/NSPBNavigatorIcon.png), and under Application, select **Jobs**.
    
2.  Find the job you want to review under **Recent Activity**, and then check if the status is **Completed**.
    
3.  If the status is **Completed with Errors** or **Error**, click the Job Status link to see the error log.
    
    Review the error log to see if there's anything you need to fix.
