To connect a user to Planning and Budgeting from NetSuite, the Planning and Budgeting Sync SuiteApp supports two types of authentication:

-   Authentication by entering a username and a password.
    
    For information, see [Using Basic Authentication to Connect to Planning and Budgeting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1121101934.html).
    
-   OAuth 2.0 that eliminates the need to store user credentials in NetSuite. This provides better security and prevents integration issues when a password expires or is changed.
    
    For information, see [Using OAuth 2.0 to Connect to Planning and Budgeting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1121101959.html).
    

#### To choose your preferred authentication method:

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Planning and Budgeting > Connection Settings.
    
2.  In the **Planning and Budgeting URL** field, enter the URL that points to the Planning and Budgeting application you want NetSuite to connect to.
    
    Depending on the Planning and Budgeting instance, the format of the URL may look similar to one of the following examples:
    
    -   `https://epm-test-idDomain.epm.dataCenterRegion.oraclecloud.com/`
        
    -   `https://acme-epmidm.epm.us-phoenix-1.ocs.oraclecloud.com/`
        
    
    To check or understand the URL components, see the Oracle Help Center topic [Sample URLs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/enterprise-performance-management-common/cgsad/1_epm_cloud_accessing_sample_urls.html).
    
3.  In the **Planning Application Name** field, enter the name of your Planning and Budgeting environment. For example, NetSuite.
    
4.  To verify that the Planning and Budgeting application identified in the **Planning and Budgeting URL** field connects to this NetSuite account, check the **Use Entered URL to Verify Inbound Connections** box.
    
    Tip:
    
    To avoid connection issues or troubleshoot:
    
    -   Go to NetSuite EPM > EPM Connector > Search Activity Logs, and then compare the `sourceinstance` value in the **Execution Parameters** column with the **Planning and Budgeting URL** value.
        
    -   Check the integration user's NetSuite Account ID entered in the **Account** field during token-based authentication setup in Data Exchange. See step 14.g in [Setting Up Token-based Authentication for Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_3142954952.html).
        
    
5.  Choose your preferred authentication method from the available options:
    
    1.  **Basic Auth**
        
        Important:
        
        This option displays a box that you must check to confirm your understanding that you should use basic authentication only for testing purposes or when OAuth 2.0 is unavailable. Until you check the displayed box, you can't start using basic authentication as your preferred authentication method.
        
        For configuration instructions, see [Using Basic Authentication to Connect to Planning and Budgeting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1121101934.html).
        
    2.  **OAuth 2.0**
        
        For configuration instructions, see [Using OAuth 2.0 to Connect to Planning and Budgeting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1121101959.html).
        
6.  Click **Save Active Connection** to save your preferred authentication method.
