To load data and metadata to Planning and Budgeting Financials, you need to manually run or schedule the following pipeline jobs in NetSuite:

-   **Pipeline\_Metadata\_Load**
    
    This pipeline job loads metadata to your Planning and Budgeting Financials.
    
-   **Pipeline\_Data\_Load**
    
    This pipeline job loads transaction data to your Planning and Budgeting Financials.
    
-   **Pipeline\_Metadata\_Data\_Load**
    
    This pipeline job runs the mentioned **Pipeline\_Metadata\_Load** and **Pipeline\_Data\_Load** jobs to load metadata and transaction data into your Planning and Budgeting Financials.
    

Important:

To run these pipeline jobs from NetSuite, you first need to add them to your NetSuite account as a default feature. For instructions, see [Setting Up Default Configurations for Planning and Budgeting Financials](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_36142750338.html).

#### To run a pipeline job manually:

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Planning and Budgeting > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  In the list of jobs, click **Execute** next to the pipeline job.
    
    This takes you to a page with the job details.
    
3.  To start loading data into Planning and Budgeting Financials, click **Execute**.
    
    The information banner shows when the job starts running.
    
4.  To check the job status, click the **Execution Logs** subtab.
    

Note:

To keep your transaction data and metadata up to date, you can schedule these default pipeline jobs to run regularly. For instructions, see [Scheduling a Job in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114011407.html).

For general information and additional setup, see [Pipeline in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0702100941.html) and [Managing Jobs in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5133320393.html).
