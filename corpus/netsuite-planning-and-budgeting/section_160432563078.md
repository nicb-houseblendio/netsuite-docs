To go to your Planning and Budgeting service directly from NetSuite, you need to store user credentials in NetSuite and define which Planning and Budgeting environment you want NetSuite to connect to. For instructions, see [Connection Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160855050373.html).

If you then go to Planning & Budgeting > Access > NetSuite Planning and Budgeting in NetSuite, you're redirected to your Planning and Budgeting environment.
