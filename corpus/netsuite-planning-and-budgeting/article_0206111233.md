Use the Income Statement - Budget form to analyze the net income (revenue and operating expenses) for the Budget Year.

#### To review the Budget income statement:

1.  From the Home page, click the **Budget** card.
    
2.  Click the **4** tab on the left.
    
    The Income Statement - Budget form opens.
    
3.  In the Point of View, choose a Scenario, Version, Subsidiary, Currency, Department, and Class.
    
4.  To refresh the form with the latest values from the Revenue and OpEx Budget forms, right-click the data grid, and select **IS Budget Data Refresh**.
    
5.  Review the Budget amounts by Account.
