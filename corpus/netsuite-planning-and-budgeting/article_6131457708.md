This section describes the Financials process flow and the financial statements that you can use in the Financials cluster. The Financials cluster has the Balance Sheet Trending, Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow cards, each with their own forms and dashboards.

The financial statements let you do the following activities:

-   Predict working capital with Balance Sheet Trending.
    
-   Review the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Report.
    
-   Make final adjustments to the Forecast.
    
-   Analyze key financial metrics and trends with the various financial statement Dashboards.
