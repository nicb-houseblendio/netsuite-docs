The Planning and Budgeting Sync SuiteApp is a shared SuiteApp. Before you can install the SuiteApp, it must be provisioned for your NetSuite account. For more information, contact your NetSuite account manager.

To install the Planning and Budgeting Sync SuiteApp, you must have permission and access to the SuiteApp Marketplace. For information about the SuiteApp Marketplace, see [SuiteApp Marketplace in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1539797981.html).

#### To install the Planning and Budgeting Sync SuiteApp: {#procedure_1027125334}

1.  In NetSuite, click the **SuiteApps** tab.
    
2.  In the **Search SuiteApps** field, enter **Planning and Budgeting Sync**.
    
3.  Click the **Planning and Budgeting Sync** tile.
    
4.  In the upper-right area of the Planning and Budgeting Sync details page, click **Install**.
    
    Note:
    
    If you can see **Limited Access** instead of the **Install** button, it means that the SuiteApp hasn't been provisioned for your NetSuite account. For assistance, contact your NetSuite account manager.
    
5.  Wait for the installation to complete.
    

Planning and Budgeting Sync is a managed SuiteApp. When a new version is available, NetSuite automatically updates your account.

For general information about installing, updating, and deleting SuiteApps from the SuiteApp Marketplace, see [SuiteApp Installation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1540236668.html).
