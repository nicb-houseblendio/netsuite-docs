Clicking a button in the Job Execution Portlet executes the job you assigned to the button, as described in help topic [Job Execution Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024144.html). To watch the progress of job execution, you need to add the corresponding Execution Logs portlet to the NetSuite EPM page. For instructions, see [Accessing NetSuite EPM Overview Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114023631.html).

The available Execution Logs portlets include the following:

-   The **NSPB | Job Execution Log 1** portlet for button 1.
    
-   The **NSPB | Job Execution Log 2** portlet for button 2.
    
-   The **NSPB | Job Execution Log 3** portlet for button 3.
