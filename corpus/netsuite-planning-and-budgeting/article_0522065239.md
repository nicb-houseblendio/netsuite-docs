The MoM Actual Var and Comment form shows the Net Income variances between two previously closed periods by Subsidiary and Department. You can use this form to review the month-over-month (MoM) Actual variance as a value and a percentage. The form also lets you add commentary at the Department level.

Note:

Substitution variables to set up Years, Current and Previous Periods, and Version members.

The form fills in values based on these calculations:

-   The **MoM $ Change** column shows the month-over-month variance amount.
    
    MoM $ Change = Period before Previous Amount - Previous Period Amount
    
-   The **MoM % Change** column shows the month-over-month variance, expressed as a percentage.
    
    MoM % Change = ((Period before Previous Amount - Previous Period Amount) ÷ Period before Previous Amount) × 100
    

#### To review month-over-month variances: {#procedure_0522065313}

1.  From the Home page, click the **Reporting and Analytics** card.
    
2.  To open Forecast Variance Reports, click the **1** tab on the left.
    
3.  Click **MoM Actual Var and Comment**.
    
4.  In the Point of View, choose a Version, Subsidiary, Currency, and Department.
    
5.  If needed, enter any comments in the open (white) cells in the last column.
    
    Note:
    
    You can't enter commentary at Total Department level.
    
    Tip:
    
    To see all the text in a row, click the cell to open the text box, then drag the box's handle to resize it.
    
6.  (Optional) To see a detailed analysis of a specific Account member, right-click the Account and select **Drill to Actual Inc Stmt Details**.
    
    The Actual Inc Stmt - Account Details form opens in a new tab. The form shows the Actual details for the selected Account by Relationship, Department, Class, Item, and Location.
    
7.  To go back to the initial form, click **MoM Actual Var and Comment** at the bottom of the page.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
    
9.  To update the data area with the latest updates, click **Refresh**.
