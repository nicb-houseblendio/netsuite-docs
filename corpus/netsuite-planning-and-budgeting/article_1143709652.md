**Income Statement**

After all Forecasts are updated, finance executives use the forms in the Income Statement card to review income statement Accounts and make final adjustments. They analyze key performance indicators and trends. They also review the Consolidated Income Statement in this card.

**Balance Sheet Trending**

Then, finance executives use the forms in the Balance Sheet Trending card to monitor and review the balance sheet. They can also project future working capital closing balances and monthly movements.

**Balance Sheet**

Finance executives use the forms in the Balance Sheet card to see the monthly movements and ending balances for Actual and Forecasted balance sheet Accounts. They review key financial metrics and trends. They also review the Group Consolidated Balance Sheet in this card.

**Cash Flow**

Finally, finance executives use the forms in the Cash Flow card to review the Actual and Forecasted Cash Flow Report and analyze key financial metrics. They also review the Group Consolidated Cash Flow.
