The NSPB Sync SuiteApp enables you to use NetSuite to access and manage features in your Planning and Budgeting service.

To access the NSPB Sync functionality in the NetSuite UI, log in to your NetSuite account, and hover over the Planning & Budgeting tab. This expands the following menu options for you:
