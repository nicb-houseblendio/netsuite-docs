Use the Exchange Rates Budget form to update the exchange rates for open Budget periods.

#### To update exchange rates for open Budget periods:

1.  From the Home page, click the **Budget** card.
    
2.  Click the **1** tab on the left.
    
3.  Click **Exchange Rates Budget**.
    
4.  In the Point of View, choose a Scenario and Version.
    
5.  For each currency, update the Ending, Average, and Historical rate values for open Budget periods, as needed.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
