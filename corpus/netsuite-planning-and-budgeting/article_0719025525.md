The Cash Flow Report form shows the net cash flow (inflow and outflow) by Subsidiary for Actual and open Forecast periods. Cash flow comes from operating, investing and financing activities. Use this form to review Actual and projected Forecast trends for operating cash flow to see if the Forecast is realistic and meets your company's goals. Analyze the Investing and Financing sections of the Cash Flow Report to make sure that they also align with your company's expectations. Finally, assess the end-of-month cash position for projected periods to see if the projections make sense.

The last four rows on this report show these values for you to review:

-   Net Change in Cash for Period
    
-   Effect of Exchange Rate on Cash
    
-   Cash at Beginning of Period
    
-   Cash at End of Period
    

#### To review the Cash Flow Report: {#procedure_0719034447}

1.  From the Home page, go to Financials > Cash Flow.
    
2.  Click **Cash Flow Report** at the bottom of the page.
    
3.  In the Point of View, choose a Version, Subsidiary, and Currency member.
    
4.  (Optional) To refresh the form with the latest Forecast values, right-click the data grid and select **All Accts Bud Data Refresh**.
    
5.  All the activities on the report are calculated by using formulas. To see the formula for a row, click the Member Formula icon ![Screenshot of the Formula icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/UserGuide/NSPBFormulaIcon.png) next to the activity.
    
6.  Click **Close** when you're done with the Member Formula window.
    
7.  Review the cash flow values to determine if the Budget looks realistic, aligns with your company's expectations, and if projections make sense.
