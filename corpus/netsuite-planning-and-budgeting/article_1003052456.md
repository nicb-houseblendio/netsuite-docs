You can manually run the jobs you've added in NetSuite for Planning and Budgeting Sync, and watch the progress as the jobs run.

Important:

When you run a job manually through the UI, it uses the user's settings, including the user's language preference. If you schedule the job, it's executed using the account's settings, including language.

#### To manually run a job in NetSuite:

1.  Go to NetSuite EPM > Planning and Budgeting > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  To manually run a job, click **Execute** next to the job. This takes you to the Execute Job page where you can do the following:
    
    -   To start executing the job, click the **Execute** button. The information banner indicates the start of the job execution.
        
    -   To watch the status of the job execution, click the **Execution Logs** subtab.
        
        Note:
        
        For batch jobs, the execution status of each data integration job included in a batch of jobs is displayed on a separate line.
        
    -   When the job is running, the **Reset Job Status** button appears on the page. Click the button if you need to terminate the job execution. This doesn't cancel the job execution in Data Exchange or your Planning and Budgeting. This resets the job status in NetSuite, and lets you reschedule and run the job again from NetSuite.
