You can view NetSuite SuitePeople payroll items and their amounts, grouped and summed by workforce categories. NetSuite loads this data into your Planning and Budgeting.

Note:

The workforce categories are dynamically populated based on the mapping formula used in your saved search. See [Customizing the NSPB (Data) SuitePeople - Employee Payroll Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1203093914.html).

Tip:

You can use this feature to verify the payroll data in your NetSuite account.

#### To view SuitePeople payroll data by category:

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Planning and Budgeting > Configuration Checklist.
    
2.  For **The Payroll Data by Category in SuitePeople is set for NSPB**, click **View**. This opens a page showing the payroll items and payroll item amounts grouped and summed by workforce categories.
    
    To filter payroll data for a specific date range, do the following:
    
    1.  In the **Subsidiary** field, select a subsidiary.
        
    2.  In the **Start Date** field, enter the start date to filter the items.
        
        Tip:
        
        Click the calendar icon to choose the start date.
        
    3.  In the **End Date** field, enter the end date to filter the items up to a specific date.
        
        Tip:
        
        Click the calendar icon to choose the end date.
        
    4.  Click **Submit**.
