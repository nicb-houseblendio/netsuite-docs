Use the Revenue - New Customer by Account Budget form to enter Budget values for potential Customers that are not listed on the Revenue by Customer Budget form. Enter data in the generic Customer rows for the Account that you select.

#### To enter Budget values for new Customers by Account:

1.  From the Home page, click the **Budget** card.
    
2.  Click the **2** tab on the left.
    
3.  Click **New Customer by Account**.
    
4.  In the Point of View (POV), choose a Scenario, Version, Subsidiary, Currency, Class, Item, and Account.
    
    Note:
    
    To enter Budget values in this form, set your POV to level 0 members.
    
5.  Enter Budget values for one or more new Customers.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
7.  To recalculate the cost of goods sold (COGS), right-click the data grid and select **Launch COGS for all Customers Budget**.
    
8.  To update top-level members with the adjusted data in input currency, right-click the data grid and select **Agg to Income Statement Budget**.
    
9.  To see the new Customer's revenue Budget:
    
    1.  Click **Revenue by Customer**.
        
    2.  In the POV, set **Customer Category** to **Total New Customer**.
