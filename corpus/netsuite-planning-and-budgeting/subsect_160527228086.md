You can schedule jobs that you've added to NetSuite for NSPB Sync.

Important:

Before you start scheduling jobs for NSPB Sync, read the following information:

-   To run the Data Exchange jobs in NetSuite, you need to schedule the following script deployments properly:
    
    -   **NSPB MR DLR Scheduler**, ID customdeploy\_nspbcs\_mr\_dlr\_scheduler
        
    -   **NSPB SS Cleanup**, ID customdeploy\_nspbcs\_ss\_cleanup
        
    -   **NSPB SS Job status update**, ID customdeploy\_nspbcs\_dlr\_status\_update
        
    
    For instructions, see [Scheduling Script Deployments for NSPB Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160829049734.html).
    
-   Some NetSuite accounts don't support scheduled jobs. This includes some, but not all, test drive accounts (that is, accounts where the account ID begins with TSTDRV). If that's your case, the scheduling of job execution won't work for you.
    

#### To schedule a job in NetSuite:

1.  Log in to NetSuite, and go to Planning & Budgeting > Jobs > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  On the **Planning & Budgeting Jobs** page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to a job of your choice.
        
    -   If you don't see the job in the list, and you have the Administrator role, click the **Add Job** button, and follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_160527233569.html).
        
3.  Click the **Schedule** subtab, and do the following:
    
    1.  From the **Frequency** dropdown list, select how often you want the job to be run within a scheduled period. The available options include the following:
        
        -   **Not Scheduled** to inactivate the schedule
            
        -   **Simple** to run the job only one time
            
        -   **Hourly** to run the job every hour
            
        -   **Daily** to run the job every day
            
        -   **Weekly** to run the job on a particular day of the week
            
        -   **Monthly** to run the job on a particular day of the month
            
    2.  Determine the date and time when you want to regularly run the job. Depending on the frequency you've selected, you have the following options:
        
        -   **Start Date**
            
        -   **End Date**
            
        -   **Hours**
            
        -   **Minutes**
            
        -   **Day of Week**
            
        -   **Day of Month**
            
        
        Note:
        
        The date and time you define for job execution reflects the time zone of your NetSuite account.
        
    3.  If you want to pause the schedule, check the **Pause Scheduler** box. Until you resume the schedule, the job that runs on the schedule stops running and doesn't show in [Jobs Calendar](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160440094269.html).
        
4.  Click **Save**.
