To personalize your home page, you can add the [Planning and Budgeting Form/Dashboard Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024026.html) portlet to the dashboard.

#### To add the portlet to your home page: {#procedure_43160400684}

1.  In NetSuite, go to your home page.
    
2.  Click the **Personalize** link in the upper-right corner of your dashboard, or use the **Personalize Dashboard** link in the **Settings** portlet.
    
3.  On the **SuiteApps** tab, click the icon of the portlet you want to appear on your dashboard, or drag it onto the dashboard.
