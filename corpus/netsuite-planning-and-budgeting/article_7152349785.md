This section covers the Revenue and Gross Margin process flow and how to use the revenue and gross margin Forecast forms and Total Revenue dashboards.

The forms available in the Revenue and Gross Margin card let you enter, validate, and adjust Revenue and Gross Margin Forecasts. You can also use the Total Revenue dashboards to analyze the gross profit margin percentages of Item members.
