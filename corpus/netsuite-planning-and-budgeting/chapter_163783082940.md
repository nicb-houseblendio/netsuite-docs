The NSPB Sync SuiteApp enables you to extract accounting reports from NetSuite saved searches and use them in your Oracle Cloud Planning and Budgeting application (Planning and Budgeting, hereafter).

With this SuiteApp, you can define saved searches with the data that you need for planning and budgeting. Through the use of Data Exchange in your Planning and Budgeting, you can run saved searches and then download the results to use in your Planning and Budgeting.

To use and get support for NSPB Sync, you must license this product. To license the product, contact your NetSuite Account Manager.

NSPB Sync is a managed SuiteApp. When a new version is available, NetSuite updates your account automatically.

For information about how to install and configure NSPB Sync, and on the functionality of NSPB Sync, see the [NSPB Sync Setup Guide](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4882032869.html).

For information about the changes in each release, see [NSPB Sync Release Notes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1501884196.html).
