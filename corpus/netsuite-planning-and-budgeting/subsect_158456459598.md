The PBCS Integration role is included in the installed SuiteApp, and has all necessary permissions to get you started. If you need to create a custom role to better suit your needs, clone the PBCS Integration role and assign your specific permissions to it.

#### To clone and customize the PBCS Integration role:

1.  Log in to NetSuite, and go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_.
    
2.  In the list of roles, click **Customize** next to the PBCS Integration role.
    
3.  On the **Permissions** subtab, add the permissions you require for your custom role one by one, including any of the following:
    
    -   To have access to employee metadata, click the **Lists** subtab, and add the **Employee Record** permission.
        
    -   To have access to record types, click the **Custom Record** subtab, and add all the required record types.
        
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Tip:

The best practice is only to add the missing permissions and avoid removing any permissions.

Important:

With every new version of the NSPB Sync SuiteApp, new permissions may be added to the PBCS Integration role. After you update your installed SuiteApp, you should go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Show Role Differences_, and compare the permissions you have in your custom role with the permissions in the updated PBCS Integration role. To make sure the integration works properly for you, add the new permissions to your custom role.
