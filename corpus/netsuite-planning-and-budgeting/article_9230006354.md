The OpEx dashboards in the OpEx card give you a quick look of high-level operating expense trends. Finance executives look at operating expense trends and year-over-year (YoY) growth variances by Department and by Account. They analyze operating expenses as an amount, as a percentage of total expenses, or as a percentage of total revenue by Account and Department.

For more information about the OpEx dashboards in the OpEx card, see the following topics:

-   [Analyzing Trends in the OpEx by Dept Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5235611944.html)
