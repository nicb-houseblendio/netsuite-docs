From Data Integration, make sure the connection's token-based authentication credentials are correct.

#### To validate your source connection:

1.  On your Planning and Budgeting Home page, click the **Application** card, then click **Data Exchange**.
    
2.  On the Data Integration page, click the **Actions** menu, and select **Setup**, then **Applications**.
    
3.  In the Applications window, click the Configure Connections icon ![Configure Connections icon in Data Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/ConfigureConnectionsIcon.png).
    
4.  Under Connections, select **NetSuite\_ERP**, and click the Edit icon ![Edit Icon in Data Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/EditIconDI.png).
    
    The Update connection: NetSuite\_ERP window opens.
    
5.  Make sure you have the correct token-based credentials and that the account number matches your company's NetSuite Account ID.
    
    User credentials are set up during initial implementation.
    
    Tip:
    
    In NetSuite, find the NetSuite Account ID under Setup > Company > Company Information.
