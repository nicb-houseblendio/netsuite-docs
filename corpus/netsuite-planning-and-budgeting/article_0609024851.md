You can check which original artifacts (such as forms and menus) have been modified in your application.

#### To review the artifacts in your application: {#procedure_0602042248}

1.  From the Home page, click **Application** and then **Configure**.
    
2.  From the **Actions** menu, select **Review Modified Artifacts**.
    
3.  In the popup window, click the **Filter** link.
    
4.  In the Filter window, select an artifact type, and then click **Apply**.
    
    You'll see a list of the modified artifacts.
