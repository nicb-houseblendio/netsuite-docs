To enable the integration with your Planning and Budgeting, you need to set up an integration user with proper permissions in NetSuite. You can assign the **NSPB Integration** role to an existing or new user, and use that user for the integration. If you need to customize the NSPB Integration role to better suit your needs, you can assign your specific permissions to it.

For instructions, see the following help topics:

-   [Setting Up the Integration User Record for Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1027021506.html)
    
-   [Customizing the NSPB Integration Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1027021748.html)
    

Note:

The NSPB Integration role, or your custom role in NetSuite, gives sufficient permissions only to the integration user. Your Planning and Budgeting roles and permissions are managed in your Planning and Budgeting environment.
