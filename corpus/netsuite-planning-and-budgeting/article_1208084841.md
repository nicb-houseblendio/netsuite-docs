For the Account dimension, update the member formulas for all zero-level accounts for certain Cash Flow dimension members. Update the member formulas of these accounts by switching from the multi-currency version of the member formula to the single currency version. To do this, place the multi-currency expression in a comment to exclude it from the member formula. Then, take the single currency expression out of the comment so it's included in the member formula.

Switch all zero-level accounts to the single currency formula for the following Cash Flow dimension members:

-   NFS\_Operating Activities
    
-   NFS\_Investing Activities
    
-   NFS\_Financing Activities
    
-   NFS\_Cash at the Beginning of Period
    

For general information about defining member formulas, see [Working with Member Formulas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/planning-budgeting-cloud/pfusa/working_with_member_formulas.html) in the Oracle Help Center.

#### To update the member formulas to the single currency formula: {#procedure_1209124950}

1.  Click the Navigator icon ![Screenshot of the Navigator icon in NSPB](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/NSPBNavigatorIcon.png), and under Create and Manage, click **Dimensions**.
    
2.  On the **Dimensions** tab, in the **Dimension** list, select **Account**.
    
3.  Click the expand arrow ![Expand arrow icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/DimensionsExpandArrowIcon.png) next to **NFS\_Cash Flow Statement**. Continue to expand the Cash Flow dimension members until you reach the zero-level members.
    
4.  Select a zero-level dimension to update. Then, click **Actions** and select **Edit**.
    
    The Edit Member page opens.
    
5.  Click the **Member Formula** tab.
    
6.  In the text box, highlight the multi-currency member formula, and then click the Comment icon ![Comment icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/NSPBCommentIcon.png).
    
    Or, you can remove the comment characters (/\*) around the multi-currency member formula.
    
7.  Highlight the single currency member formula, and click the Uncomment icon ![Uncomment icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/NSPBUncommentIcon.png).
    
    Or, you can add the comment characters (/\*) to the start and end of the member formula.
    
    The following screenshot shows a sample NFS\_CF Accounts Receivable member formula. In the member formula, the multi-currency formula is excluded and the single currency formula is included:
    
    ![Sample single currency member formula for NFS\_CF Accounts Receiveable.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/NSPBCFSUpdateMemberFormulaExample.png)
8.  (Optional) To check the validity of the member formula, click **Validate**.
    
    If there are errors in the member formula, click **Show Details** to see a description of the problem.
    
9.  Click **Save** to save your changes and go back to the Dimensions page.
    
10.  Repeat steps 3 - 9 for each zero-level accounts for Cash Flow that needs an update.
