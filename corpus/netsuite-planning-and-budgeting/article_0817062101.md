If you added a new revenue account in NetSuite between updates, verify the account details. Then, run the **Pipeline\_MetaData\_Load** job to reload metadata into Planning and Budgeting Financials. This action ensures your revenue account is set up correctly and that the integration process is complete.

#### To verify account details and run the Pipeline\_MetaData\_Load job: {#procedure_0818044947}

1.  On your Planning and Budgeting Home page, click the **Application** card, then **Overview**.
    
2.  Click the **Dimensions** tab.
    
3.  Click **Account**.
    
4.  To find your revenue account, click the arrow next to **Account**, then **NFS\_Functional Rollups**, and then **NFS\_Total\_Revenue** to expand the rows.
    
5.  Make sure the **Default Data Storage** for the new revenue account is set to **Shared**.
    
6.  To close the window, click **Cancel**.
    
7.  To run the Pipeline\_Metadata\_Load job:
    
    1.  Go back to your Home page, click the **Application** card, then **Data Exchange**.
        
    2.  On the Data Integration page, in the search bar, enter **Pipeline\_Metadata\_Load**.
        
    3.  Click the Run icon ![Run icon in Data Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/RunIcon.png).
        
    4.  Click **Run**.
        
    5.  To see the status of the pipeline running, click the Actions icon, and select **Process Details**.
