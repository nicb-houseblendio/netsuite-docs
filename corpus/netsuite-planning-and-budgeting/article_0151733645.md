The Income Statement Dashboard shows key metrics and trends for Actual and Forecasted values of the income statement. You can view trends as an amount or percentage of total revenue.

You can use the Income Statement Dashboard to:

-   Analyze the Income Statement's key performance indicators (KPIs).
    
-   Evaluate revenue and expense trends by Department, Class, Subsidiary, or Currency dimension members.
    
-   Drill down into the Revenue Drill and Expense Drill form's trends at a detailed level.
    

This section gives general instructions for using these interactive subforms:

-   **KPIs** - Shows gross profit, earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA), earnings before interest and taxes, (EBIT) and net income trends for the current and next financial years.
    
-   **Revenue Drill** - Shows the gross profit trend as an amount or a percentage of total revenue. The subform lets you drill down into the gross profit trend and then further into the detailed income and costs of sales trends.
    
-   **Expense Drill** - Shows the expense trend as an amount, percentage of total expenses, or percentage of total revenue. The subform lets you drill down into the trend details by expense type and Account.
    

#### To analyze the Income Statement's key metrics and trends: {#procedure_0711035059}

Note:

Maximize or refresh each trend graph by clicking the Maximize icon ![Maximinze icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/UserGuide/NSPBMaximizeIcon.png) and Refresh icon in each subform ![Refresh icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/UserGuide/NSPBRefreshIcon.png).

1.  From the Home page, go to Financials > Income Statement.
    
2.  Click **Income Statement Dashboard** at the bottom of the page.
    
3.  In the Point of View (POV), choose a Version, Subsidiary, Currency, Department, and Class.
    
4.  To see the key metrics in the KPIs subform:
    
    1.  From the dropdown list in the KPIs subform, choose one of these options:
        
        -   To see the graph by value, select **Amount**.
            
        -   To see the graph by percentage of total revenue, select **% of Total Revenue**.
            
    2.  Hover over a series line in the graph to see the series name, period (group), and value.
        
5.  To see the gross profit trend in the Revenue Drill subform:
    
    1.  From the dropdown list in the Revenue Drill subform, choose one of these options:
        
        -   To see the graph by value, select **Amount**.
            
        -   To see the graph by percentage of the total revenue, select **% of Total Revenue**.
            
    2.  Hover over a series line in the graph to see the series name, period (group), and value.
        
    3.  (Optional) Click the line in the graph or **Gross Profit** in the legend to drill down into the trend. Drill further to see the income and cost of goods sold at the Account level.
        
6.  To see the Expense trends in the Expense Drill subform:
    
    1.  From the dropdown list in the Expense Drill subform, choose one of these options:
        
        -   To see the graph by value, select **Amount**.
            
        -   To see the graph by percentage of total expenses, select **% of Total Expenses**.
            
        -   To see the graph by percentage of the total revenue, select **% of Total Revenue**.
            
    2.  Hover over a series line in the graph to see the series name, period (group), and value.
        
    3.  (Optional) Click the line in the graph or **Expense** in the legend to drill down into the Expense trend. Drill further to see expense trends by type and at the Account level.
