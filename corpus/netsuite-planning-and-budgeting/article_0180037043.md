If you have the Administrator role, you can create new forms, reports, and dashboards to fit your business needs. Review the following procedures for the component you want to create:

-   Create new data forms to meet your needs. Data forms are grids for entering data.
    
    See [Creating Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/planning-budgeting-cloud/pfusa/creating_simple_forms.html) in the Oracle Help Center.
    
-   Create reports by using report components, report objects, and grid components.
    
    See [Designing with Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/enterprise-performance-management-common/dmepr/designing_reports_110x8ba01f55.html) in the Oracle Help Center.
    
-   Use forms and other artifacts to build dashboards that summarizes data. Dashboards let users chart, evaluate, highlight, comment on, and change key business data.
    
    See [Creating and Managing Dashboards](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/planning-budgeting-cloud/pfusa/creating_and_managing_dashboards_100x30f3f606.html) in the Oracle Help Center.
