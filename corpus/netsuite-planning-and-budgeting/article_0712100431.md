Use the Days Sales Outstanding form to forecast the closing Accounts Receivable balances for open Forecast periods. To calculate the projected closing Trade Receivables balances, enter the expected percentage of sales on credit for the open periods. Also, use the historical Actual Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) as a reference to enter the expected number of days for sales outstanding.

After you save, the form values update based on the following calculations:

-   Sales on Credit = Total Sales × % Sales on Credit
    
-   Credit Sales per Day = Sales on credit ÷ Days in Month
    
-   Trade Receivables Closing Balance = (Days Sales Outstanding) × (Credit Sales Per Day)
    
-   Trade Receivables Monthly Movements = Previous Period Closing Balance - (Days Sales Outstanding × Credit Sales Per Day)
    
    Or
    
    Trade Receivables Monthly Movements = Previous Period Closing Balance - Forecast Period Closing Balance
    

Important:

If you haven't already done so, make sure the Days in Month form is filled out before you start following the steps.

#### To calculate the expected Accounts Receivable closing balances for open periods: {#procedure_0712121243}

1.  From the Home page, go to Financials > Balance Sheet Trending.
    
2.  Click **Days Sales Outstanding**.
    
3.  In the Point of View, choose a Version, Subsidiary, and Currency.
    
4.  In the **% Sales on Credit** row, click a cell and enter the percentage of sales that you expect to be sold on credit. This value is based on historical Actuals and your company's billing practices. For example, enter 0.01 for a 1% increase or -0.01 for a 1% decrease.
    
5.  In the **Days Sales Outstanding** row, click a cell and enter the number of days that you expect sales to be outstanding. This value is based on your past collection history.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
7.  (Optional) To update the Balance Sheet with the adjusted data, right-click the data grid and select **Aggregate to Balance Sheet**. The form fills with data in the input currency.
    
8.  (Optional) To update the relevant financial statements in both input and reporting currencies:
    
    1.  Go to the Home page and click the **Rules** card.
        
    2.  Beside the ruleset **NFS\_All Accts Forecast Data Refresh**, click the Launch arrow.
