In Data Management, update the data load mappings for both the Account and Subsidiary dimensions in these transaction-related data load rules:

-   IncStmt\_Trans
    
-   IncStmt\_Trans\_RC
    
-   BalSht\_Trans
    
-   BalSht\_Trans\_SRC
    

For both dimensions, update the **Like** and **Explicit** mappings. For the Account dimension, update the mapping scripts.

#### To update the data load mappings of the Account and Subsidiary dimensions: {#procedure_0213125647}

1.  In Data Management, click the **Workflow** tab, and then under Data Load, click **Data Load Mapping**.
    
2.  In the POV bar, click the link next to **Location**.
    
    The Select Point of View window opens.
    
    1.  In the **Location** field, enter the data load rule name.
        
        For example, enter **IncStmt\_Trans**.
        
    2.  Click **OK**.
        
3.  From the **Dimensions** list, select **Account**.
    
4.  Click the **Like** tab.
    
5.  To edit the Account script, click the Edit icon under the **Script** column.
    
6.  In the Edit Script window, delete the `ENTITYx=ENTITY,` line, and then click **OK**.
    
7.  From the **Dimensions** list, switch to **Subsidiary**.
    
8.  Click the **Like** tab.
    
9.  Click **Add**.
    
10.  In the new row, enter an asterisk (**\***) in the **Source Value** field.
     
11.  Enter **Ignore** in the **Target Value** field.
     
12.  Click the **Explicit** tab.
     
13.  Click **Add**.
     
14.  In the new row, for the **Source Value** and **Target Value** fields, enter the subsidiary value that matches your base currency.
     
     For example, if your base currency is USD, you would enter **SUB\_2** for United States.
     
15.  Click **Save**.
     
16.  Repeat steps 2 - 15 for each required data load rules.
