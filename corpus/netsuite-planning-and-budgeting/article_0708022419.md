Use the Income Statement Adj (adjustments) form to make final adjustments to the income statement by Department, Class, and Account, as needed. You can also add commentary about any adjustments in the Commentary column.

Note:

The adjustments you make in the Income Statement Adj form don't automatically update to the related Revenue or OpEx forms.

#### To make final adjustments to the income statement: {#procedure_0708030431}

1.  From the Home page, go to Financials > Income Statement.
    
2.  Click **Income Statement Adj**.
    
3.  In the Point of View, choose a Version, Subsidiary, Currency, Department, and Class.
    
    Note:
    
    You enter adjustments at detail level, so you must select a Department and Class member.
    
4.  Find the Account you want to adjust and enter the adjustment value in the **Adj** row.
    
5.  (Optional) In the **Commentary** column, click a cell and enter the reason for the adjustment.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
7.  If you made adjustments to revenue accounts, then you need to manually update the cost of goods sold (COGS) accounts.
    
8.  To update the Income Statement with the adjusted data in input currency, right-click the data grid and select **Aggregate to Income Statement**.
    
9.  (Optional) To update the relevant financial statements in both input and reporting currencies:
    
    1.  Go to the Home page and click the **Rules** card.
        
    2.  Beside the ruleset **NFS\_All Accts Forecast Data Refresh**, click the Launch arrow.
