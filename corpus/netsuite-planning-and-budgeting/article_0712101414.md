Use the Days Payable Outstanding form to estimate the closing Accounts Payable balances for open periods. To calculate the projected closing Accounts Payable balances, enter the expected percentage of purchases on credit for open periods. Also, use the historical Days Payable Outstanding (DPO) as reference to enter the expected number of days for purchases outstanding.

The form values update based on the following calculations:

-   Purchase on Credit = Total Cost of Goods Sold × % Purchases on Credit.
    
-   Trade Creditor Purchases per Day = Purchases on Credit ÷ Days in Month.
    
-   Accounts Payable - Trade Closing Balance = Days Payable Outstanding × Trade Creditor Purchases per Day
    
-   Accounts Payable - Trade Monthly Movements = Previous Period Closing Balance - (Days Payable Outstanding × Trade Creditor Purchases Per Day)
    
    Or
    
    Accounts Payable - Trade Monthly Movements = Previous Period Closing Balance - Forecast Period Closing Balance.
    

Important:

If you haven't already done so, make sure the Days in Month form is filled before you start following the steps.

#### To calculate the expected Accounts Payable closing balances for open periods: {#procedure_0712010151}

1.  From the Home page, go to Financials > Balance Sheet Trending.
    
2.  Click **Days Payable Outstanding**.
    
3.  In the Point of View, choose a Version, Subsidiary, and Currency.
    
4.  Review the **Total Cost of Goods Sold** projection for the Forecast periods.
    
    Note:
    
    The Total Cost of Goods Sold is derived from the Income Statement.
    
5.  In the **% of Purchase on Credit** row, click a cell and enter the percentage of purchases that you expect to be on credit. This value is based on historical Actuals and your company's purchasing practices. For example, enter 0.01 for a 1% increase or -0.01 for a 1% decrease.
    
6.  In the **Days Payable Outstanding** row, click a cell and enter the number of days that you expect payables to be outstanding. This value is based on past payment history.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    
8.  (Optional) To update the Balance Sheet with the adjusted data, right-click the data grid and select **Aggregate to Balance Sheet**. The form fills with data in the input currency.
    
9.  (Optional) To update the relevant financial statements in both input and reporting currencies:
    
    1.  Go to the Home page and click the **Rules** card.
        
    2.  Beside the ruleset **NFS\_All Accts Forecast Data Refresh**, click the Launch arrow.
