Important:

You shouldn't use the NSPB SuitePeople Integration role for integration with your Planning and Budgeting. To integrate with Planning and Budgeting, set up the integration user record as described in the help topic [Setting Up the Integration User for Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7141303101.html).

If you have the Administrator role, you can assign the NSPB SuitePeople Integration role to a new or existing user in NetSuite. This role lets users run NetSuite SuitePeople saved searches to load data into Planning and Budgeting. If the role doesn't provide sufficient permissions for your customization, you can clone this role and assign your specific permissions to it.

For instructions, see the following help topics:

-   For information about the NetSuite SuitePeople saved searches, see [NSPB SuitePeople - Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202102105.html).
    
-   To assign the NSPB SuitePeople Integration role to a Planning and Budgeting Sync user, see [Assigning the NSPB SuitePeople Integration Role to a Planning and Budgeting Sync User](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1203115346.html).
    
-   To customize the NSPB SuitePeople Integration role to better suit your needs, see [Customizing the NSPB SuitePeople Integration Role for Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1203115448.html).
