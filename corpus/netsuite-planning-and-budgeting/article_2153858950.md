The NetSuite Planning and Budgeting Financials User Guide covers tasks that are common to most Planning and Budgeting Financials users. When you log in to Planning and Budgeting, you see clusters (menus) and cards (sub-menus) that determine what you can do in the application. Your assigned role determines which cards and clusters you see.
