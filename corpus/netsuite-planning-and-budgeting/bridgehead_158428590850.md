NSPB Sync doesn't include a default saved search for custom segments. To push the hierarchy of your custom segment to Data Exchange, you need to set up the custom segment and create the saved search properly in NetSuite.

#### To set up your custom segment and create a saved search:

1.  Go to _Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types_.
    
2.  Click the custom segment of your choice.
    
3.  Check the **Include in Search Menu** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    
5.  Go to _Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Custom Segments_.
    
6.  Click **Edit** for the updated custom segment.
    
7.  Click the **Permissions** subtab.
    
8.  In the **Role** column, select the **PBCS Integration** role.
    
9.  Select **View** as the access level for this role.
    
10.  Click **Add** to assign this role to the custom segment.
     
11.  Click **Save**.
     
12.  Go back to _Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types_.
     
13.  Click **Search** for the updated custom segment.
     
14.  Click the **Create Saved Search** button.
     
15.  Enter a title for the search.
     
16.  In the **ID** field, make sure you prefix the saved search ID with **customsearch\_nspbcs\_sync\_cseg**.
     
17.  Check the **Public** box.
     
18.  On the **Criteria** subtab, add filters.
     
19.  On the **Results** subtab, in the **Columns** sublist, add the first five columns exactly as illustrated below:
     
     ![Columns subtab showing five columns to be added](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/NSPBSync/NSPBCSCustomSegSetting.png)
     
     You can substitute Prefix\_ in the formula for the Child column with any prefix to help you identify the saved search. For example, formula 'Seg\_' ||{internalId} will list all the custom segment records under the top-level parent named Seg.
     
20.  Click **Save**.
     

For information about custom segments in NetSuite, refer to [Custom Segment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1552570607.html) and [Custom Segments in Record Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4317008667.html) in the NetSuite Help Center. If you need to create a custom segment, refer to [Creating a Custom Segment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4313465979.html) in the NetSuite Help Center.
