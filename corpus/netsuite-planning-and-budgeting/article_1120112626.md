To make sure the DIO, DSO, and DPO forms work with new custom dimensions, add them to the **NFS\_BS Trending Days in Month** member formula.

#### To add custom dimensions to the NFS\_BS Trending Days in Month member formula: {#procedure_1120113622}

1.  Click the Navigator icon ![The Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/IntegrationGuide/NSPB-SP_MenuIcon.png), and then under Create and Manage, select **Dimensions**.
    
2.  On the **Dimensions** tab, in the **Dimension** field, select **Account**.
    
3.  Click the expand arrow ![Expand arrow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/IntegrationGuide/DimensionsExpandArrowIcon.png) next to **NFS\_Balance Sheet Trending**, and then again for **NFS\_DSO Model**.
    
4.  Select the **NFS\_BS Trending Days in Month** member. Click **Actions**, then and select **Edit**.
    
5.  On the **Member Properties** tab, set the **Account Type** to **Revenue**.
    
6.  Click the **Member Formula** tab.
    
7.  To add your custom dimensions to the member formula, on the first line, enter `AND @ISMBR("<``_CustomDimension_``>")`, where <`_CustomDimension_`\> is a variable for the custom dimension. Repeat this step for each custom dimension you enabled.
    
    For example, if the new custom dimension is **TaskType**, enter `AND @ISMBR("TaskType")`.
    
    The following screenshot shows `AND @ISMBR("TaskType")` added to the **NFS\_BS Trending Days in Month** member formula:
    
    ![Sample member formula for NFS\_BS Trending Days in Month that includes a new custom dimension.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/IntegrationGuide/CustomDimensionAddedToNFS_BSTrendingDiMMemberFormula.png)
8.  Click **Save** to save the member formula and go back to the **Dimensions** tab.
    
9.  Click **Actions**, and then select **Refresh Database**.
    
    1.  If you see a warning message, click **Refresh**.
        
    2.  Wait for the refresh to finish, and then click **Finish**. Or, you can click **Run in Background**.
