The CFO dashboards gives you a quick look at high-level trends and totals. Finance executives and other executives use the CFO dashboards to analyze key performance indicators, year-over-year variances, and trends. They also drill down into detailed levels to investigate data.

To go to the CFO dashboards, from your Planning and Budgeting Home page, click the **Reporting and Analytics** card. Then, click the **4** tab on the left.

For more information about the CFO dashboards available in the Reporting and Analytics card, see the following topics:

-   [Analyzing Trends in the CFO KPI Income Statement Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_8231848495.html)
