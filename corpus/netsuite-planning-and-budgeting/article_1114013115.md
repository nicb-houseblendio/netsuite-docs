If you want to use NetSuite to schedule and run a business rule configured in your Planning and Budgeting, you need to add the business rule in NetSuite. Then, you can set up the business rule as needed.

#### To add and set up a business rule: {#procedure_11140039652}

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Planning and Budgeting > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  On the **Manage Jobs** page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to a business rule of your choice.
        
    -   If you don't see the business rule in the list, click the **Add Job** button, and follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114010611.html).
        
3.  Click the **Job Parameters** subtab.
    
    Enter the **Key** and **Value** of a business rule defined in your Planning and Budgeting. The field values must match exactly, including upper and lower case. Repeat if needed to create a list of parameters.
    
4.  If you want to schedule the business rule, click the **Schedule** subtab, and follow step 3 in [Scheduling a Job in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114011407.html).
    
5.  For optional setup of the business rule, click the **Options** subtab.
    
    From the **Post-Processing Plug-ins** list, select a plug-in of your choice. The field is set to **None** by default.
    
    For information about how you can use post-processing plug-ins for the integration purposes, see [Post-Processing Plug-ins in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5132559979.html).
    
6.  Click **Save**.
