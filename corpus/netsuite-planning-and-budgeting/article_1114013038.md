If you want to use NetSuite to schedule and run a batch job configured in Data Exchange or your Planning and Budgeting, you need to add the batch job in NetSuite. Then, you can set up the batch job as needed.

Note:

Planning and Budgeting Sync only supports the Serial batch execution mode. See [Using Open Batch Job Types](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/enterprise-performance-management-common/diepm/integrations_pipeline_openbatches.html).

#### To add and set up a batch job: {#procedure_85140032158}

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Planning and Budgeting > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  On the **Manage Jobs** page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to a batch job of your choice.
        
    -   If you don't see the batch job in the list, click the **Add Job** button, and follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114010611.html).
        
3.  If you want to schedule the batch job, click the **Schedule** subtab, and follow step 3 in [Scheduling a Job in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114011407.html).
    
4.  For optional setup of the batch job, click the **Options** subtab.
    
    From the **Post-Processing Plug-ins** list, select a plug-in of your choice. The field is set to **None** by default.
    
    For information about how you can use post-processing plug-ins for the integration purposes, see [Post-Processing Plug-ins in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5132559979.html).
    
5.  Click **Save**.
