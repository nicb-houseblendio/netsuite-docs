Use the Days Inventory Outstanding form to forecast the closing Inventory balances at the General Ledger level. To calculate the expected closing Inventory balances, enter the expected number of days for inventory outstanding for open periods. Use the historical Actual Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO) as reference.

The form values update based on the following calculations:

-   COGS Per Day = Total COGS ÷ Trending Days in Month.
    
    The Total Cost of Goods Sold is derived from the Income Statement.
    
-   Inventory in Stock Closing Balance = Days Inventory Outstanding × COGS Per Day.
    
-   Inventory in Stock Monthly Movements = Previous Period Closing Balance - (Days Inventory Outstanding × COGS Per Day)
    
    Or
    
    Inventory in Stock Monthly Movements = Previous Period Closing Balance - Forecast Period Closing Balance.
    

Important:

If you haven't already done so, make sure the Days in Month form is filled before you start following the steps.

#### To calculate the closing balances of Inventory in-stock for open periods: {#procedure_0712125324}

1.  From the Home page, go to Financials > Balance Sheet Trending.
    
2.  Click **Days Inventory Outstanding**.
    
3.  In the Point of View, choose a Version, Subsidiary, and Currency.
    
4.  Review the **Total Cost of Goods Sold** projection for the Forecast periods.
    
5.  In the **Days Inventory Outstanding** row, click a cell and enter the number of days that you expect inventory to be outstanding. This value is based on past historical Actuals.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
7.  (Optional) To update the Balance Sheet with the adjusted data, right-click the data grid and select **Aggregate to Balance Sheet**. The form fills with data in the input currency.
    
8.  (Optional) To update the relevant financial statements in both input and reporting currencies:
    
    1.  Go to the Home page and click the **Rules** card.
        
    2.  Beside the ruleset **NFS\_All Accts Forecast Data Refresh**, click the Launch arrow.
