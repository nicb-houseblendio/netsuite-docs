NSPB Sync lets you use NetSuite to access the forms and dashboards that are stored in your Planning and Budgeting. You first need to record the forms and dashboards in NetSuite.

#### To create a record of a form or dashboard in NetSuite:

1.  Go to Planning & Budgeting > Tools > Forms/Dashboards.
    
2.  If you have the Administrator role, you can click the **Add Form/Dashboard** button.
    
3.  In the **Name** field, enter the name of a form or dashboard that is configured in your Planning and Budgeting. The value must match exactly, including upper and lower case.
    
4.  In the **Description** field, enter a description to help you identify this form or dashboard in NetSuite.
    
5.  From the **Type** dropdown list, specify what you're adding to NetSuite, whether **Form** or **Dashboard**.
    
6.  In the **POV (Point of View)** text box, specify a list of POVs that can be applied to this form or dashboard. Each combination of POVs should be a separate line. For example: SUB\_1, USD.
    
7.  If you want the form or dashboard to be displayed in the Planning & Budgeting Form/Dashboard portlet in NetSuite, check the **Show First POV in Planning & Budgeting Form/Dashboard Portlet** box. The portlet shows only the first line of POVs. For information about the portlet, see [Planning & Budgeting Form/Dashboard Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160632428995.html).
    
8.  Click **Save**.
    

If you've already created a list of forms and dashboards at Planning & Budgeting > Tools > Forms/Dashboards, you can click **View** next to the form or dashboard you want to access. If you're logged in to your Planning and Budgeting account, the form or dashboard opens directly in a new tab.

To update an existing record, click **Edit** next to the record you want to update. Make any necessary changes, and click **Save**.
