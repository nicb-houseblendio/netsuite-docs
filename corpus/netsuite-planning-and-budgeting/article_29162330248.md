The Planning and Budgeting Sync SuiteApp includes the following predefined metadata datasets:

-   NSPB Sync (Metadata) Balance Sheet Accounts
    
-   NSPB Sync (Metadata) Class
    
-   NSPB Sync (Metadata) Customer
    
-   NSPB Sync (Metadata) Customer Project
    
-   NSPB Sync (Metadata) Department
    
-   NSPB Sync (Metadata) Employee
    
-   NSPB Sync (Metadata) Income Statement Accounts
    
-   NSPB Sync (Metadata) Item
    
-   NSPB Sync (Metadata) Location
    
-   NSPB Sync (Metadata) Project
    
-   NSPB Sync (Metadata) Subsidiary
    
-   NSPB Sync (Metadata) Vendor
    

You can create, review, or customize the datasets as described in help topics [Managing the Planning and Budgeting Sync Datasets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_28162703900.html) and [Dataset Settings in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_94163304747.html).
