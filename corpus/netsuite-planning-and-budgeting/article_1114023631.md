If you go to NetSuite EPM > NetSuite EPM Overview, you can access the following portlets:

-   [Planning and Budgeting Substitution Variables Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024110.html)
    
-   [Job Execution Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024144.html)
    
-   [Execution Logs Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024214.html)
    
-   [Planning and Budgeting Account Balance Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024243.html)
    
-   [Planning and Budgeting Sync Checklist Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024313.html)
    

You need to add these portlets manually to the NetSuite EPM page. You can then customize the setup and layout of the portlets on the page.

#### To access the NetSuite EPM Overview portlets: {#procedure_1160408805}

1.  Go to NetSuite EPM > NetSuite EPM Overview.
    
2.  Click the **Personalize** link in the upper-right corner of the page.
    
3.  On the **Standard Content** tab, click **Custom Portlet**.
    
4.  In the Custom Content portlet's menu, click **Set Up**.
    
5.  From the **Source** list, select the name of the portlet you want to appear on the page.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
