You can access several Planning and Budgeting Sync portlets in NetSuite:

-   To check the current status of your Planning and Budgeting Sync configuration, or to set it up quickly, you can use the [Planning and Budgeting Sync Checklist Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024313.html).
    
-   For monthly maintenance of your NetSuite account, you can use the following Planning and Budgeting Sync portlets:
    
    -   [Job Execution Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024144.html)
        
    -   [Execution Logs Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024214.html)
        
    -   [Planning and Budgeting Substitution Variables Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024110.html)
        
    -   [Planning and Budgeting Account Balance Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024243.html)
        
    
    Tip:
    
    Best practices for doing the monthly maintenance on your NetSuite account:
    
    1.  Check the [Planning and Budgeting Substitution Variables Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024110.html) to make sure that all the substitution variables in your Planning and Budgeting are up to date.
        
    2.  Run a job from the [Job Execution Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024144.html).
        
    3.  Watch the progress of the job execution in the corresponding Execution Logs portlet. See [Execution Logs Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024214.html).
        
    4.  Click the Refresh icon on the [Planning and Budgeting Account Balance Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024243.html) to upload the account balance from your Planning and Budgeting.
        
    
-   On your home page, you can access the [Planning and Budgeting Form/Dashboard Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114024026.html) portlet.
    

For information about how you can add or access the portlets, see the following help topics:

-   [Adding the Planning and Budgeting Sync Portlet to Your Home Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114023235.html)
    
-   [Accessing NetSuite EPM Overview Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114023631.html)
