Use the revenue adjustment forms in the Budget card to make adjustments to your sales Budget. To access these forms, from the Budget card, click the **2** tab on the left.

The Budget process has these revenue adjustment forms:

-   Revenue - New Customer by Account Budget
    
    For more information, see [Adding New Customers to Revenue Budget](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206024659.html).
    
-   Revenue by Customer Budget
    
    For more information, see [Entering or Generating Sales Budget for Revenue by Customer](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206024930.html).
    
-   Revenue by Item Budget
    
    For more information, see [Entering or Generating Sales Budget for Revenue by Item](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206025025.html).
    
-   Revenue Top Level Adjustment Budget
    
    For more information, see [Making Top-Level Adjustments to the Revenue Budget](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206025134.html).
    
-   Gross Margin Percentage Budget
    
    For more information, see [Reviewing and Adjusting Gross Margin Budget Percentages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206025205.html).
