In Planning and Budgeting, you can change an account's alias name to better describe the account member. Aliases are alternative names for database member names.

#### To change an account's alias name: {#procedure_0907064244}

1.  Click the Navigator icon ![Screenshot of the Navigator icon in NSPB](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/NSPBNavigatorIcon.png), and under Application, select **Overview**.
    
2.  Click the **Dimensions** tab.
    
3.  Click the **Account** link.
    
    The Edit Member Properties window opens.
    
4.  To drill down to the numbered account level, click the arrow icon to expand the rows.
    
    The following screenshot shows a sample Edit Member Properties window, drilled down to the 1011 account row:
    
    ![A portion of the Edit Member Properties window for Accounts, with rows expanded to drill down to the 1011 cash account.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/ChangeFinancialAccountNames.png)
5.  Select the account you want to update to highlight its row.
    
6.  In the data cell where the account's row and **Default Alias Table** column intersect, update the account name.
    
7.  Click **Save** to go back to the Application page.
    
8.  Refresh the database to make sure your changes are applied:
    
    1.  Click **Actions**, and then select **Refresh Database**.
        
    2.  Click **RefreshPlanCube**.
        
        The Refresh Database window opens.
        
    3.  Click **Refresh Database**.
        
    4.  A warning message appears. Click **Refresh**.
        
    5.  Wait for the refresh to finish, and then click **Finish** or **Run in Background**.
