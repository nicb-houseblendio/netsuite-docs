You can map any account type from your NetSuite account to any account type in your Planning and Budgeting.

#### To create the mapping: {#procedure_0721080647}

1.  Go to _Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types_.
    
2.  In the list of record types, locate the **NSPB | Account Type Mapping** record type.
    
3.  Click **New Record** for the record type.
    
4.  From the **Source Account Type** dropdown list, select the account type you want to map from NetSuite to your Planning and Budgeting.
    
5.  In the **Target Account Type** field, enter the name of the account type in your Planning and Budgeting you want to map to.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
    Note:
    
    If you create a duplicate mapping record, the latter record is used in the generated saved search result.
    
7.  Click **List** in the upper-right corner of the page to view the mapping you've created.
