Use the OpEx Trend Dashboard to analyze operating expense trends. The OpEx Trend Dashboard shows OpEx trends by Department for the selected Account and by Account for the selected Department. The subforms also lets you drill down into specific Departments for the selected Account or into specific Accounts.

This section gives general instructions for using the following interactive subforms in the OpEx Trend Dashboard:

-   **OpEx by Department Trend** - Uses Actual and Forecast values to show the trend from historical to projected operating expense by Department for the selected Account. You can see values as an amount or percentage of total expenses. The subform lets you drill down into specific Departments for the selected Account.
    
-   **OpEx by Account Trend** - Uses Actual and Forecast values to show the trend by Account for the selected Department. You can see values as an amount or percentage of total expenses. The subform lets you drill down into specific Accounts.
    

#### To analyze the operating expense trends: {#procedure_0725111606}

Note:

Maximize or refresh each trend graph by clicking the Maximize icon ![Screenshot of the Maximinze icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/UserGuide/NSPBMaximizeIcon.png) and Refresh icon in each subform ![Screenshot of the Refresh icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/UserGuide/NSPBRefreshIcon.png).

1.  From the Home page, click the **OpEx** card.
    
2.  To open OpEx Planning forms, click the **1** tab on the left.
    
3.  Click **OpEx Trend Dashboard**.
    
4.  In the Point of View (POV), choose a Version, Subsidiary, Currency, and Tracker member. For Tracker, choose one of the following options:
    
    -   To see the graph by value, select **Amount**.
        
    -   To see the graph by percentage of the total expenses, select **% of Total Expenses**.
        
5.  To review the operating expense trend by Department for an Account:
    
    1.  In the OpEx by Department Trend subform, select an Account in the **Account** list.
        
    2.  (Optional) Click a line in the graph or a Department name in the legend to drill down into that department's operating expense. Drilling shows the operating expenses at the individual Account level.
        
6.  To review the operating expense trend by Account for a Department:
    
    1.  In the OpEx by Account Trend subform, select a Department in the **Department** list.
        
    2.  (Optional) Click a line in the graph or click the Account name in the legend to drill into the Account's operating expense.
