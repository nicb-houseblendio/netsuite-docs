The Planning and Budgeting Financials Administrator Guide covers tasks that are common to users with the Administrator role.

When you log in to NetSuite Planning and Budgeting, you see clusters (menus) and cards (sub-menus) that show what you can do within the application. Your assigned role determines which cards and clusters you see. If you have the Administrator role, you'll have access to more cards and clusters so you can do tasks unique to that role
