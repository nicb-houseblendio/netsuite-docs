Update the **NSP\_SYS\_NoIntersection** substitution variable to work with the custom dimension members you want to use.

#### To update the NSP\_SYS\_NoIntersection substitution variable: {#procedure_1120074708}

1.  Click the Navigator icon ![The Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/IntegrationGuide/NSPB-SP_MenuIcon.png), and then under Tools, select **Variables**.
    
2.  Click the **Substitution Variables** tab.
    
3.  Find the **NSP\_SYS\_NoIntersection** variable.
    
4.  In the **Value** column, add the custom dimension member to the end of the string. Enter **\->"<CustomDimensionMember>"**, where **<CustomDimensionMember>** a variable for the custom dimension member you want to use.
    
    For example, if your custom dimension is **TaskType** and the dimension member you want is **No TaskType**, enter **\->"No TaskType"**.
    
    The following screenshot shows **"No TaskType"** added to the **NSP\_SYS\_NoIntersection** substitution variable:
    
    ![A portion of NSP\_SYS\_NoIntersection substitution variable string with a new custom dimension added to the end of the string.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/IntegrationGuide/NSPBNSP_SYS_NoIntersection.png)
5.  Repeat step 4 for each custom dimension you enabled by adding each dimension member to the end of the string.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
