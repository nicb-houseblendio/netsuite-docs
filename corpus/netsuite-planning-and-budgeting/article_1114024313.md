This portlet gives you an overview of the current status of your Planning and Budgeting Sync configuration. You can use the portlet to quickly set up any missing features.

For details, see [Planning and Budgeting Sync Configuration Checklist](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6124225652.html).

For instructions on adding the portlet to the NetSuite EPM page, see [Accessing NetSuite EPM Overview Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114023631.html).
