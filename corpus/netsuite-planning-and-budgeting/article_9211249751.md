NetSuite Planning and Budgeting is an Enterprise Performance Management (EPM) tool built on the Oracle Planning and Budgeting Cloud Services Platform. It shares many of the Oracle Cloud EPM infrastructure, components, and tasks. Therefore, documentation for Planning and Planning Modules in the Planning and Budgeting Cloud library and Cloud EPM Common library applies to NetSuite Planning and Budgeting.

Note:

Some functions and steps in the Oracle Help Center's guides might be different from what you should do in NetSuite Planning and Budgeting. However, the concepts should still apply to your environment.
