Note:

You can't schedule data load rules or batch jobs in Data Management anymore.

You need to use the EPM Platform's Job Scheduler page to run all scheduled rules and jobs. You can migrate existing batches from Data Management to Job Scheduler with the **Migrate Schedules to Platform Jobs Scheduler** utility.

#### To migrate batches to Job Scheduler:

1.  Click the Navigator icon ![Navigator Icon in NSPB](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/NSPBNavigatorIcon.png).
    
2.  Under Integration, click **Data Management**.
    
3.  From the **Workflow** tab, click **System Maintenance Tasks**.
    
4.  Select **Migrate Schedules to Platform Jobs Scheduler** from the list, then click **Execute**.
    
5.  In the Execute Script window, do the following:
    
    1.  For **Execution Mode**, enter **Migrate**.
        
    2.  For **Migrate Batch Schedules**, enter **Yes**.
        
6.  Click **OK**.
