To create custom plug-ins to use with Planning and Budgeting Sync, you can do the following:

-   Customize the Planning and Budgeting Sync post-processing plug-ins. See [Using Planning and Budgeting Sync Post-Processing Plug-ins](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1201045944.html).
    
-   Use plug-ins implemented in NetSuite to create and format your custom plug-ins.
    
-   Create additional plug-ins to process files generated in your Planning and Budgeting.
    

You can then use the plug-ins to process any data generated by a job and downloaded from Inbox/Outbox Explorer or File Browser's inbox to NetSuite. For information, see [Using Custom Plug-ins with Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1201050504.html).

Note:

You can use post-processing plug-ins with the following job types:

-   Batch jobs
    
-   Business rules
    
-   Data integration jobs
