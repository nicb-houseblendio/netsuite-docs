Update the **NFS\_Form\_DiM** business rule to use the new custom dimensions.

#### To add custom dimensions to the NFS\_Form\_DiM business rule: {#procedure_1120094931}

1.  Click the Navigator icon ![The Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/IntegrationGuide/NSPB-SP_MenuIcon.png), and then under Create and Manage, select **Rules**.
    
    The Calculation Manager window opens.
    
2.  Click the expand arrow ![Expand arrow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/IntegrationGuide/DimensionsExpandArrowIcon.png) next to **Planning**, **NetSuite**, **NSP\_NFS**, and **Rules**.
    
3.  Double-click the **NFS\_Form\_DiM** business rule.
    
4.  The business rule opens in Rule Designer. If you see a message, click **OK**.
    
5.  To add your custom dimensions, enter the dimension members you want to the fifth line of the business rule. Repeat this step for each custom dimension you enabled.
    
    For example, if your custom dimension is **TaskType** and the dimension member you want is **No TaskType**, enter `"No TaskType"`.
    
    The following screenshot shows `"No TaskType"` added to the **NFS\_Form\_DiM** business rule:
    
    ![An example of the NFS\_Form\_DiM business rule which includes a new custom dimension.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/IntegrationGuide/CustomDimensionMemberAddedToNFS_Form_DiM.png)
6.  To save your changes, click the Save icon ![Save icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/IntegrationGuide/CalculationManagerSaveIcon.png).
    
7.  Click the Validate and Deploy icon ![Validate and Deploy icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/IntegrationGuide/CalculationManagerValidateAndDeployIcon.png).
    
    If deployment works, you'll see a confirmation message. Click **OK**.
