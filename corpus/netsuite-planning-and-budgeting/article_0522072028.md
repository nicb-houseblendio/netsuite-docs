The FY Act vs Fcst Var and Comment form shows net income variances between the full-year Actual and full-year Forecast by Subsidiary and Department. You can use this form to review and compare the full-year Actuals against an active or archived Forecast. Each variance shows as a value and a percentage. The form also lets you add commentary at the Department level.

Note:

Substitution variables set up Years, Current and Previous Periods, and Version members.

The form fills in values based on these calculations:

-   The **Act vs Fcst $** column shows the variance between the full-year Actual and Forecast values.
    
    Act vs Fcst $ = Full-Year Actual - Full-Year Forecast
    
-   The **Act vs Fcst %** column shows the variance between the full-year Actual and Forecast values, expressed as a percentage.
    
    Act vs Fcst % = ((Full-Year Actual - Full-Year Forecast) ÷ Full-Year Forecast) × 100
    

#### To review full-year Actual and Forecast variances: {#procedure_0522072343}

1.  From the Home page, click the **Reporting and Analytics** card.
    
2.  To open Forecast Variance Reports, click the **1** tab on the left.
    
3.  Click **FY Act vs Fcst Var and Comment**.
    
4.  In the Point of View, choose a Version, Subsidiary, Currency, and Department.
    
5.  The full year Actuals show in the **Actual** column, and the full year Forecast values show in the **Forecast** column for comparison.
    
6.  If needed, enter any comments in the open (white) cells in the last column.
    
    Note:
    
    You can't enter commentary at Total Department level.
    
    Tip:
    
    To see all the text on a row, click the cell to open the text box, then drag the box's handle to resize it.
    
7.  (Optional) To see a detailed analysis of a specific Account member, right-click the Account and select **Drill to Income Statement Details**.
    
    The Income Statement - Account Details form opens in a new tab. The form shows Actual and Forecast data at a detailed level by Relationship, Department, Class, Item, and Location.
    
8.  To go back to the initial form, click **FY Act vs Fcst Var and Comment** at the bottom of the page.
    
    Click **Save**.
    
9.  To update the data area with the latest updates, click **Refresh**.
