The Cash Flow card has the following forms:

-   Cash Flow Report
    
    For more information, see [Reviewing the Cash Flow Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0719025525.html).
    
-   Cash Flow Report - Group Consolidated
    
    For more information, see [Reviewing the Consolidated Cash Flow Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0719043441.html).
    

The Cash Flow card also includes a Cash Flow Dashboard. The Cash Flow Dashboard shows cash flow trends, ratios, and activities. For more information, see [Analyzing Trends in the Cash Flow Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9100245116.html).

Finance executives look at the Actual and Forecasted cash flow activities by Subsidiary and analyze key financial metrics. Finance executives and other executives review the Cash Flow Report - Group Consolidated form. They also review Actual and Forecasted Cash Flow activities for all Subsidiaries side-by-side. Finally, finance executives review the Cash Flow Dashboard to analyze the Cash Flow trends, ratios, and activities.
