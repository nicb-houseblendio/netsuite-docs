If you want to use NetSuite to schedule and run a data map job configured in your Planning and Budgeting, you need to add the job in NetSuite. Then, you can set up the job as needed.

#### To add and set up a data map job: {#procedure_0622023844}

1.  Log in to NetSuite, and go to Planning & Budgeting > Jobs > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  On the **Planning & Budgeting Jobs** page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to a data map job of your choice.
        
    -   If you don't see the job in the list, and you have the Administrator role, click the **Add Job** button, and follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_160527233569.html).
        
3.  Click the **Job Parameters** subtab.
    
4.  If you want to clear the target database before copying data, check the **Clear Data** box.
    
5.  If you want to schedule the job, click the **Schedule** subtab, and follow step 3 in [Scheduling a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_160527228086.html).
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

For information about using data maps in your Planning and Budgeting, see [Pushing Data Using Data Maps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/planning-budgeting-cloud/epbca/general_pushing_data_with_data_maps_100x142f17b1.html) in the Oracle Help Center.
