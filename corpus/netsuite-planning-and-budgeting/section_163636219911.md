To check the integration status, you can create saved searches from the available NSPB Sync record types. For example, you can create a saved search that daily sends you an email with a list of jobs that failed during a specified time period. You can also create a saved search that collects execution logs for you.

The NSPB Sync record types include the following:

-   NSPB | Activity Logs
    
-   NSPB | Event Logs
    
-   NSPB | Job
    
-   NSPB | Job Execution Logs
    

#### To create a saved search from a record type:

1.  Go to _Lists > Search > Saved Searches > New_.
    
2.  Click the record type of your choice.
    
    Note:
    
    In NetSuite, the terms 'jobs' and 'projects' are used interchangeably. Therefore, NetSuite displays NSPB | Project and NSPB | Project Execution Logs for the NSPB | Job and NSPB | Job Execution Logs record types.
    
3.  Enter a title for the search.
    
4.  Make any other necessary changes, and click **Save**.
