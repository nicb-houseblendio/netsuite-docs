This portlet shows the status of saved searches executed in your account in the last 30 days.

Note:

The list of displayed saved search executions is limited to the 10 latest.

In the portlet, you can sort each column in the displayed saved searches list by the following characteristics:

-   The saved search name.
    
-   The saved search record type, for example, from Account to Vendor.
    
-   The total number of records each saved search contains. The count of records includes the header row.
    
-   The latest execution parameters, as defined for each saved search in Data Exchange.
    
-   The last date and time of each saved search execution.
    
-   The status of each saved search execution, which is either Success or Error.
    

To ensure the portlet shows the latest data, you can manually refresh it at any time by clicking the portlet's Refresh icon on the right of the title bar.

For instructions on adding the portlet to your dashboard, see [Adding NSPB Sync Portlets to Your Home Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1551958192.html#subsect_162704383176).
