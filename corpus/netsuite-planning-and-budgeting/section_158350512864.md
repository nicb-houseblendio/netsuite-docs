The integration acknowledges saved search IDs the prefixes of which begin with **customsearch\_nspbcs\_**. If you have the Administrator role and want to use other saved searches in your Planning and Budgeting, you need to place prefixes of the search IDs on an allowlist.

#### To place a saved search on an allowlist:

1.  In NetSuite, go to Planning & Budgeting > Setup > Saved Search Allowlist.
    
2.  Enter the prefixes of saved search IDs, separated with commas. The prefixes must begin with **customsearch\_nspbcs\_**.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

All saved searches the IDs of which begin with these prefixes become available in Data Exchange. If you change the list of these prefixes in NetSuite after executing the saved searches from Data Exchange, your changes have no effect on data synchronization. To reflect the changes in Data Exchange, you have to make corresponding updates in Data Exchange.
