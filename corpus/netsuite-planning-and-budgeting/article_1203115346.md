Using the Administrator role, you can assign the NSPB SuitePeople Integration role to users who should load NetSuite SuitePeople data into your Planning and Budgeting. You also need to set up the user properly in NetSuite.

#### To assign the NSPB SuitePeople Integration role to a user:

1.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Employees_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the employee to whom you want to assign the NSPB SuitePeople Integration role.
    
3.  In the **Subsidiary** field, select the top-level parent subsidiary.
    
    Note:
    
    The top-level parent subsidiary selection ensures that searches return all data. If you can't select the top-level parent subsidiary, go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_ to customize the **NSPB SuitePeople Integration** role or your custom role, and select **All** in the Subsidiary Restrictions section.
    
4.  Click the **Access** subtab.
    
5.  In the **Role** column, select **NSPB SuitePeople Integration**, and then click **Add**.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
