Before you export the Accounts metadata from NetSuite, check the **Use Account Numbers** preference box by going to Setup > Accounting > Accounting Preferences.

When you import the Accounts metadata to your Planning and Budgeting, and an account from NetSuite doesn't have an account number, the account name is used instead of the account number.

Important:

To customize the hierarchy of the Accounts metadata, consider checking or clearing the **Exclude Upper Level Hierarchy Members** box at NetSuite EPM > Planning and Budgeting > Manage Saved Searches. For instructions, see [Customization of Executed Saved Searches in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114111339.html).
