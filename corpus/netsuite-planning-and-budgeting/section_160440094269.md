You can view a calendar of all the jobs that you've scheduled in NetSuite for the integration with your Planning and Budgeting. For information about the jobs that you can schedule and run in NetSuite for this integration, see [Manage Jobs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160461398107.html).

To access the calendar, go to Planning & Budgeting > Jobs > Jobs Calendar. You can view the calendar by **Day**, **Week**, or **Month**. You can move the calendar forward and backward in time by clicking the **< Previous** and **Next >** buttons. Click **Today** to display jobs scheduled for today or close to today.

If you've checked the **Pause Scheduler** box to pause the schedule for a job, the job doesn't show in the calendar until you resume the schedule. Also, the end dates of scheduled jobs are excluded from the calendar view. For setup information, see [Scheduling a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_160527228086.html).
