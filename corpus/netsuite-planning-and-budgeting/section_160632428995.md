This portlet enables you to access forms and dashboards that are stored in your Planning and Budgeting. If you want to list a form or dashboard in the portlet, you need to add and set it up in NetSuite, as described in [Forms/Dashboards](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160510908737.html).

In the portlet, you can sort each column in the displayed list of forms and dashboards by the following characteristics:

-   The type, whether the form or dashboard.
    
-   The name of the form or dashboard, as defined in your Planning and Budgeting.
    
-   The description identifying each form or dashboard.
    
-   The first line of POVs specified for each form or dashboard.
    

Click the **View** link next to a form or dashboard to view it in your Planning and Budgeting. If you're logged in to your Planning and Budgeting account, the form or dashboard opens directly in a new tab.

For instructions on adding the portlet to your dashboard, see [Adding NSPB Sync Portlets to Your Home Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1551958192.html#subsect_162704383176).
