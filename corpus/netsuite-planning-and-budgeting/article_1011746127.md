The Total Revenue tab in the Revenue and Gross Margin card includes the Customer Revenue, Item Revenue, and Class Revenue dashboards. These dashboards show you high-level Revenue trends of Actual and Forecasted data by the selected Version, Subsidiary, Currency, and Account.

Finance executives look at total revenue trends and year-over-year (YoY) variances by Customer, Item, and Class. They also analyze revenue as an amount or as a percentage of total revenue by Account.

For more information about the Total Revenue Dashboards in the Revenue and Gross Margin card, see the following topics:

-   [Analyzing Trends in the Customer Revenue Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_4103339512.html)
    
-   [Analyzing Trends in the Item Revenue Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_8103503135.html)
