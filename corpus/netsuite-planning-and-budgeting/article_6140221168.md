The Income Statement card has the following forms:

-   Income Statement
    
    For more information, see [Reviewing the Income Statement](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0708022348.html).
    
-   Income Statement Subsidiary Consolidated
    
    For more information, see [Reviewing the Income Statement Subsidiary Consolidated](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0708022401.html).
    
-   Income Statement Adj
    
    For more information, see [Making Final Adjustments With Income Statement Adj](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0708022419.html).
    

The Income Statement card also includes an Income Statement Dashboard. The Income Statement Dashboard shows key metrics and trends for Actual and Forecast values. For more information, see [Analyzing Trends in the Income Statement Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0151733645.html).

Finance executives look at the income statement Accounts by Subsidiary, Department, and Class. Executives review the Income Statement Subsidiary Consolidated form and validate Actual and Forecasted Income Statement Account balances for all Subsidiaries side by side. They also make final income statement adjustments and analyze key performance indicators and trends with the Income Statement Dashboard.
