Before you install the SuiteApp, make sure you enable features required by the NSPB Sync SuiteApp in your NetSuite account.

#### To enable the required features:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **CRM** subtab.
    
3.  Under the Basic Features section, check the **Customer Relationship Management** box.
    
4.  Click the **SuiteCloud** subtab, and check the following boxes:
    
    -   Custom Records
        
    -   Client SuiteScript
        
    -   Server SuiteScript
        
    -   Token-based Authentication
        
5.  Click **Save**.
    

To use single sign-on when navigating to your Planning and Budgeting from your NetSuite account, make sure you enable the SuiteSignOn feature.
