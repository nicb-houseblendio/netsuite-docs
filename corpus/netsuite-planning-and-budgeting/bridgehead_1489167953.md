To enable the integration with your Planning and Budgeting, you need to set up an integration user with proper permissions in NetSuite. You can assign the **PBCS Integration** role to an existing or new user, and use that user for the integration. If the PBCS Integration role doesn't provide sufficient permissions for your customization, you can clone the PBCS Integration role and assign your specific permissions to it.

For instructions, refer to the following help topics:

-   [Setting Up the Integration User Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160810961778.html)
    
-   [Customizing the PBCS Integration Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_158456459598.html)
    

Note:

The PBCS Integration role, or your custom role in NetSuite, gives sufficient permissions only to the integration user. You manage your Planning and Budgeting roles and permissions directly in your Planning and Budgeting environment.
