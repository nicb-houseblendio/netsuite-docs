Important:

The integration with Planning and Budgeting recognizes search IDs the prefixes of which begin with **customsearch\_nspb\_**. Therefore, after you install Planning and Budgeting Sync, you should update IDs of existing metadata saved search and metadata dataset records in your NetSuite account to include the **\_nspb\_** prefix in place of \_nspbcs\_. For information, see [Updating Existing Records to Include the Supported \_nspb\_ Prefix](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0712061757.html).

The Planning and Budgeting Sync SuiteApp includes predefined saved searches that you can use to load data and metadata from NetSuite to your Planning and Budgeting. The saved searches are listed in the following groups:

-   [NSPB - Data Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202100630.html)
    
-   [NSPB - Metadata Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202101245.html)
    
-   [NSPB - Social Impact Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202101513.html)
    
-   [NSPB Financials - Demand Forecasting Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202101749.html)
    
-   [NSPB Financials - Data Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202101859.html)
    
-   [NSPB Financials - Metadata Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202101954.html)
    
-   [NSPB SuitePeople - Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202102105.html)
    
-   [NSPB SuiteSuccess (Premium Edition) - Data Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202102233.html)
    
-   [NSPB SuiteSuccess (Premium Edition) - Metadata Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202102346.html)
    
-   [NSPB SuiteSuccess (Standard Edition) - Data Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202102446.html)
    
-   [NSPB SuiteSuccess (Standard Edition) - Metadata Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202102604.html)
    
-   [Miscellaneous Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202102654.html)
    

To use the saved searches included in the Planning and Budgeting Sync SuiteApp, you need to create them in NetSuite for your integration with Planning and Budgeting. You can then review or customize the saved searches in NetSuite. For information, see [Managing the Planning and Budgeting Sync Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_34174642403.html).
