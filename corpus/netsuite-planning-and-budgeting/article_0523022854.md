You can use single sign-on (SSO) to enable users to go from NetSuite to your Planning and Budgeting without having to enter login credentials. To do this, you must have an account with an SSO service that uses SAML 2.0. This is not included with the NSPB Sync SuiteApp. To set up SSO, follow the instructions provided by your SSO service for setting up an application connection between NetSuite and your Planning and Budgeting.
