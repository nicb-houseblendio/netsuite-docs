If you want to use NetSuite to schedule and run a business rule configured in your Planning and Budgeting, you need to add the business rule in NetSuite. Then, you can set up the business rule as needed.

#### To add and set up a business rule:

1.  Log in to NetSuite, and go to Planning & Budgeting > Jobs > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  On the **Planning & Budgeting Jobs** page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to a business rule of your choice.
        
    -   If you don't see the business rule in the list, and you have the Administrator role, click the **Add Job** button, and follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_160527233569.html).
        
3.  Click the **Job Parameters** subtab.
    
    Enter the **Key** and **Value** of a business rule defined in your Planning and Budgeting. The field values must match exactly, including upper and lower case. Repeat if needed to create a list of parameters.
    
4.  If you want to schedule the business rule, click the **Schedule** subtab, and follow step 3 in [Scheduling a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_160527228086.html).
    
5.  For optional setup of the business rule, click the **Options** subtab.
    
    From the **Post-processing Plug-in** dropdown list, select a plug-in of your choice. The field is set to **None** by default.
    
    For information about how you can use post-processing plug-ins for the integration purposes, see [Using Post-Processing Plug-ins with NSPB Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_160527537382.html).
    
6.  Click **Save**.
