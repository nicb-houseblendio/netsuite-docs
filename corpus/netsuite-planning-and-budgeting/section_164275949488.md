You can use NetSuite to export your NSPB Sync configuration into a single file. Then, you can use the single file as a configuration backup, or import it to another NetSuite account.

You can view the history of your imports and exports on the respective **Logs** subtabs at Planning & Budgeting > Tools > Import/Export Configuration.

For information, refer to the following topics:

-   [Exporting NSPB Sync Configuration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164277662343.html)
    
-   [Importing NSPB Sync Configuration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164277679575.html)
