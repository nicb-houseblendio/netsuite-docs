This portlet gives you an overview of the current status of your NSPB Sync configuration. You can use the portlet to quickly set up any missing features.

For details, refer to [Sync Checklist](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162636206743.html).

For instructions on adding the portlet to the Planning & Budgeting page, see [Accessing Planning & Budgeting Overview Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1551958192.html#subsect_162704504393).
