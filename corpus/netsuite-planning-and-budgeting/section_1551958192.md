You can access several NSPB Sync portlets in NetSuite:

-   To check the current status of your NSPB Sync configuration, or to set it up quickly, you can use the [NSPB Sync Checklist Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162574049970.html).
    
-   For monthly maintenance of your NetSuite account, you can use the following NSPB Sync portlets:
    
    -   [Job Execution Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162565492489.html)
        
    -   [Execution Logs Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162824769683.html)
        
    -   [Planning & Budgeting Substitution Variables Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162573815157.html)
        
    -   [Planning & Budgeting Account Balance Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162563948204.html)
        
    
    Tip:
    
    Best practices for doing the monthly maintenance on your NetSuite account:
    
    1.  Check the [Planning & Budgeting Substitution Variables Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162573815157.html) to make sure that all the substitution variables in your Planning and Budgeting are up to date.
        
    2.  Run a job from the [Job Execution Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162565492489.html).
        
    3.  Watch the progress of the job execution in the corresponding Execution Logs portlet. See [Execution Logs Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162824769683.html).
        
    4.  Click the Refresh icon on the [Planning & Budgeting Account Balance Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162563948204.html) to upload the account balance from your Planning and Budgeting.
        
    
-   On your home page, you can access the following NSPB Sync portlets:
    
    -   [Planning & Budgeting Activity Logs Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1551962298.html)
        
    -   [Planning & Budgeting Form/Dashboard Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160632428995.html)
        

## Adding NSPB Sync Portlets to Your Home Page {#subsect_162704383176}

To personalize your home page, you can add the following portlets to the dashboard:

-   [Planning & Budgeting Activity Logs Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1551962298.html)
    
-   [Planning & Budgeting Form/Dashboard Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160632428995.html)
    

#### To add a portlet to your home page:

1.  In NetSuite, go to your home page.
    
2.  Click the **Personalize** link in the upper-right corner of your dashboard, or use the **Personalize Dashboard** link in the **Settings** portlet.
    
3.  On the **SuiteApps** tab, click the icon of the portlet you want to appear on your dashboard, or drag it onto the dashboard.
    

## Accessing Planning & Budgeting Overview Portlets {#subsect_162704504393}

If you go to Planning & Budgeting > Planning & Budgeting Overview, you can access the following portlets:

-   [Planning & Budgeting Substitution Variables Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162573815157.html)
    
-   [Job Execution Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162565492489.html)
    
-   [Execution Logs Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162824769683.html)
    
-   [Planning & Budgeting Account Balance Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162563948204.html)
    
-   [NSPB Sync Checklist Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162574049970.html)
    

You need to add these portlets manually to the Planning & Budgeting page. You can then customize the setup and layout of the portlets on the page.

#### To access the Planning & Budgeting Overview portlets:

1.  Go to Planning & Budgeting > Planning & Budgeting Overview.
    
2.  Click the **Personalize** link in the upper-right corner of the page.
    
3.  On the **Standard Content** tab, click **Custom Portlet**.
    
4.  In the Custom Content portlet's menu, click **Set Up**.
    
5.  From the **Source** dropdown list, select the name of the portlet you want to appear on the page.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
