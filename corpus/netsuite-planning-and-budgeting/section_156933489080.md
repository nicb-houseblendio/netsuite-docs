You can use the NSPB Sync saved searches included in the SuiteApp. If you need to customize an NSPB Sync saved search to better suit your needs, make sure you customize it properly in NetSuite.

#### To customize a saved search: {#procedure_1512147618}

1.  Go to _Reports > Saved Searches > All Saved Searches_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to a saved search of your choice.
    
3.  Change the saved search title.
    
4.  Click **Save As**.
    
    This creates a copy of the saved search.
    
5.  On the Saved Searches page, click **Edit** next to the new saved search.
    
6.  Verify that the saved search is public.
    
7.  To change the search ID, click **Change ID**.
    
    Important:
    
    Saved searches the IDs of which begin with **customsearch\_nspbcs** are available in the RESTlet. The saved search ID can't begin with **customsearch\_nspbcs\_req**.
    
8.  On the **Criteria** subtab, add or remove filters.
    
9.  On the **Results** subtab, add or remove columns.
    
    Note:
    
    When you enter a value in the **Summary Type** column, and then run the saved search in NetSuite, the Total row appears at the bottom of the saved search result. This Total row is excluded from CSV files that are exported to your Planning and Budgeting with Import Data/Metadata jobs.
    
10.  Click **Save**.
