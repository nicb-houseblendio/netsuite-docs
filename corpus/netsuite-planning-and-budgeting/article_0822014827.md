Compare the ending totals in the Income Statement form in Planning and Budgeting Financials with the ending totals in NetSuite for the current reporting month. Complete this task to make sure both environments have matching ending totals.

#### To compare the Income Statement ending totals in NetSuite against ending totals in NSPB: {#procedure_0822022022}

1.  On your Planning and Budgeting Home page, click the **Financials** card, then click **Income Statement**.
    
2.  Click **Income Statement** at the bottom of the page.
    
3.  Review the form against your NetSuite account and verify that the data imported successfully into your Planning and Budgeting application.
