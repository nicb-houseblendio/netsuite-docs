Clicking a button in the Job Execution Portlet runs the job you assigned to the button, as described in help topic [Job Execution Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162565492489.html). To watch the progress of job execution, you need to add the corresponding Execution Logs portlet to the Planning & Budgeting page. For instructions, see [Accessing Planning & Budgeting Overview Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1551958192.html#subsect_162704504393).

The available Execution Logs portlets include the following:

-   The **NSPB Job Execution Log 1** portlet for button 1.
    
-   The **NSPB Job Execution Log 2** portlet for button 2.
    
-   The **NSPB Job Execution Log 3** portlet for button 3.
