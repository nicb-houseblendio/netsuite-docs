You need to add a Data Exchange or Planning and Budgeting job in NetSuite, and then select a plug-in to process data for the job.

#### To add a plug-in to a job: {#procedure_29174416072}

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Planning and Budgeting > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  On the **Manage Jobs** page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to a job of your choice.
        
    -   If you don't see the job in the list, click the **Add Job** button, and follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in Planning and Budgeting Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114010611.html).
        
3.  Click the **Options** subtab.
    
    From the **Post-Processing Plug-ins** list, select the plug-in you want to use to process data.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
