In Data Exchange, you need to update certain data integrations to support your single currency setup. Update the data integrations by registering and removing certain business rules. Do these updates in Embedded mode. For more information about Embedded mode, see the help topic [Using Business Rules](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/enterprise-performance-management-common/diepm/integration_business_rules_100x41e05598.html) in the Oracle Help Center.

Complete the following data integration updates to work with a single currency:

-   [Updating BalSht\_Trans Integration](#subsect_1115060309)
    
-   [Updating BalSht\_Trans\_SRC Integration](#subsect_1115060901)
    
-   [Updating the Consol\_FxRate\_BudAct Integration](#subsect_1115061207)
    

## Updating BalSht\_Trans Integration {#subsect_1115060309}

In the BalSht\_Trans data integration, remove the NFS\_BS\_Act\_Calc business rule.

#### To update the business rules of data integrations:

1.  From the Planning and Budgeting Home page, click **Application** and then **Data Exchange**.
    
2.  Click the **Data Integration** tab.
    
3.  Under the **Name** column, click the link for **BalSht\_Trans**.
    
    1.  The Edit Integration: BalSht\_Trans window opens. Click the **Options** tab.
        
    2.  Click the **Business Rules** subtab.
        
    3.  Click **Embedded** to show more options.
        
    4.  Select **NFS\_BS\_Act\_Calc**, and then click the Delete icon ![Delete icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/DeleteIcon.png).
        
    5.  Click **Save**.
        

## Updating BalSht\_Trans\_SRC Integration {#subsect_1115060901}

The following table lists the business rules you need to register to the BalSht\_Trans\_SRC data integration and the sequence values to set:

| Business Rule | Sequence |
| --- | --- |
| NFS\_BS\_Act\_Calc | 1 |
| NFS\_BS\_Forecast\_Calc | 2 |
| NFS\_AGG - Single Curr - BalSht - Actual | 3 |
| NFS\_AGG - Single Curr - BalSht - Forecast | 4 |

#### To update the business rules of data integrations:

1.  From the Planning and Budgeting Home page, click **Application**, and then click **Data Exchange**.
    
2.  Click the **Data Integration** tab.
    
3.  Under the **Name** column, click the link for **BalSht\_Trans\_SRC**.
    
    1.  The Edit Integration: BalSht\_Trans\_SRC window opens. Click the **Options** tab.
        
    2.  Click the **Business Rules** subtab.
        
    3.  Register the business rules listed in the table, and enter the corresponding sequence values. To register a business rule in embedded mode:
        
        1.  Click **Embedded**.
            
        2.  Click the Add icon ![Add icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/AddIcon.png).
            
        3.  Under **Name**, enter the name of the business rule that you want to run.
            
        4.  For **Sequence**, enter a number to set the order for running the rule if there are multiple rules or rulesets.
            
    4.  Repeat step 3c for each business rule in the table.
        
    5.  Click **Save**.
        

## Updating the Consol\_FxRate\_BudAct Integration {#subsect_1115061207}

In the Consol\_FxRate\_BudAct data integration, remove the following business rules:

#### To update the business rules of data integrations:

1.  From the Planning and Budgeting Home page, click **Application** and then **Data Exchange**.
    
2.  Click the **Data Integration** tab.
    
3.  Under the **Name** column, click the link for **Consol\_FxRate\_BudAct**.
    
    1.  The Edit Integration: Consol\_FxRate\_BudAct window opens. Click the **Options** tab.
        
    2.  Click the **Business Rules** subtab.
        
    3.  Click **Embedded** to show more options.
        
    4.  To remove a business rule, select it, and then click the Delete icon ![Delete icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuitePlanningandBudgeting/PlanningandBudgetingFinancials/AdminGuide/DeleteIcon.png).
        
        Remove these business rules:
        
        -   NFS\_CConv\_CashFlow\_Actual
            
        -   NFS\_CConv\_CashFlow\_Forecast
            
        -   NFS\_CConv\_Fx Rates Copy
            
        -   NFS\_CConv\_Plan\_Actual
            
        -   NFS\_CConv\_Plan\_Forecast
            
        -   NFS\_BS\_Forecast\_Calc
            
    5.  Click **Save**.
