You can see the GST Cess under the **Details** column on the **Tax Details** subtab. The following example shows how GST Cess looks in the **Details** column.

**Advol**: 25, **Non Advol**: 0

GST Cess is calculated with 5%.

In the example, Advol and Non Advol show the amounts calculated using the cess percentage and cess rate per unit from the tax rate rule. The GST Cess percentage is taken from the value you set in the tax rate rule.
