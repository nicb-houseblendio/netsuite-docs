## Overview {#bridgehead_1536796013}

These topics are for NetSuite accounts with SuiteTax. For accounts without SuiteTax, see [Legacy Tax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_3710626699.html).

The India Localization SuiteTax Reports SuiteApp is designed for accounts that use the India Localization SuiteTax Engine. It lets you generate Tax Deduction at Source (TDS) and India Goods and Services Tax (GST) reports.

You can view the India Localization SuiteTax Reports SuiteApp pages in multiple languages. For information about supported languages, see [Supported Languages in India Localization SuiteTax Engine SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1023023140.html).

### Supported Features in India Localization SuiteTax Reports {#bridgehead_1536796580}

The India Localization SuiteTax Reports SuiteApp lets you generate the following reports under TDS and GST:

-   **TDS** - Income Tax Form 26Q, Income Tax Form 27Q
    
-   **GST** - [GSTR-1](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0105080453.html), [GSTR-3B](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0105102305.html), [GST ANX-1](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0105104652.html), [GST Purchase Register Without Cess](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0105104918.html), and [GSTR-9](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0105105130.html)
