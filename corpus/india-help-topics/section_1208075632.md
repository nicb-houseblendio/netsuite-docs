The following table lists the data provided by the GSTR9 5 Outward report to file GSTR-9.

| Nature of Supplies | Description | Transaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (5a) Zero rated supplies (export) without payment of tax. | Total value of exports, excluding supplies made to SEZs, where no tax payment is required. Customer Registration Type - Overseas Tax Rate - 0.00% Reverse Charge - Not applicable (tax is without reverse charge). | Outward supplies (sales transactions), Cash Sale, Invoice |
| (5b) Supplies to SEZs without payment of tax. | Total value of supplies made to SEZs where no tax payment is required. Customer Registration Type - SEZ. Tax Rate - 0.00% Reverse Charge - Not applicable (tax is without reverse charge). | Outward supplies (sales transactions), Cash Sale, Invoice |
| (5c) Supplies on which reverse charge applies. | Total value of supplies made to registered persons where the reverse charge mechanism applies. Customer Registration Types - Regular, Composite, SEZ, Overseas. Tax Rate - 0.00% Reverse Charge - Tax is by reverse charge. Debit and Credit Notes - Details of debit and credit notes are reported separately. Default Value - The default value for these fields is **0.00**, as reverse charge on sales is not supported. | Outward supplies (sales transactions) |
| (5d) Exempted | Total value of exempted supplies. Customer Registration Types - Regular, Composite, SEZ, Unregistered, Overseas. Tax Rate - Exempted | Outward supplies (sales transactions), Cash Sale, Invoice |
| (5e) Nil rated | Total value of Nil rated supplies. Tax Rate - Nil Customer Registration Types - Regular, Composite, SEZ, Unregistered, Overseas. | Outward supplies (sales transactions), Cash Sale, Invoice |
| (5f) Non-GST supplies (includes No Supply) | Total value of non-GST supplies, including No Supply. Customer Registration Types - Regular, Composite, SEZ, Unregistered, Overseas. | Outward supplies (sales transactions), Cash Sale, Invoice |
| (5h) Credit Notes issued for transactions specified in A to F above (-). | Total value of credit notes issued for supplies declared in the following sections - 5a, 5b, 5d, 5e, and 5f. | Outward supplies (sales transactions), Credit Memo |

## GSTR9 5 Outward Limitations {#subsect_1208064951}

The GSTR9 5 Outward report doesn't contain the following data.

-   Debit notes issued for transactions specified in A to F above (+).
    
-   Supplies declared through amendments (+).
    
-   Supplies reduced through amendments (-).
