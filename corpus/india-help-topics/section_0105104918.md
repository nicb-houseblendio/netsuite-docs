Under the Simplified GST Tax Return Filing system, a registered taxpayer must maintain a purchase register that contains details of purchased goods and services. The India Localization SuiteTax Reports SuiteApp helps maintain a register of purchased goods and services for reconciliation and tax return filing.

**Export formats** - CSV (.csv), DAT (.dat), Excel (.xls), and Text (.txt).

| Report | Description | Transaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GST Purchase Register (without Cess) | Inward supply of goods or services from composite, regular, SEZ vendors | Bill, Credit Memo, Vendor Credit |
