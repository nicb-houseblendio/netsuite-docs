To process sales transactions for export without IGST and with an LUT or Bond, you must create a LUT or Bond Detail record. Then, assign that record to any sales transaction where the export type is **With LUT or Bond**.

You can select a LUT or Bond Detail record only if the sales transaction falls within its effective period and there's still some usable value left.

#### To set up a LUT or Bond detail record:

1.  Go to Setup > India Localization > GST Tax Rate Rules.
    
2.  Click the **LUT and Bond Details** subtab.
    
3.  Click **New LUT or Bond Details**.
    
4.  In the **LUT or Bond Number** field, enter the LUT or Bond number you'll use for the export transaction.
    
5.  In the **LUT or Bond Value** field, enter the LUT or Bond value you'll use for the export transaction.
    
6.  In the **Effective From** and **Valid Until** fields, enter the effective period for this record.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
