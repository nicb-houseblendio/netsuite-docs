The India Localization SuiteTax Reports SuiteApp lets you generate India TDS and GST reports. It's a managed SuiteApp that is automatically updated with enhancements and new features.

This SuiteApp is free, but it must be shared with your NetSuite account before you can install it. To request access, contact your NetSuite account manager.

When the SuiteApp is shared with your account, perform the following steps to install it.

#### To install the India Localization SuiteTax Reports SuiteApp:

1.  Go to _Customization > SuiteBundler > Search & Install Bundles_.
    
2.  In the **Keywords** field, search for the India Localization SuiteTax Reports.
    
3.  Click **Search**.
    
4.  Click the link of the SuiteApp with the following details:
    
    -   SutieApp Name - India Localization SuiteTax Reports
        
    -   SuiteApp ID - 400554
        
5.  On the Bundle Details page, click **Install**.
    
    If the **Install** button in unavailable, the SuiteApp may not be shared yet. Contact your NetSuite account manager for assistance.
