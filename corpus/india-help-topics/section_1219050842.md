After you've created scenarios for a localization feature, you must assign them to the feature to send custom notifications when the scenario occurs.

#### To assign a notification scenario to a localization feature: {#procedure_1219051107}

1.  Go to Setup > India Localization > GST Tax Rate Rules.
    
2.  Based on the scenario type, click the **Tax Rate Rule** or **LUT Bond and Details** subtab.
    
3.  Click **Edit** beside the record you want to assign the scenario to.
    
4.  Click the **Customized Notification** subtab.
    
5.  Check the **Enable Notification** box.
    
6.  To assign scenarios to a GST tax rate rule, select the scenarios from the **GST Notification Scenarios** sublist.
    
    To assign scenarios to LUT bond and details, select the scenarios from the **LUT Notification Scenarios** sublist.
    
7.  Click **Add**.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
