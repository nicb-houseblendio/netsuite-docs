You can create notification scenarios for GST tax rate rules and LUT bond and details. You can use predefined criteria to create a scenario.

Tip:

To view the list of predefined criteria for each feature, click the **Criterias** subtab on the Customized Notifications page.

Before you create a scenario, consider the following points:

-   Only users with the Administrator and India Tax Manager roles can create, edit, view, or delete notification scenarios.
    
-   You can't create GST tax rate rule and LUT bond and details scenarios through CSV.
    
-   You can't add, delete, or edit criteria. All criteria are predefined.
    
-   You can't add duplicate criteria, email content, or recipients to a scenario.
    
-   You can't create duplicate notification scenarios for a feature.
    
-   If a GST tax rate rule or LUT bond and details notification scenario has dependent records, you can't delete the scenario.
    
-   When you create a scenario, you must add at least one criterion and email content details.
    

#### To create a scenario for a feature: {#procedure_1219040042}

1.  Go to Setup > India Localization > Customized Notifications.
    
2.  Based on the feature you want to work with, click the **GST Tax Rate Rule** or **LUT Bond and Details** subtab.
    
3.  On the **Scenarios** subtab, click **New GST Notification Scenario** or **New LUT Notification Scenario**.
    
4.  Enter a unique name for the scenario.
    
5.  Enter a description for the scenario.
    
6.  Click the **Criteria** subtab.
    
7.  In the Details section, fill out the following fields:
    
    1.  **Criteria** - Select a predefined criterion available for a localization feature. This field is required.
        
    2.  **Record Type** - Select a record type to apply the criterion. This field is required.
        
    3.  **Field Name** - Select a field on the selected record type to apply the criterion. This field is required.
        
    4.  **Operation** - Select the operation that you want to perform on the selected field. This field is required.
        
    5.  **Operation Value** - Enter a value based on the selected operation.
        
    6.  **Description** - Enter a description as required. This field is optional.
        
8.  To add the criterion to the scenario, click **Add Criteria**.
    
9.  Click the **Email Content** subtab.
    
10.  In the Details section, fill out the following fields:
     
     1.  **Record Type**
         
     2.  **Field Name** - Select a field associated with the record type.
         
11.  To add an email content to the scenario, click **Add Email Content**.
     
12.  Click the **Email Recipients** subtab.
     
13.  In the **Recipient Email ID** field, enter the recipient's email ID.
     
14.  In the **Recipient Name** field, enter the recipient's name.
     
15.  Click **Save**.
     

After you create notification scenarios for a localization feature, assign the scenario to the feature. For more information, see [Assigning Custom Notifications for GST Rules and LUT Bond and Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1219050842.html).

## Editing Notification Scenarios {#bridgehead_79165252407}

The India Localization SuiteTax Engine SuiteApp lets you edit a notification scenario.

#### To edit a notification scenario: {#procedure_1219045503}

1.  Go to Setup > India Localization > Customized Notifications.
    
2.  Based on the scenario you want to edit, click the **GST Tax Rate Rule** or **LUT Bond and Details** subtab.
    
3.  Click **Edit** beside the scenario you want to edit.
    
4.  Modify the scenario details as required.
    
5.  Click **Update Criteria**.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

## Viewing Notification Scenarios {#bridgehead_45165321402}

To view the list of scenarios for GST tax rate rules or LUT bond and details, click the **Scenarios** subtab for the feature.
