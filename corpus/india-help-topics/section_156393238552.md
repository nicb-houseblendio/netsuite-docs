After you export the TDS tax report from NetSuite, submit the data online to your tax authority to file your TDS return. Verify the data before submitting it.

## Important Considerations Before Filing TDS Returns {#subsect_156393275545}

Consider the following points before filing TDS tax returns:

1.  You must have a valid Tax Deduction Account Number (TAN) and register for e-Filing.
    
2.  Prepare e-TDS using the Return Preparation Utility (RPU) and validate it with the File Validation Utility (FVU). Download both utilities from the Tin-NSDL website. Online TDS files must be in .txt format.
    
3.  After you validate and generate the file, upload and submit it online to your tax authority.
