Read the following points to understand the limitations of the India Localization SuiteTax Reports SuiteApp.

-   You can use the SuiteApp to generate reports for subsidiaries with a **Regular** registration type only.
    
-   For GSTR1 filing, the SuiteApp doesn't support supplies taxed at a differential percentage (65%) of the existing tax rate.
    
-   The following is not supported in GSTR 3B:
    
    -   Supplies made to persons with Unique Identification Number (UIN)
        
    -   Import of goods
        
-   If you make tax-related adjustments for a sales or purchase transaction using a journal entry, the tax reports won't include them. You should summarize the amount and adjust the tax reports manually.
