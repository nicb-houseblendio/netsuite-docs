If you edit tax fields that affect TDS calculation in a vendor bill, you can't do the following:

-   Save the vendor bill.
    
-   Preview tax on the bill.
    

If TDS has already been calculated on a vendor bill and you need to change the TDS tax fields, void the bill first. Then, create a new bill with the changes you need.
