To enable the Customized Notification feature, you must check the **Customized Notification** box on the Configuration page.

#### To check the Customized Notification box: {#procedure_1219035152}

1.  Go to Setup > India Localization > Configuration.
    
2.  Click the **Customized Notification** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Customized Notification** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

You can now create notification scenarios and assign them to India localization features. For more information, see [Creating Notification Scenarios](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1219035525.html) and [Assigning Custom Notifications for GST Rules and LUT Bond and Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1219050842.html)
