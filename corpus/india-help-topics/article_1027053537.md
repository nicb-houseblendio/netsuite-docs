The India Localization SuiteTax Engine SuiteApp provides two tax plug-ins for tax calculation. You can use either plug-in to calculate taxes.

Note:

Changing the tax plug-in during the fiscal year doesn't affect your tax calculations. To switch tax engine plug-ins, follow the steps in [To select a tax engine plug-in:](#procedure_0213054114).

-   **NetSuite India SuiteTax Engine SS2** - If you choose this plug-in, use only the Administrator or India Tax Manager roles. This helps you avoid permission issues.
    
-   **NetSuite India SuiteTax Plug-in** - If you choose this plug-in, you can use any role for tax calculation. By default, it uses the India Tax Plug-in Manager role, which already has all the permissions you need.
    

#### To select a tax engine plug-in: {#procedure_0213054114}

1.  Go to Customization > Plug-ins > Manage Plug-ins.
    
2.  On the Manage Plug-in Implementations page, check the box beside the tax engine plug-in you want to use.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
