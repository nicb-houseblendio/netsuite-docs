After you install the India Localization SuiteTax Engine SuiteApp, you'll get the India Tax Manager role by default. This role has all the permissions you need to use the SuiteApp.

## Assigning the India Tax Manager Role {#subsect_1027052054}

You can assign the India Tax Manager role to your employees using the Administrator role. Go to Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles and assign the India Tax Manager role to employees who need access.

## Configuring Roles for India Localization {#subsect_1027052144}

If you need custom roles, base them on the India Tax Manager role. Add the permissions listed below to your custom roles. For information about customizing and creating roles, see [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html).

For information about assigning permissions, see [NetSuite Permissions Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N325094.html).
