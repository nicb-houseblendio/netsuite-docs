In NetSuite, go back to your exported transactions and update the **E-Way Bill No.** and **E-Way Bill Date** fields in the India E-Way Bill Details section.

You can add these details by editing each transaction or update several at a time through the Generate E-Way Bill page. For more information, see [Updating E-Way Bill Details on Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1111054904.html).

After you enter the EBN and E-Way Bill date, the E-Way Bill status for those transactions will change to **Updated**.
