Assigning tax nature to an item means selecting whether it's goods, services, or capital goods. When you select the item in a transaction, its tax nature populates automatically.

#### To assign a default India tax nature to an item:

1.  Go to _Lists > Accounting > Items_.
    
2.  To assign tax nature to an item, click **Edit** next to it.
    
3.  Click the **Accounting** subtab.
    
4.  Under the India Tax Information section, in the **Nature** field, select the tax nature for the item.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
