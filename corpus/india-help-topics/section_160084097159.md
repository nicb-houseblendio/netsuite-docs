You must add owner IDs for each tax registration number in a subsidiary record. These IDs are used for authentication during the certification, and your GST Suvidha Provider (GSP) will provide them to you.

#### To add owner IDs for tax registration numbers in a subsidiary record:

1.  Go to a subsidiary record.
    
2.  Click the **Tax Registrations** subtab.
    
3.  In the **Owner ID** column, enter the owner ID next to each tax registration number.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

For single instance accounts, the **Owner ID** column is available on the **Tax Registrations** subtab of the Company Information page.
