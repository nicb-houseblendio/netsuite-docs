The India Localization SuiteTax Reports SuiteApp lets you generate India TDS and GST reports.

You can install the India Localization SuiteTax Reports SuiteApp only after the following SuiteApps are shared and installed in your account. Contact you NetSuite account manager to have these SuiteApps shared.

After the SuiteApps are shared with your account, install the SuiteApps in the order specified:

1.  India Localization SuiteTax Engine (SuiteApp ID 400562)
    
2.  Tax Reporting Framework (SuiteApp ID 237699) - For information about the list of features to be enabled for the Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp, see [Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1528262949.html).
