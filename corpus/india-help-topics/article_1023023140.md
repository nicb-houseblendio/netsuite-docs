The India Localization SuiteTax Engine SuiteApp user interface is available in multiple languages, except for India-specific custom forms and custom lists. If you select an unsupported language, the interface defaults to English (U.S.).

-   Chinese (Simplified)
    
-   Chinese (Traditional)
    
-   Danish
    
-   Dutch (Netherlands)
    
-   Finnish
    
-   French (Canada)
    
-   French (France)
    
-   German
    
-   Indonesian
    
-   Italian
    
-   Japanese
    
-   Korean
    
-   Norwegian
    
-   Portuguese (Brazil)
    
-   Russian
    
-   Swedish
    
-   Spanish (Argentina)
    
-   Spanish (Spain)
    
-   Thai
    
-   Turkish
    
-   Vietnamese
    

For information about changing your language settings, see [Setting Language Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543591052.html).
