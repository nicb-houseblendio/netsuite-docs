The GSTR-1 report summarizes all sales (outward supplies) for a taxpayer by month or quarter, and you can use it to file the GSTR-1 tax return.

**Frequency** - Monthly or quarterly return for every registered dealer.

**Key sections** - The generated GSTR-1 report supports all required sections of the GSTR-1 tax return form (B2B, B2CL, B2CS, CDNR, CDNUR, EXEMP, EXP, HSN, DOCS).

**Export formats** - CSV (.csv), DAT (.dat), Excel (.xls), and Text (.txt).

The following table lists GSTR-1 invoice detail reports by section of the GSTR-1 form.

| Report | Description | Transaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GSTR1 B2B** | Taxable sales to regular, composite dealers, intrastate, interstate, SEZ (excludes Nil Rated and Exempted supplies). | Invoice, Cash Sale |
| **GSTR1 B2CL** | Interstate sales to unregistered customers and sales greater than 2.5 lakh INR (excludes Nil Rated and Exempted supplies). | Invoice, Cash Sale |
| **GSTR1 B2CS** | Sales to unregistered customers, intrastate sales, and interstate sales equal or less than Rs 2.5 lakh INR (excludes Nil Rated and Exempted supplies). | Invoice, Cash Sale |
| **GSRT1 CDNR** | Credit notes from taxable sales from Regular,Composite Dealers,Interstate, Intrastate, and SEZ (excludes Nil Rated and Exempted supplies). To include only taxable transactions when generating the report, go to Reports > Saved Searches > Configure Country Tax Reports. Then, choose **Include Taxable Transactions Only** to exclude nontaxable transactions (such as customer refunds associated with credit memos) from the report. | Credit Memo, Cash Refund |
| **GSRT1 CDNUR** | Credit notes for interstate taxable sales to unregistered or overseas customers and interstate sales greater than Rs 2.5 lakh INR (excludes Nil Rated and Exempted supplies). | Credit Memo, Cash Refund |
| **GSRT1 EXEMP** | Net value of Nil rated, Exempted, and Non-GST supplies made from Regular,Composite Dealers,Interstate, Intrastate, and SEZ customers. | Invoice, Credit Memo, Cash Sale, Cash Refund |
| **GSTR1 EXP** | Taxable sales to overseas customers. | Invoice, Cash Sale |
| **GSTR1 HSN** | HSN-wise summary of sales. | Invoice, Credit Memo, Cash Sale, Cash Refund |
| **GSTR1 DOCS** | Details of the following documents by transaction types.
-   Invoice for Outward Supplies
-   Credit Notes
