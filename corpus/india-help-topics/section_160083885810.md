The guide provides step-by-step instructions for configuring electronic invoicing (e-invoicing) for India in NetSuite. It is intended for employees responsible for compliance with India's GST e-invoicing requirements.

Before setting up the India Localization SuiteTax Engine SuiteApp for e-invoicing, ensure you have the following.

-   NetSuite account with the India Localization SuiteApp installed.
    
-   An administrator role or a role with the required permissions. To review the required permissions for India Localization, see [Configuring Roles for India Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1027053649.html#subsect_1027052144)
    
-   Credentials from your GSP (such as ClearTax) to connect with NetSuite.
    

Use the following checklist to track your progress as you configure India e-invoicing in NetSuite. To ensure a successful setup, complete each step in order.

<a id="table_1114032610"></a>

| Task | Reference Section |
| --- | --- |
| Assign e-document sending methods to subsidiaries. Note: If you want to customize the default templates, make a copy first, then make your changes. By doing so, your changes won't get lost during SuiteApp updates. To find the default templates, go to Setup > E-Documents > E-Document Templates. | [Assigning E-Document Templates and Sending Methods to Subsidiaries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160083892441.html). |
| Assign e-document templates and sending methods to subsidiaries. | [Assigning E-Document Templates and Sending Methods to Subsidiaries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160083892441.html). |
| Assign e-document packages to customers and vendors who need e-invoicing. | [Assigning E-Document Packages to Customers and Vendors](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160069757680.html). |
| Add owner IDs to subsidiaries. | [Adding Owner IDs in Subsidiary Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160084097159.html). |
| Connect with GST Suvidha Providers (GSPs). | [Connect NetSuite with GST Suvidha Providers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160069534329.html). |
| (Optional) Customize and activate E-Way Bill by IRN template. | [Customize and Activate E-Way Bill by IRN Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114034850.html). |
