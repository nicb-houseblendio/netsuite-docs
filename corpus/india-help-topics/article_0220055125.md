Warning:

You shouldn't uninstall the India Localization SuiteTax Engine SuiteApp.

When you uninstall a customization SuiteApp, all its objects and any data in them are deleted.

Any custom records you've created from the custom record types in the SuiteApp are also deleted.

To avoid losing data, don't uninstall the SuiteApp.

To uninstall the India Localization SuiteTax Engine SuiteApp, you must disable the tax engine plug-in first using the following steps.

#### To uninstall the NetSuite India SuiteTax Engine Plug-in: {#procedure_0220055253}

1.  Go to _Customization > Plug-ins > Manage Plug-ins_.
    
2.  On the Manage Plug-in Implementations page, clear the box next to the tax engine plug-in in your account.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    
4.  Go to Customization > Plug-ins > Plug-in Implementations.
    
5.  Click **Edit** next to the tax engine plug-in in your account.
    
6.  From the **Actions** dropdown, click **Delete**.
    
7.  Uninstall the SuiteApp.
    
    For more information about uninstalling SuiteApps, see [Uninstalling a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3400972.html).
