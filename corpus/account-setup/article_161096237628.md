You can define your company's fiscal year at _Setup > Accounting > Manage G/L > Manage Accounting Periods_. Fiscal years don't have to align with calendar years. For more information, see [Fiscal Calendars](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1449211.html).

You can use the following fiscal year expressions to show the fiscal year in your advanced numbering rules. You can use any of these expressions if your fiscal year matches the calendar year.

-   **${YYYY\_START}**: When your fiscal year covers two years, this uses the four-digit year from the first part as the fiscal year.
    
-   **${YY\_START}**: When your fiscal year covers two years, this uses the two-digit year from the first part as the fiscal year.
    
-   **${YYYY\_END}**: When your fiscal year covers two years, this uses the four-digit year from the second part as the fiscal year.
    
-   **${YY\_END}**: When your fiscal year covers two years, this uses the two-digit year from the second part as the fiscal year.
    

The following table shows examples of fiscal year expressions in advanced numbering rules.

| Your Company's Fiscal Year | Transaction Date | Expression in Advanced Numbering Rule | Year Used in Document or Transaction Number |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| From July 2020 to June 2021 | September 16, 2020 | ${YYYY\_START} | 2020 |
| From July 2020 to June 2021 | September 16, 2020 | ${YYYY\_END} | 2021 |
| From July 2020 to June 2021 | September 16, 2020 | ${YYYY} | 2020 |
| From July 2020 to June 2021 | September 16, 2020 | ${MM} | 09 |
| From July 2020 to June 2021 | September 16, 2020 | ${DD} | 16 |
| From July 2020 to June 2021 | January 5, 2021 | ${YYYY\_START} | 2020 |
| From July 2020 to June 2021 | January 5, 2021 | ${YYYY\_END} | 2021 |
| From January 2020 to December 2020 | September 16, 2020 | ${YYYY\_START} | 2020 |
| From January 2020 to December 2020 | September 16, 2020 | ${YYYY\_END} | 2020 |
| From January 2021 to December 2021 | January 5, 2021 | ${YYYY\_START} | 2021 |
| From January 2021 to December 2021 | January 5, 2021 | ${YYYY\_END} | 2021 |
| Any defined fiscal year | January 5, 2021 | ${YYYY} | 2021 |
| Any defined fiscal year | January 5, 2021 | ${MM} | 01 |
| Any defined fiscal year | January 5, 2021 | ${DD} | 05 |
