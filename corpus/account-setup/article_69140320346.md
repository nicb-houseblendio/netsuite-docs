If your company needs to update the primary contact for your NetSuite account, you can submit a change request. This ensures the correct person receives important billing communications and account notifications.

Note:

The change may take up to two business days to take effect in your account.

#### To submit a primary contact change request:

1.  Log into your NetSuite account with the NetSuite 360 - Plus Financials role.
    
2.  Click the **Support** tab.
    
3.  Click the **NetSuite 360** link in the NetSuite 360 portlet.
    
4.  In the left panel in NetSuite 360, click the **Financial Dashboard** tab.
    
5.  On the Financial Dashboard page, in the **Quick Access** portlet, click the **Submit Primary Contact Change Request** link.
    
6.  In the popup window that opens, review the **Current Primary Contact**, and fill out the **Primary Contact Name** and **Primary Contact Email** fields with new primary contact information.
    
7.  Click the **Submit** button.
