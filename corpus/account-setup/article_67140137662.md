If you have the right permissions, you can log in to NetSuite 360 to see the status of enhancements associated with your account. For more information about NetSuite 360 roles and permissions, see [NetSuite 360](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4350949235.html). To learn more about NetSuite 360 access, see [Assigning NetSuite 360 Access](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1112111427.html).

You can view enhancements on the NetSuite 360 Dashboard page.

#### To review the status of an enhancement:

1.  Log into your NetSuite account with a role that lets you access NetSuite 360.
    
2.  Click the **Support** tab.
    
3.  Click the **NetSuite 360** link in the NetSuite 360 portlet.
    
4.  On the NetSuite 360 Dashboard page, in the **Cases/Issues/Enhancements** portlet, click the **Enhancements** tab. The Enhancements list displays a list of enhancements associated with your account.
    
    Note:
    
    You can filter enhancements based on their type, status, and abstract.
    
5.  To view a detailed enhancement record, click the number of the enhancement. The detailed enhancement record opens in a new tab of your browser.
