The following table summarizes the Partner Center and standard role for the center. The table also describes the features the role can access. For more information, see [The Partner Center Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1167882.html). Available features may vary based on what's enabled in your account.

| Standard Role for this Center | Description | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partner Center | Lets partners access their own reports, promotion codes, and customer and partner records online. | 
-   Partner records can be for a company or an individual.
-   Partners can give their customers access to the Customer Center.
