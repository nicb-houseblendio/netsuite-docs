The following table summarizes the Advanced Partner Center and standard role for the center. The table also describes the features the role can access. For more information, see [The Advanced Partner Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1168130.html). Available features may vary based on what's enabled in your account.

| Standard Role for this Center | Description | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Advanced Partner Center | Lets partners access the Partner Center and additional Sales Center and Support Center features online. | Administrators must enable both the Partner Access and the Advanced Partner Access features. |
