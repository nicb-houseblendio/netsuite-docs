You can see the following details for your account:

-   **Current:** The number of resources currently used in, or licenses purchased for, your account.
    
-   **Service Tier Limit:** The maximum number of resources you can purchase under your current service tier.
    
-   **% of Service Tier Limit:** The current value of resources used, as a percentage of your resources limit.
