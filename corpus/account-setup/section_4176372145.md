You can edit an existing department at any time. Any changes you make overwrite historical transactions. For example, if you change the department name from NorthEast to North East, all historical transactions for that department show as North East.

You can delete a department only **if** there aren't any dependent transactions. If there are any historical transactions, you can't delete the department.

#### To modify a department record: {#procedure_4176372145}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Departments_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the department you want to change.
    
3.  On the Department page, make any changes as needed.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To delete a department record: {#procedure_4392024362}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Departments_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the department you want to delete.
    
3.  On the Department page, in the **Actions** menu, click **Delete**.
    
4.  Click **OK** to confirm you want to delete the department.
