NetSuite offers a variety of preferences that you can set to meet your needs. These preferences are available at the levels shown in the following table:

| Preference Type | Applies To | Set By | Set On | For details, see |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| User | Current user only | User | _Home > Set Preferences_ page | [Setting Personal Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N475297.html) |
| Role | Users logging in with the role | Account Administrator | Role record, Preferences subtab | [Setting Role-Based Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N292753.html) |
| 
Subsidiary

(OneWorld only)



 | Users assigned to the subsidiary | Account Administrator | Subsidiary record, Preferences subtab | [Editing Subsidiary Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N273122.html) |
| Company | All users in the account | Account Administrator | _Setup > Company > Preferences > General Preferences ( Administrator )_ page | [Set Company Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N242860.html) |

-   The preferences you can set at each level depend on your NetSuite edition and which features are enabled.
    
-   The same preferences are available at the user and role levels, and some are also available at the company and subsidiary levels.
    
-   If a company-level preference doesn't allow overrides, you can't set it at the subsidiary, role, or user levels. See [Overriding Company Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N246888.html).
    
-   If the same preference is set at more than one level, NetSuite uses the following order:
    
    -   A user preference overrides role, subsidiary, and company preferences.
        
    -   A role-based preference overrides subsidiary and company preferences.
        
    -   A subsidiary-based preference overrides company preference.
        
    -   A company preference overrides the system default.
