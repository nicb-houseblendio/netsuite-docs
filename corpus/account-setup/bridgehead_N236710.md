Important:

To use some of the employee and payroll features, you need SuitePeople HR or SuitePeople U.S. Payroll set up on your account. For more information, see [SuitePeople Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1495573671.html).

If you're an Administrator, you can enable the following features on the **Employees** subtab of the Enable Features page at _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.

-   [Enabling SuitePeople HR Information System Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1112110056.html)
    
-   [Enabling SuitePeople HR Performance Management Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1112110417.html)
    
-   [Enabling SuitePeople U.S. Payroll Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1112110521.html)
    
-   [Enabling Time and Expenses Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1112110701.html)
    
-   [Enabling Commissions Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1112111002.html)
    
-   [Enabling Employee Permissions Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1112111050.html)
