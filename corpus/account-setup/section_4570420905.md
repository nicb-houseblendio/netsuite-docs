The person who manages NetSuite for your organization is the main contact for NetSuite and your users. This person:

-   Oversees the initial setup of NetSuite
    
-   Manages the on-going administration of NetSuite
    

Key administrator tasks may include:

-   Handles daily account maintenance and management
    
-   Takes care of technical and system administration, including configuration and integration
    
-   Acts as the point of contact for end users and NetSuite Support
    
-   Helps users adopt the system and stay satisfied through training, support, and meeting their needs
    
-   Plans, reviews, and customizes NetSuite to meet user needs
    
-   Helps with data extraction through reporting and searches
    
-   Monitors usage and system performance
    
-   Makes sure account data is high quality, runs regular data audits, and fixes data integrity issues
    
-   Develops tests, does functional testing, and rolls out customizations, custom objects, new enhancements, application releases, and system integration based on user or business needs
    

The person who administers NetSuite uses [The Administrator Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4610746707.html).
