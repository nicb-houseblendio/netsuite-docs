On the Company Information page, enter general settings for your company in this NetSuite account.

#### To enter company general information:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Company Information_.
    
2.  In the **Company Name** field, enter your company name as you want it to appear in on all forms except checks.
    
3.  In the **Legal Name** field, enter the legal name of your company.
    
4.  In the **Taxonomy Reference** field, select the taxonomy reference your company uses. This field is available if the Tax Audit Files SuiteApp is installed. For more information, see [Tax Audit Files](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2073244.html).
    
5.  In the **Recipient of E-Document Notifications** field, enter the email address for notifications when bulk processes finish or have errors. If you don't enter an email address, notifications go to account administrators.
    
6.  In the **Company Logo (Forms)** field, select your company logo to show on printed and emailed transaction forms.
    
    For more information, see the following topics.
    
    -   [Uploading and Setting Your Logo](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N542584.html)
        
    -   [Uploading Your Logo to the File Cabinet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161963493389.html)
        
    -   [Setting Your Logo to Appear on Forms and Centers Associated with your NetSuite Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161963499495.html)
        
    -   [Logos in OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N276203.html)
        
7.  In the **Company Logo (Pages)** field, select a company logo to show on your Customer, Partner, and Vendor Centers pages.
    
8.  Check the **Display Logo Internally** box to show your company logo on all NetSuite pages next to the Oracle NetSuite logo.
    
9.  Enter company location information:
    
    -   In the **Web Site** field, enter your website URL.
        
    -   In the **County/State/Province** field, set up counties, states, and provinces. For information, see [Setting Up States, Provinces, and Counties](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N253697.html).
        
    -   The **Country** field shows the country set for the company. For information, see [Supported Countries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N253846.html).
        
    -   In the **Return Email Address** field, enter the return email address used to send forms and reports from NetSuite.
        
    -   In the **Fax** field, enter a fax number for your company.
        
10.  In the **First Fiscal Month** field, select the first month of your fiscal year.
     
11.  In the **Time Zone** field, select your company's time zone. Individuals can set their own time zone at _Home > Set Preferences_.
     
12.  The **Allowed IP Addresses** field, is available when IP Address Rules is enabled. Enter the IP addresses you want to allow for NetSuite access. For more information, see [Enabling and Creating IP Address Rules](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4726612641.html).
     
13.  You can specify that NetSuite automatically generate journal entries (copy and adjustment) when changes are made to a transaction that posted to the general ledger. To specify this behavior, check the **GL Impact Locking** box.
     
     When this box is checked, transactions that post to the general ledger display the GL Impact subtab. The GL Impact subtab records the general ledger impact of a transaction and any modifications.
     
     Note:
     
     This option is visible in implementations that are not OneWorld and only when GL Audit Numbering is enabled. For more information, see [GL Audit Numbering](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3735573963.html) and [GL Impact Locking](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4725785967.html).
     

The **Account ID** field shows your NetSuite account ID, which is required for support cases.

The **Data Center** field shows the data center where your account is.

Next, enter [Company Currency Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1104024406.html).
