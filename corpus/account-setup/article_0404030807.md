The Set Up Auto-Generated Numbers page enables youi to choose the record types that should be automatically numbered in the order in which they are created. The Administrator role is required to set up auto-genearted numbering. For information about field settings, see [Auto-Generated Numbering Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0404032958.html) and [Auto-Numbering for Document and Transaction Numbers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1124122336.html).

#### To set up auto-generated numbering:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > General Preferences_
    
2.  Check the **Show Transaction Numbering Setup** box and click **Save**.
    
3.  Go to _Setup > Company > Auto-Generated Numbers_.
    
4.  Customize your auto-generated numbering settings as required. Select **Within Parent Group** if you want to attach the subrecord number to the parent record number.
    
    For example, you may want customer records to always have the prefix CUST and at least 6 digits. Click the **Entities** subtab and check the **Enable** box next to the customer record type. In the **Prefix** column, enter **CUST**, and in the **Minimum Digits** column, enter **6**. The first number will be CUST000001.
    
    ![Entities subtab with the Customer and Subcustomer boxes checked.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/AccountAdministration/AccountSetup/AutoGenNumExample1.png)
    
    Or, you want assembly build document numbers to end with DN, have at least 5 digits, and let you enter your own name or number. Click the **Document Numbers** subtab and in the **Suffix** column, enter **DN** next to the document numbers record type. In the **Minimum Digits** column, enter **5**. The first number will be 00001DN. Check the **Allow Override** box to enter your own name or number on records.
    
    ![Entities subtab with the Customer and Subcustomer boxes checked.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/AccountAdministration/AccountSetup/AutoGenNumExample2.png)
5.  Click **Save**.
    

To reset auto-generated transaction numbers, go to the Set Up Auto-Generated Numbers page and click **Reset**.
