The following table summarizes the Customer Center and standard role for the center. The table also describes the features the role can access. Available features may vary based on what's enabled in your account.

| Standard Role for this Center | Description | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer Center | Lets customers access their own estimates, orders, invoices, and payments online. They can also see support information, such as the knowledge base and their own cases and issues. | 
-   Customer records can be for either a company or an individual.
-   To let customers access NetSuite, a user with the Administrator role must first enable the Customer Access feature.
