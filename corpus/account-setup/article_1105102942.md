The pages available and roles supported in the Accounting Center are listed in the following table.

Note:

The revenue roles and tab are available only when Advanced Revenue Management (essentials) is enabled.

| Standard Roles in this Center | Pages (Tabs) Displayed |
| --- | --- |
| A/P Clerk A/R Clerk Accountant Accountant (Reviewer) Bookkeeper CFO Payroll Manager Revenue Accountant Revenue Manager | Activities Customers Revenue Vendors Payroll and HR (Except for A/P Clerk role) Financial Reports Documents Setup Payments (Payroll Manager role only) Support SuiteApps |
