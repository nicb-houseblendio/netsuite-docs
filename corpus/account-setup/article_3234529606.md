If you are a NetSuite user with the Administrator role, you can view account-level AI Units usage on the Billing Information page. Go to _Setup > Company > View Billing Information_.

From the Billing Components tab, you can review the number of AI Units provisioned and the current used quantity.

For more information, see [AI Units FAQ](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_8144753226.html).
