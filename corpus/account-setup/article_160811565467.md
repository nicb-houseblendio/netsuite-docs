The following procedure describes how to copy an advanced numbering rule.

#### To copy an advanced numbering rule:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Auto-Generated Numbers (Administrator)_ and click the Document Numbers or Transaction Numbers subtab.
    
2.  Click the **Setup** link next to the transaction type with the rule set that includes the rule you want to copy.
    
3.  On the **Rules** subtab, click the rule you want to copy.
    
4.  In the Actions menu, select **Make Copy**.
    
5.  Enter a new name for the rule.
    
6.  Update the prefix, suffix, minimum digits, initial number, Sequences by Segment, and Criteria as needed.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    
8.  The Advanced Numbering Rules page displays a list of all rules in the rule set. Click **Edit**.
    
9.  Set the order of your rules in the rule set. Click a rule and click Move Up, Move Down, Move To Top, or Move to Bottom.
    
10.  Click **Save**.
