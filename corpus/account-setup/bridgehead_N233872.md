Many of the accounting features create system-generated accounts when enabled. The accounts created are included in the descriptions. For a complete list of system-generated accounts, see [Feature-Specific, System-Generated Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4078513386.html).

Administrators can enable the following features on the **Accounting** subtab of the Enable Features page at _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.

-   [Enable Accounting Basic Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1110092533.html)
    
-   [Enable Advanced Accounting Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1110092646.html)
    
-   [Enable Revenue Accounting Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1110092929.html)
    
-   [Enable Multi-Book Accounting Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4176305598.html)
