The Service Tier upgrade begins immediately after the sales order is billed.

Note:

Within three days after your Service Tier license is activated, all components are provisioned, and you can use your new service tier right away.

In most cases, the tier upgrade is complete after your new license is activated. After your new license is activated, you can use the increased capacity of your new tier. Sometimes, additional maintenance is needed. You will be alerted through an in-account notification at least 48 hours before the scheduled maintenance date, if required.
