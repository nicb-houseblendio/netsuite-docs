Enable the following features on the **Company** subtab of the Enable Features page at _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Departments | Track and classify your transactions by category. This adds a link to Departments on your Lists page. |
| Locations | Track activity by location. This adds a link to Locations on your Lists page. |
| Classes | Sort transactions in any way you find helpful. This adds a link to Classes on your Lists page. |
