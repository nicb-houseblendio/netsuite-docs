If your company needs to update the billing or shipping address used for NetSuite invoices, you can submit a change request. This ensures that invoices for your NetSuite subscription are sent to the correct address for timely processing and payment.

Note:

The change may take up to two business days to take effect in your account.

#### To submit a billing/shipping address change request:

1.  Log into your NetSuite account with the NetSuite 360 - Plus Financials role.
    
2.  Click the **Support** tab.
    
3.  Click the **NetSuite 360** link in the NetSuite 360 portlet.
    
4.  In the left panel in NetSuite 360, click the **Financial Dashboard** tab.
    
5.  On the Financial Dashboard page, in the **Quick Access** portlet, click the **Submit Billing/Shipping Address Change Request** link.
    
6.  In the popup window that opens, select the type of address you'd like to update-billing address, shipping address, or both.
    
7.  Fill out all applicable fields and then click **Submit**.
