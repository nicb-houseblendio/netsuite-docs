The Oracle Cloud Services Strategic Subcontractor List identifies the strategic subcontractors (including their subsidiaries) used by Oracle to support its provisioning of Oracle Cloud Services in the Public Cloud. Certain users in your NetSuite account can access the strategic subcontractor list. To ensure you are informed of changes, sign up to receive notifications when the strategic subcontractor list is updated.

#### To view the Oracle Cloud Services Strategic Subcontractors List:

1.  Log into your NetSuite account with a role that lets you access NetSuite 360.
    
2.  Click the **Support** tab.
    
3.  Click the **NetSuite 360** link in the NetSuite 360 portlet.
    
4.  In the left panel, hover over the **Privacy and Compliance** tab, and then select **Privacy Compliance Request**.
    
5.  Click the **Privacy Compliance Request** link.
    
6.  Click the **New Compliance Request** button.
    
7.  To sign up for update notifications, do the following:
    
    1.  On the New Compliance Request page, click the **Subscribe: Strategic Subcontractor Updates** button.
        
    2.  In the pop-up window that opens, click **Subscribe**. This action sends a confirmation email to your email address.
        
    3.  Open the email and click the confirmation link provided.
        
8.  Click the **View Strategic Subcontractors List** button. The Oracle Cloud Services Strategic Subcontractors List page opens.
    

Important:

Usually, only a few users in any company also have one of the two roles necessary to access NetSuite 360. The user logs in to NetSuite with their usual role. On the Support tab, a user with one of the two special roles can see the link in the NetSuite 360 portlet.

If you do not have access, discuss this with a person in your company who has permission to add a role to a user. Usually, this is a person with an Administrator or System Administrator role in NetSuite.

The person assigning the special role should exercise good judgment when adding one of these special roles to a user.
