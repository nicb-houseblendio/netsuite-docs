If a user requires access to financial reports, but should not be able to view personal employee information on these reports, an administrator can mask this information by adding the Hide Employee Information on Financial Reports permission to a role assigned to the user. This permission prevents a role from seeing employee information, such as the employee name on the following financial reports:

-   [Financial Statements Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2092547.html)
    
-   [Expense Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1515295.html)
    
-   [Other Current Liability Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1517969.html)
    
-   [Income Statement Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2094110.html)
    
-   [Balance Sheet Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2098310.html)
    
-   [General Ledger Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1520771.html)
    
-   [Trial Balance Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1520986.html)
    
-   [Viewing the Chart of Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1442506.html)
    
-   [Transaction Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N569351.html)
    
-   [Account Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1522428.html)
    

Important:

The Payroll feature must be enabled on your account to use the Hide Employee Information on Financial Reports permission.

#### To hide employee information on financial reports:

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_ and select a role from the list.
    
2.  On the **Permissions** tab, click the **Reports** subtab.
    
3.  In the **Permission** column, select **Hide Employee Information on Financial Reports**, and click **Add**.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Now users with roles that have this permission will not be able to see personal employee information about financial reports.
