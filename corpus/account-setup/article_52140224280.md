The Support Dashboard lets you capture key account information that is automatically available to Customer Support whenever you file a support case. You can see an overview of your NetSuite usage and how your account is set up. This helps the Support staff use this information in the context of the cases you open, so that they can investigate reported specific problems more effectively.

You can provide the following information, which personalizes Customer Support for you:

-   Business description
    
-   Account information/setup
    
-   Customizations/Integrations
    
-   Important dates
    

The Support Dashboard is accessible through NetSuite 360. For more information about NetSuite 360, see .

To access the NetSuite 360 Support Dashboard, click **View/Update Your Support Dashboard** in the **Quick Access** portlet on the NetSuite 360 Dashboard page.

#### To update your Support Dashboard:

1.  Log into your NetSuite account with a role that lets you access NetSuite 360.
    
2.  Click the **Support** tab.
    
3.  Click the **NetSuite 360** link in the NetSuite 360 portlet.
    
4.  On the NetSuite 360 Dashboard page, in the **Quick Access** portlet, click the **View/Update Your Support Dashboard** link. The Support Dashboard opens in a new tab in your browser.
    
5.  To update your information, on the Support Dashboard page, do one of the following:
    
    -   To update **Business Description**, **Account Information/Setup**, or **Emails to Copy on Case Communications**:
        
        1.  Click the **Edit** button.
            
        2.  Fill out the respective fields.
            
        3.  Click **Save**.
            
    
    -   To add a new integration/customized process:
        
        1.  Click the **New Integration/Customized Process** button.
            
        2.  Fill out the applicable fields.
            
        3.  Click **Save**. To view a list of your customizations/integrations, click the **Customizations/Integrations** tab.
            
    
    -   To add a new critical report:
        
        1.  Click the **New Critical Report** button.
            
        2.  In the **Name** field, enter a name of the critical report.
            
        3.  In the **Explanation of why report is critical**, describe why this report is critical.
            
        4.  From the **Run Frequency** list, select the run frequency of the report.
            
        5.  In the **Search/Report Id** field, enter the report id.
            
        6.  If the report is inactive, check the **Inactive** box.
            
        7.  Click **Save**. To view a list of your critical reports, click the **Critical Reports** tab.
            
    
    -   To add a new important date:
        
    
    1.  Click the **New Important Date** button.
        
    2.  In the **Name** field, enter the name.
        
    3.  In the **Description** field, describe the important date.
        
    4.  In the **From** and **To** fields, enter the important dates.
        
    5.  Click **Save**.
        
    6.  To view a list of your important dates, click the **Important Dates** tab.
