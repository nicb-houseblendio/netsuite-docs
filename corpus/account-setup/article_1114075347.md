If you're an Administrator, you can enable the following features on the **Web Presence** subtab of the Enable Features page at _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Customer Access | Give customers online access to their invoice and payment information. |
| Online Ordering | Give customers limited access to your account so they can place orders online. If you check this, you must also check Allow Customer Access. |
| Vendor Access | Give vendors online access to their transaction history and purchase orders. |
| Partner Access | Give partners online access to view reports, promotions codes and their partner records. |
| Advanced Partner Access | Assign an Advanced Partner Center role to partners that gives access to more records and transactions than the Standard Partner Center. |
