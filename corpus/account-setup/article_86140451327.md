Creating and managing compliance requests in NetSuite helps your organization ensure adherence to internal policies, regulatory requirements, and industry standards. You can create a compliance request from the Privacy and Compliance Dashboard in NetSuite 360.

#### To create a compliance request:

1.  Log into your NetSuite account with a role that lets you access NetSuite 360.
    
2.  Click the **Support** tab.
    
3.  Click the **NetSuite 360** link in the NetSuite 360 portlet.
    
4.  Click the **Privacy and Compliance** tab.
    
5.  Hover over the **Privacy Compliance Request** tab, and then select **Privacy Compliance Request**. This opens the Compliance Request List page.
    
6.  Click the **New Compliance Request** button.
    
7.  In the **Subject** field, enter the subject of your compliance request.
    
8.  From the **Compliance** list, select the type of your compliance request.
    
9.  From the **Product** list, select the NetSuite product related to your compliance request.
    
10.  In the **Email** field, enter the requester email address, typically your own email address.
     
11.  In the **Request Details** field, enter the details of your compliance request.
     
12.  Click **Save**. You can see a list of your compliance requests on the Compliance Request List page.
