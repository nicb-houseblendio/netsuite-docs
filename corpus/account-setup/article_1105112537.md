The following table summarizes the Vendor Center and standard role for the center. The table also describes the features the role can access. For more information, see [The Vendor Center Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2369118.html). Available features may vary based on what's enabled in your account.

| Standard Role for this Center | Description | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Advanced Partner Center | Lets vendors access their own transaction history and purchase orders online. | Vendor records can be for a company or an individual. |
