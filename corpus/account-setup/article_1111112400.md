Administrators can enable the following features on the **Items & Inventory** subtab of the Enable Features page at _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Inventory | Manage your inventory quantities and profits from buying and selling. Note: When you enable this feature, it creates system-generated Inventory Asset and Cost Of Goods Sold accounts. |
| Multi-Location Inventory | Track inventory across multiple locations. You'll also need to enable Locations, Inventory, Advanced Shipping, and Advanced Receiving. Note: After you enable Multi-Location Inventory and distribute items, you can't turn it off without contacting Customer Support. Note: When you enable this feature, it creates system-generated Transfer Orders and Inventory In Transit accounts. |
| Advanced Bill of Materials | Check this box to enable Advanced Bill of Materials. Note: Enabling this feature can change assemblies, item revisions, manufacturing routing, work orders, assembly builds, and unbuilds, which can affect related customizations and integrations. For more information, see [Advanced Bill of Materials](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1501506444.html). |
| Assembly Items | Build assembly items using raw materials (inventory items) in NetSuite. |
| Work Orders | Use assembly work orders to track the production of items for stock or to fill orders - they'll track quantities of needed assemblies and components. Note: When you enable this feature, it creates a system-generated Work Orders account. |
| Serialized Inventory | Assign serial numbers to each item to track inventory. Note: After you've enabled Serialized Inventory and created serialized items, you can't turn it off. |
| Lot Tracking | Use lot numbers to track groups of items. Note: After you've enabled Lot Tracking and created tracking items, you can't turn it off. |
| Bin Management | Create bin records to track inventory storage in your warehouse and on-hand quantities. Bins help warehouse staff quickly find items when picking and fulfilling orders, and show where to place items when receiving stock. |
| Advanced Bin/Numbered Inventory Management | Use bins with serial and lot numbered inventory for efficient management. |
| Inventory Status | Check this box to assign inventory statuses and manage your processes. You can also choose to make items unavailable for allocation based on their status. For more information, see [Inventory Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1515696627.html). |
| Advanced Inventory Management | Let NetSuite predict item demand and adjust ordering suggestions. New stock purchases are based on past purchases and sales. |
| Consigned Inventory Management | Track consigned inventory from receiving to shipping. |
| Warehouse Management | Use mobile devices to manage tasks from receiving to shipping. Use wave transactions to send orders to the warehouse, fulfill items, and track picking and staging as pickers process orders on their devices. Note: You'll need the SCM Mobile and Oracle NetSuite WMS SuiteApps to use Warehouse Management. See [Installing the NetSuite WMS SuiteApps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156399372911.html). For more information, see [NetSuite WMS Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_156382517509.html). |
| Landed Cost | Track extra costs like duty fees, insurance, and shipping when you buy items. |
| Supply Allocation | Use Supply Allocation to consider future and current inventory when making commitments. |
| Sales Channel Allocation | Use Sales Channel Allocation to track order-to-cash processes by sales channel. |
| Demand Planning | Predict future demand for items and use demand and supply plans to manage inventory replenishment. You'll need to enable Advanced Inventory Management to use this feature. For more details, read [Demand Planning](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2286970.html). |
| Distribution Resource Planning | Use Distribution Resource Planning to optimize inventory across locations and, for OneWorld accounts, subsidiaries. Transfer items between locations to meet supply needs within a preset distribution network. |
| Material Requirements Planning (includes Demand Planning) | Check the box to activate Supply Planning. With this feature, you can ensure materials and products are available for production and delivery to customers. You can also maintain the lowest possible inventory levels. |
| Available to Promise | Check item availability based on open transfers, purchase orders, work orders, and sales orders. For more information, read [Available to Promise](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2300269.html). |
| Standard Costing | Identify costing opportunities by tracking standard costs and analyzing variances. For more information, read [Standard Costing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2199708.html). |
| Group Average Costing | Group average costing lets you track one average cost for an item across multiple locations in a group. Group average costing is only available when you've enabled Multi-Location Inventory, and it works for both inventory and assembly items. |
| Manufacturing Work In Process | Track work-in-progress with work order issue and completion. For more information, read [Manufacturing Work In Process (WIP)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2335392.html). |
| Outsourced Manufacturing | Track outsourced manufacturing for components you own, and automate workflows for work orders, purchase orders, item receipts, and assembly builds. |
| Intercompany Cross-Subsidiary Fulfillment | In NetSuite OneWorld, check this box to use Intercompany Cross-Subsidiary Fulfillment for order fulfillment and returns across subsidiaries. For more information, see [Intercompany Cross-Subsidiary Fulfillment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1515621767.html). |
| Centralized Purchasing and Billing | Use Centralized Purchasing and Billing in your OneWorld account to manage orders and vendor returns across subsidiaries. |
| Inventory Count | This helps you track inventory better and control assets more tightly. With this feature, you can do regular inventory counts to stay accurate, reduce safety stock, and lower overhead costs. |
| Manufacturing Routing and Work Center | Record quantity completions, resource costs, and overheads for each work order operation. |
| Supply Chain Control Tower | Check this box to simulate inventory supply and demand across your supply chain. For more information, see [Supply Chain Control Tower](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519947103.html). |
