NetSuite Service Tiers are designed to meet your needs and give you the best experience at different levels of scale. Each service tier supports a maximum number of users, file storage, and monthly transaction lines created.

Note:

If you exceed one or more service tier limits, administrators will see a banner message and receive an email notification with further instructions.

If the limit you have exceeded is File Cabinet Storage, you will be granted a 90 day grace period for this limit only. You can use this time to either optimize your current storage, or discuss service tier upgrade options with your Account Manager.

The table below summarizes the service characteristics of the NetSuite Service Tiers.

| Service Tier | Users | File Storage (GB) | Monthly Transaction Lines1 | SuiteCloud Plus Licenses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Up to 100 users | Up to 100 GB | Up to 200K | Up to 1 |
| Premium | Up to 1,000 users | Up to 1,000 GB | Up to 2M | Up to 3 |
| Enterprise | Up to 2,000 users | Up to 2,000 GB | Up to 10M | Up to 6 |
| Ultimate | Up to 4,000 users | Up to 4,000 GB | Up to 50M | Up to 12 |

(1) Total transaction lines per month across all transaction types, averaged over the most recent six months.

NetSuite Service Tiers also set the base and maximum concurrency for platform web services transactions, and the number of processors available for scheduled scripts, through SuiteCloud Plus licenses.

For more information and numeric breakdown, see [SuiteCloud Plus Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N259503.html).
