On the Printing & Fax Preferences page, users with the Administrator role can set up printing and fax preferences. These preferences affect everyone in your NetSuite account.

Note:

Use the Web Site Setup page to set preferences for sending email from your website. For more information, see [Set Website Email Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2572962.html).

To set your printing and faxing preferences, go to _Setup > Company > Printing & Fax Preferences_. See the following topics for preference descriptions.

-   [General Printing Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163594333567.html)
    
-   [Transaction Printing Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_163594339857.html)
    
-   [Advanced PDF/HTML Printing Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_163594345448.html)
    
-   [Check Printing Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_163594347608.html)
    
-   [PDF Printing Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_163594349800.html)
    
-   [Message Printing Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_163594353950.html)
    
-   [Fax Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163594359424.html)
