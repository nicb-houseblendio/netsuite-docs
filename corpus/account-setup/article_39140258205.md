The Financial Dashboard provides a centralized location to view and pay your NetSuite invoices. Using the dashboard, you can check outstanding balances, review invoice details, and make secure payments.

#### To pay NetSuite invoices using the Financial Dashboard:

1.  Log into your NetSuite account with the NetSuite 360 - Plus Financials role.
    
2.  Click the **Support** tab.
    
3.  Click the **NetSuite 360** link in the NetSuite 360 portlet.
    
4.  In the left panel in NetSuite 360, click the **Financial Dashboard** tab.
    
5.  In the **NetSuite Invoices** portlet, check the box of the invoices you'd like you pay.
    
    Note:
    
    You can filter the invoices by their status, issuer, currency, date range, and due date range.
    
6.  Click the **Pay Invoice** button. The Payment page opens.
    
7.  (Optional) In the **Customer Memo** field, enter a memo.
    
8.  In the Enter Credit Card Information section, from the **Payment Option** list, select your preferred payment card.
    
9.  In the **CSS** field, enter your card security code.
    
10.  Click **Submit**.
