#### To enable the Advanced Numbering feature:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features (Administrator)_. In the Company subtab, scroll to ERP General and check the **Advanced Numbering** box.
    
2.  Click **Save**.
    

Next, enable Advanced Numbering for document or transaction numbers by supported transaction type:

-   To enable Advanced Numbering for document numbers, see [Enabling Advanced Numbering for Document Numbers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160811455145.html).
    
-   To enable Advanced Numbering for transaction numbers, see [Enabling Advanced Numbering for Transaction Numbers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160811468455.html).
