If you're no longer tracking information for a location, you can make the location inactive so it doesn't appear in location lists on records and transactions.

#### To inactivate a location: {#procedure_N265265}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Locations_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the location you want to inactivate.
    
3.  Check the **Location is Inactive** box.
    
    Note:
    
    If you inactivate a main location record, its child location records are inactivated automatically.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

To inactivate locations from the Locations list, go to _Setup > Company > Locations_, check the **Show Inactives** box, check one or more boxes in the **Inactive** column and then click **Submit**.
