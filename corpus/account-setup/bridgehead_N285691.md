You can use the role search to find a particular role or set of roles, or to get a list of roles and their details. To use this search, go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles > Search_. You can also use role fields as criteria filters and results for Employee searches. For more information, see [Use Searches to Audit Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N292328.html).

Note:

To see a list of all users assigned a particular role, go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Users_, and select the role from the Role dropdown list at the bottom of the Manage Users page.
