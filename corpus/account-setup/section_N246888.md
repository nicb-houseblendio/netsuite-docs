Users with the Administrator role, someone with setup permissions, or the system itself can set a company preference on the preference pages.

Some company preferences can be overridden by subsidiary (OneWorld only), role, and user preferences. You can stop individual overrides of company preferences on the Overriding Preferences subtab of the General Preferences page.

#### To stop individual overrides of company preferences: {#procedure_N246908}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > General Preferences_.
    
2.  On the **Overriding Preferences** subtab, clear the **Allow Override** box next to any preferences you don't want users to override.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

After you block an override, users can't set their own preferences. You can't block overrides of company preferences from subsidiary-level or role-level preferences.
