Important:

To use some of the employee and payroll features, you need SuitePeople HR or SuitePeople U.S. Payroll set up on your account. For more information, see [SuitePeople Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1495573671.html).

If you're an Administrator, you can enable the following features on the **Employees** subtab of the Enable Features page at _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Compensation Tracking | Track basic wage and salary information for employees directly from their records. |
| Job Management | Create jobs with job descriptions and assign them to employees. |
| Job Requisitions | Create job requisitions for recruiting, link them to jobs, and track their status through the hiring process. |
| Effective Dating | Make changes to an employee record with back-dated or future dates. View an employee's timeline to see key milestones and career changes. For details, see [Viewing an Employee's Timeline](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156216462597.html). |
| Time-Off Management | Define and manage time-off plans according to company policies and assign them to employees by location, department, class, or subsidiary. |
| Employee Change Requests | Manage change approvals for the employee record. Managers can create employee change requests for their direct reports and send them through approval in the Employee Center. |
| Termination Reason Tracking | Specify various termination details on the employee record. If Workforce Analytics is enabled, you can get a snapshot and synopsis of employees leaving your company and why. To see the Turnover Analysis page, go to Reports > Employees/HR. |
| Basic Government-Issued ID Tracking | Track various government-issued IDs for your employees. You can enable either Basic or Advanced Government-Issued ID Tracking, but not both. |
| Advanced Government-Issued ID Tracking | Create, edit, and delete custom types of government-issued IDs to match your company's employee identification policies. You can enable either Basic or Advanced Government-Issued ID Tracking features, but not both. |
| Workforce Analytics | Measure and analyze your company's headcount, hiring, and turnover trends. To open the Turnover Analysis page (Reports > Employees/HR), make sure the **Termination Reason Tracking** box is checked. |
| Employee Center Dashboard Publishing | Set up default dashboard portlets and layout for the Employee Center, including a manager dashboard, and publish them to users. Create news items for Employee Center dashboards, and limit who sees them by location or manager. |
