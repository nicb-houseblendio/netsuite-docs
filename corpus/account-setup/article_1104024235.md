After you enter third party settings for your company, enter the identification number settings. The field that you see depends on your country.

Enter third party settings. For more information, see [Company Third Party Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1104024505.html).

Enter the national business identification number for your company. The field name shown varies from country to country and is usually assigned by a national tax agency.

-   **Australia - ABN**: The Australian Business Number is required if your company needs to register for the goods and services tax (GST). If you don't have an ABN, go to the Australian Taxation Office website at [http://www.ato.gov.au](http://www.ato.gov.au).
    
-   **ACN (Australia only)**: Australian Company Number. For information, go to [http://www.asic.gov.au](http://www.asic.gov.au).
    
-   **Canada - Business Number**: Enter your 15-digit registration number for the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA). If you don't have a Business Number, go to the CRA website at [http://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency.html](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency.html).
    
-   **European Union (EU)**: If your account has the EU One Stop Shop feature enabled:
    
    -   **OSS Applies**: Check this box if the One Stop Shop (OSS) VAT scheme applies to this company.
        
    -   **OSS Nexus**: Select the EU member state (tax nexus) where you're registered for OSS VAT returns.
        
-   **U.K. - VAT Registration No.**: VAT registration number. If you don't have a VAT registration number, go to the HMR&C website at [http://www.hmrc.gov.uk](http://www.hmce.gov.uk).
    
-   **U.S. - Employer Identification Number**: Federal employer identification number (EIN). If you don't have an EIN, go to the IRS website at [http://www.irs.gov](http://www.irs.gov).
    
-   **U.S. - SSN or TIN (Social Security Number or Tax ID Number)**: If your business is a proprietorship or you don't have an EIN, enter your SSN or TIN.
    

Enter company address information. For more information, see [Enter Company Address Information](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156710111536.html).
