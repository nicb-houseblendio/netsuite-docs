Administrators can review details about notifications that all required recipients have confirmed. Go to _Setup > Company > Administrative Notifications_. On the Administrative Notifications page, click the **Confirmed** subtab.

-   **Notification Type**: Type of notification.
    
-   **Confirmed By**: Names of users who have confirmed that they read the notification.
    
-   **Date Confirmed**: Date and time when the users confirmed they read the notification.
    
-   **Message Text**: Text of the notification message.
    
-   **Date emailed**: Date when the notification was sent by email.
