After you enter electronic invoicing settings for your company, enter third party settings.

Note:

The fields described in this topic are available only when Third Party Billing is enabled on the Preferences subtab at _Setup > Accounting > Shipping_.

#### To enter company third party settings:

1.  Enter electronic invoicing settings for your company. For more information, see _Company Electronic Invoicing Settings_.
    
2.  In the **Ship to Attention** field, enter the name of the contact person for your company listed on your UPS account.
    
    Note:
    
    This field is required for UPS Integration.
    
3.  You can enter the UPS account number to use by default when Third Party Billing is selected on a UPS-integrated item fulfillment. Enter the account number in the **UPS 3rd Party Billing Account Number** field. This can be your company account number for all locations. You can override this account number on item fulfillments to enter a different 3rd party account.
    
    To set a default for customers when billing recipients, enter the customer's UPS or FedEx account number on their record.
    
4.  Youi can enter the FedEx account number to use by default when Bill Third Party is selected on FedEx-integrated item fulfillments. Enter the account number in the **FedEx 3rd Party Billing Account Number** field.
    
5.  In the **3rd Party Billing Zip** field, enter the zip code or postal code associated with the 3rd party accounts.
    
6.  In the **3rd Party Billing Country** field, select the country associated with the 3rd party account numbers.
    

Enter identification numbers for your company. For more information, see [Company Identification Number Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1104024235.html).
