NetSuite has standard SuiteFlow workflows in SuiteApps that you can use for approval routing. These workflows are ready to install and don't require you to create a custom SuiteFlow workflow. However, you can customize standard SuiteFlow workflows to better meet your needs.

You don't need to enable Approval Routing to use this workflow. To learn more, see [Enabling SuiteFlow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1489429767.html).

-   [Setting up the Blanket Purchase Order Approval Workflow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4236992073.html)
    
-   [Using SuiteFlow for Journal Entry Approvals](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4643662034.html)
    
-   [Setting up the Purchase Contract Approval Workflow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4210424087.html)
    
-   [Purchase Order Approval Workflow Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2398992.html)
