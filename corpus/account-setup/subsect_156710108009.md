Enter information about your company for this NetSuite account.

Important:

To set up a OneWorld account with multiple subsidiaries, NetSuite uses the Company Information to create your root (top-level) subsidiary. After you define your root subsidiary, you can create child subsidiaries at lower levels in your organization. For more information about configuring subsidiaries, see [Set up NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N268052.html).

#### To enter basic company information: {#procedure_N240929}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Company Information_.
    
2.  Enter your company information, referring to the following topics as needed.
    
3.  [Company General Information Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1104025021.html)
    
4.  [Company Currency Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1104024406.html)
    
5.  _Company Electronic Invoicing Settings_
    
6.  [Company Third Party Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1104024505.html)
    
7.  [Company Identification Number Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1104024235.html)
    
8.  [Enter Company Address Information](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156710111536.html).
