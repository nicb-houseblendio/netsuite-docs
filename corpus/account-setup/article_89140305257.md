The Financial Dashboard in NetSuite enables you to manage payment cards for paying NetSuite invoices. You can securely add new credit or debit cards for invoice payments.

#### To add a payment card using the Financial Dashboard:

1.  Log into your NetSuite account using the NetSuite 360 - Plus Financials role.
    
2.  Click the **Support** tab.
    
3.  Click the **NetSuite 360** link in the NetSuite 360 portlet.
    
4.  In the left panel in NetSuite 360, click the **Financial Dashboard** tab.
    
5.  On the Financial Dashboard page, click the **Add Payment Card** button.
    
6.  In the popup window that opens, fill out the **Cardholder Name**, **Expiration Date**, and **Card Number** fields.
    
7.  (Optional) To make this card your default card for paying NetSuite invoices, check the **Is Default** box.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
    

If you want to edit a payment card record, click **Edit** next to the applicable card. In the popup window that appears, you can set the card as inactive. To do this, ensure the **Is Default** box is cleared, check the **Is Inactive** box, and then click **Save**.
