If a user cannot see the Transactions subtab on customer or vendor records, an administrator can make this subtab visible by adding the Financial History permission to a role assigned to the user.

The Financial History permission gives a role access to the Transactions subtab. Additionally, the role must be given access to the specific types of transactions shown on the Transactions subtab.

For examples of where to find the Transactions subtab, see [Transactions Subtab on Entity Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N327037.html).

See the following procedures and topics:

-   [To create a custom role with access to the Transactions subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/procedure_N326851.html)
    
-   [To assign employees this custom role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/procedure_N326965.html)
