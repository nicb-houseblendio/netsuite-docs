The _Setup > Company > Rename Records/Transactions_ page includes the following sections:

-   [Rename Records/Transactions Page Body (Record Names)](#bridgehead_4622201521)
    
-   [Rename Records/Transactions Transaction Names Subtab](#bridgehead_4622202262)
    
-   [Rename Records/Transactions Account Type Names Subtab](#bridgehead_4622203109)
    

## Rename Records/Transactions Page Body (Record Names) {#bridgehead_4622201521}

In the main section of the Rename Records/Transactions page, you can change the names of standard NetSuite non-transactional record types.

![Rename Records/Transactions page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/AccountAdministration/AccountSetup/RenameRecTransBody.png)

## Rename Records/Transactions Transaction Names Subtab {#bridgehead_4622202262}

On the Transaction Names subtab of the Rename Records/Transactions page, you can change the names and abbreviations of standard NetSuite transaction types.

![Transaction Names subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/AccountAdministration/AccountSetup/RenameRecTransNames.png)

## Rename Records/Transactions Account Type Names Subtab {#bridgehead_4622203109}

On the Account Type Names subtab of the Rename Records/Transactions page, you can change the names of standard NetSuite account types.

![Account Type Names subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/AccountAdministration/AccountSetup/RenameRecAccountType.png)

You can move account type names up or down by selecting a line and clicking Move To Top or Move To Bottom. However, the order of the list on this subtab doesn't affect how users see account type lists.
