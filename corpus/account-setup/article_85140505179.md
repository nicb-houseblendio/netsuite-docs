Multi Language Support enables you to access the NetSuite Support Portal in one of the following languages:

-   French
    
-   German
    
-   Spanish
    
-   Portuguese
    
-   Japanese
    
-   Chinese
    

You can access the Multi Language Customer Support from the left panel in NetSuite 360. To learn about how to access NetSuite 360, see [Accessing NetSuite 360](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N337869.html). To create a new support case, click the **Cases** tab, and then click **New Case**. For more detailed instructions on how to use multi-language customer support in each supported language, refer to the following article [https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a\_id/74087/loc/en\_US](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/74087/loc/en_US).
