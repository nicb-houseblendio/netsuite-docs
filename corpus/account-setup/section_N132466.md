When you open NetSuite for the first time, you'll see a default set of tabbed pages called a center. The tabbed pages in the center are based on your default role.

The following topics list the NetSuite standard centers and the roles they support.

-   [Standard Accounting Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1105102942.html)
    
-   [Standard Classic Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1105102602.html)
    
-   [Standard E-Commerce Management Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1105095411.html)
    
-   [Standard Engineering Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1105095015.html)
    
-   [Standard Executive Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1105094838.html)
    
-   [Standard Marketing Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1105094316.html)
    
-   [Standard Project Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1105093753.html)
    
-   [Standard Sales Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1105093446.html)
    
-   [Standard Shipping Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1105093256.html)
    
-   [Standard Support Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1105092911.html)
    
-   [Standard System Administrator Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1105092150.html)
    

Also, see [Pages Common to all Centers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N133606.html) for more information about tabs that are included in all centers and are accessible for all roles.

Note:

If these centers don't meet your needs, you can add custom tabs and links. For more information, see [Creating Center Tabs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2890512.html) and [Creating Center Links](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2891250.html).

You can also create custom centers. For information, see [Custom Centers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2890160.html).
