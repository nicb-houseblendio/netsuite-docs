NetSuite has specialized centers that provide limited access to employees, customers, partners, and vendors based on their roles.

With a specialized center role, a user can access:

-   Limited features for their role
    
-   Data that pertains only to the person or to their company
    
-   Files related to transactions and cases that the person created, or that were shared with them
    
-   Settings for the person's NetSuite preferences, profile, email marketing campaign subscriptions, and authentication
    
-   Online help that shows only the topics specific to the center
    

Note:

Users with NetSuite specialized center roles can see only files on records they have access to, plus files with the Company-Wide Usage, or the Available Without Login boxes checked. For more information, see [Preferences on File Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0530052000.html).
