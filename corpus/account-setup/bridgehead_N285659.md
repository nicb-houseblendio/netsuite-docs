To see a list of roles available in your account, go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_ to open the Manage Roles page. This page shows the center associated with each role and whether the role is standard or custom. For information about center types, see [Centers Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N131898.html).

Click **Customize** to create a custom version of a standard role. Click **Edit** to make changes to a custom role.
