When you enable Advanced Numbering for a transaction type, NetSuite moves your existing numbering settings into a standard advanced numbering rule for that transaction type. When NetSuite moves your numbering settings:

-   The prefix, suffix, minimum digits, and initial number from your old settings go into the same fields in the standard rule.
    
-   The subsidiary or location is added to the prefix field in the standard rule.
    
-   The advanced numbering rule uses your existing document or transaction number prefixes for subsidiaries or locations. For more information, see [Creating Subsidiary Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N272471.html) and [Creating Locations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N263263.html).
