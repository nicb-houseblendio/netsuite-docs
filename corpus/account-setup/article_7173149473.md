You can use Prompt Studio to create new, custom Text Enhance actions and their prompts for your company's account. After you save new Text Enhance actions and prompts, NetSuite users will see them in the applicable Text Enhance menus.

Note:

You can access Prompt Studio if you're a NetSuite user with an Administrator role or with a customized role that has the Setup Prompts (ADMI\_PROMPTS) permission.
