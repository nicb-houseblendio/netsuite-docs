The following procedure describes how to let users enter website translation strings in a specific language.

#### To enable the entry of translation strings for a specific language: {#procedure_N247686}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > General Preferences_.
    
2.  Click the **Languages** subtab.
    
3.  Select a language you want to make available for translation.
    
    Note:
    
    Your 'base language' doesn't appear in the list. That is, the language determined by your country when your account was created is not available as a translation language. For example, if your base language is Spanish, then you won't see it in the list, but you can see Spanish (Latin America).
    
4.  Click **Add**.
    
5.  Repeat steps 3 and 4 for each language you want to add.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

Now you can go to _Lists > Mass Update > Bulk Update Translation_ to enter item translations for each language. For more information, see [Working With Multi-Language Names and Descriptions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2178279.html) and [Setting Up a Site for Multiple Languages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2463004.html).
