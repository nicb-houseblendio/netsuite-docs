You can see the URLs for all NetSuite services for this account on the Company URLs subtab. This information is not editable but is provided for your convenience. Developers and other users in your account may need the account-specific URLs for a certain services. The URLs are unique to each account, so your sandbox and Release Preview URLs are different from your production account URLs. Log in to a specific account to see its unique URLs.

For more information about the **Company URLs** subtab, see [URLs for Account-Specific Domains](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1498251763.html).

To view the URLs for services in this account:

Click the **Company URLs** subtab to view your company URLs for the following services:

-   Customer Center Login
    
-   NetSuite UI
    
-   SuiteTalk (SOAP and REST) web services
    
-   RESTlets
    
-   External Forms
    
-   SuiteAnalytics Connect
    

Share these URLs with users who don't have access to view the Company Information page.
