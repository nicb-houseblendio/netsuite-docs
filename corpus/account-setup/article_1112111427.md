To use the NetSuite 360 portlet, you must be assigned either the NetSuite 360 or the NetSuite 360 - Plus Financials role.

These roles don't appear in standard role searches (_Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles > Search_), and they're also not listed in the [Standard Roles Permissions Table](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N295396.html).However, you can see these roles in the dropdown list when assigning roles to a user.

Note:

Use good judgment when deciding which users should be assigned either of these roles. Only give access to users who need to perform specific tasks related to your NetSuite account.

#### To give an employee access to NetSuite 360: {#procedure_21135550073}

1.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Employees_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the employee you want to give access.
    
3.  On the **Access** subtab, in the **Role** field, select **NetSuite 360** or **NetSuite 360 - Plus Financials**.
    
    Important:
    
    Carefully review the permissions given to each role and decide which role is the most appropriate for the user. Only assign the NetSuite 360 - Plus Financials role to users who require financial access.
    
4.  Click **Add**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

After assigning the role, the employee may need to log out and log back in to see the NetSuite 360 link.

For more information, see [Accessing NetSuite 360](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N337869.html).
