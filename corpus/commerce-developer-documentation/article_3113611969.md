From March 11, 2025, the behavior for how a custom record is checked for its existence will be updated. After this update, instead of performing a create record (CreateRecord) to check for the existence of a custom record, a search (lookupfield) is performed. This change improves performance and ensures that your web store continues to function as expected.

In some implementations of SuiteCommerce Advanced (SCA) and Site Builder, you must apply a patch to ensure the correct custom record behavior is implemented for your website.

If you have one of the following implementations, you need to apply a patch before March 11, 2025 to avoid any loss of website functionality.

-   SCA, SiteBuilder Extensions, or SiteBuilder Extensions Premium - Vinson, Mont Blanc, or Denali
    
-   SCA Reference My Account - 1.04 to 1.06
    
-   SCA Reference ShopFlow - 1.04 to 1.07
    

Be sure to follow the corresponding patch instructions for the version of SCA you have implemented.

-   [SCA and Site Builder Vinson, Mont Blanc, and Denali](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0212115207.html)
    
-   [SCA Reference My Account 1.04 - 1.06 and ShopFlow 1.04 - 1.07](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0212115248.html)
