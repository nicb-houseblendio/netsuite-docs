Scriptable Cart is an advanced feature that lets you customize your Commerce website's checkout flow. You should only use Scriptable Cart when it is not possible to achieve your customization through other means. Although originally created as a Site Builder feature, you can use Scriptable Cart to customize SuiteCommerce and SuiteCommerce Advanced (SCA) websites.

The topics in this section describe how to get started with Scriptable Cart for your SuiteCommerce or SCA website customizations and then these topics lead you through several detailed examples to help you better understand Scriptable Cart's capabilities.

Note:

For more information, see [Scriptable Cart](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2545000.html).
