You must assign the Administrator or a custom role with the correct permissions to the Employee record for each user who requires developer access to NetSuite.

You can assign the necessary role in **Role** field of the **Employee** record (**Access** tab, **Role** subtab). For detailed steps, see [Assigning Roles to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N897798.html).
