The Elbrus release of SuiteCommerce Advanced and later support the latest Mastercard 2-Series BIN range by default. To provide this capability for implementations prior to the Elbrus release, you need to perform the updates described in the section corresponding to your implementation:

-   [Denali - Mastercard Regex Patch](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1505316322.html)
    
-   [Mont Blanc - Mastercard Regex Patch](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1505242840.html)
    
-   [Vinson - Mastercard Regex Patch](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1505242733.html)
