## addFreeGiftItem(item) {#bridgehead_1510766076}

Adds the eligible item to the cart and marks it as a free item.

## Parameters {#bridgehead_1510766082}

-   `item` \[required\] {Object with values for fields}
    
    -   `item_id` \[required\] {Number}
        
    -   `promotion_id` \[required\] {Number}
        
    -   `quantity` \[required\] {Number or String}
        
    -   `options` \[required for item with item options\]
        

**Note**: _quantity_ can be a negative number.

## Returns {#bridgehead_1510766088}

String - the `orderitemid` of added item.

## Supported Domains {#bridgehead_1510766095}

Checkout, Shopping

## Login Required? {#bridgehead_1510766100}

No

## addItem(item) {#bridgehead_4482707689}

Adds an item to order.

Note:

If your selected Out Of Stock Behavior option is **Disallow back orders but display out-of-stock message** or **Remove out-of-stock items from store**, you might still be able to add an out of stock item to your cart using the `addItem(item)` order method. However, when you try to place the order with such out of stock item in your shopping cart, an error message is displayed and you will not be able to place the order successfully.

## Parameters {#bridgehead_3995046008}

-   `item` \[required\] {Object with values for fields}
    
    -   `internalid` \[required\]
        
    -   `quantity` \[required\] {Number or String}
        
    -   `options` \[required for item with item options\]
        
    -   `fulfillmentPreferences` \[optional\] {fulfillmentPreferences object}
        

**Note**: _quantity_ can be a negative number.

## Returns {#bridgehead_4479562101}

String - the `orderitemid` of added item.

## Supported Domains {#bridgehead_4479562218}

Checkout, Shopping

## Login Required? {#bridgehead_4479562328}

No

**Back to** [Order Methods](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2506263.html) | **Back to** [Shopping Objects](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2496577.html)

## addItems(items) {#bridgehead_3995047899}

Adds multiple items to cart/order within a single request.

Note:

If your selected Out Of Stock Behavior option is **Disallow back orders but display out-of-stock message** or **Remove out-of-stock items from store**, you might still be able to add out of stock items to your cart using the `addItems(items)` order method. However, when you try to place the order with such out of stock items in your shopping cart, an error message is displayed and you will not be able to place the order successfully.

## Parameters {#bridgehead_3995047898}

-   `items` \[required\]{Array of objects with values for fields}
    
    -   `internalid` \[required\]
        
    -   `quantity` \[required\] {Number or String}
        
    -   `options` \[required for item with item options\]
        
    -   `fulfillmentPreferences` \[optional\] {fulfillmentPreferences object}
        

## Returns {#bridgehead_4479562437}

String - the `orderitemid` of added items.

## Supported Domains {#bridgehead_4479562542}

Checkout, Shopping
