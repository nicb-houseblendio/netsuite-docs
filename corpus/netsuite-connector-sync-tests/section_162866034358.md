This topic covers manual testing procedures for the following:

-   Order sync
    
-   Fulfillment sync
    
-   Price and quantity sync
    
-   Full product sync
    
-   Matrix items
    

## Testing Order Sync {#bridgehead_162866079111}

First, retrieve the order into NetSuite Connector, then send it to NetSuite.

For information about retrieving an order into NetSuite Connector, read [Importing Orders from the Storefront in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162987170038.html).

#### To send an order to NetSuite:

1.  Go to Data Flows > Orders.
    
2.  Click the **Orders** tab.
    
3.  Find the order you want to send to NetSuite in the list.
    
    The order should be blue and show a status of either **In FarApp (Automatic Sync Disabled)** or **Order Pending Posting to NetSuite**.
    
4.  Click the pencil icon on the order line.
    
5.  (Optional) Click **Show Order Mapping** to preview what will be sent to NetSuite.
    
6.  Click **Post Order to NetSuite**.
    
    The status of the order changes to **Posting in Progress**.
    
    After the order is posted to NetSuite, the order status is updated on the dashboard. If the order is successfully sent as cash sales, the dashboard shows the status as **Complete**. If the order is successfully sent to sales orders, the dashboard shows the status of **Order Posted Waiting For Shipment**. If there's an error sending the order, you'll see an error message.
    

## Testing Fulfillment Sync {#bridgehead_162866184273}

This procedure assumes you've already sent an order to NetSuite through NetSuite Connector and fulfilled it in NetSuite. After fulfilling the order in NetSuite, follow these steps to manually retrieve the fulfillment information and send it to the marketplace or cart.

#### To manually retrieve fulfillment information:

1.  Go to Data Flows > Orders.
    
2.  Find the order you want to send to NetSuite in the list.
    
    The order should show a status of **Order Posted Waiting For Shipment**.
    
3.  Click the pencil icon on the order line and select **Upload Shipment from NetSuite**.
    
    NetSuite Connector retrieves the fulfillment information from NetSuite and sends the information to marketplace or cart.
    
    If the sync is successful, the status or the order changes to **Complete**. If there's an error, you'll see it on the order line.
    

## Testing Price and Quantity Sync {#bridgehead_162866239597}

To finish the price and quantity sync test, ensure your mappings are set up correctly.

For more information, read the following topics:

-   The required Category field - [Mapping NetSuite Connector Test Products](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163116656894.html)
    
-   The price and quantity fields - [Mapping Inventory and Price for NetSuite Connector Test Products](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163116800977.html)
    

To test an item, you must have the **Flag** field set to **Add/Update Item** in NetSuite.

## Pulling the Product into NetSuite Connector {#bridgehead_162866297344}

To test the price and quantity sync, you must first pull the product into NetSuite Connector.

#### To pull the product into NetSuite Connector:

1.  Go to Data Flows > Products.
    
    The Products dashboard opens.
    
2.  Click **Retrieve**.
    
    The Retrieve Product into FarApp window opens.
    
3.  In the **Product SKU or Internal ID** field, enter the SKU or internal ID of the product.
    
4.  Click **Retrieve**.
    
    The product is added to the list of products in your Products dashboard.
    

## Sending the Product to Marketplace or Cart {#bridgehead_162866304624}

After the product is pulled into NetSuite Connector, send the product to the marketplace or cart.

#### To send the product to marketplace or cart:

1.  Go to Data Flows > Products.
    
    The Products dashboard opens.
    
2.  Find the product you want to send to the marketplace or cart in the list.
    
    The product should be blue and show a status of either **In FarApp (Automatic Sync Disabled)** or **Product Updates Scheduled to Post to X**. Where **X** is the marketplace or cart connector name that you are testing.
    
3.  (Optional) To preview the data that NetSuite Connector will sync, read [Viewing Product Mapping in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162859790663.html).
    
4.  Click the pencil icon on the product line.
    
5.  Click **Update Inventory and Price on X** . Where, X is the name of the marketplace or cart connector that you are testing.
    
    The status of the product changes to **Posting in Progress**.
    
    After the product is posted, the product status is updated on the Products dashboard. If the posting is successful, the status changes to **Product Synced to X**. If there's an error posting the product, you'll see an error message.
    

## Testing Full Product Sync {#subsect_163721820757}

To test full product sync, ensure you have a category mapping and at least one other field set up.

For more information, see the following topics:

-   The required Category field - [Mapping NetSuite Connector Test Products](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163116656894.html)
    
-   The price and quantity fields - [Mapping Inventory and Price for NetSuite Connector Test Products](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163116800977.html)
    
-   Other mappings - [Mapping Required and Optional Fields for Product Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164500136779.html)
    

To test an item, you must have the **Flag** field set to **Add/Update Item** in NetSuite.

To test product sync, pull the product into NetSuite Connector, then send it to your marketplace or cart. For more information, read [Pulling the Product into NetSuite Connector](#bridgehead_162866297344).

After the product is pulled into NetSuite Connector, send the product to the marketplace or cart. For more information, read [Sending the Product to Marketplace or Cart](#bridgehead_162866304624).

### Testing Matrix Items {#subsect_163721826059}

Testing matrix items is similar to testing standalone items, with a few extra steps to ensure correct posting for family items.

Note:

These steps don't apply to price and quantity sync.

1.  Set the **Flag** field correctly for both parent and child items. To post both parent and child items, set the **Flag** field to **Add/Update Item** for both. To post child items individually, set the parent item to **Post Children as Standalone** and the child item to **Add/Update Item**.
    
2.  Manually pull the parent item of all child items into NetSuite Connector.
    
3.  Post only the parent item to your marketplace or cart. The child items will be posted automatically with the parent item.
