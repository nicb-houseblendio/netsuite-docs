Price and inventory fields are often the most mapped across all connectors, because most users manage their pricing and inventory in NetSuite.

## Mapping Inventory Fields {#bridgehead_163116835966}

To map inventory fields, see [Mapping Inventories in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162989752594.html). After inventory mapping, proceed with price mapping.
