Before using syncs in your live production environment, you should test the syncs with test or sample orders first.

Follow these guidelines when providing a test order or product to your NetSuite Connector integration specialist:

-   Carefully select the order or product for testing because there is a chance that something may not sync correctly. Do not select your top-selling SKU product for product sync testing. There is a chance that the price, quantity, or some other important data does not sync correctly.
    
    You shouldn't provide a test order containing items that are out of stock in NetSuite, because such orders won't be fulfilled in NetSuite.
    
-   Choose an order or product that represents your data well. For instance, if most of your SKUs are matrix items, test with a matrix item in NetSuite.
    
    Make sure your test order has data in the fields you want NetSuite Connector to sync. For example, assume you want the Memo field to sync over from the marketplace or cart orders. Make sure your test order contains data in the field you map to the Memo field in NetSuite.
