Amazon settlement sync pulls reports from Amazon Seller Central. TThe settlement sync imports fees, credits, refunds, and other transactions from Amazon Seller Central into NetSuite.

## Setting Up Settlement Sync {#bridgehead_163089754238}

Before you start, make sure you've read and understood the steps in the topic [Importing Amazon Settlement Reports in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162884600373.html).

Choose a customer in NetSuite to associate with non-order transactions, like FBA Warehouse fees. Note the customer's internal ID.

Tip:

Create a dedicated customer for settlement sync. TYou can then find all non-order cash sales and refunds by looking at that customer's transactions.

#### To set up settlement sync:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select Amazon connector and the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Settings > Other Transactions.
    
4.  Click the **Settlement** tab.
    
5.  In the **NetSuite SKU Field**, enter the field ID of your SKU field.
    
    For information about field ID, read [NetSuite SKU and Storefront SKU in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162917913231.html).
    
6.  In the **Customer Internal ID** field, enter the internal ID of the customer that you want to associate with non-order related fees and credits.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
