Before setting up product sync, make sure you've prepared your NetSuite product sync. For more information, read [Setting Up NetSuite Connector Product Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162756652235.html).

The mapping category tells NetSuite Connector which fields to use for your products. You'll need to add and set a category mapping for most marketplaces and carts. The category mapping you create should depend on your use of either Full Product or Price and Quantity sync.

When choosing a product sync type, keep in mind that you can only use one sync per connector or account combination. You can't use both Price and Quantity sync and Full Product sync on the same connector or account combination. But you can use different sync types across different accounts and connectors. For instance, if you have an Amazon connector with two accounts, you can choose different product sync types for each account. You can also use the same one if you prefer.

To decide which sync type you should be using, refer to [Price/Quantity Sync vs. Full Product Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162971705699.html).

After deciding on the sync you want to set up, click the link appropriate for your company and follow the procedure.

-   For carts (Shopify, Magento, etc.), see [Mapping Cart Categories in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162989341143.html).
    
-   For Walmart and NewEgg, contact NetSuite Customer Support for assistance.
    
-   For eBay, no category mapping is required. Your products will be categorized automatically based on whether they have variations.
    

## Mapping Inventory and Price for NetSuite Connector Test Products {#bridgehead_164914291907}

Price and inventory fields are often the most mapped across all connectors, because most users manage their pricing and inventory in NetSuite.
