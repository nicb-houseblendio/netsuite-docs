Narrative Insights uses generative artificial intelligence (AI) to provide concise summaries of supported reports and records. The feature helps you quickly identify the most relevant information and may surface trends, anomalies, risks, opportunities, or data gaps.

The Narrative Insights dialog displays a summary based on the content you're viewing. The structure, sections, and level of detail may vary depending on the report or record type and the available data. Insights may emphasize different aspects of the data or note when there is insufficient information to generate an insight.

The content may differ each time you generate an insight, even when viewing the same data. If there isn't enough data to generate an insight for the report or record, you may receive an error message.

Narrative Insights is available in all NetSuite regions and system-supported languages. For more information, see [Generative AI Feature Availability in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_3132707887.html).

The **Enable Narrative Insights** preference on the AI Preferences page controls whether you can generate insights. This preference is enabled by default. Users with an Administrator role can disable this preference at any time. For more information about Narrative Insights preferences, see [Managing Narrative Insights Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6150024470.html).

#### To generate an insight:

1.  Open a supported report or record.
    
2.  Click the **Generate Insight** button. The Narrative Insights dialog is displayed.
    

You can generate the insight again at any time by clicking the **Generate Insight** button.

Note:

The Narrative Insights dialog displays a disclaimer. The message is as follows: "This content is generated by AI which can make mistakes, so check output before use. It should not be considered professional advice."

Warning:

The content in the Narrative Insights dialog is generated using AI. The information provided may not be completely free of errors or fully accurate. You should verify the summary by reviewing the underlying reports and records. For questions about security, access permissions, or related topics, contact NetSuite Customer Support. Oracle NetSuite does not assume responsibility or liability for the use or interpretation of content in the summary.

## Tips and Troubleshooting {#subsect_0424023944}

You may receive an error if:

-   The report or record contains a large amount of data
    
-   The generation process takes too long to complete
    

If this happens, try the following:

-   Shorten the time span on reports
    
-   Apply filters on reports (for example, by customer or subsidiary)
    
-   Reduce the amount of data on records (for example, fewer lines)
