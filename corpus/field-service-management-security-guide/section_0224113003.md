The `inject` property within `routes/mobile` or `routes/scheduler` allows you to add HTML directly into the page of the FSM Mobile and Scheduler applications. This is commonly used to introduce styles to support edge cases for more complex customer requirements, but it can also be used to inject scripts. Do not use this property without clear guidance from the FSM product team. It will soon be removed from the product
