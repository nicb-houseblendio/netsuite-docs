Through your login page URL, you can access the SCM Mobile Login page. You can log in using your NetSuite account credentials or through the single sign-on option. To reset your password, you can click the **Forgot your password?** link.

For more information about the login URL or signing in, see [SCM Mobile App Access](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161497091941.html). To enable requirements to log into and access SCM Mobile apps, see [SCM Mobile SuiteApp Installation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161495429711.html#subsect_161495552374) and [Setting Up the Online Form User Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0814113835.html).

![SCM Mobile Login](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/WarehouseManagement/SCMMobile/LoginPage.png)

After you log in, the apps and processes that you can access may depend on your role. Some settings may also affect your access to a mobile app capability. See [Configuring SCM Mobile App Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161496866735.html).
