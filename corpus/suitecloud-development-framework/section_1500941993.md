Resolve the following conflicts to disable the `<feature_name>` feature: `<conflict(s)>`

## Cause: The feature is dependent on the state of more than one feature with a conflicting state {#bridgehead_1500944353}

-   **Solution**
    
    Adjust the states of the features referenced in the error message.
