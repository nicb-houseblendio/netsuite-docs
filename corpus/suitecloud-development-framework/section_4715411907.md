Important:

You cannot deploy SDF custom objects from account customization projects if the objects are locked in the target NetSuite account. You also cannot use account customization records to add subrecords to locked record types. For more information about locked record types, see [Locking SDF Custom Objects in the SuiteApp Type of SuiteCloud Project](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1515949845.html) and [Locking Objects in Customization Bundles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3376982.html).
