For information about adding translations in SDF custom objects, see [Translatable Fields on SDF Custom Objects](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156951732735.html) and [Translation Collections as XML Definitions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1552071708.html).
