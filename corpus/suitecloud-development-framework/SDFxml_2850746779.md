For information about developing mapreducescript SDF custom objects, see [Map/Reduce Script Objects as XML Definitions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157616847317.html).

The recurrence field group is a SELECTION.

SELECTION field group types require you to choose exactly one non-mandatory field or exactly one non-mandatory field group event. In addition to this selection, all mandatory fields and field group events must be included in your XML. Choose one of the non-mandatory field group events from the following list:

-   [daily](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/SDFxml_617656060.html)
    
-   [everyweekday](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/SDFxml_263541958.html)
    
-   [monthly](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/SDFxml_3184833990.html)
