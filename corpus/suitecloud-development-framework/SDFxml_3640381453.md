This topic is part of the workflow SDF custom object documentation.

For information about developing workflow SDF custom objects, see [Workflows as XML Definitions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1500992003.html).

The recurrence field group is a SELECTION.

SELECTION field group types require you to choose exactly one non-mandatory field or exactly one non-mandatory field group event. In addition to this selection, all mandatory fields and field group events must be included in your XML. Choose one of the non-mandatory field group events from the following list:

-   [daily](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/SDFxml_2329558672.html)
    
-   [every30minutes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/SDFxml_247768421.html)
    
-   [everyweekday](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/SDFxml_3116438147.html)
    
-   [monthly](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/SDFxml_2224270371.html)
