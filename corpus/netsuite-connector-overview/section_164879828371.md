The NetSuite Connector push item update scripts help NetSuite Connector find records that affect inventory. With this information, NetSuite Connector updates the relevant records to keep your NetSuite inventory accurate. If you have product sync or price/qty sync enabled, these scripts ensure your inventory is synced correctly to your marketplace or cart.

You can disable the scripts if you don't want to use them. For more information about disabling the scripts, read [Undeploying Scripts Installed by NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162911892289.html).
