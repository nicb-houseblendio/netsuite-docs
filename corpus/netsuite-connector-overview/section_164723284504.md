NetSuite Connector connects NetSuite to various carts, marketplaces, third-party logistics (3PLs), and shipping software. NetSuite Connector uses specific connectors to connect to ecommerce platforms. Each connector has several syncs that facilitate the import and export of transaction and item-related data between NetSuite and the ecommerce, logistics, and point-of-sales providers.

NetSuite Connector pulls the data from one end and pushes the data to the other. The data flow depends on the availability and integrity of the data in both the storefront and NetSuite. The transfer of data is facilitated by syncs that pull data into NetSuite Connector and pushes the data to the destination at defined intervals. This ensures that the data between the storefront and NetSuite are synchronized. After a successful sync, the data can be verified in the storefront, NetSuite Connector, and NetSuite. Understanding the data flows helps with troubleshooting when there's missing or incorrect data.
