Order Sync is the process of moving orders from your storefront to NetSuite Connector and then to NetSuite.

The diagram below shows how orders and fulfillments are synced.

<a id="svg_1"></a>

                                                                                                                                                                 

Note:

Orders sync to NetSuite, and fulfillments sync from NetSuite.
