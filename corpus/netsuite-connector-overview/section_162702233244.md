NetSuite Connector handles gift card data similarly to credit card data. Like credit card, the marketplace or cart does not allow NetSuite Connector to access the gift card information. Therefore, NetSuite Connector cannot sync the remaining balance of a gift card back to NetSuite.

For more information, read [Configuring Gift Cards for NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164967821723.html).
