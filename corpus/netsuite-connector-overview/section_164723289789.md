The following storefronts and third-party logistics (3PLs) providers are supported in NetSuite Connector:

-   Amazon Seller Central
    
-   Amazon Vendor Central
    
-   BigCommerce
    
-   eBay
    
-   Magento 2
    
-   Shipstation
    
-   Shopify
    
-   Shopify POS
    
-   Walmart
    
-   WooCommerce
