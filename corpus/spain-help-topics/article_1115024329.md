Note:

To access the Spain Localization SuiteApp documentation in Spanish of Spain (español de España), see [Spain Localization](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=SpainLocalization.pdf).

After you generate your SII report and submit it to AEAT, you're provided with a downloadable XML file as a response from AEAT. The Spain Localization SuiteApp provides you with a platform where you can upload this response and let the system interpret it for you.

After the upload, the reporting status of transactions in the report is visible in the SII Reporting Submission Status field on the SII Reporting subtab. For more information about fields on this subtab, see [SII - Transaction Record Information](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1114102907.html).
