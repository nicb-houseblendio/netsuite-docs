Setting the tax registration information for your customer/vendor is required for the generation of all Spain VAT reports provided by the Spain Localization SuiteApp. For instructions on setting this information, see [Assigning Tax Registrations to an Entity in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4513820587.html).

Note:

Entering and sourcing tax registrations for customers/vendors follow the same logic as in the case of the subsidiary. For more information, see [Setting the Tax Registration Number for Your Spanish Subsidiary](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_72132104883.html).
