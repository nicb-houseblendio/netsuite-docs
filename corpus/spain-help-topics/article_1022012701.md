This article documents the manual submission of the generated SII reports to AEAT provided by the Spain Localization SuiteApp.

Note:

If you want to set up an automated schedule of SII report submissions, see [Automated Submission of SII Reports to AEAT](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1022012821.html).

Before attempting to submit your SII reports to AEAT, make sure you've followed steps **1** and **2** described in the [Prerequisites for Submitting SII Reports to AEAT](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1022012552.html).

#### To manually submit your SII reports to AEAT:

1.  For roles that use the accounting center and classic center, go to Reports > Spain Reports > Country Tax Reports.
    
    For roles that use other types of centers, go to Reports > Tax > Country Tax Reports.
    
2.  Click **View** next to your generated SII report to access the **Country Tax Report - Spain** page.
    
    Before you submit your report, you can review the following details on this page:
    
    -   Report name
        
    -   Reporting period
        
    -   Subsidiary
        
    -   VAT Number
        
    -   Digital certificate
        
    -   Secure Verification Code (CSV)
        
    -   List of transactions included in the report
        
3.  Click **Submit to AEAT**.
    
4.  Confirm your legal declaration in the Report Submission Status Confirmation window and click **Submit**.
    
    Note:
    
    After successfully submitting your SII report to AEAT, verify the submission details in an email notification or on the SII - AEAT Response page. The submission date will be added to the information displayed on the Country Tax Report - Spain page.
    
    If your submission is unsuccessful, you'll also be informed about the reasons for the failure. For more information, see [Resolving Errors in Submitting SII Reports to AEAT](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1022012914.html).
