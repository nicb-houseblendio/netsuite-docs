The Approval Status field on the transaction record determines the transaction's GL Impact and its inclusion in the Spanish tax reports. See the table below to find out how Approval Status field, GL Impact, and inclusion in Spanish reports are interconnected.

| Approval Status | GL Impact | Inclusion in Spanish tax reports (SII, Modelo 303, Modelo 390, Modelo 347, Modelo 349) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approved | **Yes** in the Posting column on the GL Impact subtab. | Yes |
| Pending Approval | **No** in the Posting column on the GL Impact subtab. | No |
| Rejected | **No** in the Posting column on the GL Impact subtab. | No |
