Items are the goods and services you sell to customers, plus the parts and raw materials you buy from vendors. Your transactions with item records are only included in the generated reports if you select a value in the Tax Item Type field. You must mark whether your items are **Goods** or **Services**.

#### To set the tax item type: {#procedure_0116100700}

1.  Go to Lists > Accounting > Items.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the item you want to update.
    
3.  Click the **Tax** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Tax Item Type** field, select a tax item type from the list.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

Even though journal entry transactions aren't linked to items, their tax lines use the same logic in the Tax Item Type field. If you leave the field on journal entry lines blank, such tax lines won't be included in the Modelo 303 and Modelo 390 reports.

The tax item type assignment doesn't affect posting and taxable transactions that aren't linked with items, like expense reports.

For more information, see [Tax Item Types in the SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557735556.html).
