To successfully generate the SII reports in the Spain Localization SuiteApp, you need to set a tax registration number and nexus for your customers/vendors.

#### To set the tax registration number and nexus for your customer/vendor:

1.  Go to Lists > Relationships > Customers/Vendors.
    
2.  From the list, click on the customer/vendor, for which you want to set the tax registration number and nexus.
    
3.  On the Customer/Vendor page, click **Edit**.
    
4.  Click on the **Financial** subtab.
    
5.  On the **Tax Registrations** subtab, click on the blank space under the **Tax Reg. Number** table column header. Clicking activates the editing mode of the table and enables you to enter the number.
    
6.  Fill in a valid tax registration number for your customer/vendor.
    
7.  On the **Tax Registrations** subtab, click on the blank space under the **Nexus** table column header. Clicking activates the editing mode of the table and enables you to enter the nexus.
    
8.  In the menu, select a nexus for your customer/vendor.
    
    Note:
    
    The menu options correspond to existing nexuses that you set in your account. If you don't see your preferred option, you'll need to set up a new nexus. For more information, see [Setting Up Nexuses in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160639612283.html).
    
9.  Click **OK**.
    
10.  Click **Save**.
