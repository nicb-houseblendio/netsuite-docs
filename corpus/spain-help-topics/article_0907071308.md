Note:

To access the Spain Localization SuiteApp documentation in Spanish of Spain (español de España), see [Spain Localization](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=SpainLocalization.pdf).

Known limitations of the Spain VAT report files provided by the Spain Localization SuiteApp include the following:

-   Limitations in the scope and number of fields that the Spain Localization SuiteApp offers for Modelo 303 and Modelo 390 file generation. Currently, the SuiteApp only offers a limited version of the General Regime (Régimen General) section of these reports. Other sections of the reports aren't provided.
    
    For more information about which fields of the reports are covered by the Spain Localization SuiteApp report generation, see [Modelo 303 - What Goes into Each Box](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0831031501.html) and [Modelo 390 - What Goes into Each Box](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0403101051.html).
    
-   Limitations of the Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp, which is responsible for tax report generation, include the following:
    
    Some of the field labels on **Detailed Report** and **Country Tax Report** pages fail to reflect your language preferences.
    
    For more information about limitations of Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp, see [Known Limitations of Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156706175950.html).
    
-   To ensure accurate sourcing and reporting of the Spain reports, you can only use the predefined SuiteTax tax codes for Spain. This means that you can't customize new tax codes or make updates to the existing ones. To see the full list of available Spain tax codes, see [Spain Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0916013503.html).
    
    Custom tax codes require customization of the relevant reports for proper inclusion. For more information, see [Customization of Spain VAT Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0511122611.html).
    
    The Spain Localization SuiteApp also doesn't support the partially deductible and non-deductible taxes in SuiteTax. For more information, see [Setting Up the SuiteTax Engine for VAT Countries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557735706.html).
    
-   **Inclusion of Transactions in SII Reports:**
    
    To ensure transactions are properly included in SII reports, all relevant transactions must be created after installing the Spain Localization SuiteApp. This also includes any related transactions in their transaction chain. For example, if you create a Sales Order before the installation and later convert it into an Invoice, it may cause discrepancies in SII reporting. Therefore, such practice should be avoided.
