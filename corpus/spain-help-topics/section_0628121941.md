If you have a Spain subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the Modelo 303 Value Added Tax Return for Spain, in English or Spanish. The report can be used as a source for filling in the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.

For more information, read the following Spain tax topics:

    -   [Tax Code Table for Spain](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1999320.html#bridgehead_N1999427)
