Setting up an account for an organization doing business in Spain requires enabling several features and installing additional SuiteApps.

## Enabling Features {#bridgehead_71083423619}

Some features available in your NetSuite account can be important or even essential for users in Spain. If they're inactive, you should consider enabling them. Follow the instructions below to enable the most common features beneficial for users in Spain. For information about the features of a specific product area, see [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161010578494.html#bridgehead_161011109563), and check the exact requirements.

If you don't have sufficient permissions to enable features, contact your account administrator.

#### To enable features: {#procedure_0613100731}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features (Administrator)_.
    
2.  On the **Company** subtab, go to the International section, and check the **Multi-language** and **Multiple Currencies** boxes.
    
3.  On the **Accounting** subtab, go to the Basic Features section, and check the **GL Audit Numbering** box.
    
4.  (Optional) On the **Accounting** subtab, go to the Advanced Features section, and check the **Period End Journal Entries** and **Foreign Currency Variance Mapping** boxes.
    
5.  On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, enable the desired features under the following sections:
    
    -   SuiteBuilder
        
    -   SuiteScript
        
    -   SuiteGL
        
    -   SuiteFlow
        
    
    For more information about SuiteCloud features, see [SuiteCloud Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N239332.html).
    
6.  If your company is registered for the EU MOSS VAT scheme, check the **EU Mini One Stop Shop** box on the **Tax** subtab.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

## Available SuiteApps {#bridgehead_13083526440}

Some additional features require installation of a specific SuiteApp. SuiteApps are applications that extend NetSuite for specific industry and business needs. For information about the required SuiteApps for specific product areas, see [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_71094120219.html).

#### To install a SuiteApp: {#procedure_66111640738}

1.  Go to _Customization > SuiteBundler > Search & Install Bundles_.
    
2.  Search the SuiteApps using their names or their Bundle IDs.
    
3.  On the Bundle Details page, click **Install**.
    

If you don't have sufficient permissions to enable features, contact your account administrator.

For more information about SuiteApps, see [SuiteApps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_3134602194.html).

For more information about SuiteApps installation, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).
