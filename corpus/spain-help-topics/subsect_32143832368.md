Before you generate your VAT reports, you must set the reporting frequency for Modelo 303 and Modelo 349 based on your business needs. You can choose either a quarterly or monthly reporting frequency.

#### To set the reporting frequency: {#procedure_0803120243}

1.  Go to Setup > Tax Reporting > Country Tax Report Preferences.
    
2.  On the **Period Filters** subtab, review and change the settings in the **Reporting Frequency** column for Modelo 303 and Modelo 349 reports.
    
    Note:
    
    Depending on your SII reporting requirements, you must set the reporting frequency for Modelo 303 accordingly. Take a look at these situations before you set the reporting frequency:
    
    -   If you're required to report SII, choose the monthly reporting frequency.
        
    -   If you report SII voluntarily, choose either the monthly or quarterly reporting frequency, depending on whether you're registered in the Monthly Refund Regime (REDEME).
        
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

For more information, see [Setting Up Country Tax Reporting Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1557127083.html).
