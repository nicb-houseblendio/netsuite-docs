Before you report your OSS transactions in the Spain Localization SuiteApp, perform the following setup steps:

-   [Adding the OSS Union Scheme to Your Spain Subsidiary](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1104030247.html)
    
    The Spain Localization SuiteApp only supports the Union scheme of OSS. When following the procedures described in the linked article, make sure you select the Union scheme.
