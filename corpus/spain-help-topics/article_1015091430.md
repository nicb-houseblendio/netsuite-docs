Note:

To access the Spain Localization SuiteApp documentation in Spanish of Spain (español de España), see [Spain Localization](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=SpainLocalization.pdf).

The Spain Localization SuiteApp enables you to submit your generated SII reports to AEAT directly from the SuiteApp. You can either submit your SII reports manually after successfully generating a report or use the new SII Report Scheduling feature to set automated submissions. For more information, see:

-   [Manual Submission of SII Reports to AEAT](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1022012701.html)
    
-   [Automated Submission of SII Reports to AEAT](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1022012821.html)
    

Note:

If you decide not to use this feature, you can still download your report and manually submit it on the AEAT online portal.
