NetSuite provides a full range of tools that you can use to report on lead generation. With online form statistics and lead source reports, you can determine which efforts are the most successful at bringing in new leads.

## Online Form Statistics {#bridgehead_N984758}

NetSuite tracks how often your online forms are viewed and filled out.

You can view stats for online lead forms on the External subtab of the form record. Here, you'll see:

-   **Number of Requests** - Number of times someone navigates to this form
    
-   **Number of Submits** - Number of times the form is submitted
    

To view online form statistics, go to Setup > Marketing > Online Customer Forms, and click View next to the online form you want to view. Then, click the External subtab.
