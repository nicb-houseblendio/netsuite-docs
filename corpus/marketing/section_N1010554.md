The Campaign ROI Analysis Summary report shows the profit and return on investment (ROI) for each marketing campaign. It also shows the number of leads, prospects, and customers generated. Campaigns are grouped by campaign category.

Profit is calculated by deducting the cost of the campaign from the amount generated by sales transactions with the campaign's lead source. ROI is calculated by dividing the profit by the cost of the campaign.

With Narrative Insights, you can use generative AI to summarize the report, helping you understand overall campaign profitability and quickly identify overall marketing ROI and standout performers.

The generated insights may include:

-   Summarized insights on total profit and ROI across marketing campaigns
    
-   Recognition of top-performing campaigns that contribute the most to ROI
    
-   Key points on overall marketing spend effectiveness and summary ROI outcomes
    

To generate an insight on the Campaign ROI Analysis Summary report, the **Enable Narrative Insights** preference must be enabled on the **Narrative Insights** tab of the AI Preferences page. This preference is enabled by default. For more information, see [Managing Narrative Insights Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6150024470.html).

For more information about Narrative Insights, see [Narrative Insights Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_135342304.html).

## To view the Campaign ROI Analysis Summary report: {#bridgehead_N1010576}

Go to _Reports > Marketing > Campaign ROI Analysis_.

You'll see a message that your report is loading. The status bar at the bottom shows the progress as your report loads. You can click Cancel Report next to the status bar if you want to stop the report from loading.

Note:

This report does not support reporting by period even when the Report by Period preference is set to All Reports. You can set the Report by Period preference at _Home > Set Preferences_ > the Analytics subtab.

## To generate an insight on the Campaign ROI Analysis Summary report: {#bridgehead_1127042716}

1.  Filter the report to include only the information you want the insight to reflect.
    
2.  Click the **Generate Insight** button.
    
    The Narrative Insights dialog is displayed. You can generate the insight again at any time by clicking the **Generate Insight** button.
    

Important:

Insights may differ each time they're generated, even when you're viewing the same data.
