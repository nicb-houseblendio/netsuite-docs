After you create your keyword campaigns, you can create a saved search to export the information you need for search engines.

To create a saved search, go to _Campaigns > Marketing > Campaigns > Search_. Enter criteria for the keyword campaigns you want to export. For example, you may want to export the campaigns for one search engine at a time.

Then, choose which information you want to show on the Results subtab. For example, you might include the keyword, the keyword family, and the destination URL in your results.

Finally, click Create Saved Search, enter a title, and then click Save. When you view the results, click Export to save the information as a CSV or Excel file.
