The Sent Email List lets you see all outgoing email sent from your account. You can see which emails were delivered successfully and which ones didn't reach the recipient.

By default, the Sent Email List and search capabilities are only accessible to users with the Administrator role. Administrators can assign the Sent Email permission to other roles, which lets users with those roles use the feature.

The Undelivered Emails feature is also available in your NetSuite account. See [Undelivered Emails Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1489004543.html).

You can access all saved searches in your account from Lists > Search > Saved Searches. The Sent Email feature provides similar capabilities as the Undelivered Emails feature. However, Sent Email also provides information about all outbound email sent from an account. The list contains search results from the previous 100 days of activity.

Note:

The data in the Sent Email List isn't available to SuiteAnalytics.

The Sent Email List displays the following details for all outbound email:

-   **Sent date**
    
-   **Log date**: Date and time when an email is prepared for sending from the NetSuite application to all recipients.
    
    Note:
    
    For Transactional email messages with multiple recipients (for example, recipients in the To, Cc, or Bcc headers) a single record is created in the Sent Email List. However, if messages to some of the recipients bounced, this would create multiple lines in the Undelivered Emails saved search. The log date on each line in the Undelivered Emails saved search reflects the date and time when the message to a particular recipient bounced.
    
-   **From**
    
-   **Subject**: Shows the first 300 bytes of the subject line of the message.
    
-   **Recipients (To/Cc/Bcc)**
    
-   **Body**: Shows the first 1,000 bytes of the body of the message. HTML tags are removed for readability. Any private or sensitive information is masked.
    
-   **Message ID**
    
-   **Status**: **Sent** or **Not Sent**. (Email delivery status per recipient is shown on the Sent Email record.)
    
-   **Compliance Verified**: Indicates whether messages comply with standards, including DKIM alignment.
    
    -   For more information about DKIM, see [DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N999544.html)
        
    -   For information about how to optimize email campaign delivery, see the **Five Golden Rules for Outbound Email** in [Email Best Practices](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N993117.html).
        

You can schedule Sent Email saved searches the same way that you scheduled Undelivered Emails saved searches.

The following procedure describes how to view the Sent Email List or to incorporate new saved searches in your account.

#### To use the Sent Email List: {#procedure_157313471242}

1.  You have several options to view the information about the Sent Email List:
    
    -   Go to Setup > Company > Sent Email List.
        
    -   Go to _Setup > Company > Sent Email List > Search_. On the Sent Email Search page, click **Personalize Search** or **Create Saved Search**.
        
    -   To create a saved search, go to Lists > Search > Saved Searches. Click **New Saved Search**. In the list, click **Sent Email List**. Customize the search as required, and click **Save**.
        
2.  The Sent Email List or Sent Email Saved Search page display the results of email generated from your NetSuite account. The status of email in the list will be either **Sent** or **Not Sent**.
    
    | Status | Description |
    | --- | --- |
    | Sent | The message was sent from NetSuite and delivered to all recipients. None of the email addresses were bounced or filtered. |
    | Not Sent | The message wasn't delivered to at least one of the intended recipients. At least one of the email addresses was bounced or was filtered before sending. |
    
3.  To view the delivery status, click the corresponding row number with the status of **Not Sent** or **Sent**.
    
    For example, an email may fail to send if a previous attempt resulted in a bounced email.
    
    See the following table for information about the meaning of various recipient statuses and failure reasons.
    
    | Delivery Status | Failure Reason | Description |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Delivered | Not applicable | Successful delivery of email to recipient. |
    | Skipped | Not sent: Blocked by mail filter | The sending rate is not accurate or is too high (similar email messages are being sent too rapidly). |
    | Skipped | Not sent: Specific address(es) recently bounced | The email wasn't sent because the recipient address appears on the Bounced Email Addresses list. |
    | Skipped | Not sent: No MX or A for domain | The email wasn't sent because there is no DNS record (an MX or an A record) for the domain. |
    | Undelivered | Permanent Failure: Delivery not authorized, message refused | The sender isn't authorized to send to the destination. This can be the result of per-host or per-recipient filtering. |
    | Undelivered | Permanent Failure: No answer from host | The outbound connection attempt was not answered. |
    | Undelivered | Permanent Failure: Bad destination mailbox address | The mailbox specified in the address does not exist. |
    | Undelivered | Permanent Failure: Unable to route | The mail system was unable to determine the next hop for the message because the necessary routing information was unavailable from the directory server. |
    | Undelivered | Permanent Failure: Mailbox disabled, not accepting messages | The mailbox exists, but isn't accepting messages. |
    
    Note:
    
    Multiple delivery statuses can exist in a single Sent Email record for emails with more than one recipient. For example, the email may be delivered to one recipient, but skip another because their email recently bounced.
