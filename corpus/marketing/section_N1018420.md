Campaign audiences describe the target audience of a marketing campaign.

You can track audiences on the Related Info subtab of campaign records. You can customize campaign reports to include information about your audience. The Audience Name field is a part of the Lead Source component. For more information, see [Report Customization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N736328.html).

To create a campaign audience, go to _Setup > Marketing > Campaign Management > Audiences_.
