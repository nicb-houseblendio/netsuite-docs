Before you create a marketing campaign, you should do the following:

1.  Set your [Marketing Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1017620.html).
    
2.  Create [Campaign Email Domains](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N996559.html).
    
    This is a required step if you send more than 10,000 bulk email per month.
    
3.  Set up an email domain key.
    
    See [DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N999544.html).
    
4.  Create [Campaign Categories](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N985787.html).
    
5.  Authorize the email addresses you want to use to receive replies to your campaign email.
    
    See [Creating a Campaign Email Address Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1000747.html).
    
6.  Create [Campaign Subscription Categories](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N996344.html) to allow customers to choose the kinds of email they receive from your company.
    
7.  Create any of the following information to help evaluate and analyze your campaigns:
    
    -   [Campaign Audiences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1018420.html)
        
    -   [Campaign Verticals](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1018615.html)
        
    -   [Campaign Families](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1006480.html)
        
    -   [Campaign Search Engines](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1018748.html)
        
    -   [Campaign Channels](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1018882.html)
        
    -   [Campaign Offers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1019075.html)
        
    -   [Promotional URLs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1016228.html)
