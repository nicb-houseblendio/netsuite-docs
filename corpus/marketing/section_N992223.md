You can track the number of leads, prospects, and customers generated by a campaign. To track this data, select the campaign in the Lead Source field on the customer record.

For more information, see [Associating a Customer With a Campaign](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N991789.html).
