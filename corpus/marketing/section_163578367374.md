Intelligent item recommendations are available on any account that has Customer Relationship Management, SuiteCommerce, or SuiteCommerce Advanced enabled. Whether a particular type of recommendation is available in a specific site or subsidiary, depends upon the following:

| Type of Recommendation | Subsidiary/Site Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| 
-   Order or cart items
-   Customers who bought this also bought
-   Recently viewed items
-   Customer purchase history

 | 

-   At least 50 items in the catalog
-   A minimum number of transactions in the past 12 months

 |
| Buy Again | A minimum number of repeat purchases in the past 12 months |
| Alternative Items | 

-   At least 50 items in the catalog
-   A system-supported language, excluding Vietnamese

 |

Your account must meet further criteria if you want to use intelligent recommendations on a SuiteCommerce or SuiteCommerce Advanced website. For more information about additional requirements, see [Account Requirements for Intelligent Item Recommendations for Websites](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_15205015355.html). For more information about system-supported languages, see [Configuring Multiple Languages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N247147.html).

To check which types of recommendations are available for a subsidiary or a site, go to _Commerce > Marketing > Intelligent Recommendations > Recommendation Availability_.
