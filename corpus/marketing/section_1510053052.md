This topic includes troubleshooting tips to determine why email messages from NetSuite aren't delivered.

For information about how to optimize email campaign delivery, see the **Five Golden Rules for Outbound Email** in [Email Best Practices](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N993117.html).

1.  If an email message to one person wasn't delivered, go to Lists > Relationships > Bounced Email Addresses. Determine if the recipient is on the bounced email list. For more information, see [Viewing the Bounced Email Address List](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3884410517.html).
    
2.  Determine whether the email was blocked by NetSuite or whether it was rejected on the destination mail system. For more information, see [Using the Sent Email List](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156569944168.html) or [Undelivered Emails Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1489004543.html).
    
3.  Go to Setup > Company > Email Preferences, click the Email Domains (DKIM) subtab and check the status of your Domain record. The status of your domain should always be **Ready**. For more information, see, [Setting Email Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4375043929.html).
