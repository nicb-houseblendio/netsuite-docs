The integrated marketing tools in NetSuite enable you to create and track all aspects of your email campaigns. You can measure return on investment and see which email campaigns bring in the most leads. You can also view reports of how many recipients get your offers.

Unlike other channels, NetSuite creates and sends your campaign emails at the date and time you set. You can also set up lead nurturing campaigns that go out based on criteria you choose. For information about lead nurturing campaigns, see [Lead Nurturing Campaigns](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1003092.html).

You can also use email campaigns to send and track email newsletters. Using campaigns enables you to track how readers respond to your newsletters. For more information, see [Sending Newsletters Through Email Campaigns](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1003698.html).

NetSuite generates campaign email from HTML templates you create. You can personalize these templates to include the name, address, and other information specific to the customer or contact receiving the email.

#### Before you create your first email campaign, complete the following tasks: {#procedure_N992571}

1.  Review [Optimizing Email Campaigns](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N993465.html) for information about how you can ensure the best results from your email campaigns.
    
2.  Set up your campaign email domain. For more information, see [Campaign Email Domains](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N996559.html).
    
3.  Set up domain key identified mail (DKIM). For more information, see [DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N999544.html).
    
4.  Set your [Marketing Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1017620.html).
    
5.  Send the customers, contacts, and other recipients subscription email to allow them to opt in to your campaigns. For information, see [Subscription Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N994827.html).
    

After you complete these five tasks, you are ready to create email campaigns.

#### To create an email marketing campaign: {#procedure_N992726}

1.  Create an email marketing template. For more information, see [Email Marketing Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1001310.html).
    
2.  Create a group of recipients. For more information, see [Working with Records, Transactions, and Lists](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N488023.html).
    
3.  Create a campaign record. For more information, see [Creating a Campaign Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N986170.html).
    
4.  Run your campaign events. For more information, see [Initiating Campaign Events](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N989408.html).
