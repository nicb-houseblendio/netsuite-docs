Campaign email addresses can appear in the From field of your marketing email and can receive replies to your marketing email.

Before you send out email campaign events, you need to validate the email address you want to use.

#### To create a campaign email address record: {#procedure_N1000779}

1.  Go to _Setup > Sales & Marketing Automation > Email Addresses > New_.
    
2.  On the Campaign Email Address page, enter the email address you want to use.
    
3.  If you check the **Private** box, only the person that entered this campaign email address record can:
    
    -   View this email address record
        
    -   Assign this email address to email templates
        
4.  Click **Save**.
    

When you save the email record, NetSuite sends an authorization code to that address. You'll need to enter the code in the campaign email address record before you can use it.

#### To validate an email address: {#procedure_N1000858}

1.  Go to _Setup > Sales & Marketing Automation > Email Addresses_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the email address you want to validate.
    
3.  In the **Authorization Code** field, enter the code included in the email you received from NetSuite.
    
    Tip:
    
    The authorization code is valid for 24 hours. If the code expires, click **Send New Code** to send a new authorization code to the email address.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

You can select a validated email address on campaign email templates to serve as the From address, the Reply to address, or both.

After you create a target group, upload an email template, and validate your marketing email addresses, you can create email marketing campaigns.

For more information about creating email campaign templates, see [Email Marketing Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1001310.html).
