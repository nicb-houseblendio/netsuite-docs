Note:

This topic is unrelated to marketing campaigns. For information about campaigns, see [Email Marketing Campaigns](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N992514.html).

You can view the status of your bulk merge operations at _Documents > Mail Merge > Merge History_.

#### To view bulk merge history:

1.  Choose Documents > Mail Merge > Merge History.
    
2.  Optional. To narrow or expand your search of merge history files, apply filters on the **Filters** subtab.
    

To view bulk merge information about each recipient's record, you must customize the entry form to include the Bulk Merge subtab.

#### To show the Bulk Merge subtab on relationship records: {#procedure_N523952}

1.  Go to _Customization > Forms > Entry Forms_.
    
2.  Click **Customize** next to the type of form you want to include the **Bulk Merge** subtab.
    
3.  On the custom entry form page, check the **Form is Preferred** box.
    
4.  Under **Lists**, **Communication** subtab, check the **Show** box next to **Bulk Merge**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

A record of each type of merge is now accessible from the **Communication** subtab.

Note:

The file attached to this subtab is not the merged PDF that contains customer data. It's the bulk merge template. To view the populated mail merge, use **Merge History**.
