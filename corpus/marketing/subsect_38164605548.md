| **1\. Always send using a FROM domain you control** | 
-   Don't send emails on behalf of customers, and never use their email addresses in the FROM field.
-   Never send from addresses that use a DNS record you don't control.
    -   For emailed forms, or email messages related to transactions, make sure the **Return Email Address** field on the Company Information page uses your domain. (A user with an Administrator role can go to Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Company Information to view the **Return Email Address** field). For more information, see the entry in the **From Address for Emailed Forms** row in the [Transaction Email Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N256994.html) section of [Setting Email Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4375043929.html).
    -   For campaign email messages, see [Campaign Email Domains](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N996559.html).

 |
| --- | --- |
| **2\. Manage Your Scripts** | Regularly check your SuiteScript outbound email scripts and quickly delete any scripts or customizations you're not using anymore. |
| **3\. Be Compliant** | 

-   Make sure you've set up the right DNS records for DKIM on the domains you send email from. See [DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N999544.html) and [DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM) and Email Domains](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N993153.html).
-   Consider setting up a DMARC policy record for your company's entire email infrastructure. See [Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting and Conformance (DMARC)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4820737714.html).
-   For NetSuite, you don't need to set up DNS records for SPF on your sending domains, but your company might still need an SPF record depending on your needs. See [Sender Policy Framework (SPF)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4820740297.html).

 |
| **4\. Be Aligned** | If you have an email relay between NetSuite and your mailbox provider (where the MX points), make sure the MAIL-FROM (ENVELOPE-FROM, RETURN-PATH) passes the SPF check. |
| **5\. Be Hygienic** | 

-   Don't send spam or unwanted email.
-   Don't send single email more than a single time.
-   Always honor unsubscribe requests.
-   Don't send or forward email with content (especially attachments) that you don't recognize
