Note:

The following information relates only to template categories used by system-automated email templates. For information about template categories used in email templates, see [Creating Email Template Categories](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4534668235.html)

Categories help contextualize and limit the list of available templates in template selection lists. For example, on the Case Alerts record, only case alert templates display in the template selection list. NetSuite automatically assigns default system email templates to one of the categories shown in the table below. You must assign all new templates to at least one of these categories. Administrators can create exceptions and assign individual templates to more than one category, if required.

| **Category Name** | **Areas Used (examples)** |
| --- | --- |
| **Access** | 
-   _Setup > Company > Email Preferences > Templates_
-   _Setup > Company > Subsidiary > Edit Subsidiary > Preferences_

 |
| **Case Alerts** | Setup > Support > Escalation Management > Case Alerts |
| **Case Notifications** | _Setup > Support > Case Management > Case Profiles > Notifications_ |
| **Issue Notifications** | _Setup > Issues > Preferences > Issue Preferences_ |
| **Online Forms** | 

-   _Setup > Support > Online Case Forms > New > Set Up Workflow_
-   _Setup > Marketing > Marketing Preferences > General_
-   _Setup > Marketing > Online Customer Forms > Set Up Workflow_

 |
| **Sales Order Notifications** | Used as part of the Advanced Order Management module. _Setup > Order Management > Advanced Order Management > Notifications_ |
| **Marketing Subscriptions** | _Setup > Marketing > Marketing Preferences_ |
| **Web Site Digital Delivery Notifications** | _Commerce > Websites > Website List_ > Email tab > Digital Delivery Emails subtab |
| **Web Site Order Notifications** | _Commerce > Websites > Website List_ > Email tab > Order Emails subtab |
| **Web Site Other Notifications** | _Commerce > Websites > Website List_ > Email tab > Other Emails subtab |

#### To assign a category to a system-automated template:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Email > System Email Templates_
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the required template.
    
3.  Click the **Categories** subtab.
    
4.  From the **Template Category** list, select the required category and click **Attach**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
