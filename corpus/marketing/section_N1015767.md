The Sales by Paid Keyword Summary report shows the total sales by keyword campaign for the date range you select. Campaigns are grouped by campaign category.

For information about how to tie a transaction to a campaign, see [Associating a Transaction With a Campaign](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N991927.html).

## To view the Sales by Paid Keyword Summary report: {#bridgehead_N1015807}

Go to _Reports > Marketing > Sales by Paid Keyword_.

You'll see a message that your report is loading. The status bar at the bottom shows the progress as your report loads. You can click Cancel Report next to the status bar if you want to stop the report from loading.
