There are several steps to creating your marketing campaign. Complete these steps to maximize your campaign reporting ability.

#### To create a marketing campaign: {#procedure_N985095}

1.  Read the [Optimizing Email Campaigns](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N993465.html) for help in optimizing the delivery rate of your marketing emails.
    
2.  For email campaigns, create a template and a template record.
    
    For more information see [Email Marketing Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1001310.html).
    
3.  Create target group of customers or contacts. For more information, see [Working with Records, Transactions, and Lists](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N488023.html).
    
    For tips on creating target groups for email campaigns, see [Choosing Email Recipients](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N993465.html#bridgehead_N993224).
    
4.  Create a campaign record.
    
    For more information, see [Creating a Campaign Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N986170.html).
    
5.  Execute your campaign.
    
    For more information, see [Initiating Campaign Events](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N989408.html).
    

After the campaign record is created and executed, you can view reports and statistics on your campaign events.
