When an email message returns an invalid address (hard bounce), records associated with that email address display a warning about the invalid email address. This includes:

-   email campaigns
    
-   customer, contact, vendor, employee, and other name records
    
-   email messages
    

Note:

NetSuite won't send messages (for a specified number of days) to addresses that trigger a hard bounce. You set the number of days in the **Remove Email Addresses From Bounced Email Address List After** preference. Go to _Setup > Company > Email > Email Preferences_.
