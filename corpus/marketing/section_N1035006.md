To send out your survey, you can do an email merge or send it through a marketing campaign. If you use a campaign, you can track how many messages were received, opened, responded to, and clicked.

First, create a customer group to use as the recipient group for the merge or campaign. To create a group, go to _Commerce > Marketing > Personalization > Groups > New_.

Finally, send the survey email through an email merge or marketing campaign. For more information, see [Merging Email](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N524089.html) and [Creating a Campaign Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N986170.html).
