You can use a mass update to unsubscribe anyone with an invalid email address from email campaigns. You can base the mass update on the Hard Bounced field on a record whenever email sent from NetSuite triggers a hard bounce.

#### To unsubscribe entities with bounced email addresses:

1.  Go to _Lists > Mass Update > Mass Updates_.
    
2.  Expand **General Updates**.
    
3.  Click the type of record you want to unsubscribe.
    
4.  On the Mass Update page, on the **Criteria** subtab, select **Bounced** in the **Filter** column.
    
5.  In the popup window, select **Yes** and click **Set**.
    
6.  In the **Filter** column, select **Global Subscription Status**.
    
7.  In the popup window, select **Soft Opt-Out** and click **Set**.
    
8.  Click **Preview** to review the records that will be affected by this update.
    
9.  On the preview page, click **Perform Update**.
    

For information about saving mass updates so they can be reused, see [Mass Updates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1103335211.html).
