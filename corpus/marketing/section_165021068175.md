When you enter a sales order, estimate, or opportunity, you'll see a list of intelligent recommendations based on the customer and items you've already picked.

#### To add a recommended item to a sales order, estimate, or opportunity record:

1.  Create a new sales order, estimate, or opportunity record.
    
2.  Choose a customer or add items to the transaction on the **Items** subtab.
    
3.  Click the **Intelligent Recommendations** button at the top of the Items list to view the recommendations available for the selected combination of customer and items.
    
4.  From the Intelligent Recommendations popup window, choose the quantity of each item you want to add to the sales order, estimate, or opportunity record.
    
    -   If you want to change the basis on which items are recommended, you can add or remove options from the **Recommendations Based On** list.
        
    -   Check the **Available items only** box to display available items only. Available items include in-stock and those available for backorder.
        
    
    ![Intelligent Recommendations popup window with Recommendations Based On and Available items only selections highlighted.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/MarketingSalesForceAutomationPartners/Marketing/intelligent_items_selection.png)
    
    Note:
    
    The recommendations shown depend upon the transaction data available in the account. For example, if only a few transactions include the selected item, you might not get any 'Order or cart items' recommendations. If no recommendations are available, popular items are displayed instead.
    
5.  If any of the recommended items are matrix items, click **Subitems >** and choose the quantity of each subitem you want to add to the sales order, estimate, or opportunity record.
    
    ![Intelligent Recommendations with Subitems.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/MarketingSalesForceAutomationPartners/Marketing/intelligent_items_subitems.png)
    
    Click **Add Subitems** when you have finished to return to the list of intelligent recommendations.
    
6.  Click **Add & Close** to add the selected quantity of the items and subitems to the sales order, estimate, or opportunity record.
    

#### To add an alternative item recommendation to a sales order, estimate, or opportunity: {#procedure_1202091919}

1.  In the **Items** tab of a sales order, estimate, or opportunity record, select the item for which you want to see alternative item recommendations.
    
2.  Click **Alternative Items** in the toolbar that appears below the item to view the recommendations.
    
    -   If you want to change the basis on which items are recommended, you can add or remove options from the **Recommendations Based On** list.
        
    -   Check the **Available items only** box to display available items only. Available items include in-stock and those available for backorder.
        
    
    ![Intelligent Recommendations button for alternative item recommendations.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/MarketingSalesForceAutomationPartners/Marketing/intelligent_items_alternative_items.png)
3.  From the Intelligent Recommendations popup window, choose the quantity of each item you want to add to the sales order, estimate, or opportunity record.
    
4.  If any of the recommended items are matrix items, click **Subitems >** to choose the quantity of each subitem you want to add to the sales order, estimate, or opportunity record.
    
5.  Click **Add & Close**.
    
    The selected quantity of the selected items are added to the sales order, estimate, or opportunity record.
