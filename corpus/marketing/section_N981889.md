Online form templates let you to customize the look and layout of your NetSuite online forms. Online form templates are helpful if you don't use a NetSuite website but still use NetSuite online customer and case forms.

Online form templates are HTML documents that you create outside of NetSuite. You either upload them to your NetSuite file cabinet or paste on the template record. Experience with HTML can be helpful when creating an online form template.

Note:

To use custom HTML templates, an administrator must enable the Marketing Automation feature.

#### To create an online form with an HTML online template: {#procedure_0914014929}

1.  Create the HTML file you'll use. For more information, see [HTML Files for Online Form Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1009112643.html).
    
2.  Upload your template to the file cabinet. Click New on the template record, or place it in the Marketing Templates folder in your file cabinet. For more information, see [Uploading an Online Form Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N984108.html).
    
3.  Create a template record at _Documents > Templates > Marketing Templates > New > Online Form_ > Online Form. For more information, see [Creating an Online Form Template Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N984310.html).
    
4.  Create your online customer form at _Setup > Marketing > Setup Tasks > Online Customer Forms > New_ or your online case form at _Setup > Support > Case Management > Online Case Forms > New_. For more information, see [Online Customer Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N976289.html).
