By default, when you create a sales transaction for a customer, the Lead Source field is blank on the transaction. You can manually change the lead source on a per-transaction basis. You can also set the lead source automatically when you use the Default Lead Source on Sales Transactions preference. Go to _Setup > Marketing > Preferences > Marketing Preferences_.

Choose one of the following if you want a default lead source to appear on sales transactions:

-   **First Campaign Sent** - The lead source for the first campaign event sent to the customer is the default.
    
-   **Most Recent Campaign Sent** - The lead source associated with the most recent campaign event sent to the customer is the default.
    
-   **First Campaign Responded To** - The lead source of the campaign event where the customer first responded is the default.
    
-   **Most Recent Campaign Responded To** - The lead source of the campaign event associated with the most recent customer response is the default.
    
-   **Based on Lead Source** - The lead source selected on the customer's record is the default.
    

Leave this field blank if you do not want a default lead source on sales transactions.
