You can schedule jobs that you've added to NetSuite in Account Reconciliation Sync.

#### To schedule a job in NetSuite: {#procedure_1209073403}

1.  Go to NetSuite EPM > Account Reconciliation > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  On the Account Reconciliation: Manage Jobs page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to a job of your choice.
        
    -   If you don't see the job in the list and have the Administrator or NSEPM Implementer role, click the **Add Job** button. Then, follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1209082459.html).
        
3.  Click the **Schedule** subtab, and do the following:
    
    1.  From the **Frequency** list, select how often you want the job to be executed within a scheduled period. The available options include the following:
        
        -   **Not Scheduled** to inactivate the schedule
            
        -   **Simple** to run the job only one time
            
        -   **Hourly** to run the job every hour
            
        -   **Daily** to run the job every day
            
        -   **Weekly** to run the job on a particular day of the week
            
        -   **Monthly (day of month)** to run the job on a particular day of the month
            
    2.  Determine the date and time when you want to regularly run the job. Depending on the frequency you've selected, you have the following options:
        
        -   **Start Date (M/D/YYYY)**
            
        -   **End Date (M/D/YYYY)**
            
        -   **Hours**
            
        -   **Minutes**
            
        -   **Day of Week**
            
        -   **Day of Month**
            
        
        Note:
        
        The date and time you define for job execution reflects the time zone of your NetSuite account.
        
    3.  If you want to pause the schedule, check the **Pause Scheduler** box. Until you resume the schedule, the related job stops running and doesn't show in [Job Calendar](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1209023654.html).
        
4.  Click **Save**.
