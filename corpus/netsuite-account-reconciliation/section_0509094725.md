To export Transaction Matching adjustments from your Account Reconciliation application, you need to create a source application in Data Exchange.

#### To create a source application:

1.  In your Account Reconciliation application, click the **Navigator** icon ![Navigator icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuiteAccountReconciliation/AccountReconciliationSync/ARNavigatorMenuIcon.png), and then, under Application, click **Data Exchange**.
    
2.  Click **Actions**, and then select **Applications**.
    
3.  In the newly opened window, click the **Add** icon ![Add icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuiteAccountReconciliation/AccountReconciliationSync/ARAddIcon.PNG).
    
4.  In the Create Application popup window, do the following:
    
    1.  For the **Category** field, select **Data Source**.
        
    2.  For the **Type** field, select **Account Reconciliation Journal Adjustments**.
        
    3.  For the **Application** field, select **Account Reconciliation Journal Adjustments**.
        
    4.  In the **Prefix** field, enter a prefix to give the source application a unique name.
        
    5.  Click **OK**.
