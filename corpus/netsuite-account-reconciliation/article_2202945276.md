After you install Account Reconciliation Sync, you need to create saved searches to load data from NetSuite to your Account Reconciliation application.

For general information about the saved searches included in the Account Reconciliation Sync SuiteApp, see [Saved Searches for Account Reconciliation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_4131759732.html).

For information about managing the saved searches, see the following help topics:

-   [Customizing an Account Reconciliation Sync Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1215085213.html)
