If you need to customize a created Account Reconciliation Sync saved search to better suit your needs, ensure you customize it properly in NetSuite.

#### To customize an Account Reconciliation Sync saved search: {#procedure_1215085328}

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Account Reconciliation > Manage Saved Searches.
    
2.  In the upper-right corner of the page, click **Create Default Saved Searches**.
    
3.  From the **Saved Search Group** list, select the saved search group.
    
4.  Click the link to a saved search in the **Created** status.
    
5.  On the saved search record page, you can modify the following:
    
    -   Saved search title.
        
        Note:
        
        Ensure the saved search name is unique in NetSuite.
        
    -   Search ID. To modify it, click **Change ID** and do the following:
        
        -   In the **New ID** field, enter the new search ID.
            
        -   Click **Save**.
            
        
        Note:
        
        Read the following details about changing the saved search ID:
        
        -   Ensure the saved search ID is unique in NetSuite.
            
        -   When you change the ID of a saved search, its existing settings will be lost.
            
        
6.  Verify that the saved search is public.
    
7.  After you've made all the necessary changes, click **Save**.
