Set up parameters for the Account Reconciliation Report job type.

#### To set up parameters for the Account Reconciliation Report job type:

1.  Go to NetSuite EPM > Account Reconciliation > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  On the Account Reconciliation: Manage Jobs page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to a job of your choice.
        
    -   If you don't see the job in the list and have the Administrator or NSEPM Implementer role, click the **Add Job** button. Then, follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1209082459.html).
        
3.  Click the **Job Parameters** subtab, and do the following:
    
    1.  From the **Report Module** list, select one of the following:
        
        -   **Reconciliation Compliance**
            
        -   **Transaction Matching**
            
    2.  From the **Report Format** list, select one of the available options:
        
        -   **PDF**
            
        -   **HTML**
            
        -   **XLSX**
            
        -   **CSV**
            
        -   **CSV2**
            
4.  Click **Save**.
    
    You can download the generated report in the job execution logs. See [Viewing the Job Execution Logs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7111204564.html).
