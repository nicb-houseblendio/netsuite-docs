With the Account Reconciliation Sync SuiteApp, you can export Account Reconciliation data in NetSuite and import it to your Account Reconciliation application.

The data types available for export include the following:

-   Organization Unit
    
-   Profile
    
-   Period
    
-   Global Period
    
-   Application Period
    
-   Currency Rates
    

For more information, see the following help topics:

-   [Exporting Account Reconciliation Data](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0103031013.html)
    
-   [Importing Account Reconciliation Data](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7113737905.html)
