If you have Transaction Matching or Reconciliation Compliance reports in your Account Reconciliation application, you can load them into NetSuite. To do that, you can use a post-processing plug-in script in NetSuite. The Account Reconciliation Sync SuiteApp provides a sample plug-in script that you can customize and attach to an Account Reconciliation Report job in NetSuite.

#### To use a plug-in script to load reports into NetSuite:

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Account Reconciliation > Integration Artifacts.
    
2.  Click **Download** to save the required plug-in script to your disc.
    
3.  Customize the plug-in script per your needs.
    
4.  Go to Customization > Plug-ins > Plug-in Implementations.
    
5.  Click **New Plug-in Implementation.**
    
6.  Click the plus (+) sign to upload the plug-in script, and then click **Save**.
    
7.  Click **Create Plug-in Implementation**.
    
8.  From the list of plug-in types, select **NSAR | PI Job Post Process.**
    
9.  In the **Name** field, enter a name for your custom plug-in script.
    
10.  Set the Status field to **Released**.
     
11.  Click **Save**.
     
12.  Add the plug-in script to an Account Reconciliation Report job in NetSuite:
     
     1.  Go to NetSuite EPM > Account Reconciliation > Manage Jobs.
         
     2.  On the Account Reconciliation: Manage Jobs page, do one of the following:
         
         -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to a job of your choice.
             
         -   If you don't see the job in the list and have the Administrator role, click the **Add Job** button. Then, follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1209082459.html).
             
     3.  Click the **Options** subtab.
         
     4.  From the **Post-Processing Plug-in** list, select the plug-in you want to use to process data.
         
     5.  Check the **Delete file once the job is complete** box if you want to delete the file after the job is executed.
         
     6.  Click **Save**.
         
     
     Note:
     
     The plug-in script is applicable only if the report format is CSV/CSV2.
     

You can find the generated report in the job execution logs. See [Viewing the Job Execution Logs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7111204564.html).
