You can view a calendar of all the jobs that you've scheduled for the NetSuite and Account Reconciliation application integration. For information about the jobs that you can schedule and execute in NetSuite for this integration, see [Managing the Account Reconciliation Jobs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_8161528693.html).

To access the calendar, go to NetSuite EPM > Account Reconciliation > Job Calendar. Jobs are displayed in a monthly view. You can move the calendar forward and backward in time by clicking the **Previous** and **Next** buttons. Click **Today** to display jobs scheduled for the current month.

All jobs are color-coded: scheduled (blue), successful (green), failed (red). To view or edit job details, click the date when it is scheduled.

To access the Classic Job Calendar, click **More**, and select **Classic Job Calendar**.
