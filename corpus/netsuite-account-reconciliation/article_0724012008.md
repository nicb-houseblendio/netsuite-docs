You can manually run the jobs you've added in NetSuite for Account Reconciliation Sync, and watch the progress as the jobs run.

#### To manually run a job in NetSuite:

1.  Go to NetSuite EPM > Account Reconciliation > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  To manually run a job, click **Execute** next to the job. This takes you to the Execute Job page where you can do the following:
    
    -   To start executing the job, click the **Execute** button. The information banner indicates the start of the job execution.
        
    -   To check the job execution status, click the **Execution Logs** subtab.
        
    -   When the job is running, the **Reset Job Status** button appears on the page. Click the button if you need to terminate the job execution. This doesn't cancel the job execution in Data Exchange or your Account Reconciliation application. This resets the job status in NetSuite, and lets you reschedule and run the job again from NetSuite.
