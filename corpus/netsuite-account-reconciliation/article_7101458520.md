Before installing Account Reconciliation Sync, make sure you've enabled features required by the Account Reconciliation Sync in your NetSuite account.

#### To enable the required features: {#procedure_1221101834}

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features.
    
2.  Click the **SuiteCloud** subtab, and check the following boxes:
    
    -   Custom Records
        
    -   Client SuiteScript
        
    -   Server SuiteScript
        
3.  Click **Save**.
    

The Account Reconciliation Sync SuiteApp requires the account numbers preference enabled. To enable this preference, go to Setup > Accounting > Preferences > Accounting Preferences. For more information, see [Chart of Account Numbering](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1440268.html).
