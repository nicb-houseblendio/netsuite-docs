Import the currency rates data in your Account Reconciliation application.

#### To import the currency rates data in Account Reconciliation: {#procedure_0103050656}

1.  Click the **Navigator** icon ![Navigator Menu Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuiteAccountReconciliation/AccountReconciliationSync/ARNavigatorMenuIcon.png), and then click **Configuration**.
    
2.  Click the **Currencies** icon ![Currecncies icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuiteAccountReconciliation/AccountReconciliationSync/ARCurrenciesIcon.PNG), and then do the following in the Filters section:
    
    1.  Click **Rate Type**, and then select a rate type from the list.
        
    2.  Click the **Period** filter, and then select a period of your choice.
        
3.  Click the **Import** icon ![Account Reconciliation Import Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuiteAccountReconciliation/AccountReconciliationSync/ARImportIcon.PNG).
    
4.  In the Import Currency Rates popup window, do the following:
    
    1.  Click **Choose File**.
        
        In the popup window, find the CSV file you want to import, and then click **Open**.
        
    2.  For **Import Type**, choose one of the following:
        
        -   **Replace**
            
        -   **Replace All**
            
    3.  For **File Delimiter**, select a file delimiter from the list.
        
    4.  Click **Import**. When the import is finished, click **OK**.
