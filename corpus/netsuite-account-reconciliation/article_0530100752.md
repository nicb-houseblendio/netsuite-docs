Before executing the data integration, ensure you have open adjustments.

#### To check whether you have open adjustments:

1.  In your Account Reconciliation application, click the **Navigator** icon ![Navigator icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuiteAccountReconciliation/AccountReconciliationSync/ARNavigatorMenuIcon.png), and go to **Matching**.
    
2.  Open the required transaction matching from the list.
    
3.  In the newly opened window, click the **Adjustments** icon ![Adjustments icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuiteAccountReconciliation/AccountReconciliationSync/ARAdjustmentsIcon.png).
    
4.  Ensure the **Extract Status** for the adjustments is Open. Otherwise, select the adjustments, click **Actions**, and then **Mark as Open**.
