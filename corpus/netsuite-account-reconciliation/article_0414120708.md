The following pages in Account Reconciliation Sync can be accessed by users logged in to NetSuite with roles other than Administrator, NSEPM Implementer, and NSAR Integration:

-   Account Reconciliation Jobs
    
-   Accounts for Group Reconciliation
    

The navigation path varies depending on the NetSuite center your role uses when you log in to NetSuite. To access the listed Account Reconciliation Sync pages, do the following:

-   If you log in using a role with the Classic Center, go to NetSuite EPM > Account Reconciliation. Then, click one of the following:
    
    -   **Manage Jobs**
        
    -   **Accounts for Group Reconciliation**
        
-   If you log in using a role with other than the Classic Center, for example the Accountant role, go to Setup > Other Setup. Then, click one of the following:
    
    -   **NSAR | Project List** - Opens the Account Reconciliation Jobs page.
        
    -   **NSAR | Profile Account Data** - Opens the Accounts for Group Reconciliation page.
        

For information about NetSuite centers, see [NetSuite Standard Centers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N132466.html). For information about the NSEPM Implementer and NSAR Integration roles, see [Role Permissions for Account Reconciliation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161507691.html).
