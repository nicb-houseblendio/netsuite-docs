You can use NetSuite to schedule or manually execute jobs configured in your Account Reconciliation application. Before you run a job for any period in NetSuite, ensure that reconciliations are created in the Account Reconciliation application from this period. For more information about creating reconciliations from periods, see the help topic [Creating Reconciliations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/account-reconcile-cloud/adarc/admin_create_reconcil_104xd8fdaddd.html) in the Oracle Help Center.

On each job listed at NetSuite EPM > Account Reconciliation > Manage Jobs, you can take one of the following actions.

| Action Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | If you're using the Administrator role, you can click this link to schedule the job execution. For information, see [Scheduling a Job in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1209073051.html). Important: Some NetSuite accounts do not support scheduled jobs. This includes some, but not all, test drive accounts (that is, accounts where the account ID begins with TSTDRV). If that is your case, scheduling of job execution will not work for you. |
| **Execute** | Clicking this link takes you to a page where you can manually execute the job and view the progress of the job execution. See [Executing a Job in Account Reconciliation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0724012008.html). |
| **Logs** | Click this link to access information about an executed job. For information, see the help topic [Viewing the Job Execution Logs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7111204564.html). |

Click **Refresh** to reload the page and get the latest status from your Account Reconciliation application.

The **Schedule** column shows how often the job runs.

The **Job Status** column shows whether the job is Scheduled, Not Scheduled, or has Started.

The **Execution Status** column shows if the job was successfully executed or not. The possible execution statuses include the following:

-   **Success** - The job was successfully executed.
    
-   **Error** - The job failed.
    
-   **In Progress** - The job execution has not yet been completed.
    
-   **Canceled** - The job was canceled.
    

Important:

The execution status is only updated for jobs that you've executed from NetSuite. If you execute a job outside of NetSuite, the status of the job doesn't show in the Execution Status column.
