You need to assign the NSAR Integration role to a user that the SuiteApp will use to integrate with Account Reconciliation application. Use the Administrator role to assign the NSAR Integration role to a new or existing user in NetSuite.

#### To assign the NSAR Integration role to users: {#procedure_0324113746}

1.  Go to Lists > Employees > Employees.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the employee you want to assign the **NSAR Integration** role to.
    
    Important:
    
    Ensure the user you choose for the integration is an active employee. If the employee record has been inactivated or removed, the integration won't work.
    
3.  Click the **Access** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Roles** column, select **NSAR Integration**, and then click **Add**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

This role isn't used by the user to log in to NetSuite.
