You can add the Account Reconciliation Sync prefix to the NetSuite EPM Connector allowlist automatically when you create the related saved searches in NetSuite. For information about creating Account Reconciliation Sync saved searches in NetSuite, see [Creating an Account Reconciliation Sync Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1215084626.html).

#### To add the Account Reconciliation Sync saved searches prefix automatically to the NetSuite EPM Connector allowlist: {#procedure_0313022333}

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Account Reconciliation > Manage Saved Searches.
    
2.  In the upper-right corner of the page, click **Create Default Saved Searches**.
    
3.  (Optional) Check the **Select** box next to the saved search of your choice in the **Not Created** status.
    
4.  Click **Submit**.
    
    This automatically adds the **customsearch\_nsar\_** prefix to the NetSuite EPM Connector allowlist. For information about the NetSuite EPM Connector allowlist, see [Data Source Allowlist](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9115749534.html).
