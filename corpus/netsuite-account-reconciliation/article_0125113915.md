Import the organization unit data in your Account Reconciliation application.

#### To import the organization unit data in Account Reconciliation: {#procedure_1221115930}

1.  Click the **Navigator** icon ![Navigator Menu Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuiteAccountReconciliation/AccountReconciliationSync/ARNavigatorMenuIcon.png), and then click **Configuration**.
    
2.  Click the **Organizations** icon ![Organizations icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuiteAccountReconciliation/AccountReconciliationSync/AROrganizationsIcon.png). Then click the **Organizational Units** subtab.
    
3.  Click the **Import** icon ![Import Icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/NetSuiteAccountReconciliation/AccountReconciliationSync/ARImportIcon.PNG).
    
4.  In the Import Organizational Units popup window, do the following:
    
    1.  Click **Choose File**. In the popup window, locate the CSV file you want to import, and click **Open**.
        
    2.  For **Import Type**, choose one of the following:
        
        -   **Replace**
            
        -   **Replace All**
            
    3.  For **File Delimiter**, select a file delimiter from the list.
        
5.  Click **Import**. When the import is finished, click **OK**.
