If you have the Administrator role, you can assign the NSEPM Implementer role to a new or existing user in NetSuite. This role allows the user to perform the following tasks:

-   Set up the connection to the Account Reconciliation application
    
-   Download the Account Reconciliation configuration package
    
-   Export Account Reconciliation data files
    
-   Create and customize saved searches that are predefined in Account Reconciliation Sync
    
-   Add, customize, and execute jobs for job types that are predefined in Account Reconciliation Sync
    
-   View job execution logs
    
-   View the Jobs Calendar page
    
-   View the Accounts for Group Reconciliation page
    

#### To assign the NSEPM Implementer role to a user: {#procedure_0324113651}

1.  Go to Lists > Employees > Employees.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the employee you want to assign the **NSEPM Implementer** role to.
    
3.  Click the **Access** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Roles** column, select **NSEPM Implementer**, and then click **Add**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
