You can use NetSuite to export your Account Reconciliation Sync configuration into a single file. Then, you can use it as a configuration backup or import it to another NetSuite account.

You can view the history of your imports and exports on the respective **Logs** subtabs at NetSuite EPM > Account Reconciliation > Import/Export Configuration.

For information, see the following topics:

-   [Exporting Account Reconciliation Sync Configuration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0724013448.html)
    
-   [Importing Account Reconciliation Sync Configuration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0724014601.html)
