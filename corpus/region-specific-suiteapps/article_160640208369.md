Note:

To access the LATAM E-Document Certification SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [LATAM E-Document Certification](https://system.netsuite.com/help/helpcenter/shared_resources/PDF/LATAMEDocumentCertification_pt_BR.pdf).

The LATAM E-Document Certification SuiteApp signs your e-documents before sending them to tax authorities. The digital signature authenticates the e-documents.

To sign your e-documents in NetSuite, you'll need to upload your digital certificates to the account. For more information, see [Uploading Digital Certificates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1542656620.html).

You must use valid digital certificates on your account. To be valid, a digital certificate must not be past its expiration date. You can't sign e-documents with an expired certificate.

The digital certificates you upload to your account must also include their private key and all certificates in the certification path. Otherwise, tax authorities may have issues certifying the e-documents signed with the certificates.

For more information about digital signing in NetSuite, see [Digital Signing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1542656608.html).
