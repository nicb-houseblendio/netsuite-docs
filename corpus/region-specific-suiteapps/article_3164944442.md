The Australia Bank Feeds SuiteApp is a single-page SuiteApp that automates the transfer of your bank data from financial institutions domiciled in Australia into NetSuite.

The Australia Bank Feeds SuiteApp enables you to:

-   Connect NetSuite to multiple bank accounts across financial institutions domiciled in Australia.
    
-   Get automated daily imports of bank transactions that are ready for bank reconciliation.
    
-   View information for financial institutions and bank accounts in a single page.
    
-   Map connected bank accounts in your feed to their relevant GL accounts.
    
-   Receive bank transactions daily from your financial institution accounts.
    

When using the Australia Bank Feeds SuiteApp, keep in mind the following:

-   Connection to financial institutions through the Australia Bank Feeds SuiteApp is limited to Australia-domiciled bank accounts.
    
-   The Australia Bank Feeds SuiteApp supports the Multi-Language feature in NetSuite.
    
    However, there may be financial institution websites that use or contain language that NetSuite does not support. In such cases, texts are either in English or in the website's original language when viewed in NetSuite.
    
-   Legal terms contained in documents, records, or websites in languages not supported by NetSuite are not translated.
