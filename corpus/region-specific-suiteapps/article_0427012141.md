France E-document notes provide additional structured information required for certain electronic invoices and credit memos. When France E-document notes are configured on subsidiary level, the workflow automatically populates them on the Invoice or Credit Memo transaction.

#### To enter E-document Note manually:

1.  Go to Transactions > Sales > Create Invoices > List.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the invoice.
    
3.  Go to the **France E-Invoicing** subtab.
    
4.  Select the **Note Subject Code** and the **Note Text**.
    
5.  Click **Add**.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

E-document Notes can be entered manually or they can be copied from the Subsidiary to newly created transaction.

#### To configure E-document Notes for Subsidiaries

1.  Go to Customization > Lists, Records & Fields > Record Types.
    
2.  Locate the EUEI FR E-Doc Notes Subsidiary Config.
    
3.  Click **New Record** in the **New Record** column.
    
4.  On the EUEI FR E-Doc Notes Subsidiary Config page, fill in the following fields:
    
    -   Note Subject Code
        
    -   Note Text
        
    -   Subsidiary
        
5.  Click Save.
    
6.  (Optional) Repeat the configuration for additional subsidiaries as needed.
