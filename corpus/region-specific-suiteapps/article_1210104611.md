The European Union Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp helps NetSuite customers across European Union countries with the processing and certification of electronic documents (e-documents) for several transactions.

This includes features for the following countries:

-   [Belgium-Specific Electronic Invoicing Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0306033635.html)
    
    Includes processing inbound and outbound vendor bill, vendor credit, invoice, and credit memo transactions using the PEPPOL standard in Belgium.
    
-   [Denmark-Specific Electronic Invoicing Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0320040708.html)
    
    Includes processing inbound and outbound vendor bill, vendor credit, invoice, and credit memo transactions using the PEPPOL standard in Denmark.
    
-   [France-Specific Electronic Invoicing Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0511123342.html)
    
    Includes sending, receiving, and processing inbound and outbound transactions in France.
    
-   [Germany-Specific Electronic Invoicing Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0306031929.html)
    
    Includes receiving and processing inbound vendor bill transactions in XRechnung format in Germany.
    
-   [Poland-Specific Electronic Invoicing Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_13173627532.html)
    
    Includes processing outbound invoice and credit memo transactions for NetSuite users in Poland through Krajowy System e-Faktur (KSeF).
    
-   [Spain-Specific Electronic Invoicing Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0306033812.html)
    
    Includes processing outbound invoice and credit memo transactions using the Veri\*Factu system in Spain.
