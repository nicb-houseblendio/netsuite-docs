The Electronic Invoicing Builder Kit feature enables you to implement e-invoicing solutions for the requirements needed for mandates by Service Providers. All the main fields and their sourcing logic are listed in the Sourcing custom data for E-documents section.

The sourcing logic for the France E-invoicing fields: [France mapping table](https://system.netsuite.com/core/media/media.nl?id=483402564&c=NLCORP&h=P-CzAM9CDnKw1G3KFCqD-SSzncOqEsmSdVWovzgZiWO97FzL&_xt=.pdf).

Important:

VAT on Accruals is not used yet. Automation is planned for future version.
