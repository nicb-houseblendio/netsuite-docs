Before installing the European Union Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp, ensure you've met all the prerequisites required for the SuiteApp installation. For more information, see [Prerequisites for Installing the European Union Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1210105542.html).

The European Union Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp is available in the SuiteApp Marketplace.

#### To install the European Union Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp:

1.  In NetSuite, go to SuiteApps.
    
2.  In the search bar, enter **European Union Electronic Invoicing**.
    
3.  Click the **European Union Electronic Invoicing** image.
    
4.  In the top right of the SuiteApp details page, click **Install**.
    
5.  Read the information about the alert, and click **Install** if you want to proceed, or **Cancel** if you don't want to install the SuiteApp.
    

For more information, see [Installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539799323.html).
