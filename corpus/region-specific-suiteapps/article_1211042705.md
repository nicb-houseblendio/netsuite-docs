The Southeast Asia Localization SuiteApp includes Singapore tax invoice and credit note templates. For more information, see [Singapore Invoicing Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1523859369.html) and [Singapore Tax Invoice and Credit Note Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1523862385.html).

When the templates are used for transactions in foreign currency and with multiple tax codes, the information required by tax authorities are included in distinct sections on the printed tax invoice or credit note. For more information, see [Support for Transactions in Foreign Currency](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1523878737.html) and [Support for Transactions with Multiple Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1523883327.html).

The SuiteApp ensures compliance with invoice and credit memo requirements for customer accounting transactions. For more information, see [Compliance with Singapore Customer Accounting Invoice Requirements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1523883492.html).
