The Southeast Asia Localization SuiteApp includes Philippine invoicing features. The SuiteApp provides templates for generating and printing Philippine VAT invoice, VAT official receipt and credit memo. It also has features for adding document numbering, inclusive serial number, and CAS (Computerized Accounting System) registration details on transactions. For more information, see [Philippines Invoicing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157890644903.html).
