The GL Matching SuiteApp is managed and gets updated automatically when new features are added.

#### To install the GL Matching SuiteApp: {#procedure_157043629390}

1.  Go to _Customization > SuiteBundler > Search & Install Bundles_.
    
    Note:
    
    Read the Installation Terms of Service on the use of the NetSuite SuiteBundler. Click **I Agree** if you agree, or **I Disagree** if you don't agree with the terms.
    
2.  In the Keywords field, enter **GL Matching**, or the Bundle ID **254263**.
    
3.  Click **Search**.
    
4.  In the **Name** column, click **GL Matching**.
    
5.  On the Bundle Details page, click **Install**.
    

For more information about installing SuiteApps, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).
