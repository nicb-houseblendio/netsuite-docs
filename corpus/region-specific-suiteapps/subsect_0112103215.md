Mapping services provide responses that adhere to the following structure and content guidelines:

-   **Comprehensive Mapping Data:**
    
    All relevant mapped values are returned in the response.
    
-   **Mapping Status Indicators:**
    
    The response clearly indicates one of the following conditions:
    
    -   The mapping exists, but no results are available (empty mapping).
        
    -   No mapping exists for the specified criteria.
        
    -   Mapping was successfully retrieved and relevant values are provided.
