The North America Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp allows NetSuite users exchange electronic documents (e-documents) between suppliers and buyers (e-documents) for business transactions. The SuiteApp allows you to exchange e-documents and, send and receive acknowledgments for the exchange on the Digital Business Network Alliance (DBNA) network through using Avalara as an access point. For more information, see [North America Electronic Invoicing Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_94105127484.html).
