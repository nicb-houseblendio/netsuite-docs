The Australia Bank Feeds SuiteApp lets you disconnect a single connection under SISS Data Services or disconnect all connections under it.

You will receive an email notification each time a connection is disconnected, and when all connections are terminated.

#### To disconnect a single connection: {#procedure_0515030504}

1.  Go to Transactions > Bank > Australia Bank Feeds.
    
2.  In the Available Connections section, next to the status of the connection that you are deleting, click **Disconnect**.
    
3.  In the Confirmation popup window, click **Proceed**.
    

#### To disconnect all connections: {#procedure_0515030606}

1.  Go to Transactions > Bank > Australia Bank Feeds.
    
2.  In the Available Connections section, next to SISS Data Services' status, click **Disconnect All**.
    
    Warning:
    
    Clicking **Disconnect All** will also deactivate the SISS Data Services in your account. To create a new connection, you must reactivate the SISS Data Services in the account.
    
3.  In the Confirmation popup window, click **Proceed**.
