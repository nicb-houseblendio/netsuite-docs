-   The Southeast Asia Localization SuiteApp includes Thailand tax invoice and credit memo templates. For more information, see [Thailand Invoicing Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1523947449.html) and [Thailand Tax Invoice and Credit Memo Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1523966422.html).
    
-   When the templates are used for transactions in foreign currency and with multiple tax codes, the information required by tax authorities are included in distinct sections on the printed tax invoice or credit memo. For more information, see [Support for Transactions in Foreign Currency](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1524036773.html) and [Support for Transactions with Multiple Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1524037077.html).
    
-   Branch ID field is added in customer and vendor records if your company or any subsidiaries has a Thailand nexus. Branch ID field is also added in sale and purchase transactions. For more information, see [Branch ID Field](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1523962840.html).
    
-   Transaction Amount in Words field is added in sale transactions if the nexus country of the transaction is Thailand. For more information, see [Transaction Amount in Words Field](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1523965899.html).
    
-   Print status is indicated on the printed tax invoice and credit memo if the templates are used. For more information, see [Print Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1524037311.html).
