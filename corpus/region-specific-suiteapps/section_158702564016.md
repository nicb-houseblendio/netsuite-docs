The GL Matching SuiteApp gives you a **GL Matching - List of Accounts** page, where you can find accounts with unmatched transaction lines.

You can filter your search by date, subsidiary (if you use NetSuite OneWorld), or accounting book (if Multi-Book Accounting features are enabled).

Note:

The List of Accounts only searches account types included in GL Matching. For more information, see [Including Accounts in GL Matching](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1516368492.html#subsect_157857887023).

Administrators can access the GL Matching - List of Accounts page by default. To give other roles access, see [Setting Access to GL Matching Pages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157104001050.html#subsect_157892519244).

#### To find unmatched accounts: {#procedure_158702603592}

1.  Go to Transactions > Financial > GL Matching - List of Accounts. For roles that use the Accounting Center, go to Financial > Other Transactions > GL Matching Checklist.
    
2.  Fill in the search filters to narrow your results.
    
3.  Check the **Only with Matchable Amounts** box to search for accounts where both debit and credit amounts can be matched.
    
4.  Click **Show Accounts** to search for accounts with unmatched credit and debit values.
    
    The results are displayed on the **Accounts** subtab.
