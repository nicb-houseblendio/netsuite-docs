The ANZ Localization SuiteApp offers these reports.

-   [Payment Times Report (PTR)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_165287398352.html) - This report is a legislative requirement for large businesses and government enterprises in Australia that have a trade credit arrangement with small businesses. It gives details about payment terms and times for small business suppliers.
    
-   [Taxable Payments Annual Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159410857892.html) - This report is a legislative requirement that certain businesses and government entities in Australia must file to report payments made to contractors for providing services.
    

You can generate these reports from the Country Tax Reports page (Reports > Tax > Country Tax Reports). The page shows the reports you can generate for your account.

If you're using an account with SuiteTax, you can generate all of the above reports. If you're not using SuiteTax, you can only generate PTR and TPAR reports.

To generate a report, see [Generating Localized Country Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528250223.html).

Before you generate these reports, read these topics:

-   [ANZ Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_158677153915.html)
