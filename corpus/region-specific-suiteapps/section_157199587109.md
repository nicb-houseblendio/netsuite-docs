EMEA Localization is a SuiteApp that provides custom lists and fields to transaction forms and item records, so you can collect information for localized tax reports generation in several EMEA countries. Country localization SuiteApps use this tax information to create local tax reports, including VAT Reports, and EC Sales List & Intrastat.

The EMEA Localization SuiteApp also includes several payment templates for EMEA countries that were originally part of the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp. Please note that you'll need to install the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp to use these templates.

You can access localized tax reports and country-specific payment formats for the following countries:

-   Austria (localized payment formats only)
    
-   Belgium
    
-   Czechia (localized payment formats only)
    
-   France
    
-   Germany
    
-   Ireland (localized payment formats only)
    
-   Italy (localized payment formats only)
    
-   Luxembourg (localized payment formats only)
    
-   Netherlands
    
-   Spain (localized payment formats only)
    
-   Sweden (localized tax reports only)
    
-   United Kingdom
    

The EMEA Localization SuiteApp works with and is required for the following SuiteApps:

-   Belgium Localization
    
-   France Localization
    
-   Germany Localization
    
-   Ireland Localization
    
-   Netherlands Localization
    
-   Norway Tax Reports
    
-   Sweden Tax Reports
    
-   United Kingdom Localization
    

For information about the tax reporting fields delivered by the EMEA Localization SuiteApp, see [EMEA Localization Tax Reporting Elements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157236301332.html).

For information about the Electronic Bank Payments templates delivered by the EMEA Localization SuiteApp, see [EMEA Localization Payment Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158858392029.html).
