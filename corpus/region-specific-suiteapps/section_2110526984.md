You can remove bank accounts from your bank feeds and exclude them from any bank data imports.

#### To remove bank accounts from your bank feeds:

1.  Go to Transactions > Bank > Australia Bank Feeds.
    
2.  In the Bank Information section, under the **Bank Accounts** subtab, click **Manage Account**.
    
3.  Check the box for the accounts that you want to remove from your bank feeds and click **Save Changes**.
    
4.  In the Revoke Accounts dialog, click **Revoke** to remove the bank accounts from your bank feeds.
    

Note:

You can add back removed bank accounts to your bank feeds.
