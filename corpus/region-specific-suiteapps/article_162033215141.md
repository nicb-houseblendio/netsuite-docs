You can associate tax regimes and lines of business with subsidiaries, customers, and vendors.

#### To associate a tax regime and a line of business with a subsidiary, customer, or vendor:

1.  Go to the list of subsidiaries, customers, or vendors.
    
    -   For subsidiaries, go to _Setup > Company > Classifications > Subsidiaries_.
        
    -   For customers, go to _Lists > Relationships > Customers_.
        
    -   For vendors, go to _Lists > Relationships > Vendors_.
        
2.  Click **Edit** next to the company or individual you want.
    
3.  In the **SuiteTax Latam Engine Line of Business** field, select the line of business in which the company or individual operates.
    
4.  In the **SuiteTax Latam Engine Tax Regime** field, select the tax regime to which the company or individual adheres.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

To apply rules to the companies or individuals that adhere to certain tax regimes and lines of business, you must create tax determination rules. The rules must use this information as criteria for determining taxes. For more information, see the following help topics:

-   [Creating Tax Determination Rules in the SuiteTax Latam Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162153526964.html)
