The Enable ANZ Features page lets you enable or disable features specific to Australia and New Zealand. To enable a feature, you will need the Administrator role or a role with the required permissions. The page displays a list of reports. Check the list and enable the reports that apply to your business.

#### To enable a feature:

1.  Go to Setup > ANZ Localization > Enable ANZ Features.
    
2.  Check the boxes next to features you want to use.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

If you are using an account with SuiteTax, check the **Taxable Payments Annual Report (TPAR)** box to display the **Settings** tab. To generate a TPAR, select the applicable tax types from the GST and ABN dropdown in the **Settings** tab.
