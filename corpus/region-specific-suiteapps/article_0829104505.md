Note:

To access the SuiteTax Latam Engine SuiteApp documentation in other languages, do the following:

-   To access the documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [SuiteTax Latam Engine](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=SuiteTaxLatamEngine_pt_BR.pdf).
    
-   To access the documentation in Spanish, select Español (Latinoamérica) from the dropdown list at the top right corner of the NetSuite Help Center.
    

The SuiteTax Latam Engine SuiteApp doesn't provide predefined plug-ins or records to calculate taxes for any specific country. The SuiteApp only includes a general plug-in for basic tax calculation. For more information, see [General Tax Plug-in for the SuiteTax Latam Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162645667775.html).

For the following countries, NetSuite offers SuiteApps that implement the SuiteTax Latam Engine and provide predefined records and plug-ins to enable tax calculation.

-   **Brazil** - [SuiteTax Latam Engine - Brazil Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162765674005.html)
    
-   **Colombia** - [SuiteTax Latam Engine - Colombia Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161970452622.html)
