ANZ Localization has the following limitations:

-   The SuiteApp only works with NetSuite editions that have the SuiteCloud Development Framework feature.
    
    Note:
    
    To check if your account has SuiteCloud Development Framework feature enabled, go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_. On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, the **SuiteCloud Development Framework** box is checked.
    
-   The SuiteApp does not support NetSuite JCurve and NetSuite JCurve Financials editions because they do not have the Server SuiteScript and SuiteCloud Development Framework features.
    
-   The SuiteApp supports the following reports and templates for accounts with SuiteTax:
    
    -   Advanced PDF/HTML Templates
        
    -   Business Activity Statement (BAS)
        
    -   GST101A (New Zealand GST Report)
        
    -   Payment Times Report (PTR)
        
    -   Supplier ABN List
        
    -   Taxable Payments Annual Report (TPAR)
        
-   The SuiteApp supports the following reports and templates for accounts without SuiteTax:
    
    -   Advanced PDF/HTML Templates
        
    -   Payment Times Report (PTR)
        
    -   Payment Times Report (PTR) 2025
        
    -   Supplier ABN List
        
    -   Taxable Payment Annual Report (TPAR)
        
-   The SuiteApp inherits the limitations of the Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp in accounts without SuiteTax. For more information, see [Known Limitations of Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156706175950.html).
    
-   The SuiteApp does not automatically migrate data from your current TPAR setup. You must manually set up and update existing records and transactions to properly generate the TPAR for Australia. You can use Mass Update or CSV Import to update your existing records and transactions. To get started, see [Setting Up the Taxable Payments Annual Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159419009511.html).
    
-   The SuiteApp does not support multi-book accounting.
    
-   The SuiteApp allows you to set up company preferences on the ANZ Localization Preferences page using the Administrator role only.
    

For more information about the known limitations of PTR, see [Payment Times Report Limitation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1019093223.html).
