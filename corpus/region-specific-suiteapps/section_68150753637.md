## Foreign Currency Accounts {#bridgehead_0805060058}

The availability of foreign currency accounts using the Australia Bank Feeds SuiteApp varies depending on the connection used when importing bank data.

-   **Bank Data Imported Using a Trusted Adviser and Using Business Disclosure Agreement** - If a bank account is available for selection, the correct foreign currency is displayed.
    
-   **Bank Data Imported Using Direct Data Access** - You must have a numeric account number.
    

## Credit Card Data {#bridgehead_0805060116}

You can connect credit card accounts to the Australia Bank Feeds SuiteApp through a trusted adviser or business disclosure agreement. When connected, credit card data is then included in your bank feeds.

For more information, see the following SuiteAnswers articles:

-   [Importing Bank Data Using a Trusted Adviser in Australia Bank Feeds](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1014242)
    
-   [Importing Bank Data Using Business Disclosure Agreement in Australia Bank Feeds](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1016470/kw/1016470)
