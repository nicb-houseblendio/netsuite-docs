Important:

To use this feature, you must install the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp. For more information,see [Electronic Bank Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1585433.html).

The EMEA Localization SuiteApp includes several payment templates for EMEA countries that were originally part of the Electronic Bank Payments SuiteApp.

These are the payment templates supported by the EMEA Localization SuiteApp.

| Name | Target countries |
| --- | --- |
| BACS - Bank of Ireland | 
-   Ireland
-   United Kingdom

 |
| SEPA Credit Transfer (HSBC) | 

-   Austria
-   Belgium
-   Czechia
-   France
-   Germany
-   Ireland
-   Italy
-   Luxembourg
-   Netherlands
-   Spain
-   United Kingdom

 |
| SEPA Credit Transfer (Austria) | 

-   Austria
-   Germany

 |
| SEPA Credit Transfer (ABN AMRO) | 

-   Belgium
-   France
-   Germany
-   Luxembourg
-   Netherlands
-   Spain
-   United Kingdom

 |
| SEPA Direct Debit (ABN AMRO) | 

-   Belgium
-   France
-   Germany
-   Luxembourg
-   Netherlands
-   Spain

 |
