To access the **Universal Mapping Assistant** page, go to Setup > Universal Mapping Assistant > Mapping Setup. The Universal Mapping Assistant menu item and this path can be accessed by roles belonging to the following centers:

-   **Classic Center roles:** Administrator
    
-   **Accounting Center roles:** Accountant and CFO
    

If you want other roles to access this menu item and this path, you must first grant them a permission. You must also make sure that they belong to one of the centers mentioned above.

#### To configure the audience for the Universal Mapping Assistant page:

1.  Go to Customization > Scripting > Single Page Applications. The system displays a list of all SPAs in your account.
    
2.  On the list, click **Universal Mapping Assistant** to open the management page for that SPA.
    
3.  On the **Configuration** tab, change the **Release Audience**. Click the **Set Up Audience...** button to specify the roles that make up the SPA audience.
    
    -   Check **All Roles** if you want to give access to everyone.
        
    -   Otherwise, select the boxes of the individual roles, click the right arrow to get them displayed on the Current Selections list.
        
4.  Click **Save**.
