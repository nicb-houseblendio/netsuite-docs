To access the LATAM E-Document Certification SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [LATAM E-Document Certification](https://system.netsuite.com/help/helpcenter/shared_resources/PDF/LATAMEDocumentCertification_pt_BR.pdf).

The E-Document Certification Monitor portlet displays information about the e-documents you you've sent for certification from your NetSuite account.

For more information about dashboard portlets, see [Dashboards](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4320707520.html).

#### To add the E-Document Certification Monitor to a dashboard:

1.  Go to the home page.
    
2.  Click **Personalize**.
    
3.  On the Personalize Dashboard menu, select **SuiteApps**.
    
4.  Click the E-Document Certification Monitor portlet, or drag the icon to the dashboard.
    
    The E-Document Certification Monitor portlet appears on your dashboard. The portlet shows the following statuses, which focus on the latest e-document certification requests sent to tax authorities.
    
    -   **Certified Today** - The amount of e-documents that were certified on the current date.
        
    -   **With Certification Errors** - The amount of e-documents that presented errors when their certification requests were sent to tax authorities.
        
    -   **Pending Certification** - The amount of e-documents that are in the queue to be sent to tax authorities for certification.
        
    -   **Pending Cancellation** - The amount of e-documents that are in the queue to be sent to tax authorities for cancellation.
        
    -   **Pending Update** - The amount of e-documents that are in the queue to be sent to tax authorities to update or amend information.
        
    -   **Pending Number Voiding** - The amount of e-documents that are in the queue to be sent to tax authorities to void their numbers.
        
    
    Clicking the amounts under a status opens a results page with a list of the e-documents that match the selected status.
