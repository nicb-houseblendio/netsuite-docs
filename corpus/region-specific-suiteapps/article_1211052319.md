The Southeast Asia Localization SuiteApp includes Malaysia invoice and credit note templates. For more information, see [Malaysia Invoicing Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1552970583.html) and [Malaysia Invoice and Credit Note Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1552972210.html).

Malaysia invoice and credit note templates support transactions in foreign currency and with multiple tax codes. For more information, see [Support for Transactions in Foreign Currency](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1552972090.html) and [Support for Transactions with Multiple Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1552972142.html).
