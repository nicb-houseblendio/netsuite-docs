The EMEA Localization SuiteApp adds several additional tax-related fields to different records, mostly transactions or items. Some of these fields only show up if you select a specific country-level nexus.

These are the additional fields:

| Field Name | Location | Countries | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Company Registration Number | 
-   Company Information record (_Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Company Information_)
-   Subsidiary record (_Setup > Company > Classifications > Subsidiaries > New_)
-   Customer record (_Lists > Relationships > Customers > New_)
-   Vendor record (_Lists > Relationships > Vendors > New_)

 | 

-   Belgium
-   France
-   Germany
-   Ireland
-   Netherlands
-   Sweden
-   United Kingdom

 | Use this field to enter your company's (or subsidiary's) registration number. For example, you can use the Chamber of Commerce registration number, SIRET, or HVB number. Do not enter the VAT registration number into this field. There is a separate field for it in the system. |
| Country of Origin | 

-   Field: **Tax Reporting** subtab of an item
-   Field: **Tax Reporting** subtab of a transaction
-   Line field: **Items** and **Billable Items** sublists

 | 

-   All EU countries
-   United Kingdom

 | Applies to Intrastat - Dispatches reports. Name of the country where the goods were harvested, manufactured, or substantially changed. For more information, see [Country of Origin](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164129144057.html) |
| Financial Contact | 

-   Company Information record (_Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Company Information_)
-   Subsidiary record (_Setup > Company > Classifications > Subsidiaries > New_)

 | 

-   Belgium
-   France
-   Germany
-   Ireland
-   Netherlands
-   Sweden
-   United Kingdom

 | Use this field to enter the email of your financial contact (the person dealing with tax authorities). |
| Incoterm | Tax Reporting subtab of sales and purchase transactions (with EU vendor or customer selected) | 

-   Belgium
-   Ireland
-   United Kingdom

 | This is a three-letter code that indicates the delivery terms. This field is available on Purchase transactions. For more information, see [Delivery Terms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2071234.html). |
| Mode of Transport | Tax Reporting subtab of transactions (with EU vendor or customer selected) | 

-   Belgium
-   Germany
-   Ireland
-   Netherlands

 | This is a one-digit code of the active vehicle that moves goods for every arrival or dispatch transaction. For more information, see [Mode of Transport](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1505110665.html). |
| Nature of Transaction Code | Tax Reporting subtab of transactions (with EU vendor or customer selected) | 

-   Belgium
-   France
-   Germany
-   Ireland
-   Netherlands
-   Sweden
-   United Kingdom

 | The code that indicates the type of transaction in the Intrastat declaration. For more information, see [Nature of Transaction Code](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157104660646.html). |
| Region of Destination (Land) | Tax Reporting subtab of transactions (with EU vendor or customer selected) | 

-   Belgium
-   France
-   Germany

 | This field applies to Intrastat - Arrivals. It refers to the region of an EU member state where the arriving goods are intended to remain and be used, or consumed, or treated. For more information, see [Region of Origin and Destination](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157104892019.html). |
| Region of Origin (Land) | Tax Reporting subtab of transactions (with EU vendor or customer selected) | 

-   Belgium
-   France
-   Germany

 | This field applies to Intrastat - Dispatches. It refers to the region of an EU member state of dispatch where the goods were produced or manufactured, assembled, processed, repaired or maintained, or where the commercial process took place. For more information, see [Region of Origin and Destination](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157104892019.html). |
| Statistical Procedure | Tax Reporting subtab of transactions (with EU vendor or customer selected) | 

-   Netherlands

 | The two-digit code indicating the particularization of the commodity flow in Netherlands. For more information, see [Statistical Procedure](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157224377384.html). |
| Statistical Value | Each item line of a transaction (with EU vendor or customer selected) | 

-   Germany
-   Ireland

 | It refers to the value of the goods from the German border. For more information, see [Statistical Value](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157224225209.html). |

To learn more about the nexuses in SuiteTax and their levels, see [Nexus Records in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4797203207.html).

For more information about EC Sales List and Intrastat reports, read the following topics:

-   [EC Sales List](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156888850442.html)
    
-   [Intrastat Supplementary Declaration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156889008497.html)
    

To generate an Intrastat Arrivals or Dispatches report, or EC Sales List, see [Generating Localized Country Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528250223.html).
