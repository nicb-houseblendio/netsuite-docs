The following describes the steps to customize the European Union (EU) country list to be used in the Revenue and Expense Reclassification by Location feature. For more information, see [Revenue/Expense Reclassification by Region](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1552020655.html).

The Revenue and Expense Reclassification by Location SuiteApp provides a default European Union (EU) country list to be used for your reclassification rules. You can customize this list according to your business needs.

#### To add new country to the European Union country list:

1.  Go to Setup > Custom > Setup Reclass. By Region Rules.
    
2.  Click the **European Union Countries** subtab.
    
3.  Click **New EU Country**.
    
4.  In the **Country** field, enter the country name.
    
5.  In the **Effective From** field, enter a date.
    
6.  (Optional) In the **Valid Until** field, enter a date.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
