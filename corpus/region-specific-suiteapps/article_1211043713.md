By mapping transaction forms with the following Philippine document type templates, you can generate and print contents in the format prescribed by the BIR:

-   Sales Invoice
    
-   Billing Statement
    
-   Collection Receipt
    
-   Official Receipt
    
-   Credit Memo
    

For more information, see [Philippines Transaction Form Mapping and Invoicing Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157890872746.html) and [Roles and Permissions for Philippines Invoicing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157891587459.html).
