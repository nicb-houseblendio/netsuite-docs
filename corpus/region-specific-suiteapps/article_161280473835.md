Note:

To access the LATAM E-Document Certification SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [LATAM E-Document Certification](https://system.netsuite.com/help/helpcenter/shared_resources/PDF/LATAMEDocumentCertification_pt_BR.pdf).

The LATAM E-Document Certification SuiteApp provides you the infrastructure to communicate with tax authorities' web services. If you are an administrator, you can create tax authority certification services to submit e-documents for certification.

Read the following topics to learn how to create a tax authority certification service and implement the communication with a tax authority's web service in NetSuite.

-   [Prerequisites for Creating a Certification Tax Authority Implementation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161348196395.html)
    
-   [Creating a Custom Plug-in Implementation for a Tax Authority](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161349601331.html)
    
-   [Creating a Tax Authority Certification Service Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161280503180.html)
    
-   [Cloning a Tax Authority Certification Service Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161946929232.html)
