If your NetSuite account has an active Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp license, you can use electronic invoicing features for multiple countries. You can still use the Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp for free if:

-   Your account does not have an active license, or
    
-   You intend to send e-documents to only one country
    

For more information, see [Defining the E-Document Country for Free Use](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4613215788.html).

Important:

To use the North America Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp, you must have the NetSuite Electronic Business Extension with NetSuite Electronic Document Processing Cloud Service license. If you do not have license the, please contact your account administrator.

To setup the North America Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp, read the following topics:

-   [Setting Up a Subsidiary Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0605115133.html)
    
-   [Setting Up a Customer Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0605115158.html)
    
-   [Setting Up a Vendor Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1106075018.html)
    
-   [Setting Up North America Tax Type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0605115214.html)
