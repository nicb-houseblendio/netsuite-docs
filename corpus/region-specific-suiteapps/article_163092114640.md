The Localization Assistant SuiteApp enables you to identify and install the SuiteApps that are mandatory to a specific location. The SuiteApp also lists recommended NetSuite SuiteApps that are optional based on the best practices in your country of operation.

The Localization Assistant SuiteApp also supports NetSuite Record Localization Context feature.

Localization Assistant SuiteApp helps manage the components of localization or partner SuiteApps by using the features of the following SuiteApps.

-   Electronic Bank Payments
    
-   Electronic Invoicing
    
-   Tax Reporting Framework
    

The SuiteApps must first integrate with Localization Assistant SuiteApp to manage the components. For more information, see [Managing the Components of Localization or Partner SuiteApps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0906052517.html)
