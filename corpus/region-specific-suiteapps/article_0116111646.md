Specific tax calculation basis or tax rate amounts may apply to transactions for which you calculate taxes with the SuiteTax Latam Engine. For example, you may have an edge case or an exceptional scenario that doesn't fit the applicable tax determination rules.

With the SuiteTax Latam Engine SuiteApp, you can override the tax calculation basis and tax rate of a given transaction. By overriding the tax basis and tax rate for the SuiteTax Latam Engine, you ensure that NetSuite recalculates the tax amounts accordingly.

Before overriding the tax basis and tax rate, ensure that your account meets the following requirements:

-   The applicable tax determination settings have the tax basis and tax rate override option enabled.
    
    For more information, see [Creating Tax Determination Settings in the SuiteTax Latam Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162153528705.html).
    
-   The applicable SuiteTax Latam Engine plug-in is prepared to consider the overridden tax basis and tax rate information.
    

Important:

SuiteTax's tax details override option is not supported on transactions to which the SuiteTax Latam Engine applies.

Be aware that you can only override tax bases and tax rates directly on transaction records. It's not possible to override tax bases and tax rates with scripts or CSV imports, for example.

#### To override the tax basis or tax rate for the SuiteTax Latam Engine: {#procedure_72111736611}

1.  Access the transaction in edit mode.
    
2.  Click the **Tax Details** subtab.
    
    Tip:
    
    If you're creating the transaction, click **Preview Tax** before trying to override the tax basis and tax rate.
    
3.  To override the tax calculation basis, in the **Tax Basis Override for the SuiteTax Latam Engine** column, enter a new tax basis.
    
4.  To override the tax rate, in the **Tax Rate Override for the SuiteTax Latam Engine** column, enter a new tax rate.
    
    Enter the tax rate in percentage.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
