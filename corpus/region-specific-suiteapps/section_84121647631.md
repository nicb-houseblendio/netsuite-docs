You can choose to receive an email notification for every scheduled bank data import. By default, no email notifications are sent until you choose to receive them.

#### To receive email notifications for bank data imports:

1.  Go to Transactions > Bank > Australia Bank Feeds.
    
2.  In the Bank Information section, click the **Bank Import History** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Receive Email Notification for Imports** box.
    
4.  Click **Confirm**.
