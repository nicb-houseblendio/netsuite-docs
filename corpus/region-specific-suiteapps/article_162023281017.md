In some countries, certain economic activities are subject to different taxation criteria. In NetSuite, a line of business record identifies the type of economical activity a company or individual conducts. You can associate a line of business with subsidiaries, vendors, or customers. For more information, see [Associating Tax Regime and Line of Business With Subsidiaries, Customers, and Vendors](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162033215141.html).

To apply certain tax rules to specific economic activities, you should associate the line of business with a tax determination rules record. For more information, see [Creating Tax Determination Rules in the SuiteTax Latam Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162153526964.html).

#### To create a line of business:

1.  Go to Setup > Latam Tax Determination > Line of Business > New.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter a name to identify the line of business.
    
3.  In the **Country** field, select the country where the business is conducted.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
