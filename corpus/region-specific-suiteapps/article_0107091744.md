The Universal Mapping Assistant SuiteApp enables you to add, edit, and delete mappings. It offers the following benefits and features:

-   Creation of mappings that are compliant with country- or region-specific tax reporting.
    
-   Different types of compliant mappings from NetSuite lists/entities and values required by the country's tax authority.
    
-   Automatic mapping of elements.
    
-   Consistency of the mapping approach across regions and SuiteApps.
    
-   Limitation of duplications resulting from each SuiteApp's own mapping system.
    

The Universal Mapping Assistant SuiteApp currently offers the following mapping types:

-   [Mapping Type: GL Accounts SAF-T 2.0](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0109102936.html)
    
-   [Mapping Type: Methods of Payment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0109103130.html)
    
-   [Mapping Type: Units of Measure](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0608120554.html)
    
-   [Mapping Type: Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0609054259.html)
