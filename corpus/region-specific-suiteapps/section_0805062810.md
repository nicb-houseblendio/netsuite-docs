You can view the status of bank account connections in the Bank Information section.

#### To view the bank account connection status:

1.  Go to Transactions > Bank > Australia Bank Feeds.
    
2.  In the Bank Information section, under the **Bank Accounts** subtab, look for the **Bank Account Status** column.
    

The following table displays the typical bank account connection statuses for all connection types.

| Bank Account Connection Status | Scenario |
| --- | --- |
| Not Yet Available | Connection and setup is in progress. |
| Updating | Connection has been established. Bank is yet to initiate account feeds. |
| Active | Bank feeds are successfully being imported. |
| Problem Encountered | Problem encountered in the bank feeds. Specific reason is sourced from SISS and displayed in the **Status Reason** column. |
