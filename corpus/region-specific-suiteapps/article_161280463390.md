To access the LATAM E-Document Certification SuiteApp documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [LATAM E-Document Certification](https://system.netsuite.com/help/helpcenter/shared_resources/PDF/LATAMEDocumentCertification_pt_BR.pdf).

The LATAM E-Document Certification SuiteApp enables you to create and set up the submission of e-documents to tax authorities.

As an administrator, you're able to set up communication with tax authorities' web services to fit local requirements and your business needs.
