You can specify details of your Permit To Use (PTU) or Acknowledgement Certificate, the CAS document issued by the BIR, on any of the following:

-   Subsidiary record
    
-   Location record
    
-   Company Information page
    

Details of the CAS document you specify, are indicated on printed Philippine documents. For more information, see [Support for Simplified System Registration for CAS Users](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161531133306.html) and [Permit To Use (PTU) for Computerized Accounting System (CAS)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157891320550.html).
