Create a case with NetSuite Customer Support to report tax calculation issues when:

-   A tax is not properly calculated
    
-   An error occurs in a transaction during the tax calculation
    

When you create the case, include the relevant tax details information from the transaction records you're reporting.

#### To troubleshoot the tax calculation in SuiteTax Latam Engine:

1.  Go to the transaction record.
    
2.  Click the **Tax Details** subtab.
    
3.  Under the **Taxes** sublist, in the **Details** column, copy the content inside the column.
    
    The column displays the following information about each tax code applied to the transaction:
    
    -   **Tax determination rules ID** - The internal ID of the tax determination rules record applied to the transaction.
        
    -   **SuiteTax Latam Engine plug-in ID** - The internal ID of the plug-in used to calculate taxes on the transaction.
        
    -   **Tax calculation detail** - Additional information provided by the plug-in.
        
4.  Paste the content from the **Details** column in your support case.
