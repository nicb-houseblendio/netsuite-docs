The GL Matching SuiteApp has a safeguard that checks if a matched group of transaction lines is linked to more than one account, subsidiary, or accounting book.

A group of transaction lines can only be linked to one account, one subsidiary, and one accounting book. If multiple associations are found, you'll get an email notification.

To resolve the issue, you'll need to unmatch the group in the account, subsidiary, or accounting book where they don't belong. Click **Group Details** in the email to go to the GL Matching Dashboard and unmatch the group. For more information, see [Undoing a Group of Transaction Lines](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157856282756.html#subsect_157856392418).
