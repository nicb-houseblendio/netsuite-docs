-   To access the documentation in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil), see [SuiteTax Latam Engine](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=SuiteTaxLatamEngine_pt_BR.pdf).
    
-   To access the documentation in Spanish, select Español (Latinoamérica) from the dropdown list at the top right corner of the NetSuite Help Center.
    

To calculate taxes with the SuiteTax Latam Engine SuiteApp, you must set the SuiteTax Latam Engine as the tax engine in your subsidiary's tax registration.

#### To set the SuiteTax Latam Engine as the subsidiary's tax engine:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Classifications > Subsidiaries_.
    
2.  Next to the subsidiary for which you want to calculate taxes with SuiteTax Latam Engine, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Assign a tax registration to the subsidiary.
    
    1.  Click the **Tax Registration** subtab.
        
    2.  Enter the relevant information.
        
        For more information, see [Assigning Tax Registrations to a Subsidiary in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4296038273.html).
        
    3.  In the **Tax Engine** column, select **SuiteTax Latam Engine Plug-in**.
        
    4.  Click **Add**.
        
4.  Click **Save**.
