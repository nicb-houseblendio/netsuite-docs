The ANZ Localization SuiteApp gives you the localization features, templates, and financial reporting needed to run businesses in Australia and New Zealand.

The SuiteApp adds these features and capabilities to your account:

-   Additional setup and rules for records and transactions tailored to customers and users in Australia and New Zealand.
    
-   Automatically updates new localization features and enhancements for Australia and New Zealand accounts.
    
-   Tax report templates to help you generate these reports on the Country Tax Reports page for businesses in Australia and New Zealand:
    
    -   [Payment Times Report (PTR)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_165287398352.html)
        
    -   [Payment Times Report (PTR) 2025](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0119100852.html)
        
    -   [Taxable Payments Annual Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159410857892.html)
        
-   A configuration page where you can set up your payment times report details for Australia accounts.
    
-   Advanced PDF/HTML templates let you print Australia-specific documents and email them. Plus, you can configure document printing preferences on the ANZ Document Printing Preferences page.
    
-   One place to handle all the setup tasks for the ANZ Localization SuiteApp. For details, visit t ls, [Using the ANZ Localization Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0329052723.html).
    

The ANZ Localization SuiteApp works best for NetSuite Australia edition, NetSuite JCurve Premium edition, and NetSuite OneWorld and International edition with Australia or New Zealand subsidiaries.

If you want to install the ANZ Localization SuiteApp, see e [Installing ANZ Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_158677437293.html).

For information about the limitations of ANZ Localization, see [Known Limitations of ANZ Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_158677431009.html).
