You must setup a subsidiary record before you start using the North America Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp. You must have access to Administrator role to setup the subsidiary record.

#### To setup the subsidiary record:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Subsidiary Settings Manager.
    
2.  Select a subsidiary and click **Edit** on the subsidiary record.
    
3.  Fill the required fields on the subsidiary record.
    
4.  The selected country for the subsidiary must be United States.
    
5.  In the **Legal Name** field, enter the legal name of the subsidiary.
    
6.  From the **North America Identification Type** list, select one of the following identification types:
    
    -   Data Universal Numbering System (DUNS)
        
    -   Employee Identification Number (EIN)
        
    -   Global Location Number (GLN)
        
    -   Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)
        
    -   Social Security Number (SSN)
        
7.  In the **North America Identification Number** field, enter the corresponding identification number based on the identification type you select from the **North America Identification Type** list.
    
8.  In the **Address** subtab, fill the required fields for North America's local address.
    
9.  Click **Save**.
    
    Note:
    
    You must complete the NetSuite Electronic Business (NSEB) setup and activate mandates required for the subsidiary. For more information about setting up NSEB and mandates, see [NetSuite Electronic Business Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0313022549.html).
    
    The same subsidiary must be set on the e-document templates and e-document transaction response templates. For more information, see [Editing E-Document Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543300222.html).
