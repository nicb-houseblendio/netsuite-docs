This report shows expenses over different periods by functional expense and program segments. It compares the previous month to the current month, and the previous year to the current year.

#### To view the Comparative Expense Summary report:

1.  Go to NFP Financial Center > Expense Reports > Comparative Expense Summary.
    
    NetSuite displays the report.
    
2.  You can click on amounts to view more transaction details.
    

To print the report, click the print icon ![Print button](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SocialImpact/FooterIconPrint.png) at the bottom of the page.
