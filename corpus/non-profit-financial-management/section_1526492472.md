The NFP Financials and NFP Reports SuiteApps have reports to meet nonprofit reporting needs. You can also use any standard NetSuite reports you find useful. For general information about NetSuite reports, see [Reporting Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N698474.html).

Nonprofit-specific reports are available from the NFP Financial Center tab. This section includes the following report categories:

-   [Non-Profit Financial Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1526492826.html) - includes financial statements for U.S. subsidiaries that are required by the Financial Accounting Standards Board (FASB), as well as other financial reports
    
-   [Non-Profit Revenue Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1526492843.html) - various reports related to revenue, such as program revenue, revenue stream distribution, and giving statistics
    
-   [Non-Profit Expense Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1526492854.html) - various reports related to expenses, such as program expense detail and net assets by program and grant
    

The NFP Reports bundle includes the following reports:

-   [Statement of Activity](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1534097812.html)
    
-   [Statement of Cash Flow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1537569297.html)
    
-   [Statement of Financial Position](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1537569353.html)
    
-   [Statement of Functional Expense](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1537569375.html)
    
-   [Grant Statement of Activity](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1537909702.html)
    
-   [Reports in Social Impact Standard Edition](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1562914072.html)
    

The remaining reports are included in the NFP Financials bundle.

For details on reports for revenue to release from restrictions, see [Revenue Restriction Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1526407369.html).

Several other reports specifically related to grants are included on the Grant Management menu. For details on grant reports, see [Grant Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1518029025.html).

For general information about reports, see [Working with Report Results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N717676.html).

For information about customizing reports in NetSuite, see [Report Customization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N736328.html).
