The expense reports provide relevant details on program and grant expenses and net assets.

The following reports are included:

-   [Program Expenses by Year](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1534105367.html)
    
-   [Program Expense Detail](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1534105326.html)
    
-   [Net Assets by Program](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1534101485.html)
    
-   [Grant Expenses by Year](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1537977836.html)
    
-   [Grant Expense Detail](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1537987850.html)
    
-   [Net Assets by Grant](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1526492915.html)
    
-   [Comparative Expense Summary](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1537924242.html)
