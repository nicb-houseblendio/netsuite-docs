This report lists all grants with outstanding payments (payment isn't received in full and is overdue).

This is a summary grouped by the Grant Revenue Type

If donors do not pay as promised, you can write off the grant. See [Writing off Pledge or Grant Receivables](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1525122459.html).

The report is a saved search. Saved searches let you filter and sort NetSuite data in advanced ways. For information about saved searches, see [Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N675442.html).

#### To view the Overdue Grant Payments report:

1.  Go to NFP Financial Center > Grant Management > Overdue Grant Payments.
    
2.  To view invoice details for an overdue grant, click on the Document No in the row.
    

To print the report, click the print icon ![Print button](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SocialImpact/FooterIconPrint.png) at the bottom of the page.
