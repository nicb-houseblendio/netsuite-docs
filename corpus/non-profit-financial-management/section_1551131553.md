To tag an account as a Grant Receivable account, go to the Edit Account page, and then select **Grant Receivable** from the **Applies To** list.

If a donor did not pay as promised, you can write off the grant. To write off grant receivables, see [Writing off Pledge or Grant Receivables](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1525122459.html).

To invoice a grant opportunity, see [Invoicing Grant Opportunities](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1551133580.html).
