Non-Profit SuiteApps tackle the unique accounting and reporting challenges nonprofits face. These industry-specific SuiteApps give you data structures, roles, and dashboards for nonprofit accounting and constituent management.

Nonprofits can connect their fundraising solution with NetSuite using structured integration points in the data model. Alternatively, nonprofits can use the SuiteApps as a basic fundraising management solution.

Important:

The Non-Profit SuiteApps are available only for North America (NoAM), United Kingdom (UK), Australia, and New Zealand (ANZ).
