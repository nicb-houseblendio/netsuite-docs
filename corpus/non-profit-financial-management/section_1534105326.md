This report shows functional expense details by program, department, region, vendor, transaction type, date, and amount.

#### To view the Program Expense Detail report:

1.  Go to NFP Financial Center > Expense Reports > Program Expense Detail.
    
    NetSuite displays the report.
    
2.  At the bottom of the page, you can change the values in **Date** or the **From** and **To** fields.
    
3.  You can click on amounts to view more transaction details.
    

To print the report, click the print icon ![Print button](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SocialImpact/FooterIconPrint.png) at the bottom of the page.
