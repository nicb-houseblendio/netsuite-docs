This report shows details of restricted revenue transactions by account. Click an amount in the report to go to the transaction record.

The total in this report matches the restricted revenue total in the Statement of Activity for the same period.

#### To view the Restricted Revenue by Account report:

1.  Go to NFP Financial Center > Financial Reports > Restricted Revenue by Account.
    
    NetSuite displays the report.
    
2.  At the bottom of the page, select values in **Date** or **From** and **To** fields.
    
3.  Click on an amount to go to the transaction record.
    

To print the report, click the print icon ![Print button](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SocialImpact/FooterIconPrint.png) at the bottom of the page.
