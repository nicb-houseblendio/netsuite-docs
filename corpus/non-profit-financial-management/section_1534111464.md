This report shows details of restricted revenue transactions by program. Click an amount in the report to go to the transaction record.

#### To view the Restricted Revenue by Program report:

1.  Go to NFP Financial Center > Financial Reports > Restricted Revenue by Program.
    
    NetSuite displays the report.
    
2.  At the bottom of the page, select values in **Date** or **From** and **To** fields.
    
3.  Click on an amount to navigate to the transaction record.
    

To print the report, click the print icon ![Print button](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SocialImpact/FooterIconPrint.png) at the bottom of the page.
