Before automatically creating configurable subscriptions:

1.  Make sure your account includes the SuiteBilling features.
    
    Note:
    
    Contact your NetSuite representative to have the SuiteBilling features provisioned for your account.
    
2.  In NetSuite CPQ Configurator, go to the options record at CPQ > Configurator > Settings. Under the **Submit** subtab, check the **Add new resources to transaction fields** box.
    
    This option makes the automatically created subscription plans and their associated records available in transaction dropdown fields that contain fewer than 300 options.
    
    Note:
    
    The option may be incompatible with custom scripts deployed on transactions.
    

After implementing the prerequisites, work with the subscription plan-either standard or configurable. For more information, see [Creating a Standard Subscription Plan](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0120030010.html) and [Creating a Configurable Subscription Plan](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0120025603.html).
