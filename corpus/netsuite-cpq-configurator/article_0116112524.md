The Configurable Items page shows all your active products. From this page, users can choose the items they want to configure. You can let users access this page from third-party websites and configure items outside of NetSuite.

To integrate this page into your third-party website, first make products available externally. Then, create the link you'll use in your implementation.

#### To launch the Configurable Items page externally:

1.  Make configurable items available externally.
    
    1.  Go to CPQ > Configurator > Settings.
        
    2.  Check the **Launch products from SuiteCommerce** box.
        
2.  Define the specific configurable items that users can open from your website.
    
    1.  Go to the product record of each configurable item you want users to access.
        
    2.  Check the **Available on SuiteCommerce** box.
        
3.  Create the link you'll use in your third-party website.
    
    1.  Go to Customizations > Scripting > Script Deployments.
        
    2.  In the **Type** field in the Filters section, select Suitelet.
        
    3.  Find and view the **CPQC-ST-SCA-Wrapper** script (ID customdeploy\_cpqc\_st\_sca).
        
    4.  Verify that the **Available without login** box is checked.
        
    5.  Click the link in the **External URL** field. A new browser tab will open.
        
    6.  Append the `&cmd=selector` string to the link in the browser's address bar.
        
    7.  Press **Enter** to verify that the Configurable Items page loads correctly.
        

After creating and testing the link, you can use it in your third-party website to show the Configurable Items page and let users configure items outside of NetSuite.
