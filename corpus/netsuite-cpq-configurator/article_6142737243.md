With SuiteBilling, you can manually create standard subscriptions and their related records. For more information, see [SuiteBilling Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1520360275.html). Alternatively, you can automatically create configurable subscriptions and associated records with NetSuite CPQ Configurator. Subscriptions and related records will be created in your account after users submit the configuration.

When working with configurable subscriptions, the most common use case is to manually create the associated subscription plan with SuiteBilling. If your business frequently adds new services and you want to create them automatically, you can use NetSuite CPQ Configurator to create configurable subscription plans.

Automatically creating subscriptions and associated records involves several NetSuite CPQ capabilities. Before working with configurable subscriptions, become familiar with these NetSuite CPQ capabilities:

-   Item creation records. For more information, see [Creating Items Required by the Configuration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7115625340.html).
    
-   Creation records. For more information, see [Creating Records Required by the Configuration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_2131623448.html)
    
-   Mapping records. For more information, see [Setting Transaction Body and Line Fields Based on Configuration Data](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_8153202018.html).
