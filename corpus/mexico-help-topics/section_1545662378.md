Note:

Before installing the Mexico Localization SuiteApp, read the [Mexico Localization SuiteApp Limitations and Best Practices](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1549538596.html) topic to know its current limitations.

Important:

You can install and use the Mexico Localization SuiteApp in these different configurations:

-   OneWorld accounts with SuiteTax enabled.
    
-   OneWorld accounts without SuiteTax enabled.
    
-   Accounts that are not OneWorld and without SuiteTax enabled.
