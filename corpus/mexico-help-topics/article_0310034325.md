The Mexico Complementary Features SuiteApp provides a predefined Certification Monitoring plug-in for you to receive from tax authorities the details about the current status of your certification requests.

To keep the certification information of your transactions and records updated, create a Certification Monitoring plug-in implementation.

#### To create a certification monitoring plug-in implementation:

1.  Go to Customization > Plug-ins > Plug-in Implementations > New.
    
2.  In the **Script File** field, select **mcf\_pl\_cert\_monitor.js**.
    
3.  Click **Create Plug-in Implementation**.
    
    NetSuite opes the Select 2.0 Plug-in Type page.
    
4.  In the **Name** field, enter **MCF Certification Monitoring**.
    
5.  In the **ID** field, enter **\_mcf\_pl\_cert\_monitor**.
    
6.  In the **Status** field, select **Released**.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
