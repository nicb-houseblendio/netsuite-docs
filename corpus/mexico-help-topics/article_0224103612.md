The Mexico Certification Tax Authorities SuiteApp expands the capabilities included in the Mexico Localization SuiteApp by providing additional features such as electronic invoicing cancellation from the Servicio de Administración Tributaria (SAT) through the authorized Proveedor Autorizado de Certificación (PACs): Solución Factible and MySuite.
