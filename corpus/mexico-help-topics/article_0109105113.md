The Mexico Complementary Features SuiteApp expands the capabilities included in the Mexico Localization SuiteApp by providing additional features such as accessing the Mexico Electronic Accounting Files reports and supporting transactions that involve general public customers.

The Mexico Electronic Accounting Files are a set of reports presented in a format supported by the Servicio de Administración Tributaria (SAT).

This SuiteApp provides support for transactions that involve general public customers, including invoices and cash sales.

To see the list of terms used in NetSuite to support Mexico-specific business processes, see [Mexico Terminology](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1538999964.html).
