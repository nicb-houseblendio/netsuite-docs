Before using the Mexico Complementary Features SuiteApp, be aware of the current limitations and best practice recommendations.

## Limitations of the DIOT 2025 Files {#bridgehead_0611093439}

-   Partial payments are not supported for expense reports and vendor credit.
    
-   Expense reports are included regardless of being paid or unpaid.
    
-   The **Tax Transaction Type** field under the Tax Details tab for DIOT and DIOT 2025 reports needs to be filled in by selecting the **Domestic** or **Import** options. In accounts with SuiteTax, the field is automatically filled in, except if you click on the **Tax Detail Override** option. Make sure you fill in the field if that is the case.
