Important:

Before installing the Denmark Tax Reports SuiteApp, make sure you're done with all necessary prerequisites. To learn more, see [Setting Up Denmark Tax Reports SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_66163815558.html).

The Denmark Tax Reports SuiteApp is available in the SuiteApps Marketplace.

#### To install the Denmark Tax Reports SuiteApp

1.  Go to SuiteApps.
    
2.  In the Search field, enter **Denmark Tax Reports**.
    
3.  Click the Denmark Tax Reports image.
    
4.  Click **Install**.
