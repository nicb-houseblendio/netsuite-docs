The report structure is based on the SAF-T standard format by the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD). The report has the following sections:

-   [Header](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1211043308.html) - General information about the company and the audit file, name and version of the software product, and selection criteria.
    
-   [Master Files](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1211042701.html)\- Information about general ledger account, suppliers, customers, products and tax rate tables.
    
-   [General Ledger Entries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1211040925.html) - Information about accounting transactions: journal entries and details of the posting transactions.
    
-   [Source Documents](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1209054613.html) - Invoices and transaction details during the selection period.
    

Important:

All the fields marked as Optional aren't included in the generated report in the current version of the Denmark Tax Reports.

Note:

All the Address fields only include the first line of the address. The Number field of the address is always empty.

Note:

The Period Year reported in GL Entries corresponds to the year of the period's end date (in case the start date and end date fall in different calendar years).
