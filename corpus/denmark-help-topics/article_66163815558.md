## Enable the required features {#subsect_31145919522}

Before installing Denmark Tax Reports SuiteApp, you must enable all the required features.

#### To enable required features:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features (Administrator)_.
    
2.  Go to the **Company** subtab.
    
3.  On the **International** subtab, check the **Multiple Currencies** box.
    
    Note:
    
    Multiple Currencies feature is required if your Denmark nexus is on a subsidiary with base currency other than DKK. In that case, the SAF-T report will primarily be denominated in that subsidiary's base currency, not in DKK, as required by the Denmark Business Authority.
    
4.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Accounting Preferences_.
    
5.  Go to the **General** subtab.
    
    On the **General Ledger** subtab, check the **Use Account Numbers** box.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
7.  Make sure that the account Number field is filled in for all accounts that are included in the report. To check that, go to: Setup > Accounting > Chart of accounts. Click **Edit** on an account record and fill in the **Number** field if it's empty.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
