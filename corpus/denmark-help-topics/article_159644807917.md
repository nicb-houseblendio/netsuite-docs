**Denmark BBAN/IBAN Nordea** is the Country-specific and Bank-specific payment file template, which is stored on a Payment Template custom record.

The custom Payment Template is stored under Setup > Customization > Payment Template. Click **Edit** on the template you want to use. The fields are the same as on the NetSuite Payment Template record.

Copy the respective values and paste them onto the standard NetSuite Payment File Format record:

Select the newly created Payment Template as the EFT template when creating Company Bank Details record according to NetSuite Electronic Payments documentation.

Create or edit Entity Bank Details records to use newly created Payment Template for your entities according to NetSuite Electronic Payments documentation.
