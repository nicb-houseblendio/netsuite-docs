Setting up an account for an organization doing business in Denmark usually requires enabling several features and installing some additional SuiteApps.

## Enabling Features {#bridgehead_157494708586}

Some features available in your NetSuite account can be very important or even essential for users in Denmark. If they're inactive, you should consider enabling them. Follow the instructions below to enable the most common features beneficial for users in Denmark. However, if you want to get access to some specific product area, go to the [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_157477118810.html#bridgehead_160700044867) help topic and check the exact requirements.

If you don't have sufficient permissions to enable features, please contact your account administrator.

#### To enable most common features:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **Company** subtab, go to the **International** subtab, and check the **Multi-language** and **Multiple Currencies** boxes.
    
3.  (Optional) On the **Accounting** subtab, go to the **Basic features** subtab, and check the **GL Audit Numbering** box.
    
4.  (Optional) On the **Accounting** subtab, go to the **Advanced features** subtab, and check the **Period End Journal Entries** and **Foreign Currency Variance Mapping** boxes.
    
5.  On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, enable the following features:
    
    -   SuiteBuilder
        
    -   SuiteScript
        
    -   SuiteGL
        
    -   SuiteFlow
        
    
    For more information about SuiteCloud features, see [SuiteCloud Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N239332.html).
    
6.  If your company is registered for EU One Stop Shop, on the **Tax** subtab check the **EU One Stop Shop** box.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

## Available SuiteApps {#bridgehead_160700060791}

Some additional features will only become active if you install a specific SuiteApp to your NetSuite account. SuiteApps are applications that extend NetSuite for specific industry and business needs. If you'd like to get access to a specific product area, see the [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_157477118810.html#bridgehead_160700044867) help topic and check which SuiteApps are required to be installed. Denmark-specific features are currently available in the following SuiteApps:

| SuiteApp | Area | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Electronic Bank Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1585433.html) | Payments | Enables bank payments of vendor bills, employee expenses, partner and employee commissions, customer refunds, and customer payments within Denmark. |
| [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html) | Taxation (Accounts without SuiteTax) | For accounts without SuiteTax, the International Tax Reports SuiteApp is used to generate the Denmark VAT reports in Danish or English, and generic sales and purchase reports by tax code, the generic EU sales list report, and the Intrastat report. |
| Supplementary Tax Calculation | Taxation (Accounts without SuiteTax) | Manages EU reverse charges though automatic posting of notional VAT to the General Ledger, and provides support for the calculation of non-deductible input tax. This SuiteApp works in conjunction with the International Tax Reports SuiteApp and isn't compatible with SuiteTax. For more information, see [Setting Up Tax Codes to Post Notional Tax Amounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4702880164.html) and [Recording Nondeductible Input Tax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1822590.html). This SuiteApp also supports VAT on prompt payment discounts. For more information, see [Recording Tax on Prompt Payment Discount](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4702990460.html). If the SuiteTax feature is enabled in your NetSuite account, don't install the Supplementary Tax Calculation SuiteApp. |
| [Tax Audit Files](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2073244.html) | Taxation (Accounts without SuiteTax) | This SuiteApp lets tax payers share their electronic records with tax auditors in support of their tax returns, and helps auditors review accounting records. **This feature isn't currently required by law in Denmark.** |
| [SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1557396965.html) | Taxation (Accounts with SuiteTax) | For accounts with SuiteTax, the SuiteTax Engine sets default tax codes based on local tax requirements. You must install these SuiteApps in the following order:
1.  SuiteTax Engine
2.  Tax Reporting Framework

 |
| [Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1528262949.html) | Taxation (Accounts with SuiteTax) | This SuiteApp enables to generate VAT reports, tax audit files, EU Cross-Border reports (Intrastat and EC Sales List). **Currently there aren't any reports available for Denmark.** You must install these SuiteApps in the following order:

1.  SuiteTax Engine
2.  Tax Reporting Framework

 |
| [Denmark Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159602682368.html) | 

-   Accounting
-   Taxation (Accounts without SuiteTax)
-   Payments

 | Adds localization features for Denmark. Important: This SuiteApp isn't compatible with SuiteTax. |
| [Denmark Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_32140350988.html) | Taxation (Accounts without SuiteTax) | Lets you generate the Denmark Standard Audit File for Tax (SAF-T) report. |
| [One Stop Shop SuiteApp Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162921054692.html) | Taxation (NetSuite Accounts With or Without SuiteTax) | Enables the EU VAT One Stop Shop Reports (OSS) generation. OSS is categorized into three specific schemes: Non-Union OSS Scheme, Union OSS Scheme, and Import OSS Scheme. These schemes allow taxable persons to declare and pay VAT due in Member States where the taxable persons aren't established. |
