**EPM**

An acronym for Oracle Enterprise Performance Management.

**NetSuite EPM**

NetSuite Enterprise Performance Management (EPM) runs on Oracle Fusion Cloud EPM and integrates with NetSuite ERP.

**NSAR**

An acronym for NetSuite Account Reconciliation.

**NSPB**

An acronym for NetSuite Planning and Budgeting.
