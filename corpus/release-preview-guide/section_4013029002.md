Report any issues you encounter during testing by calling NetSuite Customer Support or by submitting a case online.

## Call NetSuite Customer Support {#bridgehead_4345829052}

Use one of the phone numbers in the following table to call NetSuite Customer Support:

Note:

These phone numbers are valid for customers in the United States and Canada. If you are calling from another country, include the appropriate exit code for your country.

| Region | Phone Number |
| --- | --- |
| All locations | 1.877.774.4271 |
| US - local | 1.925.948.1084 |

#### To submit a case:

1.  Go to SuiteAnswers in your Release Preview account. You can access SuiteAnswers in the following ways:
    
    -   In the NetSuite user interface:
        
        -   Click **Support > Go to SuiteAnswers**.
            
        -   Click **Support > Support Overview**, and in the SuiteAnswers portlet, click **Explore SuiteAnswers**.
            
    -   In the NetSuite Help Center, click the **SuiteAnswers** link on the toolbar.
        
2.  Click the **Contact Support** link.
    
3.  Under Online Support, click **Create Support Case** and provide details of your issue.
    
    A case is created in the NetSuite Support system under your Release Preview account and is flagged as a problem.
