You should request a Release Preview account to verify that your existing business workflows function as expected in the new release. This helps to ensure a smooth and seamless upgrade of your production account(s) to the new NetSuite release.

Note:

The main goal of a Release Preview account is for you to test your business workflows to prevent any potential issues you might encounter and to ensure everything functions as expected with the newly released NetSuite features. If you find any issues during testing, see [How to Report Release Preview Issues](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4013029002.html). You are **not** expected to test the new features for NetSuite.

Release Preview account data is taken from a backup copy of your production data after a refresh is requested. It will take approximately 5-7 calendar days to create your Release Preview after you submit your request. The Snapshot Date of a refresh determines when a copy of all configurations, data, user passwords, and customizations from your source account are copied into your target account.

Various factors determine how long it takes to refresh your Release Preview account, including the size of your production account, the number of integrations in your production account, and the number of customers requesting a refresh during the same period as your request was submitted. To request a refresh for your Release Preview account, contact NetSuite Customer Support.

Notifications with details about Release Preview availability are sent to administrators and Release Preview availability information is posted on the New Release portlet.

Note:

The New Release portlet is located on your home page dashboard in your production account. If you do not see the portlet, see [Add the New Release Portlet to Your Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4013011333.html#subsect_162326453804).

Your Release Preview account is available until your source account is upgraded to NetSuite 2026.2 or until there has been no login activity for 14 consecutive days, in which case the Release Preview account will be purged.

Note:

Release Preview access ends on the date your production account is upgraded to the new release. This date is listed in your New Release portlet.

## Requesting a Release Preview Account {#subsect_1544193526}

If you would like to receive a 2026.2 Release Preview account, a user with the Administrator role should opt in to Release Preview.

Note:

You can request a Release Preview account using either your production or sandbox account. If you would like to use a specific sandbox to test the new release, request your Release Preview from that sandbox account.

#### To opt in to Release Preview: {#procedure_46144040463}

1.  From your production or sandbox account, go to Setup > Company > Release Preview.
    
2.  Click **Request Release Preview**.
    
    Note:
    
    After a request has been made, you can monitor the progress of your request in the Account Status and Account Notifications fields. Users with the Administrator role receive an email notification when your Release Preview account is ready to use.
    

## Release Preview Notifications {#subsect_29095223450}

Release Preview email notifications are sent to users with the Administrator role.

In addition to the Release Preview reminder and the Upgrade reminder email messages, in-account notifications are also sent to administrators and others. You can ask your administrator to set you up to receive in-account Service Release Notifications. For more information, see [Administrative Notification Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N258657.html).

Note:

You are responsible for keeping the names of your administrators up-to-date. To make changes to these positions, please contact your account manager about updating the contact records. Be aware that if these contacts have 'opted out', they will not receive these important notifications. To opt back in, see [Subscription Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N994827.html).
