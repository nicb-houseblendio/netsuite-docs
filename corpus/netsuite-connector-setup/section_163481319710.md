You can manage the notifications you receive from NetSuite Connector in the following areas:

-   For NetSuite and billing notifications, check your NetSuite account. In the NetSuite Connector menu, go to Settings > Notifications and set up your preferences.
    
-   For notifications to a particular connector, go to NetSuite Connector. Select the connector, then click **Notifications**.
    

NetSuite Connector sends notifications only when your account is processing that type of feed.

For example, if you don't set up NetSuite Connector to process custom record updates for NetSuite, setting the notification won't have any effect. On the Notifications page for NetSuite, even if you enter your email address in the **Send NetSuite Custom Record Update Error Notifications To** field, you will not receive any notifications.
