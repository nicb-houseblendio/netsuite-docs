To create a NetSuite Connector account, you need a valid license. To purchase a license, contact your NetSuite account manager. After you purchase the license, your NetSuite account manager will provide information to help you create your NetSuite Connector account.

Buying a license doesn't create an account automatically. Create your account manually using the sign-up link your account manager provides. This may be part of a larger set of information materials you are given regarding your NetSuite Connector license.
