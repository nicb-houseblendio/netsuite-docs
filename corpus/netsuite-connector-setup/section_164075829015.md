You can disable a connector or remove a connector from NetSuite Connector. To disable a connector, make it inactive.

#### To disable or remove a connector:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the connector and then select the relevant account that you want to disable or remove.
    
3.  Go to Data Flows > Manage Data Syncs.
    
4.  Click the **Toggle this connector active/inactive** button to switch it to **OFF**.
    
    The connector will be disabled.
    
5.  To remove the connector, click the **Trash** icon next to the **Toggle this connector active/inactive** button.
