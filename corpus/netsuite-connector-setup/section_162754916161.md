Add connectors to transfer data between marketplaces/carts and NetSuite.

Contact your NetSuite Account Manager to add a new connector or account.

## Authorizing a Connector {#bridgehead_162755087967}

After creating a connector, authorize it to enable data transfer.

#### To authorize a connector:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select a connector and then select the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Settings > Credentials.
    
    The Credential Settings page opens. Credentials vary depending on the connector. This page has a link to instructions for getting credentials from the marketplace or cart.
    
4.  Enter the required information.
    
5.  Click **Save Settings**.
    

## Enabling the Connector and Account {#bridgehead_162755165704}

To test the sync, enable your connectors and account first.

Note:

Enabling connectors doesn't automatically enable individual syncs

#### To enable a connector and an account:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the connector and then select the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Data Flows > Manage Data Syncs.
    
4.  Click the **Toggle this connector active/inactive** button on the top right to enable the activation.
