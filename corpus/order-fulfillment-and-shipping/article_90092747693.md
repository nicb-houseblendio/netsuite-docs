Note:

This documentation is about NetSuite Ship Central only, which is a separate shipping solution from NetSuite Connector's ShipStation connector. For information about the connector, see [Managing the ShipStation Connector in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163162030389.html).

NetSuite Ship Central enables packing or shipping capabilities with multi-carrier integration within your NetSuite account. It also provides the app that lets you pack and ship orders through a tablet or kiosk device. Based on your fulfillment requirements and setup, you can pack or ship through the app or NetSuite UI. If you set up shipping for the app, you can process orders packed through the app or NetSuite UI, separately. Without packing capabilities in your account, you can process orders for shipping only.

NetSuite Ship Central, together with SCM Mobile, extends packing and shipping capabilities to enable you to:

-   Pack items into cartons and transfer packed cartons onto pallets
    
-   Identify picked items across multiple orders for a customer and pack them at the same time
    
-   Ship through carriers that you integrate with your NetSuite ShipStation API account
    
    If you want to enable shipping, you must create a ShipStation API account within NetSuite. Accounts created outside of NetSuite cannot be integrated with Ship Central. For more information about the NetSuite ShipStation API account, see [https://www.shipengine.com/netsuite/](https://www.shipengine.com/netsuite/).
    
-   Use the app to ship through the regular shipping flow or to generate labels through bulk shipping, or use the NetSuite UI to ship directly from item fulfillments
    
-   View estimated and actual shipping costs for a variety of shipping services and options
    
-   Gain visibility into which items are packed across multiple cartons or pallets and shipped out of your location

## Ship Central Availability {#subsect_3141135712}

As NetSuite's standard shipping solution, the NetSuite Ship Central SuiteApp is available for all NetSuite accounts.

## Ship Central Supported Transactions and Item Types {#subsect_14092812049}

NetSuite Ship Central supports the following types of transactions and items:

-   Sales and transfer orders in Pending Fulfillment status
    
-   With picking and packing capabilities, item fulfillments in Picked status for packing and in Packed status for shipping
    
    You can ship orders packed through the NetSuite UI or WMS separately from those packed through the Ship Central app.
    
-   Items that belong to the following supported item types:
    
    -   Inventory and assembly items, including, matrix, lot numbered, and serialized items
        
    -   Kit/package items and item groups
        
    -   Non-inventory items and, if you use Warehouse Management, service items
        
        To pack both types, items must be fulfillable and either for sale or for resale.
        
        The following item types cannot be assigned tariff codes, so they cannot be shipped internationally: service item for resale and service item for sale.
        
    -   Items that ship individually, if enabled on the item record
        
    -   HazMat or dangerous goods items, if enabled on the item record
        
        This option applies to inventory and assembly items that you want to ship locally through USPS only.
        
    
    For more information about item settings, see [Creating Items for Ship Central](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1111040001.html).
