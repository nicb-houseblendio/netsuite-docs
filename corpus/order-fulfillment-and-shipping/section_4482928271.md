The way in which NetSuite assigns locations automatically to sales order lines is determined by the settings of an automatic location assignment configuration and the rules in the configuration. You need to create at least one configuration and then create at least one rule and add it to the configuration. Both the sequence of rules in a configuration, and the attributes of the rules themselves, are used by the automatic location assignment engine to assign the ideal fulfillment location.
