NetSuite can send an email notification to the customer when all items in a fulfillment request have been picked and the order is ready to be picked up. You can check if the pickup email notification was successfully sent to the customer.

Note:

The pickup email message is only sent when the fulfillment request status is Picked.

Note:

The order ready for pickup email notification is sent only if the setting is turned on. See [Store Pickup Email Notifications](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4817105615.html) for more information.

#### To check pickup email messages sent:

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Customers_ and open the customer record you want to work with.
    
2.  Click the **Communications** subtab.
    
3.  Click **Messages** to view all email messages sent to the customer from your NetSuite account.
    
4.  To view the content of an email message, click View or the message number.
