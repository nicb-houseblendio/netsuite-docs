### How can I get assistance when setting up or when I encounter issues within my NetSuite ShipStation API account?

On [ShipEngine's Help Center](https://help.shipengine.com/hc/en-us), you can find the following topics related to requirements or capabilities supported within NetSuite Ship Central:

1.  To set your billing details, see [ShipEngine Carriers US](https://help.shipengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406863496219-ShipEngine-Carriers-US).
    
    If you choose to add funds, make sure that you always have a sufficient amount to cover all shipping costs and charges.
    
2.  To connect your carriers, see [Connect Your Carriers](https://help.shipengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/12932815059355-Connect-Your-Carriers).
    
3.  For supported carriers, to add a company logo and assign it as the default logo, see [Create Custom Shipping Labels With Images/Branding](https://www.shipengine.com/docs/labels/branding/)
    
4.  For international shipping documents, if your carrier requires a letterhead and signature, you can upload them accordingly. See [FedEx Integration Requirements](https://help.shipengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406863434523-FedEx).
    
5.  For information about ParcelGuard for your NetSuite ShipStation API account, see [ParcelGuard](https://help.shipengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/26099415057435-ParcelGuard).
    

For further assistance or inquiries, contact NetSuite Support.

For troubleshooting guidelines on common issues during packing or shipping, see [Handling Common Packing and Shipping Issues](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1207104019.html)
