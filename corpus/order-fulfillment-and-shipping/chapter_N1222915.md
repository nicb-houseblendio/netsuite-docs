Fulfilling an order is shipping the items to fill an open order. When you fulfill an order, you pull the items from inventory, package them and send them to the recipient. You can fulfill sales orders that go to customers or transfer orders that go to other company locations. See [Order Fulfillment Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4737787881.html) for a summary of the main aspects of order fulfillment in NetSuite.
