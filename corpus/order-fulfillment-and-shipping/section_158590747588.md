If you are an Administrator, you can create new roles or customize roles for the Bill of Lading SuiteBundle. For more information, see [Assigning Roles to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N897798.html) and [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html).

## Adding Center Category and Center Types {#bridgehead_158624475673}

For custom roles, add the Bill of Lading center category to the Lists tab. Use the following information when creating the center category:

-   Center category label - Bill of Lading
    
-   Add the following links:
    
    -   Bill of Lading Preferences
        
    -   Bill of Lading
        
    -   Bill of Lading Template
        

For more information, see [Creating Center Categories](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2890916.html) and [Creating Center Links](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2891250.html).
