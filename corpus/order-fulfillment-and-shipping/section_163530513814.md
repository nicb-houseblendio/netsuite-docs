Bill of Lading includes predefined templates, which you can readily use to print stand-alone and master bill of lading documents.

You can also create custom templates using Advanced PDF/HTML Templates. For more information, read the following help topics:

-   [Creating a Custom Bill of Lading Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158626549146.html)
    
-   [Adding a Custom Bill of Lading Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158626557591.html)
    
-   [Removing a Custom Bill of Lading Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161882994230.html)
    

On the Bill of Lading Preferences page, you can select the default template to use when printing stand-alone bill of lading. For more information, see [Setting Bill of Lading Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158626558182.html)

The default template is used when you print directly from an item fulfillment transaction. When you print from the Print Bill of Lading page, you can choose the stand-alone or master bill of lading template that you want to use.
