You can view submitted pickup requests to look for any upcoming ones. Before the earliest pickup time of a scheduled pickup, you can submit a cancellation request.

The app automatically cancels a pickup request when all associated shipping labels have been voided. For more information about voiding labels, see [Voiding Shipping and Return Labels](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0921101257.html).

You may ask your carrier for any guidelines or limitations regarding pickup cancellations. After you submit a pickup request, you can't add or remove shipments but you can cancel it. If you try to create a new request, the original scheduled date and time may not be available anymore, depending on your carrier.

#### To view or cancel scheduled pickups:

1.  From the Options menu, tap **Manage Pickups**.
    
2.  On the Manage Pickups page, tap **View Pickup Details** or **Cancel Pickup**.
    
3.  On the next page, do one of the following:
    
    Tip:
    
    If you can't find a request or to find earlier requests, change the date filter to **All** or to another number of months. Tap the table icon to open the popup window with the **Filter By Date** setting. For more information, see [Filtering Table Data by Date](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0307034044.html).
    
    -   From the Pickup list, tap the pickup request number.
        
        Note that Date columns appear in UTC format and follow your Time Zone general preference.
        
    -   For pickup cancellations only, in the **Scan/Enter Label** field, scan or enter a shipping label, order number, or pickup request number. For orders packed through the app, you can also provide the pack carton ID. If you enter the data, tap **Cancel Pickup** to proceed.
        
4.  On the popup window, you can view the details, and then tap **OK** to confirm and close it.
    

Shipments removed from a canceled pickup can be included in a new pickup request. For instructions, see [Scheduling Pickups with Carriers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0717022004.html).
