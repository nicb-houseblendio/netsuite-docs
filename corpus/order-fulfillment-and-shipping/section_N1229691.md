The Pick, Pack, and Ship feature lets you fulfill sales orders in different stages: you first pick the items, then you pack the items, and finally you ship the items. You can use pick, pack, and ship with advanced shipping, which lets you bill sales orders in a separate transaction. Otherwise, NetSuite bills the items in a sales order when you mark the items as shipped.
