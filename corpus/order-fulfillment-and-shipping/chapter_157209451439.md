It is a common practice in the food and beverage industry that delivery trucks operate on predefined routes. Each route contains multiple orders for multiple customers. The Static Route Management SuiteApp helps in distribution management of trucks and routes.

You can perform the following actions using the Static Route Management SuiteApp:

-   Create truck records
    
-   Define routes with stops and truck information
    
-   Add route, stop, and delivery time window information to a sales order
    
-   Filter and view routes and orders assigned to the routes
    
-   Change the stop sequence for orders in a route
    
-   Move orders between routes as well as ship dates
