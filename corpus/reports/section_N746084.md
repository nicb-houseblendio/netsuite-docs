Inline editing of reports gives you all the usual inline editing features. If you're editing a lot of reports, it might be quicker to use the mass update feature. See Mass updating of report ownership.

#### To change the owner of several reports: {#procedure_N746104}

1.  Go to: _Reports > Saved Reports > All Saved Reports_.
    
2.  Switch inline editing on.
    
3.  Arrange the list so the reports you want to edit are on the same page.
    
4.  Click the owner of one of the reports. A dropdown list will show all possible owners. Click the owner again to hide the list.
    
5.  Hold down the Ctrl key and click the owner in another row to add that row.
    
6.  Add all the rows you want to edit, then release the Ctrl key.
    
7.  Click the dropdown list and pick the new owner for the selected rows.
    
8.  Click away from the owner column, and the selected reports will update right away.
