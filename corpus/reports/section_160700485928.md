You can follow the steps below to add your company's logo to your report. The logo on your report is the one selected in the Company Logo field at _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Company Information_.

#### To add your company logo to your report: {#procedure_N745853}

1.  Go to _Home > Set Preferences_.
    
2.  On the **Analytics** subtab, check Print Company Logo.
    
    Note:
    
    This setting adds the company logo to printed reports only. To see the logo on onscreen reports, make sure you check the **Display Report Title on Screen** box on the Set Preferences page or in the report footer.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    
4.  Run the report
