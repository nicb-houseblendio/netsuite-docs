Click this button ![Collapse Footer icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Reports/FooterCollapse2023.png) to view only the top levels of the report.

This setting is saved, so you don't have to click this button every time you view the report. Also, this setting stays the same when you print, email, schedule, or export the report to Microsoft Word or Adobe PDF.
