Date ranges are available as alternate options in customized reports. For reports using accounting periods, period ranges are used as filters instead of date ranges. A date range shows a span from one time to another. See [Choosing a Date or Period Range for a Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N725800.html). The following assumptions are for the purposes of date range examples.

-   Fiscal quarters fall under May 1-July 31, August 1-October 31, November 1-January 31, and February 1-April 30, however, fiscal quarters can vary depending on your business preference.
    
-   In the United States, a week starts on a Sunday.
    
-   Calendar weeks for a month start with the previous Sunday before the first day of the month, so a calendar week for August could begin in July.
    
-   Fiscal periods are calendar months by default-but yours may be different. In this example, the report gives a snapshot as of July 31 at 11:59 p.m.
    
-   Calendar weeks for a month start the Sunday before the first of that month. That means a week for August could start in July.
    

For more information about counting behavior descriptors, see [Behavior Descriptors for Selectors](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4125904112.html).
