An **Aging Options** button ![Aging options button](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Reports/aging2.png) on the footer provides options for aging reports.

Click the **Aging Options** button, and a box opens with options for aging.

![Aging footer options.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Reports/aging3.png)

When you click the Age As Of dropdown arrow, a list of selectors appear.

![Age As Of dropdown Available Selectors.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Reports/aging4.png)

You can use Quick Filters to filter selectors by Period, Quarter, or Year. For example, if you select the Quick Filter of Year, the list includes only selectors containing the word **Year**.

![Year Quick Filter aging options.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Reports/year.png)

You can type into the search field a selector's value and filter selectors so that only selectors that match the search will appear. In the following screenshots, the selectors are filtered by the search term **fiscal**.

![Aging search field](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Reports/fiscal.png)

You can make a selector a favorite by putting a star next to the selector. Selectors with stars appear under Favorites, making them more accessible.

![Example of making a selector a favorite.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Reports/uiredesign3.png)
