You can't delete a standard report in NetSuite. However, you can delete a custom report if Report Customization is enabled and you have permission to view the report.

#### To delete a custom report:

1.  Go to _Reports > Saved Reports > All Saved Reports_ and find the custom report you want to delete.
    
2.  Click the **Delete** icon ![Delete report icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Reports/DeleteReport_icon.png) in the **Delete** column.
    

Note:

Deleted custom reports can't be recovered.
