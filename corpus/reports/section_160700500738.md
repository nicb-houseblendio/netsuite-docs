You can add a custom description to your report by entering text in the Description field on the Report Builder (or Financial Report Builder) page. This text has a limit of 200 characters.

As you add customization options to your report, this field updates with text describing your selections.

Note:

After you edit this field, the system won't update the text automatically when you add more customization options.

#### To add a custom description to your report: {#procedure_N745968}

1.  Click **Customize** in the footer of the report.
    
2.  Click **More Options**.
    
3.  In the **Description** field, enter a custom description, or add customization options and the field updates automatically .
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    
5.  Run the report.
    
6.  Click the **Options** button in the footer of the report.
    
7.  Check the **Print Description** box.
    
8.  Click **Close**.
