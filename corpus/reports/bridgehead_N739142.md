When needed, you can change the order of columns in your report.

#### To reorder columns on your report : {#bridgehead_N739151}

1.  Click **Customize** in the footer of the report.
    
2.  On the Report Builder (or Financial Report Builder) page, click **Edit Columns**.
    
3.  In the Report Preview pane, select the column you want to move.
    
4.  Use the **Move** buttons to move the column to the left or right.
    
5.  Repeat steps 3 and 4 to reorder more columns on your report.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

In the Report Preview pane, you can also drag columns to change their order.

![Columns in the Report Preview pane.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Reports/date2.png)

Click and hold any area within the column or the edit icon. When you see an icon with double arrows, you can move the column to a new position in the preview.
