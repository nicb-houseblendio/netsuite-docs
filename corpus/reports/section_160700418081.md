Adding horizontal and vertical grid lines makes it easier to identify related data in each row.

#### To Add Grid Lines to Your Report {#procedure_N745683}

1.  Click the **Options** button in the footer of the report.
    
2.  In the **View** field, select **Report-Grid**.
    
3.  Click **Close**.
