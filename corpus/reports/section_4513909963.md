After you've finished customizing the report in the Report Builder, you can save it as a custom report.

#### To save the report as a custom report:

1.  On the **Edit Columns** tab of the Report Builder, use the **Name** field to give your custom report a descriptive name.
    
2.  When ready, click **Save** in the lower right corner of the Report Builder.
    

If you want to make more changes to the report after saving, you can find the report in your saved custom reports at _Reports > Saved Reports > All Saved Reports_. To customize the report further, click Edit next to its name. For more information, see [Viewing or Editing a Saved Custom Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N748491.html).
