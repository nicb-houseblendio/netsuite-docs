Click this button ![Expand report footer option.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Reports/FooterExpand2023.png) to view all top and sublevels of the report.

This setting is saved, so you don' need to click this button each time you view the report. Also, this setting is maintained when you print the report, email the report, schedule the report, and export the report to Microsoft Word or Adobe PDF.
