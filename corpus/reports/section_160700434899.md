Viewing your report in Microsoft® Word or Excel lets you access more tools to add color, highlight sections, change fonts, and make other changes. If you open your report in Microsoft® Excel, the formulas NetSuite used to calculate totals are exported as well.

#### To View Your Report in Microsoft® Word or Microsoft® Excel {#procedure_N745740}

1.  On the Reports page, click the report you want to view.
    
2.  In the report footer, select Export - Microsoft® Word, Export - Microsoft® Excel, or Export - PDF.
    
3.  When the File Download window appears, click **Open**.
