Subsidiary Navigator enables you to limit the information displayed on your dashboard, searches, and reports to a specific subsidiary or group of subsidiaries. The portlet has a chart where you can select the subsidiary whose records you want to display. Choosing a subsidiary is equivalent to setting the Restrict View to a subsidiary option in _Home > Set Preferences_.

Note:

Subsidiary Navigator only works for NetSuite OneWorld.
