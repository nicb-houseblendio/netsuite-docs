Note the following impact on netting settlements if you edit netted transactions:

-   If you unapply some transactions that were netted, some amount of the netting settlement will be available as credit that can be applied again. This available netting settlement as credit won't appear on the Netting Workbench. That means, the Netting Workbench will display a smaller amount than the netted amount.
    
-   If you unapply all netted transactions, the Netting Workbench may not show any netted transaction. View the corresponding netting settlements to see more details.
