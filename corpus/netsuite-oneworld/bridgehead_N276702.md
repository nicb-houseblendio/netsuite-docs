The subsidiary you assign to a partner limits the records that the partner can access to those associated with that subsidiary.

#### To assign a subsidiary to a partner: {#procedure_N276716}

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Partners_, and then click the **Edit** link next to a partner.
    
2.  Select from the **Subsidiary** list and click **Save**.
