The Multi-Subsidiary Customer feature enables you to view a multi-subsidiary customer's hierarchy (primary subsidiary, any assigned secondary subsidiary, and any subcustomer). The customer hierarchy is located on the Customers list page at _Lists > Relationships > Customers_.

Note:

In the customer hierarchy, users can view only those subsidiaries to which they are granted access.
