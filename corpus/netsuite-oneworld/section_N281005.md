NetSuite OneWorld lets you run your global sales force within a single NetSuite implementation.

Each customer, or other entity, is associated with a single subsidiary, letting you track forecasts and quotas on a subsidiary basis.

For more information about specific tasks, see the following:

-   [Consolidated Quotas and Forecasts in OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N281162.html)
    
-   [Employee and Partner Commission in OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N281296.html)
    
-   [Subsidiaries on Online Customer Forms in OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N282433.html)
