The Multi-Subsidiary Customer feature lets you create a multi-subsidiary vendor record from a multi-subsidiary customer record. This customer vendor relationship produces a single entity. This entity is useful when you want to use one entity to represent both a customer and a vendor on core transactions.

Note:

The customer and vendor records must share the same primary subsidiary to create a multi-subsidiary customer vendor relationship.

For information about creating relationships between record types, see [Records as Multiple Types](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1099012.html).
