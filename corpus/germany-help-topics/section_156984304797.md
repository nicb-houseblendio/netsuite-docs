To access the Central Bank Reports settings, go to Setup > Customization > Central Bank Reports. Use this screen to customize the behavior of the Germany Central Bank Reports SuiteApp.

## Subsidiary-specific Settings {#bridgehead_156984678557}

The following settings must be separately specified for each subsidiary within this NetSuite account:

-   **Reporting Company Number:** Unique registration ID assigned to this company by the German Central Bank. Must be 8-9 digits long.
    
-   **Archivation Email:** Enter an email address to which generated reports will be sent for archivation purposes. Whenever a report is exported, both versions (XML and PDF) will be delivered there. For more information, see [Viewing and Exporting Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_156993154162.html).
    
-   **Contact Person Information:** Use this section to add contact information for each subsidiary. This information will be a part of every report generated by the Germany Central Bank Reports SuiteApp (for this particular subsidiary).
