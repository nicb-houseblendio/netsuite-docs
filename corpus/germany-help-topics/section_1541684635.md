Before installing the Germany Localization SuiteApp, your account administrator must enable several features and preferences in your NetSuite account.

## Required Features {#bridgehead_160759942380}

To install Germany Localization SuiteApp and use Germany-specific functionality, you must enable required features.

#### To enable required features

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, check the following boxes:
    
    -   **Custom Records** - Required to create custom records such as report templates and precalculated balances.
        
    -   **Advanced PDF/HTML Templates** - Required to render templates and to generate PDF files.
        
    -   **Client SuiteScript** - Required to enable client-side scripts for financial statements.
        
    -   **Server SuiteScript** - Required to deliver bundles.
        
    -   **Accounting** - Required to use NetSuite for your business accounting.
        
    -   **GL Audit Numbering** - Required to apply gapless numbering sequences to all general ledger posting transactions.
        
    -   **Multi-language** - Required to display UI elements and generated-file content in different languages.
        
    -   **SOAP Web Services** - Required to enable programmatic access to your NetSuite data and business processes through an XML-based application programming interface (API).
        
    
    For more information, see [Enabling Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N232138.html).
    
3.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Preferences > Accounting Preferences_.
    
4.  On the **General** subtab, check **Use Account Numbers** box. For more information, see [Chart of Account Numbering](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1440268.html).
    
5.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Manage G/L > Manage Accounting Periods_.
    
6.  Click **Set Up Full Year**. For more information, see [Accounting Period Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1445585.html).
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

To complete the account setup, you should also enable the SuiteTax Plug-In and assign a tax registration to a subsidiary. However, this isn't required. If you don't want to use the SuiteTax-related features of the Germany Localization SuiteApp, you can skip the following instruction.

Enabling the SuiteTax Plug-In permits you to select SuiteTax as your tax engine. You can enable the plug-in at _Customization > Plug-Ins > Manage Plug-Ins_. For more information, see [Enabling the SuiteTax Plug-In](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557399475.html).

#### To add a tax registration for a subsidiary

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Classifications > Subsidiaries_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the subsidiary.
    
3.  On the **Tax Registrations** subtab, select a country, nexus, and tax engine for this nexus.
    
4.  Enter a tax registration number.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

For more information, see [Assigning Tax Registrations to a Subsidiary in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4296038273.html).

## Required SuiteApps {#bridgehead_1542966980}

Before installing the Germany Localization SuiteApp, you must first install the **EMEA Localization SuiteApp**. This SuiteApp enables you to create SuiteTax-based tax reports for certain countries in the EMEA region. For more information, see [Installing the EMEA Localization SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157201390489.html).

## Recommended SuiteApps {#bridgehead_157251718488}

To use the additional functions of the Germany Localization, the following SuiteApps are required:

| SuiteApp | Description | Installation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contra Account Sheet | This SuiteApp enables companies to generate comprehensive transaction reports containing contra account information. | To install, see [Installing Contra Account Sheet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157830256115.html) |
| Germany Central Bank Reports | This SuiteApp enables German companies to track and report incoming and outgoing payments to fulfill the requirements of the German Central Bank. | To install, contact your account administrator. |
| Tax Reporting Framework | This SuiteApp helps business meet the tax determination and reporting requirements. Necessary for generation of tax reports for Germany. | To install, see [Installing Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528245293.html#bridgehead_1528246665). |
| Country-Specific Reports | This SuiteApp serves as a framework supporting various localization SuiteApps. It's required to display different country-specific financial statements. | To install, see [Installing the Country-Specific Reports SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1526393807.html) |
| Electronic Bank Payments | This SuiteApp enables you to process bank payments of your vendor bills, employee expenses, partner and employee commissions, and customer refunds, and also enables you to receive bank payments from customers. | To install, see [Electronic Bank Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1585433.html). |

For more information about installing SuiteApps, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).
