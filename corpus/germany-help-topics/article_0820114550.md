The DATEV Connector Role SuiteApp is currently available as a public SuiteApp in the SuiteApp Marketplace.

The DATEV Connector Role SuiteApp is a managed bundle, and is automatically updated whenever improvements or new features are added.

#### To install the DATEV Connector Role SuiteApp:

1.  Go to SuiteApps.
    
2.  In the search bar, enter **DATEV Connector Role**.
    
3.  Click the DATEV Connector Role image.
    
4.  Click **Install**.
    
5.  Read the information on the alert, and click **Install** if you want to proceed, or **Cancel** if you don't want to install the SuiteApp.
    

Note:

The NetSuite DATEV Connector is a third-party solution offered by fybu (formerly Digitastic). It manages the setup and testing of the connection based on each customer's specific processes and requirements. For further details, including information about the DATEV Connector Role setup, please contact info@fybu.de.
