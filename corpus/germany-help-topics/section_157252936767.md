Germany localization SuiteApp offers SuiteTax-based Germany-specific tax reports compliant with requirements of German tax authorities. After installing the Germany Localization SuiteApp, Germany-specific tax reports become available for installation. Germany Tax Reports require the following SuiteApps:

-   [Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1528262949.html)
    
    Note:
    
    If the Tax Reporting Framework is installed in your account prior to the installation of Germany Localization SuiteApp, the Tax Reports component will be installed automatically.
    

Following tax reports for Germany are available:

-   [Germany Recapitulative Statement (EU Sales List)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_165399151339.html)
    
-   [Intrastat Dispatches Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156889008497.html#subsect_156889317966)
    
-   [Germany Periodic VAT Return - USt 1A](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156775791511.html)
    
-   [Germany Yearly VAT Report - USt 2A (v2023)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0121103232.html)
    
-   [Germany Audit Files: GoBD Data Extract](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539585682.html)
    

#### Installing Germany tax reports:

1.  Go to Setup > Customization > Germany Localization
    
2.  Find **Tax Reports** In the **Components** section.
    
3.  In the Action column, click **Install**.
    
4.  Wait until the installation process completes.
    

This makes the Germany tax reports available among the country-specific templates in Reports > Tax > Country Tax Reports.

Warning:

Modifying the **Subsidiary** values in the **Audience** tab of **Nexus List Service SU** deployment of script **Country Tax Report Options Service SU** may prevent the successful loading of Reports > Tax > Country Tax Reports. This happens when the user trying to access the report page isn't included in a subsidiary included in the audience values. If you encounter this issue, either clear the Audience tab, or make sure all correct subsidiaries are included.
