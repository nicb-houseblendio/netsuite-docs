If you have a Germany subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the VAT declaration for Germany, in English or German. The report can be used as a source for filling in the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, Recapitulative Statement (EU Sales List) reports, and Intrastat reports.
