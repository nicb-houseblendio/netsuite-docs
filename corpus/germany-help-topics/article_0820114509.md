The DATEV Connector Role SuiteApp creates a role supporting data exchange between NetSuite and DATEV through Digitastic DATEV Connector. The key benefits of this SuiteApp include the following:

-   **Role supporting bidirectional data transfer between NetSuite and DATEV**
    
    The SuiteApp creates a dedicated role with permissions required to exchange data between NetSuite and DATEV through Digitastic DATEV Connector.
    
-   **Automated role setup**
    
-   **Effortless Digitastic DATEV Connector setup**
    

Note:

The NetSuite DATEV Connector is a third-party solution offered by fybu (formerly Digitastic). It manages the setup and testing of the connection based on each customer's specific processes and requirements. For further details, including information about the DATEV Connector Role setup, please contact info@fybu.de.
