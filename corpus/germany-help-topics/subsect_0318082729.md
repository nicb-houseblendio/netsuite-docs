To be able to properly generate reports provided by the Germany Localization SuiteApp, the Region field of your German company must be set.

Note:

This setting applies to the Single Instance account types. If you use a One World account type, see [Setting the Region of Your German Subsidiary](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_0318082707.html).

#### To set your company's region:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Company Information.
    
2.  In the **Region** field on the Company Information page, select an appropriate region from the list.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
