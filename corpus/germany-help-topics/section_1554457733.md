Before installing the Germany Central Bank Reports SuiteApp, make sure you've fulfilled the prerequisites. For more information, see [Prerequisites for Installing Germany Central Bank Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554457686.html).

#### To install the Germany Central Bank Reports SuiteApp:

1.  Go to Customization > SuiteBundler > Search & Install Bundles (Administrator).
    
2.  In the Keywords field, enter **Germany Central Bank Reports**, or the Bundle ID **302124**.
    
3.  Click **Search**.
    
4.  Click the Germany Central Bank Reports link in the **Name** column.
    
5.  On the Bundle Details page, click **Install**.
    

For more information about installing SuiteApps, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).

The Germany Central Bank Reports SuiteApp is a managed bundle, and is automatically updated whenever improvements or new features are added.
