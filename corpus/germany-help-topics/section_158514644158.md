This SuiteApp must be shared with your NetSuite account before you can install it. To get access to Deferred VAT Recognition, contact your NetSuite account administrator.

The following prerequisites need to be configured or enabled before installing the Deferred VAT Recognition SuiteApp:

-   Accounting Periods - see [Accounting Period Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1445226.html).
    
-   Revenue Recognition - see [Revenue and Expense Recognition Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1675871.html).
    
-   Advance Revenue Management - see [Advanced Revenue Management (Essentials) and (Revenue Allocation)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4328435538.html).
    
-   Revenue Recognition - see [Setting Up the Revenue Recognition Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1678353.html).
    
-   Advanced Taxes - see [Advanced Taxes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1803438.html).
