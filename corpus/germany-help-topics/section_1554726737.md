Setting up an account for an organization doing business in Germany usually requires enabling several features and installing some additional SuiteApps.

## Enabling Features {#bridgehead_160164372324}

Some features available in your NetSuite account are essential for users in Germany. If they are inactive, you should consider enabling them. Follow the instructions below to enable the most common features beneficial for users in Germany. However, if you want to get access to some specific product area, go to the [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_160156190769.html) help topic and check the exact requirements.

If you don't have sufficient permissions to enable features, please contact your account administrator.

#### To enable most common features:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features (Administrator)_.
    
2.  On the **Company** subtab, go to the **International** subtab, and check the **Multi-language** and **Multiple Currencies** boxes.
    
3.  (Optional) On the **Accounting** subtab, go to the Basic features section and check the **GL Audit Numbering** box.
    
4.  (Optional) On the **Accounting** subtab, go to the Advanced features section and check the **Period End Journal Entries**, and **Foreign Currency Variance Mapping** boxes.
    
5.  On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, enable the following features:
    
    -   SuiteBuilder
        
    -   SuiteScript
        
    -   SuiteGL
        
    
    For more information about SuiteCloud features, see [SuiteCloud Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N239332.html).
    
6.  If your company is registered for EU One Stop Shop, check the **EU One Stop Shop** box on the **Tax** subtab.
    
7.  Click **Save**.

If you would like to get access to some specific product area, go to the [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_160156190769.html) help topic and check which SuiteApps are required to be installed. ## Germany Central Bank Reports SuiteApp {#bridgehead_157286296353}

Beta version of Germany Central Bank Reports SuiteApp is currently available. This SuiteApp requires Germany Localization as a prerequisite. To comply with German accounting laws, several standard NetSuite financial reports have been customized. Germany Central Bank Reports SuiteApp enables you to track and report incoming and outgoing payments and generate reports in XML or PDF. Account Z4 is necessary.

## Contra Account Sheet SuiteApp {#bridgehead_158452650939}

Contra Account Sheet SuiteApp enables companies to generate transaction records with contra account information. Transaction records can be used by accountants for viewing the account balance or transactions.

For more information, see [Contra Account Sheet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_157830168935.html).
