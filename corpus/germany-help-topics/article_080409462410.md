Germany Localization delivers a Germany-specific print template **GL Defer VAT Layout Invoice Template**. This template is adjusted for scenarios where taxable prepayments are applied to a final invoice and can be used to print invoices only.

Note:

This template can be used in accounts with SuiteTax and without SuiteTax.

#### Selecting GL Defer VAT Layout Invoice Template

1.  Go to Customization>Forms>Transaction Forms
    
2.  Click **Customise** in the line corresponding to the Invoice type of your choice.
    
3.  For the **Printing Type**, select **Advanced**.
    
4.  In the **Print Template** list, select **GL Defer VAT Layout Invoice Template**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
