The search results contain the following columns:

-   **Purpose of Payment:** Displays the purpose of payment (set by the German law). Every purpose of payment has its unique CBR Transaction ID.
    
-   **BA:** Displays the special code particularizing a group of incoming or outgoing transactions (in accordance with German laws).
    
-   **CBR Transaction ID:** Displays the unique Central Bank Reports transaction number (set by the German law). Every purpose of payment has its unique CBR Transaction ID.
    
-   **Country:** Displays the country for this group of incoming or outgoing payments.
    
-   **Country Code:** Displays the code of the country for this group of incoming or outgoing payments.
    
-   **Incoming Amount (Thousands of EUR):** Displays the amount of this group of payments (if it's incoming).
    
-   **Outgoing Amount (Thousands of EUR):** Displays the amount of this group of payments (if it's outgoing).
    
-   **Netting and clearing (V)/Contribution of assets and rights (E):** Displays one of the two categories that this group of payments belongs to (in accordance with German laws).
