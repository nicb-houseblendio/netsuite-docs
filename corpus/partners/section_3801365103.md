At any time you can view the status of authorized commission payments. Go to _Transactions > Commissions > Authorize Partner Commissions > Status_.

The Process Status page displays the identification number of the submitted commission and the process type such as commission authorization. The page displays the status of the commission authorization process, such as completed. The page also displays the completion percentage of the authorization process, any error messages, and the authorization creation date and time stamp.

Note:

You can't authorize commissions for partners whose commissions are currently being authorized.
