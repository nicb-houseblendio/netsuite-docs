The Customers by Partner Detail Report shows you which customers are associated with each partner and the status of the customers. It also shows the sales reps assigned to those customers. A customer is associated with a partner if the partner is selected in the Partner field on the customer record.

If you use the Multi-Partner Management feature, customers are listed under each partner that is associated with the customer. For more information, see [Associating Partners With Customers and Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1167413.html).

## To see the Customers by Partner Detail report: {#bridgehead_N1177484}

Go to _Reports > Customer/Receivables > Customers by Partner > Detail_.

You'll see a message that your report is loading. The status bar at the bottom shows the progress as your report loads. You can click Cancel Report next to the status bar if you want to stop the report from loading.

Note:

This report does not support reporting by period even when the Report by Period preference is set to All Reports. You can set the Report by Period preference at _Home > Set Preferences_ > the Analytics subtab.
