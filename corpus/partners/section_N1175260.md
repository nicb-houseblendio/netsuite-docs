The Authorized Commissions Detail report shows the commission that is due to be paid to each partner. The commission amounts on this report have been authorized and approved. This report includes commission resulting from sales orders, cash sales, invoices, and refunds.

## To see the Authorized Partner Commission Detail report: {#bridgehead_N1175282}

Click the _Reports > Commissions > Authorized Partner Commission > Detail_.

You'll see a message that your report is loading. The status bar at the bottom shows the progress as your report loads. You can click Cancel Report next to the status bar if you want to stop the report from loading.

Note:

This report does not support reporting by period even when the Report by Period preference is set to All Reports. You can set the Report by Period preference at _Home > Set Preferences_ > the Analytics subtab.
