The Sales by Partner Detail report lists each transaction according to the partner associated with the transaction. Partners are associated with transactions when a partner is selected on the customer's record.

If you use the Multi-Partner Management feature, a sales transaction is listed under each partner that is associated with the sale. For more information, see [Setting Up the Multi-Partner Management Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1167284.html).

This report shows you which partners are bringing in the most revenue, most transactions, or both for your business.

## To view the Sales by Partner Detail report: {#bridgehead_N1177065}

Go to _Reports > Sales > Sales by Partner > Detail_.

You'll see a message that your report is loading. The status bar at the bottom shows the progress as your report loads. You can click Cancel Report next to the status bar if you want to stop the report from loading.
