The Sales by Partner Summary report lists the sales total for each partner.

Sales are attributed to a partner when a partner is on the record of the customer who is making the purchase.

If you use the Multi-Partner Management feature, sales transactions are included under each partner that is associated with the sale. For more information, see [Setting Up the Multi-Partner Management Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1167284.html).

This report shows you which partners are bringing in the most revenue for your business.

## To view the Sales by Partner Summary report: {#bridgehead_N1176850}

Go to _Reports > Sales > Sales by Partner_.

You'll see a message that your report is loading. The status bar at the bottom shows the progress as your report loads. You can click Cancel Report next to the status bar if you want to stop the report from loading.
