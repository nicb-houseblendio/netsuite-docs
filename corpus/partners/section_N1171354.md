You can assign a partner to a single commission plan at a time. You can assign multiple partners to each commission plan.

#### To assign a commission plan to a partner: {#procedure_N1171384}

1.  Go to _Lists > Commissions > Partner Plans_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the commission plan to which you want to assign a partner.
    
3.  Click the **Assign Plan to Partners** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Partner** column, select the partner you want to assign to the plan.
    
5.  In the **From** column, enter the date this plan begins to calculate commission for this partner.
    
6.  In the **To** column, enter the last date this plan calculates commission for this partner.
    
7.  Click **Add**.
    
8.  Repeat steps 4 - 7 for each partner to which you want to assign this plan.
    
9.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

You can assign a partner to only one commission plan at a time.

You can update a commission plan by adding or removing partners, or by changing the commission schedules that are on the plan. When you update a commission plan during the period it is assigned, NetSuite recalculates commission for the entire period. For more information, see [Recalculating Commission](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1147526.html).

You can view a list of each partner and the commission plan to which they are assigned. Go to _Lists > Commissions > Partner Plan Assignments_.
