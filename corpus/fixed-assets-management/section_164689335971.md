The Fixed Assets Management SuiteApp lets you migrate the fixed asset data from your previous system to NetSuite using the CSV import feature.

After you import the assets to the Fixed Assets Management system, you can view them at Fixed Assets > Lists > Assets. You can view the depreciation history on the asset record's Depreciation History subtab.

Important:

Values for assets migrated through CSV import are not automatically posted to the fixed asset general ledger accounts. You must create the journal entries manually to post the values for these assets.

SuiteConsulting Professional Services can help implement and customize NetSuite for your business needs. Contact your NetSuite sales representative for information.
