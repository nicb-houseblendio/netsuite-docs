#### Create an alternate depreciation method to use for group tax depreciation {#procedure_N2158926}

1.  Go to Fixed Assets > Setup > Alternate Methods.
    
2.  Enter values for the fields and check the **Pool Flag** box. For more information, see [Creating Alternate Methods (Tax Depreciation Methods)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2140809.html).
    
3.  Click **Save**.
