#### To use a tax depreciation method: {#procedure_N2141692}

1.  Add a tax method on the asset record's **Depreciation History** subtab. For information, see [Depreciation History Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N2154557.html).
    
2.  Run a Depreciation Schedule report for the asset type of the asset record. For information, see _Generating Asset Reports_.
    
    Note:
    
    See also [Asset Depreciation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2158182.html).
