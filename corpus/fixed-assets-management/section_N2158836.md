To use group tax depreciation methods for a group of assets, complete these tasks in the following order:

1.  [Create an Alternate Depreciation Method for Group Tax Depreciation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202052250.html).
    
2.  [Define Assets for Group Tax Depreciation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202052244.html).
    
3.  [Create a Group Master Record for Group Tax Depreciation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1202052238.html).
    
4.  Run [Asset Depreciation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2158182.html) for the group of assets.
    
5.  [Generating Fixed Asset Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1202061114.html)
