You can add or update lease payments to add the interest journals through CSV import. To do so, you must perform the following steps:

-   [Creating a Saved Search for the Lease Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1551252116.html)
    
-   [Adding Interest Journals to the CSV File](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1551252109.html)
    
-   [Importing Lease Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1543972424.html)
    

Important:

If you created a lease record through lease migration, you must update lease payments first before you can complete the migration.
