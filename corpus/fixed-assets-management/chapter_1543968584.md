The Lease Accounting feature in the Fixed Assets Management SuiteApp helps you comply with the IFRS 16 and ASC 842 lease standards. These standard require lessees to recognize nearly all leases and transfer all operating leases to the balance sheet starting January 1, 2019.

The Lease Accounting feature lets you do the following tasks from the SuiteApp:

-   [Creating a Lease Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543968614.html)
    
-   [Adding Lease Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543971704.html)
    
-   [Generating the Lease Amortization Schedule](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543971736.html)
    
-   [Lease Journal Entries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543971770.html)
    
-   [Recording Lease Interest](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543971788.html)
    
-   [Lease Asset Proposal and Generation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543977465.html)
    
-   [Migrating Existing Lease Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1550097769.html)
    
-   [Lease Record Modification](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157969052439.html)
