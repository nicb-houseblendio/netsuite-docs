You can assign tax depreciation methods to an asset from the asset record's Depreciation History subtab. These assignments let you track tax or corporate reporting methods for the asset.

To manually add tax methods to an asset, read the [Depreciation History Subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N2154557.html) topic.

To add alternate depreciation methods to asset records using CSV import, read [Adding Alternate Depreciation Methods Using CSV Import](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2148707.html).
