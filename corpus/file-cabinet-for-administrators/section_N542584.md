When you upload your logo to the File Cabinet, you can print it on all forms except checks. You can also show your logo in your Customer, Partner, or Vendor Center, and on your website.

Logo files must be in GIF or JPG format, and the file size should be less than 10 KB. To print your logo on standard forms, the logo must be no more than 200 pixels wide and 60 pixels high. Image resolution may change how your logo appears on a standard form. If your logo doesn't display correctly, change the image resolution.

First, upload your logo to the File Cabinet. For information, see [Uploading Your Logo to the File Cabinet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161963493389.html).

After you've uploaded your logo, choose your next step based on the result you want to get.

-   [Setting Your Logo to Appear on Forms and Centers Associated with your NetSuite Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161963499495.html)
    
-   [Setting the Logo for your Site Builder Website](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161963512330.html)
    
-   [Setting the Logo to Display on NetSuite Pages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162332898985.html)
    

You can choose a logo that displays for each subsidiary when viewing pages or printing transactions. For more information, see [Logos in OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N276203.html).
