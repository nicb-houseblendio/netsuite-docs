To complete this task, you need the Administrator role or a role with access to the Company Information page in NetSuite.

#### To upload your logo: {#procedure_N542607}

1.  Go to _Documents > Files > Images_.
    
    Note:
    
    You can upload your logo to a different File Cabinet folder, but you shouldn't use the Attachments Received or Attachments Sent folders.
    
2.  Click **Add File**.
    
3.  Select your logo image.
    
4.  Click **Open**.
    

Next, set your logo to appear on forms, in centers, and in your website. For more information, see the following help topics:

-   [Setting Your Logo to Appear on Forms and Centers Associated with your NetSuite Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161963499495.html)
    
-   [Setting the Logo for your Site Builder Website](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161963512330.html)
    
-   [Setting the Logo to Display on NetSuite Pages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162332898985.html)
