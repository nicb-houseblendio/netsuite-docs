After you've uploaded your logo to the File Cabinet, you can set the logo for your Site Builder website.

#### To set the logo for your Site Builder website:

1.  Go to _Commerce > Websites > Website List_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the website you want to modify.
    
3.  Click the **Appearances** subtab.
    
4.  Select your image under **Website Logo.**
    
5.  Set alignment in the **Web Site Logo Alignment** field. Your logo shows up at the top of your site, and you can align it left, right, or center.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

After you click Save on the Web Site Setup page, click _Commerce > Websites > Preview Website_ to see the results.
