Usage limits enable you to specify how many times a customer can use a promotion. You can choose to permit unlimited usage per customer or limit each customer to use the promotion one time only.

Usage limits are defined on the **Usage Limits** subtab on the promotion record. For more information, see [Setting Usage Limits on SuitePromotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519209942.html).
