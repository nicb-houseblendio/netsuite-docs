Promotions let you create special offers that motivate your customers to buy more products. Promotions can help you clear out old stock, boost sales, and reward your best clients. Promotions provide discounts that customers can apply to web store orders and orders placed with sales reps. NetSuite promotions also let you target certain customers, locations, channels, or time periods.
