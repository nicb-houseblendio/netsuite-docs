To use Merchandise Hierarchy in NetSuite, an administrator must first ensure the required features are enabled.

## Enabling Merchandise Hierarchy Features {#bridgehead_1527514646}

An administrator can enable the Merchandise Hierarchy feature at _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_ on the **Items & Inventory** subtab.

The following features must be enabled before you can use Merchandise Hierarchy:

-   Merchandise Hierarchy
    
-   Custom Segments
    
-   Custom Record
    

#### To enable the Merchandise Hierarchy feature:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_, and click the **Items & Inventory** subtab.
    
2.  Under Items check the **Merchandise Hierarchy** box.
    
3.  Click **OK** to enable the Custom Segments feature if prompted.
    
    Note:
    
    Merchandise Hierarchy requires the Custom Segments feature to be enabled. If Custom Segments isn't enabled, you'll be prompted to enable it when you check the Merchandise Hierarchy box. For more information, see [Custom Segments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4313464438.html).
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To enable the Custom Record feature:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_, and click the **SuiteCloud** subtab.
    
2.  Under SuiteBuilder check the **Custom Records** box.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

For more information regarding attributes, see [Applying Attributes to Items in a Merchandise Hierarchy](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554803178.html).

## Merchandise Hierarchy Roles and Permissions {#bridgehead_1538150446}

If you use custom roles that you want to use to create merchandise hierarchies, assign the following permissions to those roles:

-   Merchandise Hierarchy Level - Full
    
-   Merchandise Hierarchy Node - Full
    
-   Merchandise Hierarchy Version - Full
    

#### To assign these permissions to a role:

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > User Management > Manage Roles_ and open the role that needs to be granted Merchandise Hierarchy permissions.
    
2.  Under the **Permissions** subtab, click **Setup** and assign the permissions to the role.
