Customers are eligible for a free gift with any order.

Tagline: Get a free T-shirt with any order!

![Promotion Banner](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/Merchandising/SuitePromotionsExample34.png)

## What the customer needs to buy {#subsect_1518081211}

The following table shows how to set up the Buy conditions for this type of promotion.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Buy Anything | Select |
| Spend a minimum order amount or buy specific items | Clear |
| Minimum Order Amount | Clear |
| Specific Items | Clear |
| Item | \- |
| Repeat Discount Incrementally | Clear |
| Item Quantity | \- |

## What the customer will get {#subsect_1518531266}

The following table shows how to set up the Get conditions for this type of promotion.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Free Gift | T-shirt Note, you can't add parent matrix items as a free gift. |

Free gift promotions without eligibility criteria are often limited to one free gift per customer. To set up a promotion limited to one free gift per customer, select **One time only** on the Usage Limits subtab. For more information, see [Setting Usage Limits on SuitePromotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519209942.html).

## Examples of what the customer gets {#subsect_1518083952}

The following table shows examples of how many free gifts this type of promotion provides when various quantities of items are added to a transaction.

| What the customer buys | Price before discount | Free gifts | Price after discount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 T-shirt | $10 | 1 T-shirt | $10 (the T-shirt in the order isn't discounted, an extra T-shirt is added to the order) |
| 1 dress | $20 | 1 T-shirt | $20 |
| 1 T-shirt, 1 dress | $30 | 1 T-shirt | $30 (the T-shirt in the order isn't discounted, an extra T-shirt is added to the order) |
| 2 T-shirts, 1 dress | $40 | 1 T-shirt | $40 (the T-shirts in the order aren't discounted, an extra T-shirt is added to the order) |
