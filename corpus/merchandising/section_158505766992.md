The Merchandise Hierarchy Workbook for SuiteAnalytics Workbook by default includes all your custom Merchandise Hierarchy fields, but can be further modified to provide more data. The workbook lists transactions entered during a specific time period that involve items assigned to a merchandise hierarchy.

As with all standard workbooks, only Administrators can access the Merchandise Hierarchy Workbook.

This workbook includes the following pivot table:

-   **Sales by Hierarchy Node** - The default definition includes rows for hierarchy levels and items, plus measures for Net Sales Amount (Sum), Net Sales Quantity (Sum), and On Hand Quantity (Sum).
