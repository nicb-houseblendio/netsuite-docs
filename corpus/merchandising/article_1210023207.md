While group synonyms are bi-directional, one-way synonyms allow you to direct searches from one term to a preferred catalog term or terms.

#### To define a one-way synonym:

1.  Go to _Commerce > Marketing > Search > One-way Synonyms_.
    
2.  On the One-way Synonyms page, click **New One-way Synonym**.
    
3.  On the One-way Synonym page, enter the search term in the **Searching For** box.
    
    Note:
    
    This should be a search term your shopper might use but doesn't find results for. For example, you can enter pants in the **Searching For** box.
    
4.  In the **Shows These Synonyms In Results** box, enter one or more synonyms, separated by commas.
    
    Note:
    
    These should be the synonyms you use most in your catalog for that product. For example, you can enter jeans and trousers in the **Shows these synonyms in Results** box.
    
5.  If you use multiple websites, select the site where you want this one-way synonym to apply.
    
6.  If your site uses multiple languages, select the language for this one-way synonym.
    
7.  (Optional) To disable the one-way synonym, check the **Inactive** box.
    
8.  (Optional) To add more one-way synonyms, click **Save & New** and repeat steps 3-7.
    
9.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

When you click Save, your search index automatically rebuilds to include these changes. When the search index is rebuilt successfully, you'll see **Synonyms Updated** under Job Name on the Search Index Job Status page.
