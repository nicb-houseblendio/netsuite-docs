After you set up search synonyms, you can view and edit them on the One-way Synonyms or Group Synonyms page. These pages show all your synonyms and their status. If there are too many, you can filter the list by Search Term, Synonyms, Site, Language, Last Modified Date, or Status.

#### To view, edit, or delete a defined synonym:

1.  Go to _Commerce > Marketing > Search > One-way Synonyms_ or _Commerce > Marketing > Search > Group Synonyms_.
    
2.  You can view the defined synonyms on the One-way Synonyms/Group Synonyms page.
    
3.  (Optional) To filter the list of defined synonyms:
    
    1.  Expand **Filters**.
        
    2.  Use the following fields to filter the list of defined synonyms:
        
        -   Search Term and Synonyms
            
        -   Site
            
        -   Language
            
        -   Last Modified Date
            
        -   Status
            
4.  (Optional) To edit a synonym, click **Edit** next to it.
    
    Note:
    
    To edit multiple search synonym records, use the Edit toggle button.
    
5.  (Optional) To delete a synonym, click **Edit** next to it and then click **Delete** under Actions.
    
    Note:
    
    To delete multiple search synonym records, use the Edit toggle button and then click **Delete Record** under the New column.
