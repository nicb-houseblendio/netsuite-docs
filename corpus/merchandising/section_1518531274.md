Customers are eligible for a free gift when they spend a minimum amount.

Tagline: Spend $50 or more and get a free T-shirt!

![Promotion Banner](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/Merchandising/SuitePromotionsExample35.png)

## What the customer needs to buy {#subsect_1518531280}

The following table shows how to set up the Buy conditions for this type of promotion.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Buy Anything | Clear |
| Spend a minimum order amount or buy specific items | Select |
| Minimum Order Amount | 100.00 |
| Specific Items | Clear |
| Item | \- |
| Repeat Discount Incrementally | Clear |
| Item Quantity | \- |

## What the customer will get {#subsect_1518531286}

The following table shows how to set up the Get conditions for this type of promotion.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Free Gift | T-shirt Note, you can't add parent matrix items as a free gift. |

## Examples of what the customer gets {#subsect_1518084204}

The following table shows examples of how many free gifts this type of promotion provides when various quantities of items are added to a transaction.

| What the customer buys | Price before discount | Free gifts | Price after discount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 T-shirt | $10 | \- | $10 |
| 1 dress | $20 | \- | $20 |
| 5 T-shirts | $50 | 1 T-shirt | $50 (the T-shirts in the order aren't discounted, an extra T-shirt is added to the order) |
| 5 dresses | $100 | 1 T-shirt | $100 |
