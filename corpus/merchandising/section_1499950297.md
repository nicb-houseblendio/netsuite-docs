Merchandise Hierarchy lets you organize sales and item performance data in SuiteAnalytics workbooks and custom reports. You can enhance your sales workbooks and reports by including merchandise hierarchy elements such as levels and nodes. These elements help you to group and organize data in the reports by product classifications. You can visualize sales by company branches, groups, departments, classes, or even attributes.

You can create workbooks and custom reports that include merchandise hierarchy internal custom fields combined with any other NetSuite field.

In NetSuite, you can use any of the following tools to analyze sales and item performance:

-   **SuiteAnalytics Workbook** - analytical tool available in NetSuite for creating custom workbooks that combine queries, pivot tables, and charts. For more information, see [SuiteAnalytics Workbook and Merchandise Hierarchy](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156335207401.html).
    
-   **Report Builder** - a tool you can use to customize reports' data and formatting. For more information, see [Creating Merchandise Hierarchy Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1547568175.html).
    
-   **Saved Searches** - reusable search definitions. For more information, see [Creating Saved Searches Based on a Merchandise Hierarchy](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1530870351.html).
