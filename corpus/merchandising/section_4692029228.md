You can create promotions for each predefined promotion type. This section explains the different promotion records in detail and how to create each type:

-   **Item promotions** - give percentage or currency amount discounts on items, see [Creating Item Promotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519050978.html).
    
-   **Fixed price item promotions** - give items for a fixed discounted price, see [Creating Fixed Price Item Promotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519050946.html).
    
-   **Order promotions** - give percentage or currency discounts at the order level, see [Creating Order Promotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519050918.html).
    
-   **Shipping promotions** - give your customers free shipping on specific shipping methods, see [Creating Shipping Promotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519050891.html).
    
-   **Free gift promotions** - give your customers a free gift that is automatically added to their order, see [Creating Free Gift Promotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519050842.html).
    

To create any type of promotion you must first go to the new promotions landing page, where you can select the type of promotion you want to create.

To open the new promotions landing page, go to _Commerce > Marketing > Upsell > Promotions > New_.

![New Promotion](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/Merchandising/SA-PromoLandingPage.png)

Note:

It is a good practice to deactivate promotions after the end of the promotion period to enhance the overall efficiency of your promotions engine. Deactivating a promotion does not delete the promotion. It simply makes the promotion unavailable for selection on a transaction.

Each type of SuitePromotion follows the same workflow. You select the promotion type, enter basic properties and scheduling information, enter the Buy conditions, specify further Get conditions, and finally create a coupon code. The main variation in the process occurs when applying the Get conditions available, which differ depending on the type of promotion.

The promotion records for all SuitePromotions contain the following sections:

-   **Basic Properties** - see [SuitePromotions Basic Properties](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519210477.html)
    
-   **Scheduling** - see [SuitePromotions Scheduling](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519210446.html)
    
-   **Further Settings** - see [SuitePromotions Further Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519210350.html)
    
-   **What the Customer Needs to Buy** - see [What the Customer Needs to Buy](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519210305.html)
    
-   **What the Customer Will Get** - see [What the Customer Will Get](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519210224.html)
    
-   **Coupon Codes subtab** - see [Associating Coupon Codes With a SuitePromotion](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519210145.html)
    
-   **Audience subtab** - see [Setting Customer Eligibility on SuitePromotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519210085.html)
    
-   **Sales Channels subtab** - see [Setting Sales Channel Eligibility on SuitePromotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519210030.html)
    
-   **Usage Limits subtab** - see [Setting Usage Limits on SuitePromotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519209942.html)
    

Each section is identical for each SuitePromotion type with the exception of the **What the Customer Needs to Buy** and **What the Customer Will Get** sections.
