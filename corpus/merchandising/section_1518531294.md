Customers are eligible for a free item when they buy a specific different item.

Tagline: Buy a dress and get a free T-shirt!

![Promotion Banner](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/Merchandising/SuitePromotionsExample36.png)

## What the customer needs to buy {#subsect_1518531298}

The following table shows how to set up the Buy conditions for this type of promotion.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Buy Anything | Clear |
| Spend a minimum order amount or buy specific items | Select |
| Minimum Order Amount | Clear |
| Specific Items | Select |
| Item | Dress |
| Item Quantity | 1 |
| Repeat Discount Incrementally | Clear |

## What the customer will get {#subsect_1518531309}

The following table shows how to set up the Get conditions for this type of promotion.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Free Gift | T-shirt Note, you can't add parent matrix items as a free gift. |

## Examples of what the customer gets {#subsect_1518084367}

The following table shows examples of how many free gifts this type of promotion provides when various quantities of items are added to a transaction.

| What the customer buys | Price before discount | Free gifts | Price after discount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 T-shirt | $10 | \- | $10 |
| 1 dress | $20 | 1 T-shirt | $20 |
| 1 T-shirt, 1 dress | $30 | 1 T-shirt | $30 (the T-shirt in the order isn't discounted, an extra T-shirt is added to the order) |
| 2 dresses | $40 | 1 T-shirt | $40 |
