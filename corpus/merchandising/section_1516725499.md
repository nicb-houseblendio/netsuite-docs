You can create promotions that provide free shipping on specified shipping methods. Shipping promotions can be:

-   **Item-based** - providing free shipping when customers buy a specific item or items
    
-   **Order-level** - providing free shipping on orders, with or without spend conditions
    

You can't offer a flat rate or percentage discount on shipping.
