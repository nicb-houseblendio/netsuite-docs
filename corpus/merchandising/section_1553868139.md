The Search Analytics feature is available for all Commerce web stores and SuiteCommerce InStore customers. By default, it's disabled in all NetSuite accounts, but you can enable it at Setup > Company > Enable Features on the **Web Presence** subtab.

Note:

Before you enable the Search Analytics feature, make sure the SuiteAnalytics Workbook feature is enabled. If you don't see the Analytics tab in your NetSuite menu, follow the steps in [Enabling SuiteAnalytics Workbook in Your NetSuite Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1504014192.html).

#### To enable the Search Analytics feature:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Enable Features, and click the **Web Presence** subtab.
    
2.  Under Web Site check the **Commerce Search Analytics** box.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

When you enable the Commerce Search Analytics feature, NetSuite starts creating commerce search activity data records with aggregated search activity data. You'll see the search data in the workbooks a few hours after enabling the feature.

Note:

If you disable the Commerce Search Analytics feature, your existing records stay in the database, but new data won't be collected until you turn the feature back on.
