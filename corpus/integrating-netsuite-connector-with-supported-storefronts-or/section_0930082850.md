To integrate the Square connector and NetSuite Connector, you must authorize NetSuite Connector in your Square account.

Before proceeding, ensure to add the Square connector to your account. To add a new connector, contact your NetSuite account manager.

#### To authorize NetSuite Connector in your Square account:

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com/).
    
2.  Select Square connector and then select the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Settings > Credentials and click **Authorize Account**. You will be redirected to the Square website.
    
4.  Follow the instructions on the website.
    
5.  You will be redirected back to the Credentials screen of the Square connector's relevant account in NetSuite Connector. The Account Authorized button indicates that NetSuite Connector has been authorized and the Square account is linked. If you get a failure message, contact NetSuite support.
    

#### Configuring Square connector in NetSuite

1.  Go to Connector > Configuration > Storefront fields.
    
2.  Select Square from the drop down in the **Storefront** field.
    
3.  Enter the number of accounts in the **Number of Accounts for Selected Storefront** field.
    
4.  Click **Submit**.
