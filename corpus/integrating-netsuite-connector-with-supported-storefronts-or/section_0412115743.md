Use the following procedure to access the BigCommerce account.

Before proceeding, make sure you have added the BigCommerce connector to your account. To add a new connector, contact your NetSuite account manager.

#### To access BigCommerce account :

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the BigCommerce connector and account from the left menu.
    
3.  Go to Settings > Credentials.
    
4.  Click **Authorize Account**
    
5.  You should now be on a **BigCommerce** screen. Follow the on-screen instructions. When you're done you should land on a **Success** screen in NetSuite Connector. If you get a failure message, please contact NetSuite support.
    

If you use legacy BigCommerce accounts, refer to [BigCommerce Account Access in NetSuite Connector](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/100550) for more information about Integrating NetSuite Connector with BigCommerce.
