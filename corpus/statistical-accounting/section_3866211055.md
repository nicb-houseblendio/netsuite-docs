At any time you can modify the attributes of a statistical schedule.

Note:

Your user role must have the Set Up Accounting permission to create a statistical schedule.

#### To modify an existing statistical schedule:

1.  Go to _Transactions > Financial > Create Statistical Schedule > List_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the statistical schedule you want to modify.
    
3.  On the Statistical Schedule page, modify the record as needed.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
