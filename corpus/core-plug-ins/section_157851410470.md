When you use a Financial Institution Connectivity Plug-in, keep these limitations in mind:

-   The plug-in does not allow you to add IP addresses to an allowlist.
    
-   The plug-in lets you retrieve ZIP files, provided that your Bank Statement Parser Plug-in is capable of handling them.
