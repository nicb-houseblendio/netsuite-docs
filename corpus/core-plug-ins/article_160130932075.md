To begin development or administration for a Workbook Builder Plug-in, make sure that the Server SuiteScript and Workbook API features are enabled in the development account.

#### To enable features for the plug-in:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **SuiteCloud** subtab.
    
3.  In the **SuiteScript** section, check the **Server SuiteScript** and **Workbook API** boxes.
