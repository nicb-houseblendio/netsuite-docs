To use the plug-in, activate your implementation. For more information, see [Managing Custom Plug-in Implementations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3976831129.html).

#### To activate the plug-in:

1.  To activate the plug-in implementation, go to _Customization > Plug-ins > Manage Plug-ins_.
    
2.  Under **Bank Connectivity**, check the box next to name of your implementation.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
