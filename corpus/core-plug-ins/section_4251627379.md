To install and set up an Email Capture Plug-in implementation, complete the following steps:

-   [Enable Features for an Email Capture Plug-in Implementation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4251628119.html)
    
-   [Install an Email Capture Plug-in Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4251628442.html)
    
-   [Enable the Email Capture Plug-in Implementation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4251628729.html)
