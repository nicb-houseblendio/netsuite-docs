With a Financial Institution Connectivity Plug-in, you can:

-   Build and manage your own custom integration to a financial institution API and configure it to fit your needs.
    
    Conversely, the Bank Feeds SuiteApp provides a generic way to connect to a large set of financial institutions. As well, the AMEX Corporate Card Integration SuiteApp enables you to only connect to American Express.
    
-   Connect to financial institutions that are not supported by SuiteApps.
    
    Note:
    
    The Bank Feeds SuiteApp is available to connect to financial institutions that operate in:
    
    -   Australia
        
    -   Asia (Hong Kong, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, and Thailand)
        
    -   Europe
        
    -   Oceania (New Zealand)
        
    -   North America (Canada and the United States)
        
    
-   Retrieve bank data from your financial institution without having to provide user credentials to a third-party service, which the Bank Feeds SuiteApp requires.
    

In addition to creating plug-ins, the Financial Institution Connectivity interface enables you to develop your own connectivity applications. These provide more options for the way that your system connects to financial institutions.
