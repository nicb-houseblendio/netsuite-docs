You'll do all configuration for a Financial Institution Connectivity Plug-in on the Format Profile page. The configuration steps required depend on the plug-in's initial implementation.

For details on configuring connectivity to a bank or credit card company, see [Configuring a Connection to a Financial Institution (Without a SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_67170247225.html).

For details on configuring connectivity to a corporate card company, see [Configuring a Connection to American Express](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159062430840.html).
