When using the Bank Connectivity Plug-in, keep in mind the following limitations:

-   To connect to a financial institution to get bank statements, you have to manually trigger the sFTP connection. For details, see [Automating Imports for Bank Reconciliation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157895120878.html).
    
-   The plug-in does not allow you to add IP addresses to an allowlist.
    
-   You cannot retrieve ZIP files.
