To begin development or administration for a Financial Institution Parser Plug-in, make sure that the Server SuiteScript feature is enabled on the development account.

#### To enable features for the plug-in:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, make sure **Server SuiteScript** is checked. If necessary, check the box and agree to the Terms of Service.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
