The Payroll Liability report shows your total unpaid liability for each payroll item. These are the amounts you owe to tax agencies and vendors for employee tax withholding, deductions, and company contributions.

The Payroll Liability report does not include previously paid liability. It includes only the amount owed.

## To see the Payroll Liability report: {#bridgehead_N960634}

Go to _Reports > Payroll > Payroll Liability_.

A message appears indicating that your report is loading. You can click Cancel Report to stop the report from loading.

Note:

This report does not support reporting by period even when the Report by Period preference is set to All Reports. The Report by Period preference can be configured at _Home > Set Preferences_, the Analytics subtab.

## Payroll Liability Detail Report {#section_N962189}

The Payroll Liability Detail report shows details of your unpaid liabilities for each payroll item. These are the amounts you owe to tax agencies and vendors for employee tax withholding, deductions, and company contributions.

The Payroll Liability Detail report does not include previously paid liability. It includes only the amount owed.

## To see the Payroll Liability Detail report: {#bridgehead_N962216}

1.  Go to _Reports > Payroll > Payroll Liability_ to open the Payroll Liability report.
    
2.  Click a number in the report to open the detail report for that line.
    
    When you click the total amount, the Payroll Liability Detail includes details for all lines in the report.
    

To return to the summary Payroll Liability report, click **Back to Summary** at the top of the report.

Note:

This report does not support reporting by period even when the Report by Period preference is set to All Reports. The Report by Period preference can be configured at _Home > Set Preferences_, the Analytics subtab.

## Generating Insights on the Payroll Liability Report {#subsect_0303092114}

With Narrative Insights, you can use generative AI to summarize your Payroll Liability report and provide key actionable information.

The insight shows the total outstanding payroll liabilities, including employee tax withholdings, deductions, and company contributions, across a given date range. It also provides the top three unpaid liabilities. This provides visibility into what liabilities remain to be paid so that the organization can meet its financial obligations.

#### To generate an insight on the Payroll Liability report:

1.  Go to _Reports > Payroll > Payroll Liability_ to open the Payroll Liability report.
    
2.  Filter the report to include only the information you want the insight to reflect. For example, select a date range.
    
3.  Click the **Generate Insight** button. The Narrative Insights popup window appears. You can generate the insight again at any time by clicking the **Generate Insight** button.
    

For more information about narrative insights, see [Narrative Insights Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_135342304.html).
