You can set a preference to automatically calculate catch-up contributions for employees aged 60 to 63. NetSuite automatically calculates the catch-up over contributions for employees who meet the following criteria:

-   The employee has a catch-up contribution 60-63 payroll item assigned to them.
    
-   The employee is 60 to 63 years old during the current calendar year.
    
-   The employee reaches their annual elective deferral limit.
    

#### To set up automatic catch-up age 60 - 63 calculations:

1.  Make sure employees in payroll have the correct birth date on their records. To add a birth date for an existing employee, follow these steps:
    
    1.  Go to Lists > Employees > Employees.
        
    2.  Click **Edit** next to an employee.
        
    3.  Click the **Human Resources** subtab.
        
    4.  In the **Birth Date** field, enter or select the employee's date of birth.
        
    5.  Click **Save**.
        
2.  Assign a catch-up age 60 - 63 payroll item to an employee record. For the steps, see [Setting up Deductions for an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N924665.html).
    
    If you need to update employee records in bulk, see [Updating Payroll Items for Multiple Employees](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N925941.html).
    
3.  Next, go to Setup > Payroll > Setup Tasks > Set Up Payroll.
    
4.  Click the **Preferences** subtab.
    
5.  Check the **Decrease Elective Deferral Limits and Automatically Calculate Catch-Up** box.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

## Important Things to Note {#subsect_0519092822}

-   To support automation, make sure both standard (catch-up over age 50) and higher catch-up contributions are assigned to the employee's record.
    
-   The automatic catch-up calculation feature doesn't apply to **Govt. EE 457(b) - Catch-up, Additional - 3 Years to Retirement-ROTH** payroll item. If you assign this payroll item to an employee, the system calculates the catch-up contribution even if they haven't reached the annual deferral limit. Check with your plan sponsor for further details on this requirement.
    

After an eligible employee reaches their annual elective deferral limit, NetSuite automatically calculates catch-up contributions for ages 60-63. When the limit is first exceeded, the excess amount is reallocated to matching catch-up items. On later paychecks, catch-up contributions are calculated using the specified rate. When a catch-up limit is reached, the contribution for each catch-up payroll item are distributed using a weighted average.

When an employee turns 60, their total contribution limit increases from $31,000 to $34,750 as the employee becomes eligible for the higher catch-up contribution. When an employee turns 64, the higher catch-up contribution no longer applies and the standard catch-up over 50 calculation takes effect.
