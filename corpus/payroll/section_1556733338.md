Use the following procedure to edit a payroll item.

#### To edit a payroll item:

1.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Payroll Items_.
    
2.  Beside the payroll item that you want to edit, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Make your changes.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
