Set up 401(k) retirement plans to automatically calculate employee contributions and employer match based on your company's policy. Your role must have the Set Up Payroll permission to set up retirement plans.

To learn how to set up retirement plans, see the following topics:

-   [Creating Retirement Plans](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162558125834.html)
    
-   [Editing a Retirement Plan](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162612273583.html)
    
-   [Inactivating a Retirement Plan](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162678958848.html)
    

After you set up a retirement plan, you can derive the pay rate of deduction and employer contribution payroll items from the retirement plan. For more information, see [Deriving a Payroll Item from a Retirement Plan](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162611447890.html).
