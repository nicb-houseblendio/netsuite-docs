After you include employees in payroll, update payroll information. Then, you can use the Employee Tax Jurisdictions workbook to identify workplaces and verify tax jurisdictions for each employee. This workbook enables you to verify tax jurisdictions from one place, instead of opening individual employee records.

Note:

If you do not have access to this workbook, contact your administrator.

#### To view the Employee Tax Jurisdictions workbook:

1.  Go to **Analytics**.
    
2.  Click **Standard Workbooks**.
    
3.  From the list of workbooks, click **Employee Tax Jurisdictions**.
