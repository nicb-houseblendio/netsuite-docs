Before you can start paying employees with SuitePeople U.S. Payroll, you need to set up taxes and jurisdiction-specific requirements for the employee. This involves first [Verifying the Jurisdictions for an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N921903.html), and then [Selecting Status Exemptions for an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1549401872.html).

After you verify jurisdictions and select status exemptions for an employee, you can set up [Employee Tax Withholdings and Allowances](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N924516.html). Optionally, depending on an employee's jurisdiction, you can complete the following tasks:

-   [Tax Override Methods for Employee Withholding](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N925327.html)
    
-   [Setting up Taxes for Employees who Work in Different States During a Pay Period](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4791613456.html)
    
-   [Adding Standard Occupational Classification Codes for Employees](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1506349052.html)
    
-   [Setting up California VDI for Employees](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1529938339.html)
    
-   [Paid Family and Medical Leave Contributions for Employees](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0113124114.html)
    
-   [Setting up Payroll for Expatriate Employees](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4099382383.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Taxable Employer Match Contributions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0309032609.html)
