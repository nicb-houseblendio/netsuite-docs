On the Company Information page, enter basic identifying information about your company. For information about setting up subsidiaries, see [Creating Subsidiary Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N272471.html).

#### To enter the company information required for Payroll: {#procedure_N918303}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Company Information_.
    
2.  Enter or select information in the following fields:
    
    -   Company Name
        
    -   Legal Name
        
    -   Address, City, State, and Zip
        
    -   Employer Identification Number (EIN)
        
    -   Phone
        
    
    Any changes made to these fields must be committed on the Update Payroll Information page. For more information, see [Updating Payroll Information](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N930407.html).
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

For more information about entering company information, see [Configuring Company Information](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N240902.html).
