This topic describes how to enable the Payroll feature. You must complete this task on the first day of the implementation process.

Important:

Enable the Payroll feature a minimum of 30 business days before your next payday with the service. Payday is the date that checks are dated and direct deposits are deposited.

#### To enable the Payroll feature: {#procedure_N917992}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Employees** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Payroll** box.
    
4.  To add more payroll features, such as time tracking and service printed checks and stubs, check the applicable boxes.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    
    Note:
    
    To enable Payroll, you must read and agree to the terms and conditions.
