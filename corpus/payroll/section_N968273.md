With this report you can review W-2 and 1099 tax information before year-end and before generating the W-2 and 1099 reports. By reviewing this information before your year-end, you can make adjustments and corrections as necessary before year-end.

This report is a snapshot of your company- and employee-level information as of the date you run the report. By validating this information, you can ensure that accurate Forms W-2 and 1099-NEC are filed with tax agencies and provided to your employees.

Information on this report appears on each employee's Form W-2 or 1099-NEC.

NetSuite sends a reminder for you to review this report toward the end of the year.

## To see the W-2 and 1099 Audit Information report: {#bridgehead_N968315}

Go to _Reports > Payroll > W-2 and 1099 Audit_.
