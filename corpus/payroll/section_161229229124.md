You can edit a payroll item group's name and the payroll items in the group.

#### To edit a payroll item group:

1.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Payroll Item Groups_.
    
2.  Next to the payroll item group that you want to edit, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Make your changes.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
