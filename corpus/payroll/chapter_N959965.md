You can customize some reports to include payroll batch information. For more information, see [Customizing Payroll Reports to Include Batch Information](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4069207820.html). You can also customize payroll reports to include employee addresses. For more information, see [Including an Employee's Address on a Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156658207314.html).

You can assign payroll items (such as earnings, deductions, and employer contributions) to report sections. For more information, see [Modifying Payroll Report Sections](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N968892.html) and [Payroll Items Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N930985.html).
