You can edit any field on an existing retirement plan except for the plan's subsidiary. You can't clear an employee contribution box if a payroll item uses it.

#### To edit a retirement plan:

1.  Go to _Setup > Payroll > Setup Tasks > Retirement Plans_.
    
2.  Click the number next to the retirement plan you want to edit.
    
3.  Make your changes.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
