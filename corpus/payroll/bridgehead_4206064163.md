When the calculation process finishes, the Payroll Batch status changes to Calculated. After a batch is calculated, you can perform several actions. After completing any of the following actions, you must recalculate the payroll batch:

-   **Add Employees** - Click **Add Employees** to open the popup window and add employees to the batch as you did when you created the batch. For more information, see [Adding Employees to a Payroll Batch](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4791610339.html).
    
-   **Change payment options** - Click **Payment Options** to edit Check Date and the payment option boxes. For more information, see [Selecting Payment Options for a Payroll Batch](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4791610504.html).
    
    Note:
    
    If you add a payment to an individual paycheck and then disable the related payment option for the batch, NetSuite only removes the system-generated items. You need to manually remove anything you added yourself. For example, if you add a commission to a paycheck and then clear Pay Commissions and recalculate, NetSuite removes the system-generated commissions but leaves any you added manually.
    
-   **Remove paychecks from the batch** - On the Pending and Errors subtabs and the Paychecks and Net Pay subtabs, select the paychecks to remove and then click Delete Paychecks. (The Paychecks and Net Pay subtabs are on the Complete subtab.) For more information, see [Removing Paychecks from a Batch](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4791610420.html).
    
-   **Bulk edit payroll items** - On the Pending and Errors subtabs and the Paychecks and Net Pay subtabs, click Bulk Edit. In the popup window, select a payroll item, and edit values for multiple employees in the batch. (The Paychecks and Net Pay subtabs are on the Complete subtab.) For more information, see [Editing Payroll Items in Bulk on a Payroll Batch](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4791610781.html).
    
-   **Edit an individual paycheck** - On the Pending and Errors subtabs and the Paychecks and Net Pay subtabs, click Edit to open an individual paycheck and make changes. (The Paychecks and Net Pay subtabs are on the Complete subtab.) For more information, see [Editing an Individual Paycheck from a Payroll Batch](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4791610184.html).
    

You can also complete the following actions, which do not require recalculation:

-   **Delete the entire batch** - To delete the batch, from the **Actions** menu, select **Delete**.
    
-   **Access the account register** - To open the register associated with the selected account, click the account.
