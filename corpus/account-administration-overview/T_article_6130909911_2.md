Note:

This topic describes functionality available in NetSuite Next. NetSuite Next is being made available to customers in phases, so these features may not yet be available in your account. You'll receive in-app notifications when your account is eligible for NetSuite Next.

This page helps you optimize your NetSuite account data for AI-powered features in NetSuite Next, including the search and Ask Oracle.

Ask Oracle uses your existing account data, roles and permissions, customizations, and navigation structure to return relevant search results, insights, and actions.

Preparing these areas helps you get more accurate and useful AI-driven results.

Use the following tasks to review your current setup, improve data quality, clarify access, and prepare users to work effectively with Ask Oracle and NetSuite Next.

-   [Review and Refine Customizations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/T_subsect_77140211325_1.html)
    
-   [Improve Data Quality](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/T_subsect_11141611262_1.html)
