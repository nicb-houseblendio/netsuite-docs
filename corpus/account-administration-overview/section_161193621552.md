NetSuite delivers changes to customers in one of three ways:

-   **Major release** - NetSuite has two major releases each year. For all major releases of NetSuite, customers are upgraded in phases over a period of a few months.
    
    Before the upgrade, you can request a Release Preview account to test the new features with your own data and business processes. For more information, see the [Requesting a Release Preview Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4471776042.html#subsect_1544193526).
    
-   **Minor release** - A scheduled update to resolve customer-reported issues that aren't urgent but should be fixed before the next major release. All customers get minor releases at the same time.
    
-   **Hot push** - An update that occurs outside of the release schedule. A hot push fixes critical customer-reported issues that cannot wait for the next scheduled minor release. A hot push is typically rolled out to a specific customer or set of customers.
