NetSuite offers a variety of resources for administrators, from communities and international resources to educational services.

The following resources are available:

**Support Resources**

-   [NetSuite Support](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N337569.html) - a summary of the customer support resources NetSuite provides.
    
-   [NetSuite Communities (User Groups)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N339082.html) - details on the online communities where you can connect with other NetSuite users.
    
-   [Voting for Enhancements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N339259.html) - a description of SuiteIdeas, where you can vote for the enhancements you'd like to see in future NetSuite versions.
    
-   [Checking NetSuite System Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N339589.html) - steps for checking the status of the NetSuite service and information about potential outages or service disruptions.
    

**International Resources**

-   [Country-Specific Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/book_4198429264.html) - a list of documentation for NetSuite features worldwide.
    
-   [Release Notes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/book_N3865324.html) - PDF release notes are available in several languages. When the latest translated release notes are ready, you can get them from this page.
    

**Additional Resources**

-   [SuiteSuccess](http://www.netsuite.com/portal/services/professional-services/suitesuccess.shtml) - learn how SuiteSuccess can help you implement NetSuite quickly.
    
-   [SuiteApp.com](http://suiteapp.com/) - browse and choose SuiteApps to install in your NetSuite account.
    
-   [NetSuite Education Services](http://www.netsuite.com/portal/services/training.shtml) - a summary of available training and certification resources.
    

Note:

For information about resources for users, see [Getting Help](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N336026.html).
