### Is the planned maintenance for the new features in the NetSuite Release the same as the NetSuite Version Upgrade?

No. The planned maintenance or the NetSuite pre-upgrade maintenance implements preparation work into your account. This work is needed to prepare for new features that will be added in the NetSuite Version Upgrade.

### How long will the NetSuite pre-upgrade maintenance take?

The NetSuite pre-upgrade maintenance should take approximately 1 hour.
