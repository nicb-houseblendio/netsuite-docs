For industrialized products produced in Brazil or imported, tax rates vary based on MCN, CFOP, item origin, and item usage.

To set up item records for Brazil, you must first create MCN codes and CFOP codes. Then, update the item records to add the Brazil codes.

See the following topics:

-   [Setting Up MCN Codes (Former Brazil Localization SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4658927327.html)
    
-   [Setting Up CFOP Codes (Former Brazil Localization SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4658927537.html)
    
-   [Adding Brazil Codes to Item Records (Former Brazil Localization SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4658927684.html)
