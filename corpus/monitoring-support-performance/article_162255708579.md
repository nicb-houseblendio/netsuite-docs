The Case Alerts feature includes a default email template you can fully customize. You can access this template at _Setup > Company > System Email Templates_.

![Screenshot of the standard email template included with the Case Alerts feature.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SupportManagement/Case_Alerts_Standard_Case_Template_Output.png)

The subject header is derived from the case name used in the name field on the case alert record. The subject header also includes the number of cases that match the search criteria.

Click **View** to open the case record in NetSuite. Click **Detail** to see the message history for that case, which is displayed further in the body of the email.
