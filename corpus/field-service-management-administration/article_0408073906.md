The Field Service Management (FSM) SuiteApp is a paid and managed SuiteApp. It updates automatically when new features or enhancements are available.

Important:

To install the FSM SuiteApp, contact your NetSuite account manager. The assistance of NetSuite Professional Services or a qualified NetSuite partner is required to set up this feature.

#### To install the FSM SuiteApp:

1.  Go to _Customization > SuiteBundler > Search & Install Bundles_.
    
2.  In the **Keywords** field, use the following information to search for the SuiteApp:
    
    -   Bundle Name - **Field Service Management**
        
    -   Bundle ID - **570821**
        
3.  On the Bundle Details page, click **Install**.
    

For more information about installing SuiteApps, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).
