You can view the time bills created on time-tracked tasks recorded from Mobile.

#### To view Field Service time bills in the employee record:

1.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Employees_.
    
2.  On the Employee list page, select the appropriate employee.
    
3.  On the Employee record, click the **Time Tracking** subtab.
    
4.  The Time Tracking subtab shows the list of time bill entries for the selected employee.
    

To view total weekly hours, open the Weekly Timesheets page. For more information, see [Weekly Timesheets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4671374137.html).

If you use SuitePeople Workforce Management (WFM) and FSM, weekly timesheets contain added features. For more information, see [Tracking Weekly Timesheets with WFM and FSM Hours](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0204014833.html).
