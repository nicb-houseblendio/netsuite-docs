If you use a supported NetSuite Center and you've customized the audience, category names, or link labels for the Field Service tab, those changes reset after a bundle update.

#### To add the audience again:

1.  Go to _Customization > Centers and Tabs > Center Tabs_.
    
2.  Under the **Name** column, find the Field Service tab for the appropriate center type, then click **Edit**.
    
3.  In the **Audience** subtab, select the appropriate role or roles under the **Roles** field.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To reset a category name:

1.  Go to _Customization > Centers and Tabs > Center Tabs_.
    
2.  Under the **Name** column, find the Field Service tab for the appropriate center type, then click **Edit**.
    
3.  In the **Content** subtab, under **Categories**, re-enter the name of the appropriate category in the **Name** column, then click **OK**.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To reset a link label:

1.  Go to _Customization > Centers and Tabs > Center Categories_.
    
2.  Under the **Label** column, find the appropriate category, then click **Edit**.
    
3.  In the **Values** subtab, re-enter the **Label** for the appropriate link.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
