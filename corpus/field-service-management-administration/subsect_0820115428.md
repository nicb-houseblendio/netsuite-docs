If you're using a NetSuite Center that's not supported, you might lose Mobile and Scheduler category links after an update.

#### To restore links to the Mobile and Scheduler:

1.  Go to _Customization > Centers and Tabs > Center Categories_.
    
2.  Under the **Label** column, find the category for the Mobile or Scheduler under your Field Service tab, then click **Edit**.
    
3.  Under the **Values** subtab, update the link and label as follows:
    
    | Category | Link | Label |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Scheduler | Field Service Scheduler Suitelet | Run |
    | Mobile | Field Service Mobile Suitelet | Run |
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    
5.  Repeat steps 2-4, make sure you restore both Mobile and Scheduler links.
