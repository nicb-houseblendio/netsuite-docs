Field Service Management (FSM) uses a mix of field service custom records and standard NetSuite records.

FSM custom records can include:

-   Field Service Asset Record
    
-   Field Service Configuration Record
    
-   Field Service Consumable Record
    
-   Field Service Recurrence Record
    
-   Field Service Time Zone Record
    

FSM commonly references these standard NetSuite records:

-   Entity and CRM records
    
    -   Project Record
        
    -   Case Record
        
    -   Task Record
        
    -   Project Task Record
        
    -   Employee Record
        
-   Transaction records:
    
    -   Quote Record
        
    -   Sales Order Record
        
    -   Item Fulfillment Record
        
    -   Expense Report Record
        
    -   Transfer Order Record
        
    -   Item Receipt Record
