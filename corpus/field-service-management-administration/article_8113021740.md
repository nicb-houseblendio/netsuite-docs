Field Service NetSuite features are the ones you'll mostly use in the NetSuite UI, not in the Field Service Management (FSM) Schedule Board or FSM Mobile. Some FSM features, like Consumables, work in all areas.

The Field Service NetSuite features are:

-   [Assets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0122012329.html)
    
-   [Consumables](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0122013734.html)
    
-   [Contact](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0122014203.html)
    
-   [File Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0122014439.html)
    
-   [Idempotency in Field Service Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1003110743.html)
    
-   [Multi-Language Support for FSM](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1021030703.html)
    
-   [Notifications](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0122014613.html)
    
-   [FSM Program Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0122014911.html)
    
-   [Recurrence](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0122020449.html)
    
-   [Script](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0122024419.html)
    
-   [Status Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0122024628.html)
    
-   [Time Zone](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0122025133.html)
    
-   [Tracking Field Service Time](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0918103711.html)
    
-   [Unbilled Amounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0122025911.html)
