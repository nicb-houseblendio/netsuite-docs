When you enable the Multi-Language feature, dates, times, time zones, and numbers on the scheduler board and mobile use your browser's language preference. To change your browser's language, open its Settings menu, find the Languages section, select your language from the list, set it as the display language, and restart your browser. If you're using a Mac, follow the instructions [here](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mh26684/mac) to set your browser's language.
