The Field Service Management (FSM) SuiteApp includes the Field Service Center-a custom center with tabs, categories, and links-to help you easily access FSM records and features. Centers are tabbed menu pages in NetSuite that change depending on your role. Center tabs include categories of links to NetSuite pages, custom records, Suitelets, or external web pages. To learn more about the NetSuite user interface and centers, see [Centers Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N131898.html).

When you update the FSM SuiteApp to a new version, you may lose some categories and links. For more information, see [Maintaining Field Service Navigation After SuiteApp Updates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0820095649.html).

## Field Service Tab, Categories, and Links {#subsect_0820102303}

The Field Service Center includes the Field Service tab. This tab also displays in some other standard NetSuite centers to make FSM features available to different user roles.

The Field Service Tab is supported for the following centers:

| Center | Type | Default Audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field Service Center | Custom | All roles |
| [Classic Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N136047.html) | Standard | All roles |
| [Sales Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N136610.html) | Standard | \- |
| [Support Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N136387.html) | Standard | Field Service Manager Field Service Technician Support Administrator |
