Here are some best practices to help you keep access to FSM features and avoid issues after a bundle update.

1.  [Use the Classic Interface](#subsect_0820035051)
    
2.  [Create a Custom Center Tab and Add Custom Links](#subsect_0820035124)
    

## Use the Classic Interface {#subsect_0820035051}

If you lose access to the Field Service tab after an update, switch to the Classic Interface to get it back.

Note:

Some roles, such as the Administrator role, use the Classic Center by default and the **Use Classic Interface** box is not available. The box is available in all custom roles not using the Classic Interface by default.

#### To switch to the Classic Interface:

1.  Go to _Home > Set Preferences_.
    
2.  On the **Appearance** subtab, under **Centers & Dashboards**, check the **Use Classic Interface** box.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

## Create a Custom Center Tab and Add Custom Links {#subsect_0820035124}

A custom center tab keeps its audience, categories, and links after a bundle update.

For example, to make sure you keep access to the Mobile or Scheduler, you can add them as custom links on a custom center tab.

To do this, perform the following steps in order:

| Step | Procedure |
| --- | --- |
| 1\. Create a custom center tab for Field Service. | See [Creating Center Tabs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2890512.html). Tip: Be sure to use a unique, simple to recall label for your custom center tab. |
| 2\. Create custom links for the Mobile and Scheduler Suitelets. | 
#### To create custom links for the Mobile and Scheduler Suitelets:

1.  Go to _Customization > Scripting > Script Deployments_.
2.  Click **View** next to the following:
    -   Script: Field Service Mobile Suitelet ID: `customdeploy_nx_mobile`
    -   Script: Field Service Scheduler Suitelet ID: `customdeploy_nx_scheduler`
3.  From the **URL** field, copy the link.
4.  Repeat steps 2-3, making sure you copy both Mobile and Scheduler Suitelet URLs.
5.  Go to _Customization > Centers and Tabs > Center Links_.
6.  On the Custom Center Links page, add new mobile and scheduler entries under the **Label** and **URL** columns. For example,
    -   **Label**: FSM Mobile **URL**: `https://system.netsuite.com/`<Mobile Suitelet URL copied from step 3> Sample: `https://system.netsuite.com/app/site/hosting/scriptlet.nl?script=4&deploy=2`
    -   **Label**: FSM Scheduler **URL**: `https://system.netsuite.com/`<Scheduler Suitelet URL copied from step 3> Sample: `https://system.netsuite.com/app/site/hosting/scriptlet.nl?script=4&deploy=3`
7.  Click **Save**.

 |
| 3\. Add the Mobile and Scheduler custom links to the custom center tab for Field Service. | 

#### To add your custom center links to a custom center tab

1.  Go to _Customization > Centers and Tabs > Center Categories > New_.
2.  In the **Label** field, enter a category name.
3.  From the **Center Type** list, select a center.
4.  From the **Center Tab** list, select the custom center tab you created.
5.  On the **Values** subtab, from the **Links** list, select the mobile and scheduler links you created.
6.  Click **Save**.

 |
