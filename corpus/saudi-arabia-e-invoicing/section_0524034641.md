By default, the Administrator and the CFO roles can configure the SuiteApp, accounts, and custom records.

Other roles can be created or customized to grant them permissions to use the SuiteApp. For more information, see [Customizing and Creating Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N286284.html) and [Setting Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N288727.html).
