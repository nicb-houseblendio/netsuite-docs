Prepayment is an advance payment made by a buyer to a seller before the delivery of goods or services. Prepayments is made to secure the delivery of goods or services and to show the buyer's commitment to the transaction. You can now make a prepayment from customer deposits.

The following topics provide the information about the required prerequisites, generating and attaching prepayment invoices and generating prepayment adjustment invoices.

-   [Attaching Prepayment Invoices to Original Invoices](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1119021557.html)
