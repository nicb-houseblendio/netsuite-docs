The Saudi Arabia e-Invoicing SuiteApp lets you send certified e-documents to your Saudi Arabian customers and vendors. E-documents are certified by ZATCA or the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority.

ZATCA allows businesses and agencies to certify e-documents through the Saudi Arabian Taxation Portal (ERAD), also known as FATOORA ([https://fatoora.zatca.gov.sa/](https://fatoora.zatca.gov.sa/)), before sending them to their customers. For Saudi Arabia, the SuiteApp applies ZATCA standards specified at [https://zatca.gov.sa/en/AboutUs/Pages/AboutZATCA.aspx](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/AboutUs/Pages/AboutZATCA.aspx).

Note:

Before using the SuiteApp, consider the following:

-   This SuiteApp is available for accounts with and without SuiteTax enabled.
    
-   Use of this SuiteApp is specific only to companies in Saudi Arabia and cannot be used for other countries.
