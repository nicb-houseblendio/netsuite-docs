As per article 53 of VAT Regulations, invoices must be in Arabic language. You can generate fields in Arabic language for the following entities:

-   Customer
    
-   Company Information
    
-   Items
    
-   Subsidiary
    

The XML file has the content in English, but the data that displays on an invoice must be in Arabic language. You can also generate an invoice with fields in different languages. The records will have custom fields to add the content in Arabic language. These fields are displayed on the PDF templates of invoice and credit memo.

For custom Records, under the **Saudi Arabia Fields** subtab, you can enter data for the following fields in Arabic language:

-   **Saudi Arabia Billing Address**
    
-   **Saudi Arabia Company Name**
    
-   **Saudi Arabia Shipping Address**
    

For other records, you can enter data for the following fields in Arabic language:

-   For accounts without OneWorld on the Company Information page:
    
    -   **Saudi Arabia Legal Name**
        
    -   **Saudi Arabia Arabic Address**
        
    
    For OneWorld accounts on the subsidiary record:
    
    -   **Saudi Arabia Legal Name**
        
    -   **Saudi Arabia Arabic Address**
        
-   For item record, **Saudi Arabia Item Name** field can be used in Arabic language.
