The Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp includes an E-Documents Dashboard Portlet that displays the number of e-documents that are pending a process. You can display the dashboard on your Home page and track e-documents based on their processing status. For more information, see [Displaying the E-Documents Portlet on the Home Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1495102371.html) and [Granting Access Permission to the E-Documents Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1495101245.html).
