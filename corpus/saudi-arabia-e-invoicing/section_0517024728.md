After completing the prerequisites, you can install the Saudi Arabia e-Invoicing SuiteApp from the SuiteApp Marketplace.

#### To install the Saudi Arabia e-Invoicing SuiteApp: {#procedure_0606023304}

1.  Go to **SuiteApps**.
    
2.  In the **Search** field enter "Saudi Arabia E-Invoicing."
    
3.  Click the SuiteApp tile.
    
4.  On the SuiteApp details page, click **Install**.
    
    For more information, see [Installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539799323.html) and [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).
    

The Saudi Arabia e-Invoicing SuiteApp is a managed SuiteApp. When improvements or new features are added to the SuiteApp, your account is automatically updated. For more information about this SuiteApp and its license, contact your NetSuite account representative.
