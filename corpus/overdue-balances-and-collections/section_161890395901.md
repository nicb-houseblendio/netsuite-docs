You can assign a dunning procedure to an invoice groups in the following ways:

-   [Assigning a Dunning Procedure to an Invoice Group Automatically](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161890610063.html)
    
-   [Assigning a Dunning Procedure to an Invoice Manually](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4047181293.html)
    
-   [Bulk Assigning Dunning Procedures](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4026429134.html)
    

Note:

You cannot use mass update to assign dunning procedures.
