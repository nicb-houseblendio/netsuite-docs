The dunning letter template record is where you select the template files to use for your dunning email letters and dunning PDF letters.

On a dunning procedure record, each dunning level that you add must be associated with a dunning letter template record.

The dunning letter template record consists of a single template file or several template files in various languages.

Create templates based on how your customers prefer to receive dunning letters. Create email templates for letters to be sent by email, and PDF templates for letters to be printed.
