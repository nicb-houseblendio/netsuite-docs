Verify the following settings:

-   In the CSV file, include all existing prices for each quantity.
    
-   Set **Data Handling to** **Update**.
    
-   In Advanced Options, check the **Overwrite Sublist box**.
    
-   Map the fields. All quantity pricing must be included in the mapping.
