For the Import Assistant a period is the decimal mark in CSV files by default. If your CSV uses a comma for decimals, you can set that on this page. The decimal mark you choose here overrides any account-level decimal mark preferences from the CSV Import Preferences page and your subsidiary's Preferences subtab.
