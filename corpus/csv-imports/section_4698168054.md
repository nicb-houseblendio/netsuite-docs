By default, imports of Sales Orders records don't cause charges to customer credit cards. If your NetSuite account has integrated credit card processing set up, you have the option of setting a Sales Orders import to create charges to customer credit cards.

#### To set up a Sales Orders import to charge customer credit cards: {#procedure_N427077}

1.  On the Field Mapping page for a Sales Orders import, double-click the Get Authorization field to display it in the center pane.
    
2.  Click the edit icon and set the default value for the field to Yes.
    
    If you don't map the Get Authorization field for a Cash Sales import, its value remains No, and no credit card charges occur.
