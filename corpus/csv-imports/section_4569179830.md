This section describes errors that you may see on an inventory detail import. For more information, see [Unable to Import Inventory Worksheet due to CSV Response/Error message 'Please configure the inventory detail for 'Item xxx'](#bridgehead_4569180483).

## Unable to Import Inventory Worksheet due to CSV Response/Error message 'Please configure the inventory detail for 'Item xxx' {#bridgehead_4569180483}

To perform the inventory worksheet import:

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Accounting Preferences_ > Items/Transactions.
    
2.  Clear the Require Bins on All Transactions Except Item Receipts box and save.
    
3.  Proceed with the CSV import of the Inventory Worksheet.
    
4.  Return to _Setup > Accounting > Accounting Preferences_ > Items/Transactions and check the **Require Bins on All Transactions Except Item Receipts** box.
    
5.  Click **Save.**
