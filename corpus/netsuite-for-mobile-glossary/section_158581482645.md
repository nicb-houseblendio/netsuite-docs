This glossary contains a list of icons used in NetSuite for Mobile help documentation. Most of the icons are platform specific - they are used for Android or iOS only. Check the following table for the platform specification and the icon definitions.

| **Icon** | **iOS** | **Android** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **iOS**: ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Account Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_account_menu_icon_1.png) **Android**: ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Navigation Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_menu_icon.png) | The Account Menu icon is located on the Navigation bar. You can tap it to access and change your app's settings. The Account Menu contains the following options:
-   **Choose Role** - Displays the role name on the top of the screen. You can change your role by tapping it.
-   **Remember Me** - Enables you to remove your login details. See [Removing your Login Details from NetSuite for Mobile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157857521373.html).
-   **Time Zone** - Configure the time zone of your app. See [Setting the Time Zone in NetSuite for iOS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3775069733.html).
-   **Phone Call Logging** - Configure phone call logging. See [Configuring Phone Call Logging in NetSuite for iOS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156163479756.html).
-   **Receipt Image Resolution** - Enables you to se a maximum resolution of image attachments. See [Attaching Files To a Record in NetSuite for Mobile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158694201455.html).
-   **Push Notifications** - Enable and configure push notifications.
-   **About** - The About screen contains information about the app and contains a link to contact support.
-   **Log Out** - Tap to log out of the app.
-   **Reset to Defaults** - Tap to perform a factory reset of the app. Note: When you reset the app to defaults all user data including login information, expense details, and time logs are removed.

 | The Navigation Menu icon is located on the Navigation bar. Tapping the Navigation Menu icon displays the menu bar, which serves as a main navigation hub for the app. The Navigation Menu contains the following options:

-   **Choose Role** - Displays the role name on the top of the screen. You can change your role by tapping it.
-   **Home** - Return to My Dashboard.
-   **Calendar** - View the calendar.
-   **Records** - Access all mobile supported record types. Default screen is the Recents screen, which displays the six most recently viewed records.
-   **Timer** - Begin recording a new time log or edit existing time logs.
-   **Expense Log** - Access existing expense items; capture new expenses.
-   (Optional) **Favorites** - This option enables you to have quick access to record types you mark as favorites.
-   **Settings** - Configure the settings for the application.
-   **About** - App, user, help, and support information.
-   **Log out** - Log out of the application.
