SuiteCommerce My Account and the NetSuite Customer Center role work together, but they serve different purposes. The Customer Center role controls customer access and forms. SuiteCommerce controls the customer-facing My Account experience. To customize the Customer Center role, see [Customizing the Customer Center Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N291685.html).

To show different fields in order history, configure Transaction List Columns in the SuiteCommerce Configuration record. See [Configure Transaction Columns](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0105031109.html). To collect additional information during checkout, use the SuiteCommerce Custom Fields extension when it's available for your domain. See [Create Custom Fields Using an Extension](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1531905521.html).

Use Customer Center role customization for access, links, and sales-order forms. Use SuiteCommerce for a branded shopping, checkout, or My Account experience. Use an extension or SuiteCommerce Advanced when configuration doesn't meet your requirements.
