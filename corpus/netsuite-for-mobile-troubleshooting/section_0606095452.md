When sharing files or images to NetSuite for iOS from your Photos app or Files app, be aware of the following known issues:

-   It's not possible to share JPEG images from Safari to NetSuite for iOS.
    
-   It's not possible to share a PDF from Messenger to NetSuite for iOS.
    
-   When you are logging an expense from within NetSuite for iOS, and you tap and hold on a file, iOS offers you the Share option again. The Share option in this context is not supported.
