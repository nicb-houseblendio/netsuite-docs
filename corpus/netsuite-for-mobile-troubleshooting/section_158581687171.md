You may experience compatibility issues with the default camera app with some Motorola phones. If you are experiencing issues while trying to capture an image from the expenses screen, Try switching to a third-party camera app. Make sure you set the third party camera app as the default camera app. The following camera app may work as a substitute:

-   Open Camera
