If you cannot log in, it could be due to any of the following:

-   A connection error - see [NetSuite for Mobile No Data Returned Error](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554197681.html).
    
-   An invalid email address or password - check your login details and try again. Make sure your entry is not changed by auto-correct. If you are sure that you are entering the correct details, contact your account administrator.
