If you cannot find the answer to your problem here, you might need to contact support. The following advice will save you time when speaking to support.

Note:

Before contacting customer support, try logging out of the app and back in again. This will renew your session. If the error persists, please contact NetSuite Customer Support.

Note:

Take note of the following:

-   Make a note of the sequence of actions that caused the error
    
-   If you receive an error dialog on the screen, make a note of it. It may be useful to take a screenshot of the error dialog.
    

To contact Support from within the app:

-   **For iOS**, go to the Account Menu ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for iOS Account Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/iOS_account_menu_icon_1.png) > About > Contact Support.
    
-   **For Android** go to Navigation Menu ![Screenshot of the NetSuite for Android Navigation Menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteMobile/Android_menu_icon.png) > About > Contact Support.
    

Go to the About screen to display information that could assist the support representative.

-   **For iOS**, tap the Account Menu icon, and then tap **About**.
    
-   **For Android** tap the Menu icon, and then tap **About**.
    

The following information is available:

-   App Version
    
-   Server Version
    
-   User Login
    
-   Account
    
-   Role
