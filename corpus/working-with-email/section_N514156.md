You can send email containing results from searches and reports of NetSuite data to other users.

An Email button is available on most report and search results pages.

-   For information about sending email directly from a report results page, see [Emailing a Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N734970.html).
    
-   For information about sending email directly from a search results page, see [Emailing Search Results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N664260.html).
    

If you're an administrator or have at least Create level of the Publish Search permission, you can enable system-generated search email messages on the Email subtab of a saved search page. Use saved search email to share complete search results with other users, to schedule long-running searches for low usage times, and to alert targeted users about changes to their important records. For more information, see [Saved Search Email](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N680983.html).
