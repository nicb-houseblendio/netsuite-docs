You can send information using your NetSuite account and can track all communication between you and those you do business with. Whether it's email, letters, or phone calls, you can plan and manage everything right in NetSuite. See [Sending Email from NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N512264.html).

You can also install NetSuite for Outlook which enables you to save messages from your Outlook mail account to NetSuite records. For more information, see [NetSuite for Outlook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_1552649469.html).

For a complete list of accepted top-level domains you can use for setting up your email address, see the [Internet Assigned Numbers Authority](http://www.iana.org) Web site.

For information about size limits for outbound and inbound email, see [Size Limits for Emails and Attachments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7130136991.html).

## Transport Layer Security Support {#bridgehead_37050570259}

NetSuite supports Transport Layer Security (TLS) for incoming and outgoing email messages. Note the following:

-   TLS support applies only to the SMTP protocol.
    
-   For outgoing email, TLS support depends on the recipient email server. NetSuite attempts to deliver outgoing email in an encrypted form, but if the recipient email server doesn't support encryption, email is delivered in plain text form.
