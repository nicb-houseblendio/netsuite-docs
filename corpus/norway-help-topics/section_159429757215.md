With the Norway Localization SuiteApp, it's possible to enable per-line classifications.

#### To enable per-line classifications:

1.  Go to Setup > Accounting > Accounting Preferences.
    
2.  On the **General** subtab, go to the Classifications section and check the **Allow Per-Line Departments** or **Allow Per-Line Classes** box, as per requirement.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

In addition, you may need to enable these transaction line fields on your respective custom form (Customization > Forms > Sublists).
