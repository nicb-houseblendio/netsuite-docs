Important:

This content is relevant for accounts without SuiteTax only.

It's necessary to map the subsidiary's tax codes to its corresponding Norwegian SAF-T standard VAT codes. These standard VAT codes are based on VAT academic logic. Without mapping tax codes, Norway Tax reports won't be able to identify relevant transactions to include in the reports.

You can also use an additional **Country** filter to generate Norway SAF-T reports that exclude other countries.

For information about mapping tax codes to Norway Standard Tax Codes, read the following topics:

-   [Mapping Norway Tax Codes (Accounts without SuiteTax)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164138030203.html)
    
-   [Mapping Tax Codes to the Norway Standard Tax Codes Category Using CSV Import](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159102247246.html)
