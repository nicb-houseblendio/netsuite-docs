## Prerequisite {#bridgehead_157018416700}

To use the Intrastat Reporting feature, the International Tax Reports SuiteApp must be installed in your NetSuite account. For more information, see [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html).

## France Intrastat DEB Report Fields on Transaction Forms {#bridgehead_157018420953}

If your NetSuite account nexus is a member of the European Union (EU), expect the following system behavior upon installation of the International Tax Reports SuiteApp:

-   If you choose an EU vendor or EU customer on a transaction form, the Tax Reporting subtab is displayed on the form.
    
-   If you choose a non-EU vendor or customer on a transaction form, the Tax Reporting subtab isn't displayed on the form.
    

The Tax Reporting subtab on transaction forms on the France Intrastat DEB Report contains the following fields:

-   **Region of Origin** - Region of an EU member state of dispatch where the goods were produced or manufactured, assembled, processed, repaired or maintained, or where the commercial process took place.
    
    Note:
    
    For the France Intrastat DEB Report, the column name for Region of Origin is listed as **Department**.
    
-   **Mode of Transport** - This field indicates the active vehicle that moves goods for every arrival or dispatch transaction. For more information, see [Mode of Transport](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1505110665.html).
    
-   **Country of Origin** - Refers to the country where the goods were harvested, manufactured, or substantially changed. For more information, see [Country of Origin](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157078252948.html).
    
-   **Regime Code (or Regime Code of Supply)** - Refers to customs-based codes that may be added to dispatches or arrival transactions.
    
-   **Nature of Transaction Code** - Type of transaction in the Intrastat declaration. For more information, see [Nature of Transaction Code](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2070738.html).
    

For more information about other EU Intrastat Report Fields on Transaction Forms, see [Intrastat Reporting Fields on Transaction Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4150707116.html#bridgehead_4150716738).

## France Intrastat DEB Report Fields on Item Records {#bridgehead_157018458076}

If the International Tax Reports SuiteApp is installed in your account, the following fields are displayed on some item record types:

-   **Commodity Code** - Field that indicates an eight-digit number to identify the type of goods or commodities for every arrival or dispatch transaction. For more information, see [Commodity Code](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2070438.html).
    
-   **Supplementary Unit** - Field that indicates the possible units measuring quantity. For more information, see [Supplementary Unit](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4605983594.html).
    

For more information about other EU Intrastat Report Fields on Item Records, see [Intrastat Reporting Fields on Item Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4150707116.html#bridgehead_4150717128).
