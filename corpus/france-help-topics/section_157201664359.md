Note:

This topic is for NetSuite accounts that use the Country Tax Report page to generate the Declaration of Exchange of Goods (DEB) report for France. If you're using the EU Intrastat Report page, see [France Intrastat DEB Report Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157018414429.html).

The Department field in the France Declaration of Exchange of Goods (DEB) report shows the two-digit numeric code indicating the French Department of the initial shipment of the goods on shipment, or the destination for the shipment. When you choose an EU vendor or customer on a transaction form for your France subsidiary, the Tax Reporting subtab displays the **French Department** field. This field must be filled for it to be included on the DEB report.
