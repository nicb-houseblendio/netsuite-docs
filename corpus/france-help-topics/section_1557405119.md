Setting up an account for an organization doing business in France usually requires enabling several features and installing additional SuiteApps.

## Enabling Features for France Account {#bridgehead_1557405141}

Some features available in your NetSuite account can be useful for businesses in France. Enable Company, Accounting, and SuiteCloud features to make your NetSuite account more suited to France's business requirements. For information about features and prerequisites for specific product areas, see [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4169635010.html#bridgehead_159834703224).

Note:

If you don't have sufficient permissions to enable features, please contact your account administrator.

#### To enable features:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features (Administrator)_.
    
2.  On the **Company** subtab, go to the International section and check the **Multi-language** and **Multiple Currencies** boxes.
    
3.  On the **Accounting** subtab, go to the Basic Features subtab and check the **GL Audit Numbering** box.
    
4.  On the **Accounting** subtab, go to the Advanced Features section and check the **Period End Journal Entries** and **Foreign Currency Variance Mapping** boxes.
    
5.  On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, enable the desired features on the following subtabs:
    
    -   SuiteBuilder
        
    -   SuiteScript
        
    -   SuiteGL
        
    -   SuiteFlow
        
    
    For more information about SuiteCloud features, see [SuiteCloud Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N239332.html).
    
6.  If your company is registered for EU One Stop Shop, check the **EU One Stop Shop** box on the **Tax** subtab.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

## Available SuiteApps {#bridgehead_158401489866}

Some additional features require installation of a specific SuiteApp. SuiteApps are applications that extend NetSuite for specific industry and business needs. For information about the required SuiteApps for specific product areas, see [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4169635010.html#bridgehead_159834703224).

France-specific features are currently available in the following SuiteApps:

| SuiteApp | Area | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Electronic Bank Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1585433.html) | Payments | Enables bank payments of vendor bills, employee expenses, partner and employee commissions, customer refunds, and customer payments within France. |
| [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html) | Taxation (NetSuite accounts without SuiteTax) | Generates France VAT report in English or French, sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports. If your account has the SuiteTax feature enabled, don't install this SuiteApp. |
| Supplementary Tax Calculation | Taxation (NetSuite accounts without SuiteTax) | Manages EU Reverse charges and supports the calculation of nondeductible input tax. For more information, see [Recording Nondeductible Input Tax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1822590.html) and [Setting Up Tax Codes to Post Notional Tax Amounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4702880164.html). This SuiteApp also supports VAT on prompt payment discounts. For more information, see [Recording Tax on Prompt Payment Discount](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4702990460.html). If your account has the SuiteTax feature enabled, don't install this SuiteApp. |
| [SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1557396965.html) | Taxation (NetSuite accounts with SuiteTax) | For NetSuite accounts with the SuiteTax feature enabled, the SuiteTax Engine sets default tax codes based on local tax requirements. |
| [Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1528262949.html) | Taxation (NetSuite accounts with or without SuiteTax) | The Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp, when installed with the France Localization SuiteApp, enables you to generate France-specific tax reports including Periodic VAT Return CA3, DEB Arrivals and Dispatches, European Services Declaration (DES), and the Fichier d'Ecritures Comptables (FEC) files. |
| [EMEA Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_157122959910.html) | 
-   Payments
-   Taxation (NetSuite accounts with SuiteTax)

 | Provides custom lists and custom fields that will be used to generate tax reports and electronic bank payments for countries in the EMEA region. This SuiteApp is required for installing the France Localization SuiteApp. |
| [France Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157294233447.html) | 

-   Accounting
-   Taxation (NetSuite accounts with or without SuiteTax)
-   Payments

 | Provides the following features:

-   France Chart of Accounts or Plan Comptable Général template.
-   France financial reports, including those in CEFRA formats.
-   Validations to ensure compliance of reports to French laws, such as ensuring that account numbers are in the correct format, that transactions contain memos, and that no more than 24 accounting periods are open at one time.
-   SuiteTax-based report templates of Periodic VAT Return CA3, DEB, and DES.
-   Fichier d'Ecritures Comptables (FEC) files that can be generated with or without the SuiteTax feature.
-   Reports for handling hybrid VAT
-   Lettrage reports for A/P and A/R accounts
-   Saved searches that list the transactions that don't conform to the FEC reporting requirements.
-   France electronic bank payment templates

Note: The France Localization SuiteApp can also be used with the International Tax Reports SuiteApp if you aren't using SuiteTax. |
| [GL Matching](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1516099228.html) | Accounting | Enables reconciliation (lettrage) for balance sheet accounts. Account types supported by the GL Matching SuiteApp aren't limited to Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable types. |
| [One Stop Shop](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162918935998.html) | Taxation (NetSuite Accounts With or Without SuiteTax) | Enables the EU VAT One Stop Shop Reports (OSS) generation. OSS is categorized into three specific schemes: Non-Union OSS Scheme, Union OSS Scheme, and Import OSS Scheme. These schemes allow taxable persons to declare and pay VAT due in Member States where the taxable persons aren't established. |

#### To install a SuiteApp:

1.  Go to _Customization > SuiteBundler > Search & Install Bundles_.
    
2.  Search the following SuiteApps using their names or their Bundle IDs.
    
3.  On the Bundle Details page, click **Install**.
    

For more information about SuiteApps, see [SuiteApps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_3134602194.html).

For more information about SuiteApps installation, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).
