This section contains details of the following reports, which are built using the Country-Specific Reports SuiteApp:

-   [France Balance Sheet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160145374751.html#subsect_160103458034)
    
-   [France Income Statement](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160145374751.html#subsect_160103487416)
    
-   [France SIG](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160145374751.html#subsect_160136787300) (Soldes intermédiaries de gestion)
    

Specifically, it shows from which account (indicated by account number prefix and account description) each item in the report is sourced.

## France Balance Sheet {#subsect_54170701023}

This report refers to the Balance Sheet (Bilan) report created in the Country-Specific Reports framework. To access this report, go to Reports > France Reports > Country-Specific Reports. For more information, see [France Reports in Country-Specific Reports Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156388724912.html#subsect_160034331797).

Note:

If you're using manual journal entries to close your Profit or Loss accounts at the end of the Fiscal Year, you have to move your accounts with prefix 12 from the **Retained Earnings Previous FY** variable (Cell ID: FrenchBS\_PLNA) to the **Financial period earnings (profit or loss)** variable (Cell ID: FrenchBS\_DI). Ensure that the previous year result is assigned. For information about customizing CSR variables, see [Modifying France Reports (Country-Specific Reports)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160086702493.html).
