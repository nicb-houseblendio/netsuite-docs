## Overview {#bridgehead_157018319741}

NetSuite's International Tax Reports SuiteApp enables you to generate the France Intrastat Déclaration d' Échanges de biens or Declaration of Exchange of Goods (DEB) Report for Arrivals and Dispatches. The France Intrastat DEB report is generated monthly, and must be submitted on the 10 th business day of the month, following the month in which the transaction takes plac e.

NetSuite's International Tax Reports SuiteApp enables you to:

-   Generate the France Intrastat DEB Report for Arrivals and Dispatches in English and French. For more information, see [Generating the France Intrastat DEB Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157018543598.html).
    
-   Export the France Intrastat DEB Report to a format acceptable for upload to the tax agency website.
    

## Requirements {#bridgehead_157018332873}

To generate the France Intrastat DEB Report, you must have the following requirements:

-   International Tax Reports SuiteApp. For more information, see [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html).
    
-   Access to Intrastat Reports. For more information, see [Granting Access to Intrastat Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2061400.html).
