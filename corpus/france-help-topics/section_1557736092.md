If you have a France subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the France TVA Form in English or French. The report can be used as a source for filling in the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.

If you're using SuiteTax, French tax report templates including VAT Return Form CA3, and Declaration of Exchange of Goods (DEB) are delivered by the France Localization SuiteApp. For more information, see [France Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157294233447.html).
