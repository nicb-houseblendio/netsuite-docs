Some France Localization components, such as the France Chart of Accounts and France financial saved reports, aren't accessible to all standard roles. By default, some standard roles are granted permissions to access these features.

The France Chart of Accounts, and France VAT saved reports, such as the FR Achats par Code TVA - sur encaissement and FR Ventes par Code TVA - sur encaissement among other reports, are accessible to the following standard roles:

-   Accountant
    
-   Accountant (Reviewer)
    
-   CEO
    
-   CEO (Hands Off)
    
-   CFO
    

If you want to grant other roles the permission to access and import the France Chart of Accounts, see [Setting Permissions to Use the France Chart of Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4730537012.html).

The France financial saved reports have different access conditions:

| FR Compte de résultat (Income Statement) | Accountant, Accountant (Reviewer), CEO, CEO (Hands Off), CFO |
