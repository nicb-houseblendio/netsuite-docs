The France Localization SuiteApp provides a France Chart of Accounts template that you can modify or keep depending on your accounting preference. The France Chart of Accounts contains all the standard accounts and subaccounts prescribed by the Plan Comptable Général.

To access and download the France Chart of Accounts template, go to Setup > France Localization > France Chart of Accounts.

As a best practice, don't install more than one Chart of Accounts. If you had earlier installed a localized Chart of Accounts (another set of accounts apart from the main Chart of Accounts in NetSuite), use an accounting context for France. For more information, see [Accounting Contexts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4640155582.html).

For information about modifying the Chart of Accounts template, see [Guidelines for Chart of Accounts Edit and Import](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163056945135.html).
