The France tax and financial reports and the France Localization SuiteApp pages, such as the France Chart of Accounts and the France Localization Configuration pages, can be displayed in the following languages:

-   English (U.S.)
    
-   Français (France)
    

For information about displaying the France Localization SuiteApp pages to your preferred language, see [Setting Language Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543591052.html).

Note:

If you set the localization preference to any language other than these supported languages, the pages will be displayed in English (U.S.).

To display a SuiteTax-based report (generated using the Country Tax Reports page) in your preferred language, after clicking **View** next to the generated tax report, select a language from the Language list.
