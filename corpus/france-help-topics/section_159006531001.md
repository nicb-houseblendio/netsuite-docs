The France Localization SuiteApp provides the SuiteTax-based Déclaration européenne de services (DES) or the European Services Declaration, a report required in France. This report can be generated using the Country Tax Reports page. For more information, see [Generating the France FEC and SuiteTax-Based Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163058970280.html). You can export the DES report to PDF and XLS. For more information, see [Exporting a Country Tax Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156645000208.html).

The DES is required for companies in France that provide services to companies established in other EU Member States. A summary of the operations and services carried out to the companies, the DES is collected to monitor VAT taxation on intra-EU services.

| Report Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Value | Amount in Euros indicated on the invoice without taxes (net amount). |
| Customer VAT Number | Customer tax registration number as indicated on the invoice. |
