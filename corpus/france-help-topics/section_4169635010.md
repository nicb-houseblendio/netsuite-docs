## Overview {#bridgehead_1557404989}

The following Help topics provide information about NetSuite features that are specific for companies doing business in France. The NetSuite International edition can be configured with capabilities and reports specific to France's business setting and requirements.

For information about localized editions of NetSuite, as well as setting up NetSuite accounts and subsidiaries, read the following topics:

-   [NetSuite Editions Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N270055.html)
    
-   [Introduction to NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N266468.html) for companies using NetSuite across multiple subsidiaries and countries.
    
-   [Set up NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N268052.html)
    
-   [Creating Subsidiary Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N272471.html) on NetSuite OneWorld.
    

General NetSuite features are covered in other chapters and topics.

## NetSuite Certificates {#bridgehead_0630013821}

NetSuite is audited and certified by local auditors in France.

You can download the certificates from this SuiteAnswers page: [Country Specific NetSuite Certificates](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1020881).
