| **Tool Name** | ns\_prompt\_library\_app |
| --- | --- |
| **Tool Description** | Helps you discover, select, and use prompt templates from the NetSuite AI Connector Service Companion SuiteApp. To use this MCP App, the NetSuite AI Connector Service Companion SuiteApp must be installed in your NetSuite account. For more information, see [NetSuite AI Connector Service Companion SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9091153093.html). The AI client can use this interactive MCP App to present a guided prompt library. The app returns standard prompt templates included in the Companion SuiteApp and public custom prompt templates available in your account. To browse or search for prompt templates, ask your AI client to open the Prompt Library MCP App. You can then browse or filter the available templates, select a template, review and adapt the prompt text in the AI client's input field, and submit it. The AI client processes the submitted prompt using the NetSuite AI Connector Service and available MCP tools. |
| **Annotation** | NetSuite Prompt Library |

## Properties {#subsect_30095223188}

| Property | Type | Required or Optional | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `filter` | string | Optional | The text used to filter the available prompts. |

## Permissions {#subsect_91095229571}

There are no specific permission requirements to see this MCP App in the AI client. The app returns standard prompt templates from the NetSuite AI Connector Service Companion SuiteApp and public custom prompt templates available in your account.

When you use a selected prompt, the data you can access and actions you can perform depend on the permissions assigned to your NetSuite role. They also depend on the MCP tools used by the prompt.
