NetSuite has adopted the Model Context Protocol (MCP), which boosts its integration capabilities and enables secure interactions between AI models and data systems. This feature provides users, such as financial users, with a seamless integration that lets supported AI clients directly access and interact with NetSuite data and functionality.

NetSuite provides the MCP Standard Tools SuiteApp with a set of tools that let you interact with your NetSuite data, but you also have the option to build your own tools. Custom tools can also support MCP Apps, which enable interactive user interfaces in compatible AI clients. For detailed installation guidance and more information about the SuiteApp, see [MCP Standard Tools SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_143403258.html). To build your own tools, see [Creating Custom Tools for the NetSuite AI Connector Service](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162020236.html).

Important:

Before working with the NetSuite AI Connector Service, consider the following:

-   The NetSuite AI Connector Service has not been assessed for compliance with the U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Accordingly, this feature is not represented or warranted as having the administrative, physical, or technical safeguards necessary to create, receive, maintain, transmit, or otherwise process electronic protected health information (ePHI). You must not use this feature to store, process, or transmit ePHI unless and until you have independently determined that such use is consistent with your own HIPAA compliance obligations and applicable law.
    
-   It's important to familiarize yourself with [Associated Risks, Controls, and Mitigation Strategies](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9002708453.html) when working with AI clients and large language models (LLMs).
    
-   To add the NS AI Connector in Claude, you'll need the Pro plan (or higher).
    
    If you're using a different AI client, please check that provider's help documentation for their requirements, and review [Creating Custom Tools for the NetSuite AI Connector Service](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162020236.html).

Note:

In user-facing contexts, AI applications that can connect to MCP servers are often called **AI assistants**, whereas applications that act autonomously may be also referred to as **AI agents**. For consistency, the NetSuite AI Connector Service and other related help topics use the term **AI clients** to refer to all such applications. Other terms used in the help topics may also differ in documentation produced by different companies, as naming conventions are not standardized yet across the industry.
