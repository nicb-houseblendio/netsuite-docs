The NetSuite AI Connector Service Companion SuiteApp provides example prompts organized by business function to help you get started with the NetSuite MCP Standard Tools SuiteApp. Prompt samples are organized into categories for specific roles and include the raw prompt text for your reference. You can copy and update them to fit your needs.

The Companion provides prompt samples in the following categories that represent common NetSuite processes and workflows:

-   **[Financial Prompts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0204100919.html)** - Period narratives, margin analysis, budget variance flags, working capital, and accounts receivable (AR) and accounts payable (AP) insights.
    
-   **[Visualization Prompts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206093311.html)** - Dashboard-like stylized HTML or charts, and scenario summaries.
    
-   **[Order to Cash (Customer Management) Prompts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206093345.html)** - Customer creation and updates, sales orders and invoices, open invoice reviews, and customer statements.
    
-   **[Procure to Pay (Vendor Management) Prompts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206093429.html)** - Vendor setup, purchase order (PO) and bill monitoring, approvals, and Automated Clearing House (ACH) and check payment runs.
    
-   **[Item Configuration Prompts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206093510.html)** - Item creation and updates, and schedule-related setup for revenue and billing.
    
-   **[Record to Report Prompts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206093553.html)** - Journal entries and intercompany journals with structured fields.
    
-   **[System Administration Prompts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0206093633.html)** - Administrator analysis of customizations, dependencies, and recommendations for keep, replace, and retire actions.
