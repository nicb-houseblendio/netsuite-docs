Depending on your store's policy, customers can receive cash for their gift card balance. Generally the balance must be more than $10.00, but your store's policy may vary.

#### To cash out an external gift card.

1.  Tap **Cash out Gift Card**.
    
2.  Swipe the card or enter the gift card **Number**.
    
3.  Tap **Enter** or tap the green check mark.
    
    The gift card balance appears as change due.
