Support is available to NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS) customers by telephone or by issuing a case. Before reaching out, NetSuite Customer Support asks that customers enter their concerns in the SuiteAnswers search field first to see if an answer is already available.

#### To contact Customer Support:

1.  Log into NetSuite.
    
2.  From the **Support** tab, click **Go to SuiteAnswers**.
    
3.  Select from the options in the right pane to contact Support.
