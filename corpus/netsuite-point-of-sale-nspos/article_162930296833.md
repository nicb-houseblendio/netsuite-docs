NSPOS 2020.1.X brings a new option to provide your shoppers with a digital receipt sent to them using email. Now your sales associates can provide a receipt using any of these configured options:

| 
-   No receipt (None)
-   Printed receipt
-   Digital receipt
-   Printed and digital receipt

 | ![Cashier prompt for receipt type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_cashier_prompt_does_want_smaller.png) |
| --- | --- |

Digital receipts are sent to the shopper's email address as a PDF attachment. The process uses NetSuite's PDF template functionality for the receipt layout. This functionality means you can customize your digital receipts with your logo and business information. Options are also available to use a default template for most locations, but special templates for others.

![Digital receipt email template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_email_template_small.png) ![Digital receipt default template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_default_template_small.png)

If your shopper is saved as a customer in NSPOS, and the sales associate adds them to the transaction, their email address is pre-filled. If the shopper is a guest, or if a saved customer wants to use a different address, the sales associate enters it in a prompt from the register.

Tip:

The application uses the employee email address you choose as the digital receipt sender's email address. The address is pulled from the senders employee record in NetSuite.

You should decide which address to use before beginning the setup. A new employee can be set up for this purpose. Go to Lists > Employees > Employees.

## Sales Associate Receipt Procedure {#subsect_162889211788}

Cashiers, sales associates and others who process transactions in NSPOS should follow these steps when providing shoppers with receipts.

#### To print or email a receipt:

1.  Ask shopper if they want their receipt printed, sent digitally by email, or both.
    
    ![Cashier prompt for receipt method](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_cashier_prompt_does_want_smaller.png)
2.  Tap the option matching the shopper's request.
    
    If printed, hand shopper receipt after printing.
    
3.  For digital or printed and digital requests:
    
    ![Receipt email address entry form](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_cashier_email_entry.png)
    
    -   If shopper is saved as a customer, the email address is pre-filled. Ask if that is the address to use.
        
        If you have not added the saved customer to the transaction, use **Search Customer** to locate them.
        
    -   A shopper saved as a customer can request to use an email address different from their saved address. If so, the associate is asked if the new email address should replace the address saved now.
        
    -   If shopper is a guest (not in NSPOS), ask for their email address.
        
        Tip:
        
        For shopper privacy and to improve accuracy, you might provide a paper and pen for the shopper to write their email address.
        
    
    1.  Enter the email address as needed.
        
    2.  Tap **Done**.
        
    3.  NSPOS validates that the email address is formatted correctly. It does not validate if the address is correct.
        
    4.  You are alerted that the receipt will be sent by email.
        
        ![Conformation message saying receipt email will be sent](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_cashier_sent_msg.png)
    5.  Notify shopper that the digital receipt will be sent to their email address.
        
4.  If a printed receipt was requested, pass the receipt to the shopper.
    

For NSPOS 2020.1.X and later, NetSuite ERP saves the receipt in the transaction history. A sales associate can reprint a receipt from the register.

## Re-send a Digital Receipt {#subsect_163820798755}

If a shopper asks to have a receipt sent again, including to a different address, the action must be performed in NetSuite ERP. The option to re-send a receipt is not available from an NSPOS register.

The list of sent receipt email is saved with the employee record that uses the **From** email address. This is the address you choose when configuring your digital receipt setup.

#### To re-send a digital receipt email:

1.  Go to Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types.
    
2.  On the **NSPOS Enable Features** row, click **List**.
    
3.  Click **View**.
    
4.  Open the **Digital Receipt** subtab.
    
5.  Click the **Sender Email Address** name to open their employee record.
    
    ![send email address](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_re_send_enable_features.png)
    
    Email sent from this employee-record address are found under the **Communication** subtab.
    
6.  Use the **Primary Recipient** column to locate the shopper's email address.
    
7.  Click **View** for the desired email.
    
    ![Primary Recipient column and View link](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_re_send_emply_record.png)
8.  Click **Reply to All**.
    
9.  Leave the **Additional Recipients** '<Type then tab>' field empty.
    
10.  Click the **Email** field and enter the shopper's email address.
     
11.  Click **Merge & Send**.
     
     Tip:
     
     You can click **Refresh** for the list to confirm the email was sent.
     

## Enabling Digital Receipts in NetSuite {#subsect_162889278874}

Use the NSPOS Enable Features page to enable the digital receipt option on your registers. This page is also where you set up the default digital receipt. This universal default is used in all stores without a by-location digital receipt setup.

You can include your logo, company information, website, and add header/footer text to customize your digital receipt.

NSPOS uses NetSuite's **Advanced PDF/HTML Template** and **Email Template** features to configure digital receipts. You should familiarize yourself with the features before attempting to customize a receipt setup. See [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_162940353860) and [Email Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_163424664062).

Tip:

Upload your receipt logo before doing these steps. See [Logos for Digital Receipts](#subsect_163424739167).

#### To enable and set up digital receipts:

1.  Go to Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types.
    
2.  On the **NSPOS Enable Features** row, click **List**.
    
3.  Click **Edit**.
    
4.  Open the **Digital Receipt** subtab.
    
5.  Check the **Enable Digital Receipt** box.
    
    Tip:
    
    If **Enable Digital Receipt** is not checked, the digital receipt setup fields will not appear in NSPOS.
    
    ![Enable features page to configure digital receipts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_enable_features_NS_ERP.png)
6.  Select the **Email Template** or use the default template.
    
    The email template formats the email used to deliver the receipt. See [Email Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_163424664062).
    
7.  For **Advanced PDF Template**, select **Custom Digital receipt PDF/HTML Template NSPOS**.
    
    The PDF template formats the digital receipt layout. You can customize a template using XML. See [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_162940353860).
    
    Note:
    
    Template procedures are standard NetSuite functionality.
    
8.  Select the employee whose email address should be used for the **Sender Email Address**. This field configures the **From:** address.
    
    Important:
    
    The owner of the sending email address must be set up as an **Employee** in NetSuite.
    
9.  Select the **Receipt Logo**. See [Logos for Digital Receipts](#subsect_163424739167).
    
10.  Enter your **Company Name** and other business information that you want included on the digital receipt:
     
11.  Enter the company **Street** address, **City** and state, **ZIP**, **Country**, and **Company Telephone** as it should appear on the receipt.
     
     Tip:
     
     Include the State in the **City** field. For example, enter `Redwood Shores, CA`.
     
12.  Use **Receipt Details-Web** to list a website to include. For example, enter `www.retailbusinessname.com`.
     
13.  Enter **Header Text** to appear at the top of the receipt.
     
14.  Enter **Footer Text** to appear at the bottom of the receipt.
     
15.  Click **Save**.
     
16.  Use any NSPOS register to finish the digital receipt setup. See [Finishing the Digital Receipt Setup in NSPOS](#subsect_162889289970).
     

## Setting Up Digital Receipts by Location in NetSuite {#subsect_162889292574}

In addition to setting up a default digital receipt, you can set up unique receipts to use at specific locations. For example, you might want to use a different logo or display company information that is different from the default.

Locations without a receipt setup will use the default setup.

NSPOS uses NetSuite's **Advanced PDF/HTML Template** and **Email Template** features to configure digital receipts. You should familiarize yourself with the features before attempting to customize a receipt setup. See [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_162940353860) and [Email Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_163424664062).

#### To set up a digital receipt for a location:

1.  Log into NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to Setup > Company > Locations.
    
3.  Find the desired location and click **Edit**.
    
4.  For **Digital Receipt - PDF Template**, select **Custom Digital receipt PDF/HTML Template NSPOS**.
    
    The PDF template formats the digital receipt layout. You can also customize a template using XML. See [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_162940353860).
    
    Note:
    
    Template procedures are standard NetSuite functionality.
    
5.  Select the receipt **Logo**. See [Logos for Digital Receipts](#subsect_163424739167).
    
    ![Location record top with digital receipts fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_configure_Location_NS_ERP_split_1.png)
6.  Open the **Digital Receipt** subtab.
    
7.  Check the **Enable Location Specific Settings** box.
    
8.  Select the **Email Template** or use the default template.
    
    The email template formats the email used to deliver the receipt. See [Email Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_163424664062).
    
9.  Select the employee whose email address should be used for the **Sender Email Address**. This field configures the **From:** address.
    
    Important:
    
    The owner of the sending email address must be set up as an **Employee** in NetSuite.
    
10.  Enter your **Company Name** and other business information that you want included on the digital receipt:
     
     ![Location record bottom with digital receipts fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_configure_Location_NS_ERP_split_2.png)
11.  Enter the company **Street** address, **City** and state, **ZIP**, **Country**, and **Company Telephone** as it should appear on the receipt.
     
     Tip:
     
     For the **City** field, enter the City and State. For example, Redwood Shores, CA.
     
12.  Use **Receipt Details-Web** to list a website to include. For example, enter `www.retailbusinessname.com`.
     
13.  Enter **Header Text** to appear at the top of the receipt.
     
14.  Enter **Footer Text** to appear at the bottom of the receipt.
     
15.  Click **Save**.
     
16.  If not already done, use any NSPOS register to finish the digital receipt setup. See [Finishing the Digital Receipt Setup in NSPOS](#subsect_162889289970).
     

## Finishing the Digital Receipt Setup in NSPOS {#subsect_162889289970}

After setting up digital receipts in NetSuite ERP, you must configure the receipts in NSPOS. You can perform these steps on any NSPOS register. The next synchronization cycle will apply automatically the settings to other stores and registers.

#### To set up digital receipts in NSPOS:

1.  Press **Ctrl+F12** on the keyboard to open the Function List.
    
2.  Enter `receipts` in the search field.
    
3.  Tap **Configure Receipts**.
    
    ![Search for receipt options](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_NSPOS_CtrlF12_Configure.png)
4.  Scroll right to the **Retail** section.
    
    ![Digital receipt options](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_NSPOS_CtrlF12_Settings.png)
5.  To enable the **Prompt** for a receipt type, enter **Ask customer** (default).
    
6.  Select a **Default receipt option** - None, printed, digital, or printed and digital. The default is the option pre-selected for the receipt prompt.
    
7.  Under **Retail: Receipt type** options, check the receipt types to offer in the prompt.
    
    If a receipt type is not selected, the **Default receipt option** is used.
    
    Tip:
    
    To prevent the receipt prompt from appearing, check the **Default receipt option** and clear any checks in the **Retail: Receipt type** boxes.
    
8.  Click **Done**.
    
    Note:
    
    The **Required Flag** text field is not required. You can leave it cleared (empty).
    

## Troubleshooting Digital Receipts {#subsect_163466105592}

This topic provides steps for troubleshooting sending digital receipts to shoppers. For issues not listed here, contact NetSuite Customer Support.

To attempt sending a receipt email again, see [Re-send a Digital Receipt](#subsect_163820798755).

-   **Receipt email was undeliverable** - To view email that could not be delivered, go to Lists > Mailing > Undelivered Emails. The page includes possible reasons that the delivery failed.
    
-   **Receipt email was not sent** - If a transaction invoice and receipt did not synchronize from the register to NetSuite, the email cannot be sent. Contact NetSuite Customer Support for assistance.
    
-   **Receipt email not received due to incorrect email address** - If a shopper contacts your business saying they had given an incorrect address, update the shopper's customer information in NSPOS. Then follow the steps to [Re-send a Digital Receipt](#subsect_163820798755).
    

### Working with Templates {#subsect_163820976038}

The NSPOS 2020.1.X bundle includes one PDF/HTML template and one email template. Both templates use elements designed to be populated with NSPOS data. You should not customize these original template layouts.

If customization is desired, you can save copies of the templates and customize only those copies. Using copies to apply customizations can prevent future bundle updates from overwriting your work.

Note that template procedures are part of standard NetSuite functionality. Use the Help Center links included in the following topics as guides to customizing templates.

Important:

You should not customize the NSPOS default PDF/HTML and email templates. Apply customizations to template copies only.

#### Advanced PDF/HTML Templates for Digital Receipts {#subsect_162940353860}

This template feature formats the digital receipt layout. You can use it to customize your digital receipts.

Important:

Customizing a PDF template requires a working knowledge of XML.

You must have the **Advanced PDF/HTML Templates** feature enabled.

#### To enable Advanced PDF/HTML Templates:

1.  Log into NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
3.  Open the **SuiteCloud** subtab.
    
4.  Check the **Advanced PDF/HTML Templates** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To work with the NSPOS PDF Template:

1.  Log into NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to _Customization > Forms > Advanced PDF/HTML Templates_.
    
3.  Locate the template **Custom Digital receipt PDF/HTML Template NSPOS**.
    
4.  Click **Edit** to view the XML source and make changes.
    
    Tip:
    
    You can also edit a template in Preview mode by toggling **Source Code** off.
    
    Read all prompts that alert you to possible saving issues when switching between Preview mode and Source Code mode.
    
    ![toggle source code on](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NS_Template_source_code_ON.png) ![toggle source code off](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NS_Template_source_code_OFF.png)
5.  Before making any changes, click **Save As** and enter a different name.
    
    Then adjust the Company and any Location setups to use the PDF template copy.
    

For more information, see [Using PDF Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N530499.html).

#### Email Templates for Digital Receipts {#subsect_163424664062}

The email template formats the email used to deliver the receipt PDF. You can customize this email.

You must have **Customer Relationship Management** enabled first.

#### To enable Customer Relationship Management:

1.  Log into NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
3.  Open the **CRM** subtab.
    
4.  Check the **Customer Relationship Management** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To work with the NSPOS digital email template

1.  Log into NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to _Documents > Templates > Email Templates_
    
3.  Locate the **Default digital receipt** template.
    
4.  Click **Edit** to view the XML source and make changes.
    
    The template **Edit** mode uses a text editor, not an XML source editor.
    
5.  If you make changes, you should use **Save As** and enter a different name.
    
    Then adjust the Company and any Location setups to use this email template.
    

For more information, see [Working with Email Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N514744.html).

## Logos for Digital Receipts {#subsect_163424739167}

You can upload a logo image file for a digital receipt using either of two methods:

-   From the NSPOS Enable Features > Digital Receipt subtab, click **+** to upload.
    
    ![Receipt logo field](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_receipt_logo.png)
    
    You can also add a **Logo** from the location record.
    
-   Upload an image file to the File Cabinet. See [Upload Images to the File Cabinet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162137397938.html).
