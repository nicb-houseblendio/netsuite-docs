Follow these procedures to unlock a NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS) employee account or to reset their password.

## Unlocking an Employee NSPOS Account {#subsect_158802396978}

Use this task when an employee is locked out of NSPOS registers due to five or more incorrect sign on attempts.

#### To unlock an account in NSPOS:

1.  Press **Ctrl+F12**.
    
2.  Search for `edit operator`.
    
3.  Tap **Edit Operator**.
    
4.  Search for the new operator by last name or open an operator group.
    
5.  Tap the operator record to open.
    
6.  Tap **Unlock User**.
    
7.  At the prompt, tap **Yes**.
    
8.  Tap **Done**.
    

Note:

You can also do this with the **Manager Functions** button. By default, this button is on the main (bottom) button bar in the Journal window. Your company's custom setup might have the button in a different location or removed it.

## Resetting an Employee NSPOS Password {#subsect_158802428561}

Follow these steps to reset a NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS) password. Use this procedure when:

-   A cashier forgets their password
    
-   They believe their password has been compromised, such as being stolen or shared
    
-   Your business practices require regular password maintenance
    
-   You believe changing a password would be beneficial
    

#### To reset a password in NSPOS:

1.  Press **Ctrl+F12**.
    
2.  Search for `edit operator`.
    
3.  Tap **Edit Operator**.
    
4.  Search for the new operator by last name or open an operator group.
    
5.  Tap the operator record to open.
    
6.  Tap **Set Password**.
    
7.  Enter the new **Password**.
    
8.  Enter the password again to **Confirm**.
    
9.  Tap **Change**.
    

Note:

You can also perform this task using the **Manager Functions** button. By default, this button is on the main (bottom) button bar in the Journal window. Your company's custom setup might have the button in a different location or removed it.
