Use the NSPOS SuiteTax tax schedules settings to assign tax codes or tax groups to your tax schedules. The codes and groups determine the tax rates to be charged.

Important:

Your tax schedules must be set up **before** configuring your tax schedule settings. See [Tax Schedules](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0313125458.html).

#### To configure your Tax Schedule Settings:

1.  Go to NSPOS > SuiteTax > Tax Schedules Settings.
    
2.  For the desired schedules, select a **Tax Code** or a **Tax Group**.
    
    ![Tax group list icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2024_1/NSPOS_SuiteTax_Tax_Schedule_Settings_list.png)
    
    Either enter the first few letters and press tab or click the list icon.
    
    Tip:
    
    The **Tax Rate** to be charged for items using each tax schedule appears on the right.
    
3.  Continue selecting a code or group for your different schedules as needed.
    
    The **Save** boxes on the left become checked for each schedule updated. You can clear the box for a schedule if you don't want to save that specific change.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

![Tax schedule settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2024_1/NSPOS_SuiteTax_Tax_Schedule_Settings.png)
