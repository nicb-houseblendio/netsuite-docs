The different customization flags for sales orders interact in different ways depending on which flags are enabled or disabled. The three flags are:

-   Disable Edit
    
-   Disable Invoice
    
-   Invoice Unfulfilled Items
    

![Sales Order checkbox fields.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_RN_17_1_0_Sales_Order_Flag_d_edit_inv_unf.png)
