Sales transactions are the primary function of NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS). The overall workflow for entering transactions is to first add a customer, then add items, optionally apply any discounts or special sales tax considerations, and finally accept payment.

<a id="kaltura_player_252"></a>

<a id="svg_1"></a>

                                                                                                                                                                                                       

Follow these steps to ring up a sales transaction your NSPOS register. Steps for applying [Discounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3904044606.html) and working with [Sales Taxes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4407866123.html) are provided in the following sections.

-   [Add Customers to a Sales Transaction](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4424397150.html)
    
-   [Work with Items in a Sales Transaction](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4424397273.html)
    
-   [Accept Payments for Purchases](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3903960544.html)
