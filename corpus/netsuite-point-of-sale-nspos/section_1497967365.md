The following table highlights common operational problems that can occur for NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS) promotions.

| **Environment** | **Problem** | **Solution** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NSPOS device | Promotion doesn't appear when testing on the sales device. | Check the following:
-   Allow enough time for the NetSuite records to sync to the NSPOS workstation.
-   Make sure the time and date of sale falls within the date range on the promotion record in NetSuite.
-   Make sure the testing location is included in the locations selected on the promotion record in NetSuite.

 |
| NSPOS device | Entering the correct amount of an item does not trigger the promotion at the time of sale. | If the promotion is a BOGO type, be sure to enter the buy and get quantities as two separate transaction lines. |
| NSPOS device | Some items included in the promotion record don't trigger the promotion at the time of sale. | Ensure that all items attached to the promotion record in NetSuite have both Trigger and Reward fields set to Yes unless Mix And Match is checked. |
| NetSuite | The amounts discounted don't correctly reflect in the General Ledger. | Check that the posting settings on the Accounting subtab of the Discount Item attached to the promotion are correctly set. |
| NetSuite | The amount of tax calculated by the promotion is incorrect. | Check that the taxation settings on the Accounting subtab of the Discount Item attached to the promotion are correctly set. |

## Downsync of Promoted Items if missing from server {#subsect_1529523905}

In early NSPOS releases, if an item included in a promotion didn't exist on your replication server, the promotion downsyc to your registers would fail for that item. The correction was to manually Edit > Save the item in NetSuite ERP to trigger an item download to the server. Then the promotion could downsyc normally.

If a promotion includes a missing item, the promotion downsyc automatically downloads the item to the server. A manual item update is not required.

Note:

Items records marked as **Do not download to POS** are automatically excluded from promotions. They don't appear during promotion creation when searching for items to include.
