These topics cover procedures that help to prevent a Windows operating system (OS) from capturing sensitive information in pagefile.sys and other virtual memory or reporting features. The steps are provided as an appendix to the NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS) Administrator Guide.

Review and follow the procedures for your version of Windows:

-   [Preventing the Accidental Capture of Sensitive Information in Windows 11](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0221113355.html)
    
-   [Preventing the Accidental Capture of Sensitive Information in Windows 10](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0221113429.html)
