To simplify visibility of mitigated records in NetSuite ERP during upsync, we have exposed related saved searches in the NSPOS menu:

![NSPOS reports menu](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2025_1/NSPOS_Mitigated_Records.png)

To see more details about NSPOS automatic error mitigation, see [Automatic Upsync Error Mitigation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1120033057.html).

For details about searches, see [Correcting Mitigated Records Through a Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1120033057.html#subsect_1126013136).
