NSPOS includes support for gateway services provided by Worldpay (YESpay).

For information about starting with Worldpay, visit their site at [www.worldpay.com](http://www.worldpay.com). Worldpay will provide installation instructions for the client terminal setup on your registers. You must configure your RA-Workstation records in NetSuite ERP to accept Worldpay (YESpay).

#### To configure Worldpay (YESpay) in NetSuite ERP: {#procedure_1214041333}

1.  Log in to NetSuite as an administrator.
    
2.  Go to _Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types_.
    
3.  Locate the **RA-Workstation** row and click **List**.
    
4.  Click **Edit** for the first workstation to update.
    
5.  For **Payment Provider**, select **Worldpay Payment Provider**.
    
    No further setup is required on this record.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
7.  Repeat for the next workstation.
    

Important:

During register staging for releases prior to NSPOS 2020.1.X, you must select to install the **Worldpay** extension. See [Extension Synchronization Selection for NSPOS 2020.1.X](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164936755439.html#subsect_163915093935).
