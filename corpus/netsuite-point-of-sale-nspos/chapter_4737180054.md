Use this section to add or reactivate NetSuite Point of Sale (POS OR NSPOS) registers in your stores.

1.  First, you'll configure an RA-Workstation record for a register. This record establishes the register in NetSuite ERP and is necessary for enabling item, price and other synchronization.
    
2.  The second task guides you through staging the register to load and activate the NSPOS application.
    

This topic also covers staging and preparing images for a self-checkout kiosk and providing details for setting up Store Server Architecture to assign server and client roles to registers in your stores.
