Follow these steps to get the balance of a external gift card in NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS).

#### To find the balance on an external gift card:

1.  Tap **Authorized Gift Card Balance**.
    
2.  At the **Please Slide Gift Card** prompt, swipe the gift card or enter the card **Number**.
    
3.  Tap **Enter** or tap the green check mark.
    
    NSPOS displays the available balance.
