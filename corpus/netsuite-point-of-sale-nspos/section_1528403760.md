Use this section to set up the reason codes displayed and logged for various types of NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS) transactions. Reason codes explain why someone took a specific action at the sales terminal or register.

You can also use these topics to require comments on sales transactions. Comments are helpful for listing an item's serial number or for noting important dates about warranties or returns. Your sales associates might use comments to describe item's physical condition.

-   [Reason Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4592782035.html)
    
-   [Require Entering a Comment with Sale](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4539055305.html)
