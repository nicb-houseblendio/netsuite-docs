NSPOS can provide shoppers with a digital receipt sent to them by email. Your sales associates can generate a receipt using any of these configured options:

| 
-   No receipt (None)
-   Digital receipt
-   Printed receipt
-   Printed and digital receipt

 | ![Cashier prompt for receipt type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_Receipts_cashier_prompt_does_want.png) |
| --- | --- |

Digital receipts are sent to the shopper's email address as a PDF attachment. The process uses NetSuite's PDF template functionality for the receipt layout. This lets you customize your digital receipts with your logo and business information. You can also use a default template for most locations and special templates for others.

![Digital receipt email template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_Receipts_email_template_small.png) ![Digital receipt default template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_Receipts_default_template_small.png)

If your shopper is saved as a customer in NSPOS and the sales associate adds them to the transaction, their email address is pre-filled. If the shopper is a guest, or if a saved customer wants to use a different address, the sales associate enters it in a prompt from the register.

Tip:

The application uses the employee email address you choose as the digital receipt sender's email address. The address is pulled from the senders employee record in NetSuite.

You should decide which address to use before beginning the setup. A new employee can be set up for this purpose. Go to Lists > Employees > Employees.

## Sales Associate Receipt Procedure {#subsect_164823761993}

Cashiers, sales associates and others who process transactions in NSPOS should follow these steps to give shoppers their receipts.

#### To print or email a receipt:

1.  Ask the shopper if they want their receipt printed, emailed, or both.
    
    ![Cashier prompt for receipt type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_Receipts_cashier_prompt_does_want.png)
2.  Tap the option matching the shopper's request.
    
    If they want it printed, hand the receipt to the shopper after it prints.
    
3.  For digital or printed and digital requests:
    
    ![Receipt email address entry form](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_Receipts_cashier_email_entry.png)
    
    -   If shopper is saved as a customer, the email address is pre-filled. Ask if that is the address to use.
        
        If you haven't added the saved customer to the transaction, use **Search Customer** to locate them.
        
    -   If a saved customer wants to use a different email address, you'll be asked if you want to update their saved address.
        
    -   If the shopper is a guest (not in NSPOS), ask for their email address.
        
        Tip:
        
        For privacy and accuracy, you can provide a paper and pen for the shopper to write their email address.
        
    
    1.  Enter the email address as needed.
        
    2.  Tap **Done**.
        
    3.  NSPOS validates that the email address is formatted correctly, but it doesn't validate if the address is real.
        
    4.  You are alerted that the receipt will be sent by email.
        
        ![Conformation message saying receipt email will be sent](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_Receipts_cashier_sent_msg.png)
    5.  Tell the shopper that the digital receipt will be sent to by email.
        
4.  If they asked for a printed receipt, hand it to the shopper.
    

NetSuite ERP saves the receipt in the transaction history. A sales associate can reprint a receipt from the register.

## Re-send a Digital Receipt {#subsect_164823763191}

If a shopper asks to have a receipt sent again, including to a different address, the action must be performed in NetSuite ERP. You can't re-send a receipt from an NSPOS register.

Sent receipt email are saved with the employee record that uses the **From** email address. This is the address you choose when configuring your digital receipt setup.

#### To re-send a digital receipt email:

1.  Go to Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types.
    
2.  On the **NSPOS Enable Features** row, click **List**.
    
3.  Click **View**.
    
4.  Open the **Digital Receipt** subtab.
    
5.  Click the **Sender Email Address** name to open their employee record.
    
    ![send email address](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_Receipts_re_send_enable_features.png)
    
    Email sent from this employee-record address are found under the **Communication** subtab.
    
6.  Use the **Primary Recipient** column to find the shopper's email address.
    
7.  Click **View** for the desired email.
    
    ![Primary Recipient column and View link](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_Receipts_re_send_emply_record.png)
8.  Click **Reply to All**.
    
9.  Leave the **Additional Recipients** '<Type then tab>' field empty.
    
10.  Click the **Email** field and enter the shopper's email address.
     
11.  Click **Merge & Send**.
     
     Tip:
     
     You can click **Refresh** to confirm the email was sent.
     

## Enabling Digital Receipts in NetSuite {#subsect_164823764084}

Use the NSPOS Enable Features page to enable the digital receipt option on your registers. This page is also where you set up the default digital receipt. This default applies to all stores without a by-location digital receipt setup.

You can add your logo, company information, website, and header/footer text to customize your digital receipts.

NSPOS uses NetSuite's **Advanced PDF/HTML Template** and **Email Template** features to configure digital receipts. You should familiarize yourself with the features before attempting to customize a receipt setup. See [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_164823768441) and [Email Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_164823769344).

Tip:

Upload your receipt logo before you start these steps. See [Logos for Digital Receipts](#subsect_164823770277).

#### To enable and set up digital receipts:

1.  Go to Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types.
    
2.  On the **NSPOS Enable Features** row, click **List**.
    
3.  Click **Edit**.
    
4.  Open the **Digital Receipt** subtab.
    
5.  Check the **Enable Digital Receipt** box.
    
    Tip:
    
    If **Enable Digital Receipt** isn't checked, the digital receipt setup fields won't appear in NSPOS.
    
6.  Select the **Email Template** or use the default template.
    
    The email template formats the email used to deliver the receipt. See [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_164823768441).
    
7.  For **Advanced PDF Template**, select **Custom Digital receipt PDF/HTML Template NSPOS**.
    
    The PDF template formats the digital receipt layout. You can customize a template using XML. See [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates for Digital Receipts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162930296833.html#subsect_162940353860).
    
    Note:
    
    Template procedures are standard NetSuite functionality.
    
8.  Select the employee whose email address should be used for the **Sender Email Address**. This field configures the **From:** address.
    
    Important:
    
    The owner of the sending email address must be set up as an **Employee** in NetSuite.
    
9.  Select the **Receipt Logo**. See [Logos for Digital Receipts](#subsect_164823770277).
    
10.  Enter your **Company Name** and other business information that you want on the digital receipt:
     
11.  Enter the company address and telephone information as they should appear on the receipt.
     
12.  Use **Receipt Details-Web** to add your website. For example, enter `www.retailbusinessname.com`.
     
13.  Enter **Header Text** to appear at the top of the receipt.
     
14.  Enter **Footer Text** to appear at the bottom of the receipt.
     
15.  Click **Save**.
     
16.  Use any NSPOS register to finish the digital receipt setup. See [Using NSPOS to Finish Setting Up Digital Receipts](#subsect_164823765924).
     

## Setting Up Digital Receipts by Location in NetSuite {#subsect_164823764975}

In addition to setting up a default digital receipt, you can set up unique receipts for specific locations. For example, you might want to use a different logo or display company information that is different from the default.

Locations without a receipt setup will use the default setup.

NSPOS uses NetSuite's **Advanced PDF/HTML Template** and **Email Template** features for digital receipts. You should familiarize yourself with the features before attempting to customize a receipt setup. See [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_164823768441) and [Email Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_164823769344).

#### To set up a digital receipt for a location:

1.  Log into NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to Setup > Company > Locations.
    
3.  Find the desired location and click **Edit**.
    
4.  For **Digital Receipt - PDF Template**, select **Custom Digital receipt PDF/HTML Template NSPOS**.
    
    The PDF template formats the digital receipt layout. You can also configure a template using XML. See [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_164823768441).
    
    Note:
    
    Template procedures are standard NetSuite functionality.
    
5.  Select the receipt **Logo**. See [Logos for Digital Receipts](#subsect_164823770277).
    
6.  Open the **Digital Receipt** subtab.
    
7.  Check the **Enable Location Specific Settings** box.
    
8.  Select the **Email Template** or use the default template.
    
    The email template formats the email used to deliver the receipt. See [Email Templates for Digital Receipts](#subsect_164823769344).
    
9.  Select the employee whose email address should be used for the **Sender Email Address**. This field configures the **From:** address.
    
    Important:
    
    The owner of the sending email address must be set up as an **Employee** in NetSuite.
    
10.  Enter your **Company Name** and other business information that you want on the digital receipt:
     
11.  Enter the company address and telephone information as they should appear on the receipt.
     
12.  Use **Receipt Details-Web** to add your website. For example, enter `www.retailbusinessname.com`.
     
13.  Enter **Header Text** to appear at the top of the receipt.
     
14.  Enter **Footer Text** to appear at the bottom of the receipt.
     
15.  Click **Save**.
     
16.  If you haven't already, use any NSPOS register to finish the digital receipt setup. See [Using NSPOS to Finish Setting Up Digital Receipts](#subsect_164823765924).
     

## Using NSPOS to Finish Setting Up Digital Receipts {#subsect_164823765924}

After you set up digital receipts in NetSuite ERP, you must configure the receipts in NSPOS. You can perform these steps on any NSPOS register. The next synchronization cycle will automatically apply the settings to other stores and registers.

#### To finish setting up digital receipts:

1.  Press **Ctrl+F12** on the keyboard to open the Function List.
    
2.  Enter `receipts` in the search field.
    
3.  Tap **Configure Receipts**.
    
    ![Search for receipt options](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_Receipts_NSPOS_CtrlF12_Configure.png)
4.  Scroll right to the **Retail** section.
    
    ![Digital receipt options](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_Receipts_NSPOS_CtrlF12_Settings.png)
5.  To enable the **Prompt** for a receipt type, enter **Ask customer** (default).
    
6.  Select a **Default receipt option** - None, printed, digital, or printed and digital. The default is the option pre-selected for the receipt prompt.
    
7.  Under **Retail: Receipt type** options, check the types you want to offer in the prompts.
    
    If you don't select a receipt type, the **Default receipt option** is used.
    
    Tip:
    
    To prevent the receipt prompt from appearing, check the **Default receipt option** and clear any checks in the **Retail: Receipt type** boxes.
    
8.  Click **Done**.
    
    Note:
    
    The **Required Flag** text field isn't required. You can leave it empty.
    

## Troubleshooting Digital Receipts {#subsect_164823766563}

This topic provides steps for troubleshooting sending digital receipts to shoppers. For issues not listed here, contact NetSuite Customer Support.

To attempt sending a receipt email again, see [Re-send a Digital Receipt](#subsect_164823763191).

-   **Receipt email was undeliverable** - To view emails that couldn't be delivered, go to Lists > Mailing > Undelivered Emails. The page lists possible reasons that the delivery failed.
    
-   **Receipt email was not sent** - If a transaction invoice and receipt didn't synchronize from the register to NetSuite, the email can't be sent. Contact NetSuite Customer Support for help.
    
-   **Receipt email not received due to incorrect email address** - If a shopper contacts your business saying they gave an incorrect address, update the shopper's customer information in NSPOS. Then follow the steps to [Re-send a Digital Receipt](#subsect_164823763191).
    

### Working with Templates {#subsect_164823767514}

The NSPOS bundle includes one PDF/HTML template and one email template. Both templates use elements designed to be populated with NSPOS data. You shouldn't customize these original templates.

If you want to customize, you can save copies of the templates and customize only those copies. Using copies to apply customizations can prevent future bundle updates from overwriting your work.

Note that template procedures are part of standard NetSuite functionality. Use the Help Center links included in the following topics as guides to customizing templates.

Important:

You should not customize the NSPOS default PDF/HTML and email templates. Apply customizations to template copies only.

#### Advanced PDF/HTML Templates for Digital Receipts {#subsect_164823768441}

This template feature formats the digital receipt layout. You can use it to customize your digital receipts.

Important:

Customizing a PDF template requires a working knowledge of XML.

You must have the **Advanced PDF/HTML Templates** feature enabled.

#### To enable Advanced PDF/HTML Templates:

1.  Log into NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
3.  Open the **SuiteCloud** subtab.
    
4.  Check the **Advanced PDF/HTML Templates** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To work with the NSPOS PDF Template:

1.  Log into NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to _Customizations > Forms > Advanced PDF/HTML Templates_.
    
3.  Locate the template **Custom Digital receipt PDF/HTML Template NSPOS**.
    
    ![Location of PDF template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NS_ERP_Digital_Receipts_NSPOS_PDF_Tmplt.png)
4.  Click **Edit** or **Customize** to view the XML source and make changes.
    
    Tip:
    
    You can also edit a template in Preview mode by toggling **Source Code** off.
    
    Read all prompts that alert you to possible saving issues when switching between Preview mode and Source Code mode.
    
    ![toggle source code on](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NS_Template_source_code_ON.png)
5.  Before making any changes, click **Save As** and enter a different name.
    
    Then adjust the Company and any Location setups to use the PDF template copy.
    

For more information, see [Using PDF Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N530499.html).

#### Email Templates for Digital Receipts {#subsect_164823769344}

The email template formats the email used to deliver the receipt PDF. You can customize this email.

You must have **Customer Relationship Management** enabled first.

#### To enable Customer Relationship Management:

1.  Log into NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
3.  Open the **CRM** subtab.
    
4.  Check the **Customer Relationship Management** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To work with the NSPOS digital email template

1.  Log into NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to _Documents > Templates > Email Templates_
    
3.  Locate the **Default digital receipt** template.
    
4.  Click **Edit** to view the XML source and make changes.
    
    **Edit** mode uses a text editor, not an XML source editor.
    
5.  If you make changes, use **Save As** and enter a different name.
    
    Then adjust the Company and any Location setups to use this email template.
    

For more information, see [Working with Email Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N514744.html).

## Logos for Digital Receipts {#subsect_164823770277}

You can upload a logo image file for a digital receipt using either of two methods:

-   From the NSPOS Enable Features > Digital Receipt subtab, click **+** to upload.
    
    ![Receipt logo field](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_Receipts_receipt_logo.png)
    
    You can also add a **Logo** from the location record.
    
-   Upload your image file to the File Cabinet. See [Upload Images to the File Cabinet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162137397938.html).
