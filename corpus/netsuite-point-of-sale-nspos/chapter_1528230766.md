-   [Employees and NSPOS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4539107183.html)
    
-   [Reports in NSPOS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4628114910.html)
