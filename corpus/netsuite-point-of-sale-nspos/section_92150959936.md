Applicable only for custo0mers implementing NSPOS for the first time using NSPOS 2024.1 or later.

The NetSuite SuiteTax feature provides standardized methods for calculating and applying taxes to most types of retail transactions. NSPOS 2024.1.0 includes the option to enable SuiteTax integration for NSPOS. Using SuiteTax with NSPOS can streamline the efforts used to maintain tax compliance in the communities you serve. You can set tax rates for NSPOS workstations globally, by subsidiary, and for areas with unique requirements, by individual locations.

## With NSPOS SuiteTax you can: {#subsect_9151010216}

-   Create NSPOS tax hierarchies that behave similar to standard tax configurations in NetSuite ERP
    
-   Configure your taxes for NSPOS on various levels - from location-specific settings through subsidiary settings to tax schedule configurations
    
-   Define global (default) settings as needed
    
-   Rely on synchronization to automatically update workstations with changes to tax codes and tax groups
    

## Important Considerations {#subsect_80151024659}

Review this list before installing NSPOS SuiteTax:

-   **New accounts only** - The 2024.1 SuiteTax integration for NSPOS should be enabled only for new NSPOS 2024.1.X environments.
    
-   **Warning for existing accounts** - Do NOT enable SuiteTax in your account if you're an existing NSPOS customer. The resulting tax data won't be usable and your business will be impacted. SuiteTax is a non-reversible feature that when enabled, can't be disabled.
    
-   **Not for NSPOS kiosks** - Don't enable the NSPOS SuiteTax feature if you want to set up kiosks using NSPOS 2024.1.0. We plan to support SuiteTax for use with NSPOS kiosks in a future release.
    
-   **Items and tax schedules** - Each item should be assigned a tax schedule. If an item is not assigned a tax schedule, NSPOS SuiteTax uses the default schedule.
    
-   **Not for tax-inclusive items** - SuiteTax doesn't support taxes included (built-into) an item's selling price.
    
-   **Tax setup is customer's responsibility** - NSPOS can't verify your tax setup. Your NSPOS SuiteTax configuration and validation should be performed by individuals who understand your tax nexus requirements.
    

Important:

Don't enable the NSPOS SuiteTax feature if you want to set up **kiosks** using NSPOS 2024.1.0. We plan to support SuiteTax for use with NSPOS kiosks in a future release.

Warning:

**Do NOT enable SuiteTax in your account if you are an existing NSPOS customer**. The resulting tax data won't be usable and your business will be impacted. SuiteTax is a non-reversible feature that when enabled, can't be disabled. There is no quick fix if your tax setup becomes corrupted!

We expect to make SuiteTax available to all NSPOS customers in a later release.

## NSPOS SuiteTax SuiteApp {#subsect_45151047713}

The integration with NSPOS requires installing the SuiteApp > SuiteTax for NetSuite Point of Sale.

![NSPOS SuiteTax app logo](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_SuiteTax_logo.png)

Important:

Do not install the NSPOS SuiteTax SuiteApp if you will not use the SuiteTax integration.

### SuiteTax for NSPOS Installation Process {#subsect_55151259584}

Follow these procedures to install SuiteTax in your new NSPOS account. The steps must be performed in the sequence shown:

1.  Enable NetSuite ERP SuiteTax
    
2.  Enable SuiteTalk REST Web Services
    
3.  Install the SuiteApp > SuiteTax for NetSuite Point of Sale
    
4.  Enable NSPOS SuiteTax
    
5.  Configure NSPOS SuiteTax
    
6.  Let NSPOS SuiteTax data downsync to your registers
    

Warning:

Enabling the NetSuite SuiteTax feature PERMANENTLY changes the way your NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS) account handles taxes.

After you enable SuiteTax in NetSuite ERP, it can't be undone. You will be unable to go back to your prior tax-management application. This stipulation applies to all NetSuite accounts changing to SuiteTax. It's not specific to NSPOS.

Important:

**Test In Sandbox First!**

You should perform testing in your sandbox BEFORE enabling NSPOS SuiteTax in your production account. Customer Support or your account manager should assist you in determining whether to enable SuiteTax.

#### To enable the NetSuite ERP SuiteTax feature:

1.  Work with Customer Support or your account manager to confirm that you can enable SuiteTax.
    
2.  Log into NetSuite ERP as an administrator.
    
3.  Go to Setup > Company > Enable Features.
    
    ![Enable SuiteTax in NetSuite ERP](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NS_ERP_SuiteTax_Enable_Features.png)
4.  From the **Tax** tab, select the **SuiteTax** box.
    
    ![SuiteTax selection box](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NS_ERP_SuiteTax_Enable_Features_box.png)
5.  From the **SuiteCloud** tab, go to the **SuiteTalk (Web Services)** section.
    
6.  Select **REST Web services**.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

Tip:

You can't install the NSPOS SuiteTax app if you haven't enabled SuiteTax.

#### To Install the SuiteTax for NetSuite Point of Sale SuiteApp: {#procedure_2151306943}

1.  Open the **SuiteApps** tab.
    
2.  Search for **NSPOS**.
    
3.  Click the app to open its overview page and then click **Install.**
    
    ![NSPOS SuiteApp logo](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_SuiteTax_logo_small.png)
4.  Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation.
    

#### To enable the NSPOS SuiteTax feature:

1.  Go to Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types.
    
2.  On the **NSPOS Enable Features** row, click **List**.
    
3.  Click **Edit** for **Bundle version** 2024.1.
    
4.  From the **Enable SuiteTax** tab, select the **Enable SuiteTax** box.
    
    Important:
    
    The NSPOS **Enable SuiteTax** tab won't appear if you haven't enabled the SuiteTax feature for NetSuite ERP.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
