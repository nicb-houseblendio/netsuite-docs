Also called a **non-validated return**, this return type doesn't require the cashier to locate the original transaction using a customer receipt or other criteria. Instead the cashier scans the returned item to g the refund amount. This method is usually quicker than a validated return, but is also more prone to fraud.

It's the store's responsibility to manage policies around non-validated returns. Because there is no proof of purchase, there is also no proof of the price for which the item sold. NSPOS will list the item at its current price.

## Restricting non-validated returns {#bridgehead_1511884336}

Administrators can require that cashiers receive approval before making non-validated returns by assigning a high **Resource ID** to the **Return w/o Receipt** button. The cashier is prompted that the return can't proceed until a store manager or other role with that Resource ID or higher enters their credentials. See [Set Button Security](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4537595751.html) in the NSPOS Administrator Guide.

To make a return without a receipt, the cashier scans or looks up the item and NSPOS provides the refund amount using the item's current price.

#### To make a return without receipt in NSPOS:

1.  In Sales Mode, tap **Voids/Returns**.
    
    ![Voids/Returns button.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_returns_1_button.png)
2.  On the sidebar, tap **Return w/o Receipt**.
    
    ![Return w/o Receipt button.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_returns_9_return_wo_receipt_button.png)
3.  At the **Scan or enter the bar code** prompt, do one of the following:
    
    -   Scan the returned item.
        
    -   Enter the item bar code and tap the green check mark ![green checkmark icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_green_checkmark.png).
        
    -   Tap the red ![red X icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_returns_2_vreceipt_search_transentry_X.png) to search for and tap the item. You can also drill through your item list to find a match.
        
    
    ![Receipt barcode manual entry screen.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_returns_2_vreceipt_search_transentry.png) ![Nonvalidated Return Item list.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_returns_9_returns_wo_receipt._item_search.png)
4.  Select a **Reason Code** and tap **Done**.
    
    Select the code that most closely matches the customer's reason for the return.
    
    ![Reason Code list](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_returns_5_reason_code_list.png)
    
    Note:
    
    Your administrator can create custom **Reason Codes** to fit your business processes. See [Reason Codes](https://system.netsuite.com/app/help/helpcenter.nl?fid=section_4592782035) in the NSPOS Administrator's guide.
    
5.  Verify that the item appears in the Journal and you see:
    
    -   The item line displays a RETURN watermark.
        
    -   The transaction shows **Change** due to the customer instead of Amt Due.
        
        ![Journal with RETURN watermark.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointofSaleAdminGuide/NSPOS_returns_9_returns_wo_receipt_item_journal_returns_line.png)
6.  Tap **Change** and select the payment option for the refund.
    
7.  Refund the change due to the customer or the customer's card.
    
8.  Provide the return receipt to the customer.
    
    If the return receipt includes a copy with a signature line, ask the customer to complete all required information. Place the completed receipt in your cash drawer and hand the customer their copy.
