Use this document as your guide to administrating and using NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS). The guide includes steps for common tasks such as performing a sales transaction or creating a promotion. It covers setting up new registers and instructions for making changes, like modifying the buttons that are available to store managers and the cashiers.
