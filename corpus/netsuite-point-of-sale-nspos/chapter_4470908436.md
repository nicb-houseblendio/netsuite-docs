Follow these steps to view and edit flags and settings for NSPOS, including universal settings and settings for groups, workstations, stores, permissions and preferences.

-   A **flag** uses a Boolean value, such as a box that is either checked or clear, or a field set to true or false, yes or no. Changing a flag enables or disables an option.
    
-   A **setting** is any configurable field value, such as a default amount or a maximum or minimum limit. Changing a setting lets you configure your NSPOS installation to match business rules in place for your stores.
    

Warning:

This topic describes making changes to the NSPOS default setup. Be aware that changes can impact previous custom configurations. Only knowledgeable administrators should adjust flags and settings.

-   [Flag List](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4665425460.html)
    
-   [Setting List](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4665426623.html)
    

## How changes replicate across your network {#bridgehead_4619641594}

Updates made from a single POS register replicate everywhere across your network in the next synchronization cycle. Some changes won't apply until you restart the workstation or register. Although you may not receive a prompt to restart, it's good practice to always exit and restart the application after making changes.

The type of flag or setting determines where changes will apply.

-   **Universal Settings** - Apply everywhere on your network.
    
-   **Store Settings** - Apply only to registers in the selected store.
    
-   **Group Settings** - Apply only to registers of the same Workstation Type. The Workstation Type is a field in NetSuite under RA-Workstation records.
    
-   **Workstation Settings** - Apply only to the selected register.
    

#### To view and edit a flag or setting:

1.  Sign in to any NSPOS register as an administrator (9090 level operator).
    
2.  Press **Ctrl+F10** to open the **Universal Settings** window.
    
3.  To adjust **Settings** for:
    
    -   Your entire system (Universal Setting), continue to the next numbered step.
        
    -   All operators and registers in a particular store, tap **Store Settings**
        
    -   All operators and registers in a particular group, tap **Group Settings**.
        
    -   The current workstation, tap **Workstation Settings**.
        
4.  In the search field, enter any part of a flag or setting name. The current configuration displays.
    
5.  If desired, edit the flag or setting.
    
    -   For field settings, tap the red and white icons near the field labels to enable or disable.
        
    -   For check box flags, tap the blue triangle and red icons to disable or enable.
        
6.  Tap **Done** when complete.
    
7.  If you made changes, tap **Sign Off** and then tap **Exit**. Restart the application.
