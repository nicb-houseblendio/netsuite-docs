Follow these steps in NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS) to sell a gift card issued by an authorized third-party. These are regular inventory items that you add to a transaction similar to physical items purchased at your register.

#### To sell an external gift card:

1.  Tap **Sell Authorized Gift Card** or **Issue Authorized Gift Card**.
    
2.  At the **Issue Gift Card Amount** prompt, enter the value of the gift card.
    
3.  If prompted **Does this Gift Card need to be activated?**, tap **Yes**.
    
4.  At the **Please slide Gift card** prompt, swipe the card or enter the card **Number**.
    
5.  Tap **Enter** or tap the green check mark.
    
6.  Hand the gift card to the customer; it is activated and ready to use.
