To further streamline the NSPOS Kiosk experience, we have introduced a new auto-close timer that improves the checkout flow. After a successful purchase, the confirmation message will automatically close after 10 seconds. This eliminates the need to manually tap **Close**, though the button will still be visible. This allows for a faster transition to the next shopper.
