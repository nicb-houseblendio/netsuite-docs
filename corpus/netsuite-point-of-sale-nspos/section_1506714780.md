Follow these steps to get the balance of an internal gift card/certificate in NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS).

#### To find the balance on a NetSuite gift card/certificate:

1.  Tap **Gift Card Balance**.
    
2.  Select whether to find the balance as a gift **Certificate** or **Card**.
    
    -   If **Certificate**, enter the **Authorization Code** and tap the green check mark.
        
    -   If **Card**, swipe the card or enter the card **Number** and tap the green check mark.
        
    
    NSPOS displays the available balance.
