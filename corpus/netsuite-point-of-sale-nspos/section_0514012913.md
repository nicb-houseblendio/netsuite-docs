With the 2025.1.0 NSPOS release, we are moving to 2025.1.0 endpoints for NSPOS integration with NetSuite ERP.

This is an internal change and no customer action is needed.

For more information about SOAP endpoints, see [Support for Existing WSDL Versions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3418621.html).
