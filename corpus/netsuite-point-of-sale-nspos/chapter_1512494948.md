Associating the forms of payment handled at your locations with your NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS) setup is an important part of your NSPOS environment. These topics describe creating and working with the tender types and payment methods your business accepts.

-   [Managing Your Tender Types and Payment Methods](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1512495090.html)
    
-   [About Transaction Payment Records in NetSuite ERP](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1512507518.html)
    
-   [About Tender Workflows](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1513279316.html)
    

Note:

The NetSuite ERP payment records listed in this topic are used to manage payment processes. The NSPOS general ledger (GL) accounting setup in NetSuite ERP is a predefined part of your NSPOS implementation.
