This document lists the updates provided in NetSuite Point of Sale (NSPOS) version 2020.1.1. The enhancements/fixes listed here are not available to NSPOS customers until they have upgraded to this version. NetSuite will modify these notes when needed to provide our customers with additional information.

## For Upgrade Steps {#subsect_74153737113}

To upgrade your installation, see [Perform the Update to NSPOS 2020.1.X](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159301995180.html).

Important:

If you are upgrading a register from a release prior to NSPOS 2020.1.X, and you have not configured your security GUID, you should do immediately after installing the NSPOS 2020.1.X bundle. See [Configuring the Security GUID](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_159301984366.html#subsect_94140001250).

### Address Field Updates for Digital Receipts {#subsect_165419917570}

We have updated the address fields available for digital receipts. The fields now are:

-   Address 1
    
-   Address 2
    
-   City
    
-   State
    
-   ZIP
    
-   Country
    

These fields are available on the NSPOS Enable Features > Digital Receipt subtab and Location record > Digital Receipt subtab.

![Digital receipt address fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Commerce/NSPOS/NetSuitePointSaleReleaseNotes/2020_1/NSPOS_Receipts_LOCATION_address_fields.png)

### Improved Messaging for Staging Issues {#subsect_165419922212}

We updated error and warning messages to provide better guidance in the event issues occur when staging a register. Updates include messaging for when:

-   NSPOS staging needs an additional \[n GB\] of free space before it can proceed.
    
-   An existing workstation is being staged as a different workstation/environment. Data currently on the workstation will be removed. However, the workstation has 'stuck transactions' that could be lost if staging continues. To save these transactions, cancel staging and contact Customer Support.
    

### Removed Non-EMV Payment Gateways {#subsect_165419914522}

Payment gateways process credit and debit card payments made at the register, usually through a PIN-pad. Over the last year, we notified customers that we were removing payment gateway setups that do not use EMV protocols.

In the NSPOS 2020.1.1 minor release, we modified the NetSuite ERP setup pages to remove non-EMV options. We removed options for:

-   Shift4 with NSPOS controlled devices
    
-   Worldpay DSIClientX application
    

Note that Worldpay (Vantiv) was previously known as Mercury Payment Systems (MPS). We will continue to support the EMV-enabled options provided by Shift4 and Worldpay.
