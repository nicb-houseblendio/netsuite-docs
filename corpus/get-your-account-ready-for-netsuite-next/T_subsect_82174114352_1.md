Note:

This topic describes functionality available in NetSuite Next. NetSuite Next is being made available to customers in phases, so these features may not yet be available in your account. You'll receive in-app notifications when your account is eligible for NetSuite Next.

You work more efficiently when your role center reflects your real tasks. Review your setup and remove clutter. Focus on:

-   Common actions that you perform every day
    
-   Outdated or rarely used links
    
-   Saved searches and reports that you need often
    
-   Consistent menus for similar roles
    

A cleaner navigation structure helps you find what you need faster. For more information, see:

-   [Centers Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N131898.html)
    
-   [Selecting a Dashboard for a Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N289178.html)
