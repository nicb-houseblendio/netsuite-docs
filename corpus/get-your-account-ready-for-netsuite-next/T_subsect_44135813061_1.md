Note:

This topic describes functionality available in NetSuite Next. NetSuite Next is being made available to customers in phases, so these features may not yet be available in your account. You'll receive in-app notifications when your account is eligible for NetSuite Next.

You get better results when you ask clear, specific questions. When you work with Ask Oracle, you should:

-   Ask for a specific outcome instead of general information.
    
-   Include business context such as time period, entity, or metric.
    
-   Use simple natural language.
    
-   Review AI-generated answers before you share them.
    

For more information, see [Tips for the Best Ask Oracle Results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5100902387.html#subsect_0305102428).

You can also learn more about AI using the available training resources. For more information, see [Training Resources](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_3710623483.html).

Encourage your users to build their prompting skills by using Ask Oracle in their current NetSuite experience. To learn how to enable Ask Oracle for roles in your account, see [Managing Ask Oracle Access for Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/T_article_4103202860_1.html).
