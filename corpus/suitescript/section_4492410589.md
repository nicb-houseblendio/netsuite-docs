| **Method Description** | Method used to asynchronously fetch the previous segment of data (bounded by [search.PageRange](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4486559010.html)). Moves the current page to another range. The promise is complete when the data for this range is loaded or rejected. Note: The parameters and errors thrown for this method are the same as those for [Page.prev()](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4486606245.html). For more information about promises, see [Promise Object](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4387812940.html). |
