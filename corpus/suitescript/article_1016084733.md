Note:

The content in this help topic pertains to SuiteScript 2.1.

The objects in this section are related to extracting text from a document. When you call [documentCapture.documentToStructure(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0704102801.html) and specify the `Feature.TEXT_EXTRACTION` feature, the returned [documentCapture.Document](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0703030933.html) object includes values for these objects.

-   [documentCapture.Line](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0704100631.html)
    
-   [documentCapture.Word](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0704102321.html)
