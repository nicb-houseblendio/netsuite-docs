| **Property Description** | Status of the submitted document capture task. A document capture task can have the following statuses, which are represented by values in the [task.TaskStatus](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4345807357.html) enum:
-   `task.TaskStatus.COMPLETE`
-   `task.TaskStatus.FAILED`
-   `task.TaskStatus.PENDING`
-   `task.TaskStatus.PROCESSING`
