| **Object Description** | A response returned from [machineTranslation.translate(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0606031958.html). A response represents the translation results from the translation service. This object includes properties for the translated documents ([Response.results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0606031415.html)) and any errors that occurred during translation ([Response.errors](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0606031259.html)). If no errors occurred, the [Response.errors](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0606031259.html) property is empty. |
