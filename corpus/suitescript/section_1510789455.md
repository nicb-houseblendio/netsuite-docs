| **Property Description** | An array of child conditions used to create the parent condition. Note: This property is applicable to only parent conditions created with the of [Query.and(conditions)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510780422.html), [Query.or(conditions)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510780444.html), or [Query.not(condition)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510780462.html). |
