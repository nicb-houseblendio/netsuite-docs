Important:

If you're using SuiteScript 1.0 or 2.0 for your scripts, or if your scripts use the 2.x value for the `@NApiVersion` JSDoc tag, consider updating these scripts to SuiteScript 2.1. Use SuiteScript 2.1 to take advantage of new features, APIs, and functionality enhancements. For more information, see [SuiteScript 2.1](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_156042690639.html).
