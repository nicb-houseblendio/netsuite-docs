| **Property Description** | Holds the task ID of the merge task. The task ID is assigned to the merge task when you call [MergeArrangementsTask.submit()](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554926010.html) or [MergeElementsTask.submit()](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554994855.html) for the task. |
