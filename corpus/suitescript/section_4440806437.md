| **Method Description** | Sends bulk email asynchronously (for use when a bounceback notification is not required). Note: The parameters and errors thrown for this method are the same as those for [email.sendBulk(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4358667505.html). For more information about promises, see [Promise Object](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4387812940.html). |
