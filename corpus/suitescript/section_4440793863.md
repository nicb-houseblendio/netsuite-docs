| **Method Description** | Performs a search for duplicate records asynchronously based on the Duplicate Detection configuration for the account. Returns an array of [search.Result](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4345767112.html) objects. This method only applies to records that support duplicate record detection. These records include customer | lead | prospect | partner | vendor | contact. Note: The parameters and errors thrown for this method are the same as those for [search.duplicates(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4345775593.html). For more information about promises, see [Promise Object](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4387812940.html). |
