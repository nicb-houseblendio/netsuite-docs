Note:

The content in this help topic pertains to SuiteScript 2.1.

| **Property Description** | The extracted footer rows from the table in a document. Footer rows refer to rows located at the bottom of a table. These rows often contain summary information, totals, or other information related to the table's content. For example, for a receipt, footer rows could show the subtotal, tax amounts, and the total amount due. Each footer row is a collection of cells (as [documentCapture.Cell](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0703022335.html) objects contained in a [documentCapture.TableRow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0704102217.html) object). |
