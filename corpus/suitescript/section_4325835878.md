| **Object Description** | A field group on [serverWidget.Assistant](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4325849970.html) objects and on [serverWidget.Form](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4325835149.html) objects. A field group is a collection of fields that can be displayed in a one or two column format. Assign a field to a field group to label, hide or collapse a group of fields. |
