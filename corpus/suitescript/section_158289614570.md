| **Object Description** | A period of time to use in query conditions. Use [query.createPeriod(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158289670344.html) to create this object. After you create this object, you can use it in the `values` parameter of [Query.createCondition(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510780329.html) or [Component.createCondition(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510784922.html). |
