| **Property Description** | Describes the date of the new revenue arrangement. This property is optional. The default value is today's date. If you specify an invalid date format, a `WRONG_PARAMETER_TYPE` error is thrown when you call [MergeElementsTask.submit()](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554994855.html) for the task. |
