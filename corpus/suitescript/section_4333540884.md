| **Property Description** | The text to display after the assistant finishes. For example 'You have completed the Small Business Setup Assistant. Take the rest of the day off'. To trigger display of the completion message, call [Assistant.isFinished()](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4333493801.html). |
