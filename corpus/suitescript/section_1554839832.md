| **Object Description** | Encapsulates a task to merge all of the revenue elements from a specified list of revenue arrangements. Use [recognition.create(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554995115.html) to create this object. After you create the object, you can populate its properties and submit the task for processing. The [MergeArrangementsTask.arrangements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554917062.html) property is required, and all other properties are optional. |
