| **Object Description** | A tab or subtab on a [serverWidget.Form](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4325835149.html) object. You can add a new tab or subtab to a form using one of the following methods:
-   [Form.addSubtab(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4338820096.html)
-   [Form.addTab(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4339625909.html)
-   [Form.insertSubtab(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4340316428.html)
-   [Form.insertTab(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4340351224.html)

The internal ID must be in lowercase, contain no spaces, and include the prefix custpage if you are adding the field to an existing page. Note: To enable your tab to appear on your form, there must be at least one object assigned to the tab. Otherwise, the tab will not appear. Note: If you have less than two tabs on your form, the tab will not appear. Instead the fields assigned to the tab will appear at the bottom of the form. |
