| **Property Description** | The label for the column. A label is important if the query object is used as the data source for printing (for example, in the [TemplateRenderer.addQuery(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156217838581.html) method in the N/render module). |
