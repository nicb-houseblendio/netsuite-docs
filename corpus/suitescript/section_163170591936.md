| **Object Description** | A selector for descendant or self nodes object. A selector for descendant or self nodes object is used as a parameter in the [workbook.createMeasureValueSelector(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162862156486.html), and [workbook.createSortByMeasure(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162886129949.html) methods. For more information about selectors, see [Selectors](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_163354461590.html). |
