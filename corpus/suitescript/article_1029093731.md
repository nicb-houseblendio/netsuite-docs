Access the Scripted Records page by going to _Customization > Scripting > Scripted Records_.

![The Scripted Records page.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteCloudCustomizationScriptingWebServices/SuiteScript/SuiteScriptDeveloper/SuiteScriptDeveloperGuide/scriptedRecordsPage2.png)

Each record is shown on the Scripted Records page. To access a specific scripted record, do the following:

-   Click **Edit** to change a record's script deployment order or to deploy or undeploy scripts for that record type, or
    
-   Click **View** to view information about the scripts associated with a record type.
