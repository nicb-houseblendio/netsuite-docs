| **Property Description** | The embeddings returned from the LLM. Important: Embeddings include the semantic information of the original data and should be treated with the same level of sensitivity. Embeddings created from sensitive data must be protected, stored, logged, shared, retained, and deleted according to the same rules as the original data. |
