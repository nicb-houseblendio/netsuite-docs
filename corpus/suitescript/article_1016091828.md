Note:

The content in this help topic pertains to SuiteScript 2.1.

The objects in this section are related to extracting fields (as key-value pairs) from a document. When you call [documentCapture.documentToStructure(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0704102801.html) and specify the `Feature.FIELD_EXTRACTION` feature, the returned [documentCapture.Document](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0703030933.html) object includes values for these objects.

-   [documentCapture.Field](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0703031446.html)
    
-   [documentCapture.FieldLabel](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0704095038.html)
    
-   [documentCapture.FieldValue](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0704095437.html)
