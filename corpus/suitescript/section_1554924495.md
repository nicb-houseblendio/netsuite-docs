| **Object Description** | Encapsulates a task to merge all of the specified revenue elements. Use [recognition.create(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554995115.html) to create this object. After you create the object, you can populate its properties and submit the task for processing. The [MergeElementsTask.elements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554925375.html) property is required, and all other properties are optional. |
