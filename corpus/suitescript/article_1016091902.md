Note:

The content in this help topic pertains to SuiteScript 2.1.

The objects in this section are related to extracting tables from a document. When you call [documentCapture.documentToStructure(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0704102801.html) and specify the `Feature.TABLE_EXTRACTION` feature, the returned [documentCapture.Document](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0703030933.html) object includes values for these objects.

-   [documentCapture.Cell](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0703022335.html)
    
-   [documentCapture.Table](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0704101225.html)
    
-   [documentCapture.TableRow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0704102217.html)
