Note: The parameters and errors thrown for this method are the same as those for [https.get(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4567631366.html). For more information about promises, see [Promise Object](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4387812940.html). |
