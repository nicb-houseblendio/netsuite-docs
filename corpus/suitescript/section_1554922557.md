| **Object Description** | Encapsulates the current status of a submitted merge task. Use [recognition.checkStatus(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1555005560.html) to create this object. The current status corresponds to one of the values in the [recognition.TaskStatus](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1555011639.html) enum: `PENDING`, `PROCESSING`, `COMPLETE`, or `FAILED`. |
