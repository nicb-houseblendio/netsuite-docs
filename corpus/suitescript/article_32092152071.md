| **Method Description** | Asynchronously returns the number of available concurrent requests remaining. You can submit up to five concurrent requests to [documentCapture.documentToText(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0704103115.html) or [documentCapture.documentToStructure(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0704102801.html) (or their promise versions). Note: The parameters and errors thrown for this method are the same as those for [documentCapture.getRemainingConcurrency()](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_70083055604.html). For more information about promises, see [Promise Object](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4387812940.html). |
