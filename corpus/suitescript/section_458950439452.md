| **Property Description** | Name of a node, depending on the type. For example, for a node of type [xml.Element](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4345167426.html), the name is the name of the element. Note: On Chrome, this property also includes the namespace or prefix. |
