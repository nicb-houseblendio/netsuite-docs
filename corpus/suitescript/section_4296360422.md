## N/auth Module Members {#bridgehead_4298155078}

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Method | [auth.changeEmail(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4298156427.html) | void | Server scripts | Changes the current user's NetSuite email address (user name). |
