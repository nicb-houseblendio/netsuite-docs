| **Object Description** | A workbook object. Workbooks are where you analyze the results of your dataset queries using different components, such as table views and pivots. All workbooks are based on a dataset, and a single dataset can be used as the basis for multiple workbooks. A workbook can include tables and pivots. Use [workbook.create(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159008744522.html) to create this object. |
