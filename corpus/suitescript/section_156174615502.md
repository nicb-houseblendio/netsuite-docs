| **Property Description** | ID that uniquely identifies the PI removal task. This ID is assigned to the PI removal task when [PiRemovalTask.save()](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156174691190.html) is called to save the task. You cannot specify your own task ID. |
