| **Property Description** | The formula used to create the condition. This property is set during the of [Query.createCondition(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510780329.html) and [Component.createCondition(options)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510784922.html). For more information about formulas, see [Formulas in Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5143710889.html) and [SQL Expressions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2833020.html). Note: This property is not applicable to parent conditions created with the of [Query.and(conditions)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510780422.html), [Query.or(conditions)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510780444.html), or [Query.not(condition)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1510780462.html). |
