## Parameters {#subsect_28120223437}

| **Parameter** | **Type** | **Required / Optional** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `options.max` | number | required | End of random range (exclusive). |
| `options.min` | number | optional | Start of random range. Default is 0. |
