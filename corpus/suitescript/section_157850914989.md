Use the A-Z index at the top of the [SuiteScript Records Browser](https://system.netsuite.com/help/helpcenter/en_US/srbrowser/Browser2026_1/script/index.html) to find a record or subrecord.

#### To find a record or subrecord:

1.  Click the appropriate letter at the top of the browser window.
    
    ![The Records Browser A-Z index.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteCloudCustomizationScriptingWebServices/SuiteScript/SuiteScriptDeveloper/SuiteScriptDeveloperGuide/RecordsBrowser1.png)
    
    The left pane shows record names starting with your selected letter. The center pane shows the details of the first record in the list.
    
2.  In the left pane, click the name of the record you're interested in.
    
    The center pane shows the record details.
    

You can also get to a subrecord page from one of its parent records. Each subrecord summary field on a record page includes a link to the subrecord page. For example, on the Sales Order page of the Records Browser, the billingaddress and shippingaddress fields include links to the address subrecord page. Click the **summary** link in the **Type** column to go to the subrecord page.

![A subset of the sales order record fields in the Records Browser.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteCloudCustomizationScriptingWebServices/SuiteScript/SuiteScriptDeveloper/SuiteScriptDeveloperGuide/RBSubrecordLink.png)
