The Contra Account Sheet SuiteApp enables companies to generate comprehensive transaction reports containing contra account information. Accountants, finance managers, or auditors can use these records to view the account balances or transactions and check they're accurate and complete. You can then export these reports in PDF or CSV format. This account practice, also known as Kontenblatt, is commonly used in Germany.

Note:

Starting with version 1.2.1, you can install the Contra Account Sheet SuiteApp only from the Marketplace.

Contra Account Sheet SuiteApp supports the Multi-Book Accounting feature. For more information, see [Multi-Book Accounting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/book_3831565332.html).

For more information BOUT Germany-specific features, see [Germany Help Topics](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4169634910.html).
