To use the Contra Account Sheet SuiteApp, make sure you've enabled all required features.

#### To enable required features:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **Company** subtab, on the **Classifications** subtab enable the **Departments**, **Locations**, and **Classes** boxes.
    
3.  On the **Company** subtab, on the **Data Management** subtab enable the **File Cabinet** feature.
    
4.  On the **Accounting** subtab, enable these features:
    
    -   Accounting
        
    -   A/R
        
    -   A/P
        
    -   Accounting Periods
        
5.  On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, enable these features:
    
    -   Custom Records
        
    -   Advanced PDF/HTML Templates
        
    -   Client SuiteScript
        
    -   Server SuiteScript
        
6.  Click **Save**.
    

For more information, see [Germany Account Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554726737.html) and [Enabling Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N232138.html).
