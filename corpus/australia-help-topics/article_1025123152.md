NetSuite generates the Payment Times Report in your transaction currency. However, the Australian government requires you to submit the Payment Times Report in Australian Dollars (AUD). So, if your subsidiary in Australia uses a currency that is not in AUD, you must set up your account to convert your transaction currency to AUD.

Note:

This report follows the 2023 Payment Times Reporting (PTR) guidelines from the Australian Taxation Office. For the new PTR 2025 report, which follows the March 2025 guidelines, see [Payment Times Report (PTR) 2025](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0119100852.html).

## Configuring Account to Show the AUD Currency in Payment Times Report {#subsect_92003414030}

#### To set up your account to show the AUD currency in your Payment Times Report: {#procedure_1025123328}

1.  Enable the **Multiple Currencies** feature.
    
    1.  Go to Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features.
        
    2.  On the **Company** subtab, under the **International** section, check the **Multiple Currencies** box.
        
    3.  Click **Save**.
        
    
    For more information, see [Multiple Currencies](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1395463.html).
    
2.  Add the AUD currency.
    
    1.  Go to Lists > Accounting > Currencies > New.
        
    2.  From the **Default Locale** list, select **Australia (English)**.
        
    3.  Complete the required fields. See [Creating Currency Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1395911.html).
        
    4.  Click **Save**.
