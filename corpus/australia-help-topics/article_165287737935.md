You can generate the Payment Times Report with transaction information for fully paid bills, see [Inclusions in the Payment Times Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_165287688792.html). The Payment Times report is composed of two parts:

-   CSV file containing the payment information
    
-   Word or PDF file containing signatures and associated declarations
    
    Note:
    
    The Payment Times Reporting portal accepts .doc or .pdf versions of the signatures and associated declarations. Currently, our SuiteApp only supports word or .doc files.
    

Note:

This report follows the 2023 Payment Times Reporting (PTR) guidelines from the Australian Taxation Office. For the new PTR 2025 report, which follows the March 2025 guidelines, see [Payment Times Report (PTR) 2025](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0119100852.html).

#### To generate the Payment Times report:

1.  Go to Reports > Tax > Country Tax Reports.
    
2.  For OneWorld accounts, select a subsidiary from the **Subsidiary** dropdown list.
    
3.  In the **Nexus** field, select Australia.
    
4.  Select a reporting period under the **Start** and **End Period** fields.
    
5.  In the **Report Name** dropdown, select **Payment Times Report**.
    
6.  Click **Generate**.
    
7.  On the **Report Execution Log** subtab, locate the generated report. The latest generated report displays at the top of the list.
    
8.  In the **View | Download** column, wait for the loading bar to complete and change to a **View** link.
    
    Note:
    
    The system sends you an email notification after generating the report. The email notification indicates the date requested, type, and period of the report generated. It also includes a link to the Country Tax Reports page where you can view and export the report.
    
9.  Click **View** to open the report.
    
10.  You must export the payment file and the signed declaration file because the Payment Times Reporting Portal requires both.
     
     1.  Click the **Export - CSV** icon at the top left of the report to get the payment information.
         
     2.  Click the **Export - Word** icon at the top left of the report to export the Signed Responsible Member Declaration.
