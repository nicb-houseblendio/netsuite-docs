The Payment Times Summary Report is a drill through per payment bracket of the Payment Times Report (PTR).

Note:

This report follows the 2023 Payment Times Reporting (PTR) guidelines from the Australian Taxation Office. For the new PTR 2025 report, which follows the March 2025 guidelines, see [Payment Times Report (PTR) 2025](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0119100852.html).

You can view a summary report of all your reportable fully paid bills per payment bracket in the generated PTR. You can do this by clicking the value under the **Within 20 Days of Receipt** box. The **Within 20 Days of Receipt** box can be found in the Number of Invoices Paid and Values of Invoices Paid columns.

You can also export a CSV file of the Payment Time Summary Report. To export, click the **Export - CSV** icon at the top left of the report. If there's no data available, the exported report will be empty.

To generate a Payment Times Report (PTR), see [Generating the Payment Times Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_165287737935.html).

The generated Summary Report contains the following information:

-   Company/Subsidiary legal name
    
-   Address 1
    
-   Address 2
    
-   City
    
-   State
    
-   ZIP
    
-   Australian Business Number (ABN)
    
-   Selected reporting period
    
-   Payment bracket
    
-   Vendor name
    
-   Transaction number
    
-   Bill date
    
-   Bill amount (Base Currency)
    
-   Bill amount (AUD)
    
-   Bill date received
    
-   Date fully paid
    
-   Number of days open
    
-   Page total
    
-   Grand total
    

To exclude bills and vendors from the Payment Times Report, see [Items and Vendors to Exclude from Reporting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_165287716541.html).
