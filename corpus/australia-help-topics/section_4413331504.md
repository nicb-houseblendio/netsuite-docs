## Enabling the Shipping Partner Plugin {#bridgehead_4274401972}

After installing the Australia Post SuiteApp, enable the Shipping Partner Plug-in implementation.

#### To enable the Shipping Partner Plug-in:

1.  As an Administrator go to: Customization > Plug-ins > Manage Plug-ins.
    
2.  On the Manage Plug-in Implementations page, check the **Australia Post** box located under the Shipping Partners section.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

## Setting Up the Australia Post Forms {#bridgehead_4274402818}

After enabling the Shipping Partner Plug-in, set the following custom forms as preferred forms:

-   Australia Post Package
    
-   Australia Post Registration
    
-   Australia Post Shipment
    

#### To set up the Australia Post forms:

1.  Go to _Customization > Forms > Entry Forms_.
    
2.  On the Custom Entry Forms list, click the Edit link for the form.
    
3.  On the Custom Entry Form page, enter a name for your preferred form.
    
4.  Check the **Form is Preferred** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

When you go back to the Custom Entry Forms list, you should see the boxes checked in the **Preferred** column for the Australia Post forms.
