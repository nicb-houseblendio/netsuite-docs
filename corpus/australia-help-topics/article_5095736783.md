The ANZ Localization SuiteApp provides your account with the Payment Times Report (PTR) Configuration page where you can set up your PTR 2025 details.

Note:

PTR 2025 is the new payment times report that follows the March 2025 guidelines from the Australian Taxation Office. To use the previous PTR 2023 report, see [Payment Times Report (PTR)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_165287398352.html).

#### To configure the Payment Times Report 2025:

1.  If you have the Administrator role, go to Setup > ANZ Localization > Reporting Setup. Otherwise, go to Setup > Other Setup > Reporting Setup.
    
2.  Select a **Subsidiary** from the list.
    
3.  Enter information in the required fields of the following subtabs:
    
    1.  [Entity and Approval Information Subtab](#bridgehead_0119101303)
        
    2.  [Payment Details Subtab](#bridgehead_0119101319)
        
    3.  [Others Subtab](#bridgehead_0119101332)
        
4.  Click **Save**.
    

## Configuring Consolidated Subsidiaries for PTR 2025 {#bridgehead_0524080310}

You can consolidate subsidiaries in PTR 2025 for a parent subsidiary and selected child subsidiaries.

#### To configure consolidated subsidiaries for PTR 2025:

Note:

The **Subsidiaries** subtab appears only when the **Consolidate Subsidiaries** box is checked.

On the Reports page, from the **PTR Report Summary** subtab, you can view the selected consolidated subsidiaries from the **View Consolidated Subsidiaries** link.

1.  Go to Setup > ANZ Localization > Reporting Setup.
    
2.  On the Payment Times Report Configuration page, check the **Consolidate Subsidiaries** box.
    
3.  Click the **Subsidiaries** subtab.
    
4.  From the **Select Subsidiaries for Consolidation** list, select the subsidiaries that you want to include.
    
    You can select multiple subsidiaries by pressing the Ctrl key.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

The following tables show the subtabs and fields on the Payment Times Report Configuration page:

## Entity and Approval Information Subtab {#bridgehead_0119101303}

| Field Group | Field Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approver Details | **First Name** | Enter the controlling corporation or the first name of the responsible member who approved the report. |
| **Last Name** | Enter the controlling corporation or the last name of the responsible member who approved the report. |
| **Approval Date** | Enter the date when the controlling corporation or approver, approved the report. The **Approval Date** must be earlier than the **Responsible Member Declaration** date. |
| **Signed** | Check this box if the controlling corporation or approver has signed the report. |
| Declarations | **Declaration- I confirm the information in the entity information form is still true and correct.** | Check this box to confirm that the information in the Entity Information form is current and accurate. |

## Payment Details Subtab {#bridgehead_0119101319}

| Field Group | Field Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payment Practices | **Did the entity offer supply chain finance arrangements during the reporting period?** | Check these boxes to enable the payment practices. |
| **Did the entity charge fees as part of the procurement process?** |
| **Do any Australian laws, voluntary codes or arrangements impose requirements on the entity's payment times and practices to small business?** |
| Payment Terms | **Receivable terms compared to most common payment term.** | Select if your receivable payment terms are longer, shorter, or same compared to your most common payment term. |
| **Expected most common payment term for next period (common)** | Enter the expected number of days for your most common payment term in the next period. |
| **Expected range of most common payment terms for next period - minimum (estimate)** | Enter the estimated minimum number of days for your most common payment term in the next period. |
| **Expected range of most common payment terms for next period - maximum (estimate)** | Enter the estimated maximum number of days for your most common payment term in the next period |

## Others Subtab {#bridgehead_0119101332}

| Field Group | Field Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comments | **Report Comments** | Enter any required information about the report. |

## Susbidiares Subtab {#bridgehead_0524074307}

| Field Group | Field Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| \- | **Select Subsidiaries for Consolidation** | Select the required subsidiaries to consolidate. You can select multiple subsidiaries from the list by pressing the Ctrl key. |
