The following are the limitations when generating the Payment Times Report (PTR) 2025 using the ANZ Localization SuiteApp:

-   Notice for payments fields are not added on the TCP and SBTCP datasets.
    
-   Payment times calculation doesn't support RCTI.
    

Note:

PTR 2025 is the new payment times report that follows the March 2025 guidelines from the Australian Taxation Office. To use the previous PTR 2023 report, see [Payment Times Report (PTR)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_165287398352.html).

For more information about the other limitations of the SuiteApp, see [Known Limitations of ANZ Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_158677431009.html).
