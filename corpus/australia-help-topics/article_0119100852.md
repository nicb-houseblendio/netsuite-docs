The Payment Times Report (PTR) 2025 is a legislative requirement for large businesses in Australia that have a trade credit arrangement with small businesses. The report contains payment terms and practices for your small business suppliers. Along with the Payment Times Report, Trade Credit Payments (TCP) and Small Business Trade Credit Payments (SBTCP) datasets are also created.

Note:

PTR 2025 is the new payment times report that follows the March 2025 guidelines from the Australian Taxation Office. To use the previous PTR 2023 report, see [Payment Times Report (PTR)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_165287398352.html).

PTR 2025 is submitted and lodged to the [Payment Times Reporting Portal.](https://portal.paymenttimes.gov.au/)

To generate PTR 2025, you must install the ANZ Localization SuiteApp and enable the PTR 2025 report in your account.

For more information about the prerequisites before installing the ANZ Localization SuiteApp, see [Installing ANZ Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_158677437293.html).

For more information about enabling the PTR 2025 report, see [Enabling ANZ Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0329054143.html).

You can generate the PTR 2025 from the Country Tax Reports page. To generate a report, see [Generating the Payment Times Report 2025](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5095736794.html).

Before generating PTR 2025, you must first do the following:

-   Configure your PTR 2025. See [Payment Times Report 2025 Configuration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5095736783.html).
    
-   Identify your small business suppliers for PTR 2025. See [Identifying Small Business Suppliers for Payment Times Reporting 2025](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5095736790.html) and [Generating a Supplier ABN List Report for PTR 2025](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5095736791.html).
    
-   Identify bills to include in the PTR 2025. See [Inclusions in the Payment Times Report 2025](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5095736792.html).
