When generating the Payment Times Report (PTR) using the ANZ Localization SuiteApp, remember the following limitation:

Bills paid in installments to small business suppliers are excluded from the Payment Times Report.

Note:

This report follows the 2023 Payment Times Reporting (PTR) guidelines from the Australian Taxation Office. For the new PTR 2025 report, which follows the March 2025 guidelines, see [Payment Times Report (PTR) 2025](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0119100852.html).

For more information about the other limitations of the SuiteApp, see [Known Limitations of ANZ Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_158677431009.html).
