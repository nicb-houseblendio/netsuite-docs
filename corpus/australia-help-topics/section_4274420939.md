The shipping rate depends on the Australia Post service linked to the shipping item. The tax amount from the schedule you chose for the shipping item is added to the shipping estimate to get the shipping cost.

Important:

Only shipping rates for addresses within Australia are calculated. Shipping rates for international addresses are not supported. For more information, see [Limitations for Australia Post Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4274389015.html#bridgehead_4286404588).

The following factors affect the calculation of the shipping rate estimate:

-   Package type (for example, parcel or letter)
    
-   Weight of the package
    
-   Item record preferences: Item Weight, Package, Ships Individually
    
-   Speed of delivery or delivery time (for example, express or same day delivery)
    
-   Delivery location
    
-   Delivery type
    
-   Additional features such as Signature on Delivery
    
-   Optional services such as Extra Cover
    
-   Manual Handling Surcharge
    
    Shipments with the following dimensions are subject to Manual Handling Surcharge:
    
    -   Longest side (any of width, height, or length) is >100 cm and <= 113 cm
        
    -   Entered weight is > 22 kg and <= 32 kg
        

Note:

Specific Australia Post services might not be available depending on your account, the shipping address, or item weight.

Other factors may affect the shipping estimate. For more information on shipping rates, please visit the Australia Post web site at [http://auspost.com.au/](http://auspost.com.au). To view the limitations of the shipping integration, see [Limitations for Australia Post Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4274389015.html#bridgehead_4286404588).

The following topics describe how the shipping rate is affected based on specific changes you make on a transaction.

## Shipping Rates on Transactions {#bridgehead_4274430750}

For transactions like Sales Orders, Estimates, Invoices, and Cash Sales, select the shipping item in the **Shipping Method** field. The shipping method you choose and the item quantity are factored into the shipping rate calculation.

For sales orders, the shipping address is also included in the shipping rate calculation. Every time you change the shipping method, delivery address, or item quantity, recalculate the shipping cost to show the updated rate.

If you use the **Multiple Shipping Route** feature, you can enter a delivery address for each line item on the **Items** subtab. On the **Shipping** subtab, click **Calculate Shipping** in the Shipment section. This list in this section is populated based on the shipping details for each line item on the **Items** subtab. The shipping rate, per line item, is calculated based on the address, shipping method, and quantity. For more information and feature requirements, see [Multiple Shipping Routes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1263041.html).

## Shipping Rates on Item Fulfillments {#bridgehead_4274431726}

For item fulfillments, the shipping rate mainly depends on the shipping method and address you picked in the sales order. When you enter packages in a fulfillment record, the shipping rate may change depending on the number and size of packages. Whenever you change package details, shipping method, or delivery address, recalculate the shipping cost.

Note:

To recalculate the shipping cost, click the **Shipping** subtab of a transaction record and then click the Calculate icon beside the **Shipping Cost** field.

For instructions on fulfilling orders with Australia Post integration, see [Fulfilling Orders with Australia Post Shipments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4274438368.html).

## Shipping Rates on the Web Store {#bridgehead_4274432200}

On your web store, the shipping rate can change depending on the Australia Post shipping item, delivery method, and delivery address the customer provides. For new orders, the default shipping address from the customer's record is shown. The shipping cost shows up in the Order Summary section of the Shipping page. The shipping cost updates automatically whenever you change the items, delivery method, or address.

For more information about shipping on your web store, see [Shipping Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2567548.html#bridgehead_N2569158) or [Customizing the Shopping Cart in Site Builder](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2571864.html).
