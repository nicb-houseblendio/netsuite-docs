If you use the Shipping Label Integration feature, the tracking number is displayed as a link on a transaction record, such as the item fulfillment. Click the tracking number link to open the Track your item page of Australia Post. This page displays the tracking summary for the package. For more information about tracking numbers on transactions, see [Displaying the Australia Post Tracking Numbers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4413365999.html).

If you use the order fulfillment email preference, the tracking numbers are included in the email sent to your customers. On the Australia Post web site, go to the Track your item page at [http://auspost.com.au/track/track.html](http://auspost.com.au/track/track.html). Enter your tracking number in the field provided, and then click **Search**. To enable the preference, see [Prerequisites](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4413330369.html#bridgehead_4274392864). For more information about the preference, see [Sending Order Fulfillment Email](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1231841.html).
