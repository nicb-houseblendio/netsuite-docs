This report is for Australia.

The GST on Sales Summary report lists the individual GST amounts posted to GST on sales accounts from transactions posted during a certain tax period.

To view the GST on Sales Summary report, go to _Reports > VAT/GST > GST on Sales_.

A message on the screen indicates that your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.
