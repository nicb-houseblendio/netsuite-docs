You can generate the following Goods and Services Tax (GST) reports for Australia:

| Tax Reports for Accounts without SuiteTax | Tax Reports for Accounts with SuiteTax |
| --- | --- |
| Tax reports available for accounts without SuiteTax:
-   [GST on Sales Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1838099.html)
-   [GST on Sales Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1838357.html)
-   [GST on Purchases Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1838645.html)
-   [GST on Purchases Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1838919.html)
-   [Business Activity Statement Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1839201.html)
-   [Taxable Payments Annual Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159410857892.html)
-   [Payment Times Report (PTR)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_165287398352.html)
-   Sales by Tax Code (Details)
-   Sales by Tax Code (Summary)
-   Purchases by Tax Code (Details)
-   Purchases by Tax Code (Summary)

Note: The Sales and Purchases by Tax Code Details and Summary reports are provided by the [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html). The Business Activity Statement Report and Taxable Payments Annual Report are provided by the [ANZ Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_158677153915.html) SuiteApp. | Tax reports available for accounts with SuiteTax:

-   [Business Activity Statement](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156688148769.html)
-   Standard Tax Report For more information, see [Generating a Standard Tax Report in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1497941633.html).
