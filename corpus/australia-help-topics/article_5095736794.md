You can generate the Payment Times Report 2025 with transaction information, see [Inclusions in the Payment Times Report 2025](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5095736792.html).

Note:

PTR 2025 is the new payment times report that follows the March 2025 guidelines from the Australian Taxation Office. To use the previous PTR 2023 report, see [Payment Times Report (PTR)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_165287398352.html).

The PTR 2025 can be viewed and exported in CSV format, along with TCP and SBTCP datasets. The following are the CSV datasets:

-   [Trade Credit Payments Dataset](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5095736795.html#bridgehead_5095736796)
    
-   [Small Business Trade Credit Payments Dataset](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5095736795.html#bridgehead_5095736797)
    
-   [PTR Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5095736795.html#subsect_0119105447)
    

#### To generate the Payment Times report 2025:

1.  Go to Reports > Tax > Country Tax Reports.
    
2.  For OneWorld accounts, select a subsidiary from the **Subsidiary** list.
    
    To consolidate subsidiaries, see [Configuring Consolidated Subsidiaries for PTR 2025](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5095736783.html#bridgehead_0524080310).
    
3.  From the **Nexus** list, select Australia.
    
4.  Select a reporting period under the **Start** and **End Period** fields.
    
5.  From the **Report Name** list, select **Payment Times Report - 2025**.
    
6.  Click **Generate**.
    
7.  On the **Report Execution Log** subtab, locate the generated report. The latest generated report displays at the top of the list.
    
8.  In the **View | Download** column, wait for the loading bar to complete and change to a **View** link.
    
    Note:
    
    The system sends you an email notification after generating the report. The email notification indicates the date requested, type, and period of the report generated. It also includes a link to the Country Tax Reports page where you can view and export the report.
    
9.  Click **View** to open the report.
    
10.  Click the **Export - CSV** icon at the top left of the report to get the payment information.
     
     You can export PTR Summary report, TCP and SBTCP datasets in CSV format.
     

After the report generation, on the Reports page, from the **PTR Report Summary** subtab, you can view the selected consolidated subsidiaries from the **View Consolidated Subsidiaries** link.
