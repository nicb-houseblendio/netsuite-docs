NSEB allows NetSuite users to communicate with third party services using OBN. The OBN manager must first register the company or its subsidiary with OBN to perform a secure document exchange.

For more information, see [Setting Up OBN Processes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0315115701.html)
