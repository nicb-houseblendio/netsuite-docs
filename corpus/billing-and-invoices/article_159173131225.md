You can generate and print an invoice group PDF statement. By default, the PDF is the summary view of the invoice group record, but you can customize it.

#### To print an invoice group PDF: {#procedure_31165738715}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Sales > Group Invoices > List_.
    
2.  Click the **View** link for the invoice group you want to print.
    
3.  Point your mouse to **Actions**, and then click **Print**.
    

Note:

The format of the PDF is determined by the Advanced PDF/HTML template. For more information, see [Advanced PDF/HTML Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4453550706.html).
