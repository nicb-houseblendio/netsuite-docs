The Avalara Setup dashboard includes the following options:

-   Mandate Activation - Opens the Mandate Activation page.
    
-   NetSuite Sandbox Guidelines - Lets you review the steps required to configure the related setup in your sandbox account, excluding Avalara setup, which is currently not supported in sandbox accounts.
    
-   Integration - Lets you update the Avalara client credentials when the client ID or client secret changes.
