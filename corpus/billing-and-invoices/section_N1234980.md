You can print a remittance slip on invoices and statements to help your customers pay you. It also helps you keep track incoming payments.

The remittance slip provides details the customer needs to make a payment. Customers fill out the information and detach the remittance slip to remit with their payment.

When you receive the payment, the enclosed remittance slip details the payment method and how the customer intends for the payment to be applied, like on specific invoices or their statement balance.

#### To activate Print Remittance Slip with Invoices & Statements: {#procedure_N1235006}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Preferences > Printing and Fax_ (Administrator).
    
2.  Click the **Printing** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Print Remittance Slip with Invoices & Statements** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Now, your printed invoices and statements show a remittance slip.

To customize remittance slips, go to _Customization > Forms > Transaction Forms_. On the Custom Transaction Forms page, click **Customize** next to Standard Remittance Slip.
