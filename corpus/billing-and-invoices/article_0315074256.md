Only users with the Avalara Manager role or Electronic Business Manager role can activate Avalara for e-invoicing using the NSEB SuiteApp. The following table lists the roles and permissions granted to perform the required Avalara processes:

| Process | Role Access | Permissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Avalara Provisioning | 
-   Avalara Manager
-   Electronic Business Manager

 | Provisioning a parent company to Avalara |
| Avalara Company Creation | 

-   Avalara Manager
-   Electronic Business Manager

 | Create company in Avalara for parent and subsidiary companies |
| Avalara Mandate Activation | 

-   Avalara Manager
-   Electronic Business Manager

 | Activate mandates for parent and subsidiary companies as per the requirements |
| Avalara Setup | 

-   Avalara Manager
-   Electronic Business Manager

 | Helps you configure e-invoicing for your parent company and subsidiaries and connects NetSuite with OBN and Avalara. |

Note:

Don't delete or deactivate the Avalara Manager role..
