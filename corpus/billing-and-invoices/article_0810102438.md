Avalara setup helps you configure e-invoicing for your parent company and subsidiaries. The guided setup connects NetSuite with Oracle Business Network (OBN) and Avalara so your organization can exchange electronic invoices with trading partners.

You can perform the Avalara setup from the Avalara Setup page. To open the Avalara Setup page, go to Setup > Electronic Business > Avalara Setup.

Avalara e-invoicing setup includes the following stages:

-   [Setting Up the Parent Company](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0728041003.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Subsidiaries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0728043127.html)
    
-   [Managing Subsidiary Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0728045453.html)
    
-   [Avalara Setup Dashboard Options](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0728045601.html)
