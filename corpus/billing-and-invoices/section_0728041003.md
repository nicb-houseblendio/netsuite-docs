Complete the parent company setup before setting up the subsidiaries. The parent company setup is a one-time task.

#### To set up the parent company:

After the provisioning is complete, you can set up the subsidiaries.

1.  Log in to NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to Setup > Electronic Business > Avalara Setup.
    
3.  On the OBN Authorization page, check the consent box.
    
4.  Click **Authorize**.
    
5.  Accept the OBN terms and conditions. The OBN Authorization success message appears.
    
6.  Click **Next** to do OBN registration.
    
7.  Review the Company Information, Address, and Contact Information sections.
    
8.  In the Company Contact Information section, select the **OBN Manager**.
    
9.  Click **Register**.
    
10.  Click **Next** to do Avalara provisioning.
     
11.  Review the Company Information, Address, and Contact Information sections.
     
12.  Under the Company Contact Information section, select the **Avalara Manager** who is authorized to accept Avalara terms and conditions on behalf of the company.
     
13.  Click **Submit.**
     
     The Avalara manager receives an email that includes:
     
     -   Avalara account information.
         
     -   Instructions for generating production and sandbox client credentials through the Avalara ELR portal.
         
14.  In the Client Key Authorization section, enter the values in the following fields:
     
     -   Production Client ID
         
     -   Production Client Secret
         
     -   Sandbox Client ID
         
     -   Sandbox Client Secret
         
15.  Click **Submit**. A success banner is displayed, indicating that Avalara provisioning is complete.
     

For more information about completing Avalara Provisioning, see [Adding Avalara Client Application API Credentials to OBN Accounts](https://suiteanswers.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1014964).
