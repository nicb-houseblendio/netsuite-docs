You can find invoice group details on the A/R aging report and a customized Open Invoices report. Use the Report Builder feature to add invoice grouping fields to the report. For more information about the Report Builder feature, see [Financial Report Builder](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2105415.html).

Here's a list of available invoice group fields:

-   Bill To
    
-   Date
    
-   Date Due
    
-   Grouping Status
    
-   Invoice Group: Invoice Group Number
    
-   Invoice Group Number
    

Open invoices that aren't in a group also appear on the report, but information appears only in the **Grouping Status** field (Not for Grouping).
