This section includes high-level overview diagrams of the billing process, both with and without the Advanced Shipping feature enabled.

## Billing Without Advanced Shipping {#bridgehead_N1233006}

The following diagram shows the workflow for billing without Advanced Shipping. For information about how to bill customers without advanced shipping, see [Adding Billing Information to a Transaction](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4588267772.html).

![Workflow diagram is a flowchart showing the sequence of events in the process of billing without advanced shipping](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/OrderManagement/BillingInvoices/billingwithout.png)

## Billing with Advanced Shipping {#bridgehead_N1233033}

The following diagram shows the workflow for billing with Advanced Shipping enabled. To enable Advanced Shipping, see [Advanced Shipping](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1224089.html). For information about billing customers using the Advanced Billing and Advanced Shipping features, see [Advanced Billing and Advanced Shipping](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1256787.html).

![Workflow diagram is a flowchart showing the sequence of events in the process of billing with advanced shipping](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/OrderManagement/BillingInvoices/billingwith.png)
