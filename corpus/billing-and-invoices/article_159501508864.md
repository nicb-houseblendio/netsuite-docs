You can create and send customers a statement that includes invoice groups.

#### To generate a customer statement that includes invoice groups: {#procedure_42165750808}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Customers > Generate Statements_.
    
    You can also go to Transaction > Customers > Individual Statements and continue with the following steps.
    
2.  Enter a date in the **Statement Date** field.
    
3.  Check the box associated with the customer to which you want to send a statement.
    
4.  From the **Form** field, select **Standard Statement**.
    
    Note:
    
    The Standard Statement PDF/HTML form lists all invoices in the invoice group. You can customize the template to only show one line for the entire invoice group.
    
5.  Click **Print**.
