If the conditions and requirements for conversion are met, you are ready to convert an inbound e-document into a vendor credit. For more information, see [Prerequisites and Conditions for Conversion](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1495533045.html#subsect_0624021307).

## To convert an inbound e-document into a sales order: {#bridgehead_63213223995}

1.  Go to the **E-Documents** portlet on the Home page, and click the number under **Inbound E-Documents for Conversion**.
    
2.  On the Inbound E-Document for Conversion results page, click **View** on the inbound e-document that you want to convert into a sales order.
    
3.  On the Inbound E-Documents page, click **Convert**.
    
    Note:
    
    Before proceeding with the conversion, ensure that the date format in the XML e-documents is the same as the date format in the Set Preferences page. The progress of conversion is displayed as a banner on the page.
    
    If conversion is successful, the created sales order is displayed. In the **E-Document** subtab of the sales order, the **Inbound E-Document** field indicates the record link of the converted inbound e-document.
    
    The **E-Document Audit Trail** subtab of the inbound e-document record will indicate the details of the successful conversion and the status is set to **Converted**. Details of any error or failure in the conversion will also be listed in the e-document audit trail.
