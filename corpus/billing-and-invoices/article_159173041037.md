After creating an invoice group, you can view individual invoices from the invoice group record.

#### To view an individual invoice in an invoice group: {#procedure_94165837905}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Sales > Group Invoices > List_.
    
2.  Find the invoice group containing the invoice you want to view, and then click the **Edit** or **View** link.
    
3.  You have two options to open an individual invoice in a popup window:
    
    1.  To open the invoice in edit mode, click the **Edit** link.
        
    2.  To open the invoice in view mode, click the invoice number.
        

The status of the invoice appears beside the customer name. For example, an unpaid grouped invoice has a status of Open - Grouped.

After you group in invoice, you can still edit the invoice, but there are some fields you can't change, such as:

-   Due Date
    
-   PO #
    
-   Terms (located on the **Billing** subtab)
