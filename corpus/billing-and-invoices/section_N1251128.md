To enable Advanced Billing, follow these steps:

#### To enable Advanced Billing: {#procedure_N1251149}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **Transactions** subtab, check the **Advanced Billing** box.
    
3.  To use Advanced Billing, you're also required to enable the Sales Orders feature by checking the **Sales Orders** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

To create a billing schedule, go to _Lists > Accounting > Billing Schedules > New_.

Note:

If you decide to turn off Advanced Shipping later, you must first process all sales orders on the _Transactions > Sales > Bill Sales Orders_ page.
