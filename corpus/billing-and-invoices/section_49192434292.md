You must link a fulfillable item to a subscription for the fulfillable item to be included in its associated subscription. To link a fulfillable item to a subscription, you must first link the fulfillable item to a subscription item that is part of a subscription. You can do the linking of the fulfillable item to a subscription when entering a sales order.

#### To link a fulfillable item to a subscription:

1.  Go to Transactions > Sales > Enter Sales Orders.
    
2.  In the **Status** field, select **Pending Approval**.
    
    Splitting of linked subscription line items into multiple lines is only supported for sales orders with a status of **Pending Approval**.
    
3.  Check the **Split Linked Subscription Items Into Multiple Lines** box.
    
    Checking this box splits subscription line items into multiple lines in the sales order. When subscription line items are split, you can enter unique additional item information for each of the fulfillable items in the item fulfillment record. Split subscription line items also makes it easier to handle partial fulfillment, activation, and returns.
    
    Splitting only happens in the following scenarios:
    
    -   The subscription line is not required in the subscription plan.
        
    -   The subscription has linked fulfillable items.
        
    -   The quantity of the subscription items is greater than one.
        
    
    When subscription line items are split into multiple lines, they cannot be merged back.
    
    If the quantity of the subscription items is 50 or more, a map/reduce script splits the subscription line items into multiple lines in bulk. Administrators can check the status of the bulk-splitting process in the Subscription Line Splitting event record. For more information, see [Viewing the Subscription Line Splitting Event Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_97170430366.html).
    
4.  Fill out the fields in the sales order.
    
    For more information, see [Creating Sales Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1216500.html).
    
5.  In the **Items** subtab, do the following steps:
    
    1.  In the **Item** column, select the fulfillable item.
        
    2.  In the **Subscription Item** column, select the subscription item that you want to link to the fulfillable item.
        
        If you have previously linked the fulfillable item to the subscription item, this field is automatically populated with the linked subscription item. For more information, see [(Optional) Linking a Fulfillable Item to a Subscription Item](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_51145124314.html).
        
    3.  In the **Subscription** column, create or select the subscription that you want to link to the subscription item.
        
        For more information about creating a subscription, see [Creating a New Stand-alone Subscription](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1547668165.html).
        
        Important:
        
        You must do the following in the subscription record:
        
        -   Ensure that you add the subscription item you selected in step 5b in the **Lines** and **Pricing** subtabs.
            
        -   In the **Lines** subtab, check the **Include** box for each of the subscription item that you want to add.
            
        -   In the **Pricing** subtab, ensure that the quantity of the subscription item matches the quantity of the fulfillable item.
            
        
        Note:
        
        If you want to edit the quantity of subscription items in the subscription, it is best practice to do so only when the sales order has not yet been approved. Any additional quantities you add for the subscription items will not be split into multiple lines when the associated sales order has already been approved.
        
    4.  Enter information as necessary in all the other columns under the **Items** subtab. For more information, see [Adding Items on a Sales Transaction](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4588373194.html).
        
    5.  Click **Add** to add the items in the sales order.
        
        The subscription line items then appear in the sales order, that includes the subscription plan, subscription items, and the fulfillable items.
        
6.  Click **Save**.
    
    If the quantity of subscription items is more than one and the **Split Linked Subscription Items Into Multiple Lines** is checked, you must update the sales order after saving the record for new sales orders. This is to show the split subscription items into their own lines in the sales order. When you edit and save a sales order, there is no need to update the sales order to split subscription items into their own lines.
