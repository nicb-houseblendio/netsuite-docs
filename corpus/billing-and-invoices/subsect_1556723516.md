After creating an add-on item in a subscription, you can't delete it. Like other line items, the add-on item is part of the subscription. If you need to omit the add-on item from the subscription, change the add-on item's status to **Not Included**.

#### To remove an add-on item from a subscription:

1.  Go to _Transactions > Subscriptions > Create Subscriptions > List_.
    
2.  For the subscription you want to edit, click the subscription name or **Edit.**
    
    Tip:
    
    To find the subscription, you can:
    
    -   Look in the **Recent Records** tab for recently completed subscriptions.
        
    -   Enter the subscription name or subscription ID into the **Global Search** field.
        
    
3.  For the add-on item to remove, clear the preference in the **Include** column.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
