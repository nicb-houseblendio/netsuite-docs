-   You can use NSEB with only one NetSuite Sandbox instance. After you start using NSEB in a specific sandbox account (SB1), you cannot switch to another sandbox account instance (SB2).
    
-   Uninstalling and reinstalling the NSEB SuiteApp in sandbox is not supported after the linking is done in sandbox account.
