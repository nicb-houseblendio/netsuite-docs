The invoice group record has three views: A/R, All Details, and Summary.

#### To view the invoice group record: {#procedure_19165832872}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Sales > Group Invoices > List_.
    
2.  Click the **Edit** link next to the invoice group you want to view.
    
3.  To change your view, click the **Details** subtab, and then select the view you want from the **View** list.
    

Use these different views to show information in the format that you want:

-   **A/R** - The A/R view only shows lines that post to the general ledger. This view includes posting discounts. All other discounts are included in the product price, wheras subtotals are excluded.
    
-   **All Details** - The details view shows tax information, subtotals, and discount amounts.
    
-   **Summary** - The summary view show a list of invoices linked to the invoice group.
