Enable the Invoice Groups feature from the Enable Features page.

Warning:

After the Invoice Groups feature is enabled and an invoice group has been created, it can no longer be disabled. Before enabling this feature in your production account, it is important to test this feature in your sandbox or release preview account. If you need further assistance, contact your NetSuite account manager.

#### To enable the Invoice Groups feature:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Transaction** subtab.
    
3.  From the **Billing** section, check the **Invoice Groups** box.
    

Tip:

If you can't see the Invoice Groups feature, switch to an administrative role or contact your NetSuite administrator.
