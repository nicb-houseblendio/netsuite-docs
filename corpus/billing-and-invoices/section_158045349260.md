An administrator can create custom templates to specify default subject and body text for e-document emails. Users can use these templates when sending e-documents by email to any entity selected in outbound e-document sending process.

After the administrator creates the e-document email templates, you can select them from the **E-Document Email Customization** list. You can find the list in subsidiary records on the Electronic Invoicing Preferences page in OneWorld accounts. For accounts without OneWorld, you can find the list in the parent company link.

For more information, see [Creating an E-Document Email Custom Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158070135810.html) and [Selecting an E-Document Email Custom Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158070488814.html).
