The Invoice Approval Workflow handles validation and approval routing of an invoice before you send it to a customer. It uses set criteria to determine whether an invoice needs approval. A supervisor or billing manager is required to review only incorrect or questionable invoices.

The Invoice Approval Workflow runs these checks:

-   Identification of a customer's first invoice
    
-   Customers with a credit hold on the account
    
-   Validation of payment terms on the invoice against the customer record
    
-   Validation of tax amount on the invoice
    
-   Identification of flagged customers
    

For more information about the validations, see [Invoice Approval Workflow Exception Criteria](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4171586942.html).

You can customize the workflow and select only the criteria that fit your business needs. To learn how workflow-based approval affects other transactions, read [Transactional Impact for Workflow Based Invoice Approvals](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4025082250.html).

## Availability {#bridgehead_4171555416}

The Invoice Approval Workflow is included in the NetSuite Approvals Workflow SuiteApp that must be installed in your account. For the steps, see [Installing the NetSuite Approvals Workflow SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4171556721.html#bridgehead_4171562457).
