An invoice closes when it's paid in full. However, the status shows Paid in Full instead of Closed.

You can't change invoices in a closed period.

Depending on your company policy or location, deleting an invoice may not be an option.

If your company policy allows, you may be able to void an invoice or issue a credit memo and apply the credit memo to the invoice.

For more information, see [Voiding, Deleting, or Closing Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N563543.html).
