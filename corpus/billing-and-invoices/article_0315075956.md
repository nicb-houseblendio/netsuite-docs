Avalara limitations and known issues in NSEB SuiteApp:

-   The Avalara manager needs to follow the following order. If you don't follow this order, the Avalara processes won't work as expected:
    
    1.  Provisioning
        
    2.  Company Creation
        
    3.  Mandate Activation
        
-   The Avalara website link received in the email after the Avalara provisioning is only valid for 6 hours. If the link expires then you must go to the Avalara website and request a new onboarding email for the username.
    
-   NSEB does not support Subsidiary Hierarchy Structure Modification feature after Registration. Changing the parent subsidiary and child subsidiary hierarchy after Avalara company creation can result in functionality breakages.
    
-   If the current Avalara manager is inactive, you'll need to assign a new one with the login credentials for the Avalara website.
