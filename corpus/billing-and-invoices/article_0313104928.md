A NetSuite user can choose to activate its access to the Avalara platform through Oracle Business Network for electronic business purposes. The key benefits of Avalara for e-invoicing are as follows:

-   Avalara global network connection - Send and receive documents through Avalara and their integrated networks.
    
-   Avalara mandate feed - Enable NetSuite users considering operating in a new country to request information from Avalara about mandate requirements of that country.
    
-   OBN participation - Enable NetSuite accounts to participate in the global OBN and interact commercially with all participating companies.
    
-   Network registration - Enable NetSuite users to register their participation on the electronic networks.
    
-   Country specific data - Lets the creation of common values used in countries, and by businesses that can be shared across all transactions. Also includes currency, tax codes, and tax categories.
