Before you begin Avalara setup, ensure that the parent company and all subsidiaries have the following information:

-   Legal name
    
-   Address
    

If any of the above information is missing, update the parent subsidiary or subsidiary record, and refresh the page.

Before you begin the setup, ensure that the parent company is active.
