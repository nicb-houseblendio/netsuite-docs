Note:

To fully use the outbound and inbound e-document processing of the Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp, make sure your account administrator has already created and applied the e-document templates, sending method, email recipients, email capture, inbound validation plug-in, and other settings. For information about setting up Electronic Invoicing, see [Electronic Invoicing Administrator Guide](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4580942529.html).
