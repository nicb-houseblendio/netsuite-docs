NetSuite has these standard invoices types:

-   **Finance Charge** - Use this only to assess finance charges, not for regular products and services. It includes the date, finance charge number, bill-to address, terms, due date, project, item, description, amount, and total.
    
    Note:
    
    This invoice type doesn't show in the list for UK accounts.
    
-   **Product** - This includes all the information from the standard service invoice. In addition, it includes the ship-to address, sales representative, ship date, FOB, and unit price.
    
-   **Professional** - This is the simplest type. It includes the date, invoice number, bill-to address, terms, due date, project, item name, description, amount, tax, and total.
    
-   **Service** - This includes all the information from the standard professional invoice. In addition, it includes the PO# and itemizes a quantity and rate for each line item in the body of the form.
    
-   **Progress** - Use progress billing to bill for portions of projects as they're completed. It includes details such as date, invoice number, bill-to address, terms, due date, PO#, sales rep, end date, shipping information, item name, current percentage completed, description, amount, and a percentage of the total cost.
    

#### To select an invoice type: {#procedure_N1240676}

1.  Go to _Billing > Sales > Create Invoices_.
    
2.  Under Primary Information, in the **Custom Form** field, select a standard invoice or a custom invoice form.
    
3.  Continue to fill out details on the Invoice page.
    
4.  When you're done, choose one of three options to post the information to your NetSuite account:
    
    -   Click **Save** to submit the information and return to the Transactions page.
        
    -   Click **Save & New** to submit the information and return to another blank Invoice page.
        
    -   Click **Print** to save the information and print the invoice form you created.
        

## Custom Invoice Forms {#bridgehead_N1240759}

To create a custom invoice form, go to _Customization > Forms > Transaction Forms_.
