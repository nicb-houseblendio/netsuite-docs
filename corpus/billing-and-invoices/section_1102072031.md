The PEPPOL templates for the following transaction types are specifically designed for SuiteTax accounts:

-   [CTT: Sales Outbound Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1102072224.html)
    
-   [CTT: Purchase Outbound Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1102072308.html)
    

Some elements in the CTT: Sales Template mappings table and CTT: Purchase Template mappings table are hard coded values, values from the transaction and its related records like subsidiary, customer and vendor, or values derived from custom data source plug-in.

You can use these templates along with the custom data source plug-in to generate the e-document for CTT: Sales and CTT: Purchase transactions. Also, you can add your own customizations in the template to get the generated e-document in the desired form.

The names and the location of the templates, custom data source in the Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp are in the table below:

| PEPPOL Template | Template or file name | Location | Plug-in Implementation Name |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Generic PEPPOL Template for CTT: Sales | CTT\_Sales\_Peppol\_Template\_Generic.txt | Bundle 436209/Sample Templates | NA |
| Generic Peppol Template for CTT: Purchase | CTT\_Purchase\_Peppol\_Template\_Generic.txt | Bundle 436209/Sample Templates | NA |
| Custom Data Source Plug-in (CTT: Sales) | pl\_custom\_data\_source\_ctt\_sales\_outbound.js | Bundle 436209/src/comp/pl | CTT Sales Outbound CDS |
| Custom Data Source Plug-in (CTT: Purchase) | pl\_custom\_data\_source\_ctt\_purchase\_outbound.js | Bundle 436209/src/comp/pl | CTT Purchase Outbound CDS |
