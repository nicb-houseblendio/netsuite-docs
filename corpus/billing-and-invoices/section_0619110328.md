You are given access to the e-document subsidiary preferences record to view or edit the account level and subsidiary level preferences in the Electronic Invoicing Preferences page.

If your role does not have permission to view the e-document subsidiary preferences record, you will get the following message in the Subsidiaries field group:

To access the subsidiary preferences records, contact your account administrator.

For more information about the Electronic Invoicing Preferences, see [Setting Up Electronic Invoicing Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0619104200.html).

Note:

You won't be able to create or delete e-document subsidiary preferences record, even if your role has full permission to access. Contact your administrator to delete an e-document subsidiary preferences record instance. You must not delete a e-document subsidiary preferences record.
