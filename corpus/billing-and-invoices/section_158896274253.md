To modify the Class, Department, or Location field of a subscription line, the line must be in the Draft status. Void any change orders for the subscription line. Change the subscription line status to Draft, then update the Class, Department, or Location field.

To move a subscription line back to Draft status when a sales order is attached, change the sales order from the Approved status to Draft status. When changing only one line, you only need to move the line back to Draft status. You don't have to void all change orders. For fundamental information about change orders, see [SuiteBilling Change Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1547761140.html).
