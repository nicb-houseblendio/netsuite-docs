You can add a **Deposit Balance** field to customer statements or remittance slips so customers can see any unapplied deposit amount left on their account. By default, the **Deposit Balance** field doesn't show on customer statements or remittance slips.

Similar to the **Deposit Balance** field, you can also add a **Net Amount Due** field to customer statements and remittance slips. If you show **Net Amount Due**, it's assumed that your company applies the deposit balance to any open invoices on or before the payment due date. Therefore, **Net Amount Due** is the **Statement Balance** less **Deposit Balance**. If you don't plan to apply the deposit balance to any open invoices, for example a security deposit on hold, don't show the **Net Amount Due** field. Please note that even if you add these on customer statements or remittance slips, these fields don't show on printed invoices.

The **Deposit Balance** and **Net Amount Due** fields use the same currency as the statement. For consolidated statements, the **Deposit Balance** on the statement is the consolidated deposit balance for the parent and all sub-customers. The **Net Amount Due** is the amount due of all customers less the consolidated deposit balance.

For more information, see either [Customer Statements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1300430.html) or see [Remittance Forms](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1234795.html).

#### To enable the Deposit Balance or Net Amount Due field on customer statements:

1.  Go to Customization > Forms > Transaction Forms.
    
2.  Click the **Customize** link for Standard Statement.
    
    If you can't see the link, contact your administrator.
    
3.  On the **Custom Transaction Form** record, click the **Body** subtab, and then check the **Print/Email** box for Deposit Bal.
    
4.  To enable the **Net Amount Due** field, check the **Print/Email** box for Net Amount Due.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To enable the Deposit Balance or Net Amount Due field on the remittance slip:

1.  Go to Customization > Forms > Transaction Forms.
    
2.  Click the **Customize** link for Standard Remittance Slip.
    
    If you can't see the link, contact your administrator.
    
3.  On the **Custom Transaction Form** record, click the **Body** subtab, and then check the **Print/Email** box for Deposit Bal.
    
4.  To enable the **Net Amount Due** field, check the **Print/Email** box for Net Amount Due.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
