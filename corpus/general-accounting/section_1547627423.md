The setup for the Balancing Segments feature requires the following three procedures:

-   [Enabling the Balancing Segments Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1547638463.html)
    
-   [Creating a Balancing Segment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1547638483.html)
    
-   [Configuring Intersegment Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157247561203.html)
