You record manual journal entries at _Transactions > Financial > Make Journal Entries_. When you're finished entering line items on the Journal page, the **Out of Balance** fields at the top should be empty. If either field contains an amount, your line items are out of balance and you can't save the journal entry.

For more information, see [Making Journal Entries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1469880.html) and [Reversing Journal Entries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1471552.html).

Note:

Intercompany journal entries, statistical journal entries, revenue recognition journal entries, and amortization journal entries are made on their own specialized pages. These journal entries are available from separate menu options.
