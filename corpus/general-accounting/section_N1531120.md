The following receivables reports are available:

-   [A/R Aging Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1531392.html)
    
-   [A/R Aging Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1532837.html)
    
-   [A/R Register Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1533154.html)
    
-   [A/R Payment History by Invoice](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1533442.html)
    
-   [A/R Payment History by Payment Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1533726.html)
    
-   [Receivables by Customer Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1534014.html)
    

There are row limitations for report results. For more information, see [Row Limitations for Report Results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N719691.html).
