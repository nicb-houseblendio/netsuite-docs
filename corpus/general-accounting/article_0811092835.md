## Employer Payroll Tax Mapping {#subsect_0811093343}

Employer-paid payroll taxes are mapped by rule order. Evaluate the rules from top to bottom and use the first matching category.

| **Employer Payroll Tax** | **Labor Cost Element** |
| --- | --- |
| Federal Employer Taxes: Social Security, Medicare, Federal Unemployment Tax (FUTA) | Employer Paid Federal Taxes |
| State Unemployment Insurance (SUI) | State Unemployment Insurance |
| Any (non SUI) employer paid state and local taxes | Employer Paid Locality Taxes |
