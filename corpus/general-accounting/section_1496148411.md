You print a list of journal entries from the specific journal entry type page. For example, if you want to print a list of general journal entries, go to _Transactions > Financial > Make Journal Entries > List_.

#### To print a list of journal entries: {#procedure_N1472419}

1.  Go to the List view of the specific journal entry type you want to print.
    
2.  Click the print icon to print the list of journal entries.
