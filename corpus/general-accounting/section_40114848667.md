Before you use email approval, you'll need to enable the email capture plug-in in your NetSuite account.

#### To enable the email capture plug-in: {#procedure_19095252418}

1.  Go to _Customization > Plug-ins > Manage Plug-ins_
    
2.  In the Email Capture Plug-in section, check the **SuiteApprovals Email Approval** box.
    
    Take note of the email address. You'll need it in the next step.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

After you've enabled the email capture plug-in, set the email approval preferences. For more information, see [Setting Up Email Approval Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1011095507.html).
