The following financial reports are available in addition to financial statements:

-   [General Ledger Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1520771.html)
    
-   [Trial Balance Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1520986.html)
    
-   [Post Closing Trial Balance Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1531245926.html) (requires the Period End Journal Entries feature)
    
-   [Transaction Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N569351.html)
    
-   [Realized Exchange Rate Gains and Losses Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1425486.html)
    
-   [Unrealized Exchange Rate Gains and Losses Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1437432.html)
    
-   [Intercompany Elimination Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1502692.html) (requires the Automated Intercompany Management feature)
    
-   [Intercompany Reconciliation Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1502183.html) (requires the Automated Intercompany Management feature)
    
-   [Amortization Forecast Summary](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1790278.html) (requires the Amortization feature)
    
-   [Amortization Forecast Detail](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1790537.html) (requires the Amortization feature)
    
-   [Deferred/Capitalized Expense](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1790793.html) (requires the Amortization feature)
    
-   [Deferred Expense Waterfall Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1507073448.html) (requires the Amortization feature)
    
-   [Deferred Expense Waterfall Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1507073483.html) (requires the Amortization feature)
    
-   [Deferred Expense Rollforward Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1496866688.html) (requires the Amortization feature)
    
-   [GL Audit Numbering Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3739913944.html) (requires the GL Audit Numbering feature)
    
-   [Labor Costs Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0811090910.html)
    
-   [Labor Costs and Revenue Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0811092002.html)
    

There are row limitations for report results. For more information, see [Row Limitations for Report Results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N719691.html).

Important:

NetSuite provides specialized customization methods for financial statements, including income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements. Financial statements are described separately from financial reports. See [Financial Statements Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2092547.html).
