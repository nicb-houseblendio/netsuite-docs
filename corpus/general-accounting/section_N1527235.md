The following purchases reports are available:

-   [Purchase by Vendor Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1527523.html)
    
-   [Purchase by Vendor Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1527792.html)
    
-   [Purchase by Item Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1528061.html)
    
-   [Purchase by Item Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1528384.html)
    
-   [Purchase Order Register Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1528721.html)
    
-   [Open Purchase Orders Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1529021.html)
    
-   [Purchase Order History Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2409777.html)
    

There are row limitations for report results. For more information, see [Row Limitations for Report Results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N719691.html).
