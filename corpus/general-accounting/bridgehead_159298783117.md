Important:

Only users with the customized Employee Center role for SuiteApprovals can access vendor bills from the Employee Center. For more information, see [Setting Up Access to Vendor Bills from the Employee Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_159298760873.html).

When you first log in using your customized Employee Center role, add the **Records for Approval** portlet to your Employee Center dashboard. For more information, see [Adding a Portlet to a Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N580906.html).

The Records for Approval portlet lets you do the following:

-   If you have vendor bills to approve, the portlet shows the count and a link to the page listing the records.
    
-   On the list page, you can open individual records to view, approve, or reject them.
    

For more information, see [Approving or Rejecting Records Using SuiteApprovals](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0603083352.html).

You can also check the approval history for every record. For more information, see [Viewing Approval History](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1506060854.html).

Note:

The same email notification guidelines apply when you approve or reject vendor bills using your custom Employee Center role. For more information, see _Guidelines for Using SuiteApprovals Email Notifications_.
