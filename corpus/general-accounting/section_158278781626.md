In Sequential Liability, when you create a customer invoice or issue a credit memo, you must associate it with its corresponding project. Customer invoices and credit memos that are not associated to a project are not reflected when you use the SuiteApp.

#### To associate customer invoices and credit memos to a project:

1.  Enter a customer invoice or issue a credit memo.
    
    For instructions on entering customer invoices, see [Creating an Invoice](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1238506.html).
    
    For instructions on issuing credit memos, see [Issuing a Customer Credit Memo](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1311510.html).
    
2.  In the **Project** field of the customer invoice or credit memo, select a project.
    
    Selecting a project in the **Project** field isn't required in the customer invoice or credit memo but it is required for this SuiteApp.
    
3.  Save the customer invoice or credit memo.
