Note:

To avoid issues with the report, ensure that all accounting periods are assigned before you use the SuiteApp. For more information, see [Accounting Period Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1445226.html).

After completing the prerequisites, installation, and role setup, you can use the Account Matching Report SuiteApp for matching transactions.

To understand how the SuiteApp works, read the following topics:

-   [Generating the Account Matching Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0421122801.html)
    
-   [Account Matching Report Components](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0421124530.html)
    
-   [Matching Transactions Manually](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0421011453.html)
