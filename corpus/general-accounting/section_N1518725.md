NetSuite provides several register reports. These reports are different from the single account registers described in [Available Account Registers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1511955.html). Each of these reports combines data for all registers of a specific type. The register reports provide more display options than single account registers, but don't provide access to transaction editing or account reconciliation.

The register reports available to you depend upon the features enabled in your account. They may include the following:

-   [A/P Register Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1530244.html) at _Reports > Vendors/Payables > A/P Register_
    
-   [A/R Register Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1533154.html) at _Reports > Customer/Receivables > A/R Register_
    
-   [Bank Register Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1554649.html) at _Reports > Banking/Budgeting > Bank Register_
    
-   [Estimates Register Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1074416.html) at _Reports > Pipeline Analysis > Estimates Register_
    
-   [Payroll Check Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N960394.html) at _Reports > Payroll > Payroll Check Register_
    
-   [Purchase Order Register Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1528721.html) at _Reports > Purchases > Purchase Order Register_
    
-   [Return Authorizations Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1316135.html) at _Reports > Order Management > Return Authorizations Register_
    
-   [Sales Order Register Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1315449.html) at _Reports > Order Management > Sales Orders Register_
    
-   Transfer Order Register Report at _Reports > Inventory/Items > Transfer Order Register_ - see [Searches, Reports, and Reminders for Transfer Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2309511.html)
