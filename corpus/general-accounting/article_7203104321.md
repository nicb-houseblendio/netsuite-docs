The Intelligent Close Manager provides a centralized view of period close tasks, showing both completed and outstanding actions that affect your accounting period. Enable the Intelligent Close Manager to monitor progress, identify bottlenecks, and take action on incomplete tasks to help close the accounting period efficiently.

#### To enable the Intelligent Close Manager:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_ and click the **Accounting** subtab.
    
2.  In the Advanced Features section, check the **Intelligent Close Manager** box.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

The Intelligent Close Manager portlet is automatically added to your Home page after enabling it. For more information about using the Intelligent Close Manager portlet, see [Using the Intelligent Close Manager Dashboard Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_3203353024.html).
