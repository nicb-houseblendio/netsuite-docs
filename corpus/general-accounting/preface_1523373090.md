In NetSuite, you can create budgets that fit your specific financial planning requirements to help you to estimate your revenue and expenses.

For an introduction to Budgets in NetSuite and instructions for setting up a budget, see:

-   [Budgets in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1503165.html)
    
-   [Setting Up a Budget](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1503407.html)
    

With the Multiple Budgets feature in NetSuite OneWorld, you can create separate budgets for each of your subsidiaries. If your business uses more than one currency, you can enable the Multiple Currencies feature and select the currency for your subsidiary budgets. For more information, see [Subsidiary Budgets in OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1506361.html).
