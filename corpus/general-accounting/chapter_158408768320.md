Sequential Liability provides a unified view of accounts payable, accounts receivable, projects, and cash balances as well as enable controllers to pay vendors confidently. This SuiteApp helps you effectively manage cash flow for transactions where payment to vendors is linked to receipt from customers. This is useful if you want to pay your vendors for goods or services based on payment received from customers.

You can do the following in Sequential Liability:

-   View a summary of project-related payable and receivable transactions per subsidiary.
    
-   View more details about the payable and receivable transactions for a specific project.
    
-   View A/P - A/R details from the project record.
    
-   View the A/P balance, A/R balance, total amounts received and paid for a project or subsidiary as KPI tiles on the dashboard.

The target audience for this SuiteApp are financial controllers, accountants, and financial users who handle accounts payable and accounts receivable transactions.

Sequential Liability is available in all languages that NetSuite supports. For more information, read [Configuring Multiple Languages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N247147.html) and [Choosing a Language for Your NetSuite User Interface](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N479109.html).
