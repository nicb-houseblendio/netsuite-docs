Users with the following roles can approve or reject records in the NetSuite user interface or by email. A user needs **View** permission to the record to approve it.

-   Supervisor
    
-   Accountant
    
-   Finance Manager
    
-   Purchasing Manager
    
-   Warehouse Manager
    
-   CEO
    
-   CFO
    
-   System Administrator
    

By default, users with the Administrator role have access to the approval logs.

Other users need a role with at least view permissions to the email approval log. For more information, see [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html) and [Assigning Roles to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N897798.html).

#### To give custom roles access to email approval log: {#procedure_0601014958}

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the role.
    
3.  On the **Permissions** subtab, click **Custom Record**.
    
4.  From the **Record** list, select **Email Approval Log**.
    
5.  Set the permission level to **View**.
    
6.  Click **Add**, then click **Save**.
