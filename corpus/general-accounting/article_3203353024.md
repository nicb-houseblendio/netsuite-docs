The Intelligent Close Manager portlet on your Home page provides a centralized view of tasks, estimated impacts, and AI-driven exceptions, including incorrect amounts and projected activity. The portlet helps you monitor close progress and complete daily tasks.

To use the portlet, you must enable the Intelligent Close Manager feature and have the Manage Accounting Periods permission at the View or Full level.

Data in the portlet refreshes every hour. The timestamp in the portlet indicates the last time there was a data refresh.

#### To add the portlet to your Home page:

1.  Go to your Home page.
    
2.  Click the **Personalize** link in the upper-right corner of your dashboard, or use the **Personalize Dashboard** link in the **Settings** portlet.
    
3.  On the **Standard Content** tab, click **Close Manager**. The Intelligent Close Manager portlet will be added to your Home page. You can drag the portlet to the location you'd like it to appear on your Home page.
    

For more information about customizing portlets, see [Dashboard Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N576951.html).

## Filtering and Locking by Period and Subsidiary {#subsect_0625070707}

Filter the portlet by period and subsidiary to view KPIs, tasks, and amounts for specific subsidiaries and accounting periods:

-   **Period filter** - Shows Prioritized tasks, A/R, A/P, Accounting, and Custom tasks by accounting period. This field defaults to your current accounting period. If your previous accounting period is open or locked, it defaults to that period instead.
    
-   **Subsidiary filter** - Shows Prioritized tasks, A/R, A/P, Accounting, and Custom tasks by subsidiary. This field defaults to your parent subsidiary.
    

After you change the Period or Subsidiary filters, click **Update** for the portlet data to reflect your selection.

You can also lock transaction types and periods from the Intelligent Close Manager by clicking the Manage lock periods icon.

## Understanding the Reviewer Field {#subsect_0625070759}

The Reviewer field on the A/R, A/P, and Accounting tabs shows who completed the applicable locking action for the selected period and subsidiary. On the A/R and A/P tabs, the Reviewer field shows the user who locked A/R or A/P. On the Accounting tab, it shows the user who locked All. If A/R and A/P weren't locked separately and one user locked All, that user is shown as the Reviewer on all three tabs.

If A/R, A/P, or All hasn't been locked for the selected period and subsidiary, the Reviewer field is empty on the respective tab.

If you filter to a consolidated subsidiary, the Reviewer field shows the user who locked the parent company for the selected period and applicable area.

Note:

If different users lock A/R, A/P, or All for different subsidiaries in the same period, the Reviewer field may show a user who locked the same area for another subsidiary in that period.

## Review Tasks by Priority {#subsect_0512041724}

Use the Prioritized tasks tab to view and prioritize close tasks. The tab shows the priority, task name, estimated impact, due date, number of tasks to complete, and status. Click the number in the Tasks column to open the task page.

The Due Date and Status columns are populated only when the portlet is filtered to the first open accounting period. When the portlet is filtered to the first open accounting period, task names in the Prioritized tasks tab include links to close manager task records. Use close manager task records to assign tasks, set due dates, and update task status. For more information, see [Viewing and Editing Close Manager Task Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_2120056461.html).

Select one of the following options when sorting tasks by priority:

-   **Default view** - Prioritizes tasks in the standard workflow order.
    
-   **AI Prioritized** - Prioritizes tasks automatically based on factors such as transaction amount, business impact, and due date.
    

You can also search and sort by prioritized tasks.

The AI Prioritized option is available only when the **Enable Narrative Insights** preference is enabled on the AI Preferences page. For more information, see [Managing Narrative Insights Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6150024470.html).
