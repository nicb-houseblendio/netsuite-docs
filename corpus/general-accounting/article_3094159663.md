To use Exception Management (EM) in your Production account, you must enable two Advanced Features: Exception Management Models and Exception Management. Exception Management Models trains the AI models on your production data and must be enabled first. Exception Management then turns on the user interface (UI) so you can view, investigate, and resolve exceptions, and it depends on Exception Management Models being enabled.

Important:

Exception Management (EM) is currently not available to all customers. The Exception Management feature depends on sufficient data volume and model training readiness. If you can't access Exception Management, contact your NetSuite Account Manager for guidance and next steps.

Note:

The setup steps below are for using Exception Management (EM) in your Production account. To use EM in a Sandbox account, see [Working With Exception Management in Sandbox Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_33817101.html).

#### To enable Exception Management (EM):

After EM is enabled, models begin training on your historical transaction data (typically the last 18 months) to learn your patterns. EM then evaluates newly created or edited transactions hourly.

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features (Administrator)_ and click the **Accounting** subtab.
    
2.  Under Advanced Features, select **Exception Management Models** and **Exception Management**.
    
    Note:
    
    Ensure the Accounting Periods feature under Basic Features is enabled before activating EM. For more information, see [Accounting Period Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1445226.html).
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To disable Exception Management (EM):

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features (Administrator)_ and click the **Accounting** subtab.
    
2.  Under Advanced Features, clear the relevant check boxes:
    
    1.  To turn off the Exception Management UI only, clear **Exception Management**. Models will continue training in the background and remain available for future use.
        
    2.  To stop model training, clear **Exception Management Models**. This action automatically disables the UI if it is still enabled.
        
3.  Click **Save**.
