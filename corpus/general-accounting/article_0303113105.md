Narrative insights for the Open Purchase Order report highlight critical open orders that you expect to receive in a location. You can view overdue and incoming orders close to the receipt date to help in prioritizing tasks and follow-ups. Potential bottlenecks and production or sales impact can be avoided when you have information about orders with high-value items and large quantities. For instructions and guidelines that apply to all AI-generated insights, see [Narrative Insights Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_135342304.html).

Each section may contain the following AI-generated content based on your report results:

-   **Key Points** (maximum of 4) - may include POs with High Outstanding Quantities, POs for Critical Items, POs with High Dollar Value Outstanding, Repeated Vendor Issues, POs Close to Expiry/Approval Deadline, POs with Price or Contract Variances, POs with Hold/Blocked Status, POs Due for Immediate Action
    
-   **Key Takeaways** - may include suggestions and guidance on proactive procurement, schedule optimization, supplier management, vendor coordination, and streamlining inventory flows
    

To enable narrative insights, see [Managing Narrative Insights Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6150024470.html).
