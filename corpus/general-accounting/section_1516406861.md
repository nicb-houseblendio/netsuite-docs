Period end journal detail pages open when you click the Detail icon ![Detail icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Accounting/GeneralAccounting/PeriodCheckIcon.png) in the period end journal line.

All period end journal detail pages include the following fields in their headers. The values for these fields are derived from the period end journal.

-   Accounting Book (only when the Multi-Book Accounting feature is enabled)
    
-   Subsidiary
    
-   Source Subsidiary
    
-   Posting Period.
    

The following are the detail pages for the period end journal types:

-   [Consolidated Activity Detail](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1516403985.html)
    
-   [Consolidated Rate Change Detail](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1516404123.html)
    
-   [Income Statement Closing Detail](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1516404404.html)
    
-   [Balance Sheet Closing Detail](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1531160351.html)
