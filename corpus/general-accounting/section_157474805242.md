You can view a project's payable and receivable balances from the Project A/P - A/R Summary page. From this page, you can also go to the Bill Payment page where you can pay bills associated with a vendor.

#### To view the project A/P - A/R summary:

1.  Go to Reports > Sequential Liability > Project A/P - A/R Summary.
    
2.  In the **Subsidiary** field, select a subsidiary.
    
    The subsidiary associated with your account is selected by default.
    
3.  In the **Bank Account** field, select a bank account.
    
    You can only select from bank accounts associated with the chosen subsidiary.
    
    Selecting a bank account displays the available balance in that account.
    
    Note:
    
    The available balance in the bank account is always displayed in the subsidiary's currency, regardless of the bank account currency.
    
4.  (Optional) In the Filters section, select a filter to narrow down the search results.
    
    You can filter the results by project, project status, or both. The results are filtered by all projects and all project statuses by default.
    
5.  Click **Refresh**.
    
    To know more about how to go to the Project A/P - A/R Summary page, read [Navigating the Project A/P - A/R Summary Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158339885730.html).
