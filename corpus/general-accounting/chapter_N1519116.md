The following types of accounting-related reports are described in this section:

-   [Financial Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1520496.html)
    
-   [Purchases Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1527235.html)
    
-   [Payables Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1529262.html)
    
-   [Receivables Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1531120.html)
    

There are row limitations for report results. For more information, see [Row Limitations for Report Results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N719691.html).

Financial statements, including income statements (profit and loss statements), balance sheets, and cash flow statements, are described elsewhere. For information, see [Financial Statements Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2092547.html).
