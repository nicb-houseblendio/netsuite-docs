Each account register lists transactions for a single account. The account registers you can access depend on the types of accounts in your chart of accounts. The following types of account registers may be available to you.

-   [Accounts Payable (A/P) Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1512361.html)
    
-   [Accounts Receivable (A/R) Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1512589.html)
    
-   [Bank Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1512829.html)
    
-   [Cost of Goods Sold Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1514305.html)
    
-   [Credit Card Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1514513.html)
    
-   [Deferred Expense Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1514701.html)
    
-   [Deferred Revenue Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1514906.html)
    
-   [Equity Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1515112.html)
    
-   [Fixed Asset Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1515479.html)
    
-   [Expense Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1515295.html)
    
-   [Income Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1515666.html)
    
-   [Long Term Liability Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1515850.html)
    
-   [Non-Posting Account Registers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1516033.html)
    
-   [Other Asset Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1517602.html)
    
-   [Other Current Asset Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1517786.html)
    
-   [Other Current Liability Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1517969.html)
    
-   [Other Expense Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1518153.html)
    
-   [Other Income Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1518336.html)
    
-   [Unbilled Receivable Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1518520.html)
    
-   [Statistical Account Register](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3872900576.html)
    

You may have access to multiple registers of the same type, depending on how many accounts of that type are included in your chart of accounts.

You also may have access to register reports, which are different from single account registers. Register reports combine data for all registers of a selected type and include more presentation options. See [Register Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1518725.html).
