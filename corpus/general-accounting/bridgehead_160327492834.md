SuiteApprovals lets users approve or reject records through email by enabling and setting up email approval preferences.

Note:

System administrators and users with the Accountant, Accountant (Reviewer), CEO, or CFO roles can do the setup.

For more information, see the following help topics:

-   [Enabling the Email Capture Plug-in for Email Approvals](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_40114848667.html)
