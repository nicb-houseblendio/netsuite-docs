NetSuite provides several ways to view balancing journals:

-   You can view balancing journals immediately after they're created. Click the link in the **Transactions** subtab on the Balancing by Segments Process page.
    
    ![Link in the Transactions subtab on Balancing by Segments Process page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Accounting/GeneralAccounting/BalancingSegmentsProcessTransactionsSubtab.png)
-   You can go to the Balancing Journals list page at _Transactions > Financial > Balancing Segments_, and click **View** for the journal you want to see.
    
-   You can view the balancing journal from the source transaction. Go to the **Balancing by Segments Details** subtab of the **GL Impact** subtab. Then click the link in the **Transaction** column.
    
    ![Balancing by Segments Details subtab of the GL Impact subtab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Accounting/GeneralAccounting/BalancingSegmentsBalancingbySegmentsDetailssubtab.png)

You can't edit balancing journals, but if you have the necessary permissions, you can delete them. For information, see [Deleting Balancing Journals](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156568289807.html).

The header of the Balancing Journal page includes the fields from the Balance Transactions by Segments page, plus the following fields:

-   **Entry Number** - The balancing journal number
    
-   **Created From** - A link to the balancing by segments process that generated the balancing journal
    
-   **Internal ID** - The internal ID of the balancing journal
    

Regular balancing journal lines include a Details link that opens a Balancing Link Default View page from which you can access the source transaction. For balancing journal lines with a Line Type value of Lines Limit Adjustment, transaction-level details or a breakdown by source transaction may not be available.
