Note:

Applicable to NetSuite OneWorld only.

By default, a role's access is restricted to the user's own subsidiary. However, if an employee is assigned a role that has access to the approval rule's subsidiary, that employee can be added as an approver.

You must set corresponding access to subsidiaries for any employee who needs to approve records across subsidiaries.

#### To set an approver's access to subsidiaries: {#procedure_0603041658}

1.  Review the approver's assigned role or roles.
    
    1.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Employees_.
        
    2.  Click the **View** link for the employee record.
        
    3.  Click the **Access** subtab.
        
    4.  Under the **Roles** subtab, review the employee's assigned role or roles.
        
2.  Customize the employee's role or roles to set role access to subsidiaries.
    
    For guidelines and procedures, refer to [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html) and [Restricting Role Access to Subsidiaries (OneWorld Only)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N286421.html).
    
3.  Assign each of the custom roles to the employee. For more information, read [Assigning Roles to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N897798.html).
