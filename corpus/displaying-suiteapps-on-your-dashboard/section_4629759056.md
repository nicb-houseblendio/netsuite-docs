You can use some SuiteApps installed in your account as dashboard portlets. These SuiteApps need a dashboard component to work as a portlet.

To add a SuiteApp portlet to your dashboard, see [SuiteApp Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4629724453.html).

Note:

You can't add SuiteApp portlets to custom dashboards.
