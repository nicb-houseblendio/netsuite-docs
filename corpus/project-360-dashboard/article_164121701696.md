The Project 360 Dashboard SuiteApp provides project managers with consolidated metrics, reports, and resources in one place so they can keep projects on track and within the budget.

You can see all your key metrics and data points in a 360-degree project centric dashboard that pulls together records and reports from across various features of NetSuite. Monitor all your assigned projects, including snippets of performance indicators using a portfolio page, and drill down into focus pages for detailed project insights.

Using the SuiteApp, project managers can:

-   Access and review key performance indicators for one or more projects in one place.
    
-   Filter projects by name, client, project manager, and due date.
    
-   View various information about the primary details of a project.
    
-   Monitor and allocate resources, and see your most important tasks and deliverables.
    
-   Keep track spending against the budget based on your project's current progress.
    
-   Track client invoices and monitor revenue for a project.
    
-   Analyze, gauge, and maintain profitability throughout a project.
    

The Project 360 Dashboard SuiteApp is available in every language that NetSuite supports. For more information, read [Configuring Multiple Languages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N247147.html) and [Choosing a Language for Your NetSuite User Interface](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N479109.html).
