The Timesheet Tracking portlet lets you track weekly timesheets of your project resources and determine the resource capacity. The portlet also helps identify what action is required related to the status of timesheets.

The following screenshot shows the Timesheet portlet with the columns included in the table:

![Project360Timesheets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Projects/Project360TimesheetTracking.png)

The system includes all timesheets associated to the project, and with dates that fall on or after the project's start date.

The Timesheet Tracking portlet displays the following information:

-   **Risk** - This field shows the risk status related to the number of timesheets that are not submitted and with pending approval.
    
    | Risk | Color | Timesheet Not Filled | Timesheet Not Approved |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | High | Red | 3 or more | 3 or more |
    | Medium | Amber | 2 | 2 |
    | Low | Green | 1 | 1 |
    | None | Gray | 0 | 0 |
    
-   **Name** - This field shows the name of each project resource. Clicking the resource name takes you to the corresponding employee or vendor record.
    
-   **Role** - This field shows the designation of each project resource.
    
-   **Not Filled** - This field shows the number of weekly timesheets that the resource has not submitted, and timesheets with approval status of **Open** and **Rejected**.
    
-   **Not Approved** - This field shows the number of weekly timesheets with a status of **Pending Approval**
    

There are two types of approval settings for time records:

-   If you check the **Require Approvals on Time Records** box on the Accounting Preferences page, the employees' time transactions require approval from supervisors. If you don't check this, time transactions can be billed to customers or included in the payroll run without the approval of a supervisor.
    
-   If you also check the **Advanced Approvals on Time Records** box, the **Supervisor Approval** field on time entries and the weekly time sheet is replaced by an **Approval Status** field. This field enables you to set the status of time entries to Open, Pending Approval, Approved, or Rejected, which supervisors, administrators, and time approvers can edit.
    

Set your preferences at _Setup > Accounting > Preferences > Accounting Preferences_. For more information about Time Tracking preferences, see [Time & Expenses Accounting Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1391368.html).

The following table shows how the approval settings affect the timesheet status:

| Require Approvals on Time Records | Advanced Approvals on Time Records | Timesheet Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Disabled | Disabled | The timesheet status is automatically set to **Approved**. |
| Enabled | Disabled | The timesheet status is set to either **Pending Approval** or **Approved**. |
| Enabled | Enabled | The timesheet status can be set to one of the following approval statuses:
-   Open
-   Pending Approval
-   Approved
-   Rejected
