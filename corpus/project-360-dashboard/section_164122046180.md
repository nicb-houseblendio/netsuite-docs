The Billing and Revenue page helps project managers monitor the cash flow and revenue for the project.

By accessing the Billing and Revenue page on the Project 360 Dashboard, you can keep track of the information from the following portlets:

-   [Client Invoice Details Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164172049039.html)
    
-   [Client Contact Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164172060238.html)
    

NetSuite provides flexible billing options for project billing. You can bill projects based on completing project work, achieving project milestones, or according to a schedule of dates. The project billing type determines which method of billing to use. The project billing schedule provides the details for when and how to bill the customer over a range of time. For information about the three types of project billing schedules, see [Project Billing Schedule Types](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1204906.html#bridgehead_N1205076).

The steps in setting up the portlets are described in [Billing and Revenue Portlets Set Up](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_164406917899.html).
