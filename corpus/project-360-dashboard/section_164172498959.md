The Team Details portlet on the Project 360 Dashboard lets you view the project resources, roles, and contact information, which are sourced from the **Resource Details** subtab of the project record.

Employees identified as a project resource, vendors, and generic resources are shown in the Team Details portlet.

The links in the portlet let you do the following:

-   Click the name of the project team resource to view their employee record.
    
-   Click the email address to open a compose email window.
    
-   Click the phone number to call the employee using a contact application supported by your browser.
    
    The phone number shown in the portlet is sourced from the **Phone** field of the employee record.
