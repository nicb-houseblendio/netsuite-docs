A Gantt chart is a bar chart that visually represents your project schedule and clearly illustrates the start, end, milestones, and progress of individual tasks.

To populate the data on the portlet, you need to complete the set up described in [Resource Planning Portlets Set Up](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_164406912729.html).

To view the entire Gantt Chart, you can click the **View Full Screen** from the Gantt Chart portlet, or click **View Gantt Chart** from the Take Action portlet. For more information, see [Gantt Chart](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1202036.html#bridgehead_N1202133).
