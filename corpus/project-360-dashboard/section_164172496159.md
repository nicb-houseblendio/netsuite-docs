The Primary Information portlet shows your project's planned delivery date and comparison of the current EAC budget to the previous EAC budget.

The following screenshot shows the Primary Information portlet and the details included in the information tile:

![Project360PrimaryInfo](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/Projects/Project360DashboardPrimaryInfo.png)

The planned delivery date is sourced from the **Calculated End Date** field of the project record.

The EAC budget here matches what you see in the Budget portlet on the Budget and EAC focus page. See [Budget Tiles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164172047267.html#subsect_165236296494).
