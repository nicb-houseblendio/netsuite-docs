You can create transactions from lead, prospect, and customer records.

#### To create a transaction from a record: {#procedure_N561424}

1.  View the record by clicking View next to a record in a list.
    
2.  Click the ![Create New icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/Records_CreateNewButton.png) menu on the record to open a list of records and transactions that you can create and link to the current record.
    
3.  Select a transaction type. Some transactions types that are commonly available are sales order, invoice, and cash sale.
    

When you create a transaction from a record, the record is automatically linked to the transaction.
