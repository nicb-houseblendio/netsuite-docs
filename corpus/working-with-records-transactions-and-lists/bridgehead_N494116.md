Dynamic groups are defined by search criteria. This means that the membership of a dynamic group changes as new contact records are created that meet your criteria and as existing contact records are edited and no longer meet the group criteria.

To create a dynamic contact group based on customer or other criteria, you should first go to _Lists > Relationships > Contacts > Search_ and create a saved search for the contacts based on fields from customer or other records.

![Saved Contact Search page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/DynamicGroup_Create.png)

#### After you've created your saved search: {#procedure_N494175}

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Groups_ > New, and choose **Dynamic**.
    
2.  In the dropdown list, select **Contact**.
    
3.  Click **Continue**.
    
4.  Enter a name for the group.
    
5.  In the **Saved Search** field, select the saved search you created.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
