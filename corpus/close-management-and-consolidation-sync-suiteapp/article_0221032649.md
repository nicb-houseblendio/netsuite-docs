Clicking a button in the Job Execution Portlet executes the job you assigned to the button, as described in help topic [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Job Execution Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_57201919673.html). To watch the progress of job execution, you need to add the corresponding Execution Logs portlet to the NetSuite EPM page. For instructions, see [Accessing NetSuite EPM Overview Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0221032515.html).

The available Execution Logs portlets include the following:

-   The **NSFC | Job Execution Log 1** portlet for button 1.
    
-   The **NSFC | Job Execution Log 2** portlet for button 2.
    
-   The **NSFC | Job Execution Log 3** portlet for button 3.
