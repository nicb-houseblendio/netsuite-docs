This portlet lets you review values of the Financial Consolidation and Close substitution variables that you selected in NetSuite for monthly maintenance. For information about how you can select substitution variables for monthly maintenance and edit their values, see [Substitution Variables in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_94145226695.html).

For instructions on adding the portlet to the NetSuite EPM page, see [Accessing NetSuite EPM Overview Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0221032515.html).
