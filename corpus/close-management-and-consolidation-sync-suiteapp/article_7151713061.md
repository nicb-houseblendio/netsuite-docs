You can access the following Close Management and Consolidation Sync portlets in NetSuite:

-   To check the current status of your Close Management and Consolidation Sync configuration, or to set it up quickly, you can use the [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Checklist Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0221032717.html).
    
-   For monthly maintenance of your NetSuite account, you can use the following Close Management and Consolidation Sync portlets:
    
    -   [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Job Execution Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_57201919673.html)
        
    -   [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Execution Logs Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0221032649.html)
        
    -   [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Substitution Variables Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0221032553.html)
        
    
    Tip:
    
    Best practices for doing the monthly maintenance on your NetSuite account:
    
    1.  Check the [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Substitution Variables Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0221032553.html) to make sure that all the substitution variables in your Financial Consolidation and Close are up to date.
        
    2.  Run a job from the [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Job Execution Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_57201919673.html).
        
    3.  Watch the progress of the job execution in the corresponding [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Execution Logs Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0221032649.html).
        
    

For information about how you can add or access the portlets, see [Accessing NetSuite EPM Overview Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0221032515.html).
