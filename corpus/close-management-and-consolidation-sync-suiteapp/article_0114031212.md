If you've created or customized datasets as described in the help topic [Managing the Close Management and Consolidation Sync Datasets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0114031054.html), you can view the datasets at NetSuite EPM > Close Management and Consolidation > Manage Datasets. There you can also view all custom datasets that you've created in NetSuite for your Financial Consolidation and Close.

Note:

Every dataset the ID of which includes the **\_nsfc\_** prefix, for example `custdataset_nsfc_sync_item_new`, is listed in Data Exchange.

On each dataset in the list, you can take any of the following actions:

-   You can customize the hierarchy of metadata you want to export in the dataset.
    
    See [Customization of Close Management and Consolidation Sync Datasets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0114031532.html).
    
-   You can manually export and review the hierarchy of metadata in the dataset.
    
    See [Manual Export of Dataset Metadata in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0114031556.html).
    
-   You can click the dataset to open and customize it directly in NetSuite.
    
    For instructions on how you can customize the dataset, see [Customizing a Created Close Management and Consolidation Sync Dataset](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0114031152.html).
