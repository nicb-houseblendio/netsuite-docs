Before installing the Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp, you must enable the features required by the SuiteApp in your NetSuite account.

#### To enable the required features:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **CRM** subtab.
    
3.  Under the Basic Features section, check the **Customer Relationship Management** box.
    
4.  Click the **SuiteCloud** subtab, and check the following boxes:
    
    -   **Custom Records**
        
    -   **Client SuiteScript**
        
    -   **Server SuiteScript**
        
    -   **Token-based Authentication**
        
    
    Note:
    
    To use single sign-on when navigating to your Close Management and Consolidation from your NetSuite account, you must configure the appropriate single sign-on feature.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
