To check the integration status, you can create saved searches from the available Close Management and Consolidation Sync record types. For example, you can create a saved search that daily sends you an email with a list of jobs that failed during a specified time period. You can also create a saved search that collects execution logs for you.

The applicable Close Management and Consolidation Sync record types include the following:

-   NSFC | Event Logs
    
-   NSFC | Jobs
    
-   NSFC | Job Execution Log
    

#### To create a saved search from a record type:

1.  Go to _Lists > Search > Saved Searches > New_.
    
2.  Click the record type of your choice.
    
    Note:
    
    In NetSuite, the terms 'jobs' and 'projects' are used interchangeably. Therefore, NetSuite displays NSFC | Projects and NSFC | Project Execution Log for the NSFC | Jobs and NSFC | Job Execution Log record types.
    
3.  Enter a title for the search.
    
4.  Make any other necessary changes, and click **Save**.
