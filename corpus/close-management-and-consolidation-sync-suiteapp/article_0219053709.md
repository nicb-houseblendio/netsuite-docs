When you import Subsidiary metadata to your Financial Consolidation and Close, the Subsidiary metadata hierarchy is built with the following logic:

-   If a subsidiary from NetSuite has a parent, the parent of the subsidiary is used in your Financial Consolidation and Close as the parent.
    
-   If a subsidiary from NetSuite doesn't have a parent, the top-level subsidiary is used in your Financial Consolidation and Close as the parent.
    
    Note:
    
    You can customize the top-level hierarchy at NetSuite EPM > Close Management and Consolidation > Manage Saved Searches. See [Customization of Executed Saved Searches in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_27204037129.html).
