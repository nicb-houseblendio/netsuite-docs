Before you export the Accounts metadata from NetSuite, check the **Use Account Numbers** preference box by going to Setup > Accounting > Accounting Preferences.

When you import the Accounts metadata to your Financial Consolidation and Close, and an account from NetSuite doesn't have an account number, the account name is used instead of the account number.

Important:

Read the following details about loading the Accounts metadata:

-   To successfully load Accounts metadata into your Financial Consolidation and Close, make sure you follow instructions in the help topic [Loading Account Metadata into Financial Consolidation and Close](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0311112432.html).
    
-   To customize the hierarchy of the Accounts metadata, consider checking or clearing the **Exclude Upper Level Hierarchy Members** box at NetSuite EPM > Close Management and Consolidation > Manage Saved Searches. For instructions, see [Customization of Executed Saved Searches in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_27204037129.html).
