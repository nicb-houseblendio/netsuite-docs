The NSEPM Implementer role is included in the installed SuiteApp, and has all the necessary permissions to get you started. If you need to customize the NSEPM Implementer role to better suit your needs, you can assign your specific permissions to it.

Important:

The NSEPM Implementer role shouldn't be cloned. A cloned role may not have access to all NetSuite EPM pages.

#### To customize the NSEPM Implementer role:

1.  In NetSuite, go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_.
    
2.  In the list of roles, click **Edit** next to the NSEPM Implementer role.
    
3.  On the **Permissions** subtab, add the required permissions one by one, including any of the following:
    
    1.  (Optional) If you need to rename records and transactions at Setup > Company > Rename Records/Transactions, do the following:
        
        1.  Click the **Setup** subtab.
            
        2.  For the **Set Up Company** permission, change the access level to **Full**.
            
    2.  If you are using a NetSuite OneWorld account, you need to add the following permissions:
        
        1.  Click the **Lists** subtab, and add the following permissions:
            
            -   **Projects**
                
            -   **Subsidiaries**
                
        2.  Click the **Setup** subtab, and add the **Accounting Preferences** permission.
            
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Tip:

The best practice is only to add the missing permissions and avoid removing any permissions.

Important:

Read the following details about managing permissions for the NSEPM Implementer role:

-   If you remove a permission from the NSEPM Implementer role, your changes can be overridden with the next upgrade of your installed SuiteApp.
    
-   With every new version of the Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp, new permissions may be added to the NSEPM Implementer role. If you're using a cloned or custom role, you should compare the permissions in your role with the permissions in the updated NSEPM Implementer role. To make sure the integration works properly for you, add the new permissions to the role you are using. For instructions, see [Managing User Role Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1023031026.html).
