To connect a user to Financial Consolidation and Close from NetSuite, the Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp supports two types of authentication:

-   Authentication by entering a username and a password.
    
    For information, see [Using Basic Authentication to Connect to Financial Consolidation and Close](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0212112442.html).
    
-   OAuth 2.0 that eliminates the need to store user credentials in NetSuite. This provides better security and prevents integration issues when a password expires or is changed.
    
    For information, see [Using OAuth 2.0 to Connect to Financial Consolidation and Close](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0212112713.html).
    

#### To choose your preferred authentication method:

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Close Management and Consolidation > Connection Settings.
    
2.  In the **Close Management and Consolidation URL** field, enter the URL that points to the Financial Consolidation and Close application you want NetSuite to connect to.
    
    Depending on the Financial Consolidation and Close instance, the format of the URL may look similar to one of the following examples:
    
    -   `https://epm-test-idDomain.epm.dataCenterRegion.oraclecloud.com/`
        
    -   `https://acme-epmidm.epm.us-phoenix-1.ocs.oraclecloud.com/`
        
    
    To check or understand the URL components, see the Oracle Help Center topic [Sample URLs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/enterprise-performance-management-common/cgsad/1_epm_cloud_accessing_sample_urls.html).
    
3.  In the **Close Management and Consolidation Application Name** field, enter the name of your Financial Consolidation and Close environment. For example, NetSuite.
    
4.  To verify that the Financial Consolidation and Close application identified in the **Close Management and Consolidation URL** field connects to this NetSuite account, check the **Use Entered URL to Verify Inbound Connections** box.
    
    Tip:
    
    To avoid connection issues or troubleshoot:
    
    -   Go to NetSuite EPM > EPM Connector > Search Activity Logs, and then compare the `sourceinstance` value in the **Execution Parameters** column with the **Close Management and Consolidation URL** value.
        
    -   Check the integration user's NetSuite Account ID entered in the **Account** field during token-based authentication setup in Data Exchange. See step 14.g in [Setting Up Token-based Authentication for Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6005307189.html).
        
    
5.  Choose your preferred authentication method from the available options:
    
    1.  **Basic Auth**
        
        Important:
        
        This option displays a box that you must check to confirm your understanding that you should use basic authentication only for testing purposes or when OAuth 2.0 is unavailable. Until you check the displayed box, you can't start using basic authentication as your preferred authentication method.
        
        For configuration instructions, see [Using Basic Authentication to Connect to Financial Consolidation and Close](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0212112442.html).
        
    2.  **OAuth 2.0**
        
        For configuration instructions, see [Using OAuth 2.0 to Connect to Financial Consolidation and Close](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0212112713.html).
        
6.  Click **Save Active Connection** to save your preferred authentication method.
