If you want to use NetSuite to schedule and run a business rule configured in your Financial Consolidation and Close, you need to add the business rule in NetSuite. Then, you can set up the business rule as needed.

#### To add and set up a business rule:

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Close Management and Consolidation > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  On the Manage Jobs page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to a business rule of your choice.
        
    -   If you don't see the business rule in the list, click the **Add Job** button, and follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_54163337626.html).
        
3.  Click the **Job Parameters** subtab.
    
    Enter the **Key** and **Value** of a business rule defined in your Financial Consolidation and Close. The field values must match exactly, including upper and lower case. Repeat if needed to create a list of parameters.
    
4.  If you want to schedule the business rule, click the **Schedule** subtab, and follow step 3 in [Scheduling a Job in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_85163503218.html).
    
5.  Click **Save**.
