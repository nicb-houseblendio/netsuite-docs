The Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp is a shared SuiteApp. Before you can install the SuiteApp, it must be provisioned for your NetSuite account. For more information, contact your NetSuite account manager.

To install the Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp, you must have permission and access to the SuiteApp Marketplace. For information about the SuiteApp Marketplace, see [SuiteApp Marketplace in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1539797981.html).

#### To install the Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp:

1.  In NetSuite, click the **SuiteApps** tab.
    
2.  In the **Search SuiteApps** field, enter **Close Management and Consolidation Sync**.
    
3.  Click the **Close Management and Consolidation Sync** tile.
    
4.  In the upper-right area of the Close Management and Consolidation Sync details page, click **Install**.
    
    Note:
    
    If you can see **Limited Access** instead of the **Install** button, it means that the SuiteApp has not been provisioned for your NetSuite account. For assistance, contact your NetSuite account manager.
    
5.  Wait for the installation to complete.
    

Close Management and Consolidation Sync is a managed SuiteApp. When a new version is available, NetSuite automatically updates your account.

For general information about installing, updating, and deleting SuiteApps from the SuiteApp Marketplace, see [SuiteApp Installation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1540236668.html).
