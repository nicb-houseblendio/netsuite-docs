When you import the Customer metadata to your Financial Consolidation and Close, the Parent Company value is used as the parent of the customer. If the Parent Company value is empty, the value from the EPM Consolidation Customer Category field is used as the parent.

#### To set the EPM Consolidation Customer Category to represent the parent company:

1.  In NetSuite, go to _Lists > Relationships > Customers_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to a customer of your choice.
    
3.  Click the **Custom** subtab.
    
4.  From the **EPM Consolidation Customer Category** list, select a value to represent the parent company.
    

Customers in NetSuite can use EPM Consolidation Customer Category values to group customers into different categories and create their own custom hierarchy. When you import Customer metadata to your Financial Consolidation and Close, the EPM Consolidation Customer Category values are used to build the Customer metadata hierarchy.

You can define the EPM Consolidation Customer Category on a customer record under the **Custom** subtab.

The Customer metadata hierarchy is built with the following logic:

-   If a customer from NetSuite has a parent, the parent of the customer is used in your Financial Consolidation and Close as the parent.
    
-   If the customer from NetSuite doesn't have a parent, but has an EPM Consolidation Customer Category, the EPM Consolidation Customer Category is used in your Financial Consolidation and Close as the parent.
    
-   If a customer from NetSuite has neither a parent nor an EPM Consolidation Customer Category, the top-level customer is used in your Financial Consolidation and Close as the parent.
    

On Customer metadata saved search records, the **Stage** filter under the **Criteria** subtab is set to **is Customer**. This ensures that Customer metadata saved searches executed from Data Exchange return only customers.

![The setup of the Stage filter to is Customer](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/CloseManagementAndConsolidation/NSFCCustSavedSearch_Stage.png)

If you disable the CRM feature, the Stage filter is no longer available to ensure that only customers are returned. For information about how to enable the CRM feature, see [Required Features for Installing the Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9235947031.html). For general instructions on how to customize a saved search, see [Customizing a Close Management and Consolidation Sync Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0114020746.html).
