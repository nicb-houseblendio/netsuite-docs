If you want to import journal entries from your Financial Consolidation and Close into NetSuite, you need to add the corresponding Import Enterprise Journal job in NetSuite. Then, you can set up the job as needed.

#### To add and set up an Import Enterprise Journal job:

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Close Management and Consolidation > Manage Jobs.
    
2.  On the Manage Jobs page, do one of the following:
    
    -   In the list of jobs, click **Edit** next to the Import Enterprise Journal job of your choice.
        
    -   If you don't see the job in the list, click the **Add Job** button, and follow steps 3 through 6 in [Adding a Job in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_54163337626.html).
        
3.  To import journal entries from your Financial Consolidation and Close for a specific period, click the **Job Parameters** subtab. Then, from the **Period Type** list, select one of the following time frames:
    
    -   **Relative** to specify the period relative to the current date.
        
        Important:
        
        Ensure that the periods configured in Data Exchange match those defined in NetSuite at Setup > Accounting > Manage Accounting Periods. For information about managing accounting periods, see [Manage Accounting Periods Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1445839.html).
        
        To select the period for which you want to retrieve the journal entries, the following options are available in the **Period** list:
        
        -   **This Period**
            
        -   **Last Period**
            
        -   **Period Before Last**
            
    -   **Absolute** to select a specific month from the **Period** list.
        
    -   **All** to import journal entries that are in the Ready to Post status in your Financial Consolidation and Close.
        
4.  From the **Posting Status** list, select **Ready to Post**.
    
5.  To schedule the Import Enterprise Journal job, click the **Schedule** subtab, and follow step 3 in [Scheduling a Job in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_85163503218.html).
    
6.  For optional setup of the Import Enterprise Journal job, click the **Options** subtab.
    
    From the **Post-Processing Plug-ins** list, select the plug-in script that you've customized as described in the help topic [Importing Enterprise Journals into NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_90220328431.html).
    
    The field is set to **None** by default.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
    

For information about Enterprise Journals in Oracle Cloud Enterprise Performance Management, see the Oracle Help Center topic [Managing Enterprise Journals](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/financial-consolidation-cloud/agfcc/ent_journals_managing_journals.html).
