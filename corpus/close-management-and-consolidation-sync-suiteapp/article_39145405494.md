For your convenience, you can move the substitution variables into custom categories that you have created in NetSuite. You can also create translations for categories.

#### To create and customize categories:

1.  Go to _Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Lists_.
    
2.  Click **NSFC | Sub Var Category**.
    
3.  On the **Values** subtab, add your custom categories one by one.
    
4.  If you have the Multi-Language feature enabled in your NetSuite account, click in the **Translation** column to enter translations for categories.
    
    For instructions on enabling the Multi-Language feature and setting up your preferred language, see [Enabling Multiple Languages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160063618979.html#subsect_158136409617), [Configuring Multiple Languages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160063618979.html#subsect_160063743343), and [Choosing a Language for Your NetSuite User Interface](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N479109.html).
    
5.  When finished, click **Save**.
    
    You can then see your custom and translated categories at NetSuite EPM > Close Management and Consolidation > Substitution Variables. To split the substitution variables into these categories, proceed as described in [Categorizing the Substitution Variables in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_98145334778.html).
