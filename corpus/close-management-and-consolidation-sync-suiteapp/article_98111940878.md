To install and set up the Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp, you must have the Administrator role and complete the following consecutive steps:

1.  Make sure you have the NetSuite EPM Connector SuiteApp installed and set up properly for your Financial Consolidation and Close.
    
    See [NetSuite EPM Connector Installation and Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0095112426.html).
    
2.  Enable the required features in your NetSuite account.
    
    See [Required Features for Installing the Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9235947031.html).
    
3.  Install the Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp.
    
    See [Installing the Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_3205417852.html).
    
4.  Assign roles to Close Management and Consolidation Sync users.
    
    See [Role Permissions for Close Management and Consolidation Sync Users](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5000447485.html).
    
5.  Create an integration user with proper permissions.
    
    See [Setting Up the Integration User for Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_4234457601.html).
    
6.  Set up token-based authentication (TBA) for Financial Consolidation and Close users.
    
    See [Setting Up Token-based Authentication for Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6005307189.html).
    
7.  Set up the connection to your Financial Consolidation and Close.
    
    See [Setting Up the Connection to Financial Consolidation and Close](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_3232205961.html).
    
8.  Schedule the required Close Management and Consolidation Sync script deployments.
    
    See [Scheduling Script Deployments for Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_79171702538.html).
    
9.  Use a checklist of tasks to validate your Close Management and Consolidation Sync setup.
    
    See [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Configuration Checklist](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7230647698.html).
    

After the SuiteApp is configured, you can use the following functionality provided by Close Management and Consolidation Sync:

-   Create and customize the saved searches that are included in the SuiteApp, or create new saved searches to use in your Financial Consolidation and Close. You can also create saved searches from the available custom record types.
    
    See [Managing the Close Management and Consolidation Saved Searches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5122158780.html).
    
-   Customize metadata saved searches included in the SuiteApp, and call RESTlets from Data Exchange to automatically export metadata from NetSuite entities to your Financial Consolidation and Close.
    
    See [Metadata Export to Financial Consolidation and Close](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_54133214582.html).
    
-   Customize upper-level members of account metadata that you want to export from NetSuite.
    
    See [Account Metadata Hierarchy in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7225241928.html).
    
-   Extract data from SuiteAnalytics datasets that you have created in NetSuite.
    
    See [Using SuiteAnalytics Datasets with Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_8150739241.html).
    
-   Define SuiteQL queries to load data and metadata from NetSuite into your Financial Consolidation and Close.
    
    See [Managing SuiteQL Queries in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5165545662.html).
    
-   Use your NetSuite account to access and manage features in your Financial Consolidation and Close account.
    
    See the following help topics:
    
    -   [Managing Jobs in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9163303650.html)
        
    -   [Substitution Variables in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_94145226695.html)
        
    -   [Saved Search Settings in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_3002017224.html)
        
-   Exchange data between NetSuite and your Financial Consolidation and Close.
    
    See [Integration Artifacts in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_80082208674.html).
    
-   Use portlets for maintenance and access to Close Management and Consolidation Sync features.
    
    See [Portlets for Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7151713061.html).
