To enable the integration with your Close Management and Consolidation, you need to set up an integration user with proper permissions in NetSuite. You can assign the **FCCS Integration** role to an existing or new user, and use that user for the integration. If you need to customize the FCCS Integration role to better suit your needs, you can assign your specific permissions to it.

For instructions, see the following help topics:

-   [Setting Up the Integration User Record for Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0212114725.html)
    
-   [Customizing the FCCS Integration Role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0212114843.html)
    

Note:

The FCCS Integration role, or your custom role in NetSuite, gives sufficient permissions only to the integration user. Your Close Management and Consolidation roles and permissions are managed in your Close Management and Consolidation environment.
