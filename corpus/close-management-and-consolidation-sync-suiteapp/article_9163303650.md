The integration lets you use NetSuite to schedule and run jobs configured in Data Exchange and your Financial Consolidation and Close.

Important:

When you schedule a job, it's executed using the account's settings, including language. If you run a job manually through the UI, it uses the user's settings, including the user's language preference.

On each job listed at NetSuite EPM > Close Management and Consolidation > Manage Jobs, you can take one of the following actions:

| Action Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | You can click this link to schedule the job execution. For information, see [Scheduling a Job in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_85163503218.html). Important: Some NetSuite accounts don't support scheduled jobs. This includes some, but not all, test drive accounts (that is, accounts where the account ID begins with TSTDRV). If that's your case, the scheduling of job execution won't work for you. For additional setup information, see [Job Types in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_95163533223.html). |
| **Execute** | Clicking this link takes you to a page where you can manually run the job and watch the progress of the job execution. See [Executing a Job in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0709102524.html). |
| **Logs** | Click this link to access information about an executed job or to troubleshoot. See [Viewing and Troubleshooting Job Execution Logs in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9130119275.html). |

Click **Refresh** to reload the page and get the current status from Data Exchange or your Financial Consolidation and Close.

For each job in the list, the **Schedule** column shows the frequency of job execution. The **Job Status** column shows whether the job is Scheduled, Not Scheduled, or has Started.

For each job in the list, the **Execution Status** column shows if the job was successfully executed or not. The possible execution statuses are:

-   **Success** - The job was successfully executed.
    
-   **Error** - The job failed. In a group of jobs, a job in the sequence failed or was manually reset.
    
-   **In Progress** - The job execution hasn't yet been completed.
    
-   **Warning** - The job hasn't processed all data successfully and requires your attention.
    
-   **Plugin Error** - The job uses a plug-in script that is erroneous. The column displays Success if the job is executed successfully in your Financial Consolidation and Close and the plug-in is executed successfully in NetSuite.
    
-   **Plugin Executing**\- The job uses a plug-in that is being processed.
    
-   **Pending Plugin Execution**\- The job uses a plug-in that is waiting to be processed.
    

Important:

The execution status is only updated for jobs that you've executed from NetSuite. If you run a job outside of NetSuite, the status of the job doesn't show in the Execution Status column.
