You can map any item type from your NetSuite account to any item type in your Financial Consolidation and Close.

#### To create the mapping:

1.  Go to _Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types_.
    
2.  In the list of record types, locate the **NSFC | Item Type Mapping** record type.
    
3.  Click **New Record** for the record type.
    
4.  From the **Source Item Type** list, select the item type you want to map from NetSuite to your Financial Consolidation and Close.
    
5.  In the **Target Item Type** field, enter the name of the item type in your Financial Consolidation and Close you want to map to.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
    Note:
    
    If you create a duplicate mapping record, the latter record is used in the generated saved search result.
    
7.  Click **List** in the upper-right corner of the page to view the mapping you have created.
