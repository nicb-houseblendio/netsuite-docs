The Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp delivers default configurations to integrate NetSuite and your Financial Consolidation and Close application in Oracle Cloud Enterprise Performance Management (EPM).

Through the use of Data Exchange in your Financial Consolidation and Close application, you can run searches defined in NetSuite and use the results in your Financial Consolidation and Close.

For information about Financial Consolidation and Close, and about using Data Exchange for the integration with NetSuite, see the following Oracle Help Center topics:

-   [Oracle EPM Financial Consolidation and Close](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/financial-consolidation-cloud/)
    
-   [Administering Data Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/enterprise-performance-management-common/diepm/epm_coe_guide.html)
    

For information about how you can install and configure Close Management and Consolidation Sync, and about the functionality of Close Management and Consolidation Sync, see [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Installation and Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_98111940878.html).

For information about changes to the Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp with each release, see [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Release Notes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_54104649820.html).
