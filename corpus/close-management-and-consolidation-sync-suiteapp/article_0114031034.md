The Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp includes the following predefined metadata datasets:

-   FCCS Sync (Metadata) Balance Sheet Accounts
    
-   FCCS Sync (Metadata) Class
    
-   FCCS Sync (Metadata) Customer
    
-   FCCS Sync (Metadata) Department
    
-   FCCS Sync (Metadata) Income Statement Accounts
    
-   FCCS Sync (Metadata) Item
    
-   FCCS Sync (Metadata) Location
    
-   FCCS Sync (Metadata) Subsidiary
    
-   FCCS Sync (Metadata) Vendor
    

You can create, review, or customize the datasets as described in help topics [Managing the Close Management and Consolidation Sync Datasets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0114031054.html) and [Dataset Settings in Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0114031212.html).
