Important:

You shouldn't use the NSEPM Implementer role for integration with your Close Management and Consolidation. To integrate with Close Management and Consolidation, set up the integration user record as described in the help topic [Setting Up the Integration User for Close Management and Consolidation Sync](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_4234457601.html).

Tip:

The best practice is to use the NSEPM Implementer role with new installations of the Close Management and Consolidation Sync SuiteApp.
