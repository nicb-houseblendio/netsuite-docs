If you go to NetSuite EPM > NetSuite EPM Overview, you can access the following portlets:

-   [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Substitution Variables Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0221032553.html)
    
-   [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Job Execution Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_57201919673.html)
    
-   [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Execution Logs Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0221032649.html)
    
-   [Close Management and Consolidation Sync Checklist Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0221032717.html)
    

You need to add these portlets manually to the NetSuite EPM page. You can then customize the setup and layout of the portlets on the page.

#### To access the NetSuite EPM Overview portlets:

1.  Go to NetSuite EPM > NetSuite EPM Overview.
    
2.  Click the **Personalize** link in the upper-right corner of the page.
    
3.  On the **Standard Content** tab, click **Custom Portlet**.
    
4.  In the Custom Content portlet's menu, click **Set Up**.
    
5.  From the **Source** list, select the name of the portlet you want to appear on the page.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
