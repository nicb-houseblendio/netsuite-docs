If a saved search is in the **Created** status, you can no longer select it for submission. To use the latest release version of the saved search, you need to delete the saved search and recreate it in NetSuite.

#### To recreate a Close Management and Consolidation Sync saved search:

1.  In NetSuite, go to NetSuite EPM > Close Management and Consolidation > Manage Saved Searches.
    
2.  In the upper-right corner of the page, click **Create Default Saved Searches**.
    
3.  From the **Saved Search Group** list, select the saved search group containing the saved search you want to recreate. The following are the available saved search groups:
    
    -   **FCCS - Metadata Saved Searches**
        
    -   **Miscellaneous Saved Searches**
        
4.  Click the link to a saved search in the **Created** status.
    
5.  On the saved search record page, under **Actions**, click **Delete**.
    
6.  At the prompt to confirm the action, click **OK**.
    
7.  Go back to NetSuite EPM > Close Management and Consolidation > Manage Saved Searches.
    
    In the list of saved searches, the status of the saved search changes to **Not Created**.
    
8.  Check the **Select** box next to the saved search.
    
9.  Click **Submit**.
    
    In the list of saved searches, the status of the saved search changes to **Created**.
    

You can click the created saved search to review or customize it. To customize the saved search, see the help topic [Customizing a Close Management and Consolidation Sync Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0114020746.html).
