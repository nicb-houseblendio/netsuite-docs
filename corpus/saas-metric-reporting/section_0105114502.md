The ARR, MRR, CARR, and CMRR roll forward report helps you monitor and analyze recurring revenue trends. Track monthly and yearly changes in revenue including the impact of new sales, upsells, downsells, and churn.

The report shows starting and ending balances for each period and provides both summary and transaction-level insights. The roll forward report is helpful for companies with subscriptions or regular billing.

For information about the detail report, see [Recurring Revenue Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0216011521.html).

## Roll Forward Report Key Components {#subsect_33142338026}

The roll forward report includes the following key components:

-   **Data sources** - Recurring revenue transactions, including new sales, upsells, downsells, churn, and balances.
    
-   **Visualization** - Summary tables and detailed transaction views of revenue changes, starting and ending balances, and revenue movement types.
    
-   **Filters** - Metric (ARR, MRR, CARR, and CMRR), subsidiary, customer, item, department, class, and location.
    
-   **Reporting periods** - Start and end periods for analyzing revenue changes over time, up to 36 months.
    
-   **Drill-down capability** - Summary amounts with clickable links to transaction-level details for each revenue movement.
    
-   **Export options** - CSV export of summary and detailed data for further analysis.
    

## Access Requirements {#subsect_0202121338}

To drill down into the roll forward reports, you must have permissions for the following:

-   Analytical Impact
    
-   Currency
    
-   Subscription Metrics
    
-   Transactions
    

Revenue KPI reports require additional permissions for the following:

-   Cash Sale
    
-   Credit Memo
    
-   Invoice
    

To view reports with more than 100,000 rows, you need access to SuiteAnalytics Connect. For more information, see [SuiteAnalytics Connect](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0320025211.html#subsect_0320025519).

Note:

When generating the report, note the following:

-   Reports include subscription records with inactive items if they match your selected subsidiary, customer, class, department, or location. Inactive items can't be filtered through the **Item** filter.
    
-   If you encounter errors during CSV import, try reducing data size using the dashboard filters.
    

#### To view the roll forward reports:

1.  Go to Subscription Metrics > Revenue Metrics > View.
    
2.  From the **Metric** filter, select a metric.
    
3.  (Optional) Apply filters to refine your results.
    
4.  Select the start period and end period you want to review.
    
5.  Review the summary report and drill down into specific amounts for transaction-level details. You can export details to CSV for further analysis.
