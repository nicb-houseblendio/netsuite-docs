NetSuite won't create analytical impact records for transactions or subscriptions in the following statuses, even for historical records.

| Transaction Type | Statuses Preventing Analytical Impact Creation |
| --- | --- |
| Sales Order | 
-   Pending Approval
-   Cancelled
-   Undefined

 |
| Invoice | 

-   Pending Approval
-   Rejected
-   Undefined

 |
| Subscription Line | 

-   Draft
-   Pending Activation
-   Not Included

 |
| Cash Sale | 

-   Unapproved Payment
-   Undefined

 |
