A KPI scorecard gives you a clear, real-time view of KPIs related to revenue and growth. The scorecard consolidates critical metrics such as MRR, ARR, CMRR, CARR, and customer growth. Use this information to make informed, data-driven decisions and improve business performance.

## Revenue KPI Scorecard Key Components {#subsect_0129042324}

The revenue KPI scorecard includes the following key components:

-   **Data sources** - Subscriptions, contracts, and billing information from NetSuite.
    
-   **Visualization** - KPI tiles for recurring revenue, bookings, growth rate, and customer growth rate. Summary tables show KPI values, variance, and percentage variance.
    
-   **Filters** - Metric (ARR, MRR, CARR, and CMRR), subsidiary, customer, item, department, class, and location.
    
-   **Reporting periods** - Period and comparison period to analyze and compare results. To set up the period and comparison period, see [Period and Comparison Period Options](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0213040508.html).
    

## KPI Definitions {#subsect_0129042437}

The following table lists the KPI definitions and what each KPI reveals.

| KPI | Definition | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue | Total income recognized from active subscriptions or service contracts during the selected period. You can click the Revenue value to open a drilldown report. For more information, see [KPI Drilldown Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0422024252.html). | Shows overall business earnings and highlights changes in financial performance over time. |
| Total Contract Value (TCV) | Full value of a customer contract, including all recurring and one-time charges, over the contract's entire term. | Indicates the total financial commitment from customers and helps assess the value of new deals. |
| Lifetime Value (LTV) | Estimated total revenue expected from a customer throughout their relationship with your business. You can click the LTV value to open a drilldown report. For more information, see [KPI Drilldown Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0422024252.html). | Reveals long-term customer profitability and supports decisions on customer acquisition and retention. |
| Growth Rate | Percentage increase or decrease in revenue over the selected period. You can click the Growth Rate value to open a drilldown report. For more information, see [KPI Drilldown Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0422024252.html). | Highlights the pace of business expansion or contraction and helps identify revenue trends. |
| Customer Growth Rate | Percentage change in the number of paying customers during the selected period. | Shows whether growth is driven by acquiring new customers or retaining existing ones. |

The scorecard updates based on your selections and access permissions.

#### To view the revenue KPI scorecard:

1.  Go to Subscription Metrics > Revenue Metrics > View.
    
2.  From the **Metric** filter, select a metric.
    
3.  (Optional) Apply filters to refine your results.
    
4.  Choose the period and comparison period to view current and earlier values for each KPI.
    
5.  Review the KPI tiles for key numbers.
    
6.  Check the summary table for values, variance, and percentage variance.
    
7.  Click the **Revenue** amount in the table to open a detailed, transaction-level report.
    
    You can export this report to CSV for further analysis.
    

For deeper analysis, explore other dashboard portlets such as roll forward reports and graphs.
