The Average Selling Price (ASP) versus Annualized Average Contract Value (AACV) portlet gives you a clear, actionable view of how your ASP and AACV change over time. The dashboard displays trend graphs for both metrics, so you can compare and analyze them in one place.

## ASP and AACV Graph Key Components {#subsect_45143149036}

The magic number graph includes the following key components:

-   **Data sources** - Subscriptions, contracts, and billing data from NetSuite.
    
-   **Visualization** - Line graphs for ASP and ACV across your selected period.
    
-   **Filters** - Metric (ARR, MRR, CARR, and CMRR ), customer, item, department, class, and location.
    
-   **Reporting periods** - Select start and end periods, and choose to view data by month, quarter, or year.
    

## Key Terms and Definitions {#subsect_27143154007}

The following table lists the key terms and their definitions.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Average Selling Price (ASP) | The average value of each sale. Helps you track pricing trends and sales effectiveness. |
| Annualized Average Contract Value (AACV) | The average annual value of customer contracts. Shows how much revenue each contract brings in on average. |

#### To view the ASP and AACV comparison chart:

1.  Go to Subscription Metrics > Unit Economics > View.
    
2.  From the **Metric** filter, select a metric.
    
3.  (Optional) Apply filters to refine your results.
    
4.  Select the reporting period (month, quarter, or year).
    
5.  Set the start and end dates for your analysis.
    
6.  Review the line graphs for key insights.
    

For deeper analysis, explore other dashboard tools and reports.
