You can rename any SaaS Metric record. You can also add custom fields to SaaS metric records. For more information, see the [Custom Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2826978.html) help topic.

To rename a SaaS Metric record:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Rename Records/Transactions.
    
2.  In the **Name for SaaS Metric** field, edit as desired.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
