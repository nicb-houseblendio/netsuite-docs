SaaS Metric Reporting brings together data from various sources, turning transactional amounts into consistent recurring values. This helps create a unified reporting solution across NetSuite. You can also import Analytical Impact records using CSV files for transactions not originally created in NetSuite. SaaS Metric Reporting matches similar transactions by date and item to track all renewals.

-   To search for SaaS Metric records, go to Reports > New Search > SaaS Metric.
    
-   To search for Analytical Impact records, go to Lists > SaaS Metric > Analytical Impacts.
