Use the Preferences tab in Subscription Metrics > Setup to define preferences used across Subscription Metrics.

## Setting the Default Metric {#subsect_0206030754}

The default metric set in the Preferences screen will be the default view used in dashboards. You can still manually change the metric used on the dashboard filters, but the metric will revert to the default when you exit the dashboard.

#### To set the default metric:

1.  Log in to NetSuite.
    
2.  On the **Subscription Metrics** tab, go to **Setup**, and click **View**.
    
3.  Click **Preferences**.
    
4.  Select the desired metric in **Default Metric**.
    

## Setting the Default Period {#subsect_0206030829}

By default, the period setting is set to the current Fiscal Year (FY). You can also set the period to YTM (Year to Month) and TTM (Trailing 12 Months). The selected period affects the start and end periods used in the monthly period type.

#### To set the default period:

1.  Log in to NetSuite.
    
2.  On the **Subscription Metrics** tab, go to **Setup**, and click **View**.
    
3.  Click **Preferences**.
    
4.  Select the **Period**.
    

The following examples illustrate how the period setting considers months. In these examples, the dashboard is viewed on August, 29, 2025 and the fiscal year is January to December:

| Period | Values | Comparison Period | Values |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Year to Month (YTM) | January 2025 - July 2025 | Last Year to Month | January 2024 - July 2024 |
| Trailing Twelve Months (TTM) | August 2024 - July 2025 | Last Trailing Twelve Months | August 2023 - July 2024 |

For portlets with the Period, Start Period, and End Period selection, the selected period affects the Start Period and End Period for Monthly as follows:

| Period | Start Period | End Period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Year to Month (YTM) | January 2025 | July 2025 |
| Trailing Twelve Months (TTM) | August 2024 | July 2025 |

## Setting the Lifetime Value (LTV) Cap {#subsect_0504042126}

You can set the LTV Cap to limit the customer lifetime used in LTV-related calculations so that results stay realistic and aligned to your planning horizon. Setting the LTV cap keeps LTV from being overstated when the churn is too low or volatile.

#### To set the LTV Cap:

This will be used as the maximum lifetime assumption for LTV-related metrics, and will affect the KPIs, LTV and LTV:CAC. If your churn rate suggests a customer lifetime longer than this cap, the cap set here will be used instead.

1.  Log in to NetSuite.
    
2.  On the **Subscription Metrics** tab, go to **Setup**, and click **View**.
    
3.  Click **Preferences**.
    
4.  Enter the desired LTV limit in months in **LTV Cap**.
