In NetSuite, analytical impact and SaaS metric records may be automatically deleted under certain conditions. Here's when and how deletion can happen.

The system may delete records if:

-   The source transaction or transaction line is deleted.
    
-   The associated subscription or subscription line is deleted.
    
-   The status of a transaction changes from Pending Fulfillment/Pending Billing to Pending Approval.
    
-   The subscription line status is updated from Active to Pending Activation.
    
-   The transaction is voided or cancelled.
    
-   The start or end date of the source transaction is cleared (both dates must be present unless a billing schedule exists).
    
-   You manually delete records that were imported by CSV (auto-generated records can't be deleted manually).
    

Removal of related records:

-   If you clear the SaaS Metric Tracking field for an item, the system removes all linked analytical impact and SaaS Metric records.
    
-   If the transaction or subscription line doesn't fall within the SaaS Metric Start and End dates, the system removes related records.
    

Note:

When you delete an analytical impact record, all related SaaS Metric records are also deleted automatically.
