The Net New Trend portlet lets you track changes in recurring revenue over time. You can see how new sales, upsells, downsells, and churn impact revenue by month, quarter, or year. The bar chart displays each revenue category over time. Use this chart to identify factors that drive growth or signal concern.

## Net New Trend Graph Key Components {#subsect_91142437522}

The net new trend graph includes the following key components:

-   **Data sources** - Recurring revenue data from NetSuite.
    
-   **Impact categories** - New sales, upsells, downsells, and churn. Each is represented as a separate category to highlight growth drivers and losses.
    
-   **Calculations** - The portlet calculates totals for new, upsell, downsell, and churn for each period:
    
    -   For monthly periods, it totals each category for the month.
        
    -   For quarterly periods, it sums each category across all months in the quarter.
        
    -   For yearly periods, it sums these categories across all months in the year.
        
    -   Net New is calculated as ending recurring revenue minus beginning recurring revenue for the selected period:
        
        -   For monthly, it's the ending balance minus the beginning balance for the month.
            
        -   For quarterly, it's the ending balance of the last month minus the beginning balance of the first month in the quarter.
            
        -   For yearly, it's the ending balance of the last month minus the beginning balance of the first month in the year.
            
-   **Visualization** - Trend bar chart using distinct colors for each impact category. The Y-axis displays revenue amounts and the X-axis shows the selected time periods (months, quarters, or years) providing a clear view of performance over time.
    
-   **Filters** - Metric (ARR, MRR, CARR, and CMRR), subsidiary, customer, item, department, class, and location.
    
-   **Reporting periods** - Monthly (1 - 12 months), quarterly (1 - 12 quarters), or yearly (1 - 3 years) duration available to select for analysis.
    

## Key Terms and Definitions {#subsect_32142443778}

The following table lists the key terms and their definitions.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| New | The total new recurring revenue added in the period. |
| Upsell | The total increase in recurring revenue from existing customers in the period. |
| Downsell | The total decrease in recurring revenue from existing customers in the period. |
| Churn | The total recurring revenue lost due to customer cancellations in the period. |
| Net New | The ending recurring revenue minus beginning recurring revenue for the period. |

#### To view the net new trend graph:

1.  Go to Subscription Metrics > Revenue Metrics > View.
    
2.  From the **Metric** filter, select a metric.
    
3.  (Optional) Apply filters to refine results.
    
4.  Select the duration, start period, and end period you want to review.
    
5.  Review the chart to see revenue changes by impact category.
    

For more insights, explore the roll forward report, KPI scorecard, and other graphs.
