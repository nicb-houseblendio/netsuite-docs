The Subscription Metrics SuiteApp helps you track and understand recurring revenue performance in NetSuite. You can monitor annual recurring revenue (ARR), monthly recurring revenue (MRR), committed annual recurring revenue (CARR), committed monthly recurring revenue (CMRR), churn, retention, and bookings using clear dashboards and guided detail views.

You can view live dashboards, apply filters, compare periods, and generate detailed reports to manage and analyze your subscription-based services.

The SuiteApp includes three dashboards to provide comprehensive insights into subscription performance.

Use the following guide to get started with the Subscription Metrics SuiteApp:

1.  **Check prerequisites** - Ensure you have NetSuite 2025.2 or higher, a OneWorld account, and the Advanced Billing and Subscription Metrics Reporting features enabled.
    
    If you need currency conversion, enable the Multi-Currency and Currency Exchange Rate Type features.
    
2.  **Install the SuiteApp** - Go to the SuiteApp Marketplace, search for **Subscription Metrics**, and click **Install**.
    
3.  **Set up permissions** - Verify that your roles have the required permissions to access and use the dashboards.
    
4.  **Set Subscription Metrics fields** - Set the subscription-related fields on your item and service records.
    
5.  **Open your dashboard** - Go to the **Subscription Metrics** tab and select the dashboard you want to view.
    
6.  **Explore your data** - Apply filters to analyze the key business metrics you want.
