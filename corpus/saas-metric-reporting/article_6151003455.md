Sometimes you'll need to make adjustments to the impact amount or change its category. Here's how to do it.

#### To adjust the monthly impact amount:

1.  Go to Lists > SaaS Metric > Analytical Impacts.
    
2.  Next to the record you want to adjust, select **Edit**.
    
3.  Select **Adjust Monthly Impact Amount**.
    
4.  Enter a new value, making sure it's less than or equal to the current monthly amount.
    
    If the new value is lower than the total impact amount, related fields will update with the remaining amount and with the impact category and subcategory.
