The Net Retention table by customer displays the change of revenue from each existing customer over time. The period can be set to monthly, quarterly, and yearly.

#### To view the customer net retention table:

1.  Log in to NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to the [Accessing the Retention Metrics Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0205035256.html).
    
3.  In the Net Retention Table by Customer, set the **Duration**, **Start Period**, and **End Period.**
