In the Subscription Metrics dashboards, you can switch between current and committed revenue metrics.

Current metrics show revenue from active subscriptions. Use current metrics to review active subscription revenue.

Committed metrics show expected revenue from contracted commitments that have not yet been realized. Use committed metrics to forecast revenue based on contracted changes, such as new contracts, upsells, downsells, and churn.

CARR and CMRR are calculated as follows:

-   CMRR = Current MRR + New Contracts + Upsells - Downsells - Churn
    
-   CARR = Current ARR + New Contracts - Expected Churn
    

For CARR and CMRR reporting, the Subscription Metrics SuiteApp automatically updates the values based on contract data to focus on contracted, expected revenue.

#### To switch between current and committed views:

1.  On a Subscription Metrics dashboard, click the **Metric** filter.
    
2.  Select the metric you want to use.
    
    1.  Current - **MRR** or **ARR**.
        
    2.  Committed - **CMRR** or **CARR**.
        
3.  Click **Apply Filters**.
