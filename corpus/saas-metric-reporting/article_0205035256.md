Dashboard data is displayed based on your role's access to subsidiaries and transactions. Metrics use the fiscal calendar assigned to your accessible subsidiaries, and amounts are shown in your lowest common parent company's base currency. For more information, see [Currency for Multiple Subsidiary Search Results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N279420.html).

To access the dashboard, go to **Subscription Metrics > Retention Metrics** tab, then click **View**.
