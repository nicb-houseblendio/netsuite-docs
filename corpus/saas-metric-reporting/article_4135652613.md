To use the SaaS Metric Reporting feature, you need to complete the following actions:

-   Enable SaaS Metric Reporting
    
-   Create items for SaaS Metric Reporting
    
-   Start SaaS Metric Tracking
    
-   Review the Criteria for Automatic Creation of Analytical Impact Records
