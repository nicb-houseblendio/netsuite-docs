You can view the formulas used by the Subscription Metrics dashboards on the **KPI Formulas** tab of the Setup page. To view KPI formulas, go to **Subscription Metrics > Setup**, and click **View**.

Currently, only the CAC formula is editable. Users with the SaaS Metric permission can edit the formula.

#### To edit the CAC formula:

1.  Log in to NetSuite.
    
2.  On the **Subscription Metrics** tab, go to **Setup**, and click **View**.
    
3.  On the **KPI Formulas** subtab, click the edit icon beside **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**.
    
4.  In the Edit Formula popup window, select a dataset.
    
    Note:
    
    Only datasets with a name that starts with SaaS are displayed on the list for selection. The CAC calculation is based on the selected dataset. For details, see [SaaS Sales and Marketing Expenses Dataset](#subsect_0911041116).
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

## SaaS Sales and Marketing Expenses Dataset {#subsect_0911041116}

The SaaS Sales and Marketing Expenses Dataset provides default criterion and formula used to aggregate sales and marketing expenses for customer acquisition cost (CAC) calculations. The default formula takes 30% of expenses posted in **Expenses** and **Other Expenses** account types.

Customer Acquisition Cost = Sales and Marketing Expenses ÷ New Customers Acquired

### Dataset Configuration {#subsect_0918074952}

To find the dataset, go to the **Analytics** tab and search for **SaaS Sales and Marketing Expenses**. You can edit this dataset to refine which transactions and criteria are included, but the following limitations apply to maintain compatibility with the Subscription Metrics. The following screenshot shows the SaaS Sales and Marketing Expenses dataset.

![SaaS Sales and Marketing Expenses Dataset.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/OrderManagement/SaaSMetrics/SaaSSalesandMarketingExpensesDataset.png)

-   Edit the dataset's criteria and formula, but do not change the output type or remove required columns.
    
-   Ensure the output type remains **FLOAT** when editing the formula.
    
-   Edit only the formula; don't alter the output type.
    
-   Adjust the criteria to refine which transactions are included as sales and marketing expenses.
    
-   Maintain the dataset's accuracy in representing CAC related expenses.
    
-   Always name the dataset with the prefix **SaaS.**
    
-   Avoid renaming the dataset or removing the required prefix.
    
-   Keep all columns to ensure the dashboard functions properly. Don't remove or delete any column from the dataset.
    

| Root Record Type | Joined Record Type(s) | Custom Formula Field(s) | Data Grid | Criteria Filters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SaaS Income and Expenses | Account | Formula Field: SMExpenses Formula: 0.3 × ({debit} - {credit}) | The following record fields are included in the dataset:
-   Accounting Book
-   Posting Period
-   Sales and Marketing Expenses
-   Source Date
-   Subsidiary

 | The following criteria is used to filter the database: **Account: Type** any of Expense, Other Expense |

After you make changes, ensure the dataset shows the results you expect, and that CAC metrics display correctly in the dashboard.

For more information, see [SaaS Sales and Marketing Expenses Dataset](#subsect_0911041116).
