The following metrics are available on the KPI Scorecard portlet:

-   Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)
    
-   Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
    
-   Gross Logo Churn Rate
    
-   Gross Revenue Churn Rate
    
-   Number of Active Customers
    
-   Logo Retention Rate
    
-   Net Logo Churn Rate
    
-   Logo Renewal Rate
    
-   Net Revenue Churn Rate
    
-   Renewal Rate (Recurring Revenue)
    

Note:

When in committed view, the following metrics are computed from CMRR/CARR.

-   Gross Revenue Retention
    
-   Net Revenue Retention
    
-   Gross Revenue Churn Rate
    
-   Net Revenue Churn Rate
    
-   Renewal Rate (Recurring Revenue)
    

#### To view retention KPIs:

1.  Log in to NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to **Subscription Metrics > Retention Metrics** tab, then click **View**.
    
3.  (Optional) Set filters for the dashboard reports.
    
4.  Set the comparison period for the KPI Scorecard portlet. The portlet displays the comparison between this month and last month by default.
    

#### To view the churn rate drilldown report:

1.  Go to the [Accessing the Retention Metrics Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0205035256.html).
    
2.  On the KPI Scorecard portlet, click the Gross Logo Churn Rate percentage or the Gross Revenue Churn Rate percentage.
    

#### To view the net revenue rate drilldown report:

1.  Go to the [Accessing the Retention Metrics Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0205035256.html).
    
2.  On the KPI Scorecard portlet, the Gross Revenue Retention percentage or the Net Revenue Retention percentage.
