You can create separate datasets for both SaaS Metrics and Analytical Impacts.

#### To create SaaS Metric datasets:

1.  On the Analytics tab, click **New Dataset**.
    
2.  In the **Record Type** column, select **SaaS Metric** or **Analytical Impact**.
    

#### To create Analytical Impact datasets:

1.  On the Analytics tab, click **New Dataset**.
    
2.  In the **Record Type** column, select **Analytical Impact**.
