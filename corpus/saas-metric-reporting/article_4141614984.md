You'll need Administrator permission to enable and use SaaS Metric Reporting.

## To enable SaaS Metric Reporting: {#T_change_me_1}

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Enable Features.
    
2.  On the **Analytics** subtab, check the **SaaS Metric Reporting** box and then click **Save.**
