The CAC and CAC Payback Period trend graph gives you a clear, actionable view of how much you spend to acquire customers and how quickly you recover those costs.

This tool combines two key metrics: CAC and CAC payback period into a single, interactive dashboard for analysis.

## CAC and CAC Payback Period Graph Key Components {#subsect_94143320336}

The CAC and CAC payback period includes the following key components:

-   **Data sources** - Subscriptions, contracts, and billing data from NetSuite.
    
-   **Visualization** - Trend line for CAC payback period and bar chart for CAC by period.
    
-   **Filters** - Only the subsidiary filter is supported.
    
-   **Reporting periods** - Select start and end periods and choose to view data by month, quarter, or year.
    

## Key Terms and Definitions {#subsect_47143327259}

The following table lists the key terms and their definitions.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) | Total sales and marketing spend divided by the number of new customers. Shows how much it costs to gain each customer |
| CAC Payback Period | Time needed to recover CAC from gross profit. Reveals how quickly you recover your investments. |

#### To view the CAC and CAC payback period chart:

1.  Go to Subscription Metrics > Unit Economics > View.
    
2.  From the **Metric** filter, select **MRR**.
    
3.  Select the reporting period (month, quarter, or year).
    
4.  Set the start and end dates for your analysis.
    
5.  Review the trend line and bar charts for key insights.
    

Use these insights to adjust your sales and marketing strategies for better financial performance.
