The Cohort Analysis portlet provides several visualizations of cohorts and performance between these cohorts over time. Select the visualization you want to display from the **Cohort Type**.

The values displayed on cohort analysis cells are in reference to the previous month

#### To view cohort data:

1.  Log in to NetSuite.
    
2.  Go to the [Accessing the Retention Metrics Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0205035256.html).
    
3.  In the Cohort Analysis portlet, select the cohort analysis or visualization you want to display in **Cohort Type**.
    
4.  Enter a **Start Period** and an **End Period**. By default, the Cohort Analysis portlet displays Net Revenue Retention (NRR) - Related to Previous Month.
