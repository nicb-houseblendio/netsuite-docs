The Subscription Metrics SuiteApp helps subscription businesses track, analyze, and report recurring revenue performance using interactive dashboards. It is designed for business users who need clear, reliable visibility into revenue, retention, and unit economics.

The SuiteApp uses the Subscription Metrics Reporting feature to automatically calculate the impact of new business, upsells, downsells, and churn on your transactions and subscriptions. For more information, see [SaaS Metric Reporting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/book_132508596.html).

The SuiteApp includes three dashboards, each focused on a key area of subscription business health:

-   [Working with the Revenue Metrics Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0105111734.html) - Track and analyze revenue growth, MRR evolution, and KPIs.
    
-   [Working with the Retention Metrics Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_4135529886.html) - Gain insights into customer retention, churn, and renewal trends.
    
-   [Working with the Unit Economics Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0105112529.html) - Assess profitability, pricing, and customer acquisition efficiency.
    

To get started, install and set up the SuiteApp. For instructions on configuring the default exchange rate type, period profile, and other initial settings, see [Setting Up KPI Formulas and Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0909053344.html).

After setup, access the dashboards to explore metrics, drill into details, and export data for further analysis.

## Features {#subsect_0911034415}

The Subscription Metrics SuiteApp lets you:

-   Drive revenue growth.
    
    -   Monitor ARR, MRR, CMRR, and CARR trends with a 12-month forecast.
        
    -   Analyze revenue drivers with interactive MRR, ARR, CMRR, and CARR bridge charts and growth KPIs.
        
    -   Review KPI breakdowns and use committed revenue views (CMRR or CARR) for better planning and forecasting.
        
    -   Drill down to transaction-level details for revenue transparency.
        
-   Strengthen customer health.
    
    -   Track customer acquisition, retention, churn, and renewal rates.
        
    -   Segment and analyze retention by customer, product, or cohort.
        
    -   Visualize trends to identify risks and opportunities.
        
-   Optimize profitability.
    
    -   Evaluate customer acquisition cost (CAC), lifetime value (LTV), gross margin, and magic number.
        
    -   Assess acquisition efficiency and payback periods.
        
    -   Customize KPIs to align with your financial strategy.
        
-   Make informed decisions.
    
    -   Benchmark performance across periods and subsidiaries.
        
    -   Export actionable data for reporting.
        
    -   Leverage flexible filters and time selectors for scenario analysis.
