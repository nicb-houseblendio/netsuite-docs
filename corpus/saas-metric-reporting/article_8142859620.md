You can use SaaS metric reporting for the following item types with subtype = For Sale, Resale:

-   Inventory Item
    
-   Non-inventory item
    
-   Service
    
-   Kit/Package
    
-   Marix Items
    

For instructions, see [Creating Item Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2166469.html).
