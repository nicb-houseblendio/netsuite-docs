You can uninstall SuiteApps when you no longer need them. To uninstall a SuiteApp, make sure you're logged into the account where it's installed.

#### To uninstall the Subscription Metrics SuiteApp:

1.  Go to Customization > SuiteCloud Development > Installed SuiteApp List (Administrator).
    
2.  In the Installed SuiteApps list, find the SuiteApp you want to uninstall.
    
3.  For that SuiteApp, select Uninstall from its action menu. The uninstallation process starts.
    

Uninstalling a SuiteApp might take some time. To check the status, see [Viewing SuiteApp Installation or Uninstallation Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539867271.html).

When the uninstallation completes, the SuiteApp is removed from the Installed SuiteApps list.
