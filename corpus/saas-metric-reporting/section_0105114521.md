Bridge charts are waterfall charts that show how recurring revenue changes from a starting balance to an ending balance based on key revenue drivers.

The chart breaks gains and losses into clear categories, helping you identify trends quickly. You can hover over chart elements to view amounts, drill down from bars to transaction details, and review supporting details in the table below the chart. The detail table updates based on the selected bar. For information, see [Recurring Revenue Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0216011521.html).

Recurring revenue bridge charts support the following metrics:

-   ARR
    
-   MRR
    
-   CARR
    
-   CMRR
    
-   ARR to CARR
    
-   MRR to CMRR
    

## Recurring Revenue Bridge Chart Key Components {#subsect_64142348022}

The recurring revenue bridge chart includes the following key components:

-   **Data sources** - Subscriptions and billing information from NetSuite.
    
-   **Visualization** - A bridge chart showing changes in recurring revenue for the selected period.
    
-   **Impact Categories** - Revenue movement grouped into four main categories: new, upsell, downsell, and churn. These categories highlight the drivers of growth or loss. For more information, see [Category and Duration Field Behavior by Metric](#subsect_0501123708).
    
-   **Impact Subcategories** - Detailed breakdowns such as new customer, item removal, price decrease, quantity decrease, and customer churn. These subcategories clarify the reasons for revenue change. For more information, see [Category and Duration Field Behavior by Metric](#subsect_0501123708).
    
-   **Filters** - Metric (ARR, MRR, CARR, CMRR), subsidiary, customer, item, department, class, and location.
    
-   **Reporting periods** - Monthly, quarterly, or yearly periods with start and end dates for analysis.
    

## Category and Duration Field Behavior by Metric {#subsect_0501123708}

The **Category** field values change based on the metric you select.

When you select **Impact Category** or **Impact Subcategory** in the **Category** field, the following happens:

-   In the **Duration** field, you can select **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Yearly**.
    
-   You can also select the start and end period for the selected duration.
    

When you select metric-specific category such as

**Impact Category - ARR to CARR** or **Impact Subcategory - MRR to CMRR**, the following happens:

-   The **Duration** field defaults to the last completed month and becomes unavailable.
    
-   The **Period** field becomes available and you can select the month you want to view.
    
-   The **Start Period** and **End Period** fields become unavailable.
    

| Metric | Field Values |
| --- | --- |
| ARR or CARR | 
-   **Impact Category**
-   **Impact Subcategory**
-   **Impact Category - ARR to CARR**
-   **Impact Subcategory - ARR to CARR**

 |
| MRR or CMRR | 

-   **Impact Category**
-   **Impact Subcategory**
-   **Impact Category - MRR to CMRR**
-   **Impact Subcategory - MRR to CMRR**

 |

## Key Terms and Definitions {#subsect_0202123145}

The following table lists the key terms and their definitions.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) | The total predictable revenue generated from active subscriptions each month. |
| Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) | The total predictable revenue generated from active subscriptions each year. |
| Committed Monthly Recurring Revenue (CMRR) | Monthly recurring revenue that is committed or contracted, including future-starting subscriptions in addition to currently active recurring revenue. |
| Committed Annual Recurring Revenue (CARR) | The annualized version of committed recurring revenue (CMRR), used to show committed recurring revenue on a yearly basis. |
| Starting Balance | The recurring revenue at the beginning of the selected period. |
| New | Revenue from new customers or contracts. |
| Upsell / Add-on | Additional revenue from existing customers upgrading or adding services. |
| Downsell / Item Removal | Revenue lost due to customers downgrading or removing services. |
| Churn / Customer Churn | Revenue lost from customers who cancel their subscriptions. |
| Ending Balance | The recurring revenue at the end of the selected period. |

#### To view the recurring revenue bridge chart:

1.  Go to Subscription Metrics > Revenue Metrics > View.
    
2.  From the **Metric** filter, select a metric.
    
3.  (Optional) Apply filters to refine your results.
    
4.  Select the following for the duration or period you want to view.
    
    1.  In the **Category** field, select a category.
        
    2.  Based on the category you selected, select the following fields:
        
        1.  **Duration**
            
        2.  **Period**
            
        3.  **Start Period**
            
        4.  **End Period**
            
        
        The availability of these fields change based on the category you selected. For more information, see [Category and Duration Field Behavior by Metric](#subsect_0501123708).
        
5.  View the summary report for your selected period.
    
6.  Click any amount, such as new sales or churn to see transaction-level details.
    
    You can export details to CSV for further analysis.
