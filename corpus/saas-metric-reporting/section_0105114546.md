The Recurring Revenue Trend portlet shows how your company's recurring revenue changes over time. The graph displays the ending balance for each period you select: monthly, quarterly, or yearly.

Use this portlet to track the stability and growth of your revenue. You can spot trends, identify risks such as customer loss, and find new opportunities.

## Recurring Revenue Trend Graph Key Components {#subsect_86142412463}

The recurring revenue trend graph includes the following key components:

-   **Data sources** - Subscriptions and billing information from NetSuite.
    
-   **Visualization** - Line graph showing the ending balance of recurring revenue over time. The Y-axis displays MRR or ARR based on your selection.
    
-   **Filters** - Metric (ARR, MRR, CARR, and CMRR), subsidiary, customer, item, department, class, and location.
    
-   **Reporting periods** - Monthly, quarterly, or yearly periods. Select start and end dates for analysis.
    

## Key Terms and Definitions {#subsect_5142418559}

The following table lists the key terms and their definitions.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) | The total predictable revenue generated from active subscriptions each month. |
| Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) | The total predictable revenue generated from active subscriptions each year. |
| Committed Monthly Recurring Revenue (CMRR) | Monthly recurring revenue that is committed or contracted, including future-starting subscriptions in addition to currently active recurring revenue. |
| Committed Annual Recurring Revenue (CARR) | The annualized version of committed recurring revenue (CMRR) used to show committed recurring revenue on a yearly basis. |
| Duration | The time interval selected: monthly, quarterly, or yearly. |
| Ending Balance | The recurring revenue at the end of the selected period. |

#### To view the recurring revenue line graph:

1.  Go to Subscription Metrics > Revenue Metrics > View.
    
2.  From the **Metric** filter, select a metric.
    
3.  (Optional) Apply filters to refine results.
    
4.  Select the duration, start period, and end period you want to review.
    
5.  Review the graph to analyze revenue trends.
    

For more insights, explore the roll forward report, KPI scorecard, and other graphs.
