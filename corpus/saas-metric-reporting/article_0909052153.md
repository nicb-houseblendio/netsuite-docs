This topic includes instructions for installing and setting up the Subscription Metrics SuiteApp.

It is intended for employees with the Administrator roles and employees with the necessary permissions to work with the SuiteApp.

In this topic, you'll find:

1.  [Prerequisites and Post-Installation Tasks for Subscription Metrics SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0105111158.html) - Requirements for NetSuite version, account type, enabling key features, and post-installation setup.
    
2.  [Installing the Subscription Metrics SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0105111204.html) - Steps to install the SuiteApp from the SuiteApp Marketplace, including important notes to prevent common installation errors.
    
3.  [Configuring Subscription Metrics Fields on Item and Service Item Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0105111217.html) - Steps to configure subscription fields on item or service item records to ensure accurate tracking of recurring revenue and other key metrics.
    
4.  [Assigning Roles and Permissions for Subscription Metrics](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0105111456.html) - Required permissions and steps to assign them to custom roles.
    
5.  [Setting Up KPI Formulas and Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0909053344.html) - Steps to set up and customize KPI formulas and configure the currency exchange rate type for your business.
    

Follow the sections in order or refer to specific steps as needed to ensure a smooth setup and optimal use of the Subscription Metrics SuiteApp.
