The Top 5 Recurring Revenue portlet shows which customers generate the most recurring revenue. It displays a donut chart of your top five customers by monthly or annual recurring revenue.

Use this chart to quickly identify your most important customers and plan how to retain and grow your best customers.

## Top Five Recurring Revenue Chart Key Components {#subsect_87142449024}

The top five recurring revenue chart includes the following key components:

-   **Data sources** - Revenue Metrics dashboard, customer subscription data, billing records from NetSuite.
    
-   **Visualization** - Donut chart displaying the top five customers by MRR or ARR, highlighting key revenue contributors.
    
-   **Metrics** - Ending balances for MRR and ARR, shown as a percentage for each customer.
    
-   **Filters** - Metric (ARR, MRR, CARR, and CMRR), subsidiary, item, department, class, and location.
    
-   **Reporting periods** - This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, Last Quarter, This Year, Last Year, Trailing Twelve Months (TTM), Year to Month (YTM), Before Last Month.
    

## Key Terms and Definitions {#subsect_68142453875}

The following table lists the key terms and their definitions.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) | The total predictable revenue generated from active subscriptions each month. |
| Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) | The total predictable revenue generated from active subscriptions each year. |
| Committed Monthly Recurring Revenue (CMRR) | Monthly recurring revenue that is committed or contracted, including future-starting subscriptions in addition to currently active recurring revenue. |
| Committed Annual Recurring Revenue (CARR) | The annualized version of committed recurring revenue (CMRR), used to show committed recurring revenue on a yearly basis. |
| Top Five Customers | Customers with the highest MRR or ARR in the selected period. |
| Churn | The total recurring revenue lost due to customer cancellations in the period. |
| Net New | The ending recurring revenue minus the beginning recurring revenue for the period. |

#### To view the top five recurring revenue chart:

1.  Go to Subscription Metrics > Revenue Metrics > View.
    
2.  From the **Metric** filter, select a metric.
    
3.  (Optional) Apply filters to refine results.
    
    Note:
    
    The chart doesn't consider the Customer filter at the top of the dashboard.
    
4.  Select the duration you want to review.
    
5.  View the donut chart to see your top five customers by recurring revenue.
    

For more insights, explore the roll forward report, KPI scorecard, and other graphs.
