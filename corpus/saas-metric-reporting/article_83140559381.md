When you track SaaS metrics for an item in NetSuite, certain source records can automatically generate Analytical Impact records to support unified SaaS Metric Reporting. These records help you manage recurring revenue by consolidating data from various transaction types.

## Automatic Creation of Analytical Impact Records {#subsect_98135912580}

Analytical Impact records are created automatically through a nightly job that runs at 1:00 AM server time. NetSuite uses the bulk processing framework, enabling fast, scalable processing as your data grows.

### Criteria for Automatic Creation {#subsect_1118024620}

For a record to generate an Analytical Impact record automatically, all the following conditions must be met:

-   The transaction or subscription line includes an item with **Track SaaS Metrics** enabled.
    
-   The **Start Date** on the transaction header or subscription line falls within the **SaaS Metric Start Date** and **SaaS Metric End Date** set on the item.
    
-   Both **Start Date** and **End Date** on the transaction or subscription are populated and the term is at least one month.
    
-   The transaction or subscription's status is one of the following:
    
    | **Sales Order Status** | 
    -   Pending Fulfillment
    -   Partially Fulfilled
    -   Pending Billing/Partially Fulfilled
    -   Pending Billing
    -   Billed
    -   Closed
    
     |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Invoice Status** | 
    
    -   Open
    -   Paid In Full
    -   Closed
    
     |
    | **Subscription Line Status** | 
    
    -   Active
    -   Suspended
    -   Terminated
    -   Closed
    
     |
    | **Cash Sale** | 
    
    -   Not Deposited
    -   Deposited
    -   Closed
    
     |
    

Source lines for Analytical Impact records can include subscriptions, sales orders (with billing schedules), contract renewal transactions, standalone sales orders, invoices, and cash sales. Downsell events can also be generated for return authorizations, credit memos, and cash refunds linked to transactions.

## Adjusting Analytical Impact Records {#subsect_4140840966}

Analytical Impact records are flexible, allowing you to tailor them to your organization's needs.

#### To adjust an Analytical Impact record:

1.  Open the record by going to Lists > SaaS Metric > Analytical Impacts and edit as required.
    
2.  To split an impact:
    
    1.  In the **Adjust Monthly Impact** field, enter a partial amount.
        
    2.  Select an **Adjust Impact category** and **subcategory**.
        
        The system automatically adjusts the remaining amount, creating two events on the SaaS Metric record (both will appear on your dashboard).
        
3.  To reclassify automatically assigned impacts:
    
    1.  In the **Adjust Monthly Impact** field, enter the full amount.
        
    2.  From the lists in the **Adjust Impact category** and **subcategory** columns, choose a category.
        

You can also:

-   Customize the default Analytical Impact form by adding custom fields.
    
-   Ignore automatically generated records or delete CSV-imported records if you don't want them included in reports.
    

Any edits to a source record that affect the line amounts, dates, or linked records are reflected in the resulting Analytical Impact records, ensuring your recurring metrics always remain accurate and up to date.

## Ignoring an Analytical Impact Record {#subsect_1215032558}

If you want NetSuite to disregard a specific analytical impact record, here's what to do.

#### To ignore an analytical impact record:

1.  Go to Lists > SaaS Metric > Analytical Impacts.
    
2.  On the record, check the **Ignore** box.
    
    -   Any existing SaaS Metric records linked to this impact will be deleted.
        
    -   No new SaaS Metric records will be created from this impact going forward.
