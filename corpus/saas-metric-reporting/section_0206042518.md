The Cohort Analysis portlet can display the following cohort analyses:

-   [Cohort Type: Number of Logos](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0206043740.html)
    
-   [Cohort Type: Gross Logo Churn Rate](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0206045616.html)
    
-   [Cohort Type: Logo Retention Rate](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0206050442.html)
    
-   [Cohort Type: Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0206050954.html)
    
-   [Cohort Type: Net Revenue Churn Rate](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0206051715.html)
    
-   [Cohort Type: Net Revenue Retention - Related to Previous Month](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0206052108.html)
    
-   [Cohort Type: Net Revenue Retention - Related to Base Period](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0206052639.html)
    
-   [Cohort Type: Cumulative Lifetime Revenue (LTR)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0206052813.html)
    
-   [Cohort Type: Customer Lifetime Revenue (CLTR)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0206053159.html)
    
-   [Cohort Type: Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0206053321.html)
    

Cohort Analysis visualizes data as a dynamic heatmap that shows Month 0 to Month X based on the selected Start and End Period. The heatmap visually shows retention, engagement, or revenue over time to highlight trends and anomalies. For details, see [Color-Coded Indicators for Key Cohort Data](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0206042443.html).
