The Class Composition portlet lets you compare recurring revenue across business classes. Each class uses a unique color, allowing you to quickly identify top revenue segments and track changes over time. Use this bar chart for a visual breakdown to find growth opportunities and address weak areas.

## Revenue Composition by Class Graph Key Components {#subsect_70142425092}

The revenue composition by class graph includes the following key components:

-   **Data sources** - Revenue and class data from NetSuite with period definitions from your reporting calendar.
    
-   **Visualization** - Bar chart where each class uses a unique color for clear comparison. The Y-axis shows the percentage of total recurring revenue. The X-axis displays periods based on your selection. The chart highlights changes in class composition and shifts in revenue distribution over time.
    
-   **Metrics** - Ending balances for MRR, ARR, CARR, or CMRR shown as a percentage for each class.
    
-   **Filters** - Metric (MRR, ARR, CARR, or CMRR), subsidiary, customer, item, department, and location.
    
-   **Reporting periods** - Monthly, quarterly, or yearly periods. Select start and end dates for analysis.
    

## Key Terms and Definitions {#subsect_87142431650}

The following table lists the key terms and their definitions.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) | The total predictable revenue generated from active subscriptions each month. |
| Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) | The total predictable revenue generated from active subscriptions each year. |
| Committed Monthly Recurring Revenue (CMRR) | Monthly recurring revenue that is committed or contracted, including future-starting subscriptions in addition to currently active recurring revenue. |
| Committed Annual Recurring Revenue (CARR) | The annualized version of committed recurring revenue (CMRR), used to show committed recurring revenue on a yearly basis. |
| Class | Revenue category or business segment. |
| Period | The time interval selected: monthly, quarterly, or yearly. |
| Ending Balance | The recurring revenue at the end of the selected period. |

#### To view the class composition graph:

1.  Go to Subscription Metrics > Revenue Metrics > View.
    
2.  From the **Metric** filter, select a metric.
    
3.  (Optional) Apply filters to refine results.
    
4.  Select the duration, start period, and end period you want to review.
    
5.  Review the graph to compare class performance.
    

For more insights, explore the roll forward report, KPI scorecard, and other graphs.
