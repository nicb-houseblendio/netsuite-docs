The Recurring Revenue Trend by Subsidiary portlet helps you track recurring revenue for each subsidiary. The portlet displays trends over time, so you can spot changes and patterns. It lets you compare subsidiaries and identify which ones are growing or need attention.

Use these insights to support data-driven planning and allocate resources effectively.

## Subsidiary Recurring Revenue Trend Graph Key Components {#subsect_98143644297}

The subsidiary recurring revenue trend graph includes the following key components:

-   **Data sources** - Revenue Metrics dashboard, subsidiary, subscription, and billing data from NetSuite.
    
-   **Visualization** - Bar chart with unique colors for each subsidiary, showing revenue trends over time. The Y-axis displays revenue amounts, and the X-axis shows the selected period.
    
-   **Subsidiary comparison** - Select individual or multiple subsidiaries for side-by-side analysis and direct performance comparison.
    
-   **Filters** - Metric (ARR, MRR, CARR, and CMRR), customer, item, department, class, and location.
    
-   **Reporting periods** - Monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
    

## Key Terms and Definitions {#subsect_0202014922}

The following table lists the key terms and their definitions.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) | The total predictable revenue generated from active subscriptions each month. |
| Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) | The total predictable revenue generated from active subscriptions each year. |
| Committed Monthly Recurring Revenue (CMRR) | Monthly recurring revenue that is committed or contracted, including future-starting subscriptions in addition to currently active recurring revenue. |
| Committed Annual Recurring Revenue (CARR) | The annualized version of committed recurring revenue (CMRR), used to show committed recurring revenue on a yearly basis. |
| Subsidiary | A distinct business unit or legal entity within the organization. |
| Period | Time interval: monthly, quarterly, or yearly. |

#### To view the subsidiary trend graph:

1.  Go to Subscription Metrics > Revenue Metrics > View.
    
2.  From the **Metric** filter, select a metric.
    
3.  (Optional) Apply filters to refine results.
    
4.  Select the duration, start period, and end period you want to review.
    
5.  Review the chart to compare recurring revenue trends across subsidiaries.
    

For more insights, explore the roll forward report, KPI scorecard, and other trend graphs.
