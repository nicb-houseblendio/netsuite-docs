Before you install the Subscription Metrics SuiteApp, make sure that your account meets the following requirements:

-   It is a OneWorld account.
    
-   It uses NetSuite 2025.2 or later.
    
-   It has the following features enabled in the order:
    
    1.  Advanced Billing
        
    2.  Subscription Metrics Reporting
        

If currency conversion is required, enable the following features:

-   Multi-Currency
    
-   Currency Exchange Rate Type
    

#### To enable the required features:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Enable Features.
    
2.  On the **Transactions** tab, in the Billing section, check the **Advanced Billing** box.
    
3.  On the **Analytics** tab, in the Subscription Metrics section, check the **Subscription Metrics Reporting** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

After installation, rename SaaS Metrics records to Subscription Metrics.

#### To rename SaaS Metrics records:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Rename Records/Transactions.
    
2.  In the **Name for SaaS Metric** field, enter **Subscription Metrics**.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
