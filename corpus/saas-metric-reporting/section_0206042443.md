You can identify performance health across months and cohorts without manually interpreting raw percentages or numbers with color-coded indicators.

The following table lists how the values of each metric determines the displayed color:

| Retention Metric | Green (Good) | Amber (Warning) | Red (Poor) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gross Logo Churn Rate | 0-2% | 2-5% | \>5% |
| Logo Retention Rate | 95-100% | 90-95% | 70-90% |
| Net Revenue Churn Rate | \-10-0% | 0-5% | 5-10% |
| Net Revenue Retention | 110-120% | 100-110% | 80-100% |
| Customer Lifetime Value Note: The Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) cell is determined based on its ratio to the Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC). | LTV/CAC ratio > 3.0 | LTV/CAC ratio between 2.0 and 3.0 | LTV/CAC ratio < 2.0 |
