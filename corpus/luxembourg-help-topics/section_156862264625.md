If you have a Luxembourg nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the monthly and quarterly Luxembourg VAT returns in English or French. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.

For more information, read the following Luxembourg tax topics:

-   [Luxembourg Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3771429290.html)
    
    [Tax Code Table for Luxembourg](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3771429290.html#bridgehead_3776748982)
    
-   [Luxembourg VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3771429635.html)
    
    [What goes into each box - Luxembourg VAT report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3771430031.html)
    

For more information about the International Tax Reports SuiteApp, see [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html).
