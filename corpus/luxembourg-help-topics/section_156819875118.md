Setting up an account for an organization doing business in Luxembourg usually requires enabling several features and installing some additional SuiteApps.

## Enabling Features for Luxembourg Account {#bridgehead_156819963678}

Some features available in your NetSuite account can be useful for businesses in Luxembourg. Enable Company, Accounting, and SuiteCloud features to make your NetSuite account more suited to Luxembourg's business requirements. For information about features and prerequisites for specific product areas, see [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_156811878876.html#bridgehead_160448120458).

Note:

If you don't have sufficient permissions to enable features, please contact your account administrator.

#### To enable features:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **Company** subtab, check the **Multi-language** and **Multiple Currencies** boxes.
    
3.  (Optional) On the **Accounting** subtab.
    
    1.  On the **Basic features** subtab, check the **GL Audit Numbering** box.
        
    2.  On the **Advanced features** subtab, check the **Period End Journal Entries** and **Foreign Currency Variance Mapping** boxes.
        
4.  On the **SuiteCloud** subtab, check the box next to the following features:
    
    -   SuiteBuilder
        
    -   SuiteScript
        
    -   SuiteGL
        
    -   SuiteFlow
        
    
    For more information about SuiteCloud features, see [SuiteCloud Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N239332.html).
    
5.  If your company is registered for EU One Stop Shop, on the **Tax** subtab, check the **EU One Stop Shop** box.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

## Available SuiteApps {#bridgehead_160448930892}

Some additional features require installation of a specific SuiteApp. SuiteApps are applications that extend NetSuite for specific industry and business needs. For information about the required SuiteApps for specific product areas, see [Functional Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_156811878876.html#bridgehead_160448120458).

Luxembourg-specific features are currently available in the following SuiteApps:

| [Electronic Bank Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1585433.html) | Payments | Enables bank payments of vendor bills, employee expenses, partner and employee commissions, customer refunds, and customer payments within Luxembourg. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html) | Taxation (NetSuite accounts without SuiteTax) | Generates the French VAT reports in English or French, sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports (DEB). If the SuiteTax feature is enabled in your NetSuite account, don't install the International Tax Reports SuiteApp. |
| Supplementary Tax Calculation | Taxation (NetSuite accounts without SuiteTax) | Manages EU Reverse charges and supports the calculation of nondeductible input tax. For more information, see [Recording Nondeductible Input Tax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1822590.html) and [Setting Up Tax Codes to Post Notional Tax Amounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4702880164.html). This SuiteApp also supports VAT on prompt payment discounts. For more information, see [Recording Tax on Prompt Payment Discount](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4702990460.html). If the SuiteTax feature is enabled in your NetSuite account, don't install the Supplementary Tax Calculation SuiteApp. |
| [Tax Audit Files](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2073244.html) | Taxation (NetSuite accounts without SuiteTax) | Enables you to generate an extract of general ledger data to a format that complies with government statutory requirements. If the SuiteTax feature is enabled in your NetSuite account, don't install Tax Audit Files SuiteApp. |
| [SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1557396965.html) | Taxation (NetSuite accounts with SuiteTax) | For NetSuite accounts with the SuiteTax feature enabled, the SuiteTax Engine sets default tax codes based on local tax requirements. |
| [Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1528262949.html) | Taxation (NetSuite accounts with SuiteTax) | For NetSuite accounts with the SuiteTax feature enabled, the Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp provides the Country Tax Reports page that enables you to generate tax reports required in Luxembourg. |
| [GL Matching](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1516099228.html) | Accounting | Enables account reconciliation for balance sheet accounts. Account types supported by the GL Matching SuiteApp aren't limited to Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable types. |
| [One Stop Shop](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162918935998.html) | Taxation (NetSuite Accounts With or Without SuiteTax) | Enables the EU VAT One Stop Shop Reports (OSS) generation. OSS is categorized into three specific schemes: Non-Union OSS Scheme, Union OSS Scheme, and Import OSS Scheme. These schemes allow taxable persons to declare and pay VAT due in Member States where the taxable persons aren't established. |

#### To install a SuiteApp:

1.  Go to _Customization > SuiteBundler > Search & Install Bundles_.
    
2.  Search for the SuiteApps that you would like to install using their names or their Bundle IDs.
    
3.  On the Bundle Details page, click **Install**.
    

For more information about SuiteApps, see [SuiteApps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_3134602194.html).

For more information about SuiteApps installation, see [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).
