If you have a Luxembourg subsidiary or nexus, and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the Luxembourg monthly and quarterly VAT reports, in English or French, from Reports > VAT/GST > Tax Reports (International).

Important:

To generate the tax report for Luxembourg, use the tax code properties provided by the International Tax Reports SuiteApp. See [Luxembourg Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3771429290.html).

NetSuite gets the values in the report from your transactions, but some boxes in the form may require you to enter data manually. For information, see [Generating VAT/GST Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2064551.html). It's important that you save a PDF file of the report for your own records because NetSuite can't audit manually entered data.

You should review all the values in the report. You can click the values in the boxes of the VAT report to view drilldown details. To understand how NetSuite uses the tax codes to get the values for the Luxembourg monthly VAT report, see [What goes into each box - Luxembourg VAT report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3771430031.html).

Important:

The NetSuite VAT/GST tax form is designed to look like the official tax form for ease of use, but it's not intended to be printed out for submission to the tax agency.

![Screenshot of Luxembourg VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/LuxembourgTaxReport.png)

Note:

You can also use customizable saved reports provided by the International Tax Reports SuiteApp to view both detail and summary reports for purchases or sales, grouped by tax code. For more information, see [Sales and Purchase Reports Grouped by Tax Code](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2067748.html). For EU sales list and Intrastat reports, see [EU Sales List (ESL) Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2068052.html) and [EU Intrastat Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2069213.html).
