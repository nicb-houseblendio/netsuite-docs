In nonprofit organizations, the Development Director's primary responsibility is to oversee fundraising.

The Development Director role includes custom dashboards. The following table highlights the key portlets on the Home dashboard.

| Center Type | Custom Center Tabs | Portlets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accounting | 
-   Home
-   Activities
-   Leads
-   Grant Opportunities
-   Constituents
-   Forecast
-   NFP CRM Center

 | 

-   Reminders (reminders with zero results still appear)
    -   Overdue Pledge Payments
    -   Overdue Grant Payments
    -   Tasks due today (standard)
    -   Tasks to complete (standard)
-   Report Snapshots
    -   Revenue Stream Distribution (on the left)
    -   Revenue By Restriction (on the right)
-   Trend Graphs
    -   Monthly Average Organization Gift Amount (on the left)
    -   Monthly Average Individual Gift Amount (on the right)
-   Navigation Shortcuts
    -   Constituents
    -   Fundraising
    -   Reports
-   Scorecard NFP Operations For details on custom scorecards, see [Social Impact KPI Scorecards](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1548270090.html).
-   Key Performance Indicators
    
    -   New constituents
    -   Total Number of Constituents
    -   Average Individual Gift Amount
    -   Average Organization Gift Amount
    -   Recurring Gifts
    -   Total Individual Donations
    -   Total Organization Donations
    -   Total Grant Revenue
    -   Fundraising Expenses
    
    For details on the custom KPIs, see [Social Impact KPIs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1548270070.html).
-   Custom Saved Search
    -   Giving Statistics By Program
    -   Last Year But Not This Year (LYBUNT)
-   Settings (standard portlet)

 |
