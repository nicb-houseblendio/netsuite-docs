The Controller is the chief accounting officer and leads the accounting department.

The Controller handles financial statements, general ledger, payroll, budgeting, taxes, and special analyses. They're responsible for financial planning, risk management, record-keeping, and reporting.

The Program Controller role includes custom dashboards. The following table highlights the key portlets on the Home dashboard.
