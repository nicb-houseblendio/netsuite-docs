You can use the NetSuite EPM Access portlet to go to the following Oracle Cloud Enterprise Performance Management (EPM) environments directly from NetSuite:

-   Account Reconciliation
    
-   Close Management and Consolidation
    
-   Planning and Budgeting
    

The following screenshot shows the NetSuite EPM Access portlet with the **View** links to the Cloud EPM environments:

![The NetSuite EPM Access portlet that lets you connect directly to your NetSuite EPM environment.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteEPM/OracleEPMConnector/NSEPMAccessPortlet.png)

To access the portlet, go to NetSuite EPM > NetSuite EPM Overview. To go to your Cloud EPM environment directly from this portlet, you need to define the login URL that NetSuite should connect to. For instructions, see the following help topics:

-   For Account Reconciliation, see [Setting Up the Connection to Account Reconciliation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5100827424.html).
    
-   For Financial Consolidation and Close, see [Setting Up the Connection to Financial Consolidation and Close](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_3232205961.html).
    
-   For Planning and Budgeting, see [Setting Up the Connection to Planning and Budgeting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_4144843069.html).
    

If you then click **View** in the portlet next to the environment you want to connect to, you are redirected to that environment.

If you cannot see the portlet at NetSuite EPM > NetSuite EPM Overview, you need to add the portlet manually to the NetSuite EPM page.

#### To add the NetSuite EPM Access portlet in NetSuite:

1.  Go to NetSuite EPM > NetSuite EPM Overview.
    
2.  Click the **Personalize** link in the upper-right corner of the page.
    
3.  On the **Standard Content** tab, click **Custom Portlet**.
    
4.  In the Custom Content portlet menu, click **Set Up**.
    
5.  From the **Source** list, select **NSEPM | NetSuite EPM Access**.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
    This adds the portlet to the NetSuite EPM page.
