You can use NetSuite to export your NetSuite EPM Connector configuration to a single file. Then, you can use the single file as a configuration backup, or import it into another NetSuite account. You can also view details and the history of your imports and exports.

For information, see the following help topics:

-   [Exporting NetSuite EPM Connector Configuration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1106030015.html)
    
-   [Importing NetSuite EPM Connector Configuration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1106030042.html)
