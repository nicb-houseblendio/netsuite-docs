NetSuite EPM Connector is a shared SuiteApp. Before you can install the SuiteApp, it must be provisioned for your NetSuite account. For more information, contact your NetSuite account manager.

To install the NetSuite EPM Connector SuiteApp, you must have permission and access to the SuiteApp Marketplace. For information about the SuiteApp Marketplace, see [SuiteApp Marketplace in NetSuite](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1539797981.html).

#### To install the NetSuite EPM Connector SuiteApp: {#procedure_1123091744}

1.  In NetSuite, click the **SuiteApps** tab.
    
2.  In the **Search SuiteApps** field, enter **NetSuite EPM Connector**.
    
3.  Click the **NetSuite EPM Connector** tile.
    
4.  In the upper-right area of the NetSuite EPM Connector details page, click **Install**.
    
5.  Wait for the installation to complete.
    

NetSuite EPM Connector is a managed SuiteApp. When a new version is available, NetSuite automatically updates your account.

For general information about installing, updating, and deleting SuiteApps from the SuiteApp Marketplace, see [SuiteApp Installation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1540236668.html).
