You can compare your custom role to an existing role and add missing permissions.

#### To compare two roles and add missing permissions:

1.  Go to NetSuite EPM > EPM Connector > Manage User Roles.
    
2.  In the **Custom Role** field, select your custom role.
    
3.  In the **Compare to Role** field, select an existing role to compare your custom role to.
    
4.  To see only the differences between the roles, check the **Only Show Missing Permissions** box.
    
5.  Click **Compare**.
    
    This displays permissions of each role in the Result section.
    
6.  To add a missing permission to the custom role, click **Add** in the Action column.
