Before installing NetSuite EPM Connector, make sure you have enabled the features required by the SuiteApp in your NetSuite account.

#### To enable the required features: {#procedure_60165604500}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **SuiteCloud** subtab, and check the following boxes:
    
    -   Custom Records
        
    -   Client SuiteScript
        
    -   Server SuiteScript
        
3.  Click **Save**.
