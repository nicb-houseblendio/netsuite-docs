To begin using cached data in datasets and table views, you must first enable the feature.

#### To enable Cached Data in Datasets in your account: {#procedure_0728111459}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Analytics** subtab.
    
3.  Check the **Cached Data in Datasets** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

After you have enabled the Cached Data in Datasets feature, you can choose to use Real-time Response or Cached Response mode in your datasets and workbooks. For information about the two options, see the following topics:

-   [Using Cached Data in SuiteAnalytics Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0728110107.html)
    
-   [Using Real-Time Data in SuiteAnalytics Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0728110150.html)
