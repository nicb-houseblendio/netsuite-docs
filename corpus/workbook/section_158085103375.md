Important:

By default, only users with the Analytics Administrator permission can access the workbook and dataset templates in your account. If you don't have the Analytics Administrator permission, ask someone who has the permission to share the templates with you.

SuiteAnalytics Workbook offers over 20 dataset templates. Each comes with preselected fields, joined record types, and filters so you can quickly create a custom workbook or your own version of the dataset. Additionally, each dataset template has an accompanying, predefined workbook template. For information about workbook templates, see [Workbook Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158085159253.html).

Here are the dataset templates you can use in SuiteAnalytics Workbook. If you have the Analytics Administrator permission, access Templates on the Datasets subtab of the Analytics Home page:

Important:

Some templates are feature dependent. If you don't see one of the listed templates in your account, contact your account administrator.
