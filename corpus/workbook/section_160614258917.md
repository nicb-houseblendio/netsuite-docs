Analytical record types are custom types made specifically for SuiteAnalytics Workbook and only exist in the [The Analytics Data Source and SuiteAnalytics Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158618649135.html). These record types are based on standard record types but have their own set of fields and predefined criteria. They combine data from the transaction, transaction line, and transaction accounting line record types. The sales (ordered), sales (invoiced), and manufacturing templates use analytical record types.

The following analytical record types are currently available in SuiteAnalytics Workbook:

-   [Manufacturing Component Analytical Record Type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160612742532.html)
    
-   [Manufacturing Transaction Analytical Record Type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160588044297.html)
    
-   [Sales (Invoiced) Analytical Record Type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160613422965.html)
    
-   [Sales (Ordered) Analytical Record Type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160613676371.html)
    
-   [Customer 360 Analytical Record Types](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7172327604.html)
