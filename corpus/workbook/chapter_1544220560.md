With the Analytics portlet, SuiteAnalytics Workbook lets you view your workbook data on your dashboards. The Analytics portlet lets you add charts, pivot tables, and table views from workbooks listed under My Workbooks and Shared with Me on the Workbook Listing page. You can add up to 10 Analytics portlets to your dashboards, each with its own workbook visualization. SuiteAnalytics Workbook also lets you translate Analytics portlet names through Manage Translation.

For more information about each of the portlet types, see the following topics:

-   [Chart-based Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1544211554.html)
    
-   [Pivot-based Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156326644450.html)
    
-   [Table View Portlets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159353034835.html)
    

For more information about how to add and set up an Analytics portlet, see [Adding an Analytics Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1546873527.html) and [Setting Up the Analytics Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1546873594.html).

For more information about translatable Analytics portlet names, see [Adding Translatable Analytics Portlet Names](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162566567856.html).
