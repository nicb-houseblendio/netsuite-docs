The **Clear cache** option removes cached data from workbook pivot tables and charts so you can get the most current results. You can verify when the data was last updated by looking at the date and time in the upper-right corner of the Viewer. Here's how you can clear the cache for your open pivot tables and charts.

#### To clear the cache for pivot tables and charts: {#procedure_84145550008}

1.  On the Pivot or Chart tab, click the menu icon ![Field menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Workbook/FieldMenuIcon18_1.png) in the upper right corner of the Viewer.
    
    A popup message appears asking if you want to clear the cache.
    
2.  Choose how you want to proceed:
    
    -   **Clear cache** - Clears the cache for the entire workbook. The time and date in the upper-right corner of the Viewer is updated to reflect when the data was updated.
        
    -   **Cancel** - The cache is not cleared. Data is updated following the default caching process.
        
3.  If you clear the cache, a popup message appears asking you to refresh all pivot tables and charts and an icon is displayed next to each pivot table and chart in the workbook. There are three available icons:
    
    -   **Warning icon** ![Warning icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Workbook/Warning-icon.png)\- The displayed results are not up to date according to the date and time shown in the **Last updated** field. To retrieve the most current results, click the **Refresh** icon ![Refresh report icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Workbook/ReportRefreshIcon.png).
        
    -   **Loading icon** ![Loading icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Workbook/Loading-icon.png)\- The data is currently being updated according to the date and time shown in the **Last updated** field. After the refresh is completed, the icon changes to either the pivot or chart icon.
        
    -   **Pivot** ![Pivot icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Workbook/Pivot-updated-icon.png)**and Chart icons** ![Chart icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Workbook/Chart-updated-icon.png)\- The data displayed is up to date according to the date and time shown in the **Last updated** field.
        
4.  To retrieve the most current results in each open pivot table and chart, click the **Refresh** icon ![Refresh report icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Workbook/ReportRefreshIcon.png) in each tab.
    

For more information about the Pivot table and Chart tabs, see [Pivot Tab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1544221045.html) and [Chart Tab](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1544221024.html).

To better understand the caching process of SuiteAnalytics Workbook, see [Understanding Data Caching in Pivot Tables and Charts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0727022719.html).
