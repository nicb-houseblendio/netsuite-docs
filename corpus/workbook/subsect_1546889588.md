By default, if you group fields or use multicolumn hierarchical fields, your pivot table expands horizontally, which can make it difficult to view all your data.

To display the table data vertically, click the Compact Mode icon ![Compact mode icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Workbook/CompactModeIcon_18_2.png).
