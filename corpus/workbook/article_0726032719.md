By default, only pivot tables and charts use cached data. If you want datasets and table views to load faster using cached data too, turn on the Cached Data in Datasets feature. After it's enabled, you can choose between two options:

-   **Real-time Response** - Datasets, workbooks, and Analytics portlets always show the latest data. Results might take longer to load with this option.
    
-   **Cached Response** - Datasets, workbooks, and Analytics portlets use cached data for quicker loading. The refresh rate adapts to your usage. Cached Response monitors when you view datasets, workbooks, or portlets and schedules data refreshes accordingly, refreshes can happen anywhere from every hour to every 24 hours, depending on your account usage.
    

For information about how to enable the Cached Data in Datasets feature, see [Enabling the Cached Data in Datasets Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0728110840.html).

For information about how to work with cached data or real-time data in SuiteAnalytics Workbook, see the following topics:
