Important:

By default, only users with the Analytics Administrator permission can access the workbook and dataset templates in your account. If you don't have the Analytics Administrator permission, ask someone who has the permission to share the templates with you.

SuiteAnalytics Workbook offers over 20 workbook templates, all based on available dataset templates in your account. Each template comes with different visualizations to help you analyze your data, including a Table View with all the fields from the underlying dataset. Use these templates to quickly analyze and visualize different parts of your company data. To access the underlying dataset template, click the dataset name in the Dataset Panel from any of the workbook visualizations and select **Open Dataset**. Some templates might also be based on a set of linked datasets. For more information, see [Dataset Linking in SuiteAnalytics Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162627392718.html).

The following workbook templates are currently available in SuiteAnalytics Workbook. If you have the Analytics Administrator permission, access Templates on the Workbooks subtab of the Analytics Home page:
