In the Criteria Builder, you can click **New Group** to create a group of criteria filters.

Grouped criteria filters work like parenthetical expressions in Saved Search. Use them to link filter conditions with AND and OR, so they're checked as a single item in your criteria list.

For example, if you set up these criteria filters, SuiteAnalytics Workbook evaluates the whole group (Sales Rep is A Wolfe-admin OR Status is Open) before the Start Date filter and after the Date filter.

![Criteria Summary filters in a dataset.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Workbook/Criteria_20_1_1.png)

You can also create a group of filters inside another group. If you do, SuiteAnalytics Workbook checks the conditions from the inside out.

For example, in this group of filters, (Status is Sales Order: Closed AND Sales Rep is Franco Africa) is checked before (Status is Bill: Open OR Sales Rep is ). And since the whole group comes before the Start Date filter, the grouped filters are checked first.

![Grouped criteria filters in a dataset.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Workbook/Criteria_20_1_2.png)
