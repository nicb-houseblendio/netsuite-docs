For information about the terms and concepts described in this topic, see [Globalization Glossary](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_157608114727.html)

Internationalization is a part of globalization and is the technical foundation for localization.

Internationalization stands for designing, developing or modifying the NetSuite application, a feature or an object so it could be localized. This section highlights features that help localize the NetSuite application. Internationalization means designing, developing, or changing NetSuite, its features, or objects so they can be localized.
