The Worldwide Support for Phone Number Formatting feature adds two new SuiteScript formatting objects: **format.PhoneNumberFormatter** and **format.PhoneNumberParser**.

For more information about these formatting methods, see [N/format/i18n Module](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543861741.html).
