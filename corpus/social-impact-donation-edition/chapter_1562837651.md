Social Impact Donation Edition includes NFP Financials and the Non-Profit financial metrics. For details on bundle dependencies, see [Deployment Considerations for NFP SuiteApps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1534794923.html#section_1526407226) or contact your administrator.

This SuiteApp includes roles, dashboards, saved searches, fields, lists, records, and forms you set up after installing.

To begin using Social Impact, see [Setup for NFP Financials](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1519241769.html).
