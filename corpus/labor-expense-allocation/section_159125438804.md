By default, the Labor Expense Allocation SuiteApp provides the following employee pay frequencies:

-   Weekly
    
-   Bi-weekly (every two weeks)
    
-   Monthly
    

You can also create custom employee pay frequencies from the Employee Pay Frequency page.

#### To create an Employee Pay Frequency:

1.  Go to Expense Management > Setup > Set Up Employee Pay Frequency > New.
    
2.  In the **Name** field, enter the name of the employee pay frequency.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
