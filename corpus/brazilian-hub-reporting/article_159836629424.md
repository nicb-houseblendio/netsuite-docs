Companies and individuals use the Escrituração Fiscal Digital de Retenções e Outras Informações Fiscais (EFD-Reinf) report for the following bookkeeping obligations:

-   Paid and withheld income tax
    
-   Contribuição Social
    
-   Gross revenue information to assess the substitution of social security contributions
