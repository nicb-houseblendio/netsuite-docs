With the Brazilian Hub Reporting SuiteApp, you can assess the Imposto de Renda das Pessoas Jurídicas (IRPJ) and the Contribuição Social sobre o Lucro Líquido (CSLL) taxes if your company adopts either the Lucro Real or the Lucro Presumido tax regimes.

To learn more about assessing IRPJ and CSLL, see the following topics.

-   [Assessing IRPJ and CSLL in the Lucro Real Regime (Brazilian Hub Reporting SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160443158734.html)
