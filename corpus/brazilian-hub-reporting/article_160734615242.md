The municipal statutory reports feature compiles existing information from the system and enables you to export it.

Brazilian Hub Reporting supports the generation of statutory reports for subsidiaries located in the following cities:

-   Campinas (SP)
    
-   Curitiba (PR)
    
-   Florianópolis (SC)
    
-   São Carlos (SP)
    

To use the feature, you must also meet the requirements covered in the following topics:

-   [Entering the Lot Sequence Number (Brazilian Hub Reporting SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162128062858.html)
    
-   [Setting Up City-Specific Requirements (Brazilian Hub Reporting SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_162128063297.html)
