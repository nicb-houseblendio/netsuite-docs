Enter the lot sequence number of the city where the subsidiary is located.

#### To enter the lot sequence number:

1.  In the **Global Search** field, enter **Número lote**.
    
2.  In the results list, click **Page: Número lote**.
    
3.  Click **New Número Lote**.
    
4.  In the **Name** field, enter a name for the lot.
    
    Tip:
    
    Use the Lote + name of the city standard. For example, **Lote Campinas**.
    
5.  In the **Número Atual Lote** field, enter the current lot sequence number.
    
    The initial lot number must be **1**. NetSuite increases the number as you generate new lots.
    
6.  In the **Município** field, select the city to which the lot sequence number applies.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
