Fiscal reports enable Brazilian businesses to view and retrieve fiscal information from transactions recorded in NetSuite. You can use the information contained in the fiscal reports for bookkeeping purposes and to create reports required by some local authorities.

To learn more about the fiscal reports provided by the Brazilian Hub Reporting SuiteApp, see the following topics:

-   [Auxiliary Withholding Tax Reports (Brazilian Hub Reporting SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160684284673.html)
