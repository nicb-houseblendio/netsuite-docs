Associate your EFD-Reinf e-document templates with layout exportação records. The association enables Brazilian Hub Reporting to identify and generate the e-documents of each event.

You don't need to associate the R-2099 status request template with a layout exportação record.

#### To associate an e-document template with a layout exportação:

1.  Go to _Customization > Lists, Records and Fields > Record Types_.
    
2.  Click **List** next to **Layout Exportação**.
    
    NetSuite opens a list of layout exportação records named after EFD-Reinf events.
    
3.  Click **Edit** next to the record with which you want to associate an EFD-Reinf e-document template.
    
4.  In the **E-Document Template** field, select the corresponding template.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Repeat the procedure until you associate every EFD-Reinf e-document template to the corresponding layout exportação record.
