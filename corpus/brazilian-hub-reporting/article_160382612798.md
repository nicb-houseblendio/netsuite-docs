The Brazilian Hub Reporting SuiteApp enables you to assess the taxes applicable to your transactions in NetSuite. In Brazil, assessing taxes means calculating the total amount of taxes that your company must pay to the government.

You can assess the following taxes with Brazilian Hub Reporting:

-   Programa de Integração Social (PIS) and Contribuição para o Financiamento da Seguridade Social (COFINS)
    
-   Imposto de Renda das Pessoas Jurídicas (IRPJ) and Contribuição Social sobre o Lucro Líquido (CSLL)
    

To learn more about the assessment of each tax, see the following topics.

-   [PIS and COFINS Assessment (Brazilian Hub Reporting SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160382621123.html)
    
-   [IRPJ and CSLL Assessment (Brazilian Hub Reporting SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160382625483.html)
