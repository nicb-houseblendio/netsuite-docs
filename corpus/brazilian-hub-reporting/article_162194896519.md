To enable Brazilian Hub Reporting (BRHub Reporting) to send EFD-Reinf events, you must set up the e-document certification on your account. The LATAM E-Document Certification SuiteApp is responsible for building certification requests, sending them to tax authorities, and managing the responses.

The Brazilian Hub SuiteApp leverages the same service to enable electronic invoicing in Brazil. For more information, see [E-Document Certification Setup (Brazilian Hub SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_161185202377.html) and [Brazilian Hub](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_158773778810.html).
