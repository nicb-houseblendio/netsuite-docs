With the Brazilian Hub Reporting SuiteApp, you can assess the Programa de Integração Social (PIS) and the Contribuição para o Financiamento da Seguridade Social (COFINS) taxes in three different assessment regimes:

-   Cumulative regime
    
-   Non-cumulative regime
    
-   Cumulative and non-cumulative regime (simultaneously)
    

To learn more about assessing PIS and COFINS, see the following topics:

-   [Assessing PIS and COFINS in the Cumulative or in the Non-cumulative Regimes (Brazilian Hub Reporting SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160398205456.html)
    
-   [Assessing PIS and COFINS in the Cumulative and Non-cumulative Regime (Brazilian Hub Reporting SuiteApp)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160398205845.html)
