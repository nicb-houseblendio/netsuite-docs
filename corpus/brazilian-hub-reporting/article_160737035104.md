The generation of municipal statutory reports compiles electronic invoices for services batches in a file that you can send to the relevant government authorities.

#### To generate a municipal statutory report:

1.  Go to Reports > BRHub - Obrigações Acessórias > Declarações Municipais.
    
2.  In the **Subsidiária** field, select the subsidiary that received the electronic invoices.
    
3.  In the **Tipo de Declaração** field, select the report you want to generate:
    
    -   For Campinas (SP), select **Campinas**.
        
    -   For São Carlos (SP), select **GISS Online**.
        
    -   For Florianópolis (SC), select **GIF-ST Florianópolis** or **DES-ST Florianópolis**.
        
    -   For Curitiba (PR), select **Curitiba - Serviços Tomados**.
        
4.  In the **Tipo** field, select **Notas Recebidas**.
    
    Note:
    
    The option **Notas Emitidas** isn't currently supported.
    
5.  In the **De** field, enter or select a start date to filter the electronic invoices.
    
6.  In the **Até** field, enter or select an end date to filter the electronic invoices.
    
    Tip:
    
    Municipal statutory reports are usually monthly reports. Therefore, you should enter the first and last date of the month in the date fields, respectively.
    
7.  Click **Pesquisar**.
    
    The system lists all matching electronic invoices on the **Transações** sublist.
    
8.  In the **Nome** field, enter a name for the report.
    
9.  In the **Transações** sublist, check the boxes next to the electronic invoices you want to include in the report.
    
10.  Click **Processar**.
     
     A message appears to confirm the system scheduled or started processing the report.
     
11.  Click the **Lotes Gerados** subtab.
     
12.  In the **Status** column, verify the processing status.
     
13.  Click **Pesquisar** to refresh the status information.
     
     Keep refreshing the page until the processing status is **Processado** (processed).
     
14.  In the **XML** column, click **Download** to download the generated report.
     
     Note:
     
     If you updated electronic invoices and need to include them in the report, click **Reprocessar** to generate the report again.
