The Sales Tax Analysis report shows sales tax liabilities on each sales transaction for the date range selected.

The following columns are included in this report:

-   **Transaction Type** - This column shows the type of transaction in this group of line items.
    
-   **Transaction Number** - This column shows the transaction numbers for each sales transaction.
    
-   **Transaction Date** - This column reports the date of each transaction.
    
-   **Total Sales - this column shows the net amount (without tax) for each transaction.**
    
-   **Non Taxable Sales** - This column shows the amount of each transaction that isn't taxable.
    
-   **Taxable Sales** - This column shows the taxable amount of each transaction.
    
-   **Tax** - This column shows the tax amount from each transaction.
    

To see the Sales Tax Analysis report, go to _Reports > Sales Tax US > Sales Tax Analysis_.

A message on the screen indicates that your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.
