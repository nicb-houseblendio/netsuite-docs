To ensure you get the right tax jurisdiction and rate on transactions, link your subsidiaries to the right nexuses. Each tax jurisdiction needs its own nexus record.

Note:

To ensure the provisioning for the Canada province nexus works, set the Canada nexus as the parent when you create a province nexus.

## Setting Up the Puerto Rico and Guam Nexuses {#rc_951949467}

For these US territories, first create a nexus with "United States" in the **Country** field and "Puerto Rico" or "Guam" in the **State** field. For more information about creating nexuses, see [Setting Up Nexuses in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4296038572.html). For Guam, create it as a new state/province/county with "United States" as the country (see instructions below).

Note:

Ensure that you don't select these territories in the **Country** field. This is needed for the correct tax calculation.

#### To create the Guam State/Province/County:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > States/Provinces/Counties > New_.
    
2.  In the **Country** field, select "United States".
    
3.  In the **Full Name** field, enter "Guam".
    
4.  In the **Short Name** field, enter "GU" for Guam.
    

Then, assign these nexuses to the related subsidiaries. For more information, see [Assigning Tax Registrations to a Subsidiary in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4296038273.html).

For information about nexuses, see [Understanding Nexuses in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4283851663.html).

For information about subnexuses, see [Understanding Subnexuses in SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4296036358.html).
