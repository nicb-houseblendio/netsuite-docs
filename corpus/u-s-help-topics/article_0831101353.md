The U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp works with accounts that have enabled the SuiteTax feature. You can use the U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp to filter, set up, and generate your sales tax transaction data in SuiteAnalytics workbooks. The following reports are included:

-   [Sales Tax Liability by Tax Item Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1201025537.html)
    
-   [Sales Tax on Sales Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1201032442.html)
    
-   [Sales Tax Analysis Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1201033532.html)
    
-   [Sales Tax by State Analysis Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0123042036.html)
    
-   [File Ready Report - California](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0323011445.html)
    

The U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp supports use by Single-Instance customers. Previously, only OneWorld customers could use these reports. U.S. Sales Tax Reports has multi-currency functionality to show relevant transactions from outside of the U.S.

## Installing the U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp {#subsect_48115335253}

To use the U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp, you must enable some additional features and install relevant SuiteApps.

Important:

You must enable the following features before installing the U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp.

### Required Features {#subsect_0220051714}

In NetSuite, go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.

-   Go to _Company > International_ and click **Multi-Language**.
    
-   Go to _Tax > Tax & Compliance_ and click **SuiteTax**.
    
-   Go to _Analytics > SuiteAnalytics Workbook_ and click **SuiteAnalytics Workbook**.
    
-   Go to _SuiteCloud > SuiteScript_ and click **Server SuiteScript**.
    

For more information, see:

-   [SuiteCloud Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_N239332.html).
    
-   [SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/preface_1496364245.html).
    

### Required SuiteApps {#subsect_0220051911}

After you have enabled the required features, install the following SuiteApps:

Important:

You must first install these SuiteApps before installing the U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp.

-   SuiteTax Engine
    
-   Localization Assistant
    

The U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp supports use by Single-Instance customers. Previously, only OneWorld customers could use these reports. U.S. Sales Tax Reports has multi-currency functionality to show relevant transactions from outside of the U.S.

When you have completed the prerequisites, you can install the U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp.

#### To install the U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp:

1.  Go to the NetSuite Marketplace.
    
2.  See [Installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539799323.html) for instructions.
    

The U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp is a managed SuiteApp. After you install the SuiteApp, your accounts are automatically updated.

## Using the U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp {#subsect_58133752922}

To generate your sales tax reports, you can access a filtering page to select the report and criteria. You can then use SuiteAnalytics to generate the report as a workbook. For more information, see [SuiteAnalytics Workbook Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1503949328.html).

#### To generate a sales tax report with the U.S. Sales Tax Reports SuiteApp: {#procedure_0914015149}

1.  Go to _Reports > Tax > US Tax Reports_.
    
2.  From the **Report** list, select the sales tax report you want to generate.
    
3.  If you use subsidiaries, from the **Subsidiary** list, select the subsidiary to filter this report.
    
4.  (Optional) If you use a third-party tax filing partner, from the **Filing Partners** list, select a filing partner.
    
5.  To filter the report by date, select a start and end date in the **Date Range** field. You also can click one of the corresponding buttons to select a predefined date range:
    
    -   **This Week**
        
    -   **This Month**
        
    -   **This Quarter**
        
    -   **This Year**.
        
6.  (Optional) If you use accounting books, in the **Accounting Book** field, select the accounting book to filter this report.
    
7.  Click **Generate Report**.
    

The selected report is generated as a workbook. For more information, see [Navigating SuiteAnalytics Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1544220943.html).
