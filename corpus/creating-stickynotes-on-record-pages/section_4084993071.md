The StickyNotes toolbar shows up at the top of a record page when it's enabled.

From the toolbar, you can add a note, change preferences, and open the StickyNotes Board. If there are notes on a record page, you can filter them by priority by clicking the priority colors on the toolbar.

Note:

If you don't see the StickyNotes Toolbar, it might be disabled on the record you're viewing. Contact your account administrator to have StickyNotes enabled on the record page.
