The StickyNotes SuiteApp lets you add notes to record pages and keep track of your notes and others' replies.

You can make it easier to communicate by adding recipients, so only certain people see your notes. They'll get an email when you tag them in a note or when someone adds a reply. You can also make your note public if you want everyone with access to the record page to see it.

With StickyNotes, you can highlight important info or action items on a record with custom-colored notes. See [Customizing StickyNotes from Record Pages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4718581627.html#bridgehead_4748790157) for more information.

You can snap a note to a field to highlight it on a record page. See [Adding a New Note](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4718581627.html#bridgehead_4719364212) for more information.

## Limitation of StickyNotes {#bridgehead_0731020848}

StickyNotes only works on records and transactions in the old NetSuite UI. Be sure to turn off the preference to show the project in the new NetSuite UI.

Go to Setup > Company > General Preferences and make sure the **Show Project In New UI** box isn't checked.
