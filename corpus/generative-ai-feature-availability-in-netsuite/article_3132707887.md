Several factors affect whether generative AI features are available in your NetSuite account. These include:

-   Whether Oracle Cloud Infrastructure Generative AI service is set up for NetSuite in your region
    
-   What languages the large language models (LLMs) used by NetSuite features can support
    
-   Your company's account settings
    
-   Your user role and permissions
    

The following tables provide details about regional and language availability for NetSuite features that use generative AI.
