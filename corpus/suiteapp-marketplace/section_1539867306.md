You can uninstall SuiteApps when you don't need them anymore. To uninstall a SuiteApp, make sure you're logged into the account where it's installed.

#### To uninstall a SuiteApp:

1.  Go to _Customization > SuiteCloud Development > Installed SuiteApp List_.
    
2.  In the Installed SuiteApps list, find the SuiteApp you want to uninstall.
    
3.  For that SuiteApp, select **Uninstall** from its action menu. The uninstallation process starts.
    
    Uninstalling a SuiteApp may take some time. To check the status, see [Viewing SuiteApp Installation or Uninstallation Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539867271.html).
    
    When the uninstall completes, the SuiteApp is removed from the Installed SuiteApps list.
