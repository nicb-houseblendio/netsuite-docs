The Accountant has primary responsibility for managing the company's accounts, including record-keeping and financial reporting.

The Accountant role includes custom dashboards. The following table highlights the key portlets on the Home dashboard.

| Center Type | Custom Center Tabs | Portlets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accounting | 
-   Home
-   Billing
-   Constituents
-   Vendors
-   Payroll and HR
-   Financial
-   NFP Financial Center
