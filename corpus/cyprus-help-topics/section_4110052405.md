Note:

Non-deductible tax codes created by checking the **100% Non-deductible** box on the Tax Code page is not supported. If you want to create nondeductible tax codes that will work with International Tax Reports SuiteApp, see [Setting Up Nondeductible Input Tax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1822730.html).

The following table shows how NetSuite uses the tax codes to get the values for the [Cyprus VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1874088.html).

| Box | Description | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | VAT due on sales and other outputs | Tax amount of sales S, R1, R2 + Tax amount of sales adjustment in box 1 with S Notional tax amount of purchases RC, IS, ESSP, ESSS |
| 2 | VAT due on acquisitions from other EU Member States | Notional tax amount of purchases ES |
| 3 | **Total output VAT (sum of boxes 1 and 2)** | Box 1 + Box 2 |
| 4 | Input VAT (including acquisitions from other EU Member States) | Tax amount of purchases S, R1, R2 + Tax amount of purchases adjustment in box 4 with S + Deduct TAX amount for S - NonDeduct TAX amount for S Notional tax amount of purchases ES, RC, IS, ESSP, ESSS |
| 5 | VAT payable or refundable (difference between boxes 3 and 4) | Box 3 - Box 4 |
| 6 | Value of total sales and other outputs (excluding VAT) (include amounts in boxes 8A + 8B, 9, 10, 11B) | Net amount of sales S, R1, R2, E,O, Z, ES, ESSS, EZ, RC Net amount of purchases IS, ESSP |
| 7 | Value of total purchases and other inputs (excluding VAT) (include amounts in box 11A + 11B) | Net amount of purchases S, R1, R2, E, Z, I, IS, ES, ESSS, EZ, ESSP, RC + Deduct NET amount for S |
| 8A | Total value of all supplies of goods (and directly related services) to other EU Member States (excluding VAT) | Net amount of sales ES, EZ |
| 8B | Total value of services provided to taxable persons in other EU Member States | Net amount of sales ESSS, ESSP |
| 9 | Total value of sales taxed at the rate of 0% (Other than those included in 8A) | Net amount of sales Z, O |
| 10 | Total value of sales which are outside the scope of Cyprus VAT | Editable field |
| 11A | Total value of acquisitions of goods (and directly related services) from other EU Member States (excluding VAT) | Net amount of purchases ES |
| 11B | Total value of services received from taxable persons residence in other EU Member States | Net amount of purchases ESSP, ESSS |
