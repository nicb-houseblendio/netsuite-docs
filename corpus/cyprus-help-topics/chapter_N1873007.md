If you have a Cyprus subsidiary and nexus, and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account as a source for filling in the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales account, you can generate the Cyprus VAT Return (Form 4) in English or Greek. The report can bes and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.

For more information, read the following Cyprus tax topics:
