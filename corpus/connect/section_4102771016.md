As an administrator, you can assign other users with the SuiteAnalytics Connect permission to give them access to the Connect Service.

#### To set up SuiteAnalytics Connect permissions using Manage Roles:

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > User Management > Manage Roles_.
    
2.  Click **Customize** next to the name of the role for which you would like to add the SuiteAnalytics Connect permission.
    
3.  Click the **Setup** subtab under the Permissions subtab.
    
4.  Add the SuiteAnalytics Connect permission.
    
    ![SuiteAnalytics Connect permission.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Connect/suiteanalytics1.png)
    
    Important:
    
    You can't add the SuiteAnalytics Connect permission to a role that has SAML Single Sign-on permission.
    
5.  Click **Add**.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To set up SuiteAnalytics Connect permissions using the Employee record:

1.  Go to _Lists > Employees > Employees_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the name of employee.
    
3.  Click the **Access** subtab.
    
4.  Select the role you would like to grant SuiteAnalytics Connect permission, and click the Open icon next to it.
    
5.  Click the **Setup** subtab under the Permissions subtab.
    
6.  Add the SuiteAnalytics Connect permission.
    
7.  Click **Add**.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
    

For more information about Connect permissions, see [Connect Permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3998867068.html).
