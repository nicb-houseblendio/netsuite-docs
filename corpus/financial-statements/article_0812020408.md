The IFRS 18 income statement layout helps you organize income statement information into IFRS 18 presentation categories. IFRS 18 is an International Financial Reporting Standard for presenting and disclosing information in financial statements. Use this layout with supported Income Statement reports.

IFRS 18 replaces IAS 1 requirements for presenting financial statements. It applies to annual reporting periods beginning on or after January 1, 2027.

NetSuite doesn't determine the appropriate classification for an account. Your organization determines the appropriate classification and configures the layout accordingly.

The **IFRS18 Standard Income Statement Layout** includes the following sections:

-   Operating
    
-   Investing
    
-   Financing
    
-   Income taxes
    
-   Discontinued operations
    

The layout also includes subtotal rows that summarize the income and expenses in the IFRS 18 presentation structure.

The sections are initially empty. Configure the filters that determine which accounts appear in each section. Use only income and expense accounts in the IFRS 18 income statement layout. For more information, [Financial Statement Sections](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2112804.html).

You can also add a formula row to display Gross Profit in the Operating section. Before you add the formula row, configure separate rows for revenue and cost of sales. For more information, see [Editing a Financial Statement Formula Row](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2117576.html).

For more information about customizing financial statements, see [Financial Report Builder](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2105415.html).

The following table shows accounts that might appear in each IFRS 18 section. Your organization determines the appropriate classification for each account.

| IFRS 18 Sections | Example Accounts |
| --- | --- |
| Operating | Product revenue, service revenue, cost of goods sold, salaries expense, rent expense, advertising expense |
| Investing | Dividend income, interest income from investments, gain on investment securities |
| Financing | Interest expense on borrowings, lease interest expense, amortization of debt issuance costs |
| Income taxes | Current income tax expense, deferred tax expense or income |
| Discontinued operations | Loss from discontinued operations, gain on disposal of discontinued operations |

Important:

These examples are illustrative. Account classification depends on your organization's activities, accounting policy, and reporting requirements.

#### To use the IFRS 18 layout:

1.  Go to an Income Statement report that has the IFRS18 Standard Income Statement Layout assigned to it. For more information about layout assignments, see [Financial Reports: Row Layout Assignment Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159644750264.html#subsect_159644791019).
    
2.  Click **Customize**.
    
3.  In the Layout list on the Edit Layout page, select **IFRS18 Standard Income Statement Layout**.
    
4.  Click **Edit Layout**.
    
5.  Add filters, such as Account Name, to each section. Make other adjustments to the report as needed.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
