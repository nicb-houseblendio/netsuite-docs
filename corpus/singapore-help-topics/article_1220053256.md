Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a tax levied on the import of goods collected by Singapore customs. GST-registered businesses in Singapore are required to file electronic GST F5 Returns. With Singapore Localization, you can generate the Singapore GST F5 report for your accounts and subsidiaries with a Singapore nexus.

#### To generate a GST F5 Return report: {#procedure_73174220822}

1.  Go to Reports > Tax > Country Tax Reports.
    
2.  From the **Subsidiary** list, select your subsidiary.
    
3.  From the **Nexus** list, if you have multiple nexuses in your subsidiary, select your Singapore nexus.
    
4.  From the **Report Name** list, select **GST F5 Return**.
    
5.  Select the **Start Period** and **End Period** or **Start Date** and **End Date,** depending on your preference settings.
    
    For more information, see [Setting Up Country Tax Reporting Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1557127083.html).
    
6.  Click **Generate**.
    
7.  On the **Report Execution** subtab, locate the generated report. The latest generated report appears at the top of the list.
    
8.  In the **View | Download** column, wait for the loading bar to complete and change to a **View** link.
    

For more information about generating reports, see [Generating Localized Country Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528250223.html). For more information about reported values on the Singapore GST F5 report, see [Singapore GST F5 Return](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1220053809.html).
