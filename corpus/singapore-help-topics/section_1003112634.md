After completing the prerequisites, you can install the Singapore PEPPOL-Ready e-Invoicing SuiteApp from the SuiteApp Marketplace.

#### To install the Singapore PEPPOL-Ready E-Invoicing SuiteApp: {#procedure_1003112817}

1.  Go to **SuiteApps**.
    
2.  In the **Search** field, enter **Singapore PEPPOL-Ready e-Invoicing**.
    
3.  Click the SuiteApp tile.
    
4.  On the SuiteApp details page, click **Install**.
    
    For more information, see [Installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539799323.html) and [Installing a Bundle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3395142.html).
    

The Singapore PEPPOL-Ready E-Invoicing SuiteApp is a managed SuiteApp. When improvements or new features are released, your account gets updated automatically. For more information about this SuiteApp and its license, contact your NetSuite account representative.
