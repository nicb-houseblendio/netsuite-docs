Note:

Singapore Localization requires SuiteTax and the Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp.

The **Singapore Localization SuiteApp** provides customizations that enable businesses in Singapore to comply with tax and government requirements.

The SuiteApp includes the following features that you can set up and customize:

-   **Invoicing** - Enables you to comply with key business invoicing requirements in Singapore with the following:
    
    -   Generate compliant tax invoices and credit notes in PDF and HTML.
        
    -   Support for invoicing transactions in currencies other than SGD.
        
    -   Support for Customer Accounting.
        
-   **Localized records** - Using custom fields, you can set up subsidiary, customer, vendor, item, tax, and other records with information required by the Singapore government.
    
-   **Reports generation** - Use custom fields and templates to generate and send reports to the Singapore tax authorities. You can:
    
    -   Generate the IRAS Audit File (IAF) required by the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) in text file format.
        
    -   Generate the Singapore Goods and Services Tax (GST) F5 Return.
        
    -   Generate the Singapore Goods and Services Tax (GST) F8 Return.
        
    -   Generate the IAF and GST F5 Return for Multi-Book accounts.
        
    -   Configure the GST tax return template.
        
-   **Tax compliance** - Enables you to comply with key business requirements in Singapore with the following:
    
    -   Automatically provisioned Singapore tax codes
        
    -   Generation of compliant tax reports
        
    -   Electronically submit your GST F5 Return
        
    -   Electronically submit your GST F8 Return
        
-   **SuiteTax compatibility** - If you have SuiteTax enabled in your NetSuite account, you can generate Singapore tax-compliant reports.
