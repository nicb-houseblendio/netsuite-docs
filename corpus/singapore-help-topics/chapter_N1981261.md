You can generate GST reports and tax audit files for Singapore by installing the following NetSuite SuiteApps:

-   [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html) - to generate the Singapore GST F5 Goods and Services Tax Return, and to view sales and purchase reports by tax code.
    
-   [Tax Audit Files](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2073244.html) - to generate a text version or an XML version of the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore Audit File, also known as IRAS Audit File or IAF.
    

The reports can be used for submission to the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore.
