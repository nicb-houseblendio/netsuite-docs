By enabling the **Process E-Document** button, the administrator can combine e-document generation and sending as a single action. You can enable and make this button available on invoices and credit memos. Before enabling the Process E-Document button, the e-document template and sending method must be specified on transactions.

#### To enable the Process E-Document button: {#procedure_1004024622}

1.  As administrator, go to Setup > E-Documents > E-Document Preferences.
    
2.  On the E-Document Preferences page, in the **E-Document Automation Type** dropdown field, select **Generate, Certify, Send**.
    
    Note:
    
    Singapore PEPPOL doesn't require e-document certification, and it is neither enabled nor set up in the SuiteApp. This means the system will ignore or skip the certification process and will only perform e-document generation and sending. Also, the **Certify** button will not be displayed.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    
    The **Process E-Document** button becomes available for transactions such as invoice, credit memo, and purchase order. This means you can generate and send e-documents in one click. For more information, see [Processing E-Documents Automatically for Individual Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163197335497.html).
