Validate the GST details for customer and vendor records before you use them with GST InvoiceNow.

Before you begin, make sure the entity record includes either a UEN or a VAT Registration Number.

#### To validate GST details:

1.  Go to Lists > Relationships > Customers or Lists > Relationships > Vendors.
    
2.  Open an entity record.
    
3.  Click **Check GST InvoiceNow**.
    
    The SuiteApp validates the GST details and displays the validation status in a banner message.
    
4.  On the **E-Document** subtab, review the validation status in the **SG PEPPOL GST Validation Status** field.
    
    Note:
    
    If the entity record does not include a UEN or VAT Registration Number, the SuiteApp displays an error message.
