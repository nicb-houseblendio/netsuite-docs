## Overview {#bridgehead_4256391899}

The following Help topics provide information about NetSuite features that are specific to Singapore business setting and requirements only. General NetSuite features are covered in other chapters and topics.

The NetSuite International edition can be configured to enable the following features and reports for companies doing business in Singapore.

## Certification {#bridgehead_1549263509}

The Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) maintains a list of accounting software that meets its technical requirements. These requirements are described in the e-Tax Guide: [IRAS Accounting Software Register](https://www.iras.gov.sg/irashome/Businesses/Self-Employed/Getting-it-right/IRAS-Accounting-Software-Register/). IRAS updates the list annually. NetSuite is included in the IRAS Accounting Software Register.
