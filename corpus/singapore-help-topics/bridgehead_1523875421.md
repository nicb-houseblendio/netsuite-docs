Open any invoice record or credit memo that you applied the Singapore tax invoice credit note template to. Click the print icon ![Print invoice](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/CountrySpecificFeatures/Singapore_print_icon.png).

The PDF file of the tax invoice or credit note is generated and displayed in a separate browser tab.
