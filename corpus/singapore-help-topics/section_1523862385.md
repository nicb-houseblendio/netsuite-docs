The Singapore Tax Invoice Template and Singapore Credit Note Template are included when you install the Southeast Asia Localization SuiteApp. The templates are stored in the _Customizations > Forms > Advanced PDF/HTML Templates_.

The templates contain FreeMarker placeholder tags for sourcing and displaying required information as well as setting the layout of the transaction document when printed. You can readily apply the templates to invoice and credit memo custom forms, or edit them according to your preferences. For more information about customizing templates, see [Advanced Templates Customization in the Template Editor](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2863632.html).

To learn more about Singapore tax invoice and credit note templates, read the following topics:

-   [Applying Singapore Tax Invoice and Credit Note Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1523875331.html)
    
-   [Singapore Tax Invoice PDF/HTML Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1523864833.html)
    
-   [Singapore Credit Note PDF/HTML Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1523870968.html)
    
-   [Printing the Singapore Tax Invoice and Credit Note](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1523875421.html)
    

Note:

When working on custom fields in Singapore Invoicing PDF/HTML templates and then viewing the Source Code or going to Preview, an error may be encountered. This error may also occur when saving advanced templates. However, you can ignore the error and proceed with saving the template, which will still work despite the error. For more information, see **Template Errors When Saving** under [Saving an Advanced Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156580675374.html).
