## Overview {#bridgehead_156697667760}

The following help topics provide information about NetSuite features that are specific to companies doing business in Taiwan (Province of China). The NetSuite International Edition can be configured with capabilities and reports specific to a Taiwanese business setting and requirements.

To learn more about localized editions of NetSuite, as well as setting up NetSuite accounts and subsidiaries, refer to the following Help topics:

-   [NetSuite Editions Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N270055.html)
    
-   [Set up NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N268052.html)
    
-   [Set up NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N268052.html)
    
-   [Introduction to NetSuite OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N266468.html)\- for companies using NetSuite across multiple subsidiaries and countries.
    

General NetSuite features are covered in other chapters and topics.

Important:

If you don't have sufficient permissions to install SuiteApps or enable features, please contact your account administrator.

## Available SuiteApps {#bridgehead_156697678138}

Some additional features will only become active if you install a specific SuiteApp to your NetSuite account. SuiteApps are applications that extend NetSuite for specific industry and business needs. If you'd like access to specific product area, refer to [Functional Areas](#bridgehead_86144349100) and see which SuiteApps need to be installed. Taiwan (Province of China)-specific features are currently available in the following SuiteApps:

| SuiteApp | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html) Note: This SuiteApp is for accounts without SuiteTax. | Supports VAT and GST reporting and online filing for more than 42 countries across Europe, Asia, and Latin America, all generated in the local format for ease and accuracy of submission. Includes EU Sales List reports, Intrastat reports, sales and purchase reports by tax code, and pre-configured tax codes when a new nexus is created. |
| [Payment Gateway](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3803057063.html) | Provides integration with AsiaPay, including AsiaPay External Checkout, and eWAY credit card payment gateways for Taiwan (Province of China). |
