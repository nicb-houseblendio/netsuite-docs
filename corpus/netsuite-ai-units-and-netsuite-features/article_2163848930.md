After they are provisioned in your account, NetSuite AI Units are consumed each time you use these generative AI features in NetSuite. The following section displays **estimates** of how many AI Units might be consumed when you use a generative AI feature for various transactions.

## AI Units Usage Estimates for Generative AI Features {#subsect_0617044212}

Important:

Depending on the complexity of the request, the actual values for the **AI Units used may vary** from the estimated values. AI Units usage will be visible on the Billing Information page. Go to Setup > Company > View Billing Information.

Be aware that the following usage values are only estimates. The amount of AI Units used in your account for similar transactions may vary from these estimates.

-   **Ask Oracle**
    
    The amount of AI Units consumed when using Ask Oracle varies. For example:
    
    -   Asking simple questions, like "How do I create a vendor credit in NetSuite?" could consume approximately 10 AI Units.
        
    -   Asking analysis questions, like "What is the remaining balance of invoice INV-1008?" could consume between 10-50 AI Units.
        
    -   Asking research questions, like "Show me the top five customers by overdue A/R balance this quarter" could consume between 50-200 AI Units.
        
-   **Case Summary** helps Customer Support Reps quickly understand complex support cases by generating concise, context-rich summaries of cases.
    
    -   Summarizing a case could consume between 5-75 AI Units.
        
    -   For more information, see [Case Summary](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1006034421.html).
        
-   **Narrative Insights** uses generative AI to create concise, contextual summaries of supported NetSuite records and reports.
    
    -   Click Generate Insight on a Customer record to understand the account's current status, recent changes, open items, key transactions, and any billing or credit issues. Narrative Insights could consume between 5-200 AI Units.
        
    -   For more information, see [Narrative Insights Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_135342304.html).
        
-   **Prompt Studio** lets authorized users create, manage, and test prompt-based AI experiences, so you can standardize how AI assists with common writing tasks.
    
    -   You could create a prompt action for your customer support reps that summarizes a case and suggests the next best response. Testing the prompt action could consume between 5-10 AI Units.
        
    -   For more information, see [Prompt Studio](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160809601.html).
        
-   **SuiteScript 2.1 N/llm Module** lets developers send prompts to an LLM and receive responses to build custom generative AI workflows in NetSuite.
    
    -   For example, a SuiteApp script sends a prompt like"Create a concise renewal reminder email for this customer using the subscription end date, renewal amount, and account manager name from the current record." Something like this request could consume from 5-25 AI Units.
        
    -   For more information, see [N/llm Module](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_9123730083.html) and [llm.ModelFamily](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1014101247.html).
        
-   **Text Enhance** helps you draft, revise, translate, summarize, and adapt text.
    
    -   Requesting that Text Enhance reword a message from your support team to your customer could consume between 5-10 AI Units.
        
    -   For more information, see [Text Enhance](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_2171112518.html).
        
-   **Transaction Matching Assistant** uses rules to find multiple possible matches, recommends the most likely one (and explains why). It leaves the final choice to the user.
    
    Note:
    
    Transaction Matching Assistant is available only to eligible customers with access to supported AI features.
    
    -   For example, a bank transaction for $1,250 indicates 'ACH payment to Acme Supplies for invoice 4812'. NetSuite has multiple possible $1,250 payment matches for the same vendor. An example like this could consume between 5-10 AI Units.
        
    -   For more information, see [Transaction Matching Assistant](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_40170235282.html).
