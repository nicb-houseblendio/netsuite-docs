NetSuite Connector enables you to map the **Call for Pricing** field on a BigCommerce product listing. This feature uses **is\_price\_hidden** attribute in the NetSuite Connector product mapper. You can find the **Call for Pricing** attribute in BigCommerce and the corresponding mapping in NetSuite Connector in the following:

#### To map Call for Pricing message in NetSuite Connector:

1.  Enable the **Call for Pricing** attribute in BigCommerce. See [Adding Products](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Adding-Products?language=en_US#details) for more information.
    
2.  Add a mapping with the attribute **is\_price\_hidden** in the NetSuite Connector product mapper. For more information, see [Setting Up Full Product Sync Mapping](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164968183329.html).
