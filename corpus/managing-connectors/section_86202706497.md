To integrate NetSuite Connector and Amazon Vendor Central, you must authorize NetSuite Connector on your Amazon Vendor Central account.

Before proceeding, make sure you have added the Amazon Vendor Central connector to your account. To add a new connector, contact your NetSuite account manager.

#### To authorize NetSuite Connector on Amazon Vendor Central account: {#procedure_0117082651}

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select Amazon Vendor Central connector and then select the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Settings > Credentials.
    
4.  In **Vendor Central Site**, select the country.
    
5.  In **Vendor Code**, enter your vendor code or merchant ID.
    
6.  Click **Login with Amazon**.
    
    You are taken to the Amazon Vendor Central website.
    
7.  Follow the instructions on the website.
    
8.  You are redirected back to the Credentials screen of the Amazon Vendor Central connector's relevant account in NetSuite Connector. The Account Authorized button indicates that NetSuite Connector has been authorized and the Amazon Vendor Central account is linked. If you get a failure message, contact NetSuite support.
