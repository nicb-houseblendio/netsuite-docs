NetSuite Connector does not mark the order as Complete in Magento 2 after the order is shipped to the customer. This is because Magento 2 should be the one to mark it as complete after an invoice is generated for the order. To have NetSuite Connector initiate the creation of an invoice in Magento 2 when it fulfills the order, see [Creating a Magento 2 Order Invoice in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163904393779.html).
