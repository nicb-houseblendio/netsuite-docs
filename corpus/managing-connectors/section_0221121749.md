Orders in Amazon Vendor Central are categorized by the vendor type. The following are the vendor types and how orders for each vendor type are handled through the Order Sync:

-   **Direct Fulfillment**
    
    Direct Fulfillment orders are shipped directly to the customer. Since the destination is a customer address rather than an Amazon warehouse, these orders must contain the customer address for fulfillment.
    
-   **Retail Procurement**
    
    Retail Procurement vendors sell items in bulk directly to Amazon. In the Retail Procurement program, Amazon places the order and the vendor ships the products to an Amazon warehouse.
