NetSuite Connector supports all Amazon categories. Amazon requires sellers to create separate templates to categorize their products. The Amazon category enables you to map all the common fields such as price, quantity, and SKU. You can add all the relevant Amazon categories in the Product Mappings page. Each category you add can have its own mappings.

## Adding a Category {#subsect_98004734506}

You can add categories for mapping from Amazon in the Product Mappings page.

#### To add a category {#procedure_0221125001}

1.  Log in to [app.farapp.com](https://app.farapp.com).
    
2.  Select the Amazon Vendor Central connector and the relevant account.
    
3.  Go to Mappings > Products.
    
4.  In the Product Mappings page, click the **Category** drop-down field.
    
5.  The drop-down list contains all the added categories. Click **Add Category**.
    
6.  Select the category you want to add from the list.
    
7.  After adding a category, you need to add at least one mapping for the category.
