When you first install NetSuite Connector for Outlook add-in, you need to integrate NetSuite Connector for Outlook with your NetSuite account to facilitate syncs.

#### To log in to NetSuite in the add-in

After setting up NetSuite Connector for Outlook, you need to pin the add-in to Microsoft Outlook. This would keep the add-in pinned and accessible when switching between emails in Outlook. If a mail or event item is open in Outlook, the add-in will display found matching records in the Sync screen.

1.  Open the NetSuite Connector for Outlook add-in in Microsoft Outlook. Click **Login with NetSuite**.
    
    Note:
    
    NetSuite Connector for Outlook displays the NetSuite login screen on a new window. If the new window does not open automatically, check the Allow the add-in to open a new window option. Click **Allow** to proceed.
    
2.  In the NetSuite login screen, enter your NetSuite email address and password then click **Log In**.
    
3.  Confirm that the account and role that you want to integrate with NetSuite Connector for Outlook is selected. You can select specific accounts and roles by clicking **Choose Account** or **Choose Role**.
    
4.  Click **Allow**.
