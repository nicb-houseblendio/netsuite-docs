Email addresses in emails and events are matched with corresponding Customer, Contact, and Opportunity records in NetSuite. All email addresses found in the following email fields are included:

-   To
    
-   From
    
-   CC
    

The email addresses in the event or email message are compared with the email address associated with the corresponding records in NetSuite for matching.

| **NetSuite Record** | **Matching Criteria** |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Match with customer email address. |
| Lead | Match with the lead record email address. |
| Prospect | Match with the prospect record email. |
| Contact | Match with contact email address. |
| Opportunity | Match with customer or any contact email address in the customer record. |

For events, only an Employee Record can be the Event Organizer. The Event Attendees can be any existing records in NetSuite. Email addresses that do not have a corresponding record are not added as attendees.
