You can use the add-in to quickly update NetSuite records. When a matching record is displayed in NetSuite Connector for Outlook, click the three-dot menu, then click Edit to update the NetSuite record.

#### To update a NetSuite record in NetSuite Connector for Outlook:

1.  In NetSuite Connector for Outlook, click the three-dot menu on a displayed record, then click **Edit**.
    
2.  In the Edit Record screen, update the fields as needed.
    
    Note:
    
    Available fields in the edit record screen can be customized. You can add fields that you frequently update.
    
    See [Customizing Record Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0403070649.html) for more information.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    
    You can view the details of the updated record on the Summary page.
