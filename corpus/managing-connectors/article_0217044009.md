Before you connect Loop Returns with your Shopify store, install the Loop app and make sure you can access it.

#### To integrate Shopify connector with Loop Returns:

1.  Log in to your Shopify Administrator account.
    
2.  In the search bar, type Loop Returns & Exchanges and open the app listing.
    
3.  Click **Install App** and follow the on-screen prompts.
    
4.  After the installation, click **Log in to Loop**.
    
5.  Enter your Loop Returns credentials and complete the login process.
    
6.  Upon successful login, you'll be redirected to your **Loop Returns Dashboard**.
