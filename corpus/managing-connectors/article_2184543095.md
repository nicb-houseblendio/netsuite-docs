You can create or edit NetSuite Records from NetSuite Connector for Outlook. When you click the three-dot menu button on matching records, you can view or edit the record in the add-in. You can also open the record in NetSuite.

When viewing, editing, or creating a record in NetSuite Connector for Outlook, only the selected fields for that record type can be viewed or modified. For more information, see [Customizing Record Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0403070649.html).
