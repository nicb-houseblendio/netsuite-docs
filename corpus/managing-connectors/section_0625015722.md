Outlook Connector Premium uses connector roles to control what users can view and update. Assign connector roles based on each user's responsibilities.

| Role | Intended User | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admin Connector User | Users who set up manage NetSuite Connector for Outlook. | Can view app-wide settings and update tenant-wide settings. Can manage broader Auto Sync configuration. |
| Outlook Connector User | Standard users of NetSuite Connector for Outlook. | Can create, view, and manage their own Auto Sync rules. |

If you cannot view the expected settings, rule actions, activity details, or retry options, contact your NetSuite Connector for Outlook administrator.
