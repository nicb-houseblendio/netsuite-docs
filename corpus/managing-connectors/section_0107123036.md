The NetSuite Connector SuiteApp supports posting dimension metafields from NetSuite to Shopify, allowing businesses to keep product dimensions in sync across both platforms.

## NetSuite Field Requirements {#subsect_0107123230}

To support dimension metafields, the following are the requirements:

-   Field Type: Use a **Free-Form** field in NetSuite to store your dimension values.
    
-   Format: Enter the value as a unit.
    
    Example: 10:mm (for 10 millimeters) or 2.5:m (for 2.5 meters)
    

## Supported Unit Types {#subsect_0107123647}

You can use any of the following unit types when using dimension metafields for Shopify through NetSuite:

-   mm: millimeters
    
-   cm: centimeters
    
-   m: meters
    
-   ft: feet
    
-   in: inches
    
-   yd; yards
    

Ensure you only use the supported unit types listed above.
