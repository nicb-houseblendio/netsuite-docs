Amazon has the following two platforms for selling products on its marketplace:

-   Seller Central
    
-   Vendor Central
    

The Amazon connector supports the Seller Central platform. Seller Central is intended for B2C (Business to Consumer) businesses because it lets you sell directly to Amazon customers.

If you are looking for information about Amazon Vendor Central, see [Managing the Amazon Vendor Central Connector in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0113014721.html).
