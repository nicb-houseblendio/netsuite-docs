The exchange process is handled by Refund Sync in Shopify POS orders. You need to enable Refund sync on all POS orders. When processing exchanges, Refund sync creates a cash refund on the original cash sale in NetSuite. New cash sales are then created for the exchanged items.

Note:

Discount applications are currently only available on the item level. Cart-level discounts are not supported.
