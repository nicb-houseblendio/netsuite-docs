This section contains the settings that you need to configure for Simphony connector in NetSuite Connector.

## Configuring Simphony Order settings {#subsect_0426012515}

You are required to configure default discount settings and NetSuite SKU field id to establish a successful connection with the storefront.

#### To configure default discount settings:

1.  Log in to [https://app.farapp.com/](https://app.farapp.com/)
    
2.  Go to Simphony > Settings > Orders.
    
3.  In the Discount settings subtab, enter the NetSuite Internal Id.
    
4.  Click **Save.**
    

#### To configure NetSuite SKU field:

1.  Log in to [https://app.farapp.com/](https://app.farapp.com/)
    
2.  Go to Simphony > Settings > Orders.
    
3.  In the General settings subtab, enter the NetSuite SKU field id.
    
4.  Click **Save.**
