You can use the Amazon Inventory Adjustments feature to adjust the inventory of items in NetSuite at a specific location to reflect the inventory for these items in Amazon warehouses.

When you enable this feature, you have the option to either only receive inventory adjustment reports and then make the manual adjustment yourself, or also run an inventory adjustment sync to automatically update NetSuite with the inventory adjustments.

The inventory adjustment feature is an add-on feature that requires additional integration and ongoing fee. To add inventory adjustment sync to your account, contact your NetSuite account representative.
