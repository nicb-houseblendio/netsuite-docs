The Out-of-Stock feature in eBay lets NetSuite Connector send 0 quantity for your product listings. When this feature is enabled, NetSuite Connector can decrease the inventory below 1. If you sell eBay items on other connectors, this feature prevents overselling.

For more information, check the following eBay documentation [https://developer.ebay.com/DevZone/guides/features-guide/default.html#development/listings-useoutofstock.html](https://developer.ebay.com/DevZone/guides/features-guide/default.html#development/listings-useoutofstock.html).

Note:

After enabling the Out-of-Stock feature, reload the item from NetSuite to NetSuite Connector manually. Else, you can make some change to the item and trigger the sync of the item. Perform this action if your item was excluded in NetSuite Connector during product sync because the Out of Stock option was not enabled in eBay. For more information about reloading items, read [Reloading Products in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164044165803.html).
