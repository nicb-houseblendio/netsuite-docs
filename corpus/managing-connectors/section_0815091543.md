The invoice sync sends invoices to Amazon. When the invoice is generated in NetSuite, NetSuite Connector posts the invoice to Amazon Vendor Central. Each invoice posted to Amazon returns a transaction ID for reference.

The invoices synced from NetSuite to Amazon can be viewed in Data Flows > Invoices. When retrieving invoice details, you need to enter the Invoice Internal ID.
