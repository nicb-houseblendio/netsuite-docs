Amazon has the following platforms for selling products on its marketplace:

-   Seller Central
    
-   Vendor Central
    

The Amazon Vendor Central connector supports the Vendor Central platform. With Vendor Central, you sell your stock directly to Amazon, who then resells your products. Vendor Central is intended for Business to Business (B2B) selling.

If you are looking for information about Amazon Seller Central, see [Managing the Amazon Connector in NetSuite Connector](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162627481052.html).
