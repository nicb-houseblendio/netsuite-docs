NetSuite Field Service Management (FSM), formerly NextService, helps you coordinate and track services performed outside the office, like repairs, installations, and maintenance. FSM is an end-to-end solution that helps field service companies:

-   Oversee users, data, and business processes for field teams
    
-   Simplify scheduling
    
-   Automate inventory and customer asset management
    
-   Increase real-time data visibility and integrity
    

## Key Benefits of FSM {#subsect_0302024142}

FSM offers the following benefits:

-   **Drag-and-drop scheduling** - Assign tasks to technicians and adjust workloads by moving tasks on the Schedule Board. You can check technician availability instantly, with no need for spreadsheet or email updates.
    
-   **Real-time mobile access for technicians** - Technicians can get task details, update statuses, and access customer and asset history from anywhere with FSM Mobile. All updates sync directly with NetSuite.
    
-   **Automate inventory and asset management** - Track spare parts, van inventory, and customer equipment in real time. When technicians use inventory during a task, it's recorded right away so your stock information stays accurate.
    
-   **Task and job lifecycle tracking** - Create, assign, monitor, and track tasks for field service jobs. Schedulers get live updates as tasks move through the workflow.
    
-   **Integrated customer and asset records** - View comprehensive information about customers, service histories, and supported assets without toggling between systems.
    
-   **Streamlined dispatch and task monitoring** - Schedulers assign, reschedule, or reassign tasks in one place, and then track progress and completion on one dashboard.
    
-   **Consistent data capture and reporting** - Make data collection consistent, across the field and office, for more accurate customer billing and reports.
    
-   **Offline task access and sync** - Technicians can continue working and updating tasks without network coverage. Updates sync automatically when they're back online.
    
-   **Configurable** - Customize FSM to meet your requirements using the Field Service Configuration Record.
    
-   **End-to-end visibility** - Managers get real-time updates on technician locations, task progress, and what parts are used, helping them run operations smoothly and provide better service.
