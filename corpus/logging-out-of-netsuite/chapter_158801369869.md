For security reasons, you should log out of NetSuite manually. To do this, hover over your name in the top right corner and choose **Log Out** from the dropdown list:

If you're inactive in the UI for a while, NetSuite logs you out automatically. By default, your session times out after 180 minutes.

An Administrator can set a custom session timeout. For more information, see [User Interface (UI) Sessions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1534882691.html).
