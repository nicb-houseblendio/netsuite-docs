To set a reminder when you respond to an event invitation, you must log in and accept the invitation in your account. You can't set reminders when using the links included in the email invitation.

#### To set up event reminders when responding to an event invitation: {#procedure_N505542}

1.  Log in to your NetSuite account.
    
2.  On the Home page, click the link in the Calendar portlet to respond to the event invitation.
    
    The event record appears in view mode with buttons to respond to the invitation.
    
    For a complete list of ways in which to respond to an event invitation, see [Responding to Event Invitations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N504722.html).
    
3.  Click **Accept** to accept the invitation, or click **Tentative** to tentatively accept the invitation.
    
    A window pops up where you can choose to email the event organizer and set your reminder for the event.
    
4.  Choose whether to send the event organizer an email including your response. If you decide to email the organizer, you can add your own message to the email.
    
5.  In the **Reminder** field, select the amount of time before the event's start time that you want to get a popup or email reminder.
    
6.  In the **Reminder Type** field, select which kind of reminder you want to get: popup, email or none.
    
    Note:
    
    If you select **None** in either the Reminder field or the Reminder Type field, you won't get a reminder.
    
7.  Click **OK**.
    

Your event now appears on your calendar with a reminder icon. Note that if you decline an event invitation, you can't set a reminder and the event doesn't show on your calendar.
