To view NetSuite events in Microsoft Outlook, you can add it as an Internet calendar.

#### To add an internet calendar to Outlook: {#procedure_41183710283}

1.  Open your calendar in Outlook.
    
2.  Right-click **My Calendars**.
    
3.  Select Add Calendar > From Internet.
    
    ![Add Calendar From Internet menu path.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/OutlookAddiCalCalendar.png)
4.  Copy the URL that was generated on the Calendar Preferences page in NetSuite and paste it. For detailed information about setting up your NetSuite calendar for sharing, see [Sharing Your Calendar with iCal-Compatible Devices and Applications](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3930228959.html).
    
5.  Click **OK**.
