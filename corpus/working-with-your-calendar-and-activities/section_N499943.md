NetSuite lets you manage your schedule using a calendar you can customize. The Activities page displays events you create in NetSuite automatically on your calendar.

To display tasks and phone calls on your calendar, check the Reserve Time box on task and phone call records, and then enter the start and end times.

![Activities page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/Calendars_CalendarView.png)

Read the following topics for information about how to set up and use your NetSuite calendar:

-   [Setting Your Calendar Time Zone](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N500123.html) describes how to share events and meetings with your co-workers across the country or around the world.
    
-   [Setting Calendar Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N500263.html) describes how to make appointments visible on your calendar and how to share your calendar with other NetSuite users in your organization.
    
-   [Navigating Your Calendar](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N500710.html) describes how to best use the calendar page.
    
-   [Synchronizing Your Calendar](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N501027.html) describes how to use the NetSuite for Outlook client for viewing your appointments and making changes to your schedule without logging in to NetSuite. You must use the Outlook Integration feature to take advantage of this functionality.
