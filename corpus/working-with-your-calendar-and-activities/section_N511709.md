When you use the Telephony Integration feature, you can initiate calls from the following pages in NetSuite:

-   Relationship record lists
    
-   Phone call list
    
-   Phone call records
    
-   Relationship records
    
-   Saved search results that include phone numbers
    
-   Subtab lists on records that include phone numbers
    
-   Any record, list, or saved search with a phone custom field
    

Note:

The Telephony Integration feature must be setup and enabled by your account administrator. See [Using Telephony Integration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N511129.html). After the feature is enabled, users must set their personal preferences. See [Personal Preferences for Telephony](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N484162.html).

To make a call, click on the phone icon or underlined phone number from a list or record.

To end the call, hang up or click the next phone number to end the current call and begin a new call.

For example, you go to _Activities > Scheduling > Phone Calls > Search_ and click Create Saved Search to create a search of the phone calls you need to make today. In your search results, click the phone icon next to the first phone number. Your phone rings to signal the call is ready. When you pick up the getr, the phone number is dialed, and your call is connected. To end the call, click the phone number of the next customer and continue with the process.
