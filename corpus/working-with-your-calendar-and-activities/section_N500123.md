The first step in setting up your NetSuite calendar is to make sure your calendar reflects the time zone in which you work. The time zone you choose affects events and the data returned by reports and searches for date fields with timestamps.

#### To set your Calendar Time Zone: {#procedure_N500135}

1.  Go to _Home > Set Preferences_.
    
2.  On the **General** subtab, in the Time Zone field, select your time zone
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

The next step in setting up your calendar is [Setting Calendar Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N500263.html).
