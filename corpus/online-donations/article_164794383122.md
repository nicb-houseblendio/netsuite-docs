The Online Donations SuiteApp lets you integrate your online donations right into NetSuite. When someone donates on your nonprofit website, the Online Donations SuiteApp automatically creates a donation transaction in NetSuite and sends out a receipt

This SuiteApp uses the Payment Link feature to accept online donations and lets donors track their donations with the Customer Center role.

The SuiteApp enables you to perform the following tasks:

-   Create a customizable HTML donation page that you can set up and add to your nonprofit's website.
    
-   Set up different donation programs and amount options for your donors.
    
-   Collect online donation payments with the Payment Link feature.
    
-   Accept one-time, anonymous, and recurring online donations.
    
-   Manage donation transactions in NetSuite and email donation confirmations.
    
-   Track, view, and analyze online donation data with predefined SuiteAnalytics workbooks.
    

The Online Donations SuiteApp supports all available languages in the Multi-Language feature of NetSuite. To set the language preference in NetSuite, see [Choosing a Language for Your NetSuite User Interface](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N479109.html).
