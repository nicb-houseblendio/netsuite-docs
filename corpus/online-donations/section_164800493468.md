The Online Donation SuiteApp lets you accept one-time, anonymous, and recurring donations from your nonprofit website.

Using the Payment Link feature, the SuiteApp lets you capture payments from online donations submitted through the Online Donation HTML page. When a donor clicks the Donate Now button, they're taken to the Payment Link page to enter their payment details.

The Payment Link page also shows a summary of the invoice for the online donation. Depending on your donation preferences and setup, NetSuite can also create a customer record.

After the invoice is processed, NetSuite emails the donor. If the payment goes through, the donor gets a Payment Link Payment Confirmation email. If not, they get a Payment Link Payment Declined Notification email.

Important:

You must not copy (Make Copy function) any transaction submitted through the Online Donation HTML page. This action may result to potential issues when processing your donation transactions.
