The Online Donations SuiteApp provides an Online Donations Report Workbook and a Donor Report Workbook. These workbooks include predefined source date, criteria, charts, and tables.

-   [Online Donations Report Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164877786489.html)
    
-   [Donor Report Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164877807278.html)
    

To access an online donation workbook, go to the Workbooks subtab of the Analytics home page and click the workbook link from the Workbook list.

For more information about SuiteAnalytics Workbook, see [SuiteAnalytics Workbook Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1503949328.html).
