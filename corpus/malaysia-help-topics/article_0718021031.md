The Malaysia Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp lets you generate and certify e-documents for transactions that meet IRBM compliance. For more information about supported transactions, see [Electronic Invoices](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1023120817.html).

For self-billed invoice transactions, you must first set up a vendor record, for more information see [Setting Up a Vendor Record for Self-billed Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0917104107.html)

After you've set up the subsidiary and customer records, you can access credit memo, cash refund, debit note, and invoice records. You can generate e-documents, send them for certification, and get a UUID and Long ID.
