Here are the scope, limitations, and best practices for using the Malaysia Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp.

## Scope {#subsect_0722123503}

The SuiteApp covers the following:

-   You can use the Malaysia Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp only for the following transactions:
    
    -   Credit Memo
        
    -   Cash Refund
        
    -   Debit Note
        
    -   Invoice
        
    -   Self-billed Credit Note
        
    -   Self-billed Debit Note
        
    -   Self-billed Invoice (Vendor Bills)
        
-   You can use the SuiteApp for companies only in Malaysia.
    

## Limitations {#subsect_0722123711}

Here are the SuiteApp's limitations:

-   The payment item type isn't supported.
    
-   Legacy Tax accounts don't support tax groups.
    
-   In SuiteTax accounts, negative expense amounts aren't allowed when withholding tax is applicable to a transaction.
