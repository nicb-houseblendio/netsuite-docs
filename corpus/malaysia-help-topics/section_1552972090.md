If amounts in a transaction are in a foreign currency, the Malaysia invoice and credit note templates insert an additional column in the item details section, indicating amounts converted to the base currency, Malaysian ringgit (MYR). The SST summary table also displays amounts converted to Malaysian ringgit (MYR).
