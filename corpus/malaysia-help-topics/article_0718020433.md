If your NetSuite account has an active Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp license, you can use electronic invoicing features for multiple countries. If your account doesn't have an active license, you can still use the Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp for free. You can use the Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp for free if you intend to send e-documents to only one country. For more information, see [Defining the E-Document Country for Free Use](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4613215788.html).

Important:

To use the Malaysia Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp, you must have the NetSuite Electronic Business Extension with NetSuite Electronic Document Processing Cloud Service license. If you do not have license the, please contact your account administrator.
