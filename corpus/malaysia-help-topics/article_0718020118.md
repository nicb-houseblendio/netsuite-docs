Before installing the Malaysia Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp, you must ensure you've met all the prerequisites required for the SuiteApp. For more information, see [Prerequisites for Installing the Malaysia Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0718015959.html).

You can install the Malaysia Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp from the SuiteApp Market place.

#### To install the Malaysia Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp:

1.  In NetSuite, go to SuiteApps.
    
2.  In the **Search Apps** input field, enter **Malaysia Electronic Invoicing**.
    
3.  Click **Malaysia Electronic Invoicing**.
    
4.  In the top right of the SuiteApp details page, click **Install**.
    
    For more information, see [Installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539799323.html)
    

Important:

The Malaysia Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp is a managed SuiteApp. When the SuiteApp has new features or upgrades, your account is automatically updated. For more information about the SuiteApp and its license, contact your NetSuite account administrator.
