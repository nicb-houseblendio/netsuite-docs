The status of an e-document is indicated in the **E-Document Status** field, on the **E-Document** subtab of a transaction. The e-document status describes the current processing state of the transaction's e-document. IRBM certification status is displayed in the **Malaysia Electronic Invoicing** subtab.

Also on the E-Document subtab, is the **E-Document Audit Trail** subtab, which logs all changes to an e-document.

Note:

You must check the IRBM Response Errors and IRBM Response Warnings fields that are populated in case of errors and warnings from IRBM.

For more information, see [Outbound E-Document Statuses](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543313021.html).
