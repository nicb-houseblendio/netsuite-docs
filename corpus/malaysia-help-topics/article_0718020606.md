You must setup a subsidiary record before you start using the Malaysia Electronic Invoicing SuiteApp. You must have access to Administrator access to setup the subsidiary record.

#### To setup the subsidiary record:

1.  Go to Setup > Company > Subsidiaries.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the required subsidiary.
    
3.  Fill in the required fields on the subsidiary record.
    
4.  Make sure the country selected for the subsidiary is Malaysia.
    
5.  Perform one of the following actions to add tax registration number:
    
    -   **For Legacy Tax accounts** - Enter the customer's tax registration number in the **Malaysia Entity TIN Number** field on the **Malaysia Electronic Invoicing** subtab.
        
    -   **For SuiteTax accounts** - enter the customer's tax registration number in the **Tax Registration Number** field on the **Tax Registration** subtab of the respective nexus.
        
6.  In the **Legal Name** field, enter the legal name of the subsidiary.
    
7.  From the **Malaysia Identification Type** list, select one of the following identification types:
    
    -   Business Registration Number
        
    -   MyKad Identification Number
        
    -   MyKAS Identification Number
        
    -   MyPR Identification Number
        
    -   MyTentera Identification Number
        
8.  In the **Malaysia Identification Number** field, enter the number that matches the identification type you select from the **Malaysia Identification Type** list.
    
9.  In the **Malaysia SST ID** field, enter the supplier's SST registration number.
    
10.  In the **Malaysia Tourism Tax Registration Number** field, enter the subsidiary's tourism tax registration number. This only applies to tourist operators.
     
11.  From the **Malaysia MSIC Code** list, select the code and description that match the subsidiary's business type and activity.
     
12.  In the **Address** subtab, fill in the required fields for local Malaysian address.
     
13.  Click **Save**.
     
     Note:
     
     You must complete the NetSuite Electronic Business (NSEB) setup and activate the required mandates for the subsidiary. For more information about setting up NSEB and mandates, see [NetSuite Electronic Business Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0313022549.html).
