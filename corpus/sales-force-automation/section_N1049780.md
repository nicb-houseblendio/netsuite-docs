The Alternate Sales Amount (ASA) feature lets you track alternate sales amounts. You'll see these amounts on sales orders, estimates, and opportunities and you can use them to calculate commission, track sales forecasts, and establish quotas.

Here are a few examples of when you could use ASA:

-   Your company recognizes annualized, monthly, or quarterly GAAP revenue. With ASA and the Revenue Recognition feature, use custom code to calculate how much of a sales amount NetSuite recognizes in this period.
    
-   You calculate commission and measure quotas on gross profit. You can add a custom field to item records for the average item cost. On your transactions, use custom code to calculate the difference between the sales price and the item's average cost. You can create commission schedules based on Alternate Sales Amounts or percent of ASA quota fulfilled.
