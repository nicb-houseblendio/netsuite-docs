The Opportunities to Close Summary report lists the projected and weighted totals of all open opportunities. It also provides the expected close dates within the period range you select for each sales rep.

To view the Opportunities to Close Summary report, go to _Opportunities > Reports > Opportunities to Close_.

A message appears indicating that your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report shows the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.
