To print estimates, you need Adobe Acrobat or Adobe Reader.

#### To print a single estimate: {#procedure_N1073572}

1.  Go to _Opportunities > Transactions > Estimates > New_.
    
2.  On the Estimate, complete the information as appropriate.
    
3.  Click **Print.**
    
    NetSuite opens the form as a PDF.
    
4.  Click the **Print** button in the Adobe Acrobat or Adobe Reader window.
    
5.  To print multiple copies, specify the number on the print message that appears.
    
6.  Click **OK**.
    

You can also print estimates by going to the _Opportunities > Transactions > Estimates_. On the Estimates page, click **Print** next to the estimate you want to print. You can also save the estimate as a PDF from the Adobe Acrobat window.

#### To print multiple estimates: {#procedure_N1073688}

1.  Go to _Customers > Transactions > Print Checks and Forms_.
    
2.  On the Print Checks and Forms page, click **Estimates**.
    
3.  On the Print Estimates page, if you use locations, choose the location you want to print estimates from.
    
4.  Check the **Allow Reprinting** box to be able to reprint previously printed transactions.
    
    When you check this box, all documents appear at the bottom of the page in segments.
    
5.  Check the box in the **Print** column by each estimate you want to print.
    
6.  Click **Print**.
    
    If you don't use the **Download PDF Files** preference at Home > Set Preferences, a preview of your estimates appears in Adobe Acrobat.
    
    If you do use the **Download PDF Files** preference, you must save your estimates first and then open them with Adobe Acrobat.
    
7.  Click the printer button in the Adobe Acrobat frame.
    
8.  If you want to print multiple copies, specify the number on the print message that appears.
    
9.  Click **OK**.
