The Pipeline by Sales Rep Summary report shows the projected and weighted totals of all open opportunities and estimates in each sales rep's pipeline. It also shows the expected close dates within a date range you define.

The projected total of a transaction is the total amount that appears on the transaction. The weighted total is the product of the projected total and the probability of close.

For more information, see [Estimates in the Forecast and Pipeline](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1047186.html) and [Opportunities in the Forecast and Pipeline](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1047903.html).

With Narrative Insights, you can use generative AI to summarize the report, helping you analyze pipeline performance, monitor trends for each sales representative, and identify opportunities that drive sales success.

The generated insights may include:

-   The top open deal with the largest value
    
-   The overall pipeline value for all open deals
    
-   The number of open deals across all sales reps
    
-   Top-performing sales reps based on total pipeline value
    

To generate an insight on the Pipeline by Sales Rep Summary report, the **Enable Narrative Insights** preference must be enabled in the **Narrative Insights** tab of the AI Preferences page. This preference is enabled by default. For more information, see [Managing Narrative Insights Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6150024470.html).

For more information about Narrative Insights, see [Narrative Insights Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_135342304.html).

## To view the Pipeline by Sales Rep Summary report: {#bridgehead_N1104156}

Go to _Opportunities > Reports > Pipeline by Sales Rep_.

A popup message appears to let you know your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.

Note:

This report does not support reporting by period even when the Report by Period preference is set to All Reports. You can configure the Report by Period preference. Go to _Home > Set Preferences_. The preference is on the Analytics subtab.

## To generate an insight on the Pipeline by Sales Rep Summary report: {#bridgehead_1124044242}

1.  Filter the report to include only the information you want the insight to reflect.
    
2.  Click the **Generate Insight** button.
    
    The Narrative Insights dialog is displayed. You can generate the insight again at any time by clicking the **Generate Insight** button.
    

Important:

Insights may differ each time they're generated, even when you're viewing the same data.
