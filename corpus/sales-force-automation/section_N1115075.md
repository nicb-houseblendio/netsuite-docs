The Sales Orders by Sales Team Detail report lists the approved sales orders and return authorizations for each sales team in your company for the date range you choose. Each sales rep's contribution percentage total appears for each sales order.

Sales orders and return authorizations appear multiple times on this report. Each sales order and return authorization appears one time for each sales rep on the sales team.

## To see the Sales Orders by Sales Team Detail report: {#bridgehead_N1115104}

Go to _Reports > Sales Orders > Sales Orders by Sales Team > Detail_.

A popup message appears to let you know your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.

Note:

This report does not support reporting by period even when the Report by Period preference is set to All Reports. You can configure the Report by Period preference. Go to _Home > Set Preferences_. The preference is on the Analytics subtab.
