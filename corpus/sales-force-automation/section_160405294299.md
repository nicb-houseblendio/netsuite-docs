## The Last Sales Activity Field {#bridgehead_N1101508}

The **Last Sales Activity** field is shown on lead, prospect, customer, contact, and opportunity records. The field displays the date of the sales activity that was last performed for those records. To see the sales activity record, click the **Last Sales Activity** link.

The date of each sales activity displayed in the **Last Sales Activity** field is dependent on their completion status, as listed in the following table:

| Sales activity | Complete | Incomplete |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing Campaign | Date of completion | \- |
| Task | Date of completion | Start date |
| Phone Call | Date of completion | Date |
| Event | Date | Date |
| Note | Date | Date of creation |
| Message | Date of creation | Date of creation |

To refresh the **Last Sales Activity** field yourself, click **Update LSA** at the top of the record page.

## Sales Activity Reminders, KPIs, and Saved Searches {#bridgehead_N1101535}

The Last Sales Activity SuiteApp includes the saved searches shown in the following table. You can use these saved searches to get notifications and summaries for leads, prospects, contacts, and opportunities that haven't had any sales activity in the last week.

| Saved search | Results fields |
| --- | --- |
| Contacts without sales activity in the last week | Name, Job Title, Company, Email, Phone, LSA Link Name, LSA Link |
| Leads, prospects, and customers without sales activity in the last week | Company name, Primary Contact, Email, Phone, Status, Lead Source, LSA Link Name, LSA Link |
| Opportunities without sales activity in the last week | Title, Customer, Contact: Name, Contact: Email, Contact: Phone, Status, Expected Close Date, Weighted Amount, LSA Link Name, LSA Link |

To view these saved searches, go to _Lists > Search > Saved Searches_.

To quickly see which contacts haven't had any sales activity in the last week, add these searches to the **Reminders** portlet on your dashboard. See [Setting Up Reminders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N581945.html).

You can also add these searches as custom KPIs to your KPI portlet. See [Setting Up the Key Performance Indicators Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N596767.html).

## Using Last Sales Activity Criteria for Searches {#bridgehead_N1101657}

You can search for leads, prospects, customers, contacts, and opportunities based on their last sales activity.

You can also define a date filter for a search based on a specific period, such as the last fiscal year any custom date range you want.

#### To include last sales activity date in a search:

1.  On the **Results** subtab of the search page, select **Last Sales Activity Fields...**.
    
2.  In the popup, select **Last Sales Activity** in the **Last Sales Activity Filter** field and set the date criteria.
    

## Last Sales Activity Saved Mass Updates {#bridgehead_N1101722}

Use the following saved mass updates to find contacts, customers, and opportunity records with outdated **Last Sales Activity** field values and update them simultaneously:

-   LSA Contact Mass Update
    
-   LSA Customer Mass Update
    
-   LSA Opportunity Mass Update
    

The Last Sales Activity mass updates are scheduled by the system to automatically run daily. Alternatively, you can run the mass updates at any time to update the **Last Sales Activity** field on your records. For information, see [Mass Updates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1103335211.html).
