With the Team Selling feature enabled, you can link sales teams to sales transactions and to lead, prospect, and customer records.

#### To associate a sales team with a lead, prospect or customer: {#procedure_N1038658}

1.  Edit the record you want to link to a sales team.
    
2.  Click the **Sales** subtab and make sure the **Sales Team** subtab is selected.
    
3.  In the **Choose Team** field, select the sales team.
    
4.  You can do the following:
    
    -   Select the primary rep for this record in the **Primary** column.
        
    -   Adjust the contribution percentages for this record.
        
    -   Add more sales team members for this record.
        
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Now, when you enter a transaction for this lead, prospect, or customer, the sale is linked to the sales team for commission, quota, reporting sales, and forecasting. When you view a record, the primary rep appears in the Sales Rep field with their contribution percentage.

When you enter a transaction for a customer, the sales team associated with the customer appears by default on the Sales subtab. You can adjust the settings for a sales team on individual transactions. For example, you can change the contribution percentages, change the primary rep, or add members to the sales team.

#### To associate a sales team with a sales transaction: {#procedure_N1038751}

1.  On the transaction, click the **Sales Team** subtab.
    
    If the customer on the transaction has a sales team, it appears by default on the **Sales Team** subtab.
    
2.  If no sales team shows up by default, select a sales team in the **Sales Team** field.
    
3.  Adjust the sales team as needed for this transaction.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Now, the transaction updates the sales team's commission, quota, sales, and sales forecast.

For sales reports that are not sorted by sales reps, transactions only show for the primary sales rep on the transaction.

Any changes you make to a sales team on a transaction don't affect the team on the group or customer record. For information about changing sales teams, see [Mass Updating Sales Teams](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1038939.html) and [Updating Individual Sales Teams](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1039075.html).

To reassign a sales team using your existing sales territories, edit the customer record. Click on the **Sales** subtab and then the **Sales Team** subtab. Remove each member of the sales team. Then, select **Reassign Using Territories** in the Employee column and set the **Contribution %** to 100.
