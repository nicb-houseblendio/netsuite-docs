You can view a summary of recent customer activity from the Show Activity option on the customer record. This is useful for sales or support representatives who want to get an overview of recent customer communication before contacting the customer.

#### To view recent customer activity:

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Customers_.
    
2.  Click **View** next to the customer whose recent activity you want to view.
    
3.  On the **Actions** menu, select **Show Activity**.
    
    A popup window displays a chronological list up to 100 of the most recent customer activities including tasks, phone calls, events, and support case communication.
    
4.  To print the activity list, click **Print** at the bottom of the popup window.
    
5.  To close the popup window, click **Close**.
