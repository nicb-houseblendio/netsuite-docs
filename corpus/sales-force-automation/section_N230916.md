The links portlets are streamlined to show only links that sales users are most likely to access.

The following new saved searches are the default dashboard views on their corresponding center tabs:

-   My Tasks
    
-   My Phone Calls
    
-   My Leads
    
-   My Prospects
    
-   My Opportunities
    
-   My Customers
    
-   My Contacts,
    

For general information about views, see [Working with List Views, Sublist Views, and Dashboard Views](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N495842.html).

The Setup tab contains a links portlet with links for Sales & Marketing Automation, Customization, User/Roles, and Import/Export in place of the standard NetSuite Setup Manager.

The More tab has a links portlet with links to the least used sales information.
