To track the last sales activity, the SuiteApp adds the **Last Sales Activity** field in lead, prospect, customer, contact, and opportunity records. This field displays the date when a sales activity was last performed for that record.

To see the record of a sales activity, click the link in the **Last Sales Activity** field on the chosen record.

You can also add reminders, key performance indicators, and saved searches to your dashboard. These tools can help you find leads, prospects, contacts, and opportunities that haven't had any sales activities in the last week. For more information, see [Using the Last Sales Activity SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_160405294299.html).

Note:

Workflows that run after an update on the lead, prospect, customer, contact, or opportunity record might conflict with the bundle script that updates the **Last Sales Activity** field. To avoid conflicts, convert these workflows to [User Event Scripts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1559344080.html#subsect_1560561865).
