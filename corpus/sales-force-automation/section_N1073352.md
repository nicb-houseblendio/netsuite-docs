Note:

In NetSuite, estimates and quotes are used interchangeably.

When a customer agrees to the estimate's terms, make required changes and then convert the estimate into a sales order, invoice, or cash sale.

#### To convert an estimate into another transaction: {#procedure_N1073381}

1.  Go to _Opportunities > Transactions > Estimates_.
    
2.  Click **View** next to the estimate you want to convert.
    
3.  Click **Sales Order**, **Cash Sale**, or **Invoice** to create the required transaction:
    
4.  When the new form opens, enter any changes as needed.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

When multiple sales orders are created from a single estimate that has a one time only usage promotion, NetSuite ignores the usage limits. The promotion will be repeated for the same customer on each of the sales orders created from the estimate. However, if you copy an estimate instead of creating a sales order, NetSuite respects the one time only promotion usage limits. A notification informs you that you can't apply the promotion. For more information regarding promotions, see [Promotions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4690873883.html).
