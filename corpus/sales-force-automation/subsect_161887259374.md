The subtabs that appear on a customer record vary depending on the features enabled in your account and the permissions included in your user role. The following list includes the most common subtabs with short descriptions and links to additional information.

-   **Subsidiaries** - If you use the Multi Subsidiary Customer feature of NetSuite OneWorld, standard customer record forms include a Subsidiaries subtab. This subtab lets you assign secondary subsidiaries to the customer. For information, see [Assigning Additional Subsidiaries to a Customer](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161895998055.html).
    
-   **Relationships** - This subtab lets you associate contacts, subcustomers, and (if you use the Multi-Partner Management feature) partners with the customer. For information, see [Associating a Contact with a Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1075740.html) and [Associating Partners With Customers and Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1167413.html).
    
-   **Communication** - Use the subtabs on this subtab to create and add activities associated with this customer or contact. NetSuite filters messages on this subtab according to those sent to the currently selected entity. For example, if you select a customer, all communication sent to that customer's primary contact is listed. If you select a contact, communication sent to that contact is listed. For more information, see [Attaching Events, Tasks, and Calls to Records and Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1084924.html).
    
    This subtab also lets you attach files and add your user notes to the record. For information, see [Attaching Files to Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N490137.html).
    
-   **Address** - This subtab is where you enter addresses for the customer. Each customer can have multiple addresses. Some customer transactions need a valid billing or shipping address (or both). For instructions, see [Entering an Address on a Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1099791.html).
    
-   **Sales** - This subtab shows information about your sales organization's work with a customer. Subtabs under this subtab include Sales Team, Transactions, Items Purchased, and more depending on the features enabled in your account and the permissions in your user role. For more information, see [Entering Sales Information for the Customer](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1077516.html).
    
-   **Marketing** - This subtab has all the information about your company's marketing efforts with a customer. For information, see [Entering Marketing Information for the Customer](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1077855.html).
    
-   **Support** - This subtab is associated with the Support Management features in NetSuite. The Cases and Issues subtabs commonly appear under this subtab. For information, see [Working With Cases](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2434254.html) and [Issue Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2438260.html).
    
-   **Financial** - This subtab has information about the customer's account with your company, like credit card data, price level, taxation, and account balance. For information, see [Entering Financial Information for the Customer](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1078064.html) and its subtopics.
    
-   **Preferences** - This subtab has the customer's language, shipping, and transaction sending preferences. For information, see [Setting Preferences on the Customer Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1079031.html).
    
-   **Access** - This subtab appears when you've enabled the Customer Access feature in your account. For information, see [Giving Customers Access](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N322750.html).
    
-   **System Information** - This subtab tracks changes to the customer record, including the user, context, type, field, old value, and new value.
