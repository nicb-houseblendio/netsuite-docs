The Sales by Sales Team Detail report shows the sales transactions and totals for each sales team for the date range you choose. Each sales team member's contribution percentage total appears for each member.

## To view the Sales by Sales Team Detail report: {#bridgehead_N1119788}

Go to _Reports > Sales > Sales by Sales Team > Detail_.

A popup message appears to let you know your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.
