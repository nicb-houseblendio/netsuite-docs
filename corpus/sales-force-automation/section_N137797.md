You can assign the following custom roles in the Enhanced Sales Center:

-   Sales Admin - Enhanced Sales Ctr
    
-   Sales Mgr - Enhanced Sales Ctr
    
-   Sales Rep - Enhanced Sales Ctr
    
-   Publisher - Enhanced Sales Ctr
    

Each custom role includes a set of permissions that determine what data sales users can see and what tasks they can perform. If you're a NetSuite Administrator, you can assign these roles to users as needed. For more information, see [Assigning Roles to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N897798.html).

The roles in the Enhanced Sales Center are locked, but you can customize them by creating copies you can edit. For more information, see [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html).

You can also compare permissions of the standard NetSuite Sales Center and the Enhanced Sales Center roles before assigning them to users. For information about comparing roles and permissions, see [Showing Role Permission Differences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N292157.html).
