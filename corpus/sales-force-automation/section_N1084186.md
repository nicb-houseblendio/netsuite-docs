If your administrator has set up customer credit card processing, you store credit card information in the customer record. For setup instructions, see [Setting Up Customer Credit Card Processing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1572221.html)

You can add or edit information directly on a customer record, or on demand as you work in a transaction. See the instructions in the following sections:

-   [Entering and Editing Credit Card Information for the Customer](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162197941185.html)
    
-   [Entering and Editing Credit Card Information from a Transaction](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162197951675.html)
    
-   [Searching for Customers by Credit Card Expiration Date](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_162198251311.html)
