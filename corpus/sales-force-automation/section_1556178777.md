You can delete a lead record in case of duplication or error.

#### To delete a lead record: {#procedure_1556178956}

1.  Go to Lists > Relationships > Leads (Administrator).
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the lead you want deleted.
    
3.  On the Lead page, click **Delete** under **Actions**.
    
4.  Click **OK** on the prompt popup window.
    

The Mass Update function doesn't support mass deletion of lead records. For information about mass updates, see [Mass Updates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1103335211.html).
