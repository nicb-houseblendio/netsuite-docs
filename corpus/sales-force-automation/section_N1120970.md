The Open Prospects snapshot is a portlet item that shows open prospects during a selected time frame. Prospects are considered open when they are assigned a status with a probability between 0% and 100%.

#### To see the Open Prospects snapshot: {#procedure_N1121013}

1.  Click the **Home** tab.
    
2.  On the **Key Performance Indicators** portlet, click the **Set Up** icon.
    
3.  Click **Add Standard KPIs** and select **Open Prospects**
    
4.  Click the trend graph icon to display a report which can be customized and exported.
