The Opportunities to Close Detail report lists all open opportunities and expected close dates within the period range you select for each sales rep. NetSuite totals the projected and weighted amounts by sales rep.

To view the Opportunities to Close Detail report, go to _Opportunities > Reports > Opportunities to Close > Detail_.

A message appears indicating that your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report shows the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.
