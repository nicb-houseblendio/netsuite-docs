The Total Pipeline by Statuses Summary report shows the projected and weighted of all the open estimates and opportunities for each customer status. You can't choose a date or period range for this report.

Only estimates that are marked to be included in forecasts appear in this report. Only opportunities that have not been converted to estimates or other transactions appear in this report.

For more information, see [Estimates in the Forecast and Pipeline](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1047186.html) and [Opportunities in the Forecast and Pipeline](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1047903.html).

## To view the Total Pipeline by Statuses Summary report: {#bridgehead_N1105277}

Go to _Reports > Pipeline Analysis > Total Pipeline by Statuses Summary_.

A popup message appears to let you know your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.
