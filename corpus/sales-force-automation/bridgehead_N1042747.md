You can change a territory by going to Setup > Sales > Sales Management > Sales Territories > List. Click the **Edit** link for the territory you want, make your changes, and click **Save**.

Note:

You can also go to _Setup > Sales > Sales Management > Sales Territories_ to change a territory. From this page, click the territory name's link. Click Edit to make changes, and then click Save.
