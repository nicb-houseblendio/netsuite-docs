The Alt. Sales Forecast vs. Quota by Department report shows each sales rep's ASA forecast and quota grouped by department. You can establish an ASA quota by department at _Forecast > Setup > Establish Quotas_.

Note:

Before you can establish quotas by department, you must enable the Departments feature at _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_

The report shows the calculated forecast and the override forecast for each sales rep. If you use [Advanced Forecasting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1047034.html), you'll see the override forecast for each category. For more information about the sales forecast, read [Sales Forecasting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1045230.html).

The total in the Forecast/Quota Variance column is the result of the forecast divided by the quota for that sales rep.

This report features an Actual column, a Quota/Actual Variance column and a Forecast/Actual column. The total in the Actual column represents closed cash sale and invoice totals for that sales rep. The Quota/Actual Variance column is the result of the quota divided by the actual closed sales for that sales rep. The Forecast/Actual Variance column is the result of the sales forecast divided by the actual closed sales for that sales rep.

## To view the Alt. Sales Forecast vs. Quota by Department report: {#bridgehead_N1065637}

Go to _Reports > Forecast > Alt. Sales Forecast vs. Quota by Department_.

A popup message appears in the middle of your screen to let you know your report is loading. You can click **Cancel** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading. Reports can take some time to load. If you'd like to work on something else while your report loads, click **Alert me when ready** at the bottom of the message. You'll get an email with a link to the report when it's ready to view.

Note:

This report doesn't support reporting by period even when the Report by Period preference is set to All Reports. You can configure the Report by Period preference. Go to _Home > Set Preferences_. The preference is on the Analytics subtab.
