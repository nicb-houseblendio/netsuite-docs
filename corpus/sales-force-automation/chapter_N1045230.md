One of the most important requirements for your business' success is being able to predict sales. How you compile and calculate sales forecasts can impact all aspects of your business, from production to inventory and from cost to revenue. All of the up-to-the-minute information you need to make sound business decisions is a few clicks away.

Note:

If you are a sales rep, refer to [A Sales Person's Guide to Forecasting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1046401.html). This topic explains how to use the NetSuite forecasting tool.

NetSuite provides a flexible forecasting tool that you can configure according to your business' forecasting needs:

-   NetSuite gives you comprehensive forecast calculation methods and a three-tiered probability system for more accuracy.
    
-   NetSuite includes key performance indicators (KPIs) like Forecast, New Opportunities, and Total Pipeline. Use these KPIs to view and manage all aspects of your forecast right from your dashboard.
    
-   The Sales Rep Forecast Editor lets sales reps leverage their experience and knowledge to adjust their forecasts. Entering forecast overrides and updates ensures that the forecast is accurate and reflects the best notions of your sales force.
    
-   NetSuite reporting tools help you compile and slice forecasting data to give you the exact information you need.
    
    You can customize standard reports to fit your individual business process. See [Sales Forecast vs. Quota Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1051599.html), [Forecast by Customer Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1058733.html), and [Calculated Forecast Accuracy Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1063024.html)
    

## Using NetSuite Sales Forecasting {#bridgehead_N1045368}

The following roles use sales forecasting:

-   **Sales Reps** - For a sales rep, an accurate representation of your sales forecast is key to stay on track to meet your quota. As you enter orders and opportunities, NetSuite updates your forecast in real time on your dashboard. You can adjust your sales strategy with the certainty that you have accurate numbers.
    
    As a sales rep, you know best which deals are likely to close and when. With NetSuite, you can update and override the forecast calculated by the system with your own numbers.
    
    For more information, refer to [A Sales Person's Guide to Forecasting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1046401.html).
    
-   **Sales Managers** - Sales managers need the most accurate forecast figures to ensure their teams meet quota.
    
    With forecast reports and KPIs, sales managers can drill down to see how each rep is performing on a customer or transaction level.
    
    Forecast accuracy reports provide historical data on how well you and your reps predicted sales. Historical data helps you make adjustments necessary to report accurate forecast amounts to company executives.
    
-   **Executives** - Company executives are constantly in touch with the company sales forecast. Forecast KPIs and reports give an up-to-the-minute picture of the sales forecast essential to monitor your company's performance with respect to revenue goals.
    

## Forecast Reporting {#bridgehead_N1046090}

NetSuite provides extensive [Forecast Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1051173.html) to enable you to manage and report your forecasting data. NetSuite gives you the following types of forecasting reports:

-   **Accuracy Reports** - Forecast Accuracy reports give you another way to check and imrpove your forecast based on past performance. See [Forecast Accuracy Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1060330.html) and [Calculated Forecast Accuracy Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1063024.html).
    
-   **Summary Reports** - Summary reports such as the [Sales Forecast vs. Quota Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1051599.html) bring together all of your forecast data. See [Forecast Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1051173.html).
    
-   **Detail Reports** - Detail reports provide a granular look at your data. For example, the [Forecast by Sales Rep Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1057440.html) lists each sales rep's forecast for the period you define.
