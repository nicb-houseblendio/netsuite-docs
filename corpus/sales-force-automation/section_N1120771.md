The New Customer Sales Detail report lists all lists all customers that had their first transaction with your company within the period or date range specified. It also provides the details of their first transactions. Transactions entered during this date range appear individually and totaled for each new customer.

## To view the New Customer Sales Detail report: {#bridgehead_N1120792}

Go to _Reports > Sales > New Customer Sales > Detail_.

A popup message appears to let you know your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.
