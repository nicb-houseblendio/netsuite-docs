Some businesses give customers a quotation or estimate before the order is accepted. Customers typically review the estimate and might ask for changes.

In NetSuite, you can create an estimate and then print, email, or fax it to your customer. When your customer accepts it, you can make any necessary changes and convert it to a sales order, invoice, or cash sale. For information, see [Converting an Estimate](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1073352.html).

You can set an expiration date for the estimate and let NetSuite remind you to follow up on the estimate before it expires. Set the expiration date in the **Primary Information** column in the **Expires** field. Expired estimates are still displayed in your Estimates list.

To enable the Estimates feature, an administrator can go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_. On the **Transactions** subtab under Basic Features, check the **Estimates** box, and then click **Save**. Users with the appropriate role can now prepare estimates at _Transactions > Sales > Prepare Estimates_.

When you use the Estimates feature, a **Renew** button appears on invoices for roles with the create estimate permission. When you click **Renew**, NetSuite converts the invoice into a pre-filled, editable, estimate. This is particularly useful when issuing new estimates for often repeated items.

![Screenshot of the Renew button that appears on invoices if you use the Estimates feature.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/MarketingSalesForceAutomationPartners/SalesForceAutomation/OpportunitiesEstimates__invoice_renew.png)

NetSuite creates a non-posting Estimate account in your chart of accounts. Estimates have no accounting impact until they are converted to invoices or cash sales.
