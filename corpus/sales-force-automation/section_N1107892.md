The Total Pipeline by Customer Detail report shows all the open estimates and opportunities for each customer. The projected and weighted totals for each transaction appear and are totaled for each customer. You can't choose a date or period range for this report.

Only estimates that are marked to be included in forecasts appear on this report. Only opportunities that have not been converted to estimates appear on this report.

## To view the Total Pipeline by Customer Detail report {#bridgehead_N1107918}

Go to _Reports > Pipeline Analysis > Total Pipeline by Customer > Detail_.

A popup message appears to let you know your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.
