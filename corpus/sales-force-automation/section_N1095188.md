You can automatically send email to sales representatives when they're assigned a new lead, prospect, or customer who submitted an online customer form. For information, see [Assigning Leads](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1093032.html).

To get to the Online Customer Form page, go to Setup > Marketing > Setup Tasks > Online Customer Forms. Edit the relevant record and click the **Set Up Workflow** subtab. Check the box for **Send Notification Email**. Select an email template in the **Template for New Leads** and **Template for Existing Customers** fields.

You can create new email templates at _Documents > Templates > Email Templates_. For more information, see [Creating New Email Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N515055.html).
