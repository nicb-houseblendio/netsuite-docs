The Total Pipeline by Sales Rep Summary report shows the projected and weighted total of open estimates and opportunities entered by each sales rep. You can't choose a date or period range for this report.

Only estimates that are marked to be included in forecasts appear on this report. Only opportunities that have not been converted to estimates appear on this report.

## To view the Total Pipeline by Sales Rep Summary report: {#procedure_N1108120}

Go to _Opportunities > Reports > Total Pipeline by Sales Rep_.

A popup message appears to let you know your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.

**Drilling down to reports and transactions**:

-   Click the names of accounts or account categories on the left side of the report to collapse or expand subaccounts.
    
-   For most record types (Accounts, Customers, Items, etc.), you can click the name on the report to drill down to that record.
