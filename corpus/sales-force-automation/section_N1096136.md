In NetSuite, prospects are the middle step in the sales cycle that progresses from lead to prospect to customer.

Prospect records let you track all the information you need to convert a prospect into a customer, such as:

-   Contacts
    
-   Events, tasks, and calls
    
-   Messages
    
-   Opportunities
    
-   Transactions history
    
-   Items purchased
    
-   Cases submitted
    
-   File cabinet documents
    
-   Campaign history
    
-   Projects or subprospects
    
-   Upsell history
    

Typically, you create a prospect record by converting a lead record. For information, see [Lead Conversion](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1086628.html).

The procedure to create a prospect record directly is the same as the process to create a customer record. The primary difference is that you select a prospect status instead of a customer status in the record status field. For instructions, see [Creating a Customer Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161860867711.html).

The following standard reports apply specifically to prospects:

-   [Prospect Analysis Summary Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1121588.html)
    
-   [Prospect Analysis Detail Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1121924.html)
