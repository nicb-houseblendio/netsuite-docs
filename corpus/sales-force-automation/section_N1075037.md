Contacts are individuals you've got business relationships with. Contacts can be colleagues, friends, or other acquaintances you'd keep in an address book. In NetSuite, you keep contact information in contact records.

The following procedures include information about working with contact records in NetSuite:

-   [Creating a Contact](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161792162006.html)
    
-   [Inactivating or Deleting Contacts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161792170332.html)
    
-   [Creating Categories for Contacts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1075554.html)
    
-   [Associating a Contact with a Record](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1075740.html)
    
-   [Saving Messages from Contacts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1076043.html)
