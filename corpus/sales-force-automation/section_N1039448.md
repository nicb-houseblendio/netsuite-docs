Important:

The following information is applicable only to users who are **not** using the Team Selling feature. Accounts with Team Selling enabled should see [Setting Up Team Selling](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1037578.html).

Sales teams in NetSuite are usually include a manager and all their subordinate sales reps. For each sales rep on your team, you need to mark their employee record as a Sales Rep and assign them to a sales manager.

Note:

Only users with roles that have full access to employees can set up sales teams.

#### To set up a sales team: {#procedure_N1039508}

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Employees_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the name of an employee you want to include in your sales team.
    
3.  Under Primary Information, in the **Supervisor** field, select the employee's supervisor.
    
4.  Click the **Human Resources** subtab.
    
5.  Under Job Information in the **Sales Role** field, select **Sales Rep**.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    
7.  Repeat these steps for each employee on your sales team.
    

You can also create team quotas. Team quotas total the sales made by all subordinate sales reps and their supervisor to determine if the quota was reached.

If you use the Commissions feature, you can create manager commission schedules based on a manager's team quota. When you assign a manager a commission schedule based on quota, you need to have an established team quota set for the manager first. For more information about quotas, see [Quotas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1043179.html).
