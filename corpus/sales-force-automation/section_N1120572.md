The New Customer Sales Summary report lists all customers that had their first transaction with your company within the period or date range specified. It also provides a total of all transactions.

## To see the New Customer Sales Summary report: {#bridgehead_N1120593}

Go to _Reports > Sales > New Customer Sales_.

A popup message appears to let you know your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.
