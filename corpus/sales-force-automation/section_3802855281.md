At any time you can view the status of authorized commission payments. Go to _Go to Transactions > Commissions > Authorize Employee Commissions > Status_.

The Process Status page displays the identification number of the submitted commission and process type, such as commission authorization. It displays the status of the commission authorization process, such as completed, and completion percentage of the authorization process. It also displays any error messages, and the authorization creation date and time stamp.

Note:

You cannot authorize commissions for employees whose commissions are currently being authorized.
