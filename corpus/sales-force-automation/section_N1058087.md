The Alt. Sales Forecast by Sales Rep Summary report lists each sales rep's calculated and override alternate sales amount (ASA) forecasts for the period you set in the report footer.

The calculated ASA forecast is determined by the sum of the alternate sales amounts on the following:

-   unbilled sales orders that have not been processed into cash sales or invoices
    
-   estimates with the Include in Forecast box checked that have not been used to create sales orders, invoices or cash sales
    
-   opportunities with no estimates attached or with estimates that are not set to be included in the forecast
    

The override forecast is manually entered by sales reps and sales managers. For more information, read [Saving Sales Rep Forecasts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1049172.html) and [Saving Sales Manager Forecasts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1048767.html).

Sales reps are grouped and totaled by sales team. Click the sales manager's name to collapse or expand their sales team.

If you use Advanced Forecasting, you'll see the forecast for each forecast type. The override forecast shown is the Most Likely.

## To view the Alt. Sales Forecast by Sales Rep Summary report: {#bridgehead_N1058240}

Go to _Forecast > Reports > Forecast by Sales Rep_.

A popup message appears in the middle of your screen to let you know your report is loading. You can click **Cancel** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading. Reports can take some time to load. If you'd like to work on something else while your report loads, click **Alert me when ready** at the bottom of the message. You'll get an email with a link to the report when it's ready to view.

Note:

This report doesn't support reporting by period even when the Report by Period preference is set to All Reports. You can configure the Report by Period preference. Go to _Home > Set Preferences_ > Analytics subtab.
