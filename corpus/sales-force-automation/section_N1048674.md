If you use the Team Selling feature, an opportunity's forecast amount is based on the sales rep's contribution percentage on that opportunity. For example, a sales rep has a 10% contribution percentage in their sales team. When a rep creates an opportunity for a team customer, 10% of the opportunity's total is reflected in the rep's sales forecast.

For more information, see [Creating a Sales Team](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1037578.html#bridgehead_N1037970).
