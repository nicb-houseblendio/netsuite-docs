When leads don't qualify for conversion and no further follow-up is required, you can change the status of the lead to **Lost Lead**.

#### To mark a lead as lost:

1.  Go to _Leads > Relationships> Leads_.
    
2.  Edit the relevant lead.
    
3.  From the **Status** list, select **Lead-Lost**.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
