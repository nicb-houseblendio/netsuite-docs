If you don't have a relationship with a contact anymore, you can inactivate their record.

#### To inactivate contacts:

The contacts you checked won't show up in the list unless you check the **Show Inactives** box.

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Contacts > Lists_.
    
2.  At the top of the Contacts list, check the **Show Inactives** box.
    
3.  Check the **Inactive** boxes next to the contacts you want to inactivate.
    
4.  At the top of the list, click **Submit**.
    

You can also delete a contact you created if it's not linked to any other records.

#### To delete a contact:

1.  Go to _Lists > Relationships > Contacts > Lists_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the contact you want to delete.
    
3.  From the **Actions** menu, select **Delete**.
