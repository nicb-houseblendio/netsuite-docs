The Total Open Opportunities Summary report provides weighted and projected totals by status of all opportunities not yet closed. NetSuite lists these totals based on the date period you select.

Create opportunities at _Opportunities > Transactions > Opportunities > New_.

To view the Total Open Opportunities Summary report, go to _Reports > Pipeline Analysis > Total Open Opportunities_.

A message appears indicating that your report is loading. The status bar in the footer of the report shows the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** next to the status bar to stop the report from loading.

By default, today's date is selected when the report is first run. This may result in no data being loaded. in such cases, adjust the date in the As Of field at the bottom of the report.
