Being incorporated isn't necessary for approval.

## Is my business qualified to apply? {#subsect_0822024419}

Most businesses are qualified to apply. Contact your NetSuite account manager and they can provide you with more information if you have questions or concerns.

## How do I calculate high ticket prices and processing volumes? {#subsect_0822024647}

**High Ticket** - This is the largest dollar amount a customer might pay for goods or services from your business in a single credit card transaction.

**Processing Volume** - This is how NetSuite Pay refers to the total of all credit card transactions within a fiscal year.
