After installing the NetSuite Pay SuiteApp, submit a merchant account application to Versapay. Each subsidiary will require a separate merchant account application but your first application must be approved or closed before submitting additional applications. NetSuite Pay is available for subsidiaries in the United States and Canada.

The application process requires details about your business, its ownership structure, and your banking information. Personal information is required due to FINCEN and card brand rules.

Gather following information ready before starting the application:

-   Your businesses tax ID
    
-   Names, addresses, and individual tax IDs of any person who owns 25% or more of the business (unless your company is public)
    
-   Your business' payment processing volume
    
-   Bank routing and account number into which you want the proceeds of your sales deposited
    

You may be asked to provide various financial documents as part of the application process. This includes the previous two years of the following documents:

-   Balance Sheet
    
-   Income Statement
    
-   Cash Flow
    

You may also need to provide the following documents for the current year:

-   Most recent interim financial statements for the current year
    
-   If you are currently processing, then three consecutive months of current processing statements
    

The application typically takes 15-20 minutes to complete if you have all the required documents. You can save and resume your application at any time.

The initial review is estimated at 2 business days. If additional documentation is required, the decision times are extended based on when the information is received. You should also receive an email for NetSuite Pay application status. If you have not received an update to the application status in 5 business days, contact NetSuite support.

If you have questions or issues completing your application, contact NetSuite support.

#### To submit a merchant account application: {#procedure_1005123319}

1.  Go to NetSuite Pay > Merchant Application > New Application.
    
2.  Select a subsidiary from the dropdown list. For OneWorld accounts, a separate application is required for each subsidiary. If an application has already been started for a subsidiary, the subsidiary will no longer appear in the subsidiary dropdown list when starting a new merchant application. For non-OneWorld accounts, a message is displayed if you try to start a new merchant application when one is already approved or pending approval.
    
3.  Review the Welcome page and click **Next**.
    
4.  Complete the **Company Information** section.
    
5.  Complete the **Ownership Information** section.
    
    If there are individuals in your company who own 25% or more of the business, select **Yes** from the list and add one or more owners. Then select an owner or someone else to be the primary contact.
    
    If there are no individuals in your company who own 25% or more of the business, select **No** from the list and enter information about the nonbusiness owner primary contact.
    
    The primary contact will be notified if there are questions about the merchant application.
    
6.  Complete the **Financial Information** section. This includes information about the processing volumes and deposit account for your business.
    
7.  Review the information and click **Submit**.
    

You will be notified about the decision of your application by Versapay through email. You can check the status of your application at any time. For more information, see [Checking Merchant Account Application Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_2123636382.html).

To change information in your merchant application after it is submitted, contact NetSuite support.
