You can connect your NetSuite account to a Versapay Test Payment Gateway. Use this connection to test NetSuite Pay functionality before processing real transactions. All merchant account applications, configurations, and payments will be treated as test records, and no funds will be settled in your bank account.

#### To connect to a test payment gateway:

1.  Go to NetSuite Pay > App Settings > Configure.
    
2.  Check the **Connect to Test Payment Gateway** box.
    

After testing, delete all merchant account applications, configurations, and associated payment records to maintain accurate accounting. You must resubmit all merchant account applications to run real transactions and settle funds.
