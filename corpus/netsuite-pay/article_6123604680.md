You can install the SuiteApp from the SuiteApp Marketplace in two ways:

-   Option 1: Click the [NetSuite Pay](https://system.netsuite.com/suiteapp/ui/marketplace.nl?sc=-180&whence=#/app?id=com.netsuite.netsuitepay) link to go directly to the SuiteApp Details page and click **Install**.
    
-   Option 2: Go to the SuiteApp Marketplace and follow the procedure below to install the SuiteApp.
    

Note:

Contact your account manager if the install option is not available.

The SuiteApp is automatically installed for new customers in the United States.

#### To install NetSuite Pay: {#procedure_43143028445}

1.  Go to **SuiteApps**.
    
2.  In the **Search** field enter "NetSuite Pay."
    
3.  Click the SuiteApp tile.
    
4.  On the SuiteApp details page, click **Install**.
    
5.  Click **Install**.
    

NetSuite Pay is a managed SuiteApp that's regularly and automatically updated with new features and improvements.

For more information about installing, updating, and deleting SuiteApps from the SuiteApp Marketplace, see [SuiteApp Installation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1540236668.html).
