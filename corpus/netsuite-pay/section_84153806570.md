## Can I temporarily suspend my NetSuite Pay account? {#subsect_0822030112}

No. When your merchant account is active, monthly fees apply even if integration is incomplete or if your merchant account isn't being actively used for processing payments.

## Will my account be closed if I don't use it? {#subsect_0822030327}

Yes. Accounts with no processing for six months are subject to closure. Versapay will reach out to you before closure to understand why no transactions are occurring.

## How do I cancel my NetSuite Pay merchant account? {#subsect_0822031354}

To cancel your NetSuite Pay merchant account, contact your NetSuite account manager.

## When will my charges be processed after closing my NetSuite Pay account? {#subsect_0822031700}

Versapay will verify all charges have been posted before closing the account. After everything is settled, they can formally close your account. Contact your NetSuite account manager for more information.

## Are there any fees for canceling my NetSuite Pay merchant account? {#subsect_0822031748}

There are no fees for canceling your account. Prior to cancellation, any outstanding fees or charges on your account must be paid in full.
