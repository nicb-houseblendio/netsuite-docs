NetSuite Pay is an integrated payment processing solution designed for NetSuite users. NetSuite Pay lets you accept credit card payments online, over the phone, at the point of sale, or through another digital payment method, all with PCI-compliant data security. NetSuite provides a complete transaction record, giving you a 360-degree view of your customer transaction history and detailed audit tracking while maintaining the security of payment card data.

NetSuite Pay provides simple, transparent pricing using a streamlined application and onboarding process.

Access the NetSuite Pay dashboard for helpful information about the benefits of NetSuite Pay, a description of the underwriting process, pricing details, and how to get help with NetSuite Pay. NetSuite Pay detects your location (United States or Canada) and displays content specific to your location on the dashboard. To access the dashboard, go to NetSuite Pay > NetSuite Pay Overview.

Tip:

If the dashboard is not visible, you need to setup the custom portlet:

1.  Go to NetSuite Pay > NetSuite Pay Overview.
    
2.  In the portlet, click the menu and select **Set Up**.
    
3.  Select **Versapay PL NetSuite Pay Dashboard**.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

NetSuite Pay clients can:

-   Complete an application and onboarding process that is embedded in NetSuite (underwriting and merchant service are powered by Versapay).
    
-   Accept credit card and ACH payments across e-commerce, point of sales, and accounts receivable channels directly in NetSuite.
    
-   Speed up collection times with automated click-to-pay invoices as part of your native workflows to maximize efficiency and eliminate hours spent reconciling payments with NetSuite.
    
-   Process payments securely and protect yourself and your customers by tokenizing and safely storing sensitive credit card data in compliance with PCI DSS.
    

NetSuite Pay works in conjunction with Payment Link to:

-   Send e-invoices with a payment link or QR code to a secure payment page.
    
-   Configure the payment link to select certain payment methods, allow partial payments, and include a company logo and information.
    
-   Send payment notifications using customized email templates to inform customers when payment is accepted or rejected.
    
-   Let customers view and pay their bills with a click by credit or debit card.
    

NetSuite Pay also works with NetSuite Customer Center, SuiteCommerce MyAccount, and SuiteCommerce Advanced My Account, letting business buyers view invoices, securely add and manage payment methods, and pay faster and more efficiently.

## Key Features and Benefits {#bridgehead_71132806929}

-   **Online Payments** - Simplifies the process of receiving your money and helps reduce the number of manual processes.
    
-   **Payment Methods Management** - Lets you choose the methods of payment you accept including types of credit cards and ACH.
    
-   **Improved processing time** - Payments are processed automatically with little to no need for human interaction.
    
-   **Payments by email** - Use [Payment Link](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_162516600357.html) to enable your customers to remit payment by clicking the payment link in an email.
    
-   **Enhanced Integration** - NetSuite Pay integrates with the Customer Center, SuiteCommerce MyAccount, SuiteCommerce Advanced MyAccount, and SuiteCommerce Advanced Webstore.
    

## NetSuite Pay Workflow {#bridgehead_48161438270}

To begin, install the NetSuite Pay SuiteApp. Then, complete a merchant application that is approved by Versapay underwriting. After approval, you can set up the SuiteApp. For more information, see [NetSuite Pay Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_5133045655.html).

When customers make payments using credit cards or ACH, Versapay processes these transactions, and they are reconciled. Daily and monthly settlement reports are available. You are notified of any open settlements that need resolution. For more information, see [Using NetSuite Pay](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_2160019210.html).
