## How do I install the NetSuite Pay SuiteApp? {#subsect_77165600214}

See [Installing NetSuite Pay](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6123604680.html).

## How do I uninstall the NetSuite Pay SuiteApp? {#subsect_77165538571}

Go to Customization > SuiteCloud Development > Installed SuiteApp List. Find the NetSuite Pay SuiteApp on the list and click Uninstall. Your merchant account will remain active unless you contact support to request the account be closed.

## Do I need separate instances of the NetSuite Pay SuiteApp for each of my subsidiaries? {#subsect_71165526954}

No. Only a single instance of the NetSuite Pay SuiteApp is required when using multiple subsidiaries. A separate merchant account application is required for each subsidiary.

## How do I install the NetSuite Pay SuiteApp in my sandbox account? {#subsect_93165521443}

See [Setting Up NetSuite Pay in Sandbox](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_4113054031.html).

## Can I copy the configuration of the NetSuite Pay SuiteApp from my sandbox to production? {#subsect_55165506780}

This is not supported.

## How do I upgrade my SuiteApp to the newest release? {#subsect_40165457601}

The NetSuite Pay SuiteApp is a managed SuiteApp. New versions are automatically pushed to your account.
