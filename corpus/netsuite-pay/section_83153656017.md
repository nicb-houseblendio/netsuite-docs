## Are there any other options available for processors? Can I choose? {#subsect_83175033665}

NetSuite Pay only supports processor options fulfilled through Versapay.

## What's a merchant account? {#subsect_62175026454}

A merchant account is a type of bank account required for businesses to accept payments in multiple ways, typically debit or credit cards.

## What's involved in submitting an application? {#subsect_23175018493}

See [Submitting a Merchant Account Application](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_2122140403.html).

## Can international merchants get a NetSuite Pay account? {#subsect_97174941315}

NetSuite Pay is available for subsidiaries in the United States and Canada.

## Can I accept both credit card and ACH payments? {#subsect_78174931416}

Yes. NetSuite Pay supports debit cards, credit cards, and ACH payments. If you have additional questions, contact NetSuite support or your account manager.

## How often are deposits made into my bank account? {#subsect_60174912245}

NetSuite Pay funding operates on a T+1 schedule. This means deposits are made to your account one day after a batch is closed. Batches on Friday aren't deposited during the weekend and will generally be in your account on Tuesday.

## What currencies can I receive payments in? {#subsect_88174906419}

Settlement can be in USD or CAD, depending on the subsidiary location.

## Is a security deposit or rolling reserve required for approval of my application? {#subsect_98174857697}

There is no security deposit. Rolling reserves may be required based on a financial review as a stipulation for approval. For additional questions, contact NetSuite support.

## Will NetSuite Pay work with my regular business bank account? {#subsect_45172915761}

Yes. You can use any personal or business US or Canada-based checking account. Savings accounts can't be used.

## Is the payment gateway included? {#subsect_84172028431}

Yes. A payment gateway is software included with NetSuite Pay. It collects customer card information and encrypts it for later processing. A payment processor uses that information to charge the customers' bank or credit card provider.

## Can I use an existing merchant account for NetSuite Pay? {#subsect_46172014957}

No. NetSuite Pay only supports merchant accounts provided through Versapay.

## Can I accept credit card payments on my website? {#subsect_33171607054}

Yes. NetSuite Pay is compatible with NetSuite for e-commerce payment.

## What are the required NetSuite features to use NetSuite Pay? {#subsect_77165606402}

See [Prerequisite Features for NetSuite Pay](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_7100838260.html).
