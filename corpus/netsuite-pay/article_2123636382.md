You can check your merchant account application status at any time.

#### To check your merchant account application status: {#procedure_1003023311}

1.  Go to NetSuite Pay > Merchant Application > My Application Status.
    
2.  The application status in NetSuite will show as one of the following:
    
    -   **Application Started** - You started your application, but haven't completed and submitted it yet.
        
    -   **Application Submitted** - Your application has been submitted to underwriting team for review; additional documents may still be required.
        
    -   **Received** - Your application has been received.
        
    -   **On Hold** - Your application is on hold, pending more information.
        
    -   **Processing** - The underwriting team is reviewing your application. They will reach out if additional information is needed.
        
    -   **Provisionally Approved** - Your application has been approved provided that some conditions are met, you can now set up your system features and settings. For more information, see [Configuring the NetSuite Pay Payment Processing Profile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_3093631811.html).
        
    -   **Complete** - You've successfully completed onboarding. You may begin transacting with your customers.
        
    -   **Application Status Unavailable** - The application status can't be retrieved. Try refreshing your page or contact NetSuite support if the issue persists.
