## Why is my personal information required to submit an application? {#subsect_20153543989}

Personal information is required due to FINCEN and card brand rules.

## Which credit reports are used? Hard or soft pull? {#subsect_42154108011}

We don't use traditional credit bureaus. Our Underwriting team leverages LexisNexis to validate PII. There is no hard or soft pull to the applicants credit.

## What format does my documentation need to be in to submit to underwriting? {#subsect_58153452496}

You will receive an email with a hyperlink to upload documents requested from Versapay underwriting. PDF is preferred, but other formats are acceptable. Ensure the documentation is not password protected. PNG and JPEG files are also acceptable.

## If my application is denied, do I have any other options? {#subsect_33153342882}

Depending on the reason for denial, there may be additional processing options available.

## Who do I contact if I have questions or concerns when my application is in underwriting? {#subsect_3153320790}

Contact NetSuite support for any questions regarding the status of the application during underwriting.
