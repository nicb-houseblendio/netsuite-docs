Important:

Most types of records are also supported in REST web services. For a list of all records supported in REST, see [REST Web Services Supported Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1558962745.html).

Important:

For information about working with this record type in REST web services, see [Kit Item](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_69204612369.html).

Kits or packages let you create items that are collected from other items. You can assign multiple price levels to your kits and even make them available in your website. Whenever you sell a kit, inventory items are deducted from inventory.

For information about working with kits and packages in the UI, see [Kit/Package Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2318089.html#bridgehead_N2318417).
