Add the limit and offset criteria to your request to get the results in multiple pages. For more information about pagination, read [Collection Paging](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156414087576.html).

          `GET {{REST_SERVICES}}/record/v1/customer?q=dateCreated ON_OR_AFTER "{{thisYearStart}}" AND dateCreated BEFORE "{{thisYearEnd}}"&limit=3&offset=6`
