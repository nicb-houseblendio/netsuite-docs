You can deprecate a released version when it is no longer needed. You might do this to stop users from installing the SuiteApp until a bug is fixed, or to end the SuiteApp's lifecycle.

Deprecated versions are removed from the SuiteApp Marketplace, so users can't install them until a new version is released. Anyone who already has the SuiteApp installed keeps that version until they uninstall it or a new version replaces it.

#### To deprecate a version:

1.  Go to Control Center > My SuiteApps > My SuiteApps.
    
2.  Do one of the following:
    
    -   For a SuiteApp, click ![SCC ellipsis button](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteCloudCustomizationScriptingWebServices/SuiteAppControlCenter/SCCEllipsisButton.png) and select **Manage Versions**.
        
    -   Click the SuiteApp name to view the SuiteApp details, then click the **Versions** tab.
        
3.  Find the SuiteApp version by its version number in the Version History list. In the **Action** column, click **Deprecate**.
    
    To confirm, click **Deprecate**. The version moves to the Deprecated state and is no longer available on the SuiteApp Marketplace.
    
4.  (Optional) Click **Done** to close the SuiteApp details and go back to the My SuiteApps page.
