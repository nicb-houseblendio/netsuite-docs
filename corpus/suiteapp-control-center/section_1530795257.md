Your install base can uninstall SuiteApps from the Installed SuiteApps page in their NetSuite accounts. For more information, see [Viewing and Uninstalling SDF SuiteApps in a NetSuite Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4849371107.html). For information about the states during the uninstall, see [Viewing SDF SuiteApp Installation Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1527799570.html).
