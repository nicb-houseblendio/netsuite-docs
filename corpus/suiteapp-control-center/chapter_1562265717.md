Your users can install any SuiteApp if it's shared to their accounts and supports their NetSuite account version. They can manually upgrade unmanaged SuiteApps or get upgrades automatically for managed SuiteApps. Upgrading a SuiteApp gives your users new customizations and settings that can help their organization.

For information about managing the installation and upgrade of your SDF SuiteApps, see the following topics:

-   [Installing SDF SuiteApps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1525982323.html)
    
-   [Viewing SDF SuiteApp Installation Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1527799570.html)
    
-   [Viewing the SDF SuiteApp Install Base](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1527799607.html)
    
-   [Developing Managed SuiteApps](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1526582924.html)
    

You can also use SuiteScript to get information about installed bundles and SuiteApps. For more information, see [N/suiteAppInfo Module](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160236086332.html).
