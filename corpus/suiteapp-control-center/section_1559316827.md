This topic explains what you need to do to make a SuiteApp available to users on the SuiteApp Marketplace. You can check the status of these actions in the SuiteApp Publishing Requirements List, which gives you one place to see everything you need to meet the SuiteApp publishing requirements. The action that is completed is represented by a green check mark icon (![Checkmark icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteCloudCustomizationScriptingWebServices/SuiteAppControlCenter/SCCSuiteAppApproval_IconCheckmark.png)), and the action that is not completed is represented by a gray cross icon (![X icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteCloudCustomizationScriptingWebServices/SuiteAppControlCenter/SCCSuiteAppApproval_IconCross.png)) in that list.

Important:

You have to complete all actions for a SuiteApp to be available to users on the SuiteApp Marketplace.

To view SuiteApp Publishing Requirements List, go to Control Center > My SuiteApps > My SuiteApps, then click the SuiteApp name to see the SuiteApp details.

Here's an example of the SuiteApp Publishing Requirements List in the navigation pane of the SuiteApp details page, where all actions are completed, meaning the SuiteApp is published on the SuiteApp Marketplace:

![SuiteApp publishing requirements list](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteCloudCustomizationScriptingWebServices/SuiteAppControlCenter/SCCSuiteAppApprovalWidgetUI.png)

Here's what you'll find in the SuiteApp Publishing Requirements list:

-   **Publishing Status** - indicates whether a SuiteApp is available to users on the SuiteApp Marketplace.
    
    The publishing status has two possible states:
    
    -   **Not published** - the SuiteApp isn't available to users on the SuiteApp Marketplace because one or more of the three required actions aren't completed.
        
    -   **Published** - the SuiteApp is currently available on the SuiteApp Marketplace for users to install because all three required actions are completed.
        
-   **Action Status** - indicates whether a required action is completed or not. The action status provides a single location to view the status of all of the actions that are required to meet the SuiteApp publishing requirements.
    
    Here are the three required actions to make a SuiteApp available to users on the SuiteApp Marketplace:
    
    -   **Released version** - at least one released version exists for the SuiteApp. To release a version, see [Releasing a SuiteApp Version](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1526583869.html).
        
    -   **Listing review** - SDN needs to approve the SuiteApp listing. To find out how to complete a listing review, contact SDN.
        
    -   **BFN review** - a Built for NetSuite (BFN) review is required. To find out how to complete a BFN review, see [Built for NetSuite Overview](http://www.netsuite.com/portal/developers/built-for-netsuite.shtml) and [BFN Verification Process](http://www.netsuite.com/portal/developers/built-for-netsuite/bfn-verification-process.shtml).
        
    
    Note:
    
    Both the listing review and the BFN review are done by using the Advanced Partner Center (APC). For more information about the Advanced Partner Center role, see [The Advanced Partner Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1168130.html).
    

Here's an example of the SuiteApp Publishing Requirements List where only some of the required actions are completed:

![SuiteApp publishing requirements list with one status not published.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteCloudCustomizationScriptingWebServices/SuiteAppControlCenter/SCCSuiteAppApprovalWidgetUIPendingActions.png)

In this example, the publishing status is Not published because only some of the required actions are done. The action status shows you have a released version and the BFN review is done, but you still need to complete the listing review.

You can also view the details about pending actions by pointing to the tooltip icon (![Tooltip icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteCloudCustomizationScriptingWebServices/SuiteAppControlCenter/SCCSuiteAppApprovalWidgetUITooltipIcon.png)) next to the publishing status (see below for an example):

![Tooltip for SuiteApp publishing requirements list.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteCloudCustomizationScriptingWebServices/SuiteAppControlCenter/SCCSuiteAppApprovalWidgetUIPendingActionsWithTooltip.png)

Note:

The preceding tooltip only shows the status of the actions that aren't completed. When all actions are completed and the publishing status changes to Published, the tooltip icon is not visible.
