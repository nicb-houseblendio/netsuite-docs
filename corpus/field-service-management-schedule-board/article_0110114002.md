You can schedule or assign resources to tasks on the board and extension panels by dragging and dropping. Then, save your changes to NetSuite. You can't edit tasks after they've started.

## Selection {#subsect_79155002401}

You can select one or more tasks on the board or extension panel by clicking them or dragging a selection box around them. To toggle the selection, hold the Shift button and click on the tasks or drag a selection box around them.

When you select a task, linked tasks are also selected. By default, this applies to tasks on the same case. Hold the shift button then select individual linked tasks to schedule them independently.

## Drag and Drop {#subsect_19155026665}

Drag and drop selected tasks on the board to change their start date and time, end date and time, primary assignee, and secondary assignees.

Apply different drag modes by holding down these keys:

![scheduling drag and drop keys](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/scheduling_drag_and_drop_icon.png)

You can also assign or unassign tasks by dragging them between the board, assigned tasks panel, and unassigned tasks panel.

## Time Zones {#subsect_98155113688}

When you edit a task, it uses the time zone of your current view. If you intend to edit a task in a different time zone, change the time zone setting first to match that task. This way, you'll see the task in its local time and that it saves as expected.

## Saving or Cancelling {#subsect_83155131294}

Tasks with unsaved changes display using a red border. The total number of unsaved tasks also displays in the **Sync** menu.

![red border on tasks with unsaved changes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/scheduling_saving_cancelling_icon_1.png)

Click **Save** to prompt all unsaved tasks to save their changes to NetSuite. Click **Cancel** to clear all unsaved changes. You can also save or cancel changes for a single task from its context menu.

![save button on scheduling](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/scheduling_saving_cancelling_icon_2.png)

If an error occurs when saving a task, it displays the Error status icon and a red border, like an unsaved task. Click **Save** again to reattempt, or contact NetSuite Support if the problem persists.

![error when saving in scheduling](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/scheduling_saving_cancelling_icon_3.png)
