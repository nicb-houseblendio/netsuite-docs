User preferences and settings are preserved between uses of the scheduler. You can open settings by clicking the icon in the header.

![settings panel](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/settings_panel_icon.png)

## Hours Shown {#bridgehead_11154708804}

The board displays a date as a range of hours. By default, this is 9:00am to 5:00pm. You can change this by adjusting the **From** and **To** sliders or select **Show 24 hrs** option to see the whole day.

## Time Zone {#bridgehead_77154726534}

Select the time zone you want to view the board in. On the first launch, the default is your NetSuite time zone preference. But the following launches remember your preferred setting.

## View {#bridgehead_30154905216}

Show or hide elements on the scheduler by checking or unchecking these options.

## Resizing {#bridgehead_19154920615}

You can resize the Map, Control Panel, and Board Resource column by dragging their borders.
