The Field Service Management (FSM) Schedule Board helps schedulers (dispatchers) plan and manage work using a calendar-style board to view resources and time-based work items. It also includes map and filtering tools.

## Schedule Board Supported Browsers and Device Requirements {#subsect_0302042212}

FSM Schedule Board is supported on the latest tested versions of the following browsers:

-   Google Chrome
    
-   Microsoft Edge
    
-   Apple Safari
    

FSM Schedule Board is designed for non-touch devices with a minimum screen resolution of 1024 x 768 pixels. It won't work as expected on touch-enabled devices like tablets and smartphones. Schedule Board is accessed through NetSuite and requires a NetSuite license to view, plus a Full NetSuite license to use drag-and-drop features.

## Accessing the Schedule Board {#subsect_0302042302}

You can access FSM Schedule Board from the Field Service tab in your NetSuite account.

To open the Schedule Board, go to Field Service > Scheduler > Run.
