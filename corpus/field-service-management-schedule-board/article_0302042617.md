The Field Service Management (FSM) Schedule Board is designed around four core areas:

1.  **Board (calendar grid)**
    
    -   Displays a time window with resources in rows and dates across columns.
        
    -   Tasks appear as rectangular elements with labels and status icons, and can be single-day or multi-day.
        
2.  **Resources list (left side)**
    
    -   Resources are employees, vendors, or other NetSuite entity records you can assign work to.
        
    -   Resources display an avatar (image or initials) and may show an online status indicator when they've recently used FSM Mobile.
        
3.  **Map (optional panel)**
    
    Displays the location of resources, assigned tasks, and unassigned tasks for the selected date range.
    
4.  **Extension panels (filters and task lists)**
    
    Collapsible panels that let you filter which resources and tasks display on the board and map.
