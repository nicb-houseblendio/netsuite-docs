Note:

Starting with version 2024.08, maps are rendered using Oracle Maps.

The map displays the location of resources, their assigned tasks, and all unassigned tasks for the current date range. To pan the map, press the space bar then click and drag. To zoom, hold the space bar and scroll your mouse wheel. Click **Fit All** button in the bottom right to zoom out and fit all resources and tasks.

![map showing resources](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/map_diagram_icon.png)

## Resources {#bridgehead_10154306990}

![resources map icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/map_resources_icon.png)

Resources display a circular marker at their last known coordinate with their initials.

## Tasks {#bridgehead_24154406937}

![tasks map icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/map_tasks_icon.png)

Tasks display a rectangular marker at their coordinate. By default, this is the coordinate of the asset being serviced. Task markers use the same color and icon as the task on the board.

## Clusters {#bridgehead_39154427831}

At different zoom levels, resources or tasks close to each other group into a single marker, displaying the total number of present markers. Left-click on a marker cluster to zoom in and view all its markers.

![resource cluster map icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/map_resource_cluster_icon.png)

Resource clusters are circular, like the resource markers.

![task cluster map icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/map_task_clusters_icon.png)

Task clusters are rectangular, like the task markers. They use the color of the highest priority task.
