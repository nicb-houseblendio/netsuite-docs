Extension panels are collapsible panels that let you customize which resources and tasks display on the board and map by filtering values of their properties.

![extension panel](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/extension_panels_icon.png)

## Header {#bridgehead_83154511342}

Click the panel header to open or close it. The header shows a filter icon if filters are available. The unassigned tasks panel also shows a count of unassigned tasks for the current date view.

## Filters {#bridgehead_91154529732}

![extension panel filters](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/extension_panels_filters.png)

Click filters to show or hide the available filters. When hidden, filters display the number of filters you've applied. Each filter shows the selected filter values.

## Add {#bridgehead_84154546767}

Add a filter by clicking the dropdown and selecting the values to filter by. To select more than one, hold Cmd/Ctrl and click on the options or hold Shift and select a range of options.

## Remove {#bridgehead_6154603026}

Remove an individual filter value by clicking its label. Remove all values for a single filter by clicking **Clear** button in its drop down. Remove all filters by clicking the **Clear All** button.

## All/Any {#bridgehead_10154617221}

Change the filter logic to require the property to have all or any of the selected values.

## Results {#bridgehead_64154639333}

The resources or tasks that match the filters are displayed in the list. You can sort this list by selecting a sort option from the dropdown. You can toggle the view mode of this list between list and detailed list on the board.

![extension panel results](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/extension_panels_results.png)
