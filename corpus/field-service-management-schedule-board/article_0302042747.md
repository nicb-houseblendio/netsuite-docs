The Field Service Management (FSM) Schedule Board Map uses Oracle Maps. The map displays:

-   Resources as circular markers with images or initials.
    
-   Tasks as rectangular markers (using the same color and icon as the task on the board).
    
-   Clusters that group nearby resources and tasks at certain zoom levels.
    

Map controls include:

-   **Pan** - Press the space bar, then click and drag.
    
-   **Zoom** - Hold the space bar and scroll the mouse wheel.
    
-   **Fit All** - Zoom out and fit all resources and tasks on your screen.
