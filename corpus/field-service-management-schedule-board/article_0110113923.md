The board shows rows of resources and their assigned tasks for the current date range.

![board showing timeline and available resources](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/board_diagram_icon.png)

## Time Zone {#bridgehead_24154053159}

Tasks are displayed local to your current time zone. To change it, see [Settings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0110113955.html).

## Groups {#bridgehead_66154112147}

Resource rows are displayed in groups to logically segment them depending on your needs. By default, they're grouped by region.

![resources in groups](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/board_groups_icon.png)

## Mode {#bridgehead_42154130716}

You can view the board in different modes to see different information for resources and tasks. Select the mode in the header.

![board view mode options](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/board_mode_icon.png)

## List {#bridgehead_30154153238}

Simplified information is displayed.

![list view mode](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/board_list_icon.png)

## Detailed {#bridgehead_4154236645}

Detailed information is displayed.

![detailed view mode](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/board_detailed_icon.png)
