Resources are employees, vendors, or other NetSuite entity records you can assign work to. In the scheduler, resources display with a circular avatar displaying their image or initials.

## Online Status {#bridgehead_51152850966}

Resources display a green status icon when they've recently used the Field Service Mobile app.

![Green online status icon on a person's profile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/resources_online_status_icon.png)

## Context Menu {#bridgehead_30153009730}

Right clicking or **Ctrl + Left clicking** a resource opens a context menu. Click the menu title to go to the record in NetSuite.

## Marker {#bridgehead_13153127153}

![marker map pin icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/resources_context_menu_marker_icon.png)

Centers the map on the resource.

## Mobile {#bridgehead_58153209824}

![mobile icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/resources_context_menu_mobile_icon.png)

Opens a mobile emulator for that resource. This only shows up if the resource is a mobile user and your role has Mobile Administrator permission.

## Letter {#bridgehead_26153240295}

![letter icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/resources_context_menu_letter_icon.png)

Opens an email draft addressed to that resource using the default email application.

## Phone {#bridgehead_13153305824}

![phone icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/FieldServiceManagement/SchedulerUserGuide/resources_context_menu_phone_icon.png)

Starts a phone call with the default VoIP application.
