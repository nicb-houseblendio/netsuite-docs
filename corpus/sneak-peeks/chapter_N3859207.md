[NetSuite Sneak Peeks](https://www.netsuite.com/portal/products/newrelease.shtml)

Enhancements and changes described in the Sneak Peeks will not be available until your NetSuite account is upgraded to the new version. The date of your scheduled upgrade is displayed in the New Release Portlet in your account. If you are not sure which NetSuite version you are using, the NetSuite version number is displayed at the bottom of your Home page.

For detailed information about the new features and changes planned for this release, please review the [NetSuite Version 2026.2](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N3944673.html).

Important:

The Sneak Peeks are subject to change.
