Important:

This content is about Finland tax reports for accounts with SuiteTax. For content relevant for accounts without SuiteTax, see [Finland Help Topics](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_157849439820.html).

Finland Tax Reports SuiteApp is supported in the following languages:

-   English (U.S.)
    
-   Finnish
    
-   Swedish
    

For more information about displaying language settings, see [Setting Language Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543591052.html).

Note:

If you set the localization preference to any language other than these supported languages, the pages will be displayed in English (U.S.).
