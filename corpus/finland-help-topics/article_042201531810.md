Important:

This content is about Finland tax reports for accounts with SuiteTax. For content relevant for accounts without SuiteTax, see [Finland Help Topics](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_157849439820.html).

When the report was successfully generated, you can view its contents before exporting it for submission with Vero Skatt.

#### To view generated Finland VAT Return report

1.  Go to Tax>Country Tax Reports.
    
2.  In the **Report Execution Log** tab, click **View** next to the report you want to view.
    

For more information about generated country tax reports, see [Viewing a Generated Country Tax Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1528251207.html).
