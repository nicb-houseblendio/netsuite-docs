If you have a Finland subsidiary and nexus and you have the International Tax Reports SuiteApp installed in your NetSuite account, you can generate the Finland Periodic Tax Return. The report can be used as a source for filling in the official tax form for submission to the tax agency. You can also view sales and purchase reports by tax code, EU sales list reports, and Intrastat reports.

For more information, read the following Finland tax topics:

-   [Finland Tax Codes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1888692.html)
    
    [Tax Code Table for Finland](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1888692.html#bridgehead_N1888751)
    
-   [Finland VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1889688.html)
    
    [What goes into each box - Finland VAT Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1525745962.html)
    
-   [EU Sales List for Finland](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1889930.html)
    

For more information on the International Tax Reports SuiteApp, see [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html).
