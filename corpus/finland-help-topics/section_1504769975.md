Note:

This topic is for NetSuite accounts that use the EU Intrastat Report page to generate the Intrastat report.

The International Tax Reports SuiteApp enables you to generate the Intrastat report for Arrivals and Dispatches. In Finland, businesses must submit their Intrastat report on the 10 th working day after the end of each month.

NetSuite's International Tax Reports SuiteApp enables you to:

-   Generate the Intrastat Supplementary Declaration for Arrivals and Dispatches. For more information, see .
    

## Requirements {#subsect_66170307882}

To generate the Finland Intrastat report, you must do the following:

-   Install International Tax Reports SuiteApp. For more information, see [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html).
    
-   Grant roles access to Intrastat Reports. For more information, see [Granting Access to Intrastat Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2061400.html).
