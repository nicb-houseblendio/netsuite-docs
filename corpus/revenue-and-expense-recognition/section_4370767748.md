When a discount is applied to a revenue arrangement, the discount is allocated to the revenue elements proportionally, based on their revenue allocation ratio.

The Never flag can be used to prevent the allocation of a discount to items. If the Permit Discount field on an item record is set to **Never**, then discounts aren't applied to the item. For more information, see [Item Configuration for Advanced Revenue Management (Essentials) and (Revenue Allocation)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4340443927.html).
