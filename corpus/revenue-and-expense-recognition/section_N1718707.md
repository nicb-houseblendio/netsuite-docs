If you plan to use the VSOE feature, you must first enable the Revenue Recognition feature. See [Enabling the Revenue Recognition Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1678611.html).

#### To enable VSOE revenue recognition: {#procedure_N1718744}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Accounting** subtab.
    
3.  If Revenue Recognition isn't already enabled, you must check the **Revenue Recognition** box.
    
4.  Check the **VSOE** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
