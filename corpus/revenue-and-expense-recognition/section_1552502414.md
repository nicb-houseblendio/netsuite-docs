The recognition treatment lookup process is part of the process that creates revenue arrangements. The treatment lookup occurs prior to allocation when new revenue arrangements are created or merged.

The treatment lookup process evaluates treatment rules to determine whether to apply a recognition treatment to a revenue element. The process begins with the highest priority rule (priority number 1) and continues until it finds a rule that matches the element. When the process finds a match, it ends. If the process doesn't find a matching rule for the revenue element, NetSuite doesn't apply a recognition treatment to the element.

The treatment lookup process doesn't consider revenue elements that are sourced from journal entries.

The treatment lookup process runs only when revenue arrangements are created if one of the following conditions is true:

-   The Advanced Revenue Management (Revenue Allocation) feature is disabled.
    
-   The **Transaction Is Allocation Bundle** box on the revenue arrangement is clear.
    

The treatment lookup process doesn't run when revenue arrangements are updated.
