You can't delete recognition treatment rules that are associated with revenue elements. You can, however, edit the recognition treatment rule and check the Inactive box to prevent the rule from being used again until you clear the box.

#### To delete a recognition treatment rule:

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Revenue > Recognition Treatment Rules_, and click **Edit** for the rule you want to delete.
    
    By default, the Recognition Treatment Rules page lists rule in alphabetical order by Rule Name.
    
2.  From the **Actions** list, select **Delete**.
    
    After you confirm that you want to delete, you'll get an error message if the recognition treatment rule is assigned to revenue elements.
