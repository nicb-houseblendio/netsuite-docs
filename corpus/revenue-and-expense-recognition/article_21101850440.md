If you have the necessary permissions, you can delete existing revenue recognition field maps at any time.

#### To delete a revenue recognition field map: {#procedure_0116103814}

1.  Go to Setup > Accounting > Revenue Field Mapping.
    
2.  On the Revenue Recognition Field Mapping page, click **Edit** next to the map you want to delete.
    
3.  On the Revenue Recognition Field Map page, click Actions, and then select **Delete**.
    
4.  From the confirmation dialog, click **Yes**.
