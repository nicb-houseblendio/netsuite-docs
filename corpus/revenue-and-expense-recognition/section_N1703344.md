Important:

The functions discussed in this topic require the Revenue Commitments feature to be enabled.

The following permissions control the creation of Revenue Commitments and Revenue Commitment Reversals. These permissions are located on the Transactions tab of the Roles page.

-   Revenue Commitment
    
-   Revenue Commitment Reversal
    

For more information about how to assign these permissions, see [Using the Global Permissions Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N326630.html) and [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html).
