NetSuite lets you run a mass update for amortization schedules to update multiple records at one time. You can update the Is Recognized field on many amortization schedules at one time using a mass update. For all schedules that match the criteria set in the update form, the Is Recognized box is either checked or unchecked.

Mass updates to set amortization schedule status can be used to migrate data where an opening balance approach is used and schedules span the period when the balance is entered. To avoid duplication, lines with posting dates prior to the period when the opening balance is entered are marked as recognized.

#### To complete an Update Is Recognized Flag mass update: {#procedure_N1777438}

1.  Go to _Lists > Mass Update > Mass Updates_.
    
2.  Click **Amortization Schedules**.
    
3.  Click **Update Is Recognized Flag**.
    
4.  Complete the mass update form to set criteria, including selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **Is Recognized** field.
    
5.  Click **Preview** or **Save**.
    

For more information on mass updates, read [Mass Updates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1103335211.html).
