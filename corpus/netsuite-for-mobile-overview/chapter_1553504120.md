NetSuite for Mobile enables you to keep track of time, expenses, and key business metrics all from your phone. The app works offline, so you can keep working without connection to internet (like on a plane) and upload to NetSuite later.

You can download the app for free from the Apple App Store or Google Play, and it's ready to use as soon as you log in. The NetSuite desktop application takes care the setup for you.

All standard roles, including Employee and Advanced Partner Center are supported and enabled by default.

Note:

NetSuite for Mobile doesn't support access to Customer Center, Vendor Center, or the standard Partner Center. See [Mobile Device Access Permission](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158383508120.html).

With NetSuite for Mobile you can:

-   Manage expenses and attach copies of receipts. See [NetSuite for Mobile Expense Reporting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554107695.html).
    
-   Log and manage billable time. See [NetSuite for Mobile Time Tracking](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554297914.html).
    
-   Manage your NetSuite calendar. See [NetSuite for Mobile Calendar](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156258892134.html).
    
-   Monitor key business information with complete support for Home and Employee Center dashboard portlets. See [NetSuite for Mobile Dashboards](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1553858494.html).
    
-   View, edit, and attach files to records. See [NetSuite for Mobile Record Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554466775.html).
    
-   Log inbound and outbound business calls from your device and assign them to companies and customers. See [Tracking Calls with Phone Call Logging in NetSuite for iOS](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4120642128.html).
    
    Note:
    
    You can only track calls with NetSuite for iOS.
