You can access all your accounts from the Change Role list or from the My Roles page, including your production, sandbox, Release Preview, and development accounts. To open the My Roles page, click your current role in the upper-right corner of the NetSuite page.

On the My Roles page:

-   Your current role is shown in bold with a check mark, and **Logged in** in the Last Login column.
    
    ![Logged in role](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_LoggedInRole.png)
-   To reorder your accounts, click ![Sort icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_SortIcon.png) the **Account Name** or **Account Type** column header. You can sort the columns in ascending order or descending order.
    
-   To see the account order changes in the **Change Roles** list, refresh the browser and open the **Change Roles** list.
    
    -   The ordering of accounts is the same in all accounts you can access.
        
    -   Your preference for ordering accounts is stored in your browser. If you use a different browser or device to access NetSuite, you'll need to sort the columns again for that browser or device.
        

![My Roles page with list of accounts.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_SwitchOrSortAccounts.png)

## Hiding an Account {#subsect_161919963674}

When you hide an account, it only disappears from your view. You don't remove your access to the account.

#### To hide an account:

1.  Open the My Roles page. For more information, see [Opening the My Roles Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161583999334.html#subsect_161764849344).
    
2.  In the row of the account you want to hide hover over the More icon ![More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_MoreIcon.png) on the right side of the page.
    
3.  Select **Hide**.
    
4.  To see these changes in the Change Roles list, refresh the browser, and open the Change Roles list again.
    

## Showing Hidden Accounts {#subsect_161919967274}

You can see any accounts that you previously hid from view by clicking ![Show All icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_ShowAllIcon.png) **Show All** on the upper-right corner of the My Roles page.

#### To show hidden accounts:

1.  Open the My Roles page. For more information, see [Opening the My Roles Page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161583999334.html#subsect_161764849344).
    
2.  Click **Show All**. A **Hidden** icon ![Hide Current Role icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_HidCurrentRoleIcon.png) identifies any hidden accounts.
    
    -   To show an account, hover over the More icon ![More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_MoreIcon.png) and select **Show**.
        
    -   To remove your hidden accounts from view, click **Show All** again.
