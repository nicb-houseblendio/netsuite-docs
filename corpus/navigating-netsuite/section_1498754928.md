You don't need to know your NetSuite account ID to use NetSuite. In special cases, however, you may want to know what your account ID is.

You can find your NetSuite account ID at the beginning of the NetSuite URL. For example, if the URL is https://1234567.app.netsuite.com/, your account ID is 1234567.

Account IDs for Sandbox and Release Preview accounts contain an underscore, for example 123456\_RP. In the account-specific domain URL, the underscore becomes a hyphen and the capital letters of the acronym become lowercase letters. For example, the URL for a Sandbox account may look as follows: https://1234567-sb.app.netsuite.com. For more information, see [URLs for Account-Specific Domains](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1498251763.html).
