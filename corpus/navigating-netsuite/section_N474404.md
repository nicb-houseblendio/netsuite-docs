This section provides an overview of the basic user interface (UI) elements and navigation features in NetSuite. It's designed to help you become familiar with the layout, menus, buttons, and interactive components found throughout the application.

Watch the following video to see the common elements and tools of the NetSuite user interface (UI).

<a id="kaltura_player_28"></a>

The UI uses progressive disclosure. This means that the pages focus on your content. When you move your pointer over areas of a page, the links, menus, and icons that are appropriate for that area appear. If you don't see a link or button you expect, move your pointer around the area and see what appears.
