The role that you use affects what pages and records you can access in NetSuite. You may have more than one role in NetSuite.

The upper-right corner of every NetSuite page shows your name and current role.

![NetSuite page with a name and the current role.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/SwitchingRoles_CurrentRole.png)

-   Hover your cursor over this area to see the Change Roles list.
    
-   Click this area to go to the My Roles page.
    

#### To switch to a different role:

1.  Find your name and role in the upper-right corner of the NetSuite page.
    
2.  Hover to open the **Change Roles** list.
    
    A check mark indicates your current role.
    
3.  Select the role you want to use.
    
    If a company name is associated with the role, you'll see it on the page.
    
    Note:
    
    The Role section shows only roles available in the current account. If you're looking for a role in a different account, select the account in the **Or switch to another account** section to view the roles in that account. Sandbox accounts are indicated by an SB icon, and Release Preview accounts are indicated by an RP icon.
    
    ![Checkmark next to the active role.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/SwitchingRoles_HoverToSwitchRoles.png)

## Opening the My Roles Page {#subsect_161764849344}

On the My Roles page, you can switch to another role or another account. You can also hide a role from view, show all your hidden roles, and set a default role for the current account.

#### To open the My Roles page:

1.  Click your name and role in the upper-right corner of any NetSuite page.
    
2.  See the following topics for more information.
    
    -   [Hiding a Role](#subsect_161763016449)
        
    -   [Showing Hidden Roles](#subsect_161763079188)
        
    -   [Specifying a Default Role](#subsect_161583766916)
        

## Hiding a Role {#subsect_161763016449}

When you hide a role, it only disappears from your view. You don't disable the role.

#### To hide a role:

1.  Open the My Roles page. For more information, see [Opening the My Roles Page](#subsect_161764849344).
    
2.  In the row of the role you want to hide from view, hover over the More icon ![More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_MoreIcon.png) on the right side of the page.
    
3.  Select **Hide**. The role disappears from the Role list.
    
    Note:
    
    If you hide your current role, it stays in the Role list and a Hidden icon ![Hide Current Role icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_HidCurrentRoleIcon.png) marks it as hidden.
    
    ![Hidden Role icon on the My Roles page.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_HideCurrentlyLoggedInRole.png)
4.  To see your changes in the Change Roles list, refresh your browser and open the Change Roles list.
    

## Showing Hidden Roles {#subsect_161763079188}

You can see any roles that you previously hid from view by clicking ![Show All icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_ShowAllIcon.png) **Show All** in the upper-right corner of the My Roles page.

#### To show hidden roles:

1.  Open the My Roles page. For more information, see [Opening the My Roles Page](#subsect_161764849344).
    
2.  Click **Show All**.
    
    -   To show a role, hover over the More icon ![More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_MoreIcon.png) and select **Show**.
        
    -   To remove your hidden roles from view, click **Show All** again.
        

## Specifying a Default Role {#subsect_161583766916}

If you don't set a default role in an account, your browser logs you in with the last role you used. However, if you want to set a default role, you can do this on the My Roles page.

To set a default role in an account, first you select the account, then you select the role in that account that you want as default.

Note:

You can set only one default role in an account, but you can set up a default role in each account you can access.

#### To specify a default role:

1.  Open the My Roles page. For more information, see [Opening the My Roles Page](#subsect_161764849344).
    
    Note:
    
    The Roles section shows only the roles in your current account. To set a default role in a different account, select that account in the **Or switch to another account** section. Then you can see the roles available in that account.
    
2.  In the row of the role you want to make default, click the More icon ![More icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/MyRolesPage_MoreIcon.png) on the right side of the page.
    
3.  Select **Make Default**. Your default role preference for this account is saved.
    
    ![Make Default option on the My Roles page.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/NetSuiteBasics/SwitchingRoles_DefaultRole.png)
