## Training how you want it, when you need it {#bridgehead_56111235143}

NetSuite Education Services teaches NetSuite administrators and users the knowledge and skills needed to increase their productivity and efficiency. It includes Learning Cloud Support (LCS), an online learning resource with a variety of on-demand learning methods, education plans and tailored user enablement support options. From expert-led training to user adoption solutions to certification opportunities, NetSuite Education Services provides all of the resources you need.

## NetSuite training courses for every user at every level {#bridgehead_161778404425}

Your NetSuite user base is diverse. What one team or individual requires education for can differ widely across other areas of your organization. NetSuite Learning Cloud Support (LCS) Company Pass course offerings cover a wide spectrum of NetSuite product areas, industry leading practices and business processes. There are sure to be courses that fit the needs of every user within your company.

To see a sample of NetSuite Learning Cloud Support Company Pass, go to [NetSuite MyLearn](https://mylearn.oracle.com/netsuite/home).

## Learn the Way You Want {#bridgehead_12112516719}

Providing multiple ways for users to learn and develop skills is imperative for fostering ERP success in your organization. On-demand and live training options provide the flexibility to suit everyone's needs.

## Develop Certified In-house Resources {#bridgehead_12112612997}

Through multiple certification options, you can help your NetSuite administrators and power users gain expert-level skills, reducing the need for outside assistance.
