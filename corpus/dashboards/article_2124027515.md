Customer 360 offers a complete, 360-degree view of the customer, including their financial information, transaction history, and other related data.

The Customer 360 page collects customer information from different areas of NetSuite and includes actionable insights. You can use the metrics presented on this page to easily track interactions made with the customer, their financial position and profitability to your business, their purchasing trends, and more.

Narrative Insights on the Customer 360 page provides an AI-generated summary of your recent customer interactions. For more information, see [Customer 360 Narrative Insights](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0128041652.html).

Note:

Customer 360 is available on all accounts and you don't need to enable it.

There are three ways to access Customer 360 from NetSuite:

-   By clicking View Customer 360 on the customer record:
    
    ![View Customer 360 on the Customer record page outlined in red.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/C360_CustomerRecord.png)
-   By clicking Switch to Customer 360 View on the customer dashboard:
    
    ![Switch to Customer 360 view on the customer dashboard outlined in red.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/C360_CustomerDashboard.png)
-   By clicking the icon located next to the customer record on the left:
    
    ![Customer 360 icon on the Customer record page outlined in red.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/C360_CustomerRecordIcon.png)
