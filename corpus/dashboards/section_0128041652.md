The Customer 360 Narrative Insights feature provides an instant, comprehensive summary of your recent customer communications, sales, and more. Narrative Insights uses generative artificial intelligence (AI) to create a context-rich summary so that you can extract actionable intelligence from business data to expedite decision-making.

To generate Customer 360 Narrative Insights, the **Enable Narrative Insights** and the **Enable Customer 360 Narrative Insights** settings must be enabled. These preferences are enabled by default. To verify or change these settings, administrators can go to Setup > Company > AI Preferences > Narrative Insights. For more information about Narrative Insights preferences, see [Managing Narrative Insights Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6150024470.html).

#### To generate the Customer 360 Narrative Insights

1.  Verify that the **Enable Narrative Insights** and **Enable Customer 360 Narrative Insights** preferences are enabled.
    
2.  Open the Customer 360 View on your customer record. To access Customer 360, open a customer record and click **View Customer 360** in the top right corner.
    
3.  In the top right corner, click **Generate Insight**. A pop-up window with your AI-generated insights will appear.
    

For more information about Narrative Insights, see [Narrative Insights Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_135342304.html).
