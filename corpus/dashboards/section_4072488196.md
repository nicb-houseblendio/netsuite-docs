## Choosing Your Dashboard Layout {#subsect_0416045528}

You can choose among four layout types for every page that supports dashboards. Choose your preference from the Layout dropdown list in the upper right.

![Layout dropdown list](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/layouts4.png)

The available layout options are:

-   Two-column, narrow on the left.
    
-   Two-column, narrow on the right.
    
-   Three-column, wide in the middle. For this layout, you'll need a screen that's at least 1400 pixels wide.
    
-   One-column.
