KPI Scorecard portlets let you compare data across multiple KPIs. NetSuite stores these comparisons as separate KPI Scorecard records. You need to create a KPI scorecard before you can add it to your dashboard. To learn how to set up a KPI scorecard, see [Creating a KPI Scorecard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N612318.html).

#### To add a KPI Scorecard portlet to your dashboard:

1.  Click **Personalize** in the upper right corner of your dashboard or use **Personalize Dashboard** in the **Settings** portlet.
    
2.  On the **Standard Content** tab of the **Personalize Dashboard** palette, click the **KPI Scorecard** icon or drag it onto the dashboard.
    
    ![KPI Scorecard portlet icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/KPIscorecard_portlet._icon.png)
3.  Click **Set Up** in the portlet or menu to configure it.
    
4.  Choose the KPI scorecard to display and click **Save**. You can also set filters, pick the orientation, and choose whether to show the date row or trend graph icon.
    

For more information about adding KPI Scorecard portlets, see [Adding a KPI Scorecard Portlet to a Dashboard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N612018.html).
