**Opportunities and Estimates Dataset**

This dataset displays Opportunities and Estimates records combined in a single view. From here, you can track the status of both elements and their relationship. This data also lets you extract projected totals, estimate conversion percentages, and more.

**Orders and Returns Dataset**

This dataset displays Orders and Returns records combined in a single view. This helps you monitor sales status alongside any related returns, making it easier to track transaction progress and assess overall sales performance.

**Receivables Dataset**

This dataset displays information about all records impacting Accounts Receivable, such as Invoices, Statement Charges, Credit Memos, Payments, and so on. This data offers financial insights, including overdue invoices, available credit memos, and more.

**Sales Overview Dataset**

This dataset displays Invoiced sales, including Invoices, Cash Sales, Credit Memos, Journal Entries, and so on. This data offers valuable insights into sales activity, letting you track total sales over a specific period and identify sales trends.

**Top Selling Items Dataset**

This dataset displays the top-selling products along with all related lines in the report, such as line items, credit memos, statement charges, and so on. This information helps you understand customer purchasing trends and supports forecasting future sales.
