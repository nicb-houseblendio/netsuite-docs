You can publish information from news sources on your dashboard. By default, NetSuite offers feeds from CNN, Reuters, Yahoo, CBS Market Watch, and more. You can also add custom RSS feeds from other sites, or create your own feed to publish messages with other NetSuite users in your account. Generally, you can have up to two RSS/Atom feeds per page.

Note:

Currently, you can add only two RSS feeds each on the home tab and Issues tab (if available), but there's no limitation on custom tabs. For information, see [Creating Center Tabs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2890512.html).

#### To add an RSS/Atom feed portlet to a page: {#procedure_N587205}

1.  Click **Personalize** in the upper right corner of your dashboard or use **Personalize Dashboard** in the **Settings** portlet.
    
2.  On the **Standard Content** tab of the **Personalize Dashboard** palette, click the **RSS/Atom Feed** icon or drag it onto the dashboard.
    
    ![RSS/Atom Feed portlet icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/RSSPortletIcon.png)

#### To set up an RSS/AtomFeed portlet: {#procedure_N587235}

1.  Click **Set Up** in the portlet menu to open the setup popup window.
    
    ![RSS Feeds popup window.](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/RSSSetup.png)
2.  Select an RSS feed from the **Source** list.
    
    If you select one of the sources that NetSuite provides, the **Feed URL** field automatically contains the correct URL.
    
    If you select **Custom**, enter the URL of the feed in the **Feed URL** field. This URL can be from a website or your own HTML pages used as an RSS feed.
    
    To find the URL for a website's feed:
    
    1.  Go to the website and look for an RSS/XML link or ![RSS icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/rssicon.png) icon.
        
    2.  Click the link or icon to open the feed page.
        
    3.  Copy and paste this page's URL into the setup window's **Feed URL** field.
        
    
    To learn how to create your own custom RSS feed, see [Creating Custom RSS/Atom Feeds](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N587461.html).
    
3.  In **Number of Links**, type the number of links you want to appear in the portlet.
    
4.  If you only want to see links without additional details, clear the **Show Details** box. The details that you see depend on the RSS feed.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    
    You'll now see the feed information in your dashboard portlet.
    

If the portlet takes up too much space on your dashboard, click **Set Up** again and reduce the number of links or clear the Show Details box.
