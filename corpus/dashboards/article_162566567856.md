You can add a translatable name to your Analytics portlet. Translation strings and collections are automatically created when you enter the portlet name in the **Default Portlet Title** field of your workbook visualizations.

#### To add translatable Analytics portlet names:

1.  On your workbook visualization, click the information icon next to the workbook name.
    
    If you want to open the workbook from your Analytics portlet, see [Opening Workbooks from the Analytics Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1546873621.html).
    
2.  On the popup window, click **Workbook Script IDs**.
    
3.  On the Workbook Script IDs window, identify the **Default Portlet Title** column.
    
4.  Click the edit icon to add a translatable name for the workbook visualization that you want. Then, click **Apply**.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

If you enter a name in the translatable **Default Portlet Title** field, don't add one in the **Custom name** field of the Analytics portlet settings. The **Custom name** field replaces the translatable field name and translations won't be available.

For more information about workbook visualizations, see [Workbook Charts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1544211366.html), [Workbook Pivot Tables](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158042187611.html), and [Workbook Table Views](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158083630457.html).

For information about how to use translation collections, see [Working with Translation Collection Strings](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1544553733.html).
