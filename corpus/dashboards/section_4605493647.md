Custom portlets enable you to display SuiteScript results on your dashboard. You can add the following custom portlets on your dashboard:

-   **Dashboard Tiles** - For more information, see [Dashboard Tiles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1501565708.html).
    
-   **Navigation Portlet** - For more information, see [Navigation Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1501567502.html).
    

To use custom portlets, you first need to create a SuiteScript portlet script. To learn how to create portlet scripts, see [SuiteScript 2.1 Portlet Script Type](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4387799288.html).

#### To add a custom portlet to your dashboard:

1.  Click **Personalize** in the upper right corner of your dashboard, or use **Personalize Dashboard** in the **Settings** portlet.
    
2.  On the **Standard Content** tab of the **Personalize Dashboard** palette, click the **Custom Portlet** icon or drag it onto the dashboard.
    
    ![Custom Portlet icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/Custom_portlet_icon.png)
3.  To specify the script to be used by the portlet, click **Set Up** in the portlet or the portlet menu. Then, select the script from the **Source** list and click **Save**.
