The only reports that you can customize to create custom report snapshots are reports that are used to generate standard report snapshots.

Note:

The reports available to you in NetSuite depend upon your role and the features you've enabled.

The following reports can be customized to create custom report snapshots.

-   Alt.Sales and Billings Forecast vs. Quota
    
-   Campaign ROI Analysis Summary
    
-   Campaign Response Summary
    
-   Closed Case Analysis Summary
    
-   Customers by Partner Summary
    
-   Customer Profitability Summary
    
-   Earned Value by Project
    
-   Forecast (Outstanding) by Customer Summary
    
-   Forecast by Item Summary
    
-   Forecast by Sales Rep Summary
    
-   Forecast by Status Summary
    
-   Forecast by Customer Summary
    
-   Hosted Page Hits Detail
    
-   Hours & Earnings
    
-   Internal Search Summary
    
-   Inventory Revenue Summary
    
-   Item Orders Summary
    
-   Lead Source Analysis Summary
    
-   Open Case Analysis Summary
    
-   Open Invoices
    
-   Open Sales Orders
    
-   Page Hits Detail
    
-   Page Hits Summary
    
-   Partner Activity Summary
    
-   Payroll Liabilities
    
-   Pipeline by Sales Rep Summary
    
-   Pipeline by Status Summary
    
-   Pipeline by Customer Summary
    
-   Prospect Analysis Summary
    
-   Referrer Summary
    
-   Sales Activity by Sales Rep Summary
    
-   Sales by Customer Summary
    
-   Sales by Item Summary
    
-   Sales by Partner Summary
    
-   Sales by Sales Rep Summary
    
-   Sales Orders by Customer Summary
    
-   Sales Orders by Item Summary
    
-   Sales Orders by Partner Summary
    
-   Sales Orders by Promotion Code Summary
    
-   Sales Orders by Sales Rep Summary
    
-   (Search Engine) Keywords Summary
    
-   Time by Customer Summary
    
-   Time by Employee Summary
    
-   Time by Item Summary
    
-   Total Open Estimates
    
-   Total Open Opportunities Detail
    
-   Total Pipeline by Customer Summary
    
-   Total Pipeline by Sales Rep Summary
    
-   Total Pipeline by Statuses Summary
    
-   Utilization by Employee (deprecated)
