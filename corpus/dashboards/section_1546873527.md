You can add an Analytics portlet to your dashboard in the same way you add other portlets.

#### To add an Analytics portlet to a dashboard: {#procedure_1547479047}

1.  Click **Personalize** in the upper right corner your dashboard. You can also use **Personalize Dashboard** in the **Settings** portlet.
    
2.  On the **Standard Content** tab of the **Personalize Dashboard** palette, click the **Analytics** icon or drag it onto the dashboard.
    
    ![Analytics portlet icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/Analytics_portlet_icon.png)
    
    If you don't see the Analytics portlet in the **Personalize Dashboard** palette, the Analytics Workbook feature isn't enabled. See [Enabling SuiteAnalytics Workbook in Your NetSuite Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1504014192.html).
    

After you add an Analytics portlet, click Set Up in the portlet to choose a workbook visualization and set the layout options. For more information, see [Setting Up the Analytics Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1546873594.html).
