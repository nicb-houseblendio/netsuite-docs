After you pick a workbook visualization for the Analytics portlet, you can access and edit the associated workbook right through the portlet.

Note:

To edit workbooks you don't own, you need the Analytics Administrator permission. For information about the Analytics Administrator permission, see [The Analytics Administrator Permission](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1536693720.html).

#### To open a workbook from the Analytics portlet:

1.  Click the menu icon in the upper-right corner of the portlet.
    
2.  Select **Open in Workbook** from the dropdown menu.
    
    The workbook opens in a new browser tab.
    

For more information about workbook visualizations, see [Workbook Charts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1544211366.html), [Workbook Pivot Tables](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158042187611.html), and [Workbook Table Views](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158083630457.html).
