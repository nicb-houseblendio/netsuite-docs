A KPI meter portlet shows data for one of the KPIs from the Key Performance Indicators portlet.

Before you can add a KPI meter, you'll need to add and set up a Key Performance Indicators portlet first. For more information, see [Setting Up the Key Performance Indicators Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N596767.html).

#### To add a KPI Meter portlet to your dashboard:

1.  Click **Personalize** in the upper right corner of your dashboard or use **Personalize Dashboard** in the **Settings** portlet.
    
2.  On the **Standard Content** tab of the **Personalize Dashboard** palette, click the **KPI Meter** icon or drag it onto the dashboard.
    
    ![KPI Meter portlet icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/KPImeter_portlet._icon.png)
3.  In the portlet's dropdown menu, choose the KPI you want to show as a meter.
    
4.  For more display options, click **Set Up** in the portlet menu. You can change the time period, comparison, and highlighting for your KPI meter.
    

To learn more about KPI meter portlets, see [KPI Meters](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N605338.html).
