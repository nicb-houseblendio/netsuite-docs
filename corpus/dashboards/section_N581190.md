You can arrange the portlets on your dashboard to best fit your needs. You can move portlets up or down and from one column to another. For more information about dashboard layouts, see [Dashboard Layouts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4072488196.html).

#### To move a portlet on the dashboard: {#procedure_N581207}

1.  Move your pointer to the title of the portlet.
    
2.  When the pointer changes to crossed arrows, drag the portlet to its new location.
    

Portlet content may change if you move a portlet from a wide column to a narrow one.

-   The Key Performance Indicators portlet displays more data and options when it is placed in the wide column than when in a narrow column. For more details, see [Setting Up the Key Performance Indicators Portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N596767.html).
    
-   Custom search and list portlets show only the first 4 results columns in a narrow column, but show the first 9 in a wide column.
    
-   Report snapshot text is cut off at 25 characters when the portlet's in a narrow column. To avoid this, place it in the wide column.
