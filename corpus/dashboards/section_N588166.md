The Recent Records portlet displays a list of records that you've recently created, changed, or viewed with quick links to access them. It doesn't have a set up link, so you can't change its contents.

In this portlet, like others, the controls are hidden until you move your cursor over it. Each record has a link to view it. If you have edit permission for the record, you'll also see an **Edit** link on the right.

![Recent Records portlet](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/RecentRecordsCenter.png)

#### To add a Recent Records portlet to your dashboard:

1.  Click **Personalize** in the upper right corner of your dashboard or use **Personalize Dashboard** in the **Settings** portlet.
    
2.  On the **Standard Content** tab of the **Personalize Dashboard** palette, click the **Recent Records** icon or drag it onto the dashboard.
    

## Recent Records Menu {#subsect_0416054241}

Recent records are also listed on the recent records menu, ![Recent Records menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/ClockIcon.png), on the left of the navigation menu.

The following screenshot displays the Recent Records menu available from the ![Recent Records menu icon](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/ClockIcon.png) icon on the navigation menu. Click a link to display an individual record, or click All Recent Records to display a list of all records you have used recently, in reverse chronological order.

![All Recent Records menu](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/SuiteAnalyticsDashboardsSearchesReports/Dashboards/RecentRecordsMenu.png)
