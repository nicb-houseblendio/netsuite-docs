The following table describes taxation features in United Kingdom:

| Feature | Description | Prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Automatic Tax Record Provisioning for VAT Countries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557735917.html) | Your tax records are created when you select SuiteTax as your tax engine. SuiteTax enables you to use different tax engines per nexus. To begin provisioning your tax records, assign a tax registration to the subsidiary or company in SuiteTax. | Your NetSuite account administrator must submit a SuiteTax enablement request and enable the SuiteTax feature in your NetSuite account. See [Enabling the SuiteTax Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1513063459.html). Install the following SuiteApp:
-   SuiteTax Engine (see [Installing the SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557397007.html) and [Enabling the SuiteTax Plug-In](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557399475.html))

 |
| Reverse Charge Posting | This feature is a part of the core SuiteTax functionality and no longer requires any additional SuiteApp. |
| [Editing Default Tax Codes for Nexuses](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557736208.html) | When using the SuiteTax Engine, default tax codes are automatically provisioned for nexuses. After the SuiteTax Engine is installed, it is suggested that you review and edit the default tax codes for each nexus. |
| [Setting Up Compliance Text on Reverse Charge Sales Invoices](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557736683.html) | The SuiteTax Engine enables you to configure the extra information required for invoices where the customers are liable for tax (sale subject to reverse charge mechanism). The compliance text is shown on invoices that use tax codes with tax types where Reverse Charge for Sales is enabled. The compliance text default configured language and text is in English. Configuration for other languages is also available. To setup compliance text, you'll need to configure this feature in the nexus preferences and in the invoice templates. |

1.  SuiteTax Engine (see [Installing the SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557397007.html) and [Enabling the SuiteTax Plug-In](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557399475.html))
2.  Tax Reporting Framework (see [Installing Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528245293.html))

EMEA Localization (see [Prerequisites for Installing the EMEA Localization SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157200876022.html) and [Installing the EMEA Localization SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157201390489.html))
4.  United Kingdom Localization SuiteApp (see [Prerequisites for Installing the United Kingdom Localization SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157131471666.html) and [Installing the United Kingdom Localization SuiteApp](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_157131502005.html))

 |
