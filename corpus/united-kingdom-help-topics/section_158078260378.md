The SuitePeople U.K. Localization (SUL) SuiteApp provides added features to the employee record and saved searches available in your implementation. Use it to gather information and generate reports that will serve as reference when you fulfill employment obligations in the United Kingdom.

## SUL Features and Benefits {#subsect_158078774585}

With SUL, you can:

-   Monitor and gather information required to meet your employment obligations in the United Kingdom.
    
-   Keep track of the ethnic origins or groups of employees in the United Kingdom.
    
-   Keep track of the ethnic origins or groups, community background, and other related information of employees in Northern Ireland.
    
-   Save time in generating employee reports that you can use as reference when completing regulatory forms in the United Kingdom.
    

## SUL Roles {#subsect_158078784094}

By default, SUL is available to the following roles:

-   Administrator
    
-   HR Generalist (HRG)
    
-   Chief People Officer (CPO)
    

For information about the available forms and fields, read [SuitePeople U.K. Localization Forms and Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158078789277.html).

To install and set up the SuiteApp as an Administrator, read [Setting Up SuitePeople U.K. Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158078263868.html).

For information about the tasks that you can do with the SuiteApp, read [Working with SuitePeople U.K. Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158078268506.html).
