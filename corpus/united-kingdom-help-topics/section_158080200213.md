If you'd prefer to use an existing form in place of the SuitePeople U.K. Localization forms, add the fields to your form.

#### To add custom SUL fields to a form:

1.  Log in with an Administrator role.
    
2.  Go to Customization > Forms > Entry Forms.
    
3.  Locate the name of the form you want to edit, then click **Customize** or **Edit**.
    
4.  On the Custom Entry Form page, click the **Fields** subtab.
    
5.  From the **Fields** subtab, click the **Custom** subtab.
    
6.  Check the box beside the SUL field you want to add. For more information, read [SuitePeople U.K. Localization Forms and Fields](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158078789277.html).
    
7.  (Optional) If you want your records to be viewed and edited with this form, check the **Store Form with Record** box.
    
8.  Click **Save**.
