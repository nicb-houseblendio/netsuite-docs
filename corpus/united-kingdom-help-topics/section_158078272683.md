To ensure that the SuiteApp works properly, check the **Compensation Tracking** box in the Enable Features page. Find the box on the HR Information System section on the **Employees** subtab.

For more information, read [Enabling Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N232138.html).

To continue setting up the SuiteApp, read [Setting Up SuitePeople U.K. Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158078263868.html).
