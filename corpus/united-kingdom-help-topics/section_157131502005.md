The United Kingdom Localization SuiteApp version 2.0.0 and later is available on the SuiteApp Marketplace. If United Kingdom Localization SuiteApp 1.06.1 and earlier is installed in your account, you'll need to uninstall it before proceeding with United Kingdom Localization SuiteApp 2.0.0 and later. For more information about uninstalling SuiteApps, see [Uninstalling SuiteApps Obtained from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539867306.html).

#### Installing the United Kingdom Localization SuiteApp:

1.  Go to Customization > SuiteCloud Development > SuiteApp Marketplace.
    
2.  In the Search field, enter **United Kingdom Localization**.
    
3.  Click the **United Kingdom Localization** image.
    
4.  Click **Install**.
    

The United Kingdom Localization SuiteApp version 2.0.0 and above is automatically updated whenever enhancements or new features are added. Previous versions (1.06.1 and earlier) are no longer supported and are not automatically upgraded.

For more information about installing SuiteApps, see [Installing from the SuiteApp Marketplace](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1539799323.html).
