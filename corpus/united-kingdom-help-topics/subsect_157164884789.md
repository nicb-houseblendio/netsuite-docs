This topic describes the United Kingdom-specific field of the Intrastat Supplementary Declaration. To learn more about Intrastat and other fields used in this declaration, see [Intrastat Supplementary Declaration](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156889008497.html).

To use the Intrastat Reporting feature, the Tax Reporting Framework SuiteApp must be installed in your NetSuite account. For more information, see [Tax Reporting Framework](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1528262949.html).

To generate the UK Intrastat reports, see [Generating Localized Country Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1528250223.html). You can also export these reports to PDF, XLS, and CSV for online filing. The CSV format can be used to bulk submit. For more information, see [Exporting a Country Tax Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_156645000208.html).

For more information about the United Kingdom Intrastat reports, read the following articles:

-   [Intrastat - Dispatches](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161113516128.html)
    
-   [Intrastat - Arrivals](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_160526338780.html)
    
-   [Net Mass Requirement in United Kingdom Intrastat](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_061509585332.html)
