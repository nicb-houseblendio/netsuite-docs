The Multiple Currencies feature must be enabled for the following to function correctly:

-   Intrastat report
    
-   Intrastat saved searches
    
-   VAT 100 report (Multiple Currencies feature needed to display currency information)
    

Important:

If you decide to enable Multiple Currencies after installing the United Kingdom Localization SuiteApp, you'll need to reinstall Saved Searches in United Kingdom Localization. To do so, go to Setup > Tax Reporting > Features/Components Register and click **Reinstall** for **Saved Searches** in **Tax Reporting Framework**, **EMEA Localization**, and **United Kingdom Localization**.
