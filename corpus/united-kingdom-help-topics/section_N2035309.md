Note:

This topic is for NetSuite accounts that use the EU Intrastat Report page to generate the Intrastat report. If you are using the Country Tax Report page, see [United Kingdom Intrastat](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_157164884789.html).

NetSuite's International Tax Reports SuiteApp enables you to generate the Intrastat report for Arrivals and Dispatches. In the United Kingdom, generated on a monthly basis and must be submitted on the 21st day of the following month.

NetSuite's International Tax Reports SuiteApp enables you to:

-   Generate the Intrastat Supplementary Declaration for Arrivals and Dispatches. For more information, see [Generating the United Kingdom Intrastat Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160802796500.html).
    
-   Export the Intrastat report to a format acceptable for upload to the tax agency website. For more information, see [Exporting the United Kingdom Intrastat Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160802803773.html).
    

Important:

For U.K. VAT-registered businesses located in Great Britain, Intrastat Supplementary Declaration for Arrivals and Dispatches are no longer required for submission starting January 2021. For U.K. VAT-registered businesses located in Northern Ireland, Intrastat Supplementary Declaration for Arrivals and Dispatches are still required for submission until 2025.

## Requirements {#subsect_160802809334}

To generate the United Kingdom Intrastat report, you must have the following requirements:

-   Installed International Tax Reports SuiteApp. See [International Tax Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2051341.html).
    
-   Access to Intrastat Reports. See [Granting Access to Intrastat Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2061400.html).
    
-   Setup for online tax filing details in NetSuite. See [Setting Up Tax Filing for United Kingdom](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2029554.html).
    

To use the Intrastat reporting feature, read the following topics:

-   [United Kingdom Intrastat Report for Dispatches and Arrivals](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_160811090063.html)
