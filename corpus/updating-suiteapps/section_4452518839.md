Most SuiteApps are automatically updated in your account whenever there are changes. Issue fixes and enhancements become available after the update.

Some NetSuite SuiteApps don't update automatically, giving you the flexibility to choose when to apply the updates. These NetSuite SuiteApps are:

-   Reference Checkout
    
-   Reference My Account
    
-   Reference My Account Premium
    
-   Reference Shopflow
    
-   Content Delivery Service
    
-   Product Merchandising
    

See [Installed Bundle Updates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3397927.html) for instructions about updating an installed bundle.
