You can set preferences for select default accounts used for consigned inventory. Your preferences for default accounts apply to all vendor consigned items, unless you assign a different one to a specific item. Either way, your account updates don't apply to existing transactions. For instructions on setting preferences, see [Accounting Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159644455630.html).

On the Accounting Preferences page, you can find the following preferences on the **Items/Transactions** subtab:

-   **Default Vendor Consignment COGS Account** - The Cost of Goods Sold expense account that tracks the cost of vendor consigned inventory for all vendor-consigned items.
    
    By default, the regular COGS account is also used for consigned items if you don't assign one in this field or per item.
    
-   **Default Vendor Consigned Asset Account** - The asset account that tracks the value of vendor consigned inventory on hand for all vendor-consigned items.
    

If you want to assign an account to a specific item, see [Setting Up Consigned Inventory per Item](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218104904.html).
