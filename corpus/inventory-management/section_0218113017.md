You can track consigned inventory purchases from vendors and item receipts in your location. You can create or generate orders for consigned items through the following transactions or forms:

-   Purchase contracts - See [Creating Purchase Contracts for Consigned Inventory](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218113933.html).
    
-   Order item - See [Creating Bulk Orders for Consigned Inventory](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218114304.html).
    
-   Purchase orders - [Creating Purchase Orders for Consigned Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218114705.html).
    

When consigned and non-consigned orders arrive at your location, you can track received quantities on item receipts. The receiving process includes sold items that have been returned by your customers. See [Receiving Consigned Inventory](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218125433.html).

After you receive consigned items, you can further process those for return or those that have been consumed or sold. See the following topics:

-   [Returning Consigned Inventory to Vendors](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218125957.html)
    
-   [Generating Vendor Bills for Consigned Inventory](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218010249.html)
    
-   [Generating Consignment Consumption Reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0305112108.html)
    

Note:

If you can't find or select your vendor consigned items on these transactions, be sure that you have set them up properly. To set up items, see [Setting Up Consigned Inventory per Item](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218104904.html).

## Limitations on Purchasing and Receiving Consigned Items {#subsect_0218113342}

Note the following limitations for purchasing or receiving vendor consigned inventory:

-   Blanket purchase orders, Inbound shipments, and vendor prepayment are not supported. These transactions may include only non-consigned quantities and amounts from associated purchase orders.
    
-   Actual and estimated landed cost can't be set up and allocated for vendor consigned items.
    
-   Consigned inventory can't be added to standalone vendor return authorizations, standalone vendor bills, and standalone vendor credits.
    
-   The **Bill in Advance of Receipt** preference does not apply to consigned items.
    
-   Auto-generated purchase orders for special orders, drop shipments, and purchase requisitions are not supported.
