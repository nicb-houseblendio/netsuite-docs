Take note of the following limitations when using Supply Chain Management SuiteApp:

-   Long text in the report might get cut off to fit in the table.
    
-   The report will not be generated if there's an invalid UPC code error.
    
-   System-generated barcodes with long item barcode names are compressed to fit into the table. Consequently, the barcode may not be readable.
    
-   The **Mark All** and **Unmark All** buttons are only applicable to entries on the current page.
    
-   If you enable the **Send Form Generation Email** Notification, the email includes a link to the report. You can view the link only when you're logged in to your NetSuite account.
    
-   The maximum report file size is 5 MB.
