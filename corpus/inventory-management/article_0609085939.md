A trigger is an event in NetSuite that tells Quality Management an inspection is about to happen.

Quality Management supports the following triggers:

-   [Fulfillment Trigger](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0609090004.html)
    
-   [Item Receipt Trigger for Outsourced Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0313015320.html)
