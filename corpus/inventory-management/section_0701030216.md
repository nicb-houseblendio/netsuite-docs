If you want to uninstall the Smart Count SuiteApp, complete the following procedure.

#### To uninstall the SuiteApp: {#procedure_0708111419}

1.  Go to Customization > SuiteCloud Development > Installed SuiteApp List.
    
2.  Hover over the Actions icon next to the Smart Count SuiteApp and click **Uninstall**.
