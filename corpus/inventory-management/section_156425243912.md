After you set up the Supply Allocation feature, you can use Supply Allocation to maximize efficiency of allocating your inventory. The following topics can assist you with day-to-day allocation functions.

-   [Creating Allocation Strategies](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156433189079.html)
    
    Create an allocation strategy to define rules used to allocate particular inventory.
    
-   [Creating Order Allocation Schedules](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156425280048.html)
    
    Create schedules to automate when NetSuite calculates allocation to periodically re-assess the demand orders that are being prioritized to be fulfilled.
    
-   [Applying Allocation Strategies to Order Lines](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156518444628.html)
    
    After you have created allocation strategies, assign them on demand orders.
    
-   [Setting Supply Required By Dates on Order Lines](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156639322112.html)
    
    Define the date when demand on an order needs to be met.
    
-   [Supply Allocation Calculations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156556825274.html)
    
    Learn more about ways that allocation is calculated in NetSuite.
    
-   [Supply Allocation Exceptions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156518739289.html)
    
    If allocation calculations determine that supply will not meet demand in time, you are alerted.
    
-   [Allocating Supply to Order Demand](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156632657442.html)
    
    Supply can be allocated manually in addition to automated calculation.
