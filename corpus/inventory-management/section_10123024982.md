You can view the Smart Count SuiteApp pages in the following languages:

-   Chinese (Simplified)
    
-   Chinese (Traditional)
    
-   Czech
    
-   Danish
    
-   Dutch
    
-   English (AU)
    
-   English (CA)
    
-   English (International)
    
-   English (UK)
    
-   English (U.S.)
    
-   Finnish
    
-   French
    
-   French (Canada)
    
-   German
    
-   Indonesian
    
-   Italian
    
-   Japanese
    
-   Norwegian
    
-   Portuguese (Brazilian)
    
-   Russian
    
-   Spanish
    
-   Spanish (Latin America)
    
-   Swedish
    
-   Thai
    
-   Turkish
    
-   Vietnamese
    

For information about displaying the Smart Count SuiteApp in your preferred language, see [Setting Language Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1543591052.html).
