Before you can view the inspection execution log, [Enabling a Pass Fail Plug-in](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0519082750.html).

#### To view the inspection execution log:

1.  Go to Customization > Scripting > Scripts.
    
2.  Beside **QM Evaluate Inspection Result Values**, click **View**.
    
3.  To view your execution logs, click the **Execution Log** subtab.
