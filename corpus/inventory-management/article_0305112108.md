You can use the Consignment Purchases workbook to view purchase orders with vendor consigned items and to report consignment consumption to your vendors. It displays the status of consigned items from purchasing to consumption and billing. It excludes purchase orders in the following statuses: Closed, Fully Billed, Pending Supervisor Approval, Rejected By Supervisor, Planned, and Undefined. For more information about this workbook, see [Consignment Purchases Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0305095907.html).

You can access the workbook by clicking the **Analytics** tab, and then from the **Workbooks** subtab, click **Consignment Purchases Workbook**. You can view purchases per vendor or status by sorting or applying a filter to the **Vendor** or **Status** columns, respectively. At the header of the table, you can click the export icon to create a CSV file.
