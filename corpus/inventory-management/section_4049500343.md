Set up Distribution Resource Planning by enabling the feature and setting preferences.

Important:

Distribution Resource Planning is part of Time-Phased Planning which is no longer supported. This functionality has been replaced by Planning Rules in Supply Planning (MRP).

#### To enable distribution resource planning: {#procedure_4049513411}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  On the **Items & Inventory** subtab, check the box next to **Distribution Resource Planning**.
    
    Note:
    
    To use Distribution Demand Planning, you must first enable **Demand Planning** and **Multi-location Inventory**.
    
3.  Click **Save**.
    

#### To set distribution resource planning preferences: {#procedure_4049539892}

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Accounting Preferences_.
    
2.  Click the **Order Management** subtab.
    
3.  In the **Transfer Orders** section, make selections from the following fields:
    
    -   **Default Transfer Order Status**
        
    -   **Generate Transfer Orders in Supply Planning**
        
    
    For details about setting these preferences, see [Setting Transfer Order Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2310077.html).
    
4.  Click **Save**.
