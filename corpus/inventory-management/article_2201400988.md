Before you use the Inventory Optimization feature, enable the required features and assign access to the roles that use inventory optimization records and tasks.

## Prerequisites {#subsect_93201401003}

Inventory Optimization requires Advanced Inventory Management.

To segment inventory by item-location, the account must also use Advanced Item Location Configuration.

After you enable Inventory Optimization, you can include eligible Reorder Point items.

#### To enable Inventory Optimization: {#procedure_87201401020}

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Setup Tasks > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Items & Inventory** subtab.
    
3.  In the **Inventory** section, check the **Inventory Optimization** box.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
    

Note:

Enabling the feature does not immediately change item planning values. You must set preferences, include eligible items, and run the related calculations.

## Inventory Optimization Permission {#subsect_71201401036}

The **Inventory Optimization** permission controls access to segment records, segmentation tasks, event logs, and result pages. For more information, see [NetSuite Roles Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285436.html).

The **Accounting Preferences** permission controls access to Inventory Management Preferences and the **Optimization Preferences** subtab.

The **Items** permission controls access to item records and the Calculate Inventory Levels page.

To run Calculate Inventory Levels for Inventory Optimization, a role needs the **Items** and **Inventory Optimization** permissions.

After you enable Inventory Optimization and assign the required permission, users can review segment records and run segmentation calculations.
