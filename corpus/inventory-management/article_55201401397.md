Calculate inventory levels to update Purchase Lead Time, Safety Stock Level, Reorder Point, and Preferred Stock Level for eligible item-locations.

You can run the calculation manually or schedule it with **Day of Week To Perform Calculation** and **Time of Day to Perform Calculation**. This schedule is separate from the Inventory Optimization segmentation schedule.

Calculations use item-location demand history, lead-time history, service level, demand variability, and Preferred Stock Level Days.

Planning calculations are always at the item-location level.

Important:

Before you run the calculation, verify that segmentation is current unless service levels are manually overridden. A missing service level can cause the Inventory Optimization run to fail.

#### To calculate inventory levels: {#procedure_25184624923}

1.  Go to Transactions > Inventory > Calculate Inventory Levels.
    
2.  Click **Save**.
