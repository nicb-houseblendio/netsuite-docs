The Inventory Status feature enables you to associate attributes to items to drive internal processes. For example, you can create an Inspection status for items that are pending quality control inspection.

You can also choose to make inventory associated with each status available or unavailable to be allocated to orders. For example, you can create an unavailable status of Damaged to associate with items that should not be sold.

## Inventory Status Assignment or Selection {#subsect_0306030902}

The Inventory Status feature provides the standard **Good** status. It is assigned as the default status and it cannot be deleted. When you create additional statuses, you can assign a different one when you receive an item, build an assembly, or adjust inventory. For existing inventory in your location, you can select the inventory status of available quantities that you want to add to a transaction. You cannot select the status of items with no available quantity or negative quantities, including component items and lot or serialized items.

For example, you enable the Inventory Status feature and create a transfer order for 12 quantities of Item Y. This item has 50 available quantities in your location. When you fulfill the order, you can assign a status to the 12 quantities that you want transfer to another location.

If you have existing transactions prior to enabling the Inventory Status feature, see [Creating Inventory Status Records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1498074763.html).

## Inventory Status and Inventory Balances {#subsect_0306031017}

On item lines of a transaction in edit mode, you can view the total on-hand quantity of an item in a location. In view or edit mode, you can view the available quantity based on any or all of the following inventory details: bin, inventory status, and lot or serial number. For more information about inventory quantities, see [Assessing Stock Levels](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2262573.html).

To track inventory balances and view negative quantities, see [Tracking Inventory Balances By Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1515699529.html).

Available and on-hand inventory balances on the following records and searches are shown by inventory status.

-   Inventory Balance Search
    
-   Inventory Detail Search
    
-   Inventory Item
    
-   Lot Numbered Item
    
-   Serialized Item
