Administrators can create or customize roles for the Supply Chain Management SuiteApp. For more information, see [Assigning Roles to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N897798.html) and [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html).

By default, these roles have full permissions to the Supply Chain Management SuiteApp's reports:

-   Accountant
    
-   Accountant (Reviewer)
    
-   Bookkeeper
    
-   CEO
    
-   CFO
    
-   Sales Vice President
    
-   Warehouse Manager
    

Other roles for Supply Chain Management require CRM groups permission to generate the Manufacturing Dispatch List and Manufacturing Traveler reports.

#### To grant the CRM groups permission to custom roles:

1.  Go to _Setup > Users/Roles > Manage Roles_.
    
2.  Click **Edit** next to the role that you want to grant permission to generate reports.
    
3.  On the **Permissions** subtab, go the **Lists** subtab.
    
4.  From the **Permission** list, select **CRM Groups**.
    
5.  In the Level column, select **View**.
    
6.  Click **Add**, and then click **Save**.
