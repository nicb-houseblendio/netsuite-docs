By default, the system uses a standard Certificate of Analysis (COA) template for COA reports. You can also make your own custom template to generate a COA in the format that works best for your organization.

To use a custom COA template for COA reports, first create and define a custom saved search for COA. Then, make a custom COA template and add it to the **QM SS Generate COA Document** script.

Perform the procedures in the following sections:

1.  [Creating a Custom Saved Search for COA](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163613570909.html)
    
2.  [Defining a COA Active Saved Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163614224680.html)
    
3.  [Creating a Custom COA Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163613701612.html)
    
4.  [Adding a COA Template](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163613710675.html)
