After you submit the supply plan generation page, some plans may take a while to generate. You can check the status to monitor progress.

Important:

The NetSuite [Supply Planning](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159171867422.html) (MRP) solution replaces Time-Phased Planning and offers more features and better performance.

New customers should use the [Supply Planning](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159171867422.html) (MRP) solution. Existing customers should plan to move from Time-Phased Planning to MRP.

#### To monitor supply plan creation status: {#procedure_N2293829}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Demand Planning > Generate Item Supply Plan > Status_.
    
2.  The Generate Item Supply Plan Status page lists all page submissions.
    
3.  For each line, the following shows:
    
    -   **Date and time created**
        
    -   **Percent complete**
        
    -   **Status**
        
4.  Filter the list of plans by entering a date range in the **From** and **To** fields.
    
5.  After all supply plan calculations are finished, the status for the line shows **Completed**.
