**Datasets**

-   [Pegging Analysis Dataset](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0730021554.html)
    

**Workbook Templates**

-   [Pegging Analysis (Demand View) Workbook](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0730023051.html)
