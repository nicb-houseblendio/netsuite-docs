The Sales Channel Allocation feature enables planners to save inventory and future supplies to sales orders in these channels.

#### To enable sales channels:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Items & Inventory** subtab.
    
3.  In the Inventory section, check the **Supply Allocation** box.
    
    For more information, see [Enabling Supply Allocation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_156425077511.html)
    
4.  Check the **Sales Channel Allocation** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
