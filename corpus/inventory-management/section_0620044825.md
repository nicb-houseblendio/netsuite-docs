This topic is intended for Administrators and Smart Count Inventory Managers who can review, approve, reject, or assign items for recount.

The Smart Count Review page is your central workspace for managing inventory counts within Smart Count SuiteApp.

You'll find up to 5,000 item count records listed for review at a time. If you're an administrator, you can also view records across all subsidiaries.

To access the Smart Count Review page, go to **Transactions > Smart Count > Count Review**.

This topic explains:

-   Optimizing performance of the Smart Count Review page.
    
-   Filtering and analyzing count data.
    
-   Understanding KPIs and reports.
    
-   Approving, rejecting, or recounting items (including serialized/lot-numbered items).
    

If you encounter errors during review, read [Smart Count Error Messages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0816013247.html) to understand the errors.
