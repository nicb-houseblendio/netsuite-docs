To begin your setup, you must enable the required features for inventory, location, and orders. You can also enable additional features that suit your business requirements. The features you enable determine which capabilities are available in your account. See [Enabling Features for Inventory Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161963741628.html).

Whether you have one or multiple locations and items, create inventory item and location records accordingly. See [Inventory Setup with Locations and Multi-Locations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2252794.html).

Depending on your business requirements, you can set the following preferences or convert non-inventory items at any time:

-   [Distributing Inventory](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2304534.html)
    
-   [Converting Non-Inventory Items to Inventory Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2258046.html)
    
-   [Setting the Inventory Level Warnings Preference](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2254698.html)
    
-   [Non-Available Inventory Settings for Locations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2307648.html)
    
-   [Configuring Per-Line Locations for Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_162024160153.html)
    

For accounts that use specific Inventory features, you can set additional preferences or settings as described in the following topics:

-   [Setting Bin Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2273755.html)
    
-   [Setting Transfer Order Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2310077.html)
    
-   [Setting Up Inventory Count](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2297156.html)
    
-   [Inventory Allocation Based on Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1517948058.html)
    
-   [Working with Item Location Attributes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1504109154.html)
