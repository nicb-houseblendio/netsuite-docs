Narrative insights for the Inventory Back Order report give an overview of the impact of back orders for items committed on sales orders. They identify the top SKUs and item types affected by your back orders, as well as the highly impacted customers. For instructions and guidelines that apply to all AI-generated insights, see [Narrative Insights Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_135342304.html).

Each section may contain the following AI-generated content based on your report results:

-   **Key Points** - may include these metrics: Top Impacted SKUs, Major Impacted Customers, Top Impacted Item Types
    
-   **Key Takeaways** - may provide suggestions around dealing with high backordered quantities and risk concentrations
    

To enable narrative insights, see [Managing Narrative Insights Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_6150024470.html).
