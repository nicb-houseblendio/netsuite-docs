The Pegging Analysis (Demand View) workbook template shows pegging data from the Supply Planning Workbench's demand view. You can view demand and supply data for each pegging level associated with an independent demand. Use it during capacity planning to determine top-level demand, demand dates, quantities, and other relevant details.

This workbook template is based on the Pegging Analysis dataset. For information about the data provided by the dataset, see [Pegging Analysis Dataset](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0730021554.html).

If you can't access this workbook, it may need to be shared by someone with the Administrator role. See [Sharing Workbooks and Datasets](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0801122218.html).
