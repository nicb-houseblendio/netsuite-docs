The Inventory Activity Detail report shows the activity associated with your inventory items. The report shows the quantity per transaction of inventory items.

You can use this report to identify trends in sales for items. Then you can make informed decisions about stocking your items.

## To see an inventory activity detail report: {#bridgehead_N2356033}

Go to _Reports > Inventory/Items > Inventory Activity Detail_.

A message appears indicating that your report is loading. The status bar indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** to stop the report from loading.

From the results of your report, you can view AI-generated narrative insights. See [Inventory Activity Detail Report Insights](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0205052048.html).
