The Inventory Profitability report shows cost, revenue, and profitability information about your inventory. You can use this report to view and analyze profitability of your items.

Note:

NetSuite returns values only if the reporting period has revenue, such as invoice or cash sales.

For each inventory item, this report details:

-   item name
    
-   description
    
-   quantity sold
    
-   total cost
    
-   total revenue
    
-   gross profit dollars
    
-   percent of total profit
    
-   gross profit percentage
    
    Note:
    
    The gross profit percentage calculations are based on selling price, not cost.
    

If you operate an online store with NetSuite, this report includes sales from your online store.

You can view this report to determine margins on your items and identify items that are most and least profitable.

## To see an inventory profitability report: {#bridgehead_N2358712}

Go to _Reports > Inventory/Items > Inventory Profitability_.

A message appears indicating that your report is loading. The status bar indicates the progress as your report loads. You can click **Cancel Report** to stop the report from loading.
