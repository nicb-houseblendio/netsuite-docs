Follow this procedure if your [Derived Field Plug-in Implementation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163346884060.html) fails and you want to configure the appropriate recipients to receive an email notification.

For more information about the status of your derived field, see [Derived Field Evaluation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163338333671.html) and [Viewing the Derived Field Plug-in Implementation Status](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_163708825766.html).

#### To configure email recipients for a Derived Field Plug-in implementation failure:

1.  Go to Customization > Lists, Records, & Fields > Record Types.
    
2.  Next to **QM Parameters**, click **New Record**.
    
    In the **Custom Form** field, the default selection is **Custom QM Dynamic Parameters Form**.
    
3.  To remove all references to this record from your account, check the **Inactive** box.
    
4.  From the **Key** list, select **Email**.
    
5.  In the **Value** field, enter each recipient's email address, separated by commas.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
