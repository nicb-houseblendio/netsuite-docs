Supply Planning enables you to create supply plans. A supply plan details when to buy and manufacture items to fulfill demand. This information is displayed on the supply planning workbench. The supply plan accounts for multiple items across multiple locations by subsidiary.

Supply Planning enables you to create multiple plans. Plan definition rules enable you to create output based on the rule definition.

Before you can build a supply plan, complete the following prerequisites activities:

-   [Creating Planning Item Categories to an Item](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161944790139.html)
    
-   [Creating Planning Item Groups](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161944820656.html)
    
-   [Creating Planning Rule Groups](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_161944833572.html)
    
-   [Creating a Supply Planning Definition and Schedule](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159180786193.html)
    
-   [Planning Repository](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159172389289.html)
    
-   [Supply Planning Process](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_159172577944.html)
