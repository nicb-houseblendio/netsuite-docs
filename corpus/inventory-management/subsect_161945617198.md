Complete the following procedure to enable the Reallocate Order items to generate intelligent supply re-allocation recommendations.

#### To set up access to reallocate order items:

1.  Go to Setup > User/Roles > Manage Roles > New.
    
2.  In the **Role** page, click the **Permissions** subtab.
    
3.  Click the **Lists** tab.
    
4.  In the **Permissions** list, select **Reallocate Order Item**.
    
5.  In the **Level** column, check that you have **View** permission.
