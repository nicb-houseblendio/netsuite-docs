You can set preferences to customize the report appearance and specify which details to include.

#### To set up supply chain preferences:

1.  Go to Transactions > Management > Supply Chain Management.
    
2.  On the Supply Chain Management page, click **Preferences**.
    
3.  Click **Edit**.
    
4.  To enable other Supply Chain Management features, read the following topics:
    
    -   [Enabling Customer Part Number](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4575970034.html#bridgehead_4605188487)
        
    -   [Enabling Engineering Change Order](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_1531289393.html)
        
    -   [Enabling the Estimated Landed Cost Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/bridgehead_4605321070.html)
        
    -   [Enabling Item Substitution on Sales Orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1527060437.html#bridgehead_1527062466)
        
5.  On the **General** subtab, select a value and check the following boxes as needed:
    
    1.  **Print Barcode** - Check this box if you want to print the barcode on the report.
        
    2.  **Send Form Generation Email Notification** - Check this box if you want to receive email notifications when supply chain management forms or reports have been generated.
        
    3.  **Item Barcode** - Select whether you want the barcode to display the **Item Name** or **UPC Code** when generating it.
        
6.  On the **Inventory** subtab, select or enter values for the following fields:
    
    1.  **Sort Inventory Count Sheet By** - Select whether to sort the inventory count sheet by **Item** or **Bin**.
        
    2.  **No. of Line for Serial/Lot Number** - Enter the number of lines you want to allot for the serial or lot number.
        
7.  Click **Save**.
