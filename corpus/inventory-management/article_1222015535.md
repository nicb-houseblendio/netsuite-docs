To use custom roles with the Smart Count SuiteApp, you must provide access to specific pages and deployment records.

## Access to Count Review and Dashboard Pages {#subsect_22164118564}

To provide your custom roles with the access to the Count Review and Dashboard pages, complete the following steps:

#### To provide access to count review and dashboard: {#procedure_0822044936}

1.  Go to Customization > Scripting > Single Page Applications.
    
2.  Click the link to the page that you want to provide access to.
    
3.  Click the **Configuration** subtab.
    
4.  In the Release Audience section, click the **Set Up Audience** button.
    
5.  Check the boxes of the roles that you want to add.
    
6.  Click **Save**.

#### To provide access to deployment records: {#procedure_0822050957}

1.  Go to Customization > Scripting > Script Deployments.
    
2.  Locate the deployment record and click **Edit**.
    
3.  Click the **Audience** subtab.
    
4.  From the **Roles** list, select the custom role that you want to give access to the deployment record.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

## Access to Mobile App for Custom Roles {#subsect_28210230101}

To provide access to Smart Count mobile app for your custom roles, complete the following steps.

#### To provide access to mobile app: {#procedure_0914090357}

1.  Go to Setup > Custom > Mobile Applications.
    
2.  Click **View** beside Smart Count.
    
3.  Click **Configure Independent Pages**.
    
4.  In the Page field, select **page\_TaskListIndependentPage**
    
5.  In the Page Element section, on the **Mobile Page Element** field, select **TaskListIcon - Icon**
    
6.  From the **Roles** list, select the custom counter role that you created.
    
7.  Click **Save and Update App**.
    
8.  Go to Customization > Center and Tabs > Center Tabs.
    
9.  Click **Edit** beside Mobile Emulator center tab with Shipping Center type.
    
10.  Click the **Audience** subtab.
     
11.  From the **Roles** list, select the custom role to which you want to provide access.
     
12.  Click **Save**.
