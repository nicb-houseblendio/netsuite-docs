The Smart Count SuiteApp lets you complete count for all items that are in paused state or due for count. To complete count for all items, perform the following steps.

#### To complete all counts: {#procedure_0701115241}

1.  Log in to NetSuite using your mobile device.
    
2.  Based on the role you are using, do any of the following:
    
    -   Go to Setup > Custom > Mobile - App.
        
    -   Go to Mobile Emulator > Mobile Emulator > Mobile Screen.
        
3.  Tap **Smart Count**.
    
4.  Tap a location.
    
5.  From the Smart Count Menu icon, tap **Complete All Counts**.
    
6.  (Optional) In the **Count Configuration** field, select a value to display items based on the selected count configuration.
    
7.  To complete count for all items, check the box beside the Bin column on the table header.
    
8.  Tap **Complete All Counts**.
