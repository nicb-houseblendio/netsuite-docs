Review Inventory Optimization segments to manage the default service level used by planning calculations for each ABC and 123 category combination. You can override or reset a segment's service level, or set a different service level for an individual item or item location.

#### To review or edit an Inventory Optimization segment: {#procedure_68201401177}

1.  Go to Lists > Supply Chain > Inventory Optimization Segments.
    
2.  Open the segment record.
    
3.  Click **Edit**.
    
4.  To use a service level other than the default, check **Override Service Level**.
    
5.  In the **Service Level** field, enter the service level percentage. The service level must be from 50% through 99.99%.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
    

To reset the values, click **Reset Service Level**, then click **Save**. When you reset a segment service level, NetSuite clears the override and restores the default service level for the segment.

## Service Level Overrides {#subsect_80201401225}

Service levels can originate from these places:

-   The Inventory Optimization segment record.
    
-   The item record, when segmentation is calculated by item.
    
-   The item-location, when segmentation is calculated by item-location.
    

If an item or item-location has an override, segmentation keeps that service level value.
