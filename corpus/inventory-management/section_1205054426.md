To view the information in all portlets, it is necessary to provide access to required features and permissions.

To enable the required features, read [Enabling Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N232138.html). To update required role permissions, read [Customizing or Creating NetSuite Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285937.html) and [Assigning Roles to an Employee](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N897798.html).

Other features and permissions:

-   You can see **units** in the portlet only if the **Multiple Units of Measure** feature and permission is enabled in your account.
    
-   The **Currency symbol** in the charts portlet appears only if the **Multiple Currencies** feature is enabled in your account.
