SuiteAnalytics offers inventory dataset and workbook templates with predefined source data and criteria. Depending on the template, a workbook can include table views, pivot tables, and charts.

This section contains the information for the Inventory SuiteAnalytics workbooks in NetSuite. For more information about SuiteAnalytics workbooks, see [Workbook and Dataset Templates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1544220378.html).
