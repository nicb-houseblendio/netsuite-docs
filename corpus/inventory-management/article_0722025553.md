Here's how to filter a quality specification queue.

#### To filter a quality specification queue:

1.  Click the **Filter** icon (![Tablet Filter](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/InventoryManagement/Quality/TabletFiltericon.png)).
    
2.  In the Filter section, select which **Quality Specification Queue** field you want to filter.
    
3.  From the list, select **Any**, or another filter from one, some, or all queue fields.
    
    To clear your previous selections, click **Clear All**.
    
    To go back to the previous page, click the **Go Back** icon (![Go Back](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/img/InventoryManagement/Quality/TabletGoBackIcon.png)).
