## Overview {#bridgehead_158374170494}

The Auto Assign UPC SuiteApp streamlines UPC management for inventory and assembly items in NetSuite.

It supports both standard and matrix items, making UPC assignment efficient and traceable.

This SuiteApp links UPC records directly to item records for tracking and management.

### Key Features {#subsect_1229045001}

The Auto Assign UPC SuiteApp lets you:

-   Automatically assigns UPCs to new items based on your preferences.
    
-   Lets you create, remove, and manage UPCs manually or through CSV import.
    
-   Tracks when UPCs are assigned or retired.
    
-   Provides saved searches for monitoring UPC status and assignments.
    
-   Supports automation scripts for daily or scheduled UPC assignment and retirement.
    

### Availability {#subsect_1229045424}

Auto Assign UPC is a managed SuiteApp and automatically updated whenever there are updates. To get access to this SuiteApp, contact your NetSuite account manager.

Proceed to [Setting Up Auto Assign UPC](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158573839837.html) to install and configure the SuiteApp.

## Auto Assign UPC Limitations {#subsect_41172958290}

This Auto Assign UPC SuiteApp has the following limitations:

-   Manually created UPCs aren't tracked by the SuiteApp.
    
-   For parent matrix items, don't change the UPC assignment option if child items already have UPCs.
