After you submit the demand plan generation page, some plans may take a while to generate. You can check the plan status to monitor progress.

#### To monitor demand plan creation status: {#procedure_N2291634}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Demand Planning > Calculate Item Demand Plan > Status_.
    
2.  The Calculate Item Demand Plan Status page lists all page submissions.
    
    For each line, the following shows:
    
    -   **Date and time created**
        
    -   **Percent complete**
        
    -   **Status**
        
3.  Filter the list of plans by entering a date range in the **From** and **To** fields.
    
4.  After all demand plan calculations are finished, the status for the line shows **Complete**.
