When you enable the Consigned Inventory Management feature, you can also enable related features for purchasing, inventory management, locations, manufacturing, and order fulfillment.

1.  [Enabling Consigned Inventory Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218102627.html) - Enable required features, as well as optional ones based on your inventory requirements.
    
2.  [Distributing Inventory](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2304534.html) - If you have existing non-consigned items prior to enabling the Multi-Location Inventory feature, you must distribute them to your locations. You must complete this procedure before you set up consigned inventory for items.
    
3.  [Setting Default Account Preferences for Consigned Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218104008.html)
    
4.  [Setting Up Consigned Inventory per Item](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218104904.html)
    
5.  [Creating Consigned Inventory Statuses](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218110128.html)
    

If you want to disable the Consigned Inventory Management feature or related item settings, see [Disabling Consigned Inventory Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0218110516.html).
