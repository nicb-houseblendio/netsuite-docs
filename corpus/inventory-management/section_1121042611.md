Item 360 Dashboard consists of multiple portlets. Click a portlet to see transactional records and other details that impact an item. The selected portlets expands and collapses in the center of the page.

The columns in the portlets are dynamically displayed based on the features enabled or selected item type. For example, if the searched item is not a lot item, then the Inventory by Lot portlet will not appear in the Inventory page.
