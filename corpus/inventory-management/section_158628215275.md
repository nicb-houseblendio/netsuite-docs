Inspections standards provide guidelines for inspectors to follow while performing inspections. These standards help establish inspection pass or fail criteria. Inspection standards can represent organizational or industry standards that define acceptable production measurements or values. They relate to data fields through inspection rules.

For more information about inspection standards, see the following topics:

-   [Creating an Inspection Standard](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_0519075414.html)
