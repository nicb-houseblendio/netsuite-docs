The Counted Items page displays only items with the status **Done** for the current day. This includes item counts that are pending approval or have tolerance errors.

#### To view all counted items: {#procedure_8142927492}

1.  Log in to NetSuite using your mobile device.
    
2.  Based on the role you are using, do any of the following:
    
    -   Go to Setup > Custom > Mobile - App.
        
    -   Go to Mobile Emulator > Mobile Emulator > Mobile Screen.
        
3.  Tap **Smart Count**.
    
4.  Tap a location.
    
5.  From the Smart Count Menu icon, tap **Counted Items**.
    
6.  (Optional) To view the counted items associated with a count configuration, select a value in the
    
    **Count Configuration** field.
