The Manufacturing page provides transactions related to the work orders activities of an item.

You can go to this page by going to Reports > Item 360 > Item 360, and then click on the **Manufacturing** page.

## Open Work Orders (Assembly) {#bridgehead_10182457888}

The Open Work Orders (Assembly) portlet shows released work orders where the item is the assembly item. Click the hyperlink in the **WO#** column to go to the corresponding work order record.

## Open Work Orders (Components) {#bridgehead_35182511368}

The Open Work Orders (Components) portlet shows released work orders where the item is used as a component. Click the hyperlink in the **WO#** column to go to the corresponding work order record.

## Planned Work Orders {#bridgehead_93182532759}

The Planned Work Orders portlet shows planned work orders for the item that are in planned or released status, including those with the status **Planned**.

## Reorder Point Work Orders {#bridgehead_92182521112}

The Reorder Point Work Orders portlet shows work orders for the item that need reordering based on the reorder point.
