After you create a demand plan, you can open it to view or edit details. For example, you might want to update projection totals or delete the plan.

#### To view or edit an existing item demand plan: {#procedure_N2291999}

1.  Go to _Transactions > Demand Planning > Item Demand Plans_.
    
2.  Do one of the following:
    
    -   Click **View** next to a plan to open it.
        
    -   Click **Edit** next to a plan to make changes.
        
        Note:
        
        The only header fields that can be edited on the plan are the **Custom Form**, **Unit of Measure**, and **Memo**.
        
        These fields are for viewing only: **Location**, **Item**, **Projection Method**, **Projection Interval**, **Projection Start Date**, **Projection Duration**, and **Historical Analysis Duration**.
        
3.  You can change your view of the demand plan by adjusting the date range or the plan view, as described below:
    
    1.  **Year** - Enter the year to view.
        
    2.  **Month** - Select a month to view.
        
    3.  **Start Date** - Enter the first date of the period you want to view.
        
    4.  **End Date** - Enter the last date of the period you want to view.
        
    5.  **View** - Choose to see the demand results in a Daily, Weekly or Monthly format.
        
4.  The **Calculated** column shows the projected demand.
    
    -   When viewing a plan, the calculated quantity is for reference only.
        
    -   When editing a plan, you can enter a new number in the **Quantity** field to change the demand for each line or interval.
        
5.  **Last Plan Modified** - This field displays the date of the most recent changes to the plan.
    
6.  **Last Projection Method** - This field displays the most recent projection method used.
    
7.  To save your changes, click **Save**.
    

#### To delete a plan:

1.  In the **Demand Plan** list, click **Edit** next to the plan you want to delete.
    
2.  On the Demand Plan page, under **Actions**, click **Delete**.
