You can set the minimum fulfillment percentage for assigning lots at customer level.

#### To set the minimum fulfillment percentage for a customer:

1.  Go to List > Relationships > Customers.
    
2.  On the Customers List page, click **Edit** on the customer to which you want to set the minimum fulfillment percentage.
    
3.  On the Customer page, click the **FEFO Shelf Life** subtab.
    
4.  In the **Minimum Fulfillment %** field, enter the minimum fulfillment percentage.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
