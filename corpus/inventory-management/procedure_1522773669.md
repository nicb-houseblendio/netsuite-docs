Use the following procedure to enable the Supply Chain Control Tower features.

#### To enable Supply Chain Control Tower features:

1.  Go to _Setup > Company > Enable Features_.
    
2.  Click the **Items & Inventory** subtab.
    
3.  Verify that these features are enabled:
    
    -   Inventory
        
    -   Multi-Location Inventory
        
    -   Advanced Inventory Management
        
4.  Check the **Supply Chain Control Tower** box.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
