You can set up inventory and assembly item records for Supply Chain Control Tower. In the Supply Chain Future Horizon field, enter the number of days ahead you want to be included in the snapshot. This will include future orders beyond the snapshot date generated for this item. For more information, see [Entering Inventory Management Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1504284487.html).
