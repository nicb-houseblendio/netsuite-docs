With the Smart Count SuiteApp, you can efficiently manage your inventory counting process. This topic covers how to:

-   [Creating and Managing Count Configurations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0620044529.html)
    
    -   Set up and customize count configuration records for regular or one-time use.
        
    -   Link saved searches to define items for counting.
        
-   [Creating Saved Searches for Task Lists](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1222041311.html)
    
    -   Create saved searches to generate task lists of items to be counted.
        
    -   Apply filters and criteria to target specific items or bins.
        
-   [Performing Inventory Count on Mobile Devices](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_49120505206.html)
    
    -   Conduct directed and spot (nondirected) counts.
        
    -   Scan GS1 barcodes and enter or tally item quantities.
        
    -   Complete all counts in a task list.
        
    -   Review, update, or recount items as needed.
        
-   [Managing Directed and Spot Inventory Counts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_1222062601.html)
    
    -   Use mass updates to adjust count intervals and next count dates.
        
    -   Deactivate or update count configuration records as your needs change.
