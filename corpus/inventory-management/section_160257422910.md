A FEFO shelf life group contains the minimum shelf life days that each associated item must have at the time of delivery. A FEFO shelf life group is defined at item level.

#### To create a FEFO shelf life group:

1.  Go to Lists > FEFO Lot Assignments > FEFO Shelf Life Groups > New.
    
2.  In the **FEFO Shelf Life Group Name** field, enter the name of the FEFO shelf life group.
    
3.  In the **Required Shelf Life Days** field, enter the number of days before the inventory can be allocated to the line item on a sales order.
    
4.  Click **Save**.
