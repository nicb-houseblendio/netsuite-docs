Use Inventory Optimization preferences to set segmentation rules and schedule segmentation calculations.

#### To set Inventory Optimization preferences: {#procedure_88201401085}

1.  Go to _Setup > Accounting > Inventory Management Preferences_.
    
2.  Click the **Optimization Preferences** subtab.
    
3.  In the **General** section, set the **Calculate Inventory Segmentation per Location** preference. This option changes the unit of analysis.
    
4.  In the **ABC Categories** section, review or enter the ABC threshold values.
    
5.  In the **123 Categories** section, review or enter the 123 threshold values.
    
6.  In the **Schedule Inventory Segmentation** section, set whether to run segmentation on a schedule.
    
7.  Click **Save**.
