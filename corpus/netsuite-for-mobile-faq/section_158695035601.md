Frequently asked questions related to security concerns.

### Is the Remember Me function secure?

Your login details are stored using Apple's Keychain security functionality for iOS or Android's KeyStore security for Android. See [Saving your Login Details in NetSuite for Mobile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554101927.html).

### Is any of my confidential information being stored on my mobile device?

None of your NetSuite data is stored on your mobile device unless you choose to enable Remember Me, in which case your login details will be encrypted and stored using using Apple's Keychain security functionality for iOS or Android's KeyStore security for Android. See [Mobile Data Security](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1553523136.html#subsect_1553611681) and [Saving your Login Details in NetSuite for Mobile](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1554101927.html).

### How secure is data transfer between the NetSuite Mobile app and NetSuite?

Data transfer between the NetSuite Mobile app and NetSuite is secured by SSL encryption by default and valid certificates with strong keys are required.
