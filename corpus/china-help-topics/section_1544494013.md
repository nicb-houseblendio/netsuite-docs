## Overview {#bridgehead_1544494129}

The China Voucher List feature provides the following:

-   Lets you create voucher lists for printing, including details like general ledger (GL) accounts and numbers, document numbers, class, location, and departments.
    
-   Lets you view and print multiple vouchers for any transaction type.
    

Important:

To ensure correct information and reporting, make sure that the transaction and record names are in their default values. Go to Setup > Company > Rename Records/Transactions to check your record and transaction names. For more information, see [Change Record and Transaction Names](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N252023.html).

## Generating the China Voucher List {#bridgehead_1544517469}

Here's how you can access and use the China Voucher List feature. You can use filters to find the vouchers you want to review and print.

To customize China Voucher List preferences, see [Setting Up Preferences for China Voucher List](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1544668945.html).

#### Printing the China Voucher List (Batch Printing) {#procedure_1545278192}

1.  Go to Transactions > Financial > China Voucher List.
    
2.  In the **Subsidiary** dropdown, choose the subsidiary you want to see.
    
3.  (Optional) In the **Date From** and **To** fields, enter the date range you want to include.
    
4.  (Optional) In the **Location** dropdown, choose the specific Location you want to include.
    
5.  (Optional) In the **GL Account** list, choose the General Ledger account you want to include.
    
6.  In the **Period From** and **To** fields, select an accounting period you want the report to display.
    
7.  (Optional) In the **Document No. From** and **To** fields, enter the specific range of document numbers you want the report to display.
    
8.  (Optional) In the **Department** dropdown, choose a Department you want to include.
    
9.  (Optional) In the **Transaction Type** list, choose a Transaction Type you want to include.
    
10.  (Optional) In the **Voucher No. From** and **To** fields, enter a voucher number range you want the report to display.
     
     Tip:
     
     You can only sort using numbers in the **Document No.** and **Voucher No.** range filters. Characters or letters are excluded from the range filter.
     
11.  In the **Accounting Context** dropdown, select an accounting context. For more information, see [Accounting Contexts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4640155582.html).
     
12.  (Optional) In the **Class** dropdown, choose the Class category you want to include in the report. For more information, see [Class](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N3201225.html).
     
13.  (Optional) In the **Original Creator** dropdown, choose the user whose report you want to view.
     
14.  (Optional) In **Related Entity**, select a **Type** from the dropdown.
     
15.  (Optional) In the **Name** dropdown that appears under **Related Entity**, select an entity from the list.
     
16.  Click **Refresh**. The page reloads to display the records.
     
17.  (Optional) To view the details of a single voucher, click **View** under the **China Voucher List** subtab. For more information, see [To Review and Print a Single Voucher](#procedure_1545278088).
     
18.  Click **Export PDF** to save the voucher list as a PDF.
     
19.  (Optional) Click **Export Excel** to save the voucher list as an Excel file.
     

Note:

If you select more than 500 voucher lines, you might need to wait a few minutes for the PDF or Excel file. When it's ready, you'll get an email with a link to download it.

#### To Review and Print a Single Voucher {#procedure_1545278088}

1.  Go to Transactions > Financial > China Voucher List.
    
2.  Fill in the required filters. For more information, see [Printing the China Voucher List (Batch Printing)](#procedure_1545278192).
    
3.  Click **View** under the **China Voucher List** subtab.
    
4.  A new screen shows a single voucher list.
    
5.  Click **Print** to save the voucher list as a PDF.
    
6.  (Optional) Click **Document No.** to view detailed transaction information.
