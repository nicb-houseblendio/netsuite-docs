## Overview {#bridgehead_1524031145}

The Ministry of Finance (MoF) of China publishes a standardized chart of accounts for companies operating in China. This standard contains both structure and an account numbering system. You'll need to set up your chart of accounts based on this standard. See [Chart of Accounts Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1439850.html).

The numbering in the Chart of Accounts is standardized according to the following six categories:

-   1xxx - Asset accounts.
    
-   2xxx - Liability accounts.
    
-   3xxx - Common accounts (used between enterprises /bank funds settlement).
    
-   4xxx - Equity Accounts.
    
-   5xxx - Cost accounts.
    
-   6xxx - Profit/Loss accounts.
    

Equity, cost, and profit/loss accounts might vary depending on your company's industry.

## OneWorld Accounts {#bridgehead_1524031373}

For NetSuite OneWorld accounts, you'll need to set up an Accounting Context. This sets up a direct link between a country's local GAAP reporting requirements and its statutory chart of accounts. For more information, see [Accounting Contexts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4640155582.html).

## China Cash Flow Item for Chart of Accounts {#bridgehead_1524031442}

You'll need to set up China Cash Flow Item behavior for your Chart of Accounts. See [Setting up China Cash Flow Item for Chart of Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1524033569.html).
