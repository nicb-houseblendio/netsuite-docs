## Overview {#bridgehead_1524053402}

The China Cash Flow Statement Report shows activities that affect a company's cash balance for a selected period. The report uses the Chinese layout and includes the following information:

-   Operating activities
    
-   Investing activities
    
-   Financing Activities
    
-   Net Change in Cash for Period
    
-   Cash at Beginning of Period
    
-   Cash at End of Period
    

Important:

You'll need the **Expense Reports** feature enabled for an accurate Cash Flow Statement report. Go to Setup > Company > Enable Features > Employees > Expense Reports to enable this feature. For more information, see [Enabling Features](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N232138.html).

Important:

To see your data in this report, tag your transactions in the Journal, Accounts Receivable, and Accounts Payable. For more information, read the following topics:

[Setting up China Cash Flow Item for Chart of Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1524033569.html)

## Generate the China Cash Flow Statement Report {#bridgehead_1524053056}

Follow these steps to generate the China Cash Flow Statement Report.

#### To Generate the China Cash Flow Statement Report:

1.  Go to Reports > Financial > China Cash Flow Statement.
    
2.  In the **Subsidiary** field, select a subsidiary.
    
3.  In the **Period From** field, select the starting accounting period.
    
4.  In the **To** field, select the ending accounting period.
    
5.  In the **Unit** field, select the unit display. This determines how numbers are displayed in the report.
    
    -   **Unit** - Displays numbers in the basic unit of ones.
        
    -   **Thousands** - Displays numbers in thousands.
        
    -   **Ten Thousand** - Displays numbers in ten thousands.
        
6.  (Optional) In the **Location** field, select a location.
    
7.  (Optional) In the **Department** field, select a department.
    
8.  (Optional) In the **Class** field, select a class.
    
9.  Click **Refresh**.
    
10.  (Optional) Click **Export - PDF** to save the report in PDF format.
     
11.  (Optional) Click **Export - Excel** to save the report in Excel (.xls) format.
