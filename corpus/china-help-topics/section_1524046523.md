## VAT Invoice Rules {#bridgehead_1524046754}

The VAT Transactions follow set rules and NetSuite China Localization automatically applies them when generating your VAT transactions:

-   Each invoice has an eight-line limit. If Sales List isn't enabled, NetSuite splits the transactions across two vouchers. For more information, see [Using Sales Lists in China VAT Invoices](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1524047078.html).
    
-   VAT Invoices are split if the amount exceeds the **China Max VAT Invoice Amount** for the subsidiary. See [Setting the China Max VAT Invoice Amount](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1524034456.html)
    
-   When issuing Special VAT Invoices, the customer must have a bank account defined. This can be set in the **China VAT** subtab of the Customer Entity record. See [Setting Up China Bank Account Name](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1524036005.html).
    
-   Discounts are counted and printed as a separate line in the Golden Tax System.
    
-   Shipping and handling costs appear on a separate line.
    

## Governance Limit {#bridgehead_1524046843}

Data governance limits apply when you generate VAT transactions. These include:

-   The Generate China VAT Transactions page can show up to 10,000 lines or 200 invoices, whichever comes first.
    
-   There is an export time out of 20 minutes after clicking Refresh.
    
-   When importing VAT Invoices from the China Golden Tax system, there's a limit of 50 invoices per file. For more information, see [Importing VAT Transactions from the China Golden Tax System](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1524050168.html).
