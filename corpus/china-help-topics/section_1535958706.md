## Overview {#bridgehead_1536018857}

NetSuite China Localization provides you the capability to activate and deactivate features that your organization does not need.

All features are enabled by default. If you disable a feature, the related fields and features in transactions and entity records are hidden.

The following are the current features that can be enabled or disabled:

-   **China VAT Integration**
    
    -   Checking the **China VAT Integration** box lets you export and import China VAT Invoices to the Golden Tax System. You can export your China VAT Invoice transactions from NetSuite, or import them from the Golden Tax System to NetSuite. For more information, see [Generating China VAT Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1524045813.html).
        
    -   Clearing the **China VAT Integration** box disables this feature. Custom fields in transaction records, entity records, and item records will be hidden. The **Generate China VAT Transaction** will also disappear from the navigation.
        
        China VAT Integration can be re-enabled again in the future.
        
        Note:
        
        Merged and exported transactions after generating China VAT Invoices can be edited after this feature is disabled.
        
-   **China Cash Flow Statement**
    
    -   Checking the **China Cash Flow Statement** box enables the China Cash Flow Statement Report feature. This report shows activities that affect a company's cash balance presented in the Chinese layout. This also adds the field China Cash Flow Item when making your transactions. For more information, see [China Cash Flow Statement Report](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1524052340.html).
        
    -   Clearing the **China Cash Flow Statement** box disables this feature. Deactivating this feature removes the **China Cash Flow Item** field and its related custom fields in your transactions, entity records, item records and chart of accounts. China Cash Flow report will not generate or show up in menu. China Cash Flow Item data in your existing transactions will not be deleted. If the China Cash Flow Item field is still visible after deactivating this feature, any value saved in the field when making a transaction will not be saved or collected.
        

## Enable or Disable a China Localization Feature {#bridgehead_1536019996}

The following describes the steps to enable or disable a China Localization feature.

#### To enable or disable a China Localization feature:

1.  Go to Setup > China Localization > Configuration.
    
2.  Click the **Enable Features** subtab.
    
3.  Select a feature to enable or disable.
    
4.  Clear the box of the feature to disable.
    
5.  (Optional) To enable a feature, check the box of the feature to enable.
    
6.  Click **Save**.
