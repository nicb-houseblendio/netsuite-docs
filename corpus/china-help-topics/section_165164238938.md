China Localization helps you meet government requirements in accounts with or without SuiteTax.

## SuiteTax Setup {#bridgehead_24132050576}

The following are the setup requirements for your account in SuiteTax:

-   Enable **SuiteTax** in the account. See [Enabling the SuiteTax Feature](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1513063459.html) and [General SuiteTax Topics](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4283850851.html) for more information.
    
-   Install **SuiteTax Engine**. See [Installing the SuiteTax Engine](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557397007.html).
    
-   Complete the tax records setup. See [Setting Up the SuiteTax Engine for VAT Countries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557735706.html)
    

Note:

Even if you install the China Localization SuiteApp before finishing the SuiteTax setup, you can safely continue setting up China Localization with SuiteTax.

## Install the China Localization SuiteApp {#bridgehead_85134723224}

Follow the installation steps in the help topic [Installing China Localization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_164499038294.html).

Important:

After installing China Localization SuiteApp and setting up subsidiaries in your account, ensure that:

-   Only one China nexus is assigned to a China subsidiary.
    
-   The tax registration of a China nexus is not changed after setup.
    

## Additional Setup For Tax Records In China Localization In Accounts With SuiteTax {#bridgehead_83145520643}

For new accounts in NetSuite, there are additional setup steps that you need to complete for your chart of accounts. The following describe the steps for you to complete after installing the China Localization SuiteApp and completing the tax records setup.

Important:

While China Localization now supports features in accounts with and without SuiteTax, some features are not available yet for accounts that will migrate their data to SuiteTax.

#### To configure your China tax records in accounts with SuiteTax {#procedure_0621122422}

After this, you can validate your nexus, VAT records, and subsidiary settings using the [Validating Your China VAT And Nexus Settings In Accounts With SuiteTax](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_0621031518.html)

1.  Assign a China nexus to one subsidiary. Assigning a China nexus to a subsidiary starts the SuiteTax Engine's automatic tax record provisioning feature. For more information, see [Automatic Tax Record Provisioning for VAT Countries](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1557735917.html).
    
    This step creates two accounts in your chart of accounts, the **Tax on Sales China** and **Tax on Purchases China**.
    
2.  Edit the new accounts.
    
    1.  Go to Lists > Accounting > Accounts.
        
    2.  Select an account:
        
        -   Tax on Sales China
            
        -   Tax on Purchases China
            
    3.  Click **Edit**
        
    4.  In the **Subsidiaries** list, select **Parent Company**.
        
    5.  Check the **Include Children** box.
        
    6.  Click **Save**.
        
3.  Continue assigning your China nexuses to your China subsidiaries.
