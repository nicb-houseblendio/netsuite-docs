## General Company Preferences {#bridgehead_159901424150}

NetSuite lets you set up your company's general preferences for how information is entered and handled in NetSuite. You can find these settings on the General Preferences page.

This page is available at Setup > Company > Preferences > General Preferences. These preferences apply to all users in the account.

For more information about general company preferences, see [Set Company Preferences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N242860.html).

Here are the general preferences you'll need to set up for your account:

-   Languages: Chinese (Simplified)
    
-   Languages: English (International)
    
    Important:
    
    This setting makes sure features in NetSuite China Localization display correctly and are accessible.
    

For more information about configuring multiple languages in your account, see [Configuring Multiple Languages](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N247147.html)
