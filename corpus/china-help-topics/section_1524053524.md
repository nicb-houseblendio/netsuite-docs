Note:

This feature requires the China Localization SuiteApp.

You can verify the Cash Flow Statement Report values using the China Cash Flow Detail Data Collection saved search. The China Localization SuiteApp provides this saved search during installation.

#### To see the China Cash Flow Detail Data Collection:

1.  Go to Lists > Search > Saved Searches.
    
2.  On the Saved Searches page,find the China Cash Flow Detail Data Collection saved search in the **Title** column.
    
3.  Click **View**.
    
    -   Under the **Export Results** column, click **Export (CSV)** to download the results in CSV format.
        
    -   Under the **Persist Results** column, click **Persist (CSV)** to download the persist results in CSV format.
        
4.  (Optional) On the Results page, click **Export - PDF** to save the results in PDF format.
    
5.  (Optional) Click **Export - Microsoft ® Excel** to save the results in Excel (.xls) format.
