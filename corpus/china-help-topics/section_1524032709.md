Note:

This feature requires the China Localization SuiteApp.

NetSuite provides Calendar Months as the predefined accounting period that meets local requirements or common business practices. This option divides the year into twelve calendar sub-periods.

NetSuite also lets you set up one or two extra periods for recording adjustments, beginning, and ending balances.

For more information about setting up accounting periods, see [Accounting Period Setup](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1445585.html).
