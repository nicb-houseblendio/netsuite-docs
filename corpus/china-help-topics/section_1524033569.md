Note:

This feature requires the China Localization SuiteApp

NetSuite China Localization lets you set default values for your general ledger accounts. The **China Cash Flow Item** value you enter in your chart of accounts is used in AP and AR transactions. For more information about the China Localization SuiteApp logic, see [Cash Flow Item Default Logic For China Cash Flow Values In Transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1524053754.html).

#### To setup China Cash Flow Item in Chart of Accounts:

1.  Go to Setup > Accounting > Chart of Accounts.
    
2.  Find an account.
    
3.  Click **Edit**.
    
4.  In the **China Cash Flow Item** field, select the China Cash Flow classification from the drop down list. See [China Cash Flow Item Classification](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1524034299.html).
    
5.  Click **Save**.
